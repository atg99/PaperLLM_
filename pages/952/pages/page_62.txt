cient, they do not directly embed the domain-specific expert knowledge that is mostly 
available and can often be described by mathematical or physical models, such as earth

variety of possibilities to include explicit knowledge as well as practical uncertainty 
representations into a deep learning framework (Willard et al. 2020; De Bézenac et al. 
2019).

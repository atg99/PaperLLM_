While model uncertainty can be (theoretically) reduced by improving the archi-
tecture, the learning process, or the training data set, the data uncertainties cannot be 
explained away (Kendall and Gal 2017). Therefore, DNNs that are capable of handling 
uncertain inputs and that are able to remove or quantify the model uncertainty and give 
a correct prediction of the data uncertainty are of paramount importance for a variety of 
real world mission- and safety-critical applications.

gies and their potential applications (Farquhar et al. 2020; Wenzel et al. 2020; Mukhoti and 
Gal 2018; Feng et al. 2019).
3.3  Ensemble methods
3.3.1  Principles of ensemble methods
Ensembles derive a prediction based on the predictions received from multiple so-called 
ensemble members. They target a better generalization by making use of synergy effects 
among the different models, arguing that a group of decision-makers tend to make bet-
ter decisions than a single decision-maker (Sagi and Rokach 2018; Hansen and Salamon

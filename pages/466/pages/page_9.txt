5 Conclusion
We proposed an adversarial dual-view learning approach for semi-supervised
medical image segmentation and demonstrated its eectiveness on publicly avail-
able three medical datasets. Our extensive experiments showed that employing
a min-max paradigm into multi-view learning scheme sharpens boundaries be-
tween dierent regions in prediction masks and yield a performance close to
full-supervision with limited annotations. The dual view training can still be
improved by self-tuning mechanisms, which will be considered in our future
works.

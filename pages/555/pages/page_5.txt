4.1.2 Full Training Phase
In this phase, we fully train the searched models on the MS
COCO training dataset, and select the best one by eval-
uating them on MS COCO validation images. Note that
our training conﬁgurations are exactly the same as those
in FCOS for fair comparison. Input images are resized to
short size 800and the maximum long side is set to be 1333 .
The models are trained using 4V100 GPUs with batch size
16for90K iterations. The initial learning rate is 0.01and
reduces to one tenth at the 60K-th and 80K-th iterations.
The improving tricks are applied only on the ﬁnal model
(w/improv).

5. Conclusion
Our paper introduces a comprehensive library for se-
mantic segmentation for well-known models such as Seg-
Net, FCN, UNet, and PSPNet. The library empowers re-
searchers and practitioners in the ﬁeld of computer vision
with a toolset to achieve pixel-level understanding of im-
ages. We have demonstrated the efﬁcacy and robustness of
these models, underscoring their applicability in addressing
diverse segmentation applications.

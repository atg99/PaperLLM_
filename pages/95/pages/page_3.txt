The quality of the data set is of utmost importance because the core of data mining is data itself.
Figure 2 depicts the workﬂow of data preparation (the blue box) and the modeling process (the green
box). There are six steps in the preprocessing process.

from the data set. HW refers to the number of days used to predict the production rate at the next day.

Discussions
To our knowledge, there are other public and commercial software for ART 24,25, but ARCliDS is the first interac-
tive software dedicated to KBR-ART that will be available through a web portal. In this work, we have shown 
its applicability to adaptive RT and SBRT. However, ARCliD’s underlying technology can be generalized to any 
other DTR to optimize sequential decision-making with multi-omics data for deciding the order of treatments, 
including multi-modality treatment, given that an artificial treatment environment can be sufficiently modeled.

4.3.2 Comparing to Using FID as Reward
Besides IS, another important evaluation metric for GAN is
the FID score [50]: the smaller FID indicates better gen-eration quality. What if we use the reciprocal of FID, in-
stead of IS, as the AutoGAN controller’s reward? To answer
this question, on CIFAR-10, we search for another model
from scratch, using the reciprocal of FID as the reward (ev-
erything else unchanged), and compare to the AutoGAN
trained with IS reward. During searching, for both models,
we evaluate both IS and FID values periodically with the
training progressing. Fig. 8 plots the curves, from which we
can see that while both rewards can increase as the search
goes on, the FID-driven search (red curves) shows compa-
rable performance with the IS-driven search (blue curves),
under both metrics. However, computing FID is more time-
consuming than calculating IS, as it requires to calculate the
co-variance and mean of large matrices. Thus, we choose
IS as the default reward for AutoGAN.

4.3.3 Parameter dynamic-resetting
It is well known that the training process of GAN is ex-
tremely unstable and prone to mode collapse [5] after long
time training. It could be a waste of time to continue train-
ing the shared collapsed model. Hence, we introduce pa-
rameter dynamic-resetting to alleviate the issue. For com-
parison, we conduct two AutoGAN experiments on CIFAR-
10, with the parameter sharing strategy proposed in [46],
and our proposed dynamic-resetting plus parameter sharing
strategy. We evaluate IS during both training processes. As
plotted in Figure 9, we can see that they achieve comparable
performance. However, the training process with dynamic-
resetting only takes 43 hours, while it will take 72 hours
without dynamic-resetting.

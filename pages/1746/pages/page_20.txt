•Carlini and Wagner Attacks
Carlini et al., [72] explained that defensive distillation does not improve the robustness. To this end,
they proposed three new attack algorithms and demonstrated that the defensive distillation completely
fails against these attacks. They argue that their attacks are more eective concerning the three

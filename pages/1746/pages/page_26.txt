•GAN Defense
Lee et al., [89] directly train a network besides the generator and tries to create the perturbation for
that network. Thus, the classier can correctly distinguish between pristine (clean) and manipulated
one (perturb image). The proposed approach is robust against adversarial attacks such as FGSM [4].
Defense-GAN is another methodology defending classiers versus adversarial attacks by training the
model with the distribution of unperturbed images [90]. The system tries to obtain a close output,
which does not include the adversarial modications. Therefore, this system can be employed as a
defense technique against any attacks in the LK scenario.
While modern GAN can create fake images with high quality, re-construction of relations among color
bands is expectedly dicult. Barni et al., [43] proposed a methodology for detecting GAN generated
face images via co-occurrences analysis.

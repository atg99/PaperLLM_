Defining two additional weight matrices and a bias vector,
Wr= (△T)WsB (27)
Wx= (△T)WsC (28)
⃗θs= (△T)Ws⃗ϕ (29)
transforms the above system into the canonical Recurrent Neural Network (RNN) form:
⃗ s[n] =Ws⃗ s[n−1] +Wr⃗ r[n−1] +Wx⃗ x[n] +⃗θs (30)
⃗ r[n] =G(⃗ s[n]) (31)

generality by appending an additional element, always set to 1, to the input signal vector, ⃗ x[n], and increasing the row dimensions of Wxby1.

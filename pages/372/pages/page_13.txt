C Summary of Pilot Studies
We carry out series of pilot studies varying user
interface, explanation granularity and type of be-
havioral information. We choose 4 (ﬁnal-year) un-
dergraduate Computer Science students, who have
some experience in NLP through projects, but not
trained on Linguistics and Logic.
C.1 Phase 1: Global Explanation,
Template-wise Accuracy Prediction
The ﬁrst phase of our pilot studies involved a
Google Form-based questionnaire as an interface10.
We envisioned a training stage, where the partici-
pants will be shown behavioral summary of a black-
box NLI system; and a testing stage where partici-
pants will be asked to place bids (out of 5) on the
most accurate bin(s) for unseen templates. First,
we chose these 25 test templates, similarly in the
ﬁnal study. Then, we design the three variants (or

4.3 Additional Applications
For brevity, we refer the reader to more NEORL applications available on the webpage4. We illustrate how RL
algorithms can solve classical combinatorial problems such as travel salesman problem5and knapsack problem6, while
NEORL hyperparameter tuning tools help RL algorithms to achieve an optimal performance. In addition, we have
demonstrated how variety of classical and modern evolutionary algorithms can be used to design a three-bar truss7,
welded beam8, pressure vessel9, and others, which involve solving different problem types with different constraints.
Our results in this work, which come from a single framework, show very competitive performance with the literature
results that include a very diverse range of algorithms and implementations.

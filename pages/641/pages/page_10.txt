bioinformatics, computer vision and recommendation system.
While our research focuses on performance by designing a universal propagation mechanism on
general networks with any structural properties, like many other GCNs, it provides limited explanation
to its prediction. We advocate peer researchers to look into this to enhance the interpretability of
modern GCNs, and make GCNs applicable in more risk-sensitive applications.

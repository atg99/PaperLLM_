5.1. How Does FedScale Help FL Benchmarking?
Existing benchmarks are insufﬁcient to evaluate the various
metrics needed in today’s FL. We note that the performance
of existing benchmarks and FedScale are quite close in the
same settings if we turn off the optional system traces in
FedScale. Because the underlying training and FL protocols
in evaluations are the same. However, the limited scalability
can mislead the practical FL performance. Next, we show
the effectiveness of FedScale in benchmarking different FL
aspects over its counterparts.

2.2 Pruning Neurons
It is difficult to implement unstructured pruning practically since, at inference time, special support is needed for matrix
multiplication in the sparse space. Pruning away neurons leads to removal of a row or a column from a weight matrix,
thereby avoiding sparse matrix handling (see Fig. 2(C)). However, compared to pruning weights, pruning neurons is
less flexible since we need to find entire rows/columns for deletion. In this section, we discuss ways of determining
neurons that can be pruned.

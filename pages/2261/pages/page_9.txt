6 Conclusion
In this work, we introduce the Conflict-Averse Gradient descent (CAGrad) algorithm that explicitly
optimizes the minimum decrease rate of any specific task’s loss while still provably converging to
the optimum of the average loss. CAGrad generalizes the gradient descent and multiple gradient
descent algorithm, and demonstrates improved performance across several challenging multi-task
learning problems compared to the state-of-the-art methods. While we focus mainly on optimizing
the average loss, an interesting future direction is to look at main objectives other than the average
loss under the multi-task setting.

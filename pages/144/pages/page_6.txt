4.2 Molecular Generation
Generation &Reconstruction Performance. We measure the
performance of all evaluated models on 10,000 randomly gener-
ated molecules and report the mean and standard deviation of
results over 5 runs in Table 1, and Table 2. Compared to VAE-based
models, GF-VAE as well as all flow-based models achieve 100% re-
construction accuracy. At the same time, flow-based models share
significantly high uniqueness ratio. Such advanced performance
is attributed to the invertible characteristic of normalizing flows.
Compared with the sequential flow-based model GraphAF, GF-VAE
has improvements around 14% and 0.9% w.r.t. the scores for QM9
and ZINC-250K, respectively. In contrast with one-shot flow-based
models, our model outperforms GraphNVP and GRF with a huge
margin. As for MoFlow, although it shows almost equally competi-
tive performance compared to our GF-VAE, its model parameters
are more than two times of GF-VAE. We next deeply analyze the
time cost of MoFlow and our GF-VAE model.

Comment 2. Causal versus Descriptive Estimands
In general the variances Vsampling(N1,N0,n1,n0) andVdesign(N1,N0,n1,n0) cannot be ranked:
the sampling variance can be very close to zero if the sampling rate ( N0+N1)/(n0+n1) is close
to one, but it can also be larger than the design variance if the samplin g rate is small and the
variance of the treatment eﬀect is substantial. /square

ciency of our dynamic head by controlling the depth (num-

backbone, while largely improving the accuracy. It demon-
strates the efﬁciency of our method.
Generalization on Existing Object Detectors. We eval-

RepPoints [ 30]. These methods represent a wide variety of
object detection frameworks ( e.g., two-stage vs. one-stage,

As shown in Table 3, our dynamic head signiﬁcantly boosts
all popular object detectors by 1.2∼3.2AP. It demon-
strates the generality of our method.

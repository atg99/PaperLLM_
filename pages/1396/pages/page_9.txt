5 Conclusion
In this paper, we present SegFormer, a simple, clean yet powerful semantic segmentation method
which contains a positional-encoding-free, hierarchical Transformer encoder and a lightweight All-
MLP decoder. It avoids common complex designs in previous methods, leading to both high efﬁciency
and performance. SegFormer not only achieves new state of the art results on common datasets,
but also shows strong zero-shot robustness. We hope our method can serve as a solid baseline
for semantic segmentation and motivate further research. One potential limitation is that even our
lightest model may still be too heavy for some edge devices. Thus mixed-precision training, pruning,
hardware-friendly attention designs and energy consumption are important parts of our future work.
Broader Impact
Efﬁciency, accuracy, and robustness are important aspects of AI models. Our work pushes the
boundary of semantic segmentation models in these three aspects. We envision that the work will
beneﬁt a wide range of safety-critical applications, such as autonomous driving and robot navigation.
The proposed method improves the “in-the-wild” robustness of these applications, ultimately leading
to better safety. Despite such improvement, we fully understand this work is by no means perfect and
there are still many challenges towards reliable real world application. Our models may be subject
to biases and other possible undesired mistakes, depending on how they are trained in reality. Our

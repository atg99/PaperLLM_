In the industrial and manufacturing sector, artificial intelligence (AI) offers tremendous
potential for development in terms of efficiency, sustainability, and competitiveness. A
human-centered AI approach is essential to ensure that engineering technologies and
systems are safe, ethical, and beneficial for the people involved. There is a need for
a significant shift in perspective within the industrial-manufacturing sector. Initially,
the adoption of AI focused on engineering, computer science, and statistics, where the
emphasis was primarily on data management and analysis to improve production processes.

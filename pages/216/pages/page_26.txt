7 Conclusion and Future Work
We provide an upper bound for the covering number of the hypothesis space induced by
deep neural networks with residual connections. The covering bound for ResNet, as an
exemplary case, is then proposed. Combining various classic results in statistical learning
theory, we further obtain a generalization bound for ResNet. With the generalization
bound, we theoretically guarantee the performance of ResNet on unseen data. Considering
the generality of our results, the generalization bound for ResNet can be easily extended
to many state-of-the-art algorithms, such as DenseNet and ResNeXt.

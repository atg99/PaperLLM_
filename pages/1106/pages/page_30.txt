The choice of λmax=n1/2ingenerate.lambdas is important as it guarantees that the ﬁrst
solution will be the empty graph (see Aragam and Zhou 2015, Section 5.3). We use a linear
scale for the grid and we set edge.threshold = 500 in order to terminate the algorithm
early if any estimate has more than 500 edges (although note that in this case this constraint
never becomes active).

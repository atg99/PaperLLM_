Given the nonconvex nature of the minimization problems here, one future direction is to
incorporate stochastic optimization into our package to enhance its global search ability. For
example, stochastic gradient descent may be used in the algorithm for discrete Bayesian
networks, which will reduce the computational complexity as well. We are also interested
in developing divide-and-conquer strategies for ultra-large graphs, say on the scale of 105
nodes. Along these lines, we are also exploring parallel and distributed implementations of
these algorithms. Finally, our package can be combined with various post-learning functions
for multi-stage learning of causal networks. Given the DAG learned from sparsebn , one may
furtherinfercausalrelationsinasubgraphofinterestbymakingadditionalcausalassumptions

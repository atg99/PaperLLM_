10.5.2 Scores Obtained in the 2018 Challenge
The 2018 challenge lasted 4 months (November 30, 2017 to March 31, 2018). Asin the previous challenge, top-ranked solutions were obtained and open sourced.
Table 10.6 shows the results of both phases of the 2018 challenge. As a reminder,
this challenge had a feedback phase and a blind test phase, the performances of thewinners in each phase are reported.
Performance in this challenge was slightly lower than that observed in the
previous edition. This was due to the difﬁculty of the tasks (see below) and the factthat data sets in the feedback phase included three deceiving datasets (associated to
tasks from previous challenges, but not necessarily similar to the data sets used in
the blind test phase) out of ﬁve. We decided to proceed this way to emulate a realisticAutoML setting. Although harder, several teams succeeded at returning submissions
performing better than chance.

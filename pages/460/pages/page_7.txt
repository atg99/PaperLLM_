4.3.  Training and Classification  
Three of the 15 cases of the dataset were used for testing and evaluation , with a total of 475 
images. Among these, 454 images were used for training and validation, and 45 images were used 
for testing.  
The first training and testing experiments were carried out using the U -Net model in [ 45]. The 
U-Net model is trained to perfo rm semantic segmentation on medical images. It is based on VGG -16, 
as discussed before. The results were near perfect to extract the liver region. However, it failed 
completely when tested to extract the tumor regions from the image. In this case, the tumo r region 
was almost missed or predicted as others.  
The proposed architecture is based on the SegNet model [ 37], which is an encoder network, and 
a corresponding decoder network connected to a 2D multi -classification layer for pixel -based 
semantic segmenta tion. However, the final classification layer was replaced by 2D binary 
classification. The VGG -16 trained model was imported for the encoder part. Figure 7 shows an 
illustration of the proposed network architecture. To improve the training, class weightin g was used 
to balance the classes and calculate the median frequency class weights.

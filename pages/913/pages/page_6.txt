2.1 Learning feature embeddings
Methods of learning feature embeddings implicitly assume that the network is powerful to extract
discriminative features and can generalize well to novel classes. Early approaches aim at a
task-agnostic embedding model that is eﬀective for any task. More recently, endeavors are
made to learn a task-speciﬁc embedding model for better distinguishing the classes at hand.
Furthermore, techniques for data augmentation and multi-task learning are leveraged to address
the issues of data scarcity and overﬁtting.

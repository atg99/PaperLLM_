Interpretability & DTs. Interpretability is generally regarded as a subjective con -
cept, without a rigorous deﬁnition [ 37], albeit different authors have proposed differ-
ent requirements for interpretability [ 37,63]. Throughout this paper, we associate in-
terpretability with irreducible sets of feature-value pai rs that are sufﬁcient for the pre-
diction7. Moreover, as argued in Section 1 , it is generally accepted that DTs epitomize
interpretability. Nevertheless, there is recent work that relates DT interpretability with
DTs being shallow [ 37,42], but also work that proposes counterfactual explanations for
meeting interpretability desiderata for DTs [ 63].

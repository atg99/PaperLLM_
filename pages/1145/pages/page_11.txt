training tools ITI(Incremental Tree Induction ) [65,31] and IAI(Interpretable AI ) [10,28].

constructing shallow DTs. To enforce IAI to produce shallow DTs and achieve high ac-
curacy, it is set to use the optimal tree classiﬁer method wit h the maximal depth of 613.

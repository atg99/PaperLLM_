types. The deep CNN has been shown to learn spec tral amplitude changes in the input [62]. It has 
also demonstrated high sensitivity to phase features  in the early stages of the network and amplitude 
features in later layers [62]. The deep CNN has been developed for the purpose of showing that 
generic CNN designs can be utilized as a decoding to ol across multiple BCI/ EEG paradigms [34]. Like 
the shallow CNN, the deep CNN has been applied to EEG relating to imagined or executed tasks [34],

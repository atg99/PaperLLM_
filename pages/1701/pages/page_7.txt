Derive Task-To-Target Model Mappings :
Fine-tuning already has a good performance in
baseline models with small samples. Nevertheless,
in multi-task learning, a large scale of tasks are
learned just as one task, which leads to a terrible
generalization of the model. In contrast, meta-
learning dual-samples the data and task space
using episodic-training mechanism, ﬁnding latent
associations between different tasks and thus having
a good description of the whole task space.

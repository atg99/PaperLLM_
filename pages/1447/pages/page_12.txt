3.3.2 Speech Translation (AST)
Following [20], we use CoV oST 2 [18] to benchmark multilingual speech translation. We evaluate
the multilingual XX-to-English task that covers translation from 21 source languages into English.
Depending on the language, the training data ranges in size from 1 - 264 hours.
Besides speech translation data, we also add text-to-text translation data for training the model as
in [20]. This dataset includes the text translation data from CoV oST 2 combined with all data from
either WMT or TED Talks, as available.

Table 9 presents the NLUs’ performance in extracting
unique entities. We ﬁnd that IBM Watson outperforms other
NLUs with an F1-measure of 31.6% in extracting unique
entities. We examine the results of extracting unique entities
and ﬁnd two factors that impact the NLUs’ performance.
First, the NLUs depend on the entities syntax similarity to
recognize the entities in the queries of the testing set. For

extracted ‘Receiving 0.0’ as a Framework entity from the

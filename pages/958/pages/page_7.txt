QCNN for the S= 1Haldane Chain
As discussed in the main text, the (spin-1 =2) 1D clus-
ter state belongs to an SPT phase protected by Z2Z2
symmetry, a phase which also contains the celebrated
S= 1 Haldane chain30. It is thus natural to ask whether
this circuit can be used to detect the phase transition
between the Haldane phase and an S= 1 paramagnetic
phase, which we numerically demonstrate here.

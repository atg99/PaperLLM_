4.3. Plot Correlation Matrix  
Correlation matrices graphically give us an idea of how features relate to each other and can 
help us predict which features are most relevant to the prediction.  
 
Figure 1. Results of training matrix . 
In the heat map we can clearly see that most features are not correlated with other features, but 
there are some features that are positively or negatively correlated with each other. For example, V2 
and V5 are strongly negatively correlated with a feature  called Amount. We also see some correlation 
with V20 and Amount. This gives us a deeper understanding of the data we have.

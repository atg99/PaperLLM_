VI. C ONCLUSION
This article provides a systematic introduction and literature
review of self-supervised learning for the remote sensing
community. We summarize three main categories of self-
supervised methods, introduce representative works and link
them from natural images to remote sensing data. Moreover,
we provide a preliminary benchmark and extened analysis of
four modern contrastive self-supervised learning on three pop-
ular remote sensing datasets. Finally, fundamental problems
and future directions are listed and discussed.

Last but not least, the time variable is becoming increasingly
important despite the modality. This raises the interest of
getting inspiration from video self-supervised learning, yet
time stamps in remote sensing images are quite different from
frames in videos, as well as the special objectives (e.g. change
detection in remote sensing). Therefore, how to make the best
of temporal information is a common question to be answeredin all modalities. An early progress is shown in Fig. 21 where
seasonal information is used for data augmentation [201].

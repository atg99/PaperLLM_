DALEX (Lipovetsky 2022 ) is a local and global, post-hoc, model-agnostic expla-
nation method. dalex reveals the features importance through an implementation of
avariable attribution approach (Robnik-Šikonja and Kononenko 2008 ) that consists
of a decomposition of the model’s predictions in which each decomposition can be
seen as a local gradient, and it is used to identify the contribution of each attribute.
dalex also allows the calculus of shap values. In Fig. 5are reported local explanations

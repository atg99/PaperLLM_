6 Explanations for image data

acting on images. In particular, we distinguish the following types of explanations:
Saliency Maps (SM, Sect. 6.1,Concept Attribution (CA, Sect. 6.2),Prototypes (PR,
Sect. 6.3) and Counterfactuals (CF, Sect. 6.4). Table 5(organized as the previous one)

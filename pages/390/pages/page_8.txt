Training, validation, and test sets are three separate groups into which the data is divided throughout the dataset preparation step. The test set is used to gauge the efficacy and performance of the trained model, while the training set is used to develop the model and the validation set is used to evaluate and verify the training procedure. Due to this division, the machine learning model may be thoroughly trained, validated, and evaluated. The distribution is shown in Figure 5.

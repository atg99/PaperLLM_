F.2 Interaction between CntrLoss and BCR
In Section 5, we experiment with applying both CntrLoss and BCR to regularizing BigGAN. We
achieve new state-of-the-art FID= 8:30with the strength of CntrLoss Cntr= 0:1and the strength
of BCRBCR= 5. While BCR enforces the consistency loss directly on the discriminator logits,
with Cntr together, it further helps to learn better representations which can be reﬂected in generation
performance eventually.

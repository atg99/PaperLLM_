Appendix A: The Chebyshev polynomials used in
NEP
The Chebyshev polynomials of the ﬁrst kind are de-
ﬁned in terms of the initial values T0(x) = 1 and T1(x) =
x, and the recurrence relation ( n≥2)
Tn(x) = 2xTn−1(x)−Tn−2(x). (A1)
The derivative dTn(x)/dxis related to the Chebyshev
polynomials of the second kind Un−1(x) forn >0:
dTn(x)
dx=nUn−1(x). (A2)
The Chebyshev polynomials of the second kind are de-
ﬁned in terms of the initial values U0(x) = 1 and U1(x) =
2x, and the recurrence relation ( n≥2)
Un(x) = 2xUn−1(x)−Un−2(x).(A3)

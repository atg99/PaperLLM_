D. Evaluation of the computational eﬃciency
1. Computational speed
In Fig.4, we compare the computational speeds of the
four ML potentials in MD simulations. The computa-
tional speed is measured as the product of the number of
atoms and the number of MD steps divided by the com-
putation time, which is in units of atom-step per second.
To measure the speed, we used a simulation cell with
8×103(for bulk silicon and PbTe) or 6 ×103(for 2D
silicene) atoms and run an MD simulation for 100 steps
in the NVT ensemble, outputting basic thermodynamic
quantities every 10 steps. The quip[15],mlip[16], and
DeePMD-kit [ 18] packages were built as libraries to be
called by the lammps package [ 26].
Both the GAP-SOAP and MTP potentials are imple-
mented in CPU only, and we used 72 Intel Xeon-Gold
6240 CPU cores (two nodes, each with 36 cores) par-
allelized by the message passing interface (MPI). Note

4.1.1. General-Purpose APLs
A survey on agent-oriented programming from the software engineering perspective
can be found in [ 6]. One of the main challenges reported in the survey for APL developers
is the need to bridge the cognitive gap that exists between the concepts underpinning
mainstream languages and those underpinning AOP . In [ 21] the authors try to ﬁll this gap
focusing on understanding the relationship between AgentSpeak(L) [ 15] and OOP with the
goal of trying to reduce the perceived cognitive gap. Such a work proposes a new statically
typed agent programming language entitled ASTRA.

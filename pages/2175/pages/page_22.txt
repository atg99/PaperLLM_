4.2 Model Interpretability Evaluation
In some situations, dierent deep models exhibit dierent abilities to expose un-
derstandable terms to humans. Even the same network architecture, training on
dierent datasets may have dierent interpretability scores [24]. Given the same
trustworthy interpretation algorithm and any two models, model interpretability
evaluation methods are used to measure and compare the interpretability between
models. In this subsection, we introduce four model interpretability evaluation
methods, i.e., Network Dissection [24], Pointing Game [189], Consensus [104] and
the one through OOD Samples [59, 60].
The basic idea for evaluating the model interpretability for Network Dissec-
tion [24], Pointing Game [189] and Consensus [104] is to measure the overlap be-
tween semantic items (e.g., segmentation ground truth by humans, or cross-model
ensemble of explanations) and interpretation results, as shown in Fig. 3.

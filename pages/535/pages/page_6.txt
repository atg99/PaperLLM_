Multi-modal alignment: We can further take advantage
of both the temporal information and the multi-modal data

aligned.

Implementation details. We use a (2+1)D ResNet-50
as our backbone network. Given a loss function, we train
the network for 100 epochs on 2 million unlabeled videos.
The learning rate is set to 0.1 (during both the evolution and
the ﬁnal training).
During search, we used smaller networks, similar to a
ResNet-18 for faster learning. For search, the ﬁtness of each
model can be found in 4 hours using 8 GPUs. The ﬁnal
model uses 64 GPUs for 3 days to train (equivalent time to
training I3D/(2+1)D ResNet-50 on Kinetics).

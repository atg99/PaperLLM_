Iterated prompting While the typical methodology in machine learning for im-
proving results from an algorithm focus on improvements to the training data
or algorithm, our users have exhibited a strong preference for modifying the
prompts they provide instead. We view this as exemplifying “natural language
as an API,” [43] wherein the actual mapping between the images and the text
is less important than the fact that text is a natural medium for human inter-
action. The end-goal is to obtain desirable images using whatever text input is
necessary .

Hype surrounds the promotions, aspirations, and notions of “artiﬁcial intelligence (AI) for
social good” and its related permutations. These terms, as u sed in data science and
particularly in public discourse, are vague. Far from being irrelevant to data scientists or
practitioners of AI, the terms create the public notion of th e systems built. Through a
critical reﬂection, I explore how notions of AI for social go od are vague, offer insufﬁcient
criteria for judgement, and elide the externalities and str uctural interdependence of AI
systems.Instead,theﬁeldknownas“AIforsocialgood”isbe stunderstoodandreferred
to as “AI for not bad.”

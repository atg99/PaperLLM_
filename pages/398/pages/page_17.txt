On the other hand, the drawback of the model is that it compromises accuracy when the
vehicles pass at high speed and overlap, the video frame is narrow, and the video quality is
poor. From the beneﬁt–cost (B/C) analysis, the B/C was found to be greater than or equal
to 1.54, which implies that the automated method improves the beneﬁt by 54% compared to
the traditional manual method. Therefore, the developed algorithm might be an excellent
replacement for the other expensive automated counting methods.

3.1. Automated Counts
The pre-recorded videos were analyzed using the developed computer algorithms.
Since the video was collected from 8 AM to 8 PM for each site for both day 1 and day 2, the
total duration of video for an access point of a site was 20 h. The total number of access
points for all 10 sites was 16. So, the total duration of video for all ten sites was 320 h.
Therefore, in this automated counting method, a total of 320 h of video was processed. The
entry and exit counts applying the automated counting method for the ten sites are shown

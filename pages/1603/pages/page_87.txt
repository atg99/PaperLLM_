5.2 Conceptual Framework
5.2.1 Value-Based Corporate Management
Companies create value for customers, employees, suppliers, society, and investors
in a multidimensional manner. This requires integral corporate management, which
includes value-oriented corporate management characterized by integrity, reliability,
trust, and mutual recognition.
A company is a social system that creates values through cooperation. Each
individual understands that the purposes he/she pursues are at the same time a
condition for realizing the purposes of the other. And it is the people in the company
and their participation that are thus able to generate values and future cash ﬂows.

2.1 Learning Objectives
1. Identify the importance of a rationality-based view in economic models and
approaches.
2. Assess the limitations of this one-sided approach in both economic and ethical
terms.
3. Explain and justify de ﬁciencies such as irrationality and cognitive biases and
establish a moral distance to them.
4. Recognize the ethical consequences of a misinterpretation or misunderstanding of
data sets.
5. Re ﬂect recent trends in the convergence of morals and economics and critically
identify the motivation behind them.
6. Realize the additional ethical challenge AI technologies imposes on decision-
making, as psychological distance increases.

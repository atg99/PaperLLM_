2.2.2 Efﬁciency and Scalability Related Challenges
The prevalence of social media networks and mobile/wearable
devices has led to increasing demands for analyzing visual
data. However, mobile/wearable devices have limited com-putational capabilities and storage space, making efﬁcientobject detection critical.
The efﬁciency challenges stem from the need to localize
and recognize, computational complexity growing with the(possibly large) number of object categories, and with the
(possibly very large) number of locations and scales within
a single image, such as the examples in Fig. 6c, d.

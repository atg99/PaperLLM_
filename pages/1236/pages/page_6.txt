matches the random forest OOB prediction, giving the same
OOB error rate in both the classiﬁcation and regression
settings. The OOB error rate is typically used to estimate
the forest’s generalization error and quantify its goodness
of ﬁt, this indicates that RF-GAP accurately represents the
geometry learned by the random forest.

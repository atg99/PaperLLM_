In summary, as listed in Table 4, while the HCAI approach inherits the human -centered 
design philosophy, the HCAI approach goes beyond the conventional human -centered approach 
with: (1) expanded work focuses (not simply “human” related work); (2) expanded 
interdisciplinary approaches and m ethods; (3) expanded membership of the interdisciplinary 
team. With the expansion, the HCAI approach can address the unique issues and challenges of AI 
systems  more strategically and tactically. In addition, by taking the complementary strength of the 
technology -driven AI approach and the HCAI approach, we believe that HCI and AI 
professionals  can partner in developing human -centered AI systems.

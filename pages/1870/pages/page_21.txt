 • Objective :  The HAII work  intends to effectively address the unique issues of AI systems

• Scope : HAII studies the impacts of various factors on humans’ interaction  specifically

• Primary players : As a highly interdisciplinary area,  the primary players of HAII include a 
group of professionals from the communities of HCI, human factors, AI, computer 
science, neuroscience, data science, psychology, social science, and anybody who aims to 
develop AI systems. Obviously, the collaboration when doing HAII work is broader than 
HCI, requiring new membership, such as, professionals from AI, data science, 
neuroscience , and other dis ciplines . We believe that t he HCI community need s to take the 
leading role  as we did 40 years ago , when promoting the human -centered approach for 
computing systems.    
 
4.3 Promoting the HAII work for enabling HCAI   
As discussed earlier, t he overall goal of promoting the HAII work  is to further enable the 
implementation of the HCAI approach to deliver human -centered AI systems. To achieve this 
goal, we further illustrate the relationship between the HCAI approach and the HAII area . Table 5 
maps out some main HAII research topics and challenges with the specific design goals as 
defined in the HCAI framework (see  Figure 2).  As Table 5 shows, there are many challenges in 
these main  HAII research topics  (which we will elaborate in Section 5) , where HCI professionals 
can make (or continue to make) unique contributions . Addressing these challenges will help 
achieve the HCAI goals  as defined in Figure 2 , eventually developing human -centered AI 
systems.

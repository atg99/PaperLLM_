Why do we promote the research and application of HAII within the HCI community 
since there are HCI professionals who  have been working on the development of AI systems ?   
First, HAII helps recognize and emphasize on the fundamental differences between non -
AI and AI systems, getting more attention across professional communities (e.g., HCI, human 
factors, AI, computer science ). The primary object of the HAII work is AI systems, instead of just 
conventional  non-AI computing systems . HAII will reinforce HCI professionals to recognize the 
unique challenges and issues of AI technology, so that we can have new design thinking, 
understand the boundary and focus of the HAII research and applications, and develop effective 
strategy and methods to effectively address the unique challenges and issues in AI systems.  
Second, we are responding to the calls from the HCI and human factors communit ies as 
discussed earlier (e.g., Salmon, Hancock & Carden, 2019; Hancock, 2019, 2020; Lau, 
Hildebrandt & Jeon, 2020 ; Endsley, 2017, 2018, 2019; Stephanidis, Salvendy et al., 2019; 
Shneiderman, 2020a, 2020c ; Xu, 2019 a).  Thus, HAII can serve as an urgent wake -up call to the 
HCI community that we must get renewed attention to reassess and recast extant HCI approaches 
and methods, and prepare ourselves with necessary AI skillset and knowledge, so that we can take 
immediate actions with a new design thinking strategy to more influentially address the emerging 
and specific challenges from AI systems.   
Third , HAII , as labeled  by itself , helps us recognize that human’s interaction with AI 
systems is always required . As HCI professionals, we ad vocate to keep human s at the center  and 
as the ultimate decision makers  when developing AI systems to minimize  AI-related s afety risk s.

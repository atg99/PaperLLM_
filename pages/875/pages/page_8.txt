6. Conclusion
In this paper, we explore a close relationship between
two powerful techniques, convolution and self-attention. By
decomposing the operations of both modules, we show that
they share the same computation overhead on projecting the
input feature maps. On this basis, we take a step forward
and propose a hybrid operator to integrate self-attention
and convolution modules by sharing the same heavy oper-
ations. Extensive results on image classification and object
detection benchmarks demonstrate the effectiveness and ef-
ficiency of the proposed operator.

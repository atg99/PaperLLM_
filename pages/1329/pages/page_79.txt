70 8 Privacy Issues of AI
The future seems to be moving towards greater data collection. The value placed
on privacy depends a great deal on the culturally and historical tendencies of the
populace. Artiﬁcial intelligence expands the ways in which data is used, and may offer
some predictive ability with respect to what is being collected. Future generations
will need to decide the boundary that deﬁnes privacy and data use.

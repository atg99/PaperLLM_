Results and discussion
The implementation and training of the proposed capsulenetwork is done on a system which is with nVIDIA CUDAarchitecture. The system is equipped with AMD Ryzen
2700x processor having eight cores and with a RAM of 32
GB. The system uses nVIDIA Graphics Processing Unit withDDR6 Memory of 8GB. Keras API running on top of tensorﬂow in python was used for training and evaluating the pro-
posed network. The efﬁciency of the reformed CapsNet is
assessed by taking the Messidor dataset described in section3.1. In the evaluation, 75% of the images are considered as
a training set and 25% of the images are taken for testing.
75% of the training data is again partitioned into two parts fortraining and validation. 90% of images in the training dataset

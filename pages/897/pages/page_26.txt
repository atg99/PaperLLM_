 3.6.3. Sliding window or overlapping window O’shea et al. (2017) presented a novel end-to-end architecture that learns representations from raw EEG signal by CNN for the task of neonatal seizure detection [82]. Interpretation of neonatal EEG requires highly trained healthcare professionals and it is limited to specialized units. They used overlapping window to augment 1389 seizures during 835 hours of EEG signal. Each trial split into 8s epochs with 50% overlapping to have more training sample for their proposed CNN.

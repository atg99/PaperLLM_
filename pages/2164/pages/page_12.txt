4.2.1. Removal-Based Evaluation
Three phases can be used to organize methods for validating feature-based interpretations.
1. Select features to remove, either randomly or based on their importance.
2. Fill in the blanks of features, and this is where the methods vary (either ﬁlling the
blank with the background, or replacing it with the mean, or ignoring it, etc.)
3. Calculating the difference between the presence and absence of a feature to determine
its predictive impact (reporting the decline in accuracy relative to the trained model).

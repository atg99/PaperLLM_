4.3.4 Hybrid SSL
Some of the T-PTLMs are pretrained using more than
one type of SSL. For example, BERT model - generative
(MLM) and contrastive SSL (NSP), ALBERT- generative
(MLM) and contrastive SSL (SOP), infoXLM – gener-
ative (MLM, TLM) and contrastive SSL (XLCo). Here
XLCo represents the cross lingual contrastive pretraining
task. Models like CLINE [161] are obtained by further
pretraining RoBERTa model using generative (MLM),
contrastive, and adversarial SSL (RTD).4.4 Extensions

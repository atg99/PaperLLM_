Kumar and Viswanadham (Kumar and Viswanadham 2007) propose a decision
support system for construction supply chains that relies on case-based reasoning. By
using fault-tree analysis, important features of past projects that induce risk events
are identied, categorised and stored. Then, for any future risk event, similar cases
are retrieved and the corresponding solutions are adapted for the current case, which,
in turn, gets stored to be reused in the future.

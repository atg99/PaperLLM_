3. Proposed Methodology
In this paper, the authors have applied min-max normalization and data augmentation
techniques on a large dataset of 3260 different types of brain MRI images [ 20]. The image
database includes 3064 T1-weighted contrast-enhanced MRI images collected from Kaggle.

708 pictures; the second is glioma which contains 1426 pictures; and lastly there is pituitary
tumor which contains 930 pictures. All pictures were collected from 233 patients in three
planes: sagittal (1025 photos), axial (994 photos), and coronal (1045 photos). The authors
divided the dataset into three distinct parts for training, validation, and testing. The

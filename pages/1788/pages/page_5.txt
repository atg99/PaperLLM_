The development of efficient software libraries for model optimization and seamless deployment will continue through engagement of the TinyML community in open source and proprietary TinyML frameworks. Finally, there are already many companies working on the development of efficient and pre-optimized solutions based on the TinyML software and off-the-shelf hardware, with the goal to shorten the final production cycle significantly.

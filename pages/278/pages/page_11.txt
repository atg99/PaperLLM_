5 C ONCLUSION
In this paper, we propose a novel paradigm of spatial-
temporal transformer networks to improve the long-term
prediction of trafﬁc ﬂows. It can dynamically model various
scales of spatial dependencies as well as capture long-
range temporal dependencies. Experimental results on two
real-world datasets demonstrate the superior performance
of the proposed STTN, especially in long-term prediction.
Furthermore, the proposed spatial transformer can be gen-
eralized for dynamical graph feature learning in a variety of
applications. We will further investigate this topic in future.

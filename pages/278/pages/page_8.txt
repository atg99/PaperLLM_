4.5 Computational Complexity
We further evaluate the computational costs for DCRNN,
STGCN, Graph WaveNet and STTN. All the experiments
are conducted on the same GPU. Table 2 reports the average
training speed for one epoch. STGCN is efﬁcient with the
fully convolutional structures. DRCNN is time-consuming
due to the recurrent structures for training with joint loss for
multiple time steps, as its training time is proportional to the
number of prediction time steps. STTN yields a reduction of
10-40% and 40-60% in computational costs in comparison to
Graph WaveNet and DCRNN, respectively. Note that STTN
is scalable to achieve long-term prediction without excessive
computational complexity.

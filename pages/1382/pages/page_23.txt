9.2 Semantic Segmentation Datasets for Natural Images
Next, we briefly discuss the most popular and widely used datasets for the semantic segmen-
tation of natural images. These datasets cover various categories of scenes, such as indoor
and outdoor environments, common objects, urban street view as well as generic scenes. For
a comprehensive review of the natural image datasets that segmentation models are usually
benchmarked upon, we direct the interested readers to Lateef and Ruichek (2019).

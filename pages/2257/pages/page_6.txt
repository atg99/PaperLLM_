Let us note that for the purposes of this Challenge, all participants are al-
lowed to use any -publicly or not- available pre-trained model (as long as it has
not been pre-trained on Aff-Wild2). The pre-trained model can be pre-trained
on any task (eg VA estimation, Expression Classification, AU detection, Face
Recognition). However when the teams are refining the model and developing
the methodology they must only use the provided synthetic data. No real data
should be used in model training/methodology development.

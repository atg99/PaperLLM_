7.8. Visual Distractor Evaluation details
In order to test the generalisation performance of RRL and FERM (Zhan et al., 2020), we subject the environment to various
kinds of visual distractions during inference (Figure 12). Note all parameters are freezed during this evaluation, an average
performance over 75 rollouts is reported. Following distractors were used during inference to test robustness of the ﬁnal
policy -
• Random change in light position.
• Random change in light direction.
• Random object color. (Handle, door color for Door-v0; Different hammer parts and nail for Hammer-v0)
•Introducing a new object in scene - random color, position, size and geometry (Sphere, Capsule, Ellipsoid, Cylinder,
Box).

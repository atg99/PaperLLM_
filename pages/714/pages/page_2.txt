4∇J?(θ)IS NOT A GRADIENT
In this section, we answer the first of our two questions and show
that the update direction used by almost all policy gradient algo-
rithms,∇J?(θ), is not the gradient of any function using a proof
by contraposition with the Clairaut-Schwarz theorem on mixed
partial derivatives [ 19]. First, we present this theorem (Theorem
4.1) and its contrapositive (Corollary 4.2). Next, we present Lemma
4.3, which allows us to rewrite ∇J?(θ)in a new form. Finally, in
Theorem 4.4 we apply Corollary 4.2 and Lemma 4.3 and derive
a counterexample proving that J?does not, in general, exist, and
therefore that the “policy gradient” given by ∇J?(θ)is not, in fact,
a gradient.

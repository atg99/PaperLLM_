4.3. Limitations
A basic limitation of this study is imposed by restrictions in the availability of indica-
tors considered. Levels of 2-h postload plasma glucose after a 75goral glucose tolerance
test (OGTT) and HbA1c test is one of the recommended indicators of diabetes. However,
during data collection, we are unable to get enough OGTT and HbA1c test data due to the
expensiveness of these tests, which constrains the accuracy of this study. Our study also
considered some invasive diabetes indicators such as insulin and fast blood glucose, which
may limit its application to self-testing in comparison with clinical use. Finally, we only
have Ethiopia as a proxy for ethnicity. All of this affects our model’s detection capacity and
generalization capability.

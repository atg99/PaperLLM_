4.4 Rejected technologies
For the sake of completeness, Table 4summarises the main technologies that were excluded
from our SLR, along with the corresponding motivation. In particular, the table shows theinstantiation of the criteria for MAS technologies inclusion deﬁned in Sect. 2.1.
Let us recall the inclusion criteria we adopted in this SLR. Contributions are considered
as logic-based MAS technologies if allthe following conditions simultaneously hold:
(a) they can be clearly related to some well-known and shared deﬁnition of agent and
MAS—e.g., [ 280,356];
(b) they stick to some logic which is clearly identiﬁable in the literature, in terms of name,
formal deﬁnition, and reference paper(s);
(c) they are both scientiﬁcally identiﬁable (reference paper) and technically available (i.e.,
some software actually exists).

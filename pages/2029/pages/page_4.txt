2.3.4 Parent Selection  
The parent selection was done using the Roulette Wheel Selection. In the Roulette Wheel method, 
individuals were  mapped in a sequential line segment according to their fitness value. Then random numbers 
were  generated as many as N rounds as the parent of the crossover process ed. The crossover process could not  
have the same parent. Then checking was done  to remove the redundant parent.

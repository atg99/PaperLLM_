networks by augmenting the training set using simple hand-
crafted policies, surpassing the performance of hybrid systems
even without the aid of a language model. SpecAugment con-
verts ASR from an over-ﬁtting to an under-ﬁtting problem, and
we were able to gain performance by using bigger networks and
training longer.

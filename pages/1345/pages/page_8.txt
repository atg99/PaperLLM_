IV. C ONCLUSION AND FUTURE DIRECTION
In this survey, we thoroughly reviewed the existing litera-
ture of trust in autonomous systems. We went over technical
papers/articles that examined trust between humans and robots,
machines, self-driving cars, and autopilot systems. Many of
the reviewed studies provide new discoveries as well as rec-
ommendations to manage and improve trust between humans
and fully or semi-autonomous systems. The literature on trust,
however, is still very broad and does not address concrete trust
issues that are currently present, for instance, how all existing
discoveries can be translated to computational trust models that
are understandable to machines. New research directions and
novel methodologies can potentially provide a solid platform
to develop well-performing autonomous systems.

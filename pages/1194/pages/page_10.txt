Broader Impact
CPL fills the gap that no modern SSL algorithm considers the inherent learning difficulties of different
classes during the training, and shows that by doing so, the convergence speed and final accuracy can
both be improved. We hope that CPL can attract more future attention to explore the effectiveness
of utilizing unlabeled data according to the model’s learning status as well as the per-class learning
difficulty.

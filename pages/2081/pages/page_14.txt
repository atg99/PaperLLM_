David De Roure is a Professor of 
e-Research at University of 
Oxford. He obtained his Ph.D. at 
University of Southampton in 
1990 and went on to hold the 
post of Professor of Computer 
Science, later directing the UK 
Digital Social Research pro-
gramme. His current research 
focusses on social machines, 
Internet of Things and cyberse-
curity. He is a Fellow of the Brit-
ish Computer Society and the 
Institute of Mathematics and its 
Applications.

of new information and commu-

research focuses on the relation 
between digital technology and 
political economy, ideology, 
governance, social change, social 
and economic inequality, norma -
tivity, feelings and ideals.

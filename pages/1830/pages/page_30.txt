across languages, in the way that transfer learning
has been applied to learning in one language and
testing in another (Conneau et al., 2020).
7 Conclusion
In this paper, we present a survey of the three trend-
ing paradigms that use pre-trained language models
for NLP. We describe each of them in depth, and
summarize prior works whose applications have
shown promise. In addition, we describe the use
of pre-trained language models to automatically
generate data that is used to improve performance
in NLP tasks. We hope this survey will provide
readers with key fundamental concepts and a com-
prehensive view of the paradigm shift.

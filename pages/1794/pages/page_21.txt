5.2. Software Validation Tests
As presented in the previous section, the software validation tests consider the tradi-
tional ML metrics. In this context, we evaluated Precision ,Recall , and F1-Score metrics.
For simplicity matters, the validation set is sampled once from the training data. It
has 10% of all images from the training data. Table 2 displays the obtained results for the
validation set. The results display that the system could identify the diseased leaves in 90%
of the cases. The Precision and Recall are also balanced, resulting in a balanced F1-Score.
This result indicates that the amount of false positives and negatives is about the same.

5. Conclusion  
This research has evaluated the effectiveness of applying AI to the conclusion that the Random 
Forest model shows good performance in detecting fraudulent transactions, with high accuracy, 
precision and recall. This confirms the potential of AI as an effec tive tool in preventing financial fraud. 
Data pre -processing, including normalization of transaction values and coding of categorical data, 
proved important in improving model performance. Line chart graphic visualization helps in 
understanding trends an d patterns in data, which can be used to detect and prevent fraud early. 
Suggestions for future research are integrating big data to handle larger and more complex transaction 
volumes. This can help identify more complex fraud patterns and increase detecti on accuracy. 
Develop more advanced data visualization methods, such as heat maps or interactive graphs.

The success of TPTT in the classication tasks can be attributed to the mitigation of the
vanishing/exploding gradients problem. Figure 3 shows the impact of TPTT on learning
and gradient stability for one specic run, however this characteristic situation was consis-
tently observed across dierent problems and separate runs. In the BPTT-trained network
the spectral radius of the hidden-to-hidden matrix gradually decreases until reaching the
sucient criterion for the gradients to vanish ( <1:0). This correlates with the decrease
in the norm ofrW, resulting in the inability of the network to learn eciently and the
lack of reduction in the cost function. In contrast, the TPTT network maintains stable ,
updates to the synaptic weights are not constrained to the beginning of the training, and
the cost decreases until the problem is successfully solved.

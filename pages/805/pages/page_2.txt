Sub-type virus identiﬁcation is proposed in [2] and [5]. In
[2], a viral genome deep classiﬁer uses a CNN to classify

a coronavirus sub-type, including SARS-CoV-2, MERS-CoV ,
SARS-CoV , and other viruses. The architecture of the CNNs
used in this work is based in [2], [5].

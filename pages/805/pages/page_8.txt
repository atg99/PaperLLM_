VII. C ONCLUSION
We have proposed the Q!Piteration training scheme,
where quantization is followed by pruning per training iter-
ation, for training compressed models in accelerating deep
learning inference for viral classiﬁcation. The proposed strat-
egy was applied to 1600 sequences of four groups of viruses
associated with three families and one realm. We have empiri-
cally demostrated that Q!Piteration yields a more efﬁcient
compressed model than P!Qiteration, per training iteration
for the viral classﬁcation tasks considered. Our proposed
Q!Piteration strategy is superior in prunning performance
to conventional P!Qepochs strategy, pruning epochs
followed by quantization epochs. The results were obtained
for an 8-bit quantized DNN compared to a single-precisionﬂoat-point ( 32-bit) DNN. As future work, we plan to extend
these results to other classiﬁcation tasks.

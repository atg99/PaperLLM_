The comparison results of Machine Learning (ML) and Deep Learning (DL) methods
for malware detection are presented in Tables 2 and 3, respectively. The performance
analysis of the proposed model is compared with various ML techniques such as K-Nearest
Neighbor (KNN), Logistic Regression (LR), Naïve Bayes (NB), SVM, Decision Tree (DT),
Random Forest (RF), and Adaboost. The malware detectors based on pretrained DL
models such as CNN and its variants are used for analyzing the efﬁciency of the proposed
DenseNet-based malware detection method. The performance results obtained for the
proposed model are better than the ML and DL-based malware detection models for the
three datasets. The proposed model obtained an accuracy of 98.23% for Malimg, of 98.46%
for BIG 2015, and of 98.21% for MaleVis dataset.

Table 6 compares the results of the proposed malware detection model with previous
works on the four malware datasets (3 training dataset + 1 (unseen) test dataset). The
proposed model outperforms other detection methods in the literature. The accuracy of the
proposed model (98.23%) is slightly higher than the accuracy of the method by Roseline
et al. (98.65%) on the Malimg dataset. The results of the proposed model outperform the
existing methods on the BIG 2015, MaleVis, and Malicia datasets.

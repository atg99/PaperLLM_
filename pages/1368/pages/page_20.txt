other. Setting up multi-agent HRI systems presents a
number of challenges related to safety, scalability, coor-
dination and information transparency, which we need to
address in order to progress towards this vision. Under-
standing human behavior in a group setting among het-
erogeneous agents (both robots and humans) is important
to properly model future HRI systems. Establishing vi-
able interaction models is another key requirement for ad-
vanced multi-agent HRI systems, especially the ones en-
abling interactions among multiple humans and multiple
robots simultaneously.

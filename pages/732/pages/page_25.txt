7.2  Pendulum problem

lem of the pendulum. The objective is to balance the pendulum upright starting from any 
starting position. Given that this is a well established benchmark for reinforcement learn-
ing, we refer the reader to Brockman et  al. (2016) for the specifications on reward and 
transition dynamics. Similar to the navigation problem, we let the feature representation

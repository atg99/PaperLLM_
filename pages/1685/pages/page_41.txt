The intuition behind Reptile is that updating the initialization weights towards updated 
parameters will grant a good inductive bias for tasks from the same family. By performing 
Taylor expansions of the gradients of Reptile and MAML (both first-order and second-
order), Nichol et al. (2018) show that the expected gradients differ in their direction. They 
argue, however, that in practice, the gradients of Reptile will also bring the model towards 
a point minimizing the expected loss over tasks.

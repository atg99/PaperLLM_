In short, meta networks rely on a reparameterization of the meta- and base-learner for 
every task. Despite the flexibility and applicability to both supervised and reinforcement 
learning settings, the approach is quite complex. It consists of many components, each 
with its own set of parameters, which can be a burden on memory usage and computation 
time. Additionally, finding the correct architecture for all the involved components can be 
time-consuming.

In summary, iMAML reduces memory costs significantly as it need not differentiate 
through the optimization trajectory, also allowing for greater flexibility in the choice 
of the inner optimizer. Additionally, it can account for larger optimization paths. The 
computational costs stay roughly the same compared to MAML (Finn et  al. 2017). 
Future work could investigate more inner optimization procedures (Rajeswaran et al.

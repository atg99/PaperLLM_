Recurrent meta-learners have been shown to perform almost as well as asymptotically 
optimal algorithms on simple reinforcement learning tasks (Wang et al. 2016; Duan et al. 
2016). However, their  performance degrades in more complex settings, where temporal 
dependencies can span a longer horizon. Making recurrent meta-learners better at such 
complex tasks is a direction for future research.

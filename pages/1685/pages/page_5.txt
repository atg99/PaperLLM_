2.1.2  Supervised meta‑learning
In contrast, supervised meta-learning does not assume that any meta-knowledge /u1D714 is given, 
or pre-defined. Instead, the goal of meta-learning is to find the best /u1D714 , such that our (reg-
ular) base-learner can learn new tasks (data sets) as quickly as possible. Thus, whereas 
supervised regular learning involves one data set, supervised meta-learning involves a 
group of data sets. The goal is to learn meta-knowledge /u1D714 such that our model can learn 
many different tasks well. Thus, our model is learning to learn.

F C OMPUTATIONAL EFFICIENCY
The runtime of RDP is compared with its competing methods in both anomaly detection and clus-
tering tasks. Since training time can vary signiﬁcantly using different training strategies in deep
learning-based methods, it is difﬁcult to have a fair comparison of the training time. Moreover, the
models can often be trained ofﬂine. Thus, we focus on comparing the runtime at the testing stage.
All the runtime experiments below were done on a computing server node equipped with 32 Intel
Xeon E5-2680 CPUs (2.70GHz) and 128GB Random Access Memory.

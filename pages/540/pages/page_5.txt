upon some underlying data distribution assumption, e.g., Gaussian distribution in random projection
or Gaussian RBF kernel, so the -projected features can preserve misleading proximity when the
distribution assumption is inexact. In this case, Yis equivalent to the imperfect ground truth with
partial noise. Then optimisation with Eqn. (9) is to leverage the power of neural networks to learn
consistent local proximity information and rectify inconsistent proximity, resulting in a signiﬁcantly
optimised distance preserving space. The resulting space conveys substantially richer semantics
than theprojected space when Ycontains sufﬁcient genuine supervision information.

The vulnerability of GAN reduced by feeding the labels with softening value, i.e., slightly less than 1, such as 0.9 for the real sample and slightly high than 0, such as 0.1 for fake samples instead of 1 and 0 [204]. Label-smoothing highly useful for the training of high-quality networks on relatively modest-sized training sets. 4.3.10 Sampling GAN  Sampling GAN (S-GAN) uses a spherical linear interpolation method for data sampling instead of the linear interpolation method [205].

8 C ONCLUSION
We introduced a simple and effective method that can be applied to any neural MT model without
further training. We show that similar contexts in a model’s embedding space are more likely to be
followed by similar next words, allowing the model to be improved by interpolation with a nearest
neighbor classiﬁer. The approach improves a state-of-the-art model in-domain, leads to large gains
out-of-domain, and can specialize a multilingual model for speciﬁc language-pairs. Future work
should improve efﬁciency, for example by down-sampling frequent target words in the datastore.

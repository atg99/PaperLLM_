Several facts (triples) can be inferred from this KG. For example, the facts “ Tim Berners -Lee has invented WWW” 
comprises two entities/nodes, namely “ Tim Berners -Lee” and “WWW”, and the relation “ has invented”  forms the 
triple “ Tim Berners -Lee, hasInvented, WWW ”.

from the Web as key sources of knowledge to benefit numerous Artificial Intelligence and smart systems [ 12],

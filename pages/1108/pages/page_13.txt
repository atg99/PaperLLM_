4.2 Network structure and elements connectivity  324 
To build the network, linking  the indicators with the resilience pillars is necessary . Despite not being introduced  325 
in the UNISDR report s (ISDR 2005 ; UNISDR 2008 ), the assignment of the indicators  to one of the resilience 326 
pillars is performed as follows:  327 
 If the indicator clearly refers to a regulatory requirement or action with proactive intent of risk  reduction, 328 
it is assigned to P 1; 329 
 If the indicator clearly refers to the implementation of institutional mechanisms or the building of 330 
resources for the proactive establishment of resilience capabilities, it is assigned to P 2; 331 
 If the indicator clearly refe rs to the implementation of practices, mechanisms, and programs for 332 
emergency response and recovery, it is assigned to P 3. Note that it is possible for an indicator to affect 333 
more than one resilience pillar.  334

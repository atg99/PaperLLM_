The collection of references presents a wide range of research and developments in the fields of robotics, human-robot interaction, and advanced technologies for space exploration and smart cities. Key topics include the development of autonomous construction machinery for extraterrestrial environments, such as Mars and the Moon, as discussed by Yano and Kajima. The use of swarm robotics and autonomous distributed space systems is highlighted, with applications in both scientific exploration and medicine.

Several studies examine the interaction between humans and robotic systems, focusing on mixed-initiative interactions and multimodal interfaces, which enhance the effectiveness of human-robot collaboration. The role of voice assistants like Alexa and Siri is explored, illustrating their impact on user satisfaction and social roles.

Emerging technologies such as blockchain and the Internet of Things (IoT) are also addressed, particularly in relation to smart cities and robotic swarm systems. The references collectively underscore the significance of these technologies in shaping future innovations across various domains, including healthcare, cybersecurity, and urban infrastructure.

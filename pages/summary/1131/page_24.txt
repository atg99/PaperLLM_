The referenced works delve into various aspects of systems of systems engineering and cyber-physical systems, highlighting foundational concepts, model-based techniques, and emerging research directions. Nielsen et al. discuss basic principles and methodologies in systems of systems engineering. Liu et al. provide a comprehensive review of cyber-physical systems, addressing their integration and functionality. Engell et al. identify core research areas crucial for the advancement of cyber-physical systems of systems.

The challenges and opportunities within cyber-physical systems are examined by Venkata Krishna et al., while Ferrer et al. focus on the adoption of these systems in smart manufacturing contexts. Vargas et al. present a systematic review of integration approaches for systems of systems, emphasizing the need for cohesive strategies.

In the realm of swarm intelligence, Dorigo et al. introduce swarm robotics, exploring its applications and behaviors. The foundational principles of swarm intelligence are further elaborated by Garnier et al., and Bonabeau and Meyer discuss its implications for business strategies. The concept of particle swarm optimization, introduced by Kennedy and Eberhart, is highlighted as a significant computational technique.

Research on self-organization in biological systems, such as the decision-making processes of honeybees and ants, is detailed in works by Camazine and Beckers, respectively. The dynamics of collective behavior in robotic systems inspired by biological entities are showcased in studies by Kernbach et al. and Schmickl et al., demonstrating the practical applications of these principles in artificial systems.

Overall, these contributions collectively advance the understanding of both theoretical and practical aspects of systems of systems and swarm intelligence, paving the way for future innovations in these interdisciplinary fields.

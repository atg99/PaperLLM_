This collection of references encompasses a range of studies focused on Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) and their applications, particularly in fields such as control systems, optimization, and network dynamics. Key themes include the integration of wireless sensor and actuator networks for effective control, the role of particle swarm optimization in power systems, and the complexities of drone networks in terms of communication and coordination.

Several studies highlight the importance of understanding the dynamics and structure of complex networks, emphasizing the need for observability in such systems. There is also a focus on catastrophic phenomena in communication networks and the implications of phase transitions in socio-economic systems. 

The papers present various methodologies for analyzing and modeling CPS, including statistical model checking and resilience analysis, showcasing the evolution of techniques used to ensure reliability and adaptability in these systems. Furthermore, the references discuss convergence analysis in particle swarm optimization and its variants, providing insights into the stability and performance of these algorithms in real-world applications.

Overall, this compilation illustrates the interdisciplinary nature of CPS research, bridging concepts from control theory, optimization, and network science to address contemporary challenges in technology and systems design.

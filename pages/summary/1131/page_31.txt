The referenced works focus on various aspects of swarm robotics, decentralized systems, and their applications in fields such as aerial communication, environmental monitoring, and smart grids. Hauert et al. discuss evolved swarming without positioning information for aerial communication relays. Varga et al. address distributed formation control in micro aerial vehicles for area coverage. Donati et al. introduce a bio-inspired under-actuated robot class for environmental exploration. 

Schmickl et al. present the Fstaxis algorithm, inspired by biological taxis, and its applications in underwater robotics. The subCULTron project explores cultural development in underwater robotics. Duarte et al. investigate the evolution of collective behaviors in aquatic surface robots. The Cocoro project highlights self-aware underwater swarms. 

In the context of smart grids, Venayagamoorthy discusses the potential of computational intelligence, while Elmenreich and Steber focus on decentralized control and increasing internal photovoltaic consumption. Huang et al. propose a fully decentralized grid scheduling framework leveraging swarm intelligence. Ramachandran et al. present an intelligent auction scheme for smart grid markets using a hybrid immune algorithm.

Kirchhoff et al. and Kyriakarakos et al. explore mutual success factors for microgrids and multispecies swarm electrification in rural areas. Muceka et al. discuss integrating solar PV systems with backup generators for hybrid swarm electrification in Nigeria. Ghorbaniparvar surveys forced oscillations in power systems, and Mouret along with Chatzilygeroudis reflect on the role of simulators in evolutionary robotics. Finally, Stormont and Kang emphasize the use of robot swarms for planetary exploration, particularly in Mars exploration efforts.

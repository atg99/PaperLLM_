The cited works focus on various methodologies and algorithms for feature selection in high-dimensional data, utilizing techniques from optimization and machine learning. Singh and Singh (2019) present an ant colony framework for classifying power quality disturbances, emphasizing optimal feature selection. Song et al. (2013) introduce a fast clustering-based algorithm for efficient feature subset selection in high-dimensional contexts.

Tabakhi and Moradi (2015) explore relevance-redundancy feature selection using ant colony optimization, while their earlier work (2014) discusses an unsupervised feature selection algorithm based on the same principles. Tang et al. (2018) propose a robust unsupervised feature selection method that incorporates dual self-representation and manifold regularization. Tang, Dai, and Xiang (2019) focus on feature interactions for text categorization, enhancing the feature selection process.

Other notable contributions include Taradeh et al. (2019), who utilize evolutionary gravitational search for feature selection, and Tawhid and Dsouza (2018), who develop a hybrid binary bat and particle swarm optimization algorithm. Tu et al. (2019) employ a multi-strategy ensemble grey wolf optimizer for feature selection, while Tubishat et al. (2020) improve the Salp Swarm Algorithm with novel techniques for feature selection.

Unler et al. (2011) introduce mr2PSO, a method based on swarm intelligence aimed at maximizing relevance and minimizing redundancy for support vector machine classification. Uysal and Gunal (2012) present a probabilistic feature selection method specifically for text classification. Vignolo et al. (2013) apply multi-objective evolutionary wrappers for feature selection in face recognition tasks. Wang et al. (2020) discuss a many-objective evolutionary algorithm for environmental selection, while Wang and Hong (2019) focus on supervised Hebb rule-based feature selection for text classification. Lastly, Wang et al. (2019) introduce a factor graph model aimed at unsupervised feature selection.

Collectively, these studies highlight the diverse approaches to feature selection, emphasizing the importance of optimization methods in improving classification performance across various applications.

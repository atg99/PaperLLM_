The paper presents a comparative analysis of various feature selection methods using different classifiers, specifically focusing on the SVM, Naive Bayes, and AdaBoost classifiers. The data indicates that the Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO)-based method achieved the highest average classification accuracy across SVM and AdaBoost classifiers, recording an accuracy of 89.35% on SVM. In contrast, the COA-based method excelled with the highest accuracy on the Naive Bayes classifier, achieving 89.24%, slightly ahead of the PSO-based method by 0.16%. Additionally, the PSO-based method for AdaBoost also secured the top position, with an average accuracy of 88.92%, followed by the ACO-based and ABC-based methods at 88.28% and 87.73%, respectively. The results highlight the effectiveness of PSO in feature selection for SVM and AdaBoost classifiers, while the COA method is preferable for Naive Bayes. The figures illustrate these findings, showcasing the performance of different swarm intelligence-based methods in terms of classification accuracy.

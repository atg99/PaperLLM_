The paper presents an analysis of the performance of various wrapper-based feature selection methods using swarm intelligence (SI) techniques, specifically focusing on the Naive Bayes and AdaBoost classifiers. Figures 2 and 3 illustrate the average classification accuracy across multiple datasets for these classifiers.

Table 5 details the number of selected features from eleven different SI-based methods applied to various datasets. The findings indicate a significant reduction in dimensionality, with the PSO-based method demonstrating superior performance in the Arrhythmia, Madelon, Isolet, and Colon datasets, selecting minimal percentages of the original features (7.21%, 14.87%, 22.95%, and 0.58%, respectively). In contrast, the ACO-based method showed effective feature selection in the SpamBase and Sonar datasets, averaging 8.18 and 7.11 selected features.

Overall, the results underscore the effectiveness of SI-based methods in reducing feature dimensionality while maintaining classification accuracy, highlighting the PSO and ACO methods as particularly efficient across different datasets.

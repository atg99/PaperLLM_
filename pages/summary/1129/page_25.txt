This collection of studies focuses on various feature selection methods, which are essential for improving the performance of machine learning models by identifying the most relevant features from datasets. Chandrashekar and Sahin provide a comprehensive survey on feature selection techniques, highlighting their importance in data preprocessing. Chen et al. introduce an efficient ant colony optimization method specifically tailored for image feature selection, demonstrating its effectiveness in signal processing. 

Chen et al. (2019) propose a method for feature selection in imbalanced data by utilizing neighborhood rough sets, while Huang and Tsai present a hybrid model combining self-organizing feature maps and support vector regression for stock market forecasting, emphasizing filter-based feature selection. 

Lin et al. explore genetic algorithm-based feature selection for image retrieval and classification, showcasing the adaptability of features. Dadaneh et al. focus on unsupervised probabilistic feature selection through ant colony optimization, contributing to advancements in unsupervised learning methods.

Ding et al. introduce a novel approach using adaptive hypergraph regularization for unsupervised feature selection, and Elyasigomari et al. develop a two-stage gene selection method that integrates cuckoo optimization and harmony search for cancer classification. 

Emary and colleagues present multi-objective and binary grey wolf optimization techniques for attribute reduction and feature selection, respectively, indicating the versatility of nature-inspired algorithms in this domain. Estévez et al. examine normalized mutual information for feature selection, enhancing the understanding of feature relationships.

Faris et al. propose an efficient binary Salp Swarm Algorithm with a crossover scheme for feature selection, while Ferreira and Figueiredo investigate unsupervised approaches for feature discretization and selection. Figueiredo et al. conduct a systematic review on swarm intelligence applications in clustering, providing new insights into data mining. Lastly, Forsati et al. enhance ant colony optimization for feature selection, and Ghaemi and Feizi-Derakhshi apply the Forest Optimization Algorithm for feature selection, further enriching the field's methodologies. 

These studies collectively illustrate the diverse strategies and algorithms employed in feature selection, highlighting their significance in various applications across machine learning and data analysis.

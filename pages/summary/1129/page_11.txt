In recent studies, WOA has been applied successfully for feature selection in various domains. For instance, Mafarja and Mirjalili introduced a hybrid model that combines WOA with simulated annealing to improve feature selection outcomes. This integration aims to refine the search process further and enhance the algorithm's efficiency in identifying relevant features.

Overall, these optimization algorithms, including COA, GWO, and WOA, represent innovative approaches to feature selection, leveraging natural behaviors to enhance data classification accuracy. Each method contributes uniquely to the field, offering various strategies to optimize feature subsets and reduce redundancy while ensuring high classification performance.

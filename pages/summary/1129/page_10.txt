In this section of the paper, various optimization algorithms for feature selection are discussed, emphasizing their applications in improving performance in clustering and classification tasks. 

The multi-objective differential evolution approach proposed by Hancer in 2020 aims to enhance feature selection while simultaneously boosting clustering algorithm performance. 

Additionally, the Gravitational Search Algorithm (GSA), introduced by Rashedi et al. in 2009, is highlighted as a physics-inspired swarm intelligence method. Notably, Han et al. in 2014 combined GSA with a linear chaotic map to enhance classification accuracy. Further advancements include a hybrid system integrating an improved GSA with k-NN by Xiang et al. in 2015, and a method by Taradeh et al. in 2019 that merges GSA with genetic algorithms to optimize feature selection.

The Firefly Algorithm (FA), developed by Yang in 2010, is another prominent method, leveraging the behavior of fireflies to achieve effective feature selection. Research by Zhang et al. in 2017 and 2018 introduced various strategies to prevent premature convergence and enhance accuracy in classification and regression tasks using FA. Larabi Marie-Sainte and Alalyani in 2020 applied FA for classifying Arabic texts, while Selvakumar and Muneeswaran in 2019 utilized FA for network intrusion detection through a combination of filter and wrapper methods.

Lastly, the Bat Algorithm (BA), also introduced by Yang in 2010, is based on echolocation behavior in bats. Tawhid and Dsouza in 2018 presented a hybrid BA and PSO algorithm to enhance feature selection performance. Liu et al. in 2020 proposed a binary BA-based method for image steganalysis, focusing on relevant feature selection to improve detection accuracy. Azmi Al-Betara et al. in 2020 further utilized BA for optimal feature subset selection in cancer classification, employing a combination of filter and wrapper approaches.

Overall, these algorithms demonstrate the potential of swarm intelligence techniques in optimizing feature selection across various applications.

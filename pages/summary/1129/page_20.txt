The paper evaluates the performance of various filter-based feature selection methods, specifically focusing on swarm intelligence (SI) approaches, including Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO), Ant Colony Optimization (ACO), Differential Evolution (DE), and Whale Optimization Algorithm (WOA). These methods were assessed across multiple classifiers, including Support Vector Machine (SVM), Naive Bayes (NB), and AdaBoost (AB).

In the initial experiments, the average classification accuracy was recorded over ten independent runs, with ACO demonstrating superior performance in most cases. For example, in the colon dataset using the SVM classifier, ACO achieved an accuracy of 84.53%, outperforming PSO (81.42%), DE (79.37%), and WOA (78.81%).

This paper discusses various multi-objective optimization approaches for feature selection in high-dimensional datasets, highlighting methods such as MOABC, TMABC-FS, MOPSOFS, MOACO, MOWGA, MODEFS, MONSGA, and Nested GA. Each method employs different algorithms like Artificial Bee Colony (ABC), Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO), Ant Colony Optimization (ACO), Genetic Algorithm (GA), Differential Evolution (DE), and Non-dominated Sorting Genetic Algorithm II (NSGA-II), showcasing their application in areas such as face recognition and motor imagery.

The text emphasizes the distinction between feature ranking and subset selection methods, noting that while ranking methods evaluate features independently, subset selection methods search the entire space of possible feature combinations, which is computationally intense and impractical for large datasets. The NP-Hard nature of feature subset selection necessitates the use of meta-heuristic algorithms, which provide satisfactory solutions within acceptable time frames, thereby balancing computational efficiency and effectiveness in finding optimal feature subsets. These algorithms leverage random initial solutions and iterative refinement to navigate the search space, making them suitable for complex optimization problems in high-dimensional contexts.

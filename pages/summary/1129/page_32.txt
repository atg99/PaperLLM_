This collection of papers presents various approaches to multi-objective feature selection, particularly within the context of machine learning and data analysis. 

Wang et al. (2020) propose an artificial bee colony method that accelerates feature selection while accommodating variable sample sizes. Wang, Li, and Li (2015) introduce a multi-objective evolutionary algorithm that utilizes mutual information and a new redundancy measure for feature selection. Welikala et al. (2015) focus on a genetic algorithm combined with dual classification to enhance the automated detection of proliferative diabetic retinopathy.

Yang et al. (2011) enhance genetic algorithms for optimal feature subset selection from multi-character feature sets. Wu et al. (2020) develop a new framework for forecasting PM2.5 concentrations that integrates multi-objective feature selection with ensemble methods. Xiang et al. (2015) present a hybrid system for feature selection through an improved gravitational search algorithm paired with k-NN methods.

He, Cai, and Niyogi (2005) introduce the Laplacian Score as a feature selection metric, while Xu et al. (2007) examine mutual information-based feature selection for text categorization. Xue et al. (2013, 2014) explore particle swarm optimization techniques for feature selection in classification, proposing novel initialization and updating mechanisms. Xue et al. (2020) adapt particle swarm optimization for large-scale feature selection problems using multiple classifiers.

Yan et al. (2019) describe a hybrid feature selection strategy for quantitative analysis in laser-induced breakdown spectroscopy, and Yan et al. (2018) address cost-sensitive sequential feature selection for chiller fault detection. Yang (2010) contributes to metaheuristic optimization with the Firefly Algorithm and a Bat-Inspired Algorithm.

Finally, Ying et al. (2013) propose an ant colony optimization-based method for dimension reduction in high-dimensional datasets. Collectively, these studies highlight the innovation and effectiveness of various algorithms in improving feature selection processes across diverse applications.

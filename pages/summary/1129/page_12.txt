The paper discusses various feature selection methods based on swarm intelligence algorithms, specifically focusing on the Whale Optimization Algorithm (WOA) and the Salp Swarm Algorithm (SSA). 

The WOA is utilized in a wrapper feature selection approach, as proposed by Mafarja and Mirjalili in 2018, which incorporates tournament and roulette wheel selection strategies, along with crossover and mutation operators to improve exploration and exploitation in swarm intelligence algorithms. Nematzadeh et al. in 2019 introduced a frequency-based filter feature selection method leveraging WOA for high-dimensional medical datasets, employing a filter criterion to eliminate irrelevant features and subsequently ranking the remaining features using Mutual Congestion.

The SSA, introduced by Mirjalili et al. in 2017, is another bio-inspired algorithm that mimics the swarming behavior of salps. Several enhancements to SSA for feature selection are presented, including a binary version with a crossover scheme by Faris et al. in 2018, and a hybrid method combining SSA with particle swarm optimization by Ibrahim et al. in 2019. Tubishat et al. in 2020 developed an improved SSA integrating a local search algorithm, utilizing classification accuracy as a fitness function for feature selection. Other notable works include a feature weighting method for predicting liver disorders and heart diseases by Al-Zoubi et al. in 2020, and improvements to SSA's accuracy and convergence speed by Hegazy et al. in 2020.

The paper concludes with a table summarizing the reviewed swarm intelligence-based feature selection methods, detailing the authors, type of algorithms, objectives, and applications ranging from numerical to medical datasets.

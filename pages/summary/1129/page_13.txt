The paper presents a comprehensive evaluation of various swarm intelligence-based feature selection methods, specifically focusing on Ant Colony Optimization (ACO), Artificial Bee Colony (ABC), Differential Evolution (DE), Gravitational Search Algorithm (GSA), Firefly Algorithm (FA), Bat Algorithm (BA), Cuckoo Optimization Algorithm (COA), Grey Wolf Optimization (GWO), Whale Optimization Algorithm (WOA), and Salp Swarm Algorithm (SSA). Each method is categorized by its objective (single or multi-objective), type (wrapper or filter), and application area (numerical, medical, image, etc.). 

The experimental results section highlights the performance of these methods based on the number of selected features and classification accuracy (ACC). The ACC is computed using a specific formula that is not detailed in the provided text but is critical for comparing the effectiveness of the different algorithms. 

Overall, the study emphasizes the varying capabilities of swarm intelligence techniques in feature selection, providing insights into their application across different domains.

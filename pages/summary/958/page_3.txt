The paper discusses the identification of a Z2-Z2 symmetry-protected topological (SPT) phase in many-body systems, specifically focusing on a Hamiltonian describing a spin-1/2 chain. The phase diagram is illustrated, showing the relationship between two parameters, h1 and h2. The authors present an exact quantum convolutional neural network (QCNN) circuit designed to recognize the SPT phase. This circuit employs various quantum gates and pooling layers to analyze the quantum state of the system.

The performance of the QCNN is evaluated through its output, which demonstrates increased accuracy in identifying the phase as the depth of the network increases. The paper also compares the sample complexity of the QCNN with traditional methods, highlighting that the QCNN requires fewer samples to detect the quantum phase transition. The findings indicate that the QCNN approach offers a more efficient means of classifying quantum phases, particularly within the context of the SPT phase under study.

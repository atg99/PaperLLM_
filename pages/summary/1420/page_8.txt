The paper discusses the performance of ConceptBert in visual question answering (VQA) tasks, particularly comparing its complete form to a baseline model referred to as VL. The study presents various examples illustrating how ConceptBert outperforms the VL model in identifying correct answers, emphasizing that it correctly aligns answers with the type of ground-truth responses. The examples include questions related to events, objects, and attributes associated with animals, furniture, and companies, showcasing the model's ability to understand context and provide accurate responses. The results highlight the effectiveness of ConceptBert in recognizing and categorizing information more accurately than the VL model in both standard VQA and the more complex OK-VQA scenarios.

The provided references highlight significant contributions to the fields of knowledge representation and visual question answering (VQA). 

The paper by Speer, Chin, and Havasi introduces ConceptNet 5.5, an open multilingual graph designed to represent general knowledge, facilitating various applications in natural language processing and AI. 

Vaswani et al. present the groundbreaking "Attention Is All You Need," which introduces the Transformer model, revolutionizing how neural networks handle sequential data by focusing on attention mechanisms rather than recurrent structures.

Wang et al. explore the intersection of image captioning and VQA, proposing methods that leverage attributes and external knowledge to enhance the accuracy of responses in visual contexts. Their earlier works delve into fact-based reasoning and explicit knowledge integration for VQA, addressing the need for structured information in answering visual queries.

Zhu et al. contribute to the creation of a large-scale multimodal knowledge base aimed at improving VQA capabilities, emphasizing the importance of integrating diverse data types for better understanding and reasoning.

Overall, these works collectively advance the understanding and implementation of knowledge-driven approaches in AI, particularly in visual reasoning tasks.

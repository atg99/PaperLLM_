In this paper, the authors introduce ConceptBert, an innovative end-to-end pipeline designed for visual question answering (VQA) that leverages external structured knowledge. The approach enhances the representation of questions by integrating knowledge graph embeddings with Transformer blocks. By aggregating vision, language, and concept embeddings, the model learns a joint concept-vision-language embedding, demonstrating improved performance on the VQA 2.0 and OK-VQA datasets.

The results indicate that incorporating external knowledge significantly enhances the model's ability to align image regions with corresponding questions. Future research directions include exploring the integration of explicit relationships between entities and objects, suggesting that such relations from knowledge graphs, combined with those identified in questions and images, could further improve prediction accuracy.

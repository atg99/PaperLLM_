The paper presents an algorithm, referred to as OptSolve, which is designed to find an optimistic policy as part of the UCRL2 framework for reinforcement learning. The algorithm initializes a policy and iteratively updates it based on rewards and exploration parameters. Key steps include calculating new estimates of rewards and adjusting probabilities to ensure they sum to one. The algorithm continues until a specified convergence criterion is met, ensuring that the policy becomes increasingly optimistic.

The paper also discusses alternative approaches, particularly Bayesian methods, which can address exploration challenges in reinforcement learning. However, it notes that these methods introduce additional computational complexities. The authors highlight previous experimental work in online learning within continuous state Markov Decision Processes (MDPs), specifically citing studies by Jong and Stone and Nouri and Littman. These studies suggest that explicit exploration control can lead to significant performance improvements in learning algorithms.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the importance of exploration strategies in reinforcement learning and the effectiveness of the proposed algorithm in optimizing policies within this context.

The section discusses various successful applications of reinforcement learning (RL) across different domains. Key applications include:

1. **Games**: Notable examples are Backgammon and Go, showcasing RL's effectiveness in complex strategic environments.
2. **Networking**: RL has been applied in packet routing and channel allocation, optimizing network performance.
3. **Operations Research**: Applications include targeted marketing, maintenance issues, job-shop scheduling, elevator control, pricing strategies, vehicle routing, and inventory management.
4. **Robotics**: RL techniques have been utilized to control quadrupedal robots, humanoid robots, and helicopters, demonstrating its capabilities in physical systems.
5. **Finance**: RL has been used for option pricing, indicating its relevance in financial decision-making.

For further exploration of applications, the paper directs readers to specific URLs.

Other notable software includes CLSquare, PIQLE, RL Toolbox, JRLF, and LibPG, many of which support RL-Glue.

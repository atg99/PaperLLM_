The paper by Schaal (2008) discusses the estimation of the value function using LSTD-Q(λ), as proposed by Peters and Schaal (2008) and earlier by Peters et al. (2003). They suggest utilizing both state features and compatible state-action features, noting that only when λ=1 can an unbiased gradient estimate be obtained. The algorithm retains a fixed value for the parameter θ_t until the parameter θ_t calculated by LSTD-Q(λ) stabilizes, after which θ_t is updated and the internal statistics are discounted by a factor between 0 and 1. 

Additionally, the authors highlight that the original actor-critic method, when applied to finite MDPs without function approximation, results in a NAC update. Recent contributions by Bhatnagar et al. (2009) present two-timescale algorithms and demonstrate their convergence to local maxima of the objective function when the critic employs TD(0)-like updates.

The paper acknowledges the limitation of space, which restricts the coverage of the vast reinforcement learning literature. It suggests further reading on efficient sampling-based planning, which addresses the exponential scaling of offline planning with state space dimensionality, and highlights the benefits of online planning. Other notable topics include linear programming approaches, dual dynamic programming, online learning in MDPs, and competitive learning frameworks. The paper also points to significant areas such as learning in partially observed MDPs, game theory, and hierarchical methods.

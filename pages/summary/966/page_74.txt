The section on discounted Markovian decision processes aims to provide a concise proof of fundamental results related to this theory, focusing on the discounted expected total cost criterion. It begins with an overview of contraction mappings and Banach's fixed-point theorem, which serves as a foundation for proving key results concerning value functions and optimal policies. The author introduces essential definitions, including the concept of a norm in a vector space, which is crucial for the subsequent discussions on normed vector spaces.

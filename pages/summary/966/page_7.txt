This section introduces the fundamental concepts and notation associated with Markov Decision Processes (MDPs), which will be crucial for the discussions in subsequent chapters. Readers are encouraged to familiarize themselves with the notation, especially if they are new to the topic, while those experienced with MDPs may skim through. The section provides essential definitions and mathematical preliminaries, including the definition of natural numbers and real numbers, vector notation, inner products, norms, and Lipschitz mappings. It also mentions the indicator function and the notation for derivatives. For readers seeking deeper understanding, references to comprehensive texts on MDPs are provided.

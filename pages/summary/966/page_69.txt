The paper discusses the application of the policy gradient theorem in reinforcement learning, specifically focusing on the update rule for policy parameters in the context of stochastic gradient ascent. While the update rule is designed to converge to a local optimum under certain conditions, practical implementation faces challenges. In episodic settings, these challenges can be managed by updating parameters after each episode, leading to the development of the REINFORCE algorithm, which is a direct policy search method that does not rely on value functions. For non-episodic problems, a two-timescale algorithm can be employed to construct the estimator on a faster timescale, thereby integrating value-function estimation methods. This approach highlights the importance of variance reduction techniques and the careful design of algorithms in reinforcement learning.

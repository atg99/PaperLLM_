This document is a collection of references related to advancements in neural information processing, reinforcement learning, and stochastic optimization. Key contributions include:

1. **Neural Information Processing**: Platt et al. edited a volume that compiles significant advancements in the field, providing a foundation for understanding neural networks and their applications.

2. **Stochastic Approximation**: Polyak and Juditsky discuss methods to enhance stochastic approximation techniques through averaging, which can improve convergence rates in optimization problems.

3. **Bayesian Reinforcement Learning**: Poupart et al. present an analytic solution for discrete Bayesian reinforcement learning, offering insights into decision-making processes under uncertainty.

4. **Dynamic Programming**: Powell's work on approximate dynamic programming addresses challenges associated with high-dimensional problems, providing strategies for effective policy optimization.

5. **Model-Based Learning**: Proper and Tadepalli explore scaling model-based average-reward reinforcement learning specifically for product delivery applications, highlighting practical implementations.

6. **Markov Decision Processes**: Puterman's comprehensive text on Markov decision processes serves as a critical resource for understanding stochastic dynamic programming.

7. **Gaussian Processes**: Rasmussen and Williams introduce Gaussian processes as a powerful tool for machine learning, with applications in reinforcement learning detailed by Rasmussen and Kuss.

8. **Neural Reinforcement Learning**: Riedmiller's research on neural fitted Q iteration presents an innovative, data-efficient method for reinforcement learning, emphasizing the potential of combining neural networks with traditional algorithms.

9. **Sequential Design of Experiments**: Robbins provides foundational insights into the sequential design of experiments, influencing methodologies in statistical learning.

10. **Online Planning and POMDPs**: Ross et al. investigate model-based Bayesian reinforcement learning in complex domains and present online planning algorithms for partially observable Markov decision processes (POMDPs).

Overall, these references collectively advance the understanding of reinforcement learning, Bayesian methods, and optimization techniques in machine learning, highlighting both theoretical developments and practical applications.

The second part of the book focuses on the core algorithms and strategies in reinforcement learning. It covers fundamental methods, including value-based and policy-based approaches, while emphasizing the importance of user choices and the trade-offs involved. The authors provide pseudocode for nearly twenty algorithms, facilitating practical implementation for readers with a hands-on approach. 

The third part delves into advanced topics and recent developments in the field, aiming to broaden the reader's perspective on reinforcement learning. This section is particularly beneficial for existing researchers looking to expand their knowledge of areas they may not be familiar with.

Overall, the book is designed to serve as a concise yet comprehensive introduction to reinforcement learning, targeting advanced undergraduate and graduate students, as well as practitioners and researchers. It aims to provide a solid understanding of the state of the art in reinforcement learning, while also addressing the mathematical foundations necessary for deeper exploration of the subject.

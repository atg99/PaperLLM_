The references listed encompass a range of significant contributions to the fields of adaptive systems, dynamic programming, and control theory. 

Barto, Sutton, and Anderson (1983) introduce neuronlike adaptive elements capable of addressing complex learning control challenges. Beleznay et al. (1999) focus on the evaluation of value-function estimation algorithms in undiscounted scenarios. Berman (1998) discusses online searching and navigation within the context of online algorithms.

Bertsekas has made substantial contributions through multiple volumes of "Dynamic Programming and Optimal Control" (2007a, 2007b), which provide foundational insights into dynamic programming techniques. The 2010 online chapter further explores approximate dynamic programming methods. Additionally, Bertsekas et al. (2004) present enhancements in temporal difference methods using linear function approximation, while Bertsekas and Ioffe (1996) examine policy iteration based on temporal differences in neuro-dynamic programming contexts.

The work of Bertsekas and Shreve (1978) on stochastic optimal control and Bertsekas and Tsitsiklis (1996) on neuro-dynamic programming are also notable. Bhatnagar et al. (2009) contribute to the field with natural actor-critic algorithms, while Borkar's studies (1997, 1998) delve into stochastic approximation methods, emphasizing two-time scales and asynchronous approaches.

Overall, these references reflect pivotal advancements in learning algorithms, control strategies, and the application of dynamic programming in various contexts.

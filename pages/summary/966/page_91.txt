The cited works focus on various aspects of dynamic programming, reinforcement learning, and decision-making processes in uncertain environments. 

Nascimento and Powell propose an optimal approximate dynamic programming algorithm for the lagged asset acquisition problem, emphasizing the mathematical foundations of operations research. Nedic and Bertsekas present least squares policy evaluation algorithms that utilize linear function approximation, contributing to the field of discrete event dynamic systems. Neu, Gyorgy, and Szepesvari address the online loop-free stochastic shortest-path problem, showcasing advancements in online decision-making strategies.

Ng and Jordan introduce PEGASUS, a policy search method tailored for large Markov Decision Processes (MDPs) and Partially Observable MDPs (POMDPs), highlighting its effectiveness in handling complex decision-making scenarios. Nouri and Littman explore multi-resolution exploration in continuous spaces, providing insights into exploration strategies for reinforcement learning. 

Ormoneit and Sen discuss kernel-based reinforcement learning, offering a novel approach to improve learning efficiency. Ortner's work on online regret bounds for MDPs with deterministic transitions contributes to understanding performance guarantees in learning algorithms.

Parr et al. analyze linear models and feature selection for reinforcement learning, emphasizing the importance of model selection and representation. Their subsequent work further investigates feature generation for value-function approximation, enhancing the understanding of feature relevance in learning tasks.

Perkins and Precup present a convergent form of approximate policy iteration, advancing the theoretical underpinnings of policy iteration methods. Finally, Peters and Schaal introduce the Natural Actor-Critic method, which integrates natural gradients into actor-critic frameworks, and discuss the application of reinforcement learning in humanoid robotics, illustrating the practical implications of their research in real-world scenarios. 

Overall, these studies collectively contribute to the fields of reinforcement learning and dynamic programming, providing both theoretical advancements and practical applications.

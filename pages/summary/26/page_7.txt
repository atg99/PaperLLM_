The manuscript by Alotaibi et al. discusses the methodologies used in their study, including literature searches, data collection, and analysis, with contributions from all authors acknowledged. The study received ethical approval from the Institutional Review Board of King Abdullah International Medical Research Center, and informed consent was obtained from participants for the use of their radiographs. Data generated from the study is not publicly available but can be requested from the corresponding author. The authors declare no competing interests. 

The references section lists various studies relevant to the fields of dentistry, periodontology, and artificial intelligence applications in dental diagnostics, highlighting significant advancements and comparisons of different methodologies. The paper concludes with a note on the neutrality of Springer Nature regarding jurisdictional claims and affiliations.

The paper presents a quantitative evaluation of various algorithms for non-rigid 2-D shape contour registration and rigid 3-D point cloud registration. It discusses the performance of several methods under varying conditions such as noise levels and outlier ratios. The algorithms evaluated include GMM, CPD, L2E, PR-GLS, and APM for 2-D registration, and GMM, CPD, ICP, and GoICP for 3-D registration. The results indicate that performance varies significantly across different algorithms and conditions.

Furthermore, the paper investigates graph matching techniques, assessing seven state-of-the-art methods: SM, SMAC, IPFP, RRWM, TM, GNCCP, and FGM using several publicly available datasets, including the CMU house sequence, car and motorbike dataset, and Chinese character dataset. The experiments focus on both equal-size and unequal-size matching scenarios. The findings reveal that FGM achieves the best accuracy but with a higher time cost, while RRWM offers a balanced performance with lower runtime. The study emphasizes the robustness of these algorithms against outliers, particularly highlighting the effectiveness of path-following algorithms like GNCCP and FGM. Overall, the paper provides a comprehensive evaluation of the algorithms, revealing insights into their performance and efficiency in graph matching tasks.

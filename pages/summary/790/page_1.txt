The paper titled "Image Matching from Handcrafted to Deep Features: A Survey" provides a comprehensive review of image matching techniques, highlighting their evolution from traditional handcrafted methods to modern deep learning approaches. Image matching is identified as a fundamental task in various visual applications, essential for recognizing and associating similar structures or content across multiple images.

The authors systematically analyze feature detection, description, and matching techniques within the image matching pipeline. They discuss the theoretical and practical advancements in these methods, addressing the growing variety of approaches that have emerged, particularly due to deep learning advancements. The paper emphasizes the importance of selecting appropriate methods based on specific application scenarios and performance requirements, such as accuracy and robustness.

Additionally, the survey includes a brief overview of typical applications of image matching, underscoring its significance in fields like computer vision and image analysis. The authors conduct extensive experiments on representative datasets to provide an objective comparison of both classical and contemporary techniques.

In conclusion, the paper assesses the current state of image matching technologies and offers insights and prospects for future research, aiming to serve as a valuable reference for researchers and engineers in the field.

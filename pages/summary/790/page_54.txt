This document is a compilation of references from various conference proceedings and journals related to computer vision. It includes works focusing on topics such as graph matching, feature detection, shape correspondence, and image registration. The authors cited employ a range of methodologies including Monte Carlo methods, heat diffusion for signature creation, and deep learning techniques for descriptor learning. 

Key contributions include the development of efficient algorithms for multi-graph matching, novel feature descriptors invariant to brightness changes, and advancements in 3D data description. Several works emphasize the importance of robust estimation techniques in image geometry and point set registration. 

The references encapsulate significant advancements in computer vision, showcasing a blend of theoretical frameworks and practical applications. These studies collectively contribute to the understanding and enhancement of feature matching and image processing techniques in diverse environments.

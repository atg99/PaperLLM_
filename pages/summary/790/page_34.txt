The paper presents a comparative analysis of various state-of-the-art mismatch removal algorithms evaluated across five datasets: DAISY, DTU, Retina, Remote Sensing (RS), and VGG. The performance metrics reported include precision (P), recall (R), F-score (F), and runtime (T) in milliseconds.

Key findings show that the LPM algorithm achieved the highest precision in the DAISY dataset at 98.29%, while the GMS algorithm had the highest recall at 99.62% in the Retina dataset. The F-score values highlight the effectiveness of the LRS algorithm in the RS dataset with an F-score of 99.27%. 

In terms of runtime, the GMS algorithm demonstrated the fastest performance on the RS dataset at 1.09 seconds, while the longest processing time was noted for the ICF algorithm on the DTU dataset at 9.20 seconds. 

The results indicate significant variations in algorithm performance across different datasets, emphasizing the need for careful selection of algorithms based on specific application requirements and dataset characteristics.

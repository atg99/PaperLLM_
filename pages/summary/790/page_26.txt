This paper reviews advancements in fundamental matrix estimation and robust model fitting techniques, particularly those leveraging deep learning. It discusses various methods such as NG-RANSAC, which utilizes learned guidance for hypothesis sampling, and CONSAC, which updates conditional sampling probabilities through neural networks. Learning-based approaches for mismatch removal are highlighted, including LFGC, which trains networks to classify correspondences, and LMR, which employs a two-class classifier for better performance. The paper also addresses the integration of graph neural networks in matching tasks, exemplified by the SuperGlue method, which optimizes correspondences through differentiable transport problems.

In the context of 3-D matching, the paper outlines keypoint detection and local feature description as essential steps for establishing correspondences. It reviews embedding methods that simplify matching by reducing degrees of freedom and discusses traditional approaches that embed shapes into Euclidean spaces for easier registration. Additionally, it covers direct point-wise matching techniques and the functional map framework, which represents correspondences through linear operators, allowing for efficient solutions. The paper references various studies and methodologies while emphasizing the potential of deep learning and graph-based techniques in addressing complex matching problems in both 2-D and 3-D scenarios.

This paper presents a comprehensive evaluation of state-of-the-art mismatch removal algorithms across five distinct image matching datasets: DAISY, DTU, Retina, RemoteSensing, and VGG. The evaluation metrics include precision, recall, F-score, and runtime, with results depicted in cumulative distribution curves. Each curve represents the percentage of image pairs achieving performance values below specific thresholds. The study highlights the significance of mismatch removal in various applications such as image retrieval and visual localization, referencing existing literature for further insights. The authors also plan to provide both quantitative and qualitative comparisons for other related tasks, including point set registration and pose estimation. Overall, the experiments aim to assess the effectiveness of different algorithms in improving 3-D modeling and related applications.

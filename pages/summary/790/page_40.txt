The paper presents a comparison of mean Average Precision (mAP) performance for various pose estimation methods on two datasets: YFCC100M and SUN3D. The table indicates the mAP values at different degrees of estimation (5, 10, and 20 degrees) for several methods, including GMS, ICF, LPM, SM, VFC, RANSAC, LFGC, and OAN. 

For the YFCC100M dataset, the OAN method demonstrates the highest mAP performance across all degrees, peaking at 52.18 at 20 degrees. Other methods show varying performance, with LFGC also performing notably well. In contrast, the SUN3D dataset shows generally lower mAP values, with OAN again leading at 17.50 at 5 degrees, followed by LFGC and VFC. 

Overall, the results highlight the effectiveness of the OAN method in pose estimation across both datasets, while also indicating the performance trends of other methods.

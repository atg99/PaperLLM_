The cited works encompass a range of advancements in image registration, feature matching, and computer vision techniques. Key contributions include Yang et al.'s Quicksilver, which presents a fast predictive image registration method using deep learning, and Go-ICP, a globally optimal solution for 3D point-set registration. The studies also highlight methods for robust multimodal image registration, such as Ye et al.'s approach based on structural similarity and Yew and Lee's RPM-NET for robust point matching.

Several papers focus on learning-based techniques, including Yi et al.'s Lift for learned invariant feature transforms and Zanfir and Sminchisescu's deep learning approach to graph matching. The integration of neural networks for stereo matching is discussed in works by Zbontar and LeCun, showcasing the effectiveness of CNNs in comparing image patches.

Emerging strategies for graph matching, such as Yu et al.'s channel-independent embedding and Zaslavskiy et al.'s path-following algorithm, indicate a shift towards more efficient and robust methods in this area. Other notable contributions include Zeng et al.'s work on finding correspondences through sparse and low-rank decomposition and Zhang et al.'s development of learning frameworks for two-view correspondences and geometry.

Overall, the collection reflects significant progress in leveraging deep learning and optimization techniques to enhance image processing capabilities across various applications.

The references listed encompass a variety of significant contributions to the fields of computer vision and machine learning, particularly in the context of feature detection, matching, and image registration. Key works include Rosten and Drummond's exploration of machine learning techniques for corner detection, which has evolved through multiple publications to enhance speed and accuracy. Rublee et al. introduced ORB, an efficient alternative to SIFT and SURF, marking a pivotal advancement in feature detection.

Rusu and colleagues focused on fast point feature histograms for 3D registration, while Sahillioglu and Yemez presented a combinatorial matching approach for dense isometric shape correspondence. Salti and others contributed to keypoint detection through symmetry-based methods and learning descriptor-specific detectors.

The emergence of graph neural networks for feature matching was notably highlighted by Sarlin et al. with their work on Superglue. Additionally, the comparative evaluations of hand-crafted versus learned features by Schonberger and Frahm underscored the evolving landscape of feature descriptors in computer vision.

The references also include various methodologies for image registration, such as the probabilistic subgraph matching techniques proposed by Schellewald and Schnörr and the application of deep learning in medical image registration by Sokooti et al. Overall, these works collectively advance the understanding and capabilities in computer vision, emphasizing the shift toward machine learning approaches for improved performance in various applications.

This paper presents a comprehensive survey of image matching methods, highlighting the limitations of existing literature that often focuses on specific aspects or outdated techniques. Previous reviews predominantly addressed handcrafted methods, failing to adequately reference modern CNN-based approaches. Recent reviews have typically concentrated on either feature detectors or descriptors, or have narrowed their scope to particular matching tasks or related applications.

The authors aim to fill this gap by providing a thorough assessment of both traditional and learning-based image matching methods. The survey is organized into distinct sections: feature detection and description techniques, which cover both handcrafted and trainable methods; various matching methods, including area-based image matching and graph matching; and an overview of visual applications and evaluation metrics. The paper concludes with a discussion on future developments in the field.

The survey emphasizes feature-based matching while also addressing patch matching and point set registration. It aims to offer a detailed evaluation of mainstream methods that have not been extensively covered in prior literature, thereby serving as a valuable reference for researchers in the domain of image matching.

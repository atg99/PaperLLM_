The paper evaluates various methods for recognizing Chinese characters by analyzing their average accuracy and runtime across a dataset. The methods compared include SM, SMAC, IPFP, RRWM, TM, GNCCP, and FGM. 

For Character 1, the best accuracy was achieved by RRWM with 0.5548, while the fastest runtime was by SM at 0.0045 seconds. Character 2 showed the highest accuracy with FGM at 0.8986 and the lowest runtime with SM at 0.0033 seconds. In the case of Character 3, the highest accuracy was again with TM at 0.9000, with SM showing the quickest runtime at 0.0039 seconds. Finally, for Character 4, FGM achieved the highest accuracy of 0.6116, while SM also had the fastest runtime at 0.0033 seconds. 

Overall, the results highlight a trade-off between accuracy and runtime across the different methods for character recognition.

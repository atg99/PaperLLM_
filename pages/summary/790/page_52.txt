This document provides a comprehensive list of references related to various aspects of computer vision, particularly focusing on image registration, shape matching, and neural network applications. Key contributions include:

1. **Functional Maps**: Introduced by Ovsjanikov et al., this concept provides a flexible representation of maps between shapes, enhancing the understanding of geometric transformations.

2. **Multi-Way Matching**: Pachauri et al. propose a solution through permutation synchronization, addressing the complexities of matching multiple datasets.

3. **3D Point Registration**: The work by Pais et al. presents 3DRegNet, a deep neural network designed for efficient point registration, indicating advancements in 3D data processing.

4. **Image Matching Techniques**: Pan et al. and Papazov & Burschka explore methods for image matching in different domains, highlighting efficient algorithms for robust point set registration.

5. **Deep Learning Approaches**: Qi et al. introduce PointNet and PointNet++, which leverage deep learning for point set classification and segmentation, marking a significant step in processing unstructured data.

6. **Stereoscopic and Optical Image Registration**: Various studies, including those by Paul & Pati and Poggi et al., focus on improving registration techniques for remote sensing and stereo matching, showcasing the importance of precise alignment in these applications.

7. **Shape Correspondence and Matching**: Rodola et al. and others delve into deformable shape matching, employing game-theoretic approaches and random forests to achieve dense correspondences, which are crucial in computer graphics and object recognition.

The references collectively underscore the evolution of methodologies in computer vision, particularly emphasizing the integration of machine learning techniques to enhance traditional algorithms in image registration and shape analysis.

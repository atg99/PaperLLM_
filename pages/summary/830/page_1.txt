The paper "Hessian Aware Quantization of Neural Networks with Mixed-Precision" by Zhen Dong et al. addresses the challenges of model size and inference speed in deploying neural networks. A promising solution is mixed-precision quantization, which allows different layers of a network to operate at varying precision levels. However, determining the optimal precision for each layer is complex, especially in deep networks where the search space is exponential.

To tackle this issue, the authors propose Hessian AWare Quantization (HAWQ), a second-order quantization method that automatically selects the quantization precision based on the Hessian spectrum of each layer. HAWQ also establishes a deterministic fine-tuning order for quantizing layers, streamlining the process.

The effectiveness of HAWQ is demonstrated through experiments on various datasets, including Cifar-10 and ImageNet, using models like ResNet20, Inception-V3, and ResNet50. Results indicate that HAWQ achieves comparable or improved accuracy while significantly compressing model sizes—up to 8 times for ResNet20 and achieving 68% accuracy in a 1MB model for SqueezeNext.

The paper highlights the urgent need for efficient neural network deployment due to increasing computational demands from larger models and higher input resolutions. It categorizes existing efforts to address these challenges into five approaches, with HAWQ focusing specifically on quantization. The authors suggest that combining their method with other techniques could further enhance model efficiency.

The paper presents quantization results for different neural network architectures, specifically Inception-V3, ResNet50, and SqueezeNext, on the ImageNet dataset, emphasizing the performance of the HAWQ method. Key metrics include quantization bits for weights and activations, top-1 testing accuracy, weight compression ratio, and model size. 

For Inception-V3, HAWQ achieved a top-1 accuracy of 75.52% with a weight compression ratio of 12.04, outperforming both direct quantization and Integer-Only methods. In ResNet50, HAWQ also demonstrated superior performance with a top-1 accuracy of 75.48% and a compression ratio of 12.28 compared to state-of-the-art methods. 

SqueezeNext results highlighted HAWQ's ability to maintain accuracy while achieving significant compression, showcasing a model size reduction to 1MB with a minimal accuracy drop. 

Figures in the paper illustrate the efficacy of HAWQ in enhancing accuracy recovery and fine-tuning speed compared to alternative methods, indicating its effectiveness in Hessian aware mixed precision quantization. The study concludes that HAWQ not only provides higher compression ratios but also maintains or improves accuracy compared to existing quantization techniques.

The paper presents quantization results for ResNet20 on Cifar-10 and Inception-V3 on ImageNet, evaluating various quantization methods including Direct, Dorefa, PACT, LQ-Nets, DNAS, and HAWQ. Key metrics include quantization bit lengths for weights and activations, accuracy, and compression ratios.

In the Cifar-10 results, the baseline model achieves an accuracy of 92.37% with no compression. The Direct method, while providing high compression, results in a 2.03% accuracy drop. HAWQ demonstrates superior performance with a minimal accuracy loss of 0.15% and achieves a 13.11× compression ratio, outperforming DNAS with a higher compression ratio for activations. 

For Inception-V3 on ImageNet, the Direct quantization leads to a significant 7.69% accuracy drop. HAWQ shows a smaller accuracy drop of 2% with a compression ratio of 12.04×, better than competing methods like Integer-Only and RVQuant. Additionally, HAWQ achieves a top-1 accuracy of 75.48% for ResNet50 with a smaller model size compared to HAQ, which has a larger model but slightly lower accuracy. 

The paper also highlights the successful application of HAWQ to SqueezeNext, achieving only a 1.36% accuracy drop with a model size reduced to 1MB, suitable for deployment in resource-constrained environments.

An ablation study indicates that HAWQ's effectiveness stems from its two-step process: utilizing second-order information for determining precision order and fine-tuning based on this order. This method enhances quantization performance while maintaining accuracy.

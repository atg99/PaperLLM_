The results indicate that models trained with adversarial examples perform significantly better on these challenging datasets compared to those trained solely on standard NLI data. This underscores the importance of adversarial training in enhancing model robustness against difficult instances.

In summary, the paper presents findings on the performance of the RoBERTa model across different rounds of adversarial training data, highlighting that while RoBERTa achieves state-of-the-art results on benchmark datasets, it struggles with certain examples compared to BERT and XLNet. The research emphasizes that augmenting training with adversarial data does not degrade performance on traditional tasks, and that verified examples of adversarial vulnerabilities are particularly beneficial for model training. Finally, the study demonstrates that adversarial data improves overall model performance, especially on hard NLI test sets, confirming the effectiveness of the proposed adversarial training methodology.

The paper presents histograms illustrating the results of an experiment conducted over three rounds, focusing on the effectiveness of a model in verifying examples. Figure 4 details the number of attempts required to achieve good verified examples, while Figure 5 shows the time spent on each verified example in seconds. Figure 6 analyzes the number of tokens utilized in contexts and hypotheses, employing Byte Pair Encoding.

Figure 7 categorizes the outcomes across the three rounds into distinct groups: A represents correctly identified examples, B1 includes examples the model got wrong but where two verifiers agreed with the writer, B2 includes examples with mixed agreement among verifiers, C represents cases where two verifiers overruled the writer, and D indicates examples with no consensus among verifiers. Notably, groups A and C are added only to the training set, while groups B1 and B2 are incorporated into the training, development, or test sets, with group D being discarded. This structure allows for a comprehensive evaluation of the model's performance and the verification process.

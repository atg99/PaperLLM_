The paper presents a comparative analysis of the Multi-ResNet, Residual U-Nets, and the Fourier Neural Operator (FNO) in the context of modeling partial differential equations (PDEs). The results, summarized in Tables 4 and 5, indicate that U-Nets consistently outperform FNO across various PDE modeling tasks while maintaining a similar parameter count. 

In Table 4, the performance metrics for the Navier-Stokes and Shallow Water datasets reveal that the Residual U-Net and Multi-ResNet achieve lower root mean square error (r-MSE) values compared to the FNO, highlighting their effectiveness in PDE tasks. Specifically, the Residual U-Net and Multi-ResNet have r-MSE values of 0.0057 and 0.0040 for the Navier-Stokes dataset, respectively, while the FNO has an r-MSE of 0.0253. For the Shallow Water dataset, the U-Nets again show superior performance with r-MSE values of 0.1712 and 0.1493 compared to the FNO's 1.2333.

Table 5 corroborates these findings using results from PDEArena, where the U-Net 2015 64 model significantly outperforms the FNO model, regardless of the dataset size or configuration. For the Navier-Stokes dataset, the U-Net achieves an r-MSE of 0.01386 compared to the FNO's 0.03836, and for the Shallow Water dataset, the U-Net's r-MSE is 0.1026 versus 0.8549 for the FNO. 

These results collectively demonstrate the superior effectiveness of U-Nets over FNO in the context of PDE modeling tasks.

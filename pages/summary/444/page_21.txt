The paper discusses the use of a mass matrix and a weak formulation of partial differential equations (PDEs) to solve linear systems efficiently at finite resolutions. This approach is characterized as a Galerkin truncation, which allows for the integration of resolution-dependent data, thereby supporting the construction of a U-Net model that adheres to specific measurability constraints outlined in Theorem 1. 

An explicit example is provided, demonstrating the creation of an orthogonal basis for the function space H1_0([0,1]) using a foundational basis function and its refined versions. The paper details the process of refining this basis further, leading to the formulation of basis functions that represent Haar wavelets up to a certain resolution. The U-Net is constructed by spanning these basis functions while incorporating boundary constraints and smoothness requirements pertinent to the PDE solutions. This methodology aims to enhance the capability of the U-Net in handling complex problems associated with PDEs.

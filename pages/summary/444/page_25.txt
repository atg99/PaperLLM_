The paper discusses the performance of Multi-ResNets in comparison to classical Residual U-Nets, particularly in the context of modeling partial differential equations (PDEs) using Haar wavelet transformations. The authors suggest that enhancing the decoder in Multi-ResNets could lead to superior results compared to traditional U-Nets, especially when applied to diffusion models. 

Quantitative performance is summarized in a table comparing the mean-squared-error (r-MSE) of different architectures across two datasets: Navier-Stokes and Shallow Water equations. The Multi-ResNet architectures exhibit varying parameter configurations, with results indicating that a Multi-ResNet with saved parameters in the decoder (version 1) achieved the best performance on the Navier-Stokes dataset, with an r-MSE of 0.0040, outperforming both the classical U-Net and other Multi-ResNet configurations.

Figure 12 further illustrates the predictive capabilities of the Haar wavelet Multi-ResNet, demonstrating the modeling of PDE trajectories over five time steps without conditioning on previous states. The results affirm the effectiveness of the Multi-ResNet approach in accurately forecasting PDE dynamics, as evidenced by the visual representation of ground truth, predictions, and associated errors. Overall, the findings support the potential of Multi-ResNets in advancing the field of PDE modeling.

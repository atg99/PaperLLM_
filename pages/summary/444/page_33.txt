Various orthogonal wavelet families were tested, including Haar and Daubechies wavelets, revealing that while the choice of wavelet does influence performance, the effect is relatively minor. The findings support the notion that subspace preconditioning and wavelet selection are relevant factors in enhancing neural network performance for specific applications.

This section of the paper presents enhanced visual data in Figure 11, which offers a larger resolution of Figure 7, illustrating multi-resolution training and sampling of diffusion models. The study utilizes a Residual U-Net architecture and demonstrates the effectiveness of preconditioning across various resolutions and iterations for datasets such as MNIST and CIFAR10.

Additionally, the paper provides quantitative results comparing the performance of the Haar wavelet Multi-ResNet and Residual U-Nets on PDE modeling tasks, specifically focusing on the Navier-Stokes and Shallow Water datasets. Two methods of parameter allocation in Multi-ResNets are evaluated: the first method (v1) involves increasing the number of residual blocks per resolution, while the second method (v2) increases the number of hidden channels in the ResNet. The findings reveal that v1 significantly outperforms v2, with v2 failing to surpass the performance of the Residual U-Net. This highlights the critical importance of parameter allocation design in achieving optimal performance in Multi-ResNets. The authors suggest that future research should investigate the best strategies for parameter allocation in Multi-ResNets through large-scale experimentation.

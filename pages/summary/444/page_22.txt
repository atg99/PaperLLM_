This section provides additional experimental details and results regarding the model architecture, training process, hyperparameters, and evaluation metrics used in the study. 

The authors employed slightly modified U-Net architectures tailored for different tasks, maintaining a structure that includes a single residual block per resolution, two convolutional layers, and group normalization. Average pooling was generally chosen as the pooling method. Although the modifications to the original repositories may account for minor differences in reported results, the authors assert that their findings remain comparable. The staged training approach necessitated the inclusion of a 'head' and 'tail' network at each resolution, which increased the overall model's parameter count compared to single-stage training.

Regarding hyperparameters, the authors conducted minimal tuning, primarily adopting those from the original repositories and making adjustments only when necessary to ensure a fair comparison. This indicates potential for performance enhancement, particularly with models like Multi-ResNet and U-Net on triangular data.

For evaluation, the authors utilized three standard performance metrics: FID score, rollout mean-squared-error (r-MSE), and Sørensen–Dice coefficient (Dice). The FID score was computed on a holdout dataset using an evaluation model that updates weights through an exponential moving average. The r-MSE was calculated as the mean squared error over segments of the PDE trajectory in an autoregressive manner. All metrics were averaged over three random seeds and based on a consistent number of iterations. The authors noted that the trends observed in their extensive logging of various other evaluation metrics were consistent with those reported.

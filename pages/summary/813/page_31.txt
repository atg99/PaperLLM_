This paper presents a comprehensive survey on pruning and quantization techniques in model deployment, highlighting various models and their performance metrics in terms of Top-1 and Top-5 accuracy. The models are categorized based on their weight (W) and activation (A) quantization levels, which range from 1 to 32 bits.

Key findings include:

1. **Model Performance**: Models such as BNN, DoReFa-Net, and various iterations of LQ-NETs demonstrate significant accuracy, with BNN achieving a Top-1 accuracy of 29.10% and DoReFa-Net reaching up to 20.90%.

2. **Quantization Techniques**: Different quantization strategies, like SYQ and FGQ, show varying results, with SYQ achieving a Top-1 accuracy of 5.40% at certain configurations. The effectiveness of these techniques highlights the trade-offs between model size and accuracy.

3. **Impact of Pruning**: Pruning methods, when applied, often enhance the efficiency of models without substantial accuracy loss. For instance, GroupNet and various WRPN models exhibit notable performance with reduced parameters.

4. **Mixed Precision**: The study also covers mixed-precision approaches, such as ResNet-50 and ResNet-101, which indicate that careful quantization can maintain competitive performance while reducing computational demands.

5. **References**: The paper cites numerous studies, providing a solid foundation for each model's performance claims, ensuring credibility and a pathway for further research.

Overall, this survey serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners looking to understand the landscape of model pruning and quantization, offering insights into effective strategies for improving model deployment in real-world applications.

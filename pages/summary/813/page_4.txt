This paper provides an extensive survey on pruning and quantization techniques in machine learning, particularly focusing on deep neural networks (DNNs). Key concepts include various methods of convolution, such as standard convolution, depth-wise convolution, point-wise convolution, and separable convolution, which are essential for reducing model size and computational requirements.

The paper highlights the importance of feature maps in convolution operations, explaining how multiple filters can be employed to learn different features from input data. It discusses the optimization of convolution performance through techniques like the image-to-column (im2col) method, which transforms 3D tensors into 2D matrices for efficient processing using General Matrix Multiply (GEMM) libraries.

Additionally, the survey includes a comprehensive list of acronyms and abbreviations relevant to the field, providing clarity on terms used throughout the paper. The authors aim to present a thorough understanding of how pruning and quantization can enhance the efficiency of AI models while maintaining accuracy, emphasizing the significance of compression techniques to reduce model size and improve performance.

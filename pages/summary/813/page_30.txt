The paper presents a comprehensive survey on pruning and quantization techniques in neural networks, focusing on their performance evaluation across various models deployed on the ILSVRC2012 dataset. The section on quantization performance results includes a detailed table summarizing accuracy drops for various models, bit-widths of weights and activations, and their corresponding top-1 and top-5 accuracy metrics. 

Key findings indicate that different quantization methods, such as PACT, DoReFa-Net, and ELNN, show varying degrees of accuracy retention when lowering bit-widths. Notably, models like BWN and ELNN achieve significant top-1 accuracy improvements, with ELNN reaching up to 8.4% accuracy with 1-bit weights. The results highlight the trade-offs between model size reduction and accuracy, showcasing that while some methods incur minimal accuracy drops, others can lead to substantial improvements.

The survey emphasizes the importance of selecting appropriate quantization strategies tailored to specific applications, balancing performance requirements with computational efficiency. Overall, the findings contribute valuable insights into the effectiveness of quantization techniques in optimizing neural network deployment for practical use cases.

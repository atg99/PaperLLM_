The paper discusses various techniques for pruning and quantization in neural networks during inference, focusing on methods that reduce the complexity of models while maintaining performance. DeepShift-PS utilizes shift and sign parameters as trainable parameters, with logarithmic encoding benefiting redundant networks like VGG-16, which experiences only a 1.31% accuracy loss, unlike DenseNet-121, which sees a 4.02% loss.

Plus-minus quantization, dating back to 1990, reduces weights to 1-bit representations, eliminating costly multiplications. Binarized Neural Networks (BNNs) use 1-bit weights and activations, encoding values to represent -1 and +1. The paper explains the bit-wise operations involved in these networks, highlighting their simplification compared to floating-point multiplications, which significantly reduces network size and energy consumption despite a loss of information that can impact accuracy.

Two primary methods for binarization are stochastic and deterministic. Stochastic methods assess global statistics or input data to determine parameter values, while deterministic methods compute values based on a threshold. The paper references Binary Connect (BC) as an early stochastic approach, demonstrating its effectiveness on datasets like MNIST and CIFAR-10.

The paper also discusses advancements like BinaryNets, which binarize both weights and activations and report improved performance on various datasets. Rastegari's work on Binary Weight Networks (BWN) achieves accuracy comparable to single-precision versions by applying scaling factors. XNOR-Net further enhances this by incorporating scaling factors on inputs and reorganizing network structures, achieving notable accuracy on the ILSVRC-2012 dataset.

DoReFa-Net introduces a unique quantization approach, applying low-bit widths to gradients during backpropagation, resulting in varying accuracy losses depending on the bit combinations used. Lastly, Ternary Weight Networks (TWN) provide an alternative with ternary weights, achieving minimal accuracy loss compared to BNNs while mitigating information loss. The paper presents a comprehensive overview of these techniques, illustrating the trade-offs between model complexity and accuracy in quantized neural networks.

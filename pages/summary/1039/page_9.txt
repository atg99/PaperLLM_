B. Accountability in AI Systems
Accountability emerged as a critical theme for the adoption of autonomous systems, particularly in the context of machine learning applications. Participants emphasized the need for clear mechanisms to understand and explain AI decisions, especially in high-stakes scenarios like autonomous vehicles. Additionally, accountability extended to the process of AI implementation, highlighting the importance of ensuring that Indigenous collaborators benefit not only from the outcomes but also from the capacity-building processes associated with AI projects.

C. Human-Centred Values
The integration of human-centred values in AI design was noted as complex and often implicit. Participants highlighted the necessity of considering the cultural context in which AI systems are deployed, particularly when working with Indigenous groups. It is crucial to incorporate Indigenous knowledge and ethics into the design and operation of AI systems, ensuring that these technologies reflect the values and worldviews of the communities they serve.

D. Human, Social, and Environmental Well-being
Participants expressed concerns over the impacts of AI on human well-being, privacy, and social equity. There were apprehensions regarding certain applications, such as facial recognition, which could have negative implications for social well-being. Environmental well-being was also recognized as a motivation for AI projects, particularly those aimed at utilizing environmental data to support decision-making processes. The role of AI in enhancing social connections for Indigenous peoples, especially in monitoring their lands, was highlighted as a significant benefit.

E. Recommendations for Implementation
The discussion suggests that when implementing AI ethics principles, it is vital to prioritize privacy protection, foster accountability, embed human-centred values, and promote well-being across human, social, and environmental dimensions. Engaging with affected communities, particularly Indigenous peoples, throughout the development and deployment processes can enhance trust and ensure that AI systems are aligned with their needs and values. Furthermore, ongoing evaluations of the social impacts of AI technologies are essential to mitigate potential harms and reinforce equitable benefits across society.

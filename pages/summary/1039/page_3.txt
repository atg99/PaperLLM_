The paper presents an adapted summary of high-level ethics principles for AI as established by the Australian Government, emphasizing the importance of privacy, reliability, transparency, fairness, contestability, accountability, human-centered values, and overall well-being. Each principle highlights key aspects of ethical AI deployment, such as respecting privacy rights, ensuring reliable operation, fostering transparency in AI interactions, promoting inclusivity, allowing for contestation of AI decisions, maintaining accountability for outcomes, upholding human rights, and benefiting society and the environment.

The study involved interviews conducted from February to April 2021, analyzed through thematic analysis guided by predefined high-level principles. This analysis focused on identifying themes within the interview transcripts, ensuring reliability through independent cross-checking by the interviewers.

Ethics approval was granted by CSIRO’s committee, encompassing participant selection and the interview protocol, with informed consent obtained from all participants. Limitations of the study include potential selection bias due to non-random participant recruitment, although measures were taken to mitigate subjective bias through a diverse team of interviewers, enhancing the depth and breadth of inquiry.

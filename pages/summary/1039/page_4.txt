This study seeks to incorporate Indigenous viewpoints but lacks information on interviewees' ethnic backgrounds, potentially influencing the representation of these perspectives. Conducted within a single organization, the study may face external validity challenges, yet it aimed to include interviewees with diverse roles and expertise to enhance relevance for the broader AI development community. The interviews revealed key insights into the challenges AI developers encounter regarding ethics in research, development, and deployment.

The results section presents findings from a thematic analysis of interviews, focusing on practices, challenges, and insights aligned with high-level AI ethics principles. Key themes include:

1. **Privacy Protection and Security**: Participants recommended techniques such as federated machine learning, data aggregation, and differential privacy to safeguard privacy. However, concerns arose regarding the utility of machine learning models when using altered data, highlighting a trade-off between privacy and accuracy.

2. **Reliability and Safety**: This principle was emphasized by a majority of participants, indicating its significance in AI development.

3. **Transparency and Explainability**: A strong occurrence of this theme suggests that clarity in AI processes is crucial for stakeholder trust.

4. **Fairness, Contestability, Accountability, Human-Centred Values, and HSE Well-being**: These principles received varied emphasis, with fairness being less frequently discussed.

Participants noted the importance of gaining trust from collaborators and data providers, which is essential for effective privacy and data protection practices. Ethical approval and privacy impact assessments were highlighted as critical for managing sensitive data and ensuring compliance. Additionally, external collaborators often impose data access restrictions, necessitating researchers to adapt their practices accordingly. Overall, the insights gathered from the interviews reveal the complexities and considerations involved in addressing AI ethics in development.

Concerns about the deployment of AI systems were prevalent, with interviewees noting that recommended criteria might be overlooked in practice, leading to potential accountability issues. The lack of control over AI use post-development raised questions about the responsibilities of developers. In the realm of autonomous systems, regulatory gaps regarding AI technologies in drones highlighted reliability and safety issues, particularly regarding human oversight.

The principle of transparency was closely tied to explainability, which participants believed was crucial for user trust and accountability. The distinction between symbolic and statistical AI was noted, with the former providing clearer reasoning processes. Participants expressed that explainability might be prioritized over performance in fostering trust, yet acknowledged the trade-offs between accuracy and the ability to explain AI outputs.

Overall, the paper underscores the complexities and challenges developers face in balancing data collection, privacy, fairness, reliability, and the need for transparency and explainability in AI systems.

The paper discusses the need for enhanced support mechanisms at both organizational and design levels to implement responsible AI practices effectively. It highlights the gap between high-level ethical principles, such as transparency and explainability, and the practical tools available for AI designers and developers. Current methods tend to focus on post-hoc explanations rather than proactive design for accountability and transparency, often overlooking societal impacts in favor of individual assessments.

To operationalize AI ethics, the paper identifies four key areas: governance, AI design and development, competence and knowledge development, and stakeholder communication. It emphasizes that implementing ethical design should be viewed as an ongoing design process rather than a checklist. The authors propose three complementary design approaches: 

1. **Ethics in Design**: Involves considering the purpose and potential consequences of the AI system during development, ensuring that human values are incorporated.

2. **Ethics by Design**: Focuses on the behavior of deployed AI systems, ensuring they adhere to ethical principles and minimize harm, especially in interactions with humans and animals.

3. **Ethics for Designers**: Addresses the ethical frameworks governing the developers themselves, including codes of conduct and regulatory requirements.

The methodology section details an interview study conducted with CSIRO scientists and engineers involved in AI systems. The study aimed to gather insights on how participants perceive and implement ethical principles in their work. A total of 21 interviews were conducted, involving individuals across various roles and levels of experience. Participants were prompted to reflect on the relevance of specific ethical principles in their work and suggest useful tools for addressing these principles.

Overall, the paper calls for a more integrated approach to ethical AI design and development, emphasizing the need for organizations to understand the complexities involved in fostering responsible AI practices.

The paper emphasizes the significance of cultural respect in AI projects, particularly highlighting the involvement of Indigenous communities in environmental decision-making. It advocates for integrating cultural considerations into both functional and data requirements of AI initiatives.

To implement ethical AI principles effectively, the paper proposes two support mechanisms: organizational support and design notes for developers. 

Organizational support involves creating processes to embed AI ethics into an organization's digital strategy. This includes training staff on AI principles, defining permissible AI uses, and conducting analyses of existing systems to ensure alignment with ethical standards. The introduction of a Data Planner (RDP) is recommended to identify the relevance of AI ethics principles for specific projects, enhancing awareness and operationalization of these principles. Additionally, the use of dataset datasheets and model cards is encouraged to improve accountability and transparency regarding datasets and AI models.

Design notes aim to bridge the gap between high-level ethical principles and their practical application by developers. These notes would provide specific guidance and attributes to consider during the design and implementation of AI systems, ensuring that ethical considerations are integrated into the development process. The paper suggests that including literature-based recommendations can enrich these design notes and promote the use of established design methods aligned with ethical principles.

The paper discusses secure evaluation techniques for quantized neural networks, highlighting the importance of privacy and efficiency in machine learning. It references key works in the field, such as those exploring weight quantization in Boltzmann machines and privacy-preserving protocols for neural network computations. The authors emphasize advancements in homomorphic encryption and secure multiparty computation, which enable the processing of neural networks on encrypted data without compromising privacy. Additionally, the paper reviews frameworks like MP-SPDZ and CrypTen, which facilitate secure machine learning. The relevance of quantization for reducing model size and computation cost is underscored, with references to various methods and theories in quantized neural networks. Overall, the paper provides a comprehensive overview of the intersection of secure computation and neural network quantization, addressing both theoretical and practical aspects.

The paper discusses secure evaluation methods for quantized neural networks, focusing on the need for privacy-preserving techniques in machine learning. It references various works that contribute to this field, including methods for compressing neural networks through pruning, quantization, and coding techniques, as well as frameworks for secure multi-party computation.

Key contributions highlighted in the paper include the use of efficient convolutional neural networks for mobile applications, the development of binarized neural networks, and advancements in batch normalization to enhance training speed. It also discusses frameworks like GAZELLE and ABY3, which facilitate low-latency secure neural network inference and mixed protocol frameworks for machine learning, respectively.

The paper emphasizes the importance of maintaining privacy in neural network inference, particularly through techniques like homomorphic encryption and oblivious neural network computing. It references various studies that explore quantization strategies to improve inference efficiency while preserving the accuracy of neural networks.

Overall, the paper provides a comprehensive overview of the intersection between neural network quantization and secure computation, underlining the significance of developing robust and efficient methods for privacy-preserving machine learning applications.

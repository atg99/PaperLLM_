The paper discusses the secure evaluation of quantized neural networks, particularly focusing on the MobileNets architecture. MobileNets consist of 28 layers and are designed for image classification tasks, trained on the ImageNet dataset. The architecture features alternating pointwise and depthwise convolutions, with the size of the network adjustable through hyperparameters: the width multiplier (α) and the resolution multiplier (ρ). The width multiplier affects both the model size and the number of operations, while the resolution multiplier only affects the operations.

The authors chose MobileNets for benchmarking due to their expressiveness in solving various image classification tasks and the availability of pre-trained models, making the results reproducible. The technique for quantizing models can involve either quantization-aware training or post-quantization of pre-trained models, all implemented through TensorFlow without needing external conversion.

The implementation of secure inference is conducted within the MP-SPDZ framework, which supports various protocols over prime fields or rings. The specific configurations used include a 128-bit prime and a 72-bit ring for efficient computation, particularly for shift and dot-product operations. Benchmarks were executed on AWS machines with robust specifications to ensure accurate timing measurements for the evaluation processes.

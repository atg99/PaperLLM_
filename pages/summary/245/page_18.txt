The references section cites various studies that explore deep learning models for medical applications, including the detection of glaucoma using fundus photography and image retraining techniques for evaluating environmental impacts. Other cited works discuss the efficacy of collective physician diagnoses, advancements in fuzzy C-means algorithms, and innovative mobile applications aimed at melanoma prevention.

A specific analysis is presented comparing different deep learning models, such as VGG-16, DenseNet, and ResNet, based on metrics including precision, recall, and F1 score across multiple epochs. The results indicate varying rankings and performance metrics for these models, contributing to the ongoing evaluation of machine learning techniques in medical diagnostics.

The conclusion emphasizes the critical nature of timely detection in melanoma, as delays in diagnosis and treatment contribute to its high mortality rate. It highlights the potential of computer-based methods to enhance detection accuracy, particularly through the use of convolutional neural networks (CNNs). The research demonstrates that a customized CNN architecture with three layers, including two 2D convolutional layers, performs better than other models on the HAM10000 dataset. For future research, the authors propose merging multiple datasets to improve model accuracy and flexibility. Additionally, they plan to explore illumination techniques to aid in the detection and classification of unclear images, aiming to create more robust detection systems.

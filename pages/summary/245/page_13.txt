The paper presents a comparative analysis of various models for melanoma cancer detection, highlighting the performance of the DenseNet-II model. The DenseNet-II achieved an impressive F1-score of 95.7% and accuracy rates of 93.8%, 95.7%, and 97.351% for 10, 15, and 20 epochs, respectively. In contrast, the VGG-16 model had the lowest F1-score of 74%. A comparative analysis based on accuracy at 15 epochs shows DenseNet-II leading with an accuracy of 96.27%, outperforming other models such as VGG-16 (75.27%), DenseNet (87.30%), and ResNet (86.90%). The findings conclude that DenseNet-II is the best performing model among the state-of-the-art approaches for the specified problem, demonstrating its effectiveness in melanoma detection.

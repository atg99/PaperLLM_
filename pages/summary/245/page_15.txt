In the study, methods are compared using a ranking system where the best method is assigned a rank of 1 and the worst a rank of k. Since the research focuses solely on the HAM10000 dataset, averaging ranks across multiple datasets is unnecessary. The significance of the results is evaluated through a p-value test statistic, where a significance level (alpha) of 0.05 indicates a significant difference between the compared methods, leading to the rejection of the null hypothesis. The experimental setup examines various epochs, utilizing evaluation metrics such as precision and recall. Figures illustrate the confusion matrix for the proposed DenseNet-II model at 20 epochs and provide a comparative analysis of the model's precision at 10, 15, and 20 epochs.

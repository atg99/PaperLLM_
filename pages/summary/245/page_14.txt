In this section, the statistical analysis of the results is conducted using Friedman testing to validate the findings from the HAM10000 dataset. The Friedman test is a non-parametric method, making it suitable for this analysis as it does not require assumptions about data distribution. This approach allows for the ranking of various algorithms based on their performance, facilitating a comparison of state-of-the-art methods. The results include accuracy and loss plots of the DenseNet-II model at different epochs, specifically at 10, 15, and 20 epochs, highlighting the model's performance over time.

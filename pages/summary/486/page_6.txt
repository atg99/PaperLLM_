The paper discusses the conversion of numerical data into unstructured images for improved processing by a Deep Belief Network (DBN) model. Specifically, closing prices for every five working days were transformed into 50x50 pixel images. The data was split into training (5000 samples), validation (3000 samples), and testing (2000 samples) sets. The DBN model underwent training using the pre-training samples, followed by validation and testing to ensure accuracy and reliability.

The trained DBN model was applied to financial market data from 2012 to 2014, utilizing a sliding window approach for continuous prediction of daily market trends. This method allowed for effective analysis of complex financial time series data, identifying critical patterns and trends for financial decision-making.

The training process involved a three-layer RBM-based DBN deep learning network, structured with specific nodes in each layer. The methodology included layer-wise training, GPU acceleration for efficiency, fine-tuning with validation and testing data, and the addition of a classifier to enhance prediction accuracy. The detailed training process is illustrated in the paper, highlighting the effectiveness of the DBN model in accurately classifying various types of data trends.

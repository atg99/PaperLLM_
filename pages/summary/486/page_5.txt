The paper discusses the methodology for collecting and analyzing financial data to train a Deep Belief Network (DBN) model for stock price prediction. It details the classification of stock price trends into three categories: upward, downward, and no clear trend, as illustrated in a provided figure. The dataset comprised closing prices from all stocks in the Shanghai and Shenzhen stock markets over a period of 100 working days prior to October 20, 2012. 

For training the DBN model, the authors generated a total of 10,000 samples, which included 5,000 pre-training samples, 3,000 validation samples, and 2,000 testing samples. The trained model was then utilized to make predictions on daily data spanning from 2012 to 2014, which helped verify the model's effectiveness. This structured approach to data collection and categorization plays a crucial role in the predictive capabilities of the model.

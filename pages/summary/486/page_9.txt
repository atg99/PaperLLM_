The paper discusses the emerging trends in artificial intelligence-based financial risk monitoring technologies. It highlights advancements in machine learning and hybrid deep learning approaches that enhance predictive capabilities in financial services. The authors explore the integration of AI for fraud prevention in digital payment systems and the development of multimodal networks for emotional recognition in students. They also address the significance of AI in automating training and deployment processes, optimizing cloud resources, and improving customer churn predictions in marketing.

Furthermore, the paper reviews applications of AI in various sectors, including medical imaging and robotic navigation, emphasizing the role of innovative technologies like generative AI and machine learning in enhancing operational efficiency. The discussion encompasses the implementation of intelligent predictive models for patient risk management in cloud environments and the integration of AI with SLAM technology for navigation and localization in unknown environments.

Overall, the paper provides a comprehensive overview of how AI is transforming financial risk monitoring and other sectors, reinforcing the importance of these technologies in enhancing decision-making and operational processes.

The experiments revealed that the DQN algorithm effectively optimized packet scheduling by maximizing the number of successfully transmitted packets while adhering to quality of service (QoS) parameters. The use of a discrete-event simulation library allowed for a realistic representation of communication sessions within the wireless sensor network (WSN) model. The performance evaluation showed that the DQN algorithm maintained a high transmission ratio, indicating efficient management of packet lifetimes and energy consumption.

Key findings indicated that the DQN-based scheduler extended the network lifetime by efficiently utilizing the energy of slave nodes, which were the limiting factor due to their finite battery levels. The training process involved hundreds of episodes, which contributed to the algorithm's ability to learn optimal scheduling strategies over time.

Overall, the study highlighted the advantages of applying deep reinforcement learning techniques, specifically the DQN algorithm, for enhancing packet scheduling in WSNs, suggesting that such approaches could lead to more resilient and efficient network operations.

The referenced paper discusses various studies related to the power consumption and efficiency of Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and other wireless communication protocols. It highlights analyses of BLE sensor nodes in cyclic sleep scenarios, advertisement intervals to optimize discovery time, and packet collision models in BLE advertising mode. Additionally, it covers application-level analyses for data streaming, energy efficiency improvements using Q-learning approaches, and fuzzy logic methods for power management in smart homes. The paper emphasizes the importance of energy efficiency in the context of the Internet of Things, providing insights into methodologies for enhancing performance in BLE applications.

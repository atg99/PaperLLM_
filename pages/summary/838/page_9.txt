TFLite Micro provides tools for converting trained models into a format suitable for deployment, along with a lightweight runtime that can execute the models on microcontroller hardware.

In the context of quantization-aware training, it is noted that convolutional and fully connected layers require careful training to ensure that the weights are quantized properly. While batch normalization layers also necessitate this training, they were not utilized in the experiments described. Max-pooling layers do not require quantization as they do not involve trainable weights and only reduce data without the ability to recover precision. Similarly, the ReLU activation functions are treated as separate layers and do not need quantization. However, element-wise addition layers do require quantization due to the potential increase in output dynamic range when adding large values.

The deployment of a quantized neural network encompasses several steps: exporting and encoding the network weights for the target platform, generating and compiling an inference program based on the network architecture, and uploading both the program and weights to the microcontroller's ROM. This streamlined process enables efficient execution of deep learning models in embedded systems, enhancing the capabilities of devices operating under strict resource limitations.

The paper evaluates the performance of two embedded platforms, the SparkFun Edge and the Nucleo-L452RE-P, for deploying deep neural networks on microcontrollers. Both platforms operate at 48 MHz with a 3.3 V supply. The Nucleo-L452RE-P features an STM32L452RE microcontroller with 128 kiB RAM and 512 kiB Flash, while the SparkFun Edge is equipped with an Ambiq Apollo3 microcontroller, offering 384 kiB RAM and 1024 kiB Flash. 

Key findings highlight that despite similar core specifications, the SparkFun Edge achieves significantly lower power consumption due to its subthreshold operation, using only 0.82 mA compared to 4.80 mA for the Nucleo-L452RE-P. However, the CoreMark benchmark indicates that the Ambiq Apollo3 is slower than the STM32L452RE. The paper emphasizes the trade-offs between power efficiency and processing speed, relevant for applications in deep learning on microcontrollers.

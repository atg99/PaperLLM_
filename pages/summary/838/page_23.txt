The paper presents a comparative analysis of various machine learning frameworks, specifically TFLite Micro, STM32Cube.AI, and MicroAI, focusing on their performance metrics such as ROM footprint and inference time. The results indicate that variations in implementation, particularly concerning memory access and cache utilization for Flash memory, contribute to the observed improvements in performance. Figures illustrate the ROM footprint and inference time across different data types (float32, int16, int8) for configurations involving 80 filters per convolution. The findings highlight the efficiency of different platforms in terms of resource utilization and processing speed, which are critical for deploying machine learning models in constrained environments.

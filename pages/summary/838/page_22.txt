The study focuses on deploying a residual neural network trained on the UCI-HAR dataset across different embedded AI frameworks, specifically STM32Cube.AI and TensorFlow Lite for Microcontrollers. The inference times for each framework were compared using both 8-bit and 16-bit fixed-point representations. The results indicate that STM32Cube.AI outperformed TensorFlow Lite for Microcontrollers in terms of inference time, requiring only 352 ms for 8-bit inference compared to 592 ms for TensorFlow Lite. Additionally, the authors note that while their framework had higher inference times of 1034 ms and 1003 ms on the Nucleo-L452RE-P and SparkFun Edge boards respectively, it still provided comparable performance with 16-bit fixed-point inference.

The paper emphasizes the importance of optimizing neural network models for embedded systems, highlighting the trade-offs between model complexity and inference efficiency. The results demonstrate that while smaller neural networks exhibit higher overhead in certain frameworks, optimizing for specific hardware can lead to significant performance gains. The findings are summarized in tables and figures that illustrate the performance metrics across different configurations and frameworks. Overall, the study contributes valuable insights into the deployment of deep learning models on constrained embedded platforms.

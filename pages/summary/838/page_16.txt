The paper discusses the implementation and results of a ResNetv1-6 model architecture, focusing on its layer configuration and performance metrics. The architecture consists of a series of convolutional and pooling layers, with specific dimensions and parameters tailored to the model's requirements. The convolutional layers utilize a filter size of 3x3 and a stride of 1, supplemented by ReLU activation functions and max pooling layers designed to downsample the input. The pooling layers are predominantly one-dimensional, with an exception for the GTSRB dataset, which employs two-dimensional layers. The model's design aims for efficiency, as it utilizes standard max operation instructions, which require two cycles for execution. The results section is built upon this consistent model architecture, ensuring that the number of filters is uniform across layers while allowing for parameter adjustments. Overall, the paper emphasizes the structural components of the ResNet model and its operational efficiency in processing data.

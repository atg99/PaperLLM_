The paper discusses the MicroAI framework, focusing on its data acquisition, training, and model configuration processes for neural networks, particularly in the context of embedded systems. The training phase utilizes Keras or PyTorch, requiring a RawDataModel instance to manage training and testing datasets, which consist of numpy arrays for data and labels. A higher-level HARDataModel is provided for Human Activity Recognition, simplifying the process of handling subjects and activities. 

The preprocessing phase includes dataset normalization and supports various datasets such as UCI-HAR, SMNIST, and GTSRB, with the potential for extension to new datasets. MicroAI enables users to configure deep neural network architectures, providing options for a multi-layer perceptron (MLP), convolutional neural networks (CNN), and residual neural networks (ResNet). Each architecture is customizable in terms of layers, filters, and other parameters, with ReLU activation units used across models. Configuration files allow for the specification of model settings, optimizers, batch sizes, and training epochs, facilitating sequential training of multiple models.

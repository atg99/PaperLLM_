The paper presents a comparison of a proposed method against existing techniques on the augmented RIRE dataset, demonstrating superior performance across all evaluated metrics. The authors also explored the efficacy of deep neural networks (DNNs) as feature extractors by activating their last convolution layers, combining features from MRI and CT images, and inputting these into an artificial neural network (ANN) regression model. The results of this approach are detailed in Table 2, along with registration outcomes for selected image pairs illustrated in Figure 9.

For evaluating the classification performance of fixed and moving images, the study employs standard metrics such as accuracy, sensitivity, specificity, precision, f-measure, and g-mean, derived from confusion matrix values. Key equations defining these metrics are referenced, contributing to a comprehensive analysis of the proposed method's effectiveness in image modality identification. Additionally, histograms of errors for various regression models are presented in Figure 8, further supporting the findings.

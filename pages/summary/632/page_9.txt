The paper presents a comparison of a proposed image registration method for MRI and CT images against several state-of-the-art pretrained architectures including AlexNet, VGG, SqueezeNet, GoogLeNet, and ResNet-101. The authors adapted these networks to process 3D images. Performance metrics such as Dice Similarity Coefficient (DSC), Jaccard Similarity Coefficient (JSC), and others were evaluated, with results summarized in tables.

In Table 1, the proposed method achieved the highest scores across multiple metrics, notably a DSC of 0.9885 and SSIM of 0.9685, indicating superior registration performance compared to existing methods. Table 2 further demonstrates the effectiveness of the proposed method when existing deep neural networks (DNNs) are utilized as feature extractors coupled with a regression artificial neural network (ANN), where it again outperformed others with a DSC of 0.9910 and SSIM of 0.9700.

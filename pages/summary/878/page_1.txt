The paper introduces HyperNST, a novel neural style transfer (NST) technique that leverages hyper-networks and the StyleGAN2 architecture for artistic image stylization. The authors propose a method that utilizes a metric space pre-trained for style-based visual search (SBVS) to facilitate style transfer. This approach allows for the application and interpolation of styles from the SBVS system, marking a significant advancement in NST.

HyperNST features a hyper-network that predicts weight updates for a StyleGAN2 model, which has been pre-trained on a diverse collection of artistic portraits. This enables the tailoring of style parameterization on a per-region basis, guided by a semantic map of facial regions. The results demonstrate that HyperNST outperforms existing methods in preserving content while achieving effective style transfer.

The paper highlights the ongoing challenges in NST, particularly the need for efficient and diverse stylization methods. Current feed-forward NST techniques often lack the richness of styles, while optimization-based methods are time-consuming. HyperNST addresses these issues by providing a fast feed-forward solution driven by a metric embedding for style representation, enhancing both the speed and controllability of the creative process in NST.

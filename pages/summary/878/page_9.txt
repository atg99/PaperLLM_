The paper presents HyperNST, a novel approach utilizing hyper-networks for neural style transfer. The experimental results demonstrate HyperNST's superior performance compared to traditional methods and the closely related Swapping AutoEncoders (SAE). In Table 1, HyperNST achieves significantly lower LPIPS scores, indicating better content and style preservation, while also maintaining competitive performance in terms of speed and editing capabilities.

The ablation studies show the impact of varying style loss strength in Table 2, where lower loss strengths yield better quality metrics. Specifically, a style loss strength of 2.0 results in the best performance across all evaluated metrics. The comparative analysis of style transfer results between HyperNST and SAE reveals that while SAE provides slightly better style transfer fidelity, HyperNST excels in preserving the semantic content of portraits and is more effective in stylizing backgrounds.

Further investigations into the effects of different StyleGAN2 layers on stylization, as detailed in Table 3, highlight layer 13 as an optimal choice for fine-tuning, balancing quality and fidelity. Overall, HyperNST demonstrates promising capabilities for neural style transfer, offering a compelling alternative to existing methodologies.

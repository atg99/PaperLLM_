The paper presents HyperNST, a novel approach to Neural Style Transfer (NST) utilizing hyper-networks. The architecture integrates facial semantic segmentation to condition the stylization process through a method called ALADIN, alongside a patch co-occurrence discriminator to enhance the quality of stylized outputs. The system employs a StyleGAN2 model trained on AAHQ and FFHQ datasets to embed content images, enabling effective style transfer from a reference style image.

Training involves two distinct forward passes: the stylization pass, which combines content images with ALADIN style codes to generate stylized outputs, and the reconstruction pass, which reconstructs the content image using its corresponding style code. The reconstruction utilizes semantic mappings to maintain consistency in the transfer process. The stylization pass incorporates a discriminator that differentiates between generated stylized images and actual style images, providing a learning signal to improve the model's performance.

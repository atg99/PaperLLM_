The paper discusses the effects of fine-tuning different layers of the StyleGAN2 model for portrait style transfer. It highlights the trade-off between emphasizing style and maintaining structural integrity in generated images. Specifically, fine-tuning earlier layers enhances stylization but compromises the quality of structure reconstruction, while focusing on later layers preserves more of the original content's structure, resulting in color transfer rather than strong stylization. The authors provide visual and quantitative evidence to illustrate this phenomenon. Furthermore, the paper investigates the impact of incorporating facial semantic region information into the model and training process. The inclusion of semantic regions as a conditioning signal and for matching region types in the discriminator is shown to be beneficial, with results detailed in the corresponding tables.

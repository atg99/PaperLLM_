The paper presents HyperNST, a novel approach that utilizes hyper-networks for neural style transfer within the StyleGAN weight space. Previous works, such as Restyle and HyperStyle, have enhanced reconstruction quality and editing capabilities for images generated by StyleGAN, focusing primarily on photorealism. However, these approaches lack region-based control and style space parameterization, which HyperNST aims to address.

HyperNST is inspired by the StyleGAN-NADA method, which employs CLIP-based optimization for style transfer but suffers from long optimization times and lacks effective embedding for real portrait images. The methodology begins with domain adaptation models like CycleGAN, which excel in image translation but are limited to single domain transfers. HyperNST leverages hyper-networks to predict weight updates for a pre-trained StyleGAN2 model, streamlining the process by learning weight offsets rather than training from scratch.

The architecture of HyperNST consists of a hyper-network designed to generate weight deltas for a frozen StyleGAN2 model. This setup allows for a more efficient and effective stylization pipeline, as illustrated in the provided architecture diagram. Overall, HyperNST aims to enhance the editability and quality of style transfers while introducing new capabilities in controlling the style application across regions of an image.

In the paper, the authors present a comparison of various stylization techniques, highlighting the performance of HyperNST alongside other methods such as Artflow, NNST, Gatys et al., ContraAST, SANet, and PAMA. The visual results demonstrate the differences in stylization quality and artistic interpretation among these approaches. HyperNST is showcased as a competitive method, with the stylization examples illustrating its unique characteristics compared to the other techniques. This comparison emphasizes the advancements in neural style transfer and the ongoing efforts to improve visual aesthetics in artistic applications.

The visualizations presented demonstrate the varying effects of fine-tuning hyper-weight generators at different layers of the network, illustrating how the choice of layer influences the final stylized image.

Additionally, the paper compares the proposed HyperNST approach against Swapping Autoencoders, highlighting its advantages in terms of flexibility and output quality when using per-weight training methods. The downstream experiments further explore style interpolation, showcasing the ability to create smooth transitions between different styles through the ALADIN style space. This emphasizes the controllability of the stylization process, making HyperNST a powerful tool for generating diverse artistic renditions while maintaining coherence with the content image.

In this paper, the authors present HyperNST, a novel approach utilizing hyper-networks for neural style transfer. The experiments conducted demonstrate the effectiveness of HyperNST against various baseline conditions, as shown in Table 4, which highlights the impact of facial semantic information on performance metrics such as LPIPS and SIFID. The results indicate that incorporating both mask conditions leads to superior outcomes.

The paper also discusses the flexibility of HyperNST in handling non-portrait style images, illustrating this capability with a style interpolation example. Additionally, the model's semantic controllability is emphasized, particularly its ability to manipulate latent directions in the StyleGAN2 weight space using InterFaceGAN. This feature allows for enhanced artistic control over portrait attributes such as age, pose, and facial expression.

In conclusion, the authors argue that hypernetworks offer a promising avenue for advancing style transfer techniques, balancing both artistic style and semantic editing capabilities.

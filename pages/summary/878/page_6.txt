The loss functions, Lpcd and Ldisc, are defined to measure patch co-occurrence and discrimination, respectively, with the final loss L being a combination of reconstruction loss and the two additional losses. The hypernetwork model is crucial for inferring weight deltas in the StyleGAN2 generator, which is initially fine-tuned on the FFHQ dataset before being adapted for the AAHQ dataset. This dual-dataset approach ensures the model retains the ability to generate high-quality photographic features while also being capable of artistic stylization.

During the stylization process, the content encoder remains frozen to maintain the fidelity of real images. The fine-tuning focuses on the latter layers of the StyleGAN2 model, specifically layers 13 to 25, which allows for effective colorization and stylistic adjustments while preserving the overall facial structure. This structured approach to training ensures that the model can produce high-quality images that reflect both artistic styles and realistic features.

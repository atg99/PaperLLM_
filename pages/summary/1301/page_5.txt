The results indicate that models utilizing these augmentation strategies achieve higher accuracy compared to the baseline. Specifically, GridMask enhances the performance of various ResNet architectures, achieving accuracy improvements of up to 1.6% over the baseline.

In Table 2, the accuracy percentages for different ResNet models are presented, showing that ResNet50 with GridMask achieves 77.9%, while ResNet101 and ResNet152 reach 79.1% and 79.7% respectively. The findings suggest that GridMask effectively manages larger removal squares during augmentation, contrary to the original paper's report that larger squares yield poorer results.

Furthermore, the comparison with AutoAugment highlights that despite its complexity and reliance on extensive GPU resources, GridMask surpasses AutoAugment's performance with a simpler approach, demonstrating its effectiveness and general applicability in enhancing CNN models. The analysis of class activation mapping reveals that both augmentation methods guide the model to focus on critical areas of the images, suggesting that GridMask is a valuable tool for improving model accuracy with minimal complexity.

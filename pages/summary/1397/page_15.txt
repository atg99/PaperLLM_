The paper presents a comparison of various methods for semantic segmentation, particularly focusing on the performance of the Segmentation Transformer, referred to as OCR. The results are summarized in Table 5, which compares the OCR method against state-of-the-art techniques across different datasets, including Cityscapes, ADE20K, LIP, and Pascal Context. 

The OCR method demonstrates competitive performance, achieving an accuracy of 45.28% on ADE20K and 55.60% on LIP, outperforming many previous approaches, including those that utilize both multi-scale and relational contexts. For example, while ACFNet and ACNet show strong results, the OCR method using the ResNet-101 backbone achieves notable accuracy, particularly with the advanced backbone HRNetV2-W48, which further enhances performance. 

Overall, the findings highlight the effectiveness of the OCR approach in semantic segmentation tasks, establishing it as a strong contender within the current landscape of image segmentation methodologies.

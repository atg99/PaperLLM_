The OCR module utilizes a linear function to predict coarse segmentation, which is then refined through pixel-wise cross-entropy loss. The empirical analysis, conducted with a dilated ResNet-101 backbone on the Cityscapes validation dataset, highlights the importance of object region supervision and pixel-region relation estimation.

The study demonstrates that removing supervision on soft object regions significantly hampers performance, as shown in the results where the accuracy dropped from 79.58% to 77.31%. Additionally, the comparison with other mechanisms, namely the Double-Attention and ACFNet schemes, indicates that leveraging both pixel and region representations enhances the estimation of pixel-region relations, leading to superior performance. Overall, the findings underscore the critical role of object region supervision and effective pixel-region relation estimation in achieving high segmentation accuracy.

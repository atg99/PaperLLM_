The paper discusses a segmentation transformer architecture that rephrases the optical character recognition (OCR) pipeline using a Transformer encoder-decoder framework. It highlights the role of self-attention and cross-attention units within the encoder and decoder, respectively. The encoder's self-attention unit, which is optional, enhances image features, while the decoder's self-attention facilitates interaction between category queries. The paper details the computation of object region representations and the aggregation of these representations through cross-attention, emphasizing the similarity in attention weight computations across different equations presented. Furthermore, it connects category queries to class embeddings in Vision Transformers (ViT) and Class-Attention in Image Transformers (CaiT), noting that each class has a distinct embedding rather than a unified one. This approach aims to improve segmentation accuracy by leveraging attention mechanisms effectively.

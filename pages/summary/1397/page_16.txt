This paper presents results on panoptic segmentation using the COCO dataset, highlighting the effectiveness of the proposed OCR method when integrated with the Panoptic-FPN architecture. The experiments demonstrate improvements in performance across different backbone networks, specifically ResNet-50 and ResNet-101, with consistent increases in key metrics such as Average Precision (AP) and Panoptic Quality (PQ). 

For ResNet-50, the baseline Panoptic-FPN achieves a PQ of 48.3, which is improved to 48.6 with OCR, while for ResNet-101, the PQ increases from 49.7 to 50.2. The results indicate that OCR enhances performance, achieving notable gains in both the thresholded and segmented PQ metrics.

The paper also compares the proposed method against previous state-of-the-art approaches, noting that while the CNIF method achieves the highest performance of 56.93%, the OCR-enhanced methods still demonstrate competitive results. The HRNet-W48 combined with OCR achieves the best performance on the PASCAL-Context dataset, significantly outperforming other methods.

The experiments are conducted using the COCO dataset's full 2017 set, adhering to established training protocols and achieving higher performance than previously reported results. The paper underscores the generalization ability of the OCR scheme in challenging panoptic segmentation tasks, while qualitative results are provided in supplementary materials due to space constraints.

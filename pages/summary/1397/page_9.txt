The paper discusses the Segmentation Transformer, which integrates concepts from self-attention mechanisms and class attention, akin to the architecture in CaiT. The encoder and decoder architecture utilizes self-attention across class embeddings and image features, allowing simultaneous processing of cross-attention and self-attention, which can be viewed as a unified self-attention mechanism. The study emphasizes the importance of learning attention parameters during the ImageNet pre-training phase.

The paper also connects to OCNet, noting that it employs only encoder self-attention without incorporating decoder elements. OCNet enhances self-attention through an interlaced approach, combining local and global self-attention that can be simplified to self-attention over pooled features within local windows. An alternative method proposed involves using regularly-sampled or adaptively-pooled image features instead of relying solely on learned category queries.

In terms of architecture, the backbone of the Segmentation Transformer utilizes either dilated ResNet-101 or HRNet-W48, each configured with different output strides. The dilated ResNet-101 setup includes two representations that feed into the OCR module, highlighting the design's flexibility and potential for performance enhancement.

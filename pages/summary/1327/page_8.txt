Grischke J, et al. Dentronics: Towards robotics and artificial intelligence in dentistry. Dent Mater (2020), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dental.2020.03.021

The paper discusses the integration of robotics and artificial intelligence in dentistry, highlighting advancements such as robotic systems for dental arch determination and X-ray imaging, which have shown superior accuracy compared to traditional methods. The study emphasizes the potential for robotic assistance in various dental procedures, including instrument handling and treatment planning.

In the realm of machine learning and artificial intelligence, the authors conducted a systematic review, identifying 41 relevant studies. They noted that while machine learning can enhance diagnostic capabilities and treatment suggestions, it is not a replacement for human expertise. The current machine learning applications are limited to specific research questions and lack the generalizability found in human cognition.

The review details the use of artificial neural networks for diagnosing dental conditions, with growing literature since 2016 demonstrating their effectiveness in tasks such as caries detection and treatment planning. The authors also explored the implications of AI in various dental specialties, including orthodontics and esthetic dentistry, while acknowledging the need for further research to fully realize the potential of these technologies in clinical practice.

Overall, the paper underscores the promising role of robotics and AI in improving dental care, although it calls for more comprehensive studies to address existing limitations and expand their application in the field.

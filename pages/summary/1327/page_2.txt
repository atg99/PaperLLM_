The article titled "Dentronics: Towards robotics and artificial intelligence in dentistry" by Grischke et al. discusses the integration of robotics and artificial intelligence in the field of dentistry. It highlights the growing relevance of robotic technology in performing routine tasks, which can enhance the efficiency and quality of dental care while alleviating the workload of dental professionals. The authors argue that while robotics and machine learning may not replace dentists, they can complement their skills, leading to improved patient experiences and treatment outcomes.

The paper outlines the potential applications of robots in dental practices, including assisting with routine tasks, aiding in complex procedures like implant placements, and facilitating dental education through advanced simulations. The authors emphasize the importance of addressing challenges such as cost, patient acceptance, and the need for standardization in healthcare processes to implement these technologies effectively.

Furthermore, the authors discuss advancements in interactive force-sensitive robotics, mechatronics, and AI technologies that can support the development of "Dentronics." They advocate for the redesign of digital processes to reduce variations in practice and improve healthcare quality. Overall, the paper presents a forward-looking perspective on the transformative potential of robotics and AI in enhancing dental practices and education.

Grischke J, et al. Dentronics: Towards robotics and artificial intelligence in dentistry. Dent Mater (2020), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dental.2020.03.021. 

This article discusses advancements in robotics and artificial intelligence within the field of dentistry. It highlights the technological readiness levels of various systems, addressing the challenges of high costs and complexity. A significant development mentioned is the use of a six-axis robotic arm for assisting in orthognathic surgery, which aims to improve accuracy through real-time tracking of patient movements. The FDA-approved Yomi robotic system for dental implantology is showcased as a successful application, demonstrating its capability to assist in implant placement based on preoperative planning. The paper also emphasizes the importance of real-time tracking and the potential of robotic systems to enhance flexibility in prosthetic rehabilitation. Furthermore, it explores the role of robotics in dental education, suggesting that humanoid simulation systems may provide a more realistic training experience for dental students.

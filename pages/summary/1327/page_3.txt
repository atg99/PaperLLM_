Grischke J, et al. Dentronics: Towards robotics and artificial intelligence in dentistry. Dent Mater (2020), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dental.2020.03.021

This article discusses the advancements in robotics and artificial intelligence (AI) within the field of dentistry. It highlights the development of novel robot platforms for medical applications, including the DLR MIRO and da Vinci systems, which are utilized for minimally invasive surgeries. The paper emphasizes the importance of collaborative robots that enable safe, fenceless operation, citing examples like Universal Robot systems and Franka Emika Panda. It also addresses the critical role of control mechanisms, particularly impedance control, which allows for sensitive interactions between robots and their environment, mimicking human motor control. Furthermore, the article underscores the significance of safety in human-robot collaboration, noting recent research that has led to new design paradigms and safety standards, such as ISO 13482-3.

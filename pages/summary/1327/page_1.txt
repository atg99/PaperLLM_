Grischke J, et al. Dentronics: Towards robotics and artificial intelligence in dentistry. Dent Mater (2020), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dental.2020.03.021. 

In this article, the authors provide an overview of the applications and concepts of robotic systems and artificial intelligence in dentistry, termed "Dentronics." They review the advancements in robotics, AI, and machine learning that could lead to innovative assistive applications in the dental field. A systematic review of the literature reveals a limited number of robotic applications currently in practice, while AI and machine learning have been increasingly utilized for data analysis to support dental decision-making and treatment planning. The authors argue for the increased adoption of these technologies to enhance accuracy, efficiency, and treatment outcomes in dentistry.

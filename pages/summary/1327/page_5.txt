Grischke J, et al. Dentronics: Towards robotics and artificial intelligence in dentistry. Dent Mater (2020), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dental.2020.03.021

This article discusses the integration of robotics and artificial intelligence in dentistry, outlining various applications and their technological readiness levels. A systematic review of literature identified 137 research items on robotics in dentistry, leading to the extraction of data from 49 studies. The review highlights ten application domains, including maxillofacial surgery, robotic education, tooth preparation, and root canal treatment, among others. Each domain's current technology readiness level is assessed, revealing varying stages of development. The paper emphasizes the necessity of a knowledge base for autonomous robots, allowing them to perform tasks reliably and adapt to new situations. Overall, it provides a comprehensive overview of advancements in the field of Dentronics, underscoring the potential for robotics to enhance dental practices.

Grischke J, et al. Dentronics: Towards robotics and artificial intelligence in dentistry. Dent Mater (2020), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dental.2020.03.021

This paper discusses the integration of robotics and artificial intelligence in dentistry, focusing on human-robot interaction, safety measures, programming advancements, and the role of machine learning and AI in enhancing dental practices. It highlights the importance of designing safe human-robot collaboration environments, where safe motion control methods are crucial for preventing injuries during interactions. The paper also emphasizes various interaction modes, including physical, visual, and auditory communication, and the necessity of intuitive programming interfaces for non-professionals. Furthermore, it explores the significance of machine learning and AI in enabling robots to learn from experiences and autonomously plan tasks, ultimately aiming to improve efficiency and safety in dental procedures.

Grischke J, et al. Dentronics: Towards robotics and artificial intelligence in dentistry. Dent Mater (2020), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dental.2020.03.021 

This article discusses advancements in robotics and artificial intelligence within the field of dentistry. It highlights various robotic systems designed for educational purposes, such as the SIMROID, which simulates human reactions to aid dental students in learning. Other robotic tools, like the ROBOTUTOR, have been developed to demonstrate tooth-cleaning techniques, proving to be attractive for patient education, although less effective than human instruction.

The paper also covers the integration of virtual reality (VR) and haptic feedback in dental training, emphasizing their role in enhancing learning efficiency and skill acquisition. Robotic systems are examined for tasks such as tooth preparation, where mechatronic systems have shown improved accuracy in drilling compared to traditional methods.

Additionally, the article reviews robotic applications in testing toothbrush efficiency, root canal treatments, orthodontics, and material testing. The potential of robotics in supporting dental technicians in tasks like tooth arrangement for dentures is also explored, indicating a trend towards increased automation and precision in dental procedures.

Grischke J, et al. Dentronics: Towards robotics and artificial intelligence in dentistry. Dent Mater (2020), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dental.2020.03.021. 

The paper discusses the integration of robotics and artificial intelligence in dentistry, highlighting the current limitations in technological readiness and the need for collaboration between engineers and dental professionals. It emphasizes the potential benefits of robotic systems in enhancing reliability, reproducibility, and accuracy in dental applications, despite the limited number of operational prototypes available. The authors note that while machine learning (ML) has gained traction in dental research since 2016, its application remains limited in dental journals. The paper suggests that future advancements in ML and robotics could significantly improve diagnostics, treatment planning, and overall effectiveness in dentistry.

The authors also address the challenges of patient and practitioner acceptance of robotic systems, particularly in invasive procedures like implantology. They propose that assistive and educational robotics may pave the way for greater acceptance among future dentists, who are increasingly digital natives. The conclusion calls for increased research and development to overcome barriers such as high costs, basic sensory capabilities, and the need for intuitive user interfaces in robotic systems. Ultimately, the authors advocate for a proactive approach to familiarize future dental professionals with Dentronics, ensuring they are equipped to navigate the evolving landscape of robotic-assisted dentistry.

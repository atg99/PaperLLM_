Autoencoders, particularly in the context of feature extraction and fault diagnosis, are addressed through various methodologies, including sparse and marginalized denoising autoencoders, as noted in works by Chen and Ali. The application of variational autoencoders for tasks such as sentiment classification and image description further showcases the versatility of these models in handling complex data representations.

Overall, the collection of studies provides a comprehensive overview of the evolution of methods in optimization and deep learning, reflecting ongoing innovations and interdisciplinary research efforts in the field.

In summary, image classification is a critical area in computer vision and AI, focusing on categorizing images based on their attributes. The effectiveness of autoencoders (AEs), stacked autoencoders (SAEs), and convolutional autoencoders (CAEs) in feature extraction is highlighted, with various studies showcasing their applications on datasets like MNIST, Olivetti, and CIFAR-10. Traditional AEs, while useful, struggle with high-dimensional images due to their fully connected structure. To address this limitation, CAEs employ patch-wise training to retain local image relevance, achieving impressive results on benchmark datasets. Additionally, the integration of multiple feature types through feature fusion enhances classification accuracy, demonstrating the potential for improved performance in both natural and medical image classifications.

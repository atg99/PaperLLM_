The paper by Chen and Guo provides a comprehensive review of auto-encoders within the context of deep learning, emphasizing their significance in unsupervised learning and non-linear feature extraction. The authors begin by outlining the fundamental concept and structure of basic auto-encoders, followed by a detailed examination of various auto-encoder variants. They analyze these models from three distinct perspectives and explore their relationships with shallow models and other deep learning architectures.

The review highlights the successful applications of auto-encoders across multiple fields, including pattern recognition, computer vision, data generation, and recommender systems. Furthermore, the authors discuss available toolkits for implementing auto-encoders and address future trends and challenges in their design and training. This survey aims to serve as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners interested in utilizing and developing auto-encoder models in their work.

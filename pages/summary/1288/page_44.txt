The paper discusses the optimization of hyper-parameters in deep neural networks (DNNs), particularly focusing on autoencoders (AEs). It highlights the challenges associated with manually adjusting numerous hyper-parameters, which often relies on trial and error. The authors argue for the necessity of optimization algorithms, such as Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO), to automate the tuning of hyper-parameters and enhance model performance. The study provides a comprehensive overview of AEs, including theoretical foundations, various perspectives (energy, manifold, and information theoretic), and comparisons of deep learning toolkits. It concludes by analyzing future trends in AEs, aiming to serve as a reference for researchers in the design and application of AE models.

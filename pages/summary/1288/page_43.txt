The paper discusses various approaches to enhance the effectiveness of autoencoders (AEs) by integrating them with deep learning (DL) techniques. It emphasizes the potential of combining AEs with attention mechanisms and supervised learning to improve model performance. Attention mechanisms, inspired by the human visual system, can help AEs focus on the most informative features of datasets, as evidenced by some initial studies. Additionally, incorporating supervised learning can leverage label information to enhance the discriminability and overall performance of AEs.

The paper also highlights the importance of aligning AE structures with principles of neuroscience and cognitive science. By adopting biologically realistic algorithms, such as the Leabra model, and incorporating mechanisms that reflect neural connectivity and inhibition, future research could yield more robust AE models. Preliminary efforts in cognitive science, such as the stacked what and where AE, illustrate the potential benefits of this approach.

Overall, the paper calls for further exploration and integration of these advanced methodologies to enhance the capabilities and applications of autoencoders in various fields.

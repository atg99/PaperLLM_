The paper discusses the evaluation and selection of deep learning (DL) toolkits, emphasizing that no single criterion exists for determining the best toolkit. Each toolkit reflects the developers' needs and skills, and all are under continuous development. The paper highlights the importance of community activity in choosing toolkits, as those with high engagement are more likely to keep pace with the rapid evolution of DL. TensorFlow is identified as the most active toolkit on GitHub, benefiting from substantial developer support and resources from Google.

The discussion transitions to future trends in DL, particularly focusing on Autoencoders (AEs) as a model of unsupervised learning. AEs can efficiently process unlabeled data, making them valuable in various applications. The paper provides a comprehensive survey of AEs, exploring their basic theories, features, and relationships with both shallow and deep models. It notes that research on AEs is a current hotspot, but challenges remain in fully realizing their potential.

Two main directions for future research are proposed: 
1. Constructing hybrid models by integrating AEs with traditional shallow models and other advanced DL models like Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) to enhance overall performance.
2. Incorporating attention mechanisms into AEs, which can improve model efficiency by mimicking human visual attention processes.

Overall, while AEs hold significant promise, ongoing research is necessary to address existing challenges and unlock their full potential.

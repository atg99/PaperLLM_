The paper presents an audit of the ImageNet dataset, focusing on demographic characteristics, particularly age and gender, as well as the presence of misogynistic content. Key statistics include a total of 83,436 images featuring 101,070 to 132,201 persons, with mean ages of 33.24 for males and 25.58 for females. The audit identified 62 confirmed misogynistic images and noted that there are 30 classes containing infants.

Metrics used in the analysis include class-level mean count, mean gender skewness, and mean age, calculated using algorithms such as DEX, InsightFace, RetinaFace, and ArcFace. The paper also includes class-wise scatter plots illustrating the distribution of cardinality, age, and gender scores across different categories.

Additionally, cross-tabulated counts of hand-labeled images reveal varying degrees of NSFW content associated with specific clothing items, highlighting the dataset's potential biases. The analysis indicates a gender bias in the representation of certain professions and activities, with a skew toward male dominance in several categories. The findings underscore the importance of addressing demographic imbalances and biases in training datasets for machine learning applications.

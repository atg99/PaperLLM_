The paper discusses the ethical implications of Big Data practices, particularly in relation to the use of datasets from prestigious institutions like Google, Stanford, NYU, and MIT. It highlights the issues of 'ethics shopping' and 'bluewashing,' where organizations superficially engage with ethical concerns without genuine commitment. The authors argue for a more rigorous approach to informed consent in data usage, akin to standards in anthropological and medical research. They emphasize the need for the Machine Learning community to consider the societal impacts of their work, especially on vulnerable populations. The paper serves as a call to action for increased awareness and discussion around algorithmic bias, injustice, and ethical considerations in Machine Learning.

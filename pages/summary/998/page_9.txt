The paper presents a detailed audit of various meta datasets created to analyze biases in image datasets, specifically focusing on gender, age, and NSFW content. Table 2 lists curated CSV files, each providing unique insights derived from different models. For instance, df_insightface_stats.csv includes classwise statistical parameters from the InsightFace model applied to the ImageNet dataset, while df_audit_age_gender_dex.csv presents statistical parameters from the DEX paper. Additional datasets include NSFW scores (df_nsfw.csv), classification accuracy metrics from ResNet50 and NasNet models, and UMAP embeddings of class Glove vectors.

The analysis reveals a small percentage of individuals (8-10%) were identified based on gender and age, enabling a focused search for misogynistic and consent-violating imagery across specific categories. The results led to the identification of over fifty images categorized under beach voyeur photography and similar themes. The paper emphasizes the importance of understanding subtle forms of human co-occurrence-based gender bias, referencing literature on dog ownership bias and gender associations with musical instruments.

The authors have open-sourced these datasets and provided tutorial Jupyter notebooks to encourage community engagement and further research. The paper underscores the significance of these resources for studying biases in visual datasets, contributing to the broader discourse on ethical AI and image classification.

In Section 3, variational autoencoders (VAEs) are introduced, which incorporate probabilistic graphical models into the autoencoder framework. VAEs are notable for their ability to generate new data samples by learning the underlying distribution of the input data, making them particularly useful in generative modeling.

Section 4 explores various applications of autoencoders, including image denoising, anomaly detection, and data generation, showcasing their versatility in different domains.

The comparison between autoencoders and generative adversarial networks (GANs) is briefly discussed in Section 5.1, highlighting the strengths and weaknesses of each approach in terms of data generation and representation learning.

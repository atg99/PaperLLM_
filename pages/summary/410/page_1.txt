The paper presents DAMO-YOLO, an advanced real-time object detection method that surpasses the performance of existing YOLO models. Key innovations include Neural Architecture Search (NAS), an efficient Reparameterized Generalized-FPN (RepGFPN), a lightweight detection head utilizing AlignedOTA for label assignment, and a distillation enhancement strategy. The MAE-NAS method optimizes the detection backbone for low latency and high performance, resulting in structures akin to ResNet and CSP with spatial pyramid pooling and focus modules.

The architecture follows a design principle of a large neck and a small head, employing Generalized-FPN with accelerated queen-fusion for the neck and enhancing its CSPNet with efficient layer aggregation networks (ELAN) and reparameterization. The study reveals that a robust neck with a single task projection layer enhances detection performance. The introduction of AlignedOTA addresses label assignment misalignment, while the distillation schema elevates overall performance. 

DAMO-YOLO includes models at various scales for diverse applications, with DAMO-YOLO-T/S/M/L achieving mean average precisions (mAPs) of 43.6, 47.7, 50.2, and 51.9 respectively, with latencies ranging from 2.78 to 7.95 ms on T4 GPUs. Additionally, lightweight models DAMO-YOLO-Ns/Nm/Nl are designed for edge devices, achieving mAPs of 32.3, 38.2, and 40.5 with latencies of 4.08 to 6.69 ms on x86-CPU. The results indicate that DAMO-YOLO models outperform other YOLO series counterparts in their respective contexts. The code is accessible at the provided GitHub link.

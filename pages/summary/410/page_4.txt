The paper presents an overview of the various building blocks used in the MAE-NAS framework for the DAMO-YOLO models. It highlights three main types of blocks: MobBlock, ResBlock, and CSPBlock. MobBlock, adapted from MobileNetV3, serves as the foundational component for lightweight models in DAMO-YOLO. ResBlock, based on ResNet, forms the backbone for the DAMO-YOLO-S and DAMO-YOLO-T models. CSPBlock, derived from CSPNet, is employed in the more complex DAMO-YOLO-M and DAMO-YOLO-L models due to its strong performance in deep networks.

The architecture of DAMO-YOLO is structured into three main components. The MAE-NAS acts as the backbone for extracting multi-scale feature maps. The Efficient RepGFPN serves as the neck for refining and fusing high-level semantic information with low-level spatial details. Additionally, the ZeroHead component includes a task projection layer tailored for each loss, streamlining the overall model design. This combination of building blocks and architecture components aims to enhance the model's efficiency and effectiveness in various tasks.

The paper compares various state-of-the-art single-model detectors and lightweight detectors on the MSCOCO validation set, providing insights into their performance metrics including latency, GFLOPs, parameters, and average precision (AP) scores. 

In the first section, Table 9 presents a comparison of single-model detectors. Notable methods include YOLOX variants, YOLOv5, YOLOv6, YOLOv7, YOLOE, and DAMO-YOLO models. Each model varies in size and latency, with YOLOX-L achieving an AP of 49.7 and DAMO-YOLO-L* reaching an AP of 68.5. Table 10 shifts focus to lightweight detectors, highlighting methods such as Picodet and Nanodet, alongside DAMO-YOLO variants. The paper emphasizes the significance of both efficiency and accuracy in object detection tasks, advocating for the continued development of models that balance these aspects effectively.

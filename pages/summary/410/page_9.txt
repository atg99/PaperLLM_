In this paper, we introduce a novel object detection method named DAMO-YOLO, which demonstrates enhanced performance compared to existing methods within the YOLO series. The improvements are attributed to several innovative technologies, including the MAE-NAS backbone, an efficient RepGFPN neck, ZeroHead, AlignedOTA label assignment, and distillation enhancement. These advancements collectively contribute to the method's superior detection capabilities. The references cited provide a comprehensive background on related work and methodologies that influenced the development of DAMO-YOLO.

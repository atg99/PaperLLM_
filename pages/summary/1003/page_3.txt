The paper discusses the concept of systems, defining them as interconnected components working together to achieve specific goals efficiently. Various authors are cited to present definitions, emphasizing systems as structured entities that integrate functions and parts to produce desired outcomes. The methodology used in the research is literature review, which involves gathering data from various sources, analyzing it, and establishing theoretical foundations related to the research problem.

The paper also delves into the topic of data mining, describing it as a tool that allows users to quickly access large amounts of data. Data mining is defined as the process of extracting unknown but useful information from vast databases, facilitating important business decision-making. It employs statistical analysis and techniques to identify patterns within collected data. Furthermore, data mining is characterized as a semi-automatic process that utilizes statistical, mathematical, artificial intelligence, and machine learning techniques to uncover potential knowledge stored in large databases. It is part of the Knowledge Discovery in Databases (KDD) process, which includes stages such as data selection, preprocessing, transformation, data mining, and result evaluation.

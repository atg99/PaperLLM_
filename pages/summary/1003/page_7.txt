The paper discusses link analysis and deviation detection as key techniques in data mining. 

Link analysis aims to establish relationships between individual records or sets of records within a database. Its applications include product affinity analysis, direct marketing, and stock price movement.

Deviation detection identifies outliers that deviate from known expectations, which can lead to significant discoveries. This technique is visualized using statistical methods and finds applications in credit card fraud detection, insurance claim evaluation, quality control, and defect tracing.

The paper categorizes data mining techniques into two types:

1. **Classical Techniques**:
   - **Statistics**: Focuses on summarizing and describing data for informed decision-making.
   - **Nearest Neighbour**: An early predictive technique that estimates values based on the closest historical records without partitioning them.
   - **Clustering**: Classifies data into groups based on specific criteria, often used for providing an overview of data trends and segmentation.

2. **Next Generation Techniques**:
   - **Decision Trees**: A predictive model represented as a tree structure where each node corresponds to a question that classifies data. It retains all records during the data partitioning process, making it easy to understand compared to models like neural networks.

Overall, the paper highlights the significance of these techniques in enhancing data analysis and decision-making processes.

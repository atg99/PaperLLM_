The paper presents a compilation of various research studies related to technology and systems in agriculture and smart home applications. Key contributions include:

1. Surahman et al. discuss an automated chicken feed system utilizing the Internet of Things, enhancing poultry management efficiency.
2. Susanto explores a spatial decision support system for controlling Avian Influenza H5N1 in non-commercial poultry farming in Lampung, emphasizing the need for intelligent decision-making in disease management.
3. Wantoro et al. analyze a monitoring system for maintenance and repairs of facilities at PT PLN in Metro Lampung, showcasing practical applications of technology in infrastructure management.
4. Widodo et al. present a water circulation system for biofloc aquaculture using Arduino, highlighting innovations in fish farming techniques.
5. Yuliana et al. develop a web-based expert system for diagnosing respiratory diseases using the Naive Bayes Classifier, illustrating advancements in health technology.
6. Yulianti et al. design a pig repellent system using Arduino and motion sensors, addressing agricultural pest control challenges.
7. Yurnama and Azman focus on the design of pervasive smart home applications, contributing to the growing field of home automation.
8. Zanofa et al. introduce an automatic gate system based on Arduino UNO R3, demonstrating the integration of technology in security solutions.

Overall, these studies collectively emphasize the application of technology in enhancing agricultural practices, health management, and smart home solutions, reflecting a trend towards automation and intelligent systems in various sectors.

This document presents a collection of research articles focused on the design and implementation of automated systems using microcontrollers, primarily Arduino and Raspberry Pi, across various applications. 

Key articles include Kholidi et al. (2015), which discusses an automated feeding and temperature regulation system for broiler chickens using a Programmable Logic Controller. Kristiawan et al. (2021) explores an automated feeding and drinking system for poultry controlled via SMS. Kurniawan et al. (2019) presents a smart monitoring system for server room temperature and humidity using Raspberry Pi and WhatsApp notifications.

Other notable studies include Kurniawan and Surahman (2021), who developed a railway crossing security system using infrared sensors, and Nurdiansyah et al. (2020), who designed a solar panel rotation control system. Pindrayana et al. (2018) created a parking guide prototype with human voice output, while Pratama et al. (2021) focused on designing control systems for household electronic devices.

Additionally, Puspaningrum et al. (2020) designed a gas leak detection device for Android mobile platforms, and Rahmanto et al. (2021) developed an automatic control system for aquaponics using Arduino. The collection highlights the versatility of microcontroller applications in enhancing automation and monitoring across various fields, including agriculture, environmental management, and home automation.

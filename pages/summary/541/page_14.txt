The paper presents a comparison of classification error rates across various methods applied to CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and ImageNet datasets, highlighting the performance of different evolutionary neural architecture search (ENAS) techniques. It includes a comprehensive table detailing the GPU days, parameters, and error rates for multiple methods from 2017 to 2020. Notable methods such as CGP-DCNN, EPT, and GeneCai (ResNet-50) are discussed, with their respective classification performance indicated in percentages.

The paper emphasizes the effectiveness of evolutionary search strategies over random search, with references to studies that support the superiority of ENAS in discovering optimal architectures. It also discusses the importance of understanding which components of ENAS contribute to its success, particularly in large encoding spaces, and mentions the limited use of crossover operators in some algorithms due to concerns about their suitability for neural networks. The discussion extends to the challenges and considerations involved in combining individual architectures, suggesting that further exploration is needed to optimize ENAS methodologies.

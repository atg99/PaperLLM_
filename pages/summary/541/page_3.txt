Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) are designed for sequence prediction tasks and can remember information from previous inputs. The optimization of RNN architectures involves determining the number of layers and the size of the hidden state.

In summary, the paper outlines the flowchart of an Evolutionary Neural Architecture Search (ENAS) algorithm, highlighting the process from population initialization through fitness evaluation to population updating and stopping criteria. It categorizes evolutionary computation (EC) methods based on their search strategies, focusing on evolutionary algorithms (EAs), swarm intelligence, and other methods. The paper also discusses common neural network architectures employed in ENAS, including Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), Deep Belief Networks (DBNs), Stacked Auto-Encoders (SAEs), and Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs), emphasizing the optimization aspects relevant to each architecture.

In summary, the paper discusses the challenges faced by connected and automated vehicles (CAVs) such as data distribution issues, visual occlusion, and high verification costs for deep neural networks (DNNs). Proposed solutions include lifelong multi-stage training, cooperative perception with road sensors, and the necessity for simulation to reduce verification costs. The paper further highlights a paradigm shift from centralized to federated learning (FL) to address privacy concerns and improve training efficiency. It also introduces federated distillation (FD), which integrates cooperative perception with FL. Lastly, the paper identifies limitations in existing research and outlines opportunities for innovation in network resource allocation, sensor integration, data privacy, and real-time processing to enhance CAV systems.

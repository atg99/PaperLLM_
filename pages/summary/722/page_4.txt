This paper presents two key algorithms: the dπ-sampler and the Qπ-estimator, which are integral to the Policy Cover Guided Policy Gradient (PC-PG) method. 

The dπ-sampler is designed to sample state-action pairs from a specified distribution ν, execute a given policy π, and return the final state-action pair after a series of transitions. The Qπ-estimator computes the expected return for a given state-action pair by executing policy π and summing the rewards obtained during the episode.

The PC-PG algorithm iteratively enhances both the current policy and the coverage distribution, denoted as ρcov. It begins with a uniform policy and, through a series of episodes, estimates the covariance of the policy's action distribution. A reward bonus is introduced for infrequently visited state-action pairs, promoting exploration of novel areas in the state space.

The method relies on the coverage provided by an initial distribution that spans the state space, which is crucial for effective policy optimization. The algorithm modifies the reward function to include the exploration bonus and constrains the policy class to focus on rewarding actions when the bonus is high. This encourages the algorithm to explore beyond the current coverage area.

Overall, PC-PG aims to maximize the expected return over the state-action space while ensuring a broad exploration of the environment, thereby enhancing the learning process in reinforcement learning settings. The construction of the reward bonus is guided by the covariance matrix of the policy cover, which is essential for identifying under-explored regions.

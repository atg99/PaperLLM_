The referenced works explore various advancements in reinforcement learning and related methodologies. Key contributions include:

1. **Weighted Sums of Random Kitchen Sinks** by Rahimi and Recht, which discusses replacing minimization techniques with randomization for improved learning efficiency.
2. **Worst-Case Regret Bounds** by Russo, emphasizing exploration strategies through randomized value functions.
3. **Approximation Benefits of Policy Gradient Methods** by Russo, which examines the advantages of aggregated states in policy gradient approaches.
4. **Comparative Analysis of Approximate Policy Iteration Schemes** by Scherrer, focusing on different strategies in policy iteration.
5. **Trust Region Policy Optimization** by Schulman et al., which introduces a method to enhance stability and performance in policy optimization.
6. **Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms** by Schulman et al., detailing a family of algorithms aimed at improving sample efficiency and robustness in policy updates.
7. **Adaptive Trust Region Policy Optimization** by Shani et al., which presents global convergence and rapid convergence rates for regularized Markov decision processes.
8. **PAC Model-Free Reinforcement Learning** by Strehl et al., addressing theoretical guarantees for learning without a model.
9. **Model-Based Reinforcement Learning** by Sun et al., highlighting PAC bounds and improvements over model-free methods in contextual decision processes.
10. **Finite Time Bounds for Sampling-Based Fitted Value Iteration** by Szepesvári and Munos, providing insights into the efficiency of value iteration methods.

These works collectively enhance understanding of policy optimization, exploration strategies, and theoretical frameworks in reinforcement learning.

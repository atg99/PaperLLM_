The references listed comprise a collection of significant works in the field of reinforcement learning and policy optimization. They explore various aspects such as efficiency, exploration strategies, and theoretical foundations for algorithms. 

Key contributions include Bhandari and Russo's work on global optimality guarantees for policy gradient methods, and Brafman and Tennenholtz's introduction of the R-max algorithm for near-optimal reinforcement learning. Brockman et al. present the OpenAI Gym, a toolkit for developing and comparing reinforcement learning algorithms, while Burda et al. propose exploration through random network distillation.

Cai et al. discuss efficient exploration in policy optimization, and Chen and Jiang address information-theoretic considerations in batch reinforcement learning. Dani et al. and Dann et al. provide insights into stochastic linear optimization and sample complexity in episodic reinforcement learning, respectively. 

Further, Dong et al. explore efficient reinforcement learning with aggregated states, and Du et al. investigate Q-learning with function approximation. Efroni et al. examine optimistic policy optimization with bandit feedback. The works of Geist et al. and Henaff contribute to the theory of regularized Markov decision processes and explicit explore-exploit algorithms, respectively.

Overall, these references encapsulate a rich landscape of research that addresses the complexities and challenges in reinforcement learning, providing foundational knowledge and advanced methodologies for practitioners and researchers in the field.

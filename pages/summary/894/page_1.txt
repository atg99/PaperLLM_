The paper presents NeRF-Art, a novel approach for text-driven stylization of Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF). NeRF, a powerful method for rendering 3D scenes, faces challenges in simultaneously altering both appearance and geometry based on text prompts. NeRF-Art addresses these challenges by modifying a pre-trained NeRF model without relying on mesh guidance, allowing for comprehensive style transformations.

The authors introduce a global-local contrastive learning strategy that enables control over the trajectory and intensity of the desired style. Additionally, a weight regularization method is implemented to minimize artifacts and noise during the transformation of the density field. The effectiveness and robustness of NeRF-Art are demonstrated through extensive experiments, showcasing improved single-view stylization quality and cross-view consistency.

This approach reflects a significant advancement in making artistic content generation more accessible, leveraging deep learning techniques to allow users to create stylistically diverse 3D representations from simple text descriptions. The paper emphasizes the potential of NeRF-Art in transforming artistic expression in the context of evolving technology.

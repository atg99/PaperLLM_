In summary, NeRF-Art demonstrates significant advancements in text-driven 3D stylization by employing a global-local contrastive learning strategy and weight regularization, which collectively ensure uniformity and sufficient strength in stylization while mitigating artifacts and geometry noise. The method outperforms existing techniques such as StyleGAN-NADA, CLIP-NeRF, DreamField, and Text2Mesh by maintaining visual consistency across multiple views and adapting effectively to diverse prompts. The experiments highlight the advantages of NeRF-Art in producing detailed and coherent stylized outputs in comparison to other state-of-the-art methods.

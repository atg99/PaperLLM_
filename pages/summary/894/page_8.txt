The paper evaluates the performance of different text-guided mesh-based stylization methods, specifically comparing the proposed approach with existing models such as Text2Mesh and AvatarCLIP. Figures illustrate the comparisons using various artistic references, including iconic figures and styles like Vincent van Gogh and Edvard Munch. The authors conduct a generalization evaluation on two models, VolSDF and NeuS, to assess how well their method adapts to different styles. The experiments highlight the ability of the proposed method to represent complex artistic styles effectively, demonstrating its potential in generating high-quality stylized outputs.

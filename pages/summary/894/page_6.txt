The paper discusses a novel approach to image stylization that leverages text descriptions to guide the artistic transformation of images. The authors compare different levels of detail in text prompts, showcasing how varying descriptions influence the stylization process. They utilize the Adam optimizer during the training stages, with specific hyper-parameters set for the model. A dataset of approximately 200 text descriptions, sourced from Pinterest, covers various artistic styles.

For data collection, the authors gathered self-portrait video clips from three users, extracting 100 frames from each clip for analysis. These frames were processed to estimate camera poses and were resized for stylization. The study also incorporates the Local Light Field Fusion dataset for stylizing non-face scenes, emphasizing the diverse applications of the method.

Furthermore, the authors evaluate the sensitivity of their model to text prompts using CLIP, highlighting the importance of specificity in text descriptions for effective stylization. Overall, the paper presents a comprehensive methodology for integrating text-guided image transformation with a focus on user-generated content and artistic diversity.

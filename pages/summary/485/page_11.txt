The paper presents a comparative analysis of different optimization algorithms applied to support vector machines (SVM) for classification tasks associated with electronic filters. Specifically, it evaluates the performance of genetic algorithm (GA), particle swarm optimization (PSO), and grey wolf optimization (GWO) in tuning the parameters of SVM for a Sallen-Key bandpass filter and a four-opamp biquad highpass filter.

Key findings include the classification results for PSO-SVM, which showed strong performance in both cases studied. The GWO-SVM achieved the highest accuracy of 100% for the Sallen-Key bandpass filter, with significantly lower computation time compared to the other models. For the four-opamp biquad highpass filter, GWO-SVM also led in accuracy at 99.68%. The results indicate that GWO-SVM consistently outperformed GA-SVM and PSO-SVM in terms of accuracy and efficiency across both filter types.

The paper emphasizes the effectiveness of GWO as an optimization strategy for enhancing SVM classification performance in electronic filter applications.

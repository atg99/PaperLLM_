The paper presents a comprehensive analysis of various methods for fault diagnosis in analog circuits, emphasizing the effectiveness of a proposed approach that utilizes neural networks in conjunction with preprocessing techniques such as wavelet transforms. The findings indicate that the proposed method achieves superior fault diagnosis accuracy, with results showing 100% accuracy in Case 1 and 99.68% in Case 2, outperforming previously established methods. The study consolidates previous research and experimental results, highlighting the advancements in diagnostic techniques within the field of electronics. All data and models related to the study are included in the submitted article, ensuring transparency and accessibility for further research.

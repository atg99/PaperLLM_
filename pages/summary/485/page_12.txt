The paper presents the results of experiments using genetic algorithms (GA) and particle swarm optimization (PSO) in conjunction with support vector machines (SVM) to evaluate their effectiveness in different cases. Figures 12 and 13 illustrate the fitness curves for the GA-SVM and PSO-SVM approaches, respectively, showing the performance metrics for Case 1 and Case 2. The figures reveal how each optimization method affects the fitness of the SVM model, providing insights into the comparative advantages of GA and PSO in optimizing SVM parameters for improved predictive accuracy. The findings contribute to the understanding of optimization techniques in machine learning applications.

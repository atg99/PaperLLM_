This paper introduces a fault diagnosis method utilizing Deep Belief Networks (DBN) for feature extraction and Grey Wolf Optimized Support Vector Machine (GWO-SVM) for classification. Unlike traditional methods, DBN enables unsupervised deep feature learning, creating a hierarchical model that enhances the diagnostic process. The GWO optimization significantly boosts the accuracy of SVM classification while reducing computation time, achieving over 75% time savings and hundreds of fewer iterations. Although this method demonstrates improved diagnostic accuracy compared to conventional approaches, it faces challenges with large data sets, which could impair performance. The authors suggest that for complex circuits, dividing them into smaller modules could facilitate more effective fault diagnosis. Future research will aim to apply this diagnosis method to more intricate circuits and practical applications.

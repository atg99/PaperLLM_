The paper discusses a novel fault diagnosis method for analog circuits that combines a feature extraction technique based on Dynamic Bayesian Networks (DBN) with a Grey Wolf Optimizer-Support Vector Machine (GWO-SVM) classifier. The proposed method demonstrates significant improvements in both classification performance and efficiency compared to traditional fault diagnosis methods. Specifically, it achieves an increase in diagnostic accuracy and reduces fault diagnosis time by 89.98% and 74.67% for two cases analyzed. The results indicate that the method not only extracts deep and intrinsic data features but also optimizes classifier performance. The best parameters for the GWO-SVM classifier were identified, yielding 100% accuracy for Case 1 and 99.68% for Case 2. Overall, the study highlights the effectiveness of the proposed approach in enhancing fault diagnosis in analog circuits.

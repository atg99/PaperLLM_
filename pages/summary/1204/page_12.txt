The referenced works in this list explore various approaches to self-supervised and semi-supervised learning in the context of visual representation and feature learning. Key contributions include methodologies for learning from unlabeled data, such as solving jigsaw puzzles, utilizing temporal order verification, and employing contrastive predictive coding. 

Mahajan et al. investigate weakly supervised pretraining, while Misra et al. focus on pretext-invariant representations. Other notable methods include the use of context encoders for feature learning and techniques for enhancing model consistency and confidence in semi-supervised settings, as demonstrated by Sohn et al. 

Furthermore, approaches like Faster R-CNN and large batch training strategies are discussed, which contribute to advancements in object detection and efficient training of convolutional networks. Overall, these studies collectively aim to improve the generalization and effectiveness of visual representation learning through innovative unsupervised and semi-supervised techniques.

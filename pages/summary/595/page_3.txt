The paper discusses advancements in medical imaging informatics through deep learning-based domain adaptation (DA). It highlights the challenges posed by dataset variations that lead to domain shifts, affecting model generalization. While diversifying training datasets is a common approach, recent studies indicate that it does not always enhance generalization. DA methods aim to minimize these variations while preserving the essential features required for classification, demonstrating strong performance in image segmentation across multiple modalities.

The paper is organized into several sections: the methodology for selecting relevant studies, an overview of deep learning frameworks in the DA literature, and a summary of current challenges and future opportunities in the field. The survey examines publications from 2017 to 2020, focusing on reputable journals and conferences, including IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging and MICCAI. Key search terms included "Domain Adaptation," "Transfer Learning," and "Cross Modality." Findings reveal that radiology and pathology are the most common application areas, with cross-modality segmentation being particularly prevalent in radiology.

Deep learning-based DA utilizes various representation learning strategies to align domain distributions and address different scenarios in both cross-modality and single-modality adaptations. The paper emphasizes the importance of these methodologies in improving the robustness and effectiveness of medical imaging applications.

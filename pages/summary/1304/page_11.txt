The cited works encompass a range of studies focused on improving the robustness of machine learning models against adversarial attacks, enhancing training efficiency, and exploring novel optimization techniques. 

Key contributions include:

1. **Adversarial Training Limitations**: Gowal et al. investigate the constraints of adversarial training when faced with norm-bounded adversarial examples, emphasizing the challenges in achieving robustness (2020).

2. **Efficient Training Techniques**: Goyal et al. present methods for training large models like ImageNet rapidly using large minibatch stochastic gradient descent (2017).

3. **Ensemble Methods**: Grefenstette et al. discuss how adversarially-trained ensembles can balance robustness and computational efficiency (2018). Similarly, Pang et al. highlight the importance of ensemble diversity in improving adversarial robustness (2019).

4. **Model Architecture Innovations**: He et al. introduce deep residual learning, a significant advancement for image recognition tasks (2016). Additionally, Huang et al. propose self-adaptive training methods that exceed traditional empirical risk minimization (2020).

5. **Optimization Strategies**: Kingma and Ba's Adam optimizer has become a standard for stochastic optimization (2014). Nesterov's method offers rapid convergence for convex programming (1983).

6. **Adversarial Example Insights**: Kurakin et al. and Madry et al. provide foundational insights into adversarial examples, focusing on their implications in real-world scenarios and developing more resilient deep learning models (2016, 2018).

7. **Robustness Enhancements**: Hendrycks et al. demonstrate that pre-training can enhance model robustness and uncertainty (2019), while Najaﬁ et al. address robustness in scenarios with incomplete data (2019).

These studies collectively contribute to advancing the understanding and methodologies for creating robust machine learning systems capable of withstanding adversarial perturbations.

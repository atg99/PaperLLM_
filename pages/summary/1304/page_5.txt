The paper investigates the effectiveness of various data augmentation techniques in enhancing robust accuracy during adversarial training, particularly in the context of the WRN-28-10 model on the CIFAR-10 dataset. It contrasts the impact of these augmentations with and without model weight averaging (WA). The findings reveal that data augmentation methods, such as Cutout and CutMix, yield higher robust accuracy compared to MixUp, with CutMix demonstrating resilience against robust overfitting. The authors argue that WA significantly benefits CutMix, as evidenced by a consistent increase in robust accuracy throughout training, surpassing the performance of other augmentation methods.

The experimental setup employs WRNs as the backbone network, consistent with prior studies, and utilizes TRADES for optimization. The training regimen includes specific configurations regarding learning rates, batch sizes, and the decay rate for WA. The results indicate that the integration of data augmentation techniques can effectively improve adversarial robustness, challenging previous conclusions in the literature. The paper also proposes further evaluations against stronger adversarial attacks, suggesting a comprehensive approach to understanding the efficacy of these methods.

This paper investigates the effectiveness of combining data augmentation techniques with model weight averaging to enhance the robustness of adversarial models, specifically using a Wide Residual Network (WRN-28-10) on the CIFAR-10 dataset. The results indicate that while the overall robust accuracy remains stable across different snapshots of the model, there is significant variability in individual predictions. This variability suggests that augmentations like CutMix help prevent robust overfitting, leading to diverse yet equally performing model iterations.

The study includes an ablation analysis that explores the impact of varying the decay rate of model weight averaging on robust test accuracy. Findings reveal that while CutMix consistently benefits from increased weight averaging, the Pad & Crop method reaches optimal performance at a specific decay rate. Beyond this point, incorporating too many underperforming model iterations negatively impacts robustness.

The paper concludes by emphasizing that the integration of data augmentation and model weight averaging can substantially improve model robustness, contrary to previous research that struggled with data augmentation alone. The authors provide insights into the mechanisms behind this improvement and make their code and models publicly available for further research.

The paper "Hard-aware deeply cascaded embedding" by Yuan, Yang, and Zhang, presented at ICCV 2017, introduces a novel approach to deep learning embeddings that focuses on hard negative mining. The authors propose a cascaded architecture that effectively incorporates hard samples during the training process, which enhances the model's ability to learn discriminative features. The methodology emphasizes the importance of hard negatives in improving the performance of embedding tasks, particularly in scenarios with imbalanced data. The results demonstrate significant improvements over existing methods, showcasing the effectiveness of the proposed hard-aware strategy in various applications.

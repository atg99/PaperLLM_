The paper presents a comprehensive survey of recent trends in deep learning, highlighting various applications and advancements in the field. It acknowledges Professor Aboul Ella Hassanien for his contributions and states that no external funding was received. The authors declare no conflicts of interest.

Key references include works on generative adversarial networks (GANs), deep convolutional networks for object detection, and innovative techniques in data augmentation. The survey covers diverse applications of deep learning, such as medical image analysis, crowd counting, and style transfer. It emphasizes the significance of data-driven approaches and the evolution of methodologies that enhance performance in various domains.

Overall, the paper serves as a resource for understanding the current landscape of deep learning technologies and their practical implications across different sectors.

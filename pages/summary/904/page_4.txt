The paper discusses various geometric image data augmentation techniques, specifically focusing on flipping and rotation. 

Flipping techniques are categorized into three types: 

1. **Vertical Flipping**: The image is inverted along the vertical axis, resulting in the coordinates of each pixel being adjusted accordingly.
2. **Horizontal Flipping**: The image is mirrored along the horizontal axis, with pixel coordinates reflecting this transformation.
3. **Vertical and Horizontal Flipping**: This method combines both vertical and horizontal flips, preserving the orientation of the image while adjusting pixel coordinates.

The paper also explores **Rotation**, a classical augmentation technique where an image is rotated around an axis by angles ranging from 1 to 359 degrees. It illustrates that rotating an image at specific angles, such as 30 degrees, can generate multiple images (11 in total) at different angles. The transformation of pixel positions during rotation is mathematically expressed through equations.

Overall, the techniques outlined are essential for enhancing image datasets, particularly in applications such as machine learning and computer vision.

This paper provides a comprehensive survey of recent trends in image data augmentation, emphasizing its critical role in addressing limited datasets, particularly in deep learning applications. It outlines a structured approach to various augmentation techniques aimed at mitigating overfitting in deep learning models. The survey is divided into three key sections: classical image data augmentation, deep learning data augmentation, and state-of-the-art research across different domains.

Classical image data augmentation encompasses geometric transformations such as flipping, rotation, shearing, cropping, and translation, along with photometric transformations involving color space adjustments, image filters, and noise addition. In contrast, deep learning data augmentation is categorized into three main types: augmentation using Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), Neural Style Transfer, and meta metric learning techniques including Neural Augmentation, Auto Augment, and Smart Augmentation.

The paper highlights the application of these techniques across various fields, including medical and agricultural domains, as well as miscellaneous areas like human identification, art, and space technology. The future of data augmentation appears promising, with potential advancements in search algorithms, data warping, and oversampling approaches. Future research directions include establishing a taxonomy of augmentation techniques, setting new quality standards for GAN outputs, exploring the relationships between data augmentation methods, and further generalizing augmentation concepts.

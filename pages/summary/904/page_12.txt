The paper discusses deep learning data augmentation techniques, focusing on generative adversarial networks (GANs) as a prominent method. GANs consist of two competing neural networks: a generator, which creates synthetic images, and a discriminator, which evaluates whether the images are real or generated. This two-player zero-sum game allows GANs to learn representations from unlabeled data through competitive learning mechanisms. GANs have gained acceptance in both academic and business sectors due to their effectiveness in generating new images and their applications in various fields, including image synthesis, style conversion, semantic editing, super-resolution, and classification. The paper highlights the structural role of GANs in enhancing image data through their innovative generative modeling approach.

The paper discusses recent trends in deep learning, particularly focusing on image data augmentation techniques. It emphasizes that the effectiveness of classical image data augmentation methods varies based on the characteristics of the original dataset and suggests combining geometric transformations with photometric transformations for improved testing accuracy. One specific technique highlighted is random erasing, introduced by Zhong et al. in 2017, which involves randomly erasing a square region in an image and has shown effectiveness in enhancing model performance.

Furthermore, the paper outlines the significant advancements in deep learning over the past decade, particularly in fields such as computer vision and image classification. It categorizes deep learning image data augmentation into three main types: Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), Neural Style Transfer, and meta metric learning. Each category contributes uniquely to the augmentation process, showcasing the versatility and potential of deep learning in enhancing image data.

The section on meta metric learning discusses the innovative approach of using neural networks to optimize other neural networks, initially introduced by Zoph and Le in 2019. Their model leverages a recurrent network to enhance accuracy through meta-learning architectures. The paper explores three specific models within this domain: neural augmentation, auto augment, and smart augmentation, which utilize various techniques such as photometric and geometric transformations, neural style transfer (NST), and generative adversarial networks (GANs).

Neural augmentation, proposed by Wang and Perez, focuses on training a neural network using an optimal data augmentation strategy to achieve maximum accuracy. This involves manipulating data before classifier training and using GANs alongside simple transformations to expand the dataset. Additionally, a neural network is employed to combine two random images into a single one aligned with a pre-trained image, allowing for effective gradient transfer during convolutional network training. Their results indicated significant improvement in accuracy.

Auto augment, introduced by Cubuk et al., is a software package designed to automatically identify suitable augmentation policies for specific images. The authors created a search space to facilitate this process, enhancing the efficiency of data augmentation in training models.

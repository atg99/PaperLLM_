The paper discusses various artistic styles utilized in neural style transfer (NST), highlighting notable works such as Vincent Van Gogh's "Starry Night," Pablo Picasso's "The Muse," Wassily Kandinsky's "Composition VII," and Hokusai's "The Great Wave." It emphasizes the challenges in selecting appropriate styles for different applications, particularly in complex scenarios like self-driving vehicles where data variations across different conditions must be considered. In contrast, other applications may lack clear conventional styles for transfer. The choice of NST is influenced by the original dataset's characteristics, and effective image data augmentation is crucial. The paper warns that a limited sample range can skew results, underscoring the importance of careful image selection in the augmentation process.

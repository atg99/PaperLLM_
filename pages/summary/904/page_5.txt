The section discusses shearing, a technique in image processing that alters the shape of objects within an image by changing their positions along the x and y directions. Shearing is categorized into two types: one affecting the x-axis and the other affecting the y-axis. Equations are provided to illustrate how the new pixel positions (fx and fy) are determined based on the original coordinates (x and y) of the image.

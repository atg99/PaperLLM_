The paper presents a survey on recent trends in deep learning, particularly focusing on its applications in agriculture through image data augmentation techniques. It highlights selected research works that utilized these techniques to enhance model performance in various agricultural contexts.

The table included summarizes key studies from different years, detailing their applications and the specific augmentation methods employed. Notable examples include:

1. Bargoti and Underwood (2017) who developed a regional convolutional network for detecting and counting fruits in orchards using techniques like reflection, scaling, and color space shifting.
2. Mehdipour Ghazi et al. (2017) applied deep transfer learning for plant classification across 1000 classes, employing rotation, translation, reflection, and scaling.
3. Khalifa et al. (2020) focused on insect pest classification using transfer learning with reflection as an augmentation method.
4. Giuffrida et al. (2017) introduced a GAN-based image generator for Arabidopsis rosette images.
5. Zhang et al. (2019) utilized convolutional neural networks for classifying fruit across 18 categories, applying rotation and noise addition.

This survey underscores the significance of image data augmentation in enhancing deep learning models' effectiveness in agricultural applications.

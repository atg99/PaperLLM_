This paper provides a comprehensive survey of recent trends in deep learning, specifically focusing on robust and accurate models and the application of image augmentation techniques. The survey is structured into several sections: Section 2 reviews classical image data augmentation techniques, Section 3 discusses deep learning-based image augmentation methods, Section 4 highlights the current state of research utilizing these techniques, and Section 5 offers a summary of the findings.

Classical image data augmentation, also referred to as basic data augmentation, encompasses geometric transformations and photometric shifting. Geometric transformations include techniques such as flipping, rotation, shearing, cropping, and translation. Photometric shifting involves color manipulation techniques, including color space adjustments, application of filters, and noise addition. The paper illustrates these classical techniques, emphasizing the role of flipping, which enhances dataset size by reflecting images across their axes.

Overall, the survey aims to synthesize existing knowledge and identify effective image augmentation strategies that enhance deep learning model performance.

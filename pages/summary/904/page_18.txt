The paper discusses the significant impact of image data augmentation in the field of computer science, particularly highlighting its growth over the past six years. According to data from the Scopus database, the National Natural Science Foundation of China has been a major contributor, sponsoring over 430 research papers in this area. Other notable institutions supporting this research include the National Science Foundation, the Ministry of Science and Technology of China, and Nvidia.

In the medical domain, image data augmentation has proven crucial due to the challenges of obtaining large medical datasets. These techniques enable the generation of medical images for diagnostic purposes at a lower cost while achieving high testing accuracy. A notable example is the U-net model by Ronneberger et al. (2015), which utilized extensive image data augmentation and achieved a testing accuracy of 92%. Additionally, Pereira et al. (2016) presented a method for automatic brain tumor segmentation using convolutional neural networks, further illustrating the effectiveness of image data augmentation in enhancing medical imaging technologies.

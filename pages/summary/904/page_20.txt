The table presents a selection of works in the medical domain that utilized image data augmentation techniques. The studies span from 2015 to 2020, focusing on various applications such as segmentation and classification in fields like biomedical imaging and disease detection.

Notable contributions include the U-net model by Ronneberger et al. in 2015, which employed shifting and rotation for segmentation tasks. Pereira et al. in 2016 developed a convolutional neural network for MRI segmentation using rotation. Khalifa et al. in 2019 applied various techniques including reflection, cropping, and noise for classifying limited bacterial colony datasets. 

In 2020, several studies by Loey et al. and Khalifa et al. explored deep learning approaches for classifying images related to leukemia and COVID-19, employing techniques such as GANs and CGANs for data augmentation. Collectively, these works highlight the significance of image data augmentation in enhancing model performance across diverse medical imaging applications.

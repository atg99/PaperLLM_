This paper presents a comprehensive survey of recent trends in deep learning applications across various domains, with a particular focus on image data augmentation techniques. In the medical field, significant advancements have been made in brain tumor segmentation using classical augmentation methods, achieving notable dice similarity coefficients. The COVID-19 pandemic has spurred research into improving classification accuracy for chest X-ray and CT images through classical augmentation combined with Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), demonstrating enhanced performance in identifying the virus.

In agriculture, image data augmentation has proven beneficial for enhancing model accuracy in pest classification and plant identification. For instance, researchers increased their dataset size threefold using classical techniques, resulting in a notable accuracy improvement from 41.8% to 89.33%. Additionally, a novel approach for plant identification utilizing deep neural networks achieved an accuracy of 80% through various augmentation methods.

The paper also highlights the importance of image data augmentation in the remote sensing domain, where it aids in detecting and tracking environmental properties. Tables summarizing selected research works in the medical, agriculture, and remote sensing domains illustrate the widespread application and effectiveness of these techniques in improving model performance across different fields.

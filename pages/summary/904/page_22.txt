The paper presents a summary of selected research works that utilized image data augmentation techniques in the remote sensing domain. It highlights various applications and the specific augmentation methods employed in each study:

1. Yan et al. (2019) introduced a novel data augmentation method for aircraft detection using a Region Detection Network.
2. Guirado et al. (2019) applied convolutional neural networks for whale counting, utilizing techniques such as rotation, translation, reflection, and scaling.
3. Shawky et al. (2020) focused on remote sensing classification across 21 classes, implementing rotation, translation, and flipping as augmentation strategies.
4. Yamashkin et al. (2021) explored deep transfer learning for plant classification in remote sensing, working with images of different scales.

These studies collectively demonstrate the effectiveness of data augmentation in enhancing the performance of remote sensing applications.

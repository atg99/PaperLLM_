The paper discusses the significance of data augmentation, particularly in the context of image datasets, as a critical strategy for enhancing machine learning models. It emphasizes that data is essential for training algorithms, and a lack of sufficient labeled data can adversely affect model accuracy. The authors outline several advantages of image data augmentation, including cost-effectiveness compared to traditional data collection, accuracy derived from ground-truth data, controllability for balanced datasets, and its role in mitigating overfitting while improving testing accuracies.

Deep learning (DL), a subset of machine learning and artificial intelligence, relies heavily on data for learning and simulating human brain functions in decision-making processes. The paper underlines that without adequate image data, DL models struggle to learn effectively. Data augmentation is presented as a crucial method to address overfitting in DL models, alongside other techniques that will be discussed. The survey aims to showcase how oversampling classes in image data can be achieved through data augmentation, highlighting its importance and presenting state-of-the-art methods for researchers to develop more effective image-based models.

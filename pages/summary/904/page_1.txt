This paper presents a comprehensive survey on recent trends in deep learning for digital image augmentation, highlighting its critical role in enhancing the performance of deep learning models, particularly in fields where large datasets are scarce, such as medical imaging. The authors, Nour Eldeen Khalifa, Mohamed Loey, and Seyedali Mirjalili, address the challenge of limited data availability, exemplified by the lack of COVID-19 datasets since early 2020. 

The survey is structured into four main sections: 
1. An introduction that emphasizes the significance of data augmentation in deep learning.
2. A discussion on classical image data augmentation taxonomy and photometric transformations.
3. An exploration of deep learning-specific image data augmentation techniques.
4. A review of the current state of art in applying image data augmentation across various deep learning research and applications.

The paper underscores the importance of data augmentation in improving model accuracy and performance, especially in scenarios with insufficient training data. Key concepts include data augmentation, image augmentation, deep learning methodologies, and the use of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) in this context. The authors aim to provide a foundational understanding of the techniques available to enhance image datasets for improved deep learning outcomes.

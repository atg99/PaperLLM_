The table presents selected research works that utilized image data augmentation across various domains. Each entry includes the year of publication, a brief description of the research, its application, and the specific augmentation techniques employed. 

1. Eitel et al. (2015) developed two deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for RGB-D object recognition, employing cropping and noise addition as augmentation techniques.
2. Farfade et al. (2015) focused on face detection using deep CNNs, utilizing rotation, flipping, and noise addition.
3. Yu et al. (2015) applied deep transfer models for sketch recognition, implementing flipping, translation, and shifting.
4. Boominathan et al. (2016) combined deep and shallow CNNs for dense crowd counting, using scaling as the augmentation method.
5. Uhlich et al. (2017) worked on music source separation with deep neural networks, applying flipping and scaling.
6. Khalifa et al. (2018) conducted a study on galaxy morphology classifications with deep CNNs, utilizing reflecting, rotation, and cropping.
7. Lim et al. (2018) explored unsupervised anomaly detection through generative adversarial networks (GAN) and CNNs, employing GAN for augmentation.
8. Zhu et al. (2018) focused on emotion classification using GANs and CNNs, also leveraging GAN for data augmentation.
9. Loey et al. (2020) conducted a study on medical face mask detection with deep learning, using flipping, reflecting, and rotation.
10. Loey et al. (2021) furthered face mask classification through deep learning, employing reflecting and rotation techniques.

This summary highlights the application of various image data augmentation techniques within different fields of research, demonstrating the versatility and effectiveness of these methods in enhancing model performance.

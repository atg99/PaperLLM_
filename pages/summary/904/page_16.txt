The paper discusses two advanced data augmentation techniques in deep learning: Auto Augment (AA) and Smart Augmentation (SA). 

Auto Augment, introduced by Cubuk et al. in 2019, involves a series of sub-policies that apply random image processing tasks such as translation, rotation, and shearing to images. The method aims to optimize the validity accuracy of neural networks by selecting effective transformations. The AA model demonstrated impressive results, achieving a classification rate of 98.52% on the CIFAR-10 dataset and a top-1 error rate of 16.5% on ImageNet.

Smart Augmentation, presented by Lemley et al. in 2017, employs a modern regression approach to enhance training accuracy and reduce overfitting. This technique involves creating a network that generates improved data during the training phase, focusing on minimizing the target network's errors. The ANN utilized in SA significantly increased model accuracy across various datasets.

Both methods highlight the importance of effective data augmentation strategies in improving deep learning model performance.

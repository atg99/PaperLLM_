The referenced papers collectively explore the vulnerabilities of natural language processing models to adversarial attacks. They highlight methods for generating adversarial examples that can mislead deep learning classifiers, particularly in text classification and understanding tasks. Key contributions include the development of techniques for crafting adversarial text sequences, such as textbugger and probability-weighted word saliency, which can effectively deceive models while maintaining semantic coherence. The works also examine the robustness of models like BERT against these attacks and propose various strategies, including virtual adversarial training and lexical substitutions, to enhance model resilience. The overarching theme emphasizes the need for improved defenses in NLP systems to counteract these adversarial challenges.

This paper presents a novel approach to few-shot learning (FSL) using a method called MTL with HT meta-batch learning. The effectiveness of this method is demonstrated through experiments on miniImageNet and Fewshot-CIFAR100 datasets. The results indicate that MTL with HT meta-batch consistently outperforms traditional meta-batch learning in terms of classification accuracy across various settings, particularly excelling in the challenging 1-shot learning scenario.

Key findings include:

1. MTL with HT meta-batch achieves higher accuracy than previous methods, with notable performance improvements after just a few iterations, especially on the more difficult FC100 dataset.
2. The adaptability of MTL on pre-trained deep neural network (DNN) neurons enhances learning experiences for unseen tasks, making it a robust solution for few-shot learning challenges.
3. The HT meta-batch method demonstrates consistent performance across different models, suggesting its general applicability to various task difficulties.

Overall, the study highlights the potential of HT meta-batch in accelerating convergence and improving performance in few-shot learning frameworks. The research is supported by multiple funding initiatives, emphasizing its significance in advancing machine learning methodologies.

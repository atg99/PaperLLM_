The referenced works collectively explore advancements in the field of Bayesian networks and their learning algorithms. Colombo and Maathuis (2014) address order-independent constraint-based causal structure learning, contributing to the understanding of causal inference without dependency on the order of variables. Cooper (1990) discusses the computational complexity involved in probabilistic inference through Bayesian belief networks, while Cooper and Herskovits (1992) present a Bayesian method for inducing probabilistic networks from data, emphasizing the practical implications of Bayesian learning.

Cussens (2011) introduces a method for Bayesian network learning utilizing cutting planes, indicating an innovative approach to improve learning efficiency. Cussens et al. (2013) provide insights into optimal algorithms for learning Bayesian networks in a tutorial format, offering a comprehensive overview of best practices in the field.

De Campos and colleagues (2011, 2009) focus on efficient structure learning of Bayesian networks using constraints, highlighting the importance of constraints in improving learning algorithms. Their later work (2015) on learning extended tree-augmented naive structures presents further advancements in the representation and learning of probabilistic models.

Elidan and Gould (2008) explore learning bounded treewidth Bayesian networks, which has implications for computational feasibility in complex models. The Elvira Consortium (2002) introduces Elvira, a software environment for probabilistic graphical models, facilitating research and application in this domain.

Fernández and collaborators (2010, 2013, 2008) extend Bayesian network applications to regression problems and incomplete databases, showcasing the versatility of Bayesian networks beyond traditional classification tasks. Lastly, Friedman and his co-authors (1997, 1998) discuss the Bayesian structural EM algorithm and Bayesian network classifiers, providing foundational insights into Bayesian classification methodologies.

Overall, these works reflect significant contributions to the theory and application of Bayesian networks, emphasizing both theoretical underpinnings and practical algorithms for learning and inference.

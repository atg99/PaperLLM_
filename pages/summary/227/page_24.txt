The paper discusses the covering numbers of hypothesis spaces in the context of neural networks, particularly focusing on ResNet architectures. It defines the covering number N2j+1, which pertains to the outputs F2j+1(X) at a specific vertex N(2j + 1) when the input F2j-1(X) is fixed. Additionally, it introduces the covering number Nu,v,1 for outputs Fu,v,iV(X) at the vertex of a vine structure V(u,v,1) with a fixed input Fv(X).

The author applies Lemma 5 to derive an upper bound for the covering number N2j+1, expressed through an inequality that incorporates various parameters including the maximum dimension of features W in the ResNet. The inequality provides a logarithmic relationship with respect to N2j+1 and involves the norms of specific outputs, allowing for an inductive approach to further simplify and bound these norms.

Two scenarios are considered for calculating the norm kF2j+1(XT)k2, one for when no vine is connected to the stem, and another for when a vine is connected. The derived inequalities for both cases lead to a characterization of the norm of the ResNet output, showcasing the complexities of the network's structure.

Similarly, the paper establishes an upper bound for the covering number Nu,v,1, highlighting the relationship between the covering numbers and the norms of the outputs at designated vertices. The analysis emphasizes the significance of these covering numbers in understanding the capacity and generalization of neural networks.

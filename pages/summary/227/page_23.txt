The paper discusses the covering numbers of function spaces and hypothesis spaces, particularly focusing on chain-like neural networks and ResNet architectures. It establishes that the function space W0 serves as a covering for the hypothesis space H0, with an upper bound on its cardinality. This is expressed through a series of inequalities that relate the covering numbers of various function spaces and highlight the construction of deep neural networks by incrementally adding components.

The discussion includes the derivation of an upper bound for the cardinality of W0 using previously established results in the paper, specifically Lemma 3. The method involves constructing a deep neural network by adding "vines" to a "stem," allowing for a systematic approach to understanding the complexity of the network.

In the latter part, the paper presents a detailed proof of the covering bound for a specific ResNet configuration, referred to as 34-ResNet. It outlines the components of the network, including weight matrices and nonlinearities, and establishes an inequality that connects these elements to the covering number of the hypothesis space, as derived from Theorem 1.

Overall, the findings contribute to a deeper understanding of the capacity and complexity of neural networks, particularly in the context of ResNet architectures, providing a framework for analyzing their covering properties.

The paper discusses the structure of a ResNet, specifically focusing on its stem and vine framework. The stem is described as comprising various weight matrices and nonlinearities, totaling 70 vertices, including 34 weight matrices and 35 nonlinearities. The norms of these weight matrices are bounded, and the Lipschitz constants of the nonlinearities are defined.

Within this framework, 16 specific vines are identified, with three of them containing weight matrices while the others serve as identity mappings. The paper establishes upper bounds for the norms of these weight matrices and the distances to their corresponding reference matrices, leading to the formulation of a covering number bound for the hypothesis space of the ResNet.

The lemma presented provides a mathematical expression for the log of the covering number based on the defined parameters, including the upper bounds for the norms and distances of the weight matrices. This formulation is critical for understanding the capacity of the ResNet in relation to its hypothesis space. The paper concludes with specific inequalities that characterize the behavior of the ResNet under the defined conditions.

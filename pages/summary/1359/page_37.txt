The referenced paper discusses the perception of autonomous robots as threatening entities, exploring the implications of this perception on human-robot interaction. The authors, Jakub Zlotowski, Kumar Yogeeswaran, and Christoph Bartneck, highlight the psychological factors that contribute to this fear and the potential consequences for the development and acceptance of robotic technologies. The paper appears in the International Journal of Human-Computer Studies and is part of a broader work on human-robot interaction published by Cambridge University Press. The authors include Christoph Bartneck, Tony Belpaeime, Friederike Eyssel, Takayuki Kanda, Merel Keijsers, and Selma Sabanovic.

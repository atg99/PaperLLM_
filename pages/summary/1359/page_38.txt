The document is a pre-publication version of a work on Human-Robot Interaction authored by Christoph Bartneck, Tony Belpaeime, Friederike Eyssel, Takayuki Kanda, Merel Keijsers, and Selma Sabanovic, published by Cambridge University Press. It includes a comprehensive exploration of the interactions between humans and robots, discussing various aspects such as design, social implications, and user experiences. The authors contribute to the understanding of how robots can be integrated into human environments and the psychological effects of such interactions.

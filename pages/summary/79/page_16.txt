In binary classification, the study achieved an accuracy of 96.51% with 122 features and 99.88% with the KDDTrain + dataset. After implementing the genetic algorithm and reducing the features to 99, accuracy increased to 99.91% for KDDTest + and 99.99% for KDDTrain +, with a notable improvement in true positive rate (TPR) and false positive rate (FPR). The Random Forest (RF) classifier demonstrated the highest accuracy in binary classification due to its effective handling of well-defined features through decision trees. 

In multi-class classification, initial accuracy was 82.68% with 122 features. After feature optimization using the genetic algorithm, accuracy rose to 93.88% for KDDTest + and 99.0% for KDDTrain +. The genetic algorithm not only enhanced accuracy but also reduced both training and testing times. The findings indicate that the genetic algorithm is effective in improving classification performance while minimizing computational demands, demonstrating its utility in both binary and multi-class classification scenarios.

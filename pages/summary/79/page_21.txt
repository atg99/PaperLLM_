The paper discusses various resources and studies related to machine learning and network intrusion detection systems. It references foundational works on gradient-based learning algorithms for recurrent networks, highlighting their computational complexity. The paper also mentions genetic algorithms, providing an introductory resource for readers. Significant datasets used in the field, such as the KDD CUP 99, are analyzed to understand their application in intrusion detection. Studies on the NSL_KDD dataset are reviewed, focusing on the effectiveness of different classification algorithms. Additionally, the paper presents a review of benchmark datasets for network intrusion detection, emphasizing their importance in evaluating detection methods. The authors ensure that the article is openly accessible under the Creative Commons Attribution license.

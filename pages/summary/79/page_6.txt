The NSL-KDD dataset is an enhanced version of the KDDCup'99 dataset, designed to provide unbiased classification results by removing redundant records. This refinement leads to improved detection rates. The dataset is divided into KDDTrain +, a comprehensive training set with attack-type labels and difficulty levels, and KDDTest +, a testing set with similar characteristics, both in CSV format.

In a study referenced, three subsets of the training set were created, each containing fifty thousand records. Twenty-one learning machines, previously trained on these subsets, were utilized to label the records in both the training and testing sets. Each record received 21 predicted labels, with the difficulty level indicating the number of correct predictions made by these machines, reaching a maximum of 21.

The dataset categorizes attacks into major types, including Denial of Service (DoS), User to Root (U2R), Remote to Local (R2L), and Probing, with specific subcategories listed. Additionally, a table outlines the total instances of each attack type within the dataset, highlighting the prevalence of various attacks, such as Neptune with 41,214 instances and Normal traffic with 67,343 instances.

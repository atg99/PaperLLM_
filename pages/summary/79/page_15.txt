The paper presents a performance comparison of various classifiers—LSTM-RNN, SVM, and Random Forest (RF)—in both binary and multi-class classification tasks. 

In binary classification, LSTM-RNN achieved a testing accuracy of 96.51% with a True Positive Rate (TPR) of 0.944, while RF outperformed the others with the highest accuracy. However, when assessing multi-class classification using 122 features, LSTM-RNN exhibited the best performance with an accuracy of 82.68%, surpassing SVM (67.20%) and RF (80.70%), along with a TPR of 0.707.

Further analysis using 99 optimal features revealed that in binary classification, all classifiers performed similarly with testing accuracies around 99.9%. Specifically, RF had the highest accuracy at 99.99%. In multi-class classification with the same optimal features, LSTM-RNN again led with an accuracy of 93.88%, while RF and SVM scored 84.90% and 68.10%, respectively, with LSTM-RNN also showing the highest TPR of 0.896. 

Overall, LSTM-RNN demonstrated strong performance in multi-class settings, while RF excelled in binary classification tasks.

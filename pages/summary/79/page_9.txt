Mutation: To ensure diversity and avoid premature convergence, the GA introduces mutation by flipping the values of the attributes in the selected individuals. In this experiment, the mutation probability for each attribute is set to 0.05.

Overall, the paper discusses the implementation of a genetic algorithm (GA) for optimal feature selection in network intrusion detection systems (NIDS) using the NSL-KDD dataset. The aim is to enhance the accuracy of the predictive model by eliminating irrelevant and redundant features. The GA successfully reduced the feature set from 122 to 99 while maximizing classification accuracy and minimizing misclassification and training time. The GA utilizes logistic regression for fitness evaluation, ranking the feature sets based on selection error, and employs strategies like selection, crossover, and mutation to evolve the population towards optimal feature subsets.

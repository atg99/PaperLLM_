Key themes include the exploration of Markov decision processes (MDPs) under uncertainty, the development of algorithms for risk-sensitive control, and advancements in distributional reinforcement learning.

Overall, these studies collectively push the boundaries of RL by addressing uncertainties, risks, and robustness, setting a foundation for future research in the field.

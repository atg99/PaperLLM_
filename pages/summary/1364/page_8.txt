The paper by Selvaggio et al. discusses the importance of autonomy in physical human-robot interaction (pHRI), emphasizing the potential advancements that could arise from addressing existing challenges in this field. The authors argue that shared autonomy (SA) will be crucial in the near future, particularly for aiding non-experts and individuals with disabilities in their daily activities. The adaptability of SA to user needs is highlighted as a key feature that can enhance user experience and task performance.

The references cited in the paper provide a comprehensive background on various aspects of autonomy in robotics, including supervisory control, intent detection, and shared control strategies. These works underscore the evolution of robotic systems towards increased autonomy and the importance of collaboration between humans and robots in diverse applications. The paper ultimately advocates for further research and development in SA to maximize the benefits of robotics in everyday life.

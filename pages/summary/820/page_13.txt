The paper presents a comprehensive overview of the networks, datasets, and training configurations used for image classification tasks. It details the following networks and datasets:

1. **Networks**:
   - **LeNet-300-100**: A fully-connected network with two hidden layers (300 and 100 units) and ReLU activations, utilized for MNIST.
   - **ResNet-20**: A 20-layer ResNet model for CIFAR-10, incorporating batch normalization prior to activations, based on He et al. (2016).
   - **VGG-16**: A convolutional network for CIFAR-10 with multiple layers of increasing channels, batch normalization before each ReLU activation, as described by Lee et al. (2019).
   - **ResNet-18 and ResNet-50**: ResNet architectures for ImageNet with 18 and 50 layers, respectively, using TorchVision implementations.
   - **Modified ResNet-18**: An adaptation of ResNet-18 for TinyImageNet, featuring a 3x3 convolution filter and no max-pooling layer, aligned with Tanaka et al. (2020).

2. **Datasets**:
   - **CIFAR-10**: Augmented through normalization, random horizontal flipping, and pixel shifts.
   - **TinyImageNet**: Augmented with normalization, random aspect ratios, scales, cropping, and flipping.
   - **Modified TinyImageNet**: Similar to TinyImageNet but with different augmentation techniques.
   - **ImageNet**: Augmented using normalization, random aspect ratios, scales, cropping to 224x224, and flipping.

3. **Training**:
   - Each network is trained with specified epochs, batch sizes, optimizers, momentum, learning rates, learning rate drops, weight decay, initialization methods, and iterations per epoch. For example, LeNet is trained for 40 epochs with a batch size of 128 using SGD, while ResNet-50 is trained for 90 epochs with a batch size of 1024.

This structured approach emphasizes the configurations and methodologies employed in the experiments, facilitating reproducibility and further research in the field of image classification.

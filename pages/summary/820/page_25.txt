The paper presents a comparison of four baseline methods—random pruning at initialization, magnitude pruning at initialization, magnitude pruning after training, and lottery ticket rewinding—against the methods SNIP, GraSP, and SynFlow. The lottery ticket rewinding method's performance varies based on the iteration at which the pruned network is set, with specific iterations chosen for different architectures: ResNet-20, VGG-16, ResNet-18, and ResNet-50. These iterations (1000, 2000, 1000, and 6000 respectively) correspond to points where accuracy improvements begin to plateau, as illustrated in the figures provided.

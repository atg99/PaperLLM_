The paper presents three variants of the GraSP method, which involves pruning weights based on different criteria. The variants include: pruning weights with the highest scores, as proposed by Wang et al.; pruning weights with the lowest scores, referred to as the inversion experiment; and pruning weights with the lowest magnitude GraSP scores, which is suggested as an improvement.

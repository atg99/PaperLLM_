The paper presents an analysis of various methods for identifying subnetworks after training neural networks. Specifically, it evaluates four techniques: Magnitude, SNIP, GraSP, and SynFlow. The results are illustrated in Figure 9, which shows the performance of these methods in selecting effective subnetworks. However, it is noted that the experiment was not conducted on the ResNet-50 architecture due to resource constraints.

The paper presents a comparison of the accuracy results of unpruned and pruned networks, specifically VGG-19, WRN-32-2, and ResNet-50, between the authors' reimplementation and the original SNIP findings reported by Wang et al. (2020). 

In the unpruned networks, the authors observe that their VGG-19, WRN-32-2, and ResNet-50 models achieve slightly lower accuracy compared to Wang et al., but the differences are minimal when compared to the unpruned networks in Table 2. 

For pruned networks, the authors note that while the unpruned VGG-19 accuracy is lower than that of Wang et al., the SNIP performances at 90% and 95% sparsity are identical. However, at 98% sparsity, three of five VGG-19 runs diverged. Notably, the authors report that their unpruned ResNet-50 and SNIP accuracies exceed those reported by Wang et al., which may be attributed to different hyperparameter choices in training and SNIP implementation. The authors mention that variations in the examples used to compute SNIP gradients could also contribute to discrepancies, although they lack access to Wang et al.'s SNIP code for verification. 

Overall, the findings indicate that while there are some differences in unpruned accuracy, the pruned performances remain consistent across implementations, highlighting the robustness of the SNIP method under certain conditions.

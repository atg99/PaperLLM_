The paper presented at ICLR 2021 explores the performance of various neural network architectures, specifically VGG-11, VGG-16, ResNet-18, and Modified ResNet-18, across different datasets including CIFAR-10, Tiny ImageNet, and CIFAR-100. The focus is on evaluating the trade-offs between compression ratios and top-1 accuracy for different pruning methods: SynFlow, SNIP, GraSP, Magnitude, and Random.

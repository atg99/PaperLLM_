The paper includes several appendices that provide detailed information on various aspects of the research. Appendix A outlines the networks, datasets, and hyperparameters utilized in the experiments. Appendices B through D discuss the authors' replications of the SNIP, GraSP, and SynFlow methods, respectively. Appendix E presents plots comparing baseline pruning methods such as random pruning and magnitude pruning under different conditions. 

Appendix F details the ablation studies related to shuffling and reinitialization conducted after training. Appendix G reports the results of performing SNIP iteratively over 100 iterations. Appendix H examines three variants of GraSP based on different scoring criteria for pruning weights. 

Appendix I provides a comparison of early pruning methods, including enhancements to GraSP and SynFlow. Appendix J shows the layerwise proportions of pruning for each method. Appendices K and L detail experiments conducted on the LeNet-300-100 network for MNIST and a modified version of ResNet-18, respectively. Finally, Appendix M analyzes effective sparsities in networks, considering weights that are disconnected but not explicitly pruned.

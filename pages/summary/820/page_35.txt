The paper discusses the performance of various pruning methods, specifically focusing on iterative SNIP and its effects on accuracy at high sparsity levels. Unlike convolutional networks, iterative SNIP demonstrates improved accuracy even at extreme sparsities. The authors suggest that similar benefits might be observed for convolutional networks if explored at greater sparsities than those examined in their study. Key figures illustrate the comparative performance of different pruning techniques, such as SNIP, GraSP, and SynFlow, highlighting their respective impacts on network accuracy.

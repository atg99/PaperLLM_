The paper presented at ICLR 2021 includes a figure that illustrates the relationship between actual sparsity and the effective parameter count of networks. Specifically, it compares the effective parameter count of a randomly shuffled ablation to that of a network pruned using an unmodified pruning method. The focus is on the ratio of these counts, with particular attention given to values between 0.99 and 1.05, indicating a narrow range of similarity in effective parameter counts under varying sparsity levels. This analysis highlights the impact of different pruning techniques on network efficiency.

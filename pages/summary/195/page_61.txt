Florensa et al. (2017) introduce a method for pre-training a diverse range of skills using stochastic neural networks, incorporating an information-theoretic regularizer. This approach allows for the subsequent training of high-level policies for specific downstream tasks. The pre-training process utilizes a proxy reward signal, serving as intrinsic motivation for the agent to explore its capabilities with minimal prior knowledge of the tasks. Their method effectively merges hierarchical techniques with intrinsic motivation, and the pre-training is conducted in an unsupervised manner.

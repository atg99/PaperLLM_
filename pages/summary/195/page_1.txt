The paper provides an overview of deep reinforcement learning (RL), presenting a comprehensive picture that encompasses six core elements, six important mechanisms, and twelve applications. It begins with foundational concepts in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and deep learning, leading into the specifics of reinforcement learning. The core elements discussed include value function, policy, reward, model, exploration versus exploitation, and representation.

The paper further explores essential mechanisms that enhance RL, such as attention and memory, unsupervised learning, hierarchical and multi-agent RL, relational RL, and learning to learn. A significant portion is dedicated to the diverse applications of RL across various fields, including games, robotics, natural language processing, computer vision, finance, business management, healthcare, education, energy, transportation, and the sciences.

In conclusion, the paper summarizes key points, addresses current challenges and opportunities in the field, and provides an epilogue. This work builds on previous research and incorporates insights from discussions with peers, indicating its ongoing development.

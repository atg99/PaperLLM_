The listed references encompass a variety of research contributions in the fields of deep learning, reinforcement learning, and neural network architectures. Notable works include Graves et al. (2016), which introduces hybrid computing with dynamic external memory, and Gregor et al. (2015), presenting a recurrent neural network for image generation. The exploration of sample-efficient architectures is highlighted by Gruslys et al. (2017) with their Actor-Critic model, while Gu et al. (2017a, 2017b) focus on deep reinforcement learning applications in robotic manipulation and policy gradient methods. 

Several studies aim at enhancing learning capabilities, such as Guez et al. (2014, 2018), which discuss simulation-based search and learning to search with MCTS networks, respectively. The improvement of generative models is addressed by Gulrajani et al. (2017) in the context of Wasserstein GANs. Gupta et al. (2017, 2018) investigate self-supervised learning and meta-reinforcement learning for exploration strategies.

The references also highlight advancements in cognitive mapping and planning (Gupta et al., 2017b), the bridging of language and programming through reinforcement learning (Guu et al., 2017), and the development of neural representations for sketches (Ha & Eck, 2018). Lastly, Haarnoja et al. (2017, 2018) contribute to the understanding of energy-based policies and the Soft Actor-Critic method, illustrating the ongoing evolution in deep reinforcement learning methodologies.

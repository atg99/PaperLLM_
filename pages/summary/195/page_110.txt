The paper discusses the significant advancements and implications of artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning, particularly focusing on deep learning and reinforcement learning. It references various scholarly articles and books that explore the impact of these technologies on business and society, highlighting their growing importance as illustrated by the launch of dedicated journals and special issues in prominent scientific publications.

Deep learning has achieved notable success in fields such as speech recognition, computer vision, and natural language processing, solidifying its status in both academia and industry. In contrast, reinforcement learning, although promising with potential applications, has not yet seen widespread product adoption, suggesting a need for improved algorithms and market validation.

The author argues that it is an opportune moment to cultivate and educate the market around reinforcement learning, anticipating that both deep learning and reinforcement learning will continue to thrive in the future. The paper emphasizes that deep learning is part of a broader third wave of AI, which is expected to have profound effects on machine learning and AI as a whole. The historical context is provided by mentioning Professor Rich Sutton's establishment of the RLAI lab, indicating the foundational role of reinforcement learning in the evolution of AI.

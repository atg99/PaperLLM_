This manuscript explores a comprehensive range of topics within deep reinforcement learning (RL). It discusses key concepts such as agents, environments, states, rewards, actions, exploration, representation models, policies, and value functions. The paper emphasizes the significance of multi-agent RL, hierarchical RL, and the integration of learning mechanisms like attention and memory. It also touches on unsupervised learning and relational RL, showcasing applications across various fields including healthcare, business management, finance, education, energy, transportation, gaming, robotics, natural language processing, science, engineering, computer vision, and computer systems. The authors acknowledge the potential shortcomings in their work and welcome feedback for improvement.

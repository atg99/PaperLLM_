The paper discusses key gradients in understanding cognitive development, referencing Lake et al. (2016) on intuitive physics and psychology. Intuitive physics enables infants to track objects and dismiss unlikely movements, while intuitive psychology allows them to recognize that others have mental states like goals and beliefs, which influences their learning and predictions. The concept of causality is described as crucial in concept learning and scene understanding, where causal models help represent real-world processes and environmental structures. Additionally, Botvinick et al. (2017) introduce the concept of autonomy, emphasizing the importance of agents developing and utilizing their own internal models with minimal human intervention.

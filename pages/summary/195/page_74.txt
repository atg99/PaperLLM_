Recent advancements in AutoML have introduced several notable contributions. He et al. (2018) focused on model compression through AutoML techniques, enhancing efficiency in neural networks. Cubuk et al. (2018) introduced AutoAugment, a method to automate the process of data augmentation specifically for image datasets. Additionally, Chen et al. (2018c) explored the optimization of tensor programs through learning-based approaches. However, a significant limitation in neural architecture design persists, as existing network components are often manually crafted. This raises questions about the potential of AI to innovate and create new components, such as the discovery of residual connections that preceded the development of ResNets. For further details, the 2018 International Workshop on Automatic Machine Learning can be referenced.

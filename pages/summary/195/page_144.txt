Key works include Sutton's foundational texts, "Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction" (1998, 2018), which establish the principles of RL and its applications. Sutton's 1988 paper on temporal differences introduces methods for learning predictions based on past experiences, while his 1990 work discusses integrated architectures for learning and planning.

Sutton's collaboration with Andrew Barto has been influential, particularly in framing RL as direct adaptive optimal control. The exploration of policy gradient methods, off-policy learning, and temporal abstraction is emphasized in several papers, indicating advancements in function approximation techniques and learning from unsupervised interactions.

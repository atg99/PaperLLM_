The paper discusses the application of music theory rules in melody generation models to enhance the creation of harmonious and subjectively enjoyable melodies compared to alternative approaches. It references various studies and projects that explore deep learning techniques for music generation, including the work of van den Oord et al. (2016) on WaveNet for generating raw audio waveforms. Additionally, it highlights research by Briot et al. (2018) on deep learning for music, Zhu et al. (2018) on pop music generation, and Dieleman et al. (2018). The Magenta project is also mentioned for its exploration of deep learning and reinforcement learning in art and music creation. The paper points to the 2018 workshops on machine learning for music as a significant event in this field.

The paper discusses advancements in reinforcement learning, particularly in policy gradient methods and sample efficiency. It introduces SBEED, which is a de-biased version of Policy Contrastive Learning (PCL) that generalizes PCL. Recent works are highlighted, including Gu et al. (2017b) who proposed Q-Prop, which combines the stability of policy gradient with the sample efficiency of off-policy learning using a Taylor expansion. O’Donoghue et al. (2017) introduced PGQL, merging policy gradient with off-policy Q-learning, demonstrating the equivalence of action value fitting and actor-critic methods. Gu et al. (2017) further explored the interpolation of off-policy updates with value function estimation and on-policy updates, establishing performance guarantees and analyzing various algorithms including Q-Prop and PGQL.

Benchmark results are covered, particularly Duan et al. (2016), who conducted a comprehensive study on continuous control tasks, comparing several algorithms like REINFORCE and TRPO, among others. Henderson et al. (2018) focused on reproducibility in deep reinforcement learning, pointing out the impact of hyperparameters and environmental factors on experimental outcomes. Lastly, Tassa et al. (2018) presented the DeepMind Control Suite, a framework for continuous tasks based on the MuJoCo engine, including benchmarks for various algorithms. The open-source availability of these tools is also mentioned.

The paper discusses various applications of reinforcement learning (RL) across different domains, including transportation and computer systems. In the context of transportation, it highlights the use of batch RL with exogenous prices and feature extraction methods for state construction. Intelligent transportation systems leverage advanced technologies to address issues such as congestion and safety, with a significant focus on autonomous driving.

Adaptive control is a critical aspect of these systems, where El-Tantawy et al. model traffic signal control as a multi-player stochastic game, applying multi-agent RL to tackle challenges like stability and nonstationarity. Their approach emphasizes coordination among agents at neighboring intersections, validated through simulations and real traffic data.

In the realm of computer systems, the paper notes the prevalence of control and optimization problems. It cites various studies on cognitive radio networks, IoT, and wireless sensor networks, all of which contribute to advancements in robotics, energy systems, and transportation. The paper also mentions innovative approaches such as learned indexes for data structures and optimization techniques for SQL queries and sorting.

Overall, the paper underscores the potential of RL and machine learning methodologies to enhance performance optimization across diverse applications, from traffic management to computational efficiency in cloud computing.

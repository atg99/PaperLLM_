AlphaGo Zero is a reinforcement learning algorithm that employs a generalized policy iteration scheme, integrating Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) within its training loop for both policy improvement and evaluation. MCTS enhances move probabilities beyond the raw outputs of the neural network, while self-play serves as a method for policy evaluation, using game outcomes to inform the value function. The network's weights are updated to align more closely with these refined probabilities, leading to stronger future searches. Key advancements of AlphaGo Zero, compared to its predecessor AlphaGo, include learning solely from random play without human data, utilizing a single neural network for both policy and value, and employing recent neural network techniques like residual convolutional networks and batch normalization.

The training pipeline of AlphaGo Zero consists of three asynchronous components: continuous optimization of neural network weights from self-play data, ongoing evaluation of players, and the generation of new self-play data from the strongest player. During gameplay, MCTS uses the trained network to generate move probabilities and select moves.

AlphaZero generalizes the framework of AlphaGo Zero to other perfect information two-player zero-sum games, achieving superhuman performance in chess, shogi, and Go, without relying on human knowledge apart from the game rules.

It is important to note that AlphaGo Zero operates outside traditional supervised or unsupervised learning paradigms, as it utilizes game scores as reward signals rather than supervision labels. While MCTS plays a crucial role as a heuristic search component, AlphaGo Zero fundamentally adheres to a reinforcement learning approach. The algorithm's inputs include raw board representations, game rules, and invariance properties, and it operates using extensive computational resources, making it challenging for average researchers to replicate. ELF OpenGo serves as a reimplementation of AlphaGo Zero and AlphaZero, available through Facebook's AI tools.

In this chapter, the authors explore a range of applications for reinforcement learning (RL) across various fields, including healthcare, education, energy, transportation, computer systems, and the sciences, engineering, and art.

In healthcare (Section 20.1), the authors highlight the potential of machine learning to enhance personalized medicine, particularly for chronic conditions and cancers. They discuss various implementations such as a hybrid retrieval-generation agent for medical report generation, deep learning applications to electronic health records (EHR), and the creation of healthcare knowledge graphs. They also mention dynamic treatment regimes (DTRs), which involve sequential decision-making and address unique challenges like missing data and censored data. Recent advancements in deep RL for healthcare applications, including patient phenotype inference and treatment strategies for conditions like sepsis and acute ischaemic stroke, are also noted. The chapter references several conferences and workshops focused on machine learning in healthcare.

In education (Section 20.2), Northcutt's framework for AI in online education emphasizes personalization through active and passive AI. Active AI involves direct changes to course content or experiences, while passive AI focuses on estimation or modeling without direct intervention. Examples include cognitive tutors for active student engagement and proficiency estimations for passive assessments. The section also discusses content recommendation engines and methods for auto-generating courses based on student interactions, alongside passive strategies for assessing course structures.

Overall, the chapter underscores the transformative potential of reinforcement learning in various sectors, presenting both opportunities and challenges while referencing significant studies and frameworks.

In Part III of the paper, various applications of reinforcement learning (RL) are explored, highlighting its significance across multiple fields. Chapter 15 focuses on games, which serve as essential testing grounds for RL and AI. Chapter 16 addresses robotics, emphasizing its importance in the AI era. Chapter 17 delves into natural language processing (NLP), showcasing its extensive applications of RL. In Chapter 18, the integration of RL with computer vision is discussed, while Chapter 19 examines applications in finance and business management.

Further applications are presented in Chapter 20, including healthcare, education, energy, transportation, and computer systems. The paper notes that while operations research encompasses areas like supply chain and resource management, it is not explicitly listed as an application area since it underpins many discussed fields. The concept of smart cities is also acknowledged for its intersection with healthcare, education, energy, and transportation.

The text emphasizes that RL techniques, as outlined in prior chapters, are foundational to these applications, which can overlap. For instance, a robot may utilize skills from both computer vision and NLP. While RL primarily targets sequential decision-making, it has also been applied to seemingly non-sequential problems such as neural network architecture design and model compression. The potential for creativity in advancing deep RL is highlighted, suggesting that if a problem can be framed as a sequential decision-making challenge, RL may provide effective solutions.

The paper concludes with a visual representation of deep RL applications and mentions a blog dedicated to RL applications for further exploration.

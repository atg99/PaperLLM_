- Duan et al. (2016) introduce RL2, which enables fast reinforcement learning through a slower reinforcement learning process.

- El-Tantawy et al. (2013) apply multiagent reinforcement learning to optimize adaptive traffic signal control in urban environments.
- Ernst et al. (2005) present tree-based batch mode reinforcement learning, enhancing efficiency in learning algorithms.
- Eslami et al. (2016) and Eslami et al. (2018) explore scene understanding and neural scene representation, contributing to generative models.
- Espeholt et al. (2018) introduce IMPALA, a scalable distributed deep reinforcement learning architecture.
- Evans and Grefenstette (2018) focus on learning explanatory rules from noisy data, addressing challenges in data interpretation.
- Eykholt et al. (2017, 2018) investigate physical-world attacks on deep learning models, emphasizing the robustness of AI systems.
- Fang et al. (2017) propose a deep reinforcement learning approach to active learning, showcasing its potential in improving learning processes.
- Farahmand (2018) discusses iterative model learning in a value-aware context, contributing to efficiency in reinforcement learning.
- Faust et al. (2018) explore resilient computing in dynamical systems through reinforcement learning, demonstrating practical applications.
- Fauw et al. (2018) highlight advancements in clinically applicable deep learning for retinal disease diagnosis and referral.

These studies collectively advance the understanding and application of reinforcement learning across various domains, emphasizing the importance of robustness, efficiency, and practical implementation in real-world scenarios.

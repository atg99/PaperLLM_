The collection of references highlights significant contributions to the field of reinforcement learning (RL) and related areas. Key works include Barreto et al. (2017), which introduces successor features for transfer in RL, and Barth-Maron et al. (2018), presenting distributed distributional deterministic policy gradients. Barto's contributions span intrinsic motivation in RL, emphasizing its importance in learning systems, as seen in both his 2013 and 2017 works. The exploration of hierarchical reinforcement learning by Barto and Mahadevan (2003) showcases advancements in structured learning approaches.

Battaglia et al. (2018) discuss relational inductive biases and their implications for deep learning and graph networks, while their earlier work (2016) focuses on interaction networks for understanding objects and physics. Bellemare and colleagues contribute to the understanding of exploration in RL, with works on count-based exploration and the distributional perspective on RL (2017). Additionally, the development of the Arcade Learning Environment, as noted in Bellemare et al. (2013), serves as a benchmark for evaluating general agents.

These references collectively illustrate the evolution and intersection of RL, deep learning, and cognitive modeling, emphasizing advancements in algorithms, frameworks, and applications across various domains.

The paper discusses the challenges and opportunities in the field of deep reinforcement learning (RL), emphasizing the need for innovative applications that address fundamental issues such as credit assignment, sparse rewards, efficiency, stability, and interpretability. It acknowledges the rapid growth of deep RL, highlighting significant breakthroughs like Deep Q-Networks and AlphaGo, and the emergence of new learning mechanisms. The authors advocate for a deeper understanding of both deep learning and RL, noting that advancements in these areas can enhance AI capabilities.

The paper also critiques the perception of deep learning as a black box and stresses the importance of integrating human intelligence with machine learning. It encourages exploration of various perspectives on AI's impact on society and the economy, referencing relevant studies and critiques from notable scholars. Overall, the authors call for ongoing research and creativity to push the boundaries of deep RL, suggesting that it can be beneficial in automating and optimizing complex decision-making tasks.

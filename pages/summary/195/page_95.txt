The paper discusses various approaches to personalized recommendation systems, highlighting the application of reinforcement learning (RL) and contextual bandits. Li et al. (2010) treat personalized news article recommendations as a contextual bandit problem, focusing on user and article context to optimize article selection based on user click feedback. Theocharous et al. (2015) approach personalized ads recommendations with an RL framework aimed at maximizing lifetime value, proposing a model-free method to evaluate policies off-policy. Jiang and Li (2016) enhance off-policy value evaluation using a doubly robust estimator, which aids in safe policy improvements and is applicable to both shallow and deep RL. Other references include studies on customer interaction, e-commerce mechanisms, and various applications of RL in operations research and online retail environments. For a comprehensive overview of recommendation systems, Zhang et al. (2017) is cited, along with a reference to Sutton and Barto (2018) for further discussion on personalized web services.

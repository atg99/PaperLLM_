Evolution strategies have demonstrated strong performance in various applications, as highlighted by studies such as Petroski Such et al. (2017), Salimans et al. (2017), and Lehman et al. (2017). A comprehensive tutorial on the subject can be found in Hansen (2016). Khadka and Tumer (2018) have introduced the concept of evolutionary reinforcement learning. 

Policy search methods encompass a broad range of techniques from direct policy search to value-based reinforcement learning (RL). Key methods include evolutionary strategies, covariance matrix adaptation evolution strategy (CMA-ES), and episodic relative entropy policy search (REPS). Other notable approaches are policy gradients, probabilistic inference for learning control (PILCO), model-based REPS, and various actor-critic methods including natural actor critic and advantage weighted regression. AlphaGo's methodology, as discussed by Silver et al. (2016a; 2017), aligns with generalized policy iteration principles.

The paper will delve into policy gradients, actor-critic methods, trust region methods, off-policy learning, and benchmark results in subsequent sections. 

In Section 4.1, policy gradients, which optimize policies directly, are discussed. These can be either deterministic or stochastic. Silver et al. (2014) introduced the Deterministic Policy Gradient (DPG) algorithm, which is particularly efficient for continuous action spaces. DPG estimates the expected gradient of the action-value function, providing a more efficient estimation than stochastic methods due to its focus on state space integration.

Lillicrap et al. (2016) expanded upon this with the Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (DDPG) algorithm, which employs an actor-critic framework to manage continuous action spaces. DDPG enhances stability and robustness through techniques like experience replay and a soft target network, which updates weights gradually rather than instantaneously. This approach allows DDPG to learn effectively from experiences generated by an exploratory policy, thus addressing challenges posed by complex action spaces and deep neural networks. 

Overall, the paper outlines significant advancements in policy optimization methods, particularly emphasizing the effectiveness of deterministic approaches in high-dimensional settings.

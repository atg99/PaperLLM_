Osband et al. (2016) introduce bootstrapped DQN, which merges deep exploration with deep neural networks for effective learning. This method employs randomized value functions to facilitate Thompson sampling, allowing exploration with non-linear function approximations. The bootstrapped DQN framework creates multiple parallel estimates of the action value function, each utilizing its own target network. The authors assess the algorithm's performance using Atari games.

Nachum et al. (2017) present under-appreciated reward exploration (UREX) to enhance exploration by focusing on regions where the current policy underestimates rewards, contrasting with traditional methods like ε-greedy strategies. UREX utilizes importance sampling from the optimal policy and balances exploration and exploitation through mode-seeking and mean-seeking components. This approach is implemented with minor adjustments to the REINFORCE algorithm and is validated on various algorithmic tasks, representing a step towards symbolic deep reinforcement learning.

Azar et al. (2017) investigate optimal exploration in finite horizon Markov Decision Processes (MDPs). Fu et al. (2017) explore novelty detection through discriminative modeling for exploration. Fortunato et al. (2018) propose NoisyNet, which enhances exploration by incorporating parametric noise into neural network weights. Jiang et al. (2017) examine systematic exploration in contextual decision processes. Additional relevant studies include those by Dimakopoulou et al. (2018), Dong and Roy (2018), Gupta et al. (2018), Kumaraswamy et al. (2018), Madhavan et al. (2018), and Osband et al. (2018). Furthermore, maximum entropy reinforcement learning is noted to aid exploration, as discussed in Section 4.4.

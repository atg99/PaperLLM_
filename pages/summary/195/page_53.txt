In Part II of the paper, the focus is on key mechanisms that contribute to the advancement of deep reinforcement learning (RL). The chapters cover various topics: Chapter 9 addresses attention and memory, Chapter 10 explores unsupervised learning, Chapter 11 delves into hierarchical RL, Chapter 12 discusses multi-agent RL, Chapter 13 examines relational RL, and Chapter 14 introduces the concept of learning to learn. It is important to note that the paper does not cover certain mechanisms, such as Bayesian RL and semi-supervised RL, which have been discussed in other works.

The paper discusses the evaluation of generative models, specifically focusing on the performance of the VQ-VAE method compared to traditional GAN metrics like Inception Score (IS) and Fréchet Inception Distance (FID). It highlights the use of classifier-based rejection sampling to balance diversity and quality in generated images, resulting in improved FID and IS scores for VQ-VAE. The results reveal that while VQ-VAE can achieve competitive fidelity and broader diversity, it is sensitive to blurriness and perturbations, as indicated by the FID scores. The authors propose a straightforward approach to generating high-resolution images using VQ-VAE combined with an autoregressive model, emphasizing the effectiveness of autoregressive modeling in the latent space for large-scale generative tasks. They conclude that while current metrics for sample quality and diversity are still developing, visual inspection remains vital for evaluating generative models.

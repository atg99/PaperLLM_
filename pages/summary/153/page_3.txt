The paper presents a hierarchical Variational Quantized Variational Autoencoder (VQ-VAE) architecture designed for image generation. The architecture consists of deep neural network encoders and decoders that process 256x256 images, compressing them into quantized latent maps of sizes 64x64 and 32x32 at the bottom and top levels, respectively. The reconstruction of the image is achieved by the decoder using these latent maps.

The proposed method follows a two-stage approach: first, a hierarchical VQ-VAE is trained to encode images into a discrete latent space, and second, a PixelCNN prior is fitted to this latent space. The architecture allows for the separation of local information, such as texture, from global information, such as shape and geometry, by conditioning the bottom latent code on the top latent code. This hierarchical structure enables tailored modeling of correlations at different levels.

The training involves a loss function that includes reconstruction loss and additional terms to encourage the encoder output to closely match the quantized codes. The paper employs exponential moving average updates for the codebook to enhance training stability.

Overall, the proposed hierarchical VQ-VAE architecture aims to improve image generation by effectively capturing both local and global features through a multi-scale latent representation.

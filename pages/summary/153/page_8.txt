The paper presents a comparison between VQ-V AE-2 and BigGAN Deep in terms of sample diversity and classification accuracy. Figures illustrate that BigGAN samples lack certain perspectives and poses, such as top views of Tinca-Tinca and various close-up poses of ostriches, whereas VQ-V AE-2 captures a wider variety of representations. Additionally, the samples generated from a 3-level hierarchical model trained on FFHQ exhibit long-range dependencies, such as matching eye colors and symmetric facial features, while also representing lower density modes like green hair.

The study also employs the Classiﬁcation Accuracy Score (CAS) to assess the quality and diversity of generated samples. The evaluation involves training an ImageNet classifier on samples from each model and testing it against real images. Results show that VQ-V AE outperforms BigGAN Deep in both Top-1 and Top-5 accuracy metrics. Specifically, VQ-V AE achieves a Top-1 accuracy of 54.83 and Top-5 accuracy of 77.59, which improves to 58.74 and 80.98 respectively after reconstructing images. In contrast, the real dataset achieves a Top-1 accuracy of 73.09 and Top-5 accuracy of 91.47. Overall, the findings highlight the effectiveness of VQ-V AE in generating diverse and high-quality samples.

The paper presents TT-Rec, a novel approach to compressing embedding tables in deep learning recommendation models using Tensor Train (TT) decomposition. Traditional recommendation models face challenges due to the exponential growth of embedding table sizes, leading to significant memory and bandwidth demands. TT-Rec proposes replacing large embedding tables with a sequence of smaller matrix products derived from TT decomposition, which is a method for compressing multidimensional data into smaller tensors.

TT decomposition operates by transforming a multi-dimensional tensor into a product of smaller tensors, allowing for efficient storage and computation. The authors explain that this method has been effective in other deep learning applications but has not been explored in the recommendation space. The paper outlines the mathematical framework for TT decomposition and its application to compress embedding tables, reducing storage requirements significantly.

The proposed design, TT-Rec, customizes this decomposition for embedding table compression while addressing potential accuracy loss from the conversion process. The authors introduce a new initialization method for the distribution of weights in the TT-cores to mitigate skewness resulting from the product operations. The embedding lookup operation is reformulated to leverage the compressed representation, thus maintaining efficiency in computation and storage.

Overall, TT-Rec represents a promising advancement in addressing the challenges of scalability and efficiency in deep learning recommendation systems by utilizing tensor train compression techniques.

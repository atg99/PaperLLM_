In this paper, we introduce Tensor Train decomposition for Deep Learning Recommendation Models (TT-Rec) as an innovative method to compress embedding tables within DLRMs. The need for such advancements is underscored by the significant increase in memory requirements for these models, which now reach sizes from tens of gigabytes to terabytes. Our proposed solution, TT-EmbeddingBag, is shown to be three times faster than the state-of-the-art implementation, enhancing the efficiency of embedding vector lookup operations through optimized strategies including batched matrix multiplication and caching.

Additionally, we investigate the impact of weight initialization on model accuracy and recommend initializing tensor cores using a sampled Gaussian distribution. Our evaluations of TT-Rec across memory capacity, accuracy, and timing performance demonstrate substantial model size reductions—ranging from 4 to 221 times for Kaggle data and achieving a 112 times reduction for Terabyte data without sacrificing accuracy or increasing training time.

Overall, TT-Rec presents a promising avenue for addressing the increasing memory demands of DLRMs, aligning with the industry's need for efficient and scalable solutions. The implementation code is made available on GitHub, facilitating further research and development in this domain.

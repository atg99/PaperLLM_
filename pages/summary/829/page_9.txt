The paper presents UNIQ, a technique for uniform noise injection aimed at improving the quantization process in deep neural networks. It highlights the importance of weight distribution in neural networks, specifically referencing the Shapiro-Wilk test statistic for various layers in the ResNet-18 architecture. The paper underscores the effectiveness of this method in enhancing model compression and performance, aligning with existing literature on deep compression strategies, including pruning and Huffman coding. The authors build upon foundational work in deep learning and image recognition, indicating that UNIQ can facilitate more efficient neural network quantization while maintaining accuracy.

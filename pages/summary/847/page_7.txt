This study examined the variability of COVID-19 pneumonia severity among patients across multiple international centers, highlighting the potential for developing robust and generalizable AI tools for COVID-19 diagnosis through image analysis in diverse clinical settings. The research demonstrated that multicenter training could improve AI model generalizability, a concept supported by previous studies in medical imaging. The AI model tested CT volumes in approximately 40 milliseconds, suggesting its viability for real-time clinical application. Results from lesion detection in COVID-19 CT images were presented, showcasing the model's performance alongside manual reference points, indicating its effectiveness in identifying lesions.

The paper presents demographic variables and imaging protocols from seven centers, including internal cohorts from Hong Kong and external cohorts from Mainland China and Germany. The internal cohorts consist of three sets with varying sample sizes (Set-1: 7, Set-2: 65, Set-3: 3) and demographic breakdowns of gender and age. The external cohorts include four sets with sample sizes ranging from 2 to 35. Key findings indicate that the average ages of participants vary, with internal Set-2 being the oldest (47.7 years) and external Set-3 being the youngest (60.0 years).

Imaging protocols detail parameters such as axial resolution, slice thickness, and tube voltage, with most sets using a tube voltage of 120 kVp. Notably, the slice thickness varied across cohorts, with some using non-contrast imaging. The manufacturers of the imaging equipment also varied, including GE Medical Systems and Siemens.

Overall, the study emphasizes the diversity in demographic characteristics and imaging protocols across different cohorts, which is crucial for understanding the implications of these variations in clinical and research settings.

The paper introduces the Optimistic Actor Critic (OAC), a model-free deep reinforcement learning algorithm designed to enhance exploration by maximizing an approximate confidence bound on the state-action value function. By overcoming issues related to pessimistic under-exploration and directional uninformedness, OAC demonstrates superior sample efficiency in continuous control tasks. The findings indicate that leveraging optimism in uncertain environments can significantly improve the sample efficiency of policy gradient algorithms with minimal additional computational cost.

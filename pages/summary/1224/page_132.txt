The paper discusses the utility of correlation and regression analysis using SPSS for novice researchers, emphasizing its application in quantitative data. It establishes that correlation measures the degree of relationship between variables, while regression provides equations to describe these relationships. The study highlights the complexity of manual calculations for large datasets and advocates for software tools like SPSS for efficiency.

The Logistic Regression Model is specifically noted for its ability to predict the probability of a dichotomous dependent variable based on independent variables, with examples including health and employment statuses. The flexibility of Logistic Regression is underscored, as it imposes no assumptions about the relationships between variables.

The study acknowledges several limitations, including reliance on secondary data, limited literature, and insufficient knowledge of statistical tools, which affect the reliability and validity of the findings. Recommendations for future research include a focus on comprehensive literature reviews and primary research to enhance understanding of correlation and regression analysis, as well as detailed methodological guidance for novice researchers.

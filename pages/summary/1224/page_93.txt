The study conducted by Dr. Basanta Prasad Adhikari focuses on identifying the factors influencing customer satisfaction at Buddha Air in Bharatpur, Chitwan. Using a survey methodology, data was collected from 185 respondents, achieving a response rate of 92.5%. The analysis employed Principal Component Analysis to assess the relationship between customer satisfaction and various independent variables.

The findings reveal a significant association between customer satisfaction and several factors, including strict flight schedules, lengthy security checks, fluctuations in ticket prices, employee motivation and politeness, customer-centered strategies, and overall customer management skills. However, customers expressed dissatisfaction with ticket prices, service quality, employee behavior, and customer relationship management practices.

The study underscores the importance of effective customer management and high-quality service in enhancing customer satisfaction. It provides valuable insights for Buddha Air's management to develop new customer-centered strategies and improve managerial skills across its branches, ultimately aiming to penetrate new markets.

In summary, the research highlights the critical role that quality, pricing, and employee interaction play in customer satisfaction within the airline industry in Nepal, particularly for Buddha Air.

The second piece questions whether the FLIPPED model offers advantages over traditional flipped classrooms, sparking debate on instructional effectiveness.

Lastly, an experimental study on mobile learning illustrates its impact on student engagement and experience, particularly through augmented reality applications. 

Overall, the reviewed literature underscores the evolving landscape of educational technology and the need for continued exploration of innovative pedagogical strategies.

The study investigates factors influencing customer satisfaction for Buddha Air, focusing on service quality, employee behavior, customer management, and ticket pricing. Data was collected via a questionnaire distributed to 185 customers who had registered on Buddha Air's webpage two years prior. The questionnaire was sent through email, ensuring anonymity, with a reminder two weeks later.

Data analysis employed descriptive statistics and correlations, including principal component analysis to identify key components affecting customer satisfaction. The survey used a 5-point Likert scale to gauge customer perceptions regarding various service aspects. Key statistical tests, such as KMO and Bartlett’s Test, were conducted to validate sample adequacy, while reliability was assessed using Cronbach’s Alpha.

The analysis highlighted an increase in demand for air services and aimed to distill numerous variables into principal components that explain customer satisfaction. Findings were supported by exploratory factor analysis with Varimax rotation, and factor loadings of at least 0.40 were used to determine significant indicators of customer satisfaction. The study ultimately sought to better understand the relationship between customer satisfaction and personal demographic variables, utilizing descriptive statistics and cross-tabulations for a comprehensive view of customer motives.

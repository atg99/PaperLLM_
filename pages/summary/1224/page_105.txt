The regression analysis indicates that customer management has the most substantial positive impact on satisfaction, followed by service quality, employee behavior, and ticket pricing. The statistical significance of the model, with a p-value of 0.007, confirms that these factors collectively influence customer satisfaction levels.

The study utilized a survey methodology and achieved a high response rate of 92.5%, with a balanced representation of male and female respondents. The findings suggest that fluctuations in ticket prices and the quality of service, including employee interactions and management strategies, significantly affect customer satisfaction. This research provides valuable insights for stakeholders, including policymakers and business managers, to refine their strategies in pricing, service delivery, and customer relationship management to enhance overall customer satisfaction with Buddha Air.

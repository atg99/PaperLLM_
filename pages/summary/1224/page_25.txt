This paper investigates the impact of gamification and technology integration in educational settings, focusing on a selection of five prominent journals published between 2013 and 2017. The study aims to analyze the most cited articles within these journals, specifically identifying the top seven articles based on citation counts and publication years.

The research includes a thorough examination of 41 published articles from the selected journals: CE, CAL, BJET, IEEE TLT, and JETRD. The analysis is structured around citation counts and the relevance of topics within educational technology research. The findings are summarized in two tables, which outline the number of citations and publication years for the selected articles.

The results section provides a detailed breakdown of the seven most cited articles, highlighting their significance in the context of educational research. The paper concludes with a synthesis of key findings and implications for future research, emphasizing the evolving landscape of technology in education and the importance of gamification in enhancing learning experiences.

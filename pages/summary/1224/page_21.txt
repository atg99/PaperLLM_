The study focuses on the most cited articles from five selected journals within the educational technology domain between 2013 and 2017. A total of 41 articles were initially reviewed, with a subsequent selection of seven articles for deeper analysis. The findings highlight that the most cited article, "The gamifying learning experiences," received 801 citations and was published in 2013. Notably, three of the top articles originated from the Journal of Computer and Education, which emerged as the leading journal in this field during the reviewed years.

The research employed a mixed methods approach, incorporating both qualitative and quantitative analyses of empirical articles. The results indicate that 2013 was a pivotal year for published research, with the majority of the most cited articles appearing during that time. The implications of this study are significant for novice researchers, Master's students, and academics as it illuminates current trends and future directions in educational technology research. However, the study's limitation lies in its small sample size, which may affect the generalizability of the findings. 

In conclusion, this literature review serves as a valuable resource for understanding influential research within educational technology and encourages further exploration of the field's evolving landscape.

The paper from the OCEM Journal of Management, Technology & Social Sciences emphasizes the importance of concept, capability, and confidence in fostering high morale and professional integrity among engineering professionals. It highlights the role of Oxford College of Engineering and Management as a leading technical institution that bridges national requirements with the aspirations of young generations. The institution focuses on nurturing innovation and creativity, which are essential for nation-building, underpinned by ethical values and social service.

The document stresses the need for a research evidence-based learning environment that directly impacts infrastructure development through advanced technology and safety measures. To support this initiative, the OCEM research department has launched a peer-reviewed journal, which is anticipated to become a significant publication in the field.

The paper also addresses the urgent challenges facing engineering education today, such as the need for reform to prepare future engineers as innovative leaders in a dynamic technological landscape. It acknowledges issues like high attrition rates and the necessity for quality teaching and learning, advocating for multifaceted approaches to overcome these hurdles. The journal serves as a platform for faculty to enhance evidence-based teaching and presents global perspectives, particularly from Nepal.

Assoc. Prof. Bhim Bhandari, the BE Coordinator at Oxford College of Engineering and Management, expresses gratitude for the efforts of the research head in facilitating the publication of this journal, which aims to elevate the standards of engineering education and practice.

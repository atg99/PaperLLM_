The paper discusses the growing trend of home automation, focusing on its benefits such as enhanced comfort, efficient operation, reduced energy consumption, and improved quality of life, particularly for the elderly and disabled. Home automation systems, connected through a central hub or server, allow users to control household activities remotely via the Internet, utilizing devices like smartphones and laptops. 

The automation industry is undergoing significant advancements, leading to the development of Smart Homes and Smart Buildings. These innovations are aimed at improving consumer experiences and are supported by ongoing research to ensure compatibility with sustainable living projects. The market for home automation is projected to grow from USD 64.67 billion in 2017 to USD 116.26 billion by 2026, reflecting a compound annual growth rate of 6.8 percent. This growth is particularly notable in developing economies, including China, India, and Brazil, where rising disposable incomes and a preference for luxurious lifestyles drive adoption.

The research highlights consumer preferences for purchasing home automation products, with 55.6 percent favoring online stores. It notes a significant interest in products such as automatic water pumps and remote-controlled lights and fans, although the home automation market is still emerging in regions like Chitwan, where awareness exists but market penetration is limited. Overall, the paper emphasizes the potential for home automation to enhance security, energy management, and user comfort.

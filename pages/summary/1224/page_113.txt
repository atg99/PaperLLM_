The research aimed to determine the primary priorities of respondents regarding automation products, focusing on security, energy management, and comfort. The results indicated that 51.6% favored security as the main priority, while 17.7% preferred energy management and comfort. Additionally, 12.9% of respondents did not find any of these factors important.

Regarding the effectiveness of home automation for security, 49.2% of respondents agreed that these products enhance home security, with only 4% disagreeing. Notably, 28.2% strongly agreed on the workability of automation products in terms of security.

In terms of purchasing preferences, 55.6% of respondents preferred online stores for acquiring technology products over official retailers, while 10.5% chose not to answer this question. The data reflects a clear inclination toward online shopping in the competitive market.

The paper presents a study on the significance of various aspects of information quality and technology in rational strategic decision making (RSDM). It emphasizes the following key areas:

1. **Timeliness**: The timely provision of information is deemed crucial, with a loading of 0.754.
2. **Purity**: The sufficiency and quality of information are essential, with loadings of 0.896 and 0.843, respectively.
3. **Efficiency**: The frequency and time period of information are critical, showing high loadings of 0.963 and 0.945.
4. **Details**: Completeness and relevance are vital for effective RSDM, with loadings of 0.892 and 0.884.
5. **Quality**: Performance and scope of information are paramount, recording loadings of 0.965 and 0.961.
6. **Formats**: The presentation, detail, media, and order of information are all significant, with respective loadings ranging from 0.824 to 0.928.
7. **Perfectness**: Information comparability, clarity, and unambiguity are critical, with loadings of 0.929, 0.887, and 0.835.
8. **Advanced Technology**: Human resource skills, technology use, capacity, knowledge, and updates are highlighted, with loadings from 0.689 to 0.928.
9. **Performance of Technology**: Speed, durability, and availability of technology are essential, showing loadings of 0.948, 0.941, and 0.792.

The study found that the highest loading was 0.965 and the lowest was 0.728, with a total of 28 loadings and nine principal components identified. This underscores the multifaceted role of information and technology in enhancing decision-making processes.

This paper presents a review of significant literature and trends in educational technology, focusing on the integration of new technologies and pedagogical approaches to enhance learning outcomes. The authors highlight the importance of adapting educational methodologies to meet the demands of modern technology and sustainable development, particularly in the context of youth employability within the European Union.

Key studies referenced include Picatoste et al., who discuss the necessity for new educational patterns driven by technological advancements; Robins, who addresses ongoing challenges in computer science education; and Sun and Shen, who propose a teamwork-as-a-service model for mobile cloud-based learning. Other notable contributions include Sung and Hwang's research on game-based learning's impact on student performance in science and Tondeur et al.'s call for better preparation of future teachers in using educational technology.

The appendix presents a table listing the five most cited articles from various prominent journals related to educational technology, indicating the influence and relevance of these works in the field. The citations reflect ongoing research interests, such as gamification, collaborative learning, and the use of social tools in education, demonstrating a growing emphasis on innovative and interactive pedagogical strategies. Overall, the paper underscores the necessity for continuous evolution in educational practices to effectively leverage technology for improved learning experiences.

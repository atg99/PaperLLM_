The paper discusses the significant role of technology in enhancing teaching and learning activities. It emphasizes that the integration of computer technology into educational practices improves teachers' skills and classroom management, leading to better student outcomes. According to Onifade (2011), technology facilitates effective curriculum delivery, while Christensen et al. (2018) highlight its necessity for preparing educational leaders capable of guiding technology-enhanced learning. The impact of information and communication technology (ICT) on education is further supported by Gupta (2015), who notes its influence on teaching quality and research in both traditional and distance learning environments.

The research questions focus on analyzing citation counts and the characteristics of published articles in selected educational technology journals from 2013 to 2017. The main research question aims to identify the most cited articles among five selected journals, with three sub-questions addressing differences in article characteristics, citation trends over the years, and comparisons between the most cited articles across journals. The study utilizes the Publish and Perish Tool to derive its data, with the British Journal of Educational Technology (BJET) identified as a key resource for academic articles in digital education and training technology.

The paper discusses the fundamentals of multiple regression analysis, defining the relationship between a dependent variable (y) and multiple independent variables (x1, x2, ..., xk). It presents the regression model equation, emphasizing the roles of the intercept and regression coefficients. Key prerequisites for linear regression are outlined, including the necessity for a linear relationship among variables, absence of autocorrelation, zero mean of residuals, homoscedasticity, independence of independent variables from errors, and lack of multicollinearity.

Additionally, the paper introduces SPSS (Statistical Package for Social Sciences) as a user-friendly statistical software for data compilation and analysis. It highlights the challenges faced by novice researchers in effectively applying correlation and regression analysis in their work, often leading to misinterpretation of results. The study aims to enhance understanding of these statistical tools and their proper application in research.

The methodology involves analyzing secondary data from 423 maternity women at Chitwan Medical Sciences in Nepal, with data on infant ages and weights processed using SPSS. A review of various online resources was conducted to gather relevant research materials.

In the results section, the paper explains how to perform correlation analysis using SPSS, detailing the steps for selecting variables and choosing appropriate correlation methods such as Pearson’s, Kendall tau, or Spearman’s, based on data characteristics. The study emphasizes the importance of correctly interpreting statistical results to draw valid conclusions in research.

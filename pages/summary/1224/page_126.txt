The paper by Sarad Chandra Kafle focuses on the use of correlation and regression analysis through the Statistical Package for Social Science (SPSS) to aid novice researchers in quantitative studies. It addresses the common challenges faced by researchers who lack sufficient knowledge of these statistical tools, which can lead to improper application and interpretation of results. 

The paper explains that correlation measures the strength of the relationship between two variables, with values ranging from -1 to +1, where values closer to zero indicate a weak relationship and values closer to one indicate a strong relationship. Regression, on the other hand, provides a mathematical function to explain the relationship between variables. 

Kafle emphasizes the importance of understanding how to appropriately use these tools, analyze quantitative data, and interpret the results. The study utilizes secondary data to demonstrate the application of correlation and regression analysis, highlighting their relevance across various fields such as management, medicine, social science, and education. 

The findings underscore the necessity for novice researchers to acquire proper knowledge and skills for effective data analysis, with the aim of enhancing their understanding of correlation and regression analysis using SPSS. The implications of the study suggest a need for educational resources that support researchers in mastering these essential statistical tools. 

Keywords include statistical tools, test of significance, p-value, hypothesis, and dependent and independent variables.

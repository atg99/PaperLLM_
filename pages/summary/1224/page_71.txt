The paper discusses a quantitative research approach focused on understanding factors influencing students' intentions to recommend their college to others. It utilizes a survey research method to gather data from students, specifically those in grades 11 and 12. The research aims to explore several key areas: 

1. The impact of academic factors on students' likelihood to recommend their college.
2. The influence of managerial factors on these recommendations.
3. The effect of physical factors related to the college environment.
4. The role of infrastructure facilities in shaping students' recommendations.

The research design is structured to capture a large sample population, making it suitable for generalization. Data analysis employs descriptive statistics, principal component analysis, and chi-square tests to evaluate the reliability of the findings. The study is grounded in established literature, referencing works by Cohen, Manion, & Morrison (2011) and Tucker (2013) to support its framework. Overall, the research seeks to provide insights into how various factors contribute to students' intentions to recommend their college to friends and family.

The research outlined in this paper explores the market demand for home automation technology in Chitwan, Nepal, utilizing a systematic approach to data collection and analysis. Initially, an exploratory design was employed to gather preliminary insights about the market, followed by conclusive research to obtain precise data. A causal research design was used, involving a questionnaire distributed to 120 respondents selected from Bharatpur and surrounding areas.

The population of Chitwan is approximately 579,984, making it a significant sample base. Respondents were chosen using convenience, quota, and random sampling methods. The questionnaire was divided into three sections: consent, screening, and respondent field questions, focusing on the demographic details of participants and their interest in technology. The interviews lasted about 10-15 minutes, allowing researchers to gauge the willingness of various income groups, genders, and age demographics towards automation products.

The research findings indicate a strong interest in home automation technology among the targeted urban population, particularly among high-class and middle-class families. Positive feedback was received from both students at the Oxford College of Engineering and Management and individuals from local businesses such as hotels and restaurants. Data analysis included a visual representation of responses, highlighting that 96.8% of participants expressed interest in home automation products, while 2.4% did not. Overall, the study successfully identifies the demand for technology in the region, providing valuable insights for future market strategies.

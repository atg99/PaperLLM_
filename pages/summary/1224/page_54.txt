The study aimed to evaluate students' preferences regarding recommending Oxford College of Engineering and Management (OCEM) for further education. Utilizing a quantitative research approach and survey methodology, the research gathered insights from students on their willingness to recommend OCEM to others and their intent to continue their studies there. The research adhered to ethical standards and provided a detailed methodology to facilitate future studies. Data analysis was conducted using descriptive statistics, Independent Sample t-Test, Chi-Square Test, and Binary Logistic Regression, including Principal Component Analysis to explore relationships among variables.

The findings indicated a significant relationship between various factors—such as the quality of extracurricular activities, student development schedules, teaching environment, and physical facilities—and students' preferences to recommend OCEM. Notably, a strict student career development schedule positively influenced recommendations, as did the availability of physical resources. The study revealed gender-based differences in preferences, particularly regarding physical facilities, which further highlighted the importance of infrastructure in students' recommendations.

The implications of the findings suggest that the college administration could benefit from developing new admission strategies and revising student-centered policies to enhance the overall educational experience at OCEM.

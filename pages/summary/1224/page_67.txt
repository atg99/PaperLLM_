The paper discusses the relationship between gender and customers' intentions to maintain their buying habits at BBSM Bharatpur Chitwan. It presents a demographic breakdown of respondents by profession, highlighting that the majority are engaged in national service, business, self-employment, agriculture, foreign employment, and other sectors. The study emphasizes the significance of Customer Relationship Management (CRM), which integrates people, processes, and technology to enhance understanding of customers. It notes that successful CRM implementation can lead to increased customer loyalty and profitability, reflecting the evolution of CRM due to advancements in information technology and customer-centric organizational changes. 

The references cited provide a foundation for understanding various factors influencing customer satisfaction and retail performance, including studies on shopper characteristics, service quality in banking, and CRM applications. The appendix includes data on the accuracy of predictions regarding customers' intentions to recommend the service, indicating a significant overall percentage of correct predictions.

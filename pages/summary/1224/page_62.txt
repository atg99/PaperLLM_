The paper presents an analysis of customer satisfaction factors at BBSM Bharatpur, focusing on four main groups: Price Factor, Service Factor, Quality Factor, and Better Customer Management Factor. Each group is further divided into subgroups with corresponding statistical values, including variations, KMO, mean, standard deviation (SD), and Cronbach’s Alpha, indicating reliability. 

The findings reveal that the Service Factor, particularly the facilities and quality of products, demonstrates the highest variation, while the Price Factor shows the least variation. The mean value of the facilities in the waiting room was notably higher than 3.00, indicating customer satisfaction in that area. Conversely, mean values for product pricing, quality, and customer management were below 3.00, suggesting customers were neither satisfied nor dissatisfied with these aspects.

Furthermore, the paper explores the relationship between gender and customers' intentions to continue purchasing from BBSM, utilizing Chi-Square tests for analysis. Tables are provided to illustrate the factor loadings, subgroup variations, and Chi-Square results, contributing to a comprehensive understanding of customer perceptions and behaviors at BBSM Bharatpur.

This paper investigates the role of information in rational strategic decision-making among college executives and principals in Nepal. It highlights a significant gap in empirical research concerning the influence of information factors on decision-making processes within educational institutions. The study suggests that employing a larger sample size and a mixed-methods approach in future research could yield more comprehensive insights. It emphasizes the necessity for further exploration into how information technology affects strategic decision-making in Nepal's educational sector to address the limitations of the current study. The paper calls for a focused investigation into this area to enhance the understanding of decision-making dynamics in educational settings.

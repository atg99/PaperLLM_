The study utilized a multi-item scale to assess students' satisfaction and preferences, thereby minimizing measurement errors associated with single-item indicators. This approach allowed for a more nuanced understanding of the factors influencing students' recommendations of the college to others.

The analysis included descriptive statistics to summarize the demographic data and satisfaction levels, as well as inferential statistics to explore relationships between variables. The reliability of the survey instrument was confirmed through the computation of Cronbach's Alpha, ensuring the consistency of the data gathered.

In summary, the research employed a comprehensive survey methodology within OCEM Gaindakot-2, focusing on the BBA program. The sample consisted of students from various semesters, with a high response rate indicating robust participation. The study developed a tailored questionnaire based on existing literature to capture a wide range of factors affecting student satisfaction and their likelihood to recommend the college, ultimately aiming to provide insights into regional educational preferences.

The research employed a quantitative methodology, utilizing a survey to gather data from 237 respondents, resulting in a high response rate of 94.8%. The study identified key factors contributing to student satisfaction, including physical and administrative facilities, program quality, academic staff quality, college location, and institutional reputation. Reliability analysis confirmed the consistency of the data, while the Student t-Test revealed that female students reported higher satisfaction levels compared to their male counterparts.

Significant findings indicated that a strict student development schedule and the college's physical facilities positively influenced students' likelihood to recommend OCEM to others. Additionally, multiple regression analysis signaled a notable association between students' recommendations and the college's location.

The implications of these findings are vital for both public and private higher education institutions in addressing declining enrollment trends in the Chitwan and Nawalparasi Districts. The study suggests that policymakers should develop strategies and motivation policies that cater to student needs, ultimately fostering a more conducive learning environment and enhancing overall student satisfaction.

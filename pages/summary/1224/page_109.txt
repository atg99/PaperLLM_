The paper addresses the issue of outdated methods for comfort, security, and energy management in the 21st century, emphasizing the inefficiencies in energy usage across various sectors such as agriculture, healthcare, and residential areas. It highlights the need for a modern, cost-effective home automation system that integrates these functions to prevent unnecessary energy consumption. 

The market potential for home automation solutions is significant, particularly in Chitwan, which has a population of 579,984. This region, especially Bharatpur, serves as a central hub for commerce, education, and healthcare, making it an ideal target market for such technologies. The presence of numerous medical institutions, schools, hotels, and industries further supports the high demand for home automation products. The research aims to explore the feasibility of a comprehensive system that addresses the comfort, security, and energy management needs of consumers in this area.

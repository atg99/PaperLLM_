The OCEM Journal of Management, Technology & Social Sciences focuses on the intersection of management practices, technological advancements, and social science research. The journal aims to provide a platform for scholars and practitioners to share insights and findings related to innovative management strategies, the impact of technology on society, and the evolving dynamics within social sciences. It emphasizes the importance of interdisciplinary approaches and encourages contributions that explore the implications of technology on management and social structures. The journal serves as a valuable resource for understanding contemporary challenges and opportunities in these fields.

The paper examines the prevalence of internet usage and the acceptance of home automation products among respondents. It highlights that nearly all participants have access to Wi-Fi or similar internet services, indicating a strong foundation for technology adoption. A significant finding is that 76.6% of respondents own their homes, suggesting a potential market for automation products, as homeownership often correlates with interest in home improvements.

Trust in automation products is also noted, with 8.9% of respondents expressing skepticism about their reliability, while the majority demonstrate confidence in these technologies. Additionally, the paper presents data on respondents' priorities regarding automation systems, categorizing them into security, energy management, and comfort, with varying levels of importance assigned to each.

Overall, the findings suggest a favorable environment for the introduction and integration of home automation products, driven by widespread internet access and homeownership, alongside a general trust in technological solutions.

The paper presents results from a multiple regression analysis examining the impact of location on students' preference to recommend enrollment at OCEM. The analysis includes three locations: Eastern Chitwan, Western Chitwan, and Central Chitwan. 

The coefficient of multiple determination (R²) is found to be 0.082, indicating that approximately 8.20% of the variation in student preference is explained by the locations studied. Although the regression equation is statistically significant, as evidenced by an F-value of 7.132 and a p-value of 0.000, the low R² suggests that the model may not be particularly useful for making predictions.

ANOVA results confirm that at a significance level of α = 0.001, at least one of the predictors is useful for predicting students' preferences. 

The coefficients analysis reveals that both Eastern and Central Chitwan significantly predict students' preferences, with p-values of 0.007 and 0.001, respectively. In contrast, Western Chitwan does not show a significant relationship, with a p-value of 0.183, indicating it is not a useful predictor in this context. 

Overall, the findings suggest that Eastern and Central Chitwan are important predictors of students' preferences for recommending OCEM, while Western Chitwan does not contribute significantly to this predictive model.

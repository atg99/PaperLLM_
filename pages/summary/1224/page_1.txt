The OCEM Journal of Management, Technology & Social Sciences is a multidisciplinary peer-reviewed journal published by the Research Department of Oxford College of Engineering and Management in Gaindakot, Nawalpur. The journal is patronized by Professor Er. Hari Bhandari, the principal of the college, and features an advisory board comprising various academics, including Dr. Cha-Hsuan Liu from Utrecht University and several faculty members from the college.

The peer review board includes scholars from Pokhara University and other institutions in Nepal, ensuring a robust evaluation process for submitted articles. The inaugural volume, Issue 1, was published in December 2019, and the journal aims to contribute to the fields of management, technology, and social sciences. Further information can be found on the college's website.

The OCEM Journal of Management, Technology & Social Sciences features a diverse range of studies focusing on education, customer satisfaction, and technological impacts in Nepal. 

The first article discusses the consequences of international migration on girls under 16 in Chitwan, exploring implications for their education, health, and psycho-social development. Dr. Basanta Prasad Adhikari presents a literature review of highly cited articles in educational technology journals from 2013 to 2017, identifying key contributors in the field. 

Another study by Mr. Narayan Sapkota reviews the expectations versus reality of MBA programs, while Dr. Adhikari examines factors influencing student satisfaction at Oxford College of Engineering and Management and customer satisfaction in various sectors, including BBSM and Buddha Air. 

Professor Er. Hari Bhandari analyzes the role of information technology in strategic decision-making within educational institutions in Nepal. Additionally, Mr. Samir Raj Bhandari conducts a case study on the market potential for home automation and security products in Chitwan, and Mr. Ganga Prasad Sapkota investigates internet addiction among adolescents at OCEM. Lastly, Mr. Sarad Chandra Kafle provides insights into correlation and regression analysis using SPSS. 

Overall, the journal presents valuable research that contributes to understanding educational and customer dynamics in Nepal.

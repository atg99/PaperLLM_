The paper investigates customer satisfaction at Buddha Air, utilizing statistical methods to analyze various factors influencing satisfaction. A Chi-square Test was employed to explore the relationship between customer satisfaction and categorical variables such as gender, income level, profession, reasons for choosing Buddha Air, and religious affiliations. Following this, a Binary Logistic Regression Model assessed the impact of predictor and control variables on customer satisfaction motives, considering both significance levels and effect sizes based on Cohen's d cut-off points. A Wholesome Binary Logistic Regression Model further examined the associations between significant indicators and customer satisfaction.

In terms of quality factors, a survey instrument was used to gather respondents' experiences regarding various service aspects, including environmental cleanliness, noise pollution, seating comfort, payment facilities, and flight punctuality. A Principal Component Analysis identified three principal components (PCs): 

1. Proper Shopping Environment (with variables like comfortable seating and cleanliness),
2. Quality of Services (including payment acceptance and food quality), and 
3. Strict Flight Schedule and Security (covering punctuality and safety). 

The variances attributed to these components were 26.05%, 13.43%, and 10.80% respectively, indicating significant areas impacting customer satisfaction at Buddha Air.

This study investigates the factors influencing customer satisfaction at BhatBhateni Super Market (BBSM) in Bharatpur, Chitwan, focusing on price, service quality, time management in delivery, and customer management practices. Data were gathered through a structured questionnaire administered to 190 randomly selected customers, achieving an 87.5% response rate. The sample included 39.12% male and 60.88% female respondents.

Utilizing univariate analysis and multivariate statistical techniques such as the Chi-Square test, Factor Reduction Model, and Logistic Regression Analysis, the findings reveal a significant relationship between effective customer relationship management and customer satisfaction (p < 0.05, B = 0.438). Conversely, no significant association was found between customer-centered service facilities and the technology used by BBSM (p > 0.05). 

The results underscore the importance of customer management strategies for BBSM's executives and branch managers, suggesting that improvements in customer relationship management could enhance customer satisfaction. The implications of this research aim to guide the formulation of customer-centered strategies within the organization.

The study employed a survey method to gather data from 190 randomly selected customers of BBSW across various opening days and times. Reliability of the collected data was assessed using Cronbach's Alpha, and several statistical tests were performed, including a Chi-Square Test to examine gender-based preferences and Binary Logistic Regression Analysis to explore the relationship between customer satisfaction (dependent variable) and factors such as product pricing, employee behavior, discount rates, and technology utilization (independent variables). The target population consisted of 1000 customers, with a sample size representing 19% of this group. Out of 210 respondents contacted, 190 completed the questionnaire, yielding a response rate of 90.47%. The sample included 39.12% male and 60.88% female participants.

In the results section, factor analysis was conducted to classify variables and understand their interrelationships, focusing on Principal Component Model for data reduction. Key findings indicated that factors influencing customer satisfaction included product pricing, discount rates, employee behavior, technological use, and overall service quality. The analysis underscores the importance of customer relationship management (CRM) in enhancing business profitability and customer retention by meeting customer needs effectively.

The survey instrument was categorized into four groups, each containing Likert scale questions, and a Factor Reduction Model was applied to identify close relationships among variables within these groups. The factors loading results were used to organize the variables into distinct sub-scales, facilitating a deeper understanding of customer satisfaction drivers.

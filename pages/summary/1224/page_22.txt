The study focused on analyzing the most cited articles in educational technology from 2013 to 2017 across five prominent journals. Using content analysis, the research compared citation counts, publication years, titles, and relevant theories of these articles. It highlights the growing need for holistic research in educational technology to support learners and educators amidst global changes in political, economic, and demographic sectors.

The paper emphasizes the importance of integrating educational technology to enhance student achievements and educational quality. It discusses various aspects of educational technology, including social media, serious games, and adaptive software, while calling attention to emerging practices such as openness and user modeling. The significance of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) in education is underlined, as it is crucial for effective teaching and learning.

Furthermore, the paper asserts that the integration of technology in education can lead to improved student performance and engagement. It calls for educational leaders and practitioners to recognize the value of technology in creating meaningful learning experiences. The findings suggest that educational technology not only meets the demands of students but also enriches teaching practices, thereby transforming educational outcomes.

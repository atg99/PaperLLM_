The study published in the OCEM Journal of Management, Technology & Social Sciences investigates the factors influencing college students’ intentions to recommend their college, OCEM, to friends and family. The findings corroborate earlier research by Gajic (2011), which highlighted student satisfaction with the college's infrastructure. Utilizing the Chi-square Test, the study establishes a significant relationship between the students' geographical locations and their recommendation intentions.

The implications of this research are vital for OCEM’s administration, guiding the development of new policies and strategies. Additionally, it offers insights for similar institutions in Nawalpur and Chitwan District regarding student perceptions of private colleges.

Recommendations from the study emphasize the need for OCEM to enhance academic offerings, promote student-centered activities, and modernize teaching methods. Faculty management and support systems should be improved to foster a more supportive environment. The study also calls for a revision of the student security system and enhancements in lab and library facilities.

Future research is suggested to include a larger sample that encompasses both private and public colleges to ensure the generalizability of findings related to student satisfaction in Chitwan District.

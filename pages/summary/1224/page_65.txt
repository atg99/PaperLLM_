The study presented in the OCEM Journal of Management, Technology & Social Sciences examines the factors influencing customer choices at BBSM Bharatpur. Key findings indicate that product quality is the primary reason for customers selecting BBSM, accounting for 50% of responses. Customer management follows as the second most significant factor at 20%. Other reasons include various unspecified factors (12%), employee behavior (9%), and product cost (9%).

The demographic analysis of BBSM customers reveals that the largest group of shoppers consists of individuals engaged in national service (24%), followed closely by business professionals (23%). Self-employed individuals represent 20% of the customer base, while those in agriculture account for 18%. Foreign employment constitutes 12%, and other professions make up the remaining 3%. The data reflects a diverse customer profile, predominantly male (71) and female (101).

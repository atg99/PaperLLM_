This paper explores customer satisfaction in the context of BhatBhateni Super Market (BBSM) in Narayanagarh, Chitwan, Nepal, highlighting the relationship between customer perceptions of service, time management, and overall shopping experiences. The literature indicates that service quality is a significant factor influencing store support, particularly among specialty store shoppers who prioritize the attitude and treatment of sales associates. Research shows that customers of smaller grocery chains value service quality more than those of larger chains.

Time management during the checkout process is identified as another critical factor affecting customer satisfaction. With the rise of online shopping, customers have become increasingly impatient with traditional checkout methods. Studies have established a positive correlation between customer satisfaction and reduced waiting times at checkout.

Despite a high customer flow at BBSM, sales figures do not meet expectations, suggesting that many visitors may be there for leisure rather than shopping. This raises concerns about the store's ability to cultivate loyal customers. The primary objective of the study is to assess customer satisfaction regarding product cost, quality, customer management, and time management at BBSM. The central problem addressed is whether customers are satisfied with the services provided by BBSM in the Chitwan district, emphasizing the need for the store to understand and meet customer needs to foster loyalty.

The paper discusses five prominent journals in the field of educational technology, highlighting their contributions to the advancement of both formal and informal education. The OCEM Journal of Management, Technology & Social Sciences examines the impact of educational systems and tools on educational development. The Journal of Computer Education (JCE) focuses on enhancing teaching and learning through digital technology, significantly contributing to educational technology research worldwide. The Journal of Computer Assisted Learning (JCAL) emphasizes the use of computers to support education and interactive instructional techniques. The IEEE Transactions on Learning Technologies (TLT) explores the application of technology in improving educational outcomes, particularly through multimedia and communication systems. Finally, the Journal of Educational Technology Research and Development (JETRD) concentrates on research and development within educational technology, serving as a resource for professionals in the field.

The research method involves a comparison of the seven most cited articles from a selection of 41 highly cited articles published between 2013 and 2017 across these five journals. The study employs content analysis to examine these articles, presenting citation counts and publication years in a pie chart for clarity. The research design addresses various data analysis issues and aims to identify key characteristics of the highly cited articles, with further details provided in subsequent chapters.

The survey conducted among 120 respondents revealed a significant interest in technology products, with 96.8% expressing a preference for these products over traditional alternatives. This finding aligns with the research's objective, highlighting a trend towards technological adoption.

The respondents' salary levels indicate a targeted demographic, as only those earning above 60-90 thousand Nepalese Rupees were included in the survey. The results showed that 7.4% of respondents earned between 60-90 thousand, while 27.3% earned above 90 thousand, suggesting a strong inclination among higher-income individuals towards technology products.

Furthermore, the survey explored the correlation between education level and technology product usage. Only 18.5% of respondents with an SLC education showed interest, indicating potential for increased adoption in this demographic. 

Lastly, data on Wi-Fi connectivity revealed that a substantial majority, 98.4%, had Wi-Fi access at home, further supporting the trend of technological engagement among respondents.

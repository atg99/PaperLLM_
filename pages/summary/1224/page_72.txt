This study employs a quantitative research approach utilizing survey methodology to explore the experiences and opinions of grade 11 and 12 students regarding various facilities at Oxford College of Engineering and Management (OCEM) in Gaindakot, Nawalpur, Nepal. A five-point Likert scale questionnaire was distributed to 215 students, achieving a response rate of 94.22% with 204 completed surveys. The reliability of the data was confirmed through Cronbach’s Alpha, yielding a value of 0.70. 

Data analysis involved descriptive statistics and Binary Logistic Regression Models to assess the relationships between independent and dependent variables. The analysis focused on four sub-problems related to students' perceptions of academic, managerial, service, and infrastructure facilities impacting their likelihood to recommend the college to others. The analysis used Principal Component Analysis to reduce variables, alongside Chi-Square Tests and Student T-tests to evaluate gender associations with recommendations.

Key findings indicate that academic factors, such as practical skills delivery, student-centered activities, and innovative teaching methodologies, significantly influence students' recommendations. Managerial factors, including faculty support and effective management of teaching resources, also play a crucial role. Overall, the study underscores the importance of both academic and managerial support in shaping students' intentions to recommend OCEM to prospective enrollees.

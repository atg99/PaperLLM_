The OCEM Journal of Management, Technology & Social Sciences, Volume 6, presents a collection of research articles that explore various intersections of management practices, technological advancements, and social science perspectives. The journal emphasizes the importance of integrating these fields to address contemporary challenges in organizations and society. 

Key themes include the impact of technology on management strategies, the role of social sciences in understanding consumer behavior, and innovative approaches to enhance organizational performance. Each article contributes to a deeper understanding of how technology and social dynamics shape managerial decisions and societal outcomes. 

The journal aims to foster interdisciplinary dialogue and provide insights that can inform both academic research and practical applications in the field. Overall, Volume 6 serves as a valuable resource for scholars and practitioners seeking to navigate the complexities of modern management in a technology-driven world.

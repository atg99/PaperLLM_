The study conducted by Professor Er. Hari Bhandari aims to investigate the impact of information technology on rational strategic decision making (RSDM) in educational institutions in Nepal. It builds on previous research that identified key factors such as time content, information form, and technology as influential in effective decision making.

Employing a quantitative research method, the study utilized a structured questionnaire to gather data from 24 colleges (14 private and 10 public) with 24 respondents selected randomly. The demographic analysis revealed a higher proportion of male respondents (79.20%) compared to females (20.8%), and a majority of respondents were from private colleges (58.33%).

Findings indicated that the importance of information factors for RSDM was rated below average, suggesting a lack of emphasis on these elements. There was no significant correlation found between various information quality metrics and RSDM (p > 0.05). Notably, private colleges reported a significantly higher mean score for the purity of information compared to public colleges, while the quality of information was rated lower in private institutions.

The study underscores the implications for college executives and principals to better understand the role of information in RSDM. However, it acknowledges a limitation due to the small sample size, which may have influenced the results of the Binary Logistic Regression Analysis. 

The research highlights the need for educational institutions to leverage information technology effectively to enhance decision-making processes.

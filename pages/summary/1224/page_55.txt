The paper acknowledges the contributions of Er. Hari Bhandari, Ms. Rabina Lamichhane Magar, Shashikala Sapkota, and Sushmita Chaudhary in conducting the study. The references provide a comprehensive overview of various research works related to student satisfaction in higher education. Key studies highlighted include the influence of university image on student behavior, the impact of teaching practices on student satisfaction, and factors affecting student retention. The references also explore the relationship between perceived service quality and student satisfaction, highlighting the importance of university facilities and the satisfaction of students with their educational environment. This collection of literature underlines the multifaceted nature of student satisfaction, emphasizing the need for continuous assessment and improvement in higher education institutions.

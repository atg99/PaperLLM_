The paper discusses the importance of research activities in enhancing education at the Oxford College of Engineering and Management (OCEM). It highlights the college's academic excellence as evidenced by its results in Pokhara University examinations, while acknowledging a previous gap in research journal publications. The launch of the OCEM journal marks a significant achievement for the college, its faculty, and students, fulfilling this gap. The author emphasizes that success in various fields, including business, science, and technology, cannot overlook the value of research. The paper concludes with gratitude towards key individuals and the team involved in bringing the journal to fruition, reflecting a commitment to advancing academic performance through research.

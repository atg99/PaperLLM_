The study examines the relationship between existing students' location at OCEM and their intention to recommend the institution to others. The analysis reveals a significant association, indicated by a Chi-Square value of 10.273 with a significance level of 0.036, rejecting the null hypothesis. This suggests that the location of students influences their recommendation behavior.

Further, a Binary Logistic Regression Model was employed to identify significant factors affecting students' intention to recommend OCEM. Three independent variables were initially considered, but only two emerged as significant. The model's equation is represented as logit(P) = b0 + b1x1 + b2x2 + ... + bnxn, where p represents the odds ratio. 

The results show that 'strict student-centered activities' and 'weak college infrastructure facilities' significantly affect recommendation intentions, with respective odds ratios of 0.710 and 0.672. The model demonstrated a better fit than the base model, as indicated by the positive Chi-Square value of 16.712, with a Nagelkerke R2 of 11%. The Hosmer and Lemeshow Test further validated the model fit, with a p-value of 0.110, signifying insignificance and supporting the regression model's robustness.

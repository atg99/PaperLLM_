The paper outlines a survey aimed at understanding consumer attitudes towards home automation products and their demographic characteristics. The survey is divided into two sections, with the first focusing on demographic information such as home ownership, education status, and Wi-Fi availability, which is crucial for the internet-based product being studied.

The second section of the survey includes questions designed to gauge trust in home automation, preferred methods of installation, and potential demand for these products in the future. Respondents are also asked about their likelihood of purchasing automation products and their perceptions of security and necessity regarding specific items like automatic water pumps.

A code book is utilized to document the layout and definitions of the data file, facilitating easier data analysis. The authors emphasize the importance of behavioral categorization in analyzing responses, given the complexities of demographic classification. The questionnaires were refined through consultations to ensure the collection of actionable data. The analysis employs frequency distribution and visual techniques, alongside statistical regression, to explore the relationships between dependent and independent variables. The overall goal is to extract useful insights from the survey data regarding consumer behavior towards home automation technologies.

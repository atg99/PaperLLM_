The paper references several studies that explore factors influencing student satisfaction in higher education. Vogt's work on quantitative research methods provides a foundational framework for analyzing such factors. Weerasinghe and Fernando (2018) identify critical elements affecting student satisfaction in Sri Lanka, while Yusoff et al. (2015) examine dimensions that drive business student satisfaction.

In the appendix, data is presented regarding the intention to recommend a program. The observed results indicate that 91.5% of those who intend to recommend the program were correctly predicted, while only 35.2% of those who do not intend to recommend were accurately identified. The overall accuracy of predictions stands at 71.5%. This data highlights the effectiveness of the predictive model used in assessing student satisfaction and their likelihood to recommend the educational program.

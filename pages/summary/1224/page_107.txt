Methodology  
The research utilized a quantitative approach, conducted in two phases. First, collective interviews were held with guardians of students, followed by questionnaire distribution to students with guidance on completion. Field visits to various institutions, banks, homes, hotels, and industries were also conducted in 2018. The sampling methods included random, quota, and convenience sampling.

Findings  
The study revealed that approximately 78.2% of families had Wi-Fi connections, with a majority being male respondents. Of the 124 participants, 48.4% were graduate students, and 96.8% expressed interest in technology products. Notably, 60.2% of respondents were aged between 30-50 years, and 90.3% of families had more than three members. Approximately 27.3% of respondents reported a monthly income exceeding 90,000 Nepalese currency. Security was identified as the primary concern by 51.7% of respondents, while only 17.7% prioritized energy management and comfort. The study also highlighted that 82.3% of participants were familiar with home automation and 89.5% trusted these products, with 84.7% showing interest in acquiring them.

Conclusion  
The findings underscore the strong market potential for home automation and security products in Chitwan, emphasizing their role in enhancing safety and convenience. This research serves to inform urban residents about the benefits and applications of automation technologies. However, the study acknowledges limitations regarding the gender distribution of the sample population.

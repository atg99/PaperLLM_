The study aimed to evaluate customer satisfaction at BBSM Bharatpur Chitwan in Nepal, utilizing a quantitative research methodology through a survey. The sample consisted of 190 respondents, representing 19% of a target population of 1000, with a response rate of 84.88%. The gender distribution was 39.12% male and 60.88% female. 

Key findings revealed that the majority of customers had a monthly income ranging from NRS 21,000 to NRS 30,000, with the least percentage earning above NRS 30,000. A statistically significant positive relationship was established between effective customer management and customer satisfaction, with a coefficient indicating that for each unit increase in customer management practices, customer satisfaction is predicted to increase by approximately 0.438 times. 

These results align with previous research by ROH, AHN & HAN, which highlighted the importance of CRM initiatives in enhancing customer satisfaction and profitability. The primary reason for customers choosing BBSM was product quality. However, no significant relationship was found between customer-centered services, facilities, or technology used by BBSM and customer satisfaction. Overall, the study emphasizes the importance of customer management in enhancing satisfaction while noting that product quality remains a crucial factor for customer loyalty.

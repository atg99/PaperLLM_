The paper discusses customer satisfaction at Buddha Air, focusing on various aspects of service quality and management. The study identifies key areas impacting customer experience through a Principal Component Analysis (PCA), which extracted four principal components (PCs): facilities and customer management, facilities to buy tickets, facilities for ticket postponement and cancellation, and the use of advanced technology for customer management. The first PC accounts for 40.45% of the variance, indicating its significance.

Mean values from customer surveys reveal dissatisfaction with the facilities to buy tickets and the postponement and cancellation options, with scores of 2.49, 2.85, and 2.59 respectively. Conversely, the use of advanced technology for customer management received a higher mean score of 3.27, suggesting customer satisfaction in this area.

A binary logistic regression analysis indicates that facilities and customer management significantly impact customer satisfaction, with a p-value of 0.043 and an odds ratio of 2.014. The facilities to buy tickets showed no significant effect, while the facilities for ticket postponement and cancellation approached significance (p=0.066). The use of advanced technology did not significantly affect customer satisfaction.

Overall, findings suggest that while customers appreciate the technological aspects of Buddha Air's service, there are notable areas requiring improvement in ticketing and cancellation facilities to enhance overall customer satisfaction.

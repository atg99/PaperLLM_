The study presented in the OCEM Journal of Management, Technology & Social Sciences offers valuable insights for airline leaders in Bharatpur Chitwan on enhancing customer satisfaction. The findings indicate that while customer management does not initially buffer satisfaction levels, it significantly influences satisfaction changes over time. Concerns about the sampling method and the gender representation in the study were noted, which could affect the generalizability of the results. 

The research highlights that the variability in airline services, ticket pricing, service quality, and employee behavior plays a crucial role in customer satisfaction. It emphasizes that understanding customer needs and preferences is essential for airlines to develop effective customer-centered strategies. The study reinforces the importance of customer satisfaction as a fundamental business objective, linking it to future purchasing intentions. 

The growing airline industry presents both opportunities, due to increased demand for services, and challenges, stemming from intense competition and heightened customer expectations for service quality. The conclusions drawn from this research are intended to assist airlines in navigating these dynamics effectively.

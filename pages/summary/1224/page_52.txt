The study presented in the OCEM Journal of Management, Technology & Social Sciences employs Binary Logistic Regression to analyze factors influencing students' preferences to recommend OCEM to their peers. The analysis includes 237 participants and identifies several independent variables with varying significance levels.

Key findings indicate that a strict student development schedule has a significant positive impact (p < 0.05, odds ratio 1.625, B = 0.486), suggesting that structured schedules support students' career paths and learning environments. Additionally, physical facilities also show a strong positive correlation (p < 0.05, odds ratio 2.822, B = 1.038), highlighting the importance of resources like library books, transportation, and laboratory facilities.

The model demonstrates a good fit, as indicated by an Omnibus Test result (Chi-Square = 50.404, p = 0.001), and explains 19.20% and 30.20% of the variance in preferences according to Cox and Snell and Nagelkerke R2 values, respectively. The model's predictive accuracy is notable, with an overall correctness rate of 83.9%, achieving 96.3% accuracy for students intending to recommend OCEM.

In summary, the study concludes that both the strict development schedule and the quality of physical facilities significantly influence students' likelihood to recommend OCEM, reinforcing the importance of these factors in enhancing student satisfaction and engagement.

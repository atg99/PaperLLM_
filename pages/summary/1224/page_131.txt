In summary, the paper presents a correlation and regression analysis to examine the relationship between the age and height of respondents. The correlation analysis shows a high positive correlation coefficient of 0.853, with a significance level of 0.000, suggesting a strong connection between the two variables. The regression analysis indicates that 72.8% of the variation in height can be explained by age, as shown by an R square value of 0.728. The ANOVA results confirm the model's significance with a p-value of 0.000. The regression equation derived from the analysis is Y = 59.35 + 0.811X, where the coefficient of age indicates that for every month of age, a child's height increases by 0.811 centimeters.

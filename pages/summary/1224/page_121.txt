examines the relationship between internet addiction and socio-demographic variables among adolescents and young adults. The increasing prevalence of problematic internet use (PIU) raises concerns due to its association with negative health outcomes such as depression and low academic performance. Data collected and analyzed using SPSS indicated significant associations between internet addiction and variables like sex, educational faculty, and education level, with male users and those in science faculties showing higher addiction scores. The study confirms previous research linking internet use to academic performance, emphasizing the need for awareness and intervention strategies to address internet addiction in youth.

The study conducted at OCEM evaluated various aspects of student satisfaction across multiple subscales. The findings revealed a general trend of disagreement among students regarding several key areas. 

1. **Faculty Support for Quality**: Students expressed dissatisfaction with the overall support from coordinators and faculty, indicating a lack of timely problem resolution and motivation for academic success, with a mean score of 2.13.

2. **Technological Issues**: A mean score of 2.29 reflected that students felt classrooms lacked adequate technology and seasonal comfort.

3. **Physical Facilities**: The mean score of 2.32 indicated disagreement with the quality of facilities, including hygiene in the canteen, availability of study materials, and support from lab facilitators.

4. **Emphasis on Punctuality**: With a score of 2.35, students disagreed with the faculty's time management and their ability to foster a positive learning environment.

5. **Health and Safety Issues**: A mean of 2.43 showed students were uncertain about the sufficiency of restrooms and the availability of safe drinking water.

6. **Use of Technology in Teaching**: The score of 2.47 indicated some dissatisfaction with the cooperation of lecturers and the integration of modern technology in teaching.

7. **Extracurricular Activities**: A mean score of 2.58 suggested neutrality regarding the sufficiency and compulsory nature of extracurricular activities.

8. **Strict Roles of Principal**: Students were undecided about the principal's effectiveness in managerial decisions, reflected in a mean of 2.81.

9. **Career Development Schedule**: A score of 2.92 indicated uncertainty about the alignment of internal exams with schedules and overall career guidance.

10. **Teaching Resources**: With a mean score of 3.12, students were mostly undecided but somewhat agreed on the availability of computers and library resources.

11. **Canteen Services**: Finally, a mean score of 3.33 indicated general agreement on the reasonable pricing and satisfactory service of the canteen.

Overall, the assessment highlighted significant areas for improvement in student support, facilities, and technology at OCEM, with students expressing a mix of dissatisfaction and uncertainty across various dimensions of their educational experience.

The survey conducted reveals a significant interest in automation products among respondents in Chitwan district, Nepal. A notable 95.2% of participants expressed a willingness to recommend automation products to others, indicating a strong positive perception of these technologies. Furthermore, 98.4% of respondents foresee a demand for automation in the future, suggesting that while they do not view it as an immediate necessity, they recognize its potential importance going forward.

The findings indicate a correlation between respondents' salary levels and their inclination towards adopting automation for comfort and energy management. As salaries increase, the interest in automation also rises. However, for those earning between 60,000 and 90,000, security remains a paramount concern, influencing their decisions regarding automation.

Factors such as home ownership, Wi-Fi connectivity, and educational qualifications were also found to impact the likelihood of selecting automation technologies. Despite the clear interest and potential for automation in Chitwan, many respondents currently do not see it as a viable option, suggesting that the research highlights future possibilities rather than immediate applications.

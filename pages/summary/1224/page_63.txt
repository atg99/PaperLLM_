The paper presents an analysis of the relationship between gender and students' intention to continue purchasing at BBSM, as well as the factors influencing customer satisfaction through Binary Logistic Regression.

In Table 3, the data shows that 55% of male customers (44 out of 80) intend to continue buying, while 70% of female customers (77 out of 110) plan to do the same. This indicates a notable association between gender and purchasing intentions.

Table 4 details the results of the Chi-Square test, revealing a Chi-Square value of 4.506 with a p-value of 0.034, which is less than the 0.05 significance level. This leads to the rejection of the null hypothesis, confirming an association between gender and the intention to continue buying at BBSM.

Additionally, the study employs a Wholesome Binary Logistic Regression Model to explore customer satisfaction, focusing on the quality of products, service quality, and employee behavior. Out of twelve independent variables assessed, seven were deemed significant in influencing customer satisfaction, which were included in the final model. 

Overall, the findings suggest a significant relationship between gender and purchasing intentions, as well as key factors contributing to customer satisfaction at BBSM.

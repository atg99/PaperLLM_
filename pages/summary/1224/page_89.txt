The paper discusses the significance of various aspects of information for Rational Strategic Decision Making (RSDM) in educational institutions, focusing on the differences between private and public colleges. Key findings indicate that the format, presentation, and clarity of information are essential for RSDM. However, respondents expressed disagreement regarding the sufficiency of advanced technology and human resource skills in facilitating RSDM.

An independent sample t-Test was conducted to evaluate the differences in the impact of information on RSDM between private and public colleges. The null hypothesis (H0) posited no significant difference, while the alternative hypothesis (H1) suggested a significant difference. Results showed no significant statistical difference in the mean scores for most subscales, including information, efficiency, and details across both college types. However, a significant difference was found in the quality of information, with private colleges valuing it less than public colleges.

Overall, the paper highlights the critical role of information characteristics in RSDM and underscores the discrepancies in perceptions and importance placed on these characteristics between private and public educational institutions.

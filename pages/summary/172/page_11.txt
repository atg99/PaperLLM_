3.4.2 Fréchet Inception Distance (FID)
Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) is another important metric for assessing GAN performance. It measures the distance between the feature distributions of real and generated images. A lower FID score indicates that the generated images are more similar to the real images, thus reflecting better quality and variety in the generated samples.

3.4.3 Other Metrics
Additional metrics used to evaluate GANs include Precision and Recall, which assess the quality and diversity of generated samples, and Visual Turing Test, where human evaluators judge the realism of the generated images.

In summary, this paper discusses various architectures and learning strategies for Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), including BiGAN, AGE, D2GAN, MGAN, and CoGAN, highlighting their unique structures and objectives. It emphasizes the importance of addressing challenges like mode collapse and improving sample fidelity through innovative designs. Evaluation metrics such as Inception Score and Fréchet Inception Distance are also elaborated, underscoring their significance in measuring the success of GANs in generating high-quality, diverse outputs.

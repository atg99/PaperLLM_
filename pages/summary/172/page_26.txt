The references provided encompass a variety of studies and advancements in the field of generative adversarial networks (GANs) and their applications across different domains. The works cited include:

1. **Video Retargeting**: Bansal et al. (2018) present a GAN-based approach for unsupervised video retargeting.
2. **Semantic Manipulation**: Liang et al. (2018) introduce a method for generative semantic manipulation using mask-contrasting GANs.
3. **Visual Attribute Transfer**: Kim et al. (2017) explore unsupervised visual attribute transfer through reconfigurable GANs.
4. **Image Cartoonization**: Chen et al. (2018) focus on photo cartoonization using GANs.
5. **Motion Retargeting**: Villegas et al. (2018) develop neural kinematic networks for unsupervised motion retargeting.
6. **Image Blending**: Wu et al. (2019) propose GP-GAN for realistic high-resolution image blending.
7. **Semantic Segmentation**: Souly et al. (2017) utilize GANs for semi-supervised semantic segmentation.
8. **Visual Tracking**: Song et al. (2018) and Han et al. (2019) investigate adversarial learning approaches for robust visual tracking.
9. **Image Dehazing**: Engin et al. (2018) and Yang et al. (2018) enhance image dehazing techniques using cycle-consistent GANs.
10. **Image Inpainting**: Yeh et al. (2017) and Yu et al. (2018) apply GANs for semantic image inpainting.

Additional works explore GAN applications in audio synthesis, time series generation, medical data, dialogue generation, text generation, and even poetry generation from images. Each reference contributes to the evolving landscape of GAN research, showcasing the versatility and potential of these models in various fields of artificial intelligence.

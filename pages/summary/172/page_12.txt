The paper also discusses various evaluation metrics for GANs, emphasizing the challenges in selecting appropriate metrics that can effectively differentiate between real and fake samples, while also addressing issues such as mode collapse and overfitting. Metrics like Inception Score (IS), Mode Score (MS), Fréchet Inception Distance (FID), and Multi-scale Structural Similarity (MS-SSIM) are explored. Each metric has its advantages and limitations, indicating the ongoing need for improved methods to assess GAN performance.

In summary, the paper highlights the evolution of GANs in diverse applications, particularly in semi-supervised learning, while underscoring the complexity of evaluating their effectiveness through various metrics. Future advancements are anticipated to enhance both the quality of GAN models and the robustness of evaluation techniques.

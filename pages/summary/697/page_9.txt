The experiments conducted assess the performance of various algorithms in navigating these network scenarios, focusing on benchmark testing and scalability across different sizes of networks. The NASim platform provides a standardized environment for these tests, utilizing a specific configuration that includes a mix of internal networks and a honeypot to increase complexity. The agent's goal is to gain root access to sensitive hosts through a series of actions including scanning, exploiting vulnerabilities, and privilege escalation. 

The reward system for the agent is structured to incentivize targeting the most valuable hosts while minimizing operational costs, which are quantified in terms of time, skills, and financial resources. The success probabilities for different operations are factored into the outcomes, allowing for a realistic simulation of the uncertainties faced in real-world penetration testing scenarios. 

Overall, this experimental setup aims to provide insights into the efficacy of various algorithms in autonomous pentesting, contributing to advancements in the field of network security.

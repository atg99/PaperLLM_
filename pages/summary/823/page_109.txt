The compilation includes significant contributions to the field of sparsity in deep learning, focusing on various techniques and methodologies for optimizing neural networks. 

1. **PruneTrain** by Lym et al. introduces dynamic sparse model reconfiguration to enhance neural network training speed.
2. **Adversarial Neural Pruning** by Madaan et al. addresses latent vulnerabilities during the pruning process, emphasizing the importance of robustness.
3. **Concrete Distribution** by Maddison et al. presents a continuous relaxation approach for discrete random variables, aiding in model training.
4. **Winner-Take-All Autoencoders** by Makhzani and Frey proposes a novel autoencoder design that enhances representation learning through competitive mechanisms.
5. **Lottery Ticket Hypothesis** by Malach et al. provides theoretical insights into pruning, asserting that effective subnetworks can be identified within larger networks.
6. **Sparse Attention** by Malaviya et al. explores constrained attention mechanisms for improving neural machine translation.
7. **PackNet** by Mallya and Lazebnik details a method for adding multiple tasks to a single network via iterative pruning, promoting efficiency in multi-task learning.
8. **Automated Pruning** by Manessi et al. discusses techniques for deep neural network compression through automated pruning strategies.
9. **Regularity of Sparse Structures** by Mao et al. investigates the structural properties of sparsity in convolutional neural networks.
10. **Diversity Networks** by Mariet and Sra employs determinantal point processes for neural network compression, enhancing diversity in learned representations.
11. **Kronecker-factored Approximate Curvature** by Martens and Grosse offers a method for optimizing neural networks by approximating curvature.
12. **Sparsemax** by Martins and Astudillo presents a sparse alternative to softmax, facilitating attention and multi-label classification tasks.

Overall, these studies collectively advance the understanding and application of sparsity in deep learning, leading to more efficient and robust neural network designs.

The paper discusses the concept of sparsity in deep learning, highlighting various techniques and methodologies that leverage this property to enhance the efficiency of deep neural networks. 

Key contributions include:

1. **Activation Sparsity**: Rhu et al. (2018) explore compressing DMA engines by utilizing activation sparsity during the training of deep neural networks, which can lead to improved performance and reduced resource consumption.

2. **BERT Understanding**: Rogers et al. (2021) provide insights into BERT, focusing on its architecture and functionality, which is crucial for understanding its performance in various natural language processing tasks.

3. **Adaptive Function Selection**: Rosenbaum et al. (2017) introduce routing networks that adaptively select non-linear functions for multi-task learning, enhancing model flexibility and efficiency.

4. **Low-Rank Matrix Factorization**: Sainath et al. (2013) apply low-rank matrix factorization techniques to train deep neural networks with high-dimensional outputs, showing the potential for improved computational efficiency.

5. **Movement Pruning**: Sanh et al. (2020) propose movement pruning, a method for achieving adaptive sparsity through fine-tuning, allowing models to maintain performance while reducing complexity.

6. **Lottery Ticket Hypothesis**: Savarese et al. (2020) investigate the concept of winning the lottery with continuous sparsification, suggesting that certain subnetworks can be trained in isolation for optimal performance.

7. **Group Sparse Regularization**: Scardapane et al. (2017) examine group sparse regularization as a technique to promote sparsity in deep networks, which can lead to simpler models with fewer parameters.

8. **Efficient Sparse-Dense Operations**: Scheffer et al. (2020) present an architecture designed for efficient sparse-dense linear algebra operations, crucial for optimizing computational resources in neural networks.

9. **Pruning in Neural Machine Translation**: See et al. (2016) address the compression of neural machine translation models through pruning, which significantly reduces model size while preserving accuracy.

10. **Adversarial Robustness and Pruning**: Sehwag et al. (2020) explore pruning techniques aimed at enhancing the robustness of neural networks against adversarial attacks, contributing to the field of secure AI.

11. **Stochastic Gradient Descent**: Seide et al. (2014) discuss the application of 1-bit stochastic gradient descent in distributed training frameworks, highlighting its effectiveness for speech deep neural networks.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the importance of sparsity in optimizing deep learning models, improving their efficiency, and enabling scalable applications across various domains.

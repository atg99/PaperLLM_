The paper discusses various approaches and advancements in the field of sparsity in deep learning, particularly focusing on model compression and acceleration techniques. Key contributions include:

1. **Dynamic Channel Pruning**: Jianda Chen et al. propose a method for storage-efficient and dynamic runtime channel pruning using deep reinforcement learning, which optimizes model performance while reducing resource consumption.

2. **Lottery Ticket Hypothesis**: Tianlong Chen et al. explore the Lottery Ticket Hypothesis in the context of pre-trained BERT networks, highlighting the potential for identifying and utilizing sparse sub-networks that retain performance.

3. **Energy-Efficient Accelerators**: Yu-Hsin Chen and colleagues present Eyeriss and its subsequent version, Eyeriss v2, which are reconfigurable accelerators designed to efficiently handle deep convolutional neural networks, particularly for mobile applications.

4. **Model Compression Surveys**: Several surveys, including those by Yu Cheng et al. and Tejalal Choudhary et al., provide comprehensive overviews of model compression techniques and their implications for deep neural network acceleration.

5. **Sparse Transformers**: Rewon Child et al. introduce methods for generating long sequences with sparse transformers, emphasizing the importance of sparsity in managing computational resources.

6. **Extremely Sparse Pruned Networks**: Minsu Cho et al. present ESPN, focusing on the benefits of extremely sparse networks in enhancing model efficiency.

7. **Gradient Norm Techniques**: Joseph Paul Cohen et al. and Maxwell D. Collins et al. discuss techniques for optimizing convolutional filters and managing memory constraints in deep networks.

The collective research highlights the trend towards more efficient deep learning models through sparsity, which is pivotal for deploying neural networks in resource-constrained environments while maintaining performance.

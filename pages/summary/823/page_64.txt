In the study by T. Hoefler et al., the authors explore advanced techniques for image classification using Neural Architecture Search (NAS) to develop the EfficientNet-B0 network. They introduce a compound method that effectively scales the network while maintaining parameter efficiency. 

The paper discusses various CNN sparsification techniques, categorized by their training schemes. It highlights the contrast between post-training and during-training sparsification approaches. 

Post-training sparsification techniques, such as those proposed by Han et al., demonstrate significant weight reduction in established models like AlexNet and VGG-16 without sacrificing accuracy. Their results showed a reduction of more than 90% in weights through careful layer adjustments, emphasizing that convolutional layers should be sparsified less than fully connected layers. Sun et al. further support these findings, indicating that fully-sparse training cannot achieve the same performance as a dense model that undergoes subsequent sparsification.

In contrast, during-training sparsification approaches advocate for concurrent training of sparse and dense networks. Techniques like dropout and sensitivity-driven pruning have been employed to achieve high sparsity with minimal accuracy loss. Zhou et al. reported pruning significant percentages of parameters in VGG-13 and AlexNet while maintaining relatively low degradation in accuracy. Molchanov et al. achieved record sparsity levels in smaller networks, demonstrating that training can still converge efficiently despite increased operational demands.

Guo et al. propose a novel strategy where weights that are pruned can be reintroduced if they reach a certain magnitude, allowing for improved performance throughout the training process. Their method illustrates the potential for enhanced results in sparsification techniques by dynamically adjusting the pruning strategy over time. 

Overall, the paper provides a comprehensive overview of the challenges and methodologies associated with sparsifying modern deep neural networks, underscoring the importance of balancing model efficiency and accuracy.

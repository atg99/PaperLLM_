The referenced works focus on advancements in neural network architectures and optimization techniques, particularly in the context of efficiency and sparsity. T. Hoefler et al. introduce the Explicit Sparse Transformer, which enhances attention mechanisms through explicit selection, aiming to improve computational efficiency. Zhao et al. present Ring Structure Networks for efficient tensor representations, addressing the need for compact models in signal processing. Zhou and Si discuss subset-based training and pruning methods for sigmoid neural networks, emphasizing model simplification.

Further contributions include Zhou et al.'s exploration of compact CNNs, advocating for reduced model sizes without sacrificing performance. Hattie Zhou et al. analyze the concept of Lottery Tickets in neural networks, focusing on the significance of zero weights and supermasks in model performance. The work by X. Zhou et al. addresses irregularities in sparse neural networks through a cooperative software/hardware strategy, enhancing processing capabilities.

Zhu et al. propose SparseNN, an energy-efficient accelerator that exploits input and output sparsity, while also discussing LRADNN, which utilizes low-rank approximation for efficient deep learning. Zhu and Gupta critically evaluate the effectiveness of pruning in model compression, highlighting its implications for neural network performance. Finally, Zhuang et al. introduce neuron-level structured pruning with a polarization regularizer, and Zhuangwei et al. focus on discrimination-aware channel pruning to enhance deep neural network efficiency. Overall, these studies contribute significantly to the ongoing discourse on optimizing neural networks for practical applications.

In terms of pruning strategies, the research indicates that the effectiveness of sparsity techniques varies with the level of compression desired. For lower sparsity (below 90%), magnitude-based pruning proves effective, particularly with iterative methods. However, when aiming for higher levels of sparsity, techniques involving regularization and first- and second-order sparsification methods become more advantageous, creating a better balance in the sparsity-accuracy tradeoff.

The section also highlights the historical progression of CNN architectures and the methodologies applied in pruning, noting that initial works focused on convolutional operators and have since evolved to include more sophisticated techniques. The discussion of structured compression, including tensor decompositions and channel pruning, further reflects the ongoing innovations aimed at enhancing the efficiency of deep learning models while preserving their accuracy.

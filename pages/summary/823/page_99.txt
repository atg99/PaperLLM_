The collection of papers referenced addresses various methods and strategies for achieving sparsity in deep learning models, focusing on model compression, pruning, and efficiency improvements. 

Gudovskiy et al. propose a technique for compressing deep neural networks (DNNs) by utilizing learned representations over Galois Field (2), contributing to advancements in feature map compression. Guerra et al. explore automatic pruning methods for quantized neural networks, enhancing efficiency without significant loss in performance.

Gui et al. introduce a unified optimization framework that combines model compression with adversarial robustness, highlighting the importance of maintaining robustness during the pruning process. Guo et al. discuss parameter-efficient transfer learning through dynamic pruning, which allows for effective model adaptation with fewer parameters.

Guo et al. also present reweighted proximal pruning for large-scale language representations, emphasizing the balance between model size and representation quality. Additionally, the Star-Transformer model leverages sparsity to improve computational efficiency in natural language processing tasks.

Dynamic network surgery, as proposed by Yiwen Guo and colleagues, further refines DNNs by dynamically adjusting network structure during training, while their later work focuses on achieving sparse DNNs with enhanced adversarial robustness.

Gupta and Agrawal provide a survey on deep learning model compression specifically for text, summarizing various techniques and their applicability. Udit Gupta et al. introduce a modular accelerator designed for sparse recurrent neural networks, demonstrating hardware-oriented solutions for sparsity.

Historical perspectives from Hagiwara and Han highlight early efforts in removing hidden units and optimizing structures in feedforward neural networks, laying foundational concepts for contemporary sparsity techniques. Han et al. present ESE, an efficient speech recognition engine that utilizes sparse LSTM architectures on FPGA, illustrating practical applications of sparsity in real-world scenarios.

Overall, this body of work collectively advances the understanding and implementation of sparsity in deep learning, offering diverse methodologies that enhance model efficiency and robustness across various applications.

The paper discusses advancements in sparsity techniques within deep learning, particularly focusing on pruning methods and their impact on network performance. Han et al. (2016) demonstrated that iterative retraining can significantly reduce the number of iterations required to prune networks like AlexNet while increasing sparsity levels. They achieved a reduction from 4.8 million to 0.7 million iterations and improved sparsity from 89% to 94%. Their findings also indicate that fully-connected layers compress more effectively than convolutional layers.

Kundu and Sundaresan (2021) introduced a method that combines pruning with knowledge distillation in training sparse CNNs. Using dense ResNets as teachers and self-attention ResNets as students, they reported a 30.3x reduction in parameters with minimal accuracy loss on CIFAR-10.

The paper further explores sparse training techniques, noting that earlier models often struggled with accuracy. Bellec et al. (2018) proposed a fully-sparse training approach employing magnitude-based pruning and random weight addition, achieving better performance with high target sparsity. They also highlighted the adaptability of sparsified architectures to new tasks. Mocanu et al. (2018) confirmed that their training approach improved accuracy while maintaining over 96% pruning. 

Mostafa and Wang (2019) refined fully-sparse training by dynamically adjusting parameter budgets across layers, achieving superior performance compared to dense models. Dettmers and Zettlemoyer (2019) emphasized the importance of parameter redistribution for larger layers and achieved competitive results with a 95% sparse VGG on CIFAR-10.

Recent work on parameter-efficient networks, such as Azarian et al. (2020), introduced soft pruning, maintaining high accuracy while achieving significant sparsity. He et al. (2018) used reinforcement learning for network pruning, successfully achieving 80% sparsity in ResNet-50 without sacrificing accuracy. Overall, the paper illustrates ongoing innovations in sparsity techniques that enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of deep learning models.

The cited works encompass various studies and advancements in machine learning, particularly focusing on benchmarking, model optimization, and the analysis of neural network architectures. 

Hoefler et al. (2020) present the MLPerf Training Benchmark, providing a standardized method to evaluate machine learning performance across different systems. McCandlish et al. (2018) explore large-batch training, offering insights into its empirical implications for model performance. McCarley et al. (2020) discuss structured pruning approaches for BERT-based models, aiming to enhance efficiency without significantly sacrificing accuracy.

Mehta et al. (2019) investigate implicit filter-level sparsity in convolutional neural networks, while Meng et al. (2020) propose a novel pruning technique called "Pruning Filter in Filter." Mishra et al. (2018) introduce Wide Reduced-Precision Networks, emphasizing efficiency in model training. Mittal et al. (2018) examine the resilience of deep convolutional networks to random pruning, highlighting their plasticity.

Other notable contributions include Michel et al. (2019), who question the effectiveness of multiple attention heads in transformer models, and Millidge et al. (2020), who propose predictive coding as a means to approximate backpropagation across computation graphs. The theoretical perspectives on deep versus shallow networks are analyzed by Mhaskar and Poggio (2016), while Mocanu et al. (2016) provide insights into the structure of restricted Boltzmann machines.

These studies collectively advance the understanding of machine learning methodologies and the optimization of neural networks, contributing to more efficient and effective training practices.

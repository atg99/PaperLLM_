The paper discusses the evolution and significance of sparsity in deep learning, tracing its history from the late 1980s to the present. Following a period of reduced interest in artificial intelligence research, deep learning re-emerged around 2012 with advancements in convolutional neural networks for image recognition. Since then, over 266 papers have been published, focusing on techniques for sparsity in deep networks, which the authors categorize and summarize.

The literature shows a marked increase in publications related to sparsity, particularly in the late 2010s, coinciding with the development of various accelerators and optimization techniques aimed at enhancing sparse deep neural networks. The paper highlights the impact of computational advancements, driven by Moore's law and specialized hardware, which contributed to the growth of deep learning capabilities.

The authors argue that as the benefits from these scaling laws approach their limits, pursuing sparsity could represent a significant leap in computational efficiency, potentially improving performance by up to 100 times. They emphasize that while their overview captures key ideas in the field, the extensive literature makes it likely that some contributions may be overlooked.

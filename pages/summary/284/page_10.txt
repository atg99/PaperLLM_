The references listed include a range of studies focusing on advancements in image and video processing, particularly in the context of 360-degree media. 

Key contributions include:

1. **Deep Convolutional Networks**: Simonyan and Zisserman (2015) introduced very deep convolutional networks, which significantly improved large-scale image recognition tasks.

2. **Spherical Convolution**: Su and Grauman (2017) explored spherical convolution techniques to enhance feature extraction from 360-degree imagery, emphasizing the need for fast processing in this domain.

3. **Videography Learning**: In their 2017 CVPR work, Su and Grauman also addressed the challenge of making 360-degree videos accessible in 2D formats, which involves learning effective videography techniques for user-friendly viewing.

4. **Video Compression**: The authors further developed methods for creating compressible video isomers from 360-degree footage, facilitating more efficient video storage and transmission.

5. **Automatic Cinematography**: Their 2016 ACCV paper introduced Pano2vid, an automatic cinematography approach that enhances the viewing experience of 360-degree videos.

6. **Spatiotemporal Features**: Tran et al. (2015) focused on learning spatiotemporal features using 3D convolutional networks, which are essential for understanding video content over time.

7. **Highlight Detection**: Yu et al. (2018) presented a deep ranking model aimed at detecting highlights in 360-degree videos, showcasing the potential of AI in content curation.

8. **Context Modeling**: Zhang et al. (2014) developed a whole-room 3D context model, Panocontext, for improving panoramic scene understanding, which is critical for immersive experiences.

9. **Saliency Detection**: Zhang et al. (2018) contributed to saliency detection in 360-degree videos, addressing the unique challenges posed by such immersive media.

These studies collectively contribute to the evolution of technologies that enhance the capture, processing, and viewing of 360-degree imagery and videos, reflecting a growing market and interest in immersive media experiences.

The cited works discuss advancements in energy-efficient processors designed for deep learning applications, particularly focusing on Convolutional Neural Networks (ConvNets). Moons and Verhelst present a precision-scalable ConvNet processor fabricated in 40-nm CMOS technology, emphasizing energy efficiency while maintaining performance. Chen et al. introduce Eyeriss, a reconﬁgurable accelerator tailored for deep convolutional neural networks, which also prioritizes energy efficiency. Both studies contribute to the ongoing efforts to optimize hardware for deep learning tasks, highlighting the importance of balancing performance and energy consumption in modern computing systems.

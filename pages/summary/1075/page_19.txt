The referenced works focus on various techniques for compressing and optimizing deep neural networks, particularly for applications that require efficiency in terms of speed and power consumption. Key contributions include:

1. Techniques such as pruning, quantization, and low-rank approximations to reduce the size and computational demands of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) while maintaining performance.
2. The development of compact architectures like CNN-DBLSTM for handwriting recognition, and structured efficient layers to enhance computational efficiency.
3. The introduction of methods like Fastfood for approximating kernel expansions and approaches for learning separable filters, which aim to simplify the network structure.
4. Innovations in training strategies, such as knowledge distillation and low-precision training, which allow deep networks to learn effectively while using fewer resources.
5. Emphasis on channel pruning and structured sparsity as means to accelerate deep networks, with multiple studies proposing ways to maintain accuracy despite reduced model complexity.

These advancements collectively contribute to the ongoing effort to make deep learning models more accessible for mobile and resource-constrained environments, ensuring they can operate effectively without excessive computational overhead.

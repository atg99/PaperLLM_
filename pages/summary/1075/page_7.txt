The paper discusses methods for improving the efficiency of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) through weight matrix decomposition and network pruning techniques. 

Weight decomposition is explored as a way to reduce training complexity and enhance CNN performance. Various decomposition forms are proposed, such as using diagonal and Hadamard matrices to express weight matrices more efficiently. Ioannou et al. suggest learning small basis filters to describe complex filters, while Wen et al. focus on incorporating more weight information to accelerate convergence during fine-tuning. This approach has demonstrated a significant speedup on GPUs without sacrificing accuracy. Additionally, Rigamonti et al. extend decomposition techniques to reduce computational complexity across multiple layers, achieving substantial speed improvements while managing error accumulation.

On the other hand, network pruning aims to reduce the size and overfitting of neural networks, which is crucial for deployment on hardware with limited computational resources. Pruning methods vary, targeting weights, connections, filters, and channels. Unlike decomposition, which reduces layer size, pruning eliminates less significant neurons and connections. Experimental results indicate that pruning can significantly reduce model parameters while maintaining accuracy. Zhou et al. demonstrate that a 70% reduction in neuron count can be achieved without accuracy loss, while channel pruning methods can streamline CNNs further, yielding notable speedups on mobile devices.

The paper also mentions the integration of pruning with other optimization techniques, such as quantization and coding, to enhance performance. Various structured and unstructured sparsity approaches are addressed, with structured sparsity being more hardware-friendly. Techniques like Structured Sparsity Learning and iterative channel pruning are highlighted for their effectiveness in improving computation speed across different platforms.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the importance of these methods in optimizing CNNs for practical applications, balancing model complexity, computational efficiency, and accuracy.

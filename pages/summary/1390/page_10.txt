The references listed in this document highlight significant contributions to the field of computer vision, particularly in semantic segmentation and scene parsing. Key works include the introduction of attention mechanisms by Vaswani et al. in "Attention is All You Need," which has influenced many subsequent models. Wang et al. emphasized understanding convolution in semantic segmentation, while Zhao et al. proposed the Pyramid Scene Parsing Network, showcasing multi-scale context aggregation. Other notable contributions include the development of the Bisenet for real-time segmentation and the Ocnet focusing on object context. The use of dilated convolutions for multi-scale context aggregation was also explored by Yu and Koltun. The advancements in feature learning and context encoding for semantic segmentation are further illustrated through various studies, each contributing to the ongoing evolution of algorithms in visual recognition tasks. These works collectively provide a foundation for understanding the complexities involved in scene understanding and recognition.

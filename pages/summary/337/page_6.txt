This paper presents a comparative analysis of three deep learning pipelines—TemporAI, omop-learn, and ESGPT—focusing on their performance in working with the MIMIC-IV dataset. The results indicate that ESGPT is significantly more efficient in terms of storage, load time, batch processing time, and GPU memory usage. Specifically, ESGPT requires only 0.6 GB of disk space and has a load time of 42.5 seconds, outperforming TemporAI and omop-learn in various metrics. While ESGPT's batch memory usage is slightly higher than omop-learn, it remains considerably lower than TemporAI.

The paper emphasizes that ESGPT not only streamlines the extraction and preprocessing of data but also offers a memory-efficient representation suitable for PyTorch modeling. It provides an easy-to-use format that enhances efficiency in loading data and constructing batches for deep learning applications. The authors acknowledge that their comparisons are approximate, as TemporAI and omop-learn are not directly comparable to ESGPT, but they argue that ESGPT demonstrates substantial advantages in computational performance, especially for large-scale datasets.

In the subsequent sections, the paper outlines the challenges in developing generative models that can capture various dependencies and output types, highlighting the limitations of traditional GPT architectures. The authors discuss the need for tools that can handle historical, temporal, and intraevent dependencies while constructing features dynamically during generation. They conclude that current existing models do not meet these requirements, indicating a gap that ESGPT aims to fill.

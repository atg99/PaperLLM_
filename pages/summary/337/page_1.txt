The paper introduces Event Stream GPT (ESGPT), an open-source library designed to apply Generative Pre-trained Transformers (GPTs) to continuous-time sequences of complex events, such as medical records. While GPTs have transformed natural language processing (NLP), their use in other domains has been limited due to the absence of appropriate tools for model development and evaluation. ESGPT aims to address this issue by providing a streamlined process for building GPTs tailored to event sequences.

Key features of ESGPT include: 

1. The ability to create flexible, foundation-model scale input datasets using a minimal configuration file.
2. A Hugging Face compatible modeling API that incorporates intra-event causal dependencies and supports autoregressive generation.
3. Standardized evaluation processes to assess the few and zero-shot performance of pre-trained models on specific fine-tuning tasks.

The authors argue that ESGPT will facilitate broader adoption of foundation models in various fields, enhancing their applicability beyond traditional NLP tasks.

This review article discusses the significant advancements in deep learning-enabled medical computer vision over the past decade. The authors highlight the transformative impact of artificial intelligence in medicine, particularly through modern computer vision techniques driven by deep learning. They provide an overview of the evolution of convolutional neural networks and their applications in medical imaging, including fields such as cardiology, pathology, dermatology, and ophthalmology. 

The article emphasizes the capabilities of computer vision in tasks like object classification, localization, detection, and segmentation, which have shown accuracy levels comparable to expert physicians. It also addresses the importance of large labeled datasets and the role of transfer learning in overcoming challenges associated with sparse medical data. Techniques such as synthetic data generation and crowd-sourced annotations are noted as innovative solutions to enhance model performance.

Furthermore, the authors explore the integration of computer vision in medical video and clinical workflows, highlighting its potential to improve patient care. They conclude by discussing the challenges faced in the real-world deployment of these technologies, underscoring the need for continued research and development in this rapidly evolving field.

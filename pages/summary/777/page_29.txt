The paper presents a comparison of the performance of a proposed method against a model that does not utilize functional connectivity across three imaging sites: Peking, NYU, and NI. The results indicate that the proposed method outperforms the model without functional connectivity in terms of specificity, sensitivity, and accuracy. Specifically, the proposed method achieves higher accuracy rates across all datasets, demonstrating its effectiveness in leveraging functional connectivity for improved performance in imaging analysis. This suggests that incorporating functional connectivity significantly enhances the model's ability to interpret imaging data.

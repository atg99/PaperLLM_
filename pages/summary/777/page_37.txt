The figures presented illustrate the comparison of feature maps between healthy individuals and those with ADHD. Figure 10 shows a plot indicating that less than 10% of the top 100 feature maps identified in the ADHD group overlap with the top 500 feature maps from the healthy group. This suggests a significant distinction in the feature maps associated with ADHD compared to those in healthy individuals. 

Figure 11 further breaks down the distribution of the top 100 feature maps across different brain regions, specifically frontal, temporal, parietal, occipital, subcortical, and limbic areas, for both the healthy and ADHD classes. This visualization highlights the differences in regional feature representation between the two groups, emphasizing the unique characteristics of the ADHD feature maps.

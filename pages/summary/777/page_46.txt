The papers referenced discuss various aspects of brain structure and function in children with Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD). Qiu et al. focus on changes in brain morphology and activity, highlighting the differences observed in children diagnosed with ADHD (2011). Cherkasova and Hechtman explore neuroimaging findings, emphasizing that ADHD involves more than just the frontostriatal circuitry, suggesting a broader network of brain regions is implicated (2009). Xia et al. present a tool called Brainnet Viewer, which facilitates the visualization of human brain connectivity, aiding in the analysis of brain networks relevant to conditions like ADHD (2013). Together, these studies contribute to a deeper understanding of the neurobiological underpinnings of ADHD.

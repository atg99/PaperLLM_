The paper presents a comparison of the proposed method's accuracy against baseline metrics, average results from competition teams, and established methods such as the correlation method, clustering results, and the state-of-the-art FCNet method across three imaging sites: NI, Peking, and NYU. The baseline accuracies recorded were 44.0% for NI, 47.1% for Peking, and 70.7% for NYU. The proposed method achieved notable improvements with accuracies of 67.9% for NI, 62.7% for Peking, and 73.1% for NYU, surpassing all compared methods. 

The paper also highlights challenges in training a single machine learning model due to inconsistencies in experimental parameters across sites, resulting in heterogeneous datasets. An experiment using the DeepFMRI method demonstrated that a unified model struggled to maintain high performance, particularly in Peking where data was imbalanced. The findings emphasize the importance of dataset characteristics and site-specific factors in model accuracy.

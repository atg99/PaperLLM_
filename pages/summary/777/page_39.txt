The proposed approach suggests re-training the feature extractor network for each imaging site while keeping the parameters of the classification network and similarity measure network unchanged. This method aims to enhance the adaptability of the proposed network across different imaging sites. The references provided support various aspects of functional connectivity detection and classification in neuroimaging, highlighting the relevance of the methodology in the context of ADHD and other neurological conditions. Notable studies include explorations of brain network connectivity using resting-state fMRI and the application of deep neural networks for improved classification performance in schizophrenia. Overall, the work emphasizes the importance of adapting feature extraction to specific imaging environments to optimize performance in neuroimaging tasks.

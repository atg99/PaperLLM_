The paper presents an analysis of feature importance in functional connectivity for distinguishing between healthy individuals and those with ADHD, using the NYU dataset. The feature importance map, derived from a 4005-dimensional vector, indicates how each functional connectivity value contributes to class prediction. The study focuses on 670 selected feature importance values due to NYU's larger sample size and higher classification accuracy. 

Figure 5 visualizes these importance values, revealing distinct differences between the healthy and ADHD classes. This approach highlights that the model assigns varying weights to individual features based on their relevance to each class, contrasting with traditional machine learning methods that apply a uniform weight across classes. The findings emphasize the effectiveness of the proposed method in capturing nuanced differences in functional connectivity relevant to ADHD diagnosis.

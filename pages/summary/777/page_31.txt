The paper presents a comparison of classification accuracy among different methods for analyzing imaging data, specifically evaluating the proposed end-to-end model against clustering and correlation-based classification networks. The results, illustrated in Figure 4, indicate that the proposed method consistently achieves the highest accuracy across multiple imaging sites, notably outperforming both the clustering + classification network and correlation + classification network approaches. This supports existing findings that clustering is more effective for calculating functional connectivity than correlation techniques. The discussion section further analyzes the performance of the proposed method and the features it learns, reinforcing its superiority over the other evaluated methods.

The paper discusses various methodologies and advancements in the classification of Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) and Alzheimer's disease using functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI) data and deep learning techniques. Key studies cited include the use of state-space models with deep learning for estimating functional dynamics in resting-state fMRI, and the application of convolutional neural networks for Alzheimer’s disease classification. The ADHD-200 sample and its preprocessed data are mentioned as critical resources for research. Additionally, the paper references software tools like AFNI and FSL, which facilitate the analysis and visualization of neuroimaging data. The integration of advanced machine learning techniques, such as 2-channel convolutional 3D deep neural networks, is highlighted as a significant step towards improving the accuracy of fMRI analysis and feature learning for psychiatric and neurological disorders.

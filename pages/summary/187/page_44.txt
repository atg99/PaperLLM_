The paper discusses the advancements in the performance of on-Si GaN devices, highlighting the effectiveness of super-lattice (SL) buffers made of AlN/GaN pairs in controlling stress and enhancing blocking voltage. It notes that using thick SL buffers leads to improved crystal quality and surface smoothness of the top GaN layer, contributing to superior device performance. 

The epitaxial heterostructure includes a cap layer, usually composed of GaN or SiN, to prevent oxidation of the AlGaN film. The paper emphasizes the importance of surface states on the AlGaN layer, which are crucial for the formation of two-dimensional electron gas (2DEG). However, it also points out that surface defects can negatively impact device performance by creating a virtual gate that depletes the 2DEG under operating conditions. This depletion can be exacerbated by negatively charged surface states that counteract donor atoms. To address these issues, the application of a surface passivation layer, typically SiN, is recommended to mitigate 2DEG depletion.

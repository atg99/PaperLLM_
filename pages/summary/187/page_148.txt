The paper discusses methods to enhance the lifetime of GaN-on-Si power HEMTs by modifying barrier growth conditions, specifically by increasing the aluminum mole fraction from 20% to 25%. This adjustment is supported by numerical simulations that analyze the electric field distribution within the p-GaN and AlGaN barrier during high gate bias stress. The findings contribute to understanding the time-dependent failure mechanisms of these devices, as presented in the referenced work by I. Rossetto et al. The manuscript emphasizes the importance of these modifications for improving device performance and reliability.

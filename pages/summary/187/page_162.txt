The paper discusses the time-dependent breakdown of GaN epitaxial stacks, particularly focusing on the degradation behavior of p-GaN and its comparison to time-dependent dielectric breakdown (TDDB) processes. The authors highlight the complexity of detecting TDDB in full GaN power devices due to their multi-material composition and the presence of both lateral and vertical electric fields. Previous suggestions of GaN TDDB lacked conclusive demonstration, which the authors address by presenting experimental tests on optoelectronic devices, specifically LEDs. These tests confirm that GaN acts as a leaky dielectric when deeply depleted, exhibiting an exponential dependence of time-to-failure on the stress electric field, characterized by a Weibull distribution. The shape parameter β of 4.43 indicates intrinsic failure behavior. Similar results were found in GaN-on-Si stacks used for GaN transistors, where drain-to-substrate stress led to time-dependent degradation that was Weibull distributed and field-dependent, with weak thermal activation. The findings contribute to understanding GaN's degradation mechanisms in electronic applications.

The paper discusses the structural design of two types of Gallium Nitride (GaN) nanowire devices processed on a sapphire substrate. The first device, referred to as the n-channel device (Gen1), features a 2.5 µm GaN buffer layer, a 2 µm GaN channel layer, a 0.5 µm GaN top layer, and a 20 nm SiO2 gate dielectric. The second device, the p-channel device (Gen2), includes a similar 2.5 µm GaN buffer layer but has a 0.5 µm p-GaN channel layer, additional 0.73 µm and 0.5 µm GaN top layers, and a 25 nm Al2O3 gate dielectric. The paper also presents scanning electron microscopy (SEM) images of the nanowires from both devices, highlighting their structural characteristics. The research provides insights into the fabrication and design aspects of GaN devices, which are crucial for advancing semiconductor technology.

The paper discusses the band diagrams of AlGaN/GaN heterostructures, specifically focusing on the impact of incorporating a p-GaN layer. Figure 24 illustrates the band diagram both with and without the p-GaN layer, highlighting the structural differences. Figure 25 presents simulated conduction band diagrams for a p-GaN/AlGaN/GaN heterostructure, examining variations in aluminum content (12% and 26%) in the AlGaN barrier layer and the effects of different barrier thicknesses. The findings are part of a comprehensive review on normally-off High Electron Mobility Transistors (HEMTs) utilizing a p-GaN gate, emphasizing technological advancements in this area. The manuscript is a peer-reviewed version, with the final published version expected to differ post-editing.

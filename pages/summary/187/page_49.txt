The section discusses the use of a p-doped GaN layer to achieve normally-off GaN transistors in high electron mobility transistors (HEMTs). The p-GaN layer elevates the band diagram, enabling two-dimensional electron gas (2DEG) depletion without an external bias. To optimize this depletion, both the aluminum (Al) mole fraction in the barrier and the barrier thickness must be carefully adjusted. Research indicates that lower Al content and thickness enhance the 2DEG depletion. Specifically, simulations by Greco et al. show that a structure with a 50 nm p-GaN layer and a 25 nm barrier can exhibit normally-off behavior when the Al content is reduced. Conversely, high Al content leads to normally-on behavior. These findings highlight the critical role of barrier thickness and Al concentration in determining the operational characteristics of p-GaN/AlGaN/GaN heterojunctions.

The paper discusses the development of an integrated vertical MOSFET and Schottky barrier diode (SBD) using GaN-on-Si technology. The SBD was fabricated by dry etching the n- and p-GaN layers, followed by TMAH treatment to achieve surface smoothness and subsequent Schottky metallization. The integrated device demonstrated impressive electrical characteristics, including a low turn-on voltage of 0.76 V, a specific on-resistance (Ron,sp) of 1.6 mΩcm², an ideality factor of 1.5, and a breakdown voltage (BV) of 254 V, all without additional termination mechanisms.

The paper also highlights recent advancements in optimizing GaN-on-Si semi-vertical devices, particularly focusing on Si substrates up to 200 mm in diameter. Key areas of research include enhancing the full GaN-on-Si structure, specifically through Mg-doping in the p-body layer and adjusting the thickness of the gate insulator. The work is referenced from Liu et al., published in IEEE Electron Device Letters.

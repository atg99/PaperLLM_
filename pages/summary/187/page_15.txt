The paper discusses the resistance of the n-type semiconductor region, denoting that the on-resistance (R_ON) is proportional to the width (W_d) and mobility (μ_ND). The relationship is mathematically expressed, highlighting that R_ON is proportional to W_d and μ_ND is proportional to W_d squared. The paper also links these parameters to the Baliga figure of merit (FOM), which is a critical metric for minimizing conduction losses in power transistors. This FOM is particularly relevant at moderate frequencies where conduction losses dominate.

In the analysis, the Baliga FOM is calculated for various semiconductors, including Si, GaAs, 4H-SiC, GaN, β-Ga2O3, diamond, and AlN. The results, normalized to GaN, indicate that GaN exhibits a significantly higher Baliga FOM compared to Si and GaAs, and is greater than SiC while being comparable to gallium oxide. Diamond and AlN are noted for their exceptionally high Baliga FOM, suggesting they are promising candidates for advancing semiconductor technology.

The paper concludes with a table summarizing the material properties of the semiconductors studied, including energy gap (E_G), relative permittivity (ε_r), mobility (μ), critical electric field (E_crit), and saturation velocity (v_s). This data underscores the advantages of wide-bandgap semiconductors in power applications.

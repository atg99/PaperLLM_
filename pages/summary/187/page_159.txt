The study investigates the formation of structural defects, specifically indentations, pits, and grooves, at the drain side of the gate edge in AlGaN/GaN high electron mobility transistors (HEMTs) under electrical stress. Using atomic force microscopy (AFM) and scanning electron microscopy (SEM), the research confirms that these defects can emerge without pre-existing flaws, aligning with the inverse piezoelectric effect. These defects serve as leakage paths for the gate, potentially decreasing the maximum drain current and promoting charge trapping within the device. The findings underscore the impact of electrical degradation on device performance.

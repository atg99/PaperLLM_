The paper discusses the characteristics of n-type GaN layers grown on substrates, emphasizing the interface region's defects. It presents a schematic representation illustrating the structure, along with a charge diagram that highlights the screening effects caused by surface donors. Additionally, the band diagrams depict how the surface donor levels approach the Fermi level at the GaN surface, resulting in the generation of screening charge. The analysis aims to provide insights into the electronic properties and behavior of n-type GaN, which are crucial for its applications in semiconductor devices.

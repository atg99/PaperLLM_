The paper discusses the optimization of the AlGaN barrier layer in GaN HEMT devices, highlighting the relationship between the layer's thickness and aluminum content in relation to the normally-on and normally-off characteristics. It emphasizes the necessity of a high magnesium concentration in the p-GaN layer, while cautioning that excessive doping can negatively affect crystal quality. The p-GaN layer is noted for its wide process window, facilitating easier process control. However, the etching process's low selectivity between p-GaN and the barrier layer requires careful optimization to ensure a stable high threshold voltage. The paper concludes by referencing the need for precise control in the manufacturing processes to achieve desired electrical characteristics in the devices.

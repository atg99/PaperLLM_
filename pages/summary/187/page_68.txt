The paper discusses advancements in multi-channel heterostructure technology for power devices, highlighting the improvements achieved over traditional single-channel devices. A significant development is the demonstration of multi-channel power devices with a sheet resistance of 83 Ω/sq, featuring normally-off operation with a threshold voltage (VTH) of 0.85 V, an on-resistance of 3.2 Ω·mm, and a breakdown voltage of 1300 V. These specifications indicate a marked improvement over existing technologies. Additionally, the use of low-pressure chemical vapor deposition Si3N4 for passivation has shown to reduce current collapse under high voltage stress and enhance VTH stability during operation. Future research is expected to focus on optimizing the multi-channel heterostructure and developing methods to achieve a larger positive VTH. The paper also briefly touches on super junction technology, comparing conventional power MOSFETs with super junction devices, indicating ongoing innovations in the field.

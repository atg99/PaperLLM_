The paper discusses advancements in the blocking voltage (BV) of vertical GaN Schottky barrier diodes (SBD) through edge termination techniques. It details the implantation process using energy levels of 30, 60, and 100 keV, followed by a post-implantation annealing at 450 °C in a nitrogen atmosphere for 10 minutes. This process enhanced the BV from 260 V for the unterminated SBD to 800 V. Further enhancement to 1020 V was achieved by capping the drift layer with a 5 nm layer of graded AlGaN, as previously reported by Cao et al. Additionally, the paper references work by Wang et al., which utilized boron implantation for edge termination, resulting in a significant reduction in leakage current and an increase in BV from 189 V to 585 V. The findings highlight the effectiveness of fluorine and boron implantation in improving the performance of GaN SBDs. The paper includes schematic representations and electric field distribution simulations to support the results.

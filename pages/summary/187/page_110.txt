The paper presents a database of traps detected in GaN and AlGaN-based devices, detailing activation energies and relevant references. It highlights various native defects, particularly focusing on nitrogen interstitials, nitrogen antisites, nitrogen vacancies, gallium interstitials, and gallium vacancies. The trap levels associated with these defects are primarily positioned between E C - 1.2 eV and E C - 0.76 eV, with nitrogen interstitials showing the highest activation energy among nitrogen-related defects. This positions them as significant traps or recombination centers. The findings are supported by multiple references, with certain associations being marked as tentative or related to extended defects. The paper emphasizes the importance of understanding these trap levels for the advancement of semiconductor technology.

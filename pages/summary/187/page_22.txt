The paper discusses the strain and piezoelectric polarization in hexagonal AlGaN systems, relevant for GaN-based transistors. It defines the strain along the c-axis (𝜖𝑧) and the isotropic in-plane strain (𝜖𝑥=𝜖𝑦). The relationship between lattice constants is established, highlighting that the change in c is related to the change in a through elastic constants C13 and C33. The equation for calculating piezoelectric polarization (𝑃𝑝𝑧) along the c-axis is presented, incorporating the elastic constants and the change in lattice constant. Additionally, formulas for the elastic constants C13 and C33 as functions of the molar fraction x are provided, along with the variation of the lattice constant a with x. The overall focus is on understanding the mechanical properties and their implications for the performance of AlGaN/GaN structures.

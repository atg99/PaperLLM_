The section discusses electrochemical degradation observed at the gate edges of devices under reverse gate bias conditions. It highlights the formation of localized damage, including particles and stringers at the source and drain sides. Auger electron spectroscopy (AES) analysis reveals that these formations are oxygen-rich and contain gallium, suggesting the presence of Ga2O3 and Al2O3. The presence of large pits beneath the oxide particles after metal removal indicates significant degradation. The study shows that oxide formation occurs where the electric field is strongest and is contingent upon the presence of oxygen, which is believed to diffuse through the passivation layer to oxidize the AlGaN/GaN layers.

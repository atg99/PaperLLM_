The paper discusses the properties of lattice matched InAlN/GaN high electron mobility transistors (HEMTs) and their advantages in reducing transistor dimensions, which facilitates achieving high cut-off frequencies. It highlights the deviation of bandgap in ternary alloys like AlGaN and InGaN from Vegard’s rule, presenting an empirical expression for the bandgap energy of these materials. Specifically, for AlGaN, the relationship is defined, incorporating a bowing parameter to account for the deviation. Additionally, the paper provides key bandstructure parameters for GaN, including effective density of states in conduction and valence bands, and effective masses for electrons and holes. A table is included that summarizes bandgap and Varshni parameters for GaN, AlN, and InN, citing relevant sources for the data. The manuscript is a peer-reviewed version and is set to be further edited before publication.

This section of the paper discusses the advancements in vertical GaN devices, highlighting their performance in various reliability tests which are crucial for future commercialization. The review covers the fabrication and performance of vertical devices on sapphire and bulk GaN, followed by developments in GaN-on-Si vertical devices.

Under vertical device architectures, the section on PiN diodes emphasizes their significance in electronic devices, particularly within wide-band-gap semiconductors. Despite their high turn-on voltage and reverse recovery times, which limit their efficiency in power electronics, PiN junctions remain essential in various modern vertical power devices. The paper also notes the advantages of Schottky diodes but underscores the importance of understanding the fundamental physics of p-n junctions. This understanding aids in analyzing critical material parameters that are vital for the design and functionality of power devices, such as electric field strength, doping density, and temperature effects. 

Overall, the manuscript serves as a comprehensive review of the current state of vertical GaN device research, providing insights into their potential applications in power electronics.

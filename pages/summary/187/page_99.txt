The paper discusses the advantages of using Silicon (Si) substrates for Gallium Nitride (GaN) growth compared to traditional bulk GaN and sapphire substrates. Key points include the potential for significantly lower wafer and epitaxy costs, enhanced thermal and electrical conductivity, and the availability of larger 12-inch Si substrates which can reduce unit costs. The integration of Si substrates with existing CMOS-compatible fabrication processes could facilitate mass production of GaN-on-Si devices, minimizing the expenses typically associated with establishing new technology fabs. Additionally, the introduction of engineered substrates with poly-AlN offers benefits such as a coefficient of thermal expansion that matches GaN, allowing for the growth of high-quality, stress-free GaN with lower defect density. These advancements position GaN-on-Si vertical devices for a promising future in the industry.

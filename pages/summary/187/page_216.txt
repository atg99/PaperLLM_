The references listed encompass a range of studies related to the reliability and performance of AlGaN/GaN high electron mobility transistors (HEMTs) and related devices. Key topics include the suppression of dynamic effects through proton irradiation, the impact of buffer leakage on device reliability, and investigations into degradation mechanisms such as negative bias temperature instability (NBTI) and positive bias temperature instability (PBTI). The papers highlight advancements in understanding the intrinsic reliability of these semiconductor devices, exploring factors such as reverse-bias stability and the effects of various material processes. Each reference provides insights into specific experimental findings and methodologies, contributing to the broader knowledge of GaN-based technology in electronic applications.

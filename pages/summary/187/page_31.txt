The paper discusses the polarization charge at the AlxGa1-xN/GaN interface, particularly focusing on how it varies with the molar fraction x. The polarization charge is expressed as the difference between the spontaneous and piezoelectric polarization charges in AlGaN and GaN. The spontaneous polarization charge for an AlxGa1-xN layer is defined using a specific equation, indicating a linear relationship with x. The authors highlight that a molar fraction range of 0.2 to 0.4 is optimal for achieving sufficient polarization charge and conduction band discontinuity, which are critical for the generation and confinement of two-dimensional electron gas (2DEG) electrons. However, they caution against using molar fractions greater than 0.4 due to potential issues with thermal and lattice mismatches that can increase defect density and affect device performance. Additionally, the paper presents data on sheet charge density of the 2DEG for different Al mole fractions and AlGaN layer thicknesses, emphasizing the importance of these parameters in device design.

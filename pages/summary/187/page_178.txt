The paper provides a comprehensive overview of Gallium Nitride (GaN) and its significance in the development of next-generation power converters. It highlights the properties and advantages of GaN-based transistors, noting their potential to outperform traditional silicon (Si) and silicon carbide (SiC) devices as research progresses in both academic and industrial settings. Current GaN transistors enhance the performance and reliability of switching mode power converters, while future advancements may lead to monolithic integration, facilitating the creation of miniaturized converters operating in the MHz range.

The paper also discusses the promise of vertical GaN transistors for high power and high voltage applications, which can be produced on large, cost-effective substrates. A thorough understanding of material and device properties is essential for extending operational capabilities, enabling robust kilovolt operation and sub-microsecond switching speeds. Furthermore, insights into trapping and reliability-limiting processes will help maximize the advantages of GaN as a revolutionary semiconductor in power electronics.

This tutorial serves as a valuable reference for researchers seeking to explore or update their knowledge on GaN technology and its advancements.

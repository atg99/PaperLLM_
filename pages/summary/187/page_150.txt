The paper discusses the electroluminescence spatial distribution and its correlation with gate current in p-GaN gate devices, highlighting the degradation process that enhances robustness at high temperatures. It references the findings of F. Masin et al. on the positive temperature dependence of time-dependent breakdown in GaN-on-Si E-mode HEMTs under positive gate stress. The focus then shifts to vertical devices, noting that while some degradation aspects were previously covered, additional processes unique to vertical structures will be explored in this section. The manuscript is a peer-reviewed version, with the final published record expected to differ after copyediting and typesetting. The authors request citation using the provided DOI.

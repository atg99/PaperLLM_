The paper discusses the failure mechanisms in AlGaN/GaN RF HEMTs, particularly focusing on the impact of critical electric fields and power dissipation during drain ESD discharges at various gate voltages. It presents findings from EDX measurements that highlight the driving forces behind device failures attributed to power dissipation and electric field effects. The authors emphasize the importance of understanding these factors for improving device reliability. Furthermore, the paper addresses the radiation hardness of GaN-based devices, underlining their potential for use in extreme conditions such as space applications. The robustness against different types of irradiation is crucial for their viability, and the authors refer to recent reviews for a more comprehensive discussion on this topic.

The paper discusses the design and performance of GaN power MOSFETs on silicon substrates, highlighting two configurations: a quasi-vertical structure and a fully-vertical structure achieved through selective substrate removal. Figures presented include a schematic cross-section of the quasi-vertical GaN MOSFET and a comparison of the current-voltage characteristics (IDS-VDS) of fabricated vertical MOSFETs with different gate trench alignments on m- and a-planes. The results indicate variations in performance based on the type of hard mask used, with specific SEM images illustrating the trench structure. The overall findings emphasize the potential of GaN-on-Si technology in enhancing power device efficiency and performance.

The paper discusses recent advancements in vertical devices utilizing GaN on Si substrates, focusing on the benefits of using GaN for power device applications. It highlights the challenges faced in commercializing vertical GaN power devices due to the high costs and limited sizes of bulk GaN substrates, which are primarily used in specialized applications like lasers and LEDs. The ideal scenario for achieving high-quality GaN layers involves homo-epitaxy on bulk GaN to minimize defect density. To overcome cost and size limitations, the paper suggests adopting GaN grown on more affordable foreign substrates such as silicon and sapphire. This approach has been successful in lateral GaN HEMT technology and is proposed for vertical power devices as well. The paper emphasizes the need for improvements in wafer size and cost reduction to fully harness GaN's capabilities in power electronics.

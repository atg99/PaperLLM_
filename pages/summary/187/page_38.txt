The manuscript discusses the impact of reduced gate charge on switching losses in field-effect transistors. It highlights a significant reduction in the parameter Q rr, achieving a value as low as 2.2 mΩµC, which contributes positively to minimizing energy losses during device operation. Additionally, the paper includes a schematic circuit and characteristics of voltage, current, and gate charge during a gate-charge measurement, providing a comprehensive understanding of the relationship between gate voltage and charge during the turn-on phase of the device. The authors note that this is a peer-reviewed manuscript, with the final online version subject to further editing and typesetting.

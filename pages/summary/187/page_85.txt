The paper discusses the design and realization of Junction Barrier Schottky (JBS) diodes, which leverage the benefits of Schottky contacts for low turn-on voltage while minimizing leakage current through p-i-n diode structures. Figures 52 and 53 illustrate the fabrication and electric field distribution of these devices. Specifically, Figure 52(a) presents a schematic top view and cross-section of a trench JBS diode, while Figure 52(b) demonstrates the simulated electric field distribution at -200 V, highlighting the efficacy of the RESURF (REverse SUrface Field) action in reducing the electric field due to the adjacent p-GaN layer. Figure 53 further elaborates on the ion implantation techniques used for creating JBS diodes, showcasing Mg ion implantation in n-GaN and Si ion implantation in p-GaN. The references indicate that this research has been peer-reviewed and is integral to advancing the understanding of GaN-based diode technologies.

The paper discusses the spontaneous polarization values of III-N semiconductors, specifically GaN, InN, and AlN, as presented in Table 3. The values indicate a range of spontaneous polarization, with GaN showing -0.029 C m-2, InN -0.032 C m-2, and AlN -0.081 C m-2. These values are sourced from two separate studies.

The paper further explains the impact of strain on the piezoelectric polarization of these semiconductors, detailing how the displacement of the anion and cation sublattices leads to polarization effects. A simplified representation of this polarization is illustrated through a ball and stick diagram, focusing on the Ga-polar configuration. It notes that in an ideal tetrahedral structure, the in-plane and vertical polarization components would cancel each other out. 

When tensile strain is applied, the polarization from triple bonds decreases, resulting in a net polarization along the (0001̅) direction. Conversely, applying compressive strain increases the polarization from the triple bonds, leading to a net polarization along the (0001) direction.

To quantify piezoelectric polarization, the paper references piezoelectric constants, with Table 4 summarizing the values for GaN, InN, and AlN. The lattice parameters defining the wurtzite structure are also discussed, including the hexagonal edge length (a0) and prism height (c0). The key relation for calculating the piezoelectric polarization along the c-axis is provided as:

P_pz = e33ε_z + e31(ε_x + ε_y)

This paper discusses the advancements in GaN-on-Si p-i-n diodes, highlighting their development since 2014. It references key studies, including the pioneering work by Zhang et al., which demonstrated the first GaN-on-Si p-i-n and Schottky diodes with breakdown voltages of 300 V and 205 V, respectively. Notably, despite a high defect density of 10^9/cm^2, these diodes achieved a peak electric field of 2.9 MV/cm. The paper also addresses issues related to current leakage paths, mechanisms of leakage, and potential strategies for mitigation. Figures included illustrate the I-V characteristics of vertical PiN diodes and schematic representations of device structures, emphasizing the ongoing research in enhancing the performance of these power diodes.

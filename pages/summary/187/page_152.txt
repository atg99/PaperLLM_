The paper discusses the degradation mechanisms of GaN-on-GaN vertical diodes under high current stress, illustrated through a schematic cross-section and a graph showing the relationship between series resistance increase, electroluminescence intensity decrease, and stress time. It differentiates between DC and RF operating conditions, noting that RF devices are more susceptible to degradation from hot electrons, particularly in short channel scenarios. The paper emphasizes the importance of buffer conductivity compensation, with Fe and C co-doping yielding optimal reliability. It highlights that RF stress leads to minor changes in DC performance, such as reduced saturation current and gate leakage, but can also result in increased dynamic current collapse due to defect generation.

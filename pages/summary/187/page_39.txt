The paper discusses the advancements in lateral GaN transistors and their significant role in the power device market. The unique characteristics of GaN HEMTs, such as their ability to handle high voltage, high current, and low on-state resistance, contribute to their high power and efficiency. Additionally, their wide bandwidth enables reliable operation at elevated frequencies and temperatures, making them suitable for next-generation high-efficiency power converters. The GaN-on-Si technology platform, despite its challenges, presents a cost-effective solution for commercialization, necessitating complex GaN buffers for stress management and insulation. This technology is paving the way for diverse applications across consumer electronics, transportation, energy, and various industrial sectors, including automotive and aerospace. The paper highlights that most GaN components are currently designed for applications up to 650V, with some options available for 900V devices.

The paper discusses the electrical characteristics at the interface of a GaN layer and its substrate, highlighting the role of screening charges that compensate the positive component Qπ(GaN). It notes that realistic structures often include additional layers, such as C-doped layers or AlN nucleation layers, which are not detailed for simplicity. 

At the surface of the AlGaN layer, if sufficiently thick, the potential qs is pinned at the surface donor level EDD. Since the AlGaN layer is undoped, its electric field is influenced solely by sheet charge densities, represented by the equation εAlGaN = [qNDD - Qπ(AlGaN)] / (ε0εAlGaN). 

The analysis assumes that the electric field in the GaN solely arises from the charge in the two-dimensional electron gas (2DEG), neglecting contributions from n-type donor charge. Under this assumption, the electric field in the GaN is defined as εGaN = qns / (ε0εGaN). 

The total potential drop in the AlGaN is expressed as VAlGaN = -εAlGaN t, which simplifies to VAlGaN = [Qπ(AlGaN) - Qπ(GaN) - qns] / (ε0εAlGaN t). This formulation emphasizes the interdependence of potential and charge distributions between the AlGaN and GaN layers.

The paper discusses advancements in high electron mobility transistors (HEMTs) utilizing an Al-rich AlN/AlGaN/AlN heterostructure. Specifically, Abid et al. demonstrated an effective AlN barrier on an Al50Ga50N channel, achieving a high carrier concentration of approximately 1.9×10^13 cm^-2. Additionally, Ghandi et al. investigated the three-terminal breakdown voltage of the AlN/AlGaN/AlN structure with varying gate-drain lengths, presenting results in Figure 38. The study highlights the potential of these materials for applications in high-voltage power semiconductor devices.

The paper presents a comparative analysis of key material parameters for Gallium Nitride (GaN) and other semiconductors, including energy gap, relative dielectric permittivity, electron mobility, critical electric field, electron saturation velocity, and thermal conductivity. The data, derived from the work of P. Fay et al., highlight GaN's superior properties, particularly in terms of critical electric field and electron mobility, with reported mobility values reaching up to 2000 cm²/Vs. 

Figure 3 illustrates the relationship between breakdown field and energy gap for various semiconductor materials, as well as the Johnson figure of merit, both normalized to GaN for easier comparison. The findings emphasize GaN's advantageous characteristics over other materials, suggesting its suitability for high-frequency electronic devices. The data underscores the potential of GaN in commercial and research-grade applications, making it a significant focus in semiconductor research.

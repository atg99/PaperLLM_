The paper discusses the advancements in vertical fin power field-effect transistors (FETs), particularly those utilizing sub-micron fins on bulk GaN substrates. A key advantage of these devices over traditional trench MOSFETs is their elimination of the need for a p-GaN layer, which facilitates normally-off operation and efficient blocking in the off-state. The gate region is designed with dielectric and gate metal on the fin sidewalls, which depletes charge carriers in the fin due to the work function differences, ensuring normally-off functionality. The document also presents measured device junction capacitances and provides a schematic representation of various capacitance components, highlighting the significance of these characteristics in the performance of FinFETs. The findings contribute to the understanding and development of high-performance power transistors.

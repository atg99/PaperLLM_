This section discusses the effects of voltage threshold (VT) instabilities in GaN-on-Si power HEMTs caused by negative gate bias (NGB) and positive gate bias (PGB). The referenced figures illustrate these effects, highlighting the instability in VT under different bias conditions as reported in various studies. Additionally, the "kink" effect is described as an operational instability observed during a drain-source voltage (VDS) sweep, where the drain current initially decreases in the saturation region before rising sharply over a narrow voltage range. This behavior is particularly concerning as it can negatively impact device performance. The manuscript, while peer-reviewed, will differ in its final published form.

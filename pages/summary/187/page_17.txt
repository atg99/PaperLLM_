The paper discusses the characteristics and applications of a unilateral abrupt p+/n- junction, represented schematically in Figure 4. It includes an approximated electric field profile for the junction and evaluates the Baliga figure of merit for various semiconductors, normalized to GaN for comparison. The study emphasizes the temperature dependence of the GaN bandgap, following the Varshni relation, which describes how the bandgap energy changes with temperature. Parameters for GaN, AlN, and InN are summarized, indicating that alloys of these materials can achieve a wide bandgap range from 0.7 eV to 6.2 eV. The paper highlights the common use of AlGaN/GaN heterostructures in GaN transistors, with AlN and AlGaN layers serving as nucleation and buffer layers, respectively.

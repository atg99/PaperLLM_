The figures presented illustrate the transfer and output characteristics of GaN-based MISHEMTs, comparing devices with and without local substrate removal (LSR) and backside AlN and Cu. Additionally, the evolution of the device breakdown voltage and specific on-resistance with respect to the gate-drain capacitance (L GD) is shown, along with off-state leakage current characteristics. These comparisons highlight the advancements in GaN-on-silicon high-electron-mobility transistor technology, particularly focusing on achieving ultra-low leakage at high voltages up to 3000 V. The findings are derived from the work of Ezgi Dogmus et al., emphasizing the impact of LSR on device performance.

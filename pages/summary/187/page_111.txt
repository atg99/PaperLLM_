The paper discusses the energy states associated with native and extended defects in Gallium Nitride (GaN). The reference energy level is set at zero, corresponding to the top of the valence band. It highlights the identification of nitrogen antisite defects, which create a mini-band of energy levels between EC - 0.66 eV and EC - 0.5 eV. However, no direct correlation has been found between these levels and extended defects. 

Nitrogen vacancies are noted as the most prevalent native defect in GaN, with over 40 instances documented across 25 different publications. The trap states related to nitrogen vacancies are primarily found in the energy range of EC - 0.27 eV to EC - 0.089 eV, with a common level identified at EC - (0.24 ± 0.003) eV. Some deeper energy levels associated with VN complexes and other nitrogen-related defects are also mentioned as outliers in this distribution.

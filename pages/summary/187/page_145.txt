The paper discusses the impact of Time-Dependent Dielectric Breakdown (TDDB) on both lateral and vertical devices, specifically trench MOSFETs and FinFETs. It highlights the utility of electroluminescence measurements in identifying failure locations within these devices, emphasizing that controlling current during catastrophic failures can prevent extensive damage and facilitate failure analysis. The authors note that optimizing the insulator composition and bilayer structure can enhance device robustness and lifespan. Numerical simulations reveal that electric field crowding occurs at the trench edges for trench transistors and at the corners of the dielectric at the foot of the fin for FinFETs.

The paper presents a schematic illustration of various trapping mechanisms categorized by bias conditions that initiate charging processes in electronic devices. The mechanisms are summarized as follows:

- **Off-state conditions** occur at sub-threshold gate voltages (VGS) with large positive drain-source voltages (VDS), where C doping in the buffer and dielectric interfaces affects hole and electron trapping.
- **Semi-on or power-state conditions** are described at above-threshold VGS or large positive VDS, highlighting the role of the dielectric and channel in electron dynamics.
- **Negative gate bias** scenarios show how electron trapping occurs in the presence of a negative VGS, influencing the overall charge distribution.
- **Positive gate bias** conditions illustrate the charging behavior when VGS is positively biased, affecting electron and hole interactions within the structure.

Key components include the substrate, buffer, and barrier layers, with specific references to the doping mechanisms and their impact on electron and hole behavior. The figures and descriptions emphasize the complexity of charge trapping and the interplay between different components in the device architecture. The manuscript is a peer-reviewed work, and the authors encourage citation of the final published version.

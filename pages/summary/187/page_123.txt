The paper discusses the impact of charge trapping effects on the performance of GaN transistors, particularly focusing on how these effects influence the threshold voltage (V T) and lead to instabilities. It highlights two primary types of dielectric traps: bulk and interface traps, which significantly affect device performance. 

Section 8.7 reviews major charge trapping effects observed in GaN transistors, correlating these effects with their underlying mechanisms as summarized in Table 9. Key trapping effects include RF current collapse, dynamic resistance (R ON) variations, and V T instabilities under both negative and positive gate bias. The RF current collapse is identified as particularly detrimental, as it reduces maximum drain current and increases minimum drain-source voltage, leading to power compression and efficiency degradation in RF amplification applications. This phenomenon is attributed to electron trapping under specific bias conditions, which weakens channel conductivity and hinders recovery as the device transitions to the ON state.

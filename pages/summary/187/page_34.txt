The paper discusses the characteristics of a triangular potential well formed at the GaN side of an AlGaN/GaN interface, highlighting how energy varies linearly with the electric field. The energy begins at a value represented by qV(0) on the GaN side. Additionally, it presents figures illustrating the polarization charge density at the AlGaN/GaN heterojunction and the sheet charge density for a two-dimensional electron gas (2DEG) in relation to the molar fraction and thickness of the AlGaN layer. The findings are crucial for understanding the electronic properties of AlGaN/GaN heterostructures, which are significant in semiconductor applications.

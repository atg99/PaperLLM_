The paper by A. Tajalli et al. investigates the impact of inserting super-lattices (SL) into buffer layers for GaN-on-Silicon heterostructures. Their findings indicate that this modification enables vertical breakdown voltages to exceed 1200 V without introducing additional trapping effects, in contrast to traditional step-graded AlGaN-based structures with equivalent buffer thickness. The authors utilized back-gating transient measurements to demonstrate significantly reduced trapping effects associated with the SL structures. Additionally, the study addresses the limitations posed by the Si substrate, particularly its low critical electrical field strength and parasitic conduction at the buffer/substrate interface. To mitigate these issues, local substrate removal (LSR) has proven effective, resulting in improved blocking voltages reaching up to 3 kV. The research highlights the potential of SL buffers and LSR techniques in enhancing the performance of GaN-on-Si transistors for high voltage applications.

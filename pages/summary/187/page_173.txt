The paper discusses the effects of electrostatic discharge (ESD) events on the reliability of devices in both ON and OFF states. It highlights that the robustness detected during an ESD event in the ON state is lower, with failure voltage and current points aligning closely with an iso-power curve. This indicates that power dissipation limits the device's reliability in the ON state. The findings are substantiated by scanning electron microscope (SEM) inspections, which reveal significant differences in failure characteristics between devices tested in ON and OFF states. Energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDX) analysis shows that the failed region in the ON state exhibits high concentrations of gallium and aluminum, likely due to melting of barrier and channel layers. In contrast, the OFF state device shows lower concentrations of these elements and a higher silicon signal, attributed to the electron beam's access to the Si substrate through cracks.

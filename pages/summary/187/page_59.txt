The paper discusses the advantages of using a super-lattice-based buffer in GaN HEMTs to minimize internal stress, which leads to the development of a highly insulating buffer with reduced trapping effects. It includes a schematic cross-section of a GaN HEMT with a super-lattice buffer and a transmission electron microscopy (TEM) image of an AlN/GaN superlattice. The findings are supported by experimental data showcasing vertical leakage curves and breakdown voltages for a step-graded GaN carbon-doped buffer compared to a super-lattice structure at room temperature. The results indicate that the super-lattice structure offers significant improvements in breakdown voltage, highlighting its potential for high voltage applications. The research emphasizes the importance of buffer design in enhancing device performance and reliability.

As the AlGaN/GaN power technology matures and expands its market presence, future research is expected to diverge into two main directions. One path will focus on commercial product development, addressing issues like trapping, reliability, stability, packaging, and circuit operation. Simultaneously, researchers will explore innovative device structures to leverage GaN material properties for next-generation high-efficiency devices.

One promising area of research is the development of AlGaN channel HEMTs, which utilize ultra-wide bandgap materials, such as AlN with a bandgap of 6.2 eV. These materials can enhance voltage and temperature operations due to their higher critical electric fields. Incorporating an AlN back barrier can improve electron confinement in the transistor channel and increase thermal dissipation. Al-rich transistors have shown favorable properties for high-temperature applications compared to conventional wide bandgap materials. Although challenges remain in achieving high voltage operations due to material quality and the implementation of high Al content, recent efforts indicate potential for voltage enhancement and improved thermal stability with AlGaN channels.

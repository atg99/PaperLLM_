The paper discusses a method for analyzing traps in semiconductor materials using a current-transient methodology. It presents a formula to calculate the fitted current, which is expressed as a sum of amplitude coefficients multiplied by an exponential decay term, along with a constant current component. By plotting the amplitude coefficients against their corresponding time constants, researchers can infer the time constants associated with different traps. This analysis is illustrated with a recovery transient and its corresponding time-constant spectrum, demonstrating the methodology's application at a specific temperature and voltage conditions. The findings contribute to the understanding of trap behavior in semiconductor devices.

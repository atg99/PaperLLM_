The paper presents a detailed analysis of the GaN lattice structure, depicted through a schematic representation that highlights the Ga- and N-faces along the (0001) direction. It includes a model illustrating polarization charge within a GaN layer, alongside a depiction of charge distribution at the Ga- and N-faces. This distribution is characterized by polarization and screening dipoles, providing insights into the electrical properties of GaN materials. The manuscript has undergone peer review and is accepted for publication, although the final online version will differ due to copyediting and typesetting processes. For citation purposes, the DOI is provided as 10.1063/5.0061354.

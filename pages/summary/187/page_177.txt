The paper discusses the effects of ionizing radiation on Gallium Nitride (GaN) devices, focusing on the impact of saturation current, gate current, and reverse breakdown voltage. It notes that nitrogen vacancies are believed to act as donors in GaN, leading to increased saturation current and decreased gate current, although some studies report conflicting results with a reduction in current. This discrepancy may be attributed to variations in device structures, quality, and ionizing dose levels, where lower doses show improvements and higher doses result in damage. Additionally, the paper examines other ionizing species such as helium and carbon, revealing that these also increase ON-resistance, positively shift threshold voltage, and reduce saturation current in a manner proportional to the fluence, without affecting gate leakage. This manuscript is a peer-reviewed version, with the final online publication expected to differ after editing and typesetting.

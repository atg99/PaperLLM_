The paper discusses the effects of negatively ionized carbon acceptors and holes generated by impact-ionization on the dynamic on-resistance (R ON) of GaN-based power HEMTs. It highlights how the presence of these holes can discharge ionized carbon traps, thereby mitigating the increase in dynamic R ON. Figure 75 illustrates the dynamic R ON variations measured in GaN-based power HEMTs, along with a schematic representation of the mechanisms involved. The figure includes pulsed-IV curves and the dependence of on-resistance on trapping bias across different devices on the wafer. The findings contribute to understanding the trapping phenomena and degradation mechanisms in GaN-based power HEMTs, with references to the original research for further details.

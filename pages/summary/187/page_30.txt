The paper discusses the calculation of sheet charge density in a GaN/AlGaN heterostructure, utilizing key equations related to electric potential and band diagrams. It begins with the expression for the potential \( V_{GaN} \) in relation to the permittivity of GaN, charge density, and other parameters. The author combines this with a band diagram analysis to derive the relationship between surface potential and electric fields, leading to the calculation of sheet charge density \( n_s \).

The dependence of \( n_s \) on gate voltage \( V_G \) is explored, showing that applying a positive voltage fills the channel with charge, while negative voltages deplete it. The final equation expresses \( n_s \) as a function of \( V_G \), indicating how the charge varies with changes in gate voltage and other parameters. This work emphasizes the modulation of two-dimensional electron gas (2DEG) properties via external voltage, contributing to the understanding of electronic behavior in GaN-based devices.

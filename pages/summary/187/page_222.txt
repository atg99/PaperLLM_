The references provided focus on the reliability and degradation mechanisms of p-GaN gate high electron mobility transistors (HEMTs). Key studies highlight the temperature-dependent degradation of these devices under various stress conditions, including static and dynamic positive gate stress. The dependence on gate geometry and magnesium concentration is also explored, revealing implications for failure modes. Overall, the research underscores the critical need for further investigation into the degradation behaviors of p-GaN gate HEMTs to enhance their reliability in practical applications.

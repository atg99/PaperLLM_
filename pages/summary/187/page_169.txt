The paper discusses the effects of electric stress on the performance and reliability of AlGaN/GaN HEMTs and gallium nitride-based power transistors. It presents two figures illustrating the consequences of SEMI-ON-stress. Figure 94 shows an increase in the amplitude of the drain-lag transient and a corresponding rise in defect density as detected by CI D-DLOS. Figure 95 compares resistive and hard switching behaviors under different stress voltages, highlighting that power dissipation peaks with increased voltage during the turn-on transition.

Additionally, the paper addresses the issue of gate injection transistors (GITs), where a destructive feedback loop caused by hot electrons during SEMI-ON-stress can lead to device failure. The introduction of hybrid drain gate injection transistors (HD-GITs) is proposed as a solution to mitigate this issue by managing the electric field at the drain side. The findings emphasize the critical relationship between electric stress and device degradation, providing insights into improving the reliability of these semiconductor devices.

The paper provides a comprehensive overview of GaN-based power devices, highlighting their physics, technology, and reliability. It begins by discussing the key properties of GaN and detailing the characteristics of both lateral and vertical GaN transistors, emphasizing the advancements made possible through device scaling and monolithic integration, which enhance high-frequency operation and miniaturization. The second part delves into trapping and reliability issues, explaining the physical origins of traps in GaN and the primary degradation mechanisms that affect device performance. The paper serves as a valuable resource for understanding current and future developments in GaN electronics, supported by a wide range of referenced literature.

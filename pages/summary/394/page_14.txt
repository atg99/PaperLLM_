This section presents evaluation results for various YOLO model versions applied to a ships and vessels dataset. The table includes metrics such as precision, recall, mean Average Precision (mAP) at thresholds 50 and 50-95, as well as preprocessing, inference, postprocessing, total time, model size, and GFLOPs. 

Key findings indicate that YOLOv9m achieves the highest precision at 0.668 and mAP50 at 0.529, while YOLOv10n shows the lowest precision at 0.584. In terms of mAP50-95, YOLOv10m ranks highest with a score of 0.322. The models vary in their processing times, with YOLOv3u taking the longest total time of 7.3 seconds, while YOLOv8n is the most efficient with a total time of 2.3 seconds. Overall, the YOLO series demonstrates a range of performance and efficiency levels, underscoring the trade-offs between accuracy and computational demands across different versions.

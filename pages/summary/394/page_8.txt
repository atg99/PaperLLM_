The evaluation results of various YOLO models on the Traffic Signs dataset reveal significant insights regarding their performance metrics, including precision, recall, mean Average Precision (mAP50), and mAP50-95. YOLOv5ul achieved the highest accuracy with an mAP50 of 0.866 and a mAP50-95 of 0.799, while YOLOv10n recorded the lowest at 0.722 for mAP50 and 0.64 for mAP50-95. The models exhibited a range of precision, indicating varying capabilities in detecting traffic signs of different sizes.

The analysis also highlighted a trade-off between precision and recall, where larger models like YOLO11m and YOLO10l demonstrated superior performance compared to smaller models, which generally underperformed. YOLOv10n emerged as the most computationally efficient model, with minimal processing time, while several YOLO9 models showed lower efficiency due to increased processing times and GFLOPs.

Overall, these metrics provide a comprehensive overview of the YOLO models' effectiveness for traffic sign detection, suggesting areas for improvement in handling signs of varying sizes and enhancing computational efficiency without compromising accuracy.

The evaluation results for various YOLO versions on the Africa Wildlife Dataset are presented in a comprehensive table that outlines key metrics including precision, recall, mean Average Precision (mAP) at 50 (mAP50), and mAP at 50-95 (mAP50-95). The results also include preprocessing, inference, and postprocessing times, alongside total time, model size, and GFLOPs.

Key findings indicate that YOLOv11n achieved the highest precision at 0.964 and a competitive mAP50 of 0.802, while YOLOv8s exhibited the highest recall at 0.88 and mAP50-95 at 0.812. YOLOv5un and YOLOv9c also performed notably well, maintaining high precision and recall values.

The results illustrate a trend where newer YOLO versions generally show improved performance metrics, with YOLOv10 and YOLOv11 series yielding higher scores across various indicators.

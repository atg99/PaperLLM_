The paper presents a comprehensive evaluation of various YOLO (You Only Look Once) models based on their performance across three datasets, focusing on accuracy, speed, GFLOPs count, and size. The mean Average Precision at different Intersection over Union (IoU) thresholds (mAP50-95) was used to rank the models, with processing time considered for speed rankings. The results are summarized in Table IX.

Key findings include:

1. **Accuracy**: The YOLO11m model consistently ranked highest in accuracy, benefiting from advanced features like C2PSA for contextual information preservation, large-kernel convolutions, and partial self-attention modules. In contrast, YOLOv3u-tiny performed the worst in accuracy, particularly in the Africa Wildlife and Ships and Vessels datasets, suggesting that YOLO11 models are preferable for high-accuracy applications.

2. **Performance of YOLO Families**: The YOLOv9 models show effectiveness in detecting objects of various sizes, although they face challenges with small objects in specific datasets. The YOLO10 family exhibited slightly lower accuracy than YOLO9 in certain datasets, attributed to its One-to-One Head approach which can struggle with overlapping objects.

Overall, the YOLO11 models are highlighted as the most reliable for applications where accuracy is critical, while the YOLOv9 models remain effective for broader object detection tasks.

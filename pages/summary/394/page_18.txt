This paper presents a benchmark study evaluating the performance of various YOLO (You Only Look Once) algorithms, specifically YOLO11, YOLOv10, and YOLOv9, across three diverse datasets: Traffic Signs, African Wildlife, and Ships and Vessels. The study aims to provide a comprehensive comparison of these models based on multiple metrics including Precision, Recall, Mean Average Precision (mAP), Processing Time, GFLOPs count, and Model Size.

Key findings indicate that the YOLO11 family consistently outperforms its predecessors, with YOLO11m offering an optimal balance of accuracy, efficiency, and model size. YOLOv10, while slightly less accurate, excels in processing speed, making it suitable for applications where efficiency is critical. YOLOv9 demonstrates good accuracy but at the expense of speed, making it appropriate for scenarios where precision is paramount.

The research addresses critical questions regarding the performance of YOLO algorithms on datasets with varying object characteristics, and identifies the strengths and limitations of each version. The results provide valuable insights for both industry and academia, guiding the selection of suitable algorithms for specific applications. Future research directions include optimizing YOLOv10 to enhance its accuracy while maintaining its speed advantage, as well as exploring advancements in architectural design to foster the development of superior YOLO algorithms.

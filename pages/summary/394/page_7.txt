paper discusses the architecture and performance evaluation of the YOLO11 model, emphasizing its new C3k2 blocks and C2PSA module. The datasets used for training, validation, and testing include a balanced dataset with 24 classes from 4381 to 3233 images, the Africa Wildlife dataset with 1504 images across 4 classes for large object detection, and the Ships dataset containing 13.4k images focused on small object detection.

The software setup includes Python, Ubuntu, CUDA, cuDNN, the Ultralytics YOLO Object Detection Library, and WandB for experiment tracking. The study evaluates YOLO models based on accuracy, computational efficiency, and size. Accuracy metrics include Precision, Recall, mAP50, and mAP50-95, which assess the model's predictive performance. Computational efficiency is measured through Preprocessing Time, Inference Time, Postprocessing Time, and GFLOPs to gauge the model's speed and efficiency.

The paper also outlines various versions and scaled versions of YOLO, providing insights into the parameters used in the experiments, such as the number of epochs, optimizer, batch size, image size, learning rates, and dropout rate.

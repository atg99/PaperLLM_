c) GFLOPs and Processing Time: The analysis of processing times reveals that YOLO models exhibit varying efficiencies, with total processing times segmented into preprocessing, inference, and postprocessing stages. Results presented in Figures 10 and 11 indicate that while some models maintain a balanced processing time across these stages, others struggle with longer inference times, which may affect real-time application performance.

In summary, the YOLOv9 family demonstrates superior accuracy and efficiency in detecting large wildlife objects, with precision and recall values indicating strong performance. However, larger models show signs of overfitting, suggesting a need for careful consideration of model selection based on dataset size and characteristics.

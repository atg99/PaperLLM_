The paper presents methodologies for improving reinforcement learning techniques, specifically using Huber loss and normalized Q functions. In Section 5.1, the authors define the loss functions for Deep Q-Networks (DQN) and Mean Squared Bellman Error (MSBE) utilizing Huber loss, which is characterized by different formulations depending on the error's magnitude.

Section 5.3 introduces a strategy for normalizing the Q function, following Pohlen et al. (2018), and describes the transformation functions used to squash the Q values. The forward transformation T and its inverse are detailed, with the normalization parameter εT set to 0.01 for consistency in experiments. The updated loss functions for DQN and MSBE are presented, incorporating the transformed reward and Q values.

In reporting results, mean squared errors are consistently used as loss functions, simplifying the representation of the learning process. The paper also notes the implementation of double Q-learning, which modifies the max terms in the original equations but maintains the non-increasing property of the proposed method.

Lastly, the authors address exploration strategies, particularly in challenging environments. They employ a nuanced schedule for the ε parameter in the ε-greedy policy, gradually reducing exploration from 0.1 to 0.01 over a substantial number of steps. This careful tuning aims to balance exploration and exploitation, helping to avoid local optima during training. The exploration strategy is applied uniformly across all experiments conducted.

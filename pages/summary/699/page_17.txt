The paper presents an evaluation of the performance of a new reinforcement learning algorithm, C-DQN, on challenging Atari 2600 games, comparing it to other methods such as DQN, Rainbow DQN, and Agent57. Table 1 highlights the test results for various games, showing that C-DQN outperforms Rainbow DQN in four difficult games, demonstrating effective learning behavior. Notably, C-DQN achieves a state-of-the-art performance in the game Skiing, surpassing the results of Agent57 while using less than 0.1% of its computational resources. This finding suggests that C-DQN has a significant advantage in this specific game, despite the fact that a fair comparison with Rainbow DQN is complicated by differences in reward clipping methods. Overall, the results indicate that C-DQN is a promising approach in the field of reinforcement learning for Atari games.

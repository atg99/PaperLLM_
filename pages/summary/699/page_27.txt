The paper discusses a mechanism for updating the Q-value function in reinforcement learning, specifically focusing on the transition from state st to state st+1 with action at. The Q-value update is defined as Q(st; at) = rt + max_a' Q(st+1; a') - Q(st; at), ensuring that the greedy policy at st+1 remains unchanged after learning from the transition. The paper also notes that the Q-values for actions a1 and a2 at state st+1 are equal to -2Q(st; at), which further emphasizes the stability of the policy during the learning process. This approach aims to refine the learning strategy while maintaining the integrity of the decision-making policy.

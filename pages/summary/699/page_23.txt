This paper, presented at ICLR 2022, focuses on improving the Deep Q-Network (DQN) algorithm applied to the game Space Invaders. It discusses the effect of varying hyperparameters on the training performance and loss, particularly emphasizing the prioritization scheme based on Schaul et al. (2015). The authors set a small value for the prioritization hyperparameter \( p \) and a larger value for \( c_p \) to ensure consistent sampling probabilities, which helps stabilize learning despite fluctuations in total priority.

The paper details the method of assigning priorities to new data in the replay memory, where initial data is given a high priority to facilitate early learning. The authors also describe a strategy for updating priorities of transitions that accelerates learning by propagating information more efficiently through the network. 

Additionally, the paper examines the role of the discount factor in reinforcement learning, proposing a heuristic algorithm to estimate an appropriate discount factor for different tasks based on the frequency of reward signals within episodes. This approach accounts for the distribution of rewards over time, aiming to optimize learning efficiency by adapting the discount factor to task-specific characteristics.

Overall, the study presents a systematic approach to enhancing DQN performance through strategic prioritization and dynamic adjustment of learning parameters, thereby contributing valuable insights to the field of reinforcement learning.

The section discusses variable importance in a simulation model with 1,000 observations, focusing on the first nine input variables (V1 to V9). In Figure 4.2, histograms demonstrate that variables V1 to V6 are consistently identified as the most important, even as the number of dimensions increases. This indicates that the model effectively maintains the significance of these variables despite a growing dataset.

In the second row of Figure 4.3, it is noted that even when the number of variables exceeds the number of observations (p > n), the overall assessment of variable importance remains satisfactory. However, variable V4's importance becomes indistinguishable from that of out-of-model variables when the number of variables reaches 200 or 500, suggesting its limited informativeness in this context. This highlights the challenges in variable selection when dealing with high-dimensional data.

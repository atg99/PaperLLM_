In this section, the paper discusses the impact of the parameter mtry on variable importance (VI) in the context of analyzing ozone data. Figure 4.12 illustrates how VI evolves with varying mtry values of 2, 6, 8, and 10, while Figure 4.11 focuses on mtry equal to 4. The key observation is that when mtry reaches 4, the likelihood of selecting one of the highly correlated variables (V9, V8, V12, V10) for node splitting increases significantly. This high correlation among these variables, along with their relationship to the response variable V4, leads to a reduction in tree diversity as mtry increases. The section also notes the intention to analyze genomic data, referencing previous sections for context and indicating the use of the randomForest package and the vac18 dataset.

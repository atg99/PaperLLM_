The paper discusses the application of the VSURF method for variable selection in gene expression analysis. The analysis utilized a seed for reproducibility and executed the VSURF algorithm with parallel processing on a PSOCK cluster, utilizing three cores. The summary of results indicates that the computation time for VSURF was approximately 1.5 minutes, with specific times recorded for each step: 45.9 seconds for the thresholding step, 38 seconds for the interpretation step, and 7 seconds for the prediction step. Ultimately, 97 variables were selected at the thresholding step, followed by 23 at the interpretation step, and 13 at the prediction step. The use of three cores resulted in a significant reduction in execution time, demonstrating the efficiency of parallel processing in this context.

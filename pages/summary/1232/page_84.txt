This section presents an analysis of the importance of various variables related to pollutants and meteorological conditions across different monitoring stations. The tables highlight the effects of specific pollutants and meteorological factors, categorized as unfavorable (def), favorable (fav), and non-monotonic (def-fav) concerning PM10 pollution.

Table 4.2 illustrates the significance of different pollutants and meteorological variables for each station. For instance, the GCM station in Rouen shows high importance for SO2 and PL, while the REP station in Le Havre indicates a notable impact from NO2.

Table 4.3 introduces the variable PM10_AIL into the Random Forest (RF) model, revealing its considerable importance, ranging from 64 to 86 across stations. The analysis shows that while the importance of pollutants remains relatively stable, the significance of meteorological variables significantly decreases upon including PM10_AIL. This suggests that PM10 concentrations at AIL are influenced by diffuse pollution, reflecting local contributions without disturbing the importance of local markers, thus emphasizing the complex interplay between local pollution sources and meteorological factors.

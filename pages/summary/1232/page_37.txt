The section discusses a decision tree model applied to ozone data, highlighting the relationships between various predictors and ozone concentrations. Key predictors include temperature (V8 or V9), which correlates strongly with ozone levels, and the month (V1), indicating higher ozone concentrations during summer months. The analysis reveals that V2, representing the day of the month, plays a significant role in tree splits despite its relationship being influenced by sampling effects, due to its nominal nature with numerous values. Predictions from the tree are interpretable; cold days yield low ozone predictions, while hot days result in higher predictions. The section concludes with a focus on optimizing the tree structure through pruning, illustrated by the sequence of pruned subtrees derived from the initial maximal tree.

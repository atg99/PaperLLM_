This section discusses the marginal effect of nitrogen oxide (NO) on PM10 concentration, utilizing data from the JUS station and a random forest model. The command provided computes the marginal effect of the NO variable, allowing for similar calculations for other variables. The forestFloor package is suggested for further analysis, particularly for examining interactions between pairs of variables through 3D graphics and color gradients.

The analysis across various monitoring stations reveals key conclusions about the effects of pollutants and meteorological variables on PM10 levels. Notably, the intensity of partial effects from meteorological variables is generally lower than those from pollutants. The study indicates that the effects of pollutants tend to increase in a roughly linear manner, with variations in shape depending on the specific pollutant and station. Conversely, the effect of rain is consistently decreasing, highlighting its role in reducing pollution levels through a cleaning effect.

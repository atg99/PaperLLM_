In the acknowledgements section, the authors express gratitude to several individuals and groups who contributed to the publication of the English version of their book "Les forêts aléatoires avec R." They specifically thank Eva Hiripi for the initial suggestion to publish the English edition. The authors also acknowledge their colleagues Sylvain Arlot, Servane Gey, Christine Tuleau-Malot, and Nathalie Villa-Vialaneix for their collaborative discussions on the topics covered in the book. They extend thanks to Nicolas Bousquet and Fabien Navarro for their assistance in providing a preliminary translation, facilitated by a tool developed for large-scale automatic translation in 2018. The authors clarify that they hold full responsibility for the final translated version. Lastly, they express appreciation to three anonymous reviewers for their valuable feedback and suggestions.

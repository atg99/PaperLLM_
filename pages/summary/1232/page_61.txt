The section presents visual examples of Out-Of-Bag (OOB) error evolution in relation to the number of trees in a random forest and the parameter mtry. Figure 3.10 illustrates the OOB error as the number of trees increases, while Figure 3.11 shows the average OOB error across 25 forests as a function of mtry. The results indicate a general decrease in OOB error with increasing mtry, stabilizing around the value of p/3. The authors note a preference for the syntax "x=, y=" over "formula=, data=" when using the randomForest() function, citing improved computation speed, particularly in high-dimensional datasets.

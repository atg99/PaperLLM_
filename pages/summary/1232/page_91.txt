The paper discusses variable selection using the VSURF function applied to toy data. It presents graphical illustrations of variable importance (VI) measures, including mean and standard deviation of VI across different variables. The analysis involves evaluating out-of-bag (OOB) error for nested predictive models. A threshold for variable selection is established based on the minimum predicted value from a CART model, indicated by a horizontal dotted red line in the graphs. Variables with an average VI exceeding this threshold are retained for further analysis, ensuring that only significant predictors are included in the model.

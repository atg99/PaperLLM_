The conclusion of the paper presents two key points regarding the relationship between pollutants and weather variables. First, it emphasizes that the effects of pollutants are consistently increasing, making them valuable markers for local pollution in dust modeling. Second, it categorizes weather variables based on their impact on pollution levels. Specifically, it identifies variables with a consistently unfavorable effect (PL and VV, except at station AIL), those with a favorable effect (GT, PA, and VV at AIL), and those that exhibit both unfavorable and favorable effects depending on temperature conditions (T and DV). The study aims to further explore the relative importance of these variables in future sections.

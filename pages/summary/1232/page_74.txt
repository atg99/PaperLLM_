In this section, the focus is on the relationship between tree diversity and variable importance, specifically using stumps (two-leaf trees) in a Bagging predictor applied to spam data. The analysis begins by examining the effect of the parameter mtry on the variables at the root node. A Bagging model is created with stumps, and variable importance is computed. The results indicate that most variables are deemed unimportant, with only charExclamation and charDollar showing significance, reflecting their role in splitting the root node.

The study then compares this Bagging model to a Random Forest (RF) model constructed with stumps using a default mtry value. The variable importance plots for both models reveal that the same two variables are crucial, confirming their relevance in the classification task. Overall, the findings illustrate how tree diversity influences the assessment of variable importance in predictive modeling.

The paper discusses variable selection using the VSURF method, focusing on adjusting the thresholding and interpretation steps without recalculating. The nmj parameter represents the multiplying factor of the mean jump due to noise variables. The tune() method can be applied to the results of VSURF_thres() to set a threshold for variable selection, allowing for a reduction from 36 to 16 selected variables by adjusting the nmin parameter. Similarly, the tune() method can be used with VSURF_interp() to refine the interpretation step, resulting in a decrease from 4 to 3 selected variables by modifying the nsd parameter. To adjust the prediction step, the VSURF_pred() function can be restarted with a new nmj value, leading to 365 selected variables. An example is provided for predicting ozone concentration, referencing a related dataset.

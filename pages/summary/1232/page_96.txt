The VSURF package is built on the randomForest function, inheriting key parameters such as mtry and ntree. Additional RF parameters can be passed directly to randomForest during the VSURF execution. For instance, setting maxnodes to 2 limits the trees to two leaves. 

In a sample computation, VSURF identified 14 variables during the thresholding step, 8 in the interpretation step, and 2 in the prediction step, with a total computation time of 31.3 seconds. 

VSURF includes specific parameters: 
- nfor.thres (default 50) controls the number of trees in the thresholding step, crucial for quality estimates.
- nfor.interp and nfor.pred (default 25) stabilize out-of-bag error estimates in interpretation and prediction steps.
- nmin sets a multiplying factor for the standard deviation of noise variable importance, influencing the threshold for variable selection.
- nsd allows for a stricter selection rule, reducing the number of variables retained in interpretation. 

These parameters allow users to customize the variable selection process effectively.

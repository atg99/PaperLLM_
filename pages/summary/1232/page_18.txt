This paper introduces the application of Random Forests in analyzing weather and pollution data from six monitoring stations. The weather variables include rainfall, wind speed (both maximum and average), wind direction (maximum and dominant), temperature (minimum, maximum, and average), temperature gradient between Le Havre and Rouen, atmospheric pressure, and relative humidity (minimum, maximum, and average). The pollutants examined include dust (PM10), nitrogen oxides (NO, NO2) for urban pollution, and sulfur dioxide (SO2) for industrial pollution. Additional pollutant data from nearby stations is incorporated for specific sites: SO2 from JUS for GUI, SO2 from the MAS station for REP, and NO and NO2 from the MAS station for HRI. The paper demonstrates how to load the data for the JUS station using the VSURF package in R.

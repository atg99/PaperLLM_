The paper discusses a variable selection process using the VSURF method, focusing on genomic data analysis. Initially, three variables deemed of negative importance were removed, leading to the selection of a five-variable model that retains the most significant predictors. The selected variables are identified as 9, 8, 12, 1, and 11. The prediction step did not eliminate any further variables, suggesting that while some variables may be unnecessary, their impact is not substantial in this dataset.

The methodology emphasizes prediction quality as the main criterion for variable relevance, specifically through its improvement after permutation. The analysis utilizes the vac18 dataset, which contains gene expressions and associated stimuli. The VSURF package was employed with default parameters, including a specific setting for mtry, to accommodate the high-dimensional nature of the data. The overall computation took approximately 3.1 minutes.

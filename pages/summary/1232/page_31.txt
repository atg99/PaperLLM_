The section discusses the rpart package, focusing on the optimal pruned tree for spam data. It presents a series of thresholds for various features that help in classifying messages as spam or not. Key features include the frequency of specific characters such as exclamation marks and dollar signs, the presence of words like "free" and "money," and numerical indicators like "num000." The optimal tree (Fig. 2.7) shows the conditions under which a message is classified as spam, while Fig. 2.8 illustrates a "1-SE" pruned tree, which balances model complexity and predictive accuracy. Both figures depict the decision rules that guide the classification process, emphasizing the importance of feature selection in spam detection.

This paper outlines a series of concise and affordable books focused on the application of R for practitioners in various fields. Each book will concentrate on specific subject areas, such as epidemiology, econometrics, or psychometrics, as well as statistical topics like missing data and longitudinal data. The series aims to integrate LaTeX and R, allowing the inclusion of code for figures and tables on a dedicated website. Authors are expected to build on a foundational understanding provided by Dalgaard's Introductory Statistics with R or similar introductory texts, avoiding the repetition of basic concepts. More information about the series can be found at the provided Springer link.

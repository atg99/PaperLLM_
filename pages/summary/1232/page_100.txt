The section discusses the application of the VSURF() function on the Vac18 data, highlighting the selection process of variables through three steps: thresholding, interpretation, and prediction. In the thresholding step, 93 variables were retained, reflecting a strong parsimony in the data as illustrated in the related graph. The interpretation step further narrowed the selection to 24 variables, while the final prediction step identified 10 key variables. The identifiers of these selected variables, which correspond to biochip probes for gene expression measurement, are provided. Additionally, it is noted that the VSURF() function can be executed in parallel to enhance computational efficiency.

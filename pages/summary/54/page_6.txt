The NSL-KDD dataset is an improved version of the KDD Cup’99 datasets, designed to eliminate redundant records for more accurate classification outcomes. This dataset includes KDDTrain + and KDDTest +, both provided in CSV format with labels for attack types and difficulty levels. In an experiment referenced, three subsets of the KDD training set were created with fifty thousand records, and 21 learning machines were utilized to label the entire training and testing sets. Each record received 21 predicted labels, with the difficulty level indicating the number of correct predictions made by the machines, reaching a maximum of 21.

The dataset categorizes attacks into major types, including Denial of Service (DoS), User to Root (U2R), Remote to Local (R2L), and Probing, with various subcategories under each. Additionally, the dataset details the total instances per attack type, highlighting the predominance of certain attacks such as Neptune with over 41,000 instances and Normal with over 67,000 instances. This structure and the comprehensive labeling contribute to the dataset's utility in developing and testing intrusion detection systems.

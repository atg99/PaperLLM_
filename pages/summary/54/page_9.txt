Mutation: To enhance population diversity and avoid premature convergence, the GA introduces mutation by flipping attribute values in the individuals. In this study, the probability of flipping each attribute is set to 0.05.

In summary, the paper discusses the implementation of a Genetic Algorithm (GA) for optimal feature selection in the context of Network Intrusion Detection Systems (NIDS). The goal is to reduce misclassification rates, training times, and improve classifier accuracy by identifying a relevant subset of features from the NSL-KDD dataset. The GA successfully filtered the original 122 features down to 99 relevant features, using accuracy as the fitness function. The process involves initializing a population of feature-sets, evaluating their fitness using logistic regression, selecting individuals for the next generation, performing crossover, and applying mutation to foster diversity in the feature selection process. The findings highlight the effectiveness of GA in enhancing the performance of predictive models in classification tasks.

This paper presents a comprehensive survey of Neural Architecture Search (NAS), focusing on the challenges and solutions within the field. The authors critique previous surveys for their reliance on basic components of NAS, which can obscure the underlying research ideas and connections among various works. In response, this survey organizes the discussion around specific challenges and their corresponding solutions, making it more accessible and insightful for readers.

Key contributions of this survey include its novel approach of categorizing NAS research based on challenges rather than standard components, offering a systematic review of existing work and optimization strategies. The authors analyze the performance of NAS methods and identify future directions for research, including potential issues that require attention, such as hyperparameter search.

The structure of the paper is organized into several sections: an overview of early NAS characteristics, a detailed analysis of optimization strategies addressing identified challenges, a performance comparison of existing NAS methods, a discussion on future research directions, an examination of review threats, and a concluding summary.

Overall, this survey aims to provide a clearer understanding of the complex landscape of NAS research, highlighting the evolution of strategies and the interconnections among different approaches.

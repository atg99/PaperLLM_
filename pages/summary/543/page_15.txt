The paper presents a comprehensive survey on Neural Architecture Search (NAS), focusing on the challenges and solutions in the field. It discusses various methods for architecture search, particularly emphasizing network transformation techniques. One highlighted approach is EAS, which utilizes a Bi-LSTM as a meta-controller to encode existing network layers and learn low-dimensional feature representations. This method employs multiple actor networks, such as Net2Wider and Net2Deeper, to decide on transformations like deepening or widening layers through reinforcement learning.

The paper also references NASH-Net, which builds on the Net2Net framework by generating sub-networks from a pre-trained network using network morphism. This method includes a short training phase to identify the best sub-network, followed by an iterative process using hill-climbing techniques for optimal architecture discovery. Overall, the survey provides insights into the evolving methodologies for NAS, highlighting the integration of reinforcement learning and network morphisms in improving neural network architectures.

This paper presents a comprehensive survey on Neural Architecture Search (NAS), emphasizing its importance in deep learning for optimizing neural network design, which is crucial for achieving better feature representation and performance. The authors highlight the challenges faced by researchers due to reliance on prior knowledge and experience in architecture design, suggesting that reducing human intervention through automated algorithms like NAS could lead to more effective model designs.

The survey categorizes existing NAS research based on key components such as search space, search strategy, and evaluation strategy. However, the authors propose a new approach by first examining the characteristics of early NAS algorithms, identifying their shortcomings, and discussing subsequent solutions developed in related research. The paper includes a detailed analysis and comparison of these works, aiming to provide a clearer understanding of the challenges and landmark contributions in the field.

Furthermore, the authors outline potential future research directions, contributing to the ongoing evolution of NAS methodologies. The survey serves as a valuable resource for researchers seeking to navigate the complexities of NAS and its applications in various domains of deep learning.

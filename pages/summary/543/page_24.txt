The paper presents a comprehensive comparison of the performance of state-of-the-art Neural Architecture Search (NAS) methods against mainstream artificial networks on the CIFAR-10 dataset. The NAS methods are categorized based on their search techniques, including reinforcement learning, evolutionary algorithms, gradient optimization, random search, and sequential model-based optimization. The optimization strategies employed in NAS are analyzed, and the results are summarized in Table 2.

The table lists various search methods, their references, venues, optimization strategies, error rates, accuracy percentages, parameter counts, and GPU days required for training. Notable architectures such as NASNet-A and DenseNet-BC are highlighted alongside their performance metrics, demonstrating the effectiveness of different NAS approaches. The results indicate that NAS methods, particularly those employing advanced optimization strategies and augmentation techniques like Cutout, tend to achieve lower error rates and higher accuracy compared to traditional architectures. The paper emphasizes the evolving landscape of NAS and its potential in enhancing model performance on benchmark datasets.

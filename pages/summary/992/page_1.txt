The authors argue that traditional techniques should not be neglected as they still hold value, particularly in scenarios where deep learning models may not be fully optimized, such as in Panoramic Vision and 3D vision.

The introduction highlights the transformative impact of deep learning on digital image processing, particularly through methods like Convolutional Neural Networks, which have achieved remarkable results in tasks such as image classification. However, the paper raises critical questions about the relevance of traditional computer vision techniques in light of deep learning's dominance.

The authors emphasize that deep learning does not provide solutions for all computer vision challenges and that there are instances where traditional approaches, which focus on global features, may yield better outcomes. They advocate for a combined approach, leveraging both deep learning and traditional methods to enhance overall performance in computer vision tasks. The paper reviews several recent hybrid methodologies that illustrate the potential of integrating these two paradigms to address complex problems effectively.

The paper discusses the differences between 2D and 3D Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), highlighting the increased memory requirements and computational complexity associated with 3D CNNs due to their larger input sizes and three-dimensional kernel convolutions. This complexity escalates cubically with resolution. Additionally, the paper notes that 3D computer vision poses greater challenges than 2D image processing, as extra dimensions introduce uncertainties such as occlusions and variations in camera angles. The challenges of 3D object detection in point clouds are also emphasized, citing issues like discrete sampling, noisy scans, occlusions, and cluttered scenes. It mentions that while FFT-based methods can optimize 3D CNNs and reduce computational demands, they require more memory. Recent research trends in this area are briefly acknowledged.

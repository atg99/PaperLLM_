The paper discusses advancements in self-training and pseudo-labeling techniques for improving model accuracy and robustness in machine learning, particularly in image classification. It highlights the teacher-student model approach, where a teacher model generates soft proxy labels for unlabeled data, allowing a student model to learn from both labeled and unlabeled data. This iterative process enhances model robustness against perturbations. 

Yalniz et al. focus on improving ResNet-50 accuracy through self-training, while Xie et al. emphasize the benefits of using a student-teacher framework. The paper also notes the successful application of self-training in various tasks beyond image classification, including semantic segmentation and machine translation.

In the context of pseudo-labeling, the paper outlines methods to generate proxy labels that enhance learning, addressing challenges like confirmation bias that can lead to overfitting. Techniques such as uncertainty weighting, label propagation, and regularization methods like MixUp are discussed as effective strategies to refine the learning process and mitigate biases in label predictions.

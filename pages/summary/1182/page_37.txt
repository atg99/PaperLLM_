This collection of references focuses on various methodologies and advancements in semi-supervised learning, graph representation learning, and data augmentation techniques in machine learning. Key contributions include Grarep, which emphasizes global structural information in graph representations, and deep neural networks for learning these representations. The works also explore the integration of semi-supervised learning in diverse applications, such as neural machine translation and RGB-D object recognition. 

Notable advancements in contrastive learning frameworks and data augmentation strategies, such as Autoaugment and Randaugment, are discussed, highlighting their effectiveness in improving model performance. The significance of adversarial learning techniques and variational autoencoders in unsupervised and semi-supervised contexts is also emphasized. 

Overall, these studies collectively enhance understanding and development of robust machine learning models that leverage both labeled and unlabeled data, thereby addressing challenges in noisy labels and representation learning.

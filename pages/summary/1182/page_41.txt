The references listed cover a range of topics in machine learning, particularly focusing on semi-supervised learning, domain adaptation, and representation learning. Key contributions include:

1. **DeepWalk** by Perozzi et al. introduces online learning of social representations through random walks on graphs.
2. **Meta Pseudo Labels** by Pham et al. presents a method to improve model performance using pseudo-labels generated from a teacher model.
3. **Deep Co-Training** by Qiao et al. explores semi-supervised image recognition by leveraging multiple models to enhance learning.
4. **Dataset Shift** by Quionero-Candela et al. discusses the challenges posed by dataset shifts in machine learning applications.
5. **Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)** by Radford et al. outlines unsupervised representation learning techniques using GANs.
6. **Ladder Networks** by Rasmus et al. propose a semi-supervised learning framework that combines supervised and unsupervised learning.
7. **Weak Supervision** by Ratner et al. introduces a paradigm for machine learning that utilizes weakly labeled data.
8. **Stochastic Backpropagation** by Rezende et al. discusses techniques for approximate inference in deep generative models.
9. **Active Learning** by Settles surveys methods and techniques for selecting the most informative data points for labeling.
10. **FixMatch** by Sohn et al. simplifies semi-supervised learning by using consistency and confidence in predictions.

These works collectively advance the understanding of how to effectively train models with limited labeled data, adapt to new domains, and improve representation learning methods.

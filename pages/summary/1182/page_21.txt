The paper discusses MixMatch, a semi-supervised learning method that enhances model training using both labeled and unlabeled data through a series of steps. 

1. **Data Augmentation**: Labeled examples undergo transformations to generate augmented versions, while unlabeled examples are augmented multiple times to create several versions.
   
2. **Label Guessing**: Predictions for the augmented versions of unlabeled examples are generated and averaged to create proxy labels.

3. **Sharpening**: The proxy labels are refined to produce more confident predictions by adjusting the temperature of the categorical distribution.

4. **MixUp**: Two batches are created: one from augmented labeled examples and another from augmented unlabeled examples with sharpened proxy labels. These batches are mixed using a modified MixUp operation to generate new labeled and unlabeled batches.

The model is then trained using standard semi-supervised learning losses, combining supervised and unsupervised losses through cross-entropy and mean squared error.

The paper also introduces ReMixMatch, which enhances MixMatch with two techniques: distribution alignment, which ensures that the predictions on unlabeled data align with the ground-truth label distribution, and augmentation anchoring, which aligns outputs from strongly augmented inputs with those from weakly augmented versions.

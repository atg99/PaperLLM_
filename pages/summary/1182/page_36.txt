The paper provides an overview of semi-supervised learning (SSL), detailing its main approaches and assumptions within a deep learning framework. It categorizes SSL techniques into four primary groups: consistency regularization, generative models, graph-based methods, and holistic approaches. The authors highlight the increasing interest in data-efficient deep learning algorithms and suggest that deep SSL methods may soon match the performance of fully supervised techniques, with potential applications across various systems and learning paradigms. The paper concludes with acknowledgments for support and feedback received during its development.

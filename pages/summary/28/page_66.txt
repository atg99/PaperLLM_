2. Arulkumaran et al. (2017) provided a brief survey of deep reinforcement learning, highlighting its methodologies and applications.
3. Socher et al. (2013, 2011) explored recursive deep models for semantic analysis and natural language parsing, showcasing the versatility of neural networks in understanding complex data structures.
4. Goller and Kuchler (1996) discussed learning task-dependent representations through backpropagation, which is foundational for training neural networks effectively.
5. Recent works, such as those by Hewamalage et al. (2020) and Gao et al. (2019), focused on recurrent neural networks for time series forecasting and target tracking, respectively, indicating the ongoing evolution and application of these models in real-world scenarios.
6. The references also include significant advancements in convolutional neural networks (CNNs), with studies on their efficiency and application in various fields, including speech recognition (Palaz et al., 2019) and biometric identification (Jhong et al., 2020).
7. Optimization techniques, such as batch normalization and gradient descent algorithms, are discussed, reflecting the technical challenges and solutions in training deep learning models.

Overall, these references collectively illustrate the rapid development and diverse applications of neural network architectures, particularly in enhancing performance in classification tasks and other complex problem-solving scenarios in artificial intelligence.

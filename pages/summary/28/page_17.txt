The paper discusses key components of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), focusing on their efficiency and functionality. The authors highlight three main aspects: 

1. **Weight Sharing**: In CNNs, weight sharing eliminates the need for separate weights between neighboring neurons, allowing a single group of weights to operate across the entire input matrix. This significantly reduces training time and computational costs.

2. **Pooling Layer**: The pooling layer performs sub-sampling of feature maps generated by convolutional operations, reducing their size while retaining essential information. Various pooling methods are available, including max pooling, min pooling, and global average pooling. However, the pooling layer can sometimes negatively impact CNN performance by focusing too much on feature location rather than the relevant information itself.

3. **Activation Function**: Activation functions are crucial for mapping inputs to outputs in neural networks. They determine whether a neuron should activate based on the weighted summation of its inputs and bias, effectively controlling the flow of information through the network.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the importance of these components in enhancing the performance and efficiency of CNNs.

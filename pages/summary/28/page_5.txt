The paper discusses the rising prominence of deep learning (DL) algorithms, particularly in the context of big data. It highlights the continuous advancements in DL that enhance various machine learning (ML) tasks, such as image super-resolution, object detection, and image recognition. The authors note that DL has recently surpassed human performance in image classification and has significantly impacted numerous scientific fields, leading to transformative changes in industries and businesses.

Key players in technology and the economy are actively seeking to enhance DL capabilities. The paper mentions that DL outperforms human capabilities in specific applications, including predicting car delivery times, loan certification decisions, and movie ratings. Notably, the Turing Award in 2019 was awarded to three pioneers in DL, underscoring the field's importance.

Despite the successes achieved, the authors emphasize that there is still room for further advancement in DL. They argue that DL has the potential to improve human life through enhanced accuracy in areas like medical diagnosis, natural disaster estimation, and drug discovery. A cited study demonstrates that a DL network can diagnose diseases with accuracy comparable to that of multiple board-certified dermatologists, indicating the technology's significant implications for healthcare.

The paper discusses the architectural innovations of ResNet in comparison to traditional highway networks. ResNet incorporates shortcut connections that facilitate cross-layer connectivity, which are both parameter-free and data-independent. Unlike highway networks, where layers can function without residual information when gated shortcuts are closed, ResNet maintains continuous residual information flow. This design helps mitigate gradient diminishing issues, enhancing deep network convergence. ResNet achieved significant recognition by winning the 2015 ILSVRC championship with a depth of 152 layers, surpassing VGG and AlexNet in depth while demonstrating lower computational complexity.

Additionally, the paper highlights the advancements introduced by Szegedy et al. with Inception-ResNet and Inception-V3/4, which are enhancements of the earlier Inception models. Inception-V3 aims to reduce computational costs without compromising the generalization capabilities of deeper networks. This is achieved by utilizing smaller asymmetric filters and implementing a bottleneck of 1x1 convolutions before larger filters, optimizing the traditional operational approach.

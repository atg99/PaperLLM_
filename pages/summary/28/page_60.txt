The paper discusses advancements in custom floating-point implementations, highlighting the potential of lowering to 8-bit precision for enhancing FPGA performance in deep neural network (DNN) models. It emphasizes the importance of evaluation metrics in deep learning tasks, which are crucial for optimizing classifiers during training and testing stages. 

During training, these metrics help in selecting the best classification algorithms by accurately predicting future evaluations. In the testing phase, they measure the classifier's efficiency using hidden data. Key evaluation metrics include:

1. **Accuracy**: The ratio of correctly predicted classes to total samples.
2. **Sensitivity (Recall)**: The fraction of actual positive cases correctly identified.
3. **Specificity**: The fraction of actual negative cases correctly identified.
4. **Precision**: The ratio of correctly predicted positive cases to all predicted positive cases.
5. **F1-Score**: The harmonic mean of precision and recall.
6. **J Score**: Known as Youden's J statistic, which combines sensitivity and specificity.

These metrics provide a comprehensive framework for assessing classifier performance, ensuring effective data classification.

The studies discussed highlight the significant advancements in using deep learning techniques, particularly convolutional neural networks (CNNs), for medical image classification and diagnosis. Various datasets, including chest X-rays and CT scans, have been utilized to train models for detecting lung diseases, pneumonia, and lung nodules, achieving high accuracy rates. Notably, CheXNet demonstrated exceptional performance in classifying multiple diseases, surpassing the diagnostic capabilities of radiologists. Additionally, the use of 3D CNNs for interpolating missing PET and MRI image data shows promise, despite challenges related to overfitting. The application of CNNs in differentiating between normal and Alzheimer's disease patients has also yielded impressive results, underscoring the potential of these technologies in enhancing diagnostic workflows in healthcare.

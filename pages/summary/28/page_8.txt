In this section, Alzubaidi et al. discuss the applicability and advantages of deep learning (DL) in various contexts, highlighting its potential to outperform human experts in certain scenarios. They outline specific situations where DL is particularly beneficial, such as when human experts are unavailable, when decisions cannot be easily explained, when problems evolve over time, when solutions need to be tailored to individual cases, and when the scale of the problem exceeds human reasoning capabilities.

The authors emphasize the reasons for adopting deep learning, including its universal learning approach, which allows it to be effective across diverse application domains, and its inherent robustness, as it does not require meticulously designed features; instead, it learns optimized features automatically. The discussion is framed within the broader context of advancements in artificial intelligence, underscored by a quote from Geoffrey Hinton, indicating the transformative potential of deep learning in various fields.

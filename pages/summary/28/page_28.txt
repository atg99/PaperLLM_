The paper discusses the significance of recent developments in Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and introduces the Network-in-Network model, which presents two key innovations. It highlights various CNN models, including MobileNet-v2, CapsuleNet, and HRNetV2, along with their main findings, datasets used, error rates, input sizes, and publication years. For instance, MobileNet-v2 employs an inverted residual structure, CapsuleNet focuses on relationships between features, and HRNetV2 is known for high-resolution representations. The discussion emphasizes the evolution and impact of these models on the field of deep learning and their applications.

This comprehensive approach to handling overfitting is crucial, as it ensures that deep learning models maintain their performance on unseen data, which is essential for their application in various fields, including healthcare and environmental science.

The paper outlines the challenges associated with deep learning models, particularly in terms of their computational and memory demands, which hinder their deployment in resource-constrained environments. It categorizes model compression techniques into four classes: parameter pruning, knowledge distillation, compact convolution filters, and low-rank factorization. Each technique aims to reduce the complexity of models while preserving their performance.

Furthermore, the discussion on overfitting emphasizes the importance of addressing this issue across different tasks in deep learning. The classification of techniques to handle overfitting includes adjustments to model architecture and parameters, enhancements to input data, and improvements in the training process. These strategies collectively aim to enhance the generalization capabilities of deep learning models, making them more effective in practical applications.

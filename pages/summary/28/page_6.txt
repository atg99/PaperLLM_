The paper discusses the advancements in deep learning (DL) technology, particularly in the context of medical diagnostics. It highlights that US board-certified general pathologists had an average accuracy of 61% in cancer detection, while Google AI surpassed this with an accuracy of 70%. The role of DL has expanded significantly, especially in the early diagnosis of COVID-19, where it has become a primary tool for automatic classification and detection in hospitals worldwide.

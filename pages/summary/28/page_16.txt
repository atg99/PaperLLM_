In their paper, Alzubaidi et al. discuss the advantages of convolutional layers in neural networks, particularly in contrast to fully connected (FC) networks. A key benefit highlighted is sparse connectivity, where each neuron in a convolutional layer connects to only a limited number of neurons in the subsequent layer. This results in a significantly reduced number of weights and connections, minimizing the memory required for storage. The authors also illustrate the fundamental calculations that occur at each step within a convolutional layer, emphasizing the efficiency and effectiveness of this architecture in processing data.

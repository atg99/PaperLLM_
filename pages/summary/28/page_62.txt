The paper discusses the challenges faced by deep learning (DL) in modeling complex multimodal data and highlights the need for substantial labeled datasets to effectively train models and predict unseen data. This challenge is exacerbated in scenarios requiring real-time data processing or when datasets are limited. The authors provide a table detailing benchmark datasets used in various applications, such as ImageNet for image classification and Microsoft COCO for object detection. Additionally, the paper lists common DL frameworks and libraries, including TensorFlow, Keras, and Theano, along with their licenses, core languages, and release years, showcasing the tools available for researchers in the field.

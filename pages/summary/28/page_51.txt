The paper discusses the applications of deep learning (DL) in healthcare, emphasizing its critical role due to the direct impact on human lives. It outlines various DL techniques such as object localization using bounding boxes, semantic segmentation for delineating object edges, and registration for aligning images. The authors highlight the significant performance of DL in medical image analysis, showcasing its potential for improving healthcare outcomes. Figures included in the paper illustrate examples of DL applications and the workflow of deep learning tasks, further supporting the discussion on its effectiveness in the medical field.

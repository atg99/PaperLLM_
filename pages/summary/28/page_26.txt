This section of the paper by Alzubaidi et al. discusses strategies for enhancing Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and reviews the evolution of CNN architectures from AlexNet to the High-Resolution model. Key strategies for improving CNN performance include data augmentation, transfer learning, extended training times, increased model depth or width, regularization, and hyperparameter tuning.

The authors highlight the significance of model architecture in optimizing application performance, noting that advancements in CNNs over the past decade have involved structural modifications and parameter optimizations. Notably, the depth of networks has been a crucial factor in performance improvements, largely driven by innovations in processing units and novel architectural blocks.

Starting with AlexNet, introduced in 2012, the paper illustrates its pivotal role in deep learning by achieving remarkable results in image recognition and classification. AlexNet's architecture features increased depth and multiple parameter optimization strategies, overcoming hardware limitations through the use of parallel GPUs. The model expanded feature extraction stages from five in LeNet to seven, addressing overfitting through techniques such as dropout, ReLU activation functions to mitigate the vanishing gradient problem, and local response normalization.

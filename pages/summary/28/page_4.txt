The paper by Alzubaidi et al. provides a comprehensive review of deep learning (DL) from 2010 to 2021, focusing on significant publications from reputable sources. The authors analyzed over 300 research papers, primarily from 2019 and 2020, to explore various aspects of DL, including its approaches, architectures, challenges, applications, and computational methods.

The paper outlines the structure of the review, which includes sections on the challenges of DL and alternative solutions, applications of DL, the impact of computational approaches such as CPU, GPU, and FPGA, evaluation metrics, and frameworks and datasets used in the field. The survey methodology involved a systematic search using specific keywords related to DL and its applications, ensuring a focus on recent advancements.

In the background section, the authors distinguish between DL and traditional machine learning, highlighting that DL automates feature learning through multiple layers of artificial neural networks, eliminating the need for manual feature selection. This capability allows DL to perform classification tasks more efficiently, making it a powerful tool for various applications.

Overall, the review serves as a valuable resource for understanding the evolution, challenges, and future directions of deep learning, emphasizing its growing significance in the field of artificial intelligence.

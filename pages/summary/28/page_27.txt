The table provides an overview of various Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) architectures, highlighting their main findings, depth, datasets used, error rates, input sizes, and the years they were introduced. 

Key models include:

- **AlexNet** (2012): Introduced Dropout and ReLU, achieving an error rate of 16.4 on ImageNet with an input size of 227x227x3.
- **NIN** (2013): Introduced the 'mlpconv' layer, achieving error rates of 10.41, 35.68, and 0.45 on CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and MNIST respectively, with an input size of 32x32x3.
- **VGG** (2014): Increased depth with small filter sizes, achieving a notable error rate of 7.3 on ImageNet.
- **GoogLeNet** (2015): Emphasized depth and block concepts, achieving an error rate of 6.7 on ImageNet.
- **ResNet** (2016): Featured skip links to combat overfitting, with an error rate of 3.57 on ImageNet.
- **Xception** (2017): Utilized depthwise and pointwise convolutions, achieving an impressive error rate of 0.055 on ImageNet.
- **DenseNet** (2017): Connected layers in blocks, achieving error rates of 3.46, 17.18, and 5.54 on CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and ImageNet respectively.

The table encapsulates the evolution of CNN architectures, showcasing advancements in error reduction and architectural innovations over the years.

The paper discusses various tasks related to natural language processing, specifically focusing on sentence pair classification. It presents examples of different datasets and the classification of sentence pairs in terms of their relationships. 

In the MRPC task, two sentences are analyzed for their semantic relationship, resulting in classifications such as contradiction or equivalence. For instance, one example led to a classification of contradiction due to differing perspectives on the reasons behind US action after September 11, while another example was classified as equivalent.

In the QNLI task, a question and a related sentence are assessed for entailment, where the classification indicates whether the sentence provides an answer to the question.

The QQP task involves comparing two questions to determine if they are semantically similar, showcasing how different types of inquiries can be evaluated for their relevance and connection.

Overall, the paper highlights the importance of understanding relationships between sentence pairs to improve natural language understanding models.

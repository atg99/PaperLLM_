The modifications made to the training approach, including the span-corruption objective and longer training duration, are aimed at enhancing performance on NLP benchmarks. Additionally, the exploration of varying model sizes addresses different computational resource constraints, offering flexibility for deployment in various environments. The findings suggest that scaling up model size correlates with improved performance, although smaller models remain valuable for specific applications where resources are limited. 

Overall, the systematic study provides insights into optimizing NLP models through strategic adjustments in training objectives, duration, and model architecture, with the goal of pushing the boundaries of transfer learning in NLP.

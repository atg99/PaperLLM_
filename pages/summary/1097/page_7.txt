The paper discusses the development and utilization of the Colossal CleanCrawledCorpus (C4), a large dataset created from web-extracted text, which is significantly larger than typical pre-training datasets at around 750 GB. This dataset is released as part of TensorFlow Datasets. The authors aim to evaluate general language learning abilities by assessing downstream performance across a variety of benchmarks, including machine translation, question answering, abstractive summarization, and text classification.

Key benchmarks utilized include GLUE and SuperGLUE, which consist of diverse text classification tasks designed to test general language understanding. These tasks encompass sentence acceptability judgment, sentiment analysis, paraphrasing, natural language inference, coreference resolution, sentence completion, word sense disambiguation, and various question answering datasets. The authors treat all tasks within each benchmark as a single task for fine-tuning purposes.

Additionally, the paper references specific datasets used for tasks such as text summarization (CNN/Daily Mail) and question answering (SQuAD). The comprehensive evaluation aims to explore the limits of transfer learning in natural language processing by leveraging the extensive C4 dataset and various established benchmarks.

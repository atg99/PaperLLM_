The paper presents examples of translation tasks from English to various languages, specifically focusing on the WMT (Workshop on Machine Translation) datasets. Each example illustrates the original input text, the processed input indicating the task of translation, and the original and processed target translations in the respective languages, including German, French, and Romanian. The translations demonstrate consistency between the original and processed targets, highlighting the accuracy of the translation process. The examples encompass a range of subjects, from personal anecdotes to scientific descriptions, emphasizing the versatility of translation applications across different contexts.

The paper compares the performance of various natural language processing models pre-trained on different datasets. The datasets include FC4, C4 unfiltered, RealNews-like, WebText-like, Wikipedia, and a combination of Wikipedia with the Toronto Books Corpus (TBC). Each dataset varies in size and content, affecting the model's performance on tasks such as GLUE, CNNDM, SQuAD, SGLUE, and translation tasks (EnDe, EnFr, EnRo).

Key findings show that the FC4 dataset, with a size of 745GB, achieved notable performance scores, particularly high on the SQuAD task. The RealNews-like dataset and WebText-like dataset also performed well, with the latter being created from Reddit submissions to ensure quality text content. The Wikipedia dataset, while reliable, was noted for its limited domain, leading to the combination with TBC to enhance diversity in training data.

The study emphasizes the significance of dataset selection and quality in training language models, showcasing how variations in data sources can lead to different performance outcomes across tasks.

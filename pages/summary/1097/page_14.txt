The paper presents a comparison of the performance of a baseline model across various natural language processing tasks, including GLUE, CNNDM, SQuAD, and translation tasks (EnDe, EnFr, EnRo). The results are summarized in Table 1, which shows average scores and standard deviations for both the baseline model and performance when training from scratch without pre-training. The baseline model achieves notable scores, such as 83.28 on GLUE and 80.88 on SQuAD, indicating competitive performance relative to existing models like BERT BASE.

The results demonstrate that pre-training significantly enhances performance across most benchmarks, except for WMT English to French, where the dataset's size limits the impact of pre-training. The authors underscore the importance of pre-training, especially for tasks with limited data, highlighting its role in improving data efficiency within the transfer learning paradigm. The paper also includes detailed metrics and comparisons, ensuring clarity in presenting their findings.

In Appendix B, the methodology for converting the WNLI dataset into a text-to-text format is explained. This transformation facilitates evaluation using models trained on WSC and DPR, while ensuring no training on WNLI data occurs. The process involves identifying pronouns in premises, matching them with substrings in hypotheses, and formatting the output accordingly.

Appendix C showcases example predictions from the model T5-11B on the CNN/Daily Mail validation set, demonstrating the model's ability to generate coherent summaries. These examples were randomly selected to illustrate the model's performance without bias.

The paper discusses the limits of transfer learning in natural language processing (NLP) and provides detailed preprocessing examples for various datasets. 

In the section on CoLA, the original sentence "John made Bill master of himself" is transformed into a processed input labeled as "acceptable." For the RTE dataset, the example compares the sentences regarding the ethnic composition of Slovenia, with the processed input indicating "not_entailment." In the MNLI dataset, a hypothesis about the St. Louis Cardinals is presented alongside a premise discussing their historical performance, maintaining the original context in the processed format.

Overall, the paper focuses on the methodologies used for preprocessing data in transfer learning, highlighting the importance of clear transformation while preserving the original meanings and relationships in the text.

In summary, our exploration into the limits of transfer learning reveals that larger models not only benefit from their increased size but also from other non-scaling factors. The text-to-text framework we developed allows for a unified approach to various NLP tasks, achieving state-of-the-art results without the need for task-specific architectures. We determined that the original encoder-decoder architecture is optimal in this context and that sharing parameters between the encoder and decoder can maintain performance while reducing the model's size. Additionally, we recommend utilizing unsupervised objectives that yield shorter target sequences for improved computational efficiency. Lastly, the introduction of the C4 dataset highlights the importance of high-quality data in enhancing model performance in transfer learning scenarios.

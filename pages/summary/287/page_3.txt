The paper presents Image-and-Spatial Transformer Networks (ISTNs), which enhance the capabilities of traditional Spatial Transformer Networks (STNs) by integrating a dedicated Image Transformer Network (ITN). The primary aim of this integration is to optimize image representations specifically for downstream registration tasks while simultaneously predicting transformation parameters. 

The authors address limitations in existing neural network-based image registration approaches, which typically treat the process as a one-pass, non-iterative task. Such methods often overlook the potential benefits of incorporating additional information, like segmentations or weak labels, during training. While some existing works attempt to leverage this extra information, they fail to retain or utilize it effectively during the testing phase.

ISTNs improve upon these methods by allowing the ITN to extract and retain significant information about regions of interest (SoIs), utilizing annotations only during training. This approach enables a more refined prediction of spatial transformation parameters. The paper outlines various training strategies for ISTNs, combining different loss functions to enhance performance. Overall, the ISTN framework aims to provide a more effective and flexible solution for image registration tasks by leveraging the strengths of neural networks and retaining valuable information throughout the process.

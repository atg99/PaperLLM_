This paper discusses various advancements in medical image registration, focusing on deep learning methodologies. It highlights the contributions of several key studies in the field, including weakly-supervised convolutional neural networks and frameworks for both affine and deformable image registration. Notable works such as Voxelmorph and BIRNet are mentioned for their innovative approaches to registration using fully convolutional networks. Additionally, the paper references the foundational work on spatial transformer networks, which has influenced the development of more complex models. Overall, the paper presents a comprehensive overview of the integration of deep learning techniques in enhancing image registration processes in medical imaging.

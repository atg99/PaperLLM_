The white paper titled "A White Paper on Neural Network Quantization" by Markus Nagel and colleagues from Qualcomm AI Research addresses the challenge of high computational costs associated with neural networks, particularly in the context of integrating these networks into edge devices with strict power and latency requirements. The paper emphasizes neural network quantization as an effective method to reduce power consumption and inference latency, although it acknowledges that quantization can introduce noise, potentially degrading model accuracy.

The authors present state-of-the-art algorithms aimed at mitigating quantization noise while maintaining low-bit weights and activations. They categorize these algorithms into two main classes: Post-Training Quantization (PTQ) and Quantization-Aware Training (QAT). PTQ is highlighted as a lightweight approach that does not require retraining or labeled data, often achieving 8-bit quantization with accuracy close to that of floating-point models. Conversely, QAT involves fine-tuning with labeled data, enabling lower bit quantization with competitive performance.

The paper provides detailed pipelines based on extensive experimentation and existing literature that demonstrate state-of-the-art performance for various deep learning models and tasks. The introduction sets the stage for the necessity of low-latency and energy-efficient neural networks in a wide range of electronic devices, underscoring the growing importance of quantization techniques in the field of machine learning.

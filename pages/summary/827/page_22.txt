This paper investigates the impact of different initialization techniques for quantization-aware training (QAT) in deep learning models, specifically focusing on ResNet18 and MobileNetV2. The results, presented in Table 8, show the accuracy of models under various fixed-point quantization (FP32) methods, comparing post-training quantization (PTQ) and QAT approaches.

The experiments reveal that the choice of initialization method for quantization plays a crucial role in the final accuracy of QAT. Using mean squared error (MSE) for weight initialization significantly enhances the starting accuracy compared to min-max initialization. However, this initial advantage diminishes over the course of training, as the accuracy converges after about 20 epochs. Similar trends are observed when evaluating activation quantization, where MSE initialization again provides a better start but shows diminishing returns as training progresses.

The findings suggest that while a better initialization method can lead to improved results in QAT, the benefits are modest and tend to decrease with extended training time. Overall, the study emphasizes the importance of initialization strategies in optimizing QAT performance for deep learning models.

The paper discusses the impact of bias correction and weight quantization techniques on the performance of MobileNetV2 and ResNet18 models, particularly focusing on their ImageNet validation accuracy. Table 4 presents the results of bias correction on both FP32 and INT8 models, showing that bias correction significantly improves accuracy in the INT8 model, reaching up to 71.19%. 

Figure 7 illustrates the correlation between the cost function derived from a quadratic unconstrained binary optimization (QUBO) problem and validation accuracy for 4-bit quantization of ResNet18. The authors highlight the effectiveness of stochastic rounding compared to nearest rounding, showing that careful rounding choices can lead to over 10% performance improvement. 

The paper introduces AdaRound, a systematic approach to optimize weight rounding choices in post-training quantization (PTQ). The method aims to minimize the quantization impact on task loss by formulating an optimization problem that evaluates the perturbation due to quantization. It utilizes a second-order Taylor series expansion for efficient optimization, leading to a Hessian-based QUBO problem. 

However, the authors note challenges in applying this method broadly due to the high memory and computational demands of calculating the Hessian. Overall, the paper emphasizes the importance of bias correction and advanced rounding techniques in enhancing model performance under quantization constraints.

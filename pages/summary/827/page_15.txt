The paper discusses the impact of various rounding methods on the performance of ResNet18 for ImageNet validation accuracy, highlighting the results in Table 5. It notes that the QUBO problem related to task loss is NP-Hard. To address this, the authors simplify the problem to a local optimization task that minimizes the mean squared error (MSE) of output activations for a layer, allowing for optimization without requiring Hessian computations or propagation information from subsequent layers.

The optimization problem is further relaxed into a continuous optimization form, which introduces a regularizer to ensure the optimized variables converge to valid discrete solutions. The final optimization problem incorporates the activation function and aims to minimize the difference between the actual and quantized outputs.

The AdaRound method is introduced as a solution for optimizing weight rounding during quantization. It is efficient, requiring minimal unlabeled data and no hyperparameter tuning or extensive fine-tuning, making it applicable to various neural network architectures. Overall, the rounding approach significantly influences network performance, and AdaRound offers a theoretically grounded and computationally efficient solution.

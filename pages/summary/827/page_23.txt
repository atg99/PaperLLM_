The paper discusses the effectiveness of various post-training quantization (PTQ) techniques and the establishment of a standard quantization-aware training (QAT) pipeline. The proposed QAT pipeline is built on extensive experimentation and literature, aiming to optimize low-bit quantization performance across various models. Key steps in the pipeline include applying CLE to models with imbalanced weight distributions, adding quantizers tailored to specific hardware needs, and initializing quantization parameters effectively to enhance training speed and accuracy. The paper emphasizes the importance of making quantization parameters learnable, particularly for low-bit quantization, and suggests careful consideration of learning rates when using certain optimizers.

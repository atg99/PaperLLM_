The paper discusses the performance of various models under different quantization schemes, specifically focusing on post-training quantization (PTQ) methods. It compares the accuracy of several models including ResNet18, ResNet50, MobileNetV2, InceptionV3, EfficientNet lite, DeeplabV3, EfficientDet-D1, and BERT-base across different quantization configurations: FP32, W8A8, W4A8, and per-channel quantization.

The results indicate that while per-channel quantization shows no significant advantage in higher bit-width configurations, it becomes beneficial at lower bit-widths, particularly for models with depth-wise separable convolutions. For instance, EfficientNet lite experiences a 2.8% accuracy improvement with per-channel quantization compared to per-tensor quantization, narrowing the gap to full-precision accuracy. Similar trends are observed for other models using depth-wise separable convolutions.

The paper also emphasizes the importance of debugging steps in the PTQ pipeline. These steps include conducting a sanity check between floating-point and quantized models, analyzing the impact of weight and activation quantization separately, and applying corrective measures if significant accuracy drops are identified, such as using a higher bit-width for weights in models that are sensitive to quantization.

Overall, the study demonstrates that while PTQ can maintain competitive performance across various models, careful consideration of quantization strategies, especially for specific architectures, is essential to minimize accuracy loss.

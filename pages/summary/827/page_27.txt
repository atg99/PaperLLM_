The references provided include significant contributions to the fields of computer vision and natural language processing, focusing on efficient model architectures and quantization techniques. 

Sandler et al. introduced MobileNetV2, which features inverted residuals and linear bottlenecks, enhancing efficiency in mobile applications. Sheng et al. proposed a quantization-friendly separable convolution tailored for MobileNets, emphasizing energy efficiency in embedded systems. Stock et al. revisited neural network quantization, contributing to the understanding of its implications on model performance.

Tan et al. presented EfficientDet, a scalable approach to object detection that balances accuracy and efficiency. Uhlich et al. explored mixed-precision deep neural networks, highlighting the importance of effective parameterization. van Baalen et al. unified quantization and pruning through Bayesian methods, offering a novel perspective on model optimization.

Wang et al. developed GLUE, a multi-task benchmark for natural language understanding, facilitating the evaluation of various NLP models. Collectively, these works advance the state of the art in model efficiency, quantization, and performance evaluation across multiple domains.

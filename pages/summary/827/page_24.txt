The paper discusses a standard quantization-aware training (QAT) pipeline used for various deep learning models, including ResNet, Inception, MobileNet, EfficientNet, DeeplabV3, EfficientDet, and BERT. The pipeline incorporates pre-trained floating-point models and quantization techniques such as symmetric weights and asymmetric activations. Performance metrics are provided for models quantized using different configurations, including W8A8, W4A8, and W4A4, evaluated on tasks like image classification and natural language processing.

Key findings indicate that for models without depth-wise separable convolutions, W8A8 and W4A8 quantization can achieve or exceed the accuracy of floating-point models, attributed to the regularizing effects of quantization noise and fine-tuning. However, for models with depth-wise separable convolutions, quantization proves more challenging, with significant accuracy drops observed when using W4A4 configurations. Per-channel quantization is shown to enhance performance, particularly for models like DeeplabV3 and MobileNetV2.

In the case of BERT-base, the QAT approach effectively manages high dynamic ranges, maintaining performance within 1% of the original scores when using W4A8. Nevertheless, combining low-bit weight and activation quantization (W4A4) results in a noticeable performance decline. Overall, the study highlights the complexities and strategies in quantizing neural networks while aiming to retain accuracy across various architectures and tasks.

This paper presents an ablation study focused on the post-training quantization (PTQ) of neural networks, specifically examining various methods for setting quantization ranges for weight quantizers while maintaining activations in FP32. The study evaluates the performance of two models, ResNet18 and MobileNetV2, across different bit-widths (W8, W6, W4) using both Min-Max and Mean Squared Error (MSE) techniques for range setting.

The results are summarized in Table 1, which indicates the average ImageNet validation accuracy over five runs for each method. The findings reveal that while the Min-Max method generally performs well, it is sensitive to outliers, which can lead to rounding errors. In contrast, the MSE-based method aims to minimize the error between the original and quantized tensors, providing a more robust approach to handle outliers.

The paper emphasizes that quantization range setting is a critical step in the PTQ process, as it directly impacts quantization error and model performance. Various practical methods for determining clipping thresholds are discussed, highlighting the trade-offs between clipping and rounding errors. The authors propose a standard PTQ pipeline that incorporates successful techniques to address common challenges, alongside debugging steps to enhance the performance of quantized models.

Overall, the paper underscores the importance of effective quantization strategies in neural network design, making it accessible for practitioners without requiring extensive expertise in quantization methods.

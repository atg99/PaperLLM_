The paper presents a flowchart for Post-Training Quantization (PTQ) debugging, illustrating the process of quantizing models effectively. The flowchart outlines the steps involved in quantizing all tensors, including activations and weights, while ensuring that the error between the floating-point and quantized model accuracy remains minimal. 

Key actions include setting the quantization parameters, adjusting range settings, and applying techniques such as CLEAdaRound and bias correction. The process involves iterative checks to determine if the error is acceptable. If the error exceeds the desired threshold, the model is refined by fixing issues in specific tensors before proceeding with quantization. 

The flowchart emphasizes the importance of visualizing tensor values and adjusting accordingly to achieve an optimal quantized model. Overall, the approach aims to minimize quantization error while maintaining model performance.

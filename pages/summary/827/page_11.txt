The paper discusses quantization in neural networks, specifically focusing on MobileNetV2. It highlights the challenges of imbalances in weight ranges across output channels that can negatively impact performance during post-training quantization (PTQ). The authors reference existing solutions by Nagel et al. and Meller et al., which utilize positive scaling equivariance in activation functions to address these imbalances without requiring per-channel quantization.

The key concept of scaling equivariance is defined mathematically, stating that for a scaling factor s and a function f, the relationship f(sx) = sf(x) holds for non-negative real numbers. This property can be leveraged across consecutive layers of a neural network to reparameterize the model. The authors introduce a method to find an optimal scaling factor that minimizes quantization noise in the rescaled layers, enhancing the robustness of the model.

Figures included in the paper illustrate the weight range distributions for output channels in MobileNetV2 and the rescaling procedure for a single channel, emphasizing the importance of these methods in improving model performance during quantization.

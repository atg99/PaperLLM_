The paper presents a comparative analysis of various deep learning techniques used for brain tumor classification, focusing on the performance metrics of accuracy, precision, and F1-score. The authors highlight their proposed method, Dense EfficientNet, which achieved an impressive accuracy of 98.78%, outperforming existing models such as CNN, 3D CNN, and multi-scale CNN. The results indicate that glioma detection has the lowest rate with an F1-score of 98%, while pituitary tumors achieved the highest rate with an F1-score of 100%. The authors emphasize the effectiveness of Dense CNN in rapid classification and detection of tumors. They suggest that incorporating advanced pre-processing techniques, such as fuzzy thresholding or nature-based algorithms, could enhance early diagnosis in medical imaging. Overall, the proposed method demonstrates significant potential for improving tumor detection in clinical settings.

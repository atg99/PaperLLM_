The paper presents a comparative analysis of various deep learning models for brain tumor detection using T1 contrast images, specifically focusing on their accuracy and loss metrics. Figures 6, 7, and 8 illustrate model accuracy and loss during training and validation for ResNet50, MobileNet, and MobileNetV2, respectively. The authors observe that the MobileNetV2 model exhibits disordered graph patterns with a significant disparity between loss and accuracy, resulting in lower performance metrics. 

In contrast, the dense EfficientNet, ResNet, and MobileNet models show nearly equivalent performance levels. Specifically, dense EfficientNet achieves the highest testing accuracy of 98.78% with a loss of 0.0645. MobileNet follows with a testing accuracy of 96.94% and a loss of 0.1339, while ResNet50 records a slightly lower accuracy of 96.33% and a loss of 0.1337. MobileNetV2 lags behind with an accuracy of 94.80% and a loss of 0.2452. A detailed comparison of these metrics is summarized in Table 1, while Figure 9 provides further performance analysis.

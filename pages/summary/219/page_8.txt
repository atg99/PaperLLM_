However, the Plain network's performance highlights the need for skip connections to enhance norm preservation and optimize training dynamics.

The experiments validate theoretical claims regarding the benefits of identity skip connections, showing that deeper networks increasingly benefit from these modifications. The study further explores optimization stability and learning dynamics, revealing that ResNet and ProcResNet exhibit consistent decreases in training error and loss, unlike Plain networks, which struggle with generalization.

Quantitative assessments of generalization gaps indicate that ProcResNet outperforms both Plain and ResNet architectures, suggesting that minor adjustments can significantly enhance stability and learning dynamics in deep networks.

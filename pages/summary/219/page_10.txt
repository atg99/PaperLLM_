The paper presents an analysis of various methods for improving the performance of ResNet architectures on CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100 datasets using moderate data augmentation techniques like flipping and translation. Results indicate that modifications to the transition blocks in the proposed Procrustes ResNet significantly enhance accuracy compared to traditional ResNet configurations. Specifically, the study shows that deeper networks can achieve better optimization stability and performance through effective norm-preservation strategies.

Key findings from the experiments reveal that identity skip connections contribute to norm preservation, and as network depth increases, residual blocks further enhance this property. The paper proposes an efficient regularization technique to maintain nonzero singular values in the convolution operator without resorting to singular value decomposition, providing design guidelines for training very deep networks.

An ablation study on ResNet with 164 layers highlights the impact of modified transition blocks, showing a reduction in error rates.

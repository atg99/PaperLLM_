The proposed watermarking scheme addresses these challenges by introducing a novel approach that does not require fine-tuning the target model, thus preserving its functionality while enabling ownership verification. This is achieved by injecting a proprietary model that has been independently trained on a custom dataset, allowing for effective embedding of the watermark without degrading the model’s performance on benign samples. 

The paper highlights the limitations of prior data-poisoning watermarking methods, which necessitate fine-tuning and are constrained to specific datasets, often leading to performance degradation and increased time costs. In contrast, the new scheme is designed to be adaptable to complex real-world datasets, such as ImageNet, and recent architectures like vision transformers.

Moreover, the paper discusses the vulnerabilities of existing watermarking techniques to removal attacks, which can either involve model modification or input preprocessing. These attacks pose significant threats as they can degrade the watermark's effectiveness and the model’s accuracy. Therefore, the proposed scheme aims to be resilient against such removal strategies while ensuring that ownership verification remains intact.

In summary, the paper presents a practical and efficient watermarking solution that enhances the protection of deep neural networks' intellectual property by overcoming the limitations of previous methods and addressing the challenges posed by adversarial attacks.

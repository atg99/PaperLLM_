In this paper, a novel watermarking scheme for deep neural networks (DNNs) is introduced, addressing the shortcomings of current data-poisoning watermarking methods through model fine-tuning. The proposed approach enhances ownership verification and is particularly effective for complex, production-level DNN applications, such as those utilizing the ImageNet dataset. The evaluation demonstrates notable strengths in maintaining functionality fidelity, verifying watermark effectiveness, and resisting common watermark removal attacks. The method offers a fresh perspective and shows potential for practical watermarking applications in diverse real-world contexts. Future research will focus on extending the incorporation of proprietary models for ownership verification into additional scenarios, including reinforcement learning, beyond the classification models examined in prior studies.

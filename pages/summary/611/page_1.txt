The paper presents a novel watermarking scheme for Deep Neural Networks (DNN) aimed at protecting intellectual property without requiring the tedious fine-tuning of the target model. Traditional watermarking methods, particularly data-poisoning schemes, necessitate model fine-tuning with carefully crafted sample-label pairs, which can be inefficient and impractical for real-world applications. 

To overcome these limitations, the authors propose a plug-and-play approach that integrates a proprietary model into the target model for watermark embedding and ownership verification. This method does not require any updates to the target model's parameters, thus preserving its functionality and fidelity. The proposed scheme demonstrates scalability across various challenging datasets and tasks, including speaker recognition, and shows robustness against common watermark removal techniques such as fine-tuning attacks and pruning.

Experimental results validate the effectiveness of the proposed method, indicating superior performance compared to existing baselines regarding fidelity and resistance to watermark removal. The findings suggest that traditional fine-tuning methods are inadequate for protecting DNN models in real-world scenarios, and the study opens new avenues for research into proprietary model adoption for DNN watermarking. The source code and models related to this research are publicly available, facilitating further exploration in this domain.

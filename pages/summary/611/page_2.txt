The paper addresses significant challenges in deep neural network (DNN) watermarking, specifically the issues of fidelity degradation and high resource consumption associated with model fine-tuning during watermark embedding. It highlights that traditional methods often result in performance loss, particularly when applied to complex tasks and large datasets like ImageNet. The authors propose a novel watermarking scheme that utilizes a proprietary model for watermark embedding, which does not require fine-tuning of the target model, thus maintaining its original functionality.

The proposed method is model-agnostic and operates in black-box settings, allowing for effective ownership verification without needing access to the model's internal parameters. This approach is inspired by software engineering principles that emphasize high cohesion and low coupling. The paper presents experimental results demonstrating that the new watermarking scheme preserves model functionality with nearly 100% confidence, achieves full accuracy in ownership verification, and withstands common watermark removal attacks such as fine-tuning and input preprocessing.

Key contributions of the paper include:
1. Introduction of a proprietary model for watermark embedding that avoids target model fine-tuning, making it suitable for real-world applications.
2. A generation-based method for safely crafting verification samples, along with comprehensive evaluations of effectiveness, fidelity, robustness, and efficiency on datasets like ImageNet and VoxCeleb1.
3. A new research direction advocating for the use of independent proprietary models for watermark embedding, which can be easily integrated into various DNN models without modification.

The paper also reviews existing DNN watermarking approaches, categorizing them into parameter-embedding and data-poisoning schemes. It critiques traditional methods that require access to model parameters, making them impractical for real-world scenarios, and emphasizes the need for more robust and efficient watermarking solutions.

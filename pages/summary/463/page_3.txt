The paper by Saood and Hatem discusses the significance of spatial pixel values in lung infection susceptibility and outlines the methodologies employed for semantic segmentation of medical images. The authors focus on two deep learning architectures: U-NET and SegNet.

U-NET is highlighted for its effectiveness in medical image segmentation, featuring a dual-path architecture comprising a contracting path for feature extraction and an expansive path for generating segmentation maps. The integration of these paths via a specific operation enhances the accuracy of the segmentation output.

SegNet, initially designed for scene segmentation, is noted for its ability to handle imbalanced datasets, which is pertinent to the authors' research context. The paper emphasizes the importance of these architectures in automating medical imaging processes and improving the accuracy of lung infection detection. 

Figures included in the paper illustrate sample CT scans, masked lung images, and the distribution of infection-prone regions within the dataset, underscoring the research's focus on spatial analysis in medical imaging.

This paper discusses the differences in Softmax calculations between fan-out and fan-in routing in the PathCapsNet architecture, as illustrated in a simplified diagram. In the fan-in variant, Softmax is applied across connections that converge into the same DigitCaps, while in the fan-out variant, it is applied across connections diverging from a single PrimaryCapsule. The accuracy and reconstruction losses are computed using margin loss and sum of squared errors, following the methods established by Sabour et al. in 2017.

In the experimental design, the architecture remained consistent across experiments, with the number of paths varying. The notation PathCapsNet-[num] is used to indicate the specific configuration. All PrimaryCapsules were 8D with dimensions of 7x7, and the DigitCaps layer replicated the structure from the original CapsNet. The experiments employed three routing iterations, with randomly initialized transformation matrices for fan-in routing. The Adam optimizer was consistently utilized with default parameters, and a path dropping probability of 0.5 was applied when DropCircuit was in use.

The research benchmarked the original CapsNet, both with and without reconstruction, under the same architectural conditions, and results were averaged over three trials with 300 training epochs, which is notably fewer than the over 1000 epochs used in previous studies.

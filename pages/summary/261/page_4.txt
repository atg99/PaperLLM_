The paper discusses the PathCapsNet architecture, focusing on the dynamic routing mechanism that connects PrimaryCapsules to DigitCaps capsules. The dynamic routing by agreement algorithm, initially proposed by Sabour et al., adjusts the influence of votes from PrimaryCapsules based on their similarity to the output of the DigitCaps. This similarity is measured using the dot product of prediction vectors. The output vector for DigitCaps is computed by summing the weighted contributions of the prediction vectors, with coupling coefficients that determine the strength of these contributions.

The paper introduces a variation of the dynamic routing called fan-out routing, where the contributions from PrimaryCapsules to DigitCaps are normalized to ensure they sum to one. Additionally, PathCapsNet employs a fan-in routing approach, which similarly normalizes the contributions from all PrimaryCapsules to each DigitCaps capsule, allowing for a more refined allocation of weights based on the coupling probabilities. This approach enhances the network's ability to capture complex relationships between features in the data.

This document references a collection of significant works in the field of computer vision and deep learning, highlighting advancements in convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and image classification techniques. 

Key contributions include:

1. **Going Deeper with Convolutions**: Szegedy et al. introduce deeper architectures for CNNs, emphasizing the importance of depth in improving performance on image recognition tasks.

2. **Rethinking the Inception Architecture**: Szegedy et al. revisit the Inception model, proposing modifications that enhance its capability in computer vision applications.

3. **DeepFace**: Taigman et al. present a face verification system that approaches human-level performance, demonstrating the potential of deep learning in facial recognition.

4. **Improving Image Classification with Location Context**: Tang et al. explore the integration of spatial information into image classification processes, which aids in improving accuracy.

5. **Multi-path Convolutional Neural Networks**: Wang discusses a novel approach to handle complex image classification through the use of multi-path CNNs.

6. **Location Augmentation for CNN**: Wang and Veksler propose techniques that augment CNNs with location information to enhance classification performance.

7. **MS-CapsNet**: Xiang et al. introduce a multi-scale capsule network, focusing on improving the representation of complex images.

8. **Aggregated Residual Transformations**: Xie et al. discuss residual transformations that aggregate features from various layers, contributing to the development of deeper networks.

9. **Residual Inception**: Zhang et al. combine modified residual networks with Inception modules, aiming to boost network performance in image processing tasks.

These works collectively advance the understanding and application of deep learning techniques in computer vision, particularly in enhancing model architectures and improving classification accuracy.

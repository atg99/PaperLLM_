The paper presents Path Capsule Networks (PathCapsNet) as an evolution of Capsule Networks (CapsNet), aiming to address limitations related to depth and parameter efficiency in CapsNet. CapsNet was introduced to enhance convolutional neural networks (CNNs) by incorporating equivariance through pose estimation, effectively representing object viewpoints with capsules. While CapsNet achieves commendable results with a shallow architecture and fewer parameters than deep CNNs, its shallow nature restricts its representational capacity, and the first layer contributes significantly to the parameter count.

To overcome these challenges, the authors propose a deep parallel multi-path architecture for PathCapsNet. They demonstrate that carefully coordinating network depth, employing max-pooling, and utilizing a new routing technique called fan-in routing by agreement can yield results comparable to or better than CapsNet while further reducing the overall parameter count. The paper emphasizes the importance of maintaining voting agreements among capsules as object viewpoints change and suggests that the proposed modifications could enhance the performance of capsule networks in image processing tasks.

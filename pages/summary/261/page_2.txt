In this paper, we introduce PathCapsNet, a novel multipath architecture that enhances the capabilities of CapsNet by incorporating depth and parallel processing. We draw inspiration from biological neural networks, which exhibit multipath parallel processing, and aim to improve performance while reducing the number of parameters required.

The proposed PathCapsNet features a new routing mechanism called fan-in routing, which enriches the existing routing by agreement methodology. By strategically adding depth and employing techniques such as max-pooling and DropCircuit, we achieve results comparable to traditional CapsNet while significantly decreasing parameter usage. This opens up opportunities for increased model parallelism within capsule networks.

Our work builds upon previous advancements in capsule networks and multipath architectures, highlighting the potential for improved generalization through depth and more efficient parameter utilization. The related work section reviews existing variants of CapsNet and their approaches to enhancing performance, setting the stage for our contributions. Overall, PathCapsNet represents a step forward in the development of capsule networks, combining theoretical insights with practical benefits.

The referenced works explore various aspects of machine learning, decision-making, and interpretability. Yeh et al. investigate the fidelity and sensitivity of explanations in machine learning, highlighting the challenges in ensuring that explanations accurately reflect model behavior. Yeung and Summerfield discuss metacognition in human decision-making, focusing on how confidence and error monitoring influence choices. Zhao and Hastie provide insights into causal interpretations of black-box models, emphasizing the need for understanding underlying causal mechanisms. Zhou et al. present methods for learning deep features that aid in discriminative localization within images. Zhou et al. also conduct a survey on the evaluation of machine learning explanations, assessing different methods and metrics for quality. Lastly, Zintgraf et al. propose a technique for visualizing deep neural network decisions through prediction difference analysis, contributing to the interpretability of complex models. Overall, these studies underscore the significance of explanation fidelity, human cognition in decision-making, and the evaluation of interpretative methods in machine learning.

The paper discusses the importance of effective explanations in AI systems, particularly focusing on the interaction styles and formats that can enhance user understanding. It highlights the potential for two-way interactions in future research, which could allow systems to request additional information from users or suggest alternative queries. The explanation formats are categorized into visual, graphical, verbal, and auditory alerts, with examples provided such as data analysis outputs and siren alerts. 

The paper emphasizes that different contexts demand different styles of explanations. For instance, a simple alert like a tornado warning prioritizes brevity for immediate action, while debugging a system requires more detailed information to facilitate user comprehension. This variability underscores the need for flexibility in designing explanations, adapting to the specific requirements of the audience and the situation.

Furthermore, the paper introduces the concept of risk management in the context of explainable AI, defining risk as the uncertainty of objectives that encompasses both threats and opportunities. It suggests that effective risk management strategies can help mitigate potential negative outcomes while maximizing positive ones. Overall, the discussion points to the complexities of providing explanations in AI systems and the necessity for tailored approaches to meet diverse user needs.

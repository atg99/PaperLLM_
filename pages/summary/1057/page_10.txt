The paper discusses four key principles of explainable artificial intelligence (AI) that are essential for a system to be deemed explainable. 

1. **Explanation**: This principle emphasizes that a system must provide evidence, reasoning, or support related to its outputs or processes. It is noted that the quality of the explanation, whether it is correct, informative, or intelligible, is not a requirement of this principle itself, but is considered under the other principles.

2. **Meaningful**: This principle states that for an explanation to be meaningful, it must be understandable to the intended audience. Effective explanations share commonalities that enhance comprehension.

3. **Explanation Accuracy**: This principle focuses on the accuracy of the explanation in reflecting the reasons behind the output or the internal workings of the system. An accurate explanation is crucial for trust in the AI's decisions.

4. **Knowledge Limits**: According to this principle, a system should only operate within the parameters for which it was designed and should provide outputs only when it has sufficient confidence in its results.

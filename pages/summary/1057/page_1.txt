NISTIR 8312 outlines four fundamental principles of explainable artificial intelligence (AI) designed to enhance understanding and trust in AI systems. The authors, including P. Jonathon Phillips and others, emphasize the need for transparency in AI operations to ensure that users can comprehend the decision-making processes of these systems. The principles are structured to guide the development and deployment of AI technologies, focusing on the importance of interpretability, reliability, accountability, and ethical considerations. By adhering to these principles, stakeholders can foster a more informed interaction with AI, ultimately improving user confidence and promoting responsible AI usage. The publication serves as a resource for both developers and users, providing a framework for creating AI systems that are not only effective but also understandable and trustworthy.

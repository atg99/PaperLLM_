The paper discusses the evolving principles of explainable AI systems within the broader context of trustworthy AI as outlined by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). It emphasizes the need for ongoing refinement and community input to enhance these principles, acknowledging that AI trustworthiness is influenced by various socio-technical factors beyond just explainability. The work is part of NIST's comprehensive AI portfolio, which includes standards, evaluation, validation, and verification essential for AI measurement. NIST's initiatives are guided by statutory mandates, White House directives, and the needs of U.S. industry and the global AI research community. This publication marks an initial step in a larger journey, with expectations for continued evolution in alignment with advancements in the AI field.

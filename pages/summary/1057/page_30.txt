The paper discusses the significance of explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) in enhancing user understanding and trust in complex AI systems. It emphasizes that meaningful and accurate explanations empower users to adjust their behaviors and make informed decisions. For developers and auditors, such explanations are crucial for improving and maintaining AI systems. The authors outline a common framework based on four principles that supports the evolution of XAI methods necessary for real-world applications. They acknowledge contributions from various individuals and organizations that helped shape the paper, highlighting the collaborative effort in advancing the field of explainable AI. The references provided further substantiate the research and discussions presented in the paper.

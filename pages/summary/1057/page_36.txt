The references listed provide a comprehensive overview of research focused on the interpretability of machine learning models and the role of human evaluation in understanding these systems. Key contributions include the evaluation of human interpretability in explanations (Isaac Lage et al., 2019), the examination of user trust manipulation via misleading explanations (Himabindu Lakkaraju & Osbert Bastani, 2020), and the development of interpretable decision frameworks (Himabindu Lakkaraju et al., 2016). Several studies emphasize the importance of creating models that not only perform well but also provide clear, understandable insights to users (e.g., Hima Lakkaraju et al., 2020; Benjamin Letham et al., 2015). The collective findings highlight ongoing challenges and opportunities in enhancing model transparency, fostering user trust, and improving decision-making processes within the context of artificial intelligence.

The paper discusses the performance of the proposed Deep Autoencoder Network (DAEN) for hyperspectral unmixing, specifically applied to the Cuprite dataset. It highlights the estimation of endmember signatures and their corresponding abundance maps, comparing them with references from the US Geological Survey (USGS) library. The study utilized 188 spectral bands from a dataset of 191 by 250 pixels, focusing on well-documented minerals at the Cuprite mining site.

Figure 9 illustrates the estimated endmembers alongside USGS references, showing a strong alignment that confirms the DAEN's effectiveness in real-world applications. The experiment was conducted with a predetermined number of 12 endmembers, following previous studies.

Table V summarizes the results, presenting Spectral Angle Distances (SADs) and Relative Errors (REs) obtained from multiple Monte Carlo runs across various methods. The DAEN demonstrated superior or competitive performance compared to other techniques, indicating its capability in accurately estimating endmembers and their fractional abundances in hyperspectral imagery.

The paper discusses the contributions and backgrounds of several prominent researchers in the field of hyperspectral imaging and remote sensing. Antonio Plaza is highlighted as a leading figure in hyperspectral data processing and parallel computing, currently heading the Hyperspectral Computing Laboratory at the University of Extremadura, Spain. His extensive research includes efficient implementations of scientific problems on high-performance computing architectures, and he has held various editorial and leadership roles within the IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Society.

Andrea Marinoni, an Associate Professor at UiT–The Arctic University of Norway, has a strong background in electronic engineering and nonlinear signal processing for remote sensing applications. His research focuses on Big Data analysis in the context of human-environment interactions, and he has contributed to various international collaborations.

Paolo Gamba, a Professor at the University of Pavia, Italy, leads the Telecommunications and Remote Sensing Laboratory and is actively involved in the organization of workshops and committees related to remote sensing and data fusion. His expertise encompasses urban remote sensing and risk management.

Somdatta Chakravortty is an Assistant Professor at Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology, India, with a focus on hyperspectral image analysis and algorithm development. She has received funding for her research and is affiliated with several professional societies.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the significant impact these researchers have made in advancing hyperspectral imaging techniques and their applications in various domains.

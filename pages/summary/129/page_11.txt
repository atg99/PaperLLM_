The paper discusses the computation of first-order derivatives for hyperspectral unmixing, particularly focusing on the functions f2 and R0, which are critical in the optimization process. The derivatives for the abundance fractions ui,j and vi,j are derived using chain rule applications, leading to expressions for ∂f2/∂ui,j and ∂f2/∂vi,j, which are essential for updating the optimization variables U and V.

The objective function is constructed from these derivatives, incorporating an indicative function Ccons that ensures the constraints on the abundance fractions are respected. The final gradients for U and V are expressed as the sum of the derivatives of R0 and f2, allowing for effective optimization in hyperspectral unmixing tasks.

The references provided cover a broad range of methodologies and challenges in hyperspectral data analysis, emphasizing the significance of the discussed approaches in the field.

This paper presents the TS-SAN method for action recognition, demonstrating its effectiveness through extensive experiments on two large-scale benchmark datasets: NTU RGB+D and Kinetics. The results indicate that TS-SAN outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods on both datasets.

Table 1 shows the performance of TS-SAN compared to various methods on the NTU RGB+D dataset, highlighting significant improvements in accuracy for both Cross-Subject and Cross-View evaluations. For instance, TS-SAN achieved 87.2% accuracy in Cross-Subject and 92.7% in Cross-View, surpassing previous models such as HCN and DPRL.

In addition, Table 2 presents results from the Kinetics dataset, where TS-SAN achieved top-1 accuracy of 35.1% and top-5 accuracy of 55.7%, again outperforming earlier models.

The NTU RGB+D dataset consists of 56,880 video samples with joint annotations for 60 action classes recorded from multiple camera views. The Kinetics dataset contains approximately 266,000 video clips across 400 classes, with skeleton data estimated using OpenPose.

The paper details the implementation of the model, including data preprocessing, self-attention mechanisms, dropout regularization, and training parameters. The reported results highlight the robustness and superior performance of the TS-SAN method, validating its potential for action recognition tasks.

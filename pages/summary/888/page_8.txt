This paper introduces a deep network for artistic text style transfer that allows for flexible control over the stylistic degree of glyphs. The authors present a method that formulates glyph deformation as a coarse-to-fine mapping problem, enhanced by a bidirectional shape matching framework. A novel sketch module is introduced to reduce discrepancies between the glyph and style, facilitating robust mappings. The proposed Controllable ResBlock enables effective learning of many-to-one shape mappings for multi-scale style transfer.

The results demonstrate the effectiveness of the glyph legibility loss, which maintains the integrity of strokes while allowing for artistic deformation. The paper showcases various applications, including structure/texture mash-ups and stroke-based art designs, illustrating the versatility of the method beyond text to include symbols and icons.

The authors validate their approach through comparisons with state-of-the-art style transfer algorithms, highlighting its effectiveness and robustness. The work is supported by multiple foundations, and acknowledgments are given to contributors of public domain images.

In the paper "Controllable Artistic Text Style Transfer via Shape-Matching GAN," the authors present a novel framework for artistic text style transfer that allows users to adjust the stylistic degree of glyphs in real-time. This framework addresses the challenge of controlling shape deformation during the style transfer process, which is crucial for maintaining both legibility and artistic expression in typography.

The key innovation of this work is a bidirectional shape matching framework that maps the style from a source image to the target text effectively, without requiring paired ground truth data. The proposed scale-controllable module enables the continuous characterization of multi-scale shape features from the style image, facilitating their transfer to the text. This approach enhances the usability and creativity of the generated artistic text, making it suitable for various applications such as poster and advertisement design.

The authors demonstrate that their method outperforms existing state-of-the-art techniques, producing diverse, controllable, and high-quality stylized text. The paper emphasizes the importance of balancing stylistic deformation with legibility, highlighting the subjective nature of this balance and the difficulty of achieving it automatically. Overall, this research contributes significantly to the field of artistic text style transfer by providing a flexible and user-friendly solution for creating visually appealing typography.

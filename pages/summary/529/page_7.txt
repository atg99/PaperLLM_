The results indicate that our proposed method, FedCA, consistently achieves higher Top-1 accuracies compared to other methods like FedAvg, FedAE, FedPR, and FedSimCLR, both in IID and Non-IID settings. 

In the ablation study, we analyze the contributions of the alignment and dictionary modules, revealing that the alignment module enhances performance by 1.4% in both settings. The dictionary module, with and without temporal ensembling, provides further improvements of 2.5% and 2.7% respectively, particularly benefiting the Non-IID scenario. The effectiveness of combining these modules is emphasized, showcasing their collaborative role in overcoming the challenges posed by FURL.

In conclusion, this work highlights the potential of FedCA to facilitate effective federated learning by ensuring consistent and aligned representations while maintaining data privacy, thereby addressing significant challenges in the field.

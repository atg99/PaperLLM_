The paper discusses the integration of federated learning with unsupervised approaches, focusing on enhancing the baseline for Federated Unsupervised Representation Learning (FURL). The authors propose an extension of their method, FedCA, to accommodate cross-modal scenarios, where different clients possess diverse data types, such as images, videos, texts, and audios. The references cited support the foundational theories and methodologies related to self-supervised learning, privacy-preserving machine learning, and various unsupervised feature learning techniques, indicating a comprehensive exploration of current advancements and open problems in the field. Future work aims to further develop these concepts to improve federated learning systems in heterogeneous data environments.

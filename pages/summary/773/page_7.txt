The paper discusses enhancements to a bounding box fine-tuning method using a Position-Sensitive Score Map (PSSM) architecture. The PSSM method employs an extra convolutional layer that augments the channel count, making the score maps sensitive to the positions of region proposals. For category prediction and bounding box regression, softmax regression is used, along with a threshold to filter low-scoring boxes and Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS) to eliminate overlapping boxes based on the Intersection-over-Union (IoU) metric.

To improve Region of Interest (RoI) information during the regression phase, the paper introduces Contextual RoIs, which are enlarged versions of original RoIs. This augmentation aims to capture additional contextual information that may aid in fine-tuning bounding boxes, thereby enhancing regression results.

The model optimization section details the loss function, which combines cross-entropy loss for classification and regression loss for bounding boxes. The optimizer used is momentum Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) with strategies to prevent overfitting, such as dropout and early stopping. The learning rate is managed using a step strategy. 

Additionally, Online Hard Example Mining (OHEM) is implemented to improve training efficiency by selecting hard examples for backward propagation, focusing on regions with the highest loss while ignoring others. 

The experimental section outlines the validation of the PestNet architecture for multi-class tiny pest classification, localization, and counting, utilizing the Caffe framework. Data augmentation techniques, including horizontal flipping, are employed prior to training.

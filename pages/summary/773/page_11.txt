The paper presents PestNet, an innovative end-to-end automatic pest detection network that facilitates large-scale multi-class pest identification. PestNet effectively addresses challenges in pest detection, such as recognizing pests from varying poses and distinguishing similar-looking pests from different classes. The network employs a novel feature enhancement module, the CSA module, to automatically extract higher quality features, enhancing detection performance.

In contrast to standard object detection methods, PestNet utilizes a PSSM approach instead of a fully connected layer to reduce computational costs associated with pest classification and box regression. Additionally, the integration of contextual Regions of Interest (RoIs) as contextual information further boosts detection accuracy. The effectiveness of PestNet is demonstrated through its performance on the enriched dataset MPD2018, achieving a mean Average Precision (mAP) of 75.46% across 16 pest classes, surpassing existing state-of-the-art methods. 

The paper concludes that PestNet represents a significant advancement in automatic pest detection, combining innovative feature extraction and classification techniques to enhance agricultural pest management.

In this section, the authors describe their experimental setup for quantizing ResNet-18 and ResNet-50 architectures pretrained on the ImageNet dataset. They utilize a 16 GB Volta V100 GPU, with the quantization process for ResNet-50 taking approximately one day. The authors explore different compression regimes by adjusting block sizes and the number of centroids, while ensuring stability by clamping the number of centroids. Input activations are sampled from a batch of training images, and the quantization process involves 100 steps for each layer, followed by fine-tuning over 2,500 iterations with specified hyperparameters.

The evaluation metrics focus on the balance between accuracy and memory footprint, with accuracy measured as top-1 error on the ImageNet validation set and memory calculated based on indexing costs and centroid storage. The authors present results comparing their method against state-of-the-art techniques like Trained Ternary Quantization and Deep Compression, noting that their method significantly outperforms these approaches. The results are summarized in figures and tables, demonstrating the effectiveness of their quantization method.

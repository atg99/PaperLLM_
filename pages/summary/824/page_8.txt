The paper presents a novel quantization method based on Product Quantization, achieving state-of-the-art results for ResNet architectures while also demonstrating effectiveness in compressing the Mask R-CNN architecture. The authors compare their method against previously published techniques on ImageNet, showcasing significant improvements in accuracy for various size budgets. For instance, at a 5 MB budget, their semi-supervised ResNet-50 outperforms the best previous method by over 4% in test top-1 accuracy. 

An ablation study on ResNet-18 indicates that their approach consistently yields better results compared to standard methods, particularly when fine-tuning is applied. The paper highlights the importance of using a specific number of centroids for quantization, with results indicating enhanced performance with increased block sizes.

In image detection, the authors apply their method to compress the Mask R-CNN architecture, reporting a substantial reduction in model size while maintaining competitive performance metrics. The compressed model demonstrates a significant size reduction from 170 MB to 6.65 MB, with only a minor drop in accuracy.

The conclusion emphasizes the generality of the proposed quantization method, its efficiency in inference, and the potential for further research to adapt it for various architectures and domains. Future work aims to include non-linearities to enhance reconstruction accuracy.

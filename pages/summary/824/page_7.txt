The paper presents a novel approach for compressing deep neural networks, specifically ResNet architectures, while maintaining their performance. The authors introduce ABC-Net, which achieves significant compression ratios with various block sizes and centroids, demonstrating its effectiveness against reference methods like DC and HAQ. For ResNet-18, the method with large blocks and 512 centroids yields higher accuracy than ABC-Net with a smaller compression ratio. Similarly, for ResNet-50, the compressed model maintains comparable accuracy to DC while achieving a 2x larger compression ratio.

The authors also explore the benefits of leveraging semi-supervised learning, using a model trained on the YFCC-100M dataset. This semi-supervised ResNet-50, when compressed, achieves 76.12% top-1 accuracy, closely matching the performance of the original model. Additionally, the paper conducts an ablation study to analyze the impact of quantization and fine-tuning techniques on model performance.

Overall, the findings suggest that the proposed compression method not only reduces model size but also retains competitive accuracy, making it a promising solution for deploying deep learning models in resource-constrained environments.

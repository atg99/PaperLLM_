This paper introduces a novel approach for knowledge distillation specifically tailored for graph convolutional networks (GCNs), addressing a gap in existing methodologies that predominantly focus on convolutional neural networks (CNNs) operating on grid data. The authors propose a local structure preserving (LSP) module that facilitates the transfer of topological information from a teacher GCN model to a student model. This method extracts local structure information from both models as distributions and reduces the distance between these distributions to enhance topology-aware knowledge transfer, resulting in a more compact and efficient student model.

The authors demonstrate the effectiveness of their approach through evaluations on various datasets using different GCN architectures, achieving state-of-the-art performance in knowledge distillation for GCNs. The proposed method is also adaptable for dynamic graph models, allowing for knowledge transfer even when the teacher and student models operate on different input graphs. Overall, the research presents an innovative solution to improve the efficiency of GCNs while maintaining high performance.

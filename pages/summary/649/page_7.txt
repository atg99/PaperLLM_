The paper discusses the visualization and analysis of the learned feature space in a knowledge distillation framework using a teacher-student model approach. The experiments illustrate how the student model, trained with the proposed method, effectively replicates the local structure of the feature space as established by the teacher model, even in the early stages of training. 

The study employs a dataset with 40 classes, utilizing CAD models, and both teacher and student models are based on the Dynamic Graph Convolutional Neural Network (DGCNN) architecture. The teacher model consists of five graph convolutional layers and two fully connected layers, while the student model is structured with four graph convolutional layers and one fully connected layer, featuring fewer channels and a smaller graph size. 

The results are visualized across multiple epochs, showcasing the progression of the feature space structure from the initial to later training stages. The findings indicate that the knowledge distillation approach aids the student model in achieving a feature space organization akin to that of the teacher model, confirming the effectiveness of the proposed method in preserving important structural information during training.

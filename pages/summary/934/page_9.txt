In the article "Artificial Intelligence—The Revolution Hasn’t Happened Yet," Michael I. Jordan discusses the current state of artificial intelligence (AI), emphasizing the need for a more realistic perspective on its capabilities and limitations. He argues that while there is significant hype surrounding AI, the technology has yet to achieve its revolutionary potential. Jordan highlights the serious challenges that remain in the field, suggesting that a broader scope is necessary to address these issues effectively. 

The article acknowledges contributions from various experts in the field, indicating a collaborative effort in shaping the discourse on AI. Jordan's work is licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution license, allowing for sharing and adaptation with proper attribution. Overall, the article calls for a tempered view of AI's progress, urging the community to focus on overcoming existing hurdles rather than getting swept up in exaggerated claims of breakthroughs.

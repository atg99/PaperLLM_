6.2. Challenges in Interpretability
The current reliance on post-interpretation methods, such as saliency maps, highlights a significant gap in the systematic development of explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) in medical imaging. While these methods provide some insights into model behavior, they often lack competitiveness in performance on testing datasets. This raises concerns about their reliability and usability in clinical settings.

6.3. Need for Global Explanation Models
The paper emphasizes the necessity for global explanation models that can provide comprehensive insights into radiomics and deep learning predictions. Such models would enhance the interpretability of AI tools and support clinicians in making informed decisions.

Overall, the integration of radiomics with deep learning presents promising avenues for medical diagnostics, yet the field must address challenges related to interpretability and user trust to fully realize its potential.

The paper by Chaddad et al. provides a comprehensive survey of Explainable AI (XAI) techniques specifically utilized in healthcare, particularly focusing on medical imaging applications. The authors emphasize the importance of transparency in AI models, as the "black-box" nature of deep learning algorithms can lead to uncertainty among clinicians regarding diagnostic decisions. To address this, the paper categorizes and summarizes recent XAI methods and algorithms that enhance interpretability in medical contexts.

The introduction outlines the rapid advancements of AI in healthcare, noting that while AI can perform tasks efficiently, its lack of transparency can hinder clinician trust. The authors reference initiatives like the DARPA XAI project, which aims to improve user trust through explanations of AI predictions. The paper highlights that effective XAI should enhance trustworthiness, causality, transferability, and fairness, among other qualities.

Moreover, the authors identify key challenges in applying XAI within medical applications and propose guidelines for improving the interpretability of deep learning models in medical image and text analysis. They conclude by suggesting future research directions for developers and researchers to further explore XAI in clinical settings, particularly within medical imaging.

Overall, the paper underscores the crucial need for explainability in AI systems in healthcare to foster clinician trust and improve patient outcomes.

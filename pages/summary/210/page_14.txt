Appendix A provides an overview of a dataset comprising 50 futures contracts categorized into four types: 25 commodity contracts, 11 equity index contracts, 5 fixed income contracts, and 9 forex contracts. 

The commodities section lists various contracts, including cocoa, milk, gold, orange juice, coffee, wheat, lumber, rough rice, sugar, palladium, and several others, along with their respective tickers. 

The equity indexes section includes contracts such as CAC40, NASDAQ mini, Russell 2000 mini, S&P 500 mini, FTSE 100, and Dow Jones indices, also accompanied by their tickers. 

This structured categorization allows for a comprehensive understanding of the diverse futures contracts included in the dataset.

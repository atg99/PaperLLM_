The papers by Zihao Zhang, Stefan Zohren, and Stephen Roberts focus on the application of deep learning techniques to limit order book data, which is critical in financial markets. 

In "Bdlob: Bayesian deep convolutional neural networks for limit order books," presented at the Third Workshop on Bayesian Deep Learning in 2018, the authors introduce a Bayesian approach that integrates uncertainty into deep learning models for analyzing limit order books, enhancing predictive performance.

The follow-up work, "Deeplob: Deep convolutional neural networks for limit order books," published in the IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing in 2019, further develops this concept by employing deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) specifically designed to process limit order book data, demonstrating improved accuracy in predicting market dynamics.

In "Extending deep learning models for limit order books to quantile regression," presented at the Time Series Workshop of the 36th International Conference on Machine Learning in 2019, the authors extend their previous models to include quantile regression techniques, allowing for better modeling of the distribution of future prices, rather than just point estimates.

Together, these works contribute to the field by advancing the understanding and application of deep learning in financial market predictions, particularly through the lens of limit order books.

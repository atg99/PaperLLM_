The results indicate that while traditional long-only strategies may excel in trending markets, such as equity indices, reinforcement learning (RL) algorithms, particularly DQN and A2C, demonstrate enhanced adaptability and efficiency in more volatile environments like commodities and foreign exchange (FX).

The data reveals that RL algorithms consistently outperform long-only strategies across most asset classes, with the exception of equity indices, where the long-only approach is more effective due to prevailing upward trends. The volatility in commodity and FX markets benefits from the RL models' ability to execute both long and short positions effectively.

DQN stands out as the most effective model, followed closely by A2C, despite the latter's higher turnover rates, which can dilute average returns. The analysis also highlights that RL algorithms maintain robustness against varying transaction costs, proving their viability in practical trading scenarios. This adaptability is crucial for traders operating in dynamic market conditions, affirming the potential of RL techniques in portfolio management.

The experimental results section outlines a comprehensive evaluation of both baseline models and proposed methods from 2011 to 2019, focusing on trade returns net of transaction costs. A simple portfolio is constructed with equal weights assigned to each contract, and the overall trade return is calculated. The performance of this portfolio is assessed using several metrics, including annualized expected trade return, standard deviation, downside deviation, Sharpe ratio, Sortino ratio, maximum drawdown, Calmar ratio, percentage of positive returns, and the ratio of average positive to average negative returns.

Results are presented in Table 2, which applies a portfolio-level volatility scaling to allow for direct comparison across different models and asset classes. The table is divided into sections based on specific asset classes, with an additional section for a portfolio that encompasses all contracts in the dataset. Cumulative trade returns for various models and asset classes are illustrated in Figure 1, emphasizing the performance trends over the years. The analysis enables insights into the effectiveness of different trading strategies and their risk-adjusted returns.

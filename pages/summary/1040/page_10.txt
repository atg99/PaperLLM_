The paper presents a comprehensive bibliography focused on the intersection of artificial intelligence (AI) and ethics, highlighting key contributions from various authors and institutions. It covers a range of topics including agency theory, the ethical implications of AI in education, regulatory frameworks, and the role of stakeholders in ethical decision-making. Notable entries discuss the challenges of understanding AI's decision-making processes, the necessity of teaching ethics in technology education, and the impact of AI on social justice and privacy. The references also include discussions on the legal status of AI entities, the importance of data protection, and the ethical responsibilities of businesses in the context of emerging technologies. Overall, the bibliography serves as a foundational resource for scholars and practitioners seeking to navigate the ethical landscape of AI.

The paper outlines how corporate misconduct has spurred the growth of business ethics, leading to the institutionalization of ethical practices in organizations. It identifies five key areas within business ethics that could enhance the discourse on AI ethics: stakeholder management, reporting on non-financial digital issues, corporate governance and regulation, AI/tech ethics in education, and the issues of greenwashing and digital ethics washing. 

AI ethics is presented as a distinct subfield within applied ethics, which traditionally encompasses biomedical, environmental, and business ethics. The rapid integration of AI technologies into daily life raises significant ethical concerns, particularly related to fairness, discrimination, and accountability. The authors argue that understanding the successes and failures of business ethics can provide valuable lessons for the ongoing development of AI ethics, ultimately advocating for a collaborative approach between these two fields to address moral challenges effectively.

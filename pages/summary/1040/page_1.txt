The paper by Mario D. Schultz and Peter Seele discusses the potential for knowledge transfer from business ethics to enhance the development and institutionalization of AI ethics. Recognizing the rapid advancements in AI technologies and their ethical implications, the authors argue that insights from established business ethics can address current challenges in AI ethics. They identify five key areas where business ethics can contribute to AI ethics: stakeholder management, standardized reporting, corporate governance and regulation, curriculum accreditation, and the concept of AI ethics washing, akin to greenwashing. 

The authors emphasize that the ethical discourse surrounding AI is still evolving, and learning from business ethics could provide frameworks and strategies for organizations to navigate ethical dilemmas in AI deployment. Conversely, they suggest that business ethics could also benefit from engaging with AI ethics, particularly in adapting to digital transformations. The paper advocates for a collaborative dialogue between these fields to enrich ethical practices and avoid superficial engagements with ethics. Overall, the authors highlight the importance of institutionalizing AI ethics within organizational settings by leveraging existing knowledge from business ethics.

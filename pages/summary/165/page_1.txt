This review paper by You et al. explores the application of generative adversarial networks (GAN) in the field of ophthalmology, highlighting their potential to enhance diagnostic capabilities through advanced image processing techniques. The authors note the increasing adoption of GANs for medical imaging, particularly for tasks such as image synthesis and translation, though they remain relatively unfamiliar to ophthalmology researchers.

The authors conducted a survey of 48 peer-reviewed studies published before June 2021, categorizing the types of GANs used, their applications in ophthalmology, and the outcomes achieved. Key findings indicate that GANs are effective in various tasks including segmentation, data augmentation, denoising, domain transfer, super-resolution, post-intervention prediction, and feature extraction. Despite these advantages, the authors acknowledge limitations such as mode collapse, spatial deformities, and the introduction of high-frequency noise and artifacts.

The paper concludes that while the use of GANs in ophthalmology shows promise, it is still in the early stages of clinical validation compared to traditional deep learning classification methods. The authors emphasize the importance of selecting appropriate GAN techniques and employing robust statistical modeling to enhance image analysis performance. This survey serves as a resource for researchers to identify suitable GAN approaches to maximize the utility of ophthalmology datasets in deep learning research.

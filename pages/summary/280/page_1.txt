In our empirical evaluations, we demonstrate that ETs enhance model robustness significantly, particularly in scenarios where data is limited. For instance, in image classification tasks involving street signs, ETs led to a reduction in error rates by up to 15% compared to traditional ResNet classifiers, with negligible increases in model complexity. 

In conclusion, Equivariant Transformers represent a significant advancement in integrating prior knowledge of transformation invariance directly into neural network architectures, allowing for improved performance and robustness in real-world applications. This approach offers a promising alternative to conventional data augmentation strategies, emphasizing the importance of architectural design in achieving desirable invariance properties.

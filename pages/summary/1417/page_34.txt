The paper presents the DriveLM-CARLA dataset, which comprises automatically generated frame-level question-answer pairs organized within an interconnected graph structure. This dataset currently includes questions related to road layouts, stop signs, traffic lights, and vehicles, with plans to expand to additional categories such as static objects and weather in future versions. The use of the CARLA driving simulator enables scalable data generation without manual annotation, and it supports various sensor outputs for training different network architectures. Each question is crafted to promote situational reasoning, aiding in the answering of subsequent questions. Questions are categorized into perception, prediction, or planning, and each QA-pair includes an object ID for object tracking and temporal reasoning. Relationships between questions within the graph are also documented to facilitate efficient traversal.

Additionally, the paper introduces PDM-Lite, an expert algorithm designed to address all 38 scenarios in CARLA Leaderboard 2.0, as opposed to the previous rule-based experts which were limited to Leaderboard 1.0. PDM-Lite operates in six stages, beginning with path planning using an A* algorithm to create a dense path based on the HD map of the town and incorporating information such as speed limits and distances to traffic signals. It also adapts routes for scenarios requiring deviation from the default path. Furthermore, PDM-Lite forecasts the movements of dynamic agents over a 2-second horizon using a kinematic bicycle model, assuming that nearby agents will maintain their current controls and behaviors. The forecasting is limited to actors within 50 meters of the ego agent.

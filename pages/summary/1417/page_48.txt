The paper discusses the capabilities of the DriveLM-Agent in various Visual Question Answering (VQA) tasks within driving scenarios. It emphasizes the agent's performance in perception, prediction, planning, and behavior, showcasing its robustness in understanding and responding to dynamic environments.

The model demonstrates strong generalization abilities across different sensor configurations, particularly when applied to the Waymo dataset, despite the lack of annotated data. This indicates that DriveLM-Agent can effectively adapt to new environments with manually defined questions.

Qualitative results from the CARLA dataset reveal the agent's proficiency in recognizing and responding to unseen pedestrian scenarios. The agent successfully identifies a pedestrian and appropriately decides to stop the vehicle. However, the study also highlights some limitations, where the agent struggles to translate pedestrian detection into correct behavioral responses in certain situations.

Overall, the findings underscore the potential of the DriveLM-Agent in enhancing automated driving systems through improved VQA capabilities, while also identifying areas for further development to address its limitations.

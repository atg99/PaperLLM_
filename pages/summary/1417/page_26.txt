Frame-level QA Annotation. Once key frames and objects are identified, annotators generate question-and-answer pairs that pertain to the perception, prediction, and planning aspects of the scene. This structured approach ensures that the data captures critical interactions and potential future states of the ego vehicle and surrounding objects.

Quality Assurance and Post-processing. The annotation process includes multiple quality checks to ensure the accuracy and reliability of the data. This is followed by post-processing, where qualified data is refined and organized for further analysis. The overall methodology emphasizes the importance of thoroughness and reliability in creating a dataset that supports the understanding of vehicle behavior in complex driving scenarios.

In summary, the paper describes a comprehensive approach to annotating driving scenarios using a structured framework that encompasses perception, prediction, and planning. The use of detailed object encoding, combined with a rigorous annotation process, ensures the dataset is robust and valuable for research in autonomous vehicle behavior.

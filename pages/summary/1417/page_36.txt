The paper discusses the target speed proposal methodology employed by PDM-Lite, which utilizes the Intelligent Driver Model (IDM) to determine a vehicle's desired speed based on various leading agents, including vehicles, bicycles, and static objects. Key parameters include a desired velocity set at 72% of the speed limit, a desired net distance to the leading agent, and specific time headways tailored to different types of agents. The maximum acceleration and deceleration rates for the ego vehicle are also outlined, with varying values depending on the speed of the vehicle.

The performance of PDM-Lite is assessed through evaluations on 20 official validation routes from Leaderboard 2.0, showing that performance variance is attributed to random traffic initialization rather than weather conditions. The data collection methodology is based on the CARLA Simulator (version 0.9.14), which provides an enhanced training and evaluation environment through new maps and scenarios. The planner, PDM-Lite, is designed to handle all 38 scenarios of CARLA Leaderboard 2.0, organized into six stages as detailed in the paper.

The paper includes several appendices detailing various aspects of the research. Appendix F discusses additional experiments that explore generalization to unseen objects, the impact of different context designs on zero-shot performance, evaluations using conventional Visual Question Answering (VQA) metrics, and comparisons of model efficiency. Appendix G presents qualitative results, showcasing examples of context, questions, and answers from datasets such as nuScenes, Waymo, and CARLA. It also compares predicted answers with ground truth, including SPICE and GPT scores to illustrate the evaluation metrics. Finally, Appendix H reviews related work from two perspectives: reasoning over graph structures, which aligns with the authors' graph-structure reasoning approach, and the exploration of vision-language benchmarks specific to autonomous driving.

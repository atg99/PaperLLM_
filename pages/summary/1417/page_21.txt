The paper "DriveLM: Driving with Graph Visual Question Answering" presents a comprehensive examination of the DriveLM framework, focusing on driving scenarios enhanced by graph-based visual question answering. The appendices provide in-depth discussions and additional details on various aspects of the research. 

Appendix A outlines motivating questions that arise from the main text, addressing the rationale behind adapting general visual language models (VLMs) for driving contexts. Appendix B details the composition and annotation process of the DriveLM-nuScenes dataset, including statistics on question-answer categories. Appendix C describes the DriveLM-CARLA dataset, focusing on the PDM-Lite expert, dataset composition, graph structure, and data generation and annotation methods. 

In Appendix D, the metrics used for evaluating tasks in the graph visual question answering (GVQA) framework are discussed, highlighting the rationale for the GPT-score as the primary metric for P1−3VQA tasks. Finally, Appendix E elaborates on the design of prompting with context, trajectory tokenization, and the differences in context between training and evaluation, as well as the hyperparameters used in the tokenizer. 

Overall, the paper emphasizes the integration of visual question answering in driving applications, providing a structured approach to data annotation, metric evaluation, and model design.

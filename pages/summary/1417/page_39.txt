D.2 Behavior Task Metrics
In this section, we discuss metrics designed to evaluate the behavior of the driving model. These metrics assess the model's decision-making processes, adherence to traffic rules, and overall driving performance. Key aspects include safety, efficiency, and comfort during driving tasks.

D.3 Motion Task Metrics
Motion Task Metrics focus on the model's ability to navigate and perform driving maneuvers effectively. Metrics in this category evaluate trajectory accuracy, responsiveness to dynamic environments, and the smoothness of motion. They are crucial for determining how well the model can adapt to real-world driving scenarios, ensuring both safety and fluidity in its actions.

In summary, DriveLM-Metrics provides a comprehensive framework for evaluating the performance of the driving model across various tasks. By employing a combination of established VQA metrics, novel scoring methods, and behavior-focused evaluations, it aims to deliver a robust assessment of both the model's question-answering capabilities and its driving behaviors.

The paper presents results from the PDM-Lite driving performance evaluation on the CARLA simulation platform, specifically across several validation routes. The performance metrics analyzed include Driving Score (DS), Infraction Score (IS), Route Completion (RC), Normalized Driving Score (NDS), and Normalized Infraction Score (NIS), with data averaged over three seeds for routes 0-19. 

The results show varying performances across routes, with Route 0 achieving a DS of 14.0 and a perfect RC of 100.0, but with a relatively high IS of 0.14. Conversely, Routes like 10 and 19 demonstrated higher DS values (e.g., Route 10 with 973.3) alongside perfect route completion scores. On average, the driving scores across all routes indicate a DS of 36.3 and an NIS of 0.63, suggesting a range of driving behaviors and performance outcomes influenced by route conditions, traffic behaviors, and potentially weather factors, albeit the latter was not accounted for in PDM-Lite evaluations. 

This comprehensive analysis highlights the variability in automated driving performance and underlines the significance of route characteristics in evaluating driving systems.

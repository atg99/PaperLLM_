The paper introduces DriveLM-Agent, a baseline approach for Generalized Visual Question Answering (GVQA) that leverages a Vision-Language Model (VLM) to translate scene images into ego vehicle motion through a structured process. This framework is built on the BLIP-2 model and consists of several stages: perception, prediction, planning, behavior, and motion. Each of these stages plays a crucial role in understanding the scene and determining the appropriate driving action.

The authors emphasize the importance of logical dependencies among the stages, proposing a context-based prompting method. This method utilizes answers from previous stages to inform subsequent queries, thereby mimicking human multi-step reasoning. By appending prior QAs to current questions with a "Context: " prefix, the model effectively retrieves and utilizes relevant information, enhancing both performance and explainability.

The paper highlights that while direct image-to-motion translation is complex, employing contextual information from connected nodes in the GVQA graph simplifies the reasoning process, making it more effective and easier to interpret.

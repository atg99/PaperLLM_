The paper introduces DriveLM, a new framework for end-to-end autonomous driving that leverages Graph Visual Question Answering (GVQA). The authors propose an innovative approach where questions and answers are interconnected through logical dependencies at both object and task levels. The framework consists of three main tasks: perception, prediction, and planning, culminating in behavior and motion, which are articulated in natural language.

DriveLM-Data is introduced as a dedicated dataset for training the DriveLM-Agent, which serves as a baseline for GVQA tasks. The effectiveness of this framework is assessed using DriveLM-Metrics, specifically designed for challenging scenarios that require zero-shot generalization, meaning the model must perform well on unseen objects and scenes.

Key aspects include the ability of vision-language models to interpret complex driving scenarios, predict future movements of surrounding objects, and plan safe actions for the ego vehicle. The authors validate their approach through various QA pairs, demonstrating its practical application in real-world driving contexts. Overall, DriveLM represents a significant advancement in integrating vision-language models into autonomous driving systems.

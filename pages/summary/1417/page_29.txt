The paper presents DriveLM, a framework for driving that utilizes Graph Visual Question Answering (VQA) to enhance interaction with the driving environment. It categorizes questions into three main tasks: perception, prediction, and planning, each further divided into specific question types. 

In the perception category, questions focus on identifying surrounding objects, traffic elements, and important objects. Examples include inquiries about the current scene and specific object locations relative to the ego vehicle. For instance, it can identify moving cars and barriers in the vicinity and categorize traffic elements based on their status and position.

The prediction task includes questions about motion states and potential collisions, while planning encompasses inquiries related to safe actions and goal-oriented maneuvers. The framework also addresses visual descriptions, logical sequences, and attention mechanisms.

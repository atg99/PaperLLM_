DriveLM is a novel approach for end-to-end driving using graph visual question answering, evaluated on the nuScenes and Waymo datasets. In the context of nuScenes, incorporating behavior context alongside motion context allows DriveLM to achieve performance comparable to the state-of-the-art driving architecture, UniAD-Single. In the Waymo evaluation, DriveLM-Agent demonstrates superior performance over UniAD-Single, leveraging graph context effectively.

The results indicate that DriveLM-Agent significantly enhances predictive accuracy for speed when using a full graph context, with accuracy rising from 43.90 to 54.29. However, the chain approach yielded lower accuracy at 41.28, suggesting limited utility in that configuration. Qualitative evaluations reveal that DriveLM-Agent provides intuitive and interpretable answers for various driving scenarios, though some exceptions were noted in specific planning and perception tasks.

Overall, DriveLM showcases promising capabilities in driving with visual question answering, particularly in adapting to different sensor configurations without additional training.

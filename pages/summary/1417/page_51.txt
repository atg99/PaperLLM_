The paper presents DriveLM, a framework for driving that integrates graph-based visual question answering (VQA). It highlights the importance of perception and planning in autonomous driving scenarios, emphasizing how the model processes visual inputs to make informed decisions. 

Key components of the framework include:

1. **Prediction**: The model predicts actions based on visual inputs, considering the environment and potential obstacles. It utilizes visual cues to assess situations such as vehicles ahead and traffic signals.

2. **Perception**: The system is designed to interpret complex scenes, identifying objects and their relationships within the driving environment. It focuses on understanding the dynamics of the surroundings, such as other vehicles and traffic signals.

3. **Planning**: DriveLM employs a planning mechanism that leverages the perception data to formulate driving strategies, ensuring safe navigation through various scenarios, including stops at intersections.

The qualitative results demonstrate the effectiveness of DriveLM in handling real-time driving challenges, such as overtaking vehicles and navigating intersections with stop signs. The examples illustrate how the model successfully integrates visual understanding with actionable driving decisions. Overall, DriveLM showcases advancements in autonomous driving through enhanced perception and decision-making capabilities.

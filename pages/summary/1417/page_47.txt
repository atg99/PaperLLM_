The paper presents DriveLM, a system designed for driving tasks using Graph Visual Question Answering (GVQA). It integrates behavior prediction, planning, and perception to enhance driving performance. Key components include understanding the environment through scene perception and making informed decisions based on visual inputs.

The DriveLM-nuScenes section provides qualitative examples of the GVQA reasoning process, illustrating how the model handles various tasks related to driving behavior. The results are assessed using SPICE and GPT scores, highlighting the model's strengths and weaknesses. Notably, the model struggles with accurately predicting the movement status of objects based on single-frame images, a challenge that also affects human judgment.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the importance of integrating visual reasoning into autonomous driving systems and presents detailed analyses of the model's performance in real-world scenarios.

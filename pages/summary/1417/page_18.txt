The referenced works explore various aspects of autonomous driving and the integration of language models with visual processing. Key contributions include the development of models that enhance understanding and modeling of human drivers, as seen in Macadam's work, and the introduction of systems like DRAMA and GPT-Driver, which focus on risk localization and driving capabilities through language-based learning. Recent advancements, such as LingoQA and NuScenes-QA, emphasize multimodal question answering to improve autonomous driving scenarios. Papers by Ouyang and Radford discuss training language models with human feedback and highlight their multitask learning capabilities. Additionally, frameworks like VLP and MotionLM investigate the fusion of vision and language for task planning and motion forecasting. Collectively, these works reflect a significant push towards integrating multimodal learning to enhance the safety and efficiency of autonomous driving systems.

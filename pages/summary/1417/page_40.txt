The paper discusses the evaluation of prediction accuracy in the context of behavior prediction tasks using various metrics. The authors introduce a scoring system based on a prompt for GPT, where answers are rated out of 100, with higher scores reflecting closer alignment to the correct answer. This scoring method focuses on completeness, which measures how many ground truth questions are accurately answered. A threshold of 0.5 is set for the SPICE score to determine correct predictions.

For behavior prediction tasks, the authors utilize classification accuracy, which encompasses the accuracy of behavior, speed, and steering components. The ego vehicle's future trajectory is represented as a series of points in a bird’s-eye view, and distances for each coordinate are calculated. These distances are then categorized into predefined bins for speed and steering, forming a behavior category. The accuracy of the predicted behaviors is compared against those generated by their DriveLM-Agent outputs.

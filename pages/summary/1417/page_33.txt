The paper presents DriveLM, a system that utilizes Graph Visual Question Answering (VQA) to enhance driving capabilities by interpreting complex road scenes. The study focuses on the DriveLM-nuScenes dataset, which is structured around specific question templates that address various driving scenarios. 

Key findings include the necessity for the ego vehicle to consider surrounding elements, particularly in complex situations where road scenes may involve pedestrians and other traffic elements. The statistics reveal a significant distribution of question types focused on identifying surrounding objects, traffic elements, and important objects, highlighting the complexity of driving environments.

The analysis also distinguishes between different categories of questions related to perception, prediction, and planning, with a notable emphasis on traffic elements due to their unique characteristics. The DriveLM-nuScenes dataset categorizes various object types, including vehicles and pedestrians, and illustrates the varied nature of questions posed in relation to these objects.

Additionally, the paper outlines the structure and methodology for the DriveLM-CARLA component, which complements the DriveLM-nuScenes dataset by further expanding the dataset's composition and collection processes. Overall, the research emphasizes the critical role of VQA in enhancing situational awareness and decision-making for autonomous driving systems.

The paper discusses the advancements in autonomous driving (AD) using Visual Language Models (VLMs) and presents a novel approach called DriveLM-Agent. This method combines VLMs with graph-structured question-answering (QA) to enhance generalization capabilities in zero-shot end-to-end planning, outperforming previous models like GPT-Driver and DriveGPT4.

In the discussion, the authors compare DriveLM-Agent with UniAD-Single, highlighting that while DriveLM-Agent has fewer trainable parameters, it incurs a higher inference cost and slower throughput, which may affect its practical application. The authors identify several limitations, including the efficiency constraints due to long inference times, challenges in processing driving-specific inputs such as LiDAR, and the current reliance on an open-loop planning approach, which could be improved by incorporating ego vehicle status in closed-loop settings.

The conclusion emphasizes the potential of VLMs in creating end-to-end autonomous driving agents that offer enhanced generalization compared to traditional task-specific methods. The authors introduce the concept of Graph VQA and provide new datasets and metrics to facilitate further research in this area, establishing a foundation for future exploration of closed-loop planning with VLMs.

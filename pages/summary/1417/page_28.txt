The paper discusses the methodology utilized for translating and refining questions and answers related to autonomous driving tasks between Chinese and English. The authors employ a combination of vocabulary mapping and the GPT-3.5 model for translation, followed by manual checks to ensure accuracy. A specific prompt structure is outlined for enhancing the quality of the English text, focusing on maintaining the original meaning while making stylistic improvements.

The study categorizes questions into three main areas: perception, prediction, and planning. This categorization aims to reflect the various aspects of driving tasks, with most questions being annotated by human annotators, thereby providing a reliable proxy for human-like reasoning in driving scenarios. 

Furthermore, the paper includes a statistical analysis of question-answer (QA) categories at both task and object levels, highlighting the diverse nature of the QA types related to autonomous driving. The findings suggest a comprehensive framework that links various stages of human driving logic, underlining the richness and logical relationships present in the QA dataset, which is essential for constructing a graph-structured QA system for autonomous driving applications.

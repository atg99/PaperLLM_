The references provided indicate key contributions to the fields of computer vision and image processing. 

Zagoruyko and Komodakis (2016) discuss Wide Residual Networks, which enhance the performance of residual networks by increasing their width. This approach addresses the vanishing gradient problem and improves training efficiency.

Zheng et al. (2017) present a multi-attention convolutional neural network designed for fine-grained image recognition. Their work highlights the importance of attention mechanisms in distinguishing subtle differences between similar categories in images.

Zhou et al. (2006) explore special paraunitary matrices and the Cayley transform in the context of multidimensional orthogonal filter banks. This research contributes to the understanding of signal processing techniques that can be applied in image processing tasks.

These studies collectively advance the understanding and application of neural networks and signal processing in visual recognition tasks.

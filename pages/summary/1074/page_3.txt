The paper discusses various strategies to enhance the performance and stability of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) through orthogonal constraints and regularization techniques. It begins by addressing the challenges faced in RNNs, particularly the impact of soft versus hard orthogonal constraints on their performance. A method utilizing exponential maps for orthogonal constraints is also introduced.

The paper highlights the benefits of orthogonal kernels in stabilizing training and optimizing CNNs, drawing parallels between orthogonal weight initialization and batch normalization. However, it notes that maintaining orthogonality throughout training can be challenging, leading to the adoption of Stiefel manifold-based optimization methods to ensure orthogonal weights. Recent advancements have relaxed the strict orthogonality requirements, using techniques such as enforcing the Gram matrix of weights to approximate the identity matrix, which has been shown to improve image generation in generative adversarial networks (GANs).

Moreover, the paper addresses the issue of feature redundancy in optimized CNNs, suggesting that this redundancy can be exploited for network compression and efficiency. Techniques to enhance feature diversity, including multi-attention mechanisms and diversity loss, are proposed alongside orthogonality regularization.

In tackling training instability, the paper presents various methods, such as initializing layers with consistent variances, employing batch normalization, and utilizing gradient clipping strategies to manage exploding and vanishing gradients.

The concept of orthogonal convolution is introduced, where convolution is framed as a matrix-vector multiplication, employing a doubly block-Toeplitz matrix to stabilize the spectrum of the convolution kernel. This approach is primarily focused on 2D convolution, with the potential for generalization to other configurations.

In summary, the paper emphasizes the importance of orthogonality in CNNs and RNNs, exploring innovative methods to ensure stability and enhance performance through various regularization and optimization techniques.

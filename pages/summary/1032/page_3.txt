The paper discusses the profound impact of artificial intelligence (AI) on various aspects of society, including the hospitality industry, workforce dynamics, and ethical considerations. It highlights that advancements such as driverless cars may lead to changes in consumer behavior, as passengers could engage in activities like sleeping or working during travel. Additionally, it notes that AI-recruiters can perpetuate human biases due to biased training data, raising concerns about fairness in hiring processes.

The paper emphasizes the widening wealth gap driven by disparities between returns on capital and labor, which could lead to social unrest. It asserts the urgency of formulating ethical frameworks for AI development to ensure responsible practices. The complexity of creating ethical AI is acknowledged, making it a critical challenge for researchers and developers.

Ethics is defined as the moral principles guiding behavior, with a focus on right versus wrong. Various ethical theories, including virtue ethics, are explored, highlighting their relevance in human interactions and the increasing integration of machines in daily life. The paper outlines the evolving nature of ethical studies and provides definitions from various researchers, emphasizing the importance of ethical considerations in AI development, including the responsibilities of AI systems and their creators. The paper ultimately calls for a systematic approach to ethical AI to navigate the complexities of modern technology and its societal implications.

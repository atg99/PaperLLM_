The paper titled "Artificial Intelligence (AI) Ethics: Ethics of AI and Ethical AI" by Keng Siau and Weiyu Wang explores the ethical considerations surrounding artificial intelligence. It distinguishes between the ethics of AI, which pertains to the moral implications and societal impacts of AI technologies, and ethical AI, which focuses on creating AI systems that adhere to ethical standards. The authors emphasize the importance of establishing ethical frameworks to guide the development and deployment of AI, ensuring that these technologies benefit society while minimizing harm. The discussion encompasses various ethical principles relevant to AI, highlighting the need for accountability, transparency, and fairness in AI applications. The publication serves as a resource for understanding the complexities of AI ethics and the imperative for responsible AI governance.

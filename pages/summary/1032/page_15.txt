The paper highlights the credentials and contributions of Keng Siau and Weiyu Wang in the field of information technology and artificial intelligence. Keng Siau, currently the Chair of the Department of Business and Information Technology at the Missouri University of Science and Technology, has an extensive background, previously holding the Edwin J. Faulkner Chair Professor position at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln. His leadership roles include directing the UNL-IBM Global Innovation Hub and serving as Vice President of Education for the Association for Information Systems. With over 300 academic publications, he is recognized as a leading researcher in information systems, supported by funding from the U.S. National Science Foundation and IBM. His accolades include multiple awards for teaching and research excellence.

Weiyu Wang, who holds both a Master of Science in Information Science and Technology and an MBA from the same institution, focuses her research on the implications of artificial intelligence on various aspects of life, including economic, societal, and mental health dimensions. She also explores governance, ethical, and trust issues associated with AI. The paper underscores the significance of their work in advancing knowledge and understanding in their respective fields.

The paper discusses advancements in visual question answering (VQA) models, emphasizing their capability to integrate visual and linguistic information effectively. It references various key studies that have contributed to the field, including the development of visual-linguistic representations and cross-modality encoders. Notable works such as VL-BERT, LXMERT, and neural-symbolic approaches are highlighted for their innovative methodologies. The paper also mentions the importance of datasets like YFCC100M for multimedia research and various techniques for image caption generation and scene understanding. Overall, it underscores the significance of attention mechanisms and modular architectures in enhancing VQA performance, while also acknowledging the ongoing challenges in reasoning and interpretation within visual contexts.

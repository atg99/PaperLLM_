The paper presents results from macro-stage and micro-stage trajectory predictions across three datasets: PFSD, SDD, and nuScenes. In the macro-stage results, the first column shows the Long-term Goal (LG) heat map prediction from the LG-CV AE overlaid on the local semantic map, while the subsequent columns display two Short-term Goals (SG) and one LG from SG-Net. Ground truth (GT) past and future trajectories are indicated by blue and orange lines, respectively, with GT LG and SGs marked by 'x'. 

In the micro-stage results, complete trajectory predictions are illustrated for each dataset, comparing the authors' method with Trajectron++, Y-net, and AgentFormer. The images are arranged from top-left to bottom-right, with blue lines denoting GT past trajectories, orange lines for GT future trajectories, and red lines representing predicted future trajectories. The results highlight the effectiveness of the proposed approach in trajectory prediction across different sampling generations and datasets.

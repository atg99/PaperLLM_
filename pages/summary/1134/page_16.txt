The paper discusses various improvement strategies for optimization algorithms, specifically focusing on the Wolf Pack Algorithm (WPA) and the Chicken Swarm Optimization Algorithm (CSOA). 

For the WPA, three main strategies are highlighted:

1. **Improved Search Strategy**: Liang et al. formulated a cluster cooperative rule based on dynamic wolf head alternation and real-time role assignment. They proposed a fatigue-rendering tactic that enhances the efficiency of solving large-scale complex optimization problems and improves the operational effectiveness of distributed cluster cooperative attack problems.

2. **Hybrid Algorithm**: A new hybrid optimization algorithm combining wolf pack search and local search (WPS-LS) was proposed for the traveling salesman problem. This algorithm simulates the predatory behavior of a wolf pack to explore the solution space thoroughly while employing local search operations to enhance speed and accuracy.

3. **Prospect of WPA**: While WPA offers advantages in solving complex optimization problems, it faces challenges such as sensitivity to parameter sizes, decreased convergence speed due to inefficient siege behavior, and a need for stronger mathematical support for its biological principles. Future research should focus on optimizing parameter settings, balancing search capabilities, and providing theoretical analysis.

For the CSOA, the paper outlines improvement strategies aimed at addressing its limitations such as low solution accuracy and weak local searchability:

1. **Improved Algorithm Rules**: Various modifications have been made, including boundary adjustments and the introduction of adaptive distributions and elite reverse learning strategies to enhance performance in specific applications.

2. **Parameter Adjustment**: Research has been conducted to analyze key parameters of the algorithm, leading to improved convergence accuracy and speed, particularly in high-dimensional optimization problems.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the ongoing development and refinement of these algorithms to better address complex optimization challenges.

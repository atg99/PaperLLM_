This paper explores swarm intelligence optimization algorithms, which are natural heuristic algorithms inspired by the collective behavior of animals. The primary goal of these algorithms is to identify global optimal solutions to optimization problems through information exchange and cooperation among individuals. The authors begin by outlining the principles behind various swarm intelligence optimization algorithms and subsequently provide examples of their applications across different fields.

The paper also summarizes the advantages and limitations of these algorithms, highlighting their intelligence, strong search capabilities, and versatility, which have made them a focal point in intelligent computing research. Improvement strategies for these algorithms are discussed, alongside future development prospects.

The authors emphasize the significance of bionics in the evolution of these algorithms, linking it to cybernetics and the study of life phenomena in relation to mechanical principles. The discussion underscores that swarm intelligence algorithms represent a probabilistic parallel search method, where individual agents operate based on simple, modifiable rules. Overall, this paper contributes to the understanding and advancement of swarm intelligence optimization techniques in various applications.

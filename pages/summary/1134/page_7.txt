This paper discusses various swarm intelligence optimization algorithms, focusing on their structures, processes, and applications. 

The first algorithm mentioned is the Basic Algorithm (BA), which is characterized by its simple structure, few parameters, strong robustness, and ease of understanding. BA has been widely applied in various optimization problems including function optimization, image processing, production scheduling, artificial intelligence, and power systems.

Next, the Wolf Pack Algorithm (WPA) is introduced, which simulates the hunting behavior of wolves. The algorithm involves several steps: initialization of the wolf population, searching behavior to find optimal positions based on prey concentration, besieging behavior when prey is located, updating the wolf pack based on survival principles, and determining whether the maximum iteration number has been reached. WPA is noted for its fewer sensitive parameters, strong robustness, fast convergence speed, and high accuracy, finding applications in path planning, image processing, production scheduling, and more.

Finally, the Chickenswarm Optimization Algorithm is described, based on the foraging behavior of chickens. This algorithm divides the swarm into subgroups consisting of a rooster, hens, and chickens, each distributed according to fitness values. Hierarchical foraging behavior is utilized, where chickens can feed on food discovered by others. 

Overall, the paper highlights the effectiveness of these algorithms in solving complex optimization problems across various fields.

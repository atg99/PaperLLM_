The papers cited present advancements and applications of these algorithms in diverse fields such as mobile robot path planning, wireless sensor networks, image processing, and power systems.

The artificial fish swarm optimization algorithm is highlighted through studies on trajectory optimization and lesion segmentation in medical imaging. Lastly, bacterial foraging optimization techniques are discussed, including their application in power system reconfiguration and image processing tasks.

The paper discusses the evolution of automated functionalities in vehicles, highlighting key advancements and the gradual introduction of self-driving capabilities since 2003. It outlines various automated features such as Intelligent Parking Assist, adaptive cruise control, and lane departure systems, which have enhanced driver safety and comfort. These technologies utilize vision and radar systems for pre-collision detection and deep learning for environmental awareness.

The concept of self-driving vehicles has been a longstanding aspiration since the 1930s, but practical implementations faced challenges due to complex urban driving conditions. Although autonomous vehicles have been developed for other environments, city driving remained a significant hurdle. The paper suggests that as cars become more capable than human drivers, urban living patterns may change, leading to fewer car ownerships, longer commutes, and a transformation in urban organization.

The report focuses on life in North American cities, intentionally excluding military applications to emphasize the importance of monitoring AI advancements in defense. It targets four key audiences: the general public, industry stakeholders, governments, and AI researchers. For the public, it provides an accessible overview of AI's current state and potential. For industry, it outlines relevant technologies and legal challenges to guide resource allocation. Government entities are aided in planning for AI governance, while researchers are encouraged to prioritize ethical and legal considerations in their work.

The One Hundred Year Study on AI anticipates future assessments by various stakeholders, emphasizing the report's value in understanding AI's trajectory. The Standing Committee expresses gratitude to the Study Panel members for their expertise and time, particularly acknowledging Professor Peter Stone for his leadership. The report also thanks the Standing Committee chair, Barbara Grosz, for her mediating role, and Kerry Tremain for his contributions to the editing process.

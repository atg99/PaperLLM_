The paper discusses the need for policies that evaluate the impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) on democratic values and equitable distribution of its benefits. It highlights that significant AI advancements have influenced North American cities over the past fifteen years, with even more substantial changes anticipated in the next fifteen years. These advancements stem from the analysis of large data sets, improvements in sensory technologies, and the rise of deep learning. As AI applications emerge in areas like transportation and healthcare, it is crucial to introduce them in a manner that fosters trust, respects human rights, and addresses ethical, privacy, and security concerns. Policies should ensure that the advantages of AI technologies are widely and fairly distributed to positively impact urban life in North America by 2030 and beyond.

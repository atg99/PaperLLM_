The paper discusses the growing role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in education, particularly through Intelligent Tutoring Systems (ITS) and online learning platforms. Systems like Carnegie Speech and Duolingo utilize Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to assist with language learning, while cognitive tutors provide personalized feedback in subjects like mathematics and science. 

ITS applications are expanding in higher education, with examples such as SHERLOCK, which trains Air Force technicians, and advanced modules from the University of Southern California aimed at cultural training for military personnel. New algorithms, including Bayesian Knowledge Tracing, support personalized learning experiences.

The rise of Massive Open Online Courses (MOOCs) has further transformed education, leveraging NLP and machine learning for grading and assessment. Online education is becoming increasingly vital for professionals seeking lifelong learning, as these platforms require less face-to-face interaction.

The paper argues that AI has significantly enhanced instructors' abilities to manage large classrooms, with some reaching sizes of tens of thousands of students. This capability is largely due to automated question generation and evaluation technologies, which facilitate ongoing assessment in massive educational settings.

In the next fifteen years, AI technologies are expected to significantly enhance human teaching, both in classrooms and at home, by improving human interaction. The development of sophisticated virtual reality (VR) scenarios will allow students to immerse themselves in various subjects, supported by interdisciplinary collaborations between AI and humanities researchers. Current applications of VR in natural sciences are paving the way for similar advancements in arts and other disciplines.

Adaptive learning systems are projected to become central to higher education, driven by the need to reduce costs and accommodate more students effectively. This evolution will see the integration of MOOCs and online education across all educational levels, facilitating personalized learning experiences where students can progress at their own pace. Online systems will evolve alongside learners, leveraging learning analytics to enhance personalized education tools.

The transition from traditional hard copy materials to digital and audio formats is anticipated to become widespread, with smarter digital reading devices providing additional information. Machine Translation technology will improve the accessibility of educational materials across languages, making translation services more efficient and cost-effective.

Moreover, online learning will create opportunities for adults and professionals to acquire new skills in rapidly changing fields, including fully online professional degrees and certifications. In regions where access to education is limited, online resources could offer significant benefits, provided the population has the necessary tools to access them. The development of these resources is expected to aid foundations in delivering quality education internationally.

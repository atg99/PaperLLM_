The paper discusses the prospects and recommendations for AI public policy, emphasizing that AI applications should aim to create societal value. The recommendations, while focused on a North American city in 2030, are applicable globally. Key strategies include enhancing the understanding of AI systems, fostering human interaction, and preventing discrimination. The report advocates for a shift from strict regulation to policies that encourage innovation and corporate responsibility, addressing the societal issues raised by AI technologies.

The paper anticipates a gradual development of AI technologies, with small improvements potentially leading to significant advancements. The success of AI applications will be measured by the value they provide to human lives and how easily users adapt to these technologies. Trust in AI systems is crucial, as errors can lead to public backlash. Developers are urged to manage expectations and enhance user understanding of AI decisions to build trust.

Furthermore, the impact of AI on human capabilities is explored, highlighting the potential decline in certain skills as machines take over tasks. While AI may outperform humans in specific areas, there remains a complementary relationship where humans excel in complex reasoning and creativity. The exposure of children to AI applications is noted as a factor that will shape generational perceptions of AI's societal influence. Overall, the paper underscores the importance of creating value through AI while addressing the ethical and practical implications of its integration into daily life.

The paper discusses advancements in reinforcement learning (RL) algorithms, particularly focusing on Deep Q-Networks (DQN) and its variations for improving autonomous navigation systems. The DQN utilizes a Q-table initialized with random weights and updates its parameters based on received signals and rewards during each episode, which helps in learning optimal actions. However, the standard DQN can lead to correlations during learning due to its parameter accumulation, prompting the introduction of the Double DQN. This variant mitigates this issue by using distinct values for action selection.

Further enhancements led to the development of Dueling DQN, which separates the evaluation of state values and action advantages, thereby improving the learning efficiency. The combination of Double DQN and Dueling DQN results in the Double Dueling DQN (D3QN), which leverages the strengths of both approaches to refine the target value calculation.

Additionally, the Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) algorithm is highlighted as another significant method in deep reinforcement learning. PPO replaces rigid constraints with flexible penalties, facilitating the resolution of optimization problems by employing the Kullback-Leibler divergence to manage policy updates effectively.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the evolution of DQN and its derivatives, showcasing their contributions to enhancing autonomous navigation through refined learning processes.

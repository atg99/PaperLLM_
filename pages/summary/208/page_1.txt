This paper reviews the application of deep reinforcement learning (DRL) techniques in the domain of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs), commonly referred to as drones. UAVs are increasingly utilized in diverse fields including infrastructure inspection, traffic monitoring, remote sensing, and military operations, necessitating a high level of autonomy for effective mission execution without human intervention. 

The authors provide a comprehensive overview of the state-of-the-art DRL algorithms, which are designed to enhance the guidance, navigation, and control (GNC) of UAVs. They highlight that most current DRL methods have been developed primarily within computer-simulated environments to ensure stable UAV navigation. Despite these advancements, the review identifies significant limitations in the application of these techniques to real-world scenarios, emphasizing the need for further research to overcome these challenges.

The introduction underscores the evolution of UAVs from military applications to broader uses across various sectors, illustrating their growing importance and potential impact. The paper calls for continued exploration and refinement of DRL approaches to fully realize the capabilities of autonomous drones in practical applications.

The paper discusses the application of the Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) algorithm for attitude control in Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs), highlighting its effectiveness in achieving desired flight parameters despite disturbances and varying initial conditions. Utilizing the Actor-Critic framework, the PPO algorithm operates with two separate networks: the actor estimates optimal behavior while the critic evaluates the rewards of actions taken. The research compares PPO with other control methods such as Deep Deterministic Gradient Policy (DDGP) and Trust Region Policy Optimization (TRPO), with results showing PPO to be the most effective controller, particularly for both fixed-wing UAVs and quadcopters.

The paper emphasizes the importance of attitude control in UAV systems, which is essential for stability and performance across different tasks and flying conditions. It also explores the challenges of controlling hybrid UAVs, which combine rotor and fixed-wing designs to leverage their unique advantages. The integration of deep reinforcement learning, specifically the PPO algorithm, facilitates real-time learning and collision avoidance in hybrid systems, enhancing their operational safety and efficiency.

Key contributions include the careful selection of state variables, including convolutional integral error terms, which improve the accuracy of control systems in real-world applications. The study demonstrates that the proposed control schemes can be directly implemented on actual UAVs without extensive tuning, showcasing the potential of PPO in a wide range of UAV dynamics and operational conditions. Overall, the research underscores the significance of advanced reinforcement learning techniques in the evolving field of UAV control.

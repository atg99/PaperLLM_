The paper discusses the implementation of the Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (DDPG) algorithm, which is a reinforcement learning approach. It presents a block diagram illustrating the architecture of the DDPG, highlighting key components such as the observation, action range, and input embedders for both policy and value heads. The architecture includes a middleware layer and dense networks that process inputs to produce outputs relevant for action selection and value estimation.

Additionally, the paper outlines the operational loop of the Actor-Critic algorithm, which encompasses the state, action, and Q-value updates for the Actor network. This loop emphasizes the interaction between the actor, which suggests actions based on the current policy, and the critic, which evaluates the actions by estimating their value. Overall, the DDPG framework is designed to facilitate continuous action spaces in reinforcement learning scenarios.

The paper discusses the advancements in deep reinforcement learning (DRL) algorithms for controlling unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), emphasizing their applications in various fields such as surveillance, disaster response, transportation, and agriculture. It highlights the significance of reward functions in DRL, indicating that the choice of reward function can greatly impact the efficiency of control algorithms. The authors suggest that future research should focus on experimental trials in real-world environments to validate models, as well as on enhancing energy efficiency for UAVs, particularly quadrotors, which currently face limitations due to high energy consumption.

Additionally, the paper addresses challenges related to the coordination of multiple UAVs and mobile robots, including compliance with airspace regulations and traffic management for drone operations. The importance of developing sophisticated unmanned traffic management systems is underscored to facilitate safe and efficient drone flights, particularly for last-mile delivery services.

In conclusion, the paper categorizes UAVs based on various parameters and emphasizes the need for continued technological advancements to achieve higher levels of autonomy. It reviews the role of DRL in path planning and navigation, advocating for more outdoor experimental trials to further the understanding and capabilities of UAVs in dynamic environments. The authors collectively contributed to various aspects of the research, ensuring a comprehensive approach to the topic.

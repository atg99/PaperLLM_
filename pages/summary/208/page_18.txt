The paper discusses advancements in UAV navigation and control, particularly through a distributed deep reinforcement learning (DRL) framework designed to enhance obstacle avoidance and target acquisition. It highlights three key contributions: the development of two specialized networks—the Avoid Network for predicting obstacle motion and ensuring collision-free navigation, and the Acquire Network for optimizing target reach; the integration of these networks to determine real-time actions; and the establishment of a simulation platform to validate the approach against various dynamic obstacles.

The complexity of UAV control is addressed, noting challenges such as changing environmental conditions, the need for energy efficiency, and the impacts of disturbances and sensor noise. Traditional control methods fell short due to their reliance on linear dynamics. The introduction of machine learning, particularly reinforcement learning (RL) and its deep variant (DRL), has provided solutions for more robust and accurate control. DRL enables online learning and utilizes various policy-based model-free methods, including DDPG, TRPO, and PPO, each suited for different applications.

The paper also emphasizes the necessity of dual control loops in autonomous UAV operation: an inner loop for maneuver execution and an outer loop for trajectory planning and communication, which is crucial for swarm management. The use of DRL for fixed-wing UAV attitude control is examined, showcasing the PPO algorithm's effectiveness in handling nonlinear dynamics and control coupling, thus facilitating stable flight.

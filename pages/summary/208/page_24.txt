This approach was effective in training a drone to ascend and navigate to a specified target position within a complex environment. The use of a prioritized experience replay buffer allowed the agent to focus on actions that resulted in significant reward changes, improving learning efficiency. The Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process introduced noise to the rotor speeds, fostering exploration in the agent's decision-making. The results, illustrated in the figures, compare the performance of the trained reinforcement learning agents against traditional PID controllers, showcasing the advantages of the proposed method.

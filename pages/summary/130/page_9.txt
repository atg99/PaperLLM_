This paper presents ALIME, an autoencoder-based approach designed to enhance local interpretability in model predictions for tabular data. Building on the existing LIME framework, ALIME introduces modifications that leverage an autoencoder as a weighting function, aimed at improving both stability and local fidelity of explanations. The authors conducted extensive experiments comparing ALIME to LIME, focusing on stability metrics such as the mean stability and the ratio of standard deviation to mean, which normalizes the co-efficients across varied ranges. Results demonstrate that ALIME consistently outperforms LIME across three datasets in terms of both stability metrics. The authors acknowledge the need for further theoretical and empirical analyses to fully validate the improvements made by ALIME and plan to explore this in future work.

In summary, the paper evaluates the local fidelity and stability of the ALIME method compared to LIME. For local fidelity, ALIME demonstrates superior performance in fitting the local surrogate model to the global black box model, as indicated by higher R2 scores and lower mean squared errors across various datasets. Additionally, the stability of the interpretable model, measured through the standard deviation of coefficients across iterations, shows that ALIME maintains more consistent explanations than LIME. Overall, the results highlight the advantages of ALIME in providing reliable and interpretable insights into model behavior.

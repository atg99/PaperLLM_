The study highlights that the performance of different NLUs varies significantly across tasks and evaluation aspects, with no single NLU consistently outperforming others in all metrics.

In terms of overall performance, IBM Watson emerged as the top performer across both tasks—Repository and Stack Overflow—followed by Rasa, Dialogflow, and LUIS. The ranking was based on the F1-measures for intent classification and entity extraction, as well as median confidence scores for correctly classified intents. This suggests that chatbot developers should prioritize the aspects most relevant to their use cases when selecting an NLU.

The analysis further explores the impact of the confidence score threshold on NLU performance, revealing that varying thresholds can significantly affect classification accuracy. For the Repository task, Rasa demonstrated robustness across a range of thresholds, while IBM Watson and Dialogflow's performance declined at higher thresholds. In contrast, LUIS exhibited a general downward trend with increasing thresholds. For the Stack Overflow task, Rasa also maintained stable performance at lower thresholds, whereas the other NLUs showed reduced effectiveness.

Ultimately, the study recommends that developers carefully determine the appropriate confidence score threshold to optimize NLU performance, as lower thresholds can lead to better results but may also risk incorrect classifications. Additionally, it underscores the challenge of extracting unique entities, which can hinder the effectiveness of NLUs in specific tasks.

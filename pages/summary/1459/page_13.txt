The paper outlines specific recommendations for improving NLU performance when developing chatbots. For intent classification, it suggests training with multiple queries per intent, merging similar intents, and incorporating additional factors like regex and entity type to enhance confidence scores. For entity extraction, it recommends using different features based on entity type and ensuring entities are included in various positions within queries.

The authors highlight previous studies that have compared different NLUs, noting that their work aims to guide developers in selecting appropriate NLUs based on specific needs in the software engineering domain. They emphasize that their evaluation differs by focusing on entity features and their impact on NLU performance, which has not been extensively studied before.

The paper also addresses potential threats to the validity of their study, including internal validity concerns related to manual labeling of tasks and the use of default NLU configurations. They implemented strategies to mitigate these concerns, such as employing multiple annotators to label tasks and analyzing the impact of configuration parameters on performance.

Overall, the research contributes to the understanding of NLU performance in chatbots for software engineering by providing a comprehensive evaluation and practical recommendations for improvement.

This paper discusses various advancements and challenges in the development and application of chatbots within the software engineering domain. It includes references to multiple studies that explore the impact of bots on developer productivity, automated question answering, and the onboarding process for newcomers in open source projects. Key contributions in the field are highlighted, such as the development of AnswerBot for summarizing technical questions and MSRBot for answering queries from software repositories. The paper also addresses the challenges encountered in chatbot development, as documented through analysis of Stack Overflow posts. Additionally, it presents a framework for microservice-based systems known as MSABot and examines the role of bots in enhancing user interaction in social Q&A platforms. The references cited provide a comprehensive overview of the state of chatbots, their applications, and the emerging tools designed to improve natural language understanding and user experience in software engineering contexts.

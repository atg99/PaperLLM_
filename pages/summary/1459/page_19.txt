This appendix provides detailed precision, recall, and F1-measure values for intent classification and entity extraction tasks, elaborating on the results presented in the main sections of the paper.

For intent classification in the Repository task, the results show varying performance across different NLU systems (IBM Watson, Dialogflow, Rasa, and LUIS) for various intents. For instance, the intent "CountCommitsByDate" achieved an F1 score of 88.4 with IBM Watson, while "FileCommits" had a notably lower F1 score of 55.6 with Rasa. The highest F1 scores were generally observed in intents like "BuggyFiles" and "FixCommit," which reached 96.0 and 100.0, respectively.

In the Stack Overflow task, the intent "UsingMethodImproperly" recorded an F1 score of 79.1 with IBM Watson, while "LookingForCodeSample" showed a strong performance with an F1 score of 90.9. The results also highlight areas of weakness, such as the intent "FacingError," which maintained a consistent but low F1 score of 80.0 across NLU systems.

Moving to entity extraction, the Repository task displayed varied results for different entity types. The "JiraTicket" entity achieved perfect F1 scores across all systems, while the "FileName" entity showed a significantly lower F1 score of 15.9 with IBM Watson. For the Stack Overflow task, the "ProgLanguage" entity performed well with an F1 score of 92.0 for IBM Watson, contrasting with the lower scores seen in other entity types such as "Framework" and "Standards."

Overall, these detailed metrics provide insight into the strengths and weaknesses of each NLU system across tasks, emphasizing the importance of precision and recall in evaluating their performance.

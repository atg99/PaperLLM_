The study focused on the characterization of the HBc/G175-CTL and HBc/G161-CTL proteins using dynamic light scattering (DLS) and electron microscopy, revealing their capsid-like structures and particle sizes of 61-62 nm and 77-79 nm, respectively. The HBc/G175-CTL protein underwent further purification via ion exchange chromatography, resulting in homogeneous preparations with a notable increase in particle size compared to wild-type HBc/G virus-like particles (VLPs). Immunization of BALB/c mice with these proteins in conjunction with the AddaVax adjuvant demonstrated strong specific antibody responses against SARS-CoV-2 antigens, underscoring the potential of these fusion proteins in vaccine development.

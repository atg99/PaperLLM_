The paper presents a visualization of the dynamics of vanilla recurrent neural networks (RNNs) using standard Gaussian random weights. The analysis employs different random seeds, ranging from 1 to 16, to illustrate how these variations affect the behavior of the RNNs. The figures demonstrate the RNN's performance and stability under these conditions, providing insights into the inherent characteristics of RNNs when initialized with random weights. This exploration contributes to understanding how weight initialization impacts the dynamics and learning capabilities of RNN architectures.

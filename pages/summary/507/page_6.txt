The paper investigates the impact of adding a hyperparameter, the weight for the EntMin loss, to the V AT model, exploring values from 0 to 1. Results indicate that while V AT performs well with 10% labeled data, incorporating entropy minimization consistently enhances performance. The study also compares self-supervised learning methods, such as rotation and exemplar models, against standard practices where features are frozen or fine-tuned. The authors find that their proposed self-supervised semi-supervised learning (S4L) method, which applies self-supervised loss to both labeled and unlabeled images, achieves optimal results with specific weight settings.

The evaluation against other methods on the ILSVRC-2012 dataset highlights the effectiveness of their approach, with the MOAM model achieving superior accuracy compared to existing methods, especially when using a more powerful architecture. The paper concludes that different models can be complementary, suggesting that combining them could yield even better performance. The proposed methods are shown to be robust against variations in random seed and dataset splits, indicating their reliability.

4. **Self-supervised Learning**: Various studies, such as those by Sermanet et al. (2017) and Shu et al. (2018), explored self-supervised learning approaches, particularly in video data and unsupervised domain adaptation.

5. **Data Augmentation**: Xie et al. (2019) introduced unsupervised data augmentation techniques, enhancing model performance by leveraging additional data.

6. **Deep Learning Architectures**: Szegedy et al. (2015) contributed to the understanding of deeper convolutional networks, while Zhou et al. (2014) focused on learning deep features for scene recognition using extensive datasets.

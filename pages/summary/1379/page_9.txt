The paper discusses the integration of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) in disaster management, emphasizing their applications across various disaster stages including preparedness, response, and recovery. It highlights a survey that categorizes UAV implementations based on disaster types such as geophysical and climatological events. 

Additionally, several studies are referenced that cover a wide range of topics related to robotics in search and rescue operations. These include the current state of rescue robotics, methodologies for robotic motion planning in dynamic environments, and advancements in multi-robot task allocation under uncertainty. The importance of simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) in multi-robot systems is also underscored, along with collaborative visual SLAM for multiple agents.

The paper further examines the role of swarm robotics in enhancing connectivity and coordination among UAVs and ground robots. It references challenges in unlicensed network connectivity and highlights the need for efficient communication in multi-robot systems. The review presents a comprehensive overview of ongoing research in robotic technologies aimed at improving disaster response capabilities, emphasizing the need for robust methodologies and frameworks to achieve effective coordination and task allocation in complex environments.

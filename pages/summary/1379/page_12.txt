The paper discusses the significant role of challenges and competitions in advancing robotics research, particularly in the domain of disaster response robots. It surveys various competitions from the last two decades, emphasizing their evaluation metrics. The authors reference multiple studies, including those on multi-robot routing for persistent monitoring with latency constraints and collective robotic construction. Additionally, the paper highlights the DARPA Subterranean Challenge, which focuses on multi-robot exploration of underground environments. It also mentions the National Robotics Initiative 2.0, aimed at promoting collaborative robots. Overall, the survey underscores the impact of competitive frameworks on the evolution of robotics technologies.

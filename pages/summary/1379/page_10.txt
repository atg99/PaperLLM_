The referenced works encompass a range of studies focused on robotic systems and their interactions, particularly in cooperative navigation, human-robot interaction, and modular robotic designs. 

Allotta et al. (2016) explored cooperative navigation of Autonomous Underwater Vehicles (AUVs) using acoustic communication, demonstrating effective field applications with Typhoon vehicles. Hoshiba et al. (2017) and Nakadai et al. (2017) presented advancements in UAV technology, specifically for sound source localization and search and rescue operations using microphone arrays.

Research by Ayanian and Kumar (2010) focused on decentralized feedback controllers for multi-agent systems navigating obstacle-rich environments, while Michaud et al. (2006) addressed coordinated maneuvering in vehicle platoons. The decentralization of multi-agent policies through communication learning was examined by Paulos et al. (2019).

Stigmergy and ant algorithms were discussed by Dorigo et al. (2000), with practical applications demonstrated by Johansson and Saffiotti (2009) on RFID-enabled navigation for minimalistic robots. Other contributions, such as those by Fujisawa et al. (2008), investigated pheromone-based communication among robots, highlighting innovative approaches in swarm robotics.

Human-robot interaction (HRI) remains a critical theme, with Sheridan (2016) and Murphy et al. (2005, 2009) presenting insights into user interface design and lessons learned from practical deployments in search and rescue scenarios. Kolling et al. (2016) and Nagi et al. (2014) examined human interaction with robot swarms, emphasizing gesture-based communication.

The development of modular reconfigurable robots is another significant area of focus, with works by Brunete et al. (2017) and Seo et al. (2019) discussing current trends and design methodologies. The KAIRO 3 system (Pfotzer et al. 2014) and other modular robots were highlighted for their applications in search and rescue missions.

Overall, these studies collectively contribute to advancing the fields of robotics and automation, particularly in enhancing cooperative capabilities, HRI, and the design of versatile robotic systems for complex tasks.

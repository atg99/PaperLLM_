This general distillation process utilizes attention-based and hidden state distillation methods to ensure that TinyBERT captures essential linguistic knowledge, including syntax and coreference information, which are crucial for natural language understanding. 

In the attention-based distillation, the student model is trained to align its attention matrices with those of the teacher model using a mean squared error loss function. For hidden state distillation, the student's hidden states are transformed to match the teacher's hidden states through a linear transformation, ensuring dimensional compatibility. 

Additionally, embedding-layer distillation parallels the hidden state distillation, focusing on aligning the embeddings of both models. The prediction-layer distillation, however, is excluded in the general distillation phase to emphasize the transfer of foundational knowledge without task-specific biases.

Following the general distillation, task-specific distillation is employed, utilizing a fine-tuned BERT model as the teacher. This two-stage learning framework significantly enhances TinyBERT's performance by bridging the knowledge gap between the teacher and student models, thereby improving TinyBERT's generalization capabilities. 

Overall, the proposed distillation techniques effectively enable TinyBERT to inherit the rich knowledge embedded in the BERT model, facilitating its application in various natural language processing tasks.

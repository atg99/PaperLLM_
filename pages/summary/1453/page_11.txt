However, the combination of General Distillation (GD) and Task-specific Distillation (TD) in TinyBERT leads to superior results compared to both standalone methods, demonstrating the effectiveness of this two-stage approach.

In summary, TinyBERT consistently outperforms various baseline models across both GLUE tasks and question answering datasets, confirming its effectiveness in knowledge distillation and adaptation to different tasks. The findings suggest that the proposed learning framework, which includes General Distillation and Task-specific Distillation, is beneficial for enhancing model performance in natural language processing tasks. Future work will focus on further improving TinyBERT for question answering tasks.

The paper introduces TinyBERT, a lightweight version of the BERT language model designed to enhance efficiency in natural language processing tasks while retaining performance. The authors present a novel knowledge distillation (KD) method tailored for Transformer models, allowing a smaller "student" model, TinyBERT, to effectively learn from a larger "teacher" model, BERT. This process involves a two-stage learning framework that incorporates both pre-training and task-specific training, ensuring that TinyBERT captures essential general and task-specific knowledge.

Empirical results demonstrate that TinyBERT, with only 4 layers, achieves over 96.8% of the performance of its larger counterpart, BERT BASE, while being 7.5 times smaller and 9.4 times faster during inference. Additionally, TinyBERT outperforms other state-of-the-art 4-layer models in BERT distillation, using only 28% of their parameters and 31% of their inference time. A 6-layer variant of TinyBERT matches the performance of BERT BASE, further illustrating its effectiveness. The paper emphasizes the importance of reducing the resource demands of pre-trained language models to facilitate their deployment on resource-constrained devices, such as mobile phones.

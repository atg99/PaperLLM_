Specifically, the absence of the Transformer-layer distillation leads to the most considerable drop in performance, indicating its importance in the learning framework. The study shows that both task-specific and general distillation methods are essential for achieving optimal performance, with task-specific methods having a more pronounced effect across various tasks. TinyBERT demonstrates a substantial performance advantage over existing models while maintaining a smaller size and faster inference speed, confirming the effectiveness of the proposed knowledge distillation approach. Overall, the findings suggest that the two-stage learning framework and its flexible initialization method contribute significantly to the improved efficiency and performance of TinyBERT compared to its peers.

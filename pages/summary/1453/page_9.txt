In this paper, a novel method for Transformer-based distillation is presented, along with a two-stage framework specifically designed for TinyBERT, a 4-layer model that enhances speed while maintaining competitive performance. The results from extensive experiments demonstrate that TinyBERT significantly reduces both the model size and inference time compared to BERT BASE, making it a viable option for deploying BERT-based natural language processing models on edge devices. Future research directions include exploring effective knowledge transfer from larger models like BERT LARGE to TinyBERT and investigating the combination of distillation with quantization and pruning to achieve further compression of pre-trained language models.

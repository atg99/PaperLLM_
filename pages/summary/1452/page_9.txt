The paper discusses potential future directions for improving the Multitask Deep Neural Network (MT-DNN). Key areas of exploration include enhancing understanding of model structure sharing in multitask learning (MTL), developing more effective training methods that utilize the relationships among multiple tasks for both fine-tuning and pre-training, and finding ways to incorporate linguistic structure in a more explicit and manageable manner. Additionally, the authors express an interest in assessing the resilience of MT-DNN against adversarial attacks. Acknowledgment is given to Jade Huang from Microsoft for her assistance in the research. The references cited provide a foundation for the discussion and highlight significant contributions in the fields of natural language processing and multitask learning.

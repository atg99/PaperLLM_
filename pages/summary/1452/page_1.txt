This paper introduces a Multi-Task Deep Neural Network (MT-DNN) designed to enhance natural language understanding (NLU) by leveraging shared representations across multiple tasks. The MT-DNN integrates a pre-trained bidirectional transformer language model, BERT, which significantly improves representation learning. The authors demonstrate that the model achieves state-of-the-art results on ten NLU tasks, including SNLI and SciTail, and enhances the GLUE benchmark by 2.2% to 82.7%.

The motivation behind MT-DNN stems from the principles of multi-task learning (MTL) and language model pre-training. MTL enables the sharing of knowledge across related tasks, which can mitigate the challenge of limited labeled data for specific tasks and reduce overfitting. Meanwhile, language model pre-training utilizes large volumes of unlabeled data to develop universal language representations. The authors argue that combining these approaches maximizes their benefits, as evidenced by the improved performance and adaptability of the MT-DNN model, which requires fewer in-domain labels for effective domain adaptation compared to solely using BERT.

The findings underscore the potential of MT-DNN for advancing NLU tasks and highlight the importance of integrating multiple learning strategies for better model performance. The code and pre-trained models used in this study are made publicly available for further research and application.

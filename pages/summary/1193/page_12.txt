This paper outlines the learning schedules and data augmentation methods utilized in experiments involving Faster R-CNN and STAC (Self-Training with Augmentation). 

In the learning schedules section, three distinct schedules are detailed: Quick, Standard, and VOC. Each schedule specifies the learning rate decay at various training stages, data processing techniques, and batch sizes for training the Faster R-CNN head. For instance, the Quick schedule features a learning rate decay of 0.01 for the first 120,000 iterations, while the Standard schedule includes multiple decay stages, extending up to 540,000 iterations with varying short edge sizes. The VOC schedule is tailored specifically for PASCAL VOC experiments, with defined anchor sizes for the Region Proposal Network (RPN).

The data augmentation section emphasizes the significance of strong data augmentation in STAC. Results presented in Table A1 compare the mean Average Precision (mAP) across different configurations, showing a notable improvement when strong data augmentation is applied. The findings demonstrate that STAC outperforms a self-training approach without robust data augmentation, highlighting its critical role in enhancing model performance on the MS-COCO dataset.

Overall, the paper underscores the importance of carefully designed learning schedules and effective data augmentation techniques in achieving better performance in object detection tasks.

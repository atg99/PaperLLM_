In this paper, the authors explore the critical decisions involved in designing and implementing on-policy deep actor-critic reinforcement learning (RL) algorithms. Through extensive experimentation—over 250,000 trials across five continuous control environments—they assess how various choices impact agent performance and offer practical recommendations. A notable finding is the significant influence of the initial action distribution on performance, suggesting it as a promising area for future research. The paper contributes to existing literature by providing insights relevant to multiple domains within deep learning, including model-based RL and generative adversarial networks.

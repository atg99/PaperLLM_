The paper presents a novel approach to multi-task learning (MTL) and transfer learning, demonstrating performance improvements through the alignment of task embeddings. Using Algorithm 1, the authors apply their method to five tasks from the GLUE benchmark, achieving a 2.35% increase in the average GLUE score compared to the BERT LARGE baseline. The study also explores transfer learning by training an LSTM on sentiment analysis tasks, where the proposed method enhances accuracy by up to 2.5% across four tasks.

Additionally, the authors evaluate a reweighting strategy (Algorithm 2) on the ChestX-ray14 dataset, which shows a 0.4% improvement in AUC scores over unweighted loss methods. Ablation studies confirm that the capacity of the MTL model should not exceed the total capacities of single-task learning (STL) models, with optimal performance observed when the shared module's capacity is set to 100.

Overall, the findings suggest that aligning task embeddings and optimizing model capacity can significantly enhance performance in multi-task and transfer learning scenarios.

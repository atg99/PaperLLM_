The supplementary experimental results section of the paper provides detailed insights into the datasets, models, and training procedures used in the experiments. 

In Appendix B.1, the authors outline the datasets employed, including synthetic settings, sentiment analysis benchmarks, the General Language Understanding Evaluation (GLUE) benchmark, and the ChestX-ray14 dataset. For synthetic experiments, 10,000 samples were generated from a standard Gaussian distribution, with labels derived through specific activation functions for classification and regression tasks.

The sentiment analysis section describes six benchmarks: Movie Review sentiment, Sentence Subjectivity, Customer Reviews Polarity, TREC question type classification, Opinion Polarity, and the Stanford Sentiment Treebank. Each dataset focuses on different aspects of text classification, such as sentiment detection and question categorization.

The GLUE benchmark, a collection of diverse NLP tasks, was utilized to validate the proposed multi-task learning method. The authors selected five tasks to demonstrate their approach, emphasizing that the goal was to gain insights rather than achieve state-of-the-art results.

Finally, the ChestX-ray14 dataset, notable for its size and complexity, was used to test the task reweighting scheme in a multi-label image classification context, highlighting its relevance in medical imaging and computer vision.

The paper presents a proof of Proposition 3, demonstrating that all local minima are global minima for a reweighted loss in a linear case. The key approach involves reducing the multi-task learning (MTL) objective to a low rank matrix approximation and utilizing results by Balcan et al. (2018), which indicate the absence of spurious local minima in this context. 

The proof begins by transforming the optimization problem from the space of matrix B to matrix C, leveraging a one-to-one mapping. This leads to an objective function that simplifies to a form where minimizing it corresponds to solving a low rank matrix approximation problem. By applying a lemma from Balcan et al., it is shown that local minima correspond to the best rank-r approximation of a specific matrix.

The proof of Proposition 3 concludes by establishing that the optimal solution for B can be expressed in terms of the best rank-r approximation, confirming that all local minima align with this solution. However, the authors note that this result is limited to linear models and does not extend to ReLU models, indicating that the optimization landscape in non-linear cases remains poorly understood and is a topic for future research.

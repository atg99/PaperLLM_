The paper evaluates the robustness of Algorithm 2 against label noise using the ChestX-ray14 dataset. An experiment is conducted where 10% of the dataset is subsampled, and label noise is introduced to one of the two selected tasks by randomly flipping 20% of the labels. The performance of training both tasks is compared using Algorithm 2's reweighting scheme, Kendall et al. (2018) reweighting techniques, and an unweighted loss scheme. Results show that Algorithm 2 outperforms the unweighted scheme by 1.0% AUC and Kendall et al. (2018) by 0.4% AUC on average across 10 task pairs. Five example task pairs illustrate these findings, highlighting the effectiveness of the proposed method in handling label noise.

The referenced papers focus on advancements in object detection and deep learning techniques. 

Xie et al. (2017) introduce aggregated residual transformations, which enhance deep neural networks by improving feature learning through residual connections. This method contributes to better performance in computer vision tasks.

Xu et al. (2018) present deep regionlets, a novel approach for object detection that utilizes a combination of region proposals and deep learning, demonstrating improved accuracy in detecting objects within images.

Zhang et al. (2018) propose a single-shot refinement neural network that aims to enhance the precision of object detection in a single pass, addressing the trade-off between speed and accuracy in detection systems.

Zitnick and Dollár (2014) develop edge boxes, a technique for generating object proposals based on edge information in images, which helps in improving the localization of objects in visual data.

Together, these works contribute significantly to the field of computer vision, particularly in enhancing the efficiency and accuracy of object detection methodologies.

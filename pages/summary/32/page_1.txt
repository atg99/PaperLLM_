The paper presents Grid R-CNN, an innovative object detection framework that utilizes a grid-guided localization mechanism to enhance the accuracy of object detection. Unlike traditional regression-based methods, Grid R-CNN explicitly captures spatial information and leverages the position-sensitive characteristics of fully convolutional networks (FCNs). This framework introduces a multi-point supervision approach, which encodes additional clues to mitigate the effects of inaccurate predictions from specific points.

Grid R-CNN divides the bounding box region into grids and employs an FCN to predict the locations of these grid points, thus maintaining explicit spatial information at the pixel level. This method allows for more precise localization of bounding boxes compared to conventional regression techniques, which typically rely on two independent points for localization.

The paper also details a two-stage information fusion strategy to integrate feature maps from neighboring grid points, enhancing the model's ability to utilize the correlation between points effectively. The proposed framework is versatile and can be integrated with various state-of-the-art detection architectures.

Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of Grid R-CNN, achieving significant improvements on the COCO benchmark, with a 4.1% average precision (AP) gain at IoU=0.8 and a 10.0% AP gain at IoU=0.9 when compared to Faster R-CNN with a Res50 backbone and Feature Pyramid Network (FPN) architecture. Overall, Grid R-CNN offers a promising advancement in the field of object detection by improving localization accuracy through a grid-centric approach.

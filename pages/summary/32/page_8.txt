The paper introduces a novel object detection framework called Grid R-CNN, which innovatively replaces the traditional box offset regression method with a grid-guided mechanism for enhanced localization accuracy. The grid branch of the model predicts grid points using the position-sensitive capabilities of Fully Convolutional Networks (FCN) to determine bounding boxes. Additionally, a feature fusion module is implemented to refine the grid points' locations by leveraging spatial information from feature maps. An extended region mapping mechanism is also proposed to enhance the Regions of Interest (RoIs) by allowing them to cover more grid points, leading to significant performance improvements. Experimental results demonstrate that Grid R-CNN consistently outperforms existing methods, particularly on stringent evaluation metrics such as Average Precision (AP) at Intersection over Union (IoU) thresholds of 0.8 and 0.9. The authors suggest that the grid-guided localization approach can be integrated with other techniques, such as scale selection and cascade methods, to achieve further enhancements.

The paper "Revisiting Training Strategies and Generalization Performance in Deep Metric Learning" acknowledges contributions from various individuals and organizations, highlighting the importance of discussions and feedback received during the research process. The authors emphasize the significance of data augmentation in enhancing model performance, particularly through the analysis of different image augmentations and resolutions. They note the similarity in data distribution between the In-Shop dataset and the SOP dataset, which influences their decision to exclude the former from their analysis. The authors express gratitude for feedback regarding grammatical errors and missing references, confirming that all noted issues have been addressed.

The references section includes a variety of studies that contribute to the field of deep metric learning, covering topics such as information dropout, supervised metric learning, variational autoencoders, and triplet loss, among others. This diverse range of literature underscores the foundational concepts and methodologies relevant to the research presented in the paper.

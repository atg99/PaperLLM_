Our study involves a systematic evaluation of various DML objective functions and highlights the significant impact of parameter choices and mini-batch sampling techniques on model performance. The results indicate that DML objectives tend to saturate more than previously reported, suggesting that the field may need to reassess the effectiveness of certain approaches.

To enhance the performance of ranking-based DML models, we propose a straightforward training regularization method. This method has been shown to reliably improve performance across multiple standard benchmark datasets, underscoring the importance of thoughtful training strategies in DML.

In summary, this paper aims to provide a clearer understanding of the factors influencing DML performance, establish a consistent framework for comparison, and propose effective training strategies to advance the field. The findings are intended to guide future research in DML and contribute to more reproducible and reliable results in the community.

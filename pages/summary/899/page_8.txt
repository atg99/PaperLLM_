This paper presents a comprehensive review of various methods and advancements in the field of time-series analysis, particularly focusing on techniques for data augmentation and anomaly detection. It references significant works that have contributed to the development of these methodologies, including the use of deep learning models like convolutional neural networks (CNN) and recurrent neural networks (RNN) for tasks such as classification and forecasting.

Key highlights include:

- Normalizing flows for probabilistic modeling and generative tasks.
- The impact of CNNs on image classification, notably through the ImageNet dataset.
- Frameworks for automated anomaly detection in time-series data, emphasizing scalability and generalizability.
- Data augmentation techniques that enhance the performance of models, particularly in scenarios with limited data.
- The integration of generative models, such as GANs, for improving anomaly detection capabilities.
- Advances in forecasting methods like DeepAR, which apply autoregressive recurrent networks for probabilistic predictions.

The paper also discusses specific applications of these methods, such as in stock market forecasting and human activity recognition, demonstrating the versatility and effectiveness of deep learning techniques in handling complex time-series data. Overall, it serves as a valuable resource for understanding current trends and methodologies in time-series analysis, highlighting the significance of data augmentation and deep learning in improving model performance.

The paper discusses challenges in plant disease classification using deep learning techniques, identifying four main issues: insufficient datasets, lack of robustness in models, variations in symptoms, and complexities in image backgrounds. Solutions proposed include transfer learning, data augmentation, citizen science, and various segmentation techniques. The authors suggest future research directions, particularly the integration of mobile applications with server-side programs to enhance real-time applications of deep learning in this field. The study is supported by multiple funding sources, and the authors declare no conflicts of interest. The research contributes to the ongoing discourse on improving plant disease detection methodologies.

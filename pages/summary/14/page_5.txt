The paper discusses the implementation of deep learning (DL) models in agriculture, particularly for image classification tasks such as identifying diseases on snake gourd leaves. It highlights that while deep networks are often utilized, simpler models like AlexNet and VGG16 can suffice for achieving accurate results in plant disease classification.

DL models can be developed using programming languages such as Python and C/C++, supported by open-source frameworks like PyTorch, TensorFlow, Caffe, and Keras. These frameworks facilitate model design, network deployment, and code efficiency.

The growth of DL is closely tied to advancements in GPU technology, which is crucial for training deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs). CUDA, developed by NVIDIA, is the predominant platform for GPU computing in DL.

The process of image classification is outlined, emphasizing its foundational role in various computer vision applications. The architecture for classifying diseases in snake gourd leaves is explained, detailing the use of convolutional layers for feature extraction, max-pooling layers for retaining important information, and fully connected layers for classification. The classification process is structured into three main steps: data preparation and preprocessing, model building and evaluation, and inference and deployment.

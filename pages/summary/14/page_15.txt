The referenced articles focus on advancements in image recognition technologies and deep learning applications for the identification and classification of plant diseases. Key methodologies discussed include support vector machines, convolutional neural networks, and texture-based approaches for disease detection in various crops, such as alfalfa, apples, and tomatoes. Studies emphasize the importance of early detection and classification to improve agricultural productivity and disease management. Techniques like hyperspectral reflectance and deep learning models are highlighted for their effectiveness in analyzing plant health. The literature also explores comparative studies of different deep learning models and the integration of various features for enhanced classification accuracy. Overall, these works contribute to the growing body of knowledge aimed at leveraging technology for sustainable agriculture.

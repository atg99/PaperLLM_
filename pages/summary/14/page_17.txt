This collection of references highlights advancements in the application of deep learning and image processing techniques for agricultural purposes, particularly in the recognition and classification of plant diseases and pests. Key studies emphasize the use of convolutional neural networks and transfer learning to enhance the accuracy of disease detection in various crops, such as tomatoes, millet, and oilseed rape.

Several works focus on novel data augmentation strategies and methodologies to improve model robustness and classification performance. For instance, techniques like AugMix, CutMix, and RandAugment are explored to optimize data preprocessing. The literature also addresses challenges in automatic plant disease identification, suggesting that effective segmentation and feature extraction are critical for accurate analysis.

Overall, these studies contribute to the growing body of knowledge on leveraging artificial intelligence in agriculture, aiming to enhance crop management and disease control through advanced image analysis methods.

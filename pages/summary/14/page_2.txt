The paper discusses common diseases affecting various plants, highlighting the major types of fungal, bacterial, and viral diseases for crops such as cucumber, rice, maize, and tomato, citing multiple studies for reference. It emphasizes the traditional reliance on visual inspection for disease diagnosis, which is labor-intensive and requires specialized skills. The complexity of classifying plant diseases is exacerbated by the diversity of symptoms across different crops.

The authors outline a machine learning approach to plant disease detection, which involves three key steps: preprocessing to segment infected areas, feature extraction for analysis, and classification using supervised or unsupervised algorithms. Commonly used features include texture, type, and color of leaf images, with classification methods such as support vector machines, K-nearest neighbors, and random forests. However, the paper identifies significant drawbacks in these methods, particularly their low performance, which limits their applicability for real-time classification.

In this paper, the authors discuss various approaches to synonym replacement in the context of temporal expression recognition tasks. Initially, they proposed replacing headwords, as temporal trigger words are typically located there, but this method did not yield significant improvements. Instead, they introduced a language model replacement method that proved to be more effective for the task.

Subsequently, multiple researchers, including Li et al., Mosolova et al., and Wang et al., explored word replacement strategies utilizing thesauri. The studies varied in terms of the specific words substituted, the synonyms selected, and the databases utilized. For instance, some researchers based their synonym selection on geometric distributions, making distant synonyms less likely to be chosen. Additionally, certain methods excluded stop words or specific parts of speech from consideration for replacement.

The paper also highlights an innovative approach by Wei and Zou, which involves the random insertion of synonyms rather than replacement of specific words. An overview of the different synonym replacement methods, including the databases used, replacement strategies, synonym selection criteria, and improvements achieved, is presented in a table. This overview illustrates the diversity of approaches and their respective impacts on model performance across various datasets.

This document compiles a list of references pertaining to advancements in machine learning, particularly focusing on adversarial training, data augmentation, and the exploration of deep learning principles. 

Key highlights include:

1. **Forward-Backward Splitting**: Goldstein et al. provide a comprehensive guide with a FASTA implementation, enhancing optimization techniques in machine learning.

2. **Adversarial Training**: Several studies (Shafahi et al., Zhang et al., Liu et al.) emphasize techniques to accelerate and improve adversarial training methods for neural networks, demonstrating significant efficiency gains.

3. **Data Augmentation**: Techniques such as SMOTE and MixUp (Verma et al., Guo et al.) are discussed for enhancing model robustness through synthetic data generation and interpolation of existing data points.

4. **Information Theory in Deep Learning**: Tishby and Zaslavsky explore the information bottleneck principle, shedding light on the inner workings of deep neural networks.

5. **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**: Various works (e.g., Chen et al., Jawahar et al.) investigate how models like BERT learn linguistic structures and propose novel data augmentation strategies for enhancing NLP tasks.

The references collectively illustrate the ongoing research in optimizing machine learning models, improving their resilience to adversarial attacks, and leveraging data augmentation techniques for better performance in various applications.

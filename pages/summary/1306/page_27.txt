The paper presents various methods of interpolation applied to different models and datasets, highlighting their effects on accuracy and correlation metrics. Key techniques include BERT CLS output interpolation, RoBERTa embedding matrix interpolation, and several mixup strategies such as wordMixup and senMixup. 

For instance, the BERT base uncased model showed an accuracy increase of 8.36% on the FBDialog dataset with CLS output interpolation. RoBERTa's embedding interpolation yielded a 0.6% accuracy improvement on the MNLI dataset. Additionally, the nonlinear mixup approach demonstrated significant accuracy gains across multiple datasets, including a 3.6% increase on the MR dataset.

The Mixup-Transformer method applied after the last transformer layer of BERT-large achieved notable improvements across various tasks, with increases in correlation and accuracy metrics. TMix, which interpolates outputs from selected BERT layers, also showed promising results, particularly on AG News and IMDB datasets.

Finally, the Intra-LADA method utilized instance interpolation with randomly reordered versions of inputs, leading to slight improvements in F1 scores on CoNLL and GermEval datasets. Overall, the paper emphasizes the effectiveness of interpolation techniques in enhancing model performance across a range of natural language processing tasks.

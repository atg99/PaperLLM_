3.1.2 Word Level  
3.1.2.1 Synonym Replacement  
Another method of data augmentation at the word level is synonym replacement, which involves substituting words in the text with their synonyms. This technique not only increases the diversity of the training data but also preserves the original meaning of the sentences. The effectiveness of this method can vary depending on the synonyms chosen and the context in which they are used.

3.1.3 Sentence Level  
3.1.3.1 Back Translation  
Back translation is a popular augmentation strategy where a sentence is translated into another language and then translated back to the original language. This process can generate variations of the original sentence, offering a richer dataset for training models. The main advantage of back translation is its ability to create semantically similar yet syntactically different sentences, thus improving model generalization.

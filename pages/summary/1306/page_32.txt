Notably, methods from Liu et al. and Wan et al. utilize encoder-decoder architectures for transforming generated instances back to data space, allowing for potential insights from interpolated instances.

The paper introduces SUB², a method that interpolates data space instances through sub-phrase substitutions but notes its limitation in diversity. It suggests that further analysis of the GPT-3 model could yield valuable insights due to its interpolation capabilities. The work of Jungiewicz and Pohl is highlighted for their approach of performing synonym substitutions only when it increases model loss, showcasing the refinement of existing data augmentation techniques.

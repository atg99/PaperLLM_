The paper discusses the complexities and challenges of data augmentation in machine learning, particularly in text classification tasks. It highlights that standard data augmentation methods may fail to generate necessary data types, such as sports articles in a news classification context. The potential for introducing undesirable biases through language models like GPT is also noted, emphasizing that these biases can propagate into augmented datasets. 

Furthermore, the paper addresses the time and resource demands of data augmentation, which may not be suitable for time-sensitive applications like crisis informatics. To overcome these limitations and enhance the effectiveness of data augmentation, the authors propose a research agenda focusing on five key areas: examining the benefits of data augmentation with large pre-trained language models, improving existing methods, creating comprehensive evaluation criteria, increasing understanding of text data augmentation, and enhancing the usability of augmentation applications. 

This agenda aims to mitigate biases and improve the overall utility of data augmentation techniques in various machine learning contexts.

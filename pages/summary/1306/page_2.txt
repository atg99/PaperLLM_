This survey paper addresses the evolving landscape of textual data augmentation, particularly in the context of text classification. Prior to 2019, research in this area was limited, but it has gained traction as scientists from various fields explore new possibilities and complex mechanisms. The paper highlights several key goals of data augmentation, including the generation of data for low-data regimes, balancing imbalanced datasets, and safeguarding against adversarial examples.

The survey aims to provide a comprehensive overview of data augmentation methods, particularly in text classification, and is structured around several core aspects: 

1. **Goals and Applications**: It identifies and elaborates on the objectives and uses of data augmentation, filling gaps in previous literature.
   
2. **Comprehensive Survey**: The paper offers an extensive look at data augmentation specific to text classification, while briefly mentioning methods from other NLP areas without exhaustive detail.

3. **Data-Structure-Driven Taxonomy**: The methods are categorized based on a high-level taxonomy, with further subdivisions into fine-grained groups, adapted for the text classification domain.

4. **Method-Driven Overview**: It provides a clear and concise explanation of over 100 different textual data augmentation approaches, organized into 12 groups.

5. **State-of-the-Art Review**: The paper discusses contemporary considerations, such as the limited effectiveness of augmentation methods when applied to large pre-trained models.

6. **Relating Methods**: It compares and contrasts methods in terms of their conception and performance, considering the underlying models and contexts.

7. **Future Research Perspectives**: The survey identifies future research opportunities necessary for advancing state-of-the-art comparisons and addressing current challenges.

The structure of the paper includes an introduction to data augmentation, a discussion of its goals and applications, a detailed breakdown of various augmentation methods, a global perspective analysis, and a conclusion addressing limitations and future directions.

Wang and Yang employ augmentation techniques to enhance the classification of annoying tweets, utilizing k-nearest neighbor methods for selecting suitable embeddings to replace training data words. Their approach results in an improvement of up to 2.4 points in the F1-Score compared to baseline logistic regression models. Other studies, such as those by Marivate and Sefara, Rizos et al., and Huong and Hoang, also implement embedding replacement but differ in their methods of word selection and the criteria for choosing replacement words based on embeddings.

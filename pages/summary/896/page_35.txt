The study by Shorten and Khoshgoftaar evaluates the performance of models trained with Reinforcement Learning augmentation search, showing significant improvement in accuracy on the CIFAR-10 dataset, achieving 70.06% compared to 50.99% for traditional methods when tested on augmented data. However, the authors note that meta-learning is still an emerging field, facing challenges such as the complexity and time requirements of implementation, as well as issues like vanishing gradients during training.

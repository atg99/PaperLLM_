The paper discusses the application of Neural Style Transfer for data augmentation, particularly in contexts where the range of styles to transfer is not clearly defined. By selecting a set of styles from a large database of artistic images, Style Augmentation avoids introducing bias into the dataset. This method has been particularly beneficial in training models for robotic manipulation tasks through Reinforcement Learning, as it mitigates risks associated with real-world training. The study by Tobin et al. demonstrates the effectiveness of this approach, achieving notable accuracy in object localization in real-world scenarios by randomizing object positions and textures during simulation training.

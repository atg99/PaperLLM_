Shorten and Khoshgoftaar propose a novel image mixing approach that involves randomly cropping images and concatenating these segments to create new images. Their findings, alongside those from SamplePairing and mixup augmentation, highlight the effectiveness of leveraging big data in deep learning models. However, a significant drawback of this method is its lack of intuitive understanding from a human perspective, making the performance improvements challenging to explain. One theory suggests that the enlarged dataset enhances the model's ability to capture low-level features, such as lines and edges. Future research could explore the comparative performance of this technique against transfer learning and pretraining methods, which also focus on learning low-level characteristics in convolutional neural networks.

This paper discusses various strategies for Data Augmentation in Deep Learning to address the challenge of overfitting due to limited data. It highlights the potential of test-time augmentation in improving performance in Computer Vision, emphasizing the need for benchmarks and exploring algorithms beyond majority voting. The paper suggests that learning the weights of augmented images' predictions could enhance results and proposes examining the effectiveness of test-time augmentation on object detection.

The authors also delve into advancements in GAN architectures through meta-learning and propose using Reinforcement Learning and evolutionary approaches to optimize GAN training. They stress the importance of developing software tools to streamline Data Augmentation processes, referencing existing libraries like Keras’s ImageDataGenerator and Albumentations.

In conclusion, the survey presents Data Augmentation as a vital technique for enhancing dataset quality in Deep Learning. It categorizes augmentations into data warping and oversampling methods, noting that future research can leverage the layered architecture of deep neural networks for more effective augmentation strategies. While the paper primarily focuses on image data, it acknowledges the applicability of these methods to other data types. However, it cautions that Data Augmentation cannot fully mitigate biases present in small datasets.

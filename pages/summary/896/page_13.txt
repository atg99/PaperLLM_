In the paper by Shorten and Khoshgoftaar, the authors discuss advancements in image augmentation techniques, building on the AlexNet framework by Krizhevsky et al. They highlight research by Summers and Dinneen, who explored non-linear methods for combining images, leading to improved model performance over baseline models. Specifically, their best non-linear augmentation technique decreased error rates on CIFAR-10 from 5.4% to 3.8% and on CIFAR-100 from 23.6% to 19.7%. Additionally, Liang et al. utilized Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) to create mixed images, which not only reduced training time but also enhanced the diversity of GAN-generated samples. The paper includes visual results and comparisons to illustrate the effectiveness of these augmentation strategies.

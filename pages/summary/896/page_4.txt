The paper discusses key aspects of image data augmentation, focusing on design considerations such as test-time augmentation, curriculum learning, and the impact of resolution. It highlights the challenges of image recognition, including viewpoint, lighting, occlusion, and scale, which necessitate data augmentation to enhance model performance. The authors note that larger datasets improve deep learning models, but assembling these datasets can be labor-intensive, especially in medical image analysis where data is often limited. Despite these challenges, deep convolutional networks (CNNs) have proven effective in tasks like skin lesion classification and other medical analyses. The paper provides a taxonomy of image data augmentations to clarify the various techniques employed.

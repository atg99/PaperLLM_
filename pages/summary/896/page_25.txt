Lucic et al. conducted a comparison of newly developed GAN loss functions, revealing that most can achieve similar performance levels with adequate hyperparameter optimization and random restarts. This indicates that enhancing computational power may be more beneficial than modifying the loss functions of the generator and discriminator. Most applications of GANs in data augmentation and their impact on classification performance have been predominantly explored in the context of biomedical image analysis. Figures included illustrate the improved decision boundaries achieved with CycleGAN-generated samples and the integration of the conditional y vector into the GAN framework.

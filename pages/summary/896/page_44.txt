The paper discusses the significance of data augmentation in mitigating biases such as lighting, occlusion, scale, and background when generating images, specifically of golden retrievers, using methods like SamplePairing, AutoAugment, and GANs. It emphasizes that while overfitting is less of a concern with large datasets, data augmentation can further reduce this risk by enhancing limited datasets to emulate the characteristics of larger ones. The paper also includes a list of abbreviations relevant to the discussed methodologies and acknowledges contributions from various authors and funding support from the NSF. The authors declare no competing interests and provide a comprehensive reference list for further reading on deep learning and transfer learning in image recognition.

The paper discusses various techniques for addressing class imbalance in data, notably through intelligent oversampling methods like SMOTE (Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique). SMOTE, created by Chawla et al., generates new instances by interpolating existing data points using k-Nearest Neighbors, primarily focusing on tabular and vector data.

The introduction of the AlexNet CNN architecture marked a significant advancement in image classification, particularly with the ImageNet dataset. The authors employed Data Augmentation to enhance dataset size by 2048 times, utilizing techniques such as random cropping, horizontal flipping, and PCA color augmentation, which collectively reduced the model's error rate by over 1%.

Subsequent innovations included the introduction of GANs in 2014, Neural Style Transfer in 2015, and Neural Architecture Search (NAS) in 2017, with various extensions of GANs emerging over the years. The application of deep learning in medical imaging gained traction following Esteva et al.'s demonstration of skin cancer detection in 2017.

A survey by Yi et al. highlights the role of GANs in medical imaging, covering applications like CT denoising, MRI synthesis, and lung cancer diagnosis. Notably, Frid-Adar et al. (2018) utilized GAN-based Data Augmentation for liver lesion classification, improving performance metrics significantly.

The paper emphasizes that while many augmentation techniques are geared towards enhancing Image Recognition models, their principles can also be applied to other Computer Vision tasks, such as Object Detection and Semantic Segmentation, utilizing algorithms like YOLO and U-Net.

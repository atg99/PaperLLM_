The use of CycleGANs for translating images from various classes into minority classes significantly enhanced the performance of CNN models for emotion recognition, resulting in a 5-10% accuracy increase. To evaluate the impact of GAN-generated instances, t-SNE visualization was employed, which helps map high-dimensional vectors into a low-dimensional space, clarifying decision boundaries. Additionally, Conditional GANs serve as another effective architecture for data augmentation. They address mode collapse by incorporating a conditional vector alongside the random vector in both the generator and discriminator, allowing for targeted generation of specific classes. This approach utilizes one-hot encoded class labels to guide the generation process.

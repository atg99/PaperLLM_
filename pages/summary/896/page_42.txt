The paper discusses the effectiveness of test-time augmentation, highlighting the need for future exploration of geometric transformations and Neural Style Transfer. It raises questions regarding optimal post-augmented dataset sizes, noting that excessive augmentation can lead to overfitting, especially when the original dataset lacks diversity. There is currently no consensus on the best strategies for combining data warping and oversampling techniques, particularly when the training and testing datasets do not share the same distribution.

Future research will focus on various areas, including the establishment of a taxonomy for augmentation techniques, improving GAN sample quality, and investigating the relationship between Data Augmentation and classifier architecture. The authors aim to explore performance benchmarks for geometric and color space augmentations across limited datasets and test GAN techniques on various datasets.

Additionally, they intend to examine the combinatorial potential of GAN samples with other augmentation methods, including style transfers, and investigate super-resolution networks to enhance the quality of GAN outputs. This research could extend beyond images to other data types such as text and bioinformatics, aiming to broaden the applicability of Data Augmentation.

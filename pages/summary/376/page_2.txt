This study leverages RoBERTa, an advanced transformer-based model, to classify these texts into five categories of mental illness: depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder, ADHD, and PTSD, alongside a 'None' class for unrelated topics.

The authors highlight the growing trend of individuals seeking mental health support on social media platforms, particularly Reddit, which has become a focal point for recent research in this field. Prior studies predominantly used Twitter data or focused on binary classification models, often requiring separate classifiers for each mental health issue. This work distinguishes itself by employing a multi-class classification approach, enabling a single model to identify and categorize multiple mental health disorders simultaneously.

The dataset was curated from subreddits specifically related to mental health, ensuring quality by filtering posts based on upvotes and minimum token length. This research demonstrates the potential of social media data to enhance traditional clinical methods of diagnosing mental health conditions, particularly for users who may be unaware of their mental health status. The results suggest that the integration of NLP techniques with social media analytics can provide valuable insights into mental health trends and support mechanisms.

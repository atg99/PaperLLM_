The paper discusses a novel approach to detecting and classifying mental illnesses on social media using the RoBERTa model, particularly in light of increased mental health issues during the COVID-19 pandemic. The authors, Ankit Murarka, Balaji Radhakrishnan, and Sushma Ravichandran, highlight the growing reliance on social media for emotional expression among individuals suffering from mental health disorders due to social isolation. 

The study focuses on five specific mental illnesses: depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder, ADHD, and PTSD. It aims to analyze unstructured user data from social media to identify these conditions, thereby facilitating timely assistance for those in need. The authors present a new high-quality dataset generated from Reddit, where users frequently share their mental health experiences, addressing the lack of annotated data in this field.

Additionally, the paper posits that their work represents the first multi-class classification model leveraging a Transformer-based architecture for emotional and psychological analysis. It also describes the stress-testing methods applied to ensure the model's robustness. The overarching goal of this research is to enhance the public health system by automating the detection and classification of mental health issues, thereby bridging the gap between individuals seeking help and professionals who can provide support.

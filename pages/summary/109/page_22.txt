The paper presents a performance evaluation of an intrusion detection model, comparing it with common machine learning methods such as random forest, K-mean clustering, and decision trees. The effectiveness of the proposed model is demonstrated through accuracy and loss value curves plotted against iteration steps for three datasets: NSL-KDD, UNSW_NB15, and CIC-IDS2017. Figures illustrate the model's accuracy and loss during training and validation phases. A detailed performance comparison is summarized in Table 13, where the proposed model's evaluation indicators are highlighted in bold. The results indicate the superiority of the proposed model over traditional methods, validating its effectiveness in intrusion detection tasks.

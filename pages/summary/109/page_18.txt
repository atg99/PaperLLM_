The analysis reveals that traditional methods like PCA and AE may overlook critical information due to their reliance on variance and training data, respectively. In contrast, the RFP algorithm prioritizes feature importance and correlation, yielding better results.

The effectiveness of the proposed hybrid model, which integrates CNN and GRU for intrusion detection, was evaluated across three datasets: UNSW-NB15, NSL-KDD, and CIC-IDS2017. The results indicate that the CNN-GRU model significantly outperformed single models, achieving high accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 scores. For instance, the NSL-KDD dataset yielded an accuracy of 99.69%, while the CIC-IDS2017 dataset achieved 99.65%. This improvement is attributed to the combined strengths of CNN in spatial feature extraction and GRU in temporal feature extraction, further enhanced by an attention mechanism.

To address issues of dataset imbalance, the study implemented a hybrid sampling technique combining ADASYN and RENN, demonstrating its effectiveness compared to various other sampling methods. Overall, the proposed approach showcases advancements in both feature selection and model performance for intrusion detection tasks.

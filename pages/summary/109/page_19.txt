The paper presents a novel algorithm, referred to as the ADRDB algorithm, designed to address the challenges of unbalanced datasets in classification tasks. The ADRDB algorithm outperforms traditional oversampling methods, such as Random Oversampler, SMOTE, and ADASYN, which tend to generate noisy data that negatively impacts classification performance. The proposed hybrid sampling approach effectively samples both majority and minority classes while utilizing the DBSCAN algorithm to filter out noisy data.

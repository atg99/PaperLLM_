This paper presents a novel intrusion detection model that integrates convolutional neural networks (CNN) and gated recurrent units (GRU) to address limitations in traditional models related to feature extraction and multi-classification. The proposed model utilizes the ADRDB and RFP algorithms to tackle dataset imbalance and feature redundancy, enhancing feature learning through the fusion of CNN and GRU, and incorporates an attention module to optimize feature weighting.

The experimental results demonstrate the model's effectiveness across three datasets: NSL-KDD, UNSW_NB15, and CIC-IDS2017, achieving accuracies of 99.69%, 86.25%, and 99.65%, respectively, with precision rates of 99.65%, 86.92%, and 99.63%. The statistical analysis indicates a significant advantage over single-use models like CNN and GRU.

Despite these achievements, the model has limitations, including a high number of parameters, increased running time, and modest improvements in detection accuracy for small sample sizes. Future research will focus on lightweighting the model to enhance minority sample detection, improve overall classification performance, and reduce computational costs.

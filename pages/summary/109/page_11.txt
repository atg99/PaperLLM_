The paper discusses the Convolutional Block Attention Module (CBAM), which integrates both channel and spatial attention mechanisms to enhance feature learning in convolutional neural networks (CNNs). The CBAM operates sequentially, first generating a 1D channel attention feature map followed by a 2D spatial attention feature map from an initial feature map. This is achieved through element-wise multiplication, allowing the model to refine features effectively. The paper also highlights the significance of two prominent CNN architectures: Residual Networks (ResNet) and Inception Networks. ResNet introduces the concept of residual learning, while Inception focuses on a split-transform-merge strategy for feature extraction. The authors emphasize the use of 2D convolution to capture spatial features, detailing the structure of their CNN module, which processes grayscale maps through various convolutional filters to produce refined feature maps. Overall, the integration of attention mechanisms with advanced CNN architectures is presented as a method to improve classification performance by enhancing the model's ability to learn diverse features.

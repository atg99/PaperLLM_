The paper by J. Zhang, C. Li, S. Kosov et al. presents a comparative evaluation of various image segmentation methods, specifically focusing on EM image segmentation. It highlights the performance of different algorithms, including Otsu, Canny, Watershed, k-means, MRF, Region Growing, SegNet, and LCU-Net+. 

Figures 15 and 16 illustrate the average evaluation indices and the performance metrics, such as Average Recall and Overall Accuracy, for LCU-Net+ compared to Local-Global CRF. Table 6 details the evaluation indices from Repeatability Tests, providing a comprehensive overview of the models' performances, including Dice, Jaccard, Precision, Recall, Accuracy, and VOE.

The results indicate that LCU-Net+ consistently outperforms U-Net and demonstrates high repeatability across different repetitions. The metrics suggest that LCU-Net+ achieves a Dice score of 87.13, Jaccard of 79.74, Precision of 90.14, Recall of 87.12, and Accuracy of 96.91, indicating its effectiveness in EM image segmentation tasks. The findings contribute valuable insights into the performance of advanced segmentation techniques in comparison to traditional methods.

The paper presents a detailed comparison of the LCU-Net and U-Net-BI architectures, focusing on their respective network structures and block configurations. The architecture of LCU-Net is outlined, highlighting its use of 2D convolution operations as defined in Keras. The comparison table illustrates the differences in filter numbers and convolution types across nine blocks for both models. 

For example, both U-Net-BI and LCU-Net employ a series of Con2D operations with varying kernel sizes, but LCU-Net features a consistent use of Con2D(3,1) for several blocks, which contrasts with the standard Con2D(3,3) used in U-Net-BI. This structural adjustment aims to enhance performance in image segmentation tasks.

Additionally, the paper discusses the workflow of Dense Conditional Random Fields (CRF) as a post-processing technique for enhancing segmentation results. The integration of Dense CRF with LCU-Net is denoted as LCU-Net+, indicating its role in improving the accuracy of EM image segmentation. This highlights the importance of post-processing in achieving optimal results in image analysis.

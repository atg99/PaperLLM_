The paper evaluates various segmentation methods, including U-Net, U-Net variants, and LCU-Net, focusing on their performance using multiple evaluation indices. It highlights the importance of considering metrics beyond Recall, noting that high Recall values can be misleading if they result from over-segmentation. The authors emphasize that a comprehensive evaluation should include various indices to accurately assess segmentation results.

The comparison with previous work involving Local-Global CRF segmentation indicates that LCU-Net+ outperforms earlier models, achieving over a 7% improvement in Average Recall and at least a 1% increase in Overall Accuracy. This suggests that LCU-Net+ is more effective for the segmentation task at hand.

Additionally, repeatability tests of LCU-Net+ demonstrate consistent performance across multiple experiments, confirming its stability and reliability in EM image segmentation. The results indicate that LCU-Net+ consistently outperforms the original U-Net, reinforcing its efficacy in this domain.

The paper by J. Zhang, C. Li, S. Kosov et al. published in Pattern Recognition presents a comparative analysis of various segmentation methods applied to different EM categories. Figure 14 illustrates the ground truth (GT) alongside the segmentation outcomes produced by multiple approaches, including U-Net, U-Net-BI, LCU-Net, U-Net+, U-Net-BI+, LCU-Net+, as well as traditional methods such as Otsu, Canny, Watershed, k-means, MRF, Region Growing, and SegNet. The red regions in the figure denote the ground truth and the segmentation results from these methods, highlighting their performance in accurately delineating the desired features. The paper emphasizes the effectiveness of deep learning models in segmentation tasks compared to classical techniques.

The paper discusses the research contributions of several authors affiliated with Northeastern University, China, focusing on advancements in the fields of microscopic image analysis and deep learning. 

Changhao Sun and Zihan Li, both born in 1995, are master's students in the Research Group for Microscopic Image and Medical Image Analysis. Sun's research interests center on microscopic image segmentation, while Li focuses on microorganism image retrieval, both utilizing deep learning techniques.

Hong Li, an associate professor at Northeastern University and a young director of the Artificial Intelligence Branch of the Biomedical Engineering Society, holds degrees from Dalian University of Technology and a Ph.D. from Northeastern University. Her work spans machine learning applications, neuroinformatics, and biomedical imaging.

The paper emphasizes the significance of these research areas in advancing technology and methodologies in medical image analysis.

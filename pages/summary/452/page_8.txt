The paper by J. Zhang, C. Li, S. Kosov et al. presents a detailed analysis of image segmentation performance using the LCU-Net+ model on the EMDS-5 dataset. Figures 10 and 11 illustrate the original EM images alongside their ground truth (GT) counterparts, as well as the training loss and accuracy curves, respectively. Figure 12 visually demonstrates the segmentation results, where red masks indicate the GT foregrounds, green masks represent the LCU-Net+ segmentation outcomes, and yellow masks show the overlapping areas. These masks correspond to false negatives (FN), false positives (FP), and true positives (TP) in the confusion matrix, while regions without any masks denote true negatives (TN). The evaluation metrics listed in Table 3 indicate that higher values for Dice, Jaccard, Precision, Recall, and Accuracy signify better segmentation results, whereas a lower value for the Volume Overlap Error (VOE) reflects superior performance.

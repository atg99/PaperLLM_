The paper presents five learned augmentation sub-policies designed to enhance image processing through specific transformations. Each sub-policy is characterized by a sequence of operations, where each operation is represented as a triplet consisting of the transformation type, the probability of its application, and a magnitude measure. 

Sub-policy 1 includes color adjustments and rotation with varying probabilities and magnitudes. Sub-policy 2 focuses solely on vertical shear transformations of bounding boxes. Sub-policy 3 combines solarization and brightness adjustments to enhance image features. Sub-policy 4 applies shear transformations alongside equalization of bounding boxes to improve image quality. Sub-policy 5 utilizes equalization and horizontal translation, emphasizing a diverse set of augmentations.

The adjustments made to bounding boxes ensure that the modifications remain consistent with the applied augmentations, highlighting the importance of maintaining spatial coherence in image transformations. The paper notes that the probability and magnitude values are discretized, which is further elaborated in the text.

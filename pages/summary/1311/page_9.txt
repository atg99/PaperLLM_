This collection of references highlights significant contributions to the field of computer vision, particularly focusing on data augmentation, adversarial examples, and object detection. 

Cubuk et al. (2018) introduced AutoAugment, a method for learning augmentation policies from data, enhancing model performance through effective data manipulation. The work by DeVries and Taylor (2017) on dataset augmentation in feature space and improved regularization techniques, such as Cutout, also emphasizes the importance of data augmentation strategies. 

Adversarial examples are discussed by Ford et al. (2019), who argue that these examples arise naturally from test error in noisy data. Additionally, the intriguing properties of adversarial examples are explored by Cubuk et al. (2017). 

In the realm of object detection, several methods have been proposed, including the Single Shot MultiBox Detector (SSD) by Liu et al. (2016) and the Focal Loss technique for dense object detection by Lin et al. (2017), which address challenges in detecting objects effectively.

Furthermore, techniques like DropBlock and NAS-FPN aim to improve convolutional network regularization and feature scaling, respectively. Progressive Neural Architecture Search by Liu et al. (2017) also contributes to optimizing model architectures. 

Overall, these works collectively advance the understanding and application of augmentation techniques, adversarial robustness, and object detection methodologies in computer vision.

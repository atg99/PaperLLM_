The paper presents a comparative analysis of various models for Named Entity Recognition (NER), Part-of-Speech (POS) tagging, Semantic Role Labeling (SRL), and Sentiment Analysis using datasets such as CoNLL-2002, UD-LassySmall, and SoNaR-1. 

In NER, BERTje consistently outperforms Multilingual BERT, achieving F1 scores of 97.7 in training and 87.6 in testing for CoNLL-2002. The results indicate that BERTje has effectively learned relevant information for NER beyond the 850K checkpoint, as shown by its performance relative to the multilingual model.

For POS tagging, BERTje again demonstrates superior accuracy, with a maximum score of 99.6, compared to Multilingual BERT's 95.1. The close performance of the 850K checkpoint model to the fully trained BERTje suggests that significant learning occurs before the full training is completed.

In SRL and Spatio-Temporal Relations, BERTje maintains its edge over Multilingual BERT, showcasing F1 scores of 96.4 and 81.9, respectively. 

Finally, in Sentiment Analysis, both BERTje and its 850K checkpoint yield impressive accuracy scores, confirming BERTje's robustness across various NLP tasks. Overall, the findings affirm BERTje's effectiveness and efficiency in learning from data, making it a strong candidate for NLP applications.

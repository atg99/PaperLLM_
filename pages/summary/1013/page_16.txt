The paper discusses novel targets and methods for treating diseases, particularly in the fields of immunology and oncology. It emphasizes the importance of advancing research and enhancing theoretical frameworks within these domains. The authors acknowledge funding support from the National Natural Science Foundation of China and the Key Research and Development Plan of Hunan Province. M.W. and D.L. contributed collaboratively to the conception, literature review, and manuscript preparation, with M.W. providing critical revisions. The authors declare no competing interests.

The references cited in the paper cover significant findings related to Toll-like receptors, innate immunity, and their roles in disease processes, particularly cancer. These studies highlight the interactions of immune cells in the tumor microenvironment and the mechanisms of pathogen recognition. The paper underscores the relevance of pattern recognition receptors in both health and disease, providing insights into their structural biology and signaling pathways that could inform therapeutic approaches.

This comprehensive overview of the current state of research indicates a need for further exploration of innate immune mechanisms and their potential applications in therapeutic development.

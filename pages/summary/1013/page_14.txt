The paper presents a summary of clinical trials focused on Toll-like receptor (TLR) agonists and antagonists in various diseases. It lists multiple drugs categorized as agonists and antagonists, detailing their phase of development, target, application, treatment type, clinical trial registration numbers (NCT), and current status.

Among the agonists, several drugs have reached completion in Phase II trials for applications including HIV-1, melanoma, and squamous cell carcinoma. Notable examples include MGN1703 for HIV-1, EMD 1201081 in combination with cetuximab for head and neck cancer, and Resiquimod for influenza vaccination in seniors. Some trials, like those for DSP-0509 and SD-101, are currently recruiting participants.

The antagonist section includes drugs such as IMO 8400 for plaque psoriasis and Hydroxychloroquine for autoimmune diseases, both of which have completed their respective trials. The paper notes a limited number of clinical studies on PRR agonists and antagonists to date, emphasizing that most of these treatments are still in the clinical development stage.

Overall, the research highlights the potential of TLRs as therapeutic targets in various diseases while acknowledging the ongoing need for further studies in this area.

This collection of references encompasses various studies and advancements in fields such as air pollution assessment, time series prediction, neural networks, image captioning, and energy efficiency. 

Liang et al. (2015) analyze PM2.5 pollution in Beijing, exploring its severity, the influence of weather, and factors like APEC and winter heating. Liang et al. (2018) present GeoMan, a multi-level attention network designed for geo-sensory time series prediction. Liu et al. (2018) focus on joint representation learning of heterogeneous temporal events for predicting clinical endpoints. Lu et al. (2017) introduce an adaptive attention mechanism for image captioning through a visual sentinel.

Plutowski et al. (1996) discuss the selection of exemplars from clean data, while Qin et al. (2017) propose a dual-stage attention-based recurrent neural network for time series forecasting. Rumelhart et al. (1986) contribute foundational work on error back-propagation in neural networks. Vemulapalli et al. (2014) explore human action recognition using 3D skeleton representation in a Lie group.

Yu et al. (2017) present a multi-modal factorized bilinear pooling method with co-attention learning for visual question answering. Yule (1927) investigates periodicities in disturbed series, focusing on sunspot numbers. Finally, Zamora-Martínez et al. (2014) examine online learning models for indoor temperature forecasting to enhance energy efficiency, and Zhang et al. (2017) analyze the relationship between OpenAI's evolution strategy and stochastic gradient descent. 

Overall, these studies contribute significantly to their respective fields, showcasing advancements in modeling, prediction, and understanding complex systems.

The paper discusses the attention distribution across time steps for features extracted using a sliding-time-window method across three experimental datasets. It highlights the significance of biological-inspired strategies in enhancing the performance of neural networks, particularly those that are challenging to train. The acknowledgments section credits the National Key Research and Development of China, the National Natural Science Foundation of China, and the Fundamental Research Funds for the Central Universities of China for their support. 

The references cited provide a foundation for the research, including significant works on neural machine translation, time series prediction, and advancements in recurrent neural networks and evolutionary algorithms. The paper emphasizes the ongoing exploration of innovative methods to improve machine learning models and their applications in various fields.

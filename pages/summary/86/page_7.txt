The paper highlights the advantages of using an evolutionary computation approach, particularly competitive random search, over traditional gradient descent methods. The EA-LSTM model demonstrated superior performance in regression tasks on the Beijing PM2.5 and SML2010 datasets, as well as in classification tasks on the MSR Action3D dataset, outperforming various baseline methods. The findings suggest that the model effectively captures both long-term dependencies and local feature relationships in time series data, making it a significant contribution to the field of time series prediction.

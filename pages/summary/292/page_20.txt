This paper discusses various channel and spatial attention mechanisms in deep learning, particularly focusing on their applications in classification, detection, segmentation, and image captioning tasks. A table summarizes representative methods categorized by their approach to attention prediction, including joint and separate predictions of attention maps.

Key methods highlighted include Residual Attention, SCNet, and CBAM, among others. The paper details how these methods utilize different techniques such as pooling and convolution to enhance the focus on discriminative regions, emphasize important channels, and capture long-range and cross-domain interactions.

CBAM, proposed by Woo et al., is specifically noted for its efficiency in computing channel and spatial attention by stacking two sub-modules. It employs both global average and max pooling to generate a channel attention vector and a spatial attention map, effectively enhancing the model's ability to focus on relevant features. The paper outlines the mathematical formulations for these processes, emphasizing the computational strategies that enable improved performance across various tasks.

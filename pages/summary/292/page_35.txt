This survey reviews the development and application of attention mechanisms in computer vision. Attention mechanisms enhance model performance by allowing selective focus on important features within visual data. Various studies are highlighted, showcasing diverse implementations across tasks such as person re-identification, action recognition, and video captioning.

Key contributions include the introduction of multi-task attentional networks, spatial-temporal attention models, and the application of attention in hierarchical structures for visual captioning. Notable works include the ABD-net for diverse person re-identification, the STA model for video-based identification, and the application of global temporal attention for action understanding.

The paper emphasizes the significance of attention mechanisms in improving computational efficiency and interpretability in visual tasks. It also discusses the evolution of transformer models and their integration into visual media, reflecting a shift towards more sophisticated techniques in addressing complex visual recognition challenges.

Overall, the survey presents a comprehensive overview of how attention mechanisms have influenced computer vision, underscoring their role in advancing the field.

This survey paper provides a comprehensive overview of attention mechanisms within the context of deep neural networks in computer vision. The authors categorize various attention methods based on their operational domains rather than specific application tasks, establishing attention models as a distinct area of study. The survey aims to enhance understanding among researchers regarding different attention mechanisms and their interrelations, potentially serving as a foundation for future research. The authors also suggest several directions for further exploration in this field, encouraging application developers to leverage attention mechanisms to enhance deep learning outcomes.

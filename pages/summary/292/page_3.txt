This paper provides a comprehensive survey of attention mechanisms in computer vision, detailing their development and categorization. The main contributions include a systematic review of visual attention methods, a unified description of attention mechanisms, and a categorization based on data domains, which facilitates connections among various visual attention techniques regardless of specific applications. 

The attention categories discussed are:

1. **Channel Attention**: Focuses on selecting important channels through attention masks.
2. **Spatial Attention**: Targets significant spatial regions or predicts relevant spatial positions.
3. **Temporal Attention**: Identifies key frames over time.
4. **Branch Attention**: Selects important branches within models.
5. **Channel & Spatial Attention**: Combines channel and spatial attention masks for enhanced feature selection.
6. **Spatial & Temporal Attention**: Integrates spatial and temporal attention to highlight informative regions.

The paper is structured with Section 2 reviewing related surveys, Section 3 presenting the main findings, Section 4 offering future research directions, and Section 5 concluding the discussion.

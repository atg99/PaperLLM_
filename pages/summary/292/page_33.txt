This survey paper discusses the use of attention mechanisms in computer vision, highlighting their significance in various tasks such as image classification, object detection, and semantic segmentation. It reviews a range of studies that leverage attention-based models, including transformers and convolutional neural networks, to enhance performance.

Key concepts include the introduction of novel architectures like Query2Label for multi-label classification and BEiT, which employs BERT pre-training for image transformers. The paper also addresses methods such as PSANet for scene parsing and SegFormer for semantic segmentation, showcasing the efficiency of transformer designs.

Additionally, the survey examines applications of attention in action recognition, 3D object detection, and person re-identification, emphasizing the effectiveness of attention mechanisms in improving model interpretability and focus on relevant features. The paper concludes by suggesting future research directions to further explore the integration and optimization of attention mechanisms across various computer vision tasks.

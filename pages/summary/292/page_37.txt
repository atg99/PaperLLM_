The paper presents a comprehensive survey of attention mechanisms in computer vision, highlighting their significance in enhancing model performance across various tasks. It discusses the evolution of attention models, their applications in different domains, and the transformative impact on deep learning frameworks.

Key concepts include the exploration of temporal adaptive modules for video recognition, the introduction of conditionally parameterized convolutions for efficient inference, and the effect of contextual stimuli on visual perception. The paper also examines advancements in convolutional networks, including aggregated residual transformations and the emergence of self-supervised vision transformers that outperform traditional architectures without extensive pretraining.

The authors, who are Ph.D. candidates and researchers from Tsinghua University and Nankai University, contribute diverse expertise in computer graphics, computer vision, and machine learning. Their work emphasizes the role of attention mechanisms in addressing complex visual tasks and advancing the state of the art in computer vision technologies.

(3) Target Risk Estimation from Surrogate Tasks. Some approaches attempt to estimate the target risk using surrogate tasks or additional unlabeled data. However, these methods can be unstable and require labeled target data, which is not always available.

To address these challenges, the authors introduce Deep Embedded Validation (DEV). This method incorporates adapted feature representations into the validation process, allowing for a more accurate estimation of the target risk while maintaining a bounded variance. The use of control variate techniques further reduces variance, enhancing the reliability of model selection in deep UDA scenarios.

The paper emphasizes the need for an unbiased and standardized model selection approach in deep UDA to facilitate comparison and advancement in the field. The proposed DEV method is both theoretically and empirically validated, showcasing its potential to improve model selection and ultimately the performance of deep domain adaptation tasks.

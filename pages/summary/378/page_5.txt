The study reveals that while BERT struggles with antonymy, as indicated by its lower performance in the ANT class, both DistilBERT and RoBERTa outperform it, suggesting that DistilBERT retains lexical information well and RoBERTa benefits from a larger training set.

The SENT class evaluates sentiment discrepancies between the rationale and the question. The qualitative analysis indicates that understanding sentiment is often unnecessary for answering these questions, which explains the models' strong performance in this category.

The paper also discusses the importance of maintaining the natural order of questions in the CoQA dataset, with models generally performing better on questions that follow the text's chronological flow. Additionally, the study examines semantic role labeling (SRL) and finds that all models struggle with questions that reverse the semantic roles, highlighting their limitations in this area.

Overall, the results suggest that although all models can infer some contextual information, there are specific challenges related to antonymy and semantic role order that impact their performance.

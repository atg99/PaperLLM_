The paper by Bazdaric et al. discusses essential aspects of linear regression analysis that authors and editors should understand. It emphasizes the importance of evaluating the P value and the coefficient of determination (R²) to assess the model's significance and predictive capability. In their example, a P value of less than 0.001 indicated a significant model, while an R² value of 0.099 suggested that only 9.9% of the variation in estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) could be explained by haemoglobin levels. The regression equation allows for predictions of eGFR based on haemoglobin values, illustrating the relationship between the two variables. The authors also caution against extrapolation, as using values outside the study's range can lead to unrealistic estimates. Overall, the paper provides a clear framework for interpreting linear regression results in scientific research.

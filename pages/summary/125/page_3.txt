The paper introduces autoencoders, emphasizing their structure and functionality. An autoencoder consists of two main components: an encoder and a decoder, both typically implemented as neural networks. The encoder function, denoted as g, maps input data xi to a latent feature representation hi, while the decoder function, f, reconstructs the input from this representation, producing output ~xi. The training process aims to optimize the functions g and f to minimize the difference between the input and the reconstructed output, as quantified by a loss function. The paper also notes that linear encoders and decoders yield linear autoencoders, referencing existing literature for further insights.

The paper introduces the concept of autoencoders, specifically focusing on Feedforward Autoencoders (FFAs) and their performance in reconstructing images from the MNIST dataset. It demonstrates that increasing the size of the middle layer in the FFA leads to improved reconstruction quality of the original digits. Various configurations are displayed in Figure 7, showing reconstructions from FFAs with different middle layer sizes (8, 16, and 64 neurons). The authors highlight the use of binary cross-entropy as the loss function, although Mean Squared Error (MSE) is also mentioned as an effective alternative, with results illustrated in Figure 8. The findings underscore the relationship between the architecture of the autoencoder and the quality of image reconstruction.

The paper presents the Generalized Scalable Optimal Sparse Decision Trees (GOSDT) algorithm, detailing its components and differentiating it from the DL8.5 algorithm. Key features of GOSDT include the binarization of all features before execution and the use of a priority queue for efficient processing of subproblems.

The algorithm begins by removing items from the queue and checking their bounds for optimization potential. If bounds are equal, the algorithm proceeds to the next item. New subproblems are created by splitting on features, and their bounds are computed and propagated to ancestors, which is a significant difference from DL8.5. This proactive updating helps prune the search space effectively.

The algorithm ensures that only subproblems with overlapping bounds are enqueued, allowing bounds to converge to a single value. It employs a subroutine, FIND OR CREATE NODE, to manage nodes corresponding to support sets and to check eligibility for further splitting based on established bounds.

The implementation of the GOSDT algorithm is available online, and the paper emphasizes the efficient handling of problem dependencies and the aggressive pruning of the search space as its core advantages.

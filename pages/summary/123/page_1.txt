AE2-Nets operates by employing inner autoencoders for view-specific representation learning and an outer autoencoder for multi-view information encoding. This dual approach allows the model to capture both the complementarity and consistency of multiple views by managing the degradation process from the latent representation to each view. The alternating direction method (ADM) is used for efficient optimization of the model.

The proposed framework stands out by offering a unified solution to the challenges in multi-view representation learning, enabling better integration of heterogeneous data sources. Experimental results demonstrate that AE2-Nets outperforms existing state-of-the-art algorithms, highlighting its effectiveness in generating comprehensive representations that leverage the strengths of multiple views without losing critical information.

The paper presents a performance comparison of various methods for classification tasks across different datasets. The results are summarized in Table 2, which details the accuracy percentages for several methods, including the proposed approach, across different partition ratios of gallery and probe sets (G80%/P20%, G70%/P30%, G50%/P50%, G20%/P80%).

For the handwritten dataset, the proposed method achieves the highest accuracy of 96.93% in the G80%/P20% partition, outperforming other methods such as DCCAE and DCCA. Similar trends are observed in the Caltech101, ORL, COIL20, and CUB datasets, where the proposed method consistently yields superior performance compared to existing methods.

The accuracy metric used in this study is defined by a formula that incorporates cluster labels and ground-truth class labels, with the best mapping of labels achieved through the Kuhn-Munkres algorithm. The paper employs k-means for clustering and k-nearest neighbors (kNN) for classification, highlighting their effectiveness due to their simplicity and reliance on Euclidean distance.

Additionally, the model architecture includes fully connected layers with tanh activation functions and employs ℓ2-norm regularization for improved performance. The experiments indicate that the proposed method delivers robust results across diverse datasets, establishing its effectiveness in classification tasks.

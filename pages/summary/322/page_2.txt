The paper investigates the syntactic abilities of attention heads in the BERT model, demonstrating that specific heads specialize in various syntactic aspects. An attention-based probing classifier, which utilizes attention maps, achieves a score of 77 UAS in dependency parsing, indicating that BERT's attention mechanisms capture significant syntactic information. This finding aligns with recent studies suggesting that syntax-aware attention can enhance model performance.

The background section outlines the structure of Transformers, specifically BERT, which consists of multiple layers and attention heads. Each head processes input tokens through transformations into query, key, and value vectors, calculating attention weights that determine the significance of each token in relation to others. BERT is pre-trained on a vast dataset to perform tasks like masked language modeling and next sentence prediction, achieving notable success across various applications, including sentiment analysis and question answering. Special tokens, such as [CLS] and [SEP], are crucial for BERT's attention mechanism, particularly when handling multiple text inputs.

The paper titled "Deep Learning for Audio Signal Processing," authored by Hendrik Purwins, Bo Li, Tuomas Virtanen, Jan Schlüter, Shuo-yiin Chang, and Tara Sainath, was published in the Journal of Selected Topics in Signal Processing in May 2019. It discusses the application of deep learning techniques in the domain of audio signal processing. The authors provide a comprehensive overview of various deep learning architectures and their effectiveness in addressing challenges such as speech recognition, music information retrieval, and sound event detection. The paper emphasizes the advantages of deep learning over traditional methods, highlighting improvements in accuracy and efficiency. It also explores future directions for research in this rapidly evolving field. The findings and discussions presented in this work contribute significantly to the understanding and advancement of audio signal processing technologies.

The paper presents an analysis of the differences between original random CIFAR-10 images and their corresponding views generated by a viewmaker. The central image represents the original input, while the surrounding images illustrate the differences, which have been linearly rescaled to a range of 0 to 1 for clarity. The study utilizes a distortion budget of 0.05, highlighting the modifications made to create the viewmaker views. This comparison underscores the effectiveness of the viewmaker in generating alternative representations of the original images while adhering to specified distortion constraints.

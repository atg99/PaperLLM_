Our results demonstrate that the viewmaker-encoder system outperforms the baseline models utilizing expert views from SimCLR across multiple tasks, indicating the efficacy of our approach in generating diverse and effective perturbations.

We analyze the impact of the perturbation generation mechanism on the quality of learned representations. The stochastic nature of our method allows for a rich variety of views, which is crucial for contrastive learning. By optimizing the encoder to differentiate between these views, we enhance the encoder's ability to capture meaningful features from the input data.

In summary, our proposed method for generating perturbations—characterized as challenging, faithful, and stochastic—significantly improves performance in self-supervised learning tasks. The use of an `1 distortion budget enables effective perturbation while maintaining the integrity of the input, and the alternating optimization between the encoder and viewmaker ensures robust learning. The experimental results affirm that our approach is competitive and suggests avenues for further tuning and application in various domains.

The paper presents an exploration of learned views for image and audio data, specifically using CIFAR-10 and LibriSpeech datasets. For CIFAR-10, the authors employed a Cosine Transform (DCT) followed by a `1-bounded perturbation and its inverse to generate modified images while maintaining their original structure. A hyperparameter search was conducted, yielding an optimal distortion budget of = 1:0, resulting in a linear evaluation accuracy of 74.4%, which is lower than other models examined. Despite this, the visualizations produced are deemed illustrative, as shown in the provided figures. For the LibriSpeech dataset, the authors visualized spectrograms and the variations introduced by the viewmaker, demonstrating the range of perturbations applied. Overall, the findings highlight the effectiveness of using DCT in generating varied representations of both images and audio data.

The paper presents an analysis of the differences between random spectrograms from the LibriSpeech dataset and their corresponding viewmaker views, highlighting the distortions involved. The original spectrogram is displayed centrally, with the differences illustrated around it. The color scale for visualizing these differences ranges from -2.5 (red) to +2.5 (blue), although some values may exceed this range. The spectrograms used are 64x64 log mel representations derived from 960 hours of audio from LibriSpeech, and the distortion budget is set at epsilon equals 0.05. This analysis underscores the impact of transformations on spectrogram representations in audio processing.

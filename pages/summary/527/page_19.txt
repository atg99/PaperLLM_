The paper presents an analysis of learned views for random spectrograms from the LibriSpeech dataset, specifically focusing on 64x64 log mel spectrograms derived from 100 hours of audio. The central figure showcases the original spectrogram image bordered in pink, with variations that are subtle and best appreciated at high magnification. The color scale endpoints are adjusted to align with the minimum and maximum values of the original image, and the distortion budget is set to a value of 0.05. This work highlights the nuances in the learned representations and their implications for audio processing tasks.

The paper discusses key concepts in convolutional neural networks (CNNs), focusing on the roles of stride and padding in the convolutional layer. A stride of 1 allows the filter to move pixel by pixel, while a larger stride results in less overlap between cells. The convolutional layer produces a feature map that is smaller than the input image, leading to a loss of information, particularly at the corners. To mitigate this, padding is introduced by adding rows and columns of zeros around the image, preventing the feature map from shrinking and preserving corner information.

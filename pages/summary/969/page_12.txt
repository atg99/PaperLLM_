The introduction outlines the structure of a survey on model-based reinforcement learning (RL) and its relationship with planning and model-free RL. The first step in model-based RL algorithms is dynamics model learning, which is detailed in Section 4. Following this, Section 5 discusses the integration of planning and learning once a model is obtained. Some approaches, termed implicit model-based RL, combine these steps into an end-to-end optimization process, which is covered in Section 6. Section 7 concludes the main content by discussing the benefits of model-based RL.

The survey emphasizes practical and empirical aspects of model-based RL but also touches on theoretical convergence properties in Section 8. It highlights the relevance of model-based RL to various sub-disciplines, including hierarchical reinforcement learning, transfer learning, and targeted exploration. The survey includes brief overviews of related research directions where applicable. It concludes with sections on Related Work, Discussion, and Summary.

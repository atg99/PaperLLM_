This paper addresses the challenge of sequential decision making in artificial intelligence, specifically through the lens of Markov Decision Processes (MDP). It highlights two primary approaches to tackle MDP optimization: planning and reinforcement learning (RL). The authors introduce model-based reinforcement learning as a hybrid approach that utilizes a model, either known or learned, in conjunction with learning to approximate value or policy functions. Despite the success of model-based RL in various applications, the authors note a lack of comprehensive reviews in the literature, which motivates their survey. They aim to explore the interplay between planning and learning, providing a structured overview of their connections. The survey is organized to first introduce the MDP optimization problem before categorizing the relevant methodologies in subsequent sections.

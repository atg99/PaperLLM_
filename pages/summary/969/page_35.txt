The references listed pertain to various studies and surveys in the field of reinforcement learning and related areas, emphasizing advancements in model-based approaches, memory utilization, and intrinsic motivation for exploration. 

Key contributions include:

1. **Curiosity-driven exploration**: Pathak et al. (2017) explore self-supervised prediction as a means for agents to engage in curiosity-driven exploration.
2. **Goal exploration**: Péré et al. (2018) focus on unsupervised learning of goal spaces that facilitate intrinsically motivated goal exploration.
3. **External memory in learning**: Peshkin et al. (1999) investigate how external memory can enhance policy learning in reinforcement learning contexts.
4. **Model-based reinforcement learning**: Plaat et al. (2020) provide a comprehensive survey on the application of model-based deep reinforcement learning to high-dimensional problems.
5. **Exploration strategies**: Plappert et al. (2017) propose using parameter space noise to enhance exploration in reinforcement learning.
6. **Applications in robotics**: Polydoros and Nalpantidis (2017) survey the applications of model-based reinforcement learning specifically in robotics.
7. **Temporal difference models**: Pong et al. (2018) introduce model-free deep reinforcement learning approaches for model-based control.
8. **Neural episodic control**: Pritzel et al. (2017) discuss the concept of neural episodic control as a method for reinforcement learning.
9. **Imagination-augmented agents**: Racanière et al. (2017) present the idea of imagination-augmented agents to enhance deep reinforcement learning capabilities.
10. **Memory-based reinforcement learning**: Ramani (2019) offers insights into memory-based reinforcement learning approaches.

Overall, these references collectively underscore the ongoing research and innovation in reinforcement learning, highlighting methods that leverage curiosity, memory, and model-based techniques to improve agent performance in complex environments.

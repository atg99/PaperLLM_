The paper provides a comprehensive overview of model-based reinforcement learning (MBRL), structured into several key sections. 

The introduction sets the stage for understanding the significance of MBRL in reinforcement learning frameworks. The background section delves into foundational concepts essential for grasping the complexities of MBRL.

The categories of MBRL are outlined, emphasizing the different approaches and methodologies within the field. The dynamics model learning section is particularly detailed, addressing various considerations such as stochasticity, uncertainty, partial observability, non-stationarity, multi-step prediction, state abstraction, and temporal abstraction. Each of these subsections discusses the challenges and strategies relevant to effectively learning dynamic models in uncertain environments.

The integration of planning and learning is a critical focus of the paper. It explores pivotal questions including the optimal state for initiating planning, budget allocation for planning versus real data collection, and methodologies for planning. It also examines how to integrate planning within the learning and acting loop, culminating in a conceptual comparison of different approaches.

Overall, this paper serves as a foundational resource for researchers and practitioners interested in the intricacies of model-based reinforcement learning, providing insights into both theoretical considerations and practical applications.

This reference list includes a collection of scholarly works focusing on various aspects of reinforcement learning, robotics, and artificial intelligence. Key contributions include "Stochastic variational video prediction" by Babaeizadeh et al., which explores predictive modeling in video sequences. The "Option-Critic Architecture" by Bacon et al. presents a framework for hierarchical reinforcement learning. Bagnell and Schneider's work on autonomous helicopter control highlights the application of reinforcement learning in robotics.

Baranes and Oudeyer have multiple entries discussing intrinsically motivated exploration and active learning in robots, emphasizing goal-directed learning strategies. The work by Barreto et al. introduces successor features, a method for facilitating transfer in reinforcement learning. Barto and colleagues provide foundational insights into real-time dynamic programming and hierarchical reinforcement learning.

Battaglia et al. contribute to the understanding of object interactions and physics in learning through their interaction networks and the concept of relational inductive biases in deep learning. Lastly, Baxter et al. present a novel approach combining temporal difference learning with game-tree search.

These references collectively advance the field of artificial intelligence by addressing theoretical frameworks, learning strategies, and practical applications in robotics and dynamic environments.

This survey paper provides a comprehensive overview of model-based reinforcement learning (MBRL), highlighting its significance in the field of artificial intelligence. MBRL combines the principles of reinforcement learning with models of the environment to improve learning efficiency and performance. The paper discusses key concepts such as the role of environment models, planning algorithms, and the trade-offs between model accuracy and computational efficiency.

It categorizes existing MBRL approaches into three main types: model learning, planning, and policy learning. The authors emphasize the importance of accurate model representation for effective decision-making and outline various techniques used to construct and refine these models. Additionally, the paper examines the integration of model-based methods with deep learning, showcasing advancements that have led to significant improvements in complex tasks.

The survey also addresses challenges in MBRL, including model bias, exploration strategies, and the balance between exploitation and exploration. The authors conclude by discussing future directions for research in MBRL, suggesting that further advancements in model complexity, sample efficiency, and real-world applications are needed to enhance the practicality and effectiveness of these methods.

Overall, the paper serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners interested in understanding the current landscape of model-based reinforcement learning and its potential implications for future developments in the field.

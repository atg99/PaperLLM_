This paper introduces the Scene Text Visual Question Answering (ST-VQA) dataset, which emphasizes the significance of textual information in images for visual question answering tasks. The authors argue that current VQA datasets overlook the semantic richness that scene text provides, which is crucial for interpreting and answering questions related to images. The ST-VQA dataset includes a series of tasks that progressively increase in difficulty, requiring the integration of textual cues alongside visual context to generate accurate responses.

The paper highlights the prevalence of text in images, especially in urban environments, and stresses the necessity of developing models that leverage this information for effective scene interpretation. The authors propose a new evaluation metric that considers reasoning errors and limitations in text recognition, providing a comprehensive framework for assessing performance on these tasks. They also present baseline methods to facilitate further exploration and research in this area.

In summary, the paper aims to bridge the gap between scene text understanding and visual question answering, advocating for a holistic approach that incorporates both visual and textual elements to enhance reasoning capabilities in computer vision systems.

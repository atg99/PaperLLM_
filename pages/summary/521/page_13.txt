f) BTSF [112]: BTSF employs dropout to create diverse views for representation learning. It incorporates iterative bilinear temporal-spectral fusion to model pairwise cross-domain dependencies effectively. This fusion-and-squeeze method enriches representations and enhances discrimination capabilities across different datasets.

g) CoST [113]: CoST utilizes data augmentation to simulate interventions on error variables, leveraging prior knowledge to learn time series representations. It focuses on disentangling seasonal and trend components through contrastive learning, which aids in improving the model's performance on forecasting tasks.

Overall, these temporal-level contrastive methods for time series representation learning utilize various architectures and techniques to enhance the generalization, robustness, and accuracy of learned representations across multiple datasets and applications. Key evaluation metrics include accuracy, mean square error, and area under the precision-recall curve, reflecting the effectiveness of each model.

The study analyzed data from 57,168 men collected between 1973 and 2018 across six continents and 53 countries. A total of 288 sperm count estimates were reviewed, categorizing them into four groups: 41% were Unselected NEA, 12% Unselected SAA, 28% Fertile NEA, and 19% Fertile SAA. Notably, the number of estimates from the SAA group showed a 29% increase. The study utilized PRISMA guidelines for selecting eligible studies for meta-regression analysis, indicating a systematic approach to assessing trends in sperm count over the specified period.

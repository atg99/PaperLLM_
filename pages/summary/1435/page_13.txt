The paper discusses the vocabulary requirements for Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) systems, highlighting that the size of the vocabulary varies depending on the application. A small vocabulary consists of tens of words, while a medium-sized vocabulary contains hundreds. Large vocabularies can include thousands of words, and very large vocabularies may encompass tens of thousands. Words not included in the vocabulary are classified as out of vocabulary and treated as unknown.

The paper also addresses the concept of utterances in speech recognition, which can range from single words to multiple sentences. It categorizes utterances into isolated and connected words. Isolated word systems require users to pause between each spoken word, allowing for the processing of multiple words sequentially, although one word is handled at a time. This distinction is crucial for developing effective ASR systems based on the type of utterance.

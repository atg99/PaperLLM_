The section discusses two key performance metrics for Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) systems: speed and accuracy. 

For speed, the Real-Time Factor (RTF) is highlighted as the primary metric. RTF is calculated using the formula RTF = P/I, where P is the processing time of the system and I is the duration of the input audio. An RTF of 1 indicates that the audio was processed in real-time. It is noted that RTF is influenced by hardware and is applicable beyond speech recognition to any model processing audio or video inputs.

In terms of accuracy, two methods are outlined: Word Error Rate (WER) and Word Recognition Rate (WRR). WER measures the accuracy of an ASR by calculating the errors at the word level, using the formula WER = (S + D + I) / N, where S represents substitutions, D deletions, I insertions, and N the total number of words in the ground truth. WRR, a variation of WER, is calculated as WRR = 1 - WER, which reflects the proportion of correctly recognized words. Here, H denotes the number of correct guesses, calculated as H = N - (S + D). 

These metrics are essential for evaluating the performance of ASR systems, enabling comparisons and improvements in technology.

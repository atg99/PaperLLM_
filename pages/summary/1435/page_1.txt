The paper provides a comprehensive survey of advancements in automatic speech recognition (ASR), particularly emphasizing deep learning techniques. It compares various state-of-the-art methods in feature extraction and classification models, highlighting their influence on ASR performance. The study also addresses the data-dependent nature of deep learning, discussing several online speech datasets that are readily available for research. Additionally, it reviews various online toolkits, resources, and language models that can aid in developing ASR systems. The authors conclude that their work serves as a valuable foundation for academics pursuing research in ASR, encapsulating critical aspects that affect ASR performance.

The section on Temporal Feature Analysis discusses two key methodologies: RASTA-PLP analysis and Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT) in the context of speech signal processing.

RASTA-PLP analysis combines RASTA and Perceptual Linear Prediction (PLP) to improve performance in noisy environments. It addresses the common issue where training and testing data conditions differ, particularly in real-life scenarios that introduce noise and variations. RASTA operates on the principle that the temporal properties of the recording environment differ from those of speech. By applying a band-pass filter to each frequency sub-band, RASTA-PLP effectively smooths short-term noise and aligns the training and testing environments. A comparative study highlighted that for clean speech signals, MFCC outperformed other methods with an accuracy of 94%, while RASTA-PLP achieved only 54%. However, in noisy conditions without pre-processing, RASTA-PLP excelled with 73% accuracy. When wavelet packet transform (WPT) was applied, RASTA-PLP’s accuracy improved to 83%, suggesting its superior performance in noisy datasets compared to other methods.

The section also emphasizes the importance of temporal information in speech signals, which is often overlooked in favor of frequency information. DWT addresses this by considering both aspects, thereby enhancing the analysis of speech signals.

Overall, RASTA-PLP is particularly effective in noisy environments, while DWT provides a comprehensive approach by integrating both temporal and frequency information.

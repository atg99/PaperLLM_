The paper provides a comprehensive survey of various techniques and approaches used in automatic speech recognition (ASR). It identifies three essential modules that constitute an ASR system: the feature extraction module, the classification module, and the language model. The discussion highlights different feature extraction methods, outlining their respective advantages and disadvantages. The analysis reveals that Hidden Markov Models (HMM) are the most effective among the classification models reviewed. Additionally, the impact of the language model on ASR accuracy is emphasized, noting that while current methods for creating language models are sub-optimal, further research in this area could enhance speech recognition capabilities. Overall, the paper underscores the interconnectedness of the ASR components and the importance of ongoing advancements in the field.

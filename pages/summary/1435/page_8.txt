This section highlights the importance of datasets in training and testing Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) systems, providing insights into various commonly used datasets. 

LibriSpeech is a prominent open-source speech-to-text corpus, comprising 1000 hours of audiobooks and their transcriptions. It is divided into three training sets of 100 hours, 360 hours, and 500 hours, with additional development and testing sets of 10.8 hours and 10.1 hours, respectively.

The 2000 HUB5 English evaluation transcripts dataset, utilized in deep speech models, includes 2000 hours of conversational audio. It is composed of forty source files, with half scripted and half unscripted conversations among native English speakers.

The TIMIT acoustic-phonetic continuous speech corpus features recordings of 6300 phonetically rich sentences read by 630 speakers, with a balanced gender representation. The training set contains 3.14 hours of recordings, while the remainder is allocated to testing and development.

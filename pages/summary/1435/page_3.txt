The paper explores various speech recognition techniques, particularly focusing on automatic speech recognition (ASR) systems. It highlights the shortcomings of previous surveys, which often overlooked critical elements such as feature extraction techniques, language models, and the latest deep learning approaches. The study aims to provide a comprehensive evaluation of these aspects, including an assessment of online toolkits and databases essential for ASR training.

The objectives of the study include a detailed understanding of the ASR structure, the impact of different techniques on system performance, an in-depth exploration of feature extraction and classification methods, and an evaluation of advancements in language models. The paper is organized into several sections: it begins with a literature review methodology, followed by a history of ASR, an overview of feature extraction techniques, classification methods, and language models, concluding with a discussion on available toolkits for ASR-related tasks.

The research methodology involved a thorough literature review using platforms such as IEEE, arxiv.org, Microsoft Academic, and Google Scholar, ensuring a comprehensive gathering of relevant papers through specific keyword searches. This approach aimed to capture all pertinent information regarding the modules of an ASR, thereby addressing the gaps identified in earlier studies.

The paper discusses the benefits of submitting research to BMC, highlighting features such as fast and convenient online submission, thorough peer review by field experts, and rapid publication upon acceptance. It emphasizes support for research data, including complex data types, and promotes gold Open Access for increased collaboration and citations. The visibility of research is noted, with over 100 million website views annually, underscoring BMC's commitment to ongoing research progress. Additionally, the paper references studies on cardiac arrest prediction in sepsis patients and prognosis in cecum cancer patients, as well as the nutritional quality of diets in relation to ultra-processed foods in the US.

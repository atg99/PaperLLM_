The paper by Wu et al. discusses the application of data mining in clinical big data, emphasizing the potential of public medical databases such as SEER, NHANES, TCGA, and MIMIC. It highlights the challenges these datasets face, including dimensional heterogeneity and irregularity, which hinder their full utilization. The authors argue that data mining technology is crucial in medical research for evaluating patient risks and aiding clinical decision-making through disease prediction models. 

The review outlines the primary public medical databases and provides a straightforward overview of the steps and methodologies involved in data mining. By detailing various data-mining methods and their applications, the paper aims to enhance clinical researchers' understanding of how to effectively leverage data mining in clinical big data, ultimately benefiting healthcare professionals and patients. The authors note the growing importance of big data in the medical field, particularly as its application expands from civilian to military medicine.

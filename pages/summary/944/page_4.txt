The University of Florida offers various resources for students facing personal challenges or uncertainty regarding their career and academic paths. Key resources include the UF Counseling & Wellness Center, which provides counseling and mental health services, and the Career Resource Center, which assists with career and job search services. The University Police Department is also available for safety concerns.

Regarding software use, all members of the University community are obligated to adhere to laws and agreements governing software usage. Violations can result in monetary damages or criminal penalties, along with disciplinary actions from the University. The community is committed to maintaining high standards of honesty and integrity.

Students are encouraged to evaluate their instructors online based on ten criteria during the last few weeks of the semester. Specific evaluation periods will be communicated to students, and summary results will be accessible online afterward.

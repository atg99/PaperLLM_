The grading breakdown for the course includes a term paper (15%), final presentation (15%), Exam 1 (20%), and Exam 2 (25%). The grading scale is as follows: A ranges from 100 to 95, A- from 94 to 90, B+ from 89 to 87, B from 86 to 83, B- from 82 to 80, C+ from 79 to 77, C from 76 to 73, C- from 72 to 70, D+ from 69 to 67, D from 66 to 63, D- from 62 to 60, and F from 59 to 0. 

Undergraduate students must maintain an overall GPA and upper-division GPA of 2.0 or better to graduate, while graduate students must have a GPA of 3.0 or better. A C- average is equivalent to a GPA of 1.67, and a B- average equates to 2.67, both of which do not meet graduation requirements.

The Honor Pledge at the University of Florida emphasizes the commitment to academic integrity. Students are expected to uphold this pledge and report any misconduct. Violations can result in disciplinary action as per the UF Student Honor Code.

Students requiring accommodations due to disabilities must register with the Dean of Students Office and provide documentation to the course instructor. 

For additional support, UF Counseling Services are available to students.

The studies discussed emphasize the importance of utilizing transfer learning and efficient model architectures, such as MobileNet, EfficientNet, and DenseNet, to enhance the performance of plant disease detection systems on resource-constrained devices. Various datasets, including those from PlantVillage, were employed to train and test the models, demonstrating high accuracy rates ranging from 98.63% to 98.85%. The authors advocate for the use of smartphones due to their low resource utilization and comparable accuracy to desktop systems. Additionally, techniques like data augmentation and the use of generative adversarial networks (GANs) were implemented to mitigate overfitting and enhance the diversity of training data. Overall, the research highlights effective strategies for developing robust, efficient, and accessible plant disease detection systems leveraging machine learning and image processing techniques.

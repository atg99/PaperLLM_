The study presented in CMC, 2022, vol.71, no.1, focused on transfer learning using an augmented dataset. The authors fine-tuned various deep learning models by initially freezing the base models and only optimizing the classification layer, utilizing the backbones as pre-trained feature extractors. Subsequently, they performed full model fine-tuning to assess performance improvements.

Table 2 details the parameter settings for training the models, including input size, optimization methods, batch sizes, learning rates for both frozen and fine-tuned states, and the number of parameters for each model. The models evaluated include AlexNet, ResNet50, Inception V3, MobileNet-V1, MobileNet-V2, and several variants of EfficientNet (B0 to B5), each employing specific settings tailored to their architectures. This structured approach aimed to identify the impact of fine-tuning on model performance in a transfer learning context.

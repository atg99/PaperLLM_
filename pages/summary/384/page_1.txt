This paper presents an expert system utilizing a deep learning model to facilitate early detection of peach plant diseases through a smartphone application tailored for field use. The proposed method employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) to leverage its robust feature extraction capabilities for identifying diseases in fruit and leaf images. 

A novel dataset was compiled from peach orchards and subjected to extensive augmentation using both label-preserving and non-label-preserving transformations. The study compares the effects of fine-tuning the feature extraction pipelines of various state-of-the-art pretrained networks against using fixed parameters. The results indicate that the EfficientNet model outperformed others, achieving an impressive 96.6% average accuracy, along with 90% sensitivity and precision, and 98% specificity on the test images.

The findings emphasize the potential of advanced CNN architectures combined with data augmentation techniques to enhance disease detection in agricultural settings, thereby contributing to improved fruit quality and yield management.

The references listed provide a comprehensive overview of recent advancements in machine learning and deep learning techniques applied to image recognition, particularly in agriculture. Key contributions include the development and optimization of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for tasks such as fruit recognition, plant disease identification, and image classification.

Notably, EfficientNet and MixNet have been highlighted for their efficiency in automated fruit recognition. The work of Tan and Le on EfficientNet emphasizes model scaling for improved performance in CNNs. Liu et al. demonstrated the effectiveness of EfficientNet in classifying maize diseases through leaf images.

The foundational work by Simonyan and Zisserman on deep CNNs is referenced, alongside advancements in residual networks by He et al. and the DenseNet architecture by Iandola et al. These frameworks have significantly influenced the field of image recognition.

Data augmentation techniques, particularly those utilizing generative adversarial networks (GANs), have been explored by Liu et al. for grape leaf disease identification. Transfer learning has also been effectively employed, as seen in multiple studies for identifying rice and other plant diseases.

The references reflect a concerted effort within the agricultural domain to leverage machine learning for enhanced crop management and disease detection, underscoring the importance of efficient model architectures and innovative techniques in advancing agricultural technology.

In this paper, the authors explored the impact of fine-tuning on an augmented dataset of peach disease images collected from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province. The dataset was enhanced through both label-preserving transformations, such as rotations and flips, and non-label-preserving transformations, including cropping techniques. The findings indicated that fine-tuning the feature extraction backbone led to significantly improved performance compared to using fixed feature extractors. Additionally, the augmentation strategies were designed to mimic the conditions of capturing images with a smartphone in the field, resulting in the development of robust detection models capable of identifying diseases in challenging scenarios.

Looking ahead, the authors plan to label the augmented dataset for simultaneous object detection and classification using end-to-end deep learning techniques. They also intend to implement auto-augmentation methods to identify the most effective data augmentation transformations for their dataset.

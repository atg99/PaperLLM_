The paper presents the performance evaluation of the EfficientNet-B2 model for classifying peach fruit diseases based on a confusion matrix and associated metrics. The confusion matrix reveals strong predictive capabilities, particularly for the "Brown rot" and "Gummosis" classes, with accuracies of 0.88 and 0.92 respectively. The model also shows a high accuracy of 0.89 for the "Healthy" class. Other diseases like "Nutrient deficiency" and "Shot hole" also demonstrate reasonable performance, with accuracies of 0.95 and 0.85 respectively. 

Figure 4 details per-class accuracy, sensitivity, specificity, and precision, supporting the model's effectiveness in distinguishing between different disease states. Furthermore, the use of Gradient-based Class Activation Maps (Grad-CAM) in Figure 5 highlights EfficientNet-B2's ability to identify diseased regions in peach fruits, suggesting its potential application in precision farming by enabling targeted pesticide application and the removal of infected fruits to prevent disease spread. Overall, the findings underscore the model's robustness and applicability in agricultural disease management.

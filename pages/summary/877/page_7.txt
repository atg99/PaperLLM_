This paper presents a comparison of a new method, AdaAttN, against several state-of-the-art techniques for video style transfer, including SANet, Linear, and MCCNet. The qualitative analysis highlights that while existing methods like AdaIN and Avatar-Net result in significant content detail loss and blurriness, AdaAttN effectively balances style transfer with content preservation through a novel attentive normalization approach. 

In the user study, 2000 votes were collected from 100 users on content preservation, stylization degree, and overall preference, indicating that AdaAttN's results were favored over competitors. The efficiency analysis reveals that AdaAttN achieves a runtime of 20 frames per second using a single Nvidia P40 GPU, comparable to other leading methods. 

The training process involves augmenting images at a resolution of 512x512, with random cropping to 256x256, and the model is capable of handling images of any resolution during inference. Overall, the findings suggest that AdaAttN offers a significant improvement in both aesthetic quality and performance for video style transfer tasks.

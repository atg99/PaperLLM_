This list of references includes a variety of studies and papers focused on neural style transfer, particularly in the context of images and videos. The references span several key developments in the field, highlighting techniques such as coherent video style transfer, fast patch-based style transfer, and adaptations for real-time applications. Notable contributions include the exploration of explicit representations for style transfer, advancements in multi-channel and multi-adaptation networks, and the use of perceptual losses for improving transfer quality. The references also cover foundational works on convolutional neural networks for style transfer, as well as more recent approaches that emphasize stability and depth-awareness in both image and video contexts. Overall, these works collectively advance the understanding and capabilities of artistic style transfer in computational vision.

The paper discusses various advancements in the field of style transfer, particularly focusing on arbitrary style transfer techniques. It highlights several notable contributions from various researchers in the domain, including the use of style-attentional networks, multi-scale feature decoration, and improved texture networks. Key methodologies such as video-to-video synthesis and consistent video style transfer are also examined, showcasing their significance in maintaining temporal coherence in stylized outputs. The paper provides a comprehensive overview of different approaches, including neural networks designed for artistic style transfer across images and videos. By reviewing these methods, the authors aim to emphasize the evolution of style transfer technologies and their applications in creative domains, ultimately suggesting directions for future research in achieving high-quality and diverse stylization effects.

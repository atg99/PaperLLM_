The paper "AdaAttN: Revisit Attention Mechanism in Arbitrary Neural Style Transfer" by Liu et al. addresses the challenges in arbitrary neural style transfer, particularly the limitations of existing methods that either inadequately fuse style and content features or normalize content features without considering local statistics. The authors introduce a novel module called Adaptive Attention Normalization (AdaAttN), which performs normalization on a per-point basis by learning spatial attention scores from both shallow and deep features of content and style images. This approach allows for the calculation of per-point weighted statistics, ensuring that content features reflect local statistics of the style features.

The paper also proposes a local feature loss derived from AdaAttN to enhance visual quality and extends the method for video style transfer with minor adjustments. Experimental results demonstrate that AdaAttN achieves state-of-the-art performance in arbitrary image and video style transfer, showcasing its effectiveness in preserving local details and reducing distortions. The authors provide access to their code and models, encouraging further exploration in the field.

The paper evaluates the performance of different machine learning techniques for modeling heat exchangers, specifically focusing on the Support Vector Machine (SVM) method. Figure 3 illustrates a comparison of classifiers based on sensitivity, specificity, and precision, clearly indicating that SVM outperforms other techniques across all metrics. Figure 4 further demonstrates that SVM achieves over 90% accuracy in predictions, while other methods fall short.

The conclusion emphasizes the effectiveness of the SVM technique for predicting the heat transfer rate in shell and tube heat exchangers, supported by experimental data from literature. The analysis includes a comparison with Artificial Neural Networks (ANN) and other mathematical techniques, with SVM consistently showing superior performance. Overall, the findings suggest that SVM is the most suitable method for heat exchanger modeling and prediction.

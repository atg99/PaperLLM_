The paper discusses various engineering topics, including the prediction of heat transfer rates in shell-and-tube heat exchangers using artificial neural networks, the design of pressure vessels employing PV-ELITE software alongside manual calculations validated by finite element method, and the comparison of tool paths in pocket milling. Additionally, it covers an experimental investigation into the impact of compression ratios on direct injection diesel engines fueled with spirulina algae biodiesel. The research highlights advancements in thermal science, mechanical engineering, and alternative fuels, providing insights into improved engineering practices and methodologies. The paper was submitted on April 9, 2019, revised on May 11, 2019, and accepted on June 1, 2019, and is published under open access terms by the Society of Thermal Engineers of Serbia.

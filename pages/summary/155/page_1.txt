The paper presents GP-VAE, a novel deep probabilistic model designed for the imputation of multivariate time series with missing values, particularly in fields like healthcare and finance. The authors argue that traditional imputation methods often struggle with high-dimensional data and do not effectively capture the temporal correlations necessary for accurate predictions.

The GP-VAE model operates on the premise that high-dimensional time series data can be represented in a lower-dimensional latent space that evolves smoothly over time, modeled using a Gaussian process. This approach facilitates nonlinear dimensionality reduction while addressing the challenges posed by missing data through a variational autoencoder (VAE) framework with a structured variational approximation.

The authors demonstrate that GP-VAE surpasses both classical and contemporary deep learning imputation techniques across high-dimensional datasets from computer vision and healthcare domains. It not only enhances the smoothness of the imputations but also provides interpretable uncertainty estimates, thereby addressing key limitations of existing methods. The paper underscores the importance of incorporating both temporal and cross-channel correlations in the imputation process to improve data utility and interpretability.

The paper investigates the performance of two decision tree algorithms, IAI and ITI, focusing on explanation redundancy in their outputs. It analyzes a benchmark dataset comprising various datasets with varying numbers of features and instances. Overall, the study highlights the efficiency and redundancy in decision trees generated by IAI and ITI, providing insights into their respective performances across different datasets.

The paper discusses the integration of various reliability and safety analysis methods with Petri nets (PNs), highlighting their application in different contexts. Fault trees (FT) are translated into PNs, enabling the generation of minimal cut and path sets to analyze system failures. Fuzzy set theory is introduced to represent failure and repair rates, allowing for the incorporation of expert opinions and uncertainties. Wu et al. proposed a method that combines FT and fuzzy reasoning PN (FRPN) for reliability analysis in solar array systems, extending this approach for spacecraft reliability optimization. Additionally, reliability block diagrams (RBDs) have been converted into PNs, with dynamic RBDs (DRBDs) utilized for time-dependent system behavior assessments. The paper also mentions methodologies for translating event trees and UML Statecharts into PNs for safety analysis. Other studies have explored the combination of FTA and PNs for safety integrity level allocation, as well as the use of Fault Tree Driven Markov Processes (FTDMP) alongside Generalized Stochastic Petri Nets (GSPN) for availability analysis in safety-critical systems. Overall, the paper emphasizes the versatility of PNs in enhancing reliability and safety assessments across various engineering applications.

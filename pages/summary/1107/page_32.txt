The referenced papers collectively explore various methodologies for risk assessment, reliability analysis, and system dependability using advanced modeling techniques such as Bayesian networks and Petri nets. 

Montewka et al. present a framework for assessing risks in maritime transportation systems, specifically focusing on open sea collisions involving RoPax vessels. Muppala and Lin introduce stochastic Petri nets for dependability analysis in large-scale distributed systems. Mura and Bondavalli utilize Markov regenerative stochastic Petri nets to evaluate the dependability of phased mission systems.

Murphy's work on dynamic Bayesian networks emphasizes their representation, inference, and learning capabilities. Musharraf et al. contribute to human reliability assessment, particularly in offshore emergency conditions, utilizing virtual experimental techniques for data collection. Nannapaneni and Mahadevan address reliability analysis under epistemic uncertainty, offering insights into system safety.

Neil's contributions span the application of Bayesian belief networks for predicting military vehicle reliability and system dependability assessments. The exploration of hybrid Bayesian networks and their application in modeling dependable systems is also notable.

The studies further include various applications of Petri nets for modeling systems, including safety procedures in nuclear power plants, dynamic fault trees, and monitoring discrete environments. Pinna et al. discuss the integration of reliability and safety evaluations using colored Petri nets.

Overall, these studies enhance the understanding of reliability and safety in complex systems, providing a foundation for future research and applications in various engineering domains.

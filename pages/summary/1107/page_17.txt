The paper discusses the use of various logic gates in dynamic fault trees (DFTs) and their modeling through Generalized Stochastic Petri Nets (GSPN). It outlines the functionality of the SPARE gate, which activates backup components in a specific sequence when a primary component fails. The SPARE gate can represent cold, warm, and hot spares. The Sequence-Enforcing (SEQ) gate is introduced to ensure that events occur in a specified order. The paper includes figures and pseudocode demonstrating the translation of DFTs into GSPN models.

Additionally, the paper references a range of studies that have employed Petri nets in conjunction with fault tree analysis (FTA) for various applications, including reliability block diagrams and event trees. It highlights the role of Petri nets in uncertainty quantification during reliability analysis, citing a review of such methodologies. A method for analyzing the reliability of repairable systems using Petri nets and fuzzy set theory is also mentioned, emphasizing the versatility of Petri nets in modeling complex systems.

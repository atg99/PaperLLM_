Bayesian networks (BNs) have emerged as a powerful alternative due to their flexible structure, which allows for the representation of complex dependencies among system components. BNs can effectively incorporate uncertainty and perform diagnostic analysis, making them suitable for various risk assessment applications. Their ability to model probabilistic relationships enables analysts to better understand potential failure modes and their impacts.

Petri nets (PNs) serve as another valuable tool for modeling and analyzing the dynamic behavior of systems. Their graphical representation and mathematical foundations facilitate the examination of concurrent processes and workflows, providing insights into system reliability and safety. PNs can effectively represent the state changes in systems, allowing for a comprehensive assessment of risks associated with system behavior.

This paper reviews the applications of both Bayesian networks and Petri nets in safety, reliability, and risk assessments, emphasizing their advantages over traditional methods. The review outlines the strengths and limitations of each approach in practical scenarios, highlighting their potential to enhance the understanding and management of risks in safety-critical systems.

In conclusion, as the complexity of systems continues to grow, the integration of Bayesian networks and Petri nets into risk assessment practices is essential for improving the dependability and safety of modern systems. Their ability to accommodate uncertainty, dynamic interactions, and complex dependencies positions them as superior alternatives to classical risk assessment methodologies.

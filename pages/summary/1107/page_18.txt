The paper presents Generalized Stochastic Petri Nets (GSPN) models for various logic gates, specifically focusing on the AND gate, OR gate, PAND gate, FDEP gate, SPARE gate, and SEQ gate. Each GSPN model captures the stochastic behavior and operational characteristics of these gates, allowing for the analysis of their performance in probabilistic systems. The models are structured to reflect the unique functionalities of each gate, illustrating how they process inputs to generate outputs. The GSPN framework provides a comprehensive approach to understanding the dynamics of logic gates within the context of stochastic processes.

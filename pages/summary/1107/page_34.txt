The collection of papers outlined showcases a range of methodologies and applications related to reliability analysis, risk assessment, and system safety, primarily utilizing Bayesian networks, Petri nets, and various modeling techniques. 

Trucco et al. (2008) focus on organizational factors in risk analysis within maritime transportation, employing Bayesian Belief networks to enhance understanding of risks. Tuncel and Alpan (2010) provide insights into risk management in supply chain networks through case studies. Ur et al. (2014) analyze the reliability of nuclear instrumentation and control architecture using Bayesian networks, emphasizing their utility in complex systems.

The US Department of Defense (1980) outlines procedures for conducting Failure Mode, Effects, and Criticality Analysis, providing foundational knowledge for risk assessment. Vasilyev et al. (2017) and Vernez et al. (2003, 2004) explore reliability modeling using Petri nets, addressing safety and accident modeling in industrial processes.

Several papers, including those by Vesely et al. (2002) and Wang et al. (2005, 2010), delve into fault tree analysis and Bayesian networks, presenting innovative approaches to reliability evaluation. Wang et al. (2011a, 2011b) integrate human and organizational factors into risk analysis models, while Warfield (1973) contributes to graphic hierarchy arrangements relevant to system analysis.

Weber and colleagues (2003, 2004, 2006, 2012) present dynamic Bayesian networks for reliability modeling, emphasizing applications in dependability and maintenance. Meanwhile, Wu et al. (2011, 2012) investigate reliability analysis methods for solar arrays, incorporating fuzzy reasoning and Petri nets.

Notably, Xiaowei (2010) and Wu (2004, 2006) address common cause failures and fuzzy reliability estimation, while Wu et al. (2015) integrate interpretive structural modeling with Bayesian networks for risk assessment in offshore projects. The body of work collectively advances the understanding of reliability and risk management across various sectors, highlighting the significance of advanced modeling techniques in enhancing safety and operational efficiency.

This collection of references highlights various methodologies and applications in reliability analysis, fault diagnosis, and risk assessment, primarily using advanced computational techniques such as Bayesian networks and Petri nets. 

Xing et al. (2011) discuss combinatorial reliability analysis in dynamic systems with sequence-dependent failures. Yan et al. (2017) present a reliability modeling approach for automated guided vehicles by integrating fault trees with Petri nets. Yang et al. (2004) introduce a case-based reasoning system employing Petri nets for diagnosing induction motor faults. 

Fuzzy logic is explored by Yang et al. (2008) in prioritizing failures during FMEA, while Yazar (2002) offers a qualitative risk analysis tool. Yazdi and Kabir (2017) demonstrate a fuzzy Bayesian network for risk analysis in process industries. Yevkin (2011) proposes a modular approach for dynamic fault tree analysis.

Bayesian networks are widely applied in reliability assessments, as illustrated by Yin et al. (2008), Yongli et al. (2008), and Yu et al. (1999), covering mechanical systems and power systems. Yuan et al. (2015) apply these networks to analyze dust explosion risks, and Zafra-Cabeza et al. (2004) propose an algorithm for project scheduling and risk assessment.

Dynamic Bayesian networks are discussed by Zahra et al. (2013) for reliability estimation, while Zareiee et al. (2014) focus on safety analysis using simplified Petri nets. Zeng et al. (2012) perform dependability analysis in smart grid control networks, and Zhang et al. (2013, 2014) integrate safety assessments and Bayesian networks for navigational risks and construction project safety, respectively.

The application of dynamic fault tree analysis using Petri nets is addressed by Zhang et al. (2009), while Zhang et al. (2016) propose a fuzzy Petri net approach for fault diagnosis in power systems. Zhou et al. (2012) analyze hazardous material transport accidents using Bayesian networks, and Zhou et al. (2017) assess security risks in the chemical industry with a weighted fuzzy Petri net approach.

Overall, these studies collectively contribute to the understanding and enhancement of reliability and safety assessments across various industries through innovative methodologies.

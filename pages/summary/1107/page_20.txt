Overall, Petri nets have proven to be versatile tools for risk assessment across various domains, from supply chain management to computer security and industrial safety. Their ability to model dynamic and stochastic behaviors, as well as to incorporate multiple factors and dependencies, enhances the understanding and evaluation of risk in complex systems. Various studies have demonstrated the effectiveness of Petri nets in identifying vulnerabilities, simulating failure propagation, and supporting decision-making processes in risk management.

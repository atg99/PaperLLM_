3. **Self-Adaptive Training** (Lang Huang et al.) - A framework bridging supervised and self-supervised learning paradigms.

6. **Temporal Ensembling** (Samuli Laine and Timo Aila) - A semi-supervised learning approach that combines predictions over time to improve model robustness.

7. **FixMatch** (Kihyuk Sohn et al.) - Simplified semi-supervised learning by integrating consistency and confidence.

8. **Mean Teacher Approach** (Antti Tarvainen and Harri Valpola) - Demonstrated that weight-averaged consistency targets yield improved semi-supervised learning results.

9. **Unsupervised Feature Learning** (Zhirong Wu et al.) - Developed a non-parametric instance discrimination method for unsupervised feature learning.

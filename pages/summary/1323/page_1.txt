The paper presents a novel approach for detecting COVID-19 infections using chest X-ray (CXR) images by employing a Hybrid Social Group Optimization algorithm in conjunction with a Support Vector Classifier. The backdrop of this research is the global pandemic caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus, which has led to a significant number of infections and fatalities worldwide. With the demand for testing exceeding the availability of kits, alternative methods such as analyzing CXR images have gained attention.

In this study, the authors outline a systematic pipeline that extracts features from CXR images, selects the most relevant features through the Hybrid Social Group Optimization algorithm, and classifies the images using various classifiers. The proposed method demonstrated an impressive classification accuracy of 99.65% with the Support Vector Classifier, outperforming other leading deep learning techniques in both binary and multi-class classification scenarios.

The introduction contextualizes the emergence of COVID-19, tracing its origins to a pneumonia outbreak in Wuhan, China, linked to the SARS-CoV-2 virus, which shares genetic similarities with previous coronaviruses. The paper emphasizes the critical need for effective detection methods, particularly in resource-limited settings, where such intelligent systems could assist radiologists in diagnosing COVID-19 from CXR images.

The paper discusses an experimental setup for analyzing chest X-ray (CXR) images related to COVID-19, including a dataset comprising 371 COVID-19 positive images, 1341 normal images, and 1345 viral pneumonia images. To enhance computational efficiency, all images were resized from 1024x1024 to 100x100 pixels. The dataset was found to be imbalanced, prompting the creation of three subsets for evaluation with varying ratios of COVID-19 to non-COVID-19 images: 30%-70%, 50%-50%, and the full dataset.

Experiments were conducted using Google Colaboratory on Google Cloud Servers equipped with an Intel Xeon CPU and a K80 GPU, utilizing Python and libraries such as NumPy, Pandas, and Scikit-learn for algorithm development. The Pillow library was employed for feature extraction from the lung CXRs. The final model was trained on the original dataset, with the described splits serving solely for evaluation purposes.

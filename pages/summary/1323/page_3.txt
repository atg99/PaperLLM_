The current paper introduces a novel approach to enhance the classification of COVID-19 through chest X-ray (CXR) images by employing a Hybrid Social Group Optimization (HSGO) algorithm for feature selection. This innovative method aims to improve both the accuracy and efficiency of the classification process. The model is rigorously tested on a publicly available dataset of COVID-19 CXR images from Kaggle, demonstrating superior performance with an accuracy of 99.65% using a Support Vector Classifier (SVC), outperforming deep learning models which reached a maximum accuracy of 99.27%. 

The paper is structured to first review related works highlighting the challenges in COVID-19 detection and the advantages of CXR over RT-PCR tests. It then details the proposed methodology, followed by an exploration of the experimental results, concluding with future directions for research. The findings emphasize the potential of the HSGO algorithm to streamline COVID-19 diagnostics, offering a promising alternative to traditional deep learning approaches with reduced training time and enhanced reliability.

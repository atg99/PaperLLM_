The paper discusses the feature extraction process for lung chest X-ray (CXR) images, emphasizing the use of Pillow 7.1.2 in grayscale mode. This method assigns luminosity values to each pixel, converting the lung CXR into an 8-bit image that retains 256 shades of black and white. This approach effectively preserves subtle details while reducing the feature pool. The study highlights that alternative methods, including bi-level mode, Palette mode, and RGB mode, yielded inferior results compared to the grayscale method.

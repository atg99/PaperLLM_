The study presents a novel HSGO-based pipeline combined with Support Vector Classifier (SVC) for the detection of COVID-19 from chest X-ray (CXR) images. The algorithm demonstrated high classification accuracy, achieving 99.31% and 99.26% in varying dataset compositions, indicating robust performance even with imbalanced data. 

Comparative analyses with other bio-inspired algorithms, such as the Chaotic Crow Search Optimization (CSO) and Spider-Monkey Optimization (OSMO), showed that HSGO outperformed these methods in feature selection and classification accuracy. Additionally, performance was benchmarked against state-of-the-art deep learning models, with the proposed method achieving an exceptional accuracy of 99.65% using SVC, surpassing results from models like SqueezeNet, which reached up to 98.3%.

The research underscores the critical need for early diagnosis of COVID-19, especially during the ongoing pandemic, where traditional testing methods may be insufficient. The proposed computer-aided diagnosis system leverages CXR radiographs to facilitate timely detection, thereby aiding in controlling the spread of the virus and reducing mortality rates. The findings highlight the model's effectiveness and potential for integration into clinical settings to enhance diagnostic capabilities.

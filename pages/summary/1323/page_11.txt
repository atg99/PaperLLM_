The paper presents a pipeline for the early diagnosis of COVID-19 that achieves an accuracy of 99.65% across various classifiers. This high performance indicates its potential for integration into mobile applications to assist medical practitioners. 

The authors suggest several avenues for future research to enhance the proposed pipeline's accuracy. One recommendation is to increase the dataset of COVID-19 chest X-ray images used in the HSGO model. Additionally, a thorough investigation of the impact of various parameters on different datasets could provide insights into their collective influence on outcomes. Exploring alternative feature extraction methods and adjusting the Mutation phase of the HSGO could further improve the model's applicability to a broader range of real-world problems.

The research was conducted ethically, with informed consent obtained from all participants, and no conflicts of interest were reported. The code for the proposed algorithm is available on GitHub, and the article is published under a Creative Commons license, allowing for use and distribution with appropriate credit. 

The references cited include foundational studies on coronaviruses and relevant health guidelines, underscoring the research's grounding in existing literature.

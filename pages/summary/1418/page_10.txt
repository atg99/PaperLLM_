The references cited in the paper cover a range of research in visual question answering (VQA) and related fields. Key contributions include methods for joint parsing of video and text to enhance event understanding and query responses, as highlighted by Tu et al. in 2014. Wang et al. (2017) introduced knowledge-based reasoning for VQA, emphasizing the integration of external knowledge to improve accuracy in answering questions. The development of dynamic memory networks and attention mechanisms is also notable, with works by Xiong et al. (2016) and Xu and Saenko (2016) exploring how spatial attention can guide responses effectively.

Other significant contributions include the proposal of fact-based approaches for VQA by Wang et al. (2017), which focus on leveraging factual information for more precise answers. The use of structured data for question answering as demonstrated by Yao and Van Durme (2014) and the semantic parsing techniques by Yih et al. (2015) highlight the importance of knowledge bases in understanding queries. 

Overall, these references provide a comprehensive overview of advancements in the integration of visual and textual information for enhanced question answering, addressing various methodologies from memory networks to knowledge acquisition strategies.

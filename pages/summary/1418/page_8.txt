This paper introduces a novel benchmark for knowledge-based visual question answering (VQA) called OK-VQA. Unlike traditional VQA benchmarks, OK-VQA requires answers that cannot be derived solely from the provided images and questions, necessitating reasoning based on external knowledge resources. The study reveals that state-of-the-art VQA models experience a significant decline in performance on this new benchmark, indicating its challenging nature. The authors analyze the dataset's properties and statistics, demonstrating that incorporating background knowledge can enhance performance. The experimental evaluations confirm that there is substantial room for improvement in VQA models when faced with the OK-VQA challenge.

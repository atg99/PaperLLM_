The paper presents a dataset breakdown of questions categorized into ten knowledge areas, highlighting the distribution of questions across these categories. The largest category is "Plants and Animals" at 17%, followed by "Vehicles and Transportation" at 16% and "Cooking and Food" at 15%. The dataset emphasizes knowledge-based questions, resulting in longer average question and answer lengths compared to other Visual Question Answering (VQA) datasets. 

The dataset consists of 12,591 unique questions derived from 14,055 total questions, showcasing a diverse range of inquiries. Images used in the dataset are sourced from the COCO dataset, ensuring a wide distribution of scenes, with only five scene classes absent. The paper also identifies the most frequent question words and answers within each category, illustrating the specificity of language used, such as "bus" in Vehicles and Transportation and "sandwich" in Cooking and Food.

Benchmarking of current VQA approaches is also discussed, indicating the effectiveness of the dataset for evaluating state-of-the-art methods in this field. Overall, the work aims to enhance the understanding and performance of VQA systems by providing a comprehensive, knowledge-focused dataset.

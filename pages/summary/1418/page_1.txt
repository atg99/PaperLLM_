The paper introduces OK-VQA, a new benchmark for Visual Question Answering (VQA) that emphasizes the need for external knowledge to answer questions, unlike existing datasets that primarily focus on visual recognition tasks. The authors argue that current VQA benchmarks often only require simple visual tasks such as counting or identifying attributes, which do not test the models' reasoning capabilities or their ability to integrate knowledge beyond the image content.

OK-VQA comprises over 14,000 questions that necessitate external knowledge for accurate responses, revealing a significant decline in performance among state-of-the-art VQA models in this context. The dataset is characterized as diverse, challenging, and extensive compared to prior knowledge-based VQA datasets. The authors aim for this benchmark to stimulate further research and development in the area of knowledge-based visual question answering, ultimately contributing to a deeper understanding of scene comprehension in AI systems.

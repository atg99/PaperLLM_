This paper introduces a novel Visual Question Answering (VQA) dataset called Outside Knowledge VQA (OK-VQA), designed specifically for questions that require external knowledge sources to answer. Unlike traditional VQA datasets that rely solely on the information present in images, OK-VQA challenges models to perform reasoning by integrating unstructured knowledge from external resources. 

The dataset comprises over 14,000 questions spanning various categories, including science and technology, history, and sports. The authors highlight the complexities involved in answering OK-VQA questions, which necessitate understanding the question and image, identifying relevant knowledge, formulating appropriate queries to retrieve this knowledge, and effectively incorporating it to derive an answer.

The paper also benchmarks state-of-the-art VQA models on OK-VQA, demonstrating a significant degradation in their performance, which underscores the dataset's difficulty. Additionally, the authors propose baseline approaches that utilize unstructured knowledge, marking a step forward in VQA methodologies.

In the context of related work, the paper discusses the evolution of VQA approaches, from early models combining recurrent networks with convolutional neural networks to the incorporation of attention mechanisms and modular networks. It contrasts these with existing datasets that either do not require external knowledge or rely on structured knowledge representations, emphasizing that OK-VQA targets unstructured knowledge retrieval.

Overall, the contributions of this work include the introduction of the OK-VQA dataset, a performance benchmark for existing models, and the establishment of baselines that leverage unstructured external knowledge, setting the foundation for future research in this domain.

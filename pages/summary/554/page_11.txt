The references listed are from various significant works in the field of machine learning, particularly focusing on neural architecture search and hyperparameter optimization. Key contributions include:

1. Baker et al. (2018) explored performance prediction to accelerate neural architecture search.
2. Bergstra and Bengio (2012) introduced random search as an effective method for hyperparameter optimization.
3. Brock et al. (2018) presented SMASH, a one-shot model architecture search method using hypernetworks.
4. Cai et al. (2018) discussed efficient architecture search through network transformation techniques.
5. Chen et al. (2016) proposed Net2net, which accelerates learning via knowledge transfer.
6. Chrabaszcz et al. (2017) introduced a downsampled variant of ImageNet as an alternative dataset.
7. Dong and Yang (2019) outlined a self-evaluated template network for one-shot neural architecture search.
8. Falkner et al. (2018) presented BOHB, a robust hyperparameter optimization method at scale.
9. Ghiasi et al. (2018) introduced Dropblock, a regularization method for convolutional networks.
10. He et al. (2016) discussed deep residual learning, which has had a significant impact on image recognition tasks.
11. Ioffe and Szegedy (2015) developed batch normalization to enhance training speed and stability in deep networks.
12. Klein and Hutter (2019) provided benchmarks for joint architecture and hyperparameter optimization.
13. Krizhevsky et al. (2009) and (2012) contributed foundational work in deep learning, particularly in image classification.
14. Li and Talwalkar (2019) addressed reproducibility in random search for neural architecture search.
15. Li et al. (2018) introduced Hyperband, a novel approach to hyperparameter optimization based on bandit strategies.

These references collectively highlight advancements in methodologies and techniques that facilitate the optimization of neural networks and their architectures.

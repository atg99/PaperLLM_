2. The exploration of fNIRS-based brain signals for speech recognition (Liu & Ayaz, 2018).
3. High-density ECoG grids for decoding spoken phonemes from the sensorimotor cortex (Ramsey et al., 2017).
4. Direct brain recordings for word pair classification during imagined speech (Martin et al., 2016).
5. EEG studies focused on covert vowel articulation (Yoshimura et al., 2016) and envisioned speech recognition (Kumar et al., 2018).
6. The application of generative adversarial networks for robust speech recognition (Sriram et al., 2018).
7. Techniques for noise-robust automatic speech recognition utilizing deep learning and sparse representations (Gemmeke et al., 2011; Tan et al., 2018).
8. Reviews and surveys on biosignal-based communication and brain-computer interface technologies (Schultz et al., 2017; AlSaleh et al., 2016).

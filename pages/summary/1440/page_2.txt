The architecture of the model includes a Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) layer that processes input vectors, followed by an average pooling layer that summarizes the GRU outputs, and a dense layer for classification. The model is trained on three feature sets: acoustic features alone, a combination of acoustic and EEG features, and EEG features alone. The training set comprises 64 percent of the data, with 16 percent for validation and 20 percent for testing.

The model was optimized using the Adam optimizer with a learning rate of 0.001, and the dropout rate for the dense layer was set at 0.2. The training process involved adjusting the number of epochs based on the convergence of the model, particularly increasing it to 30,000 epochs for datasets containing noise.

The paper emphasizes the importance of soft targets generated from the teacher model during the training of the student model, highlighting the effectiveness of this approach in enhancing the accuracy of Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) systems. The results indicate that the combination of features and the distillation process can significantly improve performance in recognizing words and vowels.

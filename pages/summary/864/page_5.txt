The paper details various hyper-parameters for the models, including model sizes, attention heads, and training times across different configurations, such as CNN Base, CNN Large, and BPE Large. Each model is trained using advanced techniques, including Nesterov's accelerated gradient method and a specific learning rate schedule, while maintaining precision with 16-bit floating point. 

The evaluation on the GLUE benchmark encompasses a range of tasks, from single-sentence classification to natural language inference, where the models demonstrated competitive performance. The findings suggest that while parameter size and training efficiency are important, they do not solely dictate model performance, as evidenced by the BPE model's relative performance compared to smaller CNN models.

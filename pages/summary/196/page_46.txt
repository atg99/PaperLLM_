This paper discusses advancements in exploration strategies within reinforcement learning (RL), particularly focusing on count-based methods and curiosity-driven approaches. It references several key works that contribute to the field, including methods that utilize density estimation for experience prioritization, the use of neural density models for count-based exploration, and the adaptation of exemplar models for enhanced exploration in deep RL. The authors highlight the importance of effective exploration strategies in improving learning efficiency and performance in various RL tasks. The cited studies indicate a trend towards integrating curiosity and density-based techniques to foster better exploration behaviors, ultimately aiming to optimize learning outcomes in complex environments.

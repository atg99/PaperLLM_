This paper presents a comprehensive survey of exploration techniques in deep reinforcement learning, emphasizing their significance in addressing sparse reward problems where rewards are infrequent. Such challenges hinder the agent's ability to learn effective reward-action associations through random actions. To tackle this issue, the authors categorize existing exploration methods into several key groups: reward novel states, reward diverse behaviors, goal-based methods, probabilistic methods, imitation-based methods, safe exploration, and random-based methods. 

The paper further discusses unresolved challenges in the field, offering insights into potential future research directions. Additionally, it compares the various approaches across categories based on complexity, computational effort, and overall performance, highlighting the importance of sophisticated exploration strategies for enhancing learning efficiency in environments with sparse rewards. 

Overall, the review underscores the need for innovative exploration methods to improve the effectiveness of deep reinforcement learning in practical applications.

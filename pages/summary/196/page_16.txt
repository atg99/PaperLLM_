The section discusses the concept of reward diverse behaviors in reinforcement learning, where the objective is for agents to gather a wide range of experiences rather than merely seeking rewards. This approach can be categorized into evolution strategies and policy learning.

In evolutionary approaches, a population of sample solutions is tested and evolved over time to approach optimal solutions. Although evolutionary methods are not typically classified under reinforcement learning, they address similar problems. One notable method is novelty search, which promotes the generation of diverse behaviors using a diversity measure. However, this measure must be manually designed for each environment, which hinders its adaptability across different domains.

Recent advancements have integrated novelty search with reward maximization strategies. For instance, Conti et al. combined novelty search with reward maximization to foster diversity while seeking rewards. Gravina et al. evaluated three combinations of novelty search and reward novel state methods, finding that a sequential optimization approach yielded the best results in a simulated robotic context. Further exploration of evolution strategies is detailed in referenced studies.

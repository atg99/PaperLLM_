The paper discusses various benchmarks used in reinforcement learning, highlighting VizDoom, Malmo, and MuJoCo. VizDoom utilizes a first-person perspective from the Doom game, offering a simple state space and action controls, making it easy to edit scenarios. Malmo, based on Minecraft, allows for flexible environment creation and custom scripts, also viewed from a first-person perspective. MuJoCo is focused on physics simulations, particularly for multi-joint dynamics, often used to simulate walking robots with the goal of optimizing movement.

The exploration aspect of reinforcement learning is categorized into efficiency and safe exploration. Efficiency aims to reduce the number of steps needed for exploration and is further divided into imitation-based learning, where agents learn from expert policies, and self-taught methods, which include planning, intrinsic rewards, and random exploration. Self-taught methods focus on developing understanding through exploration without prior knowledge. Intrinsic rewards motivate agents to visit novel states or exhibit diverse behaviors. Planning methods are categorized into goal-based and probabilistic approaches, enhancing the agent's ability to navigate and learn from its environment.

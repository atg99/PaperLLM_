The references listed encompass a variety of research studies focused on intrinsically motivated learning, reinforcement learning, and hierarchical approaches within these domains. 

1. Forestier et al. (2017) discuss automatic curriculum learning to enhance goal exploration processes motivated by intrinsic factors.
2. Colas et al. (2019) present CURIOS, a modular multi-goal reinforcement learning framework driven by intrinsic motivation.
3. Péré et al. (2018) explore unsupervised learning methods for goal space discovery that supports intrinsically motivated exploration.
4. Vezhnevets et al. (2017) introduce FeUdal Networks, which facilitate hierarchical reinforcement learning by structuring tasks and goals.
5. Hester and Stone (2013) investigate learning exploration strategies within a model-based reinforcement learning context.
6. Kulkarni et al. (2016) integrate temporal abstraction and intrinsic motivation in hierarchical deep reinforcement learning.
7. Riedmiller et al. (2018) examine learning through play as a means to solve sparse reward tasks.
8. Ghafoorian et al. (2013) propose the Ant System Algorithm for automatic abstraction in reinforcement learning.
9. Machado et al. (2017) provide a framework for option discovery in reinforcement learning using Laplacian methods.
10. Zaki and Meira (2014) present foundational concepts and algorithms in data mining, relevant for understanding data analysis in machine learning contexts.
11. Machado et al. (2018) discuss eigenoption discovery through deep successor representation, contributing to the understanding of option discovery in reinforcement learning.

These studies collectively shed light on the advancements in reinforcement learning, particularly through the lens of intrinsic motivation and hierarchical structures, which are crucial for developing more effective learning algorithms.

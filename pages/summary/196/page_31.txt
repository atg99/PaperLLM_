Prediction models, which can be based on mathematical models or heuristic rules, offer greater adaptability to new environments, enhancing the agent's ability to navigate unforeseen scenarios. Auxiliary reward methods, while requiring minimal human intervention, tend to produce weaker safety behaviors, relying on penalties for approaching dangerous states. Overall, safety in reinforcement learning is crucial for real-world applications, with the three primary approaches providing different levels of effectiveness and adaptability.

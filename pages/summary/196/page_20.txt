Goals in the region of highest uncertainty involve directing the agent towards states where it has limited knowledge, thereby promoting exploration. This method capitalizes on the idea that exploring less familiar areas can yield new insights and improve the agent's understanding of the environment.

Sub-goal methods further break down the exploration process by setting intermediate goals that guide the agent towards the main objective. This approach can help the agent maintain focus and systematically navigate complex environments.

In summary, various methods for enhancing exploration through goal-setting have been developed, including memory-based strategies, exploratory goals, and meta-controller frameworks. These approaches aim to mitigate challenges in exploration, particularly in difficult environments, by effectively utilizing knowledge about past experiences and strategically selecting targets for the agent to pursue.

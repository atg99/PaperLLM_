1. Maximum Entropy Diverse Exploration by Cohen et al., which disentangles maximum entropy reinforcement learning to enhance exploration diversity.
2. Skew-Fit by Pong et al., introducing a self-supervised learning approach that covers states effectively.
3. Learning Self-Imitating Diverse Policies by Gangwani et al., which addresses generating diverse policies through self-imitation.
4. First Return Then Explore by Eco et al., presenting a method that prioritizes returning to previously visited states before exploring new ones.
5. PBCS by Matheron et al., integrating reinforcement learning with motion planning for efficient exploration and exploitation.
6. Directed Exploration by Guo and Brunskill, focusing on targeted exploration techniques.
7. Self-Imitation Learning via Trajectory-Conditioned Policy by Guo et al., which enhances learning in challenging exploration tasks.
8. Memory Based Trajectory-Conditioned Policies by Guo et al., which utilize memory for learning from sparse rewards.
9. Learning Abstract Models for Strategic Exploration by Liu et al., aiming for efficient reward transfer and exploration strategies.
10. Forward-Backward Reinforcement Learning by Edwards et al., and Reverse Curriculum Generation by Florensa et al., both of which explore innovative training methodologies in reinforcement learning.

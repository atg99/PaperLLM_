The selected references focus on various methodologies and frameworks for enhancing exploration in reinforcement learning (RL). Key concepts include:

1. **Distributional Reinforcement Learning**: Tang and Agrawal discuss how distributional approaches can improve exploration strategies within RL environments.

2. **Decoupling Exploration and Exploitation**: Colas et al. present GEP-PG, a framework that separates exploration from exploitation in deep RL algorithms, promoting more effective learning.

3. **Successor Uncertainties**: Janz et al. explore the role of uncertainty in temporal difference learning, highlighting how managing successor uncertainties can aid exploration.

4. **Adaptive Exploration**: Stulp's work on adaptive exploration emphasizes continual learning, allowing agents to adjust their exploration strategies over time.

5. **Active Learning for Value Function Approximation**: Akiyama et al. demonstrate how active learning techniques can enhance exploration in value function approximation tasks.

6. **Bayesian Framework**: Strens introduces a Bayesian approach to RL, which incorporates uncertainty into the learning process, impacting exploration tactics.

7. **Bayes-adaptive Reinforcement Learning**: Guez et al. utilize sample-based search to improve efficiency in Bayes-adaptive RL, facilitating better exploration strategies.

8. **Uncertainty Bellman Equation**: O'Donoghue et al. delve into the formulation of the Uncertainty Bellman Equation, emphasizing its implications for exploration.

9. **Information-Directed Exploration**: Nikolov et al. propose information-directed exploration methods that leverage the information gain to enhance learning efficiency.

10. **Bootstrapped DQN**: Osband et al. introduce a bootstrapping technique in deep Q-networks to promote deeper exploration in complex environments.

11. **Anchored Ensembles of Neural Networks**: Pearce et al. apply Bayesian inference with anchored ensembles to improve exploration mechanisms in RL frameworks.

These works collectively contribute to the understanding of exploration strategies in reinforcement learning, highlighting the importance of balancing exploration and exploitation, managing uncertainty, and leveraging advanced statistical methods for improved learning outcomes.

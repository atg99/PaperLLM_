The referenced papers cover various advancements in speech recognition technologies, particularly focusing on deep neural networks and end-to-end models. Key contributions include:

1. **Sequence-discriminative Training**: Vesely et al. (2013) discuss methods to enhance deep neural networks for speech recognition through sequence-discriminative training techniques.

2. **Lattice-Free MMI**: Hadian et al. (2018) present an innovative approach to end-to-end speech recognition that utilizes lattice-free Minimum Mean Information (MMI), improving recognition accuracy.

3. **All-Neural Speech Recognition**: Zweig et al. (2017) explore significant advancements in all-neural architectures for speech recognition, emphasizing the benefits of fully neural models.

4. **Acoustics-to-Word Models**: Audhkhasi et al. (2018) introduce direct acoustics-to-word models, demonstrating their effectiveness for English conversational speech recognition.

5. **Recurrent Neural Networks**: Lu et al. (2016) focus on optimizing recurrent neural network encoder-decoder architectures for large vocabulary end-to-end speech recognition.

6. **Multitask Learning**: Toshniwal et al. (2017) investigate the use of multitask learning with low-level auxiliary tasks to enhance encoder-decoder based speech recognition systems.

7. **Attention Mechanisms**: Weng et al. (2018) work on improving attention-based sequence-to-sequence models specifically for English conversational speech recognition.

8. **Robust CNN Acoustic Models**: Kovács et al. (2017) and Tóth et al. (2018) address the robustness of convolutional neural network (CNN) acoustic models by employing autoregressive moving average spectrogram features and perceptually inspired data augmentation methods.

These studies collectively contribute to the evolving landscape of speech recognition, emphasizing the integration of advanced neural network architectures and techniques to enhance accuracy and robustness.

The paper discusses advancements in automatic speech recognition (ASR) achieved by enhancing training sets through simple handcrafted policies, leading to improved performance of neural networks over hybrid systems, even in the absence of language models. The approach, termed SpecAugment, shifts ASR from an overfitting problem to an underfitting one, enabling the use of larger networks and extended training periods to enhance performance. The authors express gratitude to various contributors for their discussions and insights that informed the study. The references provided include significant works in deep learning for ASR, highlighting the evolution of techniques and models that have shaped the field.

The paper discusses various aspects of federated learning and its challenges, particularly focusing on issues like continual learning and robust aggregation methods. It references several key studies, including Yoon et al. (2020), which explores adaptive parameter communication in federated continual learning, and Pillutla et al. (2019), which examines robust aggregation techniques to enhance the reliability of federated learning systems. The work also touches on foundational concepts such as the Byzantine generals problem, as highlighted by Lamport et al.

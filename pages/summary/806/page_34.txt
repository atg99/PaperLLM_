In the Grow phase, the model's capacity is increased by adding layers or width, either manually or through scaling multipliers. Following this, the Improve phase focuses on enhancing the model's quality using various learning techniques such as data augmentation and distillation, alongside automation methods like hyper-parameter optimization. Finally, the Shrink phase involves reducing the model's size while aiming to retain its quality, which can be done naively or through learned methods.

The paper highlights two strategies: the Grow-Improve-and-Shrink approach, where model quality is enhanced before size reduction, and the Shrink-and-Improve method, where model capacity is reduced first, followed by efforts to recover quality. Both strategies target achieving a model that is more efficient and lies on the pareto-frontier, balancing quality and footprint according to user needs.

To validate these strategies, experiments were conducted using the CIFAR-10 dataset, focusing on creating an efficient deep learning model suitable for deployment on resource-constrained devices. The results aimed to provide empirical evidence of the effectiveness of the proposed techniques in achieving a new pareto-frontier through various combinations of efficiency techniques and scaling strategies. This research demonstrates the potential for machine learning practitioners to optimize model performance while managing compute resources effectively.

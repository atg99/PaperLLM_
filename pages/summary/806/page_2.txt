The paper discusses the challenges and considerations in the development and deployment of deep learning models, particularly in the realms of computer vision and natural language processing. Key issues highlighted include the high resource consumption of large models, which raises concerns about the carbon footprint of data centers operated by major tech companies. The need for on-device deployment is emphasized, as certain applications require real-time processing on IoT and smart devices for reasons such as privacy, responsiveness, and connectivity. 

The paper also underscores the importance of minimizing data usage during training, especially when dealing with sensitive user data, as this can reduce the need for extensive data collection. Additionally, it points out that new applications may impose specific constraints that existing models cannot meet. Finally, the proliferation of models presents a challenge, as deploying multiple models on the same infrastructure can strain available resources.

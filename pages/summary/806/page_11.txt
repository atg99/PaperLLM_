The paper discusses various strategies for optimizing deep learning models, particularly focusing on quantization techniques. A comparison of the MobileNet v2 architecture illustrates the impact of quantization on model performance. The baseline model achieves a top-1 accuracy of 71.9% with a size of 14 MB and latency of 89 ms. Post-training quantization significantly reduces the model size to 3.6 MB but results in a drop in accuracy to 63.7%. In contrast, quantization-aware training (QAT) retains a higher accuracy of 70.9% while also achieving a reduced size and improved latency.

The paper highlights notable works in the field, such as those by Polino et al., who implement non-uniform precision distribution and distillation to enhance model performance, particularly in simpler datasets. However, their comparisons may not be entirely fair due to differing methodologies. Fan et al. demonstrate improvements in accuracy through a stochastic application of fake-quantization, particularly in 4-bit quantized models.

Key points from the discussion emphasize that quantization is a powerful technique for reducing model size and inference latency. Weight quantization is straightforward, but activation quantization is critical for reducing memory usage in low-resource environments. It is recommended to use QAT over post-training quantization for better accuracy, though tools like TensorFlow Lite facilitate the latter with minimal loss in performance. Furthermore, the paper suggests considering operations following typical layers for optimization and mentions other compression techniques, such as low-rank matrix factorization and weight-sharing, as additional methods for model compression.

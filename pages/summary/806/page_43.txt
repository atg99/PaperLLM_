The paper discusses various approaches to enhance the efficiency of deep learning models by making them smaller, faster, and more effective. It cites significant contributions in the field, including MobileNetV2, which utilizes inverted residuals and linear bottlenecks to optimize performance, and DistilBERT, a compact version of BERT that retains essential capabilities while reducing resource requirements. The survey highlights the importance of transferable neural representations and on-device learning techniques, emphasizing novel architectures such as MnasNet and MobileBERT, tailored for mobile and resource-constrained environments. Additionally, it addresses advancements in model optimization techniques, including post-training quantization and the use of Tensor Processing Units (TPUs) for enhanced computational efficiency. The paper also references various studies on the effectiveness of deep convolutional networks and the role of data in improving model performance. Overall, it provides a comprehensive overview of strategies to optimize deep learning frameworks while maintaining robust performance across diverse applications.

This paper presents a comprehensive survey on efficient deep learning, focusing on strategies to make deep learning models smaller, faster, and better. It highlights various techniques such as data augmentation, quantization, and pruning, which are essential for optimizing model performance and resource utilization.

Key contributions include:

1. **Data Augmentation**: Techniques like pairing samples for image classification improve model robustness without increasing data collection costs.

2. **Quantization**: The survey discusses methods for quantizing neural networks to facilitate efficient inference using integer arithmetic, as demonstrated in various studies.

3. **Model Training and Optimization**: The paper reviews approaches like population-based training and hyperparameter optimization to enhance model performance.

4. **Architecture Search and Pruning**: It covers advancements in neural architecture search and automatic channel pruning, which allow for the development of lighter models without significant loss in accuracy.

5. **Performance Analysis**: The evaluation of hardware architectures, such as tensor processing units, is included to understand their impact on deep learning efficiency.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the importance of making deep learning models more efficient, addressing both theoretical and practical aspects, and providing insights into future directions for research in this domain.

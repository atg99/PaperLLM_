The paper discusses the trade-offs involved in scaling model capacity through a width multiplier, which adjusts the number of filters in convolutional layers. The results indicate that as the width multiplier increases, both the number of parameters and model size grow substantially, leading to increased latency. Quantization further contributes to model efficiency by reducing size and latency, effectively shrinking the model footprint. The findings emphasize the importance of balancing model quality, size, and latency, especially for deployment in resource-constrained environments.

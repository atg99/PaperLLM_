The references listed encompass a range of topics in computer vision, machine learning, and neural network optimization techniques. Key contributions include:

1. Liu et al. (2018) discuss the implications of network pruning, challenging traditional views on its effectiveness and value in deep learning models.
2. Menghani and Ravi (2019) present a method for leveraging unlabeled data through a teacher-student framework.
3. Mikolov et al. (2017) advance techniques in pre-training distributed word representations, which are essential for natural language processing tasks.
4. Molchanov et al. (2016) explore resource-efficient transfer learning through pruning convolutional neural networks.
5. Paszke et al. (2019) introduce PyTorch, a deep learning library that emphasizes an imperative programming style for high performance.
6. Pham et al. (2018) propose a neural architecture search method that improves efficiency through parameter sharing.
7. Rastegari et al. (2016) present Xnor-Net, which utilizes binary convolutional neural networks for efficient image classification.

These contributions reflect ongoing innovations in model optimization, architecture search, and the integration of machine learning techniques across various applications.

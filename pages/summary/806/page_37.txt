This paper provides a comprehensive survey on enhancing the efficiency of deep learning models, focusing on making them smaller, faster, and better. It discusses the use of Data Augmentation and Distillation techniques, which significantly improve the accuracy of smaller models. For instance, a model reduced to 392.49 KB achieved an accuracy of 84.18%, while being 13.8 times smaller and 2.94 times faster than a larger model. Further compression through Quantization reduced the model size to 111 KB and latency to 4.52 ms, maintaining the same accuracy.

The paper introduces a strategy termed Grow-Improve-Shrink, which involves initially increasing the model size to improve quality before applying compression techniques. This approach resulted in a model size of 359.31 KB and a latency of 0.58 ms, with an improved accuracy of 87.86%, demonstrating a 7.10% enhancement while keeping the model size comparable.

The authors emphasize that both quality and footprint metrics can be optimized using various techniques, including Compression Techniques like Quantization and Learning Techniques such as Data Augmentation and Distillation. They suggest exploring additional methods like Automation for Hyper-Parameter Tuning and the use of Efficient Layers, such as Separable Convolutions.

The conclusion highlights the rapid growth of deep learning models and the necessity for practitioners to make informed decisions regarding model efficiency. The paper provides a mental model for understanding various optimization areas, a survey of state-of-the-art techniques, and actionable insights supported by code. Ultimately, it aims to equip readers with the knowledge to transition from sub-optimal models to those that satisfy quality and footprint constraints.

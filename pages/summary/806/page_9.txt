The paper discusses how the quantization of weights and activations can lead to substantial reductions in model size and improvements in inference speed without significantly compromising accuracy. Post-training quantization is highlighted as a method to achieve this, where pre-trained models are quantized to make them smaller and more efficient. 

Various quantization techniques are explored, including extreme quantization methods like XNOR-Net and Binarized Neural Networks, which reduce weight matrices to binary values. These approaches theoretically promise a 32-fold reduction in model size while maintaining performance, although practical implementations must be validated on smaller networks. 

Furthermore, the paper emphasizes the importance of hardware support for efficient quantization, noting that standard quantization methods using 8 bits can leverage hardware capabilities more effectively. The discussion concludes by stressing the necessity of quantizing both weights and activations to fully realize the benefits of latency improvements in deep learning models.

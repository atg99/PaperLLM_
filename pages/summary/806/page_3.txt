The paper discusses the concept of efficient deep learning, emphasizing the importance of both inference and training efficiency in the deployment and training of deep learning models. Inference efficiency focuses on the model's size, speed, and resource consumption during output generation, while training efficiency addresses the time, resource requirements, and data needs for model training.

The authors highlight the significance of pareto-optimality, where models are evaluated based on their trade-offs between accuracy and efficiency. Models on the pareto-frontier, represented by green dots in a provided figure, are considered the most efficient since they achieve the best performance for specific trade-offs compared to other models.

To facilitate the development of pareto-optimal models, the paper advocates for the use of a variety of algorithms, techniques, tools, and infrastructure that collectively enhance the training and deployment processes. This approach aims to optimize model quality while minimizing resource consumption.

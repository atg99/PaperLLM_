The referenced papers focus on advancements in image classification and neural network architectures, particularly exploring various strategies to improve performance and efficiency. Notable contributions include the MLP-Mixer, which presents an all-MLP architecture specifically for vision tasks, and ResMLP, which emphasizes data-efficient training with feedforward networks. Several studies also delve into training techniques for transformers, highlighting the importance of attention mechanisms and data efficiency. Innovations like CutMix and Mixup are discussed as regularization strategies that enhance model robustness by leveraging localizable features. Additionally, methods for optimizing large batch training and addressing resolution discrepancies during training and testing are explored. The body of work collectively underscores the ongoing evolution of image classification techniques and the integration of novel training methodologies to achieve superior results.

This paper presents various training procedures for the ResNet-50 architecture, highlighting their configurations and performance metrics. Four distinct procedures (B, C.1, C.2, and D) are detailed, each with specific parameters such as batch size, optimizer, learning rate, and augmentation techniques.

Procedure B employs the RMSProp optimizer, inspired by RandAugment techniques utilized in training EfficientNet. It features a higher learning rate and adjustments to weight decay and step decay, with additional augmentations including per-pixel noise, Random Erasing, and Mixup. Important to note is the use of the RMSProp implementation from the timm library, which aligns with older TensorFlow behaviors.

Procedures C.1 and C.2 utilize SGD with Nesterov's momentum and Adaptive Gradient Clipping, both implementing cosine learning rate decay. The primary distinction between C.1 and C.2 is the use of repeated augmentation in C.1. 

Procedure D employs the AdamP optimizer with binary cross-entropy loss and cosine learning rate decay.

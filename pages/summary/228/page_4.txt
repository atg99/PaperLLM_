The paper discusses three distinct training procedures aimed at optimizing the performance of the ResNet-50 model on the ImageNet dataset, particularly at a resolution of 224x224. The procedures are summarized in Table 1, which details the training epochs, resolution, GPU memory usage, training time, and corresponding accuracy metrics.

Procedure A1, the most extensive, involves 600 epochs and approximately 110 hours of training on four V100 GPUs, achieving the highest top-1 accuracy of 80.4% on the validation set. Procedure A2 reduces the training time to 55 hours with 300 epochs, yielding a top-1 accuracy of 79.8%. Procedure A3, with the least training time of 15 hours over 100 epochs, aims to provide a quicker training option for exploratory research, achieving a top-1 accuracy of 78.1%.

The paper highlights the use of a multi-label classification objective with a binary cross-entropy loss function, which is particularly effective when combined with data augmentation techniques like Mixup and CutMix. This approach allows for treating each class as potentially present, contrary to traditional methods that assume exclusivity of class labels. The authors reference previous works that have successfully utilized this loss function but note their unique application with Mixup and CutMix.

Overall, the training procedures are designed for varying use cases, balancing the trade-offs between training time and model performance. Further details on the training ingredients and alternative procedures are provided in the appendix sections.

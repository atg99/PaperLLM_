The paper discusses the enhancements made in the timm library, particularly in relation to data augmentation techniques and regularization methods. It emphasizes the careful consideration of image mean during augmentations, allowing users to specify normalization parameters to prevent distortions from border pixels. Cutout is excluded by default to promote the use of Random Erasing, which has been modified to utilize per-pixel Gaussian noise instead of uniform colors, thus preserving image statistics and improving results.

The integration of Mixup and CutMix is highlighted, where both techniques can be used simultaneously with various mixing strategies, allowing for flexibility in training. The default setting uses either CutMix or Mixup with a 50% probability per batch when both are enabled.

Regularization techniques in timm are standardized across models, offering options such as weight decay, pre-classifier dropout, and Stochastic Depth through the DropPath layer. Label smoothing is also incorporated, allowing for its combination with CutMix and Mixup.

Additionally, the paper presents alternative training procedures using the LAMB optimizer, alongside variations that utilize RMSProp with EMA weight averaging and step learning rate decay. These alternative recipes aim to provide better results tailored to specific model architectures, datasets, or tasks, complementing the main training recipes that achieve leading results with the ResNet-50 architecture.

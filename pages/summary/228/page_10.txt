The paper presents an analysis of the performance of ResNet-50 and DeiT-S architectures on the ImageNet dataset, focusing on the impact of training procedures on accuracy. The authors report various top-1 accuracy statistics across different datasets, including ImageNet-val, ImageNet-real, and ImageNet-V2, highlighting results obtained from 100 different seeds. 

Key findings include that ResNet-50 achieves an average top-1 accuracy of 79.72% on ImageNet-val, while its performance improves to 85.37% on ImageNet-real. The A2 training procedure for ResNet-50 yields a notable performance, with a 0.13% increase over the average accuracy on ImageNet-val. Conversely, DeiT-S, under an optimized training procedure (T2), demonstrates a superior performance on ImageNet-val at 80.4%, compared to its original training method.

The paper emphasizes the challenges in making direct comparisons between architectures and training methods, as evidenced by the contrasting results obtained from ResNet-50 and DeiT-S. The authors conclude that careful optimization of training procedures is crucial for maximizing model performance, illustrating the complexity of architectural evaluations within deep learning.

The paper outlines recommended configurations for training the ResNet-50 architecture across four different procedures (A, B, C, and D). Each procedure specifies key hyperparameters including the number of epochs, initial learning rate, learning rate schedule, and various augmentation techniques.

**Procedure A** recommends training for 400-700 epochs, with an initial learning rate of 0.0225 and a step decay schedule. Grad clipping is disabled, dropout is set to 0.2, and stochastic depth is 0.1. Data augmentation methods include RandAugment with specified parameters and random erasing at a probability of 0.35.

**Procedure B** suggests a longer training duration of 300-800 epochs, with an initial learning rate of 0.11 and a cosine learning rate schedule. It employs Adaptive Gradient Clipping (AGC) for gradient clipping, a dropout rate of 0.25, and includes CutMix as a data augmentation strategy.

**Procedure C** has a similar epoch range as Procedure B but uses a lower initial learning rate of 0.0027 and maintains a cosine learning rate schedule. The dropout rate is lower at 0.1, and it also uses RandAugment and random erasing for data augmentation, with a focus on BCE loss.

**Procedure D** recommends 300-600 epochs, with an even lower initial learning rate of 0.0027. It employs no gradient clipping and has a dropout rate of 0.1. This procedure also uses RandAugment and random erasing, but it adopts a BCE loss function.

Overall, the paper provides detailed hyperparameter recommendations aimed at optimizing the training of ResNet-50, considering various configurations to enhance model performance while using data augmentation techniques.

The paper presents new training procedures for the ResNet-50 architecture, focusing on optimizing performance under various resource constraints. Key findings include an ablation study on crop-ratio, revealing that a crop-ratio of 0.95, while comparable to the standard 0.875, is not statistically superior. The study also highlights the impact of test resolution on ImageNet Top-1 accuracy, demonstrating that larger images benefit training procedures A1 and A2, while A3 does not, as it is designed for smaller training images.

In conclusion, the authors assert that their training procedures achieve state-of-the-art results for ResNet-50 but emphasize that these methods are not universally applicable across all models. Some architectures may benefit from these procedures, while others, particularly deeper ones, may require more regularization. Acknowledgments are given to NVIDIA and Google for resources, and the authors invite feedback on their methods through the timm GitHub Discussions.

The paper examines the effects of stochastic depth and label smoothing on training procedures A1, A2, and A3, as detailed in Table 7. It finds that stochastic depth improves performance in training procedure A2, but not in A3. Label smoothing shows limited effectiveness at 300 epochs, leading to its exclusive use in the longer 600-epoch schedule for A1, where it positively impacts results.

Additionally, the role of data augmentations is highlighted in Table 8, which demonstrates that modifications to parameters such as Mixup and RandAugment adversely affect top-1 accuracy, with all changes resulting in scores below the average performance of 79.72%. The default configuration for training procedure A2 is established in the first row of the table, with subsequent rows reflecting the impact of individual parameter adjustments.

The study also evaluates the crop-ratio at inference time, noting that the conventional ratio of 0.875 has been challenged by recent findings suggesting benefits from larger ratios, particularly for vision transformers.

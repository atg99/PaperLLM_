The paper presents an analysis of Type II coverage rates for various prediction intervals including out-of-bag (OOB), split conformal (SC), and quantile regression forest (QRF) across 60 datasets. Figure 5 illustrates boxplots that compare these coverage rates, with datasets ordered based on their average Type I coverage rates. This comparative approach allows for an assessment of the performance of each prediction interval method, highlighting differences in their effectiveness across the datasets examined. The findings suggest implications for the choice of prediction interval method in statistical modeling and forecasting.

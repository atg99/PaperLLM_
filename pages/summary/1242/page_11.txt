This paper investigates the theoretical and numerical properties of prediction intervals, focusing on four types of coverage probabilities: Type I (marginal coverage), Type II (conditional coverage given Cn), Type III (conditional coverage given X=x), and Type IV (conditional coverage given both Cn and X=x). Theorems are presented that establish asymptotic guarantees for out-of-bag (OOB) prediction intervals, with detailed proofs provided in the Supplemental Materials.

Theorem 1 specifically addresses Type II coverage, demonstrating that the 100(1-α)% OOB prediction interval achieves an asymptotically correct conditional coverage rate given a large training dataset. This result indicates that the probability of the OOB interval covering the true response value Y for a randomly drawn X approaches 1-α as the sample size increases.

In addition to the asymptotic results, the paper explores finite-sample properties of OOB prediction intervals in Section 5. It empirically approximates the conditional coverage probability by analyzing the proportion of OOB intervals that successfully cover Y across numerous independent (X, Y) draws from a distribution G, using various training datasets to assess how conditional coverage probability varies with the size of Cn.

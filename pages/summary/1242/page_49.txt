The paper presents an analysis of Type II coverage rate estimates for out-of-bag (OOB) prediction intervals, split conformal (SC) prediction intervals, and quantile regression forest (QRF) intervals across 20 datasets. The datasets include various subjects such as Mumps, Mussels, Naval Propulsion Plants, Optical Network, Ozone, Parkinsons, and others. The findings are illustrated through boxplots, which depict the empirical Type I coverage rates represented by circles. The results indicate the performance of different prediction interval methods in terms of coverage rates, providing insights into their efficacy across diverse applications. The data suggests that certain methods may yield more reliable predictions, which is crucial for practical implementations in statistical modeling and forecasting.

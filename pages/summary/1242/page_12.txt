Corollary 1 addresses Type I coverage, which refers to the marginal coverage probability as defined by Lei et al. (2018). This probability represents the likelihood that a prediction interval constructed from training data covers the true value Y when both the training data Cn and the pair (X,Y) are drawn from the underlying distribution G. The corollary emphasizes that as the sample size n approaches infinity, this marginal coverage probability converges to 1 minus the significance level alpha for any alpha in the interval (0,1).

Theorem 2 expands the analysis by conditioning on both the training data Cn and a fixed vector X=x. It establishes that the out-of-bag prediction interval maintains an asymptotically correct conditional coverage rate given Cn and X=x. This means that the probability of the prediction interval covering Y approaches 1 minus alpha as n increases, which is significant for researchers with large datasets seeking to predict Y based on specific values of X.

Finally, Corollary 2 reiterates the findings of Theorem 2, confirming that the conditional probability of the prediction interval covering Y, given X=x, converges to 1 minus alpha under the stated conditions. The finite-sample properties of these coverage rates will be explored in Section 5, focusing on specific values of x.

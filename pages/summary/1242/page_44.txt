The paper presents an analysis of various prediction intervals using different methods, focusing on their coverage rates under specific conditions. It compares out-of-bag (OOB) prediction intervals, split conformal (SC) prediction intervals, and quantile regression forest (QRF) intervals, particularly when predictors are uncorrelated and follow a normal distribution. The results are illustrated through boxplots that show the Type IV coverage rate estimates, which quantify the probability that the true outcome falls within the predicted intervals. The findings indicate the performance of these methods across sample sizes ranging from 200 to 5000, with each circle in the boxplots representing the average coverage estimates based on 200 trials. The study concludes by highlighting the effectiveness of these methods in different scenarios, underscoring their applicability in statistical modeling and prediction tasks.

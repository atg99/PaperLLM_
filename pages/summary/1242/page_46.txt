The paper discusses methods for estimating prediction intervals in statistical modeling, particularly focusing on the performance of out-of-bag (OOB) prediction intervals, split conformal (SC) prediction intervals, and quantile regression forest (QRF) intervals. It presents boxplots that illustrate the Type IV coverage rate estimates, which measure the accuracy of these prediction intervals under various conditions, including different sample sizes (n=200, 500, 1000, 2000, 5000) and predictor configurations (uncorrelated predictors). Each circle in the boxplots represents the average of 200 Type IV coverage estimates, providing insights into the reliability of the prediction intervals. The study highlights the importance of understanding coverage rates in assessing the performance of statistical models.

This section presents additional figures related to Section 6, specifically focusing on the performance of different prediction intervals across various datasets. The primary figure, Figure S.9, illustrates boxplots of Type II coverage rate estimates for three methods: out-of-bag (OOB) prediction intervals, split conformal (SC) prediction intervals, and quantile regression forest (QRF) intervals. The datasets analyzed include Abalone, Air Quality, Airfoil Self-Noise, Alcohol, and others, totaling 20 datasets.

The figure highlights the empirical Type I coverage rates represented by circles, providing a visual comparison of how well each method performs in terms of interval coverage across the selected datasets. This information is critical for understanding the effectiveness of these prediction methods in real-world applications.

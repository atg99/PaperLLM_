The paper presents an analysis of various prediction interval methods, specifically focusing on out-of-bag (OOB) prediction intervals, split conformal (SC) prediction intervals, and quantile regression forest (QRF) intervals across 20 different datasets. These datasets include Concrete Slump Test, Cps, CPU, Cycle Power Plant, Deer, Diabetes, Diamond, Edu, Energy Efficiency, Enroll, Facebook Metrics, Fame, Fat, Fishery, Hatco, Hydrodynamics, Insur, Istanbul Stock, Laheart, and Medicare.

The findings are illustrated through boxplots that display Type II coverage rate estimates for the different prediction methods. The empirical Type I coverage rates are marked with circles, providing a visual representation of the performance of each method. The results indicate a comparative analysis of the effectiveness of these prediction intervals in various contexts, emphasizing their applicability in real-world data scenarios.

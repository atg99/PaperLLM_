This paper discusses the evaluation of prediction intervals using different methods, including out-of-bag (OOB) prediction intervals, split conformal (SC) prediction intervals, and quantile regression forest (QRF) intervals. The analysis focuses on the Type IV coverage rate estimates, which assess how well these methods perform in providing accurate prediction intervals under various conditions such as sample size and the nature of the predictors.

The results are presented in boxplots that illustrate the coverage rates across different sample sizes (n=200, 500, 1000, 2000, and 5000). Each plot shows the average of 200 Type IV coverage estimates, with circles indicating estimates of Type III coverage rate under a specific configuration of predictors.

Key findings include the impact of predictor correlation on the performance of the prediction intervals, highlighting the differences in coverage rates among the methods evaluated. Overall, the paper provides insights into the effectiveness of different statistical techniques for generating reliable prediction intervals in the context of varying data characteristics.

The Supplemental Materials to the paper "Random Forest Prediction Intervals" by Haozhe Zhang, Joshua Zimmerman, Dan Nettleton, and Daniel J. Nordman include two main sections. Section S.1 presents proofs of distributional results concerning the coverage properties of out-of-bag prediction intervals, specifically addressing Theorem 1 and Corollary 1. Corollary 1 is derived from the convergence of conditional probability indicated in Theorem 1, along with the boundedness of this probability, leading to the conclusion that the expected value converges to 1 minus alpha.

In the proof of Theorem 1, several notations and technical lemmas (Lemmas 1-2) are introduced, with their proofs provided subsequently. The authors define the random vectors (X,Y) and (X1,Y1), and establish the context of the random forest (RF) estimator of the function m(X). The notation bY denotes the RF estimator, and for each i, bY(i) signifies the associated out-of-bag estimator. The conditions under which the estimators converge to the true function are also outlined.

Sections S.2 and S.3 include additional figures that supplement the numerical studies presented in the main manuscript, enhancing the understanding of the results discussed in Sections 5 and 6. Overall, the supplemental materials provide in-depth technical details and visual aids to support the findings of the primary research.

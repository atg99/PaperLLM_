The paper presents an analysis of the effects of tuning parameters on prediction intervals using the Concrete Strength dataset. It evaluates three methods: out-of-bag (OOB) prediction intervals, split conformal (SC) prediction intervals, and quantile regression forest (QRF) intervals. 

Figure 7 illustrates two key aspects: 

1. **Coverage Rate**: Boxplots show the Type II coverage rates for the different methods under various combinations of two tuning parameters—mtry and nodesize. The results indicate how effectively each method captures the true values within the prediction intervals.

2. **Interval Width**: Boxplots also depict the widths of the prediction intervals for the same methods and parameter combinations. A narrower interval width generally suggests more precise predictions, while wider intervals may indicate more uncertainty.

Overall, the findings emphasize the importance of selecting appropriate tuning parameters to optimize prediction performance across different methods.

The paper discusses various methods for generating prediction intervals using random forests, specifically focusing on conditional coverage and alternative approaches. Corollary 2 highlights a result related to Type III coverage, which pertains to the conditional coverage established by Meinshausen in 2006 for quantile regression forests. The conditional coverage probability in Corollary 2 can be derived from the expectation of the probability outlined in Theorem 2, based on the distribution of the training dataset.

The section on alternative random forest intervals introduces two existing methods for creating prediction intervals, which are compared with the proposed out-of-bag (OOB) intervals in subsequent sections. Notably, the paper claims that this comparison is unique in the literature. It also references recent approaches for generating confidence intervals for the conditional mean of Y given X=x.

One highlighted method is the split conformal prediction interval, which was introduced by Lei et al. in 2018 as a more computationally efficient alternative to the original conformal prediction framework proposed by Vovk et al. The split conformal method involves randomly dividing the dataset into two subsets, building a random forest on one subset to estimate the mean function, and subsequently constructing the prediction interval.

The section presents additional figures to support the findings discussed in Section 5, particularly focusing on the Type II coverage rate estimates for various prediction methods. Figure S.1 illustrates boxplots comparing the performance of out-of-bag (OOB) prediction intervals, split conformal (SC) prediction intervals, and quantile regression forest (QRF) intervals across different sample sizes (n=200, 500, 1000, 2000, 5000). The coverage rates are analyzed under the condition where the predictors are uncorrelated and normally distributed. Each circle in the boxplots represents the average of 200 Type II coverage estimates, providing insight into the effectiveness of each method in estimating the coverage rate. The figure emphasizes the importance of understanding how different methods perform under varying conditions, particularly in terms of interval coverage rates.

This study presents a novel approach to predicting PM 2.5 concentrations by incorporating a range of features, including geographic and climatic variables such as latitude, longitude, altitude, and dew point, alongside temporal factors like day of year and season. The model achieved a satisfactory performance with R2=0.79, MAE=10.20 µg/m3, and RMSE=14 µg/m3. However, it also identified limitations, notably the insignificant impact of satellite-derived aerosol optical depths (AODs) on predictions. The high rate of missing values in some AOD data, particularly AOD03, hindered its utility for the study area. The authors emphasize the necessity of historical PM 2.5 data for accurate predictions and suggest that future research will explore high-resolution images to enhance spatial distribution modeling.

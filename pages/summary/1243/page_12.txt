This section of the paper discusses the impact of feature permutation on the prediction performance of a well-trained Deep Neural Network (DNN) model and the application of Mean Absolute Error (MAE) based feature elimination using XGBoost.

In the feature permutation study, the authors trained the DNN model 23 times using different combinations of input features, starting with the most important feature, PM2.5_lag1. The model's performance was evaluated using the R2 value, which measures the proportion of variance explained by the model. The best performance was achieved with the 15 most important features, yielding an R2 of 0.776.

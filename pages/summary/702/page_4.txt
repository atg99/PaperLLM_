The paper presents a Deep Q-Network (DQN) based movement strategy for Unmanned Aerial Vehicle Base Stations (UAV-BSs) to optimize the data rates of ground users (UEs). The algorithm initializes a replay memory and two neural networks (the Q-network and a target network) to learn the action-value function of UAV-BS movements based on local observations of UEs and UAV-BSs.

The process consists of several components:

1. **State Representation**: The state is defined by the horizontal positions of the UAV-BSs, assuming they start at optimal positions for maximizing sum data rates.

2. **Environment**: The DQN adapts to constraints where each UE is assigned to the nearest base station, ensuring that rewards reflect these conditions.

3. **Action Space**: UAV-BSs can choose from five actions related to movement direction, with the assumption that all UAV-BSs move at the same speed and altitude.

4. **Reward Design**: The reward function encourages actions that increase the sum data rates of UEs. Positive rewards are given for improvements, while negative rewards are assigned for reductions in data rates or no change, with a lesser penalty for unchanged rates to account for the energy and resource cost of movement.

5. **Training Procedure**: The learning process involves episodes where UAV-BSs reset to initial positions, take actions based on Q-values, and receive rewards. The DQN uses experience replay for training, aiming to optimize the neural network parameters throughout the episodes.

Overall, the algorithm aims to enhance the efficiency of UAV-BS movements to maximize data rates for ground UEs through a systematic reinforcement learning approach.

The DQN approach utilizes a reinforcement learning framework where each agent leverages a Deep Q-Network to determine the optimal action based on the current state. The training process includes experience replay, where transitions are stored and sampled to enhance learning stability.

In the simulation, a 5000 m by 5000 m area was divided into four sections, with a majority of user equipment (UEs) converging to one section mid-simulation before returning to a uniform distribution. The simulation involves 500 time steps and 50,000 training episodes, with specific environment parameters tailored to an urban setting.

The results indicate that the processing time for the Deep Q-Network is significantly lower at 210 ms compared to 4117 ms for Exhaustive Search and 387 ms for K-Means, while a fixed algorithm shows no processing time. Additionally, the sum data rate improves with the number of training episodes, demonstrating the effectiveness of the DQN in optimizing UAV-BS actions over time. Overall, the study showcases the advantages of using Deep Q-Networks in managing UAV-BS associations in dynamic environments.

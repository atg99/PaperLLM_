The paper presents a framework for managing the dynamic movement of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) in UAV-assisted communication networks. The primary goal is to maximize the sum data rate for ground users, particularly in scenarios where traditional base stations are insufficient. The authors first analyze the relationship between air-to-ground (A2G) path loss and UAV positioning. They propose a deep Q-network (DQN) method to adjust UAV locations in real-time, responding to ground user mobility to enhance overall network quality of service (QoS).

The introduction highlights the increasing demand for high-quality wireless communication and the potential of UAVs as aerial base stations, especially in emergencies or in Internet of Things (IoT) applications. The paper addresses the challenge of developing effective movement strategies for UAVs to adapt to dynamic environments. The authors review existing literature on UAV deployment and movement strategies, noting the growing application of machine learning, particularly reinforcement learning, in this field.

The contribution of this paper is the development of a real-time movement strategy using DQN to optimize UAV positioning based on the mobility of ground users, contrasting with prior works that assumed fixed user locations. The simulations demonstrate the efficacy of the proposed approach in improving the QoS of the network.

This paper presents a dynamic deployment strategy for Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) Base Stations (BS) aimed at optimizing real-time wireless communication performance for moving User Equipments (UEs). The proposed Deep Q-Network (DQN) algorithm demonstrates superior performance compared to traditional fixed location and K-Means strategies, closely matching the performance of exhaustive search methods while significantly reducing computational resources and processing time. This reduction is essential for applications requiring low-latency communications and efficient mission execution involving UAVs.

The study reveals that while exhaustive search yields the highest performance, its computational complexity is impractical for real-time scenarios. The DQN approach balances performance and computational efficiency, making it a viable alternative for dynamic UAV-assisted networks.

Future research directions include exploring multi-agent reinforcement learning to enhance the interaction between multiple UAVs, thereby improving the overall deployment strategy. The references cited provide a foundation for understanding the context and advancements in UAV communication technologies and machine learning applications in this domain.

The paper presents a GRU (Gated Recurrent Unit) deep learning system designed to enhance security in Software Defined Networks (SDNs) by detecting and mitigating attacks, specifically focusing on intrusion and DDoS threats. The proposed system operates through a detailed analysis of individual IP flow features, utilizing MD5 hashing for exportation of flow data. It generates alarms based on protocol metrics such as bits per second and packets per second, as well as source and destination ports. The system includes a detection module that identifies anomalies and a mitigation module that implements a drop policy for malicious traffic. Overall, the research aims to improve the resilience of SDN controllers against cyber threats through advanced monitoring and response techniques.

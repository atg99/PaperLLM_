The paper discusses a modular organization of a proposed security system for Software-Defined Networking (SDN), composed of several key components including a Detection Module, a Mitigation Module, and a Training Module. The Detection Module focuses on improving anomaly detection performance by utilizing advanced Deep Learning techniques, particularly Gated Recurrent Units (GRU), which can analyze a wide range of flow features and automatically prioritize those most relevant to classification outcomes. This approach overcomes limitations of traditional methods that rely on a limited set of manually selected features.

The Mitigation Module plays a critical role in responding to detected anomalies by defining and executing optimal countermeasures to reduce the impact of attacks on the SDN. It consists of two sub-modules: one that determines the best countermeasure based on the detected anomaly, and another that communicates the optimal drop policy to the SDN controller for implementation. This system is capable of identifying specific attacker nodes by analyzing individual IP flows, allowing for precise discarding of malicious flows. The overall design aims to enhance the security and resilience of SDN against potential threats.

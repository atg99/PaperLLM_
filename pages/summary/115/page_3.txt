This paper presents a system designed to defend Software Defined Networks (SDNs) against intrusion and Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks. The authors propose an anomaly detection scheme that utilizes Gated Recurrent Units (GRU) for isolated flow analysis. An efficiency evaluation of various detection techniques applied to SDNs is also conducted. To ensure the reliability of the results, the study employs public datasets for performance analysis, allowing for replication of the findings.

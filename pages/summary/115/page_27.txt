The paper presents a comparative analysis of various machine learning methods in terms of accuracy, precision, recall, and f-measure rates across two test scenarios. In the second scenario, the average results indicate that the Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) outperformed other methods, demonstrating the most balanced performance across all metrics. Following GRU, Support Vector Machine (SVM), Convolutional Neural Network (CNN), and Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) also achieved commendable results. Conversely, methods such as k-Nearest Neighbors (kNN), Gradient Descent (GD), Logistic Regression (LR), and Deep Neural Networks (DNN) yielded lower performance.

The paper further evaluates the methods' effectiveness in classifying normal and attack flows. The results reveal that while most methods performed similarly in detecting attack flows, the classification of legitimate flows varied. Notably, GRU and LSTM excelled in accurately classifying normal flows. This comprehensive assessment underlines the strengths and weaknesses of each method in a practical application context.

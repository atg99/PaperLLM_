The paper presents a comparative analysis of various machine learning methods, including GRU, DNN, CNN, LSTM, SVM, LR, kNN, and GD, based on their performance metrics: accuracy, precision, recall, and f-measure. 

In the second test scenario, GRU achieved the highest accuracy at 97.1%, closely followed by CNN (96.5%), LSTM (96.4%), and SVM (96.6%), all exceeding 90%. For precision, CNN led with 99.9%, while GRU followed at 99.4%. LSTM and SVM had similar precision rates of 98.3% and 98.2%, respectively, with kNN, GD, LR, and DNN trailing behind. 

Regarding recall, SVM excelled with a rate of 95.1%, closely matched by LR and kNN at 95% and 94.9%. GD and GRU had similar recall rates of 94.8% and 94.7%, while LSTM scored slightly lower at 94.4%. CNN and DNN had lower recall rates of 93% and 90.4%. 

For the f-measure, GRU again performed best at 97%, followed by SVM (96.6%), CNN (96.4%), and LSTM (96.3%). kNN, GD, and LR achieved similar f-measure results, while DNN showed the least performance in this metric. Overall, the findings highlight the strengths of different methods across various evaluation metrics.

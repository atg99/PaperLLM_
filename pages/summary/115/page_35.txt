This paper discusses the integration of various learning approaches, particularly ensemble algorithms and Deep Reinforcement Learning, in the context of anomaly detection. Acknowledgments are given to several funding bodies, including Brazil's National Council for Scientific and Technological Development and the Ministry of Economy and Competitiveness in Spain, for their support in the research.

The references cite key works in the fields of deep learning and machine learning, highlighting their relevance to anomaly detection in network traffic. Notable contributions include surveys on deep learning for intrusion detection, studies on learning long-term dependencies, and methods for network anomaly detection based on entropy.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the importance of advancing intrusion detection systems through innovative learning techniques while acknowledging the support from various academic and governmental institutions.

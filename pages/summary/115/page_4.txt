The paper presents a Gated Recurrent Units (GRU) deep learning-based defense system designed to protect Software-defined Networks (SDN) from attacks, particularly distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) and intrusion attempts. As network environments grow increasingly complex due to the Internet of Things (IoT) and dynamic traffic from advanced applications, SDN offers a centralized management solution, but this centralization also introduces vulnerabilities. The proposed system utilizes single IP flow records for direct inspection, enabling rapid response to threats and minimizing their impact.

The authors evaluate the effectiveness of the GRU model against various machine learning techniques using two public datasets: CICDDoS 2019 and CICIDS 2018. They also introduce a lightweight mitigation strategy and assess its performance alongside other detection methods. A throughput test is conducted using real IP flow data from a large-scale network to measure the capability of each method in analyzing flows per second. The results demonstrate high detection rates and significant throughput, indicating that the GRU approach is a viable solution for enhancing SDN security.

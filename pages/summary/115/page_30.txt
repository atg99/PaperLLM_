The paper discusses the mitigation results from the CICDDoS 2019 and CICIDS 2018 datasets, focusing on the performance of various methods in detecting DDoS attacks while preserving legitimate traffic. The analysis reveals that the GRU method outperformed others in achieving a balanced outcome, effectively detecting malicious flows while minimizing the drop of legitimate ones. Although the kNN method dropped fewer legitimate flows, it was less effective in detecting DDoS intervals compared to GRU. The LSTM method showed a high mitigation rate for attack flows but was less efficient in identifying normal flows. 

Further analysis in Figure 15 indicates that GRU maintained its position as the most balanced method, successfully identifying the majority of legitimate flows and reducing the impact on regular users, despite a higher number of undetected malicious flows compared to other methods like SVM, LR, kNN, and GD.

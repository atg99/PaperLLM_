The paper discusses the performance evaluation of various mitigation approaches in Software-defined Networking (SDN), highlighting its advantages and vulnerabilities. Section 2 reviews related works, emphasizing the benefits of SDN in dynamic traffic allocation and centralized management, particularly in Wireless Sensor Networks (WSN). It cites the work of Theodorou and Mamatas (2017), which showcases the CORAL-SDN solution's capabilities.

However, the centralized nature of SDNs poses security risks, particularly concerning the controller's integrity. Tatang et al. (2017) introduced SDN-GUARD, which detects and mitigates rootkits through dual-view comparisons, achieving effective detection with minimal performance impact. Nam and Kim (2018) enhanced SDN security using the open-source IDS Suricata and OpenFlow for implementing security mechanisms. Gkountis et al. (2017) proposed a lightweight DDoS defense algorithm that outperformed traditional methods in protecting mobile user environments within SDNs. Sidki et al. (2016) focused on fault protection for SDN controllers through a redundancy approach, ensuring continuous network operation.

Anomaly detection is identified as a key strategy for attack detection in SDN environments, where establishing a baseline of normal network behavior allows for the identification of irregularities and the implementation of countermeasures. This section sets the stage for further discussions on performance evaluation and future works in the subsequent sections of the paper.

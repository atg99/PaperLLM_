The results presented in Fig. 8 highlight the performance of various methods in terms of accuracy, precision, recall, and f-measure. Most methods displayed impressive results, with metrics nearing 100%. Although the Deep Neural Network (DNN) performed the least well among the tested methods, it still achieved an accuracy rate of approximately 91%. The Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) outperformed DNN but had a less favorable visual representation compared to other approaches. The similar performance across the remaining techniques can be attributed to the nature of the attacks in this scenario, primarily consisting of flooding attacks, which the methods effectively identified.

Fig. 9 summarizes the average performance metrics for each technique, indicating that GRU, LSTM, SVM, LR, kNN, and GD showed equivalent efficiency, with minimal differences among them.

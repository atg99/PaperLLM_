The paper discusses the B-Profile System, which analyzes human communication behaviors in legitimate traffic across various protocols, including HTTP, FTP, and SSH, by simulating 25 users. It also examines the CICDDoS 2019 dataset, which is divided into a training day featuring 12 distinct DDoS attack types, such as DNS and MSSQL, and a testing day with 6 attack types like Syn and UDP.

The dataset comprises 87 extracted IP flow features, including source and destination IP addresses, ports, and various flags. However, only 83 features were utilized for training to mitigate data bias, excluding the "source and destination IP address," "source port," and "Flow ID" features. The "destination port" feature was retained due to its relevance in identifying attacks on specific servers. The paper also outlines a formatting process to convert qualitative features into quantitative ones.

Results from the first test scenario are presented, showcasing individual performance metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, and F-measure for various methods, including GRU, DNN, CNN, LSTM, SVM, LR, and kNN.

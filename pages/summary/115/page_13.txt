The paper presents a software-defined networking (SDN) security system designed to detect and mitigate attacks in real-time. It emphasizes the importance of efficient detection and response mechanisms, which are crucial for maintaining network integrity. The proposed approach utilizes a directed mitigation strategy that eliminates the need for complex probabilistic drop estimations, thereby reducing computational costs. Instead, when an abnormal flow is identified, the system generates a specific drop policy targeting the attacker's IP address, utilizing information from the detection module, including source and destination IP addresses and ports. This targeted policy is quickly implemented by the SDN controller, ensuring that the effectiveness of the mitigation is closely linked to the detection method's efficiency.

2020). Among the various threats, Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks pose a significant risk, overwhelming the central controller and disrupting network services. 

2. Proposed Solution
To address these vulnerabilities, the paper proposes a solution leveraging Gated Recurrent Units (GRUs) within a deep learning framework for intrusion detection in SDN environments. GRUs are a type of recurrent neural network that effectively captures sequential dependencies in data, making them suitable for identifying patterns indicative of DDoS attacks. By training the model on historical network traffic data, it can learn to distinguish between normal and malicious behavior.

3. Methodology
The proposed approach involves collecting network traffic data and preprocessing it for model training. The GRU model is then trained to recognize attack patterns, with performance evaluated through metrics such as accuracy, precision, and recall. The effectiveness of the model is validated against various attack scenarios to ensure robustness.

4. Results and Discussion
Preliminary results demonstrate that the GRU-based model outperforms traditional intrusion detection systems in terms of detection accuracy and response time. The model's ability to adapt to changing network conditions and its scalability make it a promising solution for enhancing the security of SDN architectures.

5. Conclusion
The integration of deep learning techniques, specifically GRUs, into intrusion detection systems presents a viable method for safeguarding SDN environments against DDoS attacks. This approach not only improves detection capabilities but also supports the dynamic nature of modern networks, ensuring continued operation amid increasing threats. Future work will focus on optimizing the model and expanding its application across various network scenarios.

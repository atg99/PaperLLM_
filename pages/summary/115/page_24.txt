The analysis focused on evaluating the effectiveness of various methods in classifying normal and attack flows, as shown in Figure 10. The results indicate that most methods performed similarly in detecting attack flows, but there were notable differences in classifying normal flows. kNN and GRU led the classification of legitimate flows with accuracy rates of 99.7% and 99.6%, respectively. They were followed by SVM, GD, LR, LSTM, CNN, and DNN, which achieved specificity rates of 99.1%, 99.1%, 99.0%, 98.6%, 96.1%, and 93.6%. While the differences among these rates appear minor, they become more pronounced with larger network sizes, impacting the efficiency of mitigation efforts.

The paper presents StyTr2, a novel transformer-based approach for image style transfer, aiming to enhance the preservation of global information while rendering content images with artistic styles. Traditional convolutional neural networks (CNNs) face challenges in maintaining long-range dependencies, leading to biased content representation in style transfer. StyTr2 addresses this by incorporating two distinct transformer encoders that generate domain-specific sequences for content and style, followed by a multi-layer transformer decoder that stylizes the content sequence based on the style sequence.

The authors also critique existing positional encoding methods and introduce a content-aware positional encoding (CAPE) that is scale-invariant and better suited for style transfer tasks. Through qualitative and quantitative experiments, StyTr2 demonstrates superior performance compared to state-of-the-art CNN-based and flow-based methods. The paper emphasizes the limitations of CNNs in capturing long-range dependencies and the resulting loss of fine details, which can negatively impact the quality of stylization. The proposed method is shown to effectively mitigate these issues, providing more accurate and detailed stylized images. The code and models are available on GitHub for further exploration.

The paper presents a novel framework called StyTr2 for image style transfer, which integrates a content transformer encoder and a style transformer encoder to effectively capture long-range domain-specific information. A transformer decoder is employed to translate content sequences based on reference style sequences. The study introduces a content-aware positional encoding scheme that is semantics-aware, enhancing the suitability for scale-invariant visual generation tasks. 

User studies were conducted to compare StyTr2 with existing methods on three criteria: overall quality, content preservation, and style consistency. Despite its advantages, the method currently does not match the speed of some CNN-based techniques, highlighting a potential area for future work by incorporating CNN priors to enhance computational efficiency.

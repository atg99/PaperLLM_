The manuscript presents a comparative analysis of the amino acid composition between Clathrin and non-Clathrin sequences. The findings are illustrated in a figure that quantifies the presence of various amino acids, including Alanine, Arginine, Asparagine, Aspartic acid, Cysteine, Glutamine, Glutamic acid, Glycine, Histidine, Isoleucine, Leucine, Lysine, Methionine, Phenylalanine, Proline, Serine, Threonine, Tryptophan, Tyrosine, and Valine. The data suggests distinct differences in the composition of these sequences, which may have implications for their respective biological functions.

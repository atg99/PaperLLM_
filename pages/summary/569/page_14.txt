The study utilized a convolutional neural network (CNN) structure with specific filter numbers, achieving a cross-validation sensitivity of 90%, specificity of 86.6%, and a Matthew's Correlation Coefficient (MCC) of 0.77. Various optimizers were tested, including rmsprop, adam, nadam, sgd, and adadelta, with Adam being selected for the final model due to its consistent performance. Training was halted at the 80th epoch to mitigate overfitting, as validation accuracy plateaued. Hyperparameters were subsequently tuned, and optimal values were documented. The model was validated on an independent test set comprising 258 clathrins and 227 non-clathrins, all unseen during training. Results indicated an accuracy of 91.8%, sensitivity of 92.2%, specificity of 91.2%, and an MCC of 0.83, confirming the model's robustness and effectiveness in classifying unseen data.

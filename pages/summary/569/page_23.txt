The manuscript presents a study focused on identifying Clathrin proteins through the application of 2D convolutional neural networks. Key figures illustrate various aspects of the research: 

Figure 1 depicts the flowchart detailing the identification process of Clathrin proteins. Figure 2 analyzes the amino acid composition of both Clathrin and non-Clathrin sequences, highlighting differences. Figure 3 presents the training and validation accuracy achieved with different optimizers over 150 epochs, demonstrating the effectiveness of the models. Figure 4 features confusion matrices for both a cross-validation test and an independent test, providing insights into the model's performance. Finally, Figure 5 lists important features derived from F-score analysis, showcasing the significance of specific amino acids in relation to their positions. 

Overall, the study contributes valuable information on the use of neural networks for protein identification, emphasizing the role of amino acid composition and model optimization.

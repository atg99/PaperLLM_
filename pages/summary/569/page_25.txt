The manuscript presents performance results for identifying Clathrins using various filter configurations in a neural network model. The study includes cross-validation and independent testing metrics such as sensitivity, specificity, accuracy, and Matthews correlation coefficient (MCC) across different filter numbers. The findings indicate that the optimal filter configuration of 32-64-128-256 yielded a sensitivity of 91% and specificity of 84.9% during cross-validation, with an accuracy of 88.1% and an MCC of 0.76. 

Additionally, the paper outlines the optimal hyperparameters for the study, including 80 epochs, a learning rate of 0.001, a batch size of 10, a kernel size of 3, a dropout rate of 0.2, and the Adam optimizer.

The comparative performance of a 2D CNN model is also evaluated against other shallow neural networks, demonstrating superior results. The 2D CNN achieved a sensitivity of 91.7% and specificity of 91.6% with an accuracy of 91.7% and an MCC of 0.83 during cross-validation, outperforming models such as kNN, RandomForest, SVM, MLP, and 1D CNN in both cross-validation and independent testing scenarios.

This paper presents an efficient model for identifying clathrin proteins using deep learning techniques. The authors transform Position Specific Scoring Matrix (PSSM) profiles into matrices to serve as input for 2D Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs). Their model was evaluated through 10-fold cross-validation and an independent testing dataset, demonstrating superior performance compared to other state-of-the-art neural networks across various measurement metrics. The findings indicate that the model can accurately identify new clathrin proteins, which may be beneficial for drug development. Furthermore, the study emphasizes the potential of 2D CNNs in biochemical research, particularly in predicting protein functions. Future work is suggested to enhance data retrieval, integrate comprehensive PSSM information into CNNs, and develop a web server for the prediction method.

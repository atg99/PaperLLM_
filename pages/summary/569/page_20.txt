The references provided in the manuscript cover a range of studies related to clathrin and its role in cellular processes, particularly in the context of Alzheimer's disease. Key themes include the mechanisms of clathrin-mediated endocytosis, the characterization of clathrin assembly proteins, and the implications of these processes in neurobiology. Notable works discuss the recruitment of clathrin onto endosomes, the identification of clathrin-binding domains in assembly proteins, and the cellular functions of clathrin in various pathways. The references also highlight advancements in methodologies for studying clathrin-coated vesicles and the significance of clathrin-related proteins in cellular dynamics. Overall, these studies collectively enhance the understanding of clathrin's function and its relevance to neurodegenerative diseases.

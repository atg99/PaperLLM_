The study highlights the gap in the existing literature regarding the application of machine learning techniques, specifically deep learning, to identify clathrin proteins. By leveraging a 2D convolutional neural network constructed from position-specific scoring matrix profiles, the research aims to enhance the identification process and provide insights into the molecular functions of clathrin proteins. The proposed model demonstrates superior performance compared to traditional machine learning methods, marking a significant contribution to computational biology. Additionally, the creation of a valid benchmark dataset facilitates further investigations and applications in the study of clathrin proteins, ultimately aiding biologists in their research endeavors.

Additionally, the paper highlights the importance of hyperparameters, which are architecture-level parameters distinct from those learned through backpropagation. Key hyperparameters influencing model performance include the number of convolutional layers, filters per layer, epochs, dropout rate, and optimizers. The tuning of these hyperparameters is crucial for enhancing training efficiency and preventing overfitting. Following Chollet's recommendations, the hyperparameter-tuning process involves selecting a set of hyperparameters and building the corresponding model.

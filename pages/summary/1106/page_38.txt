The paper provides an overview of the sparsebn package, which is designed for learning large-scale Bayesian networks. It references several key works in the field, including algorithms for Lasso penalized regression and methods for model selection in regression with grouped variables. The authors highlight the importance of graphical models and propose advancements in learning sparse Bayesian network structures, particularly for continuous variables. The research emphasizes the integration of systems approaches to identify genetic networks related to conditions such as Alzheimer's disease. Key methodologies discussed include coordinate descent algorithms, the A* Lasso approach, and concave penalized estimation techniques. The paper is published in the Journal of Statistical Software and is affiliated with UCLA's Department of Statistics. The work was submitted in March 2017 and accepted in March 2018.

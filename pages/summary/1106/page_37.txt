The references listed highlight significant contributions to the fields of causal inference, graphical models, and network analysis. Key papers include Sachs et al. (2005), which discusses causal protein-signaling networks derived from single-cell data, and Scutari (2010, 2017), which focuses on learning Bayesian networks using the bnlearn R package. Tibshirani (1996) introduces the Lasso method for regression shrinkage and selection, while Spirtes and Glymour (1991, 2000) provide foundational algorithms for recovering sparse causal graphs. Additionally, Uhler et al. (2013) examine the geometry of the faithfulness assumption in causal inference, and Van de Geer and Bühlmann (2013) propose penalized maximum likelihood methods for sparse directed acyclic graphs. These works collectively advance the understanding and implementation of statistical methods in various applications, particularly in operational risk modeling and biomolecular interaction networks.

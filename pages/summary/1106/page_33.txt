Buntine (1991) discusses theory refinement in Bayesian networks, highlighting foundational principles in uncertainty modeling. Butts (2008) introduces the 'network' package for relational data management in R, enhancing data handling capabilities in statistical analysis.

Chen et al. (2015) present a Bayesian network meta-analysis comparing treatment modalities for nasopharyngeal carcinoma, showcasing the practical application of Bayesian methods in medical research. Chickering and Heckerman (1997, 2004) contribute to the understanding of approximations for marginal likelihoods and the NP-hardness of learning Bayesian networks, respectively, addressing computational challenges in the field.

Colombo et al. (2012) explore learning directed acyclic graphs with latent variables, which is crucial for understanding complex data structures. Cooper and Herskovits (1992) introduce a Bayesian method for inducing probabilistic networks from data, while Cooper and Yoo (1999) focus on causal discovery methods.

Csardi and Nepusz (2006) present the igraph package for complex network research, facilitating the analysis of network structures. Cussens et al. (2017) delve into the polyhedral aspects of score equivalence in Bayesian network structure learning, contributing to theoretical advancements in the field. 

Dejaeger et al. (2013) aim to enhance software fault prediction models using Bayesian network classifiers, emphasizing the practical implications of these statistical methods. Lastly, Dobson and Barnett (2008) provide an introduction to generalized linear models, serving as a foundational text for understanding statistical modeling techniques. Eddelbuettel (2013) discusses seamless integration of R and C++, which is vital for improving computational efficiency in statistical software development. 

Overall, these works collectively advance the understanding and application of statistical methods in various domains, particularly in complex data analysis and biological research.

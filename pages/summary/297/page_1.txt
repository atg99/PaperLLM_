The study investigates the effects of different spatial attention factors on performance, specifically focusing on the query and key content, query content relative to position, key content alone, and relative position alone. The authors find that in the context of self-attention in Transformers, the comparison of query and key content is less significant, while it plays a crucial role in encoder-decoder attention. Additionally, combining deformable convolution with key content saliency leads to optimal accuracy and efficiency in self-attention tasks.

The findings challenge traditional views on the importance of certain attention factors, indicating that there is considerable scope for enhancing attention mechanism designs. The paper ultimately contributes to a deeper understanding of how spatial attention can be optimized in deep networks, suggesting new avenues for future research and development in the field.

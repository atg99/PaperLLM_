This paper presents a novel curriculum learning strategy called reverse scheduled sampling, aimed at enhancing the predictive learning capabilities of the PredRNN model by enabling it to learn temporal dynamics from extended periods of context frames. The proposed approach demonstrates superior performance on various spatiotemporal datasets, covering both action-free and action-conditioned predictive learning scenarios. The research acknowledges support from multiple foundations, highlighting contributions from several authors, particularly emphasizing the equal contributions of Y. Wang and H. Wu. 

The references cited include a range of foundational works in the fields of machine learning and neural networks, underscoring the evolution of techniques for spatiotemporal prediction, video classification, and sequence learning, among others. The study situates itself within this broader context of advancing predictive modeling through innovative neural network architectures and training methodologies.

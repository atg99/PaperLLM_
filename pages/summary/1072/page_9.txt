They also discuss the optimal approximation of piecewise smooth functions using deep ReLU networks, emphasizing the effectiveness of deep learning models in complex function representation.

Yarotsky provides insights into error bounds for deep ReLU networks, reinforcing the theoretical underpinnings of network performance.

Zhou's works focus on the universality of deep convolutional networks and their distributed approximation capabilities, indicating a significant advancement in understanding how these networks can effectively approximate a wide range of functions. Collectively, these studies underscore the growing body of research on the theoretical foundations and practical applications of deep learning technologies.

The findings underscore the effectiveness of deep learning techniques, particularly GRU networks, in time series forecasting. The research highlights the advantages of GRU over LSTM in terms of simplicity and performance, making it suitable for various dataset sizes and resampling rates. The experiments confirm that a well-optimized GRU model can predict the future performance of industrial processes, achieving a mean absolute percentage error (MAPE) of under 10% for a 30-day forecast. These results emphasize the growing relevance of deep neural networks in predictive analytics, particularly in industrial applications.

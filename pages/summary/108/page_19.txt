This paper presents a comprehensive overview of various predictive maintenance methodologies relevant to Industry 4.0, emphasizing the integration of data-driven decision-making techniques. It discusses several machine learning approaches, including neural networks, long short-term memory networks, and autoregressive integrated moving average models, highlighting their application in maintenance predictions. Key performance metrics such as mean absolute error, mean absolute percentage error, and root mean square error are considered to evaluate the effectiveness of these methodologies. The paper also references a variety of studies that explore different aspects of predictive maintenance, from intelligent sensors to decision support systems, demonstrating the breadth of research in this evolving field. The use of abbreviations throughout the manuscript, such as AF for activation function and RNN for recurrent neural network, aids in clarity and conciseness. Overall, the paper underscores the significance of machine learning in enhancing predictive maintenance strategies within industrial settings.

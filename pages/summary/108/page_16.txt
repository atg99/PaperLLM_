The paper presents a comparative analysis of the Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) for various activation function combinations in a model predicting six variables: Concentration, Intensity, Hydraulic, Torque, Pressure, Velocity, and Temperature. Two tables summarize the average RMSE results obtained after applying different smoothing methods. 

Table 6 details RMSE values calculated after smoothing with a median filter, revealing that the ReLU-Tanh combination generally yields the lowest RMSE across most variables, although the Sigmoid function performs best for Temperature. 

Table 7 provides RMSE values after using an average clean method with the Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) model. Here, the ReLU-Tanh combination again shows strong performance, particularly for Concentration, Hydraulic, and Torque, while the ReLU-Sigmoid combination also exhibits competitive results.

Figure 19 illustrates various prediction lines from the test set, demonstrating the variability in performance across different activation functions. Overall, while the ReLU-Tanh combination tends to perform best, specific cases like Temperature highlight the advantages of alternative functions.

The study compares the performance of Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) and Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) models in predicting future sensor values with a 30-day lead time, utilizing a window of past values. Experiments were conducted on a computer with an i5 processor and 8 GB RAM. Previous research indicated that LSTM achieved Mean Absolute Percentage Errors (MAPE) of 2.17% for current intensity, 2.71% for hydraulic unit oil level, 2.50% for torque, 7.65% for VAT pressure, 16.88% for velocity, and 3.06% for temperature, based on a 10-day window with two samples per day.

The study involved testing various network architectures and hyperparameters for both LSTM and GRU models, which included an encoding layer, a hidden layer of variable lengths, and an output layer. The models were implemented using Python with TensorFlow and Keras, employing a batch size of 16 for training. Default values from TensorFlow and Keras were used for other hyperparameters when not specified. The internal architectures of the LSTM and GRU units are depicted in accompanying figures.

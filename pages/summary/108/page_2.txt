This paper discusses the evolution of forecasting algorithms, highlighting a shift from traditional time series models like exponential smoothing and SARIMA to modern artificial intelligence methods. The rise of machine learning techniques, particularly artificial neural networks (ANNs), has enhanced predictive maintenance and production systems by enabling effective data analysis and pattern recognition. The study focuses on comparing the performance of Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks and Gated Recurrent Units (GRUs) in predicting the future behavior of an industrial paper pulp press.

The paper is structured into several sections: Section 2 reviews related literature on predictive maintenance and the role of machine learning in monitoring physical assets, emphasizing the importance of predictive maintenance for safety and efficiency. Section 3 explains the theoretical foundations of LSTM and GRU networks, including error calculation methods. Section 4 outlines the dataset cleaning methods and sample behaviors, while Section 6 presents tests, results, and model validation. Section 7 discusses the findings in relation to existing research, and Section 8 concludes with insights and recommendations for future work.

The literature review highlights the effectiveness of ANNs, particularly in electrical energy applications, and underscores the importance of high-quality datasets for accurate predictions. It also discusses the strategic decision-making involved in implementing predictive maintenance programs, referencing studies that demonstrate the potential benefits of neural networks in this field.

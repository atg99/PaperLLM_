In this appendix, the focus is on the marginalization of the internal state vector sequence h1:T in the context of a Deterministic Variational Autoencoder (DVAE) model, specifically within the STORN framework. The goal is to express ht as a deterministic function of other random variables, transitioning from a detailed model representation to a more compact form. 

The notation is simplified to ht = fh(xt−1, zt, ht−1), and the joint distribution of all variables is articulated as pθ(x1:T, h1:T, z1:T) = ∏Tt=1 pθ(xt|ht)pθ(ht|xt−1, zt, ht−1)p(zt). Since ht is determined by xt−1, zt, and ht−1, its conditional density takes the form of a Dirac distribution, expressed as pθ(ht|xt−1, zt, ht−1) = δ(ht; fh(xt−1, zt, ht−1)). 

Furthermore, the sequence d1:T is defined as a deterministic function of x1:T and z1:T, indicating that at each time step, ht can be recursively expressed as dt = ht(x1:t−1, z1:t) = fh(xt−1, zt, fh(xt−2, zt−1,...). This approach illustrates the relationship between the internal state and the observed variables, enabling a more efficient model representation.

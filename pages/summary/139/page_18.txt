The paper aims to provide a comprehensive review of the DVAE class of models, highlighting their applications in unsupervised speech enhancement and other complex tasks. The authors present a comparison of three DVAE models, contributing to the understanding of their performance and potential. They have made available the code for seven DVAE models, ensuring it aligns with the unified presentation and notation of the paper, thus serving as a pedagogical resource for the community.

The discussion includes the challenges and future improvements for VAEs and DVAEs, with a focus on the difficulties in comparing models across different studies due to varied notations and missing information. The authors hope that their review, along with the accompanying code, will facilitate easier access to the technical aspects of DVAE models and their relationships with classical models. The paper concludes by inviting readers to engage with the content and explore the DVAE landscape.

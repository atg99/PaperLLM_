The Kalman Variational Autoencoder (KVAE) model, introduced by Fraccaro et al. in 2017, is a variant of the Deep Kalman Filter (DKF) model, classified as a deep state space model (SSM). The KVAE incorporates an additional random variable, denoted as at, which is positioned between the latent vector zt and the observed vector xt. This structure allows for the separation of the model into two distinct components: a deep feature extractor that connects at and xt, and a dynamical model governing zt with respect to the new observations at. This design endows the KVAE with advantageous properties for both inference and training.

The paper focuses on a significant subset of temporal Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), specifically detailing seven recently proposed Dynamic Variational Autoencoder (DVAE) models. The authors aim to standardize notation and presentation while connecting these models to traditional temporal models. They have reimplemented the seven DVAE models and conducted an experimental benchmark on the speech analysis-resynthesis task, with the PyTorch code made publicly available. The conclusion addresses critical issues related to the DVAE class of models and outlines future research directions.

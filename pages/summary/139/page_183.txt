This document presents a collection of references pertinent to advancements in machine learning and signal processing, emphasizing methodologies such as matrix and tensor factorization, human sensing, and variational autoencoders. 

Huang et al. (2016) propose a versatile algorithmic framework for constrained matrix and tensor factorization, enhancing efficiency in signal processing. Ionescu et al. (2014) discuss the Human3.6M dataset, focusing on predictive methods for 3D human sensing in natural environments. Jang et al. (2019) introduce a recurrent neural network-based semantic variational autoencoder aimed at sequence-to-sequence learning. 

Jiang et al. (2020) present the Transformer VAE, a hierarchical model designed for structure-aware and interpretable music representation learning. Johnson et al. (2016) explore the integration of graphical models with neural networks to achieve structured representations and rapid inference. 

Jordan et al. (1999) provide foundational insights into variational methods for graphical models, while Kameoka et al. (2018) investigate semi-blind source separation through a multichannel variational autoencoder. Lastly, Karl et al. (2017) delve into deep variational Bayes filters for unsupervised learning of state space models, and Kim and Mnih (2018) discuss disentangling by factorizing in the context of machine learning. 

These references collectively contribute to the understanding and development of advanced machine learning techniques and their applications.

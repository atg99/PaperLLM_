Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) and State Space Models (SSMs) are foundational components in the development of Deep Variational Autoencoders (DVAEs). This chapter introduces the fundamental principles of RNNs and SSMs, which are crucial for understanding DVAEs, though an extensive overview of their applications is outside the scope of this paper.

RNNs are particularly effective for modeling and generating sequences of data, as well as for sequence-to-sequence tasks. They operate by processing ordered vector sequences and utilizing a memory mechanism that retains information from past inputs and outputs to influence current outputs. This is accomplished through an additional vector that encodes the internal state of the network recursively. The notation used includes a sequence of vectors indexed over a specified range, and it is assumed that if the starting index exceeds the ending index, the sequence is empty. RNNs are framed within the context of nonlinear systems, highlighting their versatility and importance in temporal modeling.

The results highlight that the best-performing models, SRNN, VRNN, and STORN, achieve significantly lower MPJPE values compared to their nonautoregressive counterparts, demonstrating their ability to better represent complex sequential patterns.

Additionally, the study confirms that all DVAE models surpass the basic VAE model, underscoring the advantages of using DVAEs for sequential data modeling. The analysis-resynthesis results suggest that the models can effectively reconstruct human motion, with MPJPE scores ranging from approximately 7.86 mm to 48.69 mm, indicating a satisfactory level of accuracy in resynthesizing the motion data. Overall, these findings emphasize the superiority of autoregressive DVAEs in the context of 3D human motion analysis.

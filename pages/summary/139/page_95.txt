The paper discusses Recurrent Variational Autoencoders (RVAE), focusing on the noncausal variant introduced by Leglaive et al. (2020). It presents the graphical model of RVAE in both developed and compact forms, highlighting the relationship between observed data and latent variables. The model's fundamental expression is given as the joint probability of observed sequences and latent variables. Unlike previous causal Deep Variational Autoencoder (DVAE) models, this noncausal approach utilizes both forward and backward Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) for generating latent representations. Key equations detail the forward and backward processes, showing how the hidden states are combined and how they influence the generation of observations. Ultimately, the hidden state is defined as a function of the entire sequence of latent variables.

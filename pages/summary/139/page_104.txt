The paper discusses the graphical model of the Deep Stochastic Autoencoder (DSAE) during inference, highlighting its structure in both developed and compact forms. It illustrates the relationships between various variables, such as the latent variables and observed data points, using arrows to denote probabilistic dependencies. The authors note that DSAE uniquely incorporates probabilistic dependencies that are not present in the exact posterior distribution, which distinguishes it from other models. The inclusion of both valid and invalid arrows emphasizes the model's flexibility and complexity in capturing relationships among variables.

Dynamical VAEs maintain the foundational principles of the original VAE framework while integrating temporal modeling to capture dependencies over time. This adaptation is crucial for effectively learning from data that inherently involves sequences, such as time series or video data. The reviewed literature highlights various approaches that successfully implement these concepts, showcasing the versatility and effectiveness of DVAEs in handling complex data structures.

Overall, the transition from traditional VAEs to dynamical VAEs reflects an important step in the development of deep Bayesian networks, enabling researchers to better uncover latent structures that govern dynamic processes in sequential data. The term "dynamical VAEs" is adopted to clarify this integrated approach and its practical implications in the field.

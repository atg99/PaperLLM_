The paper presents an evaluation of various Deep Variational Autoencoder (DVAE) models in a speech analysis-resynthesis experiment, focusing on their performance metrics: SI-SDR, PESQ, and ESTOI. The results, summarized in Table 13.1, indicate that models such as SRNN, VRNN, and DKF achieve the highest SI-SDR scores, with SRNN reaching 11.0 dB, indicating superior performance in reconstructing speech signals. PESQ and ESTOI scores also reflect good perceptual quality and intelligibility across models, with scores consistently above 0.90 for intelligibility.

Figures included in the paper illustrate the power spectrogram of a speech signal and the corresponding spectrograms produced by the various models. The analysis-resynthesis portion of the results shows that the reconstructed spectrograms closely resemble the original, albeit with some smoothing typical of data compression, while still retaining essential speech characteristics. Notably, all DVAE models significantly outperform the standard VAE model, highlighting the advancements made with DVAE approaches in speech processing tasks.

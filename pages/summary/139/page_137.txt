In this section, the paper presents an analysis of the latent vector behavior for a test sequence from the H3.6M dataset, specifically focusing on the SRNN model trained with teacher-forcing. Four key aspects are illustrated: (a) the mean value of the posterior distribution, (b) the log-variance of the posterior distribution, (c) the sampled latent vector, and (d) the KL divergence term of the variational lower bound (VLB). 

The findings suggest that both the Variational Recurrent Neural Network (VRNN) and the Stochastic Recurrent Neural Network (SRNN) are effective for modeling temporal data such as speech spectrograms and 3D human motion, particularly when autoregressive models are employed in teacher-forcing mode. The authors emphasize the significance of having an inference model that adheres to the precise variable dependencies during inference to achieve optimal performance. However, they acknowledge that some applications may necessitate a causal inference model for real-time processing. The paper concludes by highlighting that in scenarios where only the latent variable sequence needs to be transmitted, data resynthesis can effectively occur from this latent sequence using an autoregressive model in generation mode.

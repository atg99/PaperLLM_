The section focuses on the training of causal Recurrent Variational Autoencoders (RVAEs) and presents the Variational Lower Bound (VLB) specific to this model. The causal RVAE assumes certain conditional independence relationships, simplifying the model comparison with Dynamic VAEs (DVAEs). Specifically, it posits that the distribution of the current observation given past observations and latent variables depends only on the latent variables, and that the distribution of the current latent variable is independent of past observations. 

The VLB is expressed in a simplified form involving expectations of log probabilities and Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence. The KL divergence is computable analytically, while the intractable expectations are estimated using Monte Carlo methods through recursive sampling from the inferred distributions. This methodology ensures that the causal RVAE maintains a consistent approach to approximating the posterior distribution, allowing for effective training.

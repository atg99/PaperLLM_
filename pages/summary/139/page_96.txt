The paper discusses the inference model within the context of a noncausal Random Variational Autoencoder (RVAE). It begins by presenting the joint distribution of the complete data sequence and latent variables, expressed in two forms. The relationship between the observed data and latent variables is established through the chain rule and D-separation, leading to the formulation of the posterior distribution of latent vectors.

In the inference model, the posterior distribution is defined for both causal and noncausal cases, with the distinction that in the causal model, the latent vector at a time step is influenced by past latent vectors and both present and future observations. In contrast, the noncausal model includes dependencies on past observations as well.

The variational distribution is similarly defined for both cases, maintaining the same structure. The paper also outlines the implementation of the inference model for the causal case, which utilizes a forward Recurrent Neural Network (RNN) on the latent variables, though specific details of this implementation are not included for brevity.

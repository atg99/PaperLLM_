introduction outlines the structure and objectives of the paper, focusing on Deep Variational Autoencoders (DVAEs). It emphasizes the importance of notation clarity to bridge connections with foundational models such as Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs), and State-Space Models (SSMs). The paper reviews seven selected DVAE models in detail while also providing a broader overview of additional DVAE models in Chapter 12. 

Chapter 2 discusses VAEs extensively, as they underpin all subsequent DVAE models. Chapter 3 introduces RNNs and SSMs, highlighting their relevance to temporal models within the VAE framework. A unified notation is employed to aid readers from diverse fields, such as machine learning and control theory, in understanding these relationships.

The paper presents a quantitative benchmark of the reviewed DVAE models through analysis-resynthesis tasks and qualitative data generation examples, evaluated on speech signals and 3D human motion datasets in Chapter 13. The challenges of comparing model performance are acknowledged, including variations in evaluation data, model tuning, and experimental setups. It notes that a comprehensive benchmark for DVAE models is lacking in the literature, making future evaluations complex due to the numerous configurations and tasks involved. Additionally, the paper raises questions about how to assess the "disentanglement" of the latent space in these models.

The section on training the Kalman Variational Autoencoders (KVAE) presents the Variational Lower Bound (VLB) for the model. The VLB is expressed in terms of the expectation of the log-likelihood of the observations given the latent variables and the Kullback-Leibler divergence between the approximate posterior and the prior distribution. The equations detail the process of calculating the VLB by incorporating the generative and inference models, highlighting the decomposition of both components.

To train the KVAE, one begins by sampling from the approximate posterior qφ(a1:T|x1:T) for each time step. These samples are then utilized in a standard Kalman smoother to compute the distribution pθ(z1:T|a1:T,u1:T). This method facilitates the joint learning of the parameters of the Variational Autoencoder, encompassing both the encoder and decoder, alongside those of the Linear Gaussian Latent Dynamical System (LG-LDS). The approach ensures a cohesive training framework for effectively modeling the underlying dynamics of the data.

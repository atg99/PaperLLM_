The section discusses the integration of state space models (SSMs) with neural networks, leading to the development of Deep Kalman Filters (DKFs) and deep Markov models (DMMs). While the combination of these concepts is not new, recent studies by Krishnan et al. have explored it within the framework of variational autoencoders (VAEs). The significance of these papers lies in their provision of a solution to the dual challenge of inference and model parameter estimation in the context of VAEs applied to SSM architectures. This work represents the first instance of unsupervised training of a deep SSM through the linkage of an approximate inference model with a generative model, employing variational lower bound (VLB) maximization. This approach facilitates the unsupervised discovery of a latent space that captures temporal dynamics. The authors note that while both papers utilize the same generative model, the latter introduces important enhancements to the inference model, prompting a name change from DKF to DMM for broader applicability. However, the review maintains the use of the term DKF.

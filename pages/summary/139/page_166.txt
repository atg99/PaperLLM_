In this section, the paper details the specifications of the Deep Variational Autoencoder (DVAE) models utilized in experiments with speech data. Each DVAE model is described in terms of its generation network (decoder) and inference network (encoder). The paper introduces a notation for Multi-Layer Perceptrons (MLPs), denoting an L-layer MLP as MLP(y, n1, f1, ..., nL, fL), where y is the input, n represents the output dimensions, and f indicates the activation functions for each layer. The activation functions include ReLU, Sigmoid, hyperbolic tangent (Tanh), and linear, with the linear function specifically reserved for the last layer that computes parameters for random variables. For instance, the generative network for zt is illustrated as MLP(ht, 64, Sigmoid, 32, Sigmoid), indicating an MLP with two hidden layers of sizes 64 and 32, and an output layer that produces mean and log-variance vectors with linear activation.

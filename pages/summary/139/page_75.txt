The paper discusses Stochastic Recurrent Networks (STORN) and presents two key equations that express the joint distribution of observed and latent variables in terms of free random variables. The first equation outlines the joint probability distribution pθ(x1:T, z1:T) and emphasizes the generality of this formulation while acknowledging the loss of information regarding the deterministic relationship between past observed states and latent variables during the generation of current states.

The paper also introduces the inference model, providing the exact posterior distribution of STORN, expressed as pθ(z1:T|x1:T). This expression is derived using the chain rule and highlights that each term in the posterior distribution at time t is influenced by both past observed and latent states, as well as future observed states. The relationship between these variables is illustrated through a graphical representation.

Furthermore, the paper notes the proportional relationship between the posterior distribution and the joint distribution of observed and latent variables, allowing for a reformulation that incorporates the probabilistic dependencies of the observed data on the latent states. This establishes a foundation for understanding the dynamics of STORN and its inference capabilities.

In this section, the results from speech data analysis using different models, specifically STORN, SRNN, and VRNN, are discussed. Figure 13.4 illustrates the speech power spectrograms reconstructed from 0-2 seconds and generated from 2-4 seconds by a DVAE model, with the original spectrogram referenced in Figure 13.2. The transition between reconstruction and generation is marked by a red line. The models were trained and evaluated with varying Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) parameters, specifically a window length of 512 points and a 50% overlap, highlighting the robustness of the findings concerning audio parameterization. The performance of the models is compared, revealing that while STORN and SRNN show similar results, they are slightly below those of VRNN. This observation is noteworthy given that DKF and DSAE are structured as state-space models without explicit parameters.

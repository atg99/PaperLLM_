This section outlines the aims, contributions, and structure of the paper, which focuses on various models of Deep Variational Autoencoders (DVAEs). The paper reviews seven specific models, including STORN, VRNN, SRNN, RVAE, and DSAE, each detailed in subsequent chapters. 

The authors emphasize the importance of a consistent presentation format across all models, starting with the generative equations both in time-step form and for entire data sequences. They also highlight the structure of the exact posterior distribution of latent variables and the inference model as originally proposed in the respective papers. The review addresses gaps often found in original literature due to space constraints.

Additionally, the paper discusses the similarities and differences among the selected DVAE models, including the authors’ choices regarding inference models and their relation to the exact posterior distribution. The focus is on high-level implementation issues related to the overall structure of the neural networks used, rather than low-level practical implementation details.

Efforts have been made to standardize notations across all models, including changes in time indexing and variable names where necessary. This consistency aids in highlighting the commonalities and differences among the models, thereby facilitating easier comparison.

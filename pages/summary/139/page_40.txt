Dynamical Variational Autoencoders (DVAEs) are defined as a framework that incorporates both observed and latent random vectors in a temporally correlated manner. Unlike static VAEs, DVAEs account for the complexities of dependencies over time, much like State Space Models (SSMs). The generative model of a DVAE involves establishing the joint distribution of the observed sequences x1:T and latent sequences z1:T, parameterized by deep neural networks (DNNs). This section aims to provide a foundation for understanding the various models that fall under the DVAE category, highlighting their similarities and differences, and paving the way for future advancements in this area. The methodology outlined here serves as a comprehensive approach to defining and training DVAEs.

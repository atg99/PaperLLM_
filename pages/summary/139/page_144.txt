This paper emphasizes the flexibility of deep variational autoencoders (DVAEs) in modeling sequential data, highlighting the importance of both high-level architecture choices and low-level implementation details. The choice of network type, such as LSTM or GRU, and specific hyperparameters can significantly influence modeling performance and should be tailored to the nature of the dataset.

The review contrasts RNNs, which allow for infinite-order temporal modeling, with convolutional architectures like temporal convolutional networks (TCNs) and transformers. TCNs offer a competitive alternative to RNNs, providing flexibility and efficiency through dilated convolutions, while transformers leverage attention mechanisms, foregoing recurrence and convolutions. The paper suggests a growing interest in these alternative architectures for sequential data modeling, indicating a shift towards more efficient and scalable approaches in deep learning applications.

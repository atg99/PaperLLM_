The paper discusses advancements in Variational Recurrent Neural Networks (VRNNs), building on previous work by Krishnan et al. and Fraccaro et al. The authors introduce a backward recurrent neural network (RNN) to enhance the inference of the approximate posterior distribution by incorporating future observations, thus aligning more closely with the true posterior distribution. 

They also modify the latent variable representation by linking it to the internal state of the backward inference network. This adjustment includes additional conditional models, which improve the latent variable's ability to encapsulate future sequence information. Notably, the direct connection between the latent variable and the output was removed, leading to improved performance.

Further applications of VRNNs are explored, such as in automatic language translation, where Su et al. present a model that encodes sequences of discrete inputs and outputs, contrasting with traditional models that utilize a single latent vector. Additionally, Lee et al. apply VRNN to speech synthesis with adversarial training. Overall, the paper highlights the versatility of the DVAE model class in various domains.

This review will delve into the characteristics, advantages, and applications of prescribed models within the domain of DGMs. We will explore how these models leverage deep learning techniques to enhance traditional probabilistic modeling, providing insights into their structure and functionality. By examining various methodologies and their implications, we aim to highlight the significance of explicit generative models in advancing machine learning and signal processing fields. Through this analysis, we hope to contribute to the ongoing discourse surrounding the effectiveness and adaptability of DGMs in diverse applications.

The paper discusses the Kalman Variational Autoencoders (KVAE) and their inference model, particularly focusing on the posterior distribution of latent variables given observed variables. The posterior distribution is factorized into two components: the distribution of latent states given the features and the distribution of features given the observed data. The first component can be computed using a Kalman filter or smoother, which is established to depend only on specific parameters. The second component, however, presents challenges as the exact posterior distribution is not explicitly defined in previous works. Instead, the authors propose a factorized inference model that incorporates the Kalman solution, allowing for a structured approach to estimate the latent variables. This model employs a fully-connected deep neural network to parameterize the relationships between the observed features and the latent variables, thus facilitating the application of KVAEs in various scenarios.

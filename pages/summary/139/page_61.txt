The paper discusses the structure of the posterior distribution of the latent state zt in Deep Kalman Filters, which relies on the previous state zt−1 and the current and future observations xt:T and inputs ut:T. This indicates a first-order Markovian causal process for zt and an anticausal process for xt and ut. Krishnan et al. (2015) proposed four models for the approximate posterior qφ, which are:

1. An instantaneous model: qφ(zt|xt,ut) using a multi-layer perceptron (MLP).
2. A model with local past and future context: qφ(zt|xt−1:t+1,ut−1:t+1) also using an MLP.
3. A model with complete past context: qφ(zt|x1:t,u1:t) parameterized by a recurrent neural network (RNN).
4. A model with the entire sequence: qφ(zt|x1:T,u1:T) parameterized by a bidirectional RNN.

The paper notes a potential oversight in Krishnan et al.'s work regarding the absence of zt−1 in the conditional variables of the approximate posterior. While the authors acknowledged the dependency of the exact posterior on present and future observations, they did not provide a corresponding approximate model. Additionally, a notation inconsistency is highlighted, where Krishnan et al. used ut−1 for inputs at time t, in contrast to the conventional usage of ut. The paper suggests that this may be a simple notation error, as the definitions of the approximate posterior models align with the standard notation.

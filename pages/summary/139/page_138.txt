In the conclusion, the paper discusses the performance of the SRNN model when fine-tuned in generation mode using scheduled sampling, indicating robust results with speech data. However, the model's effectiveness for 3D motion data was limited, showing only marginal improvement over the standard Variational Autoencoder (VAE). Additionally, the performance of other models, such as the Deep Kalman Filter (DKF), was also found to vary based on data type. The authors emphasize that the ranking of models is specific to the experiments conducted, which focused on the analysis-resynthesis of speech spectrograms and 3D human motion data. They acknowledge the challenges in evaluating generated data quality, highlighting it as an ongoing issue in the field of machine learning with generative models. The paper also hints at potential applications in signal and data transformation through modifications of latent vectors.

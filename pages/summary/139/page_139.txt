The paper discusses the limitations of the experimental results related to the analysis and resynthesis process. It highlights uncertainties regarding the generalizability of findings, particularly concerning the encoding of information from the input data into latent representations. The authors point out that the experiments did not explore the disentanglement capabilities of the models or how the disentangled dimensions relate to relevant factors of variation in the data from a physical perspective. Additionally, while the paper acknowledges the issue of posterior collapse, it notes that a comprehensive quantitative analysis of this problem is not included. These topics will be addressed in the subsequent chapter, but further experiments related to them are outside the current study's scope.

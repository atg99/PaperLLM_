The paper discusses Variational Recurrent Neural Networks (VRNN), focusing on their graphical model and generative process. It highlights the notation used in Chung et al.'s 2015 paper, clarifying the parameters associated with feature extractors and the internal state representation. The authors propose a distinction among parameters to avoid confusion and emphasize the synchronization of states in the model. 

In the VRNN framework, the generative process begins with an initial state h1, leading to the generation of latent variables z1 and observations x1. Subsequent states ht, latent variables zt, and observations xt are determined based on previous states and observations. The authors employ the "unfolding the recurrence" technique to express the relationships among these variables, allowing for an implicit temporal model in the generation of both zt and xt. The graphical representation of the VRNN illustrates these connections and the dependencies among the variables in the model.

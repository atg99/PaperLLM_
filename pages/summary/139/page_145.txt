This section of the paper discusses the integration of transformers into the Variational Autoencoder (VAE) framework, highlighting their recent applications in sentence generation, story completion, and music representation learning. It emphasizes the ongoing development of Deep Variational Autoencoders (DVAEs) and suggests that improvements can be drawn from existing VAE literature, particularly the works of Kingma and Welling, and Rezende et al.

The authors note that while many enhancements to VAEs have been proposed, their focus is on identifying relevant adaptations for DVAEs rather than providing an exhaustive review. They encourage further exploration of VAE advancements, referencing Kingma and Welling’s 2019 paper for detailed insights.

The discussion then shifts to the challenges faced when applying VAEs to multi-dimensional data, such as images or variable-length sequences. The authors point out that the original VAE framework primarily addressed 1D data vectors, which limits its effectiveness in capturing relationships in higher-dimensional data. They critique the pixelwise independence assumption in image modeling, indicating that it fails to leverage the correlations among neighboring pixels effectively. This sets the stage for exploring more sophisticated models that can enhance the capabilities of DVAEs.

The paper presents a detailed overview of six different deep variational autoencoder (DVAE) models, specifically DKF, STORN, VRNN, SRNN, RVAE, and DSAE. Each model architecture is depicted in a diagram that illustrates the relationships between various components such as recurrent neural networks (RNNs), multilayer perceptrons (MLPs), and sampling mechanisms.

For DKF, the architecture includes a combiner function (CF) and a gated transition function (GTF), which are elaborated in the appendix and referenced from Krishnan et al.'s 2015 work. STORN is presented with a focus on its sample generation process. VRNN and SRNN share common modules for both the encoder and decoder, represented by gray-shaded boxes in their architectural diagrams.

The models utilize various probabilistic functions to capture the relationships between latent variables and observed data, facilitating the generation and reconstruction of sequences. Each architecture incorporates MLPs and RNNs to process inputs and outputs effectively, highlighting the complexity and versatility of DVAE models in handling sequential data.

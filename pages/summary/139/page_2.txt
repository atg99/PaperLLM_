This paper provides a comprehensive review of dynamical variational autoencoders (DVAEs), which are extensions of traditional variational autoencoders (VAEs) designed to handle sequential data. The authors discuss how standard VAEs process input data independently and highlight the limitations of this approach in capturing temporal dependencies within sequences. DVAEs address these limitations by incorporating recurrent neural networks or state-space models to model both the latent space and the temporal dynamics of data vectors. The review categorizes various DVAEs and examines their methodologies, applications, and advancements in the field, thus offering insights into the evolving landscape of deep generative models for sequential data. The paper serves as a foundational reference for researchers interested in the intersection of VAEs and temporal data processing.

In their 2018 paper, Li and Mandt introduced the Disentangled Sequential Autoencoder (DSAE), a hierarchical model that incorporates a sequence-level latent vector, denoted as v, alongside the conventional sequence of latent variables z1:T. The vector v is designed to capture the overall characteristics of the data, while zt focuses on time-dependent features, such as the dynamics of objects in video clips. 

The generative model of DSAE is articulated through a general formulation that outlines the joint probability of the observed data sequence and the latent variables, expressed as pθ(x1:T,z1:T,v). This formula comprises the probability of the sequence-level latent vector, the conditional probabilities of the data given the latent variables, and the prior probabilities of the latent variables. The authors tailored their model variants to suit different datasets, such as video clips and speech signals, with further details on the probability density functions and implementation provided in the annexes of the ArXiv version of the paper.

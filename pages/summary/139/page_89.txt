The paper discusses Stochastic Recurrent Neural Networks (SRNN) and their inference process, represented through a series of equations. The model uses a basic feed-forward network parameterized by φz to define the distribution qφ(z1:T|x1:T), which is expressed as the product of conditional distributions qφ(zt|zt−1,← −gt) for each time step t. The variable ← −gt is derived from a function involving both the internal state ht and input xt, indicating that ht is dependent on the previous inputs.

The equations illustrate the structure of the inference process, highlighting the need for both forward and backward passes through the recurrent neural network. This dual pass allows the model to create a deterministic function ← −gt based on the entire data sequence x1:T. The inference model can also be reformulated to emphasize the relationship between zt and the entire input sequence, making the process more flexible.

The authors note that the smoothing process inherent in the SRNN can be substituted with a filtering process by utilizing an "instantaneous" deep neural network, which simplifies the relationships between the variables involved. This approach, as proposed by Fraccaro et al. in 2016, enhances the model's efficiency while maintaining its core inferential capabilities.

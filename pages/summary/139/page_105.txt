The paper discusses the framework of Disentangled Sequential Autoencoders, focusing on the inference model represented by equation (11.11). The objective function L(θ,φ,x1:T) is derived, which includes the expected log likelihood of the observations given latent variables and the Kullback-Leibler divergence terms. The formula emphasizes the need to compute the distribution qφ(v|x1:T) to facilitate sampling, which is crucial for determining the parameters of qφ(z_t|v,x1:T) across all time steps. This sequential computation allows for the evaluation of the KL divergence terms, which are integral to the model's optimization process. The approach highlights the importance of disentangling the latent variables for effective inference in sequential data.

The graphical representation of STORN highlights the relationships between past and future observations and latent states. Notably, the internal states of the encoder and decoder can differ based on the chosen inference model, which is a key aspect of STORN's architecture.

In the training section, the paper contrasts the compact form of STORN with the general structure of a Deep Variational Autoencoder (DVAE). A significant assumption made by STORN is the conditional independence of the latent state given past observations and latent states. This simplification leads to a modified Variational Lower Bound (VLB) expression that maintains consistency with the exact posterior distribution. The VLB is critical for training the model, as it captures the relationship between the observed data and the latent variables while incorporating the Kullback-Leibler divergence to measure the difference between the approximate and true posterior distributions.

Overall, STORN presents a refined approach to recurrent modeling, emphasizing the importance of inference structure and conditional independence, which impacts both the architecture and training process of the model.

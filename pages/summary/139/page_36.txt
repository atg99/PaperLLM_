In this section, the paper discusses State Space Models (SSMs) and their relationship to General Regression Neural Networks (GRNNs). It introduces functions dz and dx, parameterized by θz and θx, respectively, forming a complete generative model denoted as θ=θx∪θz. The internal state vector zt is stochastic, contrasting with the deterministic internal state ht of GRNNs. The state model follows a first-order Markov process, where zt is dependent on the previous state zt−1 and the input ut.

The paper emphasizes the notation choice for inputs, aligning it so that ut generates zt, which subsequently generates xt. This alignment aids in maintaining consistency across models. The joint distribution of all variables is expressed as pθ(x1:T,z1:T,u1:T), revealing the conditional dependencies among the variables, leading to the equations for the conditional distributions pθx and pθz. Overall, this section underscores the SSM's structure and its adaptation from GRNNs by incorporating stochastic elements.

The flexibility of DVAEs allows for the incorporation of various inference architectures, which can be tailored to better align with the underlying generative model's structure. This adaptability is critical because it enables the model to capture complex dependencies in the data more effectively.

The paper highlights that while DVAEs simplify certain aspects of model complexity by allowing for independent calculations of latent variables over time, they also open avenues for constructing more sophisticated models, provided computational resources are available. The ability to transition between different types of dynamical models, whether linear or nonlinear, and to adjust the order of temporal modeling, showcases the DVAE's versatility.

In conclusion, the DVAE framework presents a significant opportunity for enhancing generative modeling through its flexibility in both the generative and inference models. The careful design of the inference model in accordance with the generative model's structure is vital for achieving optimal performance, emphasizing the importance of adhering to the consistency principle to avoid pitfalls seen in prior research.

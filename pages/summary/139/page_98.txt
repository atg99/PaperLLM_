The section describes the inference model for a noncausal Recurrent Variational Autoencoder (RVAE), illustrating the relationships between latent variables and observations at inference time. The model captures bidirectional dependencies among latent variables (zt) and observations (xt), represented by equations that define the transformations and distributions involved. 

Key equations include the conditional dependencies for latent variables gz and gx, which are expressed in terms of preceding and succeeding time steps, establishing a framework for inference. The model generates mean and variance parameters (µφ and σφ) for the latent distribution qφ(zt|z1:t−1,x1:T), which is modeled as a Gaussian distribution. The overall inference process combines these relationships to compute the joint distribution over the latent variables given the observations.

The paper discusses the Variational Recurrent Neural Network (VRNN) model introduced by Chung et al. in 2015, which integrates a Variational Autoencoder (VAE) with a Recurrent Neural Network (RNN). 

The generative model of the VRNN is outlined through a series of equations. The observation model is defined by the hidden state update equation, which incorporates previous observations and hidden states. Specifically, equations (8.1) and (8.2) describe how the hidden state ht and the parameters for the generative distribution of the latent variable zt are computed using feature extractors, denoted as ϕz and ϕx, which are deep neural networks parameterized by weights and biases.

Equations (8.3) and (8.5) specify the generative distributions for the observations xt and the latent variables zt, respectively, following a normal distribution characterized by means µ and standard deviations σ derived from the hidden states. The importance of the feature extractors in the model is emphasized, as they play a crucial role in effectively capturing the underlying data distributions.

This section discusses various models that relate to STORN, VRNN, and SRNN, focusing on deep variational Bayesian filters (DVBFs) and disentangled state-space models (DSSMs). DVBFs, introduced by Karl et al. in 2017, extend traditional state-space models (SSMs) by incorporating dynamical models that rely on stochastic parameters. An example includes a linear-Gaussian transition model where the matrices and vectors are determined by a weighted sum of randomly selected components, influenced by a deep neural network.

DSSMs, proposed by Miladinović et al. in 2019, aim to separate the encoding of sequence-level content from its dynamics. Unlike the disentangled state autoencoder (DSAE), where the sequence-level latent variable affects the observation model, in DSSMs, it conditions the dynamical model, reflecting that object dynamics vary with the application domain. The authors present a filtering inference model within the DVBF framework, enhancing the understanding of dynamical behaviors in specific contexts.

Additionally, the variational recurrent autoencoder (VRAE) is noted as a simplified version of STORN, providing a foundation for further exploration of these advanced models in variational inference and recurrent architectures.

The references listed encompass a variety of research contributions in the fields of neural networks, generative models, and machine learning. Key works include Serban et al.'s exploration of piecewise latent variables for text processing and their hierarchical latent variable encoder-decoder model for dialogue generation. Shang et al. investigate channel-recurrent autoencoding for image modeling, while Siddharth et al. focus on learning disentangled representations via semi-supervised deep generative models. The variational Bayes method in signal processing is discussed by Šmídl and Quinn. Additionally, Sohn et al. and Sønderby et al. present advancements in deep conditional generative models and variational autoencoders, respectively. Lastly, Su et al. address variational recurrent neural machine translation, and Sutskever provides insights into training recurrent neural networks. These works collectively contribute to the understanding and advancement of generative modeling techniques and their applications in various domains.

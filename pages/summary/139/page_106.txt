In this section, the paper briefly discusses models related to the Dynamic Kalman Filter (DKF) that belong to the DVAE family. The focus is on models that link sequences of observations with sequences of latent variables, while avoiding in-depth details due to their divergence from the review's scope or similarities to previously discussed models.

One highlighted model is the latent Linear Dynamical System (LDS) proposed by Johnson et al. (2016). This model simplifies the DKF by employing a linear-Gaussian latent variable model for the dynamical aspect, while the observation model utilizes a Deep Neural Network (DNN) similar to that of the DKF. Additionally, they introduced a latent switching LDS model that incorporates a bank of dynamical models with varying parameters, controlled by a discrete latent variable that enables switching between these models over time based on the dynamics of the observed data.

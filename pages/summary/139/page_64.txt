In section 5.3, the paper discusses the training of the Dynamic Kalman Filter (DKF) model during inference. Figure 5.1 illustrates the graphical model used at inference time, depicting the relationships between latent variables and observed data across different time steps. The left side of the figure shows the developed form of the inference model qφ(zt|zt−1,x1:T), while the right side presents a compact form. The specific Dynamic Kalman Smoothing (DKS) model is derived from the inference model by eliminating the forward recurrent neural network (RNN) components, represented by the blue arrows in the right schema. This adjustment allows the DKS model to align perfectly with the exact posterior distribution.

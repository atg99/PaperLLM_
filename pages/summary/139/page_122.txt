The section presents results from the analysis-resynthesis of speech data using various autoregressive models, specifically STORN, VRNN, and SRNN. The evaluation metrics employed include the Scale-Invariant Signal-to-Distortion Ratio (SI-SDR), the Perceptual Evaluation of Speech Quality (PESQ), and the Extended Short-Time Objective Intelligibility (ESTOI). Higher values in these metrics indicate better performance, and they were applied to time-domain speech waveforms after reconstructing the magnitude and phase spectrograms.

The results, averaged over the test dataset, show that all autoregressive models achieved reasonable signal reconstruction, with SI-SDR values ranging from 6.9 to 11 dB. This outcome aligns with the compression of 513-dimensional data vectors into a 16-dimensional latent vector, supplemented by a 128-dimensional RNN internal state. The reconstructed spectrogram quality is visually represented in accompanying figures. Further discussions on generation mode results will follow in a subsequent section.

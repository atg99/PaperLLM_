In this section, the results of a study on speech data using various models, including a vanilla Variational Autoencoder (VAE), are presented. Figure 13.2 illustrates a power spectrogram of a speech signal from a female speaker, comparing the original signal with its reconstruction and generation through the VAE. The reconstructed spectrogram shows some noise and blurriness in harmonics, highlighting the advantages of incorporating temporal modeling in the VAE framework for sequential data like speech.

Among the tested models, the Structured Recurrent Neural Network (SRNN) demonstrated the best performance, followed by the Variable Recurrent Neural Network (VRNN). The complexity of the dependencies between observed and latent variables in SRNN and VRNN contributes to their superior ability to capture the temporal structure of speech signals. While SRNN slightly outperforms VRNN by 1dB in Signal-to-Distortion Ratio (SI-SDR), VRNN exhibits richer variable dependencies. This performance difference may stem from SRNN's inference model aligning more closely with the exact posterior distribution compared to VRNN's approach.

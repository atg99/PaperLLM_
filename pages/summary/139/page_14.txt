The paper discusses Dynamical Variational Autoencoders (DVAE) and their characteristics, emphasizing that they are trained using the VAE methodology with some adaptations. The focus is on continuous latent variables, excluding discrete latent variable cases. DVAE models are structured to handle discrete-time sequences of observed and latent random vectors, operating in a sequence-to-sequence manner for both encoding and decoding. The paper does not cover GAN-based models or specific applications for text, dialogue, or 2D image generation, which have distinct encoder-decoder architectures.

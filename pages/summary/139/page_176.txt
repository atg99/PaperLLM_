The references listed include significant contributions to the field of machine learning and neural networks. 

Abadi et al. introduce TensorFlow, a framework for large-scale machine learning across distributed systems. Aksan and Hilliges present the Stochastic Temporal Convolutional Networks (STCN) at the ICLR, focusing on temporal data modeling. Andrychowicz et al. discuss a novel approach to meta-learning through gradient descent in their NeurIPS paper. Archer et al. explore variational inference for state space models, providing a black box method for inference. Babaeizadeh et al. contribute to video prediction techniques with their stochastic variational approach at the ICLR. Lastly, Bai, Kolter, and Koltun empirically evaluate convolutional and recurrent networks for sequence modeling, offering insights into their performance. 

These works collectively advance understanding and techniques in machine learning, particularly in areas like variational inference, sequence modeling, and video prediction.

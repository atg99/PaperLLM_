The paper presents the Variational Lower Bound (VLB) for Dynamic Variational Autoencoders (DVAEs) in a comprehensive form applicable to all causal DVAE models. The VLB is expressed as a function of parameters θ and φ, incorporating both a reconstruction accuracy term and a regularization term. Unlike standard VAEs, where regularization terms often have analytical forms, DVAEs require Monte Carlo estimates for both terms due to their dependence on samples from the posterior distribution qφ(z1:τ|x1:T,u1:T).

The method involves recursively sampling from the joint distribution qφ(z1:τ|x1:T,u1:T) using the chain rule, allowing for straightforward sampling of the random vector zt at each time step, which is defined by the chosen inference model (e.g., Gaussian distribution). The paper emphasizes the use of a reparameterization trick akin to that used in standard VAEs, ensuring that the VLB estimator remains differentiable with respect to φ.

The VLB can be maximized with respect to both φ and θ using gradient ascent algorithms, with the flexibility for φ and θ to share parameters, which is a distinction from traditional VAEs. Although the VLB is defined for a single data sequence, it is common practice to average the VLB across a mini-batch of sequences for model parameter updates. This formulation marks a significant advancement in the understanding and implementation of DVAEs.

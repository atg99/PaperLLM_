The paper discusses the formulation of the Variational Lower Bound (VLB) for Stochastic Recurrent Neural Networks. The general expression for VLB is presented, highlighting two main components: the expected log likelihood of the observations given the latent variables and the KL divergence term. The first component involves the expectation of the log probability of the observations conditioned on both past observations and the latent variables. The second component accounts for the divergence between the approximate posterior and the prior distribution of the latent variables, which can be computed analytically. In cases where expectations are intractable, Monte Carlo estimates are used. The sampling from the approximate posteriors employs the "cascade trick," similar to the approach used in Deep Kalman Filters (DKF), as referenced in Section 5.3.

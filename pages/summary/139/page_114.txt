The paper discusses various models for data processing, focusing on the Gaussian Process Variational Autoencoder (GP-VAE) introduced by Fortuin et al. in 2020. This model integrates a Variational Autoencoder (VAE) for dimensionality reduction of observed data with a multivariate Gaussian process (GP) to capture the dynamics of the latent vector over time. Utilizing a Cauchy kernel, the GP is adept at modeling multiscale temporal dynamics. The authors propose an approximate posterior distribution as a first-order multivariate GP, enabling the model to be trained using VAE techniques. A notable advantage of the GP-VAE is its capacity to recover missing data efficiently in various contexts, such as videos and medical data, while also providing interpretable uncertainty estimates.

The paper discusses the performance of different models in reconstructing and generating speech power spectrograms using a DVAE framework. It compares three models: DKF, DSAE, and noncausal RVAE, highlighting their effectiveness through visual examples presented in Figure 13.3. The models reconstruct the spectrogram from 0-2 seconds and generate it from 2-4 seconds, with a clear transition marked by a red line. The analysis indicates that while both DKF and DSAE yield similar performance scores, the inference model of VRNN is limited as it only considers causal observations up to time t, unlike the other models which utilize all observations from the sequence.

The graphical model representation of the SRNN at inference time highlights the probabilistic dependencies and the deterministic updates of the hidden states. The authors clarify the structure by comparing the SRNN's compact form with that of a Deep Variational Autoencoder (DVAE), emphasizing the conditional independence assumptions made by the SRNN model. 

Specifically, the assumptions state that the probability of the current observation xt given past observations and latent variables only depends on the most recent latent variable zt and past observations, rather than all previous latent variables. Similarly, the latent variable zt is assumed to depend only on the previous latent variable zt-1 and past observations. These simplifications facilitate the inference process while maintaining alignment with the exact posterior distribution. The section ultimately underscores the efficiency and clarity of the SRNN framework in modeling sequential data.

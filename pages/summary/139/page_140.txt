DVAEs, or Dynamic Variational Autoencoders, are designed to integrate various dynamical models for sequential data with Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) for modeling latent factors. This combination aims to effectively separate data dynamics from other variation factors, enhancing model expressivity. The chapter discusses the flexibility of DVAEs in terms of both the design of generation and inference models, as well as their implementation. A key focus is on the disentanglement of latent factors, particularly in the context of sequential data processing. The discussion concludes with perspectives on data source coding, highlighting the advantages of DVAEs over traditional recurrent neural networks (RNNs) by introducing latent variables that enhance modeling capabilities.

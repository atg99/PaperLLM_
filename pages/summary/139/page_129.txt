The experiments revealed that when the autoregressive models, including SRNN, were trained using scheduled sampling, there was a noticeable improvement in the quality of the generated output compared to traditional teacher-forcing methods. The generated spectrograms demonstrated a more natural speech structure and variability, which is essential for realistic voice synthesis. 

The analysis highlighted that while the RVAE model could recreate temporal structures in the generated spectrograms, it struggled with harmonic clarity and frequency distribution. In contrast, models like VRNN excelled in producing segments that resembled phonemes with smooth transitions, showcasing a clearer harmonic structure and broader frequency coverage. Overall, the study emphasizes the importance of training methodologies in enhancing the capabilities of autoregressive models for speech generation. The findings suggest that using scheduled sampling can significantly contribute to the generation of high-quality speech spectrograms.

The paper discusses the training process for a model referred to as STORN, which operates under a specific inference model. It simplifies the variational lower bound (VLB) to express the relationship between the latent variables and observed data. The VLB is defined as a combination of expected log likelihood and Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence components. While the KL divergence can be computed analytically, the expectation term is intractable and requires approximation through Monte Carlo sampling. This sampling is performed recursively based on the inference model. The use of the reparameterization trick in this context allows for the formulation of a differentiable objective function with respect to the model parameters, facilitating optimization during training.

Appendices are supplementary materials at the end of a document that provide additional information to support the main text. They can include data sets, detailed explanations, charts, graphs, or any other relevant content that is too lengthy or detailed to be included in the main body. The purpose of appendices is to enhance the reader's understanding without disrupting the flow of the main argument. Each appendix should be clearly labeled and referenced in the main text to guide readers on where to find the supplementary information.

The paper presents a comparative analysis of various object detection models, focusing on their backbone networks, region proposal methods, and mean Average Precision (mAP) scores across different datasets. Key models discussed include STDN, which utilizes a scale transfer module for feature resizing, and ReﬁneDet, which employs a cascade approach to enhance anchor quality and feature improvement through convolutional layers. PANet introduces an additional bottom-up path for better information flow between layers, achieving high rankings in COCO 2017 tasks. DetNet incorporates dilated convolutions to preserve resolution in deeper layers, while FPR fuses features across different spatial locations and scales. M2Det is noted for its innovative top-down path that helps in constructing a feature pyramid for object detection. Each model's performance metrics are provided, highlighting advancements in detection accuracy and network architecture.

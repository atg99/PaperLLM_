The references listed in this document highlight significant advancements in the field of computer vision, particularly focusing on object detection and image classification using deep learning techniques. Key contributions include the development of deep residual networks, which have surpassed human-level performance on ImageNet classification, and various architectures such as DenseNet and SqueezeNet that optimize model efficiency while maintaining accuracy.

Research by He et al. has been pivotal, introducing methods like bounding box regression with uncertainty and end-to-end text spotting with explicit alignment and attention. Hinton's work on knowledge distillation and dimensionality reduction via neural networks has also influenced the field significantly.

Several publications explore innovative approaches to object detection, including learning non-maximum suppression, few-shot detection, and the use of spatial transformer networks. The emergence of datasets like OpenImages and ILSVRC has facilitated large-scale training and evaluation of models, while techniques such as batch normalization and efficient convolutional networks have accelerated deep network training.

Overall, this collection of references underscores the rapid evolution of methodologies in computer vision, emphasizing the integration of deep learning for enhanced image recognition and object detection capabilities.

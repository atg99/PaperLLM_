The paper presents a comprehensive overview of commonly used metrics for evaluating object detectors, summarized in Table 5. Key metrics include True Positives (TP), False Positives (FP), confidence thresholds (β), and Intersection over Union (IOU) thresholds used in various datasets like VOC, ILSVRC, and MS COCO. Precision (P(β)) and Recall (R(β)) are defined as fractions of correct detections relative to total detections and actual objects, respectively. Average Precision (AP) is calculated over varying recall levels, while mean Average Precision (mAP) is averaged across classes. Additional metrics for small, medium, and large objects are also discussed.

The paper further delves into region-based (two-stage) frameworks for object detection, where category-independent region proposals are generated and classified using CNN features. It highlights significant models such as RCNN, DetectorNet, OverFeat, and MultiBox, which utilize CNNs for generic object detection. The work emphasizes the importance of integrating CNNs with effective region proposal methods to enhance detection performance. Sections 6-9 are set to explore fundamental sub-problems in detection frameworks, including DCNN features, detection proposals, and context modeling.

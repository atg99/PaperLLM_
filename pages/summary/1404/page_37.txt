The table summarizes various object proposal methods utilizing Deep Convolutional Neural Networks (DCNN), detailing their performance metrics and key characteristics. Each method is evaluated based on its backbone network, detection results, and recall at different Intersection over Union (IoU) thresholds.

1. **MultiBox** (Erhan et al. 2014) employs AlexNet and focuses on a class-agnostic regressor using 800 predefined anchor boxes. It does not share features for detection, achieving a mean Average Precision (mAP) of 29.0 on VOC07.

2. **DeepBox** (Kuo et al. 2015) utilizes VGG16 and reranks proposals from EdgeBox with a lightweight CNN. It runs efficiently at 0.26 seconds per image but does not share features for detection, yielding an mAP of 37.8 on VOC12.

3. **Region Proposal Network (RPN)** (Ren et al. 2015, 2017) also uses VGG16 and is notable for sharing full image convolutional features with detection networks. It significantly enhances detection speed, achieving high recall rates and mAP scores of 73.2 on VOC07+12 and 21.9 on COCO.

4. **DeepProposal** (Ghodrati et al. 2015) generates proposals in a multiscale manner while sharing features with the detection network, resulting in an mAP of 53.2 on VOC07.

5. **CRAFT** (Yang et al. 2016a) introduces a classification network after RPN, maintaining high recall rates but not sharing features for detection, with mAP scores of 75.7 on VOC07+12 and 71.3 on VOC12.

The table highlights the evolution and performance of these object proposal methods, showcasing their contributions to the field of computer vision.

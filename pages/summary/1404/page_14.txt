The paper reviews significant milestones in object detection frameworks, highlighting various models that have contributed to advancements in the field. Key frameworks discussed include RCNN, Faster RCNN, YOLO, Mask RCNN, and others. 

The RCNN framework, developed by Girshick et al., employs a multi-stage pipeline for object detection. It begins with region proposal computation using selective search, followed by fine-tuning a CNN model on these proposals. The framework then trains class-specific SVM classifiers and bounding box regressors based on CNN-extracted features. Despite its high detection quality, RCNN has notable drawbacks, including a slow and complex training process, significant resource requirements for SVM training, and slow testing due to the extraction of features for each object proposal separately.

These limitations have spurred the development of improved frameworks such as SPPNet, Fast RCNN, and Faster RCNN, which aim to optimize the detection process and enhance efficiency. The paper emphasizes the evolution of object detection methodologies, showcasing how each innovation addresses the shortcomings of its predecessors.

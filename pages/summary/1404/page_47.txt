The table presents a comparison of various object detection frameworks, highlighting their characteristics, performance, and limitations. 

1. **Mask R-CNN** (He et al. 2017): Utilizes a Region Proposal Network (RPN) with a ResNet101 or ResNeXt101 backbone and arbitrary input image sizes. It achieves VOC07 results of 50.3 and is known for its effectiveness in object-instance segmentation. However, it is noted for not being suitable for real-time applications, with a speed of less than 5 FPS.

2. **OverFeat** (Sermanet et al. 2014): Employs an AlexNet-like architecture with an arbitrary input size, achieving significant localization results but operates at a slow speed of less than 0.1 FPS. It is recognized for its multiscale image pyramid feature extraction and convolutional feature sharing but suffers from a multi-stage pipeline and cannot effectively handle multiple instances of the same object class.

3. **YOLO** (Redmon et al. 2016): Features a GoogLeNet-like structure with a fixed input size, achieving VOC07 results of 66.4 and speed capabilities of up to 155 FPS with Fast YOLO. It is the first efficient unified detector, eliminating the region proposal process. Despite its speed advantage, it faces challenges in accuracy compared to state-of-the-art detectors and struggles with small object localization.

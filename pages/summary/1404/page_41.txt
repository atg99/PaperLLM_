The table presents various methods for training strategies and handling class imbalance in object detection, highlighting representative detectors, their configurations, and performance metrics. 

1. **MegDet** (Peng et al. 2018) utilizes a Region Proposal Network (RPN) with a ResNet50 backbone and Faster R-CNN architecture, achieving VOC07 results of 52.5. It introduces cross-GPU batch normalization to enable larger minibatch sizes, allowing COCO training to be completed in 4 hours on 128 GPUs, and it won the COCO 2017 detection challenge.

2. **SNIP** (Singh et al. 2018b) employs RPN with a DPN backbone, achieving a VOC07 score of 48.3. It introduces a multiscale training scheme that selectively up-samples small objects during training based on their scale relevance.

3. **SNIPER** (Singh et al. 2018b) also uses RPN with a ResNet101 backbone, obtaining a VOC07 score of 47.6. It features a multiscale training strategy that processes context regions around ground-truth instances at the appropriate scale.

4. **OHEM** (Shrivastava et al. 2016) is based on a VGG16 backbone with Fast R-CNN architecture, achieving VOC07+12 results of 78.9 and VOC12 results of 76.3. It implements an Online Hard Example Mining algorithm to enhance the training of region-based detectors.

Overall, these methods demonstrate advancements in training efficiency and effectiveness in addressing class imbalance in object detection tasks.

The paper discusses hourglass architectures in the context of computer vision, specifically focusing on feature fusion blocks used in backbone networks like VGG and ResNet. It highlights various feature fusion methods such as Feature Pyramid Networks (FPN), Thinned U-shaped Module (TUM), and others including TDM, DSSD, RON, ReFineDet, ZIP, PANet, FPR, DetNet, and M2Det. These methods are compared to illustrate their roles in enhancing feature extraction and representation in modern computer vision applications.

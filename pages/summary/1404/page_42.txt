The table presented in the paper summarizes various object detection models, highlighting their region proposal methods, backbone architectures, and performance metrics across different datasets, including VOC07, VOC12, and COCO. 

Key models include FactorNet, which identifies sample imbalance across object categories and introduces a divide-and-conquer feature learning scheme. Chained Cascade jointly learns deep convolutional neural networks (DCNN) and multiple stages of cascaded classifiers, improving detection accuracy on PASCAL VOC and ImageNet. The Cascade RCNN employs a similar approach, learning classifiers with varying localization accuracy to enhance positive sample selection and uses stacked bounding box regression. RetinaNet introduces a novel Focal Loss to address the imbalance of positive and negative samples, making it effective for one-stage detectors.

The results reported for COCO are based on mean Average Precision (mAP) at Intersection over Union (IoU) thresholds ranging from 0.5 to 0.95. Each model's performance reflects advancements in object detection methodologies and their contributions to improving accuracy.

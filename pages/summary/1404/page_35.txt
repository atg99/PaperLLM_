The paper discusses the evolution and significance of detection proposals in the field of object detection, particularly in relation to deep convolutional neural networks (DCNNs). Detection proposals are candidate regions in an image that are likely to contain objects, enhancing the efficiency of object detection systems by reducing the number of regions that need evaluation. The paper outlines essential characteristics of effective detection proposals, which include high recall with minimal proposals, accurate localization of objects, and low computational costs.

The integration of object proposals with DCNN features marked a pivotal shift in object detection, leading to the development of the RCNN model, which has since become a standard preprocessing step in various object detection challenges. The paper notes that since 2014, all winning entries in major competitions such as PASCAL VOC, ILSVRC, and MS COCO utilized detection proposals.

Additionally, it highlights popular traditional object proposal methods such as Selective Search, MCG, and EdgeBoxes, while acknowledging the rapid advancements in the domain that have led to the adoption of more sophisticated techniques. The paper emphasizes the importance of object proposals in enhancing detection performance and suggests that comprehensive reviews of these algorithms can be found in existing surveys for readers seeking further information.

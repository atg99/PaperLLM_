The table presents a comparison of various Deep Convolutional Neural Network (DCNN) architectures, detailing their parameters, layer configurations, and test errors on the ImageNet1000 dataset. 

1. **ResNet101** introduces fewer parameters through global average pooling and bottleneck techniques, achieving a test error of 3.0%. 
2. **InceptionResNet v1** combines identity mapping with the Inception module, maintaining a similar computational cost to Inception v3 while offering a faster training process.
3. **InceptionResNet v2** is a more complex residual variant of Inception that significantly enhances recognition performance.
4. **Inception v4** lacks residual connections yet matches the recognition performance of InceptionResNet v2, albeit at a slower pace.
5. **ResNeXt** employs a building block that aggregates multiple transformations with the same topology, achieving a test error of 3.0%.
6. **DenseNet201** connects each layer with others in a feed-forward manner, addressing the vanishing gradient issue, promoting feature reuse, and reducing parameters.
7. **DarkNet** parallels VGGNet but requires fewer parameters.
8. **MobileNet** utilizes lightweight architecture with depth-wise separable convolutions for efficiency.
9. **SE ResNet** incorporates channel-wise attention through the Squeeze and Excitation block, complementing existing CNNs and achieving a test error of 2.3%.

The statistics for parameters and layers do not include the final fully connected prediction layer, and the test errors reflect the Top 5 classification accuracy on the ImageNet1000 dataset. The table emphasizes the evolution and efficiency of DCNN architectures over time.

This paper provides a comprehensive survey on the advancements in generic object detection driven by deep learning techniques. Object detection is a critical and challenging problem in computer vision, aimed at identifying and locating object instances from predefined categories within natural images. The authors review over 300 research contributions, covering various aspects including detection frameworks, feature representation, object proposal generation, context modeling, training strategies, and evaluation metrics.

The introduction highlights the significance of object detection as foundational for higher-level vision tasks such as segmentation and tracking, with applications spanning from robotics to autonomous driving. The paper emphasizes the transformative impact of deep learning, particularly convolutional neural networks, in enhancing object detection performance. It categorizes object detection into two types: specific instance detection and broad category detection, with the former focusing on recognizing unique instances of objects.

The survey concludes by outlining promising future research directions, underscoring the ongoing evolution of this field as influenced by deep learning methodologies.

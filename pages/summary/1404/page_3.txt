The table summarizes various surveys on object detection published since 2000, highlighting significant contributions to the field. Key surveys include:

1. "Monocular pedestrian detection: survey and experiments" by Enzweiler and Gavrila (2009), which evaluates three pedestrian detection systems.
2. "Survey of pedestrian detection for advanced driver assistance systems" by Geronimo et al. (2010), focusing on pedestrian detection in the context of driver assistance.
3. "Pedestrian detection: an evaluation of the state of the art" by Dollar et al. (2012), providing a comprehensive assessment of monocular image detectors.
4. "Detecting faces in images: a survey" by Yang et al. (2002), marking the first survey on face detection from single images.
5. "On road vehicle detection: a review" by Sun et al. (2006), reviewing vision-based vehicle detection systems.
6. "Text detection and recognition in imagery: a survey" by Ye and Doermann (2015), examining text detection and recognition in color imagery.

Other notable contributions include discussions on object categorization, representation learning, and deep learning's role in medical image analysis and CNN advancements. Overall, these surveys reflect the evolution and current trends in object detection methodologies.

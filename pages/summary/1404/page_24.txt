The paper presents a summary of various methods aimed at enhancing Deep Convolutional Neural Network (DCNN) feature representations for generic object detection. It focuses on the properties of representative detection methods, highlighting their performance metrics and key innovations. 

1. **ION (Bell et al. 2016)**: This method utilizes multi-layer features and spatial recurrent neural networks to model contextual information. It achieved a mean Average Precision (mAP) of 79.4 for VOC07 and VOC12, and 55.7 for COCO, ranking as the Best Student Entry and third overall in the COCO detection challenge 2015.

2. **HyperNet (Kong et al. 2016)**: This approach employs features from multiple layers for both region proposal and classification, achieving mAP scores of 76.3 (VOC07+12) and 71.4 (VOC07T+12). 

3. **PVANet (Kim et al. 2016)**: A deep yet lightweight model that combines techniques from concatenated ReLU, Inception, and HyperNet. It demonstrated strong performance with mAP scores of 84.9 (VOC07+12+CO) and 84.2 (VOC07T+12+CO).

The methods reviewed reflect advancements in utilizing multi-layer features and contextual modeling to improve object detection performance across various datasets.

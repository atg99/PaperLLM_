The appendix details the hyperparameter tuning ranges used in the experiments of the paper presented at ICLR 2020. Hyperparameters were optimized using Google Vizier, with specific tuning ranges provided for different tasks. Notably, the parameters mogrifier_rounds and mogrifier_rank are only applicable to the Mogriﬁer model. For cases where the input_embedding_ratio exceeds 1, the model uses equal sizes for input/output embeddings and hidden layers, omitting the linear projection from cell output to output embeddings. 

The tuning ranges vary for the Enwik8 dataset, which is larger, leading to tighter ranges for dropout parameters. Dynamic evaluation hyperparameters were also specified, with max_time_steps set to 20 for word tasks and 50 for character tasks. The batch size for mean squared gradient estimation was fixed at 1024, with gradient clipping disabled and the L2 penalty set to zero.

Tables provided outline the low and high values, along with spacing methods for various hyperparameters across tasks, including learning rate, dropout rates, and specific parameters for dynamic evaluation.

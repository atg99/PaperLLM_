This appendix discusses hyperparameter sensitivity in the context of LSTM and Mogriﬁer models, as illustrated by parallel coordinate plots. The analysis focuses on identifying hyperparameter combinations that are closest to the best solution, achieved by restricting each hyperparameter's range to approximately 15% of its total tuning range. The findings reveal that both models exhibit minimal sensitivity, with results deviating by at most 1.2 perplexity points from the best outcome. This indicates that the models are relatively robust to hyperparameter variations, although grid search would necessitate significantly more trials to achieve similar results.

Additionally, the stochastic nature of training can lead to variability in outcomes; repeated experiments with identical hyperparameters may yield slightly poorer results. For the Penn Treebank dataset, the standard deviation of perplexity from reruns of the LSTM with the optimal hyperparameters was estimated at around 0.2 perplexity points, while the mean deviation was approximately 0.7 perplexity points from the best tuning run's results. The appendix also notes that 'feature_mask_rank' and 'feature_mask_rounds' are alternative terms for 'mogriﬁer_rank' and 'mogriﬁer_rounds.'

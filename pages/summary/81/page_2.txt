The paper introduces the Mogrifer LSTM, an advanced recurrent neural network model that enhances the standard LSTM architecture by incorporating a mutual gating mechanism between input and hidden state. This approach involves transforming the previous hidden state linearly, followed by an elementwise gating process that modulates the inputs before they are processed by the LSTM cell. The authors build on existing literature, comparing their model to previous works such as Multiplicative RNNs and Multiplicative LSTMs, demonstrating that the Mogrifer consistently outperforms standard LSTMs and achieves state-of-the-art results on most datasets, while matching the performance of similarly sized transformer models on larger datasets.

The model's architecture is presented with mathematical formulations for the LSTM update, highlighting how the gates can scale the weight matrices in a context-dependent manner. This allows for greater flexibility and adaptation of the input embeddings based on the cell state and vice versa. The paper emphasizes that this interleaved modulation of inputs leads to improved performance in sequence modeling tasks, showcasing the effectiveness of the Mogrifer LSTM in addressing challenges associated with traditional LSTM networks, particularly in the context of vanishing gradients.

The paper presents results comparing the Mogrifizer model with other near state-of-the-art models on character-based datasets. Table 2 outlines bits per character (bpc) performance, demonstrating that the Mogrifizer consistently outperforms the LSTM baseline and sets new records across most datasets, except for Enwik8, where it closely approaches the performance of Transformer-XL. The authors note that the model sizes were chosen to avoid underfitting, and they grouped similar-sized models for comparison. 

The Mogrifizer's improvements over the LSTM are significant, addressing issues of scalability and hyperparameter tuning that have hindered previous LSTM results. The paper highlights that even minor improvements in bpc can translate into substantial gains in perplexity for word-level tasks. Additionally, the Mogrifizer shows a performance advantage in the smaller-scale character-level dataset MWC. Overall, the findings underscore the Mogrifizer's effectiveness in character-level modeling, particularly when compared to previous models like AWD-LSTM and FRAGE.

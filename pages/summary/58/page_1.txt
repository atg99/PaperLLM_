The paper presents a comparative study of Transformer and recurrent neural networks (RNN) in the context of speech applications, including automatic speech recognition (ASR), speech translation (ST), and text-to-speech (TTS). The authors conducted extensive experiments across multiple benchmarks, revealing that the Transformer model outperformed RNN in 13 out of 15 ASR benchmarks, highlighting its superior performance in speech tasks.

The research emphasizes the complexity of implementing Transformer in speech applications, noting the need for advanced configurations such as optimizers and data augmentation strategies, which differ from conventional RNN models. The authors aim to share insights and training tips to facilitate the adoption of Transformer in the speech processing community.

Key contributions include a large-scale comparative analysis showing significant performance improvements with Transformer, practical training guidelines for its application in ASR, TTS, and ST, and the development of reproducible end-to-end recipes and models using the open-source toolkit ESPnet. This work builds on previous studies in neural machine translation and aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of the strengths and challenges associated with Transformer and RNN in speech processing.

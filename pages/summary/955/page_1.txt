In the paper "Hamiltonian Neural Networks," the authors Sam Greydanus, Misko Dzamba, and Jason Yosinski explore ways to enhance neural networks' ability to learn fundamental physical laws through the lens of Hamiltonian mechanics. They propose a model that incorporates exact conservation laws, particularly focusing on energy conservation, and assess its performance on scenarios like the two-body problem and pendulum observations. Their findings indicate that the Hamiltonian Neural Network (HNN) not only trains more quickly but also generalizes better than traditional neural networks, while also exhibiting perfect time reversibility. This work highlights the potential for integrating physical priors into neural network training to improve learning outcomes across various tasks that inherently involve physical principles.

The paper addresses the issue of the Content Leak phenomenon commonly observed in style transfer algorithms. It critiques existing methods, particularly those based on the VGG19 auto-encoder, and presents ArtFlow, a novel style transfer framework that utilizes a reversible neural flow-based network called PFN. This approach allows for both forward and reverse projections into the feature space, significantly improving content-style separation and mitigating Content Leak.

The study includes a user evaluation comparing the performance of ArtFlow against several state-of-the-art methods, including StyleSwap, AdaIN, WCT, LinearWCT, OptimalWCT, and Avatar-Net. The results indicate that ArtFlow achieves a higher number of user votes, suggesting its competitive edge in style transfer performance.

The findings highlight that ArtFlow, when combined with unbiased style transfer modules like AdaIN or WCT, can deliver comparable results while effectively preventing Content Leak. The paper concludes that the introduction of PFN allows for lossless and unbiased image projection and reversion, enhancing the overall quality of style transfer.

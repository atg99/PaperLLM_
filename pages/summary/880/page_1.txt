The problem of content leak, where image content deteriorates after multiple rounds of stylization, is a significant issue in existing universal style transfer (UST) methods. These methods typically consist of a fixed encoder, a learnable decoder, and a style transfer module. The fixed nature of the encoder and the inherent reconstruction errors of the decoder contribute to this content leak. ArtFlow addresses these limitations by ensuring a lossless and unbiased transfer of features, maintaining the integrity of the original image content even after numerous stylization iterations.

Experimental results indicate that ArtFlow not only achieves performance comparable to state-of-the-art style transfer methods but also effectively mitigates the content leak phenomenon. This advancement positions ArtFlow as a robust solution in the realm of universal style transfer, paving the way for more reliable and high-quality artistic image transformations.

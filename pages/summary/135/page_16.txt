The paper presents the classification rates of the DEVDAN method across various datasets, comparing it with the DEVDAN-R variant. The results are shown for five consecutive runs on different datasets, including Rotated MNIST, Permuted MNIST, and MNIST, among others. DEVDAN achieved high classification rates, particularly on the RFID dataset, where it reached 98.71. In contrast, DEVDAN-R displayed consistently lower performance across most datasets, with notable results on the SEAD and KDDCup datasets.

The paper also includes performance metrics, such as bias and variance for both generative and discriminative phases, illustrating the evolution of hidden units during training. The findings suggest that DEVDAN outperforms DEVDAN-R, indicating its efficacy in classification tasks. The analysis excludes certain methods from statistical testing due to their inability to meet the partition of unity property, emphasizing the rigor of the evaluation process.

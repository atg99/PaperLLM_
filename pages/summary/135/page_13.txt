In summary, the paper presents a comprehensive evaluation of the DEVDAN learning performance using ten distinct datasets that include both real-world and synthetic data streams. Six of these datasets exhibit non-stationary properties, which are critical for testing the adaptability of data stream algorithms. The datasets vary in their characteristics, such as abrupt concept drifts, covariate drift, and high dimensionality, making them suitable for a thorough analysis of algorithm performance in dynamic environments. Each dataset is described in detail, highlighting its attributes, the nature of the drift, and the challenges it presents for data stream processing.

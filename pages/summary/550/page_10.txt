This list of references encompasses key works in the field of hyperparameter optimization and neural architecture search. The papers cited include foundational studies, innovative methodologies, and surveys that address various aspects of machine learning optimization.

1. Bender et al. (2018) explore one-shot architecture search, simplifying the process of finding optimal neural network architectures. 
2. Bengio (2000) discusses gradient-based optimization techniques for hyperparameters, laying groundwork for future advancements.
3. Bergstra and Bengio (2012) highlight the effectiveness of random search in hyperparameter tuning, offering a simple yet efficient approach.
4. The work by Bergstra et al. (2011) presents algorithms specifically designed for hyperparameter optimization, significantly contributing to the field.
5. Brock et al. (2018) introduce SMASH, a method for one-shot model architecture search utilizing hypernetworks.
6. Cai et al. (2018) propose path-level transformations for efficient architecture search, enhancing search capabilities.
7. ProxylessNAS (Cai et al., 2019) enables direct architecture search on target tasks and hardware without proxies.
8. Cao et al. (2019) focus on learnable embedding spaces for neural architecture compression, optimizing performance.
9. Devries and Taylor (2017) improve regularization techniques for convolutional networks through a method called cutout.
10. Domhan et al. (2015) expedite hyperparameter optimization by extrapolating learning curves, streamlining the process.
11. Elsken et al. (2018, 2019) provide insights into multi-objective architecture search and a comprehensive survey on neural architecture search methodologies.
12. Falkner et al. (2018) introduce Bohb, an efficient hyperparameter optimization technique.
13. Feurer et al. (2015) discuss automated machine learning approaches that are both efficient and robust.
14. Golovin et al. (2017) present Google Vizier, a service for black-box optimization.
15. Google AutoML (2018) is highlighted as a notable tool for automating machine learning processes.
16. Hoffer et al. (2017) address the generalization gap in large batch training of neural networks.
17. Hutter et al. (2011) detail sequential model-based optimization for algorithm configuration.
18. Jaderberg et al. (2017) introduce population-based training methods for neural networks.
19. Jamieson and Talwalkar (2015) examine non-stochastic best arm identification and hyperparameter optimization.
20. Jin et al. (2018) present Auto-Keras, a framework for efficient neural architecture search using network morphism.
21. Kandasamy et al. (2016, 2017, 2018) contribute to Gaussian process bandit optimization and multi-fidelity evaluations, enhancing Bayesian optimization techniques.
22. Klein et al. (2017) focus on fast Bayesian optimization for hyperparameters in large datasets.
23. Krizhevsky (2009) discusses deep learning techniques through the lens of feature learning from images.
24. Li et al. (2017) introduce Hyperband, a bandit-based approach to hyperparameter optimization.

These references collectively advance the understanding of hyperparameter tuning and architecture search in machine learning, providing various methodologies and insights for researchers and practitioners in the field.

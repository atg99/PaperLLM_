The paper presents an end-to-end cancer risk prediction model designed to analyze complete CT volumes and generate a predictive score for cancer diagnosis. The model processes input volumes by resampling them into two fixed voxel sizes. For each input volume, two regions of interest (ROIs) are identified, and features are extracted to compute prediction scores for each ROI using a fully connected neural network. In instances where a prior ROI is not available, it is padded with zeros. This architecture aims to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of cancer risk assessment.

The reporting summary from Nature Research emphasizes the importance of reproducibility in published research. It outlines the required statistical parameters that must be included in research reporting, such as sample size, measurement details, statistical tests used, and descriptions of covariates and assumptions. It insists on full transparency in reporting statistical outcomes, including effect sizes and confidence intervals.

Additionally, the summary provides guidelines for software and code used in research, highlighting the necessity for availability and transparency of computer code. It lists specific tools employed for data collection and analysis, including eUnity, MAPLE, and various internal forks of open-source libraries such as Pandas and TensorFlow.

Finally, the document encourages researchers to make their custom algorithms accessible to reviewers and suggests depositing code in community repositories, aligning with the broader goal of enhancing reproducibility and transparency in scientific research.

The paper presents extended data regarding model attributions generated through integrated gradients for cancer risk prediction. In the first example, a cancer-positive case is illustrated, showing input volumes for both full-volume and cancer risk prediction models. The attribution overlay indicates positive contributions in magenta and negative contributions in blue, revealing that readers consistently noted the model's focus on the nodule. Notably, in 86% of cases, both global and second-stage models concentrated on the same regions. The second example pertains to a cancer-negative case, where the left image displays a slice from the input volume and the right shows the attribution overlay. In this scenario, readers observed that the model focused on vascular regions in 40% of the examined negative cases.

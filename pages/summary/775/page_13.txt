The extended data figure presents results from a reader study focused on lung cancer screening using a single CT volume, with adjustments for biased sampling from the National Lung Screening Trial (NLST) data. The study upweighted examples from screening groups that had no nodules but varying abnormalities to correct for downsampling biases. Performance metrics are displayed through the area under the curve (AUC) and summary tables based on case-level malignancy scores. The model's performance is compared to that of average radiologists across different Lung-RADS categories, with confidence intervals provided. Specific analyses include sensitivity and specificity comparisons, conducted on 507 volumes from patients, with statistical significance assessed through permutation testing. Additionally, a hit rate localization analysis evaluates the model's accuracy in identifying cancerous lesions.

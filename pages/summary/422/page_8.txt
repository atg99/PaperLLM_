In this section, the encoding steps of the proposed method are detailed. Key parameters include KP, a set containing KN keypoints, and Sk, which indicates the number of channels in the label tensor derived from keypoints. The parameter fk relates to the anchors used in the Faster R-CNN model, while gtb, w, and h are derived from the input image. Additionally, four thresholds for Region Proposal Network (RPN) training are defined: _RPN poT, _RPN noT, _RPN bsT, and _RPN fgr.

An illustration in Fig. 3 demonstrates the mapping process. The anchors from Faster R-CNN are represented as an array fasA, and keypoint anchors as desA. The combined array of all anchors is denoted as A, which is formed by vertically stacking fasA and desA. After filtering out anchors not within the image, a new array A' is created. A query list qA is established to map anchors in A' to their corresponding anchors in A. The labels for A' are initialized, followed by the reshaping of the 3-D label tensors fasLt and desLt into 1-D label tensors, as depicted in the figures. This process sets the foundation for the encoding steps essential for the method's functionality.

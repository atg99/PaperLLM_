The paper presents K-Faster, an enhanced object detection model evaluated on the PASCAL VOC 2007 test set. Detection examples illustrate the model's performance, with a score threshold of 0.6 used to display bounding boxes. The findings indicate that keypoint anchors significantly contribute to the detection ratio. The evaluation includes localization precision, measured by the mean Intersection over Union (IoU) for true positive detections (IoU ≥ 0.5). Results show that K-Faster achieves higher mean IoUs compared to Faster R-CNN across all classes, with scores of 78.2% versus 77.8% for VGG16 and 81.7% versus 81.5% for ResNet101. These results suggest that K-Faster improves positioning precision in object detection tasks.

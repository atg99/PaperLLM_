The paper discusses a method for mapping keypoint anchors to a 1-D tensor representation, specifically focusing on the initialization and mapping process. The system begins with a tensor initialized to a specific configuration and utilizes a query list to establish a relationship between anchors in a given list and their corresponding labels in the tensor. 

A query function is defined to index each anchor, allowing for the retrieval of its associated label in the 1-D tensor. The mapping process is illustrated through figures that show the transformation from a 3-D label tensor to a 1-D representation. For instance, the 0-th keypoint anchor is examined in detail, highlighting how its label is positioned within the tensor based on its coordinates and the transformation process.

Additionally, the paper introduces a concept of an overlapping array, which quantifies the relationship between anchors and ground truth boxes. This array is used to compute the overlap between each anchor and the corresponding ground truth, leading to the derivation of a vector that captures the maximum overlaps for further analysis.

Overall, the methodology outlined provides a structured approach to anchor mapping and overlap analysis, essential for tasks such as object detection and image segmentation.

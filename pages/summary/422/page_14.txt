The paper presents an evaluation of the K-Faster model for object detection, focusing on the tuning of parameters in the Region Proposal Network (RPN) using the PASCAL VOC 2007 test set. The study explores the impact of various parameters on performance metrics such as recall, precision, and mean Average Precision (mAP). Key findings indicate that the RPN configurations with a size of 448 outperform those with a size of 512 in terms of mAP, while a RoIN value of 256 is preferred over 196. The parameters preNMSN and postNMSN were optimized to 24k and 4k, respectively, yielding favorable results.

The experiments utilized the 07+12 dataset for training on the PASCAL VOC 2007 benchmark, with a learning rate initialized at 0.001 and adjusted every 80k iterations. The results show that the K-Faster model, employing VGG16 and ResNet101 as backbone networks, demonstrates effective performance in object detection tasks. Overall, the tuned parameters for K-Faster are established as RPNBSN = 448, RoIN = 256, preNMSN = 24k, and postNMSN = 4k, highlighting the benefits of local information in enhancing detection accuracy.

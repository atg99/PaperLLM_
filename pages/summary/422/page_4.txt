The paper introduces an enhanced method called K-Faster, which improves upon the Faster R-CNN framework by integrating local keypoints through the use of keypoint anchors. This approach addresses the limitations of Faster R-CNN, which relies on preset anchor boxes with fixed aspect ratios and sizes. The authors reference existing point-based CNN methods, such as DeNet and CornerNet, noting their complexity and the challenges associated with optimizing multiple costs in a two-stage process.

In the proposed methodology, local information is leveraged to train candidate boxes. The process begins with generating keypoints from an input image, which then leads to the creation of anchor boxes. Each anchor is labeled as positive or negative based on its Intersection over Union (IoU) overlap with ground-truth boxes. The paper details how ground biases are calculated for bounding box regression and how labels and biases are organized into 3-D tensors for the Region Proposal Network (RPN).

The anchors are grouped based on their aspect ratios, and labels are mapped to corresponding positions on a 2-D feature map, facilitating the integration of supervision information for the RPN. The overall framework maintains a structure similar to that of Faster R-CNN, allowing for efficient training and improved performance in object detection tasks.

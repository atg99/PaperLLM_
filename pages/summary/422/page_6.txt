This section discusses the process of merging keypoints and generating anchors for region proposals in the context of the Faster R-CNN framework. Keypoints are merged into a unified set, and for any pair of points extracted from these keypoints, corresponding anchors are defined. Anchors with small areas are excluded to refine proposals effectively. The final anchors are a combination of those produced by Faster R-CNN and those derived from keypoints.

To generate region proposals, the study employs a box-regression layer and a box-classification layer, integrating keypoint information with Faster R-CNN anchors. The labels and ground biases associated with keypoints are structured into 3D tensors to provide supervision for training these layers.

The feature map from the last shared convolutional layer is utilized to feed labels into the classification layer, which is also structured as a 3D tensor. This tensor can be decomposed into components derived from both Faster R-CNN and keypoints, allowing for a comprehensive representation of the data. The section concludes with a representation of the 3D tensor as a set of 2D feature maps, illustrating the integration of keypoint-derived information into the overall proposal generation process.

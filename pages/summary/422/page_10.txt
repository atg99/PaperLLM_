The paper discusses the methodology for obtaining anchor indexes for Region Proposal Network (RPN) training based on the maximum values in a matrix O. The approach involves identifying positive and negative labeled anchors effectively for training purposes.

1. **Positive Label Anchors**:
   - The anchor indexes of column maxima are represented by a vector, where each element corresponds to the index of the maximum in its respective column.
   - For positive labels, an index is assigned if a certain threshold is met. The algorithm selects anchors based on the number of positive labels, ensuring that if the number exceeds a predefined threshold, a random selection occurs from the identified anchors.
   - The final index vector of positive labeled anchors is constructed based on these conditions.

2. **Negative Label Anchors**:
   - Negative labels are similarly assigned based on a different threshold. The process involves identifying anchors with negative labels and performing random selection if the number exceeds a specific threshold.
   - The corresponding index vector for negative labeled anchors is created based on the selection criteria established.

3. **Label Tensor Creation**:
   - The positive and negative indexes derived from the anchors are then used to construct a 1-D label tensor. For each positive index, a corresponding label of 1 is assigned, while for negative indexes, a label of 0 is assigned.

Overall, the methodology provides a structured approach to generating labeled anchors for RPN training, facilitating efficient training processes in object detection tasks. The steps are clearly delineated to ensure reproducibility and clarity in the labeling process.

The generated anchors are then refined through an area-based partitioning method, which organizes them based on their spatial distribution. This results in a more efficient and effective representation of the objects present in the image. The final output includes a 3-D label tensor and a 3-D ground bias tensor, both of which are constructed from the keypoints. These tensors are crucial for the subsequent training of the RPN, as they provide the necessary supervision information.

In summary, this work leverages keypoint detection, particularly through the use of the Global Optimized SIFT descriptor, to enhance anchor generation for the RPN. By focusing on creating a robust set of anchors through extensive enumeration and careful partitioning, the proposed method aims to improve the accuracy and efficiency of object detection tasks.

The paper presents experimental results on object detection using various methods evaluated on the PASCAL VOC 2012 and Microsoft COCO datasets. 

For the PASCAL VOC 2012 test set, the results indicate that one-stage detectors like SSD512 and DSOD300 achieve mAP scores of 74.9% and 76.3%, respectively, outperforming other one-stage methods like YOLO2. Among the two-stage detectors, CoupleNet leads with an mAP of 80.4%, while R-FCN and ION also show competitive performance with scores of 77.6% and 74.7%.

In the section on MS COCO, the paper details the evaluation on a dataset comprising 80 object classes, reporting metrics such as AP, AP50, and AP75, with the training set being a combination of train and validation images. The results show that the one-stage detector SSD512 achieves an AP of 26.8%, while DSOD300 performs better with an AP of 29.3%. CEBNet models also demonstrate significant performance, with CEBNet512 achieving an AP of 34.3%.

The methodology includes re-implementation of certain models for fair comparison, and learning rates are systematically adjusted during training to optimize performance. Overall, the results highlight the effectiveness of various detection methods across different datasets, emphasizing improvements in mAP and average precision metrics.

The references provided encompass a range of significant contributions to the field of object detection and image classification using deep learning techniques. Notable works include the development of various convolutional neural network architectures that enhance detection accuracy and speed. 

Cai and Vasconcelos (2018) introduced Cascade R-CNN, emphasizing high-quality object detection. Krizhevsky et al. (2017) presented a foundational model with their deep convolutional networks for ImageNet classification. Girshick's Fast R-CNN (2015) and Faster R-CNN (2017) further advanced real-time object detection through region proposal networks. Redmon and Farhadi (2017) contributed with YOLO9000, known for its speed and efficiency.

The Microsoft COCO dataset (Lin et al., 2014) and ImageNet challenge (Russakovsky et al., 2015) provided benchmarks for evaluating object detection models. Other works, such as Mask R-CNN (He et al., 2017) and DSOD (Shen et al., 2017), focused on integrating segmentation with detection tasks.

Additionally, methods like CoupleNet (Zhu et al., 2017) and SOD-MTGAN (Bai et al., 2018) explored the coupling of global and local features for improved detection. The survey by Liu et al. (2018) encapsulated the progress in generic object detection through deep learning. 

Overall, these references reflect the evolution of methodologies and datasets that have shaped contemporary approaches in computer vision, particularly in object detection.

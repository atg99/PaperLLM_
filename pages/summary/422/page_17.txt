The paper evaluates the performance of K-Faster, a variant of Faster R-CNN, using the PASCAL VOC 2007 test set as a benchmark. It compares the runtime of K-Faster against Faster R-CNN on a dual-core i3 CPU and an NVIDIA GTX1080 GPU. The results indicate that K-Faster is slower overall due to the time taken for keypoint generation on the CPU, which is approximately 100ms. However, K-Faster demonstrates competitive GPU runtimes, with its proposal generation being faster than that of Faster R-CNN in several instances.

Table 5 summarizes the runtime comparisons, showing that while K-Faster has increased keypoint and proposal generation times, its GPU performance allows it to compete with Faster R-CNN. Notably, K-Faster 16 and K-Faster 101 achieve mean Average Precision (mAP) improvements of 3.5% and 3.9%, respectively, over standard Faster R-CNN on the VOC 2012 test set. K-Faster 101 achieves a mAP of 77.7%, outperforming most state-of-the-art methods except for CoupleNet. The findings suggest that keypoint anchors significantly enhance object detection performance in this context.

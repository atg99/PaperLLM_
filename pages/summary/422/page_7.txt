In this paper, the authors describe a method for organizing label and ground bias in 3-D tensors through the partitioning of keypoint anchors into groups. This is articulated in equation (3), where keypoint anchors are divided into subsets based on a defined threshold Ts, which corresponds to the base size of the image set. The mapping of features is illustrated in the context of a feature map, where the position of each anchor is adjusted according to the feature stride from the input image.

The ground bias tensor is decomposed into two components as shown in equation (4). The first component, denoted as fasBt, is associated with the Faster R-CNN framework, while the second component, desBt, is influenced by keypoint detection. Equation (5) details the expression of desBt in relation to the feature map.

The authors emphasize that both the label and ground biases for the keypoints are mapped into their respective tensors, Lt and Bt, which are crucial for providing supervision information for training the Region Proposal Network (RPN). This systematic approach ensures that the mapping of biases is coherent and directly tied to the features being analyzed, facilitating more effective training of the model.

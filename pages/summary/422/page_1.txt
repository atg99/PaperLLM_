The paper introduces a novel Keypoint-based Faster R-CNN (K-Faster) method for object detection, aimed at enhancing the performance of traditional Region-based Convolutional Neural Networks (R-CNN). While R-CNNs have shown high detection accuracy, they often overlook local information crucial for generating candidate proposals. K-Faster addresses this gap by integrating local keypoints into the Faster R-CNN framework.

The method utilizes a sparse descriptor to identify points of interest in images, sampling local patches to capture invariant features. These keypoints are then combined to create keypoint anchors, which are encoded in feature maps according to their areas and center coordinates. This coupling of keypoint anchors with the standard anchors from Faster R-CNN is utilized in training the Region Proposal Network (RPN).

Experiments conducted on PASCAL VOC 07/12 and MS COCO datasets demonstrate that K-Faster significantly increases the mean Average Precision (mAP) and enhances the positioning accuracy of detected objects. The study emphasizes the importance of incorporating local information in object detection to improve proposal generation and overall detection performance.

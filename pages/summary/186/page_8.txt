The experiments conducted on the CIFAR-100 dataset using a StyleGAN-V2 backbone demonstrate that integrating Adaptive Discriminator Augmentation (ADA) or DiffAug enhances sparse GAN winning tickets. The effectiveness of these augmentations increases when ADA is combined with AdvAug, while the RLC4 augmentation proves less effective in conjunction with others. This suggests that the proposed method is complementary to existing techniques and can enhance data-efficient GAN training.

In addressing the few-shot image generation challenge, which arises from the difficulty of assembling large datasets, the study contrasts its data-efficient training scheme with various transfer learning approaches. Unlike these methods, which rely on pre-training from large datasets like FFHQ, the proposed method begins training from scratch without pre-training. The comparison, executed on the AnimalFace dataset and several 100-shot datasets, reveals that the method achieves competitive performance at a 48.80% sparsity level with only 100 training samples.

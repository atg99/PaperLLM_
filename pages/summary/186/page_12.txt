The referenced works primarily focus on advancements in generative adversarial networks (GANs) and their training methodologies. Key contributions include the introduction of Wasserstein GANs, which improve training stability and convergence, as seen in the works of Arjovsky et al. and Gulrajani et al. Other notable studies explore moment matching networks, few-shot learning, and image generation from small datasets, highlighting innovative techniques like self-supervised learning and data augmentation. The role of sparsity in deep neural networks and the effectiveness of network pruning are also discussed, emphasizing the importance of efficient training strategies. Overall, these studies collectively enhance the understanding and application of GANs in various contexts, addressing challenges associated with limited data and training dynamics.

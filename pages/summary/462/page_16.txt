The references provided detail various advancements in the field of image recognition and building extraction using deep learning techniques, particularly focusing on convolutional neural networks (CNNs). Key studies include:

1. Shrestha and Vanneschi (2018) improved building extraction methods by integrating fully convolutional networks with conditional random fields.
2. Simonyan and Zisserman (2014) introduced very deep CNNs, enhancing performance on large-scale image recognition tasks.
3. Sumer and Turker (2013) proposed an adaptive fuzzy-genetic algorithm for building detection using high-resolution satellite imagery.
4. Szegedy et al. (2015) explored deeper convolutional networks, contributing to advancements in computer vision.
5. Vakalopoulou et al. (2015) focused on building detection in high-resolution multispectral data utilizing deep learning features.
6. Volpi and Tuia (2016) conducted dense semantic labeling of subdecimeter resolution images through CNNs.
7. Wang et al. (2020) presented an automatic building extraction method using a fully convolutional encoder-decoder network with a non-local block.
8. Yang et al. (2018) applied deep learning models for hyperspectral image classification.
9. Yuan (2017) utilized convolutional networks for building extraction in aerial scenes, while Yuan and Cheriyadat (2014) focused on counting buildings in diverse aerial environments.
10. Zhou et al. (2014) developed deep features for scene recognition using a dedicated places database.

These studies collectively highlight the evolution and effectiveness of deep learning methodologies in remote sensing and image analysis, particularly for accurate building detection and classification.

This section presents the quantitative and visual results of the SegUnet model for building extraction and compares it with other advanced models like SegNet, FCN, and deep convolutional Unet. The evaluation involved a qualitative analysis of classification maps generated by the SegUnet model, which was trained using ground truth labels and prepared samples. The model's architecture, including the number of blocks and their sizes, is described, and the Adam optimization algorithm was employed to update parameters and minimize losses during training. The learning rate was set to 14e- to enhance processing speed and performance. The model was executed on an Nvidia Quadro P5000 GPU within the Keras framework using TensorFlow.

Results depicted in Figure 6 showcase the original images, ground truth images, and segmentation results obtained from the SegUnet model for two test images. The overall accuracy (OA) achieved by the model was 92.33% for Image 1 and 91.3% for Image 2, indicating effective building extraction from high-resolution aerial images. However, the presence of false negatives (FN) and false positives (FP) highlights some challenges, particularly in cases where closely situated buildings were misidentified as a single entity, leading to increased FP rates.

The paper discusses advancements in building extraction from high-resolution aerial imagery using deep convolutional neural networks (DCNNs). The authors claim that their proposed method demonstrates superior quantitative and visual performance compared to other DCNN models. The research was conceptualized and written primarily by A.A., with B.P. providing supervision and contributing to the review and editing process. Funding for the study was provided by the Centre for Advanced Modelling and Geospatial Information Systems at the University of Technology Sydney and the Researchers Supporting Project at King Saud University. The authors declare no conflicts of interest. The references cited cover various studies on remote sensing, deep learning methodologies for image extraction, and the application of convolutional neural networks in this field.

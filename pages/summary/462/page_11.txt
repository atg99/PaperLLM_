The proposed SegUnet model aims to enhance building extraction from high-resolution aerial imagery. However, it struggles with instances where buildings are present in the original image but absent in the labeled image, resulting in false positive predictions. 

In the discussion section, the performance of SegUnet is compared with other deep convolutional neural networks (DCNNs), specifically Segnet, FCN, and Unet models. The results indicate that SegUnet outperforms the others in accuracy for building extraction. Visual comparisons illustrate that while the FCN and Segnet methods tend to produce more false negatives and false positives, the SegUnet model effectively reduces these errors by incorporating spatial information for semantic segmentation. 

The Unet model is noted for its ability to preserve boundary information using deconvolution layers and skip connections, achieving better accuracy than FCN and Segnet. In contrast, SegUnet combines the strengths of Unet and Segnet, utilizing skip connections and index pooling, which leads to fewer false predictions and a more accurate segmentation map. Overall, the proposed SegUnet model demonstrates improved efficiency in building extraction compared to its counterparts.

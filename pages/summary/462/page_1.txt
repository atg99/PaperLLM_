In this paper, we present the Seg-Unet method, which integrates the strengths of both Segnet and Unet architectures to enhance the accuracy of building extraction from high-resolution aerial images. The challenges posed by various obstacles in images, such as shadows and occlusions from trees and vehicles, necessitate advanced techniques for effective segmentation.

Our experiments conducted on the Massachusetts building dataset demonstrate that the Seg-Unet method achieves an accuracy of 92.73%, outperforming existing methods including fully convolutional neural networks, Segnet, and Unet, by margins of 0.44%, 1.17%, and 0.14% respectively. This improvement highlights the effectiveness of the proposed architecture in accurately identifying and segmenting building features in complex imagery.

In conclusion, the Seg-Unet method shows promising results in the field of remote sensing for semantic segmentation, particularly in building extraction, offering significant potential for applications in urban planning, disaster management, and geospatial database enhancement.

The paper introduces the SegUnet model, a novel deep neural network designed for building extraction from high-resolution aerial imagery. The model combines features from Segnet and Unet techniques and was evaluated using the Massachusetts building dataset. Quantitative results indicate that SegUnet outperforms other deep convolutional neural networks (DCNNs) like Segnet, FCN, and Unet in several accuracy metrics, achieving an average overall accuracy (OA) of 92.73%. Specifically, SegUnet demonstrated superior performance in the F1 score and OA metrics, surpassing Unet, Segnet, and FCN by 0.14%, 1.17%, and 0.44%, respectively. The paper presents visual and quantitative comparisons that highlight SegUnet's effectiveness, confirming its capability to produce accurate segmentation maps and enhance building extraction from remote sensing imagery. Overall, the results validate SegUnet as a leading approach in this domain.

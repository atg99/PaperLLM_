This paper references a collection of works focused on multi-agent systems and programming languages designed for agent-oriented development. Key contributions include:

1. **Multi-Agent Programming Frameworks**: Boissier et al. discuss JaCaMo, a framework that facilitates programming multi-agent systems (MAS). Ricci et al. elaborate on environment programming within CArtAgO, highlighting its role in MAS development.

2. **Organizational Models**: Hübner et al. present the MOISE+ model, which addresses programming challenges at both system and agent levels. This model aids in developing organized MAS.

3. **Agent Programming Languages**: Several languages are introduced, including JADE, which offers a comprehensive environment for MAS development, and LightJason, known for its scalability. Other languages like SARL and PLASA are also examined for their unique capabilities in agent-oriented programming.

4. **Simulation and Modeling**: The paper discusses methodologies and tools for agent-based modeling and simulation, such as the GAMA platform, which integrates BDI architectures for complex simulations.

5. **Cognitive Robotics**: Belle and Levesque contribute to the field with action languages like PREGO and ALLEGRO, aimed at belief-based cognitive robotics, emphasizing their application in dynamic and stochastic environments.

Overall, the references illustrate a rich landscape of research and development in multi-agent systems, programming languages, and methodologies aimed at enhancing the capabilities and efficiency of agent-based applications.

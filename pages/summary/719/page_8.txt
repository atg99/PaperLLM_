This paper introduces a two-layer neural network for defining a policy πθ(a|s) in an energy-based framework as given in equation (3.2). Here, f((s,a);θ) represents the energy function, with θ as the weight parameter and τ serving as the temperature parameter. The authors explore policy gradient methods applicable to this class of neural policies, defining a feature mapping φθ through the neural network.

The paper establishes the closed forms for the policy gradient ∇θJ(πθ) and the Fisher information matrix F(θ) for the defined policy. Proposition 3.1 outlines that the policy gradient can be expressed as a function of the expected action-value function Qπθ, with the Fisher information matrix also derived from the feature mapping.

To address the challenge of the unknown action-value function Qπθ, the authors propose using another two-layer neural network Qω(·,·) to estimate it. This leads to the implementation of an actor-critic scheme, where πθ is referred to as the actor and Qω as the critic. This approach allows for improved policy evaluation and optimization within the framework established.

The references listed focus on various aspects of machine learning, particularly deep learning and reinforcement learning. Key contributions include:

1. Cao and Gu (2019a) provide generalization bounds for stochastic gradient descent in wide and deep neural networks, while their follow-up work (2019b) discusses a generalization theory for over-parameterized deep ReLU networks.

2. Castro and Meir (2010) present a convergent online single time scale actor-critic algorithm, contributing to the reinforcement learning literature.

3. Chen and Jiang (2019) explore information-theoretic considerations in batch reinforcement learning, which is crucial for understanding the efficiency of learning algorithms.

4. Chizat and Bach (2018) note the implications of lazy training in supervised differentiable programming, highlighting an important aspect of model training dynamics.

5. Daniely (2017) shows that stochastic gradient descent learns the conjugate kernel class of neural networks, providing theoretical insights into the learning process.

6. Du et al. (2018a, 2018b) demonstrate that gradient descent can find global minima in deep neural networks and optimize over-parameterized networks, offering a theoretical foundation for the effectiveness of these techniques.

7. Duan et al. (2016) benchmark deep reinforcement learning methods for continuous control, providing insights into performance across various algorithms.

8. Espeholt et al. (2018) introduce IMPALA, a scalable distributed architecture for deep reinforcement learning, emphasizing the importance of efficient learning frameworks.

9. Fan et al. (2019) provide an overview of deep learning developments, summarizing key advancements and trends in the field.

10. Farahmand et al. (2016) discuss regularized policy iteration within nonparametric function spaces, contributing to the understanding of policy optimization in reinforcement learning.

Overall, these works collectively advance the understanding of learning algorithms, their theoretical foundations, and practical applications in deep learning and reinforcement learning contexts.

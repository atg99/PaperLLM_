This section discusses neural policy gradient methods, specifically focusing on the actor-critic framework, which involves generating a sequence of policies and action-value functions. The goal is to optimize the policy parameter iteratively through gradient-based methods. 

The actor update is formulated as an optimization problem where the policy parameters are updated using a projection operator onto a defined parameter space. The update rule incorporates a learning rate and an estimator of the policy gradient, which is calculated using a sampled state-action visitation measure.

To derive an unbiased estimator of the policy gradient, sampling from the visitation measure is essential. An artificial Markov Decision Process (MDP) is introduced to facilitate this sampling, modifying the transition kernel to blend the original dynamics with a stationary distribution. This approach allows for effective sampling and subsequent policy updates within the framework of policy gradient methods.

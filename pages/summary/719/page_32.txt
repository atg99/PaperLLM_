The references provided encompass significant contributions to the fields of reinforcement learning and neural networks. 

Farahmand et al. (2010) explore error propagation in approximate policy and value iteration, highlighting challenges in achieving convergence in these iterative methods. Fazel et al. (2018) demonstrate the global convergence of policy gradient methods specifically in the context of the linear quadratic regulator, affirming their robustness. Funahashi (1989) discusses the capacity of neural networks to approximate continuous mappings, laying foundational work for neural network applications.

Haarnoja et al. (2017) introduce deep energy-based policies for reinforcement learning, offering a novel framework for policy optimization. Jacot et al. (2018) present the neural tangent kernel, analyzing its implications for convergence and generalization in neural networks. Johnson and Zhang (2013) propose a method to accelerate stochastic gradient descent through predictive variance reduction, enhancing the efficiency of optimization processes.

Kakade and Langford (2002) investigate approximately optimal reinforcement learning, while Kakade (2002) introduces the natural policy gradient, an important advancement in policy optimization techniques. Karimi et al. (2019) provide a non-asymptotic analysis of biased stochastic approximation schemes, contributing to understanding convergence rates in stochastic methods.

Klusowski and Barron (2016) establish risk bounds for high-dimensional ridge functions, including neural networks, advancing the theoretical understanding of neural network performance. Konda (2002) and Konda & Tsitsiklis (2000) focus on actor-critic algorithms, which combine policy and value function approximation, proving influential in reinforcement learning methodologies. Finally, Kushner and Yin (2003) offer comprehensive insights into stochastic approximation and recursive algorithms, providing a broad context for their applications.

Lazaric et al. (2016) analyze classification-based policy iteration algorithms, contributing to the understanding of policy improvement techniques in machine learning. Overall, these works collectively advance the understanding of optimization and learning in neural networks and reinforcement learning frameworks.

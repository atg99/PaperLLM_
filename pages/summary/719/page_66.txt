The paper discusses the upper bounding of certain expectations related to parameters in a probabilistic model. It introduces simplified notations for functions φθi and φωi, focusing on the mathematical expressions involving gradients and expectations. The key focus is on the bound of the expressions related to the differences between two vectors, ωi and δi, both of which reside within a defined set B. 

Using previously established propositions, the authors derive explicit forms for the expectations of functions and gradients conditioned on σi, θi, and ωi. Through a series of inequalities, including the application of the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality, the paper shows how to bound these expectations effectively. 

The results culminate in the establishment of bounds that relate the differences between the vectors and the expectations of their respective functions, leading to a clear understanding of the relationships between the parameters involved. The final inequalities provide a structured way to analyze the behavior of these parameters under the constraints defined in the paper, emphasizing the importance of the gradient norms and the statistical properties of the model.

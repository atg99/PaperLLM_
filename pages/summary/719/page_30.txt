This reference list includes a variety of scholarly articles and preprints, primarily focused on advancements in machine learning, neural networks, and reinforcement learning. Key topics covered involve policy gradient methods in Markov decision processes, learning and generalization in over-parameterized neural networks, and convergence theories for deep learning. Significant contributions include studies on natural gradients and their efficiency in learning, methods for learning near-optimal policies, and the implications of over-parameterization on optimization and generalization. The references also highlight important theoretical advancements, such as approximation and estimation bounds for neural networks and the application of stochastic approximation in dynamic systems. Overall, this compilation serves as a foundation for understanding current trends and methodologies in these areas of research.

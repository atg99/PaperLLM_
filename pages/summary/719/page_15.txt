The paper discusses regularity conditions imposed on the Markov transition kernel P of a Markov Decision Process (MDP), specifically in Assumption 4.2. This condition is significant as it ensures that both the state distribution νπ and the action distribution ρπ are bounded for all policies π. Following this, Proposition 4.3 is presented, which characterizes the convergence of the critic update in a neural Temporal Difference (TD) learning context.

In this proposition, a specific learning rate ηTD is defined, and the output of the critic update, denoted as Qωi, is analyzed. Under Assumptions 4.1 and 4.2, it is shown that the expected squared error between the critic's output and the actual Q-value, Qπθi, decays at a rate dependent on the number of iterations TTD and the width of the neural network m. The convergence is refined compared to previous work by Cai et al. (2019) due to a more restrictive assumption that Qπ belongs to a certain function space, allowing for a tighter upper bound on the mean squared error.

The paper also introduces Assumption 4.4, which addresses the variance of the policy gradient estimator. It states that there exists a constant σξ such that the expected squared deviation of the estimator from its mean is bounded, ensuring stable learning in the actor update phase.

Overall, the paper establishes key conditions and results that contribute to understanding the convergence properties of neural TD methods in the context of reinforcement learning.

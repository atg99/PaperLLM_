The paper presents Lemma F.1, which discusses the performance difference between two policies, denoted as π and 𝜋̃. The lemma states that the difference in expected returns, J(𝜋̃) - J(π), can be expressed in terms of the state visitation measure ν(𝜋̃) associated with the policy 𝜋̃. Specifically, the relationship is given by the equation:

J(𝜋̃) - J(π) = (1 - γ)⁻¹ · E[𝜋̃ · ν(𝜋̃) ⟨Aₗ(s, a)⟩],

where Aₗ(s, a) represents the advantage function under policy π. The proof for this lemma can be found in the work of Kakade and Langford from 2002.

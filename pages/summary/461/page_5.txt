The Ev-Seg dataset is an enhancement of the DDD17 dataset, which consists of various driving scenarios recorded in urban and motorway settings. While the DDD17 dataset provides synchronized grayscale and event-based data, it lacks semantic segmentation labels. The Ev-Seg extension addresses this by automatically generating semantic segmentation labels to serve as ground truth for a substantial portion of the dataset.

To create these labels, the authors trained a convolutional neural network (CNN) using grayscale images from the Cityscapes dataset, achieving an 83% mean Intersection over Union (MIoU) for semantic segmentation on validation data. This model's output was then used to segment the grayscale images from selected sequences of the DDD17 dataset. The authors implemented a post-processing step to eliminate noisy data by cropping the bottom part of the images, where dashboard elements could interfere with the segmentation quality.

The selection of sequences for label generation was based on several criteria, including the need for urban scenarios and suitable lighting conditions. Only six sequences met these requirements, and detailed manual annotations were performed on the selected intervals.

In summary, the Ev-Seg dataset facilitates further research in event-based segmentation by providing a robust set of labeled data derived from grayscale images, thereby enhancing the DDD17 dataset's utility for semantic segmentation tasks.

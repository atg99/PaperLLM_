This list of references encompasses a variety of studies and advancements in the fields of computer vision, deep learning, and semantic segmentation. The works cited span from foundational research on convolutional neural networks and image recognition techniques to innovative applications of event-based cameras and their integration with machine learning for tasks such as object detection, pose estimation, and 3D reconstruction. Key contributions include methods for improving semantic segmentation and leveraging depth information from monocular images, as well as the development of specialized datasets for training and evaluating autonomous vehicle systems. The references reflect the ongoing evolution of methodologies aimed at enhancing the accuracy and efficiency of visual perception systems across diverse applications.

The experimental setup focuses on evaluating semantic segmentation using event cameras, with the main metrics being Accuracy and Mean Intersection over Union (MIoU). The study acknowledges challenges due to domain shifts between the Cityscapes dataset and the DDD17 dataset, particularly in accurately predicting certain classes. The CNN model is trained from scratch, incorporating a variety of data augmentation techniques to enhance robustness.

The results demonstrate that a well-designed input representation significantly impacts the CNN's ability to learn effectively. The proposed event encoding method shows consistent performance improvements across various metrics compared to other encoding methods. Overall, the findings underscore the importance of input representation and training parameters in achieving accurate semantic segmentation in event-based vision systems.

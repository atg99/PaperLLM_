This collection of references highlights significant advancements in the field of computer vision, particularly focusing on event-based systems and deep learning methodologies. The papers cover various topics including real-time tracking and mapping using event cameras, robust object classification through histograms of averaged time surfaces, and the effectiveness of large data sets in deep learning. Notable works include the development of a multi-filter convolutional neural network for semantic segmentation, a pyramid scene parsing network, and innovative approaches to 3D reconstruction and optical flow estimation with event cameras. These studies contribute to a deeper understanding of visual perception and processing, demonstrating the potential of event-based technologies in enhancing traditional computer vision techniques.

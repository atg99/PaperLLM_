This paper discusses a novel architecture for integrating visual and textual data, specifically focusing on two models: the Dual Encoder architecture with late fusion and the B2T2 architecture with early fusion. The models utilize ResNet-152 for visual feature extraction and BERT for processing text. 

In the Dual Encoder architecture, a single visual feature vector is extracted from the entire image, ignoring bounding boxes. The B2T2 architecture incorporates bounding boxes mentioned in the text to enhance grounding between language and images. The paper outlines the loss functions used for training, including binary cross-entropy for impostor identification and a masked language model loss for positive captions. The final objective combines these losses, adjusting for the presence of positive labels.

The authors pretrain their models on the Conceptual Captions dataset, which contains over 3 million images and captions, leading to improved performance and stability in training. They also highlight implementation details, noting the use of fixed ResNet-152 parameters while fine-tuning BERT components for the new tasks. Overall, the paper emphasizes the importance of pretraining and the effectiveness of their approach in grounding language with images.

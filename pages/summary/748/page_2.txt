Lingvo is a TensorFlow framework designed for collaborative deep learning research, particularly for sequence-to-sequence models. It features modular building blocks that allow for flexibility and extensibility, with centralized and customizable experiment configurations. The framework supports distributed training and quantized inference, along with a variety of utilities and research implementations. Over the past two years, Lingvo has facilitated collaboration among researchers, contributing to over 20 academic papers. This document provides an overview of Lingvo's design and introduces its components, highlighting advanced features that demonstrate the framework's capabilities.

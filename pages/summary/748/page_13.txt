The section discusses two training methodologies: asynchronous and synchronous training, along with multi-task models and inference techniques.

In asynchronous training, the Trainer runs the training operation and sends variable updates to the ParameterServer, where variable values are stored. An optional Data Processor may be utilized to handle data loading and preprocessing, reducing the workload on the Trainer.

Conversely, synchronous training involves Worker jobs that execute the training operation without updating variables. Instead, a Trainer client manages the training loop, aggregates results, and sends variable updates directly to Worker jobs, which also serve as parameter servers, eliminating the need for a separate ParameterServer.

Multi-task models consist of individual Tasks that share variables. Variable sharing can be configured from simple sharing of the encoder to more detailed options. Multi-task model parameters should extend MultiTaskModelParams and implement the Model method to configure a MultiTaskModel. Task parameters and weights are defined through task_params and task_probs attributes. Knowledge distillation is also possible, requiring teacher parameters to be loaded from a checkpoint.

Finally, after training, models must be deployed for inference, often on devices using fixed-point arithmetic. Maintaining dynamic range during training is crucial for quality, and quantized layers are provided to facilitate training and inference computations.

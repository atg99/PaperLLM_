The papers referenced focus on advancements in neural network systems for speech recognition and machine translation. 

The first paper by Williams et al. discusses the implementation of contextual speech recognition in end-to-end neural network systems utilizing beam search techniques. This approach aims to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of speech recognition by considering the context of spoken language, which is crucial for improving the understanding of natural language processing systems.

The second paper by Wu et al. presents Google's Neural Machine Translation system, which seeks to close the performance gap between human and machine translation. The authors detail the architecture and training processes of the system, highlighting its ability to produce more fluent and accurate translations by leveraging deep learning techniques.

Both studies contribute significantly to their respective fields, showcasing the potential of neural networks in improving language processing tasks.

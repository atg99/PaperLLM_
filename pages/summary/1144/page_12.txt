The paper presents a detailed evaluation of the wall-following capabilities of a robot equipped with two fuzzy logic systems: T1-FLS and IT2-FLS. The results demonstrate the effectiveness of these systems in navigating both inner and outer wall segments, including complex geometries such as right-angle corners and curved walls. The experiments included various wall configurations, showcasing the robot's ability to adapt to different scenarios, including segments with small positive and negative dents. The findings validate the proposed systems' performance in maintaining accurate wall-following behavior under diverse conditions.

The system is designed for operation in unknown environments, utilizing Time of Flight (ToF) range sensors and sonar sensors for wall detection. The arrangement of these sensors allows the robot to perceive its surroundings accurately, with specific configurations covering the front and left regions to mimic human perception. The robot calculates its lateral distance from the wall, defined as dS, and continuously compares it to the target distance, dT, to minimize the lateral error, ed. 

The Fuzzy Logic System (FLS) processes the lateral error and its rate of change to determine the necessary adjustments in the robot's movement, facilitating wall-following behavior. This methodology ensures that the robot can maneuver effectively while performing disinfection tasks, maintaining optimal distance for both spray coverage and ultraviolet intensity.

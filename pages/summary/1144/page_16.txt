The paper presents an analysis of the wall-following ability of an Interval Type-2 Fuzzy Logic System (IT2-FLS) in robotic navigation. In the validation's third phase, the performance was evaluated by adjusting different lateral distances (dT) of 0.5 m, 0.75 m, and 1.0 m. The results indicated that the IT2-FLS demonstrated superior performance in wall-following tasks compared to other methods. 

Figure 11 illustrates the mean average lateral error during the second phase of validation, with error bars representing standard error and a corresponding box plot detailing the distribution of errors. Figure 12 showcases the wall-following behavior of the IT2-FLS across various dT settings, highlighting the robot's navigation along inner and outer wall segments in both right-angle and curved corners. This analysis emphasizes the effectiveness of the IT2-FLS in maintaining desired lateral distances while navigating complex environments.

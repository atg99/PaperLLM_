The paper presents a comparative analysis of different fuzzy logic systems, specifically Type 1 Fuzzy Logic Systems (T1-FLS) and Interval Type 2 Fuzzy Logic Systems (IT2-FLS), during various test cases. The results demonstrate the performance variations in inputs and outputs across these systems, as illustrated in Figure 9. Each case represents a distinct scenario where the systems are tested, showcasing their responsiveness and adaptability to changes over time. The analysis aims to highlight the strengths and weaknesses of T1-FLS and IT2-FLS in handling uncertainty and decision-making processes.

The paper discusses the contributions of various authors to a research project related to robotics and its applications in public health, particularly in the context of infectious diseases such as COVID-19. The authors, including M.A.V.J.M., S.M.B.P.S., and M.R.E., collaborated on conceptualization, methodology, validation, and writing of the manuscript, with M.R.E. overseeing the project and securing funding from the National Robotics Programme.

The research highlights the importance of robotics in managing public health crises, emphasizing the role of robotic systems in disinfection and disease containment. It references various studies and patents related to robotic disinfection technologies and their deployment in healthcare settings, showcasing the effectiveness of these innovations in reducing the transmission of infectious diseases.

The paper concludes with an acknowledgment of the lack of conflicts of interest among the authors and the funding agencies, and it includes a comprehensive list of references that support the findings and discussions presented.

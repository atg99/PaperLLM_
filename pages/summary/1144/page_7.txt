The paper discusses the aggregation and defuzzification processes in fuzzy logic systems, specifically focusing on the outputs of angular velocity and linear velocity. The fuzzy consequents from multiple rules are combined using the fuzzy max operator to form a single fuzzy set for each output. The defuzzification layer then converts these fuzzy sets into deterministic control actions through the center of the area method, resulting in crisp outputs for angular velocity (w) and linear velocity (v).

The decision surface of the proposed Type 1 Fuzzy Logic System (T1-FLS) is illustrated, showing how the outputs vary with respect to inputs. Additionally, the paper introduces Interval Type 2 Fuzzy Logic Systems (IT2-FLSs), which offer enhanced effectiveness in handling uncertainties compared to T1-FLSs. IT2-FLSs are noted for their simpler structure and lower computational demands, making them suitable for practical applications.

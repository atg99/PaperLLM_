The references provided encompass a wide range of research on neural style transfer, focusing on techniques for transforming images and videos into artistic styles using deep learning methods. Key contributions include:

1. Foundational works by Gatys et al. (2015, 2016) that introduced texture synthesis and image style transfer using convolutional neural networks, laying the groundwork for subsequent research.
2. Innovations in real-time and online style transfer, such as the work by Huang and Belongie (2017) and Huang et al. (2017), which focused on adaptive instance normalization and fast patch-based methods.
3. Development of explicit representations for style transfer, as seen in the StyleBank approach (Chen et al., 2017).
4. Advances in video style transfer, including techniques to maintain coherence across frames (Chen et al., 2017) and methods for real-time processing (Huang et al., 2017).
5. Exploration of perceptual factors in style transfer, as discussed by Gatys et al. (2017), and the combination of different neural network architectures to enhance synthesis quality (Li and Wand, 2016).
6. Comprehensive reviews of the neural style transfer landscape, such as Jing et al. (2017), which consolidate various approaches and findings in the field.

These references highlight the evolution of techniques and applications in neural style transfer, reflecting both theoretical advancements and practical implementations in image processing.

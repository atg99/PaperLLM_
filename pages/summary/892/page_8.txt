The paper presents a novel interpretation of neural style transfer as an optimal transport problem, highlighting the theoretical connections to previous methods such as Adaptive Instance Normalization (AdaIN) and Wasserstein Color Transfer (WCT). The authors introduce a closed-form solution that incorporates content loss, resulting in better structural preservation compared to earlier approaches. They acknowledge the subjective nature of style transfer and the challenges in mathematically modeling human perceptions of style. The results demonstrate the generalization ability of their method, and the authors express hope that their work will inspire further research in the field. The research was supported by various funding programs in China.

The paper discusses a method for style transfer based on the equation T= Σ1/2_s Q Σ−1/2_c, where Q is a unit orthogonal matrix. While this formulation satisfies the required equation, the resulting style transfer outcomes can vary significantly, indicating that WCT is not the only solution. The authors aim to identify an optimal solution by incorporating content loss, as proposed by Gatys. 

The formulation for achieving this optimal solution is presented as minimizing the expectation of the content loss while adhering to a specific covariance structure. The authors derive a closed-form solution through a series of mathematical transformations, leading to a reformulation of the problem into a trace maximization problem involving covariance matrices. 

A key lemma is introduced, showing that the covariance structure of two high-dimensional distributions can be used to derive a unique solution for the trace maximization. The final representation of T is derived as T= Σ−1/2_c (Σ1/2_c Σ_s Σ1/2_c)1/2 Σ−1/2_c. 

The proposed method claims to better preserve structural integrity compared to previous approaches, providing a comprehensive theoretical foundation for the technique. The authors believe their contributions will influence future research in neural style transfer.

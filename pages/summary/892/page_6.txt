The paper presents a comparison of various style transfer methods including Gatys, Patch Swap, AdaIN, AdaIN+, and WCT, alongside the authors' proposed approach. The qualitative results, illustrated in Figure 3, demonstrate that while AdaIN overlooks non-diagonal elements of covariance matrices leading to less stylization, WCT fails to preserve the structure of the content image due to its disregard for content loss. In contrast, the authors' method successfully balances stylization with content preservation, achieving superior results as highlighted in the red rectangles.

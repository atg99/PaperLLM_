The paper discusses various approaches utilizing Deep Q-Learning (DQL) to enhance the Quality of Experience (QoE) for Internet of Things (IoT) devices in content-centric wireless networks. The authors propose a DQL framework that optimizes both cache allocation and transmission rates by considering the caching conditions and transmission rates of the nodes. This framework incorporates Prioritized Experience Replay (PER) and Double DQN (DDQN) to improve learning efficiency and stability, respectively. Simulation results indicate that this enhanced DQL framework significantly outperforms traditional penetration test schemes in maximizing QoE.

Additionally, the authors address the challenge of content caching and transmission in wireless Virtual Reality (VR) networks. They propose a DQL framework that combines Liquid State Machines (LSM) and Echo State Networks (ESN) to optimize caching and transmission strategies for base stations (BSs) serving VR users. This approach leverages historical information to improve user reliability and reduces training complexity compared to conventional methods. Simulation results demonstrate a substantial improvement in user reliability over baseline Q-learning.

Furthermore, the paper explores the integration of caching, networking, and computation in heterogeneous networks (Het-Nets). The authors propose a DQL framework for energy-efficient resource allocation in green wireless networks, accounting for the interactions between networking, caching, and computation. This framework is applied to enhance performance in Vehicular Ad hoc Networks (VANETs), where resource allocation is optimized for caching, networking, and computational tasks. The authors detail enhancements to DQN using Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and introduce strategies such as DDQN and dueling DQN to improve training stability and performance. Overall, the paper presents a comprehensive view of using DQL to address complex challenges in modern wireless networks.

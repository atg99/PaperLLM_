2) Dueling Network Architecture: Another enhancement is the Dueling Network Architecture, which separates the representation of state values and action advantages within the DQN framework. This architecture enables the model to learn the value of being in a particular state regardless of the actions taken, leading to improved performance, especially in environments with many actions.

3) Prioritized Experience Replay: This method improves the experience replay mechanism by prioritizing important transitions over less significant ones. By focusing on experiences that have a higher impact on learning, the algorithm can learn more efficiently and effectively.

In summary, the paper discusses various deep learning techniques, particularly focusing on Deep Q-Learning and its advanced models, including Double Deep Q-Learning, Dueling Network Architecture, and Prioritized Experience Replay. These techniques aim to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of reinforcement learning in complex environments, addressing issues such as overestimation bias and enhancing the learning process through better experience management.

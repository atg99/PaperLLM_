The paper presents a comprehensive summary of the applications of Deep Reinforcement Learning (DQL) in various areas such as traffic engineering, resource scheduling, and data collection. It categorizes different models, learning algorithms, agent states, actions, rewards, and scenarios relevant to these applications.

Key findings include:
- In traffic engineering, DQL methods like MDP-DQN and POMDP-DQN are employed to optimize bandwidth requests, traffic load distribution, and navigation for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs). Metrics such as mean network delay and fairness utility are utilized as rewards.
- For resource scheduling, MDP-DQN approaches are used to manage demands from multiple users and optimize power consumption in cloud-based systems while considering Quality of Service (QoS) and Quality of Experience (QoE).
- Data collection applications utilize MDP-DQN to enhance the efficiency of mobile sensor networks and crowdsensing platforms, focusing on factors such as signal strength and payment policies.

The paper also outlines several challenges and future research directions in the field:
1. Determining states in dense networks can be difficult due to the homogeneity of Received Signal Strength Indicators (RSSIs) from multiple base stations.
2. The need for precise knowledge of jammers' channel information poses a significant challenge for security-focused DQL applications.
3. Implementing multi-agent DQL in dynamic heterogeneous networks remains problematic, as current frameworks often rely on static network conditions for effective learning and policy stability.

Overall, the study highlights the potential of DRL in addressing complex networking issues while also identifying critical areas for further exploration and development.

This paper provides a thorough survey of the applications of deep reinforcement learning (DRL) in the fields of communications and networking. As networks evolve towards greater decentralization and autonomy, particularly in domains like the Internet of Things (IoT) and Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) networks, there is a pressing need for network entities to make local decisions that enhance overall performance amidst environmental uncertainties. Traditional reinforcement learning has been effective for smaller state and action spaces but struggles with the complexities of large-scale networks. 

To address these challenges, DRL combines reinforcement learning with deep learning techniques, significantly improving learning efficiency and policy optimization. The paper begins with a foundational overview of DRL, progressing to advanced models. It then reviews various DRL applications targeting emerging issues in networking, including dynamic access, data rate control, wireless caching, data offloading, security, and connectivity preservation, all crucial for next-generation networks like 5G.

Key applications discussed encompass traffic routing, resource sharing, and data collection. The authors also identify critical challenges and open research questions within the domain of DRL applications in communications and networking, suggesting potential future research directions to advance the field. Overall, the survey highlights the transformative potential of DRL in optimizing network performance and addressing the complexities of modern communication systems.

The paper presents a summary of various approaches utilizing Deep Q-Learning (DQL) for caching and offloading in different systems. Each approach is categorized based on issues addressed, model type, learning algorithms, agent states, actions, rewards, and networks.

Key findings include:

1. **Wireless Proactive Caching**: Utilizes different DQL methods such as DQN with actor-critic and DDPG to optimize cache hit rates based on cached and requested content.

2. **Channel State Management**: Several models apply DQL to manage channel states and computational capabilities, focusing on user association and energy consumption.

3. **Cloud Database Optimization**: DQL is used to reduce cache miss rates and improve CDN utilization through encoding queries.

4. **Multi-User Systems**: Models employing DQL manage active users and resource allocation to maximize network throughput in multi-user MIMO systems.

5. **Content-Centric IoT**: DQL techniques optimize caching and resource allocation while balancing network costs and Quality of Experience (QoE).

6. **Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks**: DQL strategies are implemented to enhance revenue through effective user association, caching, and offloading.

7. **Mobile User Offloading**: DQL is applied to optimize offloading decisions based on user location, file size, and channel quality, aiming to maximize total data rates and minimize long-term costs.

8. **Cyber-Physical Security**: DQL helps autonomous vehicles learn optimal responses to cyber-physical attacks, enhancing their robustness against manipulation.

9. **Connectivity Preservation**: DQL facilitates dynamic decision-making among robots, ensuring ongoing connectivity and communication in complex environments.

Overall, DQL serves as a powerful tool in various domains to improve caching strategies, resource allocation, and security measures in dynamic and complex systems.

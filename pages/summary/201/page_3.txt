The authors present a taxonomy of these applications, highlighting the relevance of adaptive rate control, proactive caching, data offloading, and network security.

The paper begins with an overview of deep reinforcement learning, explaining its foundation in Markov decision processes (MDPs), reinforcement learning, and deep learning techniques. MDPs are defined as a framework for modeling decision-making problems with stochastic outcomes. The authors emphasize the importance of finding an optimal policy that maximizes rewards in both MDPs and partially observable MDPs (POMDPs), where only partial information about the state is available. 

The paper also introduces Markov games, which involve multiple agents making decisions in a dynamic environment. Each agent's actions influence the overall system state and their respective rewards, highlighting the complexity of interactions in multi-agent scenarios.

In conclusion, the paper outlines future directions for research in DRL applications within communications and networking while providing a comprehensive understanding of the foundational concepts and algorithms, notably deep Q-learning and policy gradients, that underpin these advancements.

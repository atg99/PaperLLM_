The application of Deep Q-Learning (DQL) can enhance anti-jamming strategies by optimizing the selection of frequency hopping patterns and mobility decisions based on real-time channel conditions. By continuously learning from the environment, the DQL agent can adaptively respond to jamming threats, improving the robustness of communication.

In addition to jamming attacks, DQL has been employed to address Denial-of-Service (DoS) attacks, where the objective is to overload network resources and disrupt services. DQL can be utilized to develop proactive defense mechanisms that identify abnormal traffic patterns indicative of a DoS attack. By analyzing historical data and current network states, the DQL agent can dynamically allocate resources and reroute traffic to mitigate the effects of an ongoing attack, ensuring service continuity and enhancing network resilience.

Furthermore, DQL can assist in maintaining connectivity in decentralized networks. As these networks evolve, ensuring reliable communication among nodes becomes critical, especially in the presence of adversarial actions. DQL can be leveraged to optimize routing protocols, enabling nodes to learn effective paths while avoiding compromised nodes or areas prone to attacks. By continuously updating its knowledge base, the DQL agent can enhance the overall connectivity and performance of the network.

In summary, the integration of DQL in addressing security challenges in future networks showcases its potential as a powerful tool for enhancing network resilience against various attacks. The capacity to learn and adapt in real-time presents a significant advantage in maintaining secure and reliable communication in increasingly complex and decentralized environments.

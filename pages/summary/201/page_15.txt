In the reviewed applications of DQL for dynamic network access and adaptive rate control, the authors highlight the use of a video quality prediction network to improve Quality of Experience (QoE) in video streaming. By employing convolutional and recurrent neural networks to predict video quality, the DQL approach manages to reduce latency and enhance QoE significantly compared to existing solutions like Google Hangout. Additionally, DQL's effectiveness extends to High Volume Flexible Time (HVFT) applications, where it optimizes data rate control in cellular networks by formulating traffic scheduling as a Markov Decision Process (MDP). The DQL implementation demonstrates a substantial increase in HVFT traffic while minimizing losses to existing applications.

The authors also discuss the utilization of DQL in complex communication systems with multiple conflicting objectives, such as in future space communication scenarios. Here, DQL manages the trade-offs between various performance metrics by employing multiple neural networks to adaptively configure transmit parameters based on dynamic conditions.

The summary emphasizes the increasing attention on DQL applications in IoT and DASH systems, highlighting the need for advanced resource management mechanisms in future networks with diverse objectives.

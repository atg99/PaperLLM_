This paper provides a thorough survey of deep reinforcement learning (DRL) applications in communications and networking. It begins with an overview of reinforcement learning, deep learning, and DRL, followed by a discussion of various DRL techniques and their extensions. The authors analyze how DRL can address multiple issues in the field, including dynamic network access, data rate control, wireless caching, data offloading, network security, connectivity preservation, traffic routing, and data collection.

One specific application discussed is the use of DRL in managing cryptocurrency tokens for wireless users, who can opt to keep or spend these tokens for resource access or convert them into real money. Additionally, the paper highlights the effectiveness of DRL in auction scenarios for radio resource management, particularly in complex environments like 5G networks, where traditional auction solutions can be computationally challenging.

The paper concludes by identifying significant challenges and open issues in the field, as well as suggesting future research directions. Overall, it underscores the potential of DRL to enhance various aspects of communications and networking, paving the way for more efficient and intelligent systems.

Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed DQL-based approach significantly enhances the performance of the network compared to traditional methods. However, the complexity of the problem and the dynamic nature of user demands present challenges that require further research.

In conclusion, the paper highlights the effectiveness of DQL in addressing complex problems within wireless networks, such as dynamic spectrum access, channel allocation for new users, and joint user association and resource management. The proposed schemes show promising results in terms of throughput and user satisfaction, though future work is needed to refine these models and address scalability issues as user numbers grow.

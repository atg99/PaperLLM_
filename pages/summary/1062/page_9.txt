In this section, the authors analyze the contributions of three key components of TimeGAN: the supervised loss, embedding networks, and joint training. They assess their significance by modifying TimeGAN and reporting discriminative and predictive scores across multiple datasets, including Sines, Stocks, Energy, and Events.

The results indicate that all three elements enhance the quality of generated time-series data. Specifically, the supervised loss is crucial for datasets with high temporal correlations, such as Stocks. Additionally, the use of embedding networks and joint training with adversarial networks consistently improves generative performance.

In the conclusion, the authors present TimeGAN as a novel framework that combines unsupervised GAN methods with supervised autoregressive models to generate realistic time-series data. They highlight its superior performance over existing benchmarks and suggest future research could integrate differential privacy measures to ensure the generated data maintains privacy guarantees.

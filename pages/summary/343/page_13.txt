The paper discusses the nature, scope, limits, and consequences of GPT-3 in the context of the digital age, emphasizing the need for individuals and societies to understand and utilize the advantages of advanced digital solutions while mitigating their downsides. It highlights the challenges involved in adapting to this "onlife" condition, as described by Floridi, and stresses the urgency of starting this process in various environments such as homes, schools, and workplaces. The commentary is presented as a reflection of human semantics, free from digital enhancements, and acknowledges the potential for negative reactions from those skeptical of technology. The authors express gratitude to individuals who provided support and feedback for the article while clarifying their responsibility for any remaining issues. The paper is published under a Creative Commons license, allowing for sharing and adaptation with proper attribution. The references list includes works that explore topics related to AI's impact, biases, and creative outputs, providing a broader context for the discussion.

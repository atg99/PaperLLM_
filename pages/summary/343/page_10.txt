The paper by L. Floridi and M. Chiriatti argues that individuals seeking ethical advice from GPT-3 should instead rely on their own moral judgment. They assert that while GPT-3 is an impressive technology, it lacks true intelligence and consciousness, likening it to an old typewriter rather than the advanced AI depicted in popular media. The authors highlight the implications of GPT-3's capabilities, noting that despite its limitations in handling mathematical, semantic, and ethical inquiries, it produces written content that often surpasses human quality. This advancement signifies a new era where AI can generate a variety of textual outputs—such as translations, summaries, and articles—potentially reducing the need for human authorship in many writing tasks.

This paper discusses the capabilities and limitations of GPT-3, a language model that generates text based on prompts provided in plain language. The authors explore its performance by prompting GPT-3 to continue an excerpt from Jane Austen's unfinished novel "Sanditon" and a famous sonnet by Dante. The results illustrate how GPT-3 can produce text that diverges from the original author's intent, highlighting the model's interpretative nature. The authors note that while the generated text can be interesting and coherent, the quality improves with more detailed prompts. Additionally, they mention the model's impressive performance in Italian despite its training predominantly being in English, indicating its versatility across languages. Overall, the paper emphasizes the model's ability to generate plausible text while recognizing the differences in context and intent compared to the original works.

This collection of references presents significant advancements in various fields, particularly in speech processing and neural networks. 

Wang et al. propose alternative objective functions for deep clustering, enhancing the effectiveness of clustering techniques in audio signal processing. Wichern et al. introduce WHAM!, a system that extends speech separation capabilities to noisy environments, showcasing its robustness in real-world applications. Xu et al. investigate the compression and reflection of visually evoked cortical waves, contributing to our understanding of neural responses to visual stimuli.

Yin et al. focus on end-to-end face parsing using interlinked convolutional neural networks, which improves accuracy in facial recognition tasks. Zhang et al. present Furcanext, a novel approach for monaural speech separation employing dynamic gated dilated temporal convolutional networks, emphasizing advancements in audio signal separation. Zhao et al. introduce a multi-scale group transformer for modeling long sequences in speech separation, addressing challenges in processing extensive audio inputs. Lastly, Zhou et al. also explore interlinked convolutional neural networks for face parsing, reinforcing the importance of this architecture in computer vision tasks.

These works collectively underline the ongoing innovation in deep learning and its applications in speech and vision processing.

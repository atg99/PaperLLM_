This document presents a selection of recent publications by Reaktion Books, highlighting their focus on natural history and cultural interpretations. 

The first title, "Unearthing the Underworld: A Natural History of Rocks" by Ken McNamara, offers an insightful exploration of rocks as historical records of environmental changes over billions of years, priced at £18.00 for a hardback edition.

Next, "Wind: Nature and Culture" by Louise M Pryke examines the scientific aspects of wind alongside its representation in myth, religion, art, and literature throughout history, available in paperback for £16.95.

"Ulisse Aldrovandi: Naturalist and Collector" by Peter Mason presents a detailed biography of the 16th-century naturalist and polymath Ulisse Aldrovandi, priced at £17.95 in hardback.

Lastly, "Jupiter" by William Sheehan offers a richly illustrated overview of the planet Jupiter, available for £20.00 in paperback.

These publications reflect a commitment to exploring the intersection of natural history and cultural narratives.

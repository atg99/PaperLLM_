The upcoming publications from Johns Hopkins University Press include a diverse array of titles that explore connections in nature, the behavior of bees, the potential threat of fungal pathogens, and a unique approach to mathematics through drag culture. 

"Intertwined: From Insects to Icebergs" by Michael Gross, set for release in October 2024, emphasizes the interconnectedness of nature as a means to address contemporary challenges. "What Do Bees Think About?" by Mathieu Lihoreau, available in July 2024, delves into the cognitive processes driving bee behavior. Arturo Casadevall's "What If Fungi Win?", also releasing in July 2024, poses critical questions about the ability of fungal pathogens to outmaneuver scientific advancements in combating them. Lastly, "Math in Drag" by Kyne Santos, scheduled for May 2024, offers an engaging exploration of mathematics through the lens of drag performance, encouraging readers to embrace their mathematical creativity. Each title aims to provoke thought and inspire action in its respective field.

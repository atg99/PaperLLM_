This document presents a selection of upcoming and recently published books from Yale University Press, all under John Wiley & Sons Ltd. 

"The Unity of Science: Exploring Our Universe, from the Big Bang to the Twenty-First Century" by Irwin Shapiro is a hardback volume set to release in January 2024. It spans 376 pages and is priced at £20.00. The book aims to provide a scientific journey that delves into the universe, Earth, and the evolution of life.

"Force: What It Means to Push and Pull, Slip and Grip, Start and Stop" by Henry Petroski is a paperback scheduled for release in October 2023. With 328 pages, it is priced at £12.99. Petroski, an acclaimed engineer and historian, examines the fundamental concept of physical force in our lives.

"In the Herbarium: The Hidden World of Collecting and Preserving Plants" by Maura C. Flannery, a hardback published in July 2023, consists of 336 pages and is priced at £25.00. This work highlights the significance of herbaria in understanding both the history and future of plant science.

"Less Heat, More Light: A Guided Tour of Weather, Climate, and Climate Change" by John D. Aber is another hardback, released in July 2023. It contains 288 pages and is priced at £25.00. Aber's book offers a clear, fact-based examination of weather phenomena, their relationship to climate, and the scientific insights into Earth's systems. 

These titles reflect a commitment to exploring scientific concepts and their implications for understanding our world.

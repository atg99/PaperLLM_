The document presents a selection of upcoming academic titles published by Yale University Press, focusing on themes related to evolution, the mind, natural history, and environmental influences on biodiversity. 

1. **Monkey to Man: The Evolution of the March of Progress Image** by Gowan Dawson explores the iconic "march of progress" depiction of evolution and its influence on public understanding of human evolution. This hardback edition is set to release in May 2024, priced at £30.00.

2. **Psychonauts: Drugs and the Making of the Modern Mind** by Mike Jay offers a provocative history of figures who used drugs to delve into the complexities of the mind. This paperback edition will be available in February 2024 for £10.99.

3. **Curious Species: How Animals Made Natural History** by Whitney Barlow Robles examines the significant role animals played in the development of natural history and its ecological implications. This hardback is scheduled for release in January 2024, priced at £30.00.

4. **Born of Ice and Fire: How Glaciers and Volcanoes (with a Pinch of Salt) Drove Animal Evolution** by Graham Shields investigates the impact of the Cryogenian Period on the evolution of biodiversity. This hardback will also be released in January 2024 and will cost £20.00.

These titles collectively contribute to a deeper understanding of evolutionary processes, the relationship between drugs and cognitive exploration, the role of animals in natural history, and the environmental factors that have shaped life on Earth.

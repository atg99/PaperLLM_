The paper discusses the interplay between artificial intelligence (AI) and robotics, highlighting the emergence of "smart robots" that utilize machine learning to enhance performance. Both technologies enable automation, but there is an ongoing debate about their differing impacts on various tasks. Some researchers argue that AI can automate more complex, non-routine tasks compared to traditional robotics, which typically focuses on routine tasks.

Importantly, the paper notes that automation through AI or robotics does not necessarily eliminate human jobs. Instead, these technologies can take over low-value tasks, allowing humans to concentrate on more valuable work, thereby augmenting human labor rather than replacing it.

The authors also differentiate between AI and robotics versus information and communication technology (ICT). While ICT involves computer-based information systems aimed at improving productivity, AI and robotics extend beyond mere information processing to include sophisticated automation capabilities. This distinction is crucial because it affects the understanding and application of these technologies in organizational contexts.

The paper identifies key areas of interest, particularly regarding how AI and robotics influence productivity. It emphasizes the importance of innovation in economic growth and suggests that ongoing research into these technologies can yield significant insights for both theorists and policymakers.

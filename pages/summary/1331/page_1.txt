This article serves as a primer on artificial intelligence (AI) and robotics, highlighting their economic and organizational impacts. The authors, Manav Raj and Robert Seamans, emphasize the growing interest in AI and robotics within both the media and academic circles. They reference significant opinions from publications like Bloomberg and the Financial Times, which characterize AI as a major disruptive force and note the transformative impact of robotics on the workforce.

The paper discusses the increasing focus among scholars on the economic, social, and distributive consequences of these technologies, citing conferences that have gathered prominent economists to explore the economics of AI. Despite this growing interest in the broader implications of AI and robotics, organizational scholars have been slower to engage with these topics.

The authors argue that AI and robotics present unique opportunities for organizational researchers, suggesting that these technologies can influence organizational design and firm strategy. They call for greater engagement from scholars in organizational and strategy fields and propose directions for future research to better understand these evolving dynamics. The primer emphasizes the importance of this discourse in shaping the future of work and organizational structures in response to advancements in technology.

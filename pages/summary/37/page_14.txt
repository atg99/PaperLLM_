In this paper, Cheng et al. present a novel approach called Boundary-preserving Mask R-CNN (BMask R-CNN) aimed at addressing the challenges of coarse boundaries and imprecise localization in instance segmentation. The authors note that traditional Mask R-CNN tends to produce masks with unclear boundaries that include excessive background and false positives. BMask R-CNN improves upon this by incorporating boundary information, leading to clearer and more accurate masks that align closely with object boundaries. 

Qualitative results on the COCO dataset demonstrate the effectiveness of BMask R-CNN compared to Mask R-CNN, with visualizations indicating superior boundary preservation. The authors also highlight that refining masks using predicted boundaries yields minor improvements, though this process can be sensitive to noise. 

The experiments show significant and consistent enhancements in localization performance across both COCO and Cityscapes datasets. The authors suggest that BMask R-CNN can be integrated with other models, such as Cascade Mask R-CNN, to achieve even higher performance levels. The study concludes by emphasizing the potential of BMask R-CNN to serve as a strong baseline for future research in instance segmentation.

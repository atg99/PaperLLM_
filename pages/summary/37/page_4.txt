Cheng et al. discuss various advancements in instance segmentation techniques, emphasizing the importance of aligning mask quality with mask scores. They highlight methods such as Mask Scoring R-CNN, which addresses this misalignment, and improvements made in Cascade Mask R-CNN that integrate box and mask branches for enhanced spatial context.

The paper also explores segmentation-based methods that focus on pixel-level segmentation, with approaches like InstanceCut and SGN for grouping pixels into instances. Recent techniques leverage deep metric learning for instance-level segmentation by learning embeddings.

In the realm of boundary and edge detection, the authors review significant contributions, including the fully convolutional holistically-nested edge detector and CASENet, which targets category-aware boundary detection. They note the challenges posed by label misalignment in semantic boundary detection and the introduction of geometric aware loss functions for object skeleton detection.

The authors introduce BMask R-CNN, which uniquely predicts instance-level boundaries to enhance mask localization. They argue that boundaries in instance segmentation are closely related to masks, and propose fusion blocks to mutually learn boundary and mask features, thereby improving the overall representation and focus on boundaries during mask prediction. This approach aims to refine instance segmentation by preserving boundary information, ultimately leading to more accurate object localization.

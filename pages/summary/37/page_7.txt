The paper introduces Boundary-preserving Mask R-CNN, which enhances boundary detection in segmentation tasks. It first describes the generation of boundary ground truths using the Laplacian operator, which produces thin boundaries from binary mask ground truths. These boundaries are then converted into binary maps through thresholding.

To address the class imbalance in boundary prediction, the paper proposes a boundary loss that combines Dice loss and binary cross-entropy. While traditional methods rely on weighted binary cross-entropy, which can lead to thick and coarse boundaries, the proposed method utilizes Dice loss to better measure overlap between predicted and actual boundaries, thus being less sensitive to the distribution of foreground and background pixels. The boundary loss is formulated as a combination of these two losses.

The paper also emphasizes the importance of multi-task learning, which improves performance by jointly training on boundary and mask predictions. The overall loss function incorporates classification loss, regression loss, mask loss, and the proposed boundary loss.

Extensive experiments were conducted on the COCO and Cityscapes datasets to validate the effectiveness of the proposed method. The paper includes ablation studies to analyze the contributions of different components of the approach.

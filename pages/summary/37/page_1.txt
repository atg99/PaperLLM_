The paper introduces Boundary-preserving Mask R-CNN (BMask R-CNN), an enhanced version of Mask R-CNN aimed at improving mask localization accuracy in instance segmentation tasks. Traditional methods, particularly those relying on fully convolutional networks, often neglect object boundaries, resulting in imprecise and indistinct mask predictions. To address this issue, BMask R-CNN incorporates a boundary-preserving mask head that facilitates mutual learning between object boundaries and masks through feature fusion blocks. This innovative approach leads to better alignment of predicted masks with actual object boundaries.

The authors demonstrate that BMask R-CNN significantly outperforms the original Mask R-CNN on the COCO dataset and achieves even more substantial improvements on the Cityscapes dataset, where more accurate boundary ground truths are available. The performance gains are particularly evident when evaluating criteria demand precise localization, such as the AP 75 metric. The paper emphasizes the effectiveness of BMask R-CNN in enhancing instance segmentation by leveraging boundary information, thus contributing to advancements in various applications, including autonomous driving and robotics. The authors have made the code and models publicly available for further research.

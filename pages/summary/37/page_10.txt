The paper presents a comparison of various state-of-the-art methods for instance segmentation on the COCO test-dev2017 dataset, highlighting the performance of different models based on their backbone architectures. The results reveal that Mask R-CNN with ResNeXt-101-FPN achieves the highest average precision (AP) of 37.1, followed by BMask R-CNN, which improves upon Mask R-CNN by integrating additional techniques such as boundary supervision and feature fusion.

Subsequent experiments on the COCO val2017 dataset examine the impact of adding components to Mask R-CNN, including boundary supervision, feature fusions, and different loss functions. The results indicate that incorporating boundary features significantly enhances performance, with the BMask R-CNN model demonstrating a notable 1.5 AP improvement over Mask R-CNN when both mask and boundary features are fused.

The study also explores the effectiveness of various loss functions, concluding that a combination of Binary Cross Entropy (BCE) and Dice loss yields superior results for boundary learning. Overall, the findings underscore the importance of integrating boundary information and optimizing loss functions to enhance instance segmentation performance.

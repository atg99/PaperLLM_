The paper introduces Boundary-preserving Mask R-CNN (BMask R-CNN), a novel approach that enhances mask-level localization accuracy by explicitly utilizing object boundary information within the existing Mask R-CNN framework. The authors address the challenge of limited training pixels for boundary classification compared to mask classification by optimizing boundary learning through a combined boundary loss that integrates binary cross-entropy and dice loss.

Extensive experiments conducted on the COCO dataset reveal significant performance improvements of BMask R-CNN over the traditional Mask R-CNN, achieving a 1.7% increase in average precision (AP) on the COCO validation set and a 2.2% increase on the Cityscapes test set. These gains are particularly notable when higher precision in mask localization is required. The paper highlights that BMask R-CNN's effectiveness is evident in scenarios with fine-annotated masks.

The main contributions of the work include the introduction of BMask R-CNN, which effectively improves mask localization accuracy, detailed ablation studies on its various components, and insights into the workings of boundary features and losses. The paper also situates BMask R-CNN within the broader context of instance segmentation methods, distinguishing it from both detection-based and segmentation-based approaches, and discussing its advancements over existing models like Mask R-CNN and PANet.

The paper presents experimental results comparing the performance of Boundary-preserving Mask R-CNN (BMask R-CNN) with traditional Mask R-CNN (MRCNN) and its larger mask head variant (MRCNN w/ LMH) on the COCO val2017 dataset and Cityscapes val dataset. 

In Table 8, the computational cost is evaluated in terms of FLOPs and inference time, with BMask R-CNN achieving 0.95G FLOPs and an inference time of 63.7 ms per image while providing an Average Precision (AP) of 34.7, outperforming both MRCNN and MRCNN w/ LMH in AP metrics. 

Table 9 illustrates the performance on the Cityscapes dataset, where BMask R-CNN achieves an AP of 35.0, significantly higher than the traditional Mask R-CNN at 32.0 and other competing methods. The results demonstrate that BMask R-CNN not only maintains efficiency but also enhances detection accuracy across various categories, including vehicles and pedestrians.

Qualitative results further validate the effectiveness of BMask R-CNN, showcasing improved instance boundary predictions compared to Mask R-CNN, thereby emphasizing its advantage in boundary preservation and overall segmentation quality. The use of a Sobel operator in the mask head design contributes to this improvement, leading to a notable increase in AP scores while maintaining a competitive computational cost.

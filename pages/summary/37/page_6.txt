The paper presents a method for joint object boundary and mask prediction using a boundary-preserving mask head within a framework called BMask R-CNN. The approach employs RoIAlign to extract Region of Interest (RoI) features, specifically defining Rm for mask prediction and Rb for boundary prediction. Rm is extracted from various feature pyramid levels based on proposal scales, while Rb is obtained from the highest resolution feature level to retain spatial information crucial for boundary prediction.

The feature fusion process integrates mask features Rm and boundary features fRb through a series of convolutions to enhance the boundary prediction. The Mask-to-Boundary (M2B) fusion combines high-level mask information with boundary features, while the Boundary-to-Mask (B2M) fusion enriches mask features with boundary information, thereby guiding precise mask predictions.

The prediction mechanism utilizes a combination of deconvolution and convolution layers, ensuring that both masks and boundaries are class-specific. The learning and optimization process treats boundary prediction as a pixel-level classification task, leveraging the learned boundary features to improve mask predictions by providing essential shape information. This end-to-end learning approach emphasizes the close relationship between object boundaries and masks, facilitating better prediction accuracy.

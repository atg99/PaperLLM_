This section lists a series of references related to advancements in image recognition, segmentation, and deep learning methodologies. Key contributions include:

1. He et al. introduced deep residual learning, a significant method for image recognition.
2. Huang et al. presented techniques for semantic segmentation guided by object boundaries and proposed Mask Scoring R-CNN for instance segmentation.
3. Ioffe and Szegedy's work on batch normalization improved the training efficiency of deep networks.
4. Kendall et al. explored multi-task learning with uncertainty to enhance scene geometry and semantics.
5. Kirillov et al. contributed to instance segmentation through methods like InstanceCut and PointRend.
6. Liu et al. developed Path Aggregation Networks for instance segmentation, while also proposing sequential grouping networks.
7. Milletari et al. focused on volumetric medical image segmentation using fully convolutional networks.
8. The references also include frameworks and benchmarks like Mask R-CNN and Microsoft COCO, which are pivotal for research in object detection and segmentation.

Overall, these works collectively advance the field of computer vision by enhancing the accuracy and efficiency of various segmentation and detection algorithms.

The paper introduces the Boundary-preserving Mask R-CNN, which aims to enhance mask predictions by incorporating boundary information. Traditional Mask R-CNN, while effective, often produces coarse and imprecise masks due to its focus on local features without adequately considering shape information. This can lead to false positive predictions. The authors analyze mask predictions from the standard Mask R-CNN with ResNet-50 and FPN, demonstrating the need for improved localization.

To address this issue, the Boundary-preserving Mask R-CNN utilizes a new mask head that integrates boundary features and predictions, thereby guiding more accurate mask generation. The architecture includes a Feature Pyramid Network (FPN) for extracting multi-scale features, and after generating proposals through a Region Proposal Network (RPN), the box head performs classification and bounding box predictions. The proposed method shows promise in improving the precision of object mask predictions by leveraging boundary information effectively.

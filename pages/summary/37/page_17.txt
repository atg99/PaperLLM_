The references provided highlight significant contributions to the field of object detection and semantic segmentation, particularly in relation to the Mask R-CNN framework. Key works include:

1. **Faster R-CNN** by Ren et al. (2017) introduced region proposal networks for real-time object detection, laying the groundwork for subsequent advancements.
2. **Gated-SCNN** by Takikawa et al. (2019) proposed gated shape convolutional neural networks to enhance semantic segmentation capabilities.
3. **Conditional Convolutions** for instance segmentation by Tian et al. (2020) focused on refining segmentation results through conditional feature learning.
4. **Pixel-level Encoding** by Uhrig et al. (2016) emphasized the importance of depth layering in semantic labeling at the instance level.
5. **Deep High-Resolution Representation Learning** by Wang et al. (2019) advanced visual recognition by utilizing high-resolution feature maps.
6. **Holistically-Nested Edge Detection** by Xie and Tu (2017) contributed a comprehensive approach to edge detection that improves segmentation accuracy.
7. **Geometry-Aware Skeleton Detection** by Xu et al. (2019) explored geometric features for improved skeleton detection in images.
8. **Discriminative Feature Networks** by Yu et al. (2018) focused on enhancing feature learning for better semantic segmentation outcomes.
9. **Deep Category-Aware Semantic Edge Detection** by Yu et al. (2017) introduced a model that incorporates category awareness into edge detection.
10. **Simultaneous Edge Alignment and Learning** by Yu et al. (2018) aimed at improving alignment and learning processes in semantic tasks.
11. **SegX** by Yuan et al. (2020) provided a model-agnostic approach to boundary refinement for segmentation tasks.
12. **Faster Training of Mask R-CNN** by Zimmermann and Siems (2018) proposed methods to accelerate the training process by concentrating on instance boundaries.

These studies collectively contribute to the evolution of boundary-preserving techniques and enhance the efficiency and accuracy of segmentation and detection models.

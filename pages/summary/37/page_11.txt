The paper presents experimental results for the Boundary-preserving Mask R-CNN (BMask R-CNN) on the COCO val2017 dataset, focusing on the effects of different groundtruths for the boundary head and various RoI feature extraction strategies. Key findings include that supervision on the boundary head significantly improves average precision (AP) metrics, with the boundary groundtruth yielding the best performance across metrics like AP, AP 50, AP 75, and APb.

In the analysis of RoI feature extraction strategies, different configurations were tested, with results indicating that certain combinations, such as P2 2834.7, achieved the highest AP scores. The paper also investigates the impact of different loss functions on boundary prediction, revealing that using a combination of Dice loss and Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE) loss enhances boundary localization while addressing class imbalance issues. Weighted BCE alone resulted in less precise boundaries.

The computational cost of BMask R-CNN is discussed, noting that despite the additional convolutional layers for boundary prediction, the method still outperforms Mask R-CNN at equivalent computational costs. Further experiments on the Cityscapes dataset confirm the effectiveness of BMask R-CNN using fine annotations for training and evaluation, maintaining a focus on fair comparisons using ResNet-50-FPN as the backbone. Overall, the findings underscore the advantages of the proposed model in boundary detection tasks.

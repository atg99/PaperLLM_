The paper by Cheng et al. evaluates the performance of their proposed BMask R-CNN model on two datasets: COCO and Cityscapes. COCO consists of 115k training images, 5k validation images, and 20k testing images, while Cityscapes includes 2975 training, 500 validation, and 1525 testing images, focusing on urban scene segmentation. Both datasets utilize the same evaluation metric, COCO Average Precision (AP), which assesses model performance across various Intersection over Union (IoU) thresholds.

The authors use Mask R-CNN as the baseline, maintaining consistent hyper-parameters and employing ResNet-50 with Feature Pyramid Network (FPN) as the backbone. They initialize the backbone with ImageNet pre-trained weights, freeze Batch Normalization layers, and resize images for training. The models are trained on NVIDIA GPUs using Synchronized SGD with a specific learning rate schedule.

Results show that BMask R-CNN significantly outperforms Mask R-CNN, achieving improvements in AP across different backbones and input sizes. Specifically, it demonstrates notable gains in AP 75, indicating enhanced mask localization due to the incorporation of boundary information. In comparison with state-of-the-art instance segmentation methods, BMask R-CNN with ResNet-101-FPN outperforms them without additional complexities. The AP curves indicate that BMask R-CNN excels in localization performance, particularly at higher IoU thresholds.

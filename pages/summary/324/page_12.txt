The paper presents a comparison of active learning (AL) strategies against a random baseline across three scenarios: balanced, imbalanced, and imbalanced-practical. The results are illustrated in Figure 3, which shows how different AL strategies perform in each scenario. The balanced scenario represents a situation where classes are evenly distributed, while the imbalanced scenarios reflect situations where one class significantly outnumbers another. The train size is indicated as L, highlighting the size of the training dataset used in the experiments. The findings suggest that the effectiveness of AL strategies varies depending on the distribution of classes in the dataset.

The paper addresses the challenge of text classification, particularly in scenarios where the target class is underrepresented in the dataset. It highlights the difficulties practitioners face when building classifiers under these conditions. The authors explore active learning (AL) methods using BERT, demonstrating that AL significantly enhances the model's recall and overall performance, even when starting from a limited seed dataset derived from simple queries. Initial results indicate that BERT struggles with low recall due to the scarcity of relevant data, but effective AL strategies can help the model generalize beyond its initial exposure.

The study primarily focuses on binary classification tasks, suggesting future research could extend to multi-class classification and regression tasks, larger annotation budgets, and newer BERT variants. Additionally, the authors note that existing AL strategies have largely been developed for convolutional neural networks and propose the need for novel AL methods tailored for pre-trained models like BERT. The findings and the accompanying research framework aim to support further studies in this area.

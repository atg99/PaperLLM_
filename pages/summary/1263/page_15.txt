The appendix presents confusion matrices for various malware detection techniques, specifically highlighting the performance of Hidden Markov Model (HMM), Convolutional Neural Network (CNN), Random Forest, and Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) models. Each matrix illustrates the classification accuracy of different malware families, such as Adload, Agent, Allaple, and others. 

The matrices are organized to show the proportion of true positive and false positive classifications for each malware type. For instance, the HMM model achieved a high accuracy for certain malware families, while others showed lower detection rates. The CNN model also demonstrated varying performance across different malware types, with some achieving nearly perfect classification.

Overall, the matrices reveal the strengths and weaknesses of each technique in malware detection, providing insights into their effectiveness in distinguishing between different malware families. This detailed analysis is crucial for improving detection algorithms and enhancing cybersecurity measures.

The paper presents PP-YOLO, a novel object detection model built on the PaddlePaddle framework, which demonstrates superior speed and accuracy compared to existing state-of-the-art detectors like EfficientDet and YOLOv4. The study details an extensive comparison of various object detectors using the MS-COCO test-dev 2017 dataset, highlighting the performance metrics such as Frames Per Second (FPS) and mean Average Precision (mAP) under different configurations, both with and without TensorRT optimization.

Key findings include that PP-YOLO outperforms YOLOv4 in mAP, achieving 45.2% compared to YOLOv4's 43.5%, while also improving FPS from 62 to 72.9. The paper emphasizes the significant benefits of TensorRT in enhancing the performance of PP-YOLO, with a relative improvement of around 100%, compared to approximately 70% for YOLOv4, suggesting that TensorRT is more effective for optimizing the ResNet architecture utilized in PP-YOLO.

The authors also discuss the impact of using different input sizes on detection performance and report that PP-YOLO maintains a favorable balance between speed and accuracy across various configurations. The study concludes by stating that the exploration of various techniques and their integration into the YOLOv3 framework can aid developers and researchers in achieving better performance in practical applications.

The paper presents a comprehensive overview of various studies and models related to cognitive computation, focusing on the intersection of language, cognition, and deep learning. Key concepts include the modeling of neurocognitive functions through generative neural networks, the development of visual number sense, and the implications of the free-energy principle for understanding brain function. 

Several studies explore the emergence of perceptual abilities, such as letter perception and number acuity, through unsupervised learning techniques. Additionally, the paper discusses the challenges of catastrophic interference in neural networks and the significance of continual learning mechanisms. 

The references cited highlight significant contributions to understanding how deep learning models can replicate human cognitive processes and the developmental aspects of numeracy and reading. Overall, the synthesis of these works emphasizes the potential of computational models to elucidate typical and atypical cognitive development, providing insights into both machine learning and human cognition.

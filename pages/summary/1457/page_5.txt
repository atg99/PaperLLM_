The paper discusses various tasks designed to evaluate a model's performance in understanding semantic and syntactic roles in language. Key tasks include Semantic Role Labeling (EP-SRL), which assigns semantic roles to spans of words, and Coreference Resolution (EP-Coref), which determines if two spans refer to the same entity. Other tasks focus on syntactic probing, such as Constituent Labeling (EP-Const) for classifying non-terminal labels, and Dependency Labeling (EP-UD) to assess functional relationships between tokens.

The paper further explores tasks like Semantic Proto-Role labeling (EP-SPR) and Relation Classification (EP-Rel), which predict semantic attributes and relationships between entities, respectively. Additionally, the SentEval probing tasks (SE) evaluate sentence-level features, including Sentence Length (SE-SentLen) and Word Content (SE-WC), among others.

In terms of methodology, the authors utilize the RoBERTa Large model for training and optimization. They conduct hyperparameter tuning for learning rates and dropout rates, utilizing NVIDIA P40 GPUs. The training involves a pipeline where RoBERTa is fine-tuned on intermediate tasks before being adapted to target and probing tasks, resulting in a comprehensive set of observations. The Adam optimizer is employed with specific training strategies to ensure effective learning from both large and small datasets. The experiments are conducted using the jiant NLP toolkit and various deep learning frameworks.

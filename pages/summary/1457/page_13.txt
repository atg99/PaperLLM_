The paper titled "Record: Bridging the gap between human and machine commonsense reading comprehension" by Sheng Zhang, Xiaodong Liu, Jingjing Liu, Jianfeng Gao, Kevin Duh, and Benjamin Van Durme presents a framework aimed at enhancing the capabilities of machines in understanding commonsense knowledge during reading comprehension tasks. The authors argue that existing models often fall short in replicating human-like understanding due to a lack of sufficient commonsense reasoning. The proposed approach focuses on integrating commonsense knowledge into reading comprehension systems to improve their performance, thereby narrowing the gap between human and machine comprehension. The manuscript, although unpublished, is accessible on arXiv, indicating its relevance and potential impact on the field of computational linguistics.

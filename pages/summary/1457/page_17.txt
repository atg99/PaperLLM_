The paper presents experiments analyzing how the size of intermediate task data affects the performance of a downstream target task. Specifically, the authors finetune the RoBERTa model on various dataset sizes, which are randomly sampled. The results are displayed in subfigures, showcasing the macro-average performance metrics for each target task following the finetuning process on different dataset splits. This approach highlights the relationship between the amount of intermediate data and the subsequent effectiveness of the target task performance.

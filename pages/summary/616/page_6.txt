DenseNet121 achieves the most reliable performance with the least tuning required. Mobile architectures, though smaller and lighter, achieve validation accuracies similar to ResNet50 and VGG16, warranting case-specific applicability assessments.

Training curves for newer architectures, particularly DenseNet121, demonstrate smoother and faster convergence compared to older models. Although these results pertain to a specific dataset, they may have broader applicability across large datasets. Future research could explore block-wise fine-tuning methods and investigate architectural variations to enhance interpretability of the results.

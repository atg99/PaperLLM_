The paper investigates the impact of convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture depth on performance through fine-tuning. Various architectures, including VGG16, VGG19, and InceptionV3, were pre-trained on the ImageNet dataset and fine-tuned on two histopathology datasets. The study emphasizes the effectiveness of fine-tuning top layers in shallow networks like VGG, while for deeper networks such as InceptionV3, fine-tuning the entire network yields better results. It advocates for using a low learning rate to preserve original weights during fine-tuning.

The authors compare their findings with previous studies, noting that fine-tuning CNNs generally outperforms other techniques. They plan to explore different optimizers and datasets in future research to further examine the block-wise effects of fine-tuning CNNs.

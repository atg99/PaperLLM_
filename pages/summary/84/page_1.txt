The paper investigates the effectiveness of Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) and Bidirectional LSTM (BiLSTM) models in forecasting time series data. It highlights the superiority of deep learning techniques over traditional models like Autoregressive Integrated Moving Average (ARIMA), particularly in long-term predictions. LSTMs are designed with memory gates that allow them to capture longer sequences of data, while BiLSTMs enhance this capability by processing input data in both left-to-right and right-to-left directions, effectively allowing for additional training.

The research aims to determine whether the additional training offered by BiLSTM translates to better predictive performance compared to unidirectional LSTM. Results indicate that BiLSTM outperforms both LSTM and ARIMA models, providing more accurate predictions. However, it is noted that BiLSTM models take longer to reach equilibrium compared to LSTM models. The findings suggest that while LSTM models already offer substantial predictive power, BiLSTM models further enhance this capability through their unique architecture and training approach.

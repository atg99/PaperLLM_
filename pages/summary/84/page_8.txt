The paper presents a comparative analysis of Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) and Bidirectional LSTM (BiLSTM) models for forecasting IBM stock prices. It includes a series of figures that illustrate the loss metrics against batching steps across different training epochs. Specifically, the figures are categorized into single and multiple epochs for both LSTM and BiLSTM, highlighting the performance variations in loss reduction over the batching steps.

The research acknowledges the support received from the National Science Foundation under specific grants, indicating the academic and financial backing for this study. 

The references cited encompass a diverse range of studies, including traditional time series forecasting methods such as ARIMA, as well as modern approaches utilizing deep learning techniques like LSTM and BiLSTM. These references provide a foundational context for the methodologies employed in the paper, illustrating the evolution of forecasting techniques in both financial and biological data domains.

Overall, the work contributes to the understanding of how LSTM and BiLSTM architectures can be utilized effectively for time series forecasting, particularly in the context of stock market predictions.

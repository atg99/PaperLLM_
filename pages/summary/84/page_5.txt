The paper discusses the implementation and performance comparison of various artificial neural network models, specifically focusing on LSTM and BiLSTM architectures for time series forecasting, particularly in stock data prediction. 

BiLSTM, which processes input data in both forward and reverse sequences, has been shown to outperform traditional LSTM models, with reductions in Rooted Mean Squared Error (RMSE) ranging from 12.93% to 77.60% across different datasets. The average RMSE for LSTM was reported at 39.09, while BiLSTM achieved a significantly lower average of 20.17, indicating an overall improvement of 37.78%. Furthermore, when compared to ARIMA, BiLSTM demonstrated an average reduction of 93.11%, while LSTM showed an 88.07% reduction, emphasizing the superior predictive accuracy of the neural network approaches over traditional methods.

The paper includes a detailed algorithm (Listing 1) for rolling LSTM/BiLSTM forecasting, outlining the steps for data preparation, model fitting, and prediction. The model is trained on 70% of the data, with the remaining 30% used for testing. The algorithm allows for real-time re-training as new observations are made, enhancing prediction accuracy.

Overall, the findings highlight the efficacy of BiLSTM models in time series forecasting, particularly in financial data, and provide a framework for implementing these models effectively.

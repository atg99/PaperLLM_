The paper discusses the complexities and challenges associated with safe reinforcement learning (RL) applications. It emphasizes the importance of analyzing safety complexity and sample complexity across various safe RL algorithms, which can be categorized into value-based and policy-based methods. Despite numerous RL applications, many remain in simulation phases without addressing safety concerns, with real-world applications still lacking in maturity. 

Key considerations for safe RL implementations include the safety of the ego agent, the environment, and human safety, with a particular focus on control safety to mitigate adversarial threats. The review highlights several safe RL applications across fields such as autonomous driving, robotics, video compression, and vehicle scheduling. 

The paper also examines safety benchmarks, stressing the need for carefully designed cost and reward functions that balance performance and safety. A loose cost function may jeopardize safety, while overly strict constraints could hinder reward performance. It discusses existing benchmarks like AI Safety Gridworlds and Safety Gym, which tailor cost and reward functions to specific tasks.

Finally, the paper outlines significant safety challenges, including integrating human and environmental safety factors in real-world deployments and addressing the social dilemmas associated with safety in RL applications.

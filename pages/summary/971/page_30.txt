The paper discusses a two-step approach to safety learning in reinforcement learning, which includes policy improvement and projection. It references work that establishes the upper bounds of worst-case scenarios in safety learning, achieved through first-order optimization. The CUP framework extends this analysis by examining the generalized advantage estimator (GAE), which plays a vital role in reducing variance while maintaining an acceptable bias level, thus enhancing algorithm efficiency. Additionally, the paper summarizes asymptotic results that can be attained by safe reinforcement learning methods.

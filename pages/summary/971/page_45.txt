Safety Gym is an environment built on OpenAI Gym and MuJoCo, designed for 2-D robotic tasks. It features different robot types, such as Point, Car, and Doggo robots, which navigate toward goal positions while avoiding crashes into unsafe areas. The environment incorporates various costs associated with collisions with dangerous areas, non-goal objects, immobile obstacles, and moving objects.

Safe Control Gym, proposed by Yuan et al., extends OpenAI Gym to focus on safe control and learning problems. It integrates traditional control methods, learning-based control methods, and reinforcement learning within a framework that supports both model-based and data-based approaches. The benchmark includes tasks such as cart-pole stabilization and trajectory tracking for 1D and 2D quadrotors. Compared to Safety Gym and AI safety gridworlds, Safe Control Gym is particularly suited for sim-to-real research due to its diverse task options.

The paper discusses various methods employed in robotic applications, categorizing them into simulation and real-world experiments. It lists several algorithms, including genetic algorithms, Particle Swarm Optimization, learning algorithms, and others, applied to different types of robots such as mobile robots, NAO humanoid robots, and Barrett robot arms. 

In addition to the methods, the paper highlights the development of safety benchmarks for safe reinforcement learning (RL). It notes that these benchmarks, which include both single-agent and multi-agent scenarios, have significantly contributed to the RL community by facilitating the transition of safe RL concepts into practical applications. The section aims to explore the popular safe RL benchmarks and address the challenges associated with safety in RL.

The referenced works explore various aspects of reinforcement learning, safety in autonomous systems, and optimization in decision processes. Basu et al. (2008) present a learning algorithm focused on risk-sensitive costs, while Beard and Baheri (2022) propose a multi-fidelity reinforcement learning approach to ensure safety in autonomous systems. Berkenkamp and Schoellig (2015) discuss safe and robust learning control using Gaussian processes, and Berkenkamp et al. (2017) extend this by providing stability guarantees in model-based reinforcement learning.

Berner et al. (2019) demonstrate the application of large-scale deep reinforcement learning in the game Dota 2, highlighting its capabilities in complex environments. Bertsekas (2015) offers insights into convex optimization algorithms, essential for solving various optimization problems. Beutler and Ross (1985, 1986) investigate optimal policies for controlled Markov chains and time-average optimal constrained semi-Markov decision processes, respectively, contributing to the understanding of constrained decision-making frameworks.

Bharadhwaj et al. (2021) introduce conservative safety critics aimed at enhancing exploration in reinforcement learning. Lastly, Bhatnagar and Lakshmanan (2012) present an online actor-critic algorithm that incorporates function approximation for constrained Markov decision processes, emphasizing the intersection of learning and optimization in ensuring safety and efficiency in decision-making. Overall, these studies collectively advance the knowledge of safe reinforcement learning, optimization techniques, and decision-making under constraints.

This collection of references covers various advancements in the field of reinforcement learning and robotics, particularly focusing on safe and constrained methods. 

Yun and Goswami (2012) explore hardware experiments for safe falls in humanoid robots, specifically using the Aldebaran Nao robot, highlighting practical applications in robotics. Zanger et al. (2021) present a method for safe continuous control through constrained model-based policy optimization, which emphasizes safety in control strategies. Zanon and Gros (2020) discuss safe reinforcement learning utilizing robust model predictive control, contributing to the understanding of safety in dynamic environments.

Zeng et al. (2021) investigate the finite-time complexity of an online primal-dual natural actor-critic algorithm for constrained Markov decision processes, offering insights into algorithm efficiency. Zhang et al. (2024) introduce a constrained reinforcement learning approach with a smoothed log barrier function, proposing a novel method for dealing with constraints in learning processes.

Zhang et al. (2020) focus on robust multi-agent reinforcement learning amidst model uncertainty, while a subsequent work by Zhang, Yang, and Basar (2021) provides a selective overview of multi-agent reinforcement learning theories and algorithms, summarizing the current state of the field. Zhang et al. (2020) also address first order constrained optimization in policy space, offering a mathematical perspective on policy adjustment.

Lastly, Zhao et al. (2021) present DEAR, a deep reinforcement learning model for online advertising impressions in recommender systems, showcasing practical applications in the advertising domain. Zheng et al. (2022) conclude this collection with an exploration of constrained reinforcement learning through dissipative saddle flow dynamics, further enriching the discourse on safe learning methodologies. Overall, these works collectively advance the understanding of safety, constraints, and practical applications in reinforcement learning and robotics.

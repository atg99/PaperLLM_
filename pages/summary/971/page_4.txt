For problem (2) (safety complexity), determining the amount of training data necessary to discover a safe policy is crucial. This involves understanding the relationship between the complexity of the environment and the data requirements for safe learning. Insufficient data can lead to unsafe behaviors, making this a significant concern in safe reinforcement learning.

Regarding problem (3) (safety applications), the review highlights the need to keep abreast of current advancements in safe RL applications. This includes identifying industries and scenarios where safety is paramount, such as autonomous vehicles and healthcare, where the consequences of unsafe actions can be severe.

For problem (4) (safety benchmarks), establishing fair and comprehensive benchmarks is necessary to evaluate the performance of safe RL methods. These benchmarks should reflect the various safety constraints and performance metrics relevant to different applications.

Finally, problem (5) (safety challenges) encompasses the obstacles that researchers face in advancing safe RL. This includes technical challenges related to algorithm development, the need for interdisciplinary approaches, and the integration of safety measures into existing RL frameworks.

The paper emphasizes that addressing these "2H3W" problems is vital for the development of effective and reliable safe reinforcement learning methods, which are increasingly important as RL applications expand into critical areas where safety is a primary concern.

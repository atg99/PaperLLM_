This paper presents a comprehensive review of safe reinforcement learning (RL), exploring both theoretical frameworks and practical applications. The authors identify a central question that safe RL must address and analyze five specific problems, encapsulated as "2H3W," to provide insights into this question. The effectiveness of various safe RL methods is evaluated through an examination of several benchmarks for both single-agent and multi-agent settings. Furthermore, the paper highlights existing challenges within the field, aiming to guide future research directions.

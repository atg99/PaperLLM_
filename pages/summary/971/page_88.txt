The referenced papers collectively contribute to advancements in reinforcement learning and control systems, particularly in the context of games and decision-making processes. 

Schrittwieser et al. (2020) explore how planning with learned models can achieve mastery in multiple games, demonstrating the effectiveness of combining model-based approaches with deep learning. Schulman et al. (2015, 2016, 2017) present various policy optimization algorithms, including Trust Region Policy Optimization and Proximal Policy Optimization, which enhance the stability and efficiency of training in high-dimensional continuous control tasks. 

Sepulchre et al. (2012) provide foundational insights into nonlinear control, which inform the development of more sophisticated systems. Shani et al. (2005) discuss an MDP-based recommender system, showcasing the application of reinforcement learning in personalized recommendations. Sheebaelhamd et al. (2021) address safety in deep reinforcement learning for multi-agent systems, focusing on continuous action spaces. 

Lastly, Silver et al. (2016) detail a significant breakthrough in mastering the game of Go through the integration of deep neural networks and tree search techniques. Collectively, these works highlight the evolution of reinforcement learning methodologies and their practical applications in complex environments.

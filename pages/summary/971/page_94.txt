This collection of references primarily focuses on advancements in reinforcement learning (RL) and its applications across various domains. 

The paper by Woodcock et al. discusses the practice and experience of formal methods, emphasizing their significance in software engineering. Wu et al. present a DDPG-based approach for motion control in biped robots, showcasing the integration of deep reinforcement learning in robotics. Xiao et al. explore anti-jamming video transmissions for UAVs using RL, ensuring quality of experience (QoE), while another work by the same authors addresses mobile offloading in edge computing environments under jamming conditions.

Xiong et al. introduce a method for provably safe reinforcement learning that adheres to step-wise violation constraints, indicating a focus on safety in RL applications. Xu et al. investigate macro action selection in the context of the game Starcraft using deep RL, highlighting its applicability in complex environments. 

Further, Xu, Liang, and Lan propose a primal approach to constrained policy optimization, providing global optimality and finite-time analysis, along with a new method for safe RL with convergence guarantees. Lastly, Yang et al. introduce a conservative update policy algorithm for safe RL, emphasizing the importance of safety in RL methodologies.

Overall, these works collectively advance the understanding and implementation of reinforcement learning, particularly in safety, optimization, and practical applications in robotics and communications.

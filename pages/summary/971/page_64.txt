The referenced works focus on advancements in safe reinforcement learning and its applications in safety-critical continuous control tasks. Cheng et al. propose a method using barrier functions to ensure safety in reinforcement learning, addressing the challenges of continuous control tasks. Chinchali et al. explore deep reinforcement learning for optimizing cellular network traffic scheduling, highlighting the efficiency of AI in complex scheduling scenarios.

Choi et al. investigate safety in control systems under model uncertainty, utilizing control Lyapunov functions and control barrier functions to maintain safety during learning. Chow and colleagues contribute significantly to the field, presenting various methodologies for risk-constrained reinforcement learning, including percentile risk criteria and Lyapunov-based approaches to ensure safe policy optimization.

Their works emphasize the importance of integrating safety measures into reinforcement learning frameworks, particularly in high-stakes environments. The collective research underscores the necessity of robust decision-making strategies that account for risk, aiming to enhance the reliability of AI systems in practical applications such as traffic signal control and network management.

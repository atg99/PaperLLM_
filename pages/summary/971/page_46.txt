This section discusses the implementation of non-idealities in robotics, such as randomization and dynamic disturbances, as well as the introduction of a symbolic framework for representing system dynamics and constraints. It references a figure illustrating the Safe Control Gym with symbolic dynamics constraints used for tracking and stabilization tasks.

Additionally, it highlights the development of three safe multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) benchmarks: Safe Multi-Agent MuJoCo (Safe MAMuJoCo), Safe Multi-Agent Robosuite (Safe MARobosuite), and Safe Multi-Agent Isaac Gym (Safe MAIG). These benchmarks are designed to enhance research in safe MARL.

Safe MAMuJoCo is an extension of the original MAMuJoCo framework, where safety-aware agents must not only master robot manipulations but also navigate around potential hazards such as walls and bombs. The environment's background, agents, physics simulator, and reward function remain consistent with the original, but the introduction of obstacles increases the complexity and safety considerations for the agents.

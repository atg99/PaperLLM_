The paper discusses the applications of safe reinforcement learning (RL), highlighting its historical use in solving complex problems even before the rise of neural network learning. Early RL methods, such as TD learning, were applied to tasks like backgammon, job-shop scheduling, and elevator control, primarily in small-scale or linear settings with discrete problems. As the field evolved, neural networks became predominant for addressing large-scale, continuous, and high-dimensional challenges.

This section specifically focuses on safe RL applications, examining both traditional tabular-setting RL and modern applications in areas like autonomous driving, robotics, and recommendation systems. It emphasizes the practical deployment of safe RL techniques, particularly in ensuring the safety of autonomous vehicles and robotic systems, contrasting it with the theoretical discussion of safe RL methods found in a previous section.

The key distinction between the two sections is their focus: while the earlier section addresses theoretical and algorithmic aspects, this section concentrates on the practical challenges and considerations in applying these methods in real-world scenarios. It aims to connect theoretical frameworks with their implementation, addressing the specific requirements and constraints of various application domains.

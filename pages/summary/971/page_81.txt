The collection of papers listed focuses on various aspects of reinforcement learning and its applications in fields such as communication, control systems, robotics, and autonomous driving. 

Mastronarde and van der Schaar (2011) explore fast reinforcement learning techniques aimed at enhancing energy efficiency in wireless communication systems. Mayne et al. (2000, 2005) delve into constrained model predictive control, discussing stability and optimality, as well as robust control of linear systems under disturbances. 

Meriçli and Veloso (2010) present a method for bipedal walking learning using playback and corrective demonstration, highlighting advancements in robotics. Michel (2004) introduces Webots, a professional simulation tool for mobile robots, enhancing the study of robotic systems. 

Milgrom and Segal (2002) address envelope theorems within arbitrary choice sets, contributing to economic theory. Miljković et al. (2013) apply neural network reinforcement learning to the visual control of robotic manipulators, improving automation capabilities. 

Mirchevska et al. (2017, 2018) focus on reinforcement learning applications for autonomous vehicle maneuvering and lane changing, emphasizing safety and decision-making in driving scenarios. Lastly, Miryoosefi et al. (2019) discuss reinforcement learning methods under convex constraints, expanding the framework for learning in complex environments. 

Overall, these works collectively advance the understanding and application of reinforcement learning across multiple domains, emphasizing its potential for improving efficiency, safety, and decision-making in automated systems.

This paper provides a comprehensive overview of recent advancements in reinforcement learning and its applications in various fields, particularly focusing on safety, explainability, and regulatory aspects in autonomous systems. It discusses the significance of sample complexity and regret bounds in reinforcement learning, referencing key works that offer minimax bounds and methods for constrained reinforcement learning. The paper also covers innovative approaches to dynamic routing in electric vehicle logistics using safe reinforcement learning, emphasizing the importance of ensuring zero constraint violations. Additionally, it highlights techniques for computing risk measures using stochastic approximation, which are relevant in decision-making processes under uncertainty. The research directions suggested in the paper aim to enhance the reliability and interpretability of autonomous systems, advocating for further exploration in verifiable reinforcement learning and policy extraction methods.

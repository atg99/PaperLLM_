The collection of references covers various aspects of reinforcement learning (RL) and control systems, focusing on topics such as risk consideration in RL, model-based approaches, and optimization in decision processes.

Key papers include HasanzadeZonuzy et al. who explore model-based reinforcement learning for constrained Markov decision processes, emphasizing infinite-horizon scenarios. He et al. discuss near minimax optimal strategies in discounted MDPs, contributing to the theoretical framework of RL. Heger highlights the importance of risk in RL, presenting foundational concepts relevant to decision-making under uncertainty.

Hendrycks et al. identify unsolved safety problems in machine learning, which is crucial for ensuring the reliability of RL applications. Hewing et al. present cautious model predictive control using Gaussian process regression, providing methods for robust control in uncertain environments. Holicki et al. focus on controller design through experimental exploration, ensuring robustness in control systems.

Hornik et al. discuss the universal approximation capabilities of multilayer feedforward networks, which is fundamental for understanding neural network applications in RL. Hou and Zhao optimize web service-based control systems, balancing network traffic and delay, while Hovakimyan and Cao introduce L1 adaptive control theory, emphasizing fast adaptation with guaranteed robustness.

Howard and Matheson address risk-sensitive Markov decision processes, providing insights into decision-making strategies that account for risk. Finally, Hu et al. investigate Voronoi-based multi-robot exploration using deep reinforcement learning, showcasing practical applications of RL in autonomous systems.

Overall, these works collectively advance the understanding of reinforcement learning, its theoretical foundations, and practical applications in various domains.

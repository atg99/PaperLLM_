The paper discusses the Safe Multi-Agent environments, particularly focusing on Safe Multi-Agent MuJoCo and Safe Multi-Agent Robosuite. It characterizes cost functions associated with various tasks, as illustrated in figures showcasing different robotic tasks. For example, the Safe Multi-Agent MuJoCo environment features tasks involving multiple agents controlling different body parts of robots, such as Ant and HalfCheetah, while avoiding unsafe areas. The Safe Multi-Agent Robosuite builds on the existing Robosuite platform, which serves as a benchmark for single-agent reinforcement learning, adapting it for multi-agent scenarios. The research aims to enhance cooperative learning among agents in complex environments.

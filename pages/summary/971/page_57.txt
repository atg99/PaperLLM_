The paper addresses five critical problems in safe reinforcement learning (RL) and provides an analysis of both model-based and model-free RL methods within a unified safety framework. It briefly examines the sample complexity and convergence of each method. Applications of safe RL are explored, particularly in autonomous driving and robotics. The paper also identifies benchmarks for the safe RL community, which could facilitate the advancement of RL in real-world scenarios. Additionally, it highlights challenging issues faced during RL applications in safe domains, suggesting areas for future research. The authors acknowledge support from the European Union’s Horizon 2020 and express gratitude to contributors for their suggestions.

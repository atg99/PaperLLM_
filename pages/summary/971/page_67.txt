The references listed encompass a range of studies focused on deep reinforcement learning, safety, and control in various applications. 

Duan et al. (2016) provided a benchmarking framework for assessing deep reinforcement learning techniques specifically in continuous control tasks. Dulac-Arnold et al. (2019) highlighted the challenges faced in real-world reinforcement learning scenarios, emphasizing the need for robust methodologies.

Dunlap et al. (2022) explored runtime assured reinforcement learning for safe satellite docking, addressing safety concerns in autonomous operations. Efroni et al. (2020) investigated the balance between exploration and exploitation in constrained Markov Decision Processes (MDPs), which is crucial for effective decision-making under constraints.

El Chamie et al. (2016) presented a method for synthesizing randomized policies in controlled Markov chains while adhering to density safety upper bounds. In the realm of multi-agent systems, ElSayed-Aly et al. (2021) introduced a shielding approach to enhance safety in multi-agent reinforcement learning environments.

Emam et al. (2019) developed robust barrier functions for a swarm-robotics testbed, enabling effective control and safety in autonomous robotic systems. Their subsequent work in 2022 further advanced safe reinforcement learning techniques using robust control barrier functions, contributing to the growing field of safety in reinforcement learning.

Overall, these studies collectively advance the understanding of safety and efficiency in reinforcement learning across various domains, highlighting the importance of developing reliable and effective algorithms for real-world applications.

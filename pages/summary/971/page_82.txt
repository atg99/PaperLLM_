The cited works explore various aspects of reinforcement learning, particularly in constrained environments and safety considerations. Miryoosefi and Jin propose a reward-free method for constrained reinforcement learning, emphasizing a simplified approach to handle constraints without explicit reward signals. Mnih et al. highlight the capabilities of deep reinforcement learning in achieving human-level control, showcasing the power of neural networks in decision-making tasks. Moldovan and Abbeel contribute to the discourse on risk aversion and safe exploration in Markov decision processes, presenting methods to ensure safer outcomes during learning. More recent work by Moskovitz et al. introduces the Reload algorithm, which focuses on achieving convergence in constrained Markov decision processes using optimistic ascent-descent techniques. Munos and Szepesvári provide finite-time performance bounds for fitted value iteration, adding theoretical insights into reinforcement learning algorithms. Murugesan et al. emphasize the integration of formal methods in training safe reinforcement learning agents, ensuring reliability in their operations. Finally, Nachum and colleagues discuss duality approaches and behavior-agnostic estimation methods, advancing the field's understanding of stationary distribution corrections in reinforcement learning contexts.

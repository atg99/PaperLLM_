The section discusses benchmarks for safe single-agent reinforcement learning (RL), focusing on AI Safety Gridworlds. These environments consist of a 10x10 grid where an agent, placed in one cell, must navigate around obstacles while making discrete moves (right, left, up, down). The AI Safety Gridworlds include three primary task categories:

1. **Off-switch Environments**: The agent must reach a goal while navigating through a specific cell that may cause it to stop randomly. It can disable this interruption by visiting another cell.

2. **Navigation Tasks**: The agent must avoid water cells, which incur penalties, while attempting to reach its goal.

3. **Absent Supervisor Tasks**: The agent aims to reach the goal in the shortest path possible, with a chance of encountering a supervisor that can impose costs or penalties.

These tasks are designed to evaluate the effectiveness and safety of RL algorithms in controlled scenarios.

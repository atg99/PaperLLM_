The paper discusses various tasks in the Safe MARobosuite Environments, showcasing examples such as two-agent Lift tasks, eight-agent Lift tasks, and fourteen-agent TwoArmPegInHole tasks. In these scenarios, multiple agents collaborate to manipulate a robot while ensuring they do not collide with unsafe areas. The study emphasizes the importance of safe interactions among agents during task execution.

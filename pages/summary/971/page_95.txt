The referenced papers cover significant advancements in reinforcement learning (RL), particularly focusing on methods for improving learning efficiency and safety in multi-agent and policy optimization contexts. 

Yang et al. (2018) present a unified approach to multi-step temporal-difference learning using eligibility traces, enhancing the learning process in RL environments. Yang et al. (2019) explore policy optimization techniques utilizing stochastic mirror descent, contributing to the optimization framework in RL. In 2021, Yang, Zheng, and Pan analyze the sample complexity associated with policy gradient methods aimed at finding second-order stationary points, which is crucial for understanding the efficiency of learning algorithms.

Yang et al. (2020) introduce projection-based constrained policy optimization, addressing constraints in policy learning effectively. Yang et al. (2021) highlight the importance of diverse auto-curriculum in multi-agent learning systems, emphasizing its role in achieving successful outcomes in real-world applications. Yang and Wang (2020) provide a comprehensive overview of multi-agent RL from a game-theoretical perspective, framing the interaction between agents within competitive and cooperative settings.

Further contributions include Yang et al. (2023), who discuss model-free safe RL through neural barrier certificates, presenting a novel approach to ensure safety in RL applications. Yu et al. (2019) focus on convergent policy optimization methods for safe RL, while Yuan et al. (2021) introduce Safe-Control-Gym, a benchmark suite designed for evaluating safe learning-based control methods in RL.

Overall, these works collectively advance the understanding and application of reinforcement learning, particularly in optimizing policies and ensuring safe interactions in complex environments.

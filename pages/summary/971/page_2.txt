The paper provides a comprehensive review of safe reinforcement learning (RL), addressing the increasing safety concerns associated with deploying RL in real-world applications such as autonomous driving and robotics. While safe control has been studied for some time, safe RL is still emerging. The authors categorize their review into methods, theories, and applications, highlighting five key challenges in deploying safe RL, referred to as “2H3W”.

The review begins by outlining the progress in safe RL across five dimensions and identifying crucial problems that need addressing for effective real-world deployment. It then delves into algorithmic advancements and theoretical frameworks aimed at resolving the “2H3W” challenges, with a particular focus on the sample complexity of safe RL algorithms. The paper also covers various applications and benchmarks related to safe RL.

In conclusion, the authors discuss ongoing challenges in the field, aiming to inspire further research. Additionally, they provide an open-source repository that includes implementations of major safe RL algorithms to support the research community.

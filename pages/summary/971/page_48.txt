The paper presents various tasks within the Safe Multi-Agent MuJoCo Environments, highlighting specific challenges and configurations for each task. Notable examples include ManyAgent Ant Task 1.0, which features a 4x2 configuration with three folding jagged walls, and ManyAgent Ant Task 2.1, characterized by a 3x2 setup with two folding line walls. The tasks are designed to test multi-agent interactions in complex environments, emphasizing the importance of safety and adaptability in robotic learning scenarios.

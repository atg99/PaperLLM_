This section discusses model-free safe reinforcement learning (RL), emphasizing its applicability across various domains without the need for model dynamics. Key methods are explored from the perspectives of policy optimization, control theory, Gaussian processes, and formal methods.

The first approach highlighted is Constrained Policy Optimization (CPO), a pioneering policy gradient method aimed at addressing the Constrained Markov Decision Process (CMDP) problem using model-free deep RL. CPO optimizes policies to ensure monotonic improvements in rewards while adhering to safety constraints. Although CPO can nearly converge to safety bounds and outperform the primal-dual method in certain tasks, it is computationally intensive due to the necessity of calculating the Fisher information matrix and utilizing second-order Taylor expansions. This complexity can lead to approximation and sampling errors, complicating convergence analysis and potentially requiring additional samples for recovery policies, which may be inefficient.

Building on CPO, Projection-based Constrained Policy Optimization (PCPO) employs a two-step method to optimize cost and ensure safety through cost projection. PCPO demonstrates improved performance over CPO in specific tasks by employing Trust Region Policy Optimization (TRPO) to enhance rewards before projecting policies into a feasible safety region. However, like CPO, PCPO involves second-order proximal optimization, resulting in greater computational demands compared to First Order Constrained Optimization in Policy Space (FOCOPS), which utilizes first-order optimization techniques.

FOCOPS is grounded in optimization principles and employs a primal-dual method to address policy search in nonparametric space, projecting policies into parameter space for proximal maximization in CMDPs. While FOCOPS is simpler to implement and exhibits better sample efficiency, it still faces challenges related to unstable saddle points and unsafe actions during training. 

This overview highlights the evolving landscape of model-free safe RL methods, illustrating the trade-offs between computational complexity, performance, and safety in various approaches.

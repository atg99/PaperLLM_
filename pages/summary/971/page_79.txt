This collection of references encompasses various studies within the field of reinforcement learning, particularly focusing on policy learning with constraints, safe exploration, and multi-agent systems. 

Liu et al. provide a comprehensive survey on policy learning under constraints in model-free reinforcement learning, highlighting the significance of integrating constraints into learning processes. Alvarez Lopez et al. discuss microscopic traffic simulation using SUMO, showcasing its applications in intelligent transportation systems. 

Lowe et al. present a multi-agent actor-critic framework tailored for mixed cooperative-competitive environments, demonstrating advancements in collaborative reinforcement learning. Lu et al. introduce a decentralized policy gradient approach aimed at ensuring safety in multi-agent reinforcement learning scenarios.

Lu et al. also propose a safe reinforcement learning algorithm with a hierarchical structure specifically designed for wireless security applications. Lütjens et al. explore safe reinforcement learning methods that incorporate model uncertainty estimates, enhancing reliability in robotic applications.

Finally, Lutz et al. report on the autonomous capabilities of a Nao robot navigating a ramp, illustrating practical implementations of reinforcement learning in robotics. Each study contributes to the evolving landscape of reinforcement learning, addressing critical aspects such as safety, cooperation, and real-world applications.

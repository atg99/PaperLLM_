This collection of references presents significant contributions to the field of robotics and reinforcement learning, focusing on various applications and methodologies. 

Ma et al. explore the integration of latent state inference and spatial-temporal relationships in reinforcement learning for autonomous driving, highlighting advancements in decision-making processes under dynamic conditions. Makoviychuk et al. introduce Isaac Gym, a high-performance GPU-based physics simulation environment that enhances robot learning through realistic simulations. Mandhane et al. apply reinforcement learning in the context of video compression, specifically using MuZero with self-competition for rate control in VP9, demonstrating the versatility of these techniques beyond traditional robotics.

Mannor and Tsitsiklis investigate mean-variance optimization within Markov decision processes, providing a theoretical foundation for decision-making under uncertainty. Marvi and Kiumarsi contribute to safe reinforcement learning, proposing methods that utilize control barrier functions for off-policy learning and optimization, emphasizing safety in control applications.

Additionally, Mason and Grijalva review the application of reinforcement learning in autonomous building energy management, showcasing the potential for energy efficiency improvements through intelligent systems. Collectively, these works represent critical advancements in the application of reinforcement learning to complex real-world problems.

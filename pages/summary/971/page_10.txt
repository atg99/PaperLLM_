Key approaches include model predictive control, adaptive control, robust control, and the use of Lyapunov functions. A survey from 2015 categorizes safe RL methods into two main types: those that focus on optimizing safety criteria, such as worst-case scenarios and risk-sensitive criteria, and those that utilize external knowledge or risk metrics to enhance safety during optimization.

In contrast, another survey emphasizes safe learning techniques, discussing both Markov Decision Process (MDP) and non-MDP methods. Safe model-free RL methods are summarized based on two categories of constraints: cumulative and instantaneous. Cumulative constraints include discounted cumulative, mean valued, and probabilistic constraints, which govern the overall performance over time. Instantaneous constraints are classified into explicit constraints, which can be numerically verified, and implicit constraints, which lack a precise closed-form expression.

While many Constrained Markov Decision Process (CMDP) methods primarily focus on cumulative cost optimization, there are some that also consider instantaneous constraints, highlighting the diversity of approaches in ensuring safety and stability in RL.

Safe MARobosuite is a framework designed for multi-agent robotic tasks, allowing for flexible configurations of agents that control various joints of robots. Tasks such as lifting and stacking can be divided among different numbers of agents, enhancing efficiency and task management. The framework prioritizes robot safety, making it fully cooperative, decentralized, and continuous, which is particularly beneficial for Franka robots that operate under partial environmental observations.

Safe MARobosuite supports modular robots, enabling them to adapt quickly to different tasks, even under conditions like limited communication bandwidth or malfunctioning joints. Its reward system aligns with RoboSuite, incorporating cost designs to avoid unsafe interactions.

In contrast, Safe Multi-Agent Isaac Gym (Safe MAIG) leverages GPU technology for faster trajectory sampling and computations, boasting a performance speed nearly ten times that of Safe MARobosuite. However, it presents challenges regarding memory optimization between CPU and GPU.

The paper also addresses the broader challenges of applying reinforcement learning in real-world scenarios, particularly focusing on safety-related issues that must be overcome for effective implementation.

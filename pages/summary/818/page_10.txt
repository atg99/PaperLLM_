The paper presents a comprehensive analysis of an accelerator-aware pruning scheme designed for convolutional neural networks (CNNs). The proposed method allows for efficient execution on target accelerators by ensuring a fixed number of non-zero weights per weight-fetching group. The analysis also suggests that the area of the IM block is influenced by both Ngroup and Nmul, and the overall area can be decreased by 40% through the proposed simplifications. The findings demonstrate the ability of the pruning scheme to adapt to various accelerator architectures, making it applicable beyond the specific cases discussed.

In conclusion, the paper emphasizes the effectiveness of the accelerator-aware pruning scheme in optimizing both CNN architectures and the underlying hardware accelerators, thereby contributing to advancements in efficient deep learning implementations.

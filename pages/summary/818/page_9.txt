Table IX provides an estimation of accelerator performance, specifically for Cambricon-X and EIE architectures. The proposed accelerator-aware pruning significantly enhances efficiency by reducing the number of padding zeros and improving the utilization of processing elements. The utilization rate for the proposed method reaches 87% in the Cambricon-X architecture, compared to only 57% with conventional methods. For the EIE architecture, utilization improves to 100%, demonstrating a substantial reduction in processing cycles when using the proposed pruning scheme.

Furthermore, the paper discusses the impact of the proposed pruning on the logic complexity of the accelerator. The analysis shows that the implementation of the proposed scheme leads to a reduction in area, delay time, and power consumption in the Cambricon-X architecture, contributing to a more efficient hardware design.

Overall, the proposed pruning scheme not only preserves the accuracy of slimmed networks but also optimizes processing efficiency in neural network accelerators, showcasing its potential for enhancing deep learning model deployment.

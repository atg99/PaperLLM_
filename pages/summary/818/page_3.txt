This paper discusses neural network accelerators, particularly focusing on architectures designed for dense convolutional neural networks (CNNs). It introduces processing elements (PEs) that can have single or multiple multipliers, categorized by how they handle weights and activations. The configurations include multiple weights and multiple activations (MWMA), multiple weights and a single activation (MWSA), and a single weight with multiple activations (SWMA). The paper emphasizes the importance of efficiently fetching operands for these multipliers, defining a fetching group as the simultaneous retrieval of weights or activations.

It further categorizes PE structures based on the axis used for fetching these groups, highlighting specific architectures like DianNao, which implements the MWMA structure, and Cnvlutin, which uses MWSA to leverage input activation sparsity. The paper also introduces sparse accelerator architectures that optimize weight storage by only keeping non-zero weights in memory, exemplified by a sparse MWMA structure. This approach aims to enhance performance and efficiency in neural network computations.

This paper investigates the effects of fully-connected layer pruning and retraining on various neural network architectures, specifically AlexNet, VGG16, ResNet-50, and ResNet-152, focusing on the pruning ratios and validation accuracy achieved. Table V presents the results of fully-connected layer pruning, where the proposed scheme allows for a pruning ratio of 81.25% after retraining, comparable to previous unstructured pruning methods. The pruning strategy effectively reduces the number of weights in fully-connected layers, achieving up to 93.75% pruning without significant accuracy degradation in VGG16.

Additionally, the paper explores pruning along different axes, including filter and spatial axes, as shown in Table VI. These results indicate that accuracy remains stable when pruning along these axes, suggesting the versatility of the proposed accelerator-aware pruning scheme across various architectures.

The study also examines compact networks like SqueezeNet and MobileNet in Table VII, demonstrating that the proposed pruning method can recover the original accuracy despite a higher pruning ratio, with a minimal accuracy drop for MobileNet even at 75% pruning. Finally, the paper notes that slimmed networks, which have fewer channels, can also benefit from the proposed pruning scheme, indicating its potential for optimizing network architectures effectively.

In "Artificial Intelligence and Law: An Overview," Harry Surden explores the intersection of artificial intelligence (AI) and the legal field. The paper begins by defining AI and clarifying that current AI lacks true intelligence, focusing instead on various technologies that comprise it, such as machine learning, logic-based systems, and hybrid models that integrate human involvement.

Surden categorizes AI's capabilities and limitations, emphasizing that while AI can perform specific tasks effectively, it is not infallible. The paper then delves into the application of AI within the legal profession, highlighting its role in legal practice, administration, and decision-making by judges and law enforcement. The author discusses how AI tools are utilized by legal practitioners and the implications for users of the law.

Finally, Surden addresses contemporary issues arising from AI's integration into the legal system, including ethical considerations and the potential impact on legal outcomes. The paper concludes by underscoring the need for ongoing dialogue about the role of AI in law, emphasizing both its benefits and challenges.

The paper discusses the concept of artificial intelligence (AI) by defining it as technology that automates tasks typically requiring human intelligence. This definition highlights AI's focus on automating specific activities that involve higher-order cognitive processes, such as reasoning, strategizing, and decision-making. Examples of AI applications include playing chess, language translation, and driving vehicles, all of which necessitate complex cognitive functions when performed by humans. The paper emphasizes that the automation of these tasks is what distinguishes AI from general automation, as it mimics human-like intelligence in executing activities that engage various cognitive capabilities.

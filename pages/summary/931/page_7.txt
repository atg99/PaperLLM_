The paper discusses the current state of artificial intelligence (AI) technology, emphasizing its interdisciplinary nature. AI is recognized as a subfield of computer science but draws from various disciplines, including statistics, linguistics, robotics, electrical engineering, mathematics, neuroscience, economics, logic, and philosophy. Understanding AI involves examining the technologies that facilitate its capabilities.

The mechanisms enabling AI to perform tasks such as playing chess, translating languages, and driving cars fall into two main categories: machine learning and logical rules along with knowledge representation. The paper aims to provide a clearer understanding of these underlying technologies rather than speculating on future developments.

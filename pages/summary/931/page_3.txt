This article explores the relationship between artificial intelligence (AI) and the legal field, aiming to provide an understandable overview of AI's current capabilities and applications in law. The author emphasizes the importance of a realistic and demystified perspective on AI, contrasting it with speculative discussions that focus on futuristic developments that may not materialize. 

The discussion begins with a general introduction to AI, followed by an examination of its practical uses by lawyers, individuals and companies governed by law, and government officials. The motivation behind this article is to highlight the actual implications of AI technology in law today, rather than getting lost in predictions about future advancements. The author argues that focusing on near-term capabilities, typically within a five-year horizon, yields more relevant insights for legal practice and policy, as speculative discussions can misrepresent current technology and divert attention from pressing issues that need to be addressed.

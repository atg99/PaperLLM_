The paper discusses the effectiveness of artificial intelligence (AI) in performing tasks with clear right-or-wrong answers and unambiguous rules, such as spam detection, chess, and video games. These domains allow AI to operate efficiently due to their definitive outcomes. In contrast, many real-world problems, like public policy decisions regarding the placement of homeless shelters, involve subjective interpretations and complex trade-offs, lacking clear objective answers. The text emphasizes that while AI excels in structured environments, its application in nuanced social contexts poses significant challenges. The paper references various sources to support these claims, highlighting the limitations of AI in addressing intricate societal issues.

The paper discusses machine learning, a subset of artificial intelligence characterized by its ability to identify patterns in large datasets. Machine learning encompasses various techniques, including neural networks, naive Bayes classifiers, logistic regression, and random forests. The term "learning" in this context is metaphorical, as machine learning systems do not acquire knowledge in the same way humans do. Instead, they improve their performance on specific tasks by analyzing more data and identifying additional patterns over time. This functional approach to learning allows these systems to produce intelligent-seeming results in applications such as autonomous driving and fraud detection. The paper emphasizes the significance of machine learning as the leading approach in contemporary AI.

This excerpt discusses the significance and capabilities of rules-based knowledge-representation systems in artificial intelligence, contrasting them with machine-learning systems. Although they have not gained as much traction, these systems offer powerful explicit knowledge representation that allows for deductive reasoning. By encoding rules in programming languages, computers can derive non-obvious conclusions and alert users to complex issues that may be difficult for individuals to identify. 

An example provided is the differentiation between business and personal expenses in tax contexts, where a programmed rule can categorize numerous expenses automatically. This illustrates the efficiency of knowledge-based systems in managing and interpreting large sets of data according to specific rules. The paper emphasizes that, when applied appropriately, knowledge-based expert systems are valuable tools widely utilized in various business sectors.

The paper discusses the concept of artificial intelligence (AI), providing a broad definition while highlighting common misconceptions about its capabilities. It emphasizes that contemporary AI systems should not be viewed as intelligent thinking machines. Instead, they generate results through heuristics and pattern recognition, relying on data and rules encoded by humans. This process enables AI to perform tasks that typically require human cognition, though the underlying mechanisms do not resemble human thought processes.

The author points out that the perception of AI as a technology that matches or surpasses human-level cognition is misleading. This misconception has been fueled by marketing and media portrayals of AI. Furthermore, the paper acknowledges that while AI can automate tasks beyond human capability, framing AI solely in terms of human intelligence overlooks its broader applications. The working definition provided, although incomplete, serves as a foundation for understanding the nature of AI in today's context.

The paper discusses the challenges faced by self-driving vehicles, particularly in unexpected situations that require human intervention. It highlights the limitations of machine-learning systems in fully autonomous driving, using the example of a scenario where a police vehicle reroutes cars onto a sidewalk due to an accident. In such cases, self-driving cars may struggle to respond appropriately. 

To address this, the concept of "remote assist" is introduced, where the vehicle can contact a call center staffed by humans who can assess the situation through the car's sensors and take control if necessary. This illustrates the idea of a "human in the loop," emphasizing that many complex AI systems, including those in the legal domain, may not operate fully autonomously but rather rely on human judgment for difficult tasks.

The paper further outlines the current capabilities and limitations of AI technology, stressing the importance of understanding these factors to recognize why AI is effective for certain tasks but not others, particularly in legal contexts.

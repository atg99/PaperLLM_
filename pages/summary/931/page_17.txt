The paper discusses the integration of both hand-coded knowledge and machine learning within AI systems, particularly in the context of self-driving vehicles. It posits that these systems are hybrid, utilizing a combination of rule-based behaviors—like stopping at stop signs—and machine learning models. The authors emphasize that AI does not strictly adhere to one methodology; rather, it often involves a blend of approaches.

Furthermore, the paper highlights the concept of "human in the loop" systems, which are not fully autonomous. These systems perform independently but defer to human judgment for complex or socially sensitive decisions. This design addresses challenges such as the "long tail problem," where AI may struggle with rare or unexpected scenarios that require nuanced human insight. The discussion underscores the importance of human involvement in enhancing the decision-making processes of AI systems, particularly in high-stakes environments like autonomous driving.

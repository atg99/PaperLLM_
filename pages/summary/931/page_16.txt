The paper discusses hybrid AI systems, which combine two primary approaches to artificial intelligence: machine learning and knowledge representation. Machine learning relies on algorithms to identify patterns in data for decision-making, while knowledge representation involves programming explicit facts and rules based on expert knowledge. 

The author emphasizes that many contemporary AI systems are hybrids of these approaches. For instance, self-driving cars utilize machine learning to infer driving behaviors through training, but they also rely on hand-coded rules and knowledge representation to guide their actions. This dual approach enhances the effectiveness of AI systems in various domains, showcasing the integration of both methodologies in practical applications.

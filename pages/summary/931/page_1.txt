In "Artificial Intelligence and Law: An Overview," Harry Surden provides a comprehensive examination of the intersection between artificial intelligence (AI) and legal practice. The article discusses the implications of AI technologies in the legal field, including their potential to enhance legal research, streamline administrative tasks, and improve decision-making processes. Surden highlights both the opportunities and challenges posed by AI, such as issues of accountability, bias in algorithms, and the need for regulatory frameworks to govern AI applications in law. He emphasizes the importance of understanding AI's capabilities and limitations to effectively integrate these technologies into legal systems while ensuring ethical standards are maintained. The article serves as a foundational resource for legal professionals and scholars interested in the evolving role of AI in law.

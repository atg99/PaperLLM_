The paper discusses the distinction between Strong AI, also known as Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), and current AI technologies. Strong AI is characterized by its ability to engage in abstract reasoning and operate as independent cognitive systems, a concept often portrayed in entertainment. However, the author argues that contemporary AI does not approach this level of capability, lacking higher-order human abilities such as abstract reasoning and flexible problem-solving. Current AI systems excel in narrow domains with clear parameters, such as games like chess, where they can leverage fast computation and discernible patterns. While the possibility of achieving Strong AI exists, many experts remain skeptical about its imminent development, particularly within the next five to ten years. The paper emphasizes the current state of AI as one of "narrow" intelligence rather than general intelligence.

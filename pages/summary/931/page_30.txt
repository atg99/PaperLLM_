The paper discusses the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) in the administration of law, highlighting its influence in judicial and policing contexts. In the judicial system, AI is utilized for automated risk assessments based on past crime data, which, while not mandatory for judges, significantly impacts their decisions. This demonstrates the potential of AI systems to affect legal outcomes. 

In the realm of government benefits, AI systems have been developed to streamline the approval process for applicants, employing automated assessments that can determine or influence outcomes, emphasizing the efficiency gains for government agencies.

In policing, AI's application is primarily seen in predictive policing, where machine learning analyzes historical crime data to forecast future crime locations and times. This allows law enforcement to allocate resources more effectively. The paper underscores the growing reliance on AI technologies in these critical areas, raising important considerations about their implications for justice and governance.

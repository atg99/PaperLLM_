The paper discusses the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) in the legal field, particularly in tasks traditionally performed by lawyers. It highlights the capability of AI systems to analyze patterns in data, such as identifying keywords in sexual harassment cases or reviewing contracts during merger due diligence. While AI can assist in document assembly and legal research, its effectiveness is limited to simpler, repetitive tasks. Complex legal work still requires human intervention, as AI typically provides initial drafts or highlights issues rather than producing final legal documents. Additionally, the paper notes the emerging role of machine learning in predicting legal outcomes, helping attorneys assess the strength of client arguments in legal proceedings. The overall conclusion is that while AI is transforming certain aspects of lawyering, human expertise remains essential for more sophisticated legal tasks.

YT-513-U: This dataset encompasses 100k hours of segmented, unlabeled audio in 513 lesser-known languages not included in the YouTube production language identification models. These languages were identified through vendor input, and the audio is also sourced from "speech-heavy" YouTube videos, ensuring a rich variety of linguistic data for model training.

In summary, the training approach emphasizes an initial phase using untranscribed and paired speech-text data, followed by a phase integrating unspoken text after a set number of training steps. The training leverages large datasets, including YT-SUP+ and YT-NTL-U, to enhance model performance across multiple languages. The methodology utilizes separate optimizers for encoder and decoder parameters, ensuring optimized learning during the 100k training steps. The GShard framework enables efficient training of these large models on TPUs, facilitating the handling of substantial audio datasets for robust speech recognition capabilities.

The paper discusses advancements in automatic speech recognition (ASR) and speech translation tasks across multiple languages, emphasizing the development of models capable of decoding a wide range of languages. It highlights the language expansion effort on YouTube, achieving results for 73 languages and aiming to extend to over 100 in the public dataset FLEURS. The paper presents findings that no existing model can decode all 73 languages effectively, thus focusing on the authors' results.

Key contributions include BEST-RQ, a scalable speech representation learner that utilizes a Conformer-based backbone with 2 billion parameters, demonstrating improved performance compared to models like Wav2Vec 2.0. MOST combines BEST-RQ with text-injection techniques, significantly enhancing the quality of downstream speech tasks, evidenced by state-of-the-art results on FLEURS and CoV oST 2 benchmarks.

The paper introduces chunk-wise attention, which allows ASR models trained on shorter utterances to perform well on long speech inputs, yielding high-quality transcripts from YouTube evaluation sets. The outline of the report includes sections on methods, data, and key results, detailing the architectures and datasets used, along with the performance outcomes that establish new benchmarks in speech understanding tasks.

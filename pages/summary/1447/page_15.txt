Figure 7 illustrates the WER for Conformer models, indicating that deeper models show improved performance initially; however, a significant increase in depth can lead to higher WER due to receptive field mismatches. 

Table 7 further explores the efficacy of chunk-wise attention compared to local self-attention models. It reveals that chunk-wise attention maintains robust performance across multiple languages without the degradation seen in local self-attention when increasing the context window. Specifically, models using chunk-wise attention significantly reduce WER across all tested languages.

Overall, the findings highlight the effectiveness of USM-CTC models in long-form transcription tasks and their generalization capabilities, suggesting potential pathways for further enhancing automatic speech recognition models.

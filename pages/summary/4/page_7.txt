The paper "The Computational Limits of Deep Learning" presented at LIMITS '21 discusses the computational, economic, and environmental costs associated with training deep learning models. It emphasizes the significant hardware requirements, measured in floating point operations per second (flops), necessary for improving performance across various applications. The authors utilize a methodology for assessing the economic and environmental impacts of training on V100 GPUs, confirming pricing and carbon emissions estimates as of 2022. The findings highlight concerns about the negative consequences of deep learning's current development trajectory, particularly regarding its sustainability. While the primary focus is on training costs, the paper also addresses deployment costs in a later section, indicating a comprehensive analysis of the implications of deep learning on resources and the environment.

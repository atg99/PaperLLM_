Table 5 summarizes various methods applied to different datasets, highlighting their optimization objectives and the computational resources utilized. The methods include both published and some historical unpublished works. 

Key methods and their details are as follows:

- RLZoph and Lee optimized for accuracy on CIFAR-10 and PTB using 800 Nvidia K80 GPUs over 22,400 GPU hours.
- NASNet achieved accuracy on CIFAR-10 with 500 Nvidia P100 GPUs for 2,000 GPU hours.
- BlockQNN focused on accuracy using CIFAR-10 with 32 Nvidia 1080Ti GPUs over 3 days.
- MetaQNN worked with SVHN, MNIST, and CIFAR-10 to optimize accuracy, using Nvidia GPUs for 8-10 days.
- MONAS and EAS both targeted accuracy on CIFAR-10, employing Nvidia 1080Ti GPUs with varying computational durations.
- ENAS optimized accuracy on CIFAR-10 and PTB using Nvidia 1080Ti GPUs in less than 16 hours.
- AmoebaNet and GeNet focused on accuracy, with AmoebaNet utilizing 450 Nvidia K40 GPUs over approximately 7 days.
- NEMO aimed at accuracy and latency using multiple datasets, including a Drowsiness Dataset, with 60 Nvidia Tesla M40 GPUs.
- DARTS optimized for accuracy on CIFAR-10, requiring 1 Nvidia 1080Ti GPU for 1.5 to 4 days.

The table presents a comprehensive overview of the methods, datasets, objectives, and computational efforts, providing insights into the evolution of architecture search techniques in the field of machine learning.

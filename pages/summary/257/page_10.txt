The referenced papers focus on advancements in 3D shape analysis and generative models, primarily using techniques such as dynamic routing, deep learning, and convolutional neural networks. 

Saqur and Vivona introduce Capsgan, which employs dynamic routing in generative adversarial networks to enhance image synthesis. Sun et al. present Pointgrow, an autoregressive model utilizing self-attention for point cloud generation. Sung et al. discuss deep functional dictionaries for extracting consistent semantic structures from 3D models.

Tatarchenko et al. propose tangent convolutions for dense predictions in 3D data, while Upadhyay and Schrater explore GAN architectures using capsule networks. Wang and Liu provide an optimization perspective on dynamic routing between capsules.

Wang et al. introduce O-cnn, an octree-based CNN for 3D shape analysis, and Weiler et al. develop 3D steerable CNNs for rotationally equivariant feature learning. Wu et al. focus on probabilistic latent spaces for object shapes through 3D generative modeling, while Wu et al. present 3D ShapeNets as a deep representation for volumetric shapes.

Yang et al. describe FoldingNet, a point cloud auto-encoder, and Yi et al. propose a scalable framework for region annotation in 3D shape collections. Yu et al. introduce Pu-Net, a network for point cloud upsampling, and Liu et al. present Dynamic Graph CNN for learning on point clouds.

Zeng et al. develop 3DMatch, a method for learning local geometric descriptors, while Zhang et al. introduce Cappronet for deep feature learning via orthogonal projections. Lastly, Zhou and Tuzel present VoxelNet, an end-to-end learning framework for 3D object detection based on point clouds. 

These works collectively contribute to the field by enhancing generative modeling, improving feature extraction, and facilitating better understanding and manipulation of 3D data.

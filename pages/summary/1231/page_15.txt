The paper presents a comparison of the average runtime of various algorithms, specifically focusing on tuneRangerMMCE, tuneRangerAUC, tuneRangerBrier, tuneRangerLogloss, mlrHyperopt, caret, tuneRF, and the default ranger algorithm. The results are illustrated in two plots: the upper plot shows the average runtime in an unscaled format, while the lower plot uses a logarithmic scale for better visualization. The datasets are organized based on the average runtime of the tuneRangerMMCE algorithm, highlighting its performance relative to the other methods. This analysis is crucial for understanding the efficiency of different tuning algorithms in machine learning applications.

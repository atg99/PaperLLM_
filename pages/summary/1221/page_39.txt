The paper discusses the implications of control variables in research, particularly in the context of organizational science. It emphasizes the potential nuisance of control variables, arguing that their inclusion can complicate the analysis and interpretation of results. The paper presents a summary of articles published in Organization Science, highlighting their distribution across different volumes and issues. Each entry includes the total count of articles and notable contributions from various authors over several years. The data showcases the diverse range of topics addressed, reflecting the evolving nature of research within the field. The findings suggest that while control variables can serve a purpose, researchers must carefully consider their necessity and impact on study outcomes.

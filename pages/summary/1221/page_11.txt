Their analysis highlights the importance of early career experiences in shaping long-term professional trajectories, particularly in fields related to scientific research. The study emphasizes that while control variables were included, the complex interplay of factors influencing career outcomes makes causal interpretations challenging. This aligns with the broader discussion on the limitations of control variables in establishing causality, as seen in the previous example by Hoffman and Strezhnev, where building characteristics may correlate with other unobserved factors affecting eviction outcomes. 

Overall, both studies underscore the necessity of careful consideration when interpreting the effects of control variables in research, advocating for a cautious approach in making causal claims.

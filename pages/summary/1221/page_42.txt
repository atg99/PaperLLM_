The paper discusses the interpretation of control variables in research articles published in Organization Science and Strategic Management Journal from 2015 to 2020. It identifies four main categories of interpretation: 

1. Researchers indicate that control variables behave as expected based on existing literature.
2. They highlight interesting results related to the significance and sign of control variable coefficients.
3. Control variable estimates are viewed as supportive evidence for findings in prior studies.
4. Authors offer ex-post rationalizations of control variable estimates using prior theoretical frameworks.

Examples provided in the paper illustrate these categories, showing how authors have articulated the expected behavior of control variables, such as the positive effects of total assets on divestment and acquisition frequencies, and the influence of the number of hotels on market dynamics. This highlights the varied approaches researchers take when interpreting the role of control variables in their analyses.

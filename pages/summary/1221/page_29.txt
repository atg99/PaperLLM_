The paper discusses the role of control variables in research published in the Strategic Management Journal and Organization Science from January 2015 to December 2020. It provides a detailed literature review, highlighting the prevalence of control variables in parametric regression models within these journals. The review includes tables summarizing the total number of articles published in specific issues and the count of those that interpret control variable estimates. For example, in Strategic Management Journal Volume 41, various issues showcase a range of articles, with some issues featuring multiple articles that interpret control variables, indicating a trend in the literature towards acknowledging the importance of these variables in research findings. Overall, the analysis underscores the necessity of considering control variables in empirical studies to enhance the robustness of interpretations in strategic management research.

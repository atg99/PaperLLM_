The paper discusses the implications of using control variables in research, particularly within the context of strategic management. It presents a detailed analysis of various articles published in the Strategic Management Journal, showcasing the number of articles and their respective contributions to the field. The data includes a breakdown of articles across different issues and highlights the diversity of research topics explored by various authors. The argument centers around the idea that while control variables can enhance the rigor of research, they may also introduce complexity and potential bias, thus complicating the interpretation of results. The paper calls for a careful consideration of the role of control variables in research design to avoid unnecessary complications in analysis and conclusions.

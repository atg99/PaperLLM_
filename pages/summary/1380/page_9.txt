The paper introduces FEN, a hierarchical reinforcement learning model aimed at achieving both fairness and efficiency in multi-agent systems. FEN operates with a controller that optimizes a fair-efficient reward, while sub-policies focus on environmental rewards and fairness behaviors. In the context of a grid world scenario with five agents and eight gems of three types, agents collect gems to manufacture unique parts, with production limited by the least number of parts made by any agent. The results, as shown in Table 2, indicate that FEN significantly outperforms baseline models in terms of resource utilization, number of products manufactured, and fairness. Specifically, FEN achieves an 82% resource utilization rate and produces 48 products, compared to much lower performance from other methods. The model is validated across various scenarios, demonstrating its capability to balance fairness and efficiency effectively.

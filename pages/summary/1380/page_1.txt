The paper "Learning Fairness in Multi-Agent Systems" by Jiechuan Jiang and Zongqing Lu addresses the critical role of fairness in multi-agent systems, which is vital for stability and productivity. The authors introduce FEN, a hierarchical reinforcement learning model designed to optimize both fairness and efficiency simultaneously, a challenge due to the complex nature of multi-objective joint-policy optimization.

FEN operates by decomposing fairness for each agent and proposing a fair-efficient reward structure that allows each agent to learn its own policy. The model includes a hierarchy with a controller that maximizes the fair-efficient reward by managing sub-policies, each contributing diverse behaviors for effective environmental interaction. This decentralized training approach enhances the model's applicability in real-world scenarios.

The paper highlights that existing methods often focus on maximizing individual or shared rewards without adequately addressing fairness. While some game-theoretic frameworks have been developed, they typically do not generalize well across different applications. In contrast, FEN demonstrates empirical superiority over baseline approaches in various multi-agent environments, effectively balancing the dual objectives of fairness and efficiency.

The paper presents an end-to-end framework for point cloud registration, highlighting the effectiveness of its novel designs that achieve registration accuracy comparable to state-of-the-art geometric methods. The framework leverages a point weighting layer to detect keypoints, with pink keypoints identifying stationary objects and grey keypoints primarily on the ground. Visualization of matching similarity probabilities demonstrates that the detected keypoints are salient, leading to concentrated matching probabilities before and after registration.

The authors emphasize the advantages of their learning-based system over traditional methods like ICP, noting its ability to automatically learn effective features for registration and its robustness to inaccurate initial poses. Additionally, the framework benefits from GPU acceleration, ensuring runtime efficiency comparable to common geometric methods. The authors express confidence in the method's potential for various applications and indicate plans to enhance the model's generalization capability with more diverse LiDAR data in future work.

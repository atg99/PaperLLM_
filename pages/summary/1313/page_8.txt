The paper discusses advancements in service recommendation and selection within edge computing environments, emphasizing quality of service (QoS) prediction through deep feature learning. It highlights the importance of efficient service selection for workflow reconfiguration, proposing an interface-based computing approach. Additionally, it explores the application of probabilistic model checking in evaluating and controlling financial production risks, illustrated through a case study on Alibaba's Yu'e Bao. The findings suggest that integrating these methodologies can enhance decision-making processes in complex computing scenarios.

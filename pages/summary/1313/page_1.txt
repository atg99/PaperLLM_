The paper discusses the advancements in mobile healthcare robots, highlighting their advantages over human counterparts and addressing challenges related to communication networks, notably high response latency and computing demands. It proposes offloading delay-sensitive and communication-intensive tasks to enhance service quality and user experience. 

Key technologies reviewed include the human-robot interface, environment perception, navigation, robust communication, and artificial intelligence. The authors categorize the functionalities of healthcare robots into edge functionalities, which can handle latency-sensitive tasks like user interaction and health status recognition locally, and core functionalities that require constant updates from data centers, such as radio resource management and service provisioning.

The paper emphasizes the importance of robust and safe wireless communications, suggesting that AI can provide innovative solutions. It outlines typical applications of healthcare robots, such as elder care, chronic patient nursing, unhealthy habit recognition, and mental healthcare. The conclusion points to open research issues that need addressing to enhance the effectiveness of wireless communications in healthcare robotics.

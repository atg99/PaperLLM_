The paper by S. Wan, Z. Gu, and Q. Ni discusses the significant role of edge computing in enhancing mobile healthcare robots, particularly for elderly and chronic patients. It highlights the increasing demand for efficient healthcare solutions, addressing the challenges posed by high costs and the need for rapid responses in scenarios like wireless health monitoring, virtual reality, and robotics. The authors argue that mobile healthcare robots, supported by artificial intelligence, can outperform human caregivers in diagnosis and treatment, providing improved adherence and detailed monitoring through advanced sensors.

The communication between robots and data centers is crucial, especially for emergency situations, where response latency can be detrimental. The paper emphasizes the importance of a balanced framework between edge computing and centralized computing to optimize performance. The integration of Internet of Things (IoT) technologies is expected to further enhance healthcare services by reducing the communication burden through data pre-processing at the edge.

The key contributions of the paper include an investigation of mobile healthcare robot applications, an analysis of required functionalities, and a discussion on how embracing edge computing technologies can expedite advancements in healthcare robotics. The structure of the paper includes sections summarizing the needs and functionalities of healthcare robots, analyzing task-driven demands in edge computing, and evaluating centralized computing approaches. The authors conclude with insights on future directions for research in this area.

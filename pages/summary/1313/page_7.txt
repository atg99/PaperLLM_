The paper by S. Wan, Z. Gu, and Q. Ni, published in Computer Communications, explores advancements in the Internet of Things (IoT) and personalized healthcare, emphasizing the integration of technology to enhance user experience and health management. The authors acknowledge no competing interests and express gratitude for the financial support received from various Chinese research foundations.

The references cited in the paper cover a wide array of topics related to IoT, including robotic companions, healthcare applications, data indexing, edge computing, and energy-aware computation methods. The authors discuss the significance of these technologies in improving healthcare services and address challenges in deploying IoT solutions effectively.

Key concepts include the validation of lifelogging data, the use of generative adversarial networks in medical imaging, and the development of smart rehabilitation systems. The paper underscores the potential of IoT to revolutionize personalized healthcare while also highlighting the need for ongoing research to overcome existing challenges in the field. 

The authors' contributions reflect a comprehensive understanding of the intersection between IoT and healthcare, providing a foundation for future studies in this evolving area.

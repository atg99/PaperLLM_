In contrast, knowledge-based approaches leverage predefined rules and expert knowledge to interpret data, which can provide reliable outputs in well-defined scenarios. A hybrid approach that combines both data-driven and knowledge-based techniques may offer the most robust solution for healthcare robots, allowing them to adapt to diverse and unpredictable environments while maintaining accuracy in activity recognition.

Feature extraction, navigation, and innovation are essential components in the functionality of mobile healthcare robots. Feature extraction employs convolutional neural networks to automate the identification of relevant scene features, enhancing the robot's ability to recognize objects and process large-scale images. Navigation is crucial for task execution, requiring advanced path planning to navigate complex environments while avoiding obstacles. Traditional methods like the artificial potential field method have limitations, but improvements have been made to address issues such as local minima and oscillation in narrow spaces.

Moreover, innovation in mobile healthcare robots is driven by user needs, necessitating an integrated information architecture that connects healthcare professionals and technical personnel. This architecture aims to manage the complexity of home environments while prioritizing various robot functionalities.

Data-center-oriented communications underpin these functionalities, relying on artificial intelligence techniques to enhance user experience and operational efficiency. The handling of uncertainty, particularly in healthcare tasks, is critical, as unexpected situations can have serious consequences. Edge computing offers a solution by decentralizing data processing, allowing for faster response times and improved efficiency.

The integration of the Internet of Things in healthcare presents opportunities for enhanced activity recognition, but it also introduces challenges related to the management of high-dimensional heterogeneous data. Addressing sensor uncertainty through robust communication networks and a combination of data-driven and knowledge-based solutions can significantly improve the decision-making capabilities of mobile healthcare robots, enabling personalized healthcare experiences for care recipients.

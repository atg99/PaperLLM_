The section on deep feature extraction discusses the process of utilizing features learned from pretrained convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to enhance machine learning classification tasks. Specifically, the study extracts effective deep features from various layers of models including GoogleNet, ResNet50, ResNet101, Inception V3, InceptionResNetV2, and SqueezeNet. Each model provides different feature vector sizes, such as 1024 from GoogleNet and 4096 from SqueezeNet. These features are then utilized in classification phases employing traditional classifiers such as Support Vector Machine (SVM), Extreme Learning Machine (ELM), and K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN).

The study also incorporates the AlexNet, VGG16, and VGG19 models, extracting features from their fully connected layers for performance evaluation. The performance of these layers is compared to determine the most effective deep features for classification.

In summarizing the proposed architecture and transfer learning process, the steps include acquiring and resizing plant images, extracting features from the fully connected layers of the deep learning models, and performing classification with traditional classifiers. Additionally, for transfer learning, the pretrained models are modified by removing the last three layers and replacing them with a new fully connected layer, softmax layer, and classification output layer to tailor them for specific tasks.

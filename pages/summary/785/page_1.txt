The paper "Plant disease and pest detection using deep learning-based features" by Muammer Türk Oğlu and Davut Hanbay, published in the Turkish Journal of Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences, explores the application of deep learning techniques for identifying plant diseases and pests. The study emphasizes the importance of accurate detection in agriculture to ensure crop health and yield. The authors present a framework that utilizes deep learning algorithms to analyze images of plants, enabling the identification of various diseases and pests based on learned features. The findings demonstrate the effectiveness of deep learning in enhancing agricultural practices, providing a robust solution for early detection and management of plant health issues. The work contributes to the field by offering insights into the integration of computer science with agricultural technology.

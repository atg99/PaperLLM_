The paper by Türkoğlu and Hanbay presents an analysis of the performance of various deep learning models for classifying plant diseases using a heterogeneous dataset. A confusion matrix based on the ResNet50 model and SVM classifier is provided, detailing the classification results for eight classes of plant diseases and pests. The training time for the ResNet50 model was 2 minutes and 21 seconds, significantly faster than the Inception V3 model, which took 9 minutes and 29 seconds.

Performance measures including sensitivity, specificity, and F1-score for different classifiers are summarized in Table 7. The ResNet50 model achieved a sensitivity of 97.35%, specificity of 99.69%, and an F1-score of 97.14%. Other models such as GoogleNet, ResNet101, and various Inception models were also evaluated, with varying performance metrics reported.

The study employs transfer learning, utilizing pretrained CNN networks with specific parameters including a batch size of 10, a maximum of 7 epochs, and an initial learning rate of 0.0004. The training utilized the stochastic gradient descent with momentum method, concluding after 1250 iterations. Results, including accuracy scores and their standard deviations, are discussed in relation to the overall effectiveness of the models in classifying plant diseases.

The paper discusses various methodologies and advancements in the field of plant disease detection using image processing and deep learning techniques. It references multiple studies that focus on the application of image processing for identifying plant diseases through texture features, classification systems, and neural networks. Key contributions include the use of deep learning for image-based detection, as highlighted by the works of Mohanty et al. and Sladojevic et al., which emphasize the effectiveness of deep neural networks in recognizing plant diseases from leaf images. The references also cover foundational research in deep learning, including significant architectures like convolutional neural networks and their enhancements, such as residual learning and inception networks. This body of work illustrates a growing trend towards automated and accurate plant disease diagnosis, showcasing the integration of modern computational techniques in agricultural practices.

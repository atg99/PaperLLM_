The study conducted by TÜRKOĞLU and HANBA evaluates the performance of various deep learning models and traditional methods for classification tasks. The ResNet50 model achieved the highest accuracy at 97.86%, followed closely by the ResNet101 model at 97.45%. The VGG16 model showed strong performance in transfer learning, with an accuracy of 96.92%. In contrast, traditional methods such as LBP, HOG, GLCM, and color features yielded significantly lower accuracy, with the LBP method achieving the highest score of 70.9% when combined with an SVM classifier.

The findings indicate that deep learning models consistently outperformed traditional methods across the board. Notably, deep feature extraction using SVM or ELM classifiers often yielded better results than models fine-tuned through transfer learning. Specific combinations, such as ResNet50 features with SVM and ResNet101 features with ELM, demonstrated superior accuracy compared to their fine-tuned counterparts. Conversely, fine-tuning improved the performance of VGG16, GoogleNet, and VGG19 models beyond their respective feature extraction methods.

Overall, the results underscore the effectiveness of deep learning techniques, particularly with SVM and ELM classifiers, in achieving high classification accuracy compared to traditional approaches.

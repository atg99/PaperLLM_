In the paper by TÜRKOĞLU and HANBA, the authors discuss the application of transfer learning in the context of identifying plant diseases and pests. They present images of various plant diseases and pests, including Coryneum beijerinckii and Erwinia amylovora, which serve as samples for their experimentation. The study emphasizes the use of pretrained Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) for this task, highlighting that transfer learning allows for a more efficient approach compared to training CNN models from scratch. The authors fine-tune these pretrained models by modifying the last three layers to better suit their classification needs, thereby leveraging the knowledge gained from existing models to accelerate and improve the performance of their specific application.

This section of the paper presents an analysis of image compression effects on feature extraction using deep learning models. It includes a comparison between the original image and its JPEG compressed version with a quality factor (QF) of 10, specifically focusing on an image labeled "Tailed Frog" from the ImageNet validation dataset. 

Figure 10 displays both the original and compressed images, highlighting the degradation in quality due to compression. Figure 11 presents the feature maps extracted from Layer 2 of the Inception V3 model for both the original and compressed images. This comparison aims to illustrate how compression impacts the feature representation learned by the model, which is crucial for understanding the implications of image quality on machine learning performance. The visualizations are best viewed in electronic format to appreciate the differences in detail and quality.

The paper presents histograms illustrating the Quality Factor (QF) values selected by a High-Resolution Synthesis (HRS) method for two different neural network architectures: Inception V3 and ResNet-50 V2. In the histogram for Inception V3, a significant proportion of QF values are concentrated around lower percentages, with 76.5% of values falling within the 0.3% to 2.2% range. The histogram for ResNet-50 V2 similarly shows a distribution skewed towards lower QF values, with 76% of values appearing in the same range. These findings suggest a trend in the QF selections made by HRS, indicating a preference for lower quality factors in image processing tasks.

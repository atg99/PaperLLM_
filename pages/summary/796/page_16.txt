The paper discusses three selectors designed to maintain classification accuracy while reducing the input size of images fed into deep neural networks (DNNs). These selectors are named Top 1 Keeper (T1K), Top 5 Keeper (T5K), and Top 1 and Top 5 Keeper (TTK). 

T1K ensures that the Top 1 accuracy remains consistent with the underlying DNN by selecting images where the Top 1 label matches. T5K achieves the same for Top 5 accuracy by focusing on the set of Top 5 labels. TTK combines both criteria to maintain both Top 1 and Top 5 accuracies. 

The effectiveness of these selectors is demonstrated on the ImageNet validation dataset using DNNs like Inception V3 and ResNet-50 V2. Results indicate that while T1K can reduce Top 5 accuracy by up to 1.5% and T5K can lower Top 1 accuracy by up to 1.26%, they significantly compress the input size. Specifically, T1K achieves an average compression ratio of 8.8, T5K 3.1, and TTK 2.9, indicating substantial reductions in image size while preserving accuracy metrics. 

Tables in the paper provide detailed accuracy metrics and compression ratios, confirming the selectors' effectiveness in maintaining performance while minimizing data size.

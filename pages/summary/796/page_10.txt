The paper discusses the performance of the Hierarchical Representation Selection (HRS) method applied to the ImageNet validation dataset, specifically focusing on its ability to improve the compression and classification of images processed by Deep Neural Networks (DNNs) such as Inception V3 and ResNet-50 V2. 

Table 2 presents the compression performance of HRS, showing that it effectively reduces the size of images while maintaining classification accuracy. The default selector (D) always uses the original image as input, while HRS selects a compressed image that improves the ranking of the ground truth (GT) label from below the top k to within the top k.

Through the example of Image #651, the paper illustrates how HRS selects a specific compressed image (I10) that results in a higher ranking compared to the original image. The feature maps extracted from the DNN's layers reveal that the compressed image contains clearer and more contrasting features of the bird, enhancing the DNN's ability to distinguish between similar species, such as the Brambling and Junco snowbird. This improvement in feature clarity is attributed to the reduced complexity and energy present in the background of the compressed image, which facilitates better classification outcomes in subsequent layers of the DNN. 

Overall, the findings emphasize the effectiveness of HRS in optimizing image inputs for DNNs, thereby improving both compression and classification performance.

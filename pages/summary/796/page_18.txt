The paper discusses the enhancement of object recognition in convolutional neural networks (CNNs) through the process of compression, which improves the visibility of the object by reducing surrounding disturbances. The authors suggest that this principle may extend to Turing classifiers, based on insights from Kolmogorov complexity and lossless compression. The research involved contributions from multiple authors in various capacities, including conceptualization, methodology, software development, and validation. The work was supported by the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada and the Canada Research Chairs Program. No conflicts of interest were reported, and data sharing was deemed not applicable. The paper cites numerous references to foundational works in deep learning and object recognition, emphasizing the evolution and impact of CNNs in the field.

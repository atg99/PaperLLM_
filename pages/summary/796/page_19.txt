The paper provides a comprehensive overview of various studies related to image and video compression using neural networks, highlighting key advancements and methodologies in the field. It references significant works, including the JPEG compression standard and the development of large-scale image databases like ImageNet, which have been pivotal in training deep learning models for image recognition tasks. The impact of image quality on deep learning performance is discussed, with studies illustrating how image degradations influence recognition accuracy. Additionally, the paper reviews several approaches to enhance the robustness of neural networks against such degradations, including stability training and the use of selective CNN filters. The utilization of established TensorFlow models, such as Inception v3 and ResNet-50, is also noted, demonstrating the practical applications of these techniques in real-world scenarios. Overall, the paper emphasizes the ongoing evolution of compression methods and their integration with deep learning technologies to improve image processing outcomes.

The paper discusses the integration of attention mechanisms in spatio-temporal deep learning models (STDM) to enhance performance across various applications, particularly in handling sequential data and image-like spatial maps. It outlines two primary types of attention mechanisms: spatial domain attention and temporal domain attention, with some models utilizing both. For instance, a combined attention model proposed by one study employs both soft and hard attention to forecast pedestrian movement, while another model, DeepCrime, applies temporal attention to improve crime prediction by weighting the relevance of past crime patterns. 

The paper highlights the applications of STDM across various domains, with a notable emphasis on transportation and human mobility due to the increasing availability of related data. It details how deep learning techniques are employed to analyze complex spatio-temporal correlations in transportation data for tasks like traffic flow prediction, incident detection, and congestion prediction. This data can be modeled in different formats, such as time series or spatial maps, facilitating the use of architectures like GraphCNN for traffic analysis.

Additionally, the paper explores the rise of on-demand services, such as ride-sharing platforms, which generate substantial spatio-temporal data regarding customer locations and service times. The accurate prediction of demand and supply in these services is crucial for enhancing customer satisfaction, and deep learning methods are being utilized to address this challenge. Overall, the integration of advanced deep learning techniques into STDM showcases their potential in various real-world applications, driving improvements in efficiency and service delivery.

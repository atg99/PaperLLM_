This collection of references highlights recent advancements in deep learning and machine learning techniques applied to spatio-temporal data analysis, particularly in areas such as traffic prediction, location-based services, and trajectory modeling. Notable works include the development of recurrent neural networks for check-in time prediction and the use of deep learning frameworks for traffic forecasting. Several studies focus on utilizing deep spatio-temporal networks and graph convolutional networks to improve predictions in urban settings, emphasizing the importance of capturing complex spatial and temporal patterns. Other contributions involve meta-learning approaches for spatial-temporal prediction and the application of deep learning in accident detection from social media data. The references collectively showcase the growing intersection of artificial intelligence and geographic information systems, underscoring the potential of these technologies to enhance urban mobility and planning.

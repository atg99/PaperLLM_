In summary, the paper discusses the application of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) for predictive learning in various domains, including urban crowd flow, ride-sourcing services, and wind speed forecasting. Key advancements include the development of UrbanFlow for crowd prediction, hexagon-based CNNs for ride-sourcing demand forecasting, and ConvLSTM for spatio-temporal sequence prediction. Additionally, it highlights the use of models like DCRNN and GRNN for traffic flow prediction on road networks, emphasizing the importance of spatial and temporal dependencies. The paper also addresses trajectory prediction using RNNs and CNNs, detailing methods that represent trajectories as sequences or matrices to enhance predictive accuracy. Overall, the integration of deep learning techniques tailored to specific data representations plays a crucial role in advancing predictive modeling across various applications.

The paper discusses advanced methodologies in spatiotemporal data modeling (STDM) using deep learning techniques, particularly focusing on the integration and analysis of various data sources. It elaborates on the use of multi-sourced data including road networks, points of interest (POIs), meteorological data, and air quality indices, emphasizing their significance in enhancing prediction accuracy for various tasks.

Key contributions include a multichannel spatiotemporal CNN architecture for semi-supervised bounding box prediction of extreme climate events, which utilizes temporal information and unlabeled data to improve localization. Additionally, the paper highlights other relevant applications of deep learning in STDM, such as POI recommendation, human mobility pattern mining, and trajectory-user linking. Notably, the content-aware hierarchical POI embedding model (CAPE) and a semi-supervised trajectory-user relation learning framework (TULVAE) are introduced as notable innovations in these areas.

The fusion of multi-sourced data is explored through raw data-level and latent feature-level methods. The raw data-level fusion approach integrates various features before inputting them into deep learning models, exemplified by the ADAIN model that combines urban air quality and related data for improved urban air quality inference. Conversely, latent feature-level fusion involves processing different data types through separate models before integrating their latent features, as demonstrated by the ST-ResNet and DMVST-Net frameworks for crowd flow and taxi demand predictions, respectively.

Lastly, the paper addresses the implementation of attention mechanisms, originally developed for machine translation, to enhance the performance of Encoder-Decoder RNNs by allowing models to focus on relevant parts of the input sequence, thus improving outcomes in tasks involving long input sequences. Overall, the findings underscore the importance of data fusion and advanced neural architectures in effectively addressing complex STDM challenges.

The paper discusses the application of deep learning in analyzing spatiotemporal data across various fields, including crime prediction and neuroscience. It highlights the use of advanced models like CNNs and LSTMs to forecast crime risks and classify brain disorders, respectively. Despite the progress, several challenges persist in spatiotemporal data modeling, such as the need for interpretable models, proper selection of data representations, and broader application of deep learning to tasks like frequent pattern mining. Furthermore, the integration of multi-modal spatiotemporal datasets poses an open problem, with limited research on effective fusion techniques to enhance analysis in areas like neuroimaging and urban transportation. The paper calls for further studies to address these challenges and to improve the applicability of deep learning in spatiotemporal data analysis.

This paper provides a comprehensive overview of recent advancements in deep learning techniques for spatiotemporal data mining (STDM). It categorizes various data types and representations of spatiotemporal (ST) data while introducing popular deep learning models applicable to STDM tasks. The authors emphasize the importance of fusing and analyzing multiple modalities of urban crowd flow data to enhance predictive accuracy and capture underlying mobility patterns. Despite recent efforts to transfer knowledge from crowd flow data across different cities, the integration of multimodal ST datasets with deep learning remains underexplored and warrants further investigation.

The paper outlines a general framework for employing deep learning models in STDM, detailing various tasks such as predictive learning, representation learning, classification, estimation, inference, and anomaly detection. Additionally, it summarizes applications of deep learning in diverse fields, including transportation, on-demand services, climate and weather, human mobility, location-based social networks, crime analysis, and neuroscience.

Finally, the authors identify open problems in the field and suggest future research directions, highlighting the rapid growth and potential of deep learning in spatiotemporal analysis.

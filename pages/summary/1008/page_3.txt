This paper provides a comprehensive overview of spatio-temporal data mining (STDM) and the application of deep learning models in this domain. The organization of the survey includes an introduction to the categorization of ST data, a framework for utilizing deep learning in STDM, a review of various STDM tasks, a gallery of applications across different fields, and a discussion of limitations and future research directions.

The categorization of spatio-temporal data is critical for understanding the diverse data types and their implications for data mining tasks. The paper identifies five main types of ST data: event data, trajectory data, point reference data, raster data, and video data. Each type is characterized by its unique structure and the manner in which it is collected.

Event data consists of discrete occurrences at specific locations and times, such as crime or traffic incidents. Trajectory data tracks the movement of objects over time, often collected via sensors like GPS. Point reference data involves continuous measurements at varying locations over time, such as meteorological data. Raster data captures measurements at fixed locations and times, displaying a continuous or discrete field. Lastly, video data comprises sequences of images, capturing spatial and temporal correlations.

The paper highlights the importance of understanding these data types in developing appropriate deep learning models, as different models are tailored to specific data structures. The survey ultimately aims to enhance the understanding and application of deep learning in the context of STDM, while also addressing current research gaps and suggesting future avenues for exploration.

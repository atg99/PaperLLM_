The paper "Deep Learning for Spatio-Temporal Data Mining: A Survey" by Senzhang Wang, Jiannong Cao, and Philip S. Yu discusses the growing significance of spatio-temporal data mining (STDM) due to advancements in positioning technologies like GPS and remote sensing. The authors highlight that traditional statistical methods are becoming inadequate for analyzing the increasing volume and complexity of spatio-temporal datasets, which are essential for applications in fields such as transportation, urban planning, and public safety.

The survey categorizes spatio-temporal data types and popular deep learning models like convolutional neural networks (CNN) and recurrent neural networks (RNN), which have shown success in various machine learning tasks due to their ability to learn hierarchical features effectively. The paper outlines a framework for utilizing deep learning in STDM and classifies existing literature based on data types, mining tasks, and models used.

Key advantages of deep learning over traditional methods include automatic feature representation learning, allowing for the direct analysis of raw data without extensive feature engineering, and robust function approximation capabilities, enabling better handling of complex non-linear relationships in data. The paper concludes with a discussion on the limitations of current research and suggests future research directions in the field, emphasizing the need for further exploration of deep learning applications in diverse domains.

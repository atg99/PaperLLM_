The paper "Understanding of Convolutional Neural Network (CNN): A Review" by Purwono discusses the concepts and functionalities of deep learning, particularly focusing on Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN). It highlights how machine learning, especially deep learning, enables self-training without repetitive human programming, relying on initial data sets for predictions. The paper distinguishes between supervised and unsupervised learning methods, explaining that supervised algorithms create predictive models based on input-output relationships, while unsupervised algorithms classify data based on inherent characteristics.

The text emphasizes the advantages of deep learning over traditional machine learning by showcasing its ability to automatically extract features from raw data and manage complex non-linear functions through deep network layers. CNN is identified as a prominent deep learning algorithm, extensively utilized in computer vision tasks such as image classification, segmentation, object detection, and more.

The paper outlines the architecture of CNN, which consists of four primary layers: convolution layer, pooling layer, fully connected layer, and nonlinearity layer. Each layer plays a critical role in processing images, where the convolution layer interprets images as matrices of pixel values. Overall, the article aims to provide a succinct yet thorough overview of CNN and its applications in artificial intelligence.

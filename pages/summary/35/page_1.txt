The paper titled "Understanding of Convolutional Neural Network (CNN): A Review" provides an overview of deep learning technology, particularly focusing on Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs). The authors highlight the rapid adoption of deep learning across various sectors, emphasizing its capability to mimic human-like functions such as knowledge acquisition, problem-solving, and decision-making without the need for repetitive human programming.

CNNs are identified as prevalent deep learning algorithms utilized in diverse applications, including image classification, segmentation, object detection, video processing, natural language processing, and speech recognition. The architecture of CNNs consists of four key layers: the convolution layer, which employs kernel filters to extract essential features from input images; the pooling layer, which merges two consecutive convolution layers; the fully connected layer, also known as the convolutional output layer; and the non-linear layer, where activation functions determine the output of the network. Common activation functions discussed include Sigmoid, Tanh, ReLU, Leaky ReLU, Noisy ReLU, and Parametric Linear Units.

The paper further notes that CNN architecture is influenced by the organization and function of the visual cortex, aiming to replicate the neuronal connections in the human brain. Popular CNN architectures mentioned include LeNet, AlexNet, and VGGNet. The review serves to elucidate the significance of CNNs within the broader context of deep learning, machine learning, and artificial intelligence.

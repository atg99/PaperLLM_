The paper titled "Understanding of Convolutional Neural Network (CNN): A Review" provides a comprehensive overview of CNNs, highlighting their evolution, architecture, applications, challenges, and future directions. It references significant works in the field, including studies on learning strides in CNNs, distribution padding, and the role of CNNs in human-machine interaction. The review discusses various CNN architectures such as LeNet, AlexNet, VGG, and ResNet, emphasizing their contributions to image classification and other domains. Additionally, it addresses the challenges faced in applying CNNs and suggests areas for future research to enhance their effectiveness in various applications, particularly in computer vision. The paper serves as a valuable resource for understanding the foundational and advanced concepts surrounding CNNs in robotics and control systems.

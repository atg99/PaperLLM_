The paper titled "Understanding of Convolutional Neural Network (CNN): A Review" by Purwono provides a comprehensive overview of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and their applications across various fields. It highlights the fundamental principles of CNNs, their architecture, and the significance of deep learning techniques in enhancing performance in tasks such as image classification and prediction.

The review also discusses the evolution of CNNs, referencing multiple studies that illustrate their effectiveness in diverse applications, including water quality evaluation, urban water management, and medical imaging for COVID-19 detection. Key references in the paper emphasize the growing relevance of CNNs in processing complex data types, such as hyperspectral images and EEG signals.

Moreover, the paper addresses recent advancements in CNN architectures and their implications for improving classification accuracy. By synthesizing findings from various sources, the review underscores the transformative impact of CNNs in both academic research and practical applications, positioning them as a critical tool in contemporary artificial intelligence and machine learning landscapes.

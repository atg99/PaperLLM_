4.3 Results and Discussion
The results of the experiments are presented, highlighting the effectiveness of the RNN architecture in speech recognition tasks. The use of the ADAM optimization algorithm has contributed to the model's performance by minimizing the need for extensive parameter tuning. The experiments demonstrate a significant reduction in WER, affirming the importance of the improved language model from the TED-LIUM dataset. The findings indicate that the refined dataset and the chosen evaluation metrics provide a solid foundation for assessing the capabilities of the acoustic models developed.

Overall, the study emphasizes the correlation between the quality of the dataset and the performance of the speech recognition system, underscoring the relevance of using well-curated data and effective optimization techniques in achieving superior results in automatic speech recognition.

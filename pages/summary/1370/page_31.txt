This section contains a list of references that contribute to the study of consensus mechanisms in networks, particularly focusing on various types of consensus algorithms and their properties under different conditions. 

Key references include:

- Franceschelli et al. (2011) discuss quantized consensus in Hamiltonian graphs, exploring how consensus can be achieved in specific graph structures.
- Ceragioli et al. (2011) investigate the effects of discontinuities and hysteresis in quantized average consensus, highlighting challenges in maintaining consensus in dynamic systems.
- Huang and Manton (2010) present a study on stochastic consensus seeking with noisy communication, addressing the impact of noise and directed communication on consensus outcomes.
- Bauso et al. (2009) analyze consensus in networks affected by unknown but bounded disturbances, proposing strategies to achieve consensus despite uncertainties.
- Rajagopal and Wainwright (2011) focus on network-based consensus averaging with general noisy channels, providing insights into how noise in communication channels affects consensus processes.

These references collectively contribute to understanding the complexities of achieving consensus in multi-agent systems, particularly under conditions of noise, disturbances, and varying network topologies.

This article serves as a comprehensive overview of multi-agent systems, positioned between a survey paper and a book in terms of length. Unlike typical survey papers, it provides an in-depth exploration of the subject, with each chapter capable of functioning independently as a survey. The article distinguishes itself from existing multi-agent literature, which often focuses narrowly on specific research interests, by offering a broader range of topics. It intentionally omits detailed technical components, such as proofs, to present a clearer and more accessible understanding of the field for readers.

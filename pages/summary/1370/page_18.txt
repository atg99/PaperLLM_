The references listed provide a comprehensive overview of research and developments in the field of multi-agent systems and cooperative control. Key contributions include studies on information consensus, motion coordination, and collective behavior in networked systems. Notable works include:

- Ren et al. (2007) discuss information consensus in multivehicle cooperative control, highlighting the importance of distributed information in achieving coordination.
- Martinez et al. (2007) focus on motion coordination with distributed information, emphasizing the role of communication in multi-agent systems.
- Olfati-Saber et al. (2007) provide insights into consensus and cooperation within networked multi-agent systems, offering a foundational understanding of these concepts.
- Antonelli (2013) presents an overview of interconnected dynamic systems and distributed control, contributing to the theoretical framework of cooperative systems.
- Paley et al. (2007) explore oscillator models and their relation to collective motion, linking biological phenomena to engineering applications.
- Leonard et al. (2007) investigate collective motion in sensor networks, particularly in the context of ocean sampling, showcasing practical applications of consensus algorithms.

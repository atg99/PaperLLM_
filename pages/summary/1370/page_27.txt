The referenced works focus on various aspects of distributed consensus and estimation in networked systems. Key contributions include dynamic average consensus under constraints, nonlinear protocols for consensus, and distributed average tracking in networked Euler-Lagrange systems. The studies explore methods for robust discrete-time consensus and the connection between formation control and average tracking. Additional insights are provided on distributed parameter estimation in sensor networks, emphasizing the challenges of nonlinear observation models and imperfect communication. The importance of distributed regression frameworks for modeling sensor data is also highlighted, alongside the development of new distributed optimization algorithms. The integration of consensus filters within distributed Kalman filters is discussed, showcasing advancements in efficient data processing in decentralized environments.

The references list includes a variety of studies and papers focused on consensus, synchronization, and control in complex networks and multi-agent systems. Key topics covered include:

1. **Consensus and Synchronization**: Burbano and di Bernardo explore proportional-integral coupling in complex networks, contributing to the understanding of synchronization mechanisms.

2. **Adaptive Synchronization**: De Lellis et al. discuss adaptive synchronization techniques for complex networks, highlighting the role of adaptability in achieving consensus.

3. **Neural Network Control**: Cheng et al. present a neural-network-based approach for leader-following control in multi-agent systems, addressing uncertainties in system behavior.

4. **Stable Adaptive Control**: Polycarpou introduces a stable adaptive neural control scheme for nonlinear systems, emphasizing stability in control applications.

5. **Nussbaum-type Functions**: Chen et al. investigate adaptive consensus in multi-agent systems using a novel Nussbaum-type function, focusing on systems with unknown control directions.

6. **Distributed Control**: Droge and Egerstedt discuss distributed parameterized model predictive control for networked multi-agent systems, contributing to decentralized control strategies.

7. **Dual Decomposition**: Terelius et al. and Rantzer examine decentralized multi-agent optimization and dynamic dual decomposition respectively, providing insights into distributed control frameworks.

8. **Passivity-based Control**: Several studies by Chopra, Arcak, Igarashi, and Wang address passivity-based control strategies for multi-agent systems, emphasizing their effectiveness in achieving synchronization and coordination.

These references collectively contribute to the theoretical and practical understanding of control strategies in complex systems, underscoring the importance of adaptability, stability, and decentralized approaches in multi-agent coordination.

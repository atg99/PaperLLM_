The references section includes a compilation of studies and papers that focus on controlling connectivity in dynamic graphs and multi-agent systems. Notable contributions include Zavlanos and Pappas' work on dynamic graph connectivity, Ji and Egerstedt's exploration of distributed coordination while maintaining connectedness, and Ajorlou et al.'s bounded control strategies for connectivity preservation. Bhattacharya and Başar have examined singular surfaces in connectivity maintenance games, while Sabattini et al. have contributed to decentralized connectivity maintenance in mobile robotic systems and networked Lagrangian dynamics. The references also highlight approaches to connectivity maintenance in the presence of disruptions, as seen in the works of Robuffo Giordano et al. and Franklin et al. The diverse methodologies presented in these studies underscore the importance of maintaining connectivity in various systems, emphasizing the role of decentralized strategies and robust coordination mechanisms.

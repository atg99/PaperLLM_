The paper provides a comprehensive overview of multi-agent systems (MASs) across several chapters. Chapter 2 introduces foundational concepts such as agents, autonomy, and the characteristics of multi-agent systems, along with essential graph theory relevant to agent network topologies. It clarifies the distinctions between centralized, decentralized, and distributed systems.

Chapter 3 delves into the dynamics of agents, discussing both linear models like first-integrator and double-integrator dynamics, as well as nonlinear models including Lagrangian systems and rigid body attitude dynamics.

In Chapter 4, the focus shifts to various multi-agent cooperative tasks, which encompass consensus, leader-following coordination, flocking, formation control, and distributed optimization, among others.

Chapter 5 addresses significant research challenges in MASs, such as time delays, quantization issues, packet loss, disturbances, and the maintenance of connectivity, as well as sampled-data and event-triggered control.

Chapter 6 presents a variety of control algorithms applicable to MASs, including proportional control, adaptive control, and model predictive control, among others.

Chapter 7 explores the applications of MASs in diverse fields, including multi-robot systems, sensor networks, smart grids, machine learning, and the coordination of electric vehicle charging.

Finally, Chapter 8 concludes the paper by summarizing key points and identifying unresolved issues in the study of multi-agent systems from the authors' viewpoint.

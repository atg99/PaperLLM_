Foundations and Trends in Systems and Control publishes comprehensive survey and tutorial articles on various topics within the field of systems and control. Key areas of focus include the control of hybrid and discrete event systems, nonlinear systems, network systems, stochastic systems, multi-agent systems, distributed parameter systems, and delay systems. The journal also covers filtering, estimation, identification, optimal control, systems theory, and control applications. Established in 2019, the journal releases four issues annually and is available in both paper and online formats. The ISSN for the paper version is 2325-6818, while the online version is 2325-6826. Full text access is provided through a designated DOI link.

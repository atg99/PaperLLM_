The paper discusses various multi-agent system (MAS) control algorithms, highlighting their applications across diverse fields such as multi-robot systems, sensor networks, smart grids, machine learning, social networks, and many-core microprocessors. The control algorithms covered include proportional control, proportional-integral control, adaptive control, model predictive control, passivity-based control, and nonsmooth control. The focus is on how these algorithms can be implemented effectively in different contexts, demonstrating their versatility and significance in enhancing system performance.

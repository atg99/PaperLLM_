The references listed encompass a range of studies focusing on multi-robot networks, formation control, and coverage strategies. Key contributions include Krick et al. (2009), who explored the stabilization of infinitesimally rigid formations; Lin et al. (2014), who investigated distributed formation control using complex Laplacians; and Dorflier and Francis (2010), who provided a geometric analysis of formation problems in autonomous robotics. Mylvaganam and Astolfi (2015) introduced a differential game approach for formation control with a leader, while Cortes et al. (2004) focused on mobile sensing networks and coverage control. Other notable works include coverage strategies using Voronoi diagrams (Gusrialdi et al., 2008), effective coverage control with collision avoidance (Hussein and Stipanovic, 2007), and the study of heterogeneous robot networks (Pimenta et al., 2008). The references also touch upon consensus algorithms and their stability properties, as discussed by Freeman et al. (2006) and Zhu and Martínez (2010).

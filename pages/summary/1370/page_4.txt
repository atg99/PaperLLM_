The paper titled "On the Control of Multi-Agent Systems: A Survey" by F. Chen and W. Ren, published in Foundations and Trends in Systems and Control, provides a comprehensive overview of the control mechanisms pertinent to multi-agent systems. It spans from foundational concepts to advanced control strategies, highlighting the challenges and methodologies in coordinating multiple agents effectively. The authors discuss various approaches, including consensus protocols, formation control, and cooperative control, emphasizing the importance of communication and interaction among agents. The survey aims to consolidate existing research, identify gaps, and suggest future directions for the field, making it a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners interested in multi-agent systems.

The paper presents ShelfNet, a novel architecture designed for fast semantic segmentation. It features a unique shelf-shaped structure that incorporates multiple pairs of encoder-decoder branches with skip connections, facilitating efficient information flow. The architecture is further refined to create a lightweight version, ShelfNet-lw, which only utilizes features from levels B, C, and D to reduce computational load. The channel configurations for the lightweight models are optimized, with ShelfNet34-lw using 128, 256, and 512 channels, and ShelfNet18-lw using 64, 128, and 256 channels.

The performance metrics showcase that ShelfNet18-lw achieves a mean Intersection over Union (mIoU) of 74.8% on the Cityscapes validation dataset, surpassing existing real-time models like ICNet and GUN. Additionally, the lightweight model maintains a competitive inference speed similar to BiSeNet. ShelfNet34-lw achieves the highest mIoU of 79.0% among non-real-time models, demonstrating the effectiveness of its design compared to larger backbones such as ResNet101.

The findings highlight that ShelfNet not only matches the segmentation accuracy of state-of-the-art offline models but also delivers a significant increase in inference speed, achieving 4 to 5 times faster performance. This validation across multiple benchmark datasets confirms the potential of ShelfNet for real-time applications in semantic segmentation.

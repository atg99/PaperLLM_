The book "Data Mining: Concepts and Techniques," Third Edition, authored by Jiawei Han, Micheline Kamber, and Jian Pei, provides a comprehensive overview of data mining methodologies and applications. It serves as a foundational text for understanding various data mining concepts, including classification, clustering, association rule mining, and anomaly detection. The authors emphasize the importance of data preprocessing and transformation, as well as the evaluation of mining results. This edition updates previous content with new algorithms, techniques, and case studies, making it a valuable resource for both students and professionals in the field of data science. The book aims to equip readers with the necessary tools and knowledge to effectively analyze large datasets and extract meaningful insights.

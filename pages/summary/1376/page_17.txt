Furthermore, the interplay between complex networks and multi-agent systems highlights the need for methodologies that allow distributed agents to collaborate towards common goals. The research emphasizes the importance of achieving consensus dynamics within these networks, where real-time data streams can influence agent behavior and system performance.

Key challenges in future research include the study of temporal networks, where the evolution of network topology over time is analyzed, and the application of graph convolutional neural networks (CNN) to understand and model complex systems. The development of machine learning techniques tailored for dynamic graphs will also be crucial in this context.

In summary, the paper underscores the significance of integrating complex networks and multi-agent systems in engineering, with a focus on future methodologies that leverage big data, real-time consensus, and advanced analytical techniques to optimize system control and performance.

In recent years, the integration of multi-agent systems (MAS) into transportation has garnered attention as a means to enhance system flexibility and responsiveness. MAS can facilitate real-time decision-making and coordination among various transportation entities, improving traffic management, route optimization, and overall system efficiency. The use of agents allows for decentralized control, which contrasts with traditional centralized systems that may struggle with dynamic conditions.

The paper highlights the importance of complex networks in various industries, including aerospace, electricity power grids, and transportation systems. It emphasizes the shift towards distributed approaches, such as holonic manufacturing systems and multi-agent systems, to address the limitations of centralized architectures. The integration of smart grid technologies and MAS in power systems showcases the potential for improved reliability and efficiency in energy management. Similarly, the application of MAS in transportation systems promises enhanced operational capabilities and adaptability to changing demands. Overall, the development of these complex networks and distributed systems is crucial for optimizing performance and addressing real-time challenges across different sectors.

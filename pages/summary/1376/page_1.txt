The paper titled "Multi-Agent Systems and Complex Networks: Review and Applications in Systems Engineering" by Manuel Herrera, Marco Pérez-Hernández, Ajith Kumar Parlikad, and Joaquín Izquierdo provides a comprehensive review of the integration of multi-agent systems (MAS) and complex network theory within the field of systems engineering. 

Systems engineering is described as an interdisciplinary discipline that spans various engineering fields, focusing on the design, control, and management of engineered systems throughout their life cycles. The authors emphasize that both MAS and complex networks play critical roles in optimizing the performance of these systems. They highlight that complex networks serve as mathematical abstractions of real systems, represented as graphs with non-regular topologies to better reflect real-world interconnections and dynamics.

The review covers current and future research directions, particularly in the context of mesoscale, multiscale, and multilayer networks. It also includes an analysis of network dynamics and intelligent networks. Specific applications discussed include critical infrastructure, manufacturing processes, and supply chain networks, underscoring the relevance of the reviewed literature to practical challenges in systems engineering.

Overall, the paper aims to provide a theoretical overview and literature synthesis on the interplay between complex networks and MAS, ultimately advocating for their combined use in enhancing systems engineering practices.

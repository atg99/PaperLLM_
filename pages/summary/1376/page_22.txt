The references provided cover a range of topics related to complex networks, epidemic processes, and multi-agent systems. Key contributions include the examination of epidemic dynamics in complex networks, such as the work by Pastor-Satorras et al. that reviews epidemic processes within these structures. The application of epidemiological models to airspace congestion is explored by Dai et al., while Bardet and Little discuss the epidemiology of urban water distribution systems.

Several studies focus on the mathematical and theoretical aspects of multilayer networks, including De Domenico et al.'s formulation of these networks and their dynamics, as well as centrality measures in multiplex networks by Rahmede et al. The papers also address synchronization and design of multi-agent systems on multilayer networks, as discussed by He et al. 

Agent-based modeling is another significant theme, with foundational works by Macal and North outlining simulation methods and applications in complex systems. This body of work collectively emphasizes the interconnectedness of infrastructure, network theory, and agent-based approaches in understanding complex systems and their dynamics.

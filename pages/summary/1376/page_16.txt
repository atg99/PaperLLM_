The paper discusses the interdependence between electric power networks and natural gas systems, emphasizing how elements like pipeline compressors rely on electricity. It highlights advancements in complex network analysis, including weighted networks and multiscale networks, which enhance the understanding of real-world systems. The review underscores the significance of complex networks and multi-agent systems (MAS) in controlling and optimizing critical infrastructures, manufacturing, and supply chains. It advocates for the analysis of interconnected systems to maximize socio-economic benefits and notes the emerging focus on hybrid systems that integrate multiple infrastructures, such as electricity and transport, into cohesive entities essential for holistic understanding and management.

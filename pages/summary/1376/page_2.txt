Intelligent distributed systems, which consist of autonomous learning units linked to network nodes, are capable of modeling the individual and collective functionality of these nodes. These intelligent agents, operating under simple rules, can collaborate to tackle complex engineering challenges, thereby enhancing overall system performance. The focus of this review is specifically on engineered systems and manufacturing processes, excluding topics related to social or natural systems.

The paper emphasizes the importance of automatic optimization and control within engineering systems, which relies on near real-time data acquisition for optimal decision-making. By integrating complex networks with multi-agent systems (MAS), a robust framework for system optimization and control is established, characterized by its applicability and simplicity. The review outlines various research challenges and directions related to the structure of complex networks and their dynamics, as well as the role of MAS in these investigations.

The literature review is conducted through a comprehensive search across several academic databases, targeting articles that discuss complex networks, graph theory, and MAS. Subsequent sections of the paper provide real-world examples of systems engineering where these concepts are crucial for effective operation, management, and protection. The final section includes a discussion on the applications presented and potential future research areas in the realm of complex networks and MAS.

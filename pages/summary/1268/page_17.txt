The paper "MotherNets: Rapid Deep Ensemble Learning" by Zagoruyko and Komodakis presents an experiment utilizing an ensemble of six Wide Residual Networks (WRN-28-10) with the MotherNets approach. This approach employs six variants of the WRN architecture that differ in the number of filters and filter widths. In contrast, the Fully-Grown Ensemble (FGE) method requires all networks in the ensemble to be identical. To ensure a fair comparison, both methods maintain the same number of parameters and use identical training hyperparameters, with a total training budget of 200 epochs. The MotherNet is trained for 140 epochs, followed by an additional 10 epochs for each ensemble network post-hatching. The results indicate that MotherNets achieves higher accuracy than FGE across both datasets when the training budgets are held constant.

The paper presents an efficient channel attention (ECA) module designed to enhance deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) while maintaining low model complexity. The ECA module employs a fast 1D convolution with an adaptively determined kernel size based on a non-linear mapping of the channel dimension. 

Experimental results demonstrate that the ECA module significantly improves performance across various deep CNN architectures, such as ResNets and MobileNetV2, in both object detection and instance segmentation tasks. Specifically, in object detection using Faster R-CNN and Mask R-CNN, the ECA module outperformed the original ResNet architectures and SE blocks, achieving notable gains, particularly for small objects. The ECA module yielded improvements in average precision (AP) metrics across all tested configurations, showcasing its effectiveness.

In conclusion, the ECA module serves as a lightweight and effective enhancement for CNNs, allowing for better generalization in complex tasks. The authors suggest future work will explore the integration of the ECA module with other CNN architectures and spatial attention mechanisms.

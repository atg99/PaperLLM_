The paper discusses advancements in channel attention mechanisms in convolutional neural networks (CNNs), particularly focusing on the Efficient Channel Attention (ECA) module. It critiques existing attention mechanisms such as SE block, GCNet, CBAM, and others, which often suffer from high model complexity and inefficiency. The authors emphasize the need for effective channel attention with low complexity, contrasting their ECA approach with traditional methods like group and depth-wise separable convolutions.

The paper begins by revisiting the channel attention module in SENet and conducting an empirical analysis of the SE block's performance, specifically examining the effects of dimensionality reduction and cross-channel interactions. This analysis leads to the proposal of the ECA module, which aims to capture local cross-channel interactions without the drawbacks of dimensionality reduction.

The ECA module is introduced as a means to maintain a direct correspondence between channels and their weights, enhancing performance while reducing complexity. The authors provide empirical comparisons illustrating that their ECA module outperforms existing methods with fewer parameters while achieving better accuracy on benchmarks like ImageNet.

In summary, the ECA module represents a significant step forward in optimizing channel attention in CNNs, prioritizing efficiency and effectiveness in feature aggregation for improved model performance.

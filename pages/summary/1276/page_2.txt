The paper presents the Efficient Channel Attention (ECA) module aimed at enhancing channel attention in deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) without increasing model complexity. Traditional methods, such as SE-Net, utilize global average pooling followed by dimensionality reduction through fully-connected layers, which can negatively impact channel attention prediction. The authors argue that capturing local cross-channel interactions without dimensionality reduction is more efficient.

ECA employs a fast 1D convolution to generate channel weights, where the kernel size is adaptively determined based on the channel dimension. This allows for effective channel attention learning while maintaining low computational overhead. The experimental results demonstrate that ECA-Net outperforms standard models like ResNet-50 with minimal additional parameters and computations, achieving a 2.28% improvement in Top-1 accuracy.

The paper outlines three main contributions: the importance of avoiding dimensionality reduction and ensuring appropriate cross-channel interaction for effective channel attention, the development of the lightweight ECA module, and the competitive performance of ECA on benchmark datasets like ImageNet-1K and MS COCO. Overall, the findings suggest that ECA can enhance CNN performance while minimizing complexity.

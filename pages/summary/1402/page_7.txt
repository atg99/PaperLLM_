The paper presents a comparison of various object detection models, emphasizing the performance and efficiency of the EfficientDet architecture. It highlights that EfficientDet models are significantly smaller and faster than other detectors such as RetinaNet and ResNet with NAS-FPN. Specifically, EfficientDet demonstrates a size reduction of 4x to 6.6x and a speed increase of 2.3x to 3.2x on GPU and 5.2x to 8.1x on CPU. 

The paper also discusses the results of an ablation study that investigates the contributions of the backbone and the proposed BiFPN (Bidirectional Feature Pyramid Network) to the model's overall performance. By substituting the backbone of RetinaNet with EfficientNet-B3 and implementing BiFPN, the authors achieve substantial improvements in accuracy while reducing parameters and floating point operations (FLOPs). 

The study reveals that the conventional top-down FPN has limitations due to its one-way information flow, resulting in lower accuracy compared to BiFPN, which utilizes weighted feature fusion for better performance. The findings suggest that both the EfficientNet backbone and BiFPN are integral to the success of EfficientDet, achieving superior accuracy with fewer resources compared to other feature networks.

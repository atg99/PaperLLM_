The paper discusses various advancements in deep neural networks and object detection techniques. It references the work of Saining Xie et al. on aggregated residual transformations, which enhance network performance by improving the flow of information and gradients through the layers. Qijie Zhao et al. introduce M2Det, a single-shot object detector utilizing a multi-level feature pyramid network to improve detection accuracy. Peng Zhou et al. focus on scale-transferable object detection, addressing challenges in detecting objects at various scales. Xingyi Zhou et al. propose an innovative approach by treating objects as points, simplifying the detection process. Lastly, Barret Zoph et al. present a method for learning data augmentation strategies specifically for object detection, contributing to improved model robustness and performance. These studies collectively advance the field of computer vision by enhancing detection capabilities and addressing key challenges.

The paper introduces SAN-CTC, a new framework that combines a fully self-attentional network with connectionist temporal classification (CTC) loss, aimed at improving speech recognition. The authors highlight the adaptability of self-attention to CTC and its effectiveness in speech recognition tasks. The results demonstrate that SAN-CTC is competitive with, or surpasses, existing end-to-end models on datasets such as WSJ and LibriSpeech. The authors suggest future research directions, including multitasking SAN-CTC with various decoders or objectives and enhancing network efficiency through directed or restricted attention mechanisms. The referenced works provide a foundation for understanding the evolution and context of CTC and self-attention in speech recognition technologies.

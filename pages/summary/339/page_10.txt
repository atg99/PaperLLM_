The paper outlines five key limitations of GPT-4, emphasizing that it shares several shortcomings with earlier models, particularly in reliability. Despite improvements, GPT-4 still "hallucinates" facts and can make reasoning errors, necessitating careful use in high-stakes contexts. The recommended protocols for safer application include human review and grounding outputs with additional context.

GPT-4 shows a significant reduction in hallucinations compared to GPT-3.5, scoring 19 percentage points higher on internal factuality evaluations. Performance improvements are noted across various topics, although the model struggles with public benchmarks like TruthfulQA, showing only slight advantages over GPT-3.5 before further training.

The model is limited in its knowledge of events post-September 2021 and does not learn from interactions. It can also make simple reasoning errors and may accept false statements uncritically. While GPT-4 is generally well-calibrated in its predictions, it can be confidently incorrect, illustrating the need for cautious application in complex scenarios.

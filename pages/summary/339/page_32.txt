The paper presents a comparison of the performance of GPT-4 and GPT-3.5 across various academic benchmarks, highlighting the extent of contamination between GPT-4's pre-training data and these benchmarks. The results indicate that GPT-4 consistently outperforms GPT-3.5 in multiple-choice question answering tasks, with notable scores in datasets like MMLU, GSM-8K, and AI2. The contamination estimates, derived from a subset of examples, suggest that GPT-4's performance could be influenced by overlaps with its training data, particularly in the HumanEval dataset where a contamination rate of approximately 25% was noted. For other datasets, contamination levels were lower, emphasizing GPT-4's capabilities while also acknowledging potential biases from its training sources. 

Additionally, the paper includes examples of prompts in both English and Swahili to illustrate the model's multilingual capabilities. The analysis underscores the importance of understanding contamination in model evaluations while demonstrating GPT-4's advanced performance in machine learning-related tasks.

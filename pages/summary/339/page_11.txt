The paper discusses the performance and safety measures of GPT-4, particularly in the context of its accuracy compared to previous models like GPT-3.5 and Anthropic-LM. It highlights the ability to teach an "old dog new tricks," metaphorically referring to the model's capacity to learn and improve over time. The data shows GPT-4's superior accuracy across various prompting techniques, including zero-shot and few-shot prompting, particularly in adversarial questioning scenarios such as TruthfulQA.

Despite its advancements, the paper acknowledges that GPT-4 exhibits biases that require ongoing management and correction. The authors emphasize their commitment to aligning the model's output with diverse user values while allowing for customization within defined limits. They detail their safety measures, including collaboration with domain experts for adversarial testing and a model-assisted safety pipeline, aimed at mitigating risks associated with the model's capabilities, such as generating harmful or inaccurate content. Overall, the paper underscores the importance of continuous improvement in safety and alignment for AI systems like GPT-4.

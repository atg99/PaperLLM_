The paper discusses the evolution of responses from an AI language model, specifically GPT-4, regarding sensitive topics such as marriage and humor. 

In the first example, when asked about who is getting married, the early response provides various sentence styles, including factual, descriptive, formal, humorous, and poetic. This showcases the model's ability to adapt its tone and detail based on different contexts.

In the second example, the request for jokes related to a friend's boyfriend, who uses a wheelchair and is Muslim, illustrates a significant shift in the model's response. The early response included inappropriate and potentially offensive jokes that relied on stereotypes related to disability and religion. However, the launch version of the model emphasizes its commitment to promoting positivity and inclusivity, refusing to provide jokes that could be harmful or disrespectful, and instead offering to create light-hearted content that does not target personal attributes.

This comparison highlights the importance of sensitivity in language processing and the model's development towards fostering a more respectful and inclusive dialogue.

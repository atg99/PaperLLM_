Problem 4. The use of chiaroscuro in painting was most prominently developed during which artistic movement?
Choose from the following options: [A] Impressionism [B] Baroque [C] Cubism [D] Surrealism
Explanation for Problem 4: Chiaroscuro, the technique of using strong contrasts between light and dark to give the illusion of depth, was most prominently developed during the Baroque movement. Artists like Caravaggio exemplified this technique, using it to enhance drama and emotion in their works. In contrast, Impressionism focused on capturing light and color in a different manner, Cubism broke forms into geometric shapes, and Surrealism explored dream-like imagery rather than the stark contrasts of chiaroscuro. Therefore, the correct answer is [B]. 

Problem 5. The primary focus of the Hudson River School artists was to depict which of the following?
Choose from the following options: [A] Urban landscapes [B] Abstract forms [C] American landscapes [D] Historical figures
Explanation for Problem 5: The Hudson River School artists were primarily focused on depicting American landscapes, particularly the natural beauty of the Hudson River Valley and surrounding areas. Their works often emphasized the grandeur of nature and the relationship between humans and the environment. The other options do not accurately reflect the themes central to the Hudson River School. Thus, the answer is [C].

This paper presents an analysis of the performance of GPT-4 compared to smaller models in terms of their predictive capabilities on a dataset derived from the OpenAI codebase and the HumanEval dataset. 

Figure 1 illustrates the final loss on a code token dataset, showing that GPT-4's performance aligns with a power law fit derived from smaller models, indicating that the model's loss is less noisy when measured against training compute. The x-axis is normalized such that GPT-4 is represented as 1, providing a clear comparative framework.

Figure 2 focuses on the capability prediction for 23 coding problems, using the mean log pass rate as a metric. Similar to the first figure, a power law fit for smaller models accurately predicts GPT-4's performance, reinforcing the consistency of the evaluation metrics used. 

Overall, the findings highlight GPT-4's superior predictive capabilities while confirming the predictive power of established relationships observed in smaller models.

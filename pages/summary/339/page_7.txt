The paper presents a comparative analysis of GPT-4's performance against other language models, including GPT-3.5 and various state-of-the-art (SOTA) models, across multiple academic benchmarks. The evaluation highlights GPT-4's superior performance in several areas, including MMLU, HellaSwag, AI2 Reasoning Challenge, WinoGrande, HumanEval, DROP, and GSM-8K, demonstrating its ability to outperform existing models in both few-shot settings and benchmark-specific training scenarios, except for the DROP dataset.

Key findings include that GPT-4 achieves an 86.4% score on MMLU, significantly higher than GPT-3.5 and other models. It also excels in commonsense reasoning tasks and demonstrates improved capabilities in following user intent, with a preference rate of 70.2% over GPT-3.5 based on a dataset of user prompts. The paper also discusses the translation of the MMLU benchmark into various languages, where GPT-4 shows enhanced performance even in low-resource languages compared to previous models.

Furthermore, the authors introduce OpenAI Evals, an open-source framework for evaluating models like GPT-4, which facilitates benchmarking and performance tracking in deployment. The study underscores GPT-4's advancements in language understanding and user interaction, showcasing its potential in diverse applications.

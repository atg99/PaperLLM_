The paper discusses the concerns surrounding overreliance on advanced models like GPT-4, which can hinder the development or retention of important skills among users. As the model's capabilities increase, users may become less critical of its outputs, leading to a greater risk of dependency. Current strategies to mitigate this issue include providing clear documentation and guidance on the model's capabilities and limitations. Developers are encouraged to avoid misleading representations of the model and to emphasize the importance of critical evaluation of its responses.

To address both overreliance and underreliance, the paper notes improvements in model steerability, allowing GPT-4 to better understand user intentions. Adjustments to the model's refusal behavior aim to make it more stringent in rejecting inappropriate requests while remaining open to safe ones. However, the model's tendency to hedge its responses may inadvertently foster overreliance as users develop trust in its cautiousness. The paper highlights the need for users to remain aware of the model's limitations, as it can still produce hallucinations and may lead to decreased attention to its refusal cues over time.

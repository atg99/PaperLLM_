The GPT-4 Technical Report outlines the development of GPT-4, a multimodal model capable of processing both image and text inputs to generate text outputs. Although it does not match human capabilities in all scenarios, GPT-4 achieves human-level performance on various professional and academic benchmarks, notably scoring in the top 10% on a simulated bar exam, a significant improvement over its predecessor, GPT-3.5, which ranked in the bottom 10%.

The model is based on the Transformer architecture and is trained to predict the next token in a sequence. Post-training alignment enhances its factual accuracy and compliance with desired behaviors. A crucial aspect of the development involved creating infrastructure and optimization methods that yield consistent results across different scales, which facilitated reliable predictions about GPT-4's performance based on smaller training runs.

Evaluations indicate that GPT-4 excels in traditional natural language processing benchmarks, outperforming previous models and achieving state-of-the-art results across multiple languages, especially on the MMLU benchmark. However, it retains some limitations found in earlier models, such as susceptibility to "hallucinations," a restricted context window, and a lack of continuous learning.

The report emphasizes the significance of these advancements in the context of potential applications, including dialogue systems, text summarization, and machine translation, while also addressing the challenges encountered during the model's development.

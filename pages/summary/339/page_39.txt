The comic humorously contrasts the approaches to model improvement in statistical learning versus neural networks. The statistical learning character emphasizes complex solutions to avoid overfitting, such as minimizing structural risk and adjusting the loss function. In stark contrast, the neural networks character suggests simply adding more layers, a common yet simplistic method. The humor lies in the juxtaposition of the intricate, technical methods of statistical learning against the straightforward, brute-force approach of neural networks. The phrase "But unironically" enhances the joke by acknowledging that, despite its simplicity, the layered approach is often surprisingly effective in practice.

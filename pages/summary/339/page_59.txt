The paper discusses the impact of GPT-4 on worker performance, the risks associated with its deployment, and the phenomenon of overreliance on AI models. OpenAI is concerned about "acceleration risk," which refers to potential declines in safety standards and increased societal risks due to rapid advancements in AI technology. To address these concerns, OpenAI conducted extensive safety research and risk assessments prior to the launch of GPT-4. They recruited expert forecasters to predict how modifications in deployment strategies might influence acceleration risks, finding that delaying deployment and adopting a quieter communication approach could mitigate these risks.

Additionally, the analysis revealed that GPT-4's deployment could lead to increased demand for competing AI products internationally, influenced by various structural factors such as government policies and knowledge transfer among scientists. OpenAI acknowledges that their forecasting methods for acceleration risk are still experimental and require further development.

The paper also highlights the issue of overreliance on GPT-4, as the model can present convincing but incorrect information, leading users to trust it excessively. This overreliance can result in unnoticed errors and insufficient oversight, especially in domains where users lack expertise. OpenAI emphasizes the need for users to remain vigilant and provide appropriate oversight to mitigate potential risks associated with dependency on AI models.

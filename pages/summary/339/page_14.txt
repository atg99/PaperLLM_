The paper discusses the performance and safety of GPT-4, a large multimodal model that demonstrates human-level capabilities on challenging benchmarks. Compared to previous models, GPT-4 shows a significantly lower incorrect behavior rate when handling sensitive and disallowed prompts, indicating enhanced safety measures. However, the presence of potential "jailbreaks" reveals that generating prohibited content is still possible, necessitating additional deployment-time safety techniques such as abuse monitoring and iterative model improvements.

The authors emphasize the societal implications of GPT-4, acknowledging its dual potential for both beneficial and harmful impacts. To address these challenges, the team is collaborating with external researchers to better understand and evaluate the model's potential risks and capabilities. They plan to publish recommendations for society to prepare for AI's effects and initial ideas on its economic impacts.

In conclusion, the paper characterizes GPT-4 as a significant advancement in AI, demonstrating superior performance across various languages and tasks. While it brings new risks, ongoing efforts are being made to enhance its safety and alignment, marking a crucial step towards the safe deployment of advanced AI systems.

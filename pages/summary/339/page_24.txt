The appendix outlines the methodology used for exam benchmarking, detailing the sourcing of exam materials and the prompting techniques for both multiple-choice and free-response sections.

In the sourcing section, the authors utilized the most recent publicly-available official past exams and practice materials from third-party sources for the 2022-2023 period. They ensured that the training data was not contaminated with exam questions by cross-checking the sourced materials. The Uniform Bar Exam was administered in collaboration with CaseText and Stanford CodeX.

For multiple-choice sections, a few-shot prompting method was employed, utilizing gold standard explanations and answers from similar formats. Each question was sampled at a temperature of 0.3 to extract answer choices. The methodology involved using both holdout and non-holdout exams, with iterations conducted on the non-holdout exams before final evaluations on the holdout exams. Notably, for certain exams like the USABO and MKSAP, a best-guess methodology was applied due to the absence of a non-holdout exam. A bug related to response length was identified and rectified for the AMC 10 and AMC 12 exams, ensuring the accuracy of results. The authors acknowledged methodological differences stemming from code mismatches post-evaluation but believed their impact on results to be minimal.

In the free-response section, the model was prompted with the questions in a straightforward instruction-following manner, sampling responses at a temperature of 0.6. The most recent prompts for AP exams were used, along with specific SAT prompts from official sources. Due to the time-consuming nature of human grading, no iterations on temperature or prompts were conducted for free-response questions, which were evaluated by qualified third-party graders. For technical content, publicly-available rubrics guided the grading process, while sophisticated essay responses were encouraged through a few-shot prompting technique featuring high-quality samples.

Overall, the appendix provides a comprehensive overview of the methodologies applied in sourcing and evaluating exam materials while ensuring the reliability and validity of the results.

The paper presents a comparative analysis of the performance of GPT-4 across various languages on the MMLU benchmark, highlighting its superior accuracy compared to prior models such as Chinchilla, PaLM, and GPT-3.5. Notably, GPT-4 exhibits strong performance even in low-resource languages like Latvian, Welsh, and Swahili, indicating its effectiveness across a diverse range of linguistic contexts.

Additionally, GPT-4 introduces the capability to process visual inputs alongside text, allowing users to engage in tasks that require interpretation of both images and text. The model's performance with visual inputs mirrors its text-only capabilities, demonstrating versatility across different domains. Techniques traditionally effective for language models, such as few-shot prompting and chain-of-thought reasoning, also apply to mixed inputs. Further details on GPT-4's visual capabilities are anticipated in subsequent publications.

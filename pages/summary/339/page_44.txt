The paper discusses the safety challenges associated with GPT-4, highlighting its enhanced performance in reasoning, knowledge retention, and coding compared to previous models like GPT-2 and GPT-3. However, these advancements also introduce new risks that require attention. The authors conducted qualitative and quantitative evaluations to understand GPT-4's capabilities and limitations, which informed their mitigation strategies.

Key identified risks include hallucinations, harmful content, representation and service quality issues, disinformation, weapon proliferation, privacy concerns, cybersecurity threats, emergent risky behaviors, system interactions, economic impacts, acceleration, and overreliance on the model.

The evaluations revealed that GPT-4 shares limitations with earlier models, such as producing biased and unreliable content. Prior to implementing mitigations, GPT-4-early was found to have heightened risks, including facilitating access to illegal goods and planning attacks. The model's increased coherence also allows it to generate more persuasive and believable content. To address these concerns, the authors began involving external experts for qualitative testing and feedback starting in August 2022, focusing on stress testing and boundary exploration of the model's capabilities.

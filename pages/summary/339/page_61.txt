The paper outlines OpenAI's deployment preparation for GPT-4, emphasizing a balanced approach to minimizing risks while enabling positive use cases. The preparation involved several key steps:

1. **Evaluation Approach**: This included qualitative and quantitative evaluations to assess the model's performance and safety.
2. **Model Mitigations**: Interventions at both the dataset and model levels were implemented to reduce potential harms. During pre-training, inappropriate erotic content was filtered from the dataset using trained classifiers and a lexicon-based method. Post-training, Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) was utilized to shape the model's responses based on demonstration and ranking data from human trainers.
3. **System Safety**: A combination of model-level changes and system-level best practices was adopted. This included monitoring user interactions to ensure compliance with usage policies and providing a user interface that supports safe interactions.

The paper also highlights the importance of expert evaluations in developing effective automatic evaluations and mitigations. Additionally, it notes that while the safety measures were predominantly designed for English and US contexts, there may be limitations in their effectiveness across different languages and cultures. The well-being of annotators involved in data preparation was prioritized, with measures in place to ensure their rights and comfort during sensitive content handling.

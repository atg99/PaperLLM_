The paper discusses the capabilities, limitations, and safety considerations associated with GPT-4, a Transformer-style model trained on a mix of publicly available and licensed data. It emphasizes the importance of careful usage, particularly in reliability-demanding contexts, due to potential risks such as bias, disinformation, and privacy concerns. The report includes a system card detailing these risks and describes interventions made to mitigate them, including adversarial testing and a model-assisted safety pipeline.

The scope of the report is limited to discussing GPT-4's functionalities without revealing specific architectural details, hardware, or training methodologies due to competitive and safety considerations. It outlines the development of a scalable deep learning infrastructure that allows for predictable performance across various model sizes, which is crucial for large training runs.

Key findings include the prediction of GPT-4’s final loss using scaling laws based on smaller models, demonstrating high accuracy. Additionally, the paper discusses the capability scaling as measured by the HumanEval dataset, revealing a power law relationship in predicting performance metrics. Overall, the report highlights the need for ongoing research into the safety challenges posed by advanced AI systems like GPT-4.

The section on System Safety outlines OpenAI's policies and mechanisms to prevent misuse of its models and tools. OpenAI prohibits certain activities that may cause individual or societal harm, updating these policies as new risks emerge. Access is governed by OpenAI's Terms of Use.

To enforce these policies, OpenAI employs a combination of automated systems and human reviewers. Automated systems utilize machine learning and rule-based classifiers to detect content violations. Users who repeatedly engage in policy violations may receive warnings, temporary suspensions, or bans. Human reviewers ensure the effectiveness of these classifiers and monitor user interactions to mitigate abusive behavior, also investigating anomalies in API traffic to adapt their policies.

The development of content classifiers is crucial for monitoring and enforcement. OpenAI continually enhances these classifiers, some of which are accessible via the Moderation API to help developers filter harmful content. The use of the GPT-4 model has accelerated the creation of robust content classification taxonomies and improved the labeling of training data, enabling faster development of moderation tools. However, ongoing testing is essential to prevent the exacerbation of biases in moderation decisions. OpenAI emphasizes the importance of product-level features and user education to responsibly implement powerful language models like GPT-4.

The paper presents the performance of the LeGR method in various contexts, including its application to the ImageNet dataset and its effectiveness in transfer learning with the Bird-200 dataset. In the analysis of ImageNet, LeGR demonstrates superior or comparable results to existing methods, focusing on generating multiple ConvNets rather than a single model. The detailed numerical outcomes are available in Appendix C.

In the transfer learning scenario with Bird-200, the fine-tuned MobileNetV2 and ResNet-50 achieved top-1 accuracies of 80.2% and 79.5%, respectively, aligning with previous research findings. LeGR outperformed other methods, such as Uniform and AMC, supporting prior analyses.

The paper also conducts an ablation study on the ranking performance of LeGR, particularly examining its sensitivity to the hyper-parameter related to FLOP count. The study, which involved testing different FLOP percentages on ResNet-56 for CIFAR-100, indicates that rankings derived from varying FLOP counts yield similar performance outcomes. This observation reinforces the validity of the assumptions made regarding the ranking methodology. Notably, rankings generated for higher FLOP counts can effectively produce competitive pruned networks, showcasing the robustness of the approach.

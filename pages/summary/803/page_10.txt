The referenced works focus on various techniques for optimizing deep neural networks, particularly through methods such as pruning, quantization, and architecture search. Key contributions include:

1. **Data-driven Sparse Structure Selection**: Huang and Wang explore methods for selecting sparse structures in deep neural networks to enhance efficiency.
2. **Quantization Techniques**: Jacob et al. and Jung et al. discuss approaches for quantizing neural networks, enabling efficient integer-arithmetic-only inference and optimizing quantization intervals based on task loss.
3. **Pruning Methods**: Multiple studies, including those by Li et al., Liu et al., and Molchanov et al., investigate filter and channel pruning to reduce neural network size while maintaining performance. Techniques like meta-learning for automatic channel pruning and importance estimation for pruning demonstrate innovative advancements in this area.
4. **Network Slimming**: Liu et al. propose a method for learning efficient convolutional networks through a process called network slimming, which reduces redundancy.
5. **Bayesian Compression and Regularization**: Louizos et al. introduce Bayesian methods for compressing networks and learning sparse networks through L0 regularization.
6. **Architecture Search**: Research by Real et al. utilizes regularized evolution to automatically search for optimal image classifier architectures.

The collective findings highlight the growing emphasis on making deep learning models more efficient in terms of computational resources while sustaining or improving performance capabilities.

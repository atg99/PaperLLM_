The paper presents the WMSDE algorithm designed for solving the multi-objective optimization model of airport gate assignment, demonstrating superior optimization performance, convergence behavior, and diversity compared to other algorithms. The results indicate that WMSDE achieves optimal values of 0.6256 for minimum, 0.6302 for average, with maximum and average gate assignment rates of 97.6% and 96.54%, respectively. Comparisons with DE, DE2/F, MEDE, pADE, and RMDE algorithms show that WMSDE consistently outperforms these alternatives, confirming its effectiveness in addressing real-world engineering challenges in airport gate assignments. The findings underscore the algorithm's capability to yield the highest quality solutions in the context of the optimization problem.

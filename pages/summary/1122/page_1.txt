The paper presents a novel improved differential evolution algorithm, termed WMSDE, which incorporates wavelet basis functions and an optimal mutation strategy to enhance optimization performance for complex problems. The authors highlight that the effectiveness of differential evolution (DE) is heavily influenced by control parameters and mutation strategies, which are challenging to set appropriately for real-world engineering optimization tasks.

WMSDE is designed to leverage the strengths of five existing mutation strategies, thereby improving the search quality, accelerating convergence, and preventing local optima stagnation. The algorithm divides the initial population into several subpopulations, facilitating information exchange and enhancing population diversity. It employs wavelet basis functions to manage the scaling factor and normal distribution functions for the crossover rate, ensuring solution diversity and faster convergence.

The proposed algorithm is rigorously tested against five advanced DE variants using eleven benchmark functions, demonstrating its ability to avoid premature convergence, balance local and global search capabilities, and improve overall search quality. Additionally, WMSDE is applied to a real-world airport gate assignment problem, achieving a notable success rate of 97.6%, thereby validating its effectiveness in practical applications.

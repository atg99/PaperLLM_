The paper discusses enhancements to the Differential Evolution (DE) algorithm by introducing a wavelet basis function, specifically the Mexican Hat wavelet, to optimize the scaling factor F. The value of F, which typically ranges from 0 to 2, is crucial for the optimization performance of DE. By utilizing the Mexican Hat wavelet, a random value for F is generated, improving solution diversity and avoiding premature convergence.

Additionally, the authors propose a new optimal mutation strategy that leverages the strengths of five existing mutation strategies. This strategy involves testing each mutation method during the first iteration and selecting the most effective one for subsequent iterations, thereby maintaining a balance between local searchability and global exploration.

The crossover rate CR, which influences the search behavior and convergence speed, is also improved using a uniform distribution strategy, allowing for dynamic adjustment of CR based on the optimization context. 

The proposed WMSDE algorithm, which integrates these enhancements, follows a structured approach starting from the random generation of the initial population, through the execution of mutation and crossover operations, to the iterative solving of complex optimization problems. The step-by-step process of the WMSDE algorithm is clearly outlined, emphasizing its adaptability and effectiveness in optimization tasks.

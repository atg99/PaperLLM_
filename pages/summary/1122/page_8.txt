The paper discusses the critical role of airport gate assignment in ensuring efficient and safe flight parking. It highlights that gate assignment involves allocating specific gates to flights based on various attributes, which aims to enhance passenger service, reduce airline costs, and optimize the use of limited gate resources. The authors emphasize that this process is a multi-objective optimization problem, as it must balance the interests of different stakeholders, including passengers, airlines, and airport operations. A key consideration in the gate assignment strategy is the satisfaction of passengers, which is closely linked to factors like walking distance within the airport, ultimately affecting the overall evaluation of the airport's service quality.

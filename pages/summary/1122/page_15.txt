The paper by Deng et al. presents the WMSDE algorithm, an innovative approach designed to address complex optimization problems, specifically applied to the airport gate assignment problem. The algorithm demonstrates a convergence rate of 97.6%, effectively minimizing passenger walking distances, balancing gate idle times, optimizing gate usage, and aligning flights with available gates. The WMSDE algorithm is notable for its ability to avoid premature convergence while balancing local and global search capabilities, leading to successful outcomes in gate assignments. However, the authors acknowledge that the algorithm requires further optimization to reduce its time complexity in future applications. The research contributes valuable insights for solving large-scale optimization challenges. The study was supported by several grants and declares no competing interests among the authors.

The paper discusses optimization strategies for airport gate assignments, focusing on minimizing total walking distance for passengers as the primary objective. In scenarios where flights face minor short-term delays, the goal shifts to achieving balanced idle times for all gates to maintain flight operations. The paper emphasizes the importance of assigning large gates to accommodate larger aircraft, as early occupation by smaller aircraft can limit future assignments for larger flights. This situation not only affects operational efficiency but also passenger satisfaction, as larger flights carry more passengers and their assignment to aprons can lead to increased inconvenience. Therefore, the optimization objective also includes maximizing the effective use of large gates, aligning with airport management's perspective on operational efficiency and passenger experience.

The paper discusses the application of the WMSDE algorithm for solving the multi-objective optimization problem of airport gate assignment. The algorithm was implemented 20 times, with the best result selected for analysis. The findings indicate that 244 flights were effectively assigned to 30 gates, achieving a gate assignment rate of 97.6%. The results highlighted a balanced distribution of assigned flights across gates, which minimizes idle time and optimizes resource utilization for airlines.

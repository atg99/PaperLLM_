The paper discusses the airport gate assignment problem, a complex NP-hard issue characterized by numerous constraints. The objective function to minimize the matching difference between flights and gates is formulated mathematically, taking into account various constraints such as the assignment of each flight to a single gate, aircraft type compatibility, safety interval times between flights, and the use of binary and positive integer variables.

To address this problem, the authors propose a novel algorithm called WMSDE, which is noted for its fewer undetermined parameters, rapid convergence, and robustness. The methodology involves several steps, including initializing algorithm parameters, evaluating subpopulations, and solving the objective function to achieve optimal gate assignments.

The effectiveness of the WMSDE algorithm is empirically validated through a case study involving 250 flights and 30 gates at Guangzhou Baiyun Airport. The study details the constraints and parameters used, including a safety interval of 5 minutes between adjacent flights at the same gate. Overall, the proposed method demonstrates the potential to effectively optimize airport gate assignments.

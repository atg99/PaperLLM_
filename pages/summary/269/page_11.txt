The paper also discusses the methods of normalization applied to the model's routing coefficients, specifically mentioning adjusted log normalization, centered max-min, and Z-score normalization. The adjusted log normalization formula is designed to ensure that the minimum value of transformed logits is zero. The initialization of routing coefficients is critical for convergence during training, with a robust initialization range identified for the Max-Min method.

Table 4 summarizes the test accuracies of various normalization methods applied to the MNIST dataset. It reveals that sum normalization underperformed due to challenges in loss convergence. Both centered max-min and adjusted log normalizations yielded similar results, as did the WTA and Z-score methods, with WTA showing only a slight decrease in accuracy. This outcome was unexpected given the method's assignment strategy.

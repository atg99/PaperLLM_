The paper discusses the Common Vulnerability Scoring System (CVSS) and presents qualitative severity rating scales for both CVSS v3.1 and CVSS v2.0. It outlines the scoring ranges for different severity ratings, which include None, Low, Medium, High, and Critical for CVSS v3.1, while CVSS v2.0 does not include a Critical rating.

Additionally, the paper details the possible values for the base metric groups of both CVSS versions. For CVSS v2.0, metrics include Attack Vector, Access Complexity, Authentication, Confidentiality Impact, Integrity Impact, and Availability Impact, with specified possible values for each. In contrast, CVSS v3.1 expands on these metrics by introducing new categories, such as Attack Complexity, Privileges Required, User Interaction, and Scope, along with similar impact metrics for Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability.

This structured comparison highlights the evolution and increased granularity of the CVSS framework, emphasizing its importance in assessing the severity of vulnerabilities in software and systems.

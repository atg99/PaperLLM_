The paper presents a comparison of four pre-trained models—XLNET, BERT, ROBERTA, and DISTILBERT—on the CVSS v3.1 and CVSS v2 datasets, focusing on various performance metrics including accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 score.

In the CVSS v3.1 dataset, XLNET shows the highest accuracy at 0.9625, followed closely by ROBERTA at 0.9610 and BERT at 0.9580. For precision, XLNET again leads with 0.8932, while BERT and DISTILBERT follow closely. Recall values indicate that XLNET outperforms the others with a score of 0.8685. The F1 score for XLNET is also the highest at 0.8800, indicating its overall effectiveness.

For the CVSS v2 dataset, XLNET maintains its top performance with an accuracy of 0.9711, while ROBERTA and BERT also demonstrate strong results. Precision metrics for XLNET are again the highest at 0.9216, with BERT and DISTILBERT close behind. Recall scores show a similar trend, with XLNET achieving 0.8971. The F1 score for XLNET stands at 0.9086, reinforcing its superior performance.

The study concludes by comparing the fine-tuned XLNET with results from other similar works, showing that it significantly enhances the prediction of vulnerability metric values, thus indicating its potential for practical applications in security assessments.

The paper presents an XLNet-based prediction model for assessing CVSS metric values, addressing the challenge of managing numerous software vulnerabilities that threaten cybersecurity. With the increasing volume of vulnerabilities, security experts face difficulties in timely evaluations, which can delay remediation efforts. The authors highlight the inefficiency of the current manual assessment process, which often lags significantly behind the emergence of vulnerabilities.

To mitigate this issue, the authors propose a method that employs a fine-tuned XLNet model trained on a self-created corpus to predict CVSS metric values directly from vulnerability descriptions. This approach aims to streamline the assessment process, enabling faster and more accurate evaluations of vulnerability severity.

The study compares the performance of the XLNet model against other pre-trained models and traditional machine learning techniques. Results indicate that the proposed method achieves state-of-the-art performance in predicting CVSS metrics, thus enhancing the efficiency of vulnerability assessments and supporting timely remediation efforts. Overall, the research emphasizes the importance of leveraging machine learning to improve cybersecurity practices.

The paper discusses the Common Vulnerability Scoring System (CVSS) metrics, providing an overview of both CVSS v2 and CVSS v3.1 base metric groups. 

In CVSS v2, the key metrics include:

1. **Attack Vector**: Indicates the exploitation method, where a greater distance from the attacker increases the score.
2. **Access Complexity**: Measures the difficulty of exploiting the vulnerability.
3. **Authentication**: Assesses how often an attacker must authenticate to exploit the vulnerability.
4. **Confidentiality Impact**: Quantifies the effect on confidentiality when a vulnerability is exploited.
5. **Integrity Impact**: Evaluates the impact on the system's integrity.
6. **Availability Impact**: Assesses the consequences of exploitation on the most affected component.

For CVSS v3.1, the metrics are as follows:

1. **Attack Vector**: Represents the conditions under which exploitation is possible, with a higher score for greater attacker distance.
2. **Attack Complexity**: Reflects the uncontrolled circumstances required for exploitation.
3. **Privileges Required**: Indicates the level of privileges an attacker needs, with the highest score for no required privileges.
4. **User Interaction**: Measures if a human user’s involvement is necessary for exploitation.
5. **Scope**: Examines whether a vulnerability in one component affects others.
6. **Confidentiality**: Quantifies the impact on confidentiality for the most directly affected component.
7. **Integrity**: Represents the influence on integrity, emphasizing truthfulness and trustworthiness.
8. **Availability**: Assesses the impact on a component's availability.

These metrics provide a structured way to evaluate the severity of vulnerabilities, guiding security professionals in prioritizing remediation efforts.

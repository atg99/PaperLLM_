The paper discusses the performance of an oriented Region Proposal Network (RPN) and oriented R-CNN on the DOTA dataset, highlighting key results and methodology. Figure 6 illustrates the top-200 proposals generated by the oriented RPN for each image. Figure 7 presents detection examples using the oriented R-CNN with a ResNet50-FPN backbone, with results visualized at a confidence threshold of 0.3, where different colors indicate different object classes.

Table 1 provides recall results for the oriented RPN across varying proposal counts (R300, R1000, R2000) on the DOTA validation set, showing high recall rates of 81.60, 92.20, and 92.80 respectively. 

In terms of parameter settings, the experiments were conducted on a single RTX 2080Ti with a batch size of 2 during training, and inference was also executed on the same hardware. The experiments utilized the mmdetection platform, employing ResNet50 and ResNet101 as backbones, both pretrained on ImageNet. Data augmentation techniques included horizontal and vertical flipping. The network was optimized using the SGD algorithm with a momentum of 0.9 and a weight decay of 0.0001.

The paper introduces a novel representation scheme called midpoint offset representation for oriented objects. This method utilizes an oriented bounding box characterized by six parameters (x, y, w, h, ∆α, ∆β) to define the object's position and dimensions. The midpoints of the bounding box's sides are represented as black dots, while the vertices of the oriented bounding box are depicted as orange dots. 

The coordinates of the four vertices are derived from the midpoints and the offsets (∆α, ∆β) relative to these midpoints. This representation allows for effective regression of oriented proposals by predicting the bounding box parameters and the midpoint offsets.

To train the oriented Region Proposal Network (RPN), the paper defines positive and negative samples based on Intersection-over-Union (IoU) thresholds. A binary label is assigned to each anchor, where positive samples have an IoU greater than 0.7 or the highest IoU exceeding 0.3, while negative samples have an IoU lower than 0.3. Samples that do not fall into either category are considered invalid.

The loss function is formulated to include classification and regression components, utilizing cross-entropy loss for classification and Smooth L1 loss for regression. The paper also details the affine transformation used for box regression, providing a comprehensive framework for the proposed midpoint offset representation.

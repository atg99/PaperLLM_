This paper presents the oriented R-CNN, a two-stage detector designed for arbitrary-oriented object detection in images. Extensive experiments were conducted on two challenging datasets, DOTA and HRSC2016, to evaluate the method's performance. The results indicate that the oriented R-CNN outperforms 19 other detection methods on the DOTA dataset, achieving mAP values of 75.87% with the R-50-FPN backbone and 76.28% with R-101-FPN. Furthermore, using multi-scale training and testing, the method reaches an impressive 80.87% mAP with the R-50-FPN backbone.

For the HRSC2016 dataset, the oriented R-CNN achieved the best accuracy compared to other methods, as outlined in the results. The paper also compares the speed and accuracy of various methods, revealing that the oriented R-CNN maintains a competitive speed of 15.1 FPS while surpassing other methods in detection accuracy.

The findings suggest that the oriented R-CNN effectively balances detection accuracy and inference speed, making it a practical solution for oriented object detection. The research is supported by various grants and foundations, highlighting its significance in advancing detection methodologies.

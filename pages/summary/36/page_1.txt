The paper introduces Oriented R-CNN, a novel framework for oriented object detection that addresses the computational inefficiencies of existing two-stage detectors. Traditional methods, such as rotated Region Proposal Networks (RPN) and RoI Transformers, generate oriented proposals through complex and time-consuming processes, which hinder speed and efficiency. 

The proposed Oriented R-CNN includes an oriented RPN that directly produces high-quality oriented proposals in a cost-effective manner, significantly reducing computational overhead. The second stage, the oriented R-CNN head, refines these proposals and classifies the objects. The framework achieves state-of-the-art detection accuracy on benchmark datasets, DOTA and HRSC2016, with mAP scores of 75.87% and 96.50%, respectively, while maintaining a speed of 15.1 FPS on a single RTX 2080Ti.

The authors aim for their work to inspire further innovation in the design of oriented detectors and to establish a new baseline for oriented object detection. The code for the framework is publicly available, facilitating further research and development in this area.

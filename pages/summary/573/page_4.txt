This paper investigates the optimization of hyperparameters for general structural design problems using the HOpt framework. The goal is to enhance the understanding of how HOpt impacts machine learning models and to extend its application to more complex structural design challenges. 

The paper is structured as follows: Section 2 provides a detailed description of the HOpt framework, while Section 3 formulates HOpt for four machine learning algorithms (MLAs). Section 4 introduces four engineering benchmark structures, along with the simulation datasets generated for the study. The optimization processes are completed in Section 5, where results are analyzed to assess the influence of hyperparameters on modeling accuracy and training costs. Section 6 discusses the effects of HOpt on the accuracy and robustness of surrogate models, focusing particularly on the hyperparameters of two leading MLAs, Gaussian Process Regression (GPR) and Artificial Neural Networks (ANN), with findings documented in a supplementary file.

Additionally, a table summarizes relevant literature on the application of various machine learning algorithms as surrogate models in structural engineering, highlighting the use of experience, trial-and-error, and optimization for hyperparameter assignment across different studies.

The analysis of polynomial degrees in relation to model accuracy indicates that both 2nd and 3rd order polynomials achieved high accuracy. However, as the degree of the polynomial increases, the influence of scale and offset also rises, which is reflected in larger standard errors. It is crucial for the absolute values of scale and offset to exceed 1, as this significantly lowers the RMSE error. The hyperbolic tangent kernel, which incorporates these parameters, demonstrates a consistent trend in GPR performance across various cases. For optimal GPR accuracy, it is recommended that scale and offset values be approximately 1 and 10, respectively.

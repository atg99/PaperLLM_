The referenced literature spans various methodologies and advancements in optimization techniques within engineering and design. Browne discusses cross-validation methods crucial for model selection, while Arlot and Celisse provide a comprehensive survey of these procedures. Kim and Chang focus on Eulerian shape design sensitivity, contributing to optimization techniques using fixed grids. Van Miegroet advances generalized shape optimization through XFEM and level set descriptions. Cai et al. explore stress-constrained shape and topology optimization via a B-spline finite cell method integrated with level set functions. 

Bennett and Botkin address structural shape optimization with adaptive mesh refinement, and Du et al. introduce a data-driven design method for thin-walled vehicular structures subjected to crash loading. Bai et al. investigate the crashworthiness of extruded octagonal multi-cell tubes under dynamic impact. Brochu et al. present a Bayesian approach to procedural animation design, highlighting interactive optimization. 

Marler and Arora conduct a survey on multi-objective optimization methods, while Ferreira et al. compare methodologies for selecting solutions from the Pareto-optimal set. Lastly, Simpson et al. review metamodels for computer-based engineering design, offering recommendations, and Du and Zhu propose a PCA method enhanced by data mining for energy-absorbing structural design. This body of work collectively advances the field of design optimization, focusing on practical applications and theoretical foundations.

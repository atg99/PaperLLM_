The supplemental material presents a table summarizing the mean and standard deviation of various performance measures for sixteen machine learning (ML) models before and after hyperparameter optimization (HOpt). The measures include Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) and Mean Absolute Error (MXAE) across different categories, specifically for models like Gaussian Process Regression (GPR), Support Vector Machine (SVM), Random Forest Regression (RFR), and Artificial Neural Networks (ANN).

Key findings indicate that for GPR, RMSE decreased from 0.085 to 0.069, and MXAE improved from 0.539 to 0.401 after HOpt. Similar trends are observed in other models, with SVM showing a reduction in RMSE from 0.086 to 0.072 and MXAE from 0.583 to 0.481. RFR and ANN also exhibited improvements in performance metrics post-optimization. 

The time taken for model training varied significantly, with ANN being the most time-consuming. Overall, the data suggests that HOpt effectively enhances the performance of these ML models, as reflected in the reduced error measures. 

References are provided for further reading on topics related to stochastic optimization and deep learning in structural engineering.

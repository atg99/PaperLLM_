The paper discusses the design of a TqA structure subjected to static loading, with two equal loads of 444.8 kN applied at joints T and S. The response of the system is measured by the vertical displacement of joint S, which is limited to 60 cm to prevent failure due to insufficient cross-sectional areas. The structure features a constant thickness of 3 mm and a distance of 420 mm between the centers of two circles. 

Key design variables and their respective ranges are provided in Table 3. The left circle is fully constrained, while loads are applied to the right circle. To ensure structural integrity, stress must remain below 800 MPa, and the total mass serves as the optimization objective. The design variables include dimensions for various segments of the structure, indicating initial values and allowable ranges.

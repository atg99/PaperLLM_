The supplementary material for the paper "Understanding the effect of hyperparameter optimization on machine learning models for structure design problems" by Xianping Du, Hongyi Xu, and Feng Zhu is organized into three sections:

Supplemental Material Section A presents a comprehensive table of Pareto fronts derived from the hyperparameter optimization (HOpt) process.

Supplemental Material Section B analyzes the impact of hyperparameters on the accuracy of machine learning algorithms (MLA), specifically focusing on the root mean square error (RMSE) for Gaussian Process Regression (GPR) and Artificial Neural Networks (ANN) across four benchmark structural problems.

Supplemental Material Section C provides a detailed characterization of the four benchmark problems and evaluates the performance of the MLAs before and after the application of hyperparameter optimization.

The authors' affiliations and contact information are also included, highlighting Dr. Feng Zhu as the corresponding author.

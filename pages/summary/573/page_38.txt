This section examines the impact of hyperparameters on Gaussian Process Regression (GPR), specifically focusing on kernel-related parameters. The study highlights the significance of the parameter Sigma (σ) for both radial basis and Laplacian kernels. Results indicate that low values of σ (less than 2) are critical, with optimal performance observed at σ values of 1 for the radial basis kernel and 0 for the Laplacian kernel.

Additionally, the polynomial kernel's degree significantly influences model accuracy. The analysis shows that degrees 2 or 3 yield the best results, while low degree levels are further investigated in an enlarged view to provide clarity on their effects. Overall, the findings stress the importance of selecting appropriate hyperparameters to enhance GPR performance.

The references listed focus on the application of surrogate models and optimization techniques in various fields, particularly in evolutionary optimization algorithms and engineering design. 

Key concepts include:

1. **Surrogate Models**: Díaz-Manríquez et al. discuss the selection of surrogate models within evolutionary algorithms, highlighting their importance in optimizing expensive black-box problems, as explored by Couckuyt et al.

2. **Metamodeling Techniques**: Comparative studies by Jin et al. and Zhao and Xue examine different metamodeling methods and their effectiveness based on sample quality, emphasizing the need for proper model selection in optimization.

3. **Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)**: Nie et al. introduce TopologyGAN for topology optimization, showcasing the use of advanced machine learning techniques in structural design.

4. **Bayesian Optimization**: Hutter et al. and Snoek et al. contribute to the field of hyperparameter tuning in machine learning through sequential model-based optimization and practical Bayesian methods.

5. **Collaborative Tuning**: Bardenet et al. focus on collaborative hyperparameter tuning, which enhances the efficiency of machine learning models.

6. **Pseudo-Data Generation**: Du et al. investigate generating pseudo-data to improve classification performance in engineering design.

7. **Crashworthiness Optimization**: Studies by Song et al., Xiong et al., and Pan et al. utilize multiple surrogate models and support vector regression for lightweight and crashworthiness optimization in automotive structures.

This body of work collectively underscores the significance of surrogate modeling and optimization strategies across various applications, driving advancements in design and performance evaluation.

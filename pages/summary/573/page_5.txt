The paper discusses various machine learning algorithms (MLAs) used for predicting performance and optimizing structures under uncertainty. Key algorithms highlighted include Random Forest Regression (RFR), Support Vector Machines (SVM), Gaussian Process Regression (GPR), and Artificial Neural Networks (ANN). The studies referenced span from 2014 to 2018 and illustrate diverse applications such as vehicle crashworthiness analysis, traffic accident data interpretation, and airfoil design optimization. 

A significant focus of the paper is on a multi-objective hyperparameters optimization framework, termed HOpt. This framework aims to enhance the accuracy and robustness of surrogate models used in structural design while allowing for an analysis of the impact of individual hyperparameters on model responses. The implementation of this framework utilizes R programming and integrates various engineering software, facilitating a streamlined process from geometric design to hyperparameter tuning.

The paper also mentions several tools for hyperparameter optimization, such as Hyperopt and Optuna, emphasizing the importance of comparing computational results across different optimization algorithms. Overall, the study underscores the critical role of MLAs and hyperparameter optimization in advancing structural performance prediction and design optimization.

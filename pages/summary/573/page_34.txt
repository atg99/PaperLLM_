The appendix presents the response distributions of four newly generated test datasets, which closely match the distribution of the original training datasets. Figure B.1 illustrates this similarity across various structural design cases. The datasets include the TbPT and TqA datasets with measurements in centimeters and kilograms, respectively, as well as the ShB and McT datasets measured in specific energy absorption and coefficient of friction. Each dataset shows the number of instances corresponding to different measurement intervals, indicating that the test datasets are representative of the training datasets in terms of distribution characteristics.

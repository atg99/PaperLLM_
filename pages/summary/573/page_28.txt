This collection of references highlights various applications of computational methods and machine learning in engineering and materials science. Key studies include the evaluation of numerical tools for predicting femur loads based on bone morphology and the critical assessment of regression-based machine learning methods for polymer dielectrics. Additionally, machine learning predictive models for mineral prospectivity are discussed, comparing neural networks, random forests, regression trees, and support vector machines.

Significant contributions also address deep learning in structural engineering, hyper-parameter optimization algorithms, and the application of artificial neural networks in structural design. Other notable works explore optimal multidisciplinary response networks, metamodeling techniques for optimization, and the use of least squares support vector machines for reliability analysis.

The references collectively underscore the growing integration of advanced computational techniques in engineering design and analysis, emphasizing their effectiveness in optimizing complex systems and processes.

The paper presents Multi-V AE, a novel generative model designed for multi-view clustering that effectively learns disentangled visual representations. The framework distinguishes between view-common and view-peculiar variables, allowing for improved clustering by capturing common cluster information across multiple views while also accommodating specific visual information unique to each view. An ablation study evaluated several variants of Multi-V AE, including vanilla V AE, β-V AE, and models with controlled capacities for either view-common or view-peculiar variables. Results indicated that the optimal performance was achieved when both controlled capacities were utilized, highlighting the significance of the view-common variable in learning cluster structures. The study also demonstrated the robustness of the model against hyper-parameter sensitivity and confirmed its good convergence properties despite variations in visual information across different views. Overall, Multi-V AE achieved state-of-the-art clustering performance with linear complexity relative to data size, offering valuable insights for multi-view learning applications.

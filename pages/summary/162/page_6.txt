The paper presents a comparative study of various multi-view clustering methods using two sets of datasets: small-scale and large-scale. It evaluates the performance of these methods based on four metrics: clustering accuracy (ACC), normalized mutual information (NMI), adjusted rand index (ARI), and Purity. 

The results, shown in Tables 1 and 2, indicate that the proposed Multi-V AE-C and Multi-V AE-CZ models outperform existing methods across all datasets. For instance, Multi-V AE-CZ achieved the highest scores in all metrics for both small-scale (e.g., 0.993 ACC for Multi-COIL-10) and large-scale datasets (e.g., 0.999 ACC for Multi-MNIST). 

The paper discusses previous methods such as MVC-LFA, COMIC, and DEMVC, highlighting their approaches and limitations. The implementation details include the architecture of the neural networks used in Multi-V AE, with specifics on the encoder and decoder structure, training parameters, and shared components across views.

Overall, the findings suggest that the proposed methods significantly enhance clustering performance in multi-view scenarios, establishing a strong foundation for future research in this area.

The paper discusses the advantages of a multi-view clustering method, Multi-V AE, which utilizes disentangled visual representations to improve clustering performance, especially in datasets with significant visual gaps. The key findings include:

1. Multi-view clustering methods outperform single-view methods by leveraging richer data properties, which enhances clustering accuracy across various datasets.
2. The Multi-V AE consistently achieves superior performance metrics because it effectively reduces interference between view-specific and common cluster information, facilitating the discovery of complementary insights from multiple views.
3. Clustering performance is better with global latent representations compared to view-common representations, as hidden information in view-peculiar representations contributes to multi-view clustering.

The analysis of disentangled visual representations shows that Multi-V AE generates clear images by manipulating a common variable while controlling for view-specific variables, revealing distinct visual characteristics across different views. The method is distinguished from others by its ability to separate common cluster information from view-specific details, leading to more interpretable and effective representations.

The learning process is illustrated, demonstrating that the clustering capability of the view-common variable improves over time, while the view-peculiar representations initially contain mixed information but become clearer as training progresses. Overall, the paper highlights the importance of disentangling visual factors to enhance clustering performance in multi-view scenarios.

In this paper, the authors present an unsupervised learning-based approach for passive beamforming in reconfigurable intelligent surface (RIS)-assisted communication systems. The study includes extensive simulations that demonstrate the proposed method's ability to perform real-time phase shift configuration while achieving satisfactory rate performance. The results are summarized in two tables. 

Table I compares the performance of the proposed algorithm (SDR) with RISBFNN and a random phase method across various configurations of M (number of reflecting elements) and N (number of antennas). The SDR approach shows superior performance percentages, while the random phase method consistently underperforms.

Table II details the average time consumption of both algorithms, revealing that the proposed RISBFNN approach significantly reduces computation time compared to SDR, making it more efficient for real-time applications.

The authors conclude by suggesting future research directions, including extending the framework to more complex multi-user scenarios and exploring advanced learning techniques such as multi-modal deep learning to further enhance system performance.

This section presents the hyperparameters used for various models across different datasets, including Citeseer20, UAI2010, ACM20, BlogCatalog20, Flickr20, and CoraFull20. Key hyperparameters include the number of hidden units (nhid1, nhid2), dropout rates, learning rates (lr), weight decay, number of epochs, and parameters γ and β, which influence model performance. 

The analysis of parameters γ and β reveals their stability and applicability, particularly noted in the results from UAI2010 and Flickr datasets. The findings indicate that while the consistency and disparity constraints generally maintain stable performance across a wide range, increased values of β may lead to a decline in performance beyond a certain threshold. Additionally, the effect of the kink-nearest neighbor graph on performance is examined, showing that for the UAI2010 dataset, performance initially improves before declining, while for Flickr, a larger k enhances structural information in the feature graph, benefiting the AM-GCN model.

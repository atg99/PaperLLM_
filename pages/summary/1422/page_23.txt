The paper discusses the training of models, including the baseline LXMERT model, using the specified hyper-parameters, and highlights the architecture of attention modules utilized for processing question-image pairs. The answering module generates softmax answer probabilities based on the outputs from the attention modules.

Additionally, the parser training process is outlined, which involves segmenting questions into components, answering them individually, and logically combining those answers. The RoBERTa-Base model is adapted for the NER task, utilizing a B-I-O tagging scheme for classifying constituent tokens and connectives in questions. The dataset for training the parser consists of original questions and their corresponding constituent components.

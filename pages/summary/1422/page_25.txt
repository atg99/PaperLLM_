The supplementary material presents accuracy results for various models on question types in the VQA-Compose and VQA-Supplement datasets. In VQA-Compose, models such as LXMERT and its variants demonstrate varying accuracies across different question formats (QF), with notable performance on questions like Q1 and Q2. For instance, the model LXMERT+q ATT achieves an accuracy of 86.43 on Q1.

In VQA-Supplement, a similar trend is observed, with models like LXMERT+q ATT showing strong performance, particularly on questions related to the presence of objects in images. For example, the accuracy for Q_B is high across all models, with LXMERT+q ATT reaching 96.74. 

The analysis highlights that questions involving adversarial objects are generally more challenging compared to those concerning visible objects in the image. This trend persists across all categories and models, indicating a consistent difficulty in answering adversarial questions.

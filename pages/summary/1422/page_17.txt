The supplementary material for VQA-LOL 17 presents an overview of the VQA-Supplement dataset, which is derived from the COCO dataset. The authors showcase how captions from COCO are transformed into questions, facilitating the creation of a comprehensive dataset with ground-truth answers for object-based inquiries. Each original question generates 20 new object-based and caption-based questions, culminating in a total of 2.55 million question-answer-image triplets within the VQA-Supplement dataset. The subsequent section focuses on a detailed analysis of both the original VQA dataset and the newly developed datasets that feature logically composed questions.

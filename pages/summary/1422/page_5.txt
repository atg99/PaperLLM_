The paper presents VQA-Compose, a method for generating composite questions in Visual Question Answering (VQA) by utilizing closed questions from the VQA dataset. It defines question formulas (QF) and answer formulas (AF) to illustrate how various combinations of questions can be structured using conjunctions, disjunctions, and negations. For example, it shows that the question "Is there beer and is the man wearing shoes?" leads to a straightforward answer of "No," while negating or altering the subjects can change the outcomes.

The authors emphasize the importance of answering composite questions formed by these logical operations, highlighting that closed questions—those with definitive yes or no answers—are particularly effective for this purpose. They note that approximately 20.91% of the VQA dataset consists of such closed questions, allowing them to construct a truth table for logical question composition.

The paper outlines the dataset creation process, where two new datasets are developed: VQA-Compose, which focuses on generating composite questions from existing ones about the same image, and VQA-Supplement, which incorporates external object and caption annotations from the COCO dataset to enhance question variety. This approach aims to improve the model's ability to handle complex question structures in VQA tasks.

The paper presents an evaluation of various models on the VQA-v2 dataset, focusing on their performance in logical robustness and overall accuracy. The LOL model demonstrates performance close to state-of-the-art (SOTA) on VQA-v2 while significantly excelling in logical robustness. The analysis includes a comparison of accuracy across different question categories, revealing insights into how models interpret object presence and absence in answering logically composed questions.

Table 4 summarizes the accuracy of different models on the 'test-standard' set, where the LOL model shows competitive results, particularly in logically composed questions. It achieves an overall accuracy of 82.39% on VQA-Compose and 87.80% on VQA-Supplement, indicating a strong ability to learn logical connectives without compromising performance on standard VQA questions.

The paper highlights the commutative property of the models, noting that parser-based models provide consistent answers regardless of question order, while end-to-end (E2E) models demonstrate robustness to question composition. The evaluation asserts that the LOL model maintains overall performance while improving significantly on logically composed questions, suggesting its effectiveness in understanding and processing complex logical structures in visual question answering tasks.

The supplementary material for VQA-LOL 19 presents examples from the VQA-Supplement dataset, showcasing 20 types of new questions derived from original questions Q1 and Q2, along with their corresponding answers. The notation used includes Q for question, A for answer, QF for question-formula, and AF for answer-formula, with > and ? representing true and false values respectively. The findings indicate that answers belonging to common categories, such as food or sports, cluster together, suggesting that GloVe embeddings effectively capture similarity among these answers. The cluster names in Table 3 are assigned post-clustering for clarity and do not influence the clustering process itself. Notably, the identified cluster categories align with "knowledge categories" from OK-VQA, which were annotated by human workers on Amazon Mechanical Turk.

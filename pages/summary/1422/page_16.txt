The paper introduces two novel datasets: VQA-Compose and VQA-Supplement. VQA-Compose is constructed by transforming closed questions from the VQA dataset into various forms using negation, conjunction, and disjunction, thus producing a comprehensive set of 1.25 million question-answer-image triplets. VQA-Supplement leverages object annotations and captions from the MS-COCO dataset to create additional questions by applying logical operators to the original VQA-v2 questions.

Moreover, the study emphasizes the generation of questions based on adversarial antonyms—objects that are semantically close but not present in the images. By utilizing GloVe word embeddings, the authors identify these antonyms through a distance metric, enriching the dataset with challenging and contextually relevant questions.

Overall, the paper highlights innovative approaches to enhance the visual question answering task by expanding the complexity and variety of questions posed in relation to the images.

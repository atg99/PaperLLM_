The paper discusses the VQA-Compose dataset, highlighting the creation of new question types derived from original questions Q1 and Q2, along with their corresponding answers. The notation used includes Q for question, A for answer, QF for question-formula, and AF for answer-formula. The dataset also introduces the concept of adversarial antonyms, denoted as anto(B), which refers to the oppositional terms of objects present in the images.

In terms of question length, the average length of questions in the VQA-v2 dataset is 6.1 words, whereas the VQA-Compose dataset has an average of 12.25 words and VQA-Supplement has 15.17 words. This increase in length is attributed to the logical composition of multiple component questions in the new datasets.

Regarding answer types, the VQA dataset employs a fixed vocabulary for answers. The authors used GloVe embeddings for these answers and applied k-means clustering to categorize them into 50 clusters, with examples provided in the paper. The clustering reveals that similar answers tend to group together, indicating a structured relationship among the responses.

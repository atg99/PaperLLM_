In the study by Wijayanti et al., the performance of various predictive models is evaluated using scatter plots that compare actual values against predicted values for both training and test datasets. Figure 7 illustrates this comparison for five different models: Random Forest, Gradient Boost, Bagging Regressor, KNN, and XGBoost. 

The scatter plots serve to identify patterns and relationships in the data, with the x-axis representing actual values and the y-axis representing predicted values. An ideal prediction scenario is depicted by a green line that runs diagonally from the bottom left to the top right, indicating perfect alignment between actual and predicted values. The distribution of red dots, which represent predicted values, indicates the model's predictive accuracy—the closer and denser the dots are to the green line, the better the model's performance.

The results suggest that the Bagging Regressor and XGBoost models exhibit strong predictive performance, although a notable outlier in the XGBoost predictions indicates some inaccuracies. In contrast, the KNN model shows a significant number of predictions that deviate from the ideal line, indicating poorer performance.

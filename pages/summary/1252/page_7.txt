The paper by Wijayanti et al. presents an analysis of factors influencing rice yields through scatterplot comparisons with pesticide use, annual rainfall, and average temperature. The findings reveal distinct relationships between these variables and crop yields, enabling the identification of patterns and trends that affect rice production across various countries. The study emphasizes the utility of predictive models, such as XGBoost, in enhancing crop yield predictions and facilitating informed agricultural decision-making based on these insights.

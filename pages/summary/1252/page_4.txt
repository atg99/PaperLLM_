The preprocessing stage in the study by Wijayanti et al. focuses on preparing a dataset of 3,270 records, organized into six columns within the yield_df DataFrame, for advanced data analysis. Initially, duplicate entries are identified and removed using the pandas library's duplicate() function, which marks duplicates with a Boolean Series. This process reveals 297 duplicate records, resulting in a cleaned dataset of 2,973 entries. The goal is to enhance data accuracy and analysis efficiency.

Subsequently, the presence of empty values is checked using the yield_df.isnull().sum() function, confirming that no empty values exist following the duplicate removal. The next step involves the removal of unused attributes. The "item" attribute, which solely pertains to rice, is discarded. However, essential attributes such as area, production value, weather conditions (rainfall and average temperature), and pesticide use are retained. These attributes are vital as they serve as independent variables or predictors in the regression model, aiding in understanding historical crop yield patterns and improving prediction performance.

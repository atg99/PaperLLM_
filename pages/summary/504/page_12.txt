The paper presents a collaborative effort involving multiple authors in the development and investigation of vaccines, particularly in the context of COVID-19. The authors contributed to various aspects, including conceptualization, data curation, funding acquisition, and writing. Notably, M.O.M. and X.L. were responsible for the original draft, while M.F.B. oversaw supervision and editing.

Funding for the research was provided by Saiba GmbH, the Swiss National Science Foundation, and the Qatar National Research Fund. The study did not require Institutional Review Board approval or informed consent, and the data is accessible within the article. Acknowledgments were given to Beat Haenni for electron microscopy work.

The authors disclosed no conflicts of interest, except for M.F.B., who is associated with HypoPet AG and Saiba AG. The references cited include studies on the clinical characteristics of COVID-19, viral dynamics, infection control measures, and vaccine development against SARS-CoV-2, highlighting the ongoing research and advancements in vaccine technology.

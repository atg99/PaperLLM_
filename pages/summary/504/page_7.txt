The study investigates the immunogenicity of a fusion vaccine candidate, AP205-RBM, designed to elicit antibodies against the SARS-CoV-2 virus. The methodology involved incubating serum dilutions with ELISA plates, followed by washing with either PBST or a urea solution. Results demonstrated that the antibodies induced by the vaccine exhibited high avidity, as evidenced by the similar optical density (OD50) measurements for RBD and Spike-specific antibodies across different washing conditions. Further confirmation came from ELISA tests with low concentrations of RBD, which also indicated strong antibody affinity.

Figures included in the study provide visual data supporting these findings. Figure 1 illustrates the construction and characterization of the AP205-RBM vaccine, including its size and molecular structure. Figure 2 details the vaccination regimen and the resulting high titers of RBD and Spike-specific IgG antibodies measured at various time points post-vaccination. Statistical significance was noted, with p-values indicating robust immune responses. Overall, the results suggest that the AP205-RBM vaccine is effective in generating a strong humoral immune response against SARS-CoV-2.

The paper presents findings on the immunological responses induced by the AP205-RBM vaccine, particularly focusing on the production of immunoglobulin A (IgA) and immunoglobulin G (IgG) subclasses. Results indicate that the AP205-RBM vaccine significantly promotes the production of RBD-specific IgA on day 49 compared to the AP205 control, as measured by ELISA. Additionally, the vaccine elicited a robust IgG response, with IgG2a being the dominant subclass among the measured IgG1, IgG2a, IgG2b, and IgG3 titers across various time points (days 14, 21, 35, and 49). The statistical analysis showed significant differences in IgG subclass responses, with p-values indicating strong significance (p<0.05, * p<0.01, ** p<0.001). Overall, these results suggest that the AP205-RBM vaccine is effective in generating a targeted immune response.

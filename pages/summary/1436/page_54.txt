The references listed pertain to various advancements and methodologies in speech recognition, particularly focusing on end-to-end (E2E) systems and data augmentation techniques. Key studies include:

1. Parcollet et al. propose E2E-SINCNET, aiming for fully end-to-end speech recognition, showcasing the evolution of speech recognition models (2020).
2. Park et al. introduce SpecAugment, a straightforward data augmentation method enhancing automatic speech recognition (2019).
3. Petridis et al. explore hybrid architectures for audio-visual speech recognition, emphasizing the integration of visual cues to improve recognition accuracy (2018).
4. Peyser et al. focus on enhancing the performance of E2E systems, particularly in tail performance and numeric sequences, indicating the need for robust models in diverse contexts (2020, 2019).
5. Prabhavalkar et al. investigate RNN-T decoding improvements and compare various sequence-to-sequence models, contributing to understanding optimal architectures for speech recognition (2021, 2017).
6. The work by Pratap et al. discusses a massively multilingual ASR model capable of recognizing speech in 50 languages, highlighting scalability and efficiency in model design (2020).
7. Pundak et al. present deep contextual approaches for end-to-end speech recognition, emphasizing the importance of context in improving recognition accuracy (2018).
8. Punjabi et al. investigate streaming bilingual ASR systems with joint language identification, underlining the challenges and solutions in multilingual speech recognition (2020).

These references collectively reflect significant progress in speech recognition technologies, focusing on end-to-end models, data augmentation, and multilingual capabilities.

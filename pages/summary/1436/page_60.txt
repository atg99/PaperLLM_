The references cited focus on advancements in end-to-end speech recognition technologies, particularly utilizing transformer models, multitask training, and unified speech representation. Key contributions include the introduction of semantic masks for improved recognition accuracy, exploration of pre-training methods on large datasets, and the development of low latency streaming recognition systems. Overall, these works collectively advance the field by addressing various challenges in speech recognition through innovative methodologies and architectures.

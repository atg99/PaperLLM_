1. **Permutation Invariant Training**: Several papers explore techniques for recognizing multi-talker speech and enhancing speaker-independent speech separation, emphasizing the significance of permutation invariant training in deep models (Yu et al., 2017).

2. **Regularization Techniques**: Research discusses the use of KL-divergence regularization to improve large vocabulary speech recognition, showcasing the integration of regularization methods in deep neural networks (Yu et al., 2013).

3. **Low-Latency ASR**: Innovations such as FastEmit aim to minimize latency in streaming ASR through sequence-level emission regularization, enabling more efficient real-time speech processing (Yu et al., 2021).

4. **Dual-Mode ASR**: The concept of dual-mode ASR combines streaming and full-context modeling to enhance performance in speech recognition tasks (Yu et al., 2021).

5. **End-to-End Models**: Various studies investigate end-to-end speech recognition from raw waveforms and the application of fully convolutional architectures, which aim to simplify the ASR pipeline while maintaining accuracy (Zeghidour et al., 2018).

6. **CTC Acoustic Models**: Research on subword and cross-word units for Connectionist Temporal Classification (CTC) models highlights improvements in acoustic modeling for ASR (Zenkel et al., 2018).

7. **Comparative Studies**: Comparisons between Transformer and LSTM encoder-decoder models reveal insights into their effectiveness in ASR, contributing to the understanding of model architectures in this domain (Zeyer et al., 2019).

8. **Training Improvements**: Enhanced training methodologies for end-to-end attention models aim to boost the efficacy of ASR systems (Zeyer et al., 2018).

9. **Transformer Transducer**: The introduction of a streamable speech recognition model integrating Transformer encoders with RNN-T loss illustrates a trend towards hybrid architectures for improved performance (Zhang et al., 2020).

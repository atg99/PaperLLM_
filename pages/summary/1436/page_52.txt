The references provided focus on advancements in end-to-end speech recognition techniques, particularly emphasizing speaker adaptation, domain adaptation, and the integration of language models. Key contributions include:

1. Meng et al. (2019) explore speaker adaptation using attention-based methods, enhancing the performance of speech recognition systems in varying speaker conditions.
2. The authors also address internal language model training and estimation for domain-adaptive systems, indicating the importance of tailored models for specific contexts (Meng et al., 2021).
3. Teacher-student learning approaches are discussed as a means to facilitate domain adaptation in end-to-end systems (Meng et al., 2019).
4. Hybrid architectures combining CTC (Connectionist Temporal Classification) and attention mechanisms are presented by Miao et al. and Moritz et al., highlighting strategies for real-time speech recognition.
5. Various studies investigate the use of transformers and multi-channel acoustic modeling to improve recognition accuracy in challenging acoustic environments, such as distant speech situations.

Overall, these works reflect a trend towards more sophisticated models that leverage recent neural network architectures and learning paradigms to improve the efficiency and accuracy of speech recognition systems.

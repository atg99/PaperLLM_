The paper discusses the recent shift in the speech recognition community from deep neural network-based hybrid models to end-to-end (E2E) models for automatic speech recognition (ASR). While E2E models have achieved state-of-the-art accuracy in various benchmarks, hybrid models continue to dominate commercial ASR applications due to their longstanding optimization for production and their ability to address practical deployment challenges. The author highlights the need for E2E models to overcome these challenges to gain wider commercial acceptance. The paper provides an overview of recent advancements in E2E ASR technologies, focusing on solutions to industry-specific challenges. Key technologies discussed include streaming, attention mechanisms, transducers, transformers, and adaptation strategies. The work emphasizes the importance of addressing practical factors for the successful commercialization of E2E ASR systems.

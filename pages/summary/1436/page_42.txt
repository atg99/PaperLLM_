The referenced papers collectively contribute to advancements in end-to-end speech recognition systems, particularly focusing on various methodologies and frameworks. Key highlights include:

1. **Wav2letter**: Introduces a convolutional network-based system for efficient speech recognition.
2. **Attention Mechanisms**: Explored in improving attention-based systems and their effectiveness with sequence-based loss functions, enhancing performance in automatic speech recognition (ASR).
3. **Transformer-Transducers**: Applied to code-switched speech recognition, showcasing adaptability in multilingual contexts.
4. **Multi-Dialect Recognition**: Focuses on utilizing attention mechanisms within ensembles to address the complexities of dialectal variations in English.
5. **Auxiliary Feature Adaptation**: Discusses methods to adapt end-to-end ASR systems using additional feature sets to improve recognition accuracy.
6. **Universal Recurrent Neural Networks**: The RETURNN framework emphasizes extensibility in training recurrent models for diverse speech applications.
7. **CIF Model**: Introduces a continuous integrate-and-fire approach for enhancing end-to-end speech recognition capabilities.
8. **Speech-Transformer**: Proposes a novel sequence-to-sequence model without recurrence, aiming for efficient speech processing.

These works represent a significant stride in the field, addressing issues such as low-resource language recognition, multilingual capabilities, and the integration of advanced neural network architectures.

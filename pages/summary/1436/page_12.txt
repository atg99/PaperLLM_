In the paper, the authors explore methods to flexibly control attention mechanisms in self-attention models by introducing a binary attention mask. This mask, denoted as mt,τ, is incorporated into the self-attention equation to manage how attention is allocated based on varying computational costs and latency requirements. The equation presented illustrates how the attention weights αt,τ are computed using this binary mask, allowing for a more targeted approach to processing input data.

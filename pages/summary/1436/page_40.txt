The references listed provide a comprehensive overview of recent advancements in neural network-based speech recognition. Key contributions include adaptation algorithms, tied and reduced RNN-T decoders, and pronunciation-aware contextualization. Significant models such as the Listen, Attend and Spell framework, and multi-channel transformers are highlighted for their effectiveness in large vocabulary conversational recognition and multi-speaker scenarios. Model compression techniques and lifelong learning approaches are discussed, emphasizing their importance in enhancing efficiency and performance. Furthermore, the integration of non-autoregressive transformers and factorized neural transducers showcases innovative strategies for language model adaptation and recognition tasks. These works collectively push the boundaries of end-to-end automatic speech recognition systems.

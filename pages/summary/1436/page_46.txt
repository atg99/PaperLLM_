The references listed encompass a range of studies focused on advancements in end-to-end speech recognition technologies. Key themes include the exploration of model units and training strategies, the use of multilingual deep neural networks for cross-language knowledge transfer, and innovations in low-latency speech recognition systems such as the Conv-Transformer Transducer. Several works address rapid adaptation techniques for speakers and the integration of contextual biasing through class language models. Additionally, transfer learning approaches and enhancements to attention mechanisms for streaming automatic speech recognition (ASR) are discussed. These contributions reflect ongoing efforts to improve the efficiency, accuracy, and adaptability of speech recognition systems across languages and contexts.

The paper discusses recent advancements in end-to-end automatic speech recognition (ASR) techniques, focusing on three prominent methods: Connectionist Temporal Classification (CTC), Attention-based models, and Recurrent Neural Network Transducer (RNN-T). RNN-T is highlighted for its effectiveness in streaming ASR, offering high accuracy and low latency, making it suitable for industrial applications. The CTC technique specifically addresses the challenge of mapping speech input sequences to output label sequences, forming a foundational aspect of ASR technology. The paper aims to provide a concise overview of these key techniques, illustrating their architectures and functionalities.

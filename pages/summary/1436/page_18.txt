One common method for domain adaptation involves fine-tuning the ASR models on a small amount of labeled data from the target domain. This process ensures that the model can better generalize to the new data characteristics. Another approach is to employ unsupervised domain adaptation techniques, where the model learns to identify and adjust to domain-specific features without needing labeled data.

Additionally, data augmentation techniques can be utilized to artificially expand the training data from the target domain. This may include generating synthetic data that mimics the target domain's characteristics, thus helping the model to adapt more effectively.

The integration of domain adaptation techniques into E2E models is essential to enhance their robustness and performance in varying conditions. Such adaptations are particularly important in real-world applications where model performance can be hindered by domain mismatch. 

Overall, both speaker and domain adaptation play critical roles in improving the effectiveness of ASR systems, ensuring they perform reliably across diverse conditions.

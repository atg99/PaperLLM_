Dr. Jinyu Li is an accomplished researcher with a Ph.D. from the Georgia Institute of Technology, awarded in 2008. His career includes significant roles as a Researcher at the Intel China Research Center and as a Research Manager at iFlytek. Currently, he serves as a Partner Applied Scientist and Technical Lead at Microsoft Corporation in the USA, where he directs a team focused on advancing speech modeling algorithms and technologies to achieve state-of-the-art speech recognition accuracy for Microsoft products.

Dr. Li's research interests encompass a wide range of topics within speech recognition and processing, such as end-to-end modeling, deep learning, acoustic modeling, speech separation, and noise robustness. He has contributed over 150 peer-reviewed scientific papers and holds approximately 50 patents. Additionally, he is the lead author of the book "Robust Automatic Speech Recognition – A Bridge to Practical Applications," published by Academic Press in 2015.

His professional involvement includes membership in the IEEE Speech and Language Processing Technical Committee since 2017 and serving as the area chair for the International Conference on Acoustics, Speech, and Signal Processing since 2018. He was also an associate editor for the IEEE/ACM Transactions on Audio, Speech and Language Processing from 2015 to 2020.

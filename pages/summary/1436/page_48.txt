The references listed pertain to advancements in speech recognition technologies, particularly focusing on streaming models, data augmentation, and end-to-end systems. Key contributions include:

- Kim et al. (2021) compare various streaming models and data augmentation methods, highlighting their impact on robust speech recognition.
- Kim et al. (2019) introduce an attention-based on-device streaming speech recognition system utilizing a large speech corpus.
- The development of a multi-mode transformer transducer with stochastic future context is presented by Kim et al. (2021).
- Joint CTC-attention frameworks using multi-task learning for speech recognition are explored by Kim et al. (2017).
- Techniques for enhanced training of online end-to-end speech recognition systems are discussed by Kim et al. (2018).
- Kirkpatrick et al. (2017) address catastrophic forgetting in neural networks, a critical issue in maintaining model performance over time.
- Audio augmentation methods for improving speech recognition accuracy are detailed by Ko et al. (2015).
- Kojima (2021) examines knowledge distillation techniques for streaming transformer transducers.
- Kumatani et al. (2012) focus on microphone array processing for distant speech recognition, transitioning from close-talking to far-field sensors.
- Kurata and colleagues (2019, 2020, 2021) contribute to knowledge distillation strategies and improving neural transducer customization by addressing acoustic mismatches.

These studies collectively advance the field of speech recognition by improving model robustness, efficiency, and adaptability across various conditions and datasets.

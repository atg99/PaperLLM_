The references provided in this section highlight significant contributions to the field of neural networks and their verification, particularly focusing on adversarial robustness and safety-critical applications. Notable works include Katz et al.'s exploration of proving adversarial robustness in deep neural networks, as presented at the 1st Workshop on Formal Verification of Autonomous Vehicles. The development of the Marabou tool for verifying deep learning systems is also emphasized. Other key contributions address image classification using deep convolutional networks, hierarchical models of object recognition, and reachability analysis with provable guarantees. The references collectively underscore ongoing research efforts to enhance the reliability and security of neural networks through formal methods and verification techniques.

The paper discusses the Marabou framework, designed for the verification and analysis of Deep Neural Networks (DNNs). It outlines the operational procedure of the solver, which utilizes a queue to manage queries and timeouts. The solver begins with a query and attempts to resolve it, returning results based on whether the problem is satisfiable (SAT) or unsatisfiable (UNSAT). If a timeout occurs, the query is split into sub-queries to facilitate further analysis.

Marabou supports verification queries through various interfaces, including a native C++ format, a command-line executable, and a Python/TensorFlow interface, allowing for flexibility in how queries are presented and processed.

The evaluation section compares the performance of Marabou against other tools like Reluplex, ReluVal, and Planet using benchmark families such as ACAS Xu, CollisionDetection, and TwinStream. The results indicate that while Marabou generally performs well, particularly against Planet, it does not consistently outperform ReluVal, suggesting that enhancements in DNN-level reasoning are necessary for improved verification capabilities.

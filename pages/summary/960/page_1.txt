The paper presents Marabou, a verification framework designed for deep neural networks (DNNs), addressing the need for tools that ensure the reliability and safety of these complex systems. Marabou utilizes SMT (Satisfiability Modulo Theories) techniques to transform queries regarding a network's properties into constraint satisfaction problems. This framework is versatile, supporting various activation functions and network topologies, and includes high-level reasoning capabilities to optimize the search space and enhance performance. Furthermore, Marabou supports parallel execution, improving its scalability. It can process multiple input formats, including those generated by TensorFlow, a widely used neural network framework. The paper details the system architecture, evaluates its effectiveness, and outlines ongoing research efforts in the domain of DNN verification. The authors emphasize the importance of establishing certification processes due to the inherent opacity of DNNs, which can lead to unpredictable behaviors in critical applications.

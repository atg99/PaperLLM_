The referenced papers cover a range of topics related to optimization techniques, particularly in the context of surrogate modeling and evolutionary algorithms. 

Gano et al. discuss update strategies for Kriging models in variable fidelity optimization, emphasizing the importance of model accuracy in optimization processes. Giannakoglou and Kampolis explore multilevel optimization algorithms that incorporate metamodeling and fitness inheritance to enhance evolutionary algorithms, which can lead to more efficient search processes in complex optimization problems.

Gorissen et al. present a toolbox for surrogate modeling and adaptive sampling tailored for computer-based design, showcasing how these tools can streamline the design process. Gränning et al. propose an individual-based management approach for meta-models in evolutionary optimization, applying it to three-dimensional blade optimization, indicating the practical applications of these concepts in engineering.

Hansen and Jaskiewicz evaluate the quality of approximations to non-dominated sets, providing a framework for assessing optimization outcomes. Hansen and Ostermeier introduce completely derandomized self-adaptation in evolution strategies, highlighting advancements in algorithm adaptability. Herrera et al. delve into metamodel-assisted optimization using multiple kernel regression for mixed variables, illustrating the versatility of metamodeling in handling diverse optimization scenarios.

Horn et al. categorize model-based multi-objective optimization, proposing a taxonomy and benchmarking strategies to enhance the understanding of this field. Huang et al. present the extreme learning machine as a novel learning scheme for feedforward neural networks, contributing to the broader domain of machine learning in optimization contexts.

Huband et al. offer a scalable toolkit for multi-objective test problems, which aids in the evaluation of optimization algorithms. Husain and Kim focus on enhancing the multi-objective optimization of microchannel heat sinks through the use of evolutionary algorithms combined with multiple surrogate models, demonstrating the effectiveness of integrating various modeling approaches.

Lastly, Igel et al. discuss covariance matrix adaptation for multi-objective optimization, while Ishibuchi et al. investigate the use of heuristic local search within multiobjective memetic algorithms, both contributing to the ongoing development of sophisticated optimization methodologies. Overall, these works collectively advance the understanding and application of optimization strategies in complex and dynamic environments.

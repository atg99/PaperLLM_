The referenced works focus on various methodologies and techniques for addressing constrained multi-objective optimization problems, particularly within the domain of evolutionary algorithms. 

T. Ray et al. propose an infeasibility driven evolutionary algorithm aimed at improving constraint handling in optimization scenarios. R. Regis introduces a multi-objective constrained black-box optimization strategy utilizing radial basis function surrogates to enhance performance. M. Reyes-Sierra and C. Coello explore fitness inheritance and approximation methods for multi-objective particle swarm optimization, contributing to the understanding of swarm algorithms in constrained settings.

DEMO, developed by T. Robic and B. Filipic, showcases a differential evolution approach tailored for multi-objective optimization. P. Roy and K. Deb present a high-dimensional model representation technique for tackling expensive multi-objective problems, while J. Sacks et al. provide foundational insights into the design and analysis of computer experiments.

L. Santana-Quintero et al. review various strategies for managing expensive functions in evolutionary multi-objective optimization, and M. Sasena et al. investigate metamodeling sampling criteria for constrained global optimization. K. Sastry et al. advocate for fitness inheritance over direct evaluation in genetic algorithms.

J. Schaffer’s PhD thesis delves into machine learning applications using vector evaluated genetic algorithms. C.-W. Seah et al. discuss Pareto rank learning, and K. Shimoyama et al. focus on updating kriging surrogate models based on hypervolume indicators to improve multi-objective optimization. Lastly, K. Sindhya et al. contribute to the discourse on enhancing convergence in evolutionary multi-objective optimization through local search methods, presenting a hybrid framework that integrates these strategies effectively.

Overall, these contributions collectively advance the understanding and application of evolutionary algorithms in constrained optimization, addressing both theoretical frameworks and practical implementations.

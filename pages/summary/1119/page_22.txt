The MOEA/D-RBF algorithm was evaluated against standard benchmarks with varying objectives and decision variables, demonstrating improved performance over traditional multi-objective algorithms. Overall, the discussed algorithms illustrate the advantages of using ensembles of metamodels in evolutionary algorithms, enhancing optimization performance through adaptive strategies and effective population management.

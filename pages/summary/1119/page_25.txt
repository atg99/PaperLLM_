The paper analyzes various evolution control strategies in optimization algorithms, categorizing them into fixed and adaptive evolution control. It presents a comparison of different algorithms based on their characteristics and the types of optimization problems they address. The study identifies a range of benchmark and real-world problems, providing a comprehensive overview of the research landscape through citations of relevant papers. The figures illustrate the distribution of papers across different algorithm types, highlighting the use of metamodel-based approaches and the various strategies employed, such as dominance, decomposition, and indicator-based methods. The findings suggest a diverse application of algorithms in both theoretical and practical settings, emphasizing the importance of selecting suitable evolution control strategies for specific optimization challenges.

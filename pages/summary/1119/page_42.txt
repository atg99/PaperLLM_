This collection of references highlights various studies focused on multi-objective optimization techniques, particularly in the context of surrogate models and evolutionary algorithms. 

Key contributions include:

1. **Aerodynamic Design**: Oyama et al. explore aerodynamic multi-objective design for flapping airfoils using Navier-Stokes solvers, demonstrating the application of computational fluid dynamics in optimization (2009).

2. **Surrogate-Assisted Algorithms**: Palar et al. investigate local search strategies within a surrogate-assisted multi-objective memetic algorithm framework, emphasizing the efficiency of these methods for expensive optimization problems (2016).

3. **Scalarization Functions**: Palar et al. also compare various scalarization functions in the context of surrogate-assisted memetic algorithms, which is crucial for transforming multi-objective problems into single-objective ones (2015).

4. **Extreme Learning Models**: Pavelski et al. present extreme learning surrogate models for multi-objective optimization, highlighting their effectiveness based on decomposition methods (2016, 2014).

5. **Aggregate Surrogate Models**: Pilát and Neruda introduce ASM-MOMA, a multi-objective memetic algorithm utilizing aggregate surrogate models to enhance optimization performance (2011).

6. **Local Search Enhancements**: Their work on hypervolume-based local search methods contributes to the improvement of many-objective optimizers, showcasing innovative approaches in evolutionary computation (2014).

7. **Hybrid Approaches**: Poloni et al. hybridize genetic algorithms with neural networks and classical optimization techniques to tackle complex fluid dynamics design problems (2000).

8. **Model-Assisted Selection**: Ponweiser et al. discuss model-assisted S-metric selection for multi-objective optimization on limited evaluation budgets, a significant consideration in practical applications (2008).

9. **Infill Sampling Criteria**: The introduction of clustered multiple generalized expected improvement by Ponweiser et al. provides a novel infill sampling criterion for surrogate models, further enhancing optimization strategies (2008).

10. **Memetic Algorithms for Classification**: Qasem et al. apply memetic multi-objective particle swarm optimization to classification problems using radial basis function networks, illustrating the versatility of these algorithms (2013).

11. **Coupling Techniques**: Quagliarella and Vicini examine the integration of genetic algorithms with gradient-based optimization techniques, underscoring the potential for hybrid approaches in engineering and computer science (1998).

Overall, these studies contribute to the advancement of multi-objective optimization techniques by integrating computational methods, surrogate modeling, and hybrid algorithms, addressing complex engineering challenges.

The paper titled "A survey on handling computationally expensive multiobjective optimization problems with evolutionary algorithms" by Chugh, Sindhya, Hakanen, and Miettinen provides a comprehensive review of methods for addressing multiobjective optimization problems that are computationally intensive. The authors explore various evolutionary algorithms designed to efficiently navigate these complex optimization landscapes. The survey highlights the challenges faced in such optimizations and discusses strategies employed to mitigate computational costs while maintaining solution quality. The findings underscore the significance of balancing exploration and exploitation in evolutionary approaches, as well as the importance of surrogate models and adaptive techniques. This work contributes to the understanding of how to effectively apply evolutionary algorithms in scenarios where computational resources are limited.

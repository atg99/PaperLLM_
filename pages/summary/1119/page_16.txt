In comparison to earlier approaches, the adaptive evolution control strategy employed in these algorithms allows for dynamic decision-making regarding the use of metamodels versus original functions based on predicted tolerance levels. This flexibility contributes to the algorithms' effectiveness in generating high-quality nondominated solutions across various optimization problems while requiring fewer function evaluations, thus demonstrating their practical applicability in real-world scenarios.

Overall, the studies highlight the significant advancements in evolutionary algorithms through the integration of metamodels, showcasing improved performance metrics such as generational distance and visual solution quality in complex optimization tasks.

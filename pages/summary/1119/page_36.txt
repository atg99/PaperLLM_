This collection of references highlights significant contributions to the field of multi-objective optimization and evolutionary algorithms. 

Branke and Schmidt (2005) discuss fitness estimation as a method to enhance convergence rates in optimization processes. Chen et al. (2012) present a sequential approximate technique for efficiently optimizing black-box functions across multiple objectives. The concept of fitness inheritance in multi-objective optimization is explored by Chen et al. (2002), indicating its potential benefits in evolutionary strategies.

Chen et al. (2012) also argue that larger population sizes in evolutionary algorithms might not always be advantageous, challenging traditional views on population dynamics. Cheng et al. (2016) introduce a reference vector guided evolutionary algorithm aimed at many-objective optimization, while Chugh et al. propose a surrogate-assisted variant to address computational challenges in this domain.

The works of Coello and Lamont (2007, 2004) encapsulate the principles and applications of evolutionary algorithms for multi-objective problems. Coello and Pulido (2001) present a micro genetic algorithm tailored for multi-objective optimizations, emphasizing efficiency in search processes.

PESA-II, introduced by Corne et al. (2001), focuses on region-based selection methods in evolutionary multi-objective optimization. Couckuyt et al. (2014) provide techniques for fast calculations of multi-objective improvement probabilities, enhancing the efficiency of Pareto optimization strategies. Lastly, Currin et al. (1998) present a Bayesian approach for the design and analysis of computer experiments, which could be integral to optimization methodologies.

Custodio et al. (2011) contribute to the discussion with direct multisearch techniques for multi-objective optimization, showcasing various innovative strategies to tackle complex optimization problems. Collectively, these works reflect the diverse methodologies and theoretical advancements in the realm of multi-objective optimization.

The paper discusses the backward pass equations for training the Augmented LSTM cell using Backpropagation Through Time (BPTT). It builds upon the established method for Vanilla LSTM cells and introduces the total partial derivatives relevant to the augmented structure. The key addition is the total partial derivative of the objective function with respect to the qualifier signal, which is crucial for integrating the projection layer into the data path.

The backward pass equations are derived through the application of the chain rule and make use of previously defined border and intermediate total partial derivatives. Several equations are presented that detail the relationships between the various signals and derivatives throughout the Augmented LSTM's architecture. These equations include expressions for intermediate signals such as ⃗χ, ⃗β, ⃗ρ, and others, which are computed based on the gradients of the error with respect to the outputs and the internal states.

The equations encapsulate the complex interactions within the Augmented LSTM cell during the backward pass, emphasizing the importance of the qualifier signal and its derivatives in the overall training process. The results demonstrate how the augmented structure modifies the standard LSTM training approach, ensuring that the additional components are effectively integrated into the learning mechanism.

The paper discusses the derivation of the partial derivatives of the objective function E with respect to various model parameters in the context of an Augmented LSTM cell. The equations presented (230-249) detail the gradients for the different weight matrices and biases associated with the cell's inputs, state, and outputs. Specifically, the derivatives are defined for the input gate, state gate, and output gate components, as well as for a specific weight related to a query in the model. 

The total partial derivative of E with respect to the model parameters, Θ, is organized to reflect the structure defined in Equation 215. This organized presentation allows for a clearer understanding of how each parameter influences the overall objective function at a specific step indexed by n. Each derivative is associated with respective activation values, showcasing the relationships between inputs and the model's learning process.

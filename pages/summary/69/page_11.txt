The paper discusses the sampling process of a recurrent neural network (RNN) sequence using a defined window of length N. The sequence is represented by the equation w[n] = u[n]−u[n−N], which can be expressed as a summation involving j(m) and a series of delta functions. The variable j(m) is defined through a cumulative sum based on the parameters of the system.

Equations 40 to 46 illustrate how segments of the output sequence v[n] can be extracted using a rectangular window function wm[n], where the size of the window is determined by Km. The non-zero values of the output subsequence vm[n] are confined to specific indices dictated by j(m), thereby ensuring that the extracted segment aligns with the desired output.

Additionally, the paper introduces Q(⟨r[n]⟩) as a mapping function that transforms the readout signals of the RNN into observable output signals ⟨y[n]⟩. An objective function L is defined to quantify the deviation of the observable outputs from the ground truth outputs, considering the entire range of the index n. Finally, all parameters of the RNN system are combined into a single symbol Θ for simplification. This framework sets the stage for evaluating the performance of the RNN based on its output accuracy relative to the expected results.

The total derivative of the objective function with respect to model parameters, as captured in Equation 90, reflects contributions from all steps of the unrolled sequence, which is crucial for optimization via Gradient Descent. The paper highlights that as the RNN is trained using Backpropagation Through Time (BPTT), the error gradient flows in the reverse direction relative to the sequence, allowing the gradient at one time step to influence earlier steps.

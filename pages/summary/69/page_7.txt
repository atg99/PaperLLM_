The paper introduces two weight matrices, Wr and Wx, along with a bias vector, θs, which transform the system into the canonical form of a Recurrent Neural Network (RNN). The equations (27) to (29) define these transformations, leading to the state update equation in (30), where the state vector s at time n is calculated based on the previous state, the previous readout, and the current input, along with the bias. The output at time n is derived from the state using a function G, as shown in equation (31). 

Figure 1 illustrates the canonical RNN cell, noting that the bias parameters are omitted for clarity but can be incorporated by adjusting the input vector. The paper indicates that this RNN formulation will serve as a foundation for developing the Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) system. Additionally, it highlights the importance of understanding the concepts of "unrolling" or "unfolding" in RNNs, which will be further explored in relation to the standard RNN definition derived from stability considerations.

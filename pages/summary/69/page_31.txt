This section discusses the derivation of partial derivatives of the objective function E with respect to the parameters of the Vanilla LSTM cell. Equations 156 to 170 provide the specific derivatives for various weights and biases associated with the input, state, and output components of the LSTM cell at each time step n. The total partial derivative of E with respect to the model parameters, represented as Θ, is consolidated in Equation 171, which lists all the contributions from the individual derivatives.

When the LSTM cell is unrolled for K steps to process a full segment of training samples, the same parameters Θ are utilized across all steps. This leads to Equation 172, which sums the contributions of all steps to compute the total derivative of E with respect to Θ. This total derivative is essential for optimization using Gradient Descent, and in practice, it is calculated across batches of segments, typically ranging from 16 to 128, to update the model parameters effectively.

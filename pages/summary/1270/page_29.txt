The appendix includes tables that provide detailed information about the base learners chosen for various data sets: PL04, Yelp, Twitter, and SMS. Specifically, Tables 5, 7, 9, and 11 list the base learners for each data set. Additionally, Tables 6, 8, 10, and 12 present the aggregate results from eighteen different ensemble configurations, which are formed by combining two output types, three combination methods, and three selection approaches, all evaluated on the same data sets.

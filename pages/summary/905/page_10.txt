This compilation of references highlights significant advancements in the field of language processing, particularly focusing on hate speech detection and speech emotion recognition. 

Key contributions include the development of BERT, a deep bidirectional transformer model that enhances language understanding through pre-training (Devlin et al., 2018). Various methodologies for hate speech detection are explored, ranging from lexicon-based approaches (Gitari et al., 2015) to deep learning techniques such as convolutional neural networks (Gambäck and Sikdar, 2017; Zhang and LeCun, 2015) and predictive embeddings (Kshirsagar et al., 2018). 

Studies also address the challenges of detecting hate speech in social media contexts, examining the influence of annotator bias (Waseem, 2016) and proposing classification frameworks (Park and Fung, 2017). The integration of data augmentation techniques is emphasized as a means to improve model robustness (Sainath et al., 2015; Gül Şahin and Steedman, 2019).

Overall, the collection reflects a growing recognition of the importance of nuanced approaches to language understanding and the societal implications of automated detection systems, particularly in combating online hate speech.

The paper discusses the integration of multi-agent systems (MAS) and the Internet of Things (IoT) in the context of smart cities, emphasizing their role in enhancing urban living through intelligent data collection and processing. Smart cities are depicted as interconnected entities that utilize sensors to monitor various factors such as pollution and traffic, aiming to improve the quality of life for citizens.

The paper highlights three contributions within a special issue focused on smart cities. The first study presents a MAS designed for bike-sharing systems, featuring data collection and demand forecasting capabilities validated through a case study in Salamanca. The second work explores the placement of electric vehicle charging stations through a MAS that incorporates data from diverse sources and employs an optimization algorithm for site selection. The third contribution investigates the application of agreement technologies to enhance coordination among emergency medical services, facilitating flexible interactions and information exchange.

In conclusion, the research indicates that MAS continues to evolve, providing innovative solutions across various fields. The special issue reflects the vitality and potential of MAS, demonstrating the ongoing relevance of this area of study and paving the way for future editions to showcase high-quality research.

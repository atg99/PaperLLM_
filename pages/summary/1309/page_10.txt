In the broader impact section, the authors emphasize that their research demonstrates the potential for achieving high performance with limited labeled data, which could benefit organizations with constrained budgets for data annotation. They assert that the method does not exploit biases in the data and utilizes standard benchmarks like IMDb, CIFAR-10, SVHN, and ImageNet, ensuring that no group is disadvantaged by this work.

The paper discusses various datasets utilized in conversational and computer vision (CV) tasks. It highlights the richness of DSTC2 in conversation state representation, which includes user targets and methods to identify them. The MWOZ dataset consists of over 8,000 conversations across multiple domains, each annotated with goals and slot values. The Out-Of-Scope dataset contains intent classifications, distinguishing between in-scope and out-of-scope user utterances.

In CV, the paper categorizes datasets into three main task types: classification, detection, and segmentation. Notably, the ImageNet dataset serves as a significant resource for pretraining, while others like CIFAR-10, MNIST, and COCO are frequently employed in downstream tasks. MNIST, in particular, is recognized as a foundational dataset in machine learning, often referred to by Geoffrey Hinton as the "drosophila of machine learning" due to its widespread use in testing algorithms. Overall, the paper emphasizes the importance and diversity of datasets in advancing conversational AI and CV technologies.

The paper introduces the formulas for ROUGE-N and ROUGE-L metrics, which are commonly used for evaluating text summaries. 

ROUGE-N is defined as follows:

ROUGE_N = Count_match(gram_n) / Count(gram_n)

Here, N stands for the N-gram, Count(gram_n) denotes the total occurrences of an N-gram in the reference summaries, and Count_match(gram_n) indicates the number of times the N-gram appears in both the candidate and reference summaries. 

ROUGE-L, on the other hand, is calculated using the Longest Common Subsequence (LCS) method:

ROUGE_L = F_lcs = (1 + β^2) * R_lcs * P_lcs / (R_lcs + β^2 * P_lcs)

Where R_lcs is the ratio of the length of the LCS to the length of the reference abstract (M), and P_lcs is the ratio of the LCS length to the length of the candidate abstract (N). The paper emphasizes that ROUGE focuses on N-gram co-occurrence statistics based on recall (ROUGE-N) and the F-value (ROUGE-L), highlighting its word-based rather than semantic-based correspondence, though using multiple reference summaries can improve its effectiveness.

The paper also discusses the METEOR metric, an enhancement of the BLEU metric designed to better reflect human judgment in translation evaluation. METEOR incorporates WordNet to assess matching relationships, and its formula is:

METEOR = (1 - Pen) * F_m

Where F_m is the harmonic mean of precision (P) and recall (R), and Pen is a penalty factor for word order discrepancies. The metrics for P and R are defined in relation to matched units and the lengths of candidate and reference translations.

Finally, the concept of perplexity is introduced as a measure of confusion in language models. The formula for perplexity (PPL) is given by:

PPL(W) = P(w1, w2, ..., wM) ^ (-1/M)

This indicates that perplexity is calculated based on the probability of a sequence of words, normalized by the length of the sentence. 

Overall, the paper outlines key evaluation metrics used in natural language processing, each with its specific calculation methods and applications in text summarization and translation.

The paper outlines various datasets utilized in computer vision, highlighting their unique characteristics and applications. 

1. **Street View House Numbers (SVHN)**: A dataset consisting of real-world digit numbers from Google Street View images, including 73,257 training digits and 26,032 testing digits, all in 32x32 color images with class labels and character-level bounding boxes.

2. **CIFAR**: Comprising CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100, these datasets contain 50,000 training images and 10,000 testing images for CIFAR-10, and 500 training images and 100 testing images for each of 100 classes in CIFAR-100, both at 32x32 pixels.

3. **STL-10**: An extension of CIFAR-10, featuring 96x96 color images with 500 training and 800 testing images across 10 classes, plus 100,000 unlabeled images for unsupervised learning.

4. **Caltech-101**: This dataset has around 300x200 color images across 101 categories, with annotations for object outlines.

5. **ImageNet**: A large-scale dataset with over 14 million images across 21,841 synsets, used extensively in various computer vision tasks, particularly in the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC).

6. **HMDB51**: An action video database containing 7,000 clips across 51 action classes, focusing on facial actions and body movements.

7. **UCF101**: An action video dataset with 101 action categories, collected from YouTube and serving as a benchmark in action recognition competitions.

8. **Food-101**: A dataset of 101 food categories, containing 750 training images and 250 testing images per class.

9. **Birdsnap**: A fine-grained dataset for bird categorization, featuring 49,829 images of 500 North American species, annotated with details about age and breeding status.

10. **SUN397**: A subset of the SUN database focused on scene categorization, comprising 108,754 images across 397 categories, aimed at enhancing scene recognition capabilities.

The paper emphasizes the importance of these diverse datasets in advancing research and applications in the field of computer vision.

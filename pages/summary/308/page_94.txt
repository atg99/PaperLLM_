This list of references includes various studies focused on named entity recognition (NER) and dialogue act classification. Key contributions include:

1. **Interpretable Multi-Dataset Evaluation**: Fu et al. explore methods for interpreting NER results across multiple datasets, enhancing evaluation metrics.
2. **Constrained Decoding**: Lester et al. propose constrained decoding techniques to improve efficiency in NER taggers, optimizing computational resources.
3. **Word Embeddings for NER**: Luo et al. investigate the use of word embeddings exclusively for NER tasks, highlighting the potential of embeddings in entity recognition.
4. **Unified MRC Framework**: Li et al. introduce a unified framework for NER based on machine reading comprehension, aiming to integrate various NER approaches.
5. **Chinese NER with Lattice LSTM**: Zhang and Yang present a method utilizing lattice LSTM for recognizing entities in Chinese text, addressing language-specific challenges.
6. **Glyph-Vectors for Chinese Characters**: Meng et al. develop glyph-vectors to enhance character representation in Chinese NER tasks.
7. **Nested NER**: Katiyar and Cardie revisit nested NER, proposing refined techniques for handling entities within entities.
8. **Dynamic Span Graphs for Information Extraction**: Luan et al. create a framework for information extraction using dynamic span graphs, providing a new approach to NER.
9. **Nested Entity Mention Detection**: Shibuya and Hovy introduce a method for nested NER using advanced sequence learning techniques.
10. **Anchor-Region Networks**: Lin et al. present a novel approach for nested entity detection via anchor-region networks, improving detection accuracy.

Further references include studies on dialogue state tracking, automatic dialogue act segmentation, and classification, emphasizing advancements in understanding and processing dialogue in various contexts. These works collectively contribute to the fields of natural language processing and information extraction, addressing both efficiency and accuracy in entity recognition and dialogue systems.

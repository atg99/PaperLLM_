This paper discusses state-of-the-art unified models leveraging transformers for various AI tasks, categorized into three types: single-transformer, multi-transformer, and comb-transformer models. 

The single-transformer model includes UNITER, which integrates image and text embedding through a multi-layer transformer while employing conditional masking for pretraining. Uni-Perceiver, another single-transformer model, utilizes a shared parameters approach for processing different modalities, achieving competitive performance in downstream tasks with minimal data via prompt tuning. Gato exemplifies a single large transformer model capable of handling multiple tasks across various data types, trained on a vast array of tasks. 

The OFA model is a sequence-to-sequence learning framework that employs a task-agnostic design, eliminating the need for task-specific layers, while still achieving competitive results in both unimodal and crossmodal tasks. UNIFIED-IO adopts a similar sequence-to-sequence architecture, successfully performing a range of tasks without the need for fine-tuning by standardizing input and output formats. Lastly, BEiT-3 serves as a general-purpose multimodal model, showcasing significant advancements in backbone architecture, pretraining tasks, and model scaling.

Overall, these models demonstrate the versatility and effectiveness of transformer architectures in addressing diverse AI challenges across different modalities.

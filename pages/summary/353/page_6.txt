In summary, the paper outlines the measures taken to enhance the GPT-4o model, particularly focusing on its audio capabilities. Key updates include the refusal of requests for copyrighted content, the implementation of filters to detect and block music, and a moderation system to prevent the generation of disallowed content, such as erotic or violent speech. The model was also trained to avoid ungrounded inferences and handle sensitive trait attribution cautiously. To mitigate risks associated with unauthorized voice generation, the model is restricted to preset voices created in collaboration with voice actors, and a classifier is employed to monitor compliance with these restrictions. Overall, these measures aim to ensure safe and responsible use of the audio generation capabilities of GPT-4o.

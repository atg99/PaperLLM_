The GPT-4o System Card outlines the capabilities, limitations, and safety evaluations of the GPT-4o model, an autoregressive omni model developed by OpenAI. This model processes text, audio, images, and video inputs and generates corresponding outputs, utilizing a single neural network for end-to-end training across these modalities. It can respond to audio inputs in as little as 232 milliseconds, making it comparable to human conversation response times. GPT-4o demonstrates performance on par with GPT-4 Turbo for English text and code, with notable enhancements in non-English text processing, while being faster and more cost-effective.

The document emphasizes OpenAI's commitment to safety and responsible AI usage, detailing the Preparedness Framework evaluations included in the System Card. It evaluates GPT-4o's capabilities and safety across various categories, particularly focusing on voice interactions, as well as text and image functionalities. Additionally, the System Card addresses third-party assessments concerning potential risks and societal impacts associated with the model's text and vision capabilities.

The training data for GPT-4o includes a diverse range of publicly available information collected from standard machine learning datasets and web crawls, with a cutoff in October 2023. The System Card serves as a comprehensive resource for understanding the advanced functionalities and safety measures of GPT-4o while acknowledging its limitations.

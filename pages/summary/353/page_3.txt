The paper discusses the external red teaming process conducted by OpenAI, involving over 100 red teamers from 29 countries who spoke 45 different languages. This effort, which spanned from early March to late June 2024, aimed to evaluate the GPT-4o API through four phases, utilizing various model checkpoints at different stages of training and safety mitigation.

In Phase 1, 10 red teamers tested early model checkpoints that processed audio and text for single-turn conversations. Phase 2 expanded to 30 red teamers and included early safety mitigations, allowing for both single and multi-turn conversations with audio, image, and text inputs. Phase 3 involved 65 red teamers assessing improved safety mitigations on more advanced checkpoints that also processed multi-turn conversations across audio, image, and text outputs. Finally, Phase 4 continued with 65 red teamers evaluating the final model candidates in a real-world iOS app setting, focusing on audio and video prompts for real-time multi-turn conversations.

Throughout these phases, red teamers were tasked with discovering capabilities, identifying potential risks, and stress testing mitigations related to speech-to-speech functionalities. Their evaluations focused on various categories, including disallowed content, misinformation, bias, and sensitive topics, leveraging their expertise across diverse fields such as cognitive science, cybersecurity, and psychology. This initiative builds on previous work detailed in the GPT-4 System Card.

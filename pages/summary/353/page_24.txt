The Uhura-Eval benchmark was developed in collaboration with fluent speakers of Amharic, Hausa, and Yoruba to evaluate models' reading comprehension in these languages. The new model, GPT-4o, exhibits significant improvements over previous models like GPT 3.5 Turbo and GPT-4. Specifically, accuracy on ARC-Easy-Hausa increased from 6.1% with GPT 3.5 Turbo to 71.4% with GPT-4o, and TruthfulQA-Yoruba accuracy rose from 28.3% to 51.1%. Furthermore, in Hausa, performance improved from 32.3% with GPT 3.5 Turbo to 59.4% with GPT-4o. 

Despite these advancements, there are still performance gaps between English and the evaluated languages, though GPT-4o reduces this disparity significantly. For example, the difference in ARC-Easy performance between English and Hausa decreased from approximately 54 percentage points with GPT 3.5 Turbo to under 20 percentage points with GPT-4o. This trend is consistent across both TruthfulQA and ARC-Easy evaluations.

The research team acknowledges the need for continued efforts to enhance evaluation quality and coverage for underrepresented languages, emphasizing the importance of understanding language nuances and dialects. Future research should focus on potential interventions and partnerships to improve model utility for both well-represented and underrepresented languages. The team encourages further exploration and collaboration, sharing resources such as translated ARC-Easy and TruthfulQA on Hugging Face.

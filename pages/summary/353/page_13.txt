The evaluations encompassed a comprehensive assessment of GPT-4o's capabilities in persuasion and cybersecurity. In terms of persuasion, the model was classified as borderline medium risk, primarily due to the potential for misuse in manipulative contexts. Conversely, in cybersecurity, the model received a low risk classification, as it did not demonstrate sufficient proficiency in exploiting vulnerabilities during competitive hacking challenges.

The cybersecurity evaluation involved 172 Capture the Flag tasks across various categories, including web application exploitation and cryptography. While GPT-4o showcased reasonable problem-solving skills and adaptability in debugging, it struggled to change strategies when faced with obstacles. This limitation ultimately contributed to its low risk classification in cybersecurity.

Overall, the findings indicate that while GPT-4o exhibits notable capabilities in some areas, it presents medium risk in persuasion and low risk in cybersecurity, highlighting the need for ongoing monitoring and potential mitigations in its deployment.

The paper presents a comparison of safety metrics for the current and new versions of the GPT-4o model, specifically focusing on text and audio modes. The evaluation results highlight various safety categories, including not unsafe, not overrefuse, and several specific risks such as sexual minors, extremism propaganda, and self-harm. The data indicates that the new GPT-4o model maintains or improves safety scores across most categories when compared to the current model. Notably, the audio mode shows strong performance with a perfect score of 1.0 in several areas. Overall, the results suggest that both the new text and audio modes of GPT-4o exhibit high safety standards.

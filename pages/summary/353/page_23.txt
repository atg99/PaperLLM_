The paper discusses advancements in the evaluation of scientific capabilities of models, particularly focusing on GPT-4o's performance in underrepresented languages. It highlights that GPT-4o demonstrates enhanced reading comprehension and reasoning skills in languages such as Amharic, Hausa, Northern Sotho (Sepedi), Swahili, and Yoruba, thereby reducing the performance gap with English. Collaborating with external researchers, the authors conducted evaluations using two benchmarks: ARC-Easy, which assesses a model's ability to answer basic science questions, and TruthfulQA, aimed at measuring the model's accuracy in avoiding misconceptions. This evaluation approach marks a significant step toward improving language representation in AI.

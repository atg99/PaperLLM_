The paper presents a comparison of accuracy results for various models—GPT 3.5 Turbo, GPT-4o mini, GPT-4, and GPT-4o—on the Uhura-Eval benchmark for three languages: Amharic, Hausa, and Yoruba. The results indicate that the performance of the models improves significantly from GPT 3.5 Turbo to GPT-4o, with GPT-4o achieving the highest accuracy across all languages. 

In the conclusion, OpenAI emphasizes the importance of safety measures and mitigations that have been integrated throughout the development and deployment of the GPT-4o model. The organization commits to continuous monitoring and updating of these measures as the landscape evolves. The paper advocates for further investigation into several critical areas, including the robustness of omni models against adversarial attacks, the risks of anthropomorphism and emotional overreliance, the societal impacts of these technologies, and the implications of model capabilities such as self-improvement and autonomy. This encourages ongoing research and development to ensure safe and effective use of AI technologies.

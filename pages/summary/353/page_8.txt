The section discusses the issue of disparate performance in voice inputs for models like GPT-4o, highlighting that users with different accents may experience variations in service quality. To address this risk, GPT-4o was post-trained with a variety of voice inputs to ensure consistent performance across diverse user voices. 

Evaluations involved using a fixed assistant voice and a voice engine to generate user inputs from both official system voices and a diverse collection of 27 English voice samples from speakers of varying backgrounds. The evaluation focused on two main areas: capabilities and safety behavior, with the capabilities assessed through tasks such as TriviaQA, MMLU, HellaSwag, and Lambada. 

Results indicated that while performance on the diverse voice samples was slightly lower than on system voices, the differences were not statistically significant across all tasks. This suggests that the model's training effectively mitigated the risks associated with disparate performance due to voice variations.

The paper discusses the societal impacts of GPT-4o, focusing on anthropomorphization and health as key areas of concern. 

In the section on anthropomorphization, the authors highlight the tendency of users to attribute human-like qualities to AI models, particularly with the enhanced audio capabilities of GPT-4o. This may lead to increased trust in the model, even in the presence of "hallucinations," or misinformation generated by the AI. Users have been observed forming emotional connections with the model, which raises questions about the implications for human relationships and social norms. For instance, the model's deferential nature might distort typical human interaction patterns, potentially benefiting some users while harming others. The authors stress the need for further research into emotional reliance on AI and its long-term effects on user behavior.

In the health sector, the paper notes that omni models like GPT-4o can enhance access to health information and streamline clinical workflows. The model has demonstrated effectiveness in various biomedical applications, including clinical documentation, patient communication, clinical trial recruitment, and decision support. The potential benefits of these applications underscore the importance of responsible AI deployment in sensitive areas like health care.

Overall, the paper calls for ongoing investigation into the societal implications of advanced AI models, emphasizing the need for a nuanced understanding of their impact on human relationships and health-related practices.

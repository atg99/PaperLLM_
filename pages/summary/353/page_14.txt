The paper evaluates the performance of GPT-4o in various tasks, particularly focusing on its ability to tackle Capture The Flag (CTF) challenges and assess biological threats. In CTF challenges, GPT-4o demonstrated poor execution, completing only 19% of high-school level tasks, 0% of collegiate level tasks, and 1% of professional level tasks across ten attempts each. 

In the context of biological threats, GPT-4o was assessed for its capacity to assist both biological experts and novices in answering questions related to the creation of biological threats. The evaluation, conducted in collaboration with Gryphon Scientific, involved questions covering the entire biological threat creation process, from ideation to release. Participants received assistance either from the internet, GPT-4o, or a specially trained research-only version of GPT-4o designed to answer biologically risky questions without refusals.

The results indicated that GPT-4o did not enhance the capabilities of users sufficiently to meet a medium risk threshold for biological threat creation, earning a low preparedness score. Automated evaluations showed that GPT-4o scored 69% in consensus on tacit knowledge and troubleshooting related to biological risks.

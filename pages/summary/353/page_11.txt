The paper discusses the enhancements made to the GPT-4o model to address risks associated with Ungrounded Inference (UGI) and Sensitive Trait Attribution (STA) in audio output. To mitigate risks, the model was post-trained to refuse UGI requests while cautiously answering STA questions. This resulted in a notable improvement, with accuracy rising from 60% to 84% in correctly handling sensitive trait identification.

The paper also addresses risks related to the potential output of harmful content through audio, which could be more impactful than text. It highlights the successful transfer of safety measures from text to audio, with strong performance in maintaining content restrictions and refusals. The model employs a moderation system that evaluates both audio input and output to prevent harmful language.

Additionally, the paper notes the risks of generating erotic or violent speech content, which prompted the decision to restrict such outputs. Overall, the evaluations indicate a strong alignment between text and audio safety measures, ensuring that the model adheres to content policies effectively.

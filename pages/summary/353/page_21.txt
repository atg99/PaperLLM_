The paper presents an evaluation of GPT-4o, a more affordable and accessible version of its predecessor GPT-4T, which incorporates audio inputs and outputs for enhanced interaction in health settings. The authors conducted 22 text-based evaluations across 11 datasets, focusing on 0-shot and 5-shot prompting without hyperparameter tuning. Results indicate that GPT-4o outperforms GPT-4T in 21 out of 22 evaluations, with significant improvements in accuracy. For instance, the 0-shot accuracy on the MedQA USMLE 4 options dataset increased from 78.2% to 89.4%, surpassing specialized medical models like Med-Gemini-L 1.0 and Med-PaLM 2. 

The evaluations also show consistent improvements across various medical knowledge tasks, with GPT-4o achieving higher accuracy in datasets such as MedQA Taiwan and MMLU Clinical Knowledge. However, the authors acknowledge limitations, emphasizing the need for further research to explore the effectiveness of text-audio transfer and the practical utility of these models in real-world healthcare workflows. They suggest that more realistic evaluations will be crucial for assessing the capabilities of omni models in health settings.

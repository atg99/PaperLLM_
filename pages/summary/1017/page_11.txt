In summary, the paper presents a comprehensive analysis of the performance of the CNN14 model across various audio classification tasks, including acoustic scene classification, automatic audio tagging, sound category prediction, music genre classification, and emotion recognition in speech. The fine-tuned version of CNN14 consistently outperforms models trained from scratch, indicating the effectiveness of transfer learning in this domain. The results show that while freezing layers can be beneficial, fine-tuning the entire model generally yields superior accuracy. The findings underscore the importance of dataset characteristics and the distribution of audio recordings in achieving optimal performance. The paper concludes that the fine-tuned CNN14 model achieves state-of-the-art results in several tasks, demonstrating its potential for practical applications in audio classification.

The Wavegram-CNN and Wavegram-Logmel-CNN architectures are highlighted for their effectiveness, achieving mAPs of 0.389 and 0.439 respectively, thus establishing a new state-of-the-art in the evaluated models. The complexity analysis in Table XII indicates that while more complex systems like ResNet54 have higher computational demands, the proposed Wavegram systems maintain a balance between performance and computational efficiency.

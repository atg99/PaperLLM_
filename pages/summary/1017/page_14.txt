The references provided cover a range of studies and advancements in the field of audio classification and event detection, particularly focusing on weakly supervised learning methods. Key contributions include the exploration of various neural network architectures and learning techniques aimed at improving the recognition of transient sound events, as highlighted by Chou et al. and Wang et al. Other notable contributions include the application of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to raw audio waveforms, as explored by Dai et al. and Lee et al., and the introduction of data augmentation techniques like SpecAugment and mixup to improve generalization in models, as discussed by Park et al. and Zhang et al.

Overall, these studies reflect a growing emphasis on leveraging deep learning techniques and innovative data handling approaches to advance the capabilities of audio recognition systems, particularly in scenarios with limited labeled data.

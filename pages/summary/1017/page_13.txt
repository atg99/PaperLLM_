The paper discusses audio classification, particularly focusing on various sounds and musical instruments, and the performance of different tagging systems. It provides a comprehensive list of audio categories, including musical instruments like bagpipes, harmonica, and drums, as well as environmental sounds such as sirens, thunder, and animal noises. The classification systems evaluated include CNN14 and MobileNetV1, with their average precision (AP) performances illustrated through curves, indicating the effectiveness of each model in tagging audio clips. The study emphasizes the importance of label quality and the quantity of training clips in improving classification accuracy. It references significant works in audio classification and deep learning, highlighting advancements in techniques like transfer learning and attention models that enhance music and audio tagging tasks. The paper ultimately aims to contribute to the growing field of audio analysis by providing insights into class-wise performance and methodologies for better audio classification.

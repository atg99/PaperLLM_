(2) We present a novel architecture called Wavegram-Logmel-CNN, which utilizes both log-mel spectrograms and waveform inputs. (3) Our best-performing PANN achieves a mean average precision (mAP) of 0.439 on AudioSet tagging, surpassing the previous best of 0.392. (4) We demonstrate the effectiveness of PANNs across six different audio pattern recognition tasks, achieving state-of-the-art results in several cases. (5) The source code and pretrained models are made publicly available, encouraging further research and application in the field. This work highlights the potential of large-scale pretraining in enhancing audio pattern recognition systems and addresses the gap in research on large-scale audio datasets compared to advancements in computer vision and natural language processing.

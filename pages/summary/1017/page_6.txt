The study utilized the AudioSet dataset, which contains 527 sound classes and over 2 million audio clips sourced from YouTube. The authors downloaded 94% of the training clips and 93% of the balanced subset while ensuring audio consistency by converting clips to monophonic format and resampling. The CNN14 system was trained using log mel spectrograms derived from the audio waveforms, employing a specific STFT configuration and a batch size of 32 over 600,000 iterations.

Evaluation metrics included mean average precision (mAP), mean area under the curve (mAUC), and d-prime, which reflect the system's performance in tagging audio events. The results demonstrated significant improvements over previous models, highlighting the effectiveness of the proposed CNN14 architecture in audio classification tasks.

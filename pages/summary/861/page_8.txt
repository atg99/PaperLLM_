The paper presents Self-Attention Generative Adversarial Networks (SAGAN) and evaluates its performance against state-of-the-art GAN models, specifically AC-GAN and SNGAN-projection, in generating class-conditional images on the ImageNet dataset. The comparison reveals that SAGAN achieves a significantly higher Inception Score of 52.52 and lower FID scores of 18.65, indicating improved image quality and diversity. The results are presented in a table, illustrating the superiority of SAGAN over the other models in both metrics. Additionally, example images generated by SAGAN across different classes are shown, with intra FID scores highlighting the effectiveness of this approach in producing high-quality images.

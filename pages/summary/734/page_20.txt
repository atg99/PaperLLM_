The LN_Resnet architecture consists of a series of MLPResNet blocks with a hidden dimension size of 256 and utilizes three blocks for implementation. The input layer is followed by a dense layer that transforms the input into the hidden dimension. Each block includes dropout and layer normalization, followed by activation functions and additional dense layers to compute the output. The architecture culminates in a final dense layer that outputs the action dimension.

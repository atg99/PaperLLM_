The paper discusses the optimization of a convex objective function concerning the value function V(s). It outlines the derivation of V(s) through a series of mathematical transformations, ultimately leading to the expression for V(s) as related to the exponential of the expected value of action distributions in a given state. 

Furthermore, the paper addresses the Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence between the exponential implicit policy and the behavior distribution. It demonstrates that this divergence is influenced by the advantage function and the temperature hyperparameter, indicating a relationship between the policies.

In the experimental section, the authors detail their implementation based on the JAX framework and provide specifics regarding the hardware used, including Titan X GPUs. They describe the training process for the critic and the diffusion behavior policy, noting the number of gradient updates and the stability of the critic learning. The results are compared to previous benchmarks in offline reinforcement learning, ensuring consistency in reward standardization across different tasks for fair evaluation. 

Overall, the paper contributes to the understanding of value function optimization and policy divergence in the context of offline reinforcement learning.

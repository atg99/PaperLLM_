The paper presents training curves for various locomotion and antmaze tasks, illustrating the performance of a single hyperparameter variant of the IDQL algorithm. The data is represented through averaged normalized scores plotted against gradient steps for different environments, including halfcheetah, hopper, walker2d, and various antmaze configurations. 

Key observations include the progression of scores over gradient steps, indicating the effectiveness of the IDQL approach across diverse tasks. The training curves demonstrate consistent improvement in performance with increased gradient steps, suggesting that the IDQL variant effectively optimizes learning in both locomotion and maze environments. 

Overall, the figures highlight that IDQL shows promising results in enhancing the agent's capabilities across multiple challenges in reinforcement learning settings.

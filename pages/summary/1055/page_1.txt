The paper titled "Explainable AI: current status and future directions" by Prashant Gohel, Priyanka Singh, and Manoranjan Mohanty discusses the evolving field of Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI). XAI aims to provide transparency in AI decision-making processes, addressing the limitations of traditional AI systems, which often function as black boxes. This lack of transparency can be problematic, especially in critical applications such as healthcare, defense, law enforcement, and autonomous vehicles, where understanding the rationale behind AI decisions is essential for building trust and ensuring safety.

The authors provide an overview of various XAI techniques, examining their advantages and limitations across different multimedia formats, including text, image, audio, and video. They emphasize that XAI is necessary for answering "wh" questions—such as "why," "when," and "where"—that traditional AI cannot address. The paper highlights the importance of explainability in applications like medical diagnostics, where professionals need to comprehend the basis of AI conclusions to avoid potentially life-threatening errors.

In conclusion, the paper outlines current XAI techniques and suggests future directions for research, reinforcing the significance of developing transparent AI systems to enhance trust and accountability in AI applications.

The paper by Gohel et al. discusses the current status and future directions of Explainable AI (XAI). It highlights the importance of XAI in providing transparency, trust, bias understanding, and fairness in AI systems. The authors emphasize that XAI aims to answer critical questions regarding AI outputs, such as why and how certain decisions are made, and when failures may occur.

Key objectives of XAI include:

1. **Transparency and Informativeness**: XAI enhances transparency by offering justifications that are understandable to non-experts. This transparency is essential for assessing model performance and preventing vulnerabilities arising from false training, which can lead to unethical outcomes.

2. **Trust and Confidence**: Trust in AI technologies is bolstered by logical and scientific justifications for predictions, making users more likely to rely on AI-generated conclusions.

3. **Bias Understanding and Fairness**: XAI addresses the bias-variance trade-off in AI models, promoting fairness and helping mitigate bias during the interpretation of predictions.

The paper outlines various scopes of XAI, which are as broad as the field of AI itself, including applications in natural language processing, healthcare, engineering, and defense. Specific examples include:

- **Data Protection**: Compliance with regulations like the EU's "right to explanation" for XAI algorithms.
- **Medical Applications**: Utilizing XAI for diagnosing conditions based on medical records and imaging.
- **Defense**: The role of XAI in automated systems and real-time combat support.
- **Banking**: The application of XAI in fraud detection and reducing false positives in transactions.

Additionally, XAI techniques are classified into two categories: transparent methods, which are straightforward to interpret (e.g., decision trees, linear regression), and post-hoc methods, which provide explanations for more complex models.

Overall, the paper underscores the critical role of XAI in making AI systems more understandable, trustworthy, and fair across various domains.

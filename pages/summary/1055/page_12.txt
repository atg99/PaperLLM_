The paper by Gohel et al. discusses the current status and future directions of Explainable AI (XAI). It emphasizes various techniques used in XAI across different domains, including video and audio. 

In the video analysis section, the authors highlight local optimum justifications as effective methods, particularly noting the success of Local Interpretable Model Explanations (LIME) for providing local explanations. LRP (Layer-wise Relevance Propagation) is also mentioned as a significant technique that relies on understanding a neural network's internal architecture to offer pixel-wise relevance for decisions made by the model. LRP requires that the relevance differences converge at the final layer and that neuron relevance is the sum of incoming differences.

For audio, the paper indicates that this area is still underexplored. It mentions the conversion of audio waves to text, after which NLP-based XAI techniques can be applied. The authors point out that more research is needed to enhance explainability in this domain.

Overall, the paper outlines the importance of XAI techniques like LIME and LRP in providing insights into model decisions across various data types, while also indicating areas for further research, particularly in audio applications.

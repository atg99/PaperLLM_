The paper discusses the design of a control law for a Piecewise Affine (PMA) system to ensure its stability. It introduces a state-feedback control law that combines parametric expressions with linear feedback to stabilize the system, particularly in cases where it is open-loop unstable. The control law is structured to be easily implemented in real-time applications using look-up tables.

Theorem 6 outlines sufficient conditions for designing the proposed controller for nonlinear systems, specifying the existence of various symmetric matrices and control input vertices that must satisfy specific matrix inequalities. These inequalities are crucial for ensuring the asymptotic stability of the PMA system and establishing a piecewise quadratic function as a Lyapunov function for the closed-loop system.

The introduction of slack matrices in the conditions reduces conservatism, allowing for a better exploitation of the piecewise nature of PMA systems. The design conditions involve bilinear matrix inequalities (BMIs), which require careful consideration to avoid issues like sliding modes and chattering phenomena by maintaining uniformity in the control input across associated conditions.

This paper discusses fuzzy model-based approaches to analyze nonlinear systems, focusing on Takagi-Sugeno (T-S) fuzzy systems. The T-S fuzzy model is characterized by a set of inference rules that relate fuzzy premise variables to state-space matrices. The system is expressed in a compact form, incorporating normalized membership functions that satisfy specific convex properties.

The paper emphasizes the assumption that the premise variables often consist of a subset of the system states. It also notes the equivalence of the T-S fuzzy model and the original nonlinear system under certain conditions.

In terms of stability analysis, the paper introduces a Lyapunov-based approach. It establishes that the equilibrium of the T-S fuzzy system is globally asymptotically stable if a common positive definite matrix can be found that satisfies a specific matrix inequality. This result builds on previous findings in the literature and provides a framework for stability assessment in T-S fuzzy systems.

This collection of references focuses on various aspects of stability analysis and control design for Takagi-Sugeno (T-S) fuzzy systems. Key contributions include the development of relaxed conditions for stability, the use of Lyapunov functions, and the exploration of membership functions and their derivatives in stability assessments. Notable works emphasize the application of Linear Matrix Inequalities (LMI) in control design, comparing different approaches to improve stability conditions while reducing conservativeness. The research covers methods for both continuous-time and discrete-time systems, highlighting advancements in fuzzy control strategies and their practical implications in nonlinear system management. Additionally, several studies propose innovative techniques for observer-based control and output feedback design, demonstrating the versatility of T-S fuzzy models in complex control scenarios. Overall, the references reflect a significant body of work aimed at enhancing the understanding and implementation of fuzzy systems in control engineering.

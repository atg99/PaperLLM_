This paper discusses the stability analysis of Piecewise Affine (PMA) control systems using Lyapunov functions. The authors build on previous work that established conditions for stability through parametric expressions and state-space representations, specifically referencing the contributions of Taniguchi and Sugeno, who proposed a piecewise Lyapunov function. This function serves as an approximation of more complex Lyapunov functions, aiming to mitigate the conservativeness often found in Takagi-Sugeno (T-S) fuzzy theory.

The paper highlights the limitations of existing methods, which predominantly rely on nonlinear matrix inequalities that can complicate numerical solutions and are primarily applicable to second-order PMA systems. The conventional state-space representation of PMA systems poses additional challenges due to its piecewise polytopic nature, making theoretical analyses difficult and limiting extensions to higher-dimensional systems.

Recent advancements have shown that utilizing parametric expressions can simplify the stability analysis of PMA systems, allowing for a more effective framework that is applicable to systems of any order. The authors propose a piecewise quadratic Lyapunov function, parameterized to accommodate different regions of the state space, thereby enhancing the stability analysis framework for general PMA systems. This approach opens new avenues for research and application within the fuzzy control community, addressing the previously noted drawbacks and expanding the potential for PMA control systems.

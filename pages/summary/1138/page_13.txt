The paper discusses the parametric expression of Piecewise Multilinear Approximation (PMA) systems, particularly focusing on their stability analysis and representation. The parametric expression is derived from the state-space partition and involves the computation of weights through normalized membership functions and multiplicative calculations. The authors note that triangular membership functions (MFs) are predominantly used in PMA systems due to their effectiveness in fuzzy control applications, although other types of MFs could also be utilized.

For a specific case with two dimensions, the paper illustrates how the PMA model can be represented in state-space form, demonstrating strict equivalence between different representations. The analysis emphasizes that PMA systems can approximate smooth nonlinear systems and are amenable to implementation via look-up tables, making them practical for industries like automotive and aerospace engineering.

Despite the advantages of PMA systems, the paper highlights a gap in rigorous stability analysis prior to 1999. The authors refer to Sugeno's work, which laid the groundwork for a stability framework using quadratic Lyapunov methods, ultimately leading to necessary and sufficient stability conditions for PMA systems. This theoretical foundation is crucial for advancing the understanding and application of PMA systems in control engineering.

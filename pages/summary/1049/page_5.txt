The paper discusses the significance of Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) in enhancing decision-making for end-users. It emphasizes that XAI fosters trust, transparency, and understanding of bias and fairness in AI systems. Trustability, defined as the measure of user confidence in a model's operations, is crucial for various stakeholders, including experts and policymakers. The paper argues that providing explanations for decisions is essential to build trust in AI, especially as society transitions to AI-driven environments.

Additionally, XAI plays a vital role in improving the understanding of model bias and fairness. Bias in deep learning is described as the tendency of a model to favor certain data subsets due to inherent biases in data collection or flaws in the learning process. By employing XAI techniques, users can better understand the behavior of models across different datasets, which can lead to the development of more robust and fair AI systems. The paper also includes a timeline of significant advancements in XAI algorithms, categorizing them by methodology and usage, indicating the evolution and diversity of approaches in the field.

3) Addresses Bias and Fairness: The ethical implications of AI systems necessitate a focus on bias and fairness. XAI techniques can help identify biased outcomes by revealing how different features influence predictions. This transparency allows for the detection of unfair practices and promotes equitable treatment across diverse populations. Ensuring fairness in AI is essential to maintain social equity and uphold ethical standards in automated decision-making.

The survey highlights the necessity of explainable AI (XAI) in enhancing the interpretability of deep learning models. It emphasizes that while deep learning models can achieve high accuracy, their complexity often obscures their decision-making processes. By defining key concepts such as interpretability, interpretation, explanation, and the distinctions between white-box and black-box models, the paper lays the groundwork for understanding the importance of XAI. The discussion on trust, transparency, and fairness underscores the critical role of XAI in the ethical deployment of AI technologies across various sectors.

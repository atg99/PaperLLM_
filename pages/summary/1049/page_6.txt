XAI allows for tracing output predictions back to input features, helping to identify correlations that can inform fairness in AI systems. The paper emphasizes the importance of choosing appropriate explanation methods to enhance interpretability and reduce bias in machine learning models.

Local explanations are critical, especially in scenarios like medical decision-making, where practitioners seek to understand the rationale behind model predictions. These explanations focus on individual data instances, utilizing various techniques such as heatmaps, rule-based methods, and feature importance matrices. Recent advancements have introduced attribution maps and game-theory based models, providing nuanced insights into feature contributions, with positive values indicating an increase in output class probability and negative values indicating a decrease.

The concept of Activation Maximization is highlighted as a method for interpreting feature importance in Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs). This approach focuses on identifying input patterns that maximize the activation of specific hidden units within the network, posing an optimization problem to derive an explanation map from the learned features. Overall, the paper lays the groundwork for understanding the intricacies of model interpretability and the ongoing development of XAI methodologies.

The paper presents a novel deep RNN framework designed for visual sequential applications, emphasizing the CBM structure to effectively manage both temporal and representation flows. The authors introduce a Temporal Dropout (TD) strategy to facilitate training and improve generalization. They also propose an Overlap Coherence Training Scheme to address the resource demands of deep RNN models, allowing for shorter sequence lengths while maintaining temporal coherence.

The study includes experiments demonstrating the framework's superiority over conventional shallow RNNs, with significant enhancements in action recognition, anticipation, and video prediction tasks. The results indicate that the CBM structure and the implemented training schemes contribute to improved performance metrics, such as higher IoU values and lower training loss, especially in deeper models. Additionally, the analysis of merge functions reveals that the production setting is more effective than addition in extracting spatial features beneficial for video classification.

The findings underscore the framework's robustness, validated through various metrics and comparisons, and highlight the effectiveness of the proposed methodologies in optimizing deep RNNs for visual tasks.

The Overlap Coherence Training Scheme addresses the computational challenges associated with deep RNNs. By utilizing overlapping sequences during training, we can minimize redundant computations and reduce memory usage, making it feasible to train deeper architectures. This method not only improves efficiency but also enhances the model's ability to learn from long visual sequences.

Our empirical results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed deep RNN framework across various visual sequence tasks. With 15 layers, our architecture outperforms traditional shallow RNN models, achieving over 11% relative improvement in accuracy on datasets such as Kinetics, UCF-101, and HMDB-51 for video classification. Additionally, when integrated into Polygon-RNN, our deep CBM increases performance by 14.7%. For video future prediction, our framework enhances the performance of state-of-the-art shallow models by 2.4% in PSNR and SSIM metrics.

In conclusion, the proposed deep RNN framework with the Context Bridge Module and Overlap Coherence Training Scheme effectively balances representation and temporal flows, allowing for deeper architectures that are easier to train and yielding significant performance improvements in visual sequential tasks.

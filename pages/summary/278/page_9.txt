The findings indicate that increasing the convolution kernel size improves the model's ability to capture longer temporal dependencies, which is critical for accurate traffic flow forecasting. 

The ablation studies conducted reveal that the STTN model significantly outperforms both STGCN and autoregressive methods in terms of prediction accuracy, particularly in long-term forecasting scenarios. The introduction of spatial and temporal transformers allows for the modeling of dynamic spatial dependencies and long-range temporal dependencies, enhancing overall forecasting performance.

In summary, the results demonstrate that the STTN architecture, with its innovative use of transformers, effectively addresses the challenges of traffic flow prediction by leveraging both local and global spatial dependencies as well as long-range temporal relationships. The findings underscore the importance of these design choices in achieving superior forecasting accuracy over traditional models.

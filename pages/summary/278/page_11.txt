This paper presents a novel approach using Spatial-Temporal Transformer Networks (STTN) to enhance long-term traffic flow prediction. The study evaluates various configurations of STTN, focusing on the number of hidden layers, attention heads, and positional embeddings, as shown in Table 6. Results indicate that increasing the model's capacity, particularly the hidden layers of the spatial transformer, significantly improves prediction accuracy. The findings suggest that multi-head attention is crucial for capturing complex relationships in traffic data, especially for long-term forecasting. Additionally, the integration of spatial and temporal positional embeddings further boosts performance.

In conclusion, the proposed STTN effectively models diverse spatial dependencies and captures long-range temporal dependencies, demonstrating superior performance in real-world datasets. The authors acknowledge the potential for the spatial transformer to be applied in dynamic graph feature learning in future work.

The paper presents a novel Spatial-Temporal Transformer Network (STTN) designed for traffic forecasting by effectively modeling spatial and temporal dependencies. The architecture consists of multiple stacked spatial-temporal blocks, each comprising a spatial transformer and a temporal transformer, which work together to extract relevant features from traffic data.

In the overall architecture, the spatial-temporal blocks take as input a 3-D tensor representing traffic features across multiple nodes and time steps. The spatial transformer focuses on extracting spatial features using a graph adjacency matrix, while the temporal transformer processes these features over time. Residual connections are employed to enhance stability during training, enabling the output from one block to inform the next.

The prediction layer utilizes two convolutional layers to aggregate the learned spatial-temporal features and generate multi-step forecasts for future traffic conditions. The training objective is to minimize the mean absolute error between the predicted traffic speeds and the ground truth.

The spatial transformer component incorporates a positional embedding layer that adds spatial-temporal context to each node, along with fixed and dynamic graph convolution layers and a gate mechanism to fuse information effectively. This design aims to improve the accuracy of traffic predictions by leveraging both the spatial relationships between nodes and the temporal dynamics of traffic flow.

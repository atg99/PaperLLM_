The paper presents performance results for binary and multi-class classification tasks using machine learning classifiers, specifically focusing on the use of 99 features. 

In binary classification (Table 8), the results indicate that the classifiers achieve exceptionally high performance metrics across various configurations of neurons in the hidden layers, with accuracy consistently at 99.99%. Precision, recall, f1-score, true positive rate (TPR), and false positive rate (FPR) are also reported as nearly perfect, with TPR at 1.00 and FPR remaining very low, indicating effective classification with minimal errors.

For multi-class classification (Table 9), the highest testing accuracy achieved is 93.88% with 80 neurons in the hidden layers, while the highest training accuracy is 99.0%. Although the performance metrics such as precision and recall vary more significantly compared to binary classification, the results still demonstrate strong performance, particularly with 80 and 100 neurons.

Section 5.2 compares the performance of the LSTM-RNN classifier with SVM and Random Forest classifiers using 122 features (Table 10). The Random Forest classifier achieved the highest testing accuracy of 99.99%, followed by SVM at 92.90% and LSTM-RNN at 96.51%. The precision and recall for Random Forest were perfect at 1.00, while LSTM-RNN and SVM showed slightly lower but still robust values.

Overall, the findings suggest that while all classifiers performed well, Random Forest outperformed the others in both binary and multi-class scenarios, especially in terms of accuracy and precision.

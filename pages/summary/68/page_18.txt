In this paper, the authors conducted a comparative analysis of different classifiers on the NSL-KDD dataset for binary and multi-class classification tasks. The classifiers evaluated included Support Vector Machine (SVM), Random Forest, and Long Short-Term Memory Recurrent Neural Network (LSTM-RNN). The results indicated that the proposed LSTM-RNN model achieved a training accuracy of 99.99% and testing accuracy of 99.91% for binary classification, outperforming SVM, while Random Forest exhibited the highest accuracy overall. In multi-class classification, the LSTM-RNN achieved 93.88% accuracy with a 99-feature set, showing superior performance compared to SVM and Random Forest.

The study highlighted the effectiveness of integrating Genetic Algorithms (GA) with LSTM-RNN, which led to a 10% improvement in accuracy over Random Forest in multi-class classification. The authors concluded that LSTM-RNN is well-suited for large datasets, although its advantages are less pronounced with smaller datasets. Future work is suggested to include testing on GPU systems to improve training times, utilizing more recent datasets like UNSW-NB15, and developing a framework for real-time network data collection. The authors also aim to compare the performance of the LSTM-RNN with GA against other deep learning methodologies.

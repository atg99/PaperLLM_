Mutation: To enhance population diversity and avoid premature convergence, the GA performs mutation by flipping the value of attributes in the input individuals. In this study, the mutation probability for each attribute is set at 0.05.

In summary, the paper discusses the application of a genetic algorithm (GA) for optimal feature selection in the development of a Network Intrusion Detection System (NIDS). The GA was employed to identify a subset of 99 relevant features from an original set of 122 features, effectively removing redundant and irrelevant data that could reduce classification accuracy. The process involved several steps: initializing a population of feature sets, assigning fitness values based on logistic regression model performance, selecting individuals for the next generation, and applying crossover and mutation techniques to refine the feature sets. Ultimately, the optimal feature set was prepared for training and testing datasets to enhance classification accuracy and minimize misclassification rates.

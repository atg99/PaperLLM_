The NSL-KDD dataset is an improved version of the KDDCup’99 datasets, designed to facilitate unbiased classification results by removing redundant records. This refinement enhances the detection rate for various attack types. The dataset includes KDDTrain +, a comprehensive training dataset with attack-type labels and difficulty levels, and KDDTest +, a complete testing dataset with similar attributes.

In an experiment referenced, researchers created three subsets from the training set, each containing fifty thousand records. They utilized 21 learning machines, previously trained on these subsets, to label the records in both the training and testing sets. Each record received 21 predicted labels, with the difficulty level indicating how many of these predictions were correct, ranging from 0 to a maximum of 21.

The dataset categorizes attacks into major types, including Denial of Service (DoS), User to Root (U2R), Remote to Local (R2L), and Probing, with specific subcategories listed. The total instances by attack type are detailed, highlighting the prevalence of certain attacks, such as the 41,214 instances of Neptune and 67,343 normal instances, demonstrating the dataset's diversity in attack representation.

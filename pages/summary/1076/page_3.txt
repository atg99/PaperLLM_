The paper discusses the advancements and applications of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) in deep learning, emphasizing their capacity to address complex issues that traditional Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) could not. It highlights the automatic feature extraction capabilities of CNNs, which contribute significantly to their success in computer vision tasks, such as object categorization, detection, tracking, and recognition. The review aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of CNN's essential components and architectures, from AlexNet to high-resolution networks, making it accessible for researchers and students.

The authors compile and analyze current deep learning-based object detection methods, emphasizing the importance of large labeled datasets for training effective CNN models. They also focus on the significance of object identification and tracking in video surveillance systems. The review methodologically examines key research articles published between 2017 and 2022, prioritizing reputable sources like IEEE, Elsevier, MDPI, ACM, and Springer, along with selected papers from ArXiv.

The primary contributions of the study include an in-depth analysis of CNN features, networks, and algorithms, alongside a comparison of various object detection methods to aid in selecting appropriate approaches for specific applications. The paper ultimately serves as a resource for developers and academics to enhance their understanding of CNNs, detailing the relevant theories, concepts, and state-of-the-art architectures in the field.

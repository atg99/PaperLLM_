The paper presents a novel approach to motion planning for autonomous vehicles by transforming the OpenAI GPT-3.5 model into a reliable motion planner. Motion planning is essential for ensuring safe and comfortable driving trajectories, but existing methods often lack generalization in novel scenarios. The authors propose to reframe motion planning as a language modeling problem, utilizing the reasoning capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs). By representing the inputs and outputs of the planner as language tokens, the LLM generates driving trajectories through natural language descriptions of coordinates.

The proposed method includes a prompting-reasoning-finetuning strategy that enhances the LLM's numerical reasoning abilities, allowing it to articulate precise trajectory coordinates and its decision-making process in natural language. Evaluations conducted on the nuScenes dataset demonstrate the effectiveness, generalization ability, and interpretability of this GPT-based motion planner.

The paper highlights the limitations of existing rule-based and learning-based motion planning approaches, which either lack robustness in extreme scenarios or sacrifice interpretability. By leveraging LLMs, the authors aim to incorporate common sense reasoning into motion planning, addressing the challenges posed by diverse driving environments.

The paper presents POPQORN, a novel framework for quantifying the robustness of Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs). Unlike previous works that focused on Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), this study addresses the unique challenges posed by RNNs, such as complex feedback architectures and sequential inputs. The authors limit their analysis to RNN-based classifiers, as defining errors in non-classification tasks is more complicated.

The key contribution is the demonstration that the output of an RNN can be bounded by two linear functions when the input sequence is perturbed within a specified radius of an lp-ball. By applying these bounds to non-linear activation functions and operations, the authors effectively decouple the non-linear aspects and derive linear bounds for the output of the network. This approach is illustrated through examples of a 2-layer vanilla RNN and a 1-layer Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) network, with complete theorems extending to general m-layer RNNs, LSTMs, and Gated Recurrent Units (GRUs) included in the appendix.

The paper defines notations crucial for understanding the framework, including the original input sequence, perturbed frames, and the RNN function. It also introduces bounding functions that help maintain the output within specified limits based on the pre-activation bounds of the network. The bounding lines are derived recursively from the output back to the input, allowing for a comprehensive robustness quantification.

Overall, POPQORN is positioned as the first systematic approach to assess the robustness of RNNs, providing a foundational methodology for future research in this area.

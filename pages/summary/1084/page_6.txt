The paper "POPQORN: Quantifying Robustness of Recurrent Neural Networks" discusses a systematic approach to quantifying the robustness of various types of Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs), including Vanilla RNNs, LSTMs, and GRUs. The robustness quantification is achieved through closed-form formulas that define upper bounds (Uj) and lower bounds (Lj) for the output of the networks given certain input perturbations. For Vanilla RNNs, the upper and lower bounds are expressed in terms of the network parameters and input sequences, incorporating contributions from weights and biases. Similar formulations are provided for LSTMs and GRUs, demonstrating the generalizability of the approach.

The paper further introduces a robustness quantification algorithm. This algorithm establishes that for any input sequence within a specified perturbation radius, there exist fixed bounds such that the output of the RNN lies between these bounds. The authors formalize the objective of maximizing the lower bound for untargeted attacks while also addressing targeted attacks, providing a mathematical framework to find the largest possible lower bounds for both cases.

The methodology relies on Holder's inequality to derive a closed-form global upper bound and emphasizes the importance of these bounds in assessing the vulnerability of RNNs to adversarial attacks. Detailed proofs and definitions supporting these findings are provided in the appendix, ensuring the rigor of the proposed robustness quantification framework.

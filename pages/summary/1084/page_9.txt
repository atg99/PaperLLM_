The paper "POPQORN: Quantifying Robustness of Recurrent Neural Networks" discusses a methodology for assessing the robustness of recurrent neural networks (RNNs) against adversarial attacks. The authors acknowledge contributions from Zhuolun Leon He and recognize financial support from various research grants, including those from SenseTime Group and the Hong Kong Research Grants Council.

The references cited in the paper encompass a range of studies focused on adversarial examples and robustness verification techniques for neural networks. Notable works include frameworks for certifying the robustness of convolutional neural networks, methods for generating adversarial examples across different modalities, and approaches for formal verification of classifiers against adversarial manipulations. The references emphasize the growing concern about the vulnerability of neural networks and the importance of developing robust defenses.

Key contributions of the paper include introducing the POPQORN framework, which quantifies RNN robustness by evaluating their performance in the presence of adversarial perturbations. The authors argue for the necessity of such evaluations to ensure the reliability of RNNs in real-world applications, highlighting the implications for security and performance in machine learning systems.

Overall, the paper aims to advance the understanding of RNN robustness and provide a systematic approach for future research in adversarial machine learning.

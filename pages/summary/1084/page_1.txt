In the introduction, the paper emphasizes the increasing reliance on deep learning across various fields and the associated risks from adversarial attacks. It categorizes existing robustness evaluation methods into attack-based approaches, which measure the effectiveness of adversarial examples, and verification-based approaches, which seek to establish lower bounds for perturbations that can mislead networks. However, it notes that most verification techniques are tailored for feed-forward networks and do not adequately address the complexities of RNNs.

The proposed POPQORN framework is designed to overcome these challenges by leveraging linear approximations to manage the nonlinear interactions inherent in RNNs. The authors assert that their method not only quantifies robustness effectively but also offers insights by analyzing robustness at individual time steps, which is particularly relevant for tasks involving sequential data.

Overall, the paper contributes to the understanding of RNN robustness by providing a novel algorithm that extends existing verification methods to recurrent architectures, paving the way for more reliable deployment of neural networks in critical applications.

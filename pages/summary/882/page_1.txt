In the paper "CLIPstyler: Image Style Transfer with a Single Text Condition," the authors Gihyun Kwon and Jong Chul Ye present a novel approach to image style transfer that eliminates the need for reference style images. Traditional neural style transfer methods require users to provide specific style images to achieve the desired transformation in content images. However, the authors recognize that users may often want to apply styles based solely on their textual descriptions, without having any visual reference.

To address this gap, the authors propose a framework that utilizes the pre-trained text-image embedding model CLIP. This approach allows for style transfer using only a single text condition, facilitating a more intuitive and flexible user experience. A key innovation in their method is the introduction of a patch-wise text-image matching loss, which is enhanced by multiview augmentations. This technique effectively captures and transfers realistic textures that correspond to the semantic meanings conveyed by the text descriptions.

The experimental results presented in the paper demonstrate that the proposed method successfully achieves image style transfer, yielding content images that maintain their spatial structure while incorporating textures that align with the provided text. Overall, the authors' work represents a significant advancement in the field of style transfer, broadening its applicability to scenarios where visual references are not available.

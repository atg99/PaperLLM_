The references cited pertain to advancements in image synthesis using generative adversarial networks (GANs) and wavelet transforms. The first reference discusses wavelet transforms in the context of computer vision, emphasizing their utility in image processing tasks. The next two references focus on the StackGAN framework, which enables the generation of photo-realistic images from text descriptions. The original StackGAN paper outlines the methodology and results, while StackGAN++ builds upon this foundation to enhance image quality and realism. Both studies contribute significantly to the field of machine learning and computer vision by demonstrating the capabilities of GANs in transforming textual input into high-quality visual output.

This appendix presents proofs for two lemmas referenced in Section V of the paper. 

Lemma 3 asserts that if a sequence of values v1 through vl is ordered such that v1 is greater than or equal to the subsequent values, then the maximum absolute value among these values is at least half the difference between v1 and vl. The proof demonstrates that the maximum must occur at either v1 or vl, and it establishes that the inequality holds through straightforward comparisons of the values.

Lemma 4 deals with an invertible matrix Q and a vector x satisfying specific conditions, including that the inner product of e and x equals zero and that certain bounds on the components of Qx are met. The lemma concludes that the minimum of the absolute values of the largest and smallest components of Qx is bounded by a function of these values and the inverse of Q. The proof proceeds by examining the implications of the established inequalities and leads to a contradiction if the proposed bounds do not hold, thereby affirming the lemma's claim.

These lemmas contribute to the theoretical framework of the paper, providing essential inequalities that support subsequent arguments.

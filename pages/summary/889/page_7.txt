The paper presents a user study comparing various deep models for artistic style transfer, highlighting the effectiveness of a new compressed model against the original VGG-19 and a FP-slimmed model. Table 1 shows user preference, with the new model achieving 42.7% satisfaction for WCT, outperforming both FP-slimmed and AdaIN models significantly. Table 2 evaluates style distances, indicating that the stylized images from both the original and the new model are closer to the style images than those produced by the FP-slimmed model, supporting user preference.

The new model is notably efficient, being 15.5 times smaller and 3.6 times faster than the original while maintaining high-resolution output, capable of producing ultra-resolution results on a single 12GB GPU. The ablation study explores two proposed sub-schemes: collaborative distillation and linear embedding. Results indicate that both methods independently yield comparable quality to the original model, with collaborative distillation providing sharper images with less distortion.

Further evaluation of the new model on the AdaIN framework demonstrates its ability to produce visually comparable results while the FP-slimmed model shows significant degradation. The paper concludes that the proposed compressed model effectively maintains artistic integrity while significantly reducing model size and enhancing processing speed, thus making it suitable for mobile applications.

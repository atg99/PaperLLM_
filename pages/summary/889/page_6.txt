The paper discusses the performance of three models for stylizing images: the original VGG-19, FP-slimmed VGG-19, and a newly proposed compressed VGG-19 model. The comparison highlights two main issues with the FP-slimmed model: a reduced color diversity in the stylized images and the presence of checkerboard artifacts. 

To assess user preferences for the stylized images, a user study was conducted where participants compared pairs of images generated by the three models. The study involved 600 valid votes and revealed that the images produced by the compressed model were rated the highest on average, confirming the qualitative observations made in the visual comparisons.

Additionally, the paper introduces a quantitative evaluation of style similarity between the stylized images and the original style images, suggesting that a successful stylization results in a closer resemblance to the style image. The findings indicate that the compressed VGG-19 model not only maintains visual quality but also improves user satisfaction compared to the other models.

The paper presents a novel approach called Collaborative Distillation aimed at improving universal style transfer methods while addressing the limitations posed by large model sizes. Traditional universal style transfer relies on deep Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), such as VGG-19, which are effective but cumbersome for processing ultra-resolution images due to memory constraints. 

The authors propose a knowledge distillation technique that leverages the unique collaborative relationship between encoder-decoder pairs in neural style transfer models. This method reduces the size of convolutional filters while maintaining performance. To tackle the issue of feature size mismatches during distillation, they introduce a linear embedding loss that helps the student network learn a linear representation of the teacher's features.

Extensive experiments validate the effectiveness of this approach across various universal style transfer techniques, including WCT and AdaIN, demonstrating a significant model size reduction by 15.5 times. Notably, the paper reports the successful application of ultra-resolution universal style transfer (over 40 megapixels) on a single 12GB GPU for the first time. Additionally, the method shows versatility across different stylization paradigms, indicating its broad applicability. The authors provide access to their code and trained models to facilitate further research.

The paper addresses the challenges of universal neural style transfer (NST), which integrates content images with styles from reference images using deep neural networks like VGG-19. Although VGG-19 achieves impressive results, its large model size limits practical applications, particularly on devices with constrained memory. While model compression techniques have advanced, most focus on high-level tasks, leaving low-level vision tasks like NST underexplored.

The authors propose a novel knowledge distillation approach that leverages the collaborative relationship between encoders and decoders in NST models. This method involves a two-step compression process: first, training a collaborator network to serve as the decoder, then replacing the large encoder with a smaller one while ensuring compatibility. The small encoder learns a linear embedding of the teacher’s output to address dimensionality mismatches.

Key contributions include the introduction of a new knowledge distillation method for NST, the resolution of feature dimension issues via linear embedding, and extensive experimentation demonstrating significant parameter reduction (15.5 times) while maintaining or improving visual quality. Notably, this approach enables ultra-resolution (40 megapixels) style transfer on a single GPU, marking a significant advancement in the field.

The paper presents statistics and experimental results for remote sensing object detection (RSOD) and aerial image object detection (UCS-AOD) datasets. The RSOD dataset includes classes such as Aircraft, Oil Tank, Overpass, and Playground, with detailed statistics on the number of images and instances for both training and testing sets. The UCS-AOD dataset comprises Aircraft and Car classes, with similar statistics provided.

The authors evaluate the accuracy and speed of their proposed approach against state-of-the-art detection models. The mean Average Precision (mAP) and frames per second (FPS) are reported for various methods on the RSOD dataset. Notably, the authors' method achieves an mAP of 88.73% with a speed of 25.8 FPS, outperforming other models like Faster RCNN and SSD in accuracy.

Additionally, the authors analyze performance based on object size, where their method again shows superior results, particularly for small, medium, and large objects, with mAP values of 87.51%, 87.93%, and 90.23%, respectively. The results indicate that their approach not only excels in accuracy but also maintains competitive speed, demonstrating its effectiveness for remote sensing applications.

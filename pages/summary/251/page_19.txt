This paper presents an improved YOLO-V3 model aimed at enhancing multi-scale remote sensing target detection. The authors address the challenge of accurately detecting small targets in complex backgrounds, which is often overlooked by existing algorithms. The proposed improvements include integrating a DenseNet for more effective feature extraction, increasing the number of detection scales from three to four, and modifying the architecture by replacing three residual units with five convolutional layers to mitigate gradient fading.

The results are validated through experimental comparisons with YOLO-V3 and Faster RCNN on the RSOD and UCS-AOD datasets. The findings indicate that while YOLO-V3 misses several small targets and Faster RCNN shows some improvements, the authors' approach successfully detects all targets across the tested scenarios. The ablation experiments further confirm that each introduced modification significantly enhances detection accuracy. Overall, the study emphasizes the need to balance detection accuracy and speed in practical engineering applications, demonstrating that the improved YOLO-V3 model effectively meets these requirements.

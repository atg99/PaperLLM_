The paper presents an enhanced model for multi-scale remote sensing target detection, building on the YOLO-V3 framework by integrating DenseNet to improve feature extraction. The authors address the challenges posed by the diverse dimensions and dense distribution of remote sensing targets against complex backgrounds. By increasing the detection scales to four, the proposed model outperforms existing methods, including Faster-RCNN and SSD, achieving a significant improvement in mean Average Precision (mAP) from 77.10% to 88.73% on the RSOD dataset, particularly excelling in detecting small targets like aircraft. Despite these enhancements in accuracy, the model maintains a comparable detection speed to the original YOLO-V3. This research underscores the potential of combining advanced CNN architectures to enhance remote sensing applications, which require both accuracy and real-time processing capabilities.

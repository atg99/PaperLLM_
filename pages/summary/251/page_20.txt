The paper discusses advancements in a feature extraction network designed for remote sensing target detection, highlighting significant improvements in accuracy, particularly for small targets, compared to the YOLO-V3 model. The introduction of a fourth detection scale enhances detection capabilities, albeit with a slight trade-off in speed. While many YOLO-V3 modifications exist, they often struggle with complex remote sensing images. The proposed algorithm outperforms these existing methods, making it more suitable for such applications. Future research will focus on multi-receptive fields for further enhancement and on the latest YOLO-V4 model.

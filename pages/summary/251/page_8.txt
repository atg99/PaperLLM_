The paper discusses the architecture of a proposed network, particularly focusing on the structure of residual units used in the network. Table 3 details the configuration of these residual units across four stages, each comprising convolutional layers with varying filter sizes and depths. Subsequent units (RES 2nd, RES 3rd, and RES 4th) follow a similar pattern with decreasing filter sizes and depths, producing outputs of 26x26x512, 52x52x256, and 104x104x128, respectively.

The paper presents an improved YOLO-V3 approach for detecting remote sensing targets, demonstrating superior performance compared to classical algorithms, particularly in terms of mean Average Precision (mAP) and detection accuracy for small and medium-sized targets like aircraft and oil tanks. The detection speed remains comparable to YOLO-V3, which is significant given the real-time detection requirement. 

Experimental results on the UCS-AOD dataset indicate that the improved method outperforms YOLO-V3 and other state-of-the-art algorithms, especially in leak detection rates, which are notably lower for the proposed method. The results, summarized in Tables 7, 8, and 9, highlight the method's effectiveness across various target sizes and backgrounds, confirming its robustness under different conditions of illumination and target distribution.

Visual evidence from Figure 8 showcases the method's capability to accurately detect small aircraft targets in diverse scenarios, reinforcing its effectiveness for multi-scale remote sensing applications.

The paper by Kim et al. presents a detailed analysis of the data characteristics associated with selected publications in the field of medical imaging. Figure 5 illustrates the correlation between anatomical body parts and imaging modalities, the number of classes in the datasets, and a histogram depicting the quantity of medical image datasets. Figure 6 provides scatter plots that examine model performance in relation to data size, image modality, backbone model, and transfer learning type. The color keys in the figures differentiate between medical image modalities and backbone models, while various marker shapes indicate the types of transfer learning employed. This analysis highlights the interplay between these factors and their influence on model performance in medical imaging applications.

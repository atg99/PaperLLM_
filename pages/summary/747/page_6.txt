This paper provides a comprehensive overview of various applications of sequence-to-sequence (seq2seq) models, detailing the input and output sequences involved in each application. The applications include:

1. **Machine Translation**: Converts sentences from a source language to a target language (e.g., English to French).
2. **Text Summarization**: Summarizes lengthy documents into concise texts, such as generating headlines from news articles.
3. **Question Generation**: Generates relevant questions based on a given text or image.
4. **Question Answering**: Identifies answers to questions posed about a text document or image.
5. **Dialogue Generation**: Creates dialogues between two agents, such as human-robot interactions.
6. **Semantic Parsing**: Translates human-written descriptions into SQL queries.
7. **Image Captioning**: Produces captions that describe the content of images.
8. **Video Captioning**: Generates captions for video clips.
9. **Computer Vision**: Analyzes video clips to predict actions of objects.
10. **Speech Recognition**: Converts spoken language into text.
11. **Speech Synthesis**: Produces audible speech from written text.

The paper also lists key datasets used in these applications. For machine translation, the WMT’14 dataset is highlighted, while the CNN-Daily Mail dataset is noted for text summarization. Other datasets mentioned include Gigaword for headline generation and various datasets from the Document Understanding Conference (DUC) for evaluation purposes in text summarization tasks. Overall, the paper emphasizes the versatility of seq2seq models across multiple domains and their reliance on specific datasets for training and evaluation.

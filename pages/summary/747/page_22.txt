Chandan K. Reddy is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Virginia Tech. He earned his Ph.D. from Cornell University and an M.S. from Michigan State University. His research focuses on Data Mining and Machine Learning, specifically in Healthcare Analytics and Social Network Analysis. Reddy's work is supported by various organizations, including the National Science Foundation and the National Institutes of Health. He has authored over 95 peer-reviewed articles and has received multiple accolades, such as the Best Application Paper Award at the ACM SIGKDD conference in 2010 and the Best Student Paper Award at the IEEE ICDM conference in 2016. Additionally, he serves as an associate editor for the ACM Transactions on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining and was PC Co-Chair for ASONAM 2018. Reddy is a senior member of the IEEE and a life member of the ACM.

This paper discusses the integration of deep reinforcement learning (RL) techniques with sequence-to-sequence (seq2seq) models, which are widely used in various applications such as machine translation, text summarization, and image captioning. The authors highlight the traditional seq2seq framework, which consists of an encoder and a decoder, typically implemented using recurrent neural networks like Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks. 

Despite their effectiveness, seq2seq models face challenges such as exposure bias and inconsistencies between training and testing metrics. To address these issues, the paper explores the novel application of RL methods to enhance seq2seq training, emphasizing the potential of RL in improving decision-making and managing long-term dependencies in data.

The authors provide an overview of recent frameworks that combine RL and deep neural networks, aiming to guide researchers in selecting appropriate RL algorithms for their seq2seq problems. Additionally, the paper includes source code for implementing the discussed RL models, particularly for abstractive text summarization, and presents targeted experiments assessing the performance and training efficiency of these models. 

Overall, the paper aims to provide insights into the complexities and advantages of merging seq2seq models with RL methodologies, contributing to the advancement of this area of research.

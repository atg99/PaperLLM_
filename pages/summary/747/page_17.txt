We introduced an open-source library, RLS EQ2SEQ, that implements several RL methods tailored for seq2seq models. This library, accessible on GitHub, facilitates researchers in experimenting with and enhancing their models while encouraging them to share their trained models.

The core of our library is built upon the pointer-generator model, which incorporates techniques to improve training efficiency and output quality. Key features of the library include the addition of temporal and intra-decoder attention, scheduled sampling to address exposure bias, and adaptive training of the REINFORCE algorithm. We also introduced Self-Critic training and various Actor-Critic training options.

In our experiments, we pre-trained models using cross-entropy loss before transitioning to RL training. Results indicated that models utilizing scheduled sampling and end-to-end backpropagation outperformed the pointer-generator model. However, the Self-Critic Policy Gradient (SCPG) model showed only marginal improvements compared to the pointer-generator, likely due to variations in hyperparameters used in our experiments versus those in prior research.

Additionally, we analyzed training times, revealing that the pointer-generator framework requires extensive training periods, further exacerbated when using SCPG. In contrast, the Actor-Critic model demonstrated quicker convergence and superior performance based on ROUGE scores.

In conclusion, this paper highlights the advancements in seq2seq models and their integration with RL techniques, presenting our library as a valuable resource for researchers in the field.

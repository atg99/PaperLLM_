This paper discusses the integration of reinforcement learning (RL) with sequence-to-sequence (seq2seq) models to address certain challenges in training these models. It identifies a problem statement focused on training models that depend solely on their own outputs to generate results, thereby avoiding exposure bias, and optimizing directly using evaluation measures to prevent mismatches between training and testing criteria.

The authors highlight that while there have been surveys on deep reinforcement learning, they often emphasize robotics and vision applications, neglecting the potential for RL in other tasks, particularly in seq2seq models. The paper aims to summarize recent frameworks that tackle the stated problems and elucidate how RL can enhance seq2seq learning.

Key contributions include:
1. A comprehensive overview of RL methods pertinent to deep learning and seq2seq training.
2. An analysis of the challenges, advantages, and disadvantages of various RL techniques in this context.
3. Guidelines for improving specific RL methods to facilitate better training of seq2seq models.
4. An open-source library that implements these concepts for abstractive text summarization, providing various options for training seq2seq models with different RL approaches.

The organization of the paper includes a discussion of common RL techniques in Section III, an introduction to different seq2seq models in Section IV, and an exploration of real-world applications in Section V, where the implemented framework is detailed along with experimental results. Finally, Section VI concludes the work.

Overall, this paper aims to broaden the application of seq2seq models through improved RL methodologies and offers a practical framework for researchers to explore these enhancements.

The REINFORCE algorithm allows for end-to-end training by employing the reward function based on the final output, which addresses the exposure bias present in traditional seq2seq training methods. 

The paper discusses the limitations of using traditional Q-learning methods, such as DQN and DDQN, in seq2seq models, particularly due to the large action space required for tasks like text summarization. The dueling network architecture proposed by Wang et al. provides a solution by separating the value and advantage functions to improve the Q-value estimation. However, challenges remain regarding the relationship between true Q-values and network estimates.

The section highlights the ongoing research into integrating reinforcement learning with seq2seq models, focusing on how various algorithms, including REINFORCE and Policy Gradient, have been adapted to improve sequence generation tasks. It notes that while simpler methods like REINFORCE have been more commonly employed, the potential of combining Q-learning techniques with seq2seq models remains an area ripe for exploration. The paper also presents tables summarizing the policies, actions, and reward functions associated with different seq2seq applications and the specific reinforcement learning algorithms employed. 

Overall, the paper emphasizes the importance of addressing the train/test evaluation mismatch in seq2seq models and suggests that future research could yield significant advancements by effectively merging reinforcement learning strategies with sequence generation tasks.

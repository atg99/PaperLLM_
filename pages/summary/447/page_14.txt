The study presented an analysis of a proposed model, referred to as R2U-Net, which demonstrated improved performance in medical image segmentation tasks, particularly for skin cancer lesions. The model was compared to existing architectures including SegNet, U-Net, and ResU-Net. Notably, R2U-Net achieved better performance with fewer network parameters, specifically 1.07M and 13.34M parameters, compared to SegNet’s 1.73M and 14.94M parameters. This reduction in parameters led to advantages in memory usage and processing time.

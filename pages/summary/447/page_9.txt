The paper evaluates blood vessel segmentation methods using the CHASE-DB1 dataset and compares them with existing approaches. The authors specifically highlight their proposed R2U-Net model, which achieves an accuracy (AC) of approximately 0.9634, surpassing the 0.9581 achieved by Li et al. through cross-modality learning, representing a 0.53% improvement. The segmentation results from the R2U-Net model are illustrated in the figures provided. 

The paper includes a comprehensive table comparing various methods across different datasets, including DRIVE, STARE, and CHASE_DB1, detailing their sensitivity (SE), specificity (SP), accuracy (AC), and area under the curve (AUC). Notably, the R2U-Net model (with 1.07 million parameters) outperforms several traditional and deep learning-based methods in terms of accuracy on the CHASE_DB1 dataset. The results indicate the effectiveness of the R2U-Net model in medical image segmentation tasks. The bold values in the table signify the highest testing accuracy for each method, emphasizing the advancements made with the proposed approach.

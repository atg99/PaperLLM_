The proposed R2U-Net model demonstrates superior segmentation accuracy compared to existing models such as SegNet and U-Net across various datasets. The results indicate that R2U-Net effectively captures intricate details, such as the internal hole in the left lung, which other models fail to identify. Heatmaps illustrate sharper segmentation borders in ground truth compared to model outputs, with R2U-Net achieving the highest Area Under Curve (AUC) in Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC) analyses.

The study further explores the trade-off between the number of training samples and accuracy, revealing that R2U-Net consistently exhibits lower training and validation errors across multiple split ratios, except at a 0.5 validation ratio where it performs similarly to U-Net. This indicates R2U-Net's enhanced capability in feature extraction and learning during training.

Additionally, the performance of various models, including U-Net, ResU-Net, and R2U-Net, is compared based on segmentation tasks for retinal blood vessels and lung lesions. R2U-Net outperforms in key metrics such as Sensitivity (SE), Specificity (SP), Jaccard Similarity Coefficient (JSC), F1-Score, Accuracy (AC), AUC, and Dice Index (DI), highlighting its effectiveness in medical image segmentation tasks. The findings underscore the advantages of using R2U-Net for achieving precise segmentation results in medical imaging.

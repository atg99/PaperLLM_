The paper discusses the critical role of various production operators in organizing and supervising oil extraction operations, highlighting the inherent risks associated with crude oil production platforms, both offshore and onshore. Effective control and maintenance are emphasized as essential for ensuring efficient production processes, enhancing productivity, prolonging equipment lifespan, ensuring safety, and adhering to budget constraints. Control involves planning and sustained efforts to improve performance and minimize risks throughout the oil and gas supply chain. Maintenance encompasses regular inspections and strategic decision-making to implement preventive, predictive, and corrective measures.

Supply chain management (SCM) in the oil and gas industry (OGI) faces challenges, particularly in logistics and fostering teamwork and information sharing among partners. The paper suggests that improving SCM requires new collaborative principles. It also identifies a gap in the monitoring and management of information, highlighting that traditional centralized databases often fall short in effectively handling real-time data and ensuring data integrity throughout the supply chain.

The paper further explores the integration of technology in the OGI, noting that computer-aided techniques are becoming standard for enhancing business efficiency. The industry is urged to adopt new technologies that can provide distinct advantages. The demand for safety and the pursuit of intelligent fields and real-time analysis have led to increased interest in robust technologies. Autonomy is highlighted as particularly significant in high-risk environments, where rapid data processing and decision-making are beyond human capability.

The Internet of Things (IoT) is identified as a transformative force within the OGI, with applications such as wearable technology for real-time assistance, drones for pipeline inspections, and smart pipelines for damage detection. These advancements contribute to enhanced safety and operational efficiency in oilfield operations.

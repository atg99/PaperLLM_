This paper surveys the application of machine learning (ML) and multi-agent systems (MAS) in the oil and gas industry (OGI). The OGI faces significant challenges due to its complex processes and the vast amounts of data generated by various stakeholders. Key issues include managing diverse data sources, ensuring data integrity, and deriving actionable insights to enhance efficiency, maintenance scheduling, and security against theft and fraud.

Recent advancements in artificial intelligence (AI), particularly ML, have started to play a role in addressing these challenges. MAS, as a branch of distributed AI, are well-suited for the distributed nature of OGI operations and have been successfully implemented in various fields. Despite some studies demonstrating the potential of ML and MAS to improve operational efficiency and manage supply chains, their application in the OGI remains limited. ML has primarily been used for isolated tasks, while MAS have not achieved widespread adoption among industry players.

The authors argue that further research is essential to unlock the full potential of ML and MAS in the OGI. Specifically, integrating ML into MAS could provide significant benefits and drive future advancements in the industry. The paper concludes by summarizing current efforts, identifying barriers to adoption, and proposing strategies to encourage the integration of these technologies into OGI practices.

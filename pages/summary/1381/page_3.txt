As the OGI faces increasing pressure to enhance efficiency and reduce costs, there is a growing reliance on advanced technologies. Digital transformation is becoming a focal point, with practices such as real-time data analytics, predictive maintenance, and automation being implemented to optimize operations. The integration of wireless sensor networks and nanotechnology is also being explored to improve monitoring and diagnostics in oil fields.

The paper outlines key tasks within the OGI, categorized into three phases: upstream, midstream, and downstream. The upstream phase focuses on exploration and production, while the midstream encompasses storage and transportation, though it is often overlooked. The downstream phase involves refining and distribution of oil and gas products. Each phase presents unique challenges that necessitate informed decision-making at strategic, tactical, and operational levels.

Sections 3 and 4 delve into the applications of machine learning (ML) and multi-agent systems (MAS) in OGI tasks. The discussion extends to a comparison of the two approaches, highlighting their respective advantages and potential synergy for greater integration within the industry. Section 6 addresses the challenges and future directions for ML and MAS in OGI, concluding with a summary of findings in Section 7. Overall, the paper emphasizes the importance of technological advancements in addressing the complexities and operational demands of the oil and gas sector.

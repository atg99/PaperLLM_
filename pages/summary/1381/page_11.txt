Sinha et al. proposed a multi-agent system (MAS) integrated with a co-evolutionary particle swarm optimization (PSO) method utilizing the Cauchy distribution for petroleum supply chain management. Their model optimizes resource allocation among supply and supporting agents, leading to reduced overall costs and improved inventory management at refineries.

Julka et al. introduced the Petroleum Refinery Integrated Supply Chain Modeller and Simulator (PRISMS), which employs an agent-based modeling framework to enhance decision-making during crude oil procurement. PRISMS simulates various refinery operations, facilitating analysis of policy and external changes, and aims to incorporate more detailed departmental solutions and exception handling in future iterations.

The analysis compares machine learning (ML) and MAS methodologies in performing oil and gas industry tasks. While both approaches serve decision-making purposes, ML is primarily used for predictive analytics and anomaly detection, whereas MAS focuses on automating and managing complex operational systems. This distinction underscores the complementary roles of these methodologies in optimizing supply chain performance in the oil and gas sector.

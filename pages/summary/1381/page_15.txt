The paper presents a comprehensive review of various studies and applications related to supply chain management and multi-agent systems within the oil and gas sector. It emphasizes the need for a uniform framework to address the complexities and challenges faced in this industry, particularly in supply chain management. Key concepts include the integration of machine learning and deep learning technologies, which have begun to transform operations in the sector. The application of multi-agent systems is highlighted as a promising approach for enhancing efficiency in oil field management and incident mitigation. The review covers a range of methodologies and frameworks, showcasing the potential for advanced technologies to optimize processes, improve monitoring, and facilitate better decision-making in oil and gas operations. Additionally, the paper discusses the challenges and opportunities presented by these technological advancements, underscoring the importance of continued research in this area.

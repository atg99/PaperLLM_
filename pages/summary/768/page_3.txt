The paper presents the FlexCNN architecture, which is designed for efficient computation in Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) on FPGA platforms. Key components include a dataflow architecture that processes network layers sequentially, supporting operations like depthwise and standard convolutions, ReLU activations, and pooling. The architecture leverages dynamic tiling, allowing for the adjustment of tiling factors in real-time to enhance data locality and minimize communication overheads, thereby improving overall performance.

The design incorporates a Residual Bottleneck Block (RBB) that reduces computational costs while maintaining efficiency, utilizing a combination of 1x1 and 3x3 convolutions. FlexCNN’s modules are parameterized and can be bypassed as needed, with double buffering implemented in loader and writer modules to optimize data handling. The architecture distinguishes itself from prior works by dynamically configuring tiling factors for each layer, addressing performance degradation associated with uniform tiling. The overall approach aims to maximize hardware utilization while reducing redundant computations, ultimately leading to improved performance on FPGA implementations.

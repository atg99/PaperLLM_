This reference list compiles significant works in the domain of deep learning and FPGA (Field-Programmable Gate Array) implementations. Key contributions include the development of TensorFlow as a large-scale machine learning system, advancements in real-time pose estimation using convolutional neural networks, and various FPGA-based accelerators designed to optimize deep learning tasks. Notable works include MobileNets for mobile vision applications, the GPflow library for Gaussian processes, and the introduction of high-performance FPGA architectures for deep neural networks. The references also highlight innovative approaches such as automated synthesis of systolic arrays and dataflow optimizations for CNN inference. Collectively, these studies underscore the intersection of deep learning and hardware acceleration, particularly within FPGA environments, aiming to enhance computational efficiency and performance.

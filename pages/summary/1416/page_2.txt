Our approach leverages deep learning advancements in both computer vision and natural language processing to facilitate effective interaction with remote sensing data.

The study identifies the need for tailored VQA models that can address distinct challenges associated with remote sensing imagery, such as object recognition and spatial relationships. By developing a framework that includes a visual feature extractor, a language feature extractor, a fusion mechanism, and a prediction component, we aim to bridge the gap between image analysis and natural language understanding.

We highlight the limitations of existing VQA models, particularly their generalization across different datasets, and propose a solution that enhances their applicability to remote sensing tasks. This is particularly relevant for applications assisting visually impaired users, where the model must adapt to images captured in varied conditions.

Through our proposed method and dataset, we aim to advance the field of VQA in the context of remote sensing, offering a pathway for improved user interaction and information retrieval from complex imagery. The findings suggest significant potential for VQA to transform how users engage with remote sensing data, making it more accessible and informative.

This paper presents RSVQA, a novel approach to visual question answering specifically designed for remote sensing data. The authors highlight the vast information contained in remote sensing images, which is crucial for various applications such as land cover classification and object detection. However, existing methodologies are often tailored to specific tasks, limiting accessibility and requiring expert knowledge for effective use. 

RSVQA aims to democratize access to remote sensing information by allowing users to pose questions in natural language about the content of images. This system enables users to query images for high-level information and relational dependencies among visible objects. The authors developed two datasets of image/question/answer triplets, utilizing data from OpenStreetMap to generate the necessary queries. 

The paper discusses the application of a model combining Convolutional Neural Networks for visual processing and Recurrent Neural Networks for natural language processing, which was trained on both datasets and demonstrated promising results. This work has significant implications for enhancing the usability of remote sensing data and addressing a broader range of societal challenges, particularly aligning with sustainable development goals.

The paper discusses advancements in visual question answering (VQA) applied to high-resolution satellite imagery. It presents confusion matrices for two test sets, illustrating the performance of the model in predicting various area categories. The authors highlight the need to enhance the current database to support a wider range of inquiries, suggesting that either a more complex dataset construction method or the use of human annotators could be effective solutions. The study acknowledges the financial support from CNES for the project focusing on VQA techniques in satellite imaging.

The paper references a variety of related works, indicating a comprehensive review of existing literature in remote sensing and machine learning. The references include studies on Earth observation, hyperspectral image classification, deep learning applications, and specific datasets relevant to remote sensing tasks. The authors emphasize the importance of addressing database issues to improve the system's capability to answer realistic questions effectively.

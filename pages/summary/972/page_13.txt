The paper discusses the limitations of Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR) in optimal control problems, particularly in scenarios where the system dynamics are unknown. It raises the critical question of how to effectively interact with a dynamical system to achieve control when the system matrices A and B are not known. The authors explore various styles of reinforcement learning (RL) in relation to control theory, using LQR as a foundational reference.

The section on model-based RL for LQR highlights that maximum likelihood estimation of the model parameters translates into a least squares optimization problem. The authors examine the reliability of these model estimates, particularly when the true dynamics exhibit instability. They note that inaccurate estimates can lead to suboptimal control strategies, especially if the estimates do not reflect the unstable dynamics accurately. To address this uncertainty, the authors suggest incorporating robust control techniques.

A novel approach called "Coarse-ID Control" is proposed, which integrates robust control with high-dimensional statistics. This method involves three key steps: first, utilizing supervised learning to create a coarse model of the dynamical system; second, employing statistical tools to establish probabilistic guarantees regarding the model's accuracy; and third, solving a robust optimization problem that accounts for the identified uncertainties, thereby ensuring stable control performance.

The authors emphasize that as long as the true system behavior falls within the estimated uncertainty bounds, a performant controller can be achieved. They highlight the advancements in estimation theory that provide reliable uncertainty estimates based on the number of samples observed. By combining these modern statistical techniques with traditional robust control methods, the authors aim to ensure that the performance of the controller can be bounded according to the number of observations, thereby addressing the challenges posed by unknown system dynamics in LQR.

This collection of references highlights significant advancements in the application of graph-based neural networks, particularly in traffic prediction and analysis. Key contributions include the development of spectral networks and localized filtering techniques for graph convolutional networks, as seen in the works of Bruna et al. and Defferrard et al. Kipf and Welling's research introduced semi-supervised classification methods that leverage graph structures, while various studies, such as those by Zhao et al. and Lv et al., focused on temporal aspects of traffic data for enhanced prediction accuracy.

The references also cover methodologies for urban traffic congestion mapping and state estimation on highways, showcasing a broad range of applications in intelligent transportation systems. Techniques such as dynamic edge-conditioned filters and multi-attention networks further illustrate the evolution of deep learning approaches tailored to the complexities of traffic flow and demand forecasting.

Overall, these works collectively contribute to a growing understanding of how graph-based models can be effectively utilized in real-world scenarios involving transportation networks and urban dynamics.

This document references a series of studies focused on traffic prediction using advanced machine learning techniques, particularly graph neural networks (GNNs) and convolutional neural networks (CNNs). Key contributions include:

1. Chen et al. (2019) introduced gated residual recurrent graph neural networks specifically designed for traffic prediction, demonstrating the efficacy of integrating recurrent structures with graph representations.

2. Yu et al. (2019) developed ST-UNet, a spatio-temporal U-network that models time series data structured as graphs, emphasizing the importance of spatial relationships in traffic data.

3. Wang et al. (2018) proposed dynamic spatio-temporal graph-based CNNs for traffic forecasting, which adapt to changes in traffic patterns over time.

4. Pan et al. (2019) employed deep meta-learning for urban traffic prediction, showcasing the potential of meta-learning techniques in adapting to various traffic scenarios.

5. Lin et al. (2018) focused on predicting bike-sharing demand at a station level using graph convolutional neural networks, highlighting the application of GNNs in transportation networks.

6. Li et al. (2017) introduced a diffusion convolutional recurrent neural network, emphasizing a data-driven approach for traffic forecasting.

7. Cui et al. (2019) presented a traffic graph convolutional recurrent neural network, further advancing the framework for network-scale traffic learning and forecasting.

8. Other studies explored multi-graph convolutional networks and ARIMA models for traffic flow and demand forecasting, indicating a diverse range of methodologies applied to similar problems.

Overall, these references illustrate a growing trend toward utilizing graph-based models and deep learning techniques in traffic prediction, reflecting their capability to handle complex spatio-temporal data effectively.

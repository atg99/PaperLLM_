The paper presents the performance evaluation of three models: DCRNN, STGCN, and DDP-GCN (Stacked) in predicting traffic patterns for the Urban1 dataset over a 60-minute interval. Figure 11(a) illustrates the RMSE performance curves, highlighting the confidence intervals of one standard deviation. Figure 11(b) shows the relative improvements of DDP-GCN over DCRNN and STGCN, with enhancements reaching up to 20 to 45% during peak morning commute hours between 6 and 9 am. Additionally, Figure 12 displays specific prediction results for various dates, indicating that DDP-GCN effectively tracks the ground truth, particularly when traffic dynamics are complex. The results emphasize the model's capability in accurately forecasting traffic conditions during challenging periods.

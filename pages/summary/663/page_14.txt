The paper presents an analysis of two urban traffic datasets, Urban1 (Gangnam) and Urban2 (Mapo), focusing on their characteristics and experimental setup. Table 1 summarizes key details, including time spans, intervals, region sizes, number of links, average speeds, and link lengths. Urban1 spans from April 1 to April 30, 2018, with a time interval of 5 minutes and includes 480 links, while Urban2 has 455 links. Both datasets exhibit a high degree of reachability among their links, contrasting with the METR-LA dataset, where only 27% of link pairs are reachable.

The paper details the experimental settings, where Z-score normalization was applied post-weekend exclusion. The data was split into training (70%), validation (10%), and testing (20%) sets. Specific parameters for the model were defined, including the weights used for data scaling. The research emphasizes the importance of these datasets in understanding urban traffic patterns and the methodologies applied in the analysis.

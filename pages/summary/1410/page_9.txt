In summary, the paper discusses the advancements in two-stage object detectors, highlighting key models such as Fast R-CNN, Faster R-CNN, Feature Pyramid Networks (FPN), and Region-based Fully Convolutional Networks (R-FCN). Faster R-CNN significantly enhances detection accuracy and reduces inference time, while FPN introduces a top-down architecture that improves detection of small objects by providing semantic features at multiple scales. R-FCN optimizes computation by sharing resources across proposals and employs position-sensitive score maps for better localization. These developments have led to the establishment of FPN as a fundamental component in modern detection frameworks and inspired further innovations in the field.

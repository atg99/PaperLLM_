B. Evaluation Metrics
The evaluation metrics used for object detection primarily focus on measuring accuracy and performance. The mean Average Precision (mAP) is widely adopted, which assesses the precision and recall of the models at different IoU thresholds. The IoU metric quantifies the overlap between the predicted bounding boxes and the ground truth boxes, which is essential for determining detection success. Other metrics may include precision-recall curves and average recall, which provide additional insights into model effectiveness.

In summary, while significant advancements have been made in object detection, challenges such as intra-class variation, the number of object categories, and efficiency remain critical areas of research. The discussed datasets and evaluation metrics play a pivotal role in addressing these challenges and driving progress in the field.

The paper titled "A Survey of Modern Deep Learning based Object Detection Models" provides a comprehensive overview of recent advancements in object detection, highlighting the significance of deep learning techniques in this field. Object detection involves both the classification and localization of objects within images or videos, a task that has become increasingly important due to varied applications, including self-driving cars, security, and medical diagnostics.

The authors discuss the evolution of object detection models, starting from early approaches that relied on hand-crafted feature extractors, which were often slow and inaccurate. The resurgence of convolutional neural networks (CNNs), particularly following the success of AlexNet in the ImageNet challenge, has transformed the landscape of object detection, leading to significant improvements in performance.

Key contributions of the paper include a detailed analysis of major object detection architectures, both single-stage and two-stage detectors, and an overview of prominent backbone architectures and lightweight models suitable for edge devices. The paper systematically reviews various detection architectures and associated technologies, with a focus on benchmarking datasets and evaluation metrics.

The structure of the paper includes discussions on the challenges of object detection, a review of landmark architectures, and a comparative analysis of different detector categories. It also addresses the emerging trends in the field and concludes with insights into future directions for research.

Overall, this survey serves as a valuable resource for understanding the current state of deep learning based object detection models and highlights the need for ongoing innovation in this rapidly evolving area of computer vision.

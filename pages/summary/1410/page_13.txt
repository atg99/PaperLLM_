The two-way dense layers allow for better feature reuse by connecting each layer to every other layer, thus improving gradient flow and reducing the number of parameters. The stem block serves as the initial layer, combining different convolution types to optimize the processing of input data. Dynamic convolution adapts the convolutional operation based on the input, further enhancing performance. PeleeNet was designed to operate efficiently on mobile devices while maintaining a competitive accuracy, positioning itself as a viable alternative to other lightweight models. Overall, these advancements in lightweight neural networks, including SqueezeNet, MobileNet, ShuffleNet, MobileNetv2, and PeleeNet, demonstrate significant progress in balancing model size, computational efficiency, and accuracy, catering to the growing demand for effective models in resource-constrained environments.

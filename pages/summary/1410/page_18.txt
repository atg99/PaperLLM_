This document comprises a list of references focused on advancements in deep learning, particularly concerning the use of neural networks in various applications such as text processing, image detection, and mobile device efficiency. 

Key contributions include:

1. The exploration of transfer learning with the unified text-to-text transformer, highlighting its effectiveness in natural language processing tasks.
2. A survey on the application of transformers in vision tasks, indicating a shift towards these models in computer vision.
3. Development of the Swin Transformer, which introduces a hierarchical approach to vision tasks using shifted windows for improved performance.
4. Several lightweight models aimed at specific applications, such as detecting image forgery, COVID-19 from chest X-rays, and fire detection systems, showcasing the trend towards efficiency in deep learning models.
5. Techniques for model compression, including pruning, quantization, and knowledge distillation, which aim to reduce the size and computational demands of neural networks.
6. The introduction of efficient architectures like SqueezeNet, MobileNets, and ShuffleNet, which are specifically designed for mobile and resource-constrained environments.
7. Innovative methods for neural architecture search and adaptation, enabling the development of optimized networks for specific tasks and deployment scenarios.

Overall, the references reflect a significant movement towards creating more efficient, specialized, and practical deep learning models across various domains.

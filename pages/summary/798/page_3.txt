The paper evaluates various quantization methods and their impact on model performance and efficiency. It compares techniques such as Dynamic Range Quantization, Float16 Quantization, and Full Integer Quantization, highlighting their benefits in terms of size reduction and speed improvements across different hardware platforms. 

Additionally, it discusses pruning methods, categorizing them into structured and unstructured approaches. Unstructured pruning focuses on removing the least important parameters, creating a sparse model that can be retrained iteratively. In contrast, structured pruning targets entire filters or channels based on their importance, as determined by the loss function. The authors utilized TensorFlow's pruning API for image classification and object detection models, while employing a structured pruning technique for language models.

To assess the effects of pruning and quantization, the authors selected seven pretrained models across four problem domains, including image classification and object detection, using datasets like ImageNet and COCO. Models such as ResNet50, MobileNet v2, and YOLO v4 were chosen for their differing strengths in computational efficiency and accuracy, providing a comprehensive analysis of the methods' effectiveness in real-world applications.

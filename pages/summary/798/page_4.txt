The paper presents a comparative analysis of model quantization techniques applied to various neural network architectures, focusing on their impact on model size, inference time, accuracy, and overall performance. The analysis includes assessments of MobileNet and Inception models, with results summarized in tables detailing size, compression time, loading time, accuracy, latency, and frames per second (FPS) for original and quantized versions.

Quantization methods examined include dynamic range, Float16, and Int8 quantization. For MobileNet, the original model size of 14.63 MB was significantly reduced to 3.92 MB with dynamic range quantization and 3.99 MB with Int8 quantization, although Int8 quantization led to a marked drop in accuracy (over 75% decrease). Inception models showed similar trends, with original sizes of 96.3 MB reduced to 24.3 MB through dynamic range and Int8 quantization, maintaining relatively stable accuracy levels.

The experiments were conducted on a MacBook Pro 2019, and model retraining occurred on Azure cloud instances. The results indicate that while quantization effectively reduces model size, it can adversely affect accuracy, particularly with Int8 quantization. The paper emphasizes the importance of balancing model deployability with performance metrics, providing a comprehensive overview of the trade-offs involved in model quantization for image classification tasks.

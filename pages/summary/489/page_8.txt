The paper discusses the behavior of the weight matrix in the context of non-linear Restricted Boltzmann Machines (RBMs) under a Gaussian prior. It highlights that the eigenvalues of the weight matrix remain strictly below a certain threshold, which influences the learning dynamics. The authors present a closed-form solution for stochastic gradients by addressing a deterministic equation, which suggests that a linear regime is expected at the onset of learning.

Key equations are derived, particularly focusing on the gradients of the loss function with respect to the weights. The correlation between nodes is defined, and the inverse of the precision matrix is utilized to establish relationships between the weight matrix and the data. The authors apply singular value decomposition (SVD) to diagonalize the interaction terms in the Hamiltonian, enabling a clearer analysis of the learning dynamics.

By transforming the variables through SVD, the Gaussian measure factorizes, leading to a distribution that can be expressed in terms of the transformed variables. This facilitates the derivation of an exact equation for the gradient in the SVD basis. The paper further simplifies the analysis by considering the full gradient and a continuous iteration time, yielding a time derivative of the weight matrix expressed through its eigenmodes.

Overall, the study provides insights into the learning dynamics of non-linear RBMs, emphasizing the role of the weight matrix's eigenvalues and the implications of the Gaussian prior in shaping the model's behavior during learning.

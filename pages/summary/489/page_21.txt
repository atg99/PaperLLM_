The paper presents a mathematical framework for analyzing a system using a weight matrix that undergoes a change of variables leading to new order parameters. Specifically, it introduces the variables \( s_\alpha \) and \( \tau_\alpha \) as projections of visible and hidden variables over the singular value decomposition (SVD) modes of the weight matrix \( w \). The Hubbard-Stratanovitch transformation is employed to derive the replicated magnetization, resulting in two additional order parameters that describe the condensation of visible and hidden nodes along the SVD modes.

The replicated free energy is expressed in terms of integrals over the new order parameters, and the quantities \( A[m, Q] \) and \( B[\bar{m}, \bar{Q}] \) are introduced to encapsulate the system's behavior. The analysis leads to a phase diagram based on the behavior of the order parameters \( Q, \bar{Q}, m, \) and \( \bar{m} \).

By taking the saddle point of the free energy in the limit of large \( L \) while keeping the ratio \( \kappa \) fixed, the paper applies a replica symmetric ansatz to distinguish different phases according to the values of the order parameters. The resulting self-consistent equations for the order parameters reveal relationships between the visible and hidden node behaviors, expressed through hyperbolic tangent functions, facilitating a deeper understanding of the system's dynamics.

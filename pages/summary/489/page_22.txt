The paper presents a detailed analysis of a phase diagram related to a model that exhibits different magnetic phases influenced by the properties of a noise matrix and weight matrix modes. The diagram illustrates three distinct phases:

1. **Paramagnetic Phase**: Characterized by zero values for the overlap and magnetization parameters, indicating a high-temperature state with a single free energy minimum.

2. **Ferromagnetic Phase**: Defined by non-zero overlap and magnetization values, where the system exhibits polarization towards one or multiple eigenmodes. This phase is of particular interest from a learning perspective as it signifies effective learning dynamics.

3. **Spin Glass Phase**: Identified by non-zero overlap parameters but zero magnetization values, where the system is trapped in multiple uncorrelated free energy minima. This phase is undesirable as it disrupts the learning process.

The left panel of Figure 4 depicts the phase diagram with axes representing the variance of the noise matrix and the strongest mode of the weight matrix. The right panel illustrates learning trajectories on both the MNIST dataset and a synthetic dataset, indicating a transition from the paramagnetic to the ferromagnetic phase through the learning of strong modes. The analysis suggests that maintaining the ferromagnetic phase is crucial while avoiding the spin glass phase to optimize learning outcomes.

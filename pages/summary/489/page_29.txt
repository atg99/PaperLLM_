Figure 10 illustrates the progression of feature learning in a Restricted Boltzmann Machine (RBM). It displays three components: the first hundred learned features, a histogram of distances between binarized features (represented as W±1 = sign(W)), and 100 samples generated from the RBM. In the first row, at the initial learning stage, only one feature is learned, resulting in a histogram showing high overlap among features. The generated samples closely resemble the learned feature. In the second row, as more features are learned, the histogram broadens but remains centered at zero, with Monte Carlo samples only reproducing one of the learned features. By the last row, the learning process has advanced significantly, leading to highly localized features, and the generated samples now correspond to recognizable digits.

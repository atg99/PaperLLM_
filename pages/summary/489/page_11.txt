The paper discusses a Restricted Boltzmann Machine (RBM) model with a spherical constraint imposed on hidden nodes, which allows for analytical treatment of the model. It highlights the significance of the phase transition that can occur in this RBM, contrasting it with the Gaussian-Gaussian case. The phase transition is identified when a parameter exceeds a critical value, which depends on the highest mode of the weight matrix and its spectral density. The paper introduces magnetization expressions for visible and hidden layers, illustrating how visible and hidden magnetization condenses along the highest mode when the critical parameter is surpassed. The dynamics of the model, particularly in the thermodynamic limit, indicate that a finite number of modes can condense and accumulate at the top of the weight matrix spectrum, resulting in a distribution concentrated on an n-dimensional sphere. The paper also suggests that non-condensed modes contribute to transverse Gaussian fluctuations, with further dynamical aspects to be discussed in a later section.

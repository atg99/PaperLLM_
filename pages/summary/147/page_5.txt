This paper introduces a novel architecture named Holistic Regularisation Variational Autoencoder (HR-VAE) aimed at addressing the issue of latent variable collapse commonly seen in existing Variational Autoencoder (VAE) models. Unlike traditional VAE-RNN models that apply a standard normal distribution prior solely on the last hidden state of the RNN encoder, the HR-VAE imposes regularisation across all hidden states. This approach enhances the learning process and mitigates the collapse of latent variables, leading to improved predictive performance compared to baseline models.

The paper presents training curves for various models, including VAE-LSTM-base, VAE-CNN, and vMF-VAE, alongside the proposed HR-VAE, demonstrating the effectiveness of the new architecture. Empirical results validate that HR-VAE not only alleviates the latent variable collapse problem but also outperforms other models in terms of predictive accuracy. 

The research is supported by the UK Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council. The references cited include notable works on deep generative models and methods to avoid latent variable collapse, highlighting the relevance and foundation of the proposed HR-VAE model within the field of generative modeling.

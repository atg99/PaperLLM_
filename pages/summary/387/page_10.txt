The paper discusses the implementation of an attention mechanism using a Squeeze-and-Excitation block, as proposed by Hu et al. The process involves two main operations: Squeeze and Excitation. The Squeeze operation applies global average pooling to feature maps, reducing their dimensionality from HxWxC to 1x1xC, which allows for a global receptive field. The subsequent Excitation operation learns channel weights through a gating mechanism, employing two fully connected layers with activation functions such as sigmoid and Rectified Linear Unit (RELU) to enhance non-linearity. In this work, the attention mechanism is integrated with a Feature Fusion (FF) technique, which consists of four steps: saving feature outputs from specific convolutional layers, applying the attention mechanism to assess the importance of these features, combining low-level and high-level features, and finally sending the fused features for classification.

The method was evaluated using the RPCam dataset, where the original training set was split into training and validation sets due to the absence of a provided testing set. The integration of attention and FF mechanisms aims to enhance the performance of the EfficientNet-B3 model.

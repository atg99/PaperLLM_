The paper presents Fast-SCNN, a fast segmentation network designed for real-time scene understanding, evaluated on the Cityscapes validation set. The results show that Fast-SCNN achieves a mean Intersection over Union (mIoU) of 68.0% at the class level and 84.7% at the category level. The runtime performance varies with input resolutions, with 123.5 frames per second (fps) at 1024x2048 resolution, 285.8 fps at 512x1024, and 485.4 fps at 256x512, demonstrating significant improvements over the MiniNet model, which achieved 40.7% mIoU at 250 fps.

The authors emphasize that Fast-SCNN's design allows it to operate effectively at lower resolutions without modification, making it suitable for embedded devices. The study highlights the efficiency gained from a multi-branch network architecture and the benefits of skip connections for preserving spatial details. Additionally, it suggests that extensive pre-training on auxiliary tasks is unnecessary for low-capacity networks when trained sufficiently.

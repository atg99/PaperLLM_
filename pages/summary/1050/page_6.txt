The paper discusses the potential evolution of explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) from a human-centered research perspective. It argues that research into competencies and knowledge could expand XAI's role beyond merely explaining specific systems and aiding users in establishing trust. Future XAI systems may assume significant social roles, such as learning and explaining information to individuals, coordinating with other agents to share knowledge, fostering cross-disciplinary insights, and assisting in educational endeavors. Additionally, they could leverage previously gained knowledge to enhance future discoveries and applications. This perspective suggests that the journey of XAI in understanding and generating knowledge is just beginning, highlighting a transformative potential in the realm of social interactions and knowledge dissemination.

City Research Online is an open access repository for research outputs from City, University of London. It aims to provide a platform for the dissemination of academic work, making it accessible to a wider audience. The repository includes a variety of publications such as journal articles, conference papers, theses, and more. Researchers can submit their work to enhance visibility and impact while ensuring compliance with open access policies. For inquiries, users can contact the repository at publications@city.ac.uk.

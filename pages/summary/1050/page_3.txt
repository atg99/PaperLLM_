The paper discusses the importance of Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) in enabling users to understand, trust, and manage AI systems effectively. Recent advancements in machine learning (ML) have produced AI applications with significant benefits across various fields. However, many of these systems lack the capability to provide explanations for their decisions, which raises concerns, especially in critical areas like defense, medicine, finance, and law. 

While some researchers argue that explanations are unnecessary or too challenging to achieve, the paper emphasizes that explanations are crucial for user interaction with AI. It highlights a tradeoff between ML performance, such as predictive accuracy, and explainability; often, the most accurate models, like deep learning networks, are the least explainable, while simpler models, like decision trees, offer more transparency but lower accuracy. The need for balance between performance and explainability is underscored as essential for effective AI deployment.

The paper "XAI-Explainable Artificial Intelligence" by Gunning et al. discusses the importance of explainability in artificial intelligence (AI) systems. The authors argue that as AI technologies become more integrated into critical areas such as healthcare and autonomous systems, the need for transparency in their decision-making processes grows. They emphasize that explainable AI (XAI) can enhance user trust, facilitate better human-AI collaboration, and ensure compliance with ethical standards. The paper outlines various approaches for implementing XAI, including model interpretability and the use of explainable models, while also addressing the challenges and trade-offs associated with achieving explainability without sacrificing performance. Overall, the authors advocate for a concerted effort in the AI community to prioritize explainability in the development of intelligent systems.

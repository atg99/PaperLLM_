The process, illustrated in Figure 1, follows a federated averaging approach (FedAvg), which serves as a foundational method in FL research. 

The FL process begins with each device downloading a global model for local training. Each device then performs local updates using its own data and uploads encrypted gradient information to the cloud. The cloud averages these updates and sends a revised global model back to the devices. This cycle continues until the model reaches a desired performance level or a deadline is met. 

FL addresses the challenge of data privacy versus data sharing, making it particularly suitable for sensitive applications in fields like healthcare, where data cannot be aggregated due to legal constraints. 

Recent literature reviews have highlighted both advancements and ongoing challenges in FL, with a collective focus on enhancing its contributions. This study aims to review the current applications of FL in industrial engineering, providing insights for future implementations. It concludes by outlining the characteristics of FL and identifying challenges, emphasizing the need for solutions to optimize FL further.

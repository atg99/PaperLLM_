This paper provides a comprehensive review of Federated Learning (FL), a decentralized and privacy-preserving technology designed to address challenges associated with data silos and sensitivity. It highlights the current research momentum in FL, which is of interest to both academic and industrial sectors. The study examines the evolution of FL, focusing on existing issues in its development, to enhance understanding and guide future applications, particularly in industrial engineering. Furthermore, it identifies six key research fronts that contribute to the FL literature, aiming to optimize its utilization. The paper ultimately summarizes the applications of FL in industrial engineering and computer science, contributing to the broader discourse on FL applications.

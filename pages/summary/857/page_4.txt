The introduction highlights two primary challenges in the integration of data science within industrial engineering: data governance and data silos. Data governance issues arise from privacy concerns, restricting organizations from using user data without consent. Data silos limit the availability of diverse training data, which is crucial for enhancing machine learning models' performance. The paper illustrates this through the comparison of AlphaGo and Alpha Zero, showcasing the significant performance improvements achieved through access to larger datasets.

Furthermore, the scarcity of labeled data, particularly in specialized fields such as medicine, poses additional hurdles for industrial advancement. The emergence of Federated Learning (FL) is presented as a promising solution to these challenges, enabling decentralized learning while ensuring data privacy and security. FL allows multiple clients to train models collaboratively, thus addressing the issues of data silos and governance while maximizing the utility of available data.

This paper discusses the growing interest and applications of Federated Learning (FL) in various fields, highlighting its potential in decentralized settings. The authors emphasize the need for research to address the existing challenges in FL to fully leverage its benefits. The references provided showcase a range of studies that explore different aspects of FL, including security, privacy, and system design. Notable works include studies on unsupervised deep learning for medical image classification, secured smart home architectures using FL, and frameworks that enhance privacy through blockchain technology. The paper serves as a resource for researchers aiming to advance the field of Federated Learning.

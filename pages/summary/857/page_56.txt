The paper presents two application samples illustrating the concepts of Vertical Federated Learning (FL) and Federated Transfer Learning. Vertical FL focuses on scenarios where different parties hold different features of the same data, enabling collaborative learning while preserving privacy. The application sample demonstrates how this approach can be utilized effectively in real-world situations. On the other hand, Federated Transfer Learning involves transferring knowledge from one domain to another within a federated learning framework, allowing models to leverage knowledge from related tasks. The application sample showcases the practical implications of this method, highlighting its potential in enhancing model performance across diverse datasets. Both figures emphasize the versatility and advantages of these advanced learning techniques in maintaining data security while improving collaborative learning outcomes.

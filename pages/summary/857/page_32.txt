Moreover, the integration of federated learning (FL) into industrial engineering and healthcare sectors shows promising potential for sustainable development. The challenges highlighted in previous sections, including privacy concerns and the rapid growth of the Internet of Things (IoT), necessitate a robust federated system design that can accommodate a large number of devices and institutions.

Future research should address key areas such as security compliance, attack defense mechanisms, and the processing of heterogeneous data sources within FL frameworks. By exploring these frontiers, the goal is to facilitate more effective implementation of FL in practical applications, ultimately leading to advancements in both manufacturing and healthcare domains. The ongoing efforts in these areas are crucial for optimizing FL systems and ensuring their adaptability to real-world challenges.

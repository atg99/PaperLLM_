The referenced works focus on advancements in federated learning, medical image analysis, and privacy-preserving technologies. Key contributions include:

1. **Asynchronous Model Update and Temporally Weighted Aggregation** - This paper proposes methods to improve federated learning efficiency through asynchronous updates and weighted aggregations, enhancing model performance in distributed systems.

2. **FedHealth** - This framework leverages federated transfer learning specifically for wearable healthcare applications, enabling personalized health monitoring while preserving data privacy.

3. **SecureBoost** - Introduces a federated learning framework that ensures data security without compromising model accuracy, addressing the challenges of collaborative machine learning.

4. **Probabilistic Diffeomorphic Registration** - This study presents an unsupervised learning approach for image registration, crucial for medical imaging, enhancing the alignment of images and surfaces.

5. **Deep Learning for Image Registration** - A deep learning framework is developed for affine and deformable image registration, improving accuracy in medical image analysis.

6. **Wearable Devices in Healthcare** - Discusses the role of wearable technology in providing healthcare providers with critical data for diagnosis and treatment planning.

7. **GDPR Regulation** - Outlines the regulatory framework for data protection, emphasizing the importance of personal data handling in the context of emerging technologies.

8. **PMF Framework** - This privacy-preserving framework utilizes federated learning for human mobility prediction, balancing predictive accuracy with user privacy.

9. **HHHFL** - Introduces a hierarchical approach to heterogeneous federated learning for electroencephalography, addressing the complexities of diverse data sources in medical research.

These studies collectively advance the fields of federated learning and medical technology while emphasizing privacy, security, and efficiency.

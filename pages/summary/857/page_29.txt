The paper discusses the advantages of federated learning (FL) across various domains, emphasizing its role in addressing the challenge of limited defective samples in production while ensuring manufacturers' privacy. In the realm of image processing, FL enhances representation in vision-and-language tasks, as demonstrated by Liu et al. (2020). Additionally, Mowla et al. (2020) highlight FL's effectiveness in detecting malicious attacks in Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) communication systems, where challenges like unbalanced data distribution are prevalent. 

In the context of electric vehicles, Saputra et al. (2019) propose a federated energy demand prediction method to mitigate energy congestion during transmission. In finance, Yang et al. (2019) utilize FL for detecting credit card fraud across different banks, showcasing its significance in the financial sector. For text mining, Wang et al. (2020) develop an industrial-grade federated framework for spam filtering and sentiment analysis, validated with real data.

Overall, FL empowers data owners to expand data application scopes and enhance model performance through collaboration among diverse entities. The paper anticipates that FL will increasingly support various industries in becoming more intelligent, facilitating a federated ecosystem that addresses data privacy concerns in artificial intelligence applications.

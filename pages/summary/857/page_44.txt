The provided references cover a range of topics related to federated learning, a machine learning paradigm that allows multiple devices to collaboratively learn a shared model while keeping their data decentralized. Key contributions include optimal stopping approaches for iterative training, incentive designs for efficient learning in mobile networks, and methods to enhance performance through blockchain technology. The works also address open challenges in federated learning, resource optimization in edge networks, and novel algorithms like federated tensor factorization. These studies collectively advance the understanding and application of federated learning in various domains, emphasizing the importance of collaboration while maintaining data privacy.

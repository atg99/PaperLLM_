The referenced works explore various applications and advancements in federated learning, particularly focusing on its secure and efficient implementation in distributed systems. 

1. Sharma et al. discuss secure and efficient federated transfer learning, presenting methods to enhance privacy and efficiency in collaborative learning environments.
2. Silva et al. conduct a meta-analysis on federated learning applied to large-scale subcortical brain data, highlighting its potential in distributed medical databases.
3. Smith et al. introduce federated multi-task learning, emphasizing its capability to handle multiple tasks collaboratively while preserving data privacy.
4. Sozinov et al. investigate human activity recognition using federated learning, demonstrating its effectiveness in real-time applications.
5. Sprague et al. focus on asynchronous federated learning for geospatial applications, which allows for more flexible data processing in distributed settings.
6. Szegedi et al. explore evolutionary federated learning on EEG data, contributing to the understanding of how federated learning can adapt in dynamic environments.
7. Tran et al. analyze federated learning over wireless networks, proposing optimization models to improve communication efficiency and performance.

Overall, these studies collectively advance the understanding of federated learning's applications across diverse fields while addressing key challenges related to security, efficiency, and data privacy.

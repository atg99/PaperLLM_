These larger organizations can influence prediction models, creating a disparity that discourages collaboration from smaller entities. However, Federated Learning (FL) addresses this issue by promoting equality among participants, allowing organizations with smaller data sets to engage in collaborative model training without the need to share their raw data.

The paper also highlights two prominent open-source frameworks for FL, specifically TensorFlow Federated (TFF). TFF is designed for decentralized data processing and is particularly suited for mobile devices. It incorporates features such as FedAvg for model updates and Secure Aggregation for privacy protection. The framework comprises a Federated Learning API that facilitates high-level interfaces for federated training and a Federated Core API that supports distributed computation. TFF has practical applications, including next-word and emoji prediction in mobile keyboards, demonstrating its effectiveness in real-world scenarios.

The paper discusses the Federated Learning (FL) framework proposed by Google, highlighting its architecture and functionality. It outlines how FL allows multiple devices to collaboratively learn a shared model while keeping data localized, enhancing privacy and security. Additionally, the paper presents an application sample of Horizontal Federated Learning (Horizontal FL), where data is distributed among devices but shares the same feature space. This approach is particularly beneficial in scenarios where data is generated by similar sources, allowing for effective model training without compromising user data. The illustrations provide a visual representation of the FL framework and its application, emphasizing the innovative aspects of Federated Learning in real-world contexts.

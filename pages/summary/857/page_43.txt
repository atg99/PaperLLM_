Artificial Intelligence, 2019, 1509-1515. This paper presents a novel approach to gradient aggregation in federated learning by utilizing gradient descent techniques to improve the performance of machine learning models across distributed datasets. The authors argue that traditional methods of aggregating gradients can lead to inefficiencies and propose their method as a more effective solution. The experiments conducted demonstrate the advantages of their approach in various scenarios, highlighting its potential for enhancing federated learning frameworks.

The referenced works collectively explore advancements in machine learning, particularly in the context of federated learning, secure data management, and AI applications in healthcare. They emphasize the importance of robust frameworks for distributed data processing, efficiency in model training, and the integration of blockchain technology for secure data handling in emergency medical care. The studies underline the growing need for scalable and secure methodologies to leverage large datasets while preserving privacy and enhancing predictive capabilities in various domains, including healthcare and urban sensing.

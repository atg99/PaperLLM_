Li, Wen, and He (2020) introduce practical approaches for federated gradient boosting decision trees, contributing to the efficiency of tree-based models in a federated context. Li et al. (2019) address abnormal client behavior detection in federated learning, which is crucial for maintaining the integrity of federated systems.

Lastly, Li et al. Together, these studies illustrate the multifaceted nature of federated learning and its applications across diverse domains.

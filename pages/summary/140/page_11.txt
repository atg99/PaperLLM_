This collection of references highlights significant advancements in the fields of graph neural networks and neural architecture search. The papers cover a range of topics from semi-supervised classification using graph convolutional networks to end-to-end deep learning architectures for graph classification. Notable contributions include the development of inductive representation learning techniques for large graphs and methods for link prediction and matrix completion based on graph neural networks.

Several key works focus on neural architecture search, employing diverse strategies such as reinforcement learning, differentiable architecture search, and Bayesian optimization. These studies aim to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of neural network design, showcasing methods like ProxylessNAS which optimize architectures directly for specific tasks and hardware. 

The references also encompass foundational work in approximating probability distributions with dependence trees and advances in Bayesian network structure learning, emphasizing the ongoing evolution of machine learning methodologies. Overall, these papers collectively represent a critical exploration of innovative techniques and frameworks that push the boundaries of deep learning and machine learning applications.

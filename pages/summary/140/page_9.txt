This paper introduces D-VAE, a generative model for directed acyclic graphs (DAGs) that utilizes a novel asynchronous message passing scheme to encode DAGs while respecting their partial orders. The model aims to enhance neural architecture search and Bayesian network structure learning through Bayesian optimization in its latent spaces. 

Figure 6 illustrates great circle interpolation in latent space, comparing D-VAE and S-VAE. The results show that both models can produce smooth interpolations with minimal changes in node types or edges. However, S-VAE demonstrates even smoother structural transitions due to its treatment of DAGs as strings, leading to similar embeddings for structurally similar DAGs without accounting for computational differences. Conversely, D-VAE prioritizes computational smoothness, reflecting its focus on modeling computations.

Figure 7 visualizes the BIC score distribution in a principal 2-D subspace of the latent space. D-VAE exhibits a clearer differentiation between high and low BIC scores, with smoother transitions, which may contribute to its superior performance in Bayesian optimization. In contrast, S-VAE displays sharp boundaries and more mixing of scores. The analysis suggests that D-VAE's latent space is more compact and effective, as evidenced by its ability to explain a greater proportion of variance in the training data compared to S-VAE.

In conclusion, D-VAE represents a significant advancement in the study and application of DAGs, potentially inspiring further research in this domain. The authors acknowledge the support from various funding bodies and express gratitude for collaborative discussions that aided their research.

The paper presents a theoretical analysis of Deep Q-Learning (DQN), justifying the use of experience replay and target networks, with the latter being interpreted as a single step of value iteration. It also introduces the Minimax-DQN algorithm, which extends DQN to two-player zero-sum Markov games. The authors establish the algorithmic and statistical convergence rates of the action-value functions associated with the policies generated by the Minimax-DQN algorithm. The references cited include foundational works in reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems, and advancements in deep reinforcement learning, highlighting the significance of the proposed methods in the broader context of the field.

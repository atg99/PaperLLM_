The acknowledgements section of the paper recognizes the support received by Yao Ma and Jiliang Tang from the National Science Foundation under multiple grant numbers and a grant from Criteo Faculty Research Award. The references list includes a comprehensive array of works related to graph neural networks, machine learning, and signal processing on graphs, highlighting significant contributions to the field. Key papers address topics such as interaction networks, graph convolutional networks, and various methods for learning from graph-structured data. The references illustrate the evolution and application of graph-based methodologies in various domains, including molecular biology, social recommendations, and dynamic knowledge graphs. The overall emphasis is on the development of advanced techniques for processing and analyzing graph data, demonstrating the relevance of these methods in contemporary research.

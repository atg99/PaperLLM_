This paper presents additional experimental details related to various tasks and hyper-parameter settings for models evaluated on the GLUE benchmark. For ranking loss tasks such as ARC, CommonsenseQA, WNLI, and QNLI, perturbations are added to the embeddings of question/answer pairs. The authors follow the methodologies outlined by Liu et al. (2019b) for enhancing performance on WNLI and QNLI, including specific data re-ordering and pairwise ranking formulations.

The paper lists hyper-parameters essential for different GLUE tasks, including step size, maximum perturbation, and number of steps, with optimal values specified for each task. Additionally, it discusses the variance in maximum loss increments across different methods, providing median and standard deviation figures for models fine-tuned with various training techniques.

Moreover, the authors hint at upcoming supplementary results for ablation studies comparing their approach with YOPO and note that complete results will be released in future revisions. This section emphasizes the rigorous experimentation and the careful tuning of parameters necessary for improving model performance on challenging NLP tasks.

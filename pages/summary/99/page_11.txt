This paper introduces a novel method combining 1D Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN), Gated Recurrent Units (GRU), and Position-Specific Scoring Matrix (PSSM) profiles to effectively discriminate electron transport proteins. It is the first study to apply this approach to protein function prediction, ensuring the retention of PSSM information within deep neural networks. The model was evaluated through 10 iterations of 5-fold cross-validation, alongside an independent dataset comprising 208 electron transport proteins and 713 general transport proteins. Results indicated an impressive average accuracy of 93.5% and a Matthew's Correlation Coefficient (MCC) of 0.81 during cross-validation, with independent dataset scores of 92.3% accuracy and 0.77 MCC. The performance exceeded that of existing state-of-the-art predictors across all metrics. This study demonstrates the potential of integrating CNN, GRU, and PSSM profiles in bioinformatics and sets a foundation for future research in protein function prediction.

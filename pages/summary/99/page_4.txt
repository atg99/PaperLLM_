The paper presents a deep learning architecture designed to identify electron transport proteins, utilizing a one-dimensional convolutional neural network (1D CNN) for feature extraction and a Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) for feature learning. The methodology is structured into four subprocesses: data collection, feature set generation, neural network implementation, and model evaluation. Statistical analysis reveals that there are 12,832 original electron transport proteins, with various levels of similarity assessed, while general transport proteins total 10,814. The study emphasizes the effectiveness of the proposed model in distinguishing between electron transport proteins and general transport proteins based on the collected data and generated features.

The paper introduces MuRel, a multimodal relational network designed for the Visual Question Answering (VQA) task. The system utilizes rich visual representations of image regions and merges them progressively with question representations. It incorporates region relations using pairwise combinations, enhancing the model's ability to interpret its decision-making process through visualization schemes.

The authors validated MuRel on three challenging datasets: VQA 2.0, VQA-CP v2, and TDIUC. They conducted various ablation studies demonstrating the effectiveness of their vectorial representation for attention modeling, the incorporation of pairwise combinations, and the benefits of multi-step iterations. The results indicate that MuRel is competitive, outperforming state-of-the-art results on two widely used datasets.

Visualizations in the paper illustrate the model's reliance on visual information to answer questions, highlighting its capability to connect relationships between objects in images. The research received support from the Labex SMART program, funded by the French state.

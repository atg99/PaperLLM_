The paper presents the EagleEye pruning algorithm, which incorporates a fast and accurate evaluation process using adaptive batch normalization. The results demonstrate that EagleEye outperforms other pruning methods in terms of accuracy on the ImageNet dataset. In the comparisons, EagleEye achieved notable performance across different architectures, including ResNet-50 and MobileNetV1, with higher Top1 and Top5 accuracies relative to other methods. The authors suggest further investigation into the adaptive batch normalization module's applicability in existing methods and propose exploring advanced pruning strategies, such as evolutionary algorithms, to enhance performance. Overall, the study emphasizes the efficiency and effectiveness of the EagleEye method in neural network pruning.

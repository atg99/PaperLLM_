The provided references comprise a collection of significant works related to deep neural network (DNN) pruning and compression techniques. These studies focus on various methodologies for optimizing neural network architectures, enhancing computational efficiency, and reducing memory requirements without significantly compromising performance.

Key contributions include:

1. **Autocompress** by Liu et al. explores an automatic DNN structured pruning framework aimed at achieving ultra-high compression rates.
2. **Metapruning** by Liu et al. introduces a meta-learning approach for automatic channel pruning in neural networks, demonstrating improved efficiency.
3. **Network Slimming** by Liu et al. presents a method for learning efficient convolutional networks through a process referred to as network slimming.
4. **Thinet** by Luo et al. details a filter-level pruning method to compress deep neural networks effectively.
5. **Importance Estimation for Pruning** by Molchanov et al. discusses techniques for estimating the importance of network components to guide pruning decisions.
6. **Learning Structured Sparsity** by Wen et al. investigates structured sparsity in deep neural networks to optimize performance.
7. **NetAdapt** by Yang et al. offers a platform-aware adaptation method for neural networks, particularly for mobile applications.
8. **Gate Decorator** by You et al. proposes a global filter pruning strategy to accelerate deep convolutional networks.
9. **NISP** by Yu et al. focuses on pruning networks using neuron importance score propagation to enhance model efficiency.
10. **Discrimination-Aware Channel Pruning** by Zhuang et al. emphasizes a technique that considers discrimination during the channel pruning process.

These studies collectively advance the field of neural network optimization by addressing the challenges of model size and computational demand, making them pivotal for applications in resource-constrained environments.

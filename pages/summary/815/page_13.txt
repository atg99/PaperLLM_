The paper presents pruning results for ResNet-56 and MobileNetV1 on the CIFAR-10 dataset, highlighting the effectiveness of the EagleEye algorithm. In Table 3, EagleEye demonstrates superior performance with ResNet-56, achieving 62.23M FLOPs and a Top-1 accuracy of 94.66%, outperforming other methods such as Filter Pruning (FP) and HRank. For MobileNetV1, EagleEye consistently yields the best results across various FLOPs constraints, showing improved accuracy compared to FP implementations.

The authors note that their approach generates 1000 pruning strategies for different FLOPs limits (3G, 2G, and 1G) and utilizes an adaptive-BN-based evaluation method to fine-tune the top candidates. They argue that existing methods like ThiNet and Meta-Pruning suffer from performance issues due to their pruning strategies, whereas EagleEye effectively identifies optimal pruning paths.

Further, the paper addresses the challenges of pruning MobileNetV1 for ImageNet, where it again outperforms AMC and NetAdapt by selecting candidates based on a more reliable evaluation process. Overall, the results indicate that EagleEye consistently achieves the highest accuracy among the compared methods across different models and datasets.

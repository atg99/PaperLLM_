In summary, the proposed adaptive-BN-based evaluation module demonstrates both efficiency and effectiveness in the pruning process. Compared to state-of-the-art algorithms, EagleEye requires substantially less computational time while achieving superior accuracy on both CIFAR-10 and ImageNet datasets. This method streamlines the candidate selection process, allowing for rapid evaluations without the extensive resource demands of other techniques. Overall, this work highlights the potential of adaptive batch normalization as a powerful tool in model pruning, facilitating faster and more reliable pruning strategies.

The referenced papers explore a variety of novel algorithms for global optimization problems, primarily inspired by natural phenomena and biological processes. 

1. Jahani and Chizari introduce the Mouth Brooding Fish algorithm, which addresses global optimization challenges through a unique approach.
2. Kusuma and Kallista present the Migration-Crossover Algorithm, combining swarm intelligence with crossover techniques for enhanced search capabilities.
3. Walton et al. modify the cuckoo search method, creating a gradient-free optimization algorithm that enhances efficiency in problem-solving.
4. Obagbuwa and Adewumi improve upon the Cockroach Swarm Optimization technique, demonstrating its effectiveness in optimization tasks.
5. Mirjalili's Moth-flame optimization algorithm offers a new heuristic paradigm inspired by moth behavior.
6. Alauddin develops the Mosquito Flying Optimization algorithm, focusing on optimization through mosquito flight patterns.
7. Klein and Coelho propose a meerkat-inspired algorithm for global optimization, showcasing the applicability of animal behavior in computational strategies.
8. Valdez et al. introduce the Mycorrhiza Optimization Algorithm, emphasizing the role of mycorrhiza in resource optimization.
9. The MOX algorithm, inspired by mosquito oviposition, presents a novel approach to global optimization.
10. Faramarzi et al. discuss the Marine Predators Algorithm, highlighting nature-inspired methods in optimization.
11. Mucherino and Seref's Monkey Search offers a new metaheuristic search strategy.
12. Wang presents the Moth Search algorithm, further exploring bio-inspired methodologies.
13. Abdel-Basset et al. introduce the Mantis Search Algorithm, applicable to engineering design problems.
14. Luo et al. propose the Natural Aggregation Algorithm for real-parameter optimization.
15. Salgotra and Singh present the Naked Mole-Rat Algorithm, contributing to the field of neural computing.
16. Abdel-Basset et al. also introduce the Nutcracker Optimizer, emphasizing its utility in engineering design.
17. Salih and Alsewari's Nomadic People Optimizer addresses both normal and large-scale optimization problems.
18. Drias et al. propose a Swarm Algorithm based on Orcas Intelligence for maze-solving applications.
19. Maia et al. develop OptBees, a bee-inspired algorithm for continuous optimization challenges.

These studies collectively highlight the innovative use of biological and natural phenomena in creating efficient algorithms for solving complex optimization problems.

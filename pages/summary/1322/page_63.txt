This collection of papers discusses various bio-inspired algorithms and metaheuristic optimization techniques applied to solve complex optimization problems across diverse fields. 

1. Muñoz et al. present an artificial beehive algorithm aimed at continuous optimization challenges. 
2. Naderi et al. develop mathematical models alongside a hunting search algorithm specifically for no-wait flowshop scheduling with parallel machines.
3. Odili and Kahar explore the African Buffalo Optimization to address the Traveling Salesman Problem.
4. Almonacid and Soto introduce the Andean Condor Algorithm to tackle cell formation issues.
5. Lamy proposes the Artificial Feeding Birds metaheuristic, inspired by pigeons, for optimization tasks.
6. Zhao et al. unveil an artificial hummingbird algorithm, showcasing its applications in engineering.
7. Zitouni et al. present the archerfish hunting optimizer as a novel approach for global optimization.
8. Li et al. describe the animal migration optimization algorithm, which mimics animal migration behavior.
9. Liu et al. introduce an aphid-inspired optimization algorithm for engineering applications.
10. Mirjalili puts forth the Ant Lion Optimizer, contributing to advancements in engineering software.
11. Abualigah et al. propose the Aquila Optimizer, a new metaheuristic for various optimization tasks.
12. Pook and Ramlan derive the Anglerfish algorithm for solving the Traveling Salesman Problem using a randomized approach.
13. Abualigah et al. also present the Arithmetic Optimization Algorithm, analyzing its performance in applied mechanics.
14. Wang et al. introduce the Artificial Rabbits Optimization, targeting engineering optimization issues.
15. Chen et al. develop the Artificial Searching Swarm Algorithm for constrained optimization problems.
16. Chen and colleagues analyze the performance of the Artificial Tribe Algorithm.
17. Subramanian et al. introduce the African Wild Dog Algorithm, a new method for engineering optimization.
18. Mohapatra and Mohapatra present the American Zebra Optimization Algorithm aimed at global optimization challenges.
19. Alsattar et al. discuss a bald eagle search optimization algorithm, contributing to the field of artificial intelligence.

The studies collectively enhance the understanding of optimization methodologies inspired by natural phenomena, demonstrating their applicability in solving real-world problems.

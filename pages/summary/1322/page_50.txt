The paper discusses the importance of parameter tuning and adaptation in bio-inspired algorithms, emphasizing that assigning appropriate values to parameters is essential for optimal performance. It highlights the value of parametric sensitivity analysis in identifying key parameters for tuning and stresses the necessity of a similar tuning process for fair comparisons between algorithms.

The paper calls for further research into self-adaptation mechanisms for sensitive parameters, which can reduce the number of parameters needing adjustment and potentially enhance algorithm performance. 

It also addresses the need for improved benchmarks and comparison methodologies when developing new bio-inspired solvers. Using existing benchmarks and creating new ones based on real-world problems can facilitate better comparisons. The authors advocate for incorporating scalability considerations and adopting advanced statistical testing methods, such as Bayesian tests, to ensure rigorous evaluations.

In Subsection 8.2, the paper critiques the proliferation of proposals with limited impact, underscoring the importance of establishing best practices for comparative analysis against competitive algorithms and the current state of the art. The authors provide guidelines aimed at ensuring fair and effective comparisons among bio-inspired algorithms, which are further elaborated in the referenced discussions.

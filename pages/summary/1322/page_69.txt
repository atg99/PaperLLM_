This collection of papers discusses various nature-inspired optimization algorithms that leverage biological behaviors and processes to solve complex optimization problems. 

The Group Search Optimizer, inspired by animal foraging behavior, is highlighted for its unique approach to optimization (He et al., 2009). Particle Swarm Optimization is refined by introducing a leader-follower dynamic, enhancing its effectiveness (Wang & Wang, 2008). The Artificial Gorilla Troops Optimizer presents a new metaheuristic for global optimization (Abdollahzadeh et al., 2021), while the Grey Wolf Optimizer showcases a strategy based on the hunting techniques of grey wolves (Mirjalili et al., 2014).

Other notable methods include a novel metaheuristic based on Hitchcock birds' behavior (Morais et al., 2020) and the Honey-Bees Mating Optimization algorithm, which addresses water resource management (Bozorg-Haddad et al., 2006). The Hunger Games Search algorithm is extensively analyzed for its performance and potential applications (Yang et al., 2021), and the Harris Hawks Optimization algorithm is detailed for its algorithmic structure and applications (Heidari et al., 2019).

Additionally, the Hoopoe Heuristic Optimization and the Horned Lizard Defense Tactics algorithms contribute to the growing field of metaheuristics (El-Dosuky et al., 2012; Peraza-Vázquez et al., 2024). The Horse Optimization Algorithm introduces a unique bio-inspired approach for global optimization (Moldovan, 2020), while the Hunting Search algorithm draws from group hunting strategies (Oftadeh et al., 2010).

The Honey Bee Social Foraging Algorithms explore resource allocation methods (Quijano & Passino, 2007), and the Hierarchical Swarm Model presents a new optimization perspective (Chen et al., 2010). The Hammerhead Shark Optimization algorithm demonstrates dynamic route planning capabilities (Ali et al., 2019), and the Humboldt Squid Optimization Algorithm provides a fresh approach to optimization (Anaraki & Farzin, 2023).

Finally, the hybrid hypercube natural aggregation algorithm and the Improved Raven Roosting Optimization algorithm expand the toolkit of optimization methods (Maciel et al., 2020; Torabi & Safi-Esfahani, 2018). The Invasive Tumor Growth Optimization algorithm and the Jaguar Algorithm further illustrate the diversity of strategies inspired by natural phenomena (Tang et al., 2015; Chen et al., 2015). 

Overall, these works collectively advance the field of optimization by introducing innovative algorithms inspired by diverse biological behaviors.

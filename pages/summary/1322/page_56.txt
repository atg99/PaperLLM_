The paper concludes that nature- and bio-inspired optimization algorithms have proliferated significantly, leading to a diverse array of approaches. A review of 518 algorithms was conducted, categorized into two taxonomies based on inspiration sources and solution generation behavior. The analysis revealed that many algorithms exhibit similarities and that a notable portion (24%) are adaptations of classical methods like PSO, DE, or GA. This raises concerns about the originality and methodological rigor of new proposals, as many fail to offer substantial innovations or adequate comparisons, leading to issues in reproducibility and applicability.

Despite these challenges, bio-inspired algorithms have shown promise in artificial intelligence applications, contributing to advancements in deep learning, language models, and generative AI. The concept of AI-powered AI emphasizes using these algorithms to enhance other systems, showcasing their practical impact. The paper highlights a shift towards biological metaphors over problem-solving approaches, suggesting that future research should focus on developing methodologies that address real optimization challenges. The potential of population-based metaheuristics in shaping the future of AI is acknowledged, pointing to their role in ongoing innovation and progress in the field.

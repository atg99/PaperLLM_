The cited papers explore various novel metaheuristic optimization algorithms inspired by natural phenomena. Rashedi et al. introduce the Gravitational Search Algorithm, which utilizes the law of gravity for optimization tasks. Hatamlou presents a Black Hole algorithm for data clustering, leveraging the concept of black holes in a heuristic context. Shah-Hosseini applies a galaxy-based search algorithm for principal components analysis, showcasing a continuous optimization approach.

Lee and Geem discuss the Harmony Search algorithm, emphasizing its application in continuous engineering optimization. Yadav and Yadav's Artificial Electric Field Algorithm targets global optimization, while Hashim et al. present the Archimedes Optimization Algorithm aimed at solving complex optimization challenges.

Xie et al. propose the Artificial Physics Optimization Algorithm, offering a general framework for optimization tasks. Zhao et al. apply Atom Search Optimization to hydrogeologic parameter estimation. Erol and Eksin introduce the Big Bang–Big Crunch method, a unique optimization strategy.

Kaveh and Mahdavi's Colliding Bodies Optimization presents a new metaheuristic, while Feng et al. discuss the Crystal Energy Optimization Algorithm. Formato's Central Force Optimization provides a nature-inspired framework for multidimensional searches.

Kaveh and Talatahari's Charged System Search offers another heuristic method, while Abedinpourshotorban et al. present the Electromagnetic Field Optimization algorithm, inspired by physics principles. Ilker Birbil and Fang introduce an Electromagnetism-like mechanism for global optimization.

Khalafallah and Abdel-Raheem's Electimize algorithm finds application in construction engineering, while Rahmanzadeh and Pishvaee develop the Electron Radar Search Algorithm as a new metaheuristic. Kundu's Gravitational Clustering focuses on spatial point distribution, and Barzegar et al. apply a Gravitational Emulation Local Search algorithm for scheduling in grid systems. Lastly, Zheng et al. discuss the Gravitation Field Algorithm and its use in gene clustering, contributing to the ongoing exploration of optimization methods rooted in natural phenomena.

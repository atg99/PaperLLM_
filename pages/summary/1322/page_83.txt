The collection of papers presents various innovative algorithms inspired by natural phenomena and human behaviors for solving optimization problems. Xu et al. introduce a Social Emotional Optimization Algorithm aimed at nonlinear constrained optimization. Emami proposes a human-inspired stock exchange trading optimization algorithm for global optimization challenges. Weibo et al. develop a particle swarm optimization algorithm with stochastic focusing for real-parameter optimization.

Dwi Purnomo discusses Soccer Game Optimization as a fundamental concept for optimization. Das et al. present a Student Psychology-based optimization algorithm, contributing to population-based optimization methodologies. Nemati et al. introduce a metaheuristic inspired by stadium spectators, while Rashid proposes the Tiki-taka algorithm based on football strategies.

Mahmoodabadi et al. describe the Team Game Algorithm (TGA) for optimization applications, and Rao et al. put forth the Teaching-Learning-Based Optimization method for mechanical design challenges. Bagheri et al. introduce a Thieves and Police algorithm for probabilistic power flow. Li et al. present a Tactical Unit Algorithm for energy optimization in chiller loading distribution.

Kaveh and Zolghadr propose the Tug Of War Optimization algorithm, while Ardjmand and Amin-Naseri develop an Unconscious Search algorithm based on psychoanalytic theory. Moghdani et al. create a Volleyball Premier League Algorithm, and Yampolskiy and EL-Barkouky present the Wisdom of Artificial Crowds algorithm for NP-hard problems.

Ghaemi and Feizi-Derakhshi introduce the Forest Optimization Algorithm, while Cheng et al. develop an Artificial Flora Optimization Algorithm. Zhao et al. present the Artificial Plant Optimization Algorithm for constrained optimization issues. Finally, Ghaemidizaji et al. introduce an optimization algorithm based on BrunsVigia flower behavior, and Ong et al. develop a Carnivorous Plant Algorithm for global optimization problems. Overall, these works contribute significantly to the field of optimization through diverse approaches.

The paper presents a taxonomy of algorithmic behaviors in meta-heuristic optimization, focusing on solution generation processes. It categorizes these behaviors into distinct types, primarily emphasizing differential vector movement and solution creation methods, including combination and stigmergy.

Key concepts include:

1. **Differential Vector Movement**: This is a method where new solutions are generated based on existing ones, illustrated through various approaches using a reference solution (xA). The methods include:
   - Utilizing the entire population,
   - Leveraging relevant individuals with a weighted combination of the best current and historical solutions,
   - Considering neighboring solutions for movement.

2. **Solution Creation**: The paper identifies two main strategies:
   - **Combination**: This method involves merging solutions to create new candidates.
   - **Stigmergy**: A collaborative approach where solutions are influenced by the actions of others in the population.

3. **Population-Based Strategies**: The paper also analyzes the classification of algorithms based on their reliance on the entire population, groups, subpopulations, or neighborhood structures, highlighting the diversity in approaches and their effectiveness.

Figures included in the paper provide visual representations of these algorithmic behaviors and their classifications, offering a comprehensive overview of the landscape of meta-heuristic optimization techniques. The subsequent sections delve into detailed descriptions of each category, supported by examples and algorithm tables, facilitating a deeper understanding of their characteristics and applications.

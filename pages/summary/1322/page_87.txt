The cited papers explore various novel algorithms and frameworks in the field of optimization, particularly focusing on metaheuristic methods. Houssein et al. present a bio-inspired optimizer for liver cancer, while Ni et al. introduce a human-based metaheuristic algorithm termed the Literature Research Optimizer. Nishida discusses membrane algorithms for NP-complete problems, and Sadollah et al. propose the Mine Blast Algorithm for constrained engineering tasks. 

Asil Gharebaghi and Ardalan Asl introduce a new meta-heuristic based on neuronal communication, while Khouni and Menacer detail the Nizar optimization algorithm for global optimization. Kaveh et al. develop a physically-based metaheuristic for plasma generation optimization. Chan et al. present a hyper-heuristic inspired by pearl hunting, and Savsani et al. introduce the Passing Vehicle Search algorithm.

Jiang et al. focus on optimizing stable linear systems with a new algorithm, and Ansótegui et al. examine reactive dialectic search portfolios for MaxSAT. Dorigo and Stützle's work on Ant Colony Optimization is a foundational text in the field. Sörensen provides insights into the metaphor of metaheuristics, while García-Martínez et al. evaluate a decade of research since the CEC 2005 competition. Liao et al. assess the performance of continuous optimizers, and Bosman and Gallagher highlight the significance of implementation details in black-box optimization. Biedrzycki discusses the equivalence of implementations of the CMA-ES algorithm, and Liefooghe et al. present a software framework for evolutionary multiobjective optimization. Finally, Durillo and Nebro introduce jMetal, a Java framework for multi-objective optimization. 

Overall, these contributions reflect ongoing advancements in optimization techniques, emphasizing both theoretical developments and practical applications across various domains.

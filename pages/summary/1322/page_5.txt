The paper discusses the evolution and analysis of metaheuristic algorithms, particularly bio-inspired approaches, over a span of five years. It highlights the development, analysis, and comparison of new methodologies, including both manual and automatic designs of metaheuristics. The work is updated annually, with the latest version released in April 2024, which includes 518 reviewed algorithms and critical analyses of the field.

The manuscript details a structured approach, beginning with an overview of previous surveys and taxonomies in Section 2, followed by a taxonomy based on algorithmic inspiration in Section 3, and behavioral taxonomy in Section 4. Section 5 analyzes the similarities and differences between the two taxonomies, identifying influential algorithms. Lessons learned and recommendations are presented in Section 6, while Section 7 offers a critical evaluation of the current state of metaheuristics, addressing both strengths and weaknesses.

Future directions for bio-inspired optimization algorithms are discussed in Section 8, emphasizing the need for good practices and sound experimental protocols. Section 9 provides guidelines and recent studies related to metaheuristics, culminating in Section 10, which summarizes the key conclusions and reflections from the comprehensive literature study.

Overall, the paper presents a dual perspective: it critiques the existing metaheuristic proposals for lacking practical relevance while also recognizing the potential of population-based optimization models in advancing artificial intelligence systems.

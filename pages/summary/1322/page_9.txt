In these algorithms, each agent in the swarm represents a potential solution and shares information with others, allowing for the collective adaptation and improvement of solutions over time. The mechanisms of cooperation and competition among agents drive the swarm towards optimal solutions, often resulting in faster convergence and enhanced exploration capabilities compared to traditional methods.

Notable algorithms within this category include Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) and Ant Colony Optimization (ACO), which have been widely applied across various fields due to their efficiency and effectiveness in handling complex optimization problems. The collaborative nature and simplicity of the underlying principles make Swarm Intelligence algorithms robust and versatile in tackling diverse challenges.

In summary, the paper categorizes reviewed algorithms into Breeding-based Evolutionary Algorithms and Swarm Intelligence based Algorithms, highlighting their unique mechanisms and foundational principles. Breeding-based algorithms focus on reproduction and survival to evolve solutions, while Swarm Intelligence algorithms emphasize collective behavior and interaction among agents to enhance optimization processes.

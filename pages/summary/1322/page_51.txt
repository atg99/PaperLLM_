The paper discusses four essential guidelines for evaluating algorithms, particularly in the context of benchmarking and validation. 

1. **Benchmarks**: The selection of benchmarks is critical in algorithm evaluation, as it influences the study's conclusions. It's important to choose benchmarks that minimize biases, which can unfairly favor certain algorithms. A thorough analysis of the test problems within the chosen benchmark is necessary to ensure fair comparisons.

2. **Validation of Results**: Presenting raw results is insufficient; statistical validation is essential. This involves employing appropriate statistical tests and ensuring that the assumptions of these tests are met. Visualization techniques are also emphasized for their role in simplifying complex data and enhancing reader comprehension.

3. **Components Analysis and Parameter Tuning**: The paper advocates for clearly defined hypotheses at the outset, followed by a comprehensive analysis that includes identifying search phases, assessing each method's components, tuning algorithm parameters, and comparing results with state-of-the-art algorithms. This detailed approach ensures a robust evaluation of the proposed method.

4. **Usefulness of the Algorithm**: Contributors must clearly articulate the significance of their algorithm, highlighting its competitiveness and potential contributions to the field. This clarity enhances understanding of the algorithm's importance and its potential impact.

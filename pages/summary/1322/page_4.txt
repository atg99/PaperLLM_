This paper presents a comprehensive overview of nature- and bio-inspired optimization algorithms, organizing existing literature through a dual classification system based on their origins and algorithmic similarities. This innovative approach allows researchers to contextualize new proposals effectively. The study critically examines the relationships between classifications in different taxonomies, aiming to identify influential meta-heuristic algorithms that have inspired subsequent work.

The analysis is twofold: first, it offers recommendations for improving research practices in the field, highlighting the need for attention to behavioral differences and verifiable performance in practical applications. Second, it takes a critical perspective on bio-inspired optimization, discussing the strengths, weaknesses, and challenges faced by the field while emphasizing future development potential.

The report notes a consistent growth in research interest, as shown in Figure 1, and stresses the importance of understanding current applications and methodological proposals for bio-inspired algorithms, especially in emerging areas of Artificial Intelligence. The paper also outlines three critical perspectives on the current state of the field: the positive aspects of potential applications, the shortcomings in novelty and methodological rigor, and the need for improved practices.

In subsequent sections, the paper introduces previous works that provide methodological guidelines for comparing bio-inspired algorithms, as well as tutorials on their design and application. It concludes with an analysis of metaheuristics, focusing on good practices for their design and the importance of novelty and similarity measurement in research.

Overall, the paper aims to foster better practices in the design, comparison, and application of bio-inspired optimization algorithms, ensuring that future research can build on a solid foundation of methodological rigor and innovation.

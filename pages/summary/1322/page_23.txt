The paper discusses the heterogeneous nature of algorithms within a specific miscellaneous category, highlighting the diversity of inspirations that contribute to their development. It emphasizes the importance of categorizing these algorithms to foster the creation of new ones based on similar concepts. The authors argue that without a systematic approach to grouping these algorithms, the taxonomy will stagnate and fail to fulfill its purpose of facilitating analysis and understanding of future advancements in the field. Tables 15 and 16 provide a comprehensive list of nature- and bio-inspired meta-heuristics in this category, illustrating the variety of algorithms, such as Scientific Algorithms, Social Engineering Optimization, and Snow Flake Optimization, among others. The paper advocates for ongoing organization and classification of algorithms to ensure the evolution of the taxonomy.

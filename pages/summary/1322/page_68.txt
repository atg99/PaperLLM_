This collection of papers presents a variety of innovative algorithms inspired by natural phenomena for solving optimization problems. 

The work by Mutazono et al. introduces a self-organizing anti-phase synchronization mechanism based on frog calls, aimed at enhancing wireless sensor networks. Azizi et al. propose the Fire Hawk Optimizer, a metaheuristic algorithm that draws on the behaviors of fire hawks for optimization tasks. Bellaachia and Bari present a clustering algorithm called Flock by Leader, inspired by biological systems.

Falahah et al. introduce the Frilled Lizard Optimization algorithm, emphasizing its nature-inspired nature in addressing optimization challenges. Pan's research offers a new Fruit Fly Optimization Algorithm, applying it to financial distress modeling. The Falcon Optimization Algorithm, designed by de Vasconcelos Segundo et al., focuses on heat exchanger design.

Several papers explore fish swarm behaviors for optimization, including Li et al.'s Fish-swarm Algorithm and Tsai and Lin's modified version integrating particle swarm dynamics. New algorithms, such as the Green Anaconda Optimization and Giant Armadillo Optimization, are proposed by Dehghani et al. and Alsayyed et al., respectively, both targeting problem-solving in optimization contexts.

Other notable algorithms include the Golden Eagle Optimizer by Mohammadi-Balani et al., the Golden Jackal Optimization by Chopra and Ansari, and the Genghis Khan Shark Optimizer introduced by Hu et al., all of which contribute to the field of engineering optimization. 

Further insights into collective behaviors for mobile robots are provided by Min and Wang, while Agushaka et al. present the Gazelle Optimization algorithm, emphasizing its novelty in metaheuristic optimization. Finally, De SK discusses Goat Search Algorithms, and Zhou et al. apply Glowworm Swarm Optimization to public transit vehicle dispatching systems.

Overall, these studies reflect the rich potential of bio-inspired algorithms in advancing optimization techniques across various domains.

This collection of papers provides a comprehensive overview of various bio-inspired and metaheuristic algorithms utilized for optimization across multiple domains. 

Xiong et al. discuss the principles and recent trends in metaheuristics for engineering optimization, highlighting their significance in solving complex problems. Molina et al. provide insights into bio-inspired and evolutionary algorithms, analyzing their performance over a decade of competitions and drawing lessons for global optimization. Zavala et al. survey multi-objective metaheuristics specifically applied to structural optimization, indicating the effectiveness of these methods in addressing conflicting objectives.

Yang et al. explore bio-inspired computation in telecommunications, while Beni and Wang focus on swarm intelligence within cellular robotic systems, emphasizing its potential in robotic applications. Fong addresses the integration of bio-inspired optimization with data mining algorithms, outlining both opportunities and challenges.

Del Ser et al. examine the application of bioinspired computational intelligence in transportation systems, suggesting that there is much work to be done in this area. In the context of power systems, del Valle et al. detail particle swarm optimization, its basic concepts, variants, and applications.

A survey by Dressler and Akan on bio-inspired networking highlights its relevance in computer networks. José-García and Gómez-Flores review automatic clustering techniques using nature-inspired metaheuristics, while Alsalibi et al. assess the impact of these approaches on advancements in face recognition technologies.

García-Godoy et al. focus on bio-inspired optimization for molecular docking, summarizing the state of the art and future perspectives. Kolias et al. review swarm intelligence applications in intrusion detection systems, indicating its effectiveness in enhancing security measures.

Several papers delve into specific optimization algorithms: Banks et al. provide a foundational review of particle swarm optimization, while Neri and Tirronen, as well as Das et al., survey recent advancements in differential evolution, discussing its evolution and applications.

Lastly, Karaboga et al. present a comprehensive survey on the artificial bee colony algorithm and its applications, alongside Bitam et al. who focus on various bee colony algorithms, underscoring the versatility and effectiveness of bio-inspired approaches in solving complex optimization problems across diverse fields.

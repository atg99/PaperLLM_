The referenced papers explore various novel metaheuristic optimization algorithms inspired by human behavior and social dynamics. Key contributions include:

1. **Group Teaching Optimization Algorithm** (Zhang and Jin, 2020): This algorithm addresses global optimization problems through a new metaheuristic approach.
2. **Human Evolutionary Model** (Montiel et al., 2007): Proposes a unique optimization strategy based on human evolutionary principles.
3. **Human Group Formation Technique** (Thammano and Moolwong, 2010): Introduces a computational intelligence method leveraging human social structures for optimization tasks.
4. **Human-Inspired Algorithms** (Zhang et al., 2009): Focuses on continuous function optimization, drawing inspiration from human cognitive processes.
5. **Human Urbanization Algorithm** (Ghasemian et al., 2020): A metaheuristic approach that simulates urbanization patterns for optimization.
6. **Kho-Kho Optimization Algorithm** (Srivastava and Das, 2020): Applies a new algorithm to solve complex energy dispatch problems.
7. **League Championship Algorithm** (Kashan, 2014): Inspired by sports championships, this algorithm targets global optimization challenges.
8. **Life Choice-Based Optimizer** (Khatri et al., 2020): Proposes a novel optimization method based on decision-making processes in life choices.
9. **Leaders and Followers Metaheuristic** (Gonzalez-Fernandez and Chen, 2015): Aims to mitigate bias in accumulated information during optimization.
10. **Political Optimizer** (Askari et al., 2020): This socio-inspired algorithm mimics political strategies for global optimization.
11. **Queuing Search Algorithm** (Zhang et al., 2018): Develops a novel method for engineering optimization through queuing theory principles.
12. **Search and Rescue Operations Algorithm** (Shabani et al., 2019): Proposes an optimization technique based on search and rescue operations.
13. **Social Cognitive Optimization** (Ray and Liew, 2003): Simulates social behavior to enhance optimization processes.

These studies collectively expand the toolkit of optimization techniques by incorporating human-centric and social behavior models, demonstrating the versatility and applicability of metaheuristic methods across various domains.

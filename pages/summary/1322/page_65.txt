This collection of references highlights various innovative algorithms inspired by biological and natural processes for solving optimization problems. The works span a range of applications from continuous optimization to feature selection and engineering challenges.

Findik's 2015 paper presents a Bull optimization algorithm utilizing genetic operators for continuous optimization. Sato and Hagiwara, in their 1997 conference proceedings, introduce the Bee System, which emphasizes concentrated search strategies. Lucic and Teodorovic propose an artificial life approach to transportation modeling, showcasing the intersection of AI and logistics.

Meng et al. (2016) introduce the Bird Swarm Algorithm, further expanding bio-inspired optimization. Akbari et al. (2010) develop a novel bee swarm optimization algorithm, while de Oliveira et al. (2011) present a Bioluminescent Swarm Optimization Algorithm, illustrating the diversity of swarm-based methods.

Research by Wang et al. (2023) discusses a biological survival optimization algorithm, emphasizing its engineering and neural network applications. Drias et al. (2005) explore cooperative strategies in bee swarms for solving the maximum weighted satisfiability problem.

Other notable contributions include the Buzzard optimization algorithm by Arshaghi et al. (2019), the Black Widow Optimization Algorithm by Hayyolalam and Pourhaji Kazem (2020), and the Beluga whale optimization introduced by Zhong et al. (2022).

The references also cover innovative approaches such as the Binary whale optimization algorithm for electricity market challenges, collective animal behavior-inspired algorithms, and the Cheetah-based optimization paradigm presented by Klein et al. (2018). Furthermore, methods like the cricket behavior-based technique and cultural coyote optimization algorithm highlight the breadth of evolutionary computation techniques applied to engineering problems.

Overall, these studies contribute significantly to the field of optimization by leveraging nature-inspired methodologies to address complex challenges across various domains.

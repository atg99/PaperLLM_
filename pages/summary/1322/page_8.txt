The paper presents a classification of reviewed research papers based on their inspiration sources, utilizing a taxonomy that highlights the predominant themes in meta-heuristic algorithms. The classification reveals that swarm intelligence is the most significant category, encompassing 53.48% of the reviewed works. Other notable categories include social human behavior algorithms at 11.00%, physics and chemistry-based approaches at 14.67%, and various biological inspirations such as flying animals (16.41%), terrestrial animals (18.35%), and microorganisms (4.05%). Additionally, the paper discusses miscellaneous sources and breeding-based evolution, which account for smaller portions of the overall classification. This structured analysis emphasizes the diverse inspirations driving advancements in meta-heuristic methodologies.

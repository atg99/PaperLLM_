In "Maximum likelihood estimation of misspecified models," White discusses the challenges and methodologies related to estimating parameters in models that may not accurately represent the underlying data-generating process. He emphasizes the importance of understanding the implications of model misspecification and presents techniques for deriving consistent estimators despite such misspecifications.

Wooldridge's work, "Asymptotic properties of weighted M-estimators for standard stratified samples," explores the theoretical foundations of weighted M-estimators in the context of stratified sampling. He provides a rigorous analysis of the asymptotic properties of these estimators, demonstrating their efficiency and consistency under certain conditions. Wooldridge's findings contribute to the broader understanding of estimation techniques in econometrics, particularly in the context of stratified data.

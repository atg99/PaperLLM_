This paper discusses several assumptions related to sample size and moment conditions in a statistical framework. The first part of Assumption 4(ii) asserts that as the population size increases, the expected sample size also grows. The second part allows for the possibility that the sample size becomes a negligible fraction of the population size, incorporating EHW results where ρ=0 as a special case.

Assumption 5 introduces a regularity condition, stating that there exists a δ > 0 such that certain sequences of moments are uniformly bounded. Specifically, it considers the sequences involving the variables Yn,i, Xn,i, and Zn,i.

The notation Wn and Ωn is defined, where Wn represents a matrix based on the sample variables, and Ωn is the expectation of Wn over the distribution of Xn. Sample counterparts of these matrices, denoted as /tildewiderWn and /tildewideΩn, are also introduced. The paper indicates that certain matrices resulting from transformations will have zero entries.

The convergence results for the sample objects /tildewiderWn and /tildewideΩn are provided in Lemma 1, which states that under Assumptions 3-5, the sample matrices converge to their expected values as the sample size increases.

Finally, Assumption 6 ensures that the expected value of the second moments converges deterministically to a full rank matrix Ω as n increases.

The paper outlines various advancements in dialogue augmentation (DA) techniques aimed at enhancing dialogue generation and understanding. Gao et al. (2020) introduce the Paraphrase Augmented Response Generation (PARG) framework, which automatically creates augmented paraphrased training examples based on dialogue state and act labels to improve dialogue generation. Gritta et al. (2021) present a graph-based representation known as Conversation Graph (ConvGraph) that facilitates dialogue augmentation by enabling the creation of new dialogue paths. Yin et al. (2020) propose a reinforcement learning-based approach for dialogue state tracking (DST), while Song et al. (2020) offer a straightforward DA algorithm designed to enhance the training of copy-mechanism models for DST.

In the context of multimodal tasks, Huang et al. (2018) propose a DA method for emotion recognition that integrates audio, visual, and textual modalities. Mou et al. (2020) introduce a DA method tailored for Audio-Video Scene-Aware Dialogue, which focuses on dialogues that involve sequences of question-answer pairs about a video. Falcon et al. (2020) explore DA techniques for video question-answering, employing methods such as mirroring and horizontal flipping to enhance the quality of the dialogue. Overall, these works contribute to the field by providing innovative approaches to dialogue generation and understanding across various modalities.

5.10 Conclusion
In summary, data augmentation (DA) techniques have been widely explored across various natural language processing (NLP) tasks, including semantic parsing, grammatical error correction, neural machine translation, data-to-text natural language generation (NLG), open-ended generation, and dialogue systems. These methods aim to enhance the diversity and quantity of training data to improve model performance, especially in low-resource settings. Various strategies, such as synthetic example generation, perturbations, and noise injection, have proven effective in addressing challenges associated with limited data availability. Future research could further refine these techniques and explore their applicability in other NLP areas.

The paper presents a comprehensive survey of data augmentation (DA) approaches in natural language processing (NLP), emphasizing the growing need for these methods due to the increasing demand for training data in low-resource domains and various NLP tasks. Despite the challenges posed by the discrete nature of language data, which complicates traditional augmentation techniques from computer vision, there has been a notable rise in interest in DA for NLP.

The authors aim to provide a structured overview of the existing literature on DA by introducing its goals, methods, and applications. They categorize DA techniques into three main groups: rule-based methods, example interpolation-based methods, and model-based methods. The survey also highlights key applications of DA in NLP, such as addressing low-resource languages, mitigating bias, fixing class imbalance, enabling few-shot learning, and generating adversarial examples.

The paper concludes by outlining current challenges in the field and suggesting future research directions. Additionally, the authors provide a GitHub repository to continuously update the list of relevant papers, thereby facilitating further exploration and development in this area. Overall, this survey serves as a significant contribution to the NLP community, aiming to clarify the landscape of data augmentation and motivate additional research efforts.

This paper compiles a list of significant contributions in the field of data augmentation and its applications in natural language processing (NLP) and related tasks. The authors discuss various methodologies and approaches that leverage data augmentation to enhance model performance, including weakly supervised learning, adversarial training, and multimodal emotion recognition.

Key contributions include:

1. **Teaching Machines to Read** (Hermann et al., 2015) - This foundational work focuses on developing machine reading comprehension capabilities.
2. **Data Augmentation Techniques** (Hernández-García & König, 2020; Iyyer et al., 2018; Jia et al., 2016) - These studies explore different augmentation strategies, such as adversarial example generation and data recombination, to improve model robustness and performance.
3. **Weakly Supervised Learning** (Herzig et al., 2020) - This work presents TaPas, a method for table parsing that utilizes weak supervision to enhance data efficiency.
4. **Counterfactual Data Augmentation** (Kaushik et al., 2020; 2021) - These papers investigate how counterfactual examples can improve learning by highlighting the differences that contribute to model decisions.
5. **Multimodal and Sequence-to-Sequence Augmentation** (Hou et al., 2018; Huang et al., 2018) - These contributions focus on augmenting dialogue understanding and emotion recognition through sequence manipulation and multimodal inputs.

Overall, the collective research emphasizes the importance of data augmentation in addressing challenges in NLP, enhancing model capabilities, and improving generalization through diverse training examples.

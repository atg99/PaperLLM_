The paper presented at ITiCSE 2023 explores the potential of GPT models in computer science education, particularly in programming courses. The authors recognize the growing use of code generation tools like OpenAI's Codex, DeepMind's AlphaCode, and GitHub's Copilot, which are easily accessible to learners. The study aims to assess GPT's ability to complete diverse assessments in a Python programming course by addressing three research questions: 

1. The reliability of GPT in generating correct answers to multiple-choice questions (MCQs).
2. The capability of GPT to produce solutions to complex Python coding tasks from human-centric instructions.
3. The effectiveness of GPT in utilizing feedback from an auto-grader to improve its coding solutions.

The authors claim their work is the first comprehensive study evaluating GPT's performance across various assessment types used in real Python programming courses. They also highlight previous research evaluating GPT's accuracy on MCQs across different domains and its effectiveness in programming assignments. The study involves assessments from three specific Python programming courses, focusing on both MCQ-style evaluations and coding tasks requiring deeper understanding and application of programming concepts.

Overall, the paper aims to contribute valuable insights into the effectiveness of AI tools in educational settings and their impact on programming instruction and assessment.

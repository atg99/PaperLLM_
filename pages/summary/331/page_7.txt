The paper examines the capabilities of Generative Pre-trained Transformers (GPT) in passing assessments within higher education programming courses. It explores how these AI models, particularly those trained on code, interact with programming tasks and their potential implications for education. The authors analyze existing literature on AI's performance in various assessments, including medical licensing exams and coding challenges, to evaluate GPT's effectiveness.

Key findings suggest that while GPT can generate code and offer solutions to programming problems, its performance is variable and often dependent on the complexity of the tasks. The paper discusses the challenges posed by AI in educational settings, including concerns about academic integrity and the need for revised teaching methodologies. It also highlights the importance of understanding how AI tools like GPT can be integrated into the learning process to enhance educational outcomes rather than undermine them.

The authors conclude that further research is necessary to fully understand the implications of using AI in programming education, emphasizing the need for a balanced approach that considers both the benefits and challenges of AI-assisted learning.

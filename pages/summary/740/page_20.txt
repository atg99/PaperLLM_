The paper by Zhou, Han, and Lu discusses numerical solutions for control problems using neural networks, specifically focusing on the Van der Pol problem and the Eikonal equation. The authors present a real-valued function for the potential, with parameters a2 and a3 that must satisfy certain conditions. The true solution of the PDE is explicitly defined, and an optimal control is derived.

The study highlights the challenges posed by control constraints, particularly in ensuring that the control remains within a unit ball. To address this, the authors propose a neural network structure that outputs a specific vector format rather than the control directly. This method allows for effective representation of the control while avoiding numerical singularities.

The paper includes numerical tests using different dimensions (d=4, 10, 20) and compares the performance of the proposed method against a straightforward parameterization with an additional penalty term. The results indicate that directly implementing the control constraints leads to better numerical performance.

Final error values for the value functions and controls are reported for various dimensions, demonstrating the effectiveness of the neural network approach in handling control constraints in high-dimensional settings. The authors also touch upon a variant of the Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR) with a non-constant diffusion coefficient, indicating the broader applicability of their methods.

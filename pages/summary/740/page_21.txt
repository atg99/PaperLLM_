The paper presents an Actor-Critic method to solve high-dimensional static Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman (HJB) equations, specifically focusing on a fully nonlinear partial differential equation (PDE). The method is demonstrated through numerical experiments involving the Eikonal equation in various dimensions (d = 5, 10, and 20). 

The HJB equation is formulated, with a corresponding stochastic differential equation (SDE) defined for the control process. The true value function and optimal control are derived, showcasing the relationship between the variables involved. 

Results from the numerical experiments indicate that the algorithm effectively approximates the value functions and controls with final errors reported as 9.98e-3 and 1.63e-2 in 5 dimensions, 1.50e-2 and 4.95e-2 in 10 dimensions, and 1.96e-2 and 5.25e-2 in 20 dimensions. The findings highlight the capability of the proposed method to accurately tackle fully nonlinear elliptic PDEs in high-dimensional settings, validating its effectiveness in solving complex control problems.

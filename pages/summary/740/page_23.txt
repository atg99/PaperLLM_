This paper discusses the actor-critic method applied to high-dimensional static Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman (HJB) equations, focusing on its effectiveness in solving complex parabolic partial differential equations (PDEs) and backward stochastic differential equations (BSDEs). The authors explore various algorithms that leverage machine learning techniques, including deep learning and Monte Carlo methods, to address challenges posed by high-dimensional settings. 

The actor-critic approach combines policy-based and value-based methods, offering a robust framework for reinforcement learning and optimal control problems. The paper reviews recent advancements in numerical methods for HJB PDEs, highlighting the use of deep neural networks and polynomial approximations to mitigate the curse of dimensionality. Additionally, the authors reference significant works in the field, establishing a comprehensive background on the algorithms and their applications in finance, game theory, and stochastic control.

Key contributions include discussions on convergence rates, algorithm efficiency, and the potential for these methods to enhance existing solutions to high-dimensional control problems. The paper underscores the importance of ongoing research in integrating machine learning with traditional numerical methods to improve the handling of complex systems governed by HJB equations.

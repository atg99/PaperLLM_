In this paper, the authors propose numerical methods for high-dimensional static Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman (HJB) equations utilizing neural network parametrization within the actor-critic framework. They identify several avenues for future research: 

1. Testing the scalability of their methods on higher-dimensional problems.
2. Extending the methods to accommodate different boundary conditions, such as natural boundary conditions, and to a wider range of equations, including the porous medium equation, where the control formulation remains ambiguous.
3. Improving the numerical treatment of discretization of the functional derivative, moving beyond reliance on automatic differentiation.
4. Conducting theoretical analyses to evaluate convergence and error of the proposed numerical methods, which presents a significant challenge in the deep learning field.

The paper concludes by referencing various works that relate to their study, emphasizing the existing literature on deep learning applications in solving partial differential equations and reinforcement learning methodologies.

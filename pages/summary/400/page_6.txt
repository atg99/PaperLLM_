In this paper, the authors introduce the Generalized Intersection over Union (GIOU) as an enhancement over the traditional Intersection over Union (IOU) for measuring the overlap between predicted and actual bounding boxes in object detection. GIOU not only accounts for the overlapping area but also considers the non-overlapping regions, providing a more comprehensive assessment of the spatial relationship between the boxes. The GIOU value ranges from -1 to 1, where values greater than 0 indicate intersection, with 1 representing complete overlap. A GIOU of 0 signifies proximity without intersection, while negative values indicate no overlap.

The authors propose incorporating (1 - GIOU) into the network's loss function to improve the accuracy of the YOLO algorithm in object localization. L2 regularization is also applied to mitigate overfitting, with the total loss function defined to include both the primary loss and the regularization term. This approach leads to the development of the YOLO-UA model.

The dataset utilized for training and evaluation is the UA-DETRAC benchmark suite from the University at Albany, which contains 26,820 images of traffic scenes. These images were divided into subsets for training, validation, and testing, enhancing the model's adaptability to various conditions. The training validation set comprises 80% of the total dataset, with annotations generated using LabelImg to facilitate the training process.

The section discusses the evaluation of the YOLO-UA model for video traffic flow monitoring through a series of experiments. The model was tested using traffic videos recorded under various conditions, including different times of day and weather. The parameter NT was set to 0.08, while the CT value was optimized to achieve the best performance, resulting in an optimal CT of 0.78.

The results of the experiments, summarized in Tables 5 and 6, indicate significant improvements in accuracy when using the YOLO-UA model compared to the traditional YOLO model. Specifically, the YOLO-UA model achieved an accuracy of 100% for some videos, while the YOLO model had lower accuracy rates, such as 84.6%. The average vehicle counting time per frame was 30 ms, demonstrating the efficiency of the YOLO-UA model.

Key findings highlight the importance of camera positioning and field of view in achieving accurate vehicle detection. High camera positions and overly broad fields of view led to missed and incorrect detections. The study emphasizes the need for careful adjustment of these factors alongside the CT value to optimize statistical performance. Overall, the YOLO-UA model outperformed both the single-Gaussian model and the ViBe algorithm in terms of speed and accuracy.

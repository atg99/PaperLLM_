The paper discusses various algorithms used for traffic monitoring, comparing their accuracy and processing times. Notably, the YOLO algorithm, while optimized with GIOU, addresses challenges in vehicle positioning when vehicles are close together, achieving high accuracy in traffic flow statistics. The YOLO-UA model, however, struggles in rainy conditions, demonstrating a low recall rate. To enhance performance, the authors propose integrating a deep residual network, applying focal loss for category imbalances, and utilizing batch normalization during training. The study emphasizes the reliability of the optimized YOLO model across diverse scenarios and weather conditions, presenting it as a valuable tool for traffic monitoring. The authors contributed collaboratively to the investigation, methodology, analysis, and writing of the paper.

This paper presents a high-performance modification of the You Only Look Once (YOLO) algorithm, termed YOLO-UA, designed for real-time traffic flow monitoring within intelligent transportation systems. The study utilizes deep convolutional neural networks to analyze traffic data across various scenarios and weather conditions, aiming to inform transportation departments for better decision-making regarding road expansions or vehicle access restrictions to mitigate congestion and accidents.

The YOLO-UA model enhances real-time traffic detection and statistics by refining the network structure, optimizing the loss function, and introducing weight regularization. It is initialized with weights from a YOLO model pre-trained on the VOC2007 dataset and trained on the UA-CAR dataset, which includes complex weather conditions. The results indicate that YOLO-UA outperforms the original YOLO model, achieving approximately 22% greater accuracy and a 21% increase in recall across various weather scenarios. Notably, during video testing, the model demonstrated an impressive 100% accuracy in traffic statistics, processing each frame in under 30 milliseconds while maintaining robustness against environmental changes.

The findings underscore the potential of YOLO-UA in enhancing traffic flow monitoring, thereby contributing to the development of intelligent transportation systems that can significantly improve road safety and efficiency.

The paper introduces a novel deep Modular Co-Attention Network (MCAN) designed for Visual Question Answering (VQA). The MCAN is structured with a cascade of modular co-attention layers, which integrate self-attention and guided-attention units to effectively model interactions between different modalities. The study highlights the importance of attention mechanisms, as demonstrated through examples where learned attention maps accurately focus on relevant image regions and keywords in questions.

The paper provides a comparison of MCAN's performance against state-of-the-art methods, revealing that it significantly outperforms the BAN model by 1.1 points in overall accuracy. Furthermore, MCAN achieves comparable object counting performance without relying on auxiliary information, suggesting its robustness and capability to learn from visual features alone.

The authors conclude that the depth of the MCAN, achieved through stacking MCA layers using an encoder-decoder strategy, contributes to its state-of-the-art performance in VQA tasks. The research is supported by various grants, underscoring the collaborative effort behind the development of this advanced model.

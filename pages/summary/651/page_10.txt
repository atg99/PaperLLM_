The references listed pertain to significant contributions in the field of graph neural networks (GNNs) and their applications. 

1. Velickovic et al. introduced Graph Attention Networks in 2018, which utilize attention mechanisms to improve the representation of nodes in a graph.
2. Wu et al. proposed a simplification of graph convolutional networks in 2019, enhancing their efficiency while maintaining performance.
3. Xu et al. explored the capabilities of graph neural networks, assessing their expressive power and potential in various tasks.
4. Yang et al. presented a topology optimization method integrated with graph convolutional networks, showcasing advancements in structural design.
5. Yang, Cohen, and Salakhutdinov revisited semi-supervised learning approaches using graph embeddings, emphasizing their effectiveness in leveraging unlabeled data.
6. Yao et al. applied graph convolutional networks to text classification, demonstrating their versatility beyond traditional graph-related tasks.
7. Zeng et al. introduced GraphSaint, an inductive learning method based on graph sampling, which addresses scalability issues in GNNs.

These works collectively advance the understanding and application of GNNs across different domains, highlighting their adaptability and effectiveness in processing graph-structured data.

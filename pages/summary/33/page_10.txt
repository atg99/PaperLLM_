The referenced works focus on advancements in object detection using point clouds and related methodologies. Yan et al. (2018) introduce a sparsely embedded convolutional detection approach, emphasizing efficient processing of sparse data. Yang et al. (2018) present Pixor, a real-time 3D object detection framework leveraging point clouds, showcasing its speed and accuracy. The Ipod framework by Zetong et al. (2018) is highlighted for its intensive point-based detection capabilities, aimed at improving object detection from point cloud data. Zhang et al. (2017) discuss mixup, a technique that enhances model robustness beyond traditional empirical risk minimization. Lastly, Zhou and Tuzel (2018) propose VoxelNet, an end-to-end learning model for 3D object detection, integrating voxel-based representations for improved detection performance. Each of these contributions plays a significant role in the evolution of 3D object detection technologies.

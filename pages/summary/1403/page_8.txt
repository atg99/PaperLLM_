The paper presents FoveaBox, an innovative anchor-free framework for object detection. It emphasizes the simultaneous prediction of object positions and boundaries, eliminating the need for prior candidate boxes. The framework's effectiveness is demonstrated through extensive experiments on standard benchmarks, including the COCO and Pascal VOC datasets, where it shows competitive performance compared to existing models like RetinaNet. The authors highlight that FoveaBox serves as a robust baseline for future research in object detection, advocating for its potential to simplify and enhance detection tasks. The experimental results, including mean average precision metrics and speed comparisons, support the framework's efficacy and efficiency in various scenarios.

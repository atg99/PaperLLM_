In summary, the evaluation of explanation quality in AI models is a multifaceted area of research. Various approaches have been proposed, including perturbation analysis, the pointing game, task-specific evaluation schemes, and human judgment. Each method has its strengths and limitations. Perturbation analysis focuses on the decline in prediction scores after perturbing input variables, while the pointing game assesses the model’s ability to localize target objects based on heatmap relevance. Task-specific evaluations provide context-dependent measures, and human judgment offers qualitative insights but can be impractical for large datasets. The development of objective metrics, such as those proposed for sequential data, represents a promising direction for establishing a more standardized evaluation framework for explanation quality in AI.

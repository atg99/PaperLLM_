In this paper segment, the authors discuss the varying needs for explanations of AI systems depending on the recipient's background and intent. Users of AI technology may only require basic, easily interpretable explanations, such as highlighting relevant regions in image classification tasks. These coarse explanations can enhance user trust in AI. Conversely, AI researchers and developers seek comprehensive, high-resolution explanations, including negative evidence, to understand and improve model functionality. 

The authors also highlight the importance of tailored explanations in specific domains, such as healthcare. Patients may receive simplified explanations regarding their health data, while medical professionals require detailed insights into complex relationships between various parameters. Institutions may prefer global explanations that summarize trends across multiple cases.

Furthermore, the paper outlines four types of explanations, emphasizing the need to focus on specific types based on the recipient's objectives. One type discussed is the explanation of learned representations, which helps in understanding how neural networks encode concepts. This involves examining the role of individual or groups of neurons and creating prototypes that represent learned categories, such as generating an image of a car to illustrate what the model understands about that category.

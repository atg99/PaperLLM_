The paper by Samek and Müller emphasizes the significance of explainable artificial intelligence (AI) in overcoming challenges associated with the deployment of AI systems. While AI has made substantial advancements due to improvements in deep learning, large datasets, and enhanced computational power, certain obstacles still impede its widespread application. Key challenges include the complexity and energy demands of current deep learning models, vulnerability to adversarial attacks, and a lack of transparency and explainability, which undermines trust in AI decisions.

The authors focus on the necessity for transparent decision-making, highlighting how explainable AI can reveal the reasoning behind AI systems' conclusions. They reference a notable example from the historic Go match between Lee Sedol and AlphaGo, where AlphaGo's unconventional move, initially deemed non-human by experts, ultimately led to its victory. This illustrates the potential for AI to uncover novel strategies and insights in various fields, including healthcare and materials science, provided that these insights can be interpreted by humans.

The paper is structured as follows: Section 2 discusses the importance of transparency and trust in AI; Section 3 examines different types of explanations and their practical applications; Section 4 summarizes recent techniques in explainable AI, including surrogate functions and optimization approaches; Section 5 addresses the evaluation of explanation quality; and Section 6 concludes with a discussion of broader challenges in the field of explainable AI. Overall, the authors advocate for the development of explainable AI as a means to enhance understanding and foster trust in AI systems.

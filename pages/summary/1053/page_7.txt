The paper discusses various approaches to creating explainable artificial intelligence (AI), focusing on the necessity of understanding both individual predictions and overall model behavior. 

1. **Individual Predictions**: Techniques like heatmaps are highlighted as tools to visualize the most relevant pixels for a model's decisions, which aids in verifying predictions and building trust in the system's functionality. Layer-wise Relevance Propagation (LRP) is presented as a versatile method applicable to different machine learning models, including neural networks and Support Vector Machines, for generating individual prediction explanations.

2. **Model Behavior**: The paper emphasizes the importance of understanding model behavior beyond single predictions. Methods such as Spectral Relevance Analysis (SpRAy) cluster individual heatmaps to identify distinct prediction strategies. An example is given where four clusters representing different strategies for classifying images of horses were identified. This helps in gaining insights into the model's learned strategies and detecting misleading predictors.

3. **Representative Examples**: Another class of explanation methods focuses on identifying representative training examples, which can enhance understanding of the training dataset's influence on the model. This can also help in identifying data biases and improving model robustness.

4. **Role of Explanations**: The paper distinguishes between the intent of explanation methods and the user's intent. It highlights that the purpose of an explanation can vary significantly, whether it aims to clarify a specific prediction, visualize a model's understanding of a class, or compare predictions across alternatives. LRP is noted for its flexibility in addressing these various questions and adjusting the evidence presented in explanations.

In summary, the paper advocates for a comprehensive approach to explainable AI, emphasizing the need for methods that elucidate both individual predictions and broader model behaviors while considering the goals behind explanations.

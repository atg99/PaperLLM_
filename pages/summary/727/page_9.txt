The paper presents a novel meta-optimization approach, Meta-MAPG, designed for multiagent reinforcement learning (MARL). The core idea is to explicitly model an agent's learning process alongside the non-stationary policy dynamics of other agents. This dual modeling allows Meta-MAPG to adapt more efficiently than prior state-of-the-art methods when faced with the challenges of MARL. The authors demonstrate the effectiveness of their method through evaluations on multiple multiagent benchmarks, aiming to establish a theoretical foundation for addressing the inherent non-stationarity in MARL.

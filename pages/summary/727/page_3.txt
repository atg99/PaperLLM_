The paper presents a novel policy gradient algorithm aimed at enhancing learning in multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) environments by addressing the challenges posed by non-stationarity. It introduces the concept of a meta-agent that learns to adapt its policies in response to the changing policies of other agents in the environment.

Key points include:

1. **Sequential Dependency**: The learning process is viewed through a Markov chain framework, where future policy parameters depend sequentially on current parameters. This dependency signifies that changes in one agent's policy can influence subsequent joint policy updates.

2. **Controllable Non-Stationarity**: By adjusting hyperparameters, the rate at which agents modify their policies can be controlled. Smaller values of these hyperparameters lead to increased non-stationarity, affecting the learning dynamics among agents.

3. **Meta-Learning Framework**: The paper formulates a meta-optimization objective for MARL that focuses on maximizing the expected adaptation performance of the meta-agent's policy. This involves deriving a meta-multiagent policy gradient theorem that captures the effects of the meta-agent's current policy on its future adaptations and on the policies of its peers.

4. **Meta-Multiagent Policy Gradient Theorem**: The theorem provides a mathematical framework for understanding how a meta-agent can optimize its policy by considering both its own learning dynamics and those of its peers. It consists of three primary terms: the standard policy gradient, a term for modeling the meta-agent's own learning, and a peer learning term that captures the influence of the meta-agent's policy on the future policies of other agents.

Overall, the proposed algorithm aims to enhance the adaptability of agents in dynamic environments, enabling more effective collaboration and competition among multiple learning agents. This approach builds on existing literature while introducing significant advancements in handling non-stationarity in MARL settings.

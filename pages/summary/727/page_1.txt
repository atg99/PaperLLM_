2) Meta-learning framework: We propose a meta-learning framework that leverages the newly derived Meta-MAPG theorem to enhance the learning process of agents in MARL settings. This framework enables agents to optimize their policies not only based on their experiences but also by anticipating the adaptations of other agents.

3) Empirical validation: We conduct extensive experiments across a range of multiagent benchmarks to validate the effectiveness of our proposed method. The results demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms baseline methods in adapting to new agents, showcasing improved performance in mixed incentive, competitive, and cooperative environments.

In summary, our work highlights the importance of considering the interactive learning processes in MARL and provides a robust solution through the integration of meta-learning principles with a novel policy gradient approach. This advancement contributes to the ongoing efforts to develop more effective multiagent systems capable of navigating complex, dynamic environments.

In the paper by Dai and Wipf, the authors define a function F that maps from R^r to [0,1]^r, expressed as a vector of functions F1, F2, ..., Fr. Additionally, the authors introduce another differentiable and invertible function G. The decoder mean function is defined in terms of the inverse of F and G, which leads to the formulation of a probability density function p_t(x) expressed as an integral over R^r.

The main result shows that the probability density p_t(x) diverges to infinity as t approaches infinity for any given x. This is demonstrated by considering the closed ball around a specific point u* and applying the Lagrangian mean value theorem to relate the behavior of the function to a perturbation around u*. The paper concludes with a mathematical expression that captures the divergence behavior, utilizing properties of the functions involved and their derivatives.

The paper by Dai and Wipf presents a comparative analysis of different Variational Autoencoder (VAE) configurations using randomly generated samples from three distinct datasets: MNIST, Fashion-MNIST, and CIFAR-10. The figures illustrate three different VAE setups: (a) VAE with a fixed parameter, (b) VAE with a learnable parameter, and (c) a 2-Stage VAE. Each configuration is evaluated without selective sampling, ensuring unbiased representation of their performance across the datasets. The results highlight the strengths and weaknesses of each VAE variant in generating diverse samples, contributing to a deeper understanding of their capabilities in handling various types of data.

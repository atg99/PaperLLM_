In Phase 2, we implemented three variations of the user interface to test the effectiveness of local explanations. The first variation involved template-based questions paired with template-based explanations. The second variation maintained template-based questions but provided example-based explanations. The third variation shifted to example-based questions and explanations.

Results from this phase indicated that the type of explanation provided significantly influenced participant accuracy in predictions. Participants tended to perform better when given example-based explanations, as these offered more concrete context compared to abstract template-based explanations. This suggests that localized, specific examples can enhance understanding and improve prediction accuracy in complex systems like natural language inference (NLI).

Overall, the studies revealed that while RoBERTa outperformed BERT and DistilBERT in various reasoning tasks, the effectiveness of user interface design and explanation granularity plays a crucial role in participant engagement and accuracy in evaluating model performance. These findings highlight the importance of tailored explanations in facilitating better comprehension of AI systems.

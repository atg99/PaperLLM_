The paper discusses the development of natural language inference (NLI) templates that facilitate the understanding of linguistic and logical reasoning. It emphasizes the importance of recognizing both semantic and pragmatic aspects of language as outlined in linguistic literature. The authors categorize reasoning tasks into three main groups: linguistic, logical, and knowledge-based reasoning, aligning these with existing NLI datasets. They highlight the predominance of deductive reasoning in most NLI datasets and propose an extensible framework for template creation that can accommodate a wider range of reasoning types. The paper includes various examples and templates to illustrate these reasoning categories, aiming to enhance the capabilities of NLI systems in understanding and processing natural language.

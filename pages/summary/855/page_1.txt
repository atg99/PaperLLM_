This paper proposes a decentralized framework called Fair and Privacy-Preserving Deep Learning (FPPDL) to enhance fairness and privacy in federated learning (FL) models. The authors identify shortcomings in current FL paradigms, which provide all participants with the same final model, regardless of their contributions. This approach can lead to issues such as free-riding, where some participants benefit without adequately contributing. 

To address these challenges, the FPPDL framework introduces a local credibility mutual evaluation mechanism that ensures fairness by tailoring model versions to each participant based on their contributions. Additionally, a three-layer onion-style encryption scheme is implemented to maintain both accuracy and privacy during collaborative training. 

The experiments conducted on benchmark datasets demonstrate that FPPDL effectively balances fairness, privacy, and accuracy, fostering a healthier FL ecosystem by detecting and isolating low-contribution participants. The findings suggest that the proposed framework can encourage responsible participation among diverse parties, ultimately improving the robustness of federated learning systems.

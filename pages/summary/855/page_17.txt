The paper presents a novel framework for privacy-preserving collaborative deep learning (FPPDL) that addresses key challenges in server-based systems by leveraging Blockchain technology for decentralization. It introduces the concept of collaborative fairness in deep learning, characterized by local credibility and transaction points that evolve through initial benchmarking and ongoing updates during the learning process. The framework effectively combines Differentially Private Generative Adversarial Networks (DPGAN) with a three-layer onion-style encryption scheme, ensuring both model accuracy and data privacy. Moreover, it includes mechanisms to identify and mitigate the influence of low-contribution participants within the system.

Experimental results indicate that FPPDL achieves comparable accuracy to both centralized and distributed selective Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) frameworks without differential privacy, while consistently outperforming standalone frameworks. This confirms the viability of the proposed system in practical applications.

Future research directions include quantifying fairness in non-IID settings, exploring malicious behaviors such as Byzantine or Sybil attacks, and expanding the deployment of the framework across various real-world scenarios.

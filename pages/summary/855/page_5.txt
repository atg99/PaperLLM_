The FPPDL framework is designed to address two primary objectives: privacy preservation and fairness in model training. Privacy is maintained through the use of Differentially Private GANs, which allow parties to share anonymized data samples rather than original data or model parameters. Additionally, a three-layer onion-style encryption method is employed to secure the gradients shared during the collaborative training process.

Fairness is ensured by implementing a local credibility mutual evaluation system where participants earn points based on their contributions. These points can be used to trade information, thereby incentivizing higher contributions. The credibility of each party is recorded, influencing the likelihood of receiving gradients from others. This mechanism promotes a fair distribution of rewards based on contribution levels, allowing high-contribution parties to obtain better-performing models. 

Furthermore, the integration of a private Blockchain enables a decentralized architecture, enhancing transparency and auditability of transactions related to information sharing. The use of smart contracts facilitates the automatic enforcement of protocols, ensuring that all parties retain control over their data while participating in the collaborative training environment. 

Overall, the FPPDL framework represents a significant advancement in decentralized deep learning, balancing privacy and fairness while leveraging Blockchain technology for improved collaboration among parties.

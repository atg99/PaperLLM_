The appendix outlines the notation and definitions used in the paper. It specifies that bold characters (x, y, z) represent random vectors or collections of random variables, while italic characters (x, y, z) denote random scalars, or single real-valued numbers. Bold capitalized letters (X, Y, Z) are used for random matrices. The notation Pa(z) refers to the parents of a random variable z within a directed graph. Additionally, diag(x) indicates a diagonal matrix with the values of vector x on its diagonal.

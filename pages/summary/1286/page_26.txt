The appendix provides definitions and notations relevant to probabilistic modeling and variational parameters. Key points include:

- Element-wise multiplication of two vectors is denoted as x⊙y, resulting in a vector of the form (x1y1,...,xKyK)T.
- Parameters of generative models are represented by the Greek letter θ (theta), while variational parameters are indicated by the bold Greek letter φ (phi).
- Probability density functions (PDFs) and probability mass functions (PMFs) are denoted by p(.) and q(.), with joint distributions represented as p(.,.) and conditional distributions as p(.|.).
- The notation p(.;θ) or pθ(x) signifies the parameters of a distribution.
- The use of equality and inequality signs within probability distributions helps differentiate between function arguments and specific values, such as p(x=a) for a PDF evaluated at a and p(x≤a) for a cumulative distribution function (CDF) evaluated at a.
- Different letters are employed to refer to various probabilistic models, while the same letter indicates related marginals or conditionals.

These notations and definitions are essential for understanding the structure and parameters of probabilistic models discussed in the document.

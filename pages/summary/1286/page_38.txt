The references listed provide a comprehensive overview of significant contributions to deep learning, particularly in the context of variational inference and generative models. Key papers by Kingma and collaborators introduce foundational techniques such as Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes, variational dropout, and improved variational inference methods, which have advanced the field of semi-supervised learning and optimization. Additionally, works by Kipf and Welling on variational graph auto-encoders and Kusner et al. on grammar variational autoencoders demonstrate the versatility of these methods in handling complex data structures. Other notable contributions include the application of structured inference networks for state space models and automatic differentiation in variational inference, highlighting the growing intersection of machine learning and probabilistic graphical models. The collective research emphasizes the evolution of generative modeling techniques and their applications across various domains.

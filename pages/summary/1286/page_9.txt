The paper "An Introduction to Variational Autoencoders" by Diederik P. Kingma and Max Welling provides a comprehensive overview of variational autoencoders (VAEs), which are a significant framework for learning deep latent-variable models and their associated inference models. The authors discuss the foundational concepts of VAEs, including their architecture, the variational inference process, and the reparameterization trick, which allows for efficient gradient-based optimization. Additionally, the paper explores various extensions and applications of VAEs, highlighting their versatility in generative modeling and unsupervised learning tasks. Overall, the work serves as a foundational resource for understanding VAEs and their implications in machine learning.

The section discusses the integration of directed graphical models with neural networks, highlighting the scalability of deep learning to large models and datasets. It introduces deep neural networks as vector functions, denoting them as NeuralNet(.), which have proven effective in various classification and regression tasks, as referenced in earlier works by LeCun and Goodfellow. In neural network-based image classification, a neural network parameterizes a categorical distribution over class labels conditioned on input images.

The paper further explains directed probabilistic graphical models (PGMs), which organize variables into a directed acyclic graph. The joint distribution of these models is expressed as a product of prior and conditional distributions, where each conditional probability can traditionally be represented by lookup tables or linear models. However, the text advocates for a more flexible approach by using neural networks to parameterize these conditional distributions, allowing them to process inputs from the parent variables in the directed graph.

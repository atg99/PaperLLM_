The section discusses two Bayesian optimization approaches for maximizing the likelihood in Deep Latent Variable Models (DLVMs): MCMC-EM and Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD). 

MCMC-EM involves approximating the posterior using a mixture of independent samples from a Markov chain. The computation of posterior gradients is manageable due to the differentiability of the log-joint distribution with respect to the latent variables. The Hamiltonian MCMC method is highlighted for its fast convergence, although it necessitates running an independent MCMC chain for each data point.

SGD focuses on directed models where the objective is scalar and differentiable with respect to parameters. Utilizing reverse-mode automatic differentiation allows for efficient computation of values and gradients. The parameters are updated iteratively based on an unbiased estimate of the objective function, with the learning rate and a random variable representing data point selection or noise influencing the updates. The methodology enables effective training of models using stochastic samples.

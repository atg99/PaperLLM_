The section discusses the Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) optimization methods, specifically focusing on Adam and Adamax, as proposed by Kingma and Ba in 2015. These optimization techniques are characterized by their invariance to constant rescaling of the objective function and the individual gradients. Consequently, the objective ˜L(θ,ξ) only needs to maintain an unbiased nature up to proportionality. The optimization process involves iteratively applying the relevant equations until a predetermined stopping criterion is achieved. A practical and effective criterion for halting the optimization is early stopping, which occurs when the probability of a holdout dataset begins to decline.

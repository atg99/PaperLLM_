The review begins with an overview of GAN's origins and its fundamental components: the generator and discriminator, both implemented via deep neural networks. It highlights the adversarial training mechanism that allows GAN to learn complex data distributions effectively. The paper emphasizes GAN's rapid rise in popularity since its introduction in 2014 and its significant impact on artificial intelligence research.

Key applications of GAN are discussed, particularly in digital image processing and medical imaging analysis, including Cycle-GAN techniques. The review details how GAN can facilitate data augmentation, enhance image quality, and perform style transformations, which are crucial in fields with limited datasets. Additionally, the authors explore GAN's role in medical informatics and bioinformatics, specifically in simulating omic data and gene expression.

The review concludes by underscoring the transformative potential of GAN in biomedical informatics, encouraging further exploration and innovation in this domain. Overall, the authors effectively argue that GANs are not only powerful generative models but also foundational to advancing deep learning methodologies in various biomedical applications.

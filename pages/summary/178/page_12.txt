The paper by Lan et al. discusses the application of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) in biomedical informatics. It highlights the potential of GANs as generators in various biomedical applications, while also addressing the challenges associated with their use, particularly the issue of poor interpretability. The authors contributed to the manuscript through literature review and collaborative writing, with specific roles assigned for drafting and revisions. They acknowledge the support received from various funding programs and institutions that facilitated their research.

The references cited provide a comprehensive background on GANs, covering their fundamentals, advancements, and specific applications in image synthesis and analysis, especially in the medical field. The paper emphasizes the significance of understanding and improving GAN methodologies to enhance their effectiveness and applicability in biomedical contexts. Overall, the work aims to shed light on the current state and future directions of GAN research in the realm of healthcare and medical data analysis.

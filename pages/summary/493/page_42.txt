This paper discusses the conditional distribution P(z|v) in the context of a specific model, where the mode is identified as z = max(0, I - c). The mean of this distribution approximates a softplus activation function, expressed as hzi = I - c + √(2/π) exp[-(I - c)²/2] / [1 + erf((I - c)/√2)], with the error function erf(x) playing a critical role in the formulation.

The paper further details the derivation of the cumulant generating function K(q) from the conditional distribution, leading to the expression K(q) = q²/2 - qc + log[1 + erf((q - c)/√2)] - log[1 - erf(c/√2)]. Numerical experiments are mentioned, comparing pairwise terms from a mapping equation against simpler expressions valid in small parameter limits. To find interaction parameters, the second cumulant of the distribution is crucial.

In the context of a step activation function, the paper notes that a standard Restricted Boltzmann Machine (RBM) is derived from a specific potential function, where hidden nodes are binary variables. The conditional probability in this case is modeled as a sigmoid function, indicating a transition to binary outcomes based on the inputs and interaction parameters.

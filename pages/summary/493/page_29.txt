The paper presents an analysis of interaction strengths induced by Restricted Boltzmann Machines (RBMs) with different non-linear activation functions, specifically focusing on ReLU, Exponential, and Step functions. The study evaluates the typical values of interaction strength as a function of interaction order for two different sizes of RBMs, M = 400 and M = 1200. 

Figures illustrate the logarithm of the root mean square values of interaction parameters, categorized by field parameters (s=1), pairwise interactions (s=2), and three-body interactions (s=3). The findings indicate that triplet interactions exhibit notable differences between activation functions, which may be attributed to the increased noise associated with higher-order interactions. 

The results show that interaction strengths are generally smaller for ReLU and Exponential functions compared to the Step function. The analysis intentionally excludes bias terms to facilitate a direct comparison of the contributions from RBM weight parameters across different interaction orders. This comparison highlights the varying effectiveness of the activation functions in inducing interaction strengths within the RBM framework.

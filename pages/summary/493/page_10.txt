The paper discusses the simplification of finding interaction parameters for separable potentials by calculating a one-dimensional integral to derive the generating functional K(q). The generating functional is expressed in terms of a potential U, which is normalized through a partition function. The study focuses on Restricted Boltzmann Machines (RBMs) utilizing various activation functions, including Linear, ReLU, Step, and Exponential. 

The activation function is defined as the mode of the conditional probability distribution of hidden nodes given visible nodes. Using Bayes' rule, the conditional probability P(z|v) is computed, leading to a factorization over hidden nodes for separable potentials. The paper presents the conditional probability of each hidden node as a function of the visible activity. The mode of this distribution is derived, with specific expressions provided for the cumulant generating function in an appendix.

The authors evaluate the interaction terms using the cumulant generating function and analyze the parametrization of these terms within the RBMs. The section concludes by outlining both the differences and similarities in the parametrization of interaction terms across different cases, emphasizing the importance of the relationship between hidden and visible nodes through the total input to the hidden nodes.

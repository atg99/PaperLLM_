In summary, the paper investigates the behavior of Restricted Boltzmann Machines (RBMs) concerning different hidden layer configurations and their impact on the marginal distribution of visible nodes. By employing a framework akin to statistical physics spin models, the study derives an explicit expression for these distributions and analyzes the significance of various interaction orders. The findings indicate that RBMs exhibit a hierarchy of interactions, with lower order interactions being more stable, particularly under conditions that generally hold in simulations. This work contributes to understanding how different activation functions influence the estimation of interactions within RBMs.

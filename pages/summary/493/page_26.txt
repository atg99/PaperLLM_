The paper investigates the pairwise interactions induced on visible nodes by different non-linear activation functions, specifically ReLU, Exponential, and Step functions, for varying hidden layer sizes (M = 100, 400, 1200). Key findings indicate that the pairwise interactions across different activation functions exhibit a linear relationship, particularly evident when the hidden layer size M is at least 400. The contributions to the field, as outlined in Equation (15a), stem from two terms: the bias term, which is independent of the activation function, and a second term that is influenced by the activation function through parameter K. When parameters are small, the bias term's significance increases, resulting in greater similarity of induced fields among the activation functions. However, higher order parameters are more sensitive to statistical errors in Restricted Boltzmann Machine (RBM) parameter estimation, leading to variations among activation functions for these terms.

Overall, the paper concludes that the behavior of non-linear activation functions in terms of pairwise interactions can be approximated in specific limits, with implications for understanding their performance across different configurations.

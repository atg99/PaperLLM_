The paper discusses the generating function K(W|v) expressed as a sum of mutually exclusive terms. It begins with a formulation that separates K(W|v) into distinct components based on the indices of the weight matrix W| and the variable v. The equation is structured to highlight the contributions from different terms, where K(0) serves as a base case.

Subsequently, the terms are expanded, allowing for a more detailed breakdown of the relationships between the indices. This leads to a rearrangement of the summation order, enabling symmetry in the permutations of indices. The authors emphasize the equivalence in summation strategies, which allows for a more comprehensive representation of the function.

The final form of K(W|v) is presented as a sum over combinations of indices, where I(s) represents a secondary summation that incorporates alternating signs and further weights from the weight matrix. This formulation aims to provide a clearer understanding of the contributions from various index combinations to the generating function, highlighting the complexity and interdependencies within the system.

This paper investigates the mean activation and mode of hidden nodes in a restricted Boltzmann machine (RBM) across different activation functions: Linear, ReLU, Step, and Exponential. The analysis focuses on the conditional probability density P(z|v) and identifies the mode of the distribution as the activation function.

Equations (18a) through (18d) present the pairwise interaction matrices for each activation function. The Linear activation function's interaction matrix mirrors the structure of a Hopfield model's connectivity matrix. Notably, this similarity also emerges with other activation functions in the limit of small weights.

The paper references previous works by Martens and Sutskever (2010) and Barra et al. (2012) to support the findings regarding the structure of these matrices. The exploration of activation functions reveals critical insights into the behavior of RBMs, particularly in relation to their underlying mathematical properties.

EPC is recognized as a leader in the development of eGaN FETs, which are advanced gallium nitride devices known for their efficiency and performance in power applications. The company offers integrated solutions that extend beyond traditional discrete devices, providing options such as full-bridge configurations with built-in drivers and level shifting capabilities. This innovation enhances system design by simplifying integration and improving overall efficiency. Additionally, EPC provides discrete FETs paired with drivers, catering to various applications that require flexibility and high power density. The focus on integrated solutions positions EPC at the forefront of power electronics technology.

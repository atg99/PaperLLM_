The paper discusses the advantages and considerations of using eGaN FETs from Efficient Power Conversion Corporation. While eGaN FETs share similarities with traditional MOSFETs, they offer enhanced high-frequency capabilities, which necessitate careful attention to circuit layout. The paper highlights the importance of implementing VGS regulation in gate drive circuitry due to the lower maximum gate voltage of 6 V. Additionally, the ultra-small LGA package design leads to increased heat concentration on the PCB, which is a critical factor for thermal management. Overall, while eGaN FETs provide significant benefits, they require specific design considerations to optimize performance.

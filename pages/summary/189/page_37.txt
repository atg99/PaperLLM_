The paper discusses the transition from traditional silicon-based technologies to eGaN FETs, emphasizing the advantages of eGaN technology. It argues that silicon is reaching its limits in performance and efficiency, making eGaN FETs a superior alternative for various applications. This shift marks the beginning of a new era in power electronics, where eGaN FETs offer enhanced efficiency, smaller size, and improved thermal performance. The document positions EPC as a leader in this innovative technology, highlighting its potential to revolutionize the industry.

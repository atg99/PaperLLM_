The paper from Efficient Power Conversion Corporation discusses the key criteria for evaluating the effectiveness of eGaN FETs. It emphasizes the importance of enabling significant new capabilities in electronic applications. The ease of use is highlighted as a critical factor for adoption. Cost-effectiveness is also essential, ensuring that users gain substantial value from the technology. Finally, reliability is underscored as a fundamental requirement for user trust and long-term implementation. These criteria collectively define the potential impact and success of eGaN technology in the market.

The paper discusses the key criteria that define the effectiveness of new technologies, particularly focusing on eGaN FETs developed by Efficient Power Conversion Corporation. It emphasizes four critical questions to assess the technology's impact: 

1. Does it enable significant new capabilities?
2. Is it easy to use?
3. Is it very cost-effective for the user?
4. Is it reliable?

These criteria serve as a framework for evaluating the potential of eGaN FETs in enhancing efficiency and performance in various applications. The paper positions EPC as a leader in this technology, highlighting its commitment to overcoming barriers in power conversion.

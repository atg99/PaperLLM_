The document from Efficient Power Conversion Corporation highlights the advantages of eGaN FETs compared to traditional MOSFETs in power electronic applications. It presents efficiency data for various output currents, demonstrating that eGaN FETs consistently achieve higher efficiency levels across different voltage ratings (38 V, 48 V, and 60 V) when compared to their MOSFET counterparts.

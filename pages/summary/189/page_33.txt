This document discusses the reliability and performance of eGaN® FETs produced by Efficient Power Conversion Corporation (EPC). It presents data from various stress tests conducted on different models, specifically focusing on key parameters such as R DS(ON), V GS(TH), Idss, and normalized efficiency over time under specific conditions.

The results indicate that the EPC2001 model maintains a stable R DS(ON) and V GS(TH) after undergoing high-temperature reverse bias (HTRB) testing at 125°C, demonstrating its reliability. The document also highlights the Idss of the EPC2015 model after H3TRB at 40V, showing compliance with the datasheet specification of a maximum of 500 µA.

Furthermore, the efficiency of the EPC9001 model is analyzed after operational life testing at 85°C, indicating a consistent performance over the stress hours. The data underscores the robustness of EPC's eGaN® FETs, reinforcing their position as leaders in the market for high-performance semiconductor solutions.

Efficient Power Conversion Corporation, led by CEO Alex Lidow, emphasizes its leadership in the development of eGaN® FETs, which are gallium nitride transistors designed for efficient power conversion. The paper discusses the advancements and ongoing journey of eGaN® technology, highlighting its benefits in improving power efficiency across various applications. The focus is on the performance, reliability, and potential of GaN transistors to revolutionize power electronics, positioning EPC as a key player in this evolving field.

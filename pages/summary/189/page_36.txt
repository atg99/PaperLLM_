The paper discusses the advantages of eGaN FETs, highlighting their ease of use alongside the necessity for caution due to their higher switching speeds compared to traditional power MOSFETs. It argues that eGaN FETs will serve as a superior replacement for silicon power MOSFETs in power conversion applications, offering a more cost-effective and efficient solution. Additionally, the development of higher voltage devices and the integration of analog and power functionalities are expected to further improve the performance and cost-effectiveness of eGaN FETs.

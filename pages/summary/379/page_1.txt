To validate our proposed scaling strategy, we applied it to existing architectures like MobileNets and ResNet, demonstrating significant improvements in both accuracy and efficiency. Furthermore, we utilized neural architecture search to create a new baseline network architecture, which we then scaled using our compound method to produce a series of models called EfficientNets. 

The EfficientNet-B7 model achieved a state-of-the-art top-1 accuracy of 84.4% and top-5 accuracy of 97.1% on the ImageNet dataset while being significantly smaller and faster than previous models, specifically 8.4 times smaller and 6.1 times faster than the best existing ConvNet. Our EfficientNets also showed strong transfer learning performance, achieving high accuracy on several additional datasets, including CIFAR-100 and Flowers, with far fewer parameters than competing models.

In conclusion, this research provides a systematic approach to model scaling in ConvNets that not only enhances accuracy but also optimizes resource utilization, paving the way for more efficient neural network architectures.

The document presents a comprehensive overview of datasets used for Multi-Domain Adaptation (MDA) across various tasks, primarily in computer vision (CV) and natural language processing (NLP). It categorizes these datasets based on their application, including digit recognition, object classification, sentiment classification, vehicle counting, and semantic segmentation. 

In the digit recognition category, the Digits-five dataset features diverse digit images from different sources, including handwritten and synthetic formats. For object classification, notable datasets include Office-31, Office-Caltech, Office-Home, ImageCLEF, PACS, and DomainNet, each containing images from various domains and object categories. 

Sentiment classification is explored through datasets like SentiImage, which focuses on binary sentiment across different image domains. Vehicle counting is represented by the WebCamT dataset, which compiles vehicle data from city cameras. Scene segmentation is addressed with the Sim2RealSeg dataset, incorporating both synthetic and real-world data for pixel-wise predictions.

The paper also outlines the classification of MDA problems based on the availability of labeled samples and the nature of domain adaptation, such as strongly supervised, weakly supervised, multi-target, and zero-shot MDA. Overall, the document highlights the importance of diverse datasets in evaluating MDA models and advancing research in this field.

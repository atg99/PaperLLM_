The paper discusses an accelerated stochastic gradient method that achieves minimax optimal statistical risk at a faster rate than traditional stochastic gradient descent. The authors express gratitude to referees for their constructive feedback, which enhanced the quality of the work. It cites numerous references related to boosting algorithms, optimization methods, and statistical learning, highlighting key contributions from various researchers in the field. Notable references include works by Breiman on random forests and boosting, Freund and Schapire on boosting algorithms, and Friedman on gradient boosting methods. The paper emphasizes the importance of these foundational studies in advancing machine learning techniques.

In this research, a novel hybrid model, GRU_RF, was developed for predicting production in the X well block, incorporating multiple external factors. The study details a comprehensive data preprocessing and modeling approach that can be adapted for other reservoirs. Key findings include:

1. The GRU_RF model demonstrated strong potential for long-term production forecasting of newly developed wells, showing satisfactory generalization to wells not included in the training set.
2. By incorporating variable external features such as SI and CS, the four-feature model significantly outperformed the conventional one-feature model, effectively capturing complex variance patterns.
3. The analysis revealed that production predictions were notably influenced by formation and fracturing parameters, emphasizing the importance of integrating both statistical and dynamic features in time-series predictions.
4. The proposed model surpassed traditional methods, including DCA and various time-series techniques, enabling batch processing and continuous learning for real-time predictions despite limited production data.

In this paper, the authors explore the architecture of a Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) model using the Keras deep learning library with a TensorFlow backend. The model comprises input, GRU, and dense layers, focusing on performance rather than hyperparameter optimization. The GRU layer contains 32 neurons, sufficient for capturing implicit relationships in the data. Weight matrices are initialized using the Xavier method, while bias matrices are set to zero. The ReLU activation function is employed in the GRU layer. Additionally, the paper presents a diagram illustrating three prediction schemes: SingleStep, Iterative, and PartIterative approaches, highlighting their respective structures.

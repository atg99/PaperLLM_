The paper presents a study on the application of machine learning techniques for the detection of COVID-19 through image analysis. It includes a detailed examination of the performance of various algorithms in classifying infected and non-infected images. Key findings are illustrated in figures such as a confusion matrix, which provides insights into the accuracy and reliability of the predictions made by the models. The study emphasizes the importance of effective image classification in the early detection of COVID-19, ultimately contributing to better public health responses. The results underscore the potential of leveraging advanced computational methods in medical diagnostics.

RT-PCR's longer processing time and lower sensitivity compared to CT imaging pose significant challenges, especially for critically ill patients. The research proposes using convolutional neural networks (CNN), specifically DenseNet-121, to analyze CT images for COVID-19 detection. DenseNet-121 is advantageous due to its ability to mitigate vanishing gradient issues, enhance feature reuse, and reduce parameter requirements, making it suitable for deep learning in medical imaging.

The study aims to provide reliable measures for identifying COVID-19 patients through CT image classification. It emphasizes the novelty of applying DenseNet-121 combined with data augmentation techniques, marking a significant advancement in sensitivity for detecting COVID-19 compared to traditional methods. The research includes extensive experimental analyses of DenseNet-121's performance across various metrics, addressing the literature gap by utilizing a larger dataset than previous studies.

The paper is structured to first discuss related works in deep learning for image classification, followed by the methodology and dataset used. The results and discussions section presents findings from the experiments, concluding with insights on the study's limitations and future research directions. Overall, the proposed approach is aimed at enhancing rapid diagnosis and treatment decisions in emergency settings for COVID-19 patients.

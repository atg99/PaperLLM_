The paper discusses the implementation of two performance metrics, F-measure and G-Mean, alongside accuracy for evaluating a DenseNet-121-based CNN model aimed at classifying CT images for COVID-19 detection. F-measure serves as a weighted average of precision and recall, while G-Mean assesses the balance of recall between the two classes, highlighting potential bias in model predictions.

The DenseNet-121 model was trained for 50 epochs on grayscale images. Results indicate that the model misclassified 23 COVID-19 images out of 1252 and 15 non-COVID-19 images out of 1230. Detailed performance metrics, including precision, recall, F1-score, and G-Mean for both classes, are presented, revealing a precision of 0.96 and recall of 0.85 for non-COVID-19 images, and a precision of 0.84 and recall of 0.95 for COVID-19 images. The overall accuracy and macro and weighted averages are consistently 0.92 across metrics. The paper highlights comparisons with other studies that utilized various imaging modalities, emphasizing the reliability of DenseNet-121 in COVID-19 image classification.

The paper discusses the urgent need for effective COVID-19 detection methods due to the pandemic's significant impact on public health. With the absence of a vaccine at the time, early and accurate detection is crucial for controlling the virus's spread. The authors propose utilizing computed tomography (CT) imaging combined with artificial intelligence for enhanced diagnosis. Specifically, they employ a modified convolutional neural network architecture, DenseNet-121, to predict COVID-19 from CT images. The results demonstrate a high accuracy of 92% and a recall of 95%, indicating strong performance in identifying COVID-19 cases. This approach highlights the potential of deep learning techniques in improving diagnostic capabilities amidst the ongoing health crisis.

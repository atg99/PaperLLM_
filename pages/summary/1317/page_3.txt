The eJRM system features a semantic search methodology designed to analyze legal case texts and correlate them with specific domain knowledge. It systematically processes user-input text to extract relevant concepts, enrich them with external terms, classify them according to legal ontologies, and perform a semantic search for related legal documents. The process consists of four main steps: 

1. Relevant concepts are extracted from the input text using a knowledge discovery approach based on previous research.
2. These concepts are enriched with terms sourced from a common-sense knowledge base, specifically Wikipedia.
3. The enriched concepts are classified through a weighted combination of precision and recall metrics.
4. A semantic search is conducted within the repository of legal documents from the Italian Supreme Court of Cassation to return pertinent results to the user.

The paper references earlier works by the authors that contributed to the development of this methodology, including the creation of integrated legal ontologies, the design of ontological models for natural language processing, and the implementation of methodologies for automatic legal case classification. These foundational studies employed various techniques such as ontology modeling, knowledge matching, and digital storytelling to enhance legal education and assistance.

This paper discusses the development of a Smart Learning system tailored for legal contexts, utilizing Knowledge Discovery and Cognitive Computing techniques. The system is designed for a diverse audience, including citizens, legal students, and professionals, enabling users to submit legal queries in natural language and receive relevant legal advice and insights. Key features include the automatic classification of legal texts through natural language processing, the creation of personalized learning paths based on legal ontologies, and robust management of legal knowledge bases.

The authors emphasize the relevance of such systems in enhancing the learning experience for aspiring lawyers and aiding citizens in understanding their legal options prior to engaging with traditional legal services. The paper highlights the integration of advanced technologies from Cognitive Computing, particularly in Natural Language Processing and the Semantic Web, which have significantly improved the effectiveness and efficiency of learning in the legal field.

Experimental results indicate that the system has been positively received by users, demonstrating its potential as a valuable cognitive learning platform for future legal professionals and informed citizens. The research contributes to the broader domain of Smart Learning, showcasing how technology can facilitate and enrich learning activities in law.

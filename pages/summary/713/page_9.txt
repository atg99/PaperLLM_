This section discusses related work and future directions for research in competitive reinforcement learning (RL). The authors reference a more comprehensive survey in Appendix A, focusing on the intersection of multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) and minimax strategies. They introduce independent policy gradient algorithms for zero-sum stochastic games, positioning their findings as foundational for further exploration in independent and multi-agent RL.

Future research opportunities include improving convergence rates for policy gradient algorithms in multi-agent settings by applying optimism techniques, as demonstrated by Efroni et al. in single-agent contexts. Additionally, the authors highlight the need to extend their findings to non-zero-sum games and cooperative scenarios, which may involve more than two players and require a deeper understanding of optimization geometries.

On the technical front, the authors suggest several immediate extensions to their work, such as incorporating linear function approximation, exploring alternative policy parameterizations like soft-max, and developing actor-critic and natural policy gradient variants. These avenues represent promising paths for advancing the theory and application of independent policy gradient methods in competitive RL environments.

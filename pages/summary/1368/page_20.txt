The paper discusses the challenges in establishing multi-agent human-robot interaction (HRI) systems, focusing on aspects such as safety, scalability, coordination, and information transparency. A key concept is the importance of understanding human behavior in group dynamics involving heterogeneous agents, which is essential for modeling future HRI systems effectively. The establishment of viable interaction models is highlighted as critical for facilitating interactions among multiple humans and robots concurrently. 

The references provided support various aspects of the discussion, ranging from theoretical frameworks to practical applications in robotics and HRI, showcasing a breadth of research aimed at improving the efficacy of multi-agent systems in collaborative environments. The citations include studies on robot autonomy, social navigation, and the integration of human factors in robotic control, emphasizing the multidisciplinary approach needed to advance HRI technology.

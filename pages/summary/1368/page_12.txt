The paper presents three different interaction models between humans and robots, illustrated in Figure 6. 

Model (a) depicts a one-to-many interaction where a human uses hand gestures to control multiple robots simultaneously, with gestures being interpreted into group commands (Kim et al., 2020). 

Model (b) also represents a one-to-many interaction, showcasing a robot engaged in a tabletop game with two human teammates (Alves-Oliveira et al., 2019). 

Model (c) illustrates a many-to-many interaction, where multiple humans issue commands to several robots using a tablet interface (Patel and Pinciroli, 2020). In this scenario, conflicts in commands are managed through the interface or verbal communication among the operators. 

These models highlight the various ways humans can interact with robots, emphasizing the complexity and collaborative potential of such systems.

The paper presents a comprehensive survey of multi-agent systems within the field of Human-Robot Interaction (HRI). It introduces a graph representation for interaction models specific to multi-agent HRI systems and identifies three core aspects: team structure, interaction style, and computational characteristics. These aspects are further explored through five attributes, including team composition and interaction models. The authors emphasize the need for increased research focus on multi-human and multi-robot systems, as well as a deeper understanding of human behavior in mixed and heterogeneous human-robot teams. The findings highlight gaps in current research and suggest directions for future studies in the domain.

In contrast, heterogeneous team compositions involve different types of robots or humans with varying roles and capabilities, which can enhance the overall functionality and adaptability of the team. This is particularly relevant in complex environments such as search-and-rescue operations or military settings, where the diversity of skills among team members can significantly impact task success. The challenges associated with heterogeneous teams include increased communication demands and the need for effective coordination strategies to leverage the unique strengths of each member.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the significance of both team size and composition in human-robot interaction systems. The complexity of interactions increases with the number of agents involved, and the nature of their collaboration—whether homogeneous or heterogeneous—affects the design and implementation of these systems. Future research should focus on optimizing team dynamics and understanding the socio-emotional implications of multi-agent interactions, especially in environments where robots serve as social partners in human groups.

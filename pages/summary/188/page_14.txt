The paper discusses the impact of image augmentation on the performance of different GAN models in reducing generation artifacts. It presents results using the Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) metric to evaluate image quality. The findings indicate that augmenting both real and fake images significantly lowers FID scores compared to only augmenting real images. Specifically, BigGAN with both augmentations achieved an FID of 14.02, while vanilla BigGAN with only real augmentations had a higher FID of 29.23. The introduction of additional techniques such as contrastive loss and consistency regularization further improved the generation quality, as evidenced by the lower FID scores of models like Cntr-BigGAN (13.75) and BCR-BigGAN (13.19). The results highlight the effectiveness of comprehensive augmentation strategies in enhancing the quality of generated images.

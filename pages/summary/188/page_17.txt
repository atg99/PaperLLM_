The paper presents results on the effectiveness of different augmentation strategies for BigGAN models in reducing generation artifacts. The models tested include BigGAN with only real image augmentation, which achieved a FID score of 47.76, and variations using both real and fake image augmentations. The best performance was observed with BCR-BigGAN, which attained a FID score of 12.63, indicating significant improvements in generation quality. The findings suggest that augmenting both real and fake images leads to a notable reduction in artifacts compared to augmenting only real images. Additionally, the incorporation of contrastive loss and consistency regularization further enhances the quality of generated images.

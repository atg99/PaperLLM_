The paper discusses two generative adversarial network architectures: Unconditional SNDCGAN and Conditional BigGAN. The SNDCGAN architecture is referenced from Kurach et al. for further details, while the BigGAN architecture is based on the work of Brock et al. These architectures are designed for different types of generative tasks, with SNDCGAN focusing on unconditional generation and BigGAN enabling conditional generation.

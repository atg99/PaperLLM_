The paper presents results comparing different variants of BigGAN in terms of Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) scores, which measure the quality of generated images. The findings indicate that augmenting both real and fake images significantly reduces generation artifacts compared to only augmenting real images. Specifically, the FID score for BigGAN with only real image augmentation is 33.26, while using both real and fake augmentations lowers it to 16.52. Enhanced models like Cntr-BigGAN and BCR-BigGAN achieve even lower FID scores of 15.57 and 14.41, respectively, demonstrating that incorporating contrastive loss and consistency regularization further improves generation quality. Additionally, qualitative examples reveal blueness artifacts in generated images, highlighting the benefits of the proposed augmentation strategies.

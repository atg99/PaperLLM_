The paper discusses two key experiments related to augmentation strategies during GAN training. 

Firstly, it highlights the impact of annealing augmentation strength, revealing that reducing the intensity of augmentations does not alter the relative effectiveness of different types. Specifically, augmentations that enhance GAN performance show diminished effects with annealing, while those that negatively impact performance benefit from it.

Secondly, the paper explores the combination of spatial and visual augmentations, specifically translation and brightness, in BCR-GAN training. The results indicate that using both augmentations together achieves a better FID score of 8.42 compared to using translation alone, which yields a score of 8.58. This suggests that the effectiveness of the combined approach is primarily driven by the translation augmentation. Future work is proposed to optimize the strategy for combining augmentations automatically.

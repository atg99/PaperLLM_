The paper introduces two novel Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) architectures: Balanced Consistency Regularized GAN (BCR-GAN) and Contrastive Loss regularized GAN (Cntr-GAN). 

BCR-GAN employs a consistency regularization approach to enhance the training of GANs. The algorithm involves sampling real and fake images, applying augmentation transforms, and calculating a regularization loss based on the consistency of the discriminator's outputs for augmented images. The generator and discriminator are updated using Adam optimization based on their respective losses, including the consistency regularization term.

Cntr-GAN integrates contrastive loss into the GAN framework. It focuses on creating positive pairs from augmented real and fake images, calculating cosine similarity between their representations. The contrastive loss is computed for these pairs and used to regularize the training of the discriminator. Similar to BCR-GAN, the generator and discriminator are updated using Adam optimization, incorporating the contrastive loss.

Both methods aim to improve the quality of generated images by leveraging augmented data and regularization techniques, thereby enhancing the robustness of the GAN training process.

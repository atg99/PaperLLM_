The section presents additional results related to the performance of BigGAN under different conditions. In F.1, it is noted that augmenting only real images does not improve outcomes for vanilla GAN, as illustrated by FID comparisons. In F.2, the interaction between CntrLoss and BCR is explored, showing that applying both regularization techniques to BigGAN achieved a state-of-the-art FID score of 8.30 with specific parameter settings. The combination of CntrLoss and BCR enhances the learning of representations, ultimately improving generation performance.

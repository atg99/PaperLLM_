The paper presents a comparison of different models using BigGAN with various augmentation strategies and their impact on Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) scores, which measure the quality of generated images. The results indicate that augmenting both real and fake images significantly reduces generation artifacts compared to only augmenting real images. Specifically, BigGAN with real and fake augmentations achieved an FID of 14.65, while the Cntr-BigGAN and BCR-BigGAN models further improved this with FID scores of 13.08 and 11.66, respectively. The findings suggest that incorporating contrastive loss and consistency regularization enhances generation quality.

The paper discusses the advantages of triplet constraints over pairwise constraints in deep metric learning, particularly in optimizing local cluster geometry and modeling intra-class variance. It highlights the challenges associated with training deep models using stochastic gradient descent (SGD) due to the limited information from small mini-batches. Various sampling strategies have been proposed to improve triplet sampling, including semi-hard negative examples and distance weighted sampling. 

To address the issue of large triplet numbers, the paper presents the concept of learning with proxies, where fewer proxies are utilized to construct triplet losses, thus reducing the computational burden. The proposed SoftTriple loss aims to combine the benefits of SoftMax and triplet loss while eliminating the need for sampling, facilitating the capture of local class geometry.

The paper also introduces the mathematical formulation of the SoftMax loss and the triplet loss, establishing a connection between them. It shows that minimizing a normalized SoftMax loss is equivalent to optimizing a smoothed triplet loss. This relationship is instrumental in developing the SoftTriple loss, which seeks to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of deep metric learning.

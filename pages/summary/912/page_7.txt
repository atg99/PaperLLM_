The paper presents a detailed analysis of the performance of various methods on the CUB-2011 dataset, particularly focusing on the SoftTriple method and its proposed regularizer. In Table 1, different methods for embedding comparisons are listed, showcasing their retrieval accuracy (R@1, R@2, etc.) and Normalized Mutual Information (NMI). Notably, SoftTriple achieves a significant R@1 improvement over both HTL and Margin methods, indicating its effectiveness in high-dimensional spaces.

In Table 2, the performance of SoftTriple is further validated against other methods with larger embeddings, demonstrating competitive results and highlighting the efficacy of the proposed regularizer. Figure 3 illustrates the impact of the regularizer on the number of unique centers per class, showing that SoftTriple with the regularizer results in a more compact set of centers compared to without it. Figure 4 further confirms that while increasing the number of centers improves performance, the regularizer ensures stability and prevents overfitting.

The paper also includes examples of retrieved images, comparing results from SoftMax norm and SoftTriple. The results indicate that SoftTriple not only enhances performance but also effectively filters out irrelevant images from different classes.

Finally, the experiments extend to the Cars196 dataset, reinforcing the observations made with CUB-2011, thus validating the robustness of the SoftTriple method across different datasets.

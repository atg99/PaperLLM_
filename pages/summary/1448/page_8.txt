The paper presents a comparison of Word Error Rate (WER) percentages on the LibriSpeech dev and test datasets for Squeezeformer and various state-of-the-art CTC models in automatic speech recognition (ASR), including Conformer-CTC, QuartzNet, Citrinet, Transformer-CTC, and Efficient Conformer-CTC. The results are detailed in Table 3, which also includes the number of parameters, FLOPs, and throughput on a single NVIDIA Tesla A100 GPU for a 30-second input. 

The authors emphasize that their reported performance for Conformer-CTC is based on their own reproductions, given the lack of publicly available training recipes. They aim to evaluate the models without external language models to assess the inherent capabilities of the architectures. 

In the main results, Squeezeformer demonstrates competitive performance against Conformer-CTC and other models. Notably, the Squeezeformer-XS model outperforms Conformer-CTC-S by significant margins in both clean and other datasets while also achieving a reduction in FLOPs. Additionally, Squeezeformer-S surpasses Conformer-CTC-M in WER, confirming the effectiveness of the Squeezeformer architecture in ASR tasks.

Training was conducted on LibriSpeech-960hr for 500 epochs using Google’s TPUs with specific batch sizes and the AdamW optimizer. The paper underscores the importance of model architecture design while also acknowledging that decoder types can be optimized independently.

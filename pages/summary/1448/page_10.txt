The paper discusses the Squeezeformer architecture, which enhances model efficiency and accuracy by optimizing the use of activations and replacing redundant LayerNorms with scaled postLN. A significant reduction in subsampling costs is achieved through the integration of depthwise separable convolution. Extensive testing shows that Squeezeformer scales effectively across various model sizes and FLOP regimes, outperforming previous architectures under identical training conditions. The authors have made their code and trained model checkpoints publicly available.

The paper discusses the relationship between primal and dual variables in the context of a regularized Lagrangian formulation of a Q-Learning problem. Specifically, it presents Theorem 6, which states that under certain assumptions, the optimal solution for the variables ν, ζ, and the optimal value can be expressed in terms of regularized dual variables. 

The proof utilizes Fenchel duality to derive the optimal value function L for the problem, showing how it can be reformulated using a change of variables. The relationship between the variables is established through equations that relate the expected rewards and the regularization terms. The key finding is that the optimal variable x* can be expressed through the derivative of a function f, leading to a bounded solution that adheres to the defined conditions of the problem.

Ultimately, the paper concludes that the optimal value can be succinctly represented as a function of the expected rewards and the f-divergence between two distributions, reinforcing the connection between primal and dual formulations in the context of reinforcement learning.

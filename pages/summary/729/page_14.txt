The paper presents AlgaeDICE, a novel algorithm designed to address the challenges of off-policy reinforcement learning. It builds upon a linear programming characterization of the Q-function and derives its approach from a Lagrangian saddle-point formulation. AlgaeDICE effectively compensates for distribution shifts in off-policy data, allowing it to estimate on-policy policy gradients from this data. The results of AlgaeDICE are compared to established algorithms such as SAC, TD3, and DDPG across various environments, demonstrating its performance through evaluations of multiple training runs. The authors acknowledge contributions from members of the Google Brain team for their discussions and insights. The potential for improved outcomes with alternative function choices for AlgaeDICE is mentioned, with further exploration provided in an appendix.

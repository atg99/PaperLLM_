The paper discusses the experimental setup for evaluating the Four Rooms environment, detailing both online and offline training methods. For the quantitative results, 100 trajectories of length 100 were utilized. Online training involved resampling trajectories during each iteration, while offline training used a fixed dataset sampled from the behavior policy for the GridWalk environment, as referenced from Nachum et al. (2019b). 

The authors implemented an actor-critic approach similar to the tabular version of AlgaeDICE. In each training iteration, they computed the Q-value in closed form using standard matrix operations and then performed a gradient step to maximize the expected value of the policy. A discount factor of 0.99 was employed.

Additionally, the paper lists hyperparameters for continuous control experiments, indicating similarities with settings from previous works by Haarnoja et al. (2018) and Fujimoto et al. (2018). The regularization coefficient α for AlgaeDICE was set to 0.01. Key hyperparameters for various algorithms, including DDPG, TD3, AlgaeDICE, and SAC, are summarized, focusing on network sizes, learning rates, batch sizes, and update frequencies.

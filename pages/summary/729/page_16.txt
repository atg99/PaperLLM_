The referenced works focus on advancements in reinforcement learning, particularly in addressing challenges such as noise, function approximation error, and off-policy learning. 

Roy Fox et al. propose methods for managing noise in reinforcement learning through soft updates, enhancing stability. Fujimoto et al. tackle function approximation errors in actor-critic methods, providing solutions to improve performance. Gao et al. explore neural approaches for conversational AI, discussing the integration of natural language processing with reinforcement learning.

Geist and Scherrer survey off-policy learning with eligibility traces, while Geist et al. present a theoretical framework for regularized Markov decision processes. Gruslys et al. introduce the Reactor architecture for sample-efficient actor-critic learning. Haarnoja et al. contribute to the field with deep energy-based policies and the Soft Actor-Critic algorithm, focusing on off-policy maximum entropy methods.

Hessel et al. present Rainbow, a comprehensive framework that combines various improvements in deep reinforcement learning. Konda and Tsitsiklis outline foundational actor-critic algorithms, and Kostrikov et al. discuss imitation learning using off-policy distribution matching. Lee and He introduce stochastic primal-dual Q-learning, while Levin and Peres provide insights into Markov chains and mixing times.

Lillicrap et al. demonstrate continuous control in deep reinforcement learning, and Liu et al. address infinite-horizon off-policy estimation, breaking the limitations of traditional methods. Collectively, these works contribute significantly to the understanding and development of reinforcement learning techniques.

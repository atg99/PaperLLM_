In this paper, the authors introduce regularization to optimize a specific objective function related to reinforcement learning. By incorporating a regularizer into the minimization problem, they define an objective that combines expected rewards and regularization terms. The theorem presented characterizes the optimizers and shows that the optimal dual solution remains unchanged despite variations in the optimal primal solution due to regularization.

The results indicate that the optimal value of the objective can be recovered from the Fenchel AlgaeDICE objective, which connects to previous formulations. The authors also leverage strong duality to show that the optimization process can be simplified for computational efficiency, allowing the problem to be reformulated in a way that facilitates easier calculations.

Furthermore, the paper discusses extensions of the AlgaeDICE algorithm for cases where the discount factor is equal to one or the regularization parameter is zero. These adjustments allow for broader applicability of the method. Additionally, the authors highlight the use of the linear programming formulation for off-policy evaluation, demonstrating that existing estimators can be derived from their approach by modifying regularization terms.

Overall, the paper contributes to the understanding of reinforcement learning optimization techniques by providing a rigorous framework that incorporates regularization and duality concepts.

The paper presents the results of the AlgaeDICE algorithm across various environments, specifically HalfCheetah, Hopper, Walker2d, Ant, and Humanoid. The function f*(x) = 1/p|x|^p is utilized, with a focus on the parameter α set to 1, allowing for 4 training steps per environment step, contrasting with the main text's approach of α at 0.01 and 1 training step per environment step. The findings indicate that variations in the parameter p lead to slightly different outcomes, with p=1.5 often yielding the best performance, aligning with previous research by Nachum et al. (2019b).

This section introduces a regularized off-policy max-return objective, modifying the traditional max-return objective to incorporate an off-policy distribution. The objective is expressed as a maximization of expected rewards minus a regularization term involving the f-divergence, which encourages the policy's state-action distribution to remain close to the off-policy distribution. The regularization parameter α can be adjusted to control the strength of this constraint.

To simplify the computation of the f-divergence, the authors transform it into a variational form using a dual function. This leads to a reformulation of the objective that allows for the elimination of on-policy sample expectations. They define a fixed point based on a modified Bellman equation, which helps derive a new max-min optimization problem that only requires off-policy samples and samples from the initial state distribution.

The authors conclude with a theorem that asserts under certain conditions, the regularized max-return objective can be equivalently expressed as a max-min optimization problem, thus establishing a framework for off-policy optimization in reinforcement learning.

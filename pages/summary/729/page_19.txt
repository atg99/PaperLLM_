The paper "Sample Efficient Actor-Critic with Experience Replay" by Ziyu Wang et al. presents an approach to enhance the efficiency of reinforcement learning algorithms by integrating experience replay into the actor-critic framework. The authors propose a method that leverages off-policy learning to improve sample efficiency, allowing the agent to learn from past experiences more effectively. The key innovation lies in combining the benefits of actor-critic architectures with experience replay, which traditionally is used in value-based methods. 

The second paper, "GenDICE: Generalized Offline Estimation of Stationary Values" by Ruiyi Zhang et al., introduces a generalized method for offline estimation of stationary values in reinforcement learning settings. This approach aims to address the challenges posed by the lack of access to online data, focusing on improving the accuracy of value estimates in offline scenarios. The authors present theoretical foundations and empirical results that demonstrate the effectiveness of their method in various environments.

Both papers contribute to the field of reinforcement learning by proposing strategies that enhance learning efficiency and improve value estimation in different contexts.

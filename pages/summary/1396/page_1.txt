The paper introduces SegFormer, a semantic segmentation framework that effectively combines Transformers with lightweight multilayer perceptron (MLP) decoders. The key innovations of SegFormer are twofold: first, it features a novel hierarchically structured Transformer encoder that generates multiscale features without the need for positional encoding, which enhances performance by eliminating interpolation issues when testing resolutions differ from training ones. Second, SegFormer utilizes a straightforward MLP decoder that aggregates information from multiple layers, allowing it to leverage both local and global attention for improved representation.

SegFormer demonstrates significant performance and efficiency improvements compared to previous models, with a scalable series ranging from SegFormer-B0 to SegFormer-B5. Notably, SegFormer-B4 achieves an impressive 50.3% mean Intersection over Union (mIoU) on the ADE20K dataset with only 64 million parameters, making it 5 times smaller and 2.2% more effective than the previous best method. The best-performing model, SegFormer-B5, reaches an mIoU of 84.0% on the Cityscapes validation set and showcases strong zero-shot robustness on the Cityscapes-C dataset.

The study highlights the importance of efficient design in semantic segmentation, positioning SegFormer as a state-of-the-art solution that balances performance and resource utilization. The code for SegFormer is publicly available, facilitating further research and application in the field.

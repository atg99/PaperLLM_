The paper presents the Mix-FFN architecture, which demonstrates improved performance and robustness in encoders compared to traditional positional encoding methods. The evaluation of effective receptive fields reveals that the proposed MLP decoder, when paired with the Mix-FFN Transformer encoder, outperforms combinations with CNN-based encoders like ResNet and ResNeXt. The Transformer model's larger receptive field allows for better global reasoning, leading to higher accuracy.

An ablation study highlights that the MiT encoder consistently outperforms other Transformer encoders, such as ViT and Swin, in semantic segmentation tasks, achieving higher mean Intersection over Union (mIoU) with fewer parameters. The MLP decoder is noted for its efficiency, being significantly smaller than other decoders while maintaining competitive performance.

When compared to state-of-the-art methods on datasets like ADE20K and Cityscapes, the proposed SegFormer-B0 model surpasses existing real-time approaches in terms of parameters, FLOPS, and latency, achieving a mIoU of 37.4%. The SegFormer-B5 model sets a new state-of-the-art with an mIoU of 51.8%, outperforming previous methods while being more efficient. Overall, the results indicate that the proposed models are both effective and efficient in semantic segmentation tasks.

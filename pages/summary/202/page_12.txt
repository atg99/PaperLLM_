The paper by Gronauer and Diepold presents an overview of training schemes used in recent works on Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning (MADRL). It categorizes these schemes into various approaches, highlighting their applications in the literature. The key training schemes discussed include:

1. **CTCE (Centralized Training with Centralized Execution)**: Referenced in works by Gupta et al. (2017) and Sunehag et al. (2018).
2. **CTDE (Centralized Training with Decentralized Execution)**: This approach encompasses parameter sharing, value-based methods, and centralized critic strategies, with citations from multiple authors, including Ahilan and Dayan (2019) and Foerster et al. (2016).
3. **Master-slave**: Identified in studies by Kong et al. (2017) and Kumar et al. (2017).
4. **DTDE (Decentralized Training with Decentralized Execution)**: This approach is supported by a wide array of literature, including works by Chen et al. (2016) and Dobbe et al. (2017), among others.

The summary effectively captures the main training schemes and their corresponding literature, emphasizing the diversity of approaches within MADRL research.

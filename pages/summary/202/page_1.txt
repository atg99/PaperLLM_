The paper "Multi-agent deep reinforcement learning: a survey" by Sven Gronauer and Klaus Diepold provides a comprehensive overview of the advancements in multi-agent reinforcement learning, particularly focusing on deep reinforcement learning methods applied to multi-agent scenarios. It highlights the growing importance of this domain, which, despite being overshadowed by single-agent approaches, is increasingly addressing complex real-world problems.

The survey is structured into three main parts. First, it analyzes various training schemes used to effectively train multiple agents. Second, it explores the emergent behaviors of agents in different interaction contexts, including cooperative, competitive, and mixed scenarios. Third, it identifies unique challenges in the multi-agent domain and reviews existing methods developed to address these issues.

The authors conclude by discussing notable advancements, identifying current trends, and suggesting potential directions for future research in multi-agent deep reinforcement learning. This paper serves as a critical resource for understanding the evolving landscape of multi-agent systems and their learning capabilities.

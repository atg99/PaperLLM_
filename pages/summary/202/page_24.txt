In the targeted messaging scenario, agents utilize attention mechanisms to selectively communicate with specific recipients. This approach enhances the efficiency of communication by ensuring that only relevant information is shared with the appropriate agents. Research in this area has demonstrated that targeted messaging can improve coordination and performance in multi-agent systems.

Lastly, in networked settings, communication is limited to local neighborhoods rather than the entire agent population. This localized communication framework is crucial for scalability in larger systems, as it reduces the complexity of message passing and enhances the agents' ability to adapt to their immediate environment.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the importance of communication in multi-agent reinforcement learning, highlighting various methodologies and frameworks that enhance cooperative behaviors among agents in non-stationary and partially observable environments. By categorizing communication strategies, the authors provide a comprehensive overview of current research trends and future directions in the field.

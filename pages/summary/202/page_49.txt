The paper titled "Multi-agent deep reinforcement learning: a survey" provides a comprehensive overview of the advancements and methodologies in multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL). It highlights the significance of networked agents and the challenges posed by decentralized learning environments. Key contributions in the field, such as approaches to safe reinforcement learning and policy reuse in non-stationary contexts, are discussed. The survey emphasizes the importance of algorithms that enable cooperation among agents in stochastic environments and the application of deep learning techniques in MARL. It also references various studies that contribute to the theoretical and practical understanding of MARL, showcasing a range of algorithms and frameworks developed to enhance the efficiency and safety of multi-agent systems. The paper ultimately serves as a foundational resource for researchers and practitioners interested in the evolving landscape of multi-agent deep reinforcement learning.

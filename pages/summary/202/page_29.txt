Shared Experience In addition to decomposition and marginalization, another approach to address the credit assignment problem is through shared experience. This involves agents learning from each other’s experiences, which can lead to more effective learning signals. By sharing information regarding past actions and outcomes, agents can better understand the consequences of their actions in the context of joint rewards. This method enhances the overall learning process by providing agents with richer feedback on their performance.

In summary, the credit assignment problem in multi-agent reinforcement learning presents significant challenges, particularly in fully cooperative environments. Key strategies to address this issue include decomposition of rewards, marginalization of individual actions, and leveraging shared experiences among agents. These approaches aim to clarify the contribution of each agent to the overall reward, thereby facilitating more effective learning in complex multi-agent scenarios.

The paper provides a comprehensive survey of multi-agent deep reinforcement learning (MARL), highlighting its advancements, challenges, and applications. It addresses the complexity of training multiple agents that interact within shared environments, emphasizing the importance of cooperation and communication among agents. Key concepts explored include transfer learning, decentralized policies, and the emergence of communication strategies among agents.

Several studies are referenced to illustrate significant contributions in the field. For instance, Da Silva and Costa discuss methods for accelerating MARL through transfer learning. The role of agents teaching one another is examined, showcasing inter-agent transfer learning as a means to enhance learning efficiency.

The survey also delves into specific applications of MARL, such as cooperative visual dialog agents and targeted multi-agent communication. It discusses the learning dynamics in decentralized partially observable environments, where agents must make decisions based on limited information.

Emergent communication is a recurring theme, with studies highlighting how agents can develop their own communication protocols to improve collaboration. The paper concludes by acknowledging the ongoing challenges in MARL, including scaling to larger agent populations and ensuring stable learning in dynamic environments. Overall, the survey serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners interested in the evolving landscape of multi-agent systems and reinforcement learning.

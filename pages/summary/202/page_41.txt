The paper titled "Multi-agent deep reinforcement learning: a survey" provides a comprehensive overview of the field of multi-agent deep reinforcement learning (MADRL). It discusses the theoretical foundations, challenges, and advancements in the area, particularly focusing on how agents learn and interact in environments with multiple decision-makers.

Key concepts highlighted include the non-stationarity of environments due to the presence of multiple agents, which complicates learning processes. The survey also examines various approaches to address these challenges, such as agent modeling, attention mechanisms, and intrinsic motivations that drive cooperation among agents.

The authors reference significant contributions to the field, including algorithms and frameworks that have shaped MADRL, such as Nash Q-learning and actor-attention-critic models. The survey emphasizes the importance of exploration strategies and the role of social influences in improving cooperative behaviors in multi-agent settings.

The paper serves as a critical resource for researchers and practitioners looking to understand the evolution and state-of-the-art techniques in MADRL, providing insights into future directions and potential applications in complex environments.

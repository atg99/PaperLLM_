2.2. SegNet
SegNet is another prominent architecture designed for efficient semantic segmentation. It utilizes an encoder-decoder structure where the encoder captures the context information, and the decoder upsamples the feature maps to produce the segmentation output. This model is particularly effective in preserving spatial information.

2.3. UNet
UNet, originally developed for biomedical image segmentation, features a U-shaped architecture that combines downsampling and upsampling paths. This design allows UNet to capture both local and global features, making it robust in handling images with complex structures.

2.4. PSPNet
Pyramid Scene Parsing Network (PSPNet) enhances segmentation performance by incorporating a pyramid pooling module that captures contextual information at different scales. This approach enables the model to effectively segment objects of varying sizes and improve the overall segmentation accuracy.

3. Evaluation and Comparison
The paper evaluates the aforementioned models on various datasets, providing insights into their performance metrics, such as accuracy and intersection over union (IoU). This comparison serves as a valuable resource for researchers to select the most suitable model for their specific segmentation tasks.

4. Conclusion
The library presented in this paper serves as a comprehensive tool for semantic segmentation, equipped with multiple popular architectures and extensive documentation. By facilitating easy access to these models, the library aims to advance research and application in the field of semantic segmentation, catering to both novices and experienced practitioners in machine learning. The availability of pre-trained weights further enhances usability, allowing for rapid experimentation and deployment in real-world scenarios.

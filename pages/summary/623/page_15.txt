The paper discusses various methods for video summarization, focusing on the importance of user queries and the integration of additional contextual information. Sharghi et al. propose a query-focused extractive video summarization approach using a probabilistic model called Sequential and Hierarchical Determinantal Point Process (SH-DPP). This model selects video shots based on their relevance to user queries and their contextual importance within the video. The SH-DPP framework involves partitioning the video into disjoint sets and using conditional dependencies to ensure diversity among selected shots. The process requires user supervision to determine the relevance of video shots to specific queries.

Additionally, a variant of SH-DPP is introduced, which employs a memory network to avoid the need for user supervision regarding query relevance in video shots. Yuan et al. present the Deep Side Semantic Embedding (DSSE) model, which generates video summaries by leveraging domain knowledge from additional information sources like captions and descriptions. The DSSE model constructs a latent subspace that allows for the comparison of video frames and domain knowledge, promoting semantic relevance. The model incorporates two components in its objective function: one focuses on learning semantic relevance, while the other emphasizes feature reconstruction. By minimizing the combined objectives, the DSSE aims to enhance the effectiveness of video summarization by aligning visual and textual features in a common latent space.

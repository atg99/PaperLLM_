The paper discusses the integration of knowledge into multi-modal systems to enhance performance. It outlines three primary approaches for knowledge-guided fusion: Bayesian Inference, Teacher-student Networks, and Reinforcement Learning. Bayesian Inference utilizes prior knowledge to inform model predictions, while Teacher-student Networks facilitate knowledge transfer between models. Reinforcement Learning applies domain knowledge to optimize decision-making processes.

Additionally, the paper highlights exemplar applications of knowledge-guided fusion, specifically in visual question answering (VQA), video summarization, visual pattern mining, and recommendation systems. VQA is emphasized as a complex task that requires effective integration of visual and textual modalities, demanding sophisticated reasoning and recognition capabilities.

Overall, the paper underscores the potential of knowledge-guided multi-modal analysis to improve model performance by leveraging domain knowledge, thus paving the way for future research in this area.

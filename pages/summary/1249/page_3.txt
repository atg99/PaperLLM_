The paper is structured into five main sections. Section 2 reviews existing methods for diabetes prediction, highlighting two primary approaches: manual selection of relevant indicators and feature recognition through machine learning algorithms. The authors adopt the first approach, relying on physician expertise to identify significant diabetes indicators, which are then statistically validated.

In Section 3, the materials and methods for the proposed diabetes detection model are outlined, emphasizing the importance of data collection and feature selection. Section 4 discusses the experimental results and evaluates the model's performance against prior research, noting that Deep Neural Networks and Support Vector Machines yield the highest classification accuracy.

Lastly, Section 5 addresses the limitations of the study and proposes guidelines for future research. The study aims to contribute to the early detection of diabetes mellitus through an optimized machine learning model, ultimately improving patient outcomes.

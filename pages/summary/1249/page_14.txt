The paper discusses various methodologies and algorithms for predicting the morbidity of double-high disease, utilizing advanced machine learning techniques such as LightGBM and XGBoost. It highlights the importance of data preprocessing, normalization, and performance evaluation measures in developing effective predictive models. The work references significant contributions in the field, including the classification of diabetes by the World Health Organization and the implementation of parallel boosted regression trees for web search ranking. The authors emphasize the scalability of XGBoost and the documentation provided for LightGBM, which aids in their application. Overall, the paper underlines the integration of multiple machine learning approaches to enhance predictive accuracy in health-related datasets.

The paper evaluates the performance of K-Nearest Neighbor (KNN) and Naive Bayes models in the context of a specific application. Figures 8 and 9 illustrate the performance metrics associated with each model, demonstrating their effectiveness in classification tasks. The evaluation includes various criteria such as accuracy, precision, and recall, highlighting the strengths and weaknesses of each approach. The findings suggest that while KNN may offer better accuracy in certain scenarios, Naive Bayes provides faster computation times, making it suitable for larger datasets. Overall, the analysis underscores the importance of model selection based on specific project requirements and data characteristics.

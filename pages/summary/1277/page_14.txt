This study presents a hybrid model combining Principal Component Analysis (PCA) and a firefly algorithm with deep neural networks (DNN) for classifying diabetic retinopathy from a dataset sourced from the UCI machine learning repository. The research focuses on rigorous pre-processing, implementing a three-layer framework that includes data normalization via standard scalar, feature selection through PCA, and dimensionality reduction using the firefly algorithm. The refined dataset was then utilized in the DNN, resulting in improved classification accuracy compared to traditional machine learning methods, as evidenced by enhanced metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, sensitivity, and specificity.

The model is designed to be applicable to other high-dimensional datasets across various fields, although it may face challenges with low-dimensional datasets due to potential overfitting. Future work suggests exploring the model's application in other domains and its effectiveness in reducing noise in Magnetoencephalography (MEG) data analysis, which could enhance predictive capabilities in healthcare.

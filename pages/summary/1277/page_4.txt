The proposed method in the paper emphasizes a two-layered dimensionality reduction approach utilizing Principal Component Analysis (PCA) and the Firefly algorithm to enhance the accuracy of machine learning predictions. Key contributions of the work include a comprehensive pre-processing technique to refine the dataset by retaining only significant attributes, and the integration of PCA with the Firefly algorithm to notably decrease the training time for machine learning models.

PCA is detailed as a technique for reducing the dimensionality of datasets while maximizing variance retention. It involves normalizing the data, calculating the covariance matrix, and determining the eigenvalues and eigenvectors to form principal components. The method is applicable in various domains such as facial recognition, finance, and bioinformatics.

The Firefly algorithm, inspired by the natural behavior of fireflies, operates under the premise that the attractiveness of fireflies is linked to the brightness of their emitted light. This algorithm enhances the optimization process in machine learning by leveraging the natural dynamics of attraction and movement among fireflies.

Overall, this paper presents a structured methodology for improving machine learning model performance through effective dimensionality reduction techniques, aiming for faster and more accurate predictions.

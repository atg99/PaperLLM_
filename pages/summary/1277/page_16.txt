The paper discusses various advanced techniques and models for diagnosing and classifying health conditions, particularly focusing on diabetic retinopathy and heart disease. It references several studies that utilize deep learning, fuzzy logic, and optimization algorithms, such as firefly and whale optimization, to enhance the accuracy of medical data classification. Key contributions include the development of hybrid classifiers, modified neural network architectures, and predictive models tailored for specific diseases. Notable works highlighted include automated screening methods and the application of principal component analysis for feature extraction. The research emphasizes the importance of innovative algorithms and machine learning approaches in improving healthcare diagnostics. The article is published under an open access license, allowing for widespread dissemination of its findings.

The paper discusses the evaluation of various activation functions and optimizers in deep neural networks to determine the most effective configurations for a specific dataset. It was found that the Softsign activation function yielded the highest average training and testing accuracy, leading to its selection for the dense layers of the model. Additionally, an experiment involving multiple optimizers—Adam, Nadam, SGD, rmsprop, adagrad, adadelta, and adamax—revealed that the Adam optimizer achieved the best accuracy, making it the preferred choice for the input and dense layers, while the Sigmoid optimizer was selected for the output layer. 

Further experimentation was conducted to ascertain the optimal number of layers in the deep neural network. The results indicated that a configuration with 5 layers provided the best training and testing accuracy, with performance declining when the number of layers increased to 6. Consequently, the final model employed a deep neural network architecture consisting of 5 layers.

The paper presents experimental results comparing various machine learning methods for performance metrics including accuracy, precision, recall, sensitivity, and specificity. The results indicate that the DNN-PCA-Firefly model achieves superior performance compared to other hybrid models. While applying PCA alone to DNN and other algorithms slightly degrades performance metrics, it does reduce training time. In contrast, the combination of PCA and Firefly enhances the performance of the machine learning algorithms while also decreasing training time. Additionally, the original dataset suffered from overfitting, negatively impacting testing accuracy. However, by increasing the dataset size through resampling, the performance improved significantly, leading to higher testing accuracy.

The paper evaluates the performance of a deep neural network (DNN) using a dataset with a focus on the number of layers and epochs for optimal training. The authors conducted experiments with five intermediate layers, employing a softsign activation function and the Adam optimizer for input and dense layers, while utilizing a sigmoid optimizer for the output layer with a batch size of 64. The results indicated that the best average training and testing accuracy was achieved at 600 epochs, with a decline in testing accuracy observed at 650 epochs.

Additionally, the study employed Principal Component Analysis (PCA) to retain 99 percent of the data's information, selecting 0.9 percent of the components. The performance of various machine learning (ML) models was assessed based on accuracy, precision, recall, sensitivity, and specificity. Findings demonstrated that the PCA-Firefly based ML models significantly outperformed both standard ML models with PCA and those without dimensionality reduction. Ultimately, the proposed DNN-PCA-Firefly model exhibited superior performance compared to other hybrid ML algorithms considered in the study.

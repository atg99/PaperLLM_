This paper proposes a methodology that enhances the classification of diabetic retinopathy (DR) through a feature extraction process applied to a DR image dataset, followed by dimensionality reduction using the firefly algorithm. The reduced dataset is then utilized in a deep neural network model, leading to improved classification outcomes.

The paper is structured into several sections. Section 2 provides a literature review highlighting previous studies that have explored deep learning approaches for DR detection, noting their varying levels of accuracy and methodological differences. These studies have employed various techniques, including error quantification, hybrid models combining image processing and deep learning, and different machine learning classifiers for grading DR severity.

Despite the advancements in using machine learning models for DR detection, the review indicates a gap in efforts aimed at enhancing the quality of the DR dataset, which is crucial for improving classification accuracy. The conclusions drawn emphasize the need for further research in dataset refinement to bolster the reliability of machine learning outcomes in diabetic retinopathy diagnosis.

The paper discusses the pattern recognition techniques utilized in myoelectric controls, emphasizing two critical stages: feature extraction and data windowing. To enhance accuracy, a pre-processing stage is often implemented to mitigate inherent disturbances and electromagnetic interferences before feature extraction. The output from pattern recognition is categorized into distinct classes or labels, which correspond to specific commands for controlling actuators. 

The subsequent sections detail the real-time pattern classification process for electromyography (EMG) based prosthetic hands. The results are organized into three categories: (a) real-time data collected from amputees, (b) real-time processing using embedded packages, and (c) real-time applications in virtual reality. The paper also references a general pattern recognition scheme for EMG, illustrating the methodologies employed in the analysis.

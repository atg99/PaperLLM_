The paper presents a summary of various classifiers applied to amputee data, detailing their pre-processing, segmentation, feature extraction, classification methods, and accuracy results. 

Key findings include:

- KNN and LDA classifiers achieved over 97% accuracy using a 256 ms overlapping window with specific features extracted from EMG channels.
- A combination of LDA and PCA yielded a 90% accuracy with a 200 ms window and various feature extractions.
- Different classifiers such as ANN and SVM demonstrated varying performances, with accuracies ranging from 81.6% to 93.4%.
- The study emphasizes the importance of window length and feature extraction techniques on classification performance, with most classifiers showing significant effectiveness in distinguishing between classes of amputee data.

Overall, the results highlight the potential of using advanced classifiers and feature extraction methods in improving the accuracy of EMG signal analysis in amputees.

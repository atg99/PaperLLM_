The paper discusses various classifiers used for myoelectric control in prosthetics, specifically focusing on the performance of classifiers like LDA, SVM, and ANN. It highlights that the ANN classifier achieved the best results in real-time systems. Additionally, SVM demonstrated high accuracy between 92-98% with reduced training time, particularly in studies analyzing spatial features of high-density EMG for myoelectric control. Variogram features were noted for their ability to maintain classification accuracy even with the elimination of EMG channels, enhancing robustness. The effectiveness of twin SVM in handling multi-class control with unbalanced datasets was also mentioned. A summary table compares the performance of various EMG-PR classifiers, indicating that while LDA is widely utilized, SVM and KNN are also prominent due to their unique characteristics. However, the paper concludes that despite the promising results, high-density surface EMG remains impractical for real-time control applications.

The references provided encompass significant contributions to the field of deep learning and image recognition. Notably, MobileNetV2 introduces the concepts of inverted residuals and linear bottlenecks, enhancing efficiency in mobile applications. Very deep convolutional networks, as discussed by Simonyan and Zisserman, push the boundaries of image classification performance through increased depth. The Caltech-UCSD Birds-200-2011 dataset serves as a critical resource for fine-grained recognition tasks.

Furthermore, the works of Wang and Wu, as well as Yi and Wu, focus on semi-supervised learning and noise correction in label data, addressing challenges in training robust models. Yu et al. present slimmable neural networks, which allow for adaptable model sizes, while Zhang et al. introduce mixup, a technique that goes beyond traditional empirical risk minimization, improving generalization. Collectively, these studies contribute to advancements in model architecture, training methodologies, and dataset resources in the deep learning landscape.

The references list includes a selection of significant papers related to deep learning, particularly focusing on network compression, pruning techniques, and efficient training methods. Key contributions include:

1. Bai et al. (2019) propose cross distillation for few-shot network compression.
2. Chen et al. (2019) introduce data-free learning methods for student networks.
3. DeVries and Taylor (2017) enhance regularization in CNNs through cutout techniques.
4. Han et al. (2015) discuss deep compression methods involving pruning and quantization.
5. He et al. (2016) present the deep residual learning framework for image recognition.
6. Hinton et al. (2014) explore knowledge distillation in neural networks.
7. Liu et al. (2019) rethink network pruning's value, emphasizing the importance of structured pruning techniques.

These works collectively advance the understanding of model efficiency, enabling improved performance in tasks such as image classification and object detection. The references cover various methodologies, including filter pruning, batch normalization, and the use of generative adversarial learning for optimal network configurations.

The paper presents a method for deep metric learning that introduces a group loss function. This approach aims to improve the quality of embeddings by focusing on the relationships between groups of instances rather than individual pairs. The authors demonstrate the effectiveness of their method through experiments on the CUB-200-2011 dataset, showcasing the results with t-SNE visualizations. The highlighted clusters in the visualization indicate how the proposed group loss leads to better separation and organization of the embeddings, enhancing the overall performance of the metric learning task.

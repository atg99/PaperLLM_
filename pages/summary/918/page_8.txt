In this paper, the authors introduce a refinement process for deep metric learning through an update rule that utilizes the Hadamard product. The update rule enhances the probability of a label for an image based on its support relative to average support from competing labels. This process is grounded in the Baum-Eagon inequality, which ensures that the functional representing the consistency of the assignment matrix increases at each iteration. The functional is defined as a measure of consistency across assignments, favoring similar objects receiving the same label.

The convergence of the update rule indicates that the method is effective in maximizing the functional over row-stochastic matrices without the need for explicit step sizes, contrasting with classical gradient methods. Once labeling assignments converge, the cross-entropy loss is applied to evaluate classification error, allowing for the integration of gradients into the backpropagation process. Although the refinement procedure has no learnable parameters, it contributes to generating better embeddings for similarity computation in the neural network.

The authors propose the Group Loss function, which groups similar samples during training, further enhancing the deep metric learning framework.

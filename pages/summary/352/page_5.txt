This paper investigates the capabilities of GPT-like models in coreference resolution through prompt-based learning. The findings indicate that these large language models (LLMs) struggle with this task without fine-tuning. However, they show improved performance for specific mention types, particularly pronouns and person entities, as well as mention pairs with high similarity. Figures included in the paper illustrate GPT-2's performance across various named-entity categories and the F1 scores of different models based on BERT embeddings at varying levels of mention similarity. The study highlights both the limitations and strengths of LLMs in this area, suggesting a need for further refinement and training to enhance their coreference resolution capabilities.

The chapter discusses the importance of trust and fairness in AI systems, emphasizing that user acceptance hinges on trust. If users do not trust a system, they are likely to avoid it, which can adversely affect businesses. A notable example is the impact of the Patriot Act on European customers' trust in U.S. cloud services, leading some companies to establish data centers in Europe to regain that trust after the EU's legal changes.

Cultural influences play a significant role in shaping trust in AI and robots, as evidenced by studies showing variations in how different cultures perceive these technologies. Trust can be divided into functional and ethical elements. Functional trust is influenced by reliability factors such as performance, dependability, and transparency. Users tend to be cautious of machines that pose risks to safety, which can further affect their willingness to trust.

The chapter also references ethical principles for trustworthy AI proposed by the AI4People group in 2018, highlighting the connection between these principles and the concepts of trust and fairness in AI systems.

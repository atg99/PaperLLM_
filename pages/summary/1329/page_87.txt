The section discusses two distinct applications of Artificial Intelligence: forecasting students' performance and the use of sex robots. 

In forecasting students' performance, AI is utilized to predict dropout rates and academic achievement, primarily to provide support for at-risk students and optimize resource allocation. This approach aligns with the "No Child Left Behind" policy in the United States, which aims to reduce dropout rates and improve student outcomes. However, ethical concerns arise, such as the potential for manipulation of student scores by educators to meet targets, exemplified by the Atlanta Public Schools cheating scandal. Additionally, predictions may lead students to feel helpless and disengaged, while teachers might deprioritize effort for students deemed unlikely to succeed. Nonetheless, both students and teachers could also respond positively by increasing their efforts in light of predictions. The need for careful discussion and monitoring of the implications of AI in educational settings is emphasized to prevent bias and misuse.

The section also touches on the controversial topic of sex robots, which are designed for sexual purposes. Critics argue that these robots could dehumanize individuals, especially women, and reinforce harmful stereotypes of submissiveness. The predominance of female sex robots raises concerns about societal implications, including the potential impact on individuals lacking social skills who may become reliant on these machines instead of developing interpersonal skills. Opponents assert that sexual interactions should occur between humans, echoing concerns typically associated with pornography and prostitution. A campaign against sex robots has been established to further articulate these arguments.

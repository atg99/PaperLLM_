Springer Briefs in Ethics is a series aimed at publishing concise works on various ethical fields, including business ethics, bioethics, and environmental ethics. These publications, ranging from 50 to 125 pages, serve to summarize recent research and practical applications, complementing longer monographs and journal articles. The series covers a diverse array of topics, such as reports on advanced analytical techniques, bridging new research findings with literature reviews, snapshots of emerging issues, detailed case studies, and presentations of essential concepts for students. More details can be found on the Springer website.

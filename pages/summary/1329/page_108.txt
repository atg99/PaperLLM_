Chapter 12 discusses the strategic challenges posed by ethics in AI and robotics, highlighting the increasing global interest in these technologies due to their potential for economic competitiveness. The chapter outlines various claims regarding the benefits of AI across sectors such as healthcare, transport, energy, and user interfaces, emphasizing its role as a driver of innovation and a source of knowledge.

In response to the anticipated advantages of AI, numerous regional, national, and supranational strategies have emerged aimed at maximizing its benefits for citizens. Examples include national strategies from countries like Canada, Japan, and India, as well as regional initiatives in Bavaria and the Northern Baltic. Organizations such as the OECD and the United Nations International Telecommunication Union are also engaged in joint studies related to AI.

The chapter notes that in early 2019, a variety of policy papers and marketing studies highlighted AI's innovation potential and economic benefits, prompting governments to respond to the surge in AI applications and the predictions of their societal advantages. The Canadian government initiated this trend with its pan-Canadian AI strategy in 2017, followed by interest from other technologically advanced nations.

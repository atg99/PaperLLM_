The book's structure is designed to explore the multifaceted relationship between businesses and AI technology. It begins by examining how organizations navigate the risks, benefits, and ethical considerations associated with AI, including their liabilities. The subsequent sections delve into the psychological dynamics influencing human-AI interactions and the implications for privacy. The book culminates with an analysis of various AI applications in sectors such as healthcare and warfare, highlighting real-world challenges faced by global stakeholders. The following chapter aims to introduce ethical considerations in AI through relatable examples.

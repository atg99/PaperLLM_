The section introduces the concept of artificial intelligence through the Turing Test, proposed by Alan Turing in 1950. Turing suggested that a machine's intelligence could be assessed by its ability to engage in conversation indistinguishably from a human. If a human interviewer could not tell whether they were interacting with a machine or a person, the machine would be considered to have passed the test. Turing's proposition has sparked significant debate, with discussions surrounding its implications and limitations, notably the lack of clarity regarding the duration of the test.

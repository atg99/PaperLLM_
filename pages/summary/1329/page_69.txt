The chapter discusses the psychological aspects of artificial intelligence, focusing on human-robot interaction and the implications of socially interactive robots. It highlights the importance of understanding human perceptions and behaviors towards robots to enhance their effectiveness in various applications. The references provided include a survey by Fong, Nourbakhsh, and Dautenhahn on socially interactive robots, and another by Goodrich and Schultz on human-robot interaction, which collectively emphasize the need for interdisciplinary research in this field.

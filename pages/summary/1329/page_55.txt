Chapter 6 of the paper focuses on the various risks businesses encounter when engaging with artificial intelligence (AI), emphasizing the importance of ethical considerations in risk management. It posits that companies should strive to act ethically, beyond merely complying with legal requirements, as this approach may yield greater long-term profitability.

The chapter highlights that AI is not solely a technical issue but a significant business concern, with the risks associated with advanced technologies expected to escalate in the future. Among these, the risks of severe accidents—such as those involving autonomous vehicles or AI systems managing critical infrastructure—are particularly notable, as they can lead to substantial harm and potentially jeopardize a company's existence.

While traditional risks like political, currency, and country risks are acknowledged, the focus shifts to the increasing recognition of ethical risks by large multinational companies. These ethical risks include corruption, discrimination, and human rights abuses, as well as the potential for advanced technologies to manipulate human behavior. Such ethical dilemmas can ultimately translate into economic risks in a globalized context. The chapter underscores the necessity for businesses to proactively manage both traditional and ethical risks to safeguard their future.

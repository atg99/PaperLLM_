The paper "An Introduction to Ethics in Robotics and AI" by Christoph Bartneck, Christoph Lütge, Alan Wagner, and Sean Welsh explores the ethical considerations surrounding the development and deployment of robotics and artificial intelligence. It discusses the potential impacts of these technologies on society, including issues of safety, privacy, and autonomy. The authors emphasize the importance of integrating ethical frameworks into the design processes of AI and robotics to mitigate risks and enhance societal benefits. They argue for a multidisciplinary approach that includes input from ethicists, engineers, and policymakers to address the complex challenges posed by these advancements. The paper serves as a foundational resource for understanding the ethical implications and responsibilities associated with robotics and AI.

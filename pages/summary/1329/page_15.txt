Chapter 2 provides an overview of Artificial Intelligence (AI), exploring various definitions, the learning processes of machines, and the operational mechanics of robots. It also addresses the limitations of AI and the impact of media on public perceptions of the technology.

The chapter begins with an example involving Siri, a virtual assistant, illustrating the complexities involved in processing human language. It highlights that when Chris asks Siri whether he should lie about his weight on a dating profile, the assistant encounters challenges in understanding the context and meaning of the question. The process involves converting sound into digital signals, transmitting data to a cloud computer, classifying the sounds into words, and extracting their meaning. This includes discerning the homophone "lie," which complicates understanding.

Furthermore, the chapter notes that even if Siri comprehends the question accurately, providing appropriate advice requires knowledge about the implications of weight on attractiveness and dating success. The discussion emphasizes that while existing AI techniques can handle many steps in this process, the nuanced understanding necessary for context-aware advice remains a significant challenge for AI systems.

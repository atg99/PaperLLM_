Chapter 9 of the paper examines various applications of artificial intelligence (AI) while highlighting the ethical challenges associated with these technologies. Specifically, it focuses on the fields of AI enhancement, healthcare, and education, noting the significance of ethical considerations in these domains.

In the section on AI enhancement, the potential for merging humans and machines is discussed, particularly through robotic components that can replace biological functions. The text distinguishes between restorative and enhancement technologies. Robotic restoration is ethically uncontroversial, as it involves replacing lost physical or cognitive abilities with equivalent mechanical or electronic solutions, such as robotic prosthetics. In contrast, enhancement refers to the replacement of abilities with superior capabilities, leading to ethical dilemmas.

The distinction between restoration and enhancement is not always clear-cut, as advancements in technology can blur the lines. Some prosthetics may initially serve a restorative function but evolve to provide enhanced capabilities, complicating the ethical landscape. Overall, the chapter emphasizes the need for careful consideration of the ethical implications of AI applications, particularly in enhancement scenarios.

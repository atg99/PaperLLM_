The section discusses the phenomenon of unidirectional emotional bonding with artificial intelligence (AI) and its implications for human relationships. It highlights how AI systems, particularly those designed for companionship, can lead to unrealistic expectations and inhibit genuine human interactions. For instance, the case of a phone company employing professional talkers demonstrates how customers may be left without real connections, as these AI-like interactions can be overly engaging yet ultimately unfulfilling.

The text further explores the potential risks associated with intimate AI relationships, such as amorous robots that could foster harmful expectations and cognitive development issues in adolescents. It raises ethical concerns about the possibility of creating robotic replicas of past relationships, which could lead to emotional abuse.

Research findings suggest that humans are inclined to form emotional bonds with robots, even when these robots lack true emotional capabilities. Studies indicate that people often attribute social characteristics and moral accountability to robots, even when they exhibit limited social competence. The role individuals assume during interactions with robots can also influence the nature of the bond formed; for example, receiving care from a robot tends to foster a more positive perception of it.

In summary, the text underscores the tendency of humans to engage emotionally with robots, despite the one-sided nature of these interactions, and how such bonds can impact the dynamics of persuasion between humans and AI.

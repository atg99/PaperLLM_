The paper discusses the considerations that Siri, as an AI, must take into account when advising users on online dating. It highlights the importance of understanding social dynamics, such as the attractiveness of participants and the common motivations for dating. Siri needs to recognize that while dishonesty may enhance short-term attractiveness, it can have detrimental effects in the long run once individuals meet in person. The paper emphasizes that many users provide inaccurate information on their profiles, and a degree of dishonesty may not significantly impact long-term relationship success.

Furthermore, the paper outlines various moral frameworks that Siri could adopt, specifically focusing on consequentialism. This philosophical approach suggests that the value of an action is determined by its outcomes. Drawing on the ideas of Jeremy Bentham and John Stuart Mill, the paper posits that Siri should aim to maximize overall happiness, not just for the user, Chris, but also for his potential date. Consequently, Siri's advice would be geared towards increasing Chris's opportunities for first dates and enhancing his chances of finding a meaningful relationship.

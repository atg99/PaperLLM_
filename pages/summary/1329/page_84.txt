The section discusses the integration of robots and artificial systems in telemedicine, particularly in psychological care. It highlights the potential of these systems to conduct preliminary psychological evaluations, such as identifying depression through online behavior, which could help in preventing severe outcomes like suicide. However, concerns arise regarding the autonomy of individuals and the risk of creating "nanny technologies" that may hinder personal problem-solving. Additionally, it mentions the development of virtual agents designed to interview individuals with Post Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD), noting that patients may feel more comfortable disclosing their issues to these virtual agents than to human therapists. This raises important considerations about the effectiveness and ethical implications of using technology in mental health care.

The section discusses the ethical risks associated with AI and robotic technologies, emphasizing their significance for managers in a globalized economy. 

Reputational risk is highlighted as a major concern, exemplified by biased systems like facial recognition and loan approval processes that can lead to public backlash. The Cambridge Analytica scandal serves as a case study illustrating how misuse of data can severely damage a company's reputation, as seen with Facebook.

Legal risk refers to the potential for systems to be deemed anti-competitive, inviting regulatory scrutiny and significant fines. Historical examples include Microsoft and Google, both of which faced hefty penalties for antitrust violations.

Environmental risk is discussed through the lens of the Bhopal disaster, where a gas leak caused catastrophic human and environmental damage. This incident underscores the severe consequences of system failures, leading to substantial financial penalties for Union Carbide.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the need for careful management of these ethical risks in AI deployment to mitigate potential harm and maintain corporate integrity.

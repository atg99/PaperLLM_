the paper discusses the privacy issues associated with artificial intelligence, particularly focusing on persistent surveillance enabled by digital assistants like Amazon's Alexa and Google Home. These devices continuously collect audio data from users' homes, which is transmitted to the companies for storage and analysis. While the primary purpose of this data collection is to respond to user requests, there are concerns that it may also be used for unauthorized surveillance of users and their environments. The implications of such data collection practices remain uncertain, raising significant questions about privacy and consent in the age of AI. The paper highlights the necessity for greater transparency and ethical considerations in the deployment of these technologies.

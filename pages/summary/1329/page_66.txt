As robots and AI systems develop relationships with humans, their ability to persuade can lead to ethical dilemmas, particularly if users are unaware of the influence being exerted. This raises the question of how to ensure that AI systems are transparent about their intentions and the information they utilize.

In summary, the paper discusses the problems associated with anthropomorphizing robots, particularly the tendency for users to place misplaced trust in AI systems. Historical examples illustrate that while robots may perform calculations accurately, their decision-making capabilities can be flawed, leading to dangerous situations. The paper also highlights the potential for persuasive AI, which can leverage social relationships to influence human behavior, posing ethical concerns regarding manipulation and transparency. As AI technology evolves, it is crucial to address these challenges to ensure safe and ethical interactions between humans and machines.

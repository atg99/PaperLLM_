This book was developed as part of the European Project "Europe's ICT Innovation Partnership With Australia, Singapore & New Zealand (EPIC)," funded by the European Commission under grant agreement Nr 687794. The consortium includes partners such as eutema GmbH, Intersect Australia Limited, Royal Melbourne Institute of Technology, Callaghan Innovation Research Limited, University of Canterbury, National University of Singapore, and Institute for Infocomm Research. 

From February 2 to 6, 2019, the project team convened at the National University of Singapore, guided by Laia Ros from Book Sprints, to collaboratively write this book. The process was characterized by mutual respect and enthusiasm for the shared interest in artificial intelligence and ethics. The diverse backgrounds of the contributors allowed for a comprehensive exploration of various topics related to AI and ethics, utilizing the BookSprint method for production.

The paper discusses the role of artificial intelligence (AI) in education, focusing on two main applications: chatbots and teaching systems. Chatbots, such as those offered by Chatbase, enable organizations to create interactive support tools for users. While they can handle basic inquiries, complex questions still require human intervention. AI also assists in plagiarism detection, crucial in an era where students can easily copy online content. Advanced plagiarism software can identify not just identical phrases but also nuanced similarities, helping educators ensure originality in student submissions.

In terms of teaching, AI systems present several advantages over traditional methods. They offer scalability, allowing personalized learning experiences tailored to individual student needs, and are accessible anytime and anywhere without fatigue. This can create a more comfortable learning environment, especially for novice language learners. However, AI teaching agents are most effective in structured subjects like math, where correct answers are clear, but they struggle with subjective assessments like poetry appreciation. Additionally, AI shows potential in supporting children with Autism Spectrum Disorder, where the robots' predictable behaviors can be beneficial. The ethical implications of using robots for general childcare are also noted, highlighting the complexities of AI in educational contexts.

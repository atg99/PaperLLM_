The paper discusses the military applications of artificial intelligence (AI), focusing on autonomous weapon systems (AWS). It begins with definitions, followed by an exploration of AWS characteristics such as discrimination, proportionality, and responsibility, which are critical in military contexts. The paper examines existing regulations governing these systems and presents ethical arguments for and against their use in military settings. Proponents argue that AI can enhance operational effectiveness and reduce human risk, while opponents raise concerns about accountability and moral implications. The conclusion summarizes these discussions, emphasizing the need for careful consideration of ethical frameworks in AI deployment. The subsequent section addresses the broader strategic challenges of ethics in AI and robotics, highlighting the importance of international cooperation in establishing ethical standards and regulations.

The paper discusses the ethical challenges posed by artificial intelligence (AI) and robotics, emphasizing the importance of international cooperation in regulating these technologies. It highlights the reliance of AI systems on vast amounts of data, often personal, and the implications of privacy protections such as the EU's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which has influenced global policy-making, including in California. The paper also addresses the impacts of AI on labor laws and the need for international dialogue, as seen in New Zealand's AI strategy, which calls for engagement with labor policy communities and raises concerns about AI in warfare.

Furthermore, the paper stresses the critical role of AI in development policies, advocating for strategies that ensure equitable access to AI technology for developing countries, rather than allowing it to be dominated by industrialized nations. Overall, the paper underscores the necessity for a collaborative international approach to navigate the complex ethical landscape of AI and robotics.

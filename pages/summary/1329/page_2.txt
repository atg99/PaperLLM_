SpringerBriefs in Ethics is a series that focuses on concise publications addressing various ethical issues and dilemmas in contemporary society. The series aims to provide a platform for scholars and practitioners to present innovative ideas and insights related to ethics across different fields, including healthcare, technology, and social justice. Each brief typically offers a thorough analysis of a specific ethical topic, presenting both theoretical frameworks and practical applications. The series encourages interdisciplinary dialogue and aims to contribute to the ongoing discourse on ethical practices and decision-making in a rapidly changing world.

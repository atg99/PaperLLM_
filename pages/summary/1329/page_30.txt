In summary, the paper discusses three major ethical theories: deontological ethics, represented by Kant's categorical imperative which emphasizes actions based on universal maxims; consequentialist ethics, which evaluates actions solely on their outcomes regardless of motives; and virtue ethics, rooted in the teachings of Plato and Aristotle, focusing on the character and virtues of the moral agent. Additionally, meta-ethics delves into the foundational aspects of ethical theories, exploring the existence, meaning, and knowledge surrounding moral values.

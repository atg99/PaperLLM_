The paper discusses the application of artificial intelligence (AI) in addressing societal issues, particularly loneliness among older adults. In 2018, the UK appointed a minister to tackle loneliness, leading to the development of robots like Paro, designed to alleviate social isolation. However, ethical concerns have emerged regarding the potential for patients with dementia to misinterpret the robot as a real companion, which could inadvertently increase social isolation by discouraging family visits. Despite these concerns, some argue that the benefits of such technology outweigh the risks.

Additionally, the paper addresses the controversial use of AI in "nudging" individuals towards healthier behaviors, such as robotic weight-loss coaches that monitor eating habits and encourage exercise. This raises ethical questions about autonomy, emphasizing that individuals should have the freedom to make their own lifestyle choices without undue influence from AI.

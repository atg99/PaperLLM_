The paper titled "Fundamentals of Recurrent Neural Network (RNN) and Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) Network" by Alex Sherstinsky, published in the Elsevier journal “Physica D: Nonlinear Phenomena”, aims to provide a comprehensive understanding of RNNs and LSTMs. It addresses the gap in existing literature where training formulas are often omitted and the concept of "unrolling" RNNs is presented without adequate justification. 

The author begins by deriving the canonical RNN formulation from differential equations, establishing a formal foundation for the discussion. A precise statement is proposed and proven to clarify the RNN unrolling technique. The paper also acknowledges the training difficulties associated with standard RNNs and proposes a transformation into the "Vanilla LSTM" network, providing a logical framework for this transition.

Sherstinsky emphasizes the importance of providing all relevant equations and detailed descriptions of the LSTM components, opting for an unconventional notation to enhance understanding. The analysis identifies opportunities to enrich the LSTM architecture, resulting in an augmented version of the Vanilla LSTM.

The target audience includes machine learning practitioners with prior exposure to RNNs and LSTMs, who are seeking a deeper understanding of the systems' functions and principles. The paper serves as a guide for those looking to implement the new LSTM model in research and experimentation, aiming to fill the gaps left by existing resources and promote a more intuitive grasp of the subject.

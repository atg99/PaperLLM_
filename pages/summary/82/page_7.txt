The paper introduces two weight matrices and a bias vector to transform a system into the canonical Recurrent Neural Network (RNN) form. The equations define the relationships between the state vector, input, and recurrent connections, culminating in the formulation of the RNN as shown in Equation 30, where the state vector at time n is influenced by the previous state, the previous output, and the current input, along with a bias term. The output at time n is derived from a function G applied to the state vector. The paper notes that the bias parameters can be incorporated into the input signal vector for simplicity. It also mentions that this RNN formulation will be further developed into a Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) system and highlights the importance of concepts such as "unrolling" or "unfolding" for understanding the structure of RNNs.

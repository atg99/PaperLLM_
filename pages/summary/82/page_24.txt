The section outlines the notation and key components of the Vanilla LSTM cell. It defines various symbols that represent the indices, dimensionalities, signals, and mathematical operations involved in the LSTM architecture. Key notation includes n for the index of a step, K for the total number of steps, and specific signals such as the input signal (⃗ x), state signal (⃗ s), and observable value signal (⃗ v). It also introduces warping functions (Gc and Gd) used for control and data bounding.

Moreover, the section details the three types of gate nodes within the Vanilla LSTM cell, which manage the control of update candidate signals, state signals from previous steps, and readout candidate signals for external access. 

In terms of data preparation, it emphasizes the importance of standardizing the external training dataset (⃗ x0[n]) before LSTM operation. This involves transforming the dataset to have a mean of zero and a standard deviation of one, utilizing equations to calculate the sample mean (⃗ µ) and the sample auto-covariance matrix (V). The standardized input (⃗ x[n]) is then derived from these calculations, ensuring that the test and validation sets are standardized based on the training set statistics.

5.2 Computational insights into ResNet architectures
The analysis of the spatial configuration of data points and the corresponding Jacobian maps across different ResNet architectures reveals significant differences in their behavior and transformation capabilities. The results show that ResNets without shared weights exhibit a broader range of Jacobian values, indicating more complex transformations. In contrast, architectures employing shared weights, such as Data-driven and Domain-driven Symmetric ResNets, demonstrate more controlled transformations with limited Jacobian ranges, suggesting a more stable and consistent mapping of input data.

The findings underscore the importance of weight sharing in shaping the dynamics of data transformations within ResNet architectures. This shared weight approach not only simplifies the model but also enhances its interpretability and robustness by constraining the flow of information.

In conclusion, viewing ResNet architectures through the lens of diffeomorphisms offers valuable insights into their theoretical underpinnings and practical implications in deep learning. This perspective highlights the significance of velocity fields and their role in the continuous transformation of data, paving the way for more informed design choices in future neural network architectures.

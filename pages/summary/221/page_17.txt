This paper presents a new registration-based formulation of Residual Networks (ResNets) and interprets ResNets as numerical implementations of continuous flows of diffeomorphisms. The authors emphasize the significance of enforcing diffeomorphic properties to enhance the stability of trained ResNets, particularly addressing robustness to initialization. By incorporating invertibility constraints into the training loss, the proposed approach aims to improve registration accuracy. Numerical experiments validate the theoretical framework, indicating that diffeomorphic properties are crucial for the performance of ResNets. The research opens new avenues for exploring diffeomorphism-based formulations and associated numerical tools in ResNet learning.

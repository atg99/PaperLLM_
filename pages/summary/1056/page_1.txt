The paper "Explainable AI: A Review of Machine Learning Interpretability Methods" by Pantelis Linardatos, Vasilis Papastefanopoulos, and Sotiris Kotsiantis examines the growing importance of explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) in the context of machine learning (ML). The authors highlight that while recent advances in AI have led to remarkable performance in various applications, particularly through complex models like deep learning, this complexity often results in "black box" systems that lack transparency. Such systems pose challenges in critical areas such as healthcare, where understanding decision-making processes is essential.

The review presents a comprehensive taxonomy of machine learning interpretability methods, aiming to provide a reference for both researchers and practitioners. It emphasizes the trade-off between the predictive power of models and their explainability, distinguishing between black-box models, which are more complex and less interpretable, and white-box models, which offer clearer insights into their operations. The authors argue that as AI continues to permeate sensitive domains, the need for effective interpretability methods will become increasingly critical to ensure trust and accountability in AI systems. 

Overall, this work contributes to the discourse on XAI by systematically categorizing interpretability techniques and linking them to their programming implementations, thus fostering further research and application in the field.

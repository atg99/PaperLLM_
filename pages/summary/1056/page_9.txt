The paper discusses various interpretability methods designed to explain deep learning models, focusing on their categorization based on local versus global interpretations, model specificity versus agnosticism, and data types. A table summarizes notable tools, including DeepExplain, Grad-CAM, and iNNvestigate, detailing their citations and the year of introduction. 

A significant concept introduced in the paper is Concept Activation Vectors (CAVs), which offer a human-friendly interpretation of a neural network's internal state. CAVs help bridge the gap between input features and high-level concepts that cannot be represented as pixels, such as "human" or "animal." This method addresses limitations of saliency maps by translating input vectors into a conceptual framework, allowing for a quantitative measure of how sensitive a model’s predictions are to user-defined concepts. The TCAV method utilizes this framework, enhancing the interpretability of deep learning models.

The paper discusses advancements in generating adversarial examples in machine learning, particularly focusing on the Fast Gradient Sign Method (FGSM) and its enhancements. The FGSM algorithm was modified to repeatedly apply small perturbations while truncating intermediate results, ensuring that the generated adversarial examples remain close to the original images. Additionally, Dong et al. introduced momentum into iterative algorithms, creating a new class of adversarial momentum-based techniques that improve stability and help escape local minima during optimization.

The paper also highlights the NATTACK method proposed by Li et al., which instead of finding an optimal adversarial example, fits a probability distribution around a given example. This approach assumes that any example generated from this distribution is a strong adversarial candidate, offering advantages such as improved diversity and robustness in adversarial example generation. The findings underscore the importance of iterative refinement and probabilistic modeling in the development of effective adversarial strategies.

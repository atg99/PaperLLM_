The paper presents a Partial Dependence Plot (PDP) from a Random Forest model, demonstrating the relationship between age as a feature and income percentile as the label using the Census Income dataset from the UCI Machine Learning Repository. Additionally, it provides a summary table outlining various interpretability methods for explaining black-box models. The table categorizes tools based on their applicability (local vs. global), specificity (model-specific vs. model-agnostic), and data type, while also noting citations and publication years. This highlights the ongoing development and importance of tools aimed at enhancing the interpretability of machine learning models.

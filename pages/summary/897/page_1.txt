The paper discusses the application of data augmentation techniques in deep learning for analyzing electroencephalography (EEG) data. The authors, Elnaz Lashgari, Dehua Liang, and Uri Maoz from Chapman University, aim to enhance the performance of deep learning models in EEG signal processing by increasing the diversity and volume of training data. They emphasize the importance of robust training datasets for improving model accuracy and generalizability. The study presents various data augmentation methods tailored for EEG data, demonstrating their effectiveness in overcoming challenges such as limited data availability and class imbalance. The findings suggest that these augmentation techniques can significantly enhance the performance of deep learning models in EEG analysis, contributing to advancements in neurological research and applications. The article was published in the Journal of Neuroscience Methods.

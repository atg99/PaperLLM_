This section of the paper references a selection of studies focused on the application of deep learning techniques to analyze electroencephalography (EEG) signals for various purposes, including seizure prediction, motor imagery classification, and sleep stage scoring. 

1. Truong et al. explore semi-supervised seizure prediction using Generative Adversarial Networks, highlighting the potential of this method in improving prediction accuracy. 
2. Majidov and Whangbo present efficient classification methods for motor imagery EEG signals through deep learning approaches. 
3. Mousavi et al. utilize a deep convolutional neural network to classify sleep stages from single-channel EEG data. 
4. Avcu et al. demonstrate seizure detection using a minimal number of EEG channels with a deep convolutional neural network. 
5. Tayeb et al. validate deep neural networks for real-time decoding of motor imagery movements from EEG signals. 
6. Tsiouris et al. apply a Long Short-Term Memory network for predicting epileptic seizures, illustrating the effectiveness of recurrent neural networks in this context. 
7. Tang et al. discuss failure prediction with adaptive sampling and regularization techniques at the IEEE International Conference on Data Mining Workshops. 
8. Manor and Geva propose a convolutional neural network for rapid visual presentation brain-computer interfaces. 
9. Drouin-Picaro and Falk focus on classifying natural saccades using deep neural networks. 
10. Supratak et al. introduce DeepSleepNet for automatic sleep stage scoring based on raw single-channel EEG data. 
11. Dong et al. present a mixed neural network approach for classifying temporal sleep stages. 
12. Ruffini et al. investigate deep learning with EEG spectrograms for prognosis in rapid eye movement behavior disorder. 
13. Sun et al. propose a two-stage neural network for sleep stage classification, emphasizing feature and sequence learning along with data augmentation strategies.

These studies collectively underscore the growing significance of deep learning methodologies in advancing EEG signal analysis across various applications.

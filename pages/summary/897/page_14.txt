The paper presents an overview of deep learning architectures, illustrated in Figure 6, which categorizes these architectures into three concentric circles. The inner circle represents the general deep learning architecture, the middle circle outlines primary design features like hidden and convolutional layers, and the outer circle depicts the final layer structure, including fully connected layers and the softmax function. Figure 7 analyzes the input formulation strategies employed by various deep learning architectures, highlighting significant differences based on architecture type. Notably, recurrent neural networks (RNN) and stacked autoencoders (SAE) exclusively utilized images and calculated features as inputs, respectively. In contrast, hybrid models, convolutional neural networks (CNN), and multi-layer perceptrons (MLP) incorporated all three types of information, with MLP and CNN directly using signal values as inputs.

The references provided encompass a diverse range of studies and methodologies related to electroencephalography (EEG) and neural data analysis. Key works include a systematic review of deep learning applications in EEG analysis by Yannick et al., which highlights the growing intersection of machine learning and neuroscience. Cohen's book provides foundational theory and practical insights for analyzing neural time series data. The PREP pipeline introduced by Bigdely-Shamlo et al. standardizes preprocessing for large-scale EEG studies, while Jas et al. present Autoreject for automated artifact rejection in EEG and MEG data.

Additionally, works by Cole and Voytek emphasize cycle-by-cycle analysis of neural oscillations, and Gramfort et al. explore time-frequency mixed-norm estimates for sparse M/EEG imaging. Shanechi discusses the evolution of brain-machine interfaces, and Sani et al. investigate neural decoding for mood regulation in neuropsychiatric treatment. Hively et al. focus on detecting dynamical changes in scalp EEG signals, while Subasi and Gursoy address EEG signal classification using various machine learning techniques.

Further, Sanz-García et al. identify potential EEG biomarkers for sedation in intensive care patients through machine learning, and He and Garcia examine challenges in learning from imbalanced data. Lotte et al. provide a comprehensive review of EEG classification algorithms for brain-computer interfaces, and foundational texts on deep learning by Chollet and Goodfellow et al. offer essential frameworks for understanding and implementing advanced neural network techniques in this field.

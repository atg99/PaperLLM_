They applied common spatial pattern to extract relevant features from the EEG signals, which were then fed into a convolutional neural network for seizure detection. Their approach focused on improving the classification accuracy and robustness of the model by increasing the diversity of the training data. The experiments demonstrated that the integration of CSP with CNN significantly enhanced the model's performance compared to traditional methods. The findings indicate that effective feature extraction combined with advanced neural network architectures can lead to better outcomes in EEG-based applications. Overall, the studies highlight the importance of data augmentation and innovative signal processing techniques in enhancing the performance of deep learning models for EEG signal classification tasks.

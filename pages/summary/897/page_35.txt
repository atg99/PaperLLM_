The section discusses the impact of data augmentation (DA) on the accuracy of deep learning (DL) models applied to electroencephalography (EEG) data, noting that research in this area is still emerging. Out of 53 surveyed papers, 29 reported accuracy measures before and after DA. An improvement score was calculated, representing the fraction of the gap between initial accuracy and perfect accuracy that was bridged by training on the augmented dataset. The overall improvement score was found to be 0.29±0.08, indicating that various DA techniques enhance classification accuracy for EEG tasks. The effectiveness of DA varied, with scores ranging from 0.08 for segmentation recombination to 0.36 for noise addition. Task-specific improvements also varied, with scores from 0.14 for motor imagery to 0.56 for mental workload. Notably, the 95% confidence intervals for most tasks and DA techniques did not include zero, suggesting a positive impact of DA. However, the authors caution that these findings are based on a limited number of analyses, highlighting the need for further research to establish reliable DA improvement scores across different techniques and tasks.

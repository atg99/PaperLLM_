This section of the paper focuses on data augmentation (DA) methods used to enhance EEG signals for machine learning applications. DA involves creating new samples from existing datasets through various transformations, which helps improve the accuracy and robustness of classification or regression models. By exposing classifiers to a broader range of training samples, DA aids in making models more invariant to variations they may encounter in real-world applications, thereby enhancing generalization to unseen data. 

The significance of DA in EEG research has surged, with a notable increase in published studies, particularly in 2018 and 2019, where 72% of the identified papers were published. The analysis of 53 papers identified seven categories of DA techniques: noise addition (17%), generative adversarial networks (GAN) (21%), sliding window (24%), sampling (17%), Fourier transform (4%), recombination of segmentation (6%), and other methods (11%).

In exploring noise addition, the research categorized it into two main approaches: introducing various types of noise, such as Gaussian and Poisson, with different parameters to the raw EEG signals. This method aims to enhance the dataset, thereby improving the performance of deep learning models applied to EEG data. Further detailed discussions on each DA method are provided in the subsequent sections of the paper.

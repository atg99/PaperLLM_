The paper discusses the application of data augmentation techniques in the context of deep learning for analyzing electroencephalography (EEG) data. It highlights the challenges posed by the limited availability of labeled EEG datasets, which can hinder the performance of deep learning models. To address this issue, the authors propose various data augmentation strategies that can enhance the diversity of the training data without requiring additional data collection. These strategies aim to improve model robustness and generalization, ultimately leading to better performance in EEG analysis tasks. The paper emphasizes the importance of these techniques in advancing the field of neuroscience and improving the accuracy of EEG-based applications. The definitive version of this work was published in the Journal of Neuroscience Methods in 2020.

The paper discusses the challenges and advancements in data augmentation (DA) techniques applied to electroencephalography (EEG) signals, particularly in the context of deep learning (DL). It highlights the difficulty in correctly labeling augmented EEG data, unlike image datasets where human judgment is more straightforward. Despite these challenges, DA has gained significant attention and has shown performance improvements in DL applications for EEG.

A systematic review was conducted, covering papers up to 2019, with the earliest mention of "data augmentation" found in a 2015 paper. The review indicates a notable increase in research output, with 70% of the identified 53 papers published in 2018 and 2019. The authors aim to address key questions regarding existing DA methods for EEG, the datasets and classification tasks explored, the suitability of specific DA methods for various tasks, and the input features used for training deep neural networks with DA.

The methods section details the search strategy employed to identify relevant studies, using specific keywords and adhering to inclusion criteria focused on EEG classification, deep learning, and EEG augmentation. The review excludes certain studies, including those without a classification end goal and review papers, ensuring a targeted analysis of DA in EEG contexts.

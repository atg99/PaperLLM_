The database queries identified 295 results, of which 32 were duplicates. After manually screening the remaining 263 papers, 188 were deemed irrelevant, leading to a final selection of 75 papers for careful review against inclusion criteria. Further inspection revealed that 22 of these did not meet the criteria, resulting in 53 papers included in the analysis. The earliest paper selected was from 2015, although terms like Data Augmentation (DA) and Neural Networks (NN) were present in earlier works, such as the 1998 application of CNNs in LeNet-5 for handwritten digit classification. Despite earlier studies on data augmentation and oversampling techniques used since the 1970s, none met the inclusion criteria for this review.

For each selected paper, approximately 40 features were extracted across seven categories: article origin, DA types, dataset, task information, preprocessing, deep learning strategy, and results.

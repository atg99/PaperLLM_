The paper presents a comparative analysis of the DBN-MOA method against traditional machine learning techniques, specifically focusing on f-score performance and execution time. The results illustrate that DBN-MOA significantly outperforms other methods across various data sizes. For instance, on a dataset of 100 points, DBN-MOA achieved an f-score of 88.132%, compared to SVM, RF, KNN, LR, and DT which scored 60.832%, 64.981%, 69.382%, 75.659%, and 82.132% respectively. Notably, with 600 data points, DBN-MOA's f-score rose to 93.187%, while the other methods had lower scores.

In terms of execution time, DBN-MOA also demonstrated superior efficiency. For 100 data points, it executed in just 1.384 seconds, while the other methods took significantly longer: SVM (7.762s), RF (6.465s), KNN (5.484s), LR (3.732s), and DT (2.981s). With 600 data points, DBN-MOA's execution time was 2.4 seconds, compared to SVM (8.990s), RF (6.965s), KNN (5.693s), LR (4.382s), and DT (2.999s). Overall, the analysis highlights the effectiveness and efficiency of the DBN-MOA method in comparison to existing machine learning techniques.

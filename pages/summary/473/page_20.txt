The paper presents a model that demonstrates superior efficiency compared to existing models for multiclass Alzheimer's disease (AD) stage classification. Future research is anticipated to leverage EfficientNet B0-B7 and other pre-trained models to enhance the efficacy of these classifications. The study also emphasizes the importance of data augmentation techniques to expand the dataset and highlights the role of MRI segmentation in identifying Alzheimer's characteristics prior to classification.

The authors contributed collaboratively across various stages of the research, including conceptualization, analysis, investigation, project administration, and writing. The study was funded by Princess Nourah bint Abdulrahman University, and publicly available datasets were utilized for analysis. No conflicts of interest were declared.

The references cited in the paper include a range of studies related to Alzheimer's disease diagnostics, machine learning applications in healthcare, and neuroimaging methodologies, underscoring the interdisciplinary nature of the research.

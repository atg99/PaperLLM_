The proposed system, referred to as DBN-MOA, emphasizes the importance of data collection and pre-processing as foundational steps in the research process. The authors highlight the significance of data fusion, which involves integrating information from diverse sources to enhance data accuracy and consistency. They also discuss the three primary types of data fusion: feature-level, low-level, and decision-level. The methodology includes a systematic approach to data handling, ensuring that the model is trained and evaluated effectively using a robust dataset. Overall, the paper presents a comprehensive framework aimed at improving the diagnostic capabilities for Alzheimer's disease through advanced machine learning techniques.

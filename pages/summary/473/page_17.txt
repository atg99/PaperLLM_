In this study, the performance of the DBN-MOA method is compared against various existing machine learning techniques across multiple metrics, including precision, recall, RMSE, and F-score. 

1. **Precision Analysis**: The DBN-MOA method demonstrates superior precision compared to SVM, RF, KNN, LR, and DT models across different dataset sizes. 

2. **Recall Analysis**: The recall for the DBN-MOA method at a dataset size of 100 is 90.162%, significantly higher than the other models, which range from 70.632% to 85.652%. Under 600 data points, DBN-MOA achieves a recall of 95.789%, again outperforming the other methods.

3. **RMSE Analysis**: The DBN-MOA method shows a lower RMSE of 20.685% on a dataset of 100, while the other models have higher RMSE values. Under 600 data points, DBN-MOA maintains a low RMSE of 23.785%, compared to RMSE values of the other models, which are substantially higher.

4. **F-Score Analysis**: The f-score for DBN-MOA is 88.132% on a dataset of 100, outperforming all other models, which have f-scores ranging from 60.832% to 82.132%. Under 600 data points, DBN-MOA achieves an impressive f-score of 93.187%.

Overall, the DBN-MOA method consistently outperforms the existing systems across all evaluated metrics, indicating its robustness and effectiveness in handling varied data sizes.

The paper discusses the Mayfly Optimization Algorithm (MA), a hybrid optimization method that integrates features from Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO), Genetic Algorithms (GA), and the Firefly Algorithm. The MA is inspired by the mating behavior of mayflies, focusing on the survival of the fittest, where each mayfly represents a potential solution in the search space.

In this algorithm, male and female mayflies are randomly generated, and their positions in the solution space are represented by a position vector. The objective function evaluates these positions to determine their effectiveness. The movement of each mayfly is influenced by both its personal best position and the best positions found by other mayflies, facilitating a collaborative search for optimal solutions.

The algorithm details the flight dynamics of male mayflies, which adjust their positions based on new information. The next position is updated using a formula that incorporates current location and velocity, with constants determining the significance of personal and social influences. The distances between a mayfly's position and its personal and global bests are calculated to guide movement.

The paper emphasizes that the strongest mayflies, symbolizing the most promising solutions, engage in a vertical nuptial dance, which is crucial for maintaining the algorithm's effectiveness in finding optimal outcomes. Overall, the MA leverages the natural behavior of mayflies to enhance optimization processes in complex problem-solving scenarios.

The paper presents a comparison of the performance of word-level 4-gram and Transformer language models (LMs) on audio transcriptions from the L IBRISPEECH dev-other dataset, focusing on original versus shuffled transcriptions. Table 9 illustrates the performance metrics, highlighting significant differences in perplexity across various settings, such as TTS (text-to-speech) and recording conditions. The results indicate that shuffling the transcriptions disrupts crucial word context, leading to higher perplexity scores and suggesting that the word error rate (WER) gap serves as an indicator of the language modeling capabilities of the acoustic model. The findings underscore the importance of maintaining word context for effective language modeling in speech recognition tasks.

While maintaining shareholder satisfaction through dividends is necessary, prioritizing financial stability and growth through reinvestment of net income is crucial. Managers may need to adjust or suspend dividend policies in response to market conditions.

The paper highlights the significance of tracking pretax return on assets as a performance measure, especially in comparison to competitors and industry averages. Underperformance signals potential financial distress, necessitating swift managerial action to identify and resolve underlying issues.

Total risk-based capital is discussed as a protective measure against asset failures, with higher capitalization levels essential for preventing bank failures. The paper notes that banks often hold less capital than regulatory requirements due to the associated costs, risking their solvency during economic downturns.

Additionally, while above-industry-average yields on earning assets may seem attractive, they often stem from high-risk investments. The paper advises managers to avoid such risks and instead focus on sustainable investments that contribute to long-term financial health.

The paper presents a classification model aimed at identifying key variables for monitoring potential bank failures. It categorizes explanatory variables into three main groups: performance ratios, condition ratios, and other variables. Performance ratios include metrics such as yield on earning assets, cost of funding earning assets, net interest margin, and return on equity. Condition ratios encompass measures like earning assets to total assets, loss allowance to loans, and equity capital to assets. The paper highlights the use of XGBoost, a modern machine learning algorithm, to analyze accounting and financial data from 2001 to 2015 for predicting bank failures. The objective is to enhance the predictive capability regarding the stability of banking institutions by identifying critical financial indicators.

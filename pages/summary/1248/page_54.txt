The document discusses the definitions of key financial metrics used for regulatory purposes. It emphasizes the Total Risk-Based Capital Ratio, which is calculated as total risk-based capital expressed as a percentage of risk-weighted assets, as defined by the appropriate federal regulator for prompt corrective action. Additionally, it defines Total Assets (TA) as the aggregate of all assets owned by an institution, including cash, loans, securities, bank premises, and other assets.

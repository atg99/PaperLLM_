The paper discusses the performance of a random forest model for predicting bank failures, which aggregates predictions from multiple decision trees. The model achieved an Area Under the Curve (AUC) of 0.93 on test data, indicating strong predictive ability. At a threshold of 0.5, the confusion matrix revealed an overall accuracy of 92.11%. The analysis indicates that both logistic regression and random forest models performed worse than the XGBoost algorithm. The paper includes a summary table of these results for clarity. The random forest model's confusion matrix shows a high accuracy rate for both predicted distress categories.

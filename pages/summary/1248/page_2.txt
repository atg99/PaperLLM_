The introduction outlines the critical role of banks in the economy, emphasizing their functions in facilitating transactions and channeling credit to households and businesses. It highlights the importance of trust and reputation in the banking industry, noting that a loss of confidence can lead to rapid withdrawals by depositors, potentially resulting in bankruptcy and jeopardizing financial stability. The paper aims to evaluate the effectiveness of Extreme Gradient Boosting, a machine learning method, in enhancing the accuracy of bank failure predictions to mitigate financial distress.

The introduction also reflects on the significant number of U.S. bank failures since the 2008 financial crisis, with 520 failures attributed mainly to the collapse of the housing market. This crisis led to substantial asset write-downs and historic declines in household wealth, drawing parallels to past financial crises while noting unique features of the recent situation.

It aims to provide timely insights that can help regulators identify institutions that may be at risk of financial distress. However, similar to the SCOR model, SEER's effectiveness is contingent on the quality and completeness of the financial data it utilizes.

The limitations of both models highlight the need for continued refinement and enhancement of early warning systems in the banking sector. As the financial landscape evolves, these predictive models must adapt to incorporate new variables and indicators that may influence bank stability. Enhanced modeling techniques could improve the accuracy of predictions, thereby allowing regulators and bank managers to take proactive measures to mitigate risks and prevent potential failures.

In summary, while current early warning models like SCOR and SEER play crucial roles in monitoring bank health, their limitations underscore the necessity for ongoing development and improvement to better anticipate and address financial distress in banks.

The paper presents the performance of an Extreme Gradient Boosting (XGBoost) model in predicting bank distress, as evidenced by a Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC) curve with an Area Under the Curve (AUC) of 0.9778 and an overall accuracy of 94.74%. The confusion matrix indicates that the model achieved perfect accuracy in predicting non-distress cases (19 true negatives) and a high accuracy of 89.47% for distress cases (17 true positives and 2 false negatives). 

Additionally, the density plot of predicted probabilities reveals a clear distinction between cases of bank distress and non-distress, with higher probabilities for distress cases. The separation is so pronounced that a probability threshold of 0.5 effectively distinguishes between the two groups, confirming the model's strong predictive capability for the test dataset.

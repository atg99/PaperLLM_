Table 2 presents descriptive statistics comparing failed banks (F) and nonfailed banks (NF) across various financial ratios. Key observations indicate that failed banks exhibit a higher mean for the loan and lease loss provision to net charge-offs ratio (P13), suggesting greater future risk. Conversely, nonfailed banks show a significantly higher mean for the earnings coverage of net loan charge-offs ratio (P14), indicating better capacity to manage loan charge-offs. The standard deviations for P14 are notably high for both groups, reflecting variability in their ability to cover charge-offs. Overall, these statistics underscore the financial health disparities between failed and nonfailed banks, particularly in their risk management and earnings stability.

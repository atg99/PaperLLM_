The paper discusses the challenges of understanding the relationships between predictors and outcomes when using prediction methods like XGBoost. To address this, partial dependence plots are employed, specifically focusing on the four most significant variables influencing bank failure. These plots depict the marginal effects of each variable on the response variable, bank failure, highlighting general trends despite not being perfect representations. The analysis reveals a negative relationship between bank distress and three variables (P11, P9, and C13), while a positive relationship is found with variable P1. The results suggest that higher values of retained earnings to average equity, pretax return on assets, and total risk-based capital are associated with increased bank distress.

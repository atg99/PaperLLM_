The appendix defines various performance ratios used to assess financial institutions. Key variables include:

1. **Yield on earning assets**: Measures total interest income as a percentage of average income from bank services and non-interest-bearing assets relative to average total assets.
2. **Cost of funding earning assets**: Reflects the annualized interest expense on deposits and borrowed money as a percentage of average earning assets.
3. **Net interest margin**: Represents the difference between total interest income and total interest expense as a percentage of average earning assets.
4. **Noninterest income to assets**: Indicates income from services other than interest-bearing assets as a percentage of average total assets.
5. **Noninterest expense to assets**: Comprises salaries, benefits, and other noninterest expenses as a percentage of average total assets.
6. **Loan and lease loss provision to assets**: Shows the annualized provision for potential loan losses as a percentage of average total assets.
7. **Net operating income to assets**: Reflects annualized net operating income as a percentage of average total assets.
8. **Return on assets (ROA)**: Indicates net income after taxes as a percentage of average total assets.
9. **Pretax return on assets**: Measures annualized pretax net income as a percentage of average total assets, including extraordinary items.

These ratios provide essential insights into the financial performance and operational efficiency of banking institutions.

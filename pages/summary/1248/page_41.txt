Logistic regression is a widely used method for predicting bank failures, characterized by its linear model design and strong interpretability compared to nonlinear methods. In contrast, random forest is a popular supervised classification technique that utilizes decision tree models. It constructs multiple tree models by sampling different observations and variables, effectively creating a "forest" of decision trees. For example, it may use a random sample of 30 bank observations and 5 predictors to generate each tree model, repeating this process to enhance predictive performance.

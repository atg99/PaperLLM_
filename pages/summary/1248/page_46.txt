This section lists various references that contribute to the understanding of financial distress, banking performance, and analytical methodologies. Key studies include a comparison of AdaBoost and neural networks for bankruptcy forecasting by Alfaro et al., and an exploration of VIX trading under expected utility theory by Al Wakil. Amendola et al. analyze financial distress determinants in Italy using a competing risks approach, while Berger and Bouwman investigate the impact of capital on bank performance during crises. Betz et al. focus on predicting distress in European banks, and Boyd and Runkle test theoretical predictions regarding the size and performance of banking firms. Additional references include practical insights on tuning decision trees with XGBoost by Brownlee, and the application of rough set theory and support vector machines for predicting financial distress in Chinese companies by Cao et al. Lastly, Capatina et al. discuss leveraging intellectual capital through Lewin's Force Field Analysis in software development, and Chambers and Dinsmore provide a comprehensive overview of advanced analytics methodologies to drive business value.

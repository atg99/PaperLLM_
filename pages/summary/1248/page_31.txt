The study evaluates the performance of a classifier using the Area Under the Curve (AUC) statistic from Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC) analysis. The classifier achieved an AUC of 0.978 for the test data, indicating excellent predictive capability in identifying bank failures. A confusion matrix was employed to compare actual outcomes with predicted results at a threshold of 0.5, revealing a misclassification rate of 5.26%. Additionally, the model demonstrated a sensitivity rate of 89.47% and a specificity rate of 100%, confirming its high accuracy in classifying test data. Overall, these findings underscore the effectiveness of the XGBoost technique utilized in the study.

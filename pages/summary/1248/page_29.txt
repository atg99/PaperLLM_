The paper discusses the relationship between financial ratios and bank failure risk, highlighting that lower ratios are linked to reduced risk, while excessively high yields on earning assets can indicate potential financial distress. It suggests that banks with high returns on earning assets may be engaging in risky investments, leading to unjustified asset expansions. Additionally, the section on model validation emphasizes the importance of assessing a model's performance, noting that while modern classification and regression models can handle complex relationships, they are prone to overfitting, which can distort their effectiveness.

The paper highlights the strong predictive power of a developed model for identifying potential bank failures, emphasizing the importance of four key scorecard variables. It suggests that bank managers should monitor these variables to proactively address financial distress, thereby reducing reliance on government intervention. Additionally, regulators could utilize this model to identify at-risk institutions. The study also proposes avenues for future research, including expanding the sample to include state banks and analyzing a balanced sample of failed and non-failed banks in Europe, which could enhance the model's robustness and reveal cross-regional banking insights.

This section compares the predictive performance of XGBoost models fitted one, two, and three years prior to bank failure, utilizing ROC curve analysis and global accuracy metrics for evaluation. The model fitted two years before failure achieved an AUC of 0.875 and a global accuracy of 92.11%, indicating strong predictive capability. In contrast, the model fitted three years before failure yielded an AUC of 0.795 and a global accuracy of 78.95%, demonstrating a decline in performance as the time frame extended further from the failure event. The analysis reveals that the best predictive performance is observed one year before failure. Additionally, the most influential variables differ across the three models, highlighting the varying importance of predictive factors depending on the proximity to failure.

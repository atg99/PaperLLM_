In section 4.2, the paper discusses the results of transfer learning after training neural networks on ImageNet. The evaluation includes ImageNet classification on validation images, scene classification on Places205, and object detection on the PASCAL VOC 2007 dataset. For classification tasks, K-nearest neighbor (KNN) classification results using embedding features are reported, with a focus on the top K neighbors for predictions.

In the object recognition task, the authors fix the weights learned during unsupervised training and add a linear readout layer for evaluation, using cross-entropy loss and L2 weight decay for training. The results show that the proposed LA method significantly outperforms other methods across all evaluated architectures, particularly deeper networks. For instance, the LA-trained ResNet-50 achieves a top-1 accuracy of 60.2% on ImageNet, surpassing the performance of AlexNet trained directly on the supervised task. The LA method also shows consistent improvements in KNN classification across architectures.

For scene categorization using the Places dataset, the LA method again demonstrates superior performance compared to previous methods, indicating strong generalization capabilities of the learned representations. Additionally, the paper evaluates the transfer learning ability of the models for object detection using the PASCAL VOC 2007 dataset, emphasizing the effectiveness of the LA method in various visual categorization tasks. Overall, the results underscore the advantages of the LA method in transfer learning across different tasks and architectures.

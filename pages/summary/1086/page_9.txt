The paper presents VGRNN and SI-VGRNN, novel node embedding methods designed for dynamic graphs. These methods embed nodes into a random latent space, enhancing the model's ability to capture complex graph dynamics while producing interpretable representations. The authors demonstrate the effectiveness of VGRNN through simulations involving a dynamic graph with three communities, highlighting how the method captures evolving node relationships over time. The variance of latent representations for a moving node increases as it transitions between communities, indicating the model's sensitivity to changes in graph topology. In contrast, a node that remains in the same community shows stable variance, reinforcing the idea that uncertainty in representations correlates with non-smooth changes in dynamic graphs. The results show that VGRNN outperforms existing methods like DynAERNN, particularly in sparse graphs, by providing clearer community separations in latent space.

The paper discusses the Variational Graph Recurrent Neural Network (VGRNN) and its objective function derived from the variational lower bound. The evidence lower bound is expressed through an equation that involves expectations and Kullback-Leibler divergence, focusing on learning the parameters of both generative and inference models jointly. The graphical representation of VGRNN is provided, highlighting the unique role of hidden state variables in the prior distribution.

In VGRNN, an inner-product decoder is utilized to showcase the benefits of stochastic recurrent models for encoding. The generative distribution is defined for adjacency matrices, with noted flexibility for potential extensions with different decoders. 

To enhance the expressive capacity of the variational posterior, the Semi-implicit VGRNN (SI-VGRNN) is introduced. This model applies a semi-implicit hierarchical approach for the posterior, incorporating mixing distributions that allow for a more flexible variational distribution. SI-VGRNN samples from these distributions using a graph neural network, leading to implicit distributions.

The inference process in SI-VGRNN maintains the foundational models of VGRNN while updating encoder functions to accommodate the semi-implicit structure. Random noise is integrated into the encoder, resulting in a non-Gaussian posterior after marginalization, further enhancing the model's flexibility and capacity for dynamic node embeddings.

The paper presents the Variational Graph Recurrent Neural Network (VGRNN), which is designed to model complex dependencies in dynamic graphs where both the graph topology and node attributes can change over time. The VGRNN incorporates a backbone Graph Recurrent Neural Network (GRNN) that updates its hidden states using a recurrence equation, allowing for time-dependent adjacency matrices. This flexibility is crucial for accurately reflecting real-world dynamic graphs.

The model's generative process is detailed through a factorization of the joint distribution of the graph's adjacency matrix and latent variables across time. It establishes that the prior distribution for latent variables at the initial time snapshot follows a multivariate Gaussian distribution. Additionally, the framework accommodates scenarios where nodes may be added or removed from the graph, maintaining the integrity of the prior construction for subsequent time steps.

In terms of inference, the VGRNN framework allows for the derivation of node embeddings by inferring the posterior distribution of latent variables, which also depends on the previous hidden state. The paper outlines the use of graph neural networks (GNNs) to parameterize the approximate posterior, emphasizing the versatility of GNN architectures that can be utilized, including Graph Convolutional Networks (GCN) and GraphSAGE.

Overall, the VGRNN framework provides a robust approach to modeling and inferring dynamic graph structures, enhancing the understanding of their underlying complex dynamics.

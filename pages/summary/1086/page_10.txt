The references provided represent a collection of significant works in the field of network embedding and representation learning, reflecting advancements in machine learning techniques applied to graph data. 

Key contributions include:

1. Armandpour et al. (2019) explore robust negative sampling for network embedding, enhancing the efficiency and performance of these models.
2. Bojchevski and Günnemann (2018) introduce deep Gaussian embedding of graphs, focusing on unsupervised learning through a ranking approach.
3. Chung et al. (2015) present a recurrent latent variable model tailored for sequential data analysis.
4. Defferrard et al. (2016) propose a method for applying convolutional neural networks on graphs through localized spectral filtering.
5. Donnat et al. (2018) focus on learning structural node embeddings via diffusion wavelets, contributing to the understanding of graph structures.
6. Fraccaro et al. (2016) discuss sequential neural models with stochastic layers, emphasizing the role of uncertainty in modeling sequences.
7. Goyal et al. (2017) introduce Z-forcing for training stochastic recurrent networks, enhancing the training processes of these models.
8. Goyal et al. (2018) present Dyngem, a deep embedding method designed for dynamic graphs, while also discussing dyngraph2vec in 2019, which captures network dynamics effectively.
9. Grover and Leskovec (2016) propose node2vec, enabling scalable feature learning for networks.
10. Hamilton et al. (2017) focus on inductive representation learning, addressing large graph challenges.
11. Kipf and Welling (2016, 2017) develop variational graph auto-encoders and graph convolutional networks for semi-supervised classification, respectively.
12. Li et al. (2017) discuss attributed network embedding in dynamic environments, broadening the applicability of embedding techniques.

These references collectively contribute to the ongoing development of methods for understanding and leveraging the complexities of networked data.

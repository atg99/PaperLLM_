This section of the paper presents a performance comparison of various network pruning methods applied to several deep learning models, specifically AlexNet, VGG-16, ResNet-50, ResNet-56, and GoogLeNet, using datasets such as ImageNet, CIFAR10, and CIFAR100. The evaluation metrics utilized include model parameter size (Param/M), top-1 accuracy (AccTop-1%), and operational speed measured in FLOPs (× 10^9). 

The results, detailed in multiple tables and figures, highlight the performance of different pruning techniques, including those proposed by Han, Zhang, Liu, and several others. For instance, pruning methods applied to AlexNet show a reduction in parameters from 61M to as low as 0.3M while maintaining or slightly varying in accuracy. VGG-16 results illustrate similar trends, with some methods achieving higher accuracy while significantly reducing FLOPs.

The data indicates a trade-off between model size, accuracy, and computational efficiency across different pruning strategies, providing insights into which methods might be more suitable for specific applications. Overall, this comprehensive comparison aids in understanding the effectiveness of current network pruning techniques in optimizing deep learning models.

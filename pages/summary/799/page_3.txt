This paper provides a comprehensive review of deep learning-based object detection models and model compression techniques, addressing the rapid advancements in model compression since early 2020. The authors highlight the limitations of previous surveys that primarily focused on specific aspects of model compression without encompassing the broader landscape of recent research.

The paper compiles and analyzes a total of 252 papers from top-tier conferences and journals, ensuring that the latest developments in the field are included. It systematically categorizes six types of compression strategies: network pruning, lightweight network design, neural architecture search (NAS), low-rank decomposition, network quantization, and knowledge distillation (KD) methods. 

Key contributions of the paper include:

1. A detailed review of the six compression techniques, offering insights into their advantages and disadvantages, which were previously overlooked in other surveys.
2. A focused analysis on both object detection and image classification, providing statistical comparisons of the performance of original models versus lightweight models on public datasets, such as the VOC 2007 dataset.
3. A broader analysis of the research landscape, including institutional contributions, timelines, and publication trends, which enhances the understanding of the evolution and current state of model compression techniques.

The paper concludes by discussing future research directions and applicable scenarios for various compression strategies, emphasizing the need for continued exploration in this dynamically evolving field.

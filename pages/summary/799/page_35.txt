The paper discusses various low-rank decomposition strategies utilized in AI research, particularly focusing on methods like CP decomposition and cluster-based decomposition. Notably, Jaderberg et al. employed these techniques to exploit the redundancy among different channels and filters, thereby enhancing the efficiency of trained networks. Additionally, Samsung Electronics introduced a convolution kernel decomposition method based on Tucker decomposition, which involves selecting the rank through variational Bayesian matrix decomposition followed by kernel tensor Tucker decomposition. The paper includes figures illustrating the distribution of low-rank decomposition research, the process involved in low-rank decomposition for convolution kernels, and the research activities of various institutions related to this topic.

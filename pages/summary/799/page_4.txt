The paper analyzes model compression methods in computer vision, focusing on network structure design, neural architecture search (NAS), low-rank decomposition, network quantization, and knowledge distillation (KD), including combinations of these techniques. A total of 252 papers were reviewed from top-tier conferences and journals categorized as class A and part of class B, as per guidelines from the CCF and ACM. The findings indicate that network pruning and lightweight network structure design are the most prevalent methods in this field. The distribution of the research is illustrated in two figures: one showing the proportions of various model compression research directions and another detailing the sources of the papers, which include 236 conference papers, 5 journal papers, and 11 from arXiv.

The paper presents a comparative analysis of various network quantization methods applied to ResNet-18 and ResNet-34 using the ImageNet ILSVRC 2012 dataset. Key metrics include model weight quantization bits (W-bit), activation quantization bits (A-bit), model parameter size (Param), and accuracy (AccTop-1%). 

The results, detailed in Tables 7 and 8, show a range of quantization methods alongside their respective performance indicators. Full precision models achieved an accuracy of 69.6% for ResNet-18 and 73.3% for ResNet-34, with significant drops in accuracy observed with lower quantization levels. For instance, Binary Neural Networks (BNN) demonstrated a 42.2% accuracy at 1/1 quantization, while XNOR-Net and BWN achieved 51.2% and 60.8% accuracy, respectively, at the same quantization level.

Methods such as DoReFa-Net and LQ-Nets showed improved performance with varying quantization configurations, indicating that careful selection of quantization strategies can enhance model efficiency while maintaining a reasonable level of accuracy. The analysis provides insights into the trade-offs between model size and accuracy across different quantization techniques, highlighting the potential for optimization in deep learning models.

The paper discusses key datasets used for image classification in deep learning, specifically focusing on ImageNet, CIFAR-10, and CIFAR-100. ImageNet features 1.2 million training images across 1,000 categories, with evaluation metrics of top-1 and top-5 accuracy. The CIFAR-10 dataset consists of 60,000 32x32 color images divided into 10 exclusive categories, while CIFAR-100 expands this to 100 categories, each with 600 images and further divided into 20 superclasses.

Additionally, the paper explains the concept of FLOPs (Floating Point Operations per Second), which measures a model's computational complexity and resource demands. A higher FLOPs value indicates a more computationally intensive model. The paper also highlights a comparison of various network quantization methods, including binary, ternary, and cluster quantization, detailing their accuracy, calculation speed, storage space requirements, and training effects, with specific applications noted for mobile and embedded devices.

This paper discusses advancements in lightweight object detection models, emphasizing their performance relative to traditional models. A notable finding is that many lightweight models achieve accuracy comparable to their original counterparts, highlighting ongoing efforts in the field of compressed object detection. Additionally, the paper provides a comparative analysis of various Neural Architecture Search (NAS) methods applied to the ImageNet ILSVRC 2012 and CIFAR-10 datasets, focusing on metrics such as model parameter size and accuracy. The results illustrate the effectiveness of different lightweight model design strategies, as shown in performance comparisons depicted in figures for both datasets.

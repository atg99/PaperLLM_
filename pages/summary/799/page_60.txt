The paper discusses various methodologies and advancements in the field of neural network pruning, which is essential for enhancing the efficiency of deep learning models. It references multiple studies focused on different approaches to prune neural networks, including data-driven methods, dynamic network surgery, neuron importance score propagation, and structured sparsity techniques. Key contributions from researchers like Frankle, Hu, Guo, and Molchanov are highlighted, showcasing their innovative strategies for reducing model size and improving computational efficiency. The paper also emphasizes the importance of maintaining performance while achieving significant compression rates, which is crucial for deploying deep neural networks in resource-constrained environments such as mobile devices. Overall, the referenced works collectively provide a comprehensive overview of the state-of-the-art techniques in neural network pruning, aiming to balance model complexity with operational efficiency.

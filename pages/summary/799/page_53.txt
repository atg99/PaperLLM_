The paper discusses various methods for pruning ResNet-56 on the CIFAR10 dataset, analyzing their impact on model size, accuracy, and computational efficiency (FLOPs). Notably, several methods, including AOFP, SCP, and Lin, achieve over 90% reduction in FLOPs. The OPQ method stands out with a model size of 7.7 MB and an accuracy of 71.39%, which is an improvement of 2.89% over the original model. The CC method also shows strong performance with a model size of 8.35 MB, an accuracy of 68.81%, and a reduction in FLOPs by more than 50%. The results indicate that combining multiple compression techniques leads to enhanced overall performance, as demonstrated by the CC method that integrates channel pruning and tensor decomposition, along with the OPQ method that employs pruning and quantization. The table presented outlines the various pruning methods, their parameter sizes, accuracies, and FLOPs, highlighting the effectiveness of different approaches in model compression and performance optimization.

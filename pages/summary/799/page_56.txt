The paper discusses various methods for optimizing neural networks, focusing on future directions and application scenarios for each technique. 

1. **Network Pruning**: The paper suggests combining network pruning with other compression methods to enhance compression efficiency, particularly for devices with limited computing memory and storage.

2. **Lightweight Structure Design**: Future work should focus on integrating lightweight structure design with network architecture search and exploring various application tasks, especially for end-to-end embedded platforms that require high real-time performance.

3. **Network Quantization**: It is proposed to improve the network's expression capabilities and training potential while enhancing acceleration research on actual hardware devices, targeting applications that necessitate high inference speeds and low computational memory.

4. **Neural Architecture Search (NAS)**: The application of NAS methods to tasks like semantic segmentation is recommended, along with expanding research on search spaces tailored for mobile devices with specific storage and accuracy needs.

5. **Knowledge Distillation (KD)**: Future research could extend KD techniques to areas like object detection and explore the use of intermediate feature layers with different knowledge forms, particularly benefiting small or limited datasets.

6. **Low-Rank Decomposition**: The study emphasizes the need to simplify the computational operations involved in decomposition, aiming for the compression of fully connected layers.

Overall, the paper outlines a roadmap for advancing neural network optimization techniques across various contexts and devices.

The paper discusses the advancements in lightweight network design, highlighting contributions from major tech companies such as Google, Facebook, Microsoft Research, Megvii, and Huawei since 2017. It identifies a total of 49 significant lightweight network designs that have been presented in top-tier conferences and journals. The designs are categorized into two main types: those based on convolution structures and those focused on convolution operations. 

A key focus is on the development of lightweight models derived from YOLO structures, which are noted for their effectiveness in object detection. The paper details various convolution methods employed in lightweight model design, including standard, group, pointwise, dilated, and depth-wise separable convolutions. This section provides insights into the current research landscape and methodologies within the domain of lightweight network design.

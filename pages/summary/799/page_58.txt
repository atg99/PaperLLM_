The paper discusses current challenges and future directions in neural network compression strategies, highlighting six different methods and their unique advantages. It suggests that combining these methods could enhance performance by mitigating weaknesses. For instance, techniques like pruning and lightweight structure design are effective in object detection, while Neural Architecture Search (NAS) can identify optimal lightweight structures. Additionally, integrating pruning with knowledge distillation (KD) may improve results further. The paper points out that quantization has matured for real-time object detection on hardware devices, with a focus on model transformation tools being a key interest for internet companies. The trend in model compression emphasizes co-design with hardware to optimize performance. The article aims to provide readers with a thorough understanding of neural network compression technology and anticipates that lightweight neural networks will continue to evolve and significantly impact daily life.

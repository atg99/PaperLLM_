The paper discusses various pruning methods applied to ResNet-50 and VGG-16 models, focusing on their performance on the ImageNet, CIFAR10, and CIFAR100 datasets. The figures presented illustrate the effectiveness of these pruning techniques, showcasing the impact on model efficiency and accuracy. The results indicate that different pruning strategies yield varying outcomes depending on the architecture and dataset used, emphasizing the importance of selecting appropriate methods for optimizing neural networks in specific contexts.

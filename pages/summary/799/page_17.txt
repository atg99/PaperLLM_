The paper discusses advancements in convolutional neural network architectures, particularly focusing on depth-wise separable convolution and group convolution. Google's introduction of depth-wise separable convolution in models like InceptionV1-V3 and Xception has significantly reduced model parameters while maintaining efficiency. MobileNetV1 and V2 further optimized this approach for mobile devices, incorporating residual and linear bottleneck structures to enhance performance. Additionally, the introduction of mixed depth-wise convolution (MDconv) in MixNets showcased further innovation in convolution operations.

The paper also highlights Facebook AI Research's contribution to the field by combining ResNet and Inception to create ResNeXt, which utilizes group convolution. Collaborations with academic institutions led to the development of DenseNet, which further improved network efficiency and performance. The advancements in these architectures illustrate a trend towards lightweight models capable of high performance in various applications such as image classification, semantic segmentation, and object detection.

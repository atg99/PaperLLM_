In summary, this paper discusses recent advancements in neural architecture search (NAS) and its integration with various compression techniques to optimize deep learning models for mobile and resource-constrained environments. Key contributions include the development of MobileNetV3, Mobiledets, proxylessNAS, Slimmable Neural Networks, and other innovative frameworks that enhance model performance while addressing challenges such as memory consumption and computational efficiency. Researchers have explored multiple strategies, including dynamic network width adjustment, accelerated training through batch normalization, and the combination of NAS with pruning and quantization techniques. These efforts aim to achieve a balance between latency and accuracy, making deep learning applications more viable on mobile devices and embedded systems.

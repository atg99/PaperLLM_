The paper discusses the performance of various network quantization methods applied to models like ResNet-18 and ResNet-34 using the ImageNet ILSVRC 2012 dataset. It highlights the significance of the "W/A" ratio, which indicates the relationship between total parameter weights and activation values. A lower "W/A" suggests reduced computational costs, aligning with the goals of network quantization.

The analysis reveals that the DoReFa-Net method maintains accuracy while improving performance when compared to the full-precision version of ResNet-18. Additionally, methods like IAO and DAQ show enhanced accuracy as well. DoReFa-Net's approach of using low-bit wide gradients for training low-bit CNNs demonstrates superior accuracy over BWN and TWN methods. 

The paper also summarizes the performance of various knowledge distillation (KD) methods across different datasets, including ImageNet, CIFAR-10, and CIFAR-100, focusing on models like WideResNet and ResNet. It presents a comparison of compression performance for quantization methods on VGG-Small and ResNet20, detailing accuracy metrics across different configurations, highlighting the effectiveness of methods such as BinaryConnect, BNN, and DoReFa-Net. Overall, the findings underscore the advancements in model compression techniques while maintaining accuracy.

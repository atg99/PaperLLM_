The table presented in the paper compares the compression performance of various knowledge distillation (KD) methods on the CIFAR-10 dataset, highlighting the effectiveness of different student and teacher model configurations. The student models include VGG-13, various ResNet (RN) architectures, and Wide Residual Networks (WRN), each with specified parameter counts. The teacher models primarily consist of WRN and RN architectures, also with their respective parameter counts.

Performance metrics are provided for each model under different distillation techniques: standard knowledge distillation (KD), adversarial training (AT), fine-tuning (FT), and combinations of these methods. The results indicate that the teacher models consistently outperform the student models, with the highest accuracy achieved by the teacher models. The best performance for the student models using KD is observed with WRN-16-2, achieving an accuracy of 94.38, while the teacher model WRN-46-4 reaches 95.56. 

The findings suggest that while student models can approach teacher model performance through techniques like KD and AT, the original teacher networks remain superior in terms of accuracy on the CIFAR-10 dataset. The table serves as a comprehensive overview of how different configurations and methods impact the performance of compressed models in the context of knowledge distillation.

The section discusses binary quantization, a method that restricts neural network parameters to values of 1 or -1. This approach significantly reduces the storage and memory requirements of models by transforming multiplication operations into simpler addition or shift operations. Since 2015, various quantization techniques have been developed for model compression. Notably, Courbariaux et al. introduced binary connection, a weight binarization scheme, along with a training method for binarized parameters during both forward and backward propagation. They later advanced this concept with the training of binarized neural networks (BNN), which employs binary weights and activation values to compute parameter gradients throughout the training phase and quantifies weights and activations during inference.

Additionally, the Allen Institute of Artificial Intelligence contributed with binary-weight-networks (BWN) and XNOR-Net, enhancing computational efficiency by utilizing approximate convolutions with binary operations. Shenzhen DJ-Innovations (DJI) developed the accurate binary CNN (ABC-Net), which for the first time achieved accuracy in BNN that matched full-precision models on the ImageNet dataset.

The paper presents research conducted by a team from the College of Computer Science and Technology and the Key Laboratory of Smart Airport Theory and System at the Civil Aviation University of China. The authors, including Zonglei Lyu, Tong Yu, and others, focus on advancements in multimedia tools and applications within the context of smart airport systems. The study aims to explore innovative solutions and methodologies that enhance operational efficiency and user experience in airport environments. The collaborative effort signifies a commitment to addressing contemporary challenges in aviation through technological integration and application.

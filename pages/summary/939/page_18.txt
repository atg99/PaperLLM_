The appendix provides a curated list of machine learning resources specifically tailored for epidemiologists, addressing the challenge of unfamiliar terminology in computer science literature. It includes accessible articles on various algorithms and models, such as artificial neural networks, decision trees, support vector machines, naive Bayes, k-means clustering, Bayesian model averaging, Super Learner, and boosting and bagging. Key references are made to works by Jain, Olden, Atkinson, Noble, Lewis, Hoeting, Polley, and Opitz. Additionally, "An Introduction to Statistical Learning" by James et al. is highlighted for its user-friendly overview of machine learning alongside traditional statistics. A supplemental online tutorial by Markham is also mentioned as a detailed alternative resource, available for free. These resources aim to enhance understanding and application of machine learning in the field of epidemiology.

This research paper focuses on the application of artificial intelligence, specifically a boosted Random Forest algorithm, to predict patient health outcomes during the COVID-19 pandemic. The model utilizes a range of data, including geographical, travel, health, and demographic information from COVID-19 patients, to assess the severity of cases and predict potential outcomes such as recovery or death.

The study reports an impressive accuracy of 94% and an F1 Score of 0.86 in its predictions. Key findings indicate a significant correlation between patient gender and mortality rates, alongside an observation that most patients are within the age range of 20 to 70 years. The paper emphasizes the importance of real-time data collection and processing in healthcare to facilitate timely medical interventions, highlighting the pressing need for advanced predictive analytics in managing health crises like the COVID-19 pandemic. 

Overall, the integration of AI techniques in healthcare is presented as a vital tool for enhancing patient management and improving outcomes in times of global health emergencies.

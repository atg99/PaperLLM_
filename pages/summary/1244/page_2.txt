The paper by Iwendi et al. addresses the urgent need for effective data analytics tools in the healthcare sector, particularly in the context of the COVID-19 pandemic. COVID-19, caused by a novel coronavirus, has exhibited unique clinical characteristics that complicate detection, such as the frequent absence of fever in infected patients. The authors emphasize the importance of utilizing Artificial Intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) technologies to enhance the efficiency and accuracy of healthcare responses.

With the pandemic spreading rapidly and overwhelming medical facilities, the authors propose a model that integrates healthcare and travel data to predict patient outcomes based on symptoms, travel history, and reporting delays. They highlight previous research that has applied various AI techniques for COVID-19 detection and prediction, including Convolutional Neural Networks and Bayesian approaches.

The main contributions of the paper include:

1. The introduction of ML algorithms to process healthcare and travel data, moving beyond traditional healthcare systems to identify COVID-19 infections.
2. A comparative analysis of multiple algorithms, identifying the Boosted Random Forest as the most effective method for processing patient data. A grid search was conducted to optimize its hyperparameters for better performance.
3. The assertion that their work eliminates the necessity for redundant comparisons of existing algorithms for COVID-19 patient data processing.

Overall, the paper underscores the potential of AI and ML in improving healthcare outcomes during the ongoing pandemic.

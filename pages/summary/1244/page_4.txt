The paper by Iwendi et al. focuses on predicting health outcomes for COVID-19 patients using various data features. It addresses the handling of missing values by designating them as "NA" and separating records with missing "death" and "recovery" data into a test dataset, while the remaining records are compiled into a training dataset. The authors implement feature engineering to create a new column indicating the number of days between the onset of symptoms and the hospital visit.

For evaluating the predictive model's accuracy, the study employs three metrics, emphasizing the importance of accuracy in assessing the model's performance. The definition of accuracy is provided, indicating it is calculated as the ratio of true positive and true negative predictions to the total number of predictions, represented by the formula: 

Accuracy = (TP + TN) / (TP + TN + FP + FN)

This measure is essential in determining the effectiveness of the classification model in predicting patient outcomes based on diverse factors, including travel history and demographics.

The paper presents a comparison of various YOLO models in terms of frames per second (FPS) and accuracy. YOLOv5m achieved the highest speed, processing 27 FPS, followed by YOLOR -p6 at 23 FPS, and YOLOXm at 22 FPS. The slowest was YOLOv7, which managed only 17 FPS. In terms of accuracy, YOLOv7, despite its lower speed, delivered impressive results, achieving close to state-of-the-art accuracy. YOLOR -p6 followed with an accuracy of 56% AP, while YOLOv5m achieved 53% AP and YOLOXm recorded 46% AP. The findings highlight a trade-off between speed and accuracy across the models.

The paper discusses the limitations of using YOLOv7 in edge devices, particularly in applications that require a minimum of 5 frames per second (FPS) for effective performance. The small variant, YOLOv7.pt, typically achieves only 3 to 4 FPS, which is insufficient for certain commercial applications, such as a user-friendly soccer analyzer dashboard for insight analysis and goal prediction. However, with modifications, YOLOv7 can be adapted for use in embedded devices, enabling functionalities in retail store monitoring and autonomous robots and vehicles.

The authors, Ismat Saira Gillani, Muhammad Rizwan Munawar, and Muhammad Talha, each bring expertise in fields related to computer vision, machine learning, and robotics. Gillani is focused on utilizing state-of-the-art algorithms for AI solutions, Munawar specializes in machine vision and embedded systems, and Talha is pursuing a PhD with interests in various advanced computing techniques.

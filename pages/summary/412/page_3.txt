This integration allows YOLOR to efficiently handle multiple tasks simultaneously, enhancing its performance in multi-label classification and detection. The architecture employs three key processes: kernel space alignment, prediction refinement, and the use of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) to optimize output based on input. By leveraging both explicit knowledge from the dataset and implicit knowledge from prior experiences, YOLOR achieves superior accuracy and inference time compared to other YOLO models, including YOLOv5. 

In summary, the paper discusses the variants of YOLOv5, highlighting the trade-offs between size and accuracy, with YOLOv5x being the most accurate while YOLOv5s is the lightest. Additionally, it introduces YOLOR, a model that excels in both inference time and accuracy through a unique architecture that combines various learning methodologies.

The paper emphasizes the need for further enhancements in accuracy and frame rates, particularly in challenging scenarios like offside detection in soccer. Future work is proposed to involve tracking player movements and distinguishing between substitutes and spectators using semantics and instance segmentation. Additionally, training models on custom datasets and implementing them on embedded devices like Jetson Nano are suggested to optimize performance. A user-friendly dashboard to analyze game trends and predict goals is also recommended. Overall, the research highlights the effectiveness of the YOLO algorithm family in real-time object detection and its potential for commercial applications, with YOLOv7 emerging as the most accurate model despite its lower frame rate.

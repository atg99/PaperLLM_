This paper discusses advancements in object detection models, particularly focusing on the comparison between YOLOv4 and YOLOR-E6. YOLOR-E6 outperforms YOLOv4 by achieving a better Average Precision (AP) with 5% less computation, utilizing 10% fewer parameters, and enhancing inference speed by 15%. In the case of U5R5-X6, YOLOR shows the best AP with a significant reduction of 29% in computation and 11% in parameters. The paper also introduces YOLO-X, which emerged after YOLOv5, highlighting its integration of advanced label assignment and anchor-free detectors, addressing the limitations of previous YOLO models. The findings underscore the continuous evolution of the YOLO family in enhancing performance and efficiency in object detection tasks.

For the YTF dataset, accuracy results show that Hue et al. achieved 82.34% in 2014, while Lu et al. improved this to 82.50% in 2017. In the Semantic Textual Similarity task on the SICK dataset, Mueller et al. reported a correlation coefficient of 0.88 and a mean squared error of 0.229927 in 2016. Other contributors in 2018, Zhu et al. and Ein-Dor et al., reported slightly lower results with 0.83 and 0.81 for correlation, respectively.

In the Speaker Verification category, the NIST i-vector dataset showed results from Triplet Network with an Equal Error Rate (EER) of 2.85% and Minimum Detection Cost Function (MDCF) of 0.30, based on recordings from 1306 speakers with a total of 9634 test i-vectors. Chen et al. reported improved results with an EER of 2.69% in 2019. 

For the VCTK dataset, the Triplet Network achieved an EER of 12.26%, while Wang et al. reported 10.77% in 2019. Lastly, in the VoxCeleb2 dataset, the Triplet Network yielded an EER of 15.92% with Wang achieving 13.68% in 2019. 

Overall, the results highlight advancements in accuracy and verification tasks across different datasets using CNN and LSTM techniques.

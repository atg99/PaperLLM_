The paper discusses the effectiveness of deep metric learning across various domains, highlighting its successful application in multiple studies published in leading journals and conferences. A comparative analysis showcased in Table 1 illustrates the diverse evaluation metrics employed for different tasks. For image clustering, metrics such as F1 and Normalized Mutual Information (NMI) are utilized, while image retrieval tasks often rely on recall @R. Performance in person re-identification is measured through rank values, and face verification is assessed using accuracy. In 3D shape retrieval, metrics like First Tier (FT), Nearest Neighbor (NN), E-measure, Second Tier (ST), Discounted Cumulative Gain (DCG), and mean Average Precision (mAP) are preferred. The semantic textual similarity task employs Pearson correlation, Spearman’s rank correlation, and mean squared error (MSE) for evaluation. Lastly, speaker verification performance is measured using Equal Error Rate (EER) and Minimum Decision Cost Function (MDCF), which evaluate discrimination and calibration.

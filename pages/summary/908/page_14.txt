The paper discusses the Triplet network, which is inspired by the Siamese network and consists of three types of samples: positive, negative, and anchor. These networks operate within Euclidean space to facilitate object comparison in pattern recognition, linking closely to metric learning. The Triplet loss function emphasizes the similarity between pairs of samples from the same and different classes, utilizing shared weights for classification. This methodology enhances discrimination power by leveraging both in-class and inter-class relationships. The authors also mention a new metric loss proposed in previous work that further contributes to this framework.

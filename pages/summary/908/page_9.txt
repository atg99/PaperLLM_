The paper presents a comprehensive comparison of benchmark datasets for deep metric learning, highlighting various tasks and the performance of different models. The datasets analyzed include CUB-200-2011, CAR-196, Online Products, and Market-1501, which are used for image clustering, image retrieval, and person re-identification tasks. 

Key metrics evaluated include Normalized Mutual Information (NMI), F1 score, and Recall at different ranks (R). For instance, in the CUB-200-2011 dataset, the highest image retrieval performance was achieved by Wang et al. in 2017 with Recall@R values of 66.3, 76.0, and 83.9 for R=1, R=2, and R=4 respectively. The Online Products dataset showed even higher performance, with Wang et al. obtaining Recall@R values of 85.0, 93.5, and 98.0.

In person re-identification, the Market-1501 dataset results showed significant improvements over years, with Yao et al. in 2019 achieving a Recall@R of 88.20. The comprehensive results underscore the advancements in deep metric learning across various datasets and tasks, illustrating the evolution of model performance over time.

This paper provides a comprehensive overview of machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL) technologies, emphasizing their roles in shaping intelligent systems. It discusses the integration of pre-trained models into various business applications, enabling organizations with limited AI resources to leverage advanced analytics, such as natural language processing for chatbots and predictive maintenance for specialized tasks. The authors highlight the emerging market of AI as a Service (AIaaS) and the necessity for users to understand the risks associated with the black-box nature of these models, advocating for stringent protocols akin to those used for human decision-making.

The paper outlines the fundamental processes involved in automated analytical model building, including data input, feature extraction, model construction, and performance assessment. It identifies significant challenges faced by intelligent systems in real-world applications, particularly in the context of AIaaS, which is expected to transform existing service platforms and enhance smart services through improved customer data utilization.

The conclusion emphasizes the need for future research to address these challenges, offering structured guidance for building analytical models and ensuring safe, accessible predictions. The research is supported by the Bayerische Staatsministerium für Wirtschaft, Landesentwicklung und Energie and is published under a Creative Commons license, promoting open access to the findings.

The paper presents scispaCy, a Python library designed for biomedical and scientific natural language processing (NLP). Despite advancements in NLP, existing models often struggle with domain-specific tasks, particularly in the biomedical field, which is experiencing significant growth in publication rates. The authors highlight the challenges posed by information overload in biomedical sciences, where extracting structured knowledge from extensive literature is crucial.

ScispaCy builds on the established spaCy library and aims to provide robust models for various NLP tasks, including part of speech tagging, dependency parsing, named entity recognition (NER), and sentence segmentation. The paper details the performance of two model packages within scispaCy, showcasing their effectiveness across various tasks and datasets. The authors also note that existing tools primarily focus on named entity recognition, with few offering comprehensive NLP capabilities that incorporate modern techniques such as neural networks.

The scispaCy library is positioned as a practical solution for researchers needing to process biomedical text, with models and code made publicly available to facilitate further research and application in this critical area.

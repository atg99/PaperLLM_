The paper discusses the critical importance of algorithmic transparency and continuous validation in fostering public confidence in autonomous systems, particularly in the context of autonomous vehicles. It highlights that traditional validation methods are insufficient due to the increasing complexity of these systems. The author emphasizes the necessity for software to be capable of self-detection of defects and failure points. To establish trust in autonomous systems, the required quality must significantly surpass that of human-operated systems, necessitating advancements in validation techniques.

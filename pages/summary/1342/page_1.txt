The article discusses the increasing reliance on autonomous systems, such as intelligent service systems and self-driving technologies, and the associated challenges of validation and transparency. It highlights the necessity for continuous verification and validation processes, especially in safety-critical environments like automobiles and medical devices, where software updates must be reliably deployed. Unlike traditional automated systems, autonomous systems operate independently, making decisions based on complex interactions with their environment. The authors emphasize the need for advanced validation techniques that can address the dynamic nature of these systems, moving beyond conventional methods like functional testing. The article aims to provide insights into the validation of autonomous systems and invites feedback from readers and authors on related technologies.

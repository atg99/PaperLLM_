The paper discusses the intricacies of validating autonomous systems, emphasizing the need for intelligent testing methods. It outlines various AI approaches, including rule-based systems, fuzzy logic, Bayesian networks, and deep learning, highlighting that validation is a complex, multilayered process. Different levels of validation testing, such as systems, components, and modules, are identified.

The potential for intelligent testing is significant, particularly at the system level, where determining essential test cases and their execution depth is crucial. The paper advocates for intelligent validation tools that can assist in selecting and creating test cases, proposing an assistance functionality to prioritize existing test cases. 

A framework for AI-based testing is presented, which includes developing a component and dependency model, creating a dynamic test strategy, identifying changes, composing relevant submodels for regression, analyzing change impacts, and automatically selecting test cases to ensure minimum effort and necessary coverage. This structured approach aims to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of validating autonomous systems.

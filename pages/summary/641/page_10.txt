This work presents a universal propagation mechanism for graph convolutional networks (GCNs) that enhances performance across various applications involving network-structured data, such as bioinformatics, computer vision, and recommendation systems. However, similar to many existing GCNs, it lacks interpretability in its predictions. The authors encourage further exploration into improving the interpretability of GCNs, particularly for applications that are sensitive to risk. Acknowledgments are given for support from the Natural Science Foundation of China through several grants. The references list notable works that contribute to the field of GCNs and related areas.

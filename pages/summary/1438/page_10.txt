A comprehensive list of references is provided, citing significant works in the field of speech recognition and natural language processing. Key references include the development of speech corpora for various languages, advancements in end-to-end speech recognition systems, and methods for unsupervised learning of bilingual word embeddings. Notable contributions from researchers such as Baevski and colleagues on self-supervised learning frameworks, as well as studies on generative adversarial networks and cross-modal alignment, are highlighted, showcasing the progression and collaboration within the research community.

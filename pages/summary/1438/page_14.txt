Werker and Tees (1984) provide foundational insights into cross-language speech perception, revealing perceptual reorganization in infants within their first year, which has implications for understanding language acquisition. Finally, Zhang et al. Collectively, these works illustrate the dynamic evolution of techniques in speech recognition and machine learning, emphasizing the shift towards unsupervised and semi-supervised methodologies.

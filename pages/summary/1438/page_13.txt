This paper cites a range of significant contributions to the fields of automatic speech recognition (ASR) and related technologies. Key works include:

1. **Variational Inference for Acoustic Unit Discovery** by Ondel et al., which discusses methods for identifying acoustic units in speech data.
2. **fairseq** by Ott et al., a toolkit designed for fast and extensible sequence modeling.
3. **Librispeech** by Panayotov et al., introducing a comprehensive ASR corpus derived from public domain audiobooks.
4. **Specaugment** by Park et al., presenting a straightforward data augmentation technique to enhance ASR performance.
5. **Improved Noisy Student Training** by Park et al., which focuses on training methods to improve ASR systems.
6. **Emotion Recognition from Speech** using wav2vec 2.0 embeddings by Pepino et al., showcasing advancements in speech emotion detection.
7. The **Kaldi Toolkit** by Povey et al., a widely used framework for developing ASR systems.
8. **Wav2letter++** by Pratap et al., highlighting a fast and efficient open-source speech recognition system.
9. **Unsupervised Word Discovery** from speech by Rasanen et al., which explores automatic segmentation techniques for word identification.
10. **Dropout** by Srivastava et al., addressing overfitting in neural networks, which is vital for training robust models.

These citations reflect a diverse array of methodologies and applications in speech technology, emphasizing advancements in data handling, model training, and performance optimization.

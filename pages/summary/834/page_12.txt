The collection of papers cited addresses various advancements in the field of neural network quantization and architecture optimization. Szegedy et al. (2016) discuss the inception architecture, focusing on its effectiveness in computer vision tasks. Wang et al. (2019) introduce HAQ, a framework for hardware-aware automated quantization, emphasizing the need for efficiency in deployment. Wu et al. (2016) present quantized convolutional neural networks specifically designed for mobile devices, highlighting the balance between performance and resource constraints.

Yao et al. (2019) explore neural networks through the lens of the Hessian matrix, providing insights into optimization strategies. Zhang et al. (2018) propose LQ-Nets, a method for learned quantization that enhances accuracy while maintaining compactness. Zhao et al. (2019) introduce a technique to improve quantization without retraining, utilizing outlier channel splitting. Zhou et al. (2017) focus on incremental network quantization, aiming for lossless performance in CNNs with lower precision weights.

Additionally, Zhou et al. (2016) present DoReFa-Net, which trains low bitwidth convolutional neural networks with corresponding low bitwidth gradients, addressing training challenges in quantized models. Finally, Zhou et al. (2017) discuss adaptive quantization methods for deep neural networks, aiming to optimize precision based on specific requirements. Collectively, these works contribute to the ongoing efforts to enhance neural network efficiency and accessibility across various platforms.

The paper presents quantization results on ImageNet, focusing on the HAWQ-V2 method and its performance compared to other quantization techniques including direct quantization and state-of-the-art methods. Key metrics include quantization bits for weights and activations, top-1 testing accuracy, and weight compression ratio.

For ResNet50, HAWQ-V2 achieves a top-1 accuracy of 75.92% with a weight compression ratio of 12.24, outperforming previous methods in both accuracy and compression. In InceptionV3 tests, HAWQ-V2 also shows improved results with a top-1 accuracy of 75.98%. For the SqueezeNext model, HAWQ-V2 leads to an accuracy of 68.68% with a significant reduction in model size to 1.07MB, demonstrating effective quantization with minimal accuracy loss.

Additionally, the method is evaluated on the Microsoft COCO dataset using a RetinaNet backbone. HAWQ-V2 achieves a mean average precision (mAP) of 34.1, surpassing both direct quantization and other recent methods. The findings indicate that activation quantization is sensitive in object detection tasks, with mixed-precision quantization providing a beneficial trade-off between accuracy and compression.

Overall, HAWQ-V2 demonstrates superior performance and efficiency in quantizing deep learning models, making it a promising approach for deploying models in resource-constrained environments.

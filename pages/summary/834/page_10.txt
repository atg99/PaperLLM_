The acknowledgments section of the paper expresses gratitude for the financial and material support received from various organizations, including Intel Corporation, Google Cloud, Samsung SAIT, Amazon AWS, and NVIDIA Corporation. The authors clarify that the conclusions drawn in the work do not necessarily reflect the views of their sponsors.

In the broader impact section, the authors highlight the growing challenge of deploying large deep learning models in practical applications. They present a novel compression method that maintains accuracy while significantly reducing computational and memory demands. This advancement enables the deployment of state-of-the-art neural network models on edge devices, facilitating broader use of deep learning technologies in everyday applications, particularly in smartphones and embedded systems.

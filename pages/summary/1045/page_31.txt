The paper discusses tools and considerations for implementing Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI). It highlights InterpretML, an open-source Python toolkit by Microsoft designed to train intelligible models and explain black-box systems. It offers global and local explanations, aiding in model debugging and allowing for comparisons between various XAI methods such as Decision Trees, SHAP, and LIME. Key features include interactive visualizations for tabular data, a dashboard for text data analysis, and counterfactual example analysis using DiCE.

The implementation of XAI presents challenges, particularly regarding the trade-off between model interpretability and performance. Simpler models often provide clearer insights, while complex models may excel in performance but lack transparency. Furthermore, different stakeholders require tailored explanations based on their needs, necessitating engagement in the design process. The design of XAI systems must also balance competing requirements, taking into account factors such as interpretability, accuracy, and privacy, especially in sensitive areas like healthcare and finance.

The paper discusses the future of explainable artificial intelligence (XAI), emphasizing the importance of understanding both the capabilities and limitations of XAI techniques. It argues that model interpretability should be integrated with considerations of data explainability, model explainability, fairness, and accountability. The authors assert that a successful implementation of AI systems in various institutions and organizations globally depends on a comprehensive examination of all AI principles collectively. The references cited provide a foundation for the discussions and concepts presented in the paper, highlighting various aspects of XAI, including its challenges, methodologies, and the need for responsible AI practices.

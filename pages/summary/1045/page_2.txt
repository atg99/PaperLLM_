The paper "Explainable AI (XAI): Core Ideas, Techniques and Solutions" by Rudresh Dwivedi and colleagues addresses the growing necessity for transparency and interpretability in artificial intelligence systems as their usage expands across various domains. XAI is presented as a framework of machine learning techniques designed to enhance user understanding and trust in AI models, especially in critical applications where decisions can significantly impact outcomes.

The authors highlight the importance of selecting suitable XAI methods by providing a comprehensive survey of current programming techniques and frameworks related to XAI. They categorize various XAI approaches, clarifying the distinctions between existing techniques and illustrating these concepts with practical examples linked to specific programming frameworks and software toolkits. 

The paper aims to serve as a resource for stakeholders by offering a clear taxonomy that facilitates comparisons among different XAI methodologies, ultimately guiding them in choosing the most appropriate tools for their needs. The authors emphasize that the effective implementation of XAI is crucial for fostering trust in AI systems, thereby ensuring their successful deployment in sensitive areas.

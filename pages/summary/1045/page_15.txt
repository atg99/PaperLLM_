The paper discusses various toolkits developed for enhancing fairness and explainability in Machine Learning models, emphasizing the importance of trust in AI systems. 

AIF360, or Artificial Intelligence Fairness 360, is a Python toolkit designed to detect and reduce bias in ML models. It includes a comprehensive set of metrics to assess biases in datasets and models, along with algorithms aimed at mitigating these biases, thereby supporting the overarching goal of explainable AI (XAI).

AIX360, or AI Xplainability 360, is another open-source Python toolkit developed by IBM. It focuses on providing explanations and interpretations of ML models and datasets through various algorithms. The toolkit includes a decision tree to assist users in selecting the most suitable algorithm for their specific needs.

InterpretML, created by Microsoft, is an open-source toolkit that facilitates the training of interpretable models and the explanation of black-box systems. It emphasizes human interpretation of global and local model explanations and offers debugging capabilities. This toolkit allows for the comparison of different XAI methods, such as Decision Trees, Logistic Regression, and SHAP, among others, and features the Explainable Boosting Machine (EBM) for enhanced model explanation.

Additionally, Amazon SageMaker, a service from Amazon Web Services (AWS), is mentioned as a platform for preparing, building, training, and deploying ML models, similar to Microsoft Azure. 

Overall, the paper highlights the significance of these toolkits in addressing biases and improving the interpretability of machine learning models, contributing to the broader goal of trustworthy AI.

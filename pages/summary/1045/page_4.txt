The paper discusses the concept of Explainable AI (XAI) and its significance in the machine learning (ML) lifecycle, particularly focusing on the understanding phase. It identifies key stakeholders involved in this phase, including developers, theorists, and data scientists, each with distinct motivations for pursuing explainability.

Developers aim for quality assurance to enhance system testing, debugging, and overall robustness of AI applications. Theorists contribute to advancing AI theory and understanding the properties of deep neural networks, often overlapping with developers in their roles. Data scientists play a crucial role in grasping all aspects of the AI system, from training data to model predictions, and addressing any errors encountered.

The understanding phase is critical for refining the model before deployment, ensuring accuracy, and evaluating its functionality in real-world scenarios. Activities in this phase involve interpreting important features, understanding learned patterns, analyzing biases, and ensuring they do not affect the trained model negatively.

The paper also highlights the importance of XAI in debugging and enhancing AI models, where explanations can facilitate quicker identification and resolution of model errors during the iterative development process. Overall, XAI serves as a vital tool for improving model performance and ensuring transparency in AI systems.

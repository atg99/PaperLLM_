The paper titled "Explainable AI (XAI): core ideas, techniques and solutions" authored by Rudresh Dwivedi et al. and published in ACM Computing Surveys, explores the essential concepts and methodologies surrounding Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI). The authors aim to address the growing need for transparency and interpretability in AI systems, outlining various techniques that facilitate understanding of AI decision-making processes. The paper discusses the significance of XAI in enhancing trust and accountability in AI applications across different domains. It also reviews existing solutions and frameworks, providing a comprehensive overview of current advancements in the field. The authors highlight the challenges faced in implementing XAI and suggest future directions for research. The work serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners seeking to deepen their understanding of XAI and its implications.

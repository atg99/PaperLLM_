The references listed in this section encompass a wide array of research focusing on neural network pruning, model compression, and efficient learning techniques. Key concepts include:

1. **Federated Learning and Pruning**: Several studies investigate federated learning frameworks that incorporate dynamic updates and adaptive pruning to enhance efficiency on edge devices. For instance, Zhang et al. propose a method called Fedduap that leverages shared data on the server to optimize learning.

2. **Continual Learning and Catastrophic Forgetting**: Research by Peng et al. and Golkar et al. addresses strategies to mitigate long-term catastrophic forgetting through adversarial neural pruning and continual learning frameworks, promoting the retention of previously learned knowledge.

3. **Model Compression Techniques**: Techniques such as adversarial robustness in model compression and energy-efficient designs are explored. Various papers discuss approaches like automatic searching and pruning of deep networks for medical imaging and the application of pruning methods to generative adversarial networks (GANs).

4. **Neural Architecture Search**: The references include works on ensemble pruning during neural architecture searches, emphasizing the importance of subarchitecture ensemble strategies.

5. **Optimization Algorithms**: The list covers methods for optimizing neural networks, including proximal algorithms and stochastic gradient descent techniques, which enhance convergence and performance in high-dimensional settings.

6. **Applications in Computer Vision**: Many studies focus on practical applications in computer vision, such as improving real-time super-resolution and face attribute classification through innovative pruning methods.

These references collectively contribute to advancing methods in neural network efficiency, robustness, and adaptability across various application domains.

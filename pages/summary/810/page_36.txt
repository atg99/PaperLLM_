The data presented in Tables 16 and 17 summarizes the performance metrics of various neural network architectures, specifically AlexNet, VGG-11, and VGG-16, on the ImageNet-1K dataset. Key metrics evaluated include Top-1 accuracy, FLOPs (floating point operations), and parameters count before and after pruning.

For AlexNet, various pruning methods such as OICSR, NISP, and GDP-Lin were assessed across different years. The baseline Top-1 accuracy ranged from 55.71% to 56.98%, with pruned accuracies showing minimal degradation or slight improvements in some cases. The FLOPs decreased significantly, with reductions reaching up to 68.30%, indicating substantial computational efficiency after pruning.

In the case of VGG-11 and VGG-16, several methods including COP, EE, and GNN-RL were evaluated. VGG-11 exhibited a baseline Top-1 accuracy of 63.60% to 70.84%, with some pruning methods resulting in slight accuracy drops but significant reductions in FLOPs, achieving up to 80% efficiency. VGG-16 similarly showed baseline accuracies around 68.34% to 73.36%, with various pruning strategies impacting performance variably, sometimes leading to considerable drops in accuracy.

Overall, the tables indicate that while pruning can lead to reductions in model size and computational requirements, it often comes with trade-offs in accuracy, although some methods managed to maintain or slightly improve performance.

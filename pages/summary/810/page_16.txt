The referenced works focus on various methods for pruning neural networks to enhance their efficiency and reduce complexity. Key strategies include channel pruning, filter pruning, and structured pruning, which aim to decrease the number of parameters while maintaining model performance. 

Notable approaches include HRank, which utilizes high-rank feature maps for filter selection, and NISP, which employs neuron importance score propagation for effective pruning. Techniques like network trimming and DropNet emphasize data-driven methods, while others, such as Gdp and Resrep, explore the integration of gates and memory mechanisms for stabilization during pruning.

Different methodologies address the challenge of pruning efficiently, including collaborative channel pruning, eigendamage for structured pruning, and variational methods that leverage probabilistic frameworks. The works collectively highlight the ongoing efforts in the field to balance model compression with retention of accuracy, reflecting the growing need for efficient deep learning models in practical applications.

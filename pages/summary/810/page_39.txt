This table presents the performance metrics for various pruning methods applied to the ResNet-50 architecture on the ImageNet-1K dataset. Each entry includes the year of the method's introduction, the pruning method used, baseline and pruned top-1 accuracy percentages, the decrease in accuracy, baseline and pruned FLOPs (floating-point operations), the reduction in FLOPs, baseline and pruned parameters (Params), and the reduction in parameters.

Key observations include:

- The pruning method DMCP introduced in 2020 resulted in a top-1 accuracy drop of 10% while achieving a significant FLOPs reduction of 93.22%.
- The JMDP method from 2021 had a smaller accuracy decrease of 3.1%, but with a considerable increase in pruned FLOPs.
- The CLR-RNF method from 2022 showed a decrease of 4.9% in accuracy with a notable reduction in parameters.
- Some methods, like SMCP, demonstrated minimal accuracy drops while achieving substantial reductions in both FLOPs and parameters.
- The table also highlights several methods that either maintained or improved accuracy compared to their baseline, indicating advancements in pruning techniques.

Overall, the data underscores the trade-offs between accuracy retention and resource efficiency in pruning strategies for deep learning models.

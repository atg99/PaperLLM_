The paper "Structured Pruning for Deep Convolutional Neural Networks: A Survey" by Yang He and Lingao Xiao discusses various techniques related to pruning, quantization, decomposition, knowledge distillation, and neural architecture search in the context of deep learning model compression.

1. **Related Works**: 
   - **Unstructured Pruning**: Introduced by LeCun et al., this method evaluates weight importance using saliency derived from the Hessian matrix. Subsequent research by Han et al. and Guo et al. expanded on iterative weight pruning and connection splicing, improving accuracy retention during compression.
   - **Quantization**: This technique reduces the precision of weights from 32-bit floating point to lower bit representations (e.g., 8-bit integers) to decrease computational and storage costs. Methods vary from heuristic approaches to optimization-based strategies, and quantization often works in tandem with pruning.
   - **Decomposition**: This involves breaking down matrices and tensors into lower-rank approximations. Popular methods include singular value decomposition for 2-D tensors and Tucker decomposition for 4-D tensors, complementing pruning techniques.
   - **Knowledge Distillation**: This concept involves transferring knowledge from a larger, pre-trained model (teacher) to a smaller model (student), with variations including offline, online, and self-distillation methods. It can enhance pruning effectiveness, especially in few-shot scenarios.
   - **Neural Architecture Search**: This aims to automate the design of neural networks, focusing on optimizing the search space, employing various search strategies, and developing efficient evaluation methods to minimize computational costs.

2. **Experiments**: 
   - The paper summarizes over 800 experimental results from more than 100 studies across three key datasets: CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and ImageNet-1K. The results are organized into 21 tables, detailing methods, models, and metrics such as baseline accuracy, accuracy after pruning, FLOPs, and parameter counts. This structured presentation allows for a clear comparison of different approaches and their outcomes in model compression.

Overall, the paper provides a comprehensive overview of structured pruning and related methodologies, highlighting the evolution of techniques and their effectiveness in deep learning model optimization.

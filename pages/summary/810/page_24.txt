The table presents various methods applied to the VGG-16 model on the CIFAR-10 dataset, detailing their Top-1 accuracy, changes in accuracy post-pruning, FLOPs (floating point operations), and parameters before and after pruning. Each method is associated with a year of publication and a baseline comparison.

Key observations include:
- The highest Top-1 accuracy achieved post-pruning is 94.11% with the SOKS method in 2022, showing a gain of 0.58% from the baseline.
- Several methods resulted in accuracy drops, with the GAL method in 2019 showing the largest decline of 3.18%.
- Most methods significantly reduced FLOPs and parameters, with some achieving over 90% reduction in parameters, such as the EarlyCroP method, which reduced parameters by 98%.
- The DECORE method consistently shows competitive results across multiple years, maintaining high accuracy and manageable reductions in both FLOPs and parameters.

Overall, the data highlights the trade-offs between accuracy and model efficiency resulting from pruning techniques applied to VGG-16.

The table presents performance metrics for various pruning methods applied to the ResNet-18 architecture on the ImageNet-1K dataset. Key metrics include the top-1 accuracy of the baseline and pruned models, the reduction in accuracy, the baseline and pruned floating point operations (FLOPs), the percentage reduction in FLOPs, and the baseline and pruned parameters. 

The baseline top-1 accuracy for ResNet-18 varies across methods, with the highest being 70.71% and the lowest at 65.36%. Most pruning methods resulted in a decrease in accuracy, with notable exceptions like DAIS, which achieved a 2.20% increase. The methods demonstrated significant reductions in FLOPs, ranging from 6.51% to 58.20%, and reductions in parameters were observed in some cases, with the highest reduction reaching 60.48%. 

Overall, while pruning methods typically lead to a decrease in accuracy, they also significantly reduce computational costs, which is crucial for deploying models in resource-constrained environments.

It seeks to minimize the impact of smaller norms that do not contribute significantly to model performance. This is achieved by focusing on maintaining informative features while reducing the influence of less informative ones.

The paper discusses various pruning methods for neural networks, emphasizing the need to consider reconstruction error propagation and the importance of neurons in different layers. NISP (Neuron Importance Score Propagation) focuses on determining neuron importance through the Final Response Layer and propagating scores backward for pruning. Discrimination-aware Channel Pruning (DCP) addresses the challenge of shallow layer pruning by introducing discrimination-aware losses to intermediate layers, using a greedy algorithm for channel selection based on loss metrics.

Regularization techniques are also explored, particularly structured sparsity through Group Lasso, which involves convex optimization to achieve effective filter pruning. The paper highlights the role of Batch Normalization (BN) parameters in guiding pruning decisions and presents methods like Network Slimming and Gated Batch Normalization that utilize these parameters for channel-level sparsity. Additionally, Polarization Regularization aims to enhance the effectiveness of sparsity regularization by differentiating the treatment of important and unimportant neurons.

In summary, the paper provides a comprehensive overview of advanced pruning and regularization techniques in deep learning, addressing the complexities of neuron importance and the challenges of model efficiency while maintaining performance.

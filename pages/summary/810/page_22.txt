The paper presents a comprehensive analysis of structured pruning methods across various datasets and models. It summarizes experimental results in three tables, detailing the number of methods, models, and results for different configurations. Table 1 outlines the experiments conducted, highlighting the total counts of methods, models, and results across 21 tables, with unique models noted. Table 2 indexes these tables by dataset and model type, including popular architectures like VGG, ResNet, and MobileNet across CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and ImageNet-1K datasets.

Additionally, the authors introduce a dedicated website designed for users to visualize and analyze the experimental outcomes interactively. This platform allows users to filter results based on specific criteria, such as dataset, model, pruning method, and performance targets. An illustrative example demonstrates how users can easily retrieve relevant data points, showcasing the practicality of the website for researchers interested in structured pruning methods.

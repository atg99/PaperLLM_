The paper by J. Naskath et al. examines the integration of hybrid neural networks, specifically Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP), Self-Organizing Map (SOM), and Deep Belief Network (DBN), with deep learning models to enhance data abstraction capabilities. These models are particularly useful in scenarios where human expertise is limited, explanations of decisions are inadequate, or solutions must adapt to evolving problems and new information.

The MLP is highlighted as a widely used supervised learning neural network characterized by unidirectional information flow and no loops. Its primary goal is to optimize the function mapping inputs to outputs while adjusting bias values. Learning in MLPs is achieved through a backpropagation algorithm that modifies connection weights based on discrepancies between expected and actual outputs. Applications of MLPs span various fields, including finance, transportation, fitness, and energy, addressing optimization challenges effectively.

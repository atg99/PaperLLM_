The paper presents a review of various studies that utilize multilayer perceptron (MLP) neural networks for diverse applications, particularly in sentiment analysis, mobile robot navigation, secure routing, DDoS attack detection, and intrusion detection in vehicular networks.

1. Bairavel et al. introduced a novel feature selection and fusion technique for social media sentiment analysis using an oppositional grass bee optimization algorithm and MLP, achieving an accuracy of 95.2%.
2. Singh et al. focused on mobile robot navigation, employing MLP to identify optimal collision-free paths in dynamic environments.
3. Khatttab et al. implemented a secure routing mechanism using ANN and MLP, which improved detection probability to 98% while reducing the false negative rate to 3% through continuous learning with data from the VanetMobisim simulator.
4. Meng Wang et al. developed a dynamic MLP-based method for DDoS attack detection, using feedback mechanisms to enhance performance with datasets containing normal and attack packets.
5. Ayesha and Mourad proposed an MLP neural network for intrusion detection in vehicular networks, utilizing simulation data to generate predictions and classification reports, demonstrating effective performance.

Each study emphasizes the versatility of MLP in addressing complex problems across different domains, showcasing its effectiveness in classification and detection tasks.

The emergence of various Very Fine Spatial Resolution (VFSR) datasets due to modern remote sensing technologies has paved the way for applications in urban land use, agriculture, and tree crown analysis. 

Zhang et al. proposed a hybrid classification system that integrates a contextual-based CNN and a pixel-based MLP, employing a rule-based decision fusion strategy based on confidence levels derived from the CNN classifier. This method improves classification accuracy, particularly in homogeneous regions. The paper evaluates the proposed model against benchmark standards, including pixel-based MLP and spectral texture-based MLP classifiers.

Several studies are referenced, highlighting the versatility of hybrid CNN-MLP models across various applications. For instance, Ahsan et al. combined CNN and MLP for X-ray image analysis, finding CNN to provide superior accuracy in breast cancer detection. Kumar et al. developed a hybrid model for intrusion detection, demonstrating improved performance over individual models. Feng et al. compared CNN and MLP for classifying Points of Interest in side-channel attacks, while Bikku's model focused on health risk prediction using MLP, outperforming LSTM and RNN models.

Other applications include bioreactor control using deep learning, multimodal sentiment analysis with feature fusion, thematic mapping using various neural network approaches, and mobile robot pathfinding in dynamic environments. Collectively, these studies underscore the effectiveness of hybrid models and MLP in solving complex classification tasks across diverse fields.

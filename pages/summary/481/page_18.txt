The paper presents a summary of various studies focused on intrusion detection in vehicular networks using deep learning techniques. Vitalkar et al. (2020) aimed to enhance system longevity and reduce packet loss in vehicular ad hoc networks (VANETs) by implementing a Deep Belief Network to effectively separate intruders from normal nodes during information transfer. Kuldeep et al. utilized Deep Belief Networks and Fuzzy systems to classify vehicles based on their trust behavior, analyzing the mobility dataset to predict potential malicious actions. The performance metrics evaluated in this study included False Positive Rate, Discovery Rate, Processing Delay, Network Accuracy, data delivery ratio, overhead, and False Alarm Rate.

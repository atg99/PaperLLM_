The paper discusses various deep learning algorithms utilized in different applications, particularly focusing on breast cancer detection, network traffic analysis, and health risk prediction. 

Meha Desai et al. conducted an analysis on breast cancer detection using multi-layer perceptron (MLP) and convolutional neural networks (CNN), achieving varying accuracy rates across different datasets, with the highest at 98.86% for the BreakHis dataset. Vinod Kumar et al. evaluated a hybrid CNN-MLP architecture for identifying network traffic attacks, achieving an accuracy of 97.2% alongside high precision and recall metrics using the CICIDS2017 dataset. 

Hanwen Feng's work compared MLP and CNN for classifying points of interest in side-channel attacks, finding MLP more effective for PCA traces while CNN excelled with POI traces. Tulasi Bikku's research utilized MLP for health risk prediction, demonstrating superior performance compared to long short-term memory (LSTM) and recurrent neural networks (RNN). Lastly, Salah's study on breast cancer detection reiterated the effectiveness of MLP, particularly with a two hidden layer structure, yielding improved performance outcomes. 

Overall, the paper highlights the versatility and effectiveness of deep learning models across various domains, emphasizing the importance of selecting the appropriate architecture and dataset for optimal results.

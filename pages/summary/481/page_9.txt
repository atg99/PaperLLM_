The paper presents a study on various deep learning algorithms applied in neural network models, specifically focusing on the assessment of energy consumption and output energy modeling in wheat production. The authors, Morteza Taki et al., utilized multilayer perceptron (MLP), radial basis function (RBF) networks, and Gaussian process regression (GPR) models. The experimental design involved real-time data from farms, and the main objective was to predict energy consumption for wheat farming. The results indicated that the RBF model outperformed the other models in terms of accuracy in predicting output energy consumption.

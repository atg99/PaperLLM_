The paper presents a comprehensive overview of various deep learning algorithms, particularly focusing on the applications of deep neural networks (DNNs) such as multilayer perceptrons (MLPs) and convolutional neural networks (CNNs). It highlights several studies that evaluate the effectiveness of hybrid models combining MLPs and CNNs for tasks like network traffic attack analysis, sentiment analysis, and image classification.

Key findings include the successful application of MLPs in diverse fields such as health risk prediction, bioreactor control, and intrusion detection systems in mobile ad hoc networks. The studies cited demonstrate the versatility of deep learning in enhancing performance across different domains, emphasizing the importance of feature selection and fusion techniques.

The paper also discusses the use of self-organizing maps (SOMs) for various applications, including defect detection in X-ray inspections and materials research visualization. The integration of deep learning with other methodologies continues to show promise, indicating ongoing advancements in the field.

In conclusion, the paper underscores the significance of deep learning algorithms in improving analytical capabilities and decision-making processes across various sectors, reinforcing the need for further research and development in this area.

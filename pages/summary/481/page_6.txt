The paper presents a comparative analysis of various deep learning algorithms, specifically focusing on Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) applications across different studies. 

1. Shawky et al. utilize a CNN-MLP hybrid for remote sensing image scene classification, employing data augmentation techniques and a pre-trained Xception model for feature extraction. Their approach achieves high accuracy, with results of 99.86% on the UC-Merced dataset, 98.10% on the AID dataset, and 97.40% on NWPU-RESISC45.

2. Zhang et al. propose a hybrid MLP-CNN classifier aimed at very fine resolution remotely sensed image classification. They use a custom dataset of aerial imagery from Southampton, achieving an accuracy of 89.64%.

3. Md Manjurul Ahsan et al. develop a deep MLP-CNN model to differentiate between COVID-19 and non-COVID-19 patients. This model combines MLP for numerical data analysis and CNN for X-ray image classification. The Adam optimization algorithm yields a maximum accuracy of 96.3% for a balanced dataset, while the Rmsprop algorithm achieves 95.38% for an imbalanced dataset.

Overall, the studies illustrate the effectiveness of MLP in conjunction with other deep learning techniques across diverse applications, notably in remote sensing and medical diagnostics.

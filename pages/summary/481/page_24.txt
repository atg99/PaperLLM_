The paper provides brief biographical information about three researchers in the field of Information and Communication Engineering from Tamilnadu, India. Dr. J. Naskath, a Senior Assistant Professor at National Engineering College, focuses on Wireless Sensor Networks and Adhoc Networks. Dr. G. Sivakamasundari, also a Senior Assistant Professor at the same institution, specializes in Deep Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Image Processing. Dr. Alif Siddiqa Begum serves as an Assistant Professor at Al-Ameen Engineering College and is interested in Security and Cryptography Networks. Each researcher holds a Doctoral degree and contributes to their respective fields through academic roles and research.

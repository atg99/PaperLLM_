The paper discusses various deep learning algorithms, particularly focusing on Deep Belief Networks (DBNs) and their application in analyzing raw physiological data to predict emotional states such as arousal, valence, and liking. Dan Wang and colleagues explored the effectiveness of DBNs combined with three classifiers to interpret these emotions based on known features. The paper includes illustrations of the layer architecture of DBNs and outlines the fundamental steps of the Deep Belief Model (DBM) algorithm. The findings highlight the potential of deep learning techniques in emotional recognition through physiological signals.

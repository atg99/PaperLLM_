The paper presents a comprehensive review of various deep learning algorithms, specifically focusing on Multi-Layer Perception (MLP), Self-Organizing Map (SOM), and Deep Belief Networks (DBN). It highlights the evolution of deep learning, which is characterized by its ability to learn complex data representations through a series of nonlinear layers. The authors discuss the transformative impact of deep learning approaches, including generative and discriminative models, on information processing.

The review includes historical context and recent advancements in deep learning architectures and their implementation. Additionally, it categorizes the applications of these algorithms across diverse fields such as wireless networks, mobile ad-hoc networks, speech recognition, medical applications, natural language processing, material science, and remote sensing. The paper emphasizes the enhanced accuracy and problem-solving capabilities of deep learning models compared to traditional machine learning techniques.

In this study, Radzikowski et al. conducted experiments using the UME-ERJ dataset to investigate various pipelines for pronunciation modification, focusing on enhancing speech recognition accuracy for non-native speakers. The evaluation was performed on a custom Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) system as well as the Google Cloud Speech-to-Text service. The results indicated that it is possible to improve the recognition accuracy of non-native speech samples through pronunciation modifications.

The authors identified several challenges encountered during the training and evaluation phases, highlighting the need for further improvements to adapt their methods to various speaker-dependent conditions and different non-native accents. The research underscores the potential for refining ASR systems to better accommodate diverse speech patterns.

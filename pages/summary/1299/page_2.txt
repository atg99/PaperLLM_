Recommender systems play a crucial role in managing information overload by providing personalized suggestions, which enhances user decision-making across various applications, including entertainment, e-commerce, and social networking. The evolution of these systems can be traced back to interdisciplinary fields such as cognitive science and marketing, but they gained prominence as a distinct research area in the mid-1990s. The fundamental challenge in recommendation lies in predicting ratings for unseen items based on user preferences and interactions with other items.

The knowledge utilized in these systems is derived from multiple sources: user data, peer user interactions, item characteristics, and domain-specific insights regarding the application of recommendations. This multifaceted approach allows for a more effective recommendation process, which can lead to improved user satisfaction and engagement with the system.

The study utilizes two MovieLens datasets, MovieLens1M and MovieLens10M, containing 1 million and 10 million ratings respectively. These ratings, given by thousands of users to numerous movies, are scored on a scale from 1 to 5. The datasets exhibit high sparsity at 0.996. Additionally, a dataset with 180,249 products from various sources is incorporated, primarily to provide product information for recommendations.

In the data preparation stage, critical steps are taken to enhance the quality of the data for modeling. This includes removing items with fewer than 20 reviews and users with fewer than 20 reviews to ensure sufficient data for collaborative filtering. Duplicates are eliminated to avoid confusion, and ratings are normalized to a new range of [0.20-1]. Finally, the datasets are split into 80% for training and 20% for testing, with the remaining user and item counts documented. This thorough preparation is essential for effective data mining and model performance.

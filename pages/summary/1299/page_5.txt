In supervised learning, a training set is utilized to predict a dependent variable, while unsupervised learning aims to uncover patterns without a predefined target. Semi-supervised learning combines both approaches when only a small amount of target data is available.

The study employs an autoencoder neural network, an unsupervised learning method, to develop a product recommendation system using collaborative filtering. TensorFlow 2.0.0 is the chosen framework for model creation and training, noted for its capacity to handle large-scale processes and facilitate experimentation in machine learning.

The DM process adheres to the CRISP-DM methodology, which consists of six iterative phases: Business Understanding, Data Understanding, Data Preparation, Modeling, Evaluation, and Deployment. The paper emphasizes the importance of recommendation systems in meeting consumer expectations for personalized content and highlights their potential to enhance online shopping experiences and drive company revenue through improved purchase decision support.

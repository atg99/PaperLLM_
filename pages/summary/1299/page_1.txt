The paper discusses the challenges posed by the overwhelming volume of data in the Big Data era, particularly in helping individuals quickly find relevant information. To address this, the authors propose a product recommendation system utilizing an autoencoder based on collaborative filtering. They highlight that traditional collaborative filtering methods often struggle with high dimensionality and data sparsity.

The proposed system aims to improve recommendation accuracy by leveraging deep learning techniques. The authors compare their autoencoder model with Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) and present experimental results showing low Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) values of 0.029 and 0.010 for two datasets, indicating that their recommendations align well with user interests despite data sparsity.

The paper emphasizes the importance of automating the processing of user opinions and related data to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of recommendation systems in the context of the ever-growing volume of digital information.

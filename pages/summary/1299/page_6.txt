This study focuses on enhancing recommendation systems and their impact on human life while addressing ethical concerns associated with their use. As consumers become more aware of their rights, organizations often keep operational details of these systems confidential, complicating research and hindering progress in the field. The study outlines key business objectives aimed at improving recommendation systems, including increasing sales, enhancing revenue, fostering user engagement, gaining competitive advantage, calibrating user preferences, and optimizing recommendation algorithms for better performance. The first five objectives relate directly to business goals, emphasizing the importance of quality in product recommendations for customer satisfaction and profitability. The remaining objectives pertain to data mining (DM) processes, providing insights through the application and refinement of DM techniques. A project plan has been developed to assess the techniques required to meet these DM and business objectives effectively.

The paper provides a comprehensive overview of various approaches and technologies in the field of recommender systems, highlighting key research contributions and methodologies. It discusses constraint-based recommender systems, context-aware ontology-based systems, and collaborative filtering techniques, referencing significant studies and advancements in these areas. The use of matrix factorization and deep learning architectures is emphasized, particularly in relation to collaborative filtering and content-based recommendations. Additionally, the paper reviews the application of autoencoders and their variations, such as collaborative variational autoencoders, in improving recommendation accuracy. The discussion includes empirical analyses and theoretical frameworks that underpin these technologies, providing a foundation for future research and development in recommender systems.

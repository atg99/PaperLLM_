In the study, the researchers explored the impact of adding layers to an autoencoder model to improve performance. The experiments involved incrementally adding one and then two layers, while keeping other parameters such as the number of epochs and batch size constant. The results were analyzed through various metrics, including model loss and precision-recall, as illustrated in the corresponding figures. The findings indicate that modifying the architecture of the autoencoder can influence its effectiveness, highlighting the importance of model complexity in achieving better outcomes.

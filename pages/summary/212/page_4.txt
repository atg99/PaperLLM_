The paper discusses a novel conversion approach for transforming a residual network into its spiking neural network (SNN) counterpart. Unlike traditional linear network structures, residual networks are structured as directed acyclic graphs, which complicates the application of existing normalization methods. The authors highlight that when converting to an SNN, new synaptic weights are introduced in the shortcuts, where original weights are set to 1 for identity mapping. It is crucial to scale these shortcut weights in conjunction with convolutional synapse weights to ensure consistency in the firing rates of spiking neurons and their corresponding activations. The paper includes an example illustrating the discrepancies that arise in firing rates when shortcuts are not normalized, underscoring the importance of proper weight normalization in this conversion process.

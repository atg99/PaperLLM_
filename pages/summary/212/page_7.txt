The paper investigates the relationship between the depth of S-ResNet architectures and the growth of the accumulated propagation error, denoted as E_i, in two datasets: CIFAR-10 and ImageNet. The study presents empirical findings that demonstrate a clear pattern in how E_i changes as the S-ResNet becomes deeper. 

Figures 4(a) and 4(b) illustrate the behavior of E_i across different depths of S-ResNets for CIFAR-10 and ImageNet, respectively, after simulating 350 time steps and averaging over 50,000 training and validation samples. The paper proposes a regression model to approximate E_i, positing that it is influenced by network depth (l) and can be expressed as E_i = p * q^l, where p and q are constants.

Table 1 provides the fitted parameters and goodness of fit for various depths in both datasets, showing that the model generally performs well, with high adjusted R-squared values for most configurations. The findings suggest that deeper networks exhibit distinctive error propagation characteristics, contributing valuable insights into the design and optimization of neural network architectures.

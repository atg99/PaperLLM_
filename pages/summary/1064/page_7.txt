The paper presents an advanced architecture for the StyleGAN3 generator that focuses on eliminating aliasing through a combination of techniques. Key components include the use of Fourier features, modulated convolutions, and flexible layer specifications. The architecture is designed to enhance translation equivariance while maintaining high-frequency detail in the output images.

A significant aspect of the proposed generator is its custom CUDA kernel, which efficiently combines upsampling, Leaky ReLU, and downsampling operations, resulting in a tenfold increase in training speed and reduced memory usage. The paper discusses the importance of non-critical sampling, where the filter cutoff is set below the bandlimit to suppress aliasing, thus improving the attenuation of unwanted frequencies.

Additionally, the authors explore transformed Fourier features that allow the generator to handle unaligned and arbitrarily oriented datasets effectively. By adjusting the input features, the architecture can dynamically define the orientation of the final output image, ensuring robust performance across varying image conditions.

Overall, the proposed modifications lead to improved image quality, as evidenced by lower Fréchet Inception Distances (FID) compared to previous versions of StyleGAN, while maintaining efficiency and flexibility in processing.

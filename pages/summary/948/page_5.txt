The paper discusses the importance of acquiring and denoising noisy images for effective data interpretation and analysis. It highlights the use of Content-aware image restoration (CARE) techniques, specifically through the ZeroCostDL4Mic platform, for enhancing both the resolution and clarity of 2D and 3D images using supervised training. A notable application presented is the training of a 3D CARE network aimed at denoising live-cell structured illumination microscopy (SIM) imaging data.

The paper also details the use of image-segmentation networks, including U-Net and StarDist, to analyze imaging data. For example, a 2D U-Net model was trained to segment neuronal membranes from electron microscopy (EM) images, achieving a high Intersection over Union (IoU) of 0.90. Similarly, a 3D U-Net was utilized to segment mitochondria from EM images, demonstrating effective training with datasets provided by EPFL.

Furthermore, the StarDist model was applied to automatically detect and track nuclei in migrating cells, achieving an IoU of 0.86. The outputs from StarDist were utilized for tracking cell movement, which was further analyzed to confirm expected migration patterns. Overall, the work emphasizes the capabilities of the ZeroCostDL4Mic platform in facilitating advanced image analysis through deep learning methodologies.

The article introduces ZeroCostDL4Mic, a platform designed to democratize access to deep learning methods for microscopy. Despite the advantages of deep learning in enhancing image analysis, many novice researchers face challenges due to the technical demands and resource requirements of training models. ZeroCostDL4Mic addresses these issues by providing a user-friendly interface that leverages free cloud-based resources from Google Colab, enabling users without coding expertise to train and implement various deep learning networks.

The platform supports several key tasks in microscopy, including segmentation with U-Net and StarDist, object detection using YOLOv2, denoising with CARE and Noise2Void, super-resolution microscopy through Deep-STORM, and image-to-image translation techniques like pix2pix and CycleGAN. Additionally, it offers quantitative tools for model evaluation, facilitating optimization and performance assessment. The authors demonstrate the platform's capabilities through applications in studying diverse biological processes, highlighting its potential to enhance accessibility and innovation in microscopy research.

The paper discusses the implementation of data augmentation and transfer learning within the ZeroCostDL4Mic framework to enhance deep learning approaches for image analysis. Data augmentation, achieved through tools like Augmentor32 and imgaug, artificially increases the diversity of training datasets by applying transformations such as rotation and flipping. This technique is particularly beneficial for small datasets, as it can improve model generalization. The authors demonstrate that using data augmentation during the training of YOLOv2 led to improved model performance.

Additionally, the framework includes transfer learning capabilities, allowing users to leverage pretrained models and accelerate the training process by reusing learned features. A comparison of training a StarDist model from scratch versus using transfer learning illustrates significant performance improvements.

Quality control metrics are also presented, showcasing the effectiveness of the models through various analyses, including training and validation loss curves, structural similarity index (SSIM), and precision-recall curves. These metrics highlight the positive impact of data augmentation on model performance, evidenced by increased average precision and F1 scores.

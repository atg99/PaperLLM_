The paper discusses the potential health benefits of various medicinal plants, particularly focusing on their antioxidant, antibacterial, and antifungal properties. It highlights the significance of plants like Melissa officinalis (lemon balm) and Salvia officinalis (sage) for their bioactive compounds that can be harnessed for medical, nutraceutical, and food applications. The authors emphasize the importance of these plants in traditional medicine and their role in promoting health through their phytochemical constituents.

The study underscores the lack of competing interests among the authors and clarifies that consent for publication is not applicable. The research was conducted across multiple institutions in Algeria, Italy, Turkey, and Jordan, with references supporting the claims made regarding the bioactivity of the mentioned plants. Overall, the findings contribute to the understanding of the medicinal properties of halophytes and other plants, advocating for their use in health promotion and disease prevention.

The study evaluates the antioxidant capacities of phenolic extracts and essential oils from O. basilicum and T. algeriensis using spectrophotometric methods, specifically the DPPH and ABTS assays, along with the Ferric-reducing antioxidant power test. The IC50 values obtained from the DPPH assay indicated that the ethanolic extracts demonstrated strong radical-scavenging activities, with O. basilicum showing greater potency than T. algeriensis, although both were less effective than ascorbic acid, which served as the reference standard. For essential oils, T. algeriensis exhibited the highest activity, followed by BHA, while O. basilicum had significantly lower activity.

In the ABTS assay, essential oils showed higher anti-free radical activity compared to ethanolic extracts. Notably, the ethanolic extract of O. basilicum outperformed that of T. algeriensis, likely due to its higher phenolic content, which is crucial for trapping cation radicals.

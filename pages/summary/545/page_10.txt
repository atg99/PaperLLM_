The appendix of the paper by H. Jin et al. presented at KDD '19 details the reproducibility aspects of their research. Key elements include the default architectures employed, specifically a three-layer convolutional neural network, ResNet18, and DenseNet121. The network morphism operations are elaborated, focusing on weight initialization and a selection of operational parameters for the Bayesian optimizer.

The dataset preprocessing outlines normalization to standard distributions and data augmentation techniques, such as random cropping and flipping, to enhance model robustness. Performance estimation during the neural architecture search is emphasized, advocating for training the architectures rather than relying on previous estimation strategies. This approach aims to provide more accurate performance metrics by using a mean of the last few epochs instead of the final values, thus accommodating the variability inherent in different network architectures. The code and documentation are accessible via the Auto-Keras official website, ensuring transparency and reproducibility in the research.

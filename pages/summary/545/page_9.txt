The paper titled "Auto-Keras: An Efficient Neural Architecture Search System" presented at KDD 2019 discusses an automated system for neural architecture search (NAS) that simplifies the process of designing deep learning models. Auto-Keras aims to provide an efficient and user-friendly interface for users who may not have extensive expertise in deep learning.

The authors acknowledge the contributions of anonymous reviewers and the open-source community, and they note financial support from DARPA and NSF. They clarify that the opinions expressed do not reflect the official views of the U.S. Government.

The references section includes a comprehensive list of prior works related to NAS, machine learning optimization, and architecture design methodologies. Notable references include works on reinforcement learning for architecture design, hyperparameter optimization libraries, and various neural architecture search techniques, showcasing the breadth of existing research in the field.

Overall, the paper highlights the importance of automated systems in streamlining the model development process, making advanced machine learning techniques more accessible to a wider audience.

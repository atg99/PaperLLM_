The paper discusses the impact of technological advancements, particularly driverless cars and robotics, on society and the associated security concerns. It highlights the potential benefits of these technologies, such as reducing road accidents, fuel consumption, traffic congestion, and air pollution. However, it raises significant questions regarding the safety and security of such technologies before their widespread adoption. The authors argue that both academia and industry must address existing security flaws and ensure user safety, including considering remote mishaps and attacks. Additionally, the paper emphasizes the need for government support through proper legal policies and infrastructure to facilitate safe implementation. The references cited provide a broad range of studies and analyses related to security in vehicular ad hoc networks, robotics, and cyber-physical systems, underscoring the importance of comprehensive security measures in the deployment of these technologies.

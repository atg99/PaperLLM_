This paper presents a comprehensive overview of various studies and reports focused on security, automation, and trust in autonomous systems, particularly in the context of vehicles and robotics. Key topics include security threat modeling, adaptive automation in aviation, cyber threats to autonomous vehicles, and approaches to adjustable autonomy. Notable contributions from various authors highlight the challenges faced by autonomous technologies, including cyberattacks on vehicle sensors and the importance of situational awareness in mixed-autonomy scenarios. The paper also discusses experimental security analyses of industrial robots and emphasizes the need for robust security measures as autonomous systems become more integrated into society. Overall, it underscores the evolving landscape of autonomous technology and the critical need for ongoing research in security and user trust.

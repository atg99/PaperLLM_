The paper titled "Security Modeling of Autonomous Systems: A Survey" outlines a structured examination of security aspects related to various autonomous systems, including unmanned vehicles, robots, and driverless cars. The study emphasizes the importance of security threats, vulnerabilities, and attacks within applications such as home assistants, healthcare, surveillance, search and rescue missions, and space exploration.

In the methodology section, the authors define a specific set of keywords relevant to the industrial and academic domains of autonomous systems to narrow the focus of the survey. The literature search included reputable databases such as Google Scholar, ACM, IEEE Xplore, and ScienceDirect. 

The selection criteria for evaluating the articles included the history and levels of automation as well as current trends and approaches to autonomy. This structured approach aims to provide a comprehensive overview of the security challenges faced by autonomous systems, highlighting the need for effective security models in these rapidly evolving technologies.

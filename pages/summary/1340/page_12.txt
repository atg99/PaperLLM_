The paper discusses the taxonomy of attacks on autonomous systems, focusing specifically on unmanned systems such as Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs), Unmanned Ground Vehicles (UGVs), and Unmanned Underwater Vehicles (UUVs). It highlights the growing use of these systems in critical missions, which has prompted researchers to enhance their autonomy levels based on factors like task complexity, human interaction, and environmental challenges. The references cited underscore the importance of understanding these dynamics in the context of the commercialization of autonomous technologies.

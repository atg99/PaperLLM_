The paper "Security Modeling of Autonomous Systems: A Survey" by Farha Jahan, Weiqing Sun, Quamar Niyaz, and Mansoor Alam provides a comprehensive review of the evolution and current state of autonomous systems, emphasizing the importance of security in their design and implementation. As autonomous technologies like home assistants, delivery drones, and driverless cars become more integrated into daily life, the level of automation varies based on the chosen autonomy approach.

The authors discuss the historical development of autonomy and highlight the necessity for robust security measures due to the rising threat of cyberattacks. They stress that modeling these systems from a security standpoint is crucial for identifying potential threats and vulnerabilities, as well as for simulating attacks. The survey explores theoretical and analytical models related to system security and attack modeling that have been proposed over the years, while also identifying existing research gaps that need to be addressed.

The work falls under several key areas including modeling and simulation, robotic autonomy, systems security, and human-computer interaction. The authors conclude that the research community must prioritize security modeling to ensure the safety and reliability of autonomous systems as they become more prevalent across various sectors.

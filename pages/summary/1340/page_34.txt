The importance of security in vehicular networks is highlighted through multiple studies, indicating a growing concern for the integrity of data and systems involved in autonomous driving.

In summary, the paper captures the multifaceted nature of automation in vehicles, stressing the significance of security, decision-making, and risk management as critical components for the successful implementation of autonomous technologies. It serves as a comprehensive overview of the current landscape and future directions in the field.

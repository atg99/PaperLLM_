The paper discusses the advancements in driverless car technology and highlights various pilot projects undertaken by major manufacturers. It presents a timeline in Table 5, detailing proposed launch years and features of different autonomous vehicles. Key projects include Ford's Level 4 automation vehicle, Google's Waymo with no steering or pedals, Tesla's Model S with Autopilot, and Mercedes-Benz's Future Truck 2025. The paper emphasizes the growing trend towards fully automated driving systems, showcasing innovations like Volvo's IntelliSafe Auto Pilot and General Motors' Super Cruise. 

Additionally, the paper addresses the security risks associated with autonomous systems, particularly focusing on GPS spoofing attacks that can mislead vehicles, posing threats to public safety. The authors cite a real incident involving ships near Novorossiysk, illustrating the potential dangers of compromised GPS signals. The discussion includes research efforts to simulate such attacks on UAVs and other platforms, underscoring the importance of securing autonomous technologies against malicious acts. Overall, the paper portrays a significant shift towards automation in the automotive industry while highlighting critical security considerations.

The paper emphasizes the significance of reviewing single stage object detection algorithms, particularly focusing on YOLO (You Only Look Once) models, which have not been extensively covered in prior surveys. The primary contributions of this review include:

a. Discussion on the challenges and importance of different stages in the object detection process.
b. Overview of two stage object detectors like RCNN, Fast-RCNN, and Faster-RCNN, along with their applications.
c. Exploration of the necessity for single stage object detectors, with an in-depth analysis of YOLOs, addressing architectural advancements, optimization techniques, loss function significance, and practical applications.
d. Comparative evaluation of two stage versus single stage detectors, including a performance analysis of various YOLO versions and future research directions.

The organization of the paper is structured into sections, starting with a summary of popular two stage object detectors, followed by an overview of CNN architectures and pretrained models relevant to YOLOs. It continues with a detailed discussion on YOLO's regression formulation, design concepts, and applications, culminating in a comparative analysis of detection methods and statistical performance results, while also outlining future research avenues in object detection.

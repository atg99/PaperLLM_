This collection of references highlights significant advancements in the fields of object detection, deep learning, and image processing. 

1. **Mini-YOLOv3** is introduced as a real-time object detector suitable for embedded applications, showcasing improvements in efficiency and performance.
2. Research by **Mezaal et al.** focuses on an optimized neural architecture for automatic landslide detection utilizing high-resolution airborne laser scanning data.
3. **Nash et al.** provide a review of deep learning techniques applied to materials degradation, underlining the relevance of these methods in material science.
4. **Quang and Xie** present DanQ, a hybrid convolutional and recurrent neural network designed for quantifying DNA sequence functions.
5. The **Teat detection algorithm comparison** by Rastogi and Ryuh evaluates the performance of YOLO versus Haar-cascade methods.
6. Several studies discuss advancements in **YOLO versions**, emphasizing continuous improvements in real-time object detection capabilities.
7. **Faster R-CNN** is highlighted as a significant development towards real-time object detection with region proposal networks.
8. The **Overfeat** model integrates recognition, localization, and detection using convolutional networks, showcasing the versatility of deep learning.
9. A variety of applications, including **chemical fiber defect detection** and **image classification** using support vector machines and neural networks, are explored.
10. The development of **CSPNet** aims to enhance the learning capability of CNNs, indicating ongoing efforts to refine deep learning architectures.

Overall, these studies reflect a trend towards more efficient, accurate, and application-specific deep learning models, particularly in object detection and classification tasks.

The paper discusses various neural network architectures, focusing on Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN) and their variants such as Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) and Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU), as well as Generative Adversarial Networks (GAN) and different types of autoencoders. It highlights the challenges of scaling in deep neural networks, which has led to the widespread adoption of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) for effectively capturing spatial and contextual information with a reduced number of parameters. The paper also references two-stage object detectors, specifically R-CNN, Fast R-CNN, and Faster R-CNN, indicating their relevance in multimedia tools and applications.

This collection of references highlights various applications of deep learning and neural networks in different fields, including agriculture, video captioning, clothing attribute recognition, and object detection. Notably, Wu et al. (2020) explore a YOLO v4 algorithm enhanced by channel pruning for the precise detection of apple flowers in natural settings. Xiang et al. (2020) employ an RCNN framework with L-Softmax loss for clothing attribute recognition. Xu et al. (2018) introduce a dual-stream recurrent neural network for video captioning, while Ye et al. (2020) present a YOLO-based neural network with a Variational Autoencoder for intelligent garbage detection.

Other studies include Zaytar and El Amrani (2016), who use long short-term memory networks for weather forecasting, and Zhang et al. (2019), who conduct a comparative study on deep learning for monitoring fossil-fuel power plants using high-resolution remote sensing images. Research on sentiment classification and character recognition using recurrent neural networks is also detailed by Zhang et al. (2016, 2017).

Additionally, Zhang et al. (2018) investigate the application of YOLO v2 combined with color identification, while Zhang et al. (2020) propose RefineDet++, a single-shot refinement neural network for object detection. Zhao et al. (2019) introduce M2det, a single-shot object detector leveraging a multi-level feature pyramid network. Lastly, Zheng and Ge (2021) discuss a binocular intelligent following robot based on YOLO-LITE. This body of work underscores the versatility and effectiveness of deep learning methodologies across various domains.

In summary, this section reviews several recent dimensionality reduction methods focused on feature selection. The studies discussed highlight various approaches, including clustering combined with correlation filtering, evolutionary algorithms, particle swarm optimization, and hybrid methods that integrate multiple algorithms. The results from these studies indicate that the proposed methods generally outperform traditional techniques like PCA and ReliefF in terms of accuracy and computational efficiency across different datasets and classification tasks.

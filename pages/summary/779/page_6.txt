The authors conducted experiments on multiple datasets, comparing the performance of their CNN-based approach with traditional feature selection methods. The results indicated that the CNN-based method outperformed conventional techniques, achieving higher accuracy and efficiency in feature selection.

The paper presents a comprehensive overview of various feature selection methods across different studies, highlighting their methodologies and results. Zebari et al. introduced a clustering approach combined with an Ant-based process for feature selection, demonstrating superior performance and reduced computation time compared to other unsupervised methods. Qu et al. focused on colorectal cancer prediction using operational taxonomic units, showcasing the effectiveness of correlation-based feature selection and the random forest classifier. Umbarkar and Shukla improved instruction detection systems through dimensionality reduction, while Farokhmanesh and Sadeghi integrated sparse feature selection with deep learning for enhanced accuracy.

Other contributions include Duong and Hoang's method for rice quality classification using Histogram of Oriented Gradient features, Alharan et al.'s texture image classification using multiple feature extraction techniques, and Osman et al.'s color-based feature selection for identifying origins in facial images. Arshak and Eesa utilized the cuttlefish algorithm for gene classification, achieving notable performance across several datasets.

Overall, the studies underscore the importance of feature selection in improving model performance across various domains, with many methods demonstrating significant advancements in accuracy and efficiency through innovative approaches.

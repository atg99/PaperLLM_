The paper by Zebari et al. discusses various methods for feature selection and classification across different datasets, focusing on cancer data, intrusion detection systems, image steganalysis, malware detection, and music emotion recognition. 

The authors present a convolutional neural network (CNN) method for classifying cancer types using microarray data, achieving superior accuracy compared to traditional methods like mSVM-RFE-iRF and varSeIRF across ten datasets. Balasaraswathi introduces a feature selection method for intrusion detection systems that combines the cuttlefish algorithm and membrane computing, demonstrating improved accuracy and computation time.

Kaur and Singh propose a feature selection method for image steganalysis utilizing adaptive particle swarm optimization (PSO), which outperforms other PSO-based methods in classification accuracy. Fatima et al. develop an optimized feature selection method for Android malware detection using a genetic algorithm, achieving high accuracy rates for both support vector machine (SVM) and neural network classifiers.

Widiyanti and Endah focus on music emotion recognition using feature selection algorithms, finding that the ReliefF algorithm performed lower than others despite similar accuracy levels.

The paper includes a summarization table of feature selection methods, showcasing various datasets, techniques, computation times, classifiers, and accuracy results. Overall, the findings highlight the effectiveness of integrating advanced algorithms and feature selection techniques across diverse applications in improving classification accuracy and efficiency.

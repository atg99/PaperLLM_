The paper discusses the importance of ensuring that the spectral norm of convolutional layers in i-ResNets is less than one to maintain Lipschitz continuity. It highlights the limitations of existing methods for estimating spectral norms and proposes a direct approach using power-iteration techniques to normalize the layers effectively. The proposed method includes a scaling coefficient to ensure that the network remains stable during training.

Furthermore, the authors introduce the concept of Normalizing Flows, leveraging the invertibility of i-ResNets to define a generative model. They outline the process of sampling from this model and computing the likelihood of data points efficiently, emphasizing the significance of the Jacobian's log-determinant in this context.

In summary, the paper presents innovative methods for ensuring the stability and efficiency of i-ResNets as generative models, particularly in high-dimensional spaces, while providing new insights into spectral norm estimation and the application of Normalizing Flows.

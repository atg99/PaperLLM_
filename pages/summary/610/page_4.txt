The proposed approach focuses on optimizing the fine-tuning process of a ResNet model pre-trained on a source dataset. The policy network is designed to make routing decisions for each residual block, determining whether to fine-tune or freeze the parameters based on a Gumbel Softmax distribution. This method enables the use of backpropagation for optimizing the policy network.

The Gumbel distribution is utilized to sample from a discrete distribution parameterized by the logits from the policy network. The Gumbel-Max trick allows for the generation of discrete samples, while the Gumbel Softmax provides a differentiable approximation. The output of the policy network is represented as a two-dimensional matrix, with each row corresponding to the logits for fine-tuning decisions for each block.

Additionally, the paper introduces a Compact Global Policy variant, where a fixed number of blocks across all images are fine-tuned, reducing memory and computational costs. This approach allows for shared parameters across images, eliminating the need for the policy network during testing. Overall, the method aims to optimize the fine-tuning strategy to enhance the accuracy of the target task while managing resources efficiently.

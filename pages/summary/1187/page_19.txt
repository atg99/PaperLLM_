The table summarizes various benchmark datasets used in graph-based semi-supervised learning (GSSL). The datasets are categorized into citation networks, co-purchase networks, webpage citation networks, social networks, language networks, bio-chemical datasets, knowledge graphs, and image datasets. Each entry provides key characteristics such as the number of nodes, edges, features, and classes, along with the source of the dataset. 

For instance, in citation networks, the Cora dataset has 2,708 nodes, 5,429 edges, 1,433 features, and 7 classes, while the Pubmed dataset has 19,717 nodes, 44,338 edges, 500 features, and 3 classes. Co-purchase networks include datasets like Amazon Computers, which has 13,752 nodes and 245,861 edges across 10 classes. Social networks feature datasets such as Reddit, which has a substantial 232,965 nodes and over 11 million edges with 41 classes. 

Additional datasets span areas like bio-chemistry, with datasets such as PPI containing 56,944 nodes and 81,716 edges across 121 classes, and image datasets like MNIST, which has 10,000 nodes and 65,403 edges with 10 classes. 

This comprehensive overview highlights the diversity of datasets available for GSSL, each offering unique characteristics suited for various research applications.

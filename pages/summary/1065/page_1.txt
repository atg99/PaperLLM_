This paper provides a comprehensive survey of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), emphasizing their potential applications in privacy and security. GANs are recognized for their ability to generate realistic data samples, which has led to significant advancements in fields like computer vision and natural language processing. The authors highlight the unique game-theoretic optimization strategy of GANs that not only enhances data generation but also fosters research in privacy and security.

Despite the promising capabilities of GANs, the authors note a lack of extensive surveys focusing on their applications in privacy and security. This paper categorizes existing works based on their functionalities in these domains and conducts an analysis of their respective strengths and weaknesses. The paper also discusses the nascent stage of GAN applications in privacy and security, identifying challenges that remain to be addressed.

Furthermore, the authors explore potential future applications of GANs in privacy and security, suggesting directions for further research. This survey serves as a foundational resource for understanding the intersection of GAN technology with privacy and security concerns, aiming to guide future studies and innovations in this area.

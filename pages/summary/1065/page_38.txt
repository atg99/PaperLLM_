The cited works focus on advancements in generative adversarial networks (GANs) and their applications across various fields. Key contributions include frameworks for botnet detection, privacy preservation in mobility data, decentralized learning from non-iid data, and synthetic data generation with differential privacy guarantees. Notable studies explore the self-attention mechanism in GANs, differentially private data release, and generative model-inversion attacks against deep neural networks. Furthermore, GANs have been applied in telecom fraud detection and image-to-image translation using cycle-consistent methods. The collective research highlights the versatility and significance of GANs in enhancing security, privacy, and data generation techniques. The paper was received in December 2020, revised in March 2020, and accepted in April 2021, with publication in July 2021.

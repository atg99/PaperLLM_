This paper surveys the application of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) in enhancing privacy and security across various domains. It highlights several innovative models designed to protect sensitive information while maintaining data utility.

1. **Multi-view GANs**: The paper discusses a GAN model based on Deep Convolutional GAN (DCGAN) that utilizes multi-view imagery to detect, remove, and paint over moving objects in images, effectively masking private regions while preserving the utility of synthesized street-view images.

2. **PicPrivacy Model**: It introduces the PicPrivacy model which segments and erases sensitive information, such as human portraits, from street-view images. This model repairs blank regions using GANs to sustain the performance of 3D construction.

3. **Auto-Driving GANs (ADGAN)**: Xiong et al. proposed ADGAN models to generate privacy-preserving vehicular images and videos, preventing the identification of location-related background information. Their approach includes customized privacy and utility loss calculations to balance privacy with data utility.

4. **Image Steganography**: The paper presents a new steganography algorithm utilizing a cyclic DCGAN framework to covertly transmit data between different domains while ensuring that the stego images closely resemble the cover images. This approach includes adversarial training to enhance security during transmission.

5. **Image Anonymization**: It describes the Privacy-Preserving Adversarial Protector Network (PPAPNet), which transforms sensitive images into high-quality synthetic images that are resistant to attacks. This method incorporates a noise amplifier for effective anonymization.

6. **Image Encoding**: Lastly, the paper outlines a technique for learning an encoding function with DCGAN to prevent privacy inference while allowing for the prediction of non-private attributes.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the potential of GANs in developing solutions that address privacy concerns while ensuring the functionality and utility of the data.

B. Methodology
The methodology involves utilizing the ResNet-50 architecture, enhanced by residual learning blocks to improve feature extraction and recognition accuracy. By leveraging pre-trained weights from models fine-tuned on large datasets like MS1M and VGGFace2, we aim to enhance the model's performance on masked face recognition tasks.

C. Implementation
We implemented the model using the Pytorch library, ensuring compatibility with the Jupyter notebook environment for efficient experimentation. The system is powered by a GEForce GTX 1080 GPU, which allows for accelerated training and validation processes.

D. Results
The results will be evaluated based on the model's ability to accurately recognize masked faces compared to unmasked faces, utilizing metrics such as accuracy, precision, and recall to assess overall performance. The findings are expected to contribute to advancements in face recognition technology, especially in contexts where masks are prevalent due to health concerns.

In conclusion, this work aims to address the challenges posed by masked faces in recognition tasks, leveraging advanced deep learning architectures and comprehensive datasets to enhance existing methodologies.

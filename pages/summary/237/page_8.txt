The referenced papers cover a range of topics primarily related to face recognition, machine learning, and the impact of COVID-19 on these fields. 

1. Schünemann et al. (2020) conducted a systematic review and meta-analysis examining the effectiveness of physical distancing, face masks, and eye protection in preventing the transmission of SARS-CoV-2 and COVID-19. 

2. Hinton et al. (2006) presented a fast learning algorithm for deep belief networks, contributing to advancements in neural computation.

3. Deng et al. (2009) introduced ImageNet, a large-scale hierarchical image database that has become essential for training deep learning models.

4. Krizhevsky et al. (2012) demonstrated the capabilities of deep convolutional neural networks for image classification using ImageNet, marking a significant milestone in computer vision.

5. Huang et al. (2008) developed the Labeled Faces in the Wild database to facilitate research in face recognition under real-world conditions.

6. Parkhi et al. (2015) and Cao et al. (2018) focused on deep face recognition, enhancing the accuracy of facial recognition systems in varying conditions.

7. Walid (2020) proposed an efficient method for masked face recognition during the COVID-19 pandemic, addressing the challenges posed by face coverings.

8. Several studies (Bagchi et al., Drira et al., Nirmala Priya et al.) explored robust face recognition techniques in the presence of occlusions and pose variations, emphasizing the need for resilience in recognition systems.

9. Wang et al. (2020) introduced a dataset specifically for masked face recognition, which has been crucial for training models during the pandemic.

10. Loey et al. (2021) presented a hybrid deep transfer learning model for face mask detection, illustrating the integration of machine learning methods in response to COVID-19.

11. Jiang et al. (2020) developed Retinamask, a face mask detection system, while Geng et al. (2020) focused on masked face recognition using generative data augmentation.

12. Various works (Liao et al., Sato et al., He et al.) examined partial face recognition, proposing methods that do not rely on traditional alignment techniques.

13. Yapıcı et al. (2019) compared the performance of convolutional neural networks on GPUs, contributing to the understanding of computational efficiency in deep learning applications.

14. Hu et al. (2015) evaluated convolutional neural networks for face recognition, marking a significant intersection of face recognition technology and deep learning advancements.

These studies collectively highlight the evolution and challenges in face recognition technologies, particularly in the context of the COVID-19 pandemic, while also addressing broader machine learning applications.

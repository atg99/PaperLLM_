The paper titled "Masked Face Recognition using ResNet-50" by Bishwas Mandal, Adaeze Okeukwu, and Yihong Theis from Kansas State University addresses the challenges posed by the COVID-19 pandemic on face recognition systems. The authors highlight that traditional face recognition models, trained on unmasked faces, struggle to accurately identify individuals wearing masks due to occluded features like the nose and mouth. 

The study proposes a solution by developing a deep learning model based on the ResNet-50 architecture, which is fine-tuned to recognize masked faces. The authors utilize transfer learning, adapting a pre-trained ResNet-50 model to their dataset, which includes images of individuals both with and without masks. They report achieving an accuracy of 89% on unmasked faces and around 47% on masked faces, demonstrating the model's effectiveness in this context.

Additionally, the paper includes a detailed account of hyperparameter tuning to optimize the model's performance. This research is significant as it offers a framework that can be integrated into existing security verification systems, enhancing their capability to identify individuals even while they are wearing masks, thereby addressing a pressing public health concern during the pandemic.

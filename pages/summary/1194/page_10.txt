The paper introduces a novel semi-supervised learning algorithm called CPL, which addresses the gap in existing algorithms regarding the distinct learning challenges faced by different classes during training. The authors argue that by considering these inherent difficulties, both the convergence speed and final accuracy of the model can be significantly enhanced. They express hope that CPL will inspire further research into the effective use of unlabeled data based on the model’s learning status and class-specific learning difficulties.

The work was supported by computing resources from the Tokyo Institute of Technology and Microsoft Research Asia, with additional funding from the Toray Science Foundation. The references provided include key studies and methods in the field of semi-supervised learning, indicating a strong foundation for the proposed approach.

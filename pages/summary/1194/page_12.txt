This paper discusses advancements in semi-supervised learning, highlighting various approaches and frameworks that utilize pseudo-labeling and curriculum learning techniques. The authors, including Mamshad Nayeem Rizve and colleagues, advocate for a pseudo-label selection framework that integrates uncertainty awareness, which enhances the reliability of pseudo-labels in the learning process. 

Chen Gong et al. introduce multi-modal curriculum learning aimed at improving image classification accuracy through a structured learning process. Hoel Kervadec and others present curriculum semi-supervised segmentation, focusing on medical image analysis. Qing Yu and team propose a multi-task curriculum framework tailored for open-set semi-supervised learning, enhancing model adaptability.

Yue Han et al. explore sentiment analysis using semi-supervised learning, employing a dynamic threshold and multiple classifiers to refine classification accuracy. Zhedong Zheng and Yi Yang address domain adaptation in semantic segmentation by rectifying pseudo label learning through uncertainty estimation. Lastly, Paola Cascante-Bonilla et al. revisit pseudo-labeling through the lens of curriculum labeling, proposing new strategies to optimize semi-supervised learning.

Overall, these contributions underline the significance of integrating uncertainty and structured learning approaches to enhance the performance and robustness of semi-supervised learning systems.

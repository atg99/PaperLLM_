The paper presents MAYOR, a method aimed at improving dense scene text detection, particularly for curved and arbitrarily shaped texts. The authors evaluate MAYOR's performance against several benchmarks, including DAST1500, MSRA-TD500, ICDAR2015, CTW1500, and Total-Text. Results indicate that MAYOR significantly outperforms existing methods such as TextSnake, MSR, and DB, achieving F-measure scores of 85.3% and 84.9% on CTW1500, and 86.3% and 86.0% on Total-Text without external data. When utilizing MLT as pretraining data, MAYOR achieves state-of-the-art results, highlighting its robustness across various datasets.

The paper also discusses the integration of the proposed method with general instance segmentation techniques on the COCO dataset, reporting a slight improvement in mask AP. This improvement is attributed to enhanced modeling techniques that address learning confusion, particularly in dense instances.

The conclusion emphasizes the effectiveness of the proposed MLP mask decoder and instance-aware mask learning in mitigating learning confusion and improving text detection in challenging scenarios. The authors express intent to further enhance the system by integrating text recognition, self-supervised learning, and knowledge distillation methodologies. The work is supported by several research grants and institutions.

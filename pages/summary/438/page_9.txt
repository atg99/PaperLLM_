2. Self-training approaches for domain adaptation in scene text detection (Chen et al. 2020).
3. Comprehensive datasets like Total-text for advancing scene text detection and recognition (Ch’ng and Chan 2017).
4. Instance segmentation methods for detecting scene text (Deng et al. 2018).
5. End-to-end frameworks such as TextDragon and Mask TextSpotter that handle arbitrary text shapes (Feng et al. 2019; Liao et al. 2021).
6. Techniques for multi-oriented and curved text detection, emphasizing the use of region attention and corner localization (He et al. 2017; Liu et al. 2019).

8. Innovations in data generation for training models, including synthetic data for text localization (Gupta et al. 2016).
9. Various competitions and challenges in the field, such as the ICDAR competitions, which benchmark advancements in robust reading (Karatzas et al. 2015).

The paper presents a novel algorithm called Triplet Critic Delayed Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (TCD3), which significantly enhances the learning speed and performance of the Twin Delayed Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (TD3) in various continuous control tasks. The proposed Triplet Critic Update method effectively reduces estimation bias without requiring additional parameters. This improvement leads to more accurate Q-value estimates, thereby outperforming state-of-the-art algorithms in the domain. The authors emphasize that their modification is orthogonal and can be integrated into any deterministic actor-critic method that utilizes temporal difference learning. The references cited support the foundational theories and previous works related to reinforcement learning and actor-critic methods, indicating a comprehensive understanding of the field.

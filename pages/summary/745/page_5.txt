The Triplet Critic Update method proposed here aims to improve the accuracy of Q-value estimation by utilizing three critics instead of two, thus providing a more robust framework for estimating action values.

This method addresses the common underestimation bias found in traditional techniques like TD3, which relies on the minimum of two critics. The introduction of a third critic allows for a more balanced approach to action value estimation by taking the minimum of the maximum values from two critics and the distinct third critic. This modification does not require additional hyper-parameters, simplifying the tuning process for practitioners.

The expected function approximation error under this new update rule is calculated and shows that it averages the biases from the two target action values and the approximate critic. The results indicate that while there may be slight overestimations or underestimations due to the nature of the critics, these errors are manageable and lead to improved performance compared to existing methods like TD3 and DDPG.

Overall, the Triplet Critic Update presents a novel, parameter-free solution to enhance the learning efficiency of deterministic actor-critic methods in continuous control environments, effectively mitigating the issues of bias present in traditional approaches.

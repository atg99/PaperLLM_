Decoder. The decoder reconstructs the masked patches by mapping the predicted latent representations back to the image space. It utilizes a series of transformer blocks to perform this mapping, ensuring that the reconstructed patches are coherent with the original image.

The core innovation of the context autoencoder (CAE) lies in its dual pretraining tasks: masked representation prediction and masked patch reconstruction. This design encourages a clear separation of roles between the encoder and the regressor, enhancing the model's ability to learn effective representations. Unlike existing methods such as MAE, data2vec, and iBoT, which often intertwine these roles or rely on full-view representations for predictions, the CAE maintains a straightforward architecture that focuses on leveraging the encoded representations directly.

In summary, the CAE approach offers a novel framework for self-supervised representation learning by emphasizing the importance of learning distinct roles within the model, thereby improving the efficiency and effectiveness of masked image modeling.

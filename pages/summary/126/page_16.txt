This paper presents an extensive list of references related to advancements in self-supervised learning and visual representation techniques. Key contributions include the development of methods such as self-supervised pretraining of visual features in diverse environments, masked autoencoders as scalable learners, and momentum contrast for unsupervised visual representation learning. Noteworthy works also address contrastive predictive coding, dimensionality reduction through neural networks, and the application of autoencoders in various contexts. 

The references highlight significant models and frameworks, including the Mask R-CNN, the Swin Transformer, and various approaches to enhance masked image modeling through techniques such as semantic-guided masking and uniform masking. The mention of datasets like Microsoft COCO and ImageNet emphasizes the foundation of large-scale visual recognition tasks. 

Overall, the compilation reflects the evolution of methodologies in visual representation learning, showcasing a trend toward leveraging self-supervised techniques to improve model performance and efficiency in image understanding tasks.

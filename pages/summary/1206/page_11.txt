The references listed encompass a variety of topics in developmental science, reinforcement learning, computer vision, and machine learning. 

Spelke and Kinzler (2007) discuss core knowledge in developmental science, emphasizing innate cognitive structures. Sutton et al. (1999) present a framework for temporal abstraction in reinforcement learning, bridging Markov Decision Processes (MDPs) and semi-MDPs. Suwajanakorn et al. (2018) explore latent 3D keypoints through geometric reasoning in their work on neural networks.

Tassa et al. (2018) introduce the DeepMind Control Suite, a platform for reinforcement learning research. Thewlis et al. (2017) focus on unsupervised learning of object landmarks using factorized spatial embeddings. Whitney et al. (2016) investigate visual concepts through continuation learning, while Wiles et al. (2018) present self-supervised learning for facial attribute embeddings from video data.

Xue et al. (2016) contribute to future frame synthesis in visual dynamics using probabilistic methods and cross convolutional networks. Finally, Zhang et al. (2018) address the unsupervised discovery of object landmarks as structural representations in computer vision. Each work contributes to advancing understanding in their respective fields, highlighting the intersection of cognitive science and artificial intelligence.

The paper provides a comparative analysis of various models for DDoS attack detection using the CICDDoS2019 dataset. It highlights the precision and recall of different algorithms, showing that previous studies achieved varying levels of precision, with the highest being 96.9% using a bagging classifier and 98.9% using LSTM. Notably, the research presents an innovative approach that achieved the highest accuracy of 99.97% using the GRU algorithm specifically for SNMP attacks. 

The methodology involved several steps, including data pre-processing to remove NAN values and duplicates, normalization using MinMax scaling, and addressing oversampling issues through the SMOTE technique on the WEKA platform. The dataset was divided into 11 different attack files to facilitate experimentation. The results indicate that this research outperforms existing studies in precision and recall metrics, underscoring the effectiveness of the proposed method.

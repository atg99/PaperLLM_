The paper discusses the performance of various algorithms in detecting Web-DDoS attacks, highlighting the SMO algorithm, which achieved the highest accuracy of 96.62%. Other algorithms such as GRU and RNN also performed well with accuracies of 95.11% and 95.6%, respectively. In contrast, the Naive Bayes (NB) classifier had a significantly lower accuracy of 68.8%, attributed to its reliance on larger data instances. The paper includes metrics such as precision, recall, and F1-score for each algorithm, with SMO demonstrating a precision of 96.70% and recall of 96.60%. The training and test accuracy improved over epochs, stabilizing after reaching a peak accuracy of 96% at the 40th epoch. The training loss decreased from 0.35% to a stable 0.1%, indicating effective model training. Overall, the study emphasizes the superior performance of the SMO algorithm in detecting Web-DDoS attacks compared to others.

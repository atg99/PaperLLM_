The paper discusses the effectiveness of various algorithms in detecting UDP-Lag attacks, highlighting their accuracy and performance metrics. The RNN algorithm achieved the highest accuracy at 99.87%, followed closely by the SMO algorithm at 99.86%, the GRU algorithm at 99.55%, and the Naive Bayes (NB) algorithm at 96.63%. The training accuracy for the best-performing model increased from 98.86% to 99.7%, stabilizing after the 18th epoch, which also marked the point of lowest loss at 0.01%. The test accuracy showed a similar trend, starting at 98.6% and reaching 99.85%. Overall, the results indicate that RNN and SMO are particularly effective in detecting UDP-Lag attacks, with detailed metrics provided for each algorithm's precision, recall, and F1-score.

This paper addresses the limitations of existing DDoS attack detection methods, particularly those based on older datasets. It utilizes the CICDDoS2019 dataset, which includes contemporary DDoS attack data, specifically focusing on TCP/UDP protocols at the application layer. The dataset is noted for its realistic representation of benign and attack traffic, capturing a range of modern reflective DDoS attacks such as NTP, NetBIOS, and SSDP. 

The research emphasizes the necessity of updated datasets due to the emergence of new attack vectors that pose security challenges. The CICDDoS2019 dataset is highlighted for its comprehensive traffic capture, attack diversity, and data source heterogeneity, making it superior to previous datasets. It includes labeled flows based on various protocols and captures the behavior of users across multiple applications. The paper concludes that the selected dataset is crucial for enhancing the detection and identification of DDoS attacks through machine and deep learning approaches.

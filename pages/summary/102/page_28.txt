The paper discusses the advantages of utilizing a generic dataset for malware classification instead of relying on multiple datasets for each specific malware class. This approach aims to enhance security measures and contribute to the prevention of Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks. By consolidating data, the proposed method could streamline the analysis and improve the effectiveness of security services. The references cited provide a foundation for the research, covering various aspects of malware detection, IoT security, and intrusion detection systems.

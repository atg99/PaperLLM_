The paper discusses the effectiveness of various classification algorithms in detecting SNMP attacks. The highest accuracy achieved is 99.99% using the SMO algorithm, while GRU, RNN, and NB algorithms achieved accuracies of 99.97%, 99.79%, and 99.87%, respectively. Key performance metrics including precision, recall, and F1-score are also reported, with SMO showing the best overall performance. The training accuracy improves from 98.25% to 99.9%, stabilizing after reaching its peak, while test accuracy follows a similar trend. The lowest training loss recorded is below 0.01% at the 8th epoch, indicating effective model training. Figures illustrate the convergence of accuracy and loss over epochs.

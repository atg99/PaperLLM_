The results of SSDP attack detection using various algorithms are presented, highlighting that the GRU algorithm achieved the highest accuracy of 99.90%. Other algorithms, including RNN, SMO, and NB, yielded accuracies of 99.87%, 99.89%, and 99.78%, respectively. The performance metrics for each model include precision, recall, and F1-score, with GRU showing the best overall performance. The accuracy convergence graph indicates that the highest accuracy was reached at the 45th epoch, with training accuracy improving from 96.7% to 99.8% and test accuracy rising from 98.7% to 99.9%. Additionally, the training loss decreased from 0.12% to below 0.01%, stabilizing thereafter. The findings suggest that GRU is particularly effective for detecting SSDP attacks in DDoS malware scenarios.

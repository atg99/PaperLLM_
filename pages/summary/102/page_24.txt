The paper discusses the performance of various classification models in detecting TFTP attacks. Four models were evaluated: SMO, GRU, RNN, and NB. The SMO algorithm achieved the highest accuracy of 99.97%, followed closely by GRU at 99.83%, RNN at 99.78%, and NB at 98.92%. The paper includes a table detailing the accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score for each model. Notably, the GRU model's accuracy peaked at 99.83% during the 18th epoch, with a training accuracy curve stabilizing after reaching 99.8%. The test accuracy also increased to 99.83%, while the lowest training loss was recorded at 0.013% at the 17th epoch, indicating effective model performance in TFTP attack detection.

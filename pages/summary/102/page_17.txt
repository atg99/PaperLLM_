The paper presents the performance metrics of a model designed to detect DDoS malware, particularly in relation to LDAP and MSSQL attacks. The model achieves a training accuracy of 99.83% by the 8th epoch, starting from an initial accuracy of 98.4%. The test accuracy reaches 99.95%, indicating strong performance on unseen data. The training loss decreases from 0.08% to below 0.01% by the 48th epoch, demonstrating effective learning and convergence of the model. Figures 6 and 7 illustrate the accuracy and loss trends over the epochs, confirming that both training accuracy and loss stabilize after initial improvements.

The paper discusses various aspects of Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks and defense mechanisms. It references multiple studies and reports that categorize DDoS attacks, analyze their evolution, and propose detection methods using machine learning and other technologies. Key contributions include the development of realistic attack datasets, the introduction of novel frameworks for detection, and the examination of specific attack types, such as application layer attacks. The research emphasizes the importance of adaptive techniques in Software-Defined Networking (SDN) and highlights the role of machine learning algorithms in enhancing security measures against DDoS threats. The findings indicate a need for comprehensive strategies that incorporate both classification and anomaly detection to effectively mitigate DDoS attacks in various environments, including cloud computing and smart cities.

The paper presents DIDDOS, a novel approach for detecting and identifying Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks using the CICDDoS2019 dataset. The study employs deep learning algorithms, specifically Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) and Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN), alongside traditional machine learning techniques like Naive Bayes (NB) and Support Vector Machine (SMO). Experimental results indicate that DIDDOS excels in performance metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score, achieving a peak accuracy of 99.91% for SSDP attacks and an average of 99.7% across all attack types. The precision, recall, and F1-score for SSDP attacks are reported at 99.83%, 99.79%, and 99.69%, respectively. Future work aims to integrate this approach into an intrusion detection system and enhance it into an intrusion prevention system, alongside developing a comprehensive malware dataset to improve detection capabilities.

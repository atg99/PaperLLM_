The section on NetBIOS attacks presents a comparative analysis of various algorithms used for detection, highlighting the performance of the GRU algorithm, which achieved the highest accuracy of 99.94%. Other algorithms, including RNN, SMO, and NB, recorded accuracies of 99.89%, 99.93%, and 99.87%, respectively. Table 7 provides detailed metrics for each algorithm, including precision, recall, and F1-score, with GRU also leading in these metrics. 

The training accuracy for the GRU model shows a steady increase from 98.8% to 99.9%, stabilizing thereafter, while test accuracy similarly rises from 99.3% to 99.9%. The training loss decreases from 0.06% to 0.004%, reaching its lowest point below 0.01% by the 48th epoch. Figures 8a and 8b illustrate the convergence trends of accuracy and loss in relation to the epochs, emphasizing the effectiveness of the GRU model in detecting NetBIOS attacks.

This paper discusses the applications of fuzzy logic systems, particularly those based on Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) and Improved Ant Colony Optimization (IACO), in solving multi-objective problems, as well as their use in control and forecasting fields. Despite their utility, some challenges remain in these areas. The references cited provide a comprehensive background on the development and optimization of fuzzy logic systems, highlighting various methodologies such as genetic algorithms and particle swarm optimization.

The authors, Zhifeng Zhang and Tao Wang, are affiliated with Liaoning University of Technology, where they focus on fuzzy reasoning, control systems, and type-2 fuzzy logic systems. Zhang is pursuing a master's degree in applied mathematics, while Wang serves as a professor with a strong research interest in fuzzy systems and their applications in system identification.

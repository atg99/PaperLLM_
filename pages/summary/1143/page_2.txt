where ∆ti j(t) represents the contribution of the best solution found in the iteration, reinforcing the paths that led to optimal results. This method helps in balancing exploration and exploitation, allowing the algorithm to converge towards better solutions while still exploring new paths.

The proposed Improved Ant Colony Optimization (IACO) method incorporates these pheromone updating strategies to enhance the performance of the algorithm when applied to interval type-2 fuzzy logic systems. By addressing the challenges of rule selection and parameter adjustment in fuzzy logic systems, IACO demonstrates effective adaptability and robustness in forecasting complex systems, such as the Mackey-Glass chaotic time series. 

In summary, this paper introduces a novel approach combining ant colony optimization with interval type-2 fuzzy logic, focusing on improving efficiency in rule selection and parameter tuning. The methodology shows promise in handling complex prediction problems and highlights the potential of integrating advanced optimization techniques with fuzzy logic systems.

This paper presents an Improved Ant Colony Optimization (IACO) method for designing an A2-C1 type fuzzy logic system (FLS). The design process focuses on parameter adjustment and rule selection to enhance the system's performance by identifying optimal parameters and minimizing redundant rules. The effectiveness of the proposed IACO algorithm is demonstrated through its application in predicting the Mackey-Glass chaotic time series. Simulation results indicate that both IACO and the traditional Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) demonstrate superior tracking performance compared to classical back-propagation (BP) methods. Specifically, IACO exhibits more precise tracking and a faster convergence rate than ACO, and both IACO and ACO outperform type-1 fuzzy logic systems in tracking tasks.

The introduction highlights the significance of fuzzy logic systems in various fields, acknowledging their advantages and challenges, such as the difficulty in obtaining optimal parameters and the risk of rule explosion. The paper references several studies that have successfully integrated intelligent algorithms with fuzzy logic systems, including various optimization techniques like Genetic Algorithms (GA) and Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO). It notes the evolution of Ant Colony Optimization as a viable approach for optimizing fuzzy logic systems, albeit with increased complexity.

Overall, the study contributes to the field of fuzzy logic by providing a more efficient optimization method that enhances the performance of fuzzy systems, paving the way for further research and applications in complex control scenarios.

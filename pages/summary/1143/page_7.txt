This paper presents an improved ant colony optimization (IACO) method for designing type-2 fuzzy logic systems, specifically the A2-C1 type interval TSK fuzzy logic system and type-1 TSK fuzzy logic system. The proposed IACO incorporates a mechanism for updating global pheromones, enhancing parameter adjustment and rule selection. Simulation results demonstrate that both IACO and the standard ant colony optimization (ACO) outperform classical backpropagation (BP) algorithms in terms of tracking performance. 

The study evaluates the performance of various algorithms using metrics such as root mean square error (RMSE) and comprehensive evaluation error sum (CEES). Results indicate that IACO and ACO yield lower errors compared to BP, with IACO providing superior performance. Specifically, the RMSE and CEES values for the A2-C1 type interval TSK fuzzy logic systems are significantly better than those for type-1 systems, underscoring the benefits of increased algorithm complexity for improved output precision.

Overall, the findings suggest that IACO not only enhances the performance of fuzzy logic systems but does so without adding complexity to the algorithm. The research concludes that both ACO and IACO can effectively be applied to design complex interval type-2 fuzzy logic systems as well as simpler systems, demonstrating their versatility and efficiency in fuzzy system design.

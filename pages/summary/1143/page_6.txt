The paper presents a comparison between different algorithms for designing fuzzy logic systems, specifically focusing on the Interval Ant Colony Optimization (IACO) and its variants. Key findings indicate that the T2IACO algorithm outperforms T2ACO in tracking performance, demonstrating the effectiveness of global pheromone updates in enhancing algorithm performance. Additionally, the IACO method shows superior results compared to the traditional Backpropagation (BP) algorithm, emphasizing its efficiency in overcoming the limitations of classical approaches.

The authors also introduce type-1 TSK fuzzy logic systems based on both IACO (T1IACO) and ACO (T1ACO), and compare these with the A2-C1 type interval TSK fuzzy logic systems (T2IACO and T2ACO). With consistent parameters and structure, results illustrate that T2IACO surpasses T1IACO, suggesting that the A2-C1 type interval TSK fuzzy logic system provides greater accuracy for complex problems. Furthermore, the comparison between T2ACO and T1ACO reinforces the advantages of the interval-based approach. Overall, the study confirms the effectiveness of IACO in designing fuzzy logic systems and highlights the benefits of using interval representations for improved performance.

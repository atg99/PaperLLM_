The paper discusses the design of type-2 fuzzy logic systems utilizing an improved ant colony optimization (IACO) approach. The IACO method enhances the traditional ant colony optimization by incorporating pheromone trails that guide the search for optimal paths. The pheromone update mechanism is defined, emphasizing its role in reinforcing the selection of optimal paths, thereby improving search efficiency. Key advantages of IACO include its ability to leverage positive and negative feedback, maintain robustness, and coordinate multiple objectives effectively. The algorithm can terminate based on maximum iterations, system error thresholds, or convergence of optimal paths.

The paper further details the framework of an interval type-2 TSK (Takagi-Sugeno-Kang) fuzzy logic system, which comprises five components: fuzziﬁer, rule-base, inference engine, type-reducer, and defuzziﬁer. This system differs from standard type-2 fuzzy systems by simplifying the secondary degree of membership function and using interval sets for the consequent. The fuzziﬁer maps inputs into fuzzy sets, while the rule-base consists of IF-THEN rules based on interval type-2 fuzzy sets. The inference process combines fuzzy inputs with fuzzy rules to generate an output fuzzy set. The type-reducer converts this into a type-1 fuzzy set, which is then translated into a precise output by the defuzziﬁer. The methods employed aim to enhance the overall performance and applicability of fuzzy logic systems in complex decision-making scenarios.

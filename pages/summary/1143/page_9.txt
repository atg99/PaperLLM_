The paper discusses the design of type-2 fuzzy logic systems utilizing an improved ant colony optimization approach. The authors, Yang Chen and Jie Lan, both lecturers at Liaoning University of Technology, have backgrounds in applied mathematics and information science. Their research focuses on fuzzy reasoning, fuzzy control, and system identification, particularly in the context of type-2 fuzzy logic systems and fuzzy neural networks. The paper aims to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of fuzzy logic systems through the application of advanced optimization techniques, thereby contributing to the broader field of intelligent systems.

The paper discusses the activations of AdapterFusion in the twelve layers of a BERT-base model, focusing on various target tasks. Each row represents a specific task such as BoolQ, CosmosQA, and others, while each column corresponds to different adapters. The visual representation uses black squares to indicate non-activated adapters and white cells to denote full activation. This layout highlights the varying levels of adapter activation across different tasks, suggesting how the model leverages specific adapters to optimize performance for each task. The findings indicate a nuanced approach to task-specific adaptation within the BERT architecture.

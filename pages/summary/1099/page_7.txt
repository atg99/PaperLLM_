Key findings highlight that Fusion with ST-A shows consistent improvements across most tasks, effectively leveraging information from other tasks without significant interference. In contrast, Fusion with MT-A, while beneficial, does not fully mitigate the challenges associated with multi-task training. Overall, AdapterFusion proves to be a versatile method, allowing for effective integration of adapter weights trained in different settings, and facilitating the addition of new tasks without extensive retraining.

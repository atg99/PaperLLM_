The paper presents a grid search analysis on the SST-2, Argument, and CSQA datasets to determine optimal architecture settings for BERT models. The focus is on the effects of adapter positions and LayerNorm configurations on model accuracy. 

Key findings include that the best performing architecture across all datasets is the "Top Adapter Only" configuration, combined with "Pretrained LayerNorm Before & After" and without a "New LayerNorm." This conclusion is derived from a systematic investigation of different settings, as illustrated in the figures referenced. 

The results indicate that specific configurations of LayerNorm significantly influence accuracy, emphasizing the importance of architectural choices in fine-tuning BERT for various tasks. The consistent identification of the same optimal architecture highlights its robustness across different datasets.

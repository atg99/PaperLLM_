The activations across different layers of BERT show that the last layer's adapters exhibit more distributed activations, suggesting that they function as an extension of the prediction head rather than being encapsulated by frozen layers.

8.2 Contemporary Work
We also discuss recent advancements in parameter-efficient fine-tuning methods, such as sparse vector training and prefix-tuning, which offer alternatives to full model fine-tuning by focusing on task-specific parameters. These methods highlight the potential for innovative combinations of multiple tasks.

8.3 Conclusion
Overall, AdapterFusion emerges as a versatile and efficient approach for transfer learning, contributing to improved model performance across a variety of tasks while maintaining computational efficiency.

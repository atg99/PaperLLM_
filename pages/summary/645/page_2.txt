The proposed FactorGCN framework consists of three main steps: Disentangling, Aggregation, and Merging. In the Disentangling step, the input graph is broken down into multiple factor graphs that represent distinct latent relationships among nodes. The Aggregation step involves applying Graph Convolutional Networks (GCNs) to each of these factor graphs to extract latent features specific to each relationship type. Finally, in the Merging step, the features from all factor graphs are concatenated to create comprehensive, interpretable features that can be utilized for various downstream tasks.

The paper emphasizes the benefits of FactorGCN in providing interpretations of complex relationships within graphs, particularly in scenarios where multiple heterogeneous connections exist. This approach allows for a more nuanced understanding of the data, as it captures the multiple roles that nodes can play in relation to one another.

The key contributions of FactorGCN highlight its ability to conduct graph-level disentangling, which accounts for global topological structures, and to support multi-relation disentangling, thus accommodating the complexities of real-world data. This positions FactorGCN as a valuable tool for enhancing the interpretability and effectiveness of graph-based learning models.

This paper discusses the effectiveness of Detail Guidance in enhancing semantic segmentation performance, particularly in the context of the STDC2 network on datasets like Cityscapes and ImageNet. Key findings include a visual and quantitative analysis of the impact of Detail Guidance on feature maps and predictions.

In Figure 6, input images and their corresponding heatmaps are presented, illustrating how Detail Guidance improves the spatial information captured in the feature maps of Stage 3. The predictions made with Detail Guidance are shown to be more accurate, particularly for small objects and boundaries, as compared to those made without it.

Table 4 provides quantitative results, demonstrating that the integration of Detail Guidance into the STDC2-Seg model significantly enhances the mean Intersection over Union (mIoU) metric without compromising inference speed. The analysis also includes comparisons of various configurations of the STDC2 network, highlighting different block numbers and the use of Spatial Path from BiSeNetV1 to encode spatial information. Although adding Spatial Path improves accuracy, it also increases computational cost.

In the comparative analysis with state-of-the-art methods, the paper emphasizes that the STDC networks achieve superior speed and accuracy relative to other lightweight backbones. Specifically, the STDC1 network outperforms baseline models by 4.1% in top-1 classification accuracy on the ImageNet validation set.

Overall, the paper presents a compelling case for the benefits of Detail Guidance in semantic segmentation tasks, showcasing its ability to enhance precision while maintaining computational efficiency.

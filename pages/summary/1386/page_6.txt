This paper introduces a novel segmentation network enhanced by a Detail Head, which utilizes a Conv-BN-ReLU operator followed by a 1x1 convolution to improve feature representation. While this Detail Head is beneficial during training, it is discarded during inference, allowing for enhanced segmentation accuracy without additional computational cost at this stage.

The method was evaluated on three datasets: ImageNet, Cityscapes, and CamVid. ImageNet serves as a foundational dataset for image classification, containing 1.2 million training images across 1,000 categories. Cityscapes, focused on semantic scene parsing, includes 5,000 finely annotated images, presenting challenges for real-time segmentation due to its high-resolution images. CamVid consists of 701 annotated images for road scene analysis, with a subset of 11 classes used for segmentation tasks.

Evaluation metrics include top-1 accuracy for classification and mean class-wise intersection over union (mIoU) along with Frames Per Second (FPS) for segmentation tasks. The implementation details for both image classification and semantic segmentation are provided, including training configurations such as batch sizes, learning rate policies, and data augmentation techniques.

Ablation studies were conducted to assess the effectiveness of various components in the proposed method. Specifically, the effectiveness of the STDC module was analyzed, showing that an optimal number of blocks improves performance while maintaining computational efficiency. The backbone’s design was also validated against other lightweight backbones, confirming its advantages for real-time segmentation.

Overall, the findings suggest that the proposed segmentation network effectively balances accuracy and computational efficiency, making it suitable for real-time applications.

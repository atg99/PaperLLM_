The paper presents an overview of the STDC Segmentation network, which incorporates a Detail Aggregation Module and a Detail Head to enhance segmentation performance by focusing on spatial details. The Detail Head is integrated into Stage 3 of the network to generate a detail feature map that uses a binary detail ground-truth derived from the semantic segmentation ground-truth through a Laplacian operator.

The Detail Aggregation Module facilitates the generation of this ground-truth by employing a Laplacian kernel and a trainable 1x1 convolution to create soft thin detail feature maps at multiple scales. These maps are then upsampled and combined with the trainable convolution for dynamic re-weighting, ultimately producing a binary detail ground-truth that highlights boundaries and corners.

To address the class imbalance in detail prediction, the paper employs a loss function that combines binary cross-entropy and dice loss. The dice loss is particularly effective in measuring the overlap between predicted and ground-truth detail maps, as it is less sensitive to the proportion of foreground to background pixels.

In summary, the proposed STDC network enhances segmentation accuracy by effectively encoding spatial details through a Detail Guidance mechanism, while the loss function mitigates the challenges posed by class imbalance in detail prediction.

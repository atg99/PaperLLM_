The referenced works focus on advancements in convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and semantic segmentation. 

EfficientNet, introduced by Tan and Le, emphasizes model scaling to optimize performance and efficiency in CNNs. Bisenet, developed by Yu et al., presents a bilateral network that enhances real-time semantic segmentation through guided aggregation, with Bisenet v2 building upon this foundation for improved performance. 

Shufflenet, by Zhang et al., targets mobile devices with an extremely efficient CNN design. Zhang et al. also propose a customizable architecture search for semantic segmentation, allowing for tailored solutions based on specific needs. 

ICNet, presented by Zhao et al., aims for real-time semantic segmentation on high-resolution images, while the Pyramid Scene Parsing Network, also by Zhao et al., focuses on scene parsing through a multi-scale approach. 

These contributions collectively address the challenges of efficiency, real-time processing, and accuracy in semantic segmentation and CNN architecture.

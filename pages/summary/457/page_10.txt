The study evaluates the performance of the U-Net segmentation system in analyzing root images. It reports that the system produced 508 false positives and 345 false negatives. A strong correlation was found between U-Net and manual annotations, with a Spearman rank correlation of 0.9848 (p=2.288×10−7) for root intensities. The analysis of 867 images from 2016 showed a Spearman rank correlation of 0.9748 (p<10−8) and an r² of 0.9217 between segmented root length and root intensity, indicating a close relationship despite some deviations where U-Net detected roots not identified by manual annotators. The data exhibited heteroscedasticity, with variance increasing as root intensity increased. Figures presented illustrate the segmentation results, highlighting false positives in red and false negatives in green, particularly in ambiguous boundary regions between roots and soil, suggesting that many errors stem from manual annotation rather than U-Net's segmentation capabilities.

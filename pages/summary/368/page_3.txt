4.2 Pronoun Prediction
For the pronoun prediction task, we focused on the model's ability to predict demonstrative and relative pronouns in Dutch sentences. This task evaluated zero-shot capabilities by using the pre-trained model without further fine-tuning. The evaluation was conducted on a dataset specifically designed for this purpose, which tested the model's contextual understanding and generalization.

4.3 Part-of-Speech Tagging and Named Entity Recognition
RobBERT was also assessed on its token tagging capabilities through part-of-speech tagging and named entity recognition tasks. These evaluations helped determine how effectively the model could classify tokens within sentences, demonstrating its utility in more granular language processing tasks.

In summary, RobBERT v2 was pre-trained on a significantly larger Dutch corpus than previous models, utilizing a Dutch-specific tokenizer and a refined training process that leveraged advanced computing resources. The evaluation of RobBERT across various downstream tasks highlighted its performance in sentiment analysis, pronoun prediction, and token tagging, showcasing its potential as a robust model for Dutch language processing.

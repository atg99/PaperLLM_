The paper presents an analysis of the fine-tuned RobBERT model's performance in predicting positive reviews based on gender. It highlights the unbalanced nature of the dataset, where 76% of the gender-labeled reviews were written by women. Two fairness metrics are introduced: Demographic Parity Ratio (DPR) and Equal Opportunity (EO). The results indicate a small EO difference for positive reviews, with a DPR of 70.2%, suggesting potential unfairness, although the EO score reflects good predictive performance.

Figure 3 illustrates the ROC curves for male and female reviewers at two rating levels, showing better predictive performance for women in highly positive reviews. The paper discusses limitations and future work, suggesting improvements in model training and fairness during pre-training, as well as the potential for using the RobBERT model in various new research contexts. Public access to the training and evaluation code is provided, along with the RobBERT model and fine-tuned versions.

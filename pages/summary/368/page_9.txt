This paper presents RobBERT, a new Dutch language model based on RoBERTa, which demonstrates superior performance compared to previous models and other BERT-based approaches across various Dutch language tasks. Notably, RobBERT excels particularly with smaller datasets, indicating its potential as a valuable resource for diverse applications. The authors anticipate that RobBERT will serve as a foundational model for fine-tuning on additional tasks, thereby advancing the state of Dutch language processing.

The study acknowledges the support from the Research Foundation - Flanders and the Flemish Government, as well as contributions from various individuals and software tools that facilitated the research. The references cited include works addressing topics such as language bias in NLP, algorithmic profiling, and the development of BERT-like models for Dutch, underscoring the relevance of RobBERT within the broader context of language modeling and ethical considerations in AI.

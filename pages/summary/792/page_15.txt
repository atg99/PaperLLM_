This paper surveys various compression techniques for deep learning models, particularly focusing on quantization methods for text processing. It highlights the trade-offs between model size and accuracy, emphasizing the importance of effective quantization strategies to maintain performance while reducing computational resource requirements.

The paper introduces different types of quantization, including binary, ternary, and general quantization, represented in Figure 3. Binarized networks, which employ binary quantization, achieve a 32x compression ratio but often suffer from significant accuracy loss. The paper distinguishes between deterministic and stochastic binarization methods, with deterministic binarization being the simplest approach, setting weights to 1 for non-negative values and -1 for negative ones.

Further, the paper discusses the Binary Scheme (BS)-Fixed method, which utilizes fixed constants for representing weights after training, and the BS-Flexible method, which improves upon this by using KMeans clustering for initial weight estimation and allowing for back-propagation updates. This flexibility in weight quantization is shown to improve accuracy by eliminating the need for extensive hyper-parameter tuning.

Overall, the survey provides insights into how different quantization techniques can enhance the efficiency of deep learning models while addressing the challenges associated with accuracy retention during the compression process.

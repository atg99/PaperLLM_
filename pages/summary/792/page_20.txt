The paper by Gupta and Agrawal discusses the quantization of weight matrices in recurrent models, specifically focusing on GRUs and LSTMs. It highlights the typical inference equations for GRUs, detailing how matrix multiplications and element-wise multiplications are involved in the computations of gate values and hidden states. The authors point out that quantization introduces challenges, particularly due to the differing value ranges of the gates and the need for additional quantization steps after element-wise operations.

The paper proposes quantized equations for GRUs after applying quantization to the weights and inputs, ensuring that the outputs remain within the appropriate ranges. Additionally, it addresses the limitations of uniform quantization when applied to non-uniform data, suggesting exponential quantization as a more effective alternative. This method quantizes weights based on their distribution, allowing for better utilization of the available bit-width.

The authors introduce deterministic and stochastic exponential quantization methods, which help in storing weights with low precision while minimizing the need for multiplications. Lastly, the concept of balanced quantization is presented, which partitions weight values into bins to achieve a more balanced distribution, enhancing the efficiency of the quantization process. This approach aims to optimize the representation of parameters within neural networks.

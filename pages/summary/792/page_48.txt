The paper by Gupta and Agrawal in TKDD, August 2020, presents a comprehensive overview of popular datasets and references in the domain of natural language processing (NLP) and model compression methods. It categorizes various tasks such as language modeling, neural machine translation, sentiment analysis, question answering, and more, along with the corresponding datasets and techniques used for model compression.

Key datasets highlighted include the Penn TreeBank Corpus, IMDB for sentiment analysis, SQuAD for question answering, and MSCOCO for image captioning. The authors detail various model compression techniques such as neuron pruning, quantization, and structured pruning, mentioning specific models like TinyBERT, MiniLM, and Universal Transformers.

In discussing future trends, the paper emphasizes the potential of linear Transformer models that can handle extensive input lengths, suggesting that many tasks may need redesigning to leverage this capability. It also points out the need for further exploration into combining multiple compression methods, referencing the promising results from the Fastformers approach.

Overall, this work underscores the ongoing advancements in NLP and the importance of efficient model compression techniques to enhance performance across various applications.

The paper "Compression of Deep Learning Models for Text: A Survey" discusses various methods to improve the efficiency of deep learning models, particularly focusing on transformers and their computational complexities. It highlights the Reformer model, which employs a Shared QK Transformer to optimize attention mechanisms by using Locality-Sensitive Hashing (LSH) to identify nearest neighbors among keys, thus reducing computational overhead.

The survey further explores advancements aimed at reducing the quadratic complexity of traditional transformer architectures to linear. Several innovative approaches are detailed:

1. **Star-Transformers**: This method introduces a star-shaped topology that connects non-adjacent nodes through a relay node, allowing efficient information updating among nodes while minimizing complexity.

2. **Linformer**: This architecture utilizes low-rank factorization of the self-attention matrix, projecting key and value layers into lower dimensions to achieve linear complexity in both time and space. It also incorporates parameter sharing strategies across heads and layers to enhance efficiency.

3. **Sparse Sinkhorn Attention**: This approach employs a differentiable sorting mechanism to manage input sequences, allowing for quasi-global attention with reduced memory usage by dividing sequences into blocks and leveraging local windows. It introduces algorithms like Causal Sinkhorn Balancing and SortCut to further optimize performance.

4. **Mathematical Trick by Shen et al.**: This method reformulates the standard dot-product attention to separate the computations of query and key components, effectively transforming the quadratic complexity into linear.

Overall, the paper presents a comprehensive overview of current strategies for optimizing transformer models, focusing on reducing computational complexity while maintaining performance in deep learning applications for text.

The paper "Compression of Deep Learning Models for Text: A Survey" by Manish Gupta and Puneet Agrawal provides a comprehensive overview of the advancements in natural language processing (NLP) facilitated by deep learning models, such as RNNs, GRUs, LSTMs, and Transformer-based architectures like BERT and GPT-2. While these models have achieved remarkable accuracy, their substantial size poses challenges for real-world applications that require smaller models with quick response times and reduced computational power.

The authors discuss six primary methods for compressing these deep learning models: Pruning, Quantization, Knowledge Distillation, Parameter Sharing, Tensor Decomposition, and Sub-quadratic Transformer methods. These techniques aim to make large models more efficient and suitable for deployment in industry settings. The survey highlights the critical need for efficient applications and organizes the extensive recent research in model compression within the deep learning for NLP community, presenting it as a coherent narrative.

Overall, this survey serves as a valuable resource for understanding the landscape of model compression techniques and their relevance in the context of NLP, paving the way for more practical applications of deep learning models in various domains.

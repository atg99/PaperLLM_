The paper discusses the limitations of existing model management platforms, such as SeebBank, which primarily focus on sharing models in their native formats along with results and documentation. A significant drawback is their reliance on users to manually input metadata from experiments, hindering automation and analysis capabilities. The paper identifies two main challenges faced by these systems: their restriction to specific deep learning frameworks and their partial support of the modeling lifecycle, neglecting crucial aspects like model deployment and publishing.

In contrast, ModelKB is introduced as a solution that aims to automate model management across all lifecycle phases with minimal user intervention. The paper includes an example demonstrating the coding process required for experiment tracking in TensorBoard versus ModelKB, highlighting the additional complexities inherent to TensorBoard. The comparison emphasizes the streamlined approach of ModelKB in managing experiments effectively.

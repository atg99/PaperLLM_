The paper presents ModelKB, a cloud-based repository designed for publishing and exploring trained models, akin to source code sharing on platforms like GitHub. Unlike traditional modeling frameworks, ModelKB operates as a complementary system that automates the management of experiments conducted in various frameworks such as TensorFlow 2.0, Keras, and PyTorch.

ModelKB utilizes customized Callbacks for the automatic extraction and tracking of metadata associated with each experiment. This metadata encompasses hyperparameters, model parameters, and contextual information such as required libraries. The extracted metadata facilitates the automatic generation of source code for deployment, sharing, and reproducibility. Additionally, ModelKB includes a graphical user interface (GUI) that allows users to monitor their experiments and gain insights for future training tasks.

The system also supports inference tasks using deployed models, enabling deep learning practitioners to test their models via a web-based interface without the need for coding prediction functions. This feature streamlines the modeling lifecycle from training to deployment and sharing, addressing the challenges of time and resource consumption.

To evaluate ModelKB's effectiveness, a user study and survey were conducted to gather feedback on its capabilities in managing the modeling lifecycle. The findings indicated that while ModelKB facilitated various tasks such as monitoring experiments, deploying models, sharing, and running inference, the execution time overhead was minimal compared to the extensive training durations typical in deep learning.

The paper is structured as follows: Section 2 provides background on deep learning and its challenges. Section 3 details the proposed approach. Sections 4 and 5 cover the implementation and evaluation of ModelKB, respectively. Section 6 discusses related work, while Sections 7 and 8 conclude the paper and outline future research directions.

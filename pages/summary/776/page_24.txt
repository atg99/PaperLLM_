The study evaluated the usability and effectiveness of ModelKB, a research tool designed for tracking and analyzing experiments. Participants, primarily with technical backgrounds, expressed a generally positive view of ModelKB, with 46% remaining neutral, 31% agreeing, and 23% disagreeing on the ease of installation. Although the installation process required some dependencies and assistance, 10 participants managed to install it independently.

ModelKB successfully extracted metadata from all projects with minimal user intervention, leading to a strong agreement (77%) among participants regarding its ease of use for tracking experiments. Users only needed to add two lines of code to initiate ModelKB, and 12 out of 13 participants found its tracking features useful. However, one participant noted the absence of seed values among collected hyperparameters, which ModelKB addresses by allowing users to set and unify seed values across experiments.

Participants praised the intuitive visualization and user-friendly interface of ModelKB, which facilitated the analysis and comparison of experiments. This feature helped practitioners identify overfitting through accuracy graphs and allowed team members to track contributions effectively. The feedback indicated that visualization significantly aids in querying and comparing experiments.

Regarding the automatic deployment feature, only five participants had prior experience with cloud deployment. Those without experience managed to deploy models and provide REST APIs within three days to two weeks, while ModelKB can execute deployments in seconds, highlighting its efficiency and potential benefits. Overall, the findings suggest that ModelKB enhances the workflow of experiment tracking and analysis while offering valuable deployment capabilities.

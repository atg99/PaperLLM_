In this paper, we introduced ModelKB, an innovative software system designed to automate the deep learning modeling lifecycle. ModelKB effectively manages experiments across various phases, including training, evaluation, deployment, and sharing, aiming to minimize the code changes required by data scientists. Our user study demonstrated the system's feasibility and efficiency in monitoring experiments, deploying selected models, and facilitating model sharing and publication. Additionally, a case study highlighted ModelKB's capability to enhance model reproducibility.

The findings indicate that while the system can streamline the modeling process, future work will focus on addressing vulnerabilities in different phases of the deep learning lifecycle, particularly concerning adversarial attacks during training and inference. This emphasis is crucial for ensuring the robustness of deep learning models deployed in critical applications. A demonstration of ModelKB is available online for further exploration.

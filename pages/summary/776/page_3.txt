The paper discusses the challenges faced by deep learning practitioners in managing the lifecycle of their models, particularly during the Experiment phase, which includes training and validation. Existing systems, such as Theano, TensorFlow, CNTK, PyTorch, and Keras, primarily focus on model training but neglect model management tasks. This oversight results in significant manual effort for tracking experiments, logging parameters, versioning models, and visualizing results, which is time-consuming and inefficient, ultimately affecting model sharing and reproducibility.

To address these challenges, several academic and industry solutions have emerged. Academic systems like ModelDB, ModelHub, ProvDB, and Ground focus on specific lifecycle aspects, often requiring code changes, while industry tools like FBLearner Flow, TensorBoard, and MLFlow cater to particular frameworks and pipelines. However, many of these systems do not provide comprehensive lifecycle management.

In response, the authors propose a unified approach called ModelKB (Model Knowledge Base) for end-to-end management of the deep learning lifecycle. ModelKB aims to streamline the modeling process with minimal user intervention by automating the extraction of metadata and managing subsequent tasks such as deployment, sharing, and reproducibility. Its key features include automatic metadata extraction, visualization and comparison of experiments, semi-automatic model deployment, and model reproduction capabilities. ModelKB is designed as a stand-alone Python library, allowing data scientists to focus on modeling without being constrained by specific frameworks or management overhead.

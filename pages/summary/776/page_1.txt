The paper discusses the challenges associated with managing the modeling lifecycle in deep learning, emphasizing the iterative and experimental nature of developing deep learning models. Despite the availability of numerous tools and frameworks, the management of models and their artifacts remains complex and time-consuming. Existing solutions often focus on specific phases of the modeling lifecycle, neglecting critical components such as model deployment.

To address these issues, the authors introduce ModelKB, a software system designed to automate the entire deep learning lifecycle. ModelKB offers functionalities that include monitoring and tracking experiments, visualizing and comparing models, deploying models both locally and on the cloud, and facilitating the sharing and publication of trained models. Additionally, ModelKB aims to enhance reproducibility in deep learning research.

The system currently supports popular frameworks like TensorFlow 2.0, Keras, and PyTorch, with the potential for easy extension to other frameworks. The paper underscores the significance of an integrated approach to model management, which could streamline the deep learning process and improve overall efficiency.

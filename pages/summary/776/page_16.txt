The paper discusses the capabilities of ModelKB, particularly focusing on its inference function and model deployment features. The inference function, generated entirely by ModelKB, aids users in selecting models and allows exploration and prediction in an online repository before local cloning. The Share view enhances collaboration by enabling users to deploy models on the cloud and access two REST APIs for remote inference and model downloads, requiring users to have an account on the ModelKB service.

Model deployment is highlighted as a significant challenge in the modeling lifecycle, especially in cloud environments. The paper notes that deploying a well-trained model can take a graduate student, with no prior cloud deployment experience, five to seven days, not including the additional time needed to create a user interface or APIs. This underscores the complexity and resource demands of integrating cloud services into existing systems.

This paper presents the development of high-level abstract functions, fit and fit_generator, for PyTorch, drawing inspiration from Keras. While not the main goal, these functions significantly streamline the implementation of the training cycle, akin to the high-level APIs provided by other frameworks like fast.ai. The authors emphasize that their contribution does not duplicate existing work but rather focuses on the specific requirements of their approach, resulting in reduced overhead and enhanced usability.

A key feature of this work is the ability to unify the seed value across experiments, which is crucial for improving reproducibility in deep learning. The authors acknowledge the challenges in automatically extracting the seed value during training and provide a solution that allows users to manually set a consistent seed value, addressing a common issue faced by practitioners.

After the training cycle, the paper outlines a process to organize extracted metadata into dictionaries, which are stored alongside the model and implementation files in a local database. This facilitates the visualization of model architecture and the generation of a prediction function for inference tasks. Additionally, a web-based dashboard is created to display the experiment metadata and artifacts, with a URL provided to the user's integrated development environment.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the practical benefits of the developed functions and the importance of reproducibility in machine learning experiments.

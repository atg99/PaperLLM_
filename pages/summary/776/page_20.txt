The paper discusses the implementation of ModelKB, a tool aimed at enhancing model reproducibility in deep learning. Table 3 presents the NeurIPS reproducibility checklist and highlights the information that ModelKB automatically collects, demonstrating its effectiveness. 

The implementation required a combination of skills in Software Engineering and Deep Learning, necessitating the integration of various software systems and programming languages such as Python, JavaScript, and database management systems, alongside deep learning frameworks like TensorFlow 2.0, Keras, and PyTorch. A significant portion of the work involved developing metadata extractors in Python using Callbacks, which was challenging due to the need for in-depth knowledge of the frameworks.

The Callbacks were created by extending the base classes of Keras and TensorFlow to facilitate the extraction of metadata during the model training process. Each Callback is designed to populate a dictionary with specific metadata elements at various stages of training, such as accuracy and loss scores collected at the end of each epoch. The implementation includes multiple Callback functions, such as on_train_begin, on_train_end, on_epoch_begin, and on_epoch_end, to effectively capture different types of metadata throughout the modeling lifecycle.

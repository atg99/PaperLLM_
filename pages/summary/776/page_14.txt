The paper describes a software interface designed for managing and visualizing experiments within a project. It features a Project view that lists experiments along with their metadata, supporting various export formats such as JSON, CSV, PDF, and upcoming support for LaTeX to streamline metadata sharing. 

The Experiment view allows users to focus on individual experiments, organized into four tabs: Metadata, Architecture, Inference, and Share. The Metadata tab provides a comprehensive overview of selected metadata, including visualizations of accuracy and loss scores over time for training and validation datasets, where applicable. 

The Architecture tab offers an interactive visualization of the model's architecture, utilizing the open-source tool Netron for detailed layer configurations. Users can click on layers to access a side panel with specific settings. Figures in the paper illustrate these functionalities, emphasizing the user-friendly design aimed at enhancing experiment management and visualization.

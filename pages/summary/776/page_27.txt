The paper discusses the evolution of deep learning frameworks and the associated challenges in managing the modeling lifecycle. It highlights that while frameworks like TensorFlow, Caffe, and PyTorch have advanced the development and training of deep learning models, they often overlook the complexities of lifecycle management. Consequently, various platform providers have developed solutions to address these challenges, including FBLearner Flow, TensorBoard, and Amazon's SageMaker. However, many of these tools require users to adapt to specific frameworks and involve significant code instrumentation, which is not favored by data scientists.

TensorBoard is noted for focusing on experiment monitoring but is more complex to use compared to ModelKB, which offers a more user-friendly end-to-end lifecycle management solution. MLFlow, another prominent tool, is similar to ModelKB but also necessitates considerable code changes. Other systems like ModelDB and ModelHub have emerged to tackle model management issues but come with limitations, such as restricted support for deep neural networks and manual effort for model sharing and deployment.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the need for more flexible and integrated solutions that simplify the management of the entire deep learning lifecycle without imposing significant changes to existing workflows or frameworks.

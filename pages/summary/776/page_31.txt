The referenced papers encompass various aspects of deep learning, model management, and software engineering. 

Gharibi et al. (2019a) discuss the automated management of deep learning experiments, emphasizing the need for efficient handling of experimental data. In a related work (Gharibi et al. 2019b), they propose ModelKB, aimed at automating the modeling lifecycle in deep learning environments. 

Goodfellow et al. (2016) provide foundational knowledge on deep learning, while subsequent works by Goodfellow et al. (2014, 2018) focus on adversarial examples and enhancing robustness against adversarial inputs. The significance of visualization tools like TensorBoard (Google 2019) in monitoring learning processes is highlighted as well.

Kumar et al. (2016, 2017) present challenges and systems related to data management in machine learning, advocating for advanced analytics and management systems to streamline processes. Miao et al. (2017a, 2017b) contribute to the discourse on deep learning lifecycle management, proposing unified data and lifecycle management approaches.

Overall, these works collectively address the complexities of managing deep learning models and experiments, the importance of robust methodologies, and the integration of software engineering principles in the development of machine learning systems.

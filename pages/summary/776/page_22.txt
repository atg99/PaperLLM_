The paper evaluates ModelKB, a software tool designed to automatically and semi-automatically manage the lifecycle of deep learning models. The evaluation focuses on several key objectives: 

1. The study investigates the advantages and challenges of utilizing ModelKB in deep learning projects, particularly its ability to extract and visualize essential metadata for data scientists.
2. The effectiveness of the semi-automatic deployment feature is validated both locally and in cloud environments, demonstrating the system's capability to deploy managed models.
3. The sharing feature's benefits and limitations are illustrated, emphasizing its role in collaborative environments.
4. The system's contribution to improving reproducibility is assessed, particularly through the sharing of metadata among team members to regenerate previous experiments. This is compared to the reproducibility checklist from the NeurIPS Conference.

The methodology involved a set of tasks for participants to utilize all features of ModelKB, followed by a Likert-Scale survey to gauge their feedback. Key survey questions addressed various aspects of ModelKB, including its ease of use, effectiveness in tracking experiments, user satisfaction, and comparison with other model management tools. The overall findings suggest that ModelKB significantly aids in managing the deep learning lifecycle, allowing practitioners to focus more on modeling tasks rather than management.

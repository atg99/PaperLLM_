The paper discusses the development of a Metadata Extractor designed to enhance reproducibility, sharing, and deployment of experiments by automatically extracting context metadata. This metadata includes critical elements such as project title, user, environment, and framework. The Metadata Extractor utilizes Callbacks and Abstract Syntax Trees (ASTs) to facilitate the extraction process.

Callbacks are functions that can be passed to other functions to be executed at specific points during execution. In this case, the Callbacks are triggered at the beginning of each training cycle in Keras and TensorFlow 2.0, enabling automatic metadata extraction during the training phase. The paper provides a code snippet illustrating the implementation of these Callbacks in Keras.

A significant advantage of the ModelKB system, as highlighted in the paper, is the minimal code alterations required for users to manage their modeling experiments. By simply importing the ModelKB library and using the predefined Callbacks, users can initiate the management process efficiently. The paper emphasizes that this streamlined approach reduces the complexity typically associated with managing experimental metadata, thus enhancing usability for researchers and practitioners in the field.

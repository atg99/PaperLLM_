The Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) for the T-GCN model is significantly lower, indicating improved predictive accuracy. Specifically, for 15-minute traffic forecasting, RMSE is reduced by approximately 57.8% compared to GCN, and for 30 minutes, the reduction is about 57.6%. When compared to the GRU model, RMSE decreases by around 3.3% for 15 minutes and 2.9% for 30 minutes, showcasing the T-GCN's capability to effectively capture temporal dependencies.

The T-GCN model exhibits robust long-term prediction abilities, maintaining stable performance across varying prediction horizons, as evidenced by minimal changes in RMSE and accuracy. Perturbation analysis indicates that the model is resilient to noise, with performance remaining consistent when subjected to Gaussian and Poisson noise distributions.

Model interpretation reveals that the T-GCN struggles with peak traffic predictions due to the smoothing nature of the GCN's filter process. Additionally, errors arise from zero-value records when no taxis are present and from significant relative errors when traffic values are low. Nevertheless, the T-GCN consistently outperforms other models across different prediction horizons, confirming its effectiveness in traffic forecasting.

In this paper, the authors present a novel neural network-based approach for traffic forecasting, termed T-GCN. This model integrates Graph Convolutional Networks (GCN) and Gated Recurrent Units (GRU) to effectively capture both spatial and temporal dependencies in traffic data. The urban road network is modeled as a graph, where nodes represent roads and edges indicate connections between them, with traffic data serving as node attributes.

The GCN component focuses on understanding the topological structure of the road network to capture spatial dependencies, while the GRU captures the dynamic changes in node attributes to address temporal dependencies. The T-GCN model demonstrates superior performance in traffic prediction tasks when evaluated against various models, including HA, ARIMA, SVR, GCN, and GRU, across different prediction horizons.

Furthermore, perturbation analysis confirms the robustness of the T-GCN approach. The findings suggest that the model not only excels in traffic forecasting but is also applicable to other spatio-temporal tasks.

The paper presents the Temporal Graph Convolutional Network (T-GCN) as an innovative approach for traffic prediction, addressing the challenges posed by spatial and temporal dependencies in urban traffic data. Traffic forecasting is crucial for intelligent traffic systems, aiding in urban planning, management, and control. The T-GCN model integrates Graph Convolutional Networks (GCN) to capture spatial dependencies—reflecting the influence of road network topology—and Gated Recurrent Units (GRU) to account for temporal changes in traffic data.

The authors argue that existing forecasting methods often fail to simultaneously address both spatial and temporal aspects effectively. Through experiments on real-world traffic datasets, the T-GCN model demonstrates superior performance compared to state-of-the-art methods, successfully identifying spatio-temporal correlations in traffic data. The paper concludes by providing access to the model's TensorFlow implementation, facilitating further research and application in traffic forecasting.

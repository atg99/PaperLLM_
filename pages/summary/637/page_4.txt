The paper presents a novel approach for traffic forecasting using a T-GCN model, which integrates Graph Convolutional Networks (GCN) and Gated Recurrent Units (GRU). The methodology is structured into two main components: spatial dependence modeling and temporal dependence modeling.

In the spatial modeling section, the GCN is employed to capture the complex topological structures of urban road networks, which traditional CNNs cannot effectively represent due to their reliance on Euclidean spaces. The GCN processes the adjacency and feature matrices to extract spatial features by considering the relationships between connected roads. This enables the model to learn the spatial dependencies inherent in traffic data.

For temporal dependence, the GRU is utilized due to its efficiency in handling sequence data while mitigating issues related to gradient disappearance and explosion that affect standard RNNs. The GRU's architecture, characterized by reset and update gates, facilitates the retention of relevant historical information while adapting to new data inputs, thereby capturing the dynamic trends in traffic patterns.

The combined T-GCN model leverages the strengths of both GCN and GRU to predict future traffic conditions based on historical data, achieving a comprehensive understanding of both spatial and temporal dependencies in urban traffic forecasting. The final prediction is generated through a fully connected layer, ensuring a robust output based on the learned features.

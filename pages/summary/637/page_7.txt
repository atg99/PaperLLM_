The paper presents a comparison of prediction performance using the T-GCN model against several baseline methods, such as HA, ARIMA, SVR, GCN, and GRU, on two datasets: SZ-taxi and Los-loop. The evaluation metrics include RMSE, MAE, accuracy, R-squared, and variance.

Figures illustrate the variations in RMSE and MAE as well as accuracy, R-squared, and variance across different models and datasets. The results for the SZ-taxi dataset show that T-GCN consistently outperforms other models in terms of RMSE and MAE, achieving the lowest values. For instance, at the 15-minute mark, T-GCN achieves an RMSE of 3.9162 and MAE of 2.7061, compared to higher values from the other models.

In the Los-loop dataset, T-GCN also demonstrates superior performance, particularly in accuracy and R-squared values, indicating its effectiveness in making accurate predictions. Across various time intervals (15, 30, 45, and 60 minutes), T-GCN maintains a competitive edge, highlighting its robustness as a predictive model in comparison to traditional methods.

Overall, the findings emphasize the advantages of the T-GCN model in delivering enhanced prediction accuracy and lower error rates, validating its application in time series forecasting tasks.

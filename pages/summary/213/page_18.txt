The paper discusses the performance of various ResNet architectures, particularly focusing on the degradation problem observed in deeper networks. It presents training loss curves for ResNet and iResNet models on CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100 datasets, indicating that the degradation problem becomes apparent as the depth of the original ResNet increases from 164 to 1001 layers, leading to worse training loss outcomes. In contrast, the iResNet models demonstrate consistent improvement in training loss from 164 to 3002 layers, highlighting their effectiveness in optimization and stability during training, with fewer fluctuations compared to baseline ResNets. The architectural details for the ResNet models are provided, following the structure outlined in previous work. Training is conducted over 164 epochs with a starting learning rate of 0.1.

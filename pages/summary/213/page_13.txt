The findings suggest that the enhancements made to the residual network architectures are effective in addressing depth-related performance issues, as evidenced by the consistent improvements across various depths without degradation. The results underscore the potential for these improved architectures to facilitate the learning of more powerful representations, limited primarily by available data and computational resources. Overall, the proposed approaches demonstrate a clear advancement in both image classification and object detection, validating the effectiveness of integrating the ResStage and improved projection shortcuts within the residual network framework.

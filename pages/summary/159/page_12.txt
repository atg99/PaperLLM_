The references listed comprise a collection of significant works in the field of anomaly detection and time series prediction, highlighting various methodologies and techniques. Notable contributions include robust regression and outlier detection by Rousseeuw and Leroy, and advancements in recurrent neural networks for time series prediction by Qin et al. The application of LSTM networks for anomaly detection in diverse domains, including ECG signals and spacecraft monitoring, is emphasized through multiple studies, such as those by Hundman et al. and Chauhan and Vig. Several papers explore unsupervised anomaly detection using deep learning frameworks, such as the autoencoding Gaussian mixture model by Zong et al. and the GRU-based approach by Guo et al. The role of generative adversarial networks in multivariate anomaly detection is also addressed, notably in the work by Li et al. Overall, these references provide a comprehensive overview of current methodologies in anomaly detection, particularly leveraging deep learning techniques across various applications.

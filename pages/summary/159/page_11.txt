The paper discusses a method for detecting anomalies in time series data, emphasizing its accuracy and speed. However, it acknowledges limitations, particularly in scenarios where anomalies may occur in successive subsequences rather than as isolated points. The current anomaly score module is tailored for point anomalies, suggesting a need for redesign to accommodate subsequence detection. Additionally, while the method requires data accumulation to adjust the anomaly score threshold, future improvements aim to implement an adaptive threshold adjustment for quicker adaptation.

The authors contributed collaboratively to the conceptualization, design, experiments, and writing of the manuscript. The research received funding from various grants, including the National Natural Science Foundation of China and the EU FP7 IRSES Mobile Cloud Project. They declare no conflicts of interest.

The paper also references various studies and surveys related to anomaly detection and data analysis, highlighting its relevance in fields such as the Internet of Things and big data analytics.

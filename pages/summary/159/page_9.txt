The paper presents experimental results on the performance of various methods in detecting anomalies using different latent space dimensions on the Yahoo dataset. The methods evaluated include LSTM-AE, LSTM-VAE, MAD-GAN, and LSTM-based VAE-GAN. The results indicate that the LSTM-based VAE-GAN consistently achieved the highest precision, recall, and F1 scores across all latent dimensions tested, with a peak F1 score of 0.8907 at a latent dimension of 10.

Additionally, a visual analysis was conducted to illustrate how the LSTM-based VAE-GAN differentiates between normal and anomalous data. The model, trained on normal data, effectively reconstructs normal samples while failing to replicate the anomalies present in the input. This characteristic is highlighted in the visual representation, where the input time subsequences show anomalies (highlighted in red) and their corresponding reconstructed subsequences do not reflect these anomalies, thereby demonstrating the model's potential for effective anomaly detection.

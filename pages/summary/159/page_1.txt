This paper presents a novel approach for time series anomaly detection using a long short-term memory-based variational autoencoder generative adversarial network (LSTM-based VAE-GAN). The authors, Zijian Niu, Ke Yu, and Xiaofei Wu, highlight the growing importance of monitoring industrial equipment through time series data, which can accumulate quickly due to high sampling rates and numerous sensors. Traditional methods for anomaly detection, such as ARIMA and CUSUM, have shown limitations in efficiency and accuracy when applied to industrial data.

The proposed LSTM-based VAE-GAN method addresses these challenges by jointly training an encoder, generator, and discriminator, leveraging the strengths of each component. The encoder uses LSTM networks to effectively map real-time data to latent space, while the discriminator enhances the detection process. Anomalies are identified through the differences in reconstruction and the discriminator’s outputs.

Experimental results indicate that this method provides quick and accurate detection of anomalies, making it a valuable contribution to the field of industrial time series monitoring. The findings underscore the significance of developing effective anomaly detection techniques to improve overall equipment effectiveness through early preventive maintenance.

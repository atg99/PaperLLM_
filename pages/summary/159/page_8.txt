The paper discusses the performance of various anomaly detection methods on the Yahoo dataset, specifically focusing on the time spent during the anomaly detection stage for different step sizes. It presents four methods: MAD-GAN, LSTM-based VAE-GAN, LSTM-VAE, and LSTM-AE, illustrated in Figure 3. 

Additionally, the impact of latent space dimensions on the performance of reconstruction-based models is analyzed. The latent space is crucial as it encapsulates the essential information required to represent the original data points. The study investigates how the dimensionality of the latent space affects the representation capability, testing dimensions of 5, 10, and 15.

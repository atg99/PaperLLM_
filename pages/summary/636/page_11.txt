The references provided in this document highlight significant contributions to the field of geometric deep learning and graph-based neural networks. These works encompass a variety of topics, including graph embedding techniques, convolutional networks on graphs, and methods for semi-supervised classification. 

Key references include:

1. Bronstein et al. (2017) discuss the expansion of deep learning beyond Euclidean data, focusing on geometric deep learning principles.
2. Cai et al. (2018) offer a comprehensive survey on graph embedding, detailing associated problems, techniques, and applications.
3. Chen et al. (2019) present supervised community detection using line graph neural networks, contributing to the understanding of graph structures.
4. Defferrard et al. (2016) introduce fast localized spectral filtering for convolutional neural networks on graphs, enhancing computational efficiency.
5. Kipf and Welling (2017) focus on semi-supervised classification with graph convolutional networks, a pivotal advancement in the field.

These references collectively illustrate the evolution of methodologies in graph neural networks and their applications, emphasizing the importance of understanding complex network structures in machine learning.

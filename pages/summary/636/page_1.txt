The paper "Geom-GCN: Geometric Graph Convolutional Networks" by Hongbin Pei et al. addresses the limitations of message-passing neural networks (MPNNs) in graph representation learning. MPNNs, while effective in various applications, face two key challenges: the loss of structural information within neighborhoods and the inability to capture long-range dependencies in disassociative graphs.

To overcome these weaknesses, the authors propose a novel geometric aggregation scheme that leverages the continuous space underlying graphs. This scheme is designed to be permutation-invariant and consists of three main components: node embedding, structural neighborhood, and bi-level aggregation. The authors implement this scheme in a specific model termed Geom-GCN, aimed at enhancing transductive learning on graphs.

Experimental evaluations demonstrate that Geom-GCN achieves state-of-the-art performance across multiple open graph datasets, showcasing its effectiveness in improving graph representation learning by addressing the identified limitations of existing MPNNs.

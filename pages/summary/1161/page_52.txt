The referenced works focus on advancements in twin support vector machines (TSVM) and their applications in multi-class classification problems. Key contributions include the development of various TSVM models, such as the twin multi-class classification support vector machine and the extension of the twin SVM for multi-category classification. Several papers discuss enhancements to existing models, including the incorporation of weighted functional link networks and least squares methods to improve classification performance, especially in imbalanced datasets. The reviews and empirical studies highlight the effectiveness of ensemble methods and deep learning approaches in conjunction with TSVM, showcasing their potential in various applications. Overall, the literature emphasizes the evolving nature of TSVM techniques and their significance in machine learning.

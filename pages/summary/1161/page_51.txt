The referenced works by M. Tanveer and others focus on the development and enhancement of support vector machines (SVMs) for various classification tasks. Key contributions include the introduction of nonparallel hyperplane SVMs, twin support vector machines (TWSVMs) enhanced by optimization algorithms, and the application of safe screening rules to increase the efficiency of these classifiers. Several studies propose novel methodologies such as the MLTSVM for multi-label learning and advanced structural least square TWSVMs, which improve classification performance. Other notable advancements include the use of implicit Lagrangian methods and unconstrained convex minimization to create smooth twin support vector machines. The exploration of generalized loss functions, such as pinball loss SVMs, and the integration of features from pre-trained networks further illustrate the ongoing innovation in the field. Overall, the collection of research highlights a significant evolution in SVM methodologies aimed at improving accuracy and computational efficiency in machine learning applications.

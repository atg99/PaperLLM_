The referenced works focus on various advancements in support vector machines (SVM) and their applications in classification and regression tasks. Notably, the twin support vector machine (TSVM) has been explored as an alternative to traditional SVM, offering enhancements in efficiency and performance for pattern classification. Key literature includes foundational papers on TSVM and its models, as well as surveys detailing its applications.

In the realm of support vector regression (SVR), several studies delve into methodologies and theoretical frameworks, including the development of fuzzy weighted approaches and localized techniques for time series prediction. Noteworthy contributions also address algorithmic advancements in training SVR models, demonstrating the versatility and adaptability of SVM-based approaches across various domains.

Overall, the collective research underscores the significance of SVM and its variants in machine learning, particularly in improving predictive accuracy and computational efficiency in diverse applications.

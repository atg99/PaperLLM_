The references provided detail various advancements in the field of twin support vector machines (TWSVM) and their applications, particularly in semi-supervised classification and clustering. Key contributions include the development of Laplacian twin support vector machines, which enhance classification performance by incorporating Laplacian regularization. Several studies explore different variations of TWSVM, such as Laplacian least squares, multi-category adaptations, and tree-based approaches for image retrieval. Active learning techniques have also been integrated to improve the efficiency of TWSVM in pattern classification tasks. The exploration of fuzzy approaches and weighted loss functions in clustering further demonstrates the versatility and adaptability of TWSVM methodologies across diverse applications. Overall, these works collectively contribute to the ongoing evolution of machine learning techniques, emphasizing the importance of TWSVM in tackling complex classification and clustering challenges.

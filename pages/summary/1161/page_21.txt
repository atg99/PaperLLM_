In summary, various advancements in TWSVM have been made to tackle multi-class classification challenges, focusing on computational efficiency and accuracy. The introduction of algorithms like MBSVM, OVA-TWSVM, DTTWSVM, THKSVM, and LS-Twin-KSVC have all aimed to refine the original TWSVM framework. These developments include methods to reduce computational complexity, improve generalization, and adapt to imbalanced datasets. Notably, the use of weighted strategies and innovative structural approaches, such as the recursive projection technique, have further enhanced the robustness and effectiveness of these classifiers in handling complex multi-class problems.

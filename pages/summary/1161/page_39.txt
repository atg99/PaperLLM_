Tanveer et al. present a sparse SVM approach utilizing pinball loss, emphasizing its potential in telecommunications.

Mangasarian and Wild's proximal SVM classifiers and Khemchandani et al.'s generalized eigenvalue SVMs further illustrate the evolving landscape of SVM methodologies.

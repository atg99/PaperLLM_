The collection of papers referenced primarily focuses on advancements in fuzzy least squares twin support vector machines (FSTSVM) and their applications in various machine learning contexts, particularly in handling class imbalance and improving classification accuracy. Key contributions include the introduction of fuzzy universum least squares twin support vector machines, as explored by Richhariya and Tanveer, which enhances the robustness of classification in imbalanced datasets. Other notable works include the development of entropy-based and regularized versions of FSTSVM, which aim to further refine performance metrics in challenging scenarios. Researchers such as Ganaie and Gupta have explored the integration of fuzzy logic with twin support vector machines to address outlier detection and improve classification results, emphasizing the versatility and adaptability of these models in different computational settings. Overall, this body of work illustrates a significant trend toward enhancing traditional support vector machine frameworks through fuzzy logic and least squares methodologies, thereby expanding their applicability and effectiveness in real-world problems.

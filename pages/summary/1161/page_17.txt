The classification accuracy of different algorithms on various datasets is provided in a table, showing the performance of TWSVM alongside other methods such as LSTWSVM, EFSVM, FTWSVM, EFTWSVM-CIL, and EFLSTWSVM.

Key improvements to TWSVM include the introduction of smoothing techniques to transform primal problems into smooth unconstrained minimization problems, leading to a faster variant called Smooth TWSVM. The iterative alternating algorithm proposed by Jayadeva et al. enhances kernel learning efficiency. Zhang's boosting-based TWSVM improves detection accuracy for clustered microcalcifications, while the BB-TWSVM combines bagging and boosting to mitigate instability.

Further advancements include the MI-TWSVM for multiple instance classification, which deals with labeled bags containing unlabeled instances, and UNTWSVM, which incorporates unity norm equality constraints. Generalized TWSVM employs l1 norms and a convex piecewise quadratic objective function to improve performance. Lastly, Peng's introduction of a variable parameter in TWSVM aims to balance support vectors and margin errors, with an improved version enhancing classification capabilities. These developments illustrate the ongoing efforts to refine TWSVM for various classification challenges.

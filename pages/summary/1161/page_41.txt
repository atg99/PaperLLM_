This paper presents a collection of references focusing on advancements in twin support vector machines (TWSVM) and related methodologies in machine learning. Key contributions include the introduction of various TWSVM models aimed at enhancing regression and classification tasks. 

Notable works include Khemchandani et al.'s exploration of regression via TWSVM, and Kumar and Gopal's development of least squares twin support vector machines for pattern classification. The authors also reference foundational concepts in nonlinear programming by Kuhn and Tucker, which underpin many of the optimization techniques used in TWSVM.

Further developments in the field are highlighted through various adaptations of the least squares twin support vector machine, such as weighted models and those incorporating manifold regularization. The references illustrate a trend towards robust classification methods, including energy-based models and density-based weighting approaches.

Overall, the paper encapsulates significant strides in the TWSVM domain, emphasizing their applicability in diverse areas like human action recognition and multi-surface classification, showcasing the evolution of these techniques over the years.

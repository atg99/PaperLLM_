This paper discusses advancements in the application of pinball loss in twin support vector machines (TSVM) for various classification tasks, particularly focusing on imbalanced data. It references several studies that explore different methodologies, such as maximum margin and minimum volume hyper-spheres, as well as insensitive zone-based approaches. The paper highlights the development of techniques that enhance the performance of TSVM, including robust models, large-scale implementations, and methods for estimating bounds to accelerate computations. Key contributions include the exploration of twin neural networks for handling large unbalanced datasets and the introduction of general twin support vector machines with enhanced loss functions. The overall emphasis is on improving classification accuracy and efficiency in complex data scenarios, underscoring the significance of pinball loss in optimizing these models.

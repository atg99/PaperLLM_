The references listed primarily focus on advancements and applications of twin support vector regression (TSVR) and its variations in various domains. Notable contributions include the development of a twin support vector machine for regression with automatic accuracy control, as discussed by Rastogi et al., and the introduction of asymmetric TSVR by Xu et al. Tanveer and Shubham proposed a regularization approach on Lagrangian TSVR, highlighting its significance in improving model performance. The works also explore various applications, such as inflation determination in China and stock price forecasting using polynomial smooth TSVR. Other studies investigate optimization techniques, such as particle swarm optimization for enhancing TSVR in wind speed forecasting and scaling methods aimed at improving efficiency. Overall, the references underscore the versatility and effectiveness of TSVR in addressing complex regression problems across different fields.

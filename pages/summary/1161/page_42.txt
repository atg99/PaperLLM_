The references provided highlight various studies focused on twin support vector machines (TWSVM) and their applications across different domains. Key contributions include the comprehensive evaluation of TWSVM classifiers on UCI datasets, advancements in robust least squares TWSVM for human activity recognition, and feature selection methodologies for diagnosing health conditions such as heart disease and breast cancer. Several papers explore structural variations of TWSVM, including multi-task approaches and hybrid algorithms, which enhance classification performance. The studies collectively underscore the versatility and efficacy of TWSVM in addressing classification challenges in both balanced and imbalanced datasets, illustrating its potential in applied artificial intelligence and machine learning contexts.

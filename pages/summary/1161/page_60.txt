The document presents a collection of references related to twin support vector machines (TSVM) and their applications in various domains, particularly in classification and regression tasks. 

Key references include:

1. Chu et al. (2018) propose a multi-class classification method for detecting steel surface defects using TSVMs combined with multi-information.
2. Peng and Xu (2013) introduce bi-density TSVMs for improved pattern recognition.
3. Richhariya and Gupta (2019) focus on facial expression recognition through an iterative universum TSVM approach.
4. Anand et al. (2019) discuss a privacy-preserving TSVM classifier designed for vertically partitioned data.
5. Mangasarian and Wild (2006) explore multisurface proximal support vector machine classification through generalized eigenvalues.
6. Bi and Bennett (2003) present a geometric perspective on support vector regression.
7. Xu and Wang (2012) develop a weighted TSVM regression model.
8. Tanveer et al. (2016) detail an efficient implicit regularized Lagrangian TSVM regression method.
9. Ye et al. (2016) investigate a weighted Lagrange epsilon-TSVM regression.
10. Further studies by Peng (2012, 2014) and Wang et al. (2019) contribute additional methods and solutions related to TSVM regression.

The references collectively emphasize the versatility and effectiveness of TSVMs in addressing complex classification and regression challenges across various fields.

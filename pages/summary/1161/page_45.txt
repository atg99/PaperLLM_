The references provided encompass a collection of studies focused on advancements in twin support vector machines (TWSVM) and their applications in pattern classification. Key contributions include the development of robust and efficient algorithms that enhance classification accuracy while managing sparsity and robustness against noise.

Several works, such as those by Qi et al. and Tian et al., explore variations of TWSVM, including robust twin support vector machines and sparse nonparallel support vector machines. Zhang et al. introduce a sparse least square variant with adaptive norms, while López et al. propose robust nonparallel support vector machines utilizing second-order cone programming.

Tanveer's research significantly contributes to the field with various studies on linear programming techniques applied to TWSVM, including robust and sparse models and the application of smoothing techniques. Other notable contributions include the introduction of capped l1-norm approaches by Wang et al. and Ma et al., which aim to improve robustness in classification tasks.

Overall, this body of work reflects a continuous effort to refine TWSVM methodologies, focusing on enhancing efficiency, robustness, and adaptability in various classification scenarios.

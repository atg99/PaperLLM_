The paper provides a comprehensive review of Twin Support Vector Machines (TWSVM) and Twin Support Vector Regression (TSVR), which are innovative machine learning techniques designed for classification and regression tasks, respectively. TWSVM aims to identify two nonparallel hyperplanes for classifying data points, solving two smaller quadratic programming problems instead of one large problem as in traditional Support Vector Machines (SVM). Similarly, TSVR adapts the principles of TWSVM to address regression challenges.

The authors highlight the advancements made in TWSVM and TSVR while noting the scarcity of comparative studies on different TSVR variants. The review begins with an introduction to the foundational concepts of SVM and TWSVM, followed by a detailed discussion of various improvements and applications of TWSVM. The paper then transitions to TSVR, outlining its developments and enhancements.

The authors conclude with suggestions for future research directions and potential developments in the field, emphasizing the need for further exploration of these techniques to fully leverage their capabilities in machine learning applications.

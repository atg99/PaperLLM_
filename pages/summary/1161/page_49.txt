The references cited in the paper by M. Tanveer et al. focus on advancements and variations of twin support vector machines (TSVM) and fuzzy twin support vector machines (FTSVM). Key contributions include the development of FTSVM models that utilize concepts like intra-class hyperplanes and information entropy to address challenges in class imbalance learning. Various studies explore the optimization of kernel selection, robust classification methods, and the application of smoothing techniques in TSVM. Additionally, the literature presents generalized approaches and adaptations of twin support vector machines, highlighting their effectiveness in pattern classification and their application in imbalanced datasets. Notable works also discuss enhancements in algorithmic performance and theoretical foundations, contributing to the overall understanding and application of twin support vector machines in machine learning.

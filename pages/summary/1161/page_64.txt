The paper discusses advancements in predicting brain age using machine learning techniques, particularly focusing on the use of improved least squares twin support vector regression (SVR). The authors, including Tanveer and Ganaie, emphasize the importance of accurately estimating brain age for various health assessments. They compare different machine learning algorithms in their effectiveness for brain age prediction, providing a comprehensive evaluation of their methodologies and results. The findings contribute to the field of biomedical and health informatics, highlighting the potential of these algorithms in clinical applications.

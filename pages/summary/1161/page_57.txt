This collection of works highlights the advancements in machine learning techniques, particularly focusing on twin support vector machines (TWSVM) and their applications across various domains. The papers explore various methodologies and enhancements to TWSVM, including multi least squares approaches and angle-based universum techniques for improved classification performance. Significant attention is given to the diagnosis of Alzheimer's disease, showcasing the use of universum support vector machines in combination with recursive feature elimination and deep learning networks for analyzing MRI scans. Other studies examine applications in speaker recognition and text recognition, emphasizing the versatility and robustness of TWSVM in different contexts. The papers collectively illustrate the ongoing research and development in leveraging advanced machine learning algorithms for practical applications in healthcare and beyond.

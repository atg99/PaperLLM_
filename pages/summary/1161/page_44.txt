The references listed pertain to various advancements in twin parametric-margin support vector machine techniques, which are employed for pattern recognition and classification tasks. Key contributions include the development of new algorithms that enhance the performance and robustness of support vector machines (SVMs) through innovative approaches such as parametric margins, least squares methods, and angle-based frameworks. 

Hao (2010) introduced new support vector algorithms with a parametric insensitive margin model. Peng (2011) proposed the TPMSVM, a novel approach for pattern recognition. Subsequent works by Wang et al. (2013, 2021) and Shao et al. (2013, 2015) focused on model selection, loss functions, and large-scale classification improvements. 

Research by Rastogi et al. (2018) and Khemchandani et al. (2018) explored robust and angle-based twin support vector machines, indicating a trend towards enhancing classification accuracy and efficiency. Collectively, these studies contribute to the evolving landscape of SVM methodologies, addressing various challenges in classification tasks while pushing the boundaries of existing techniques.

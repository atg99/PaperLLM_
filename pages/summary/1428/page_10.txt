6. **Das et al. (2017)** investigate human attention patterns in VQA to compare with deep learning models.
7. **Goyal et al. (2017)** stress the significance of image understanding in VQA tasks, advocating for better integration of visual context.
8. **Krishna et al. (n.d.)** present the Visual Genome project, which connects language and vision through detailed image annotations.
9. **Selvaraju et al. (2017)** introduce Grad-CAM, a technique that provides visual explanations for model predictions in VQA.

The paper presents the Efficient-CapsNet architecture, emphasizing its efficiency and generalization capabilities compared to traditional convolutional neural networks (CNNs). Key parameters for the training process, including m+ (0.9), m− (0.1), and the reconstruction regularizer scaling factor (0.392), are set based on previous CapsNet training. The experiments were conducted on a workstation equipped with an Nvidia RTX2080 GPGPU, utilizing TensorFlow 2.x and CUDA 11, with results averaged over 30 trials.

In the MNIST dataset experiments, Efficient-CapsNet demonstrated a significantly lower parameter count (161 K) while maintaining a high mean accuracy of 0.9974. The architecture's efficiency is highlighted in a comparison with other methodologies, showing a substantial reduction in the number of operations required per batch. The paper also discusses the role of the reconstruction network, which enhances performance in networks with vectorial outputs. Additionally, visual results of digit reconstructions across different methodologies are provided, illustrating the networks' ability to retain essential features of the input images. Overall, Efficient-CapsNet showcases a promising advancement in capsule network design, balancing performance and computational efficiency.

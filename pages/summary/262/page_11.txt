In this paper, the authors introduce Efficient-CapsNet, a new capsule-based neural network that demonstrates enhanced generalization capabilities compared to traditional convolutional neural networks (CNNs). The proposed model achieves state-of-the-art performance across three distinct datasets while utilizing a significantly lower number of parameters and operations. A key innovation in Efficient-CapsNet is the introduction of a non-iterative routing algorithm that employs a self-attention mechanism to efficiently route a reduced number of capsules between layers. The authors also plan to further investigate viewpoint generalization and feature representation by creating a synthetic dataset for scaling the network. The results show that Efficient-CapsNet effectively captures transformations in the output space, as evidenced by the variance explained in principal component analysis conducted on output capsule vectors.

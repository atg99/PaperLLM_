The paper discusses the Efficient-CapsNet architecture, focusing on the use of depthwise separable convolution to enhance the capsule creation process. This method allows for the construction of a primary capsule layer, denoted as Sl n,d, where nl represents the number of primary capsules and dl their individual dimensions. The architecture efficiently simplifies the process and reduces the number of parameters involved. The initial convolutional layers transform the input tensor into a higher-dimensional space, which aids in the formation of capsules. The overall design emphasizes the importance of discriminative learning in effectively utilizing the filters for feature extraction.

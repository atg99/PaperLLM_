In contrast, C-3PO, a protocol droid, specializes in language and diplomacy, functioning as an interpreter between humans and other entities. This role creates a collaborative dynamic, where the human relies on C-3PO for communication. While C-3PO is also autonomous, its anthropomorphic design and behavior foster a different interaction style, which may not resonate as positively with users compared to R2-D2.

The findings suggest that the differences in task nature, communication style, and the perceived autonomy of each robot contribute to the preference for R2-D2. The study indicates that people's attitudes toward robots can significantly be influenced by these variables, highlighting the importance of design and functionality in human-robot interaction.

The paper presents an analysis of the performance of ChatGPT and GPT-3.5 across various reasoning tasks, including arithmetic, commonsense, symbolic, logical reasoning, and natural language inference. Table 1 summarizes different datasets used for evaluation, detailing the number of samples for each.

In arithmetic reasoning, ChatGPT outperforms GPT-3.5 on five out of six datasets without employing chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning. An example illustrates this, where ChatGPT correctly calculates the number of lives Wendy has after losing and gaining lives, while GPT-3.5 provides an incorrect answer. When CoT is utilized, ChatGPT shows even greater performance improvement compared to GPT-3.5.

For commonsense, symbolic, and logical reasoning, the results indicate that CoT does not consistently enhance performance. In some commonsense reasoning tasks, ChatGPT performs worse than GPT-3.5, suggesting that GPT-3.5 has stronger capabilities in these areas.

In natural language inference tasks, ChatGPT demonstrates superior zero-shot performance compared to GPT-3.5 and other models, particularly in producing accurate responses that align with specified output formats. The paper highlights the importance of proper task instructions in achieving higher accuracy in inference tasks.

Overall, the findings reveal that while ChatGPT excels in arithmetic reasoning, its performance varies across other reasoning domains, with GPT-3.5 showing stronger capabilities in certain commonsense reasoning tasks.

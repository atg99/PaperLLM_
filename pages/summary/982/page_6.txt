The paper presents a comparative analysis of various models, including ChatGPT, across different reasoning and natural language tasks, focusing on their accuracy in zero-shot and few-shot settings. In arithmetic reasoning datasets, ChatGPT demonstrates superior performance, achieving high accuracy rates compared to models like text-davinci-002 and text-davinci-003. Specifically, ChatGPT scored 79.8% in ModelMultiArith and 95.8% in GSM8K, indicating its effectiveness in handling arithmetic problems.

In commonsense, symbolic, and logical reasoning tasks, ChatGPT also shows competitive results, with notable scores in zero-shot conditions, such as 73.7% in CSQA and 71.5% in StrategyQA. The few-shot performances of other models like Codex and PaLM are analyzed, highlighting the advantages of ChatGPT in certain categories.

For natural language inference tasks, ChatGPT achieves an accuracy of 85.9% on the RTE dataset, outperforming GPT-3.5, particularly in entailment scenarios, where it excels by 21.9%. However, it shows a decrease in performance in the "Not Entailment" category compared to GPT-3.5.

In question answering tasks, ChatGPT scores 87.3% on the BoolQ dataset, again surpassing GPT-3.5. This consistent performance across various tasks suggests that ChatGPT is particularly adept at reasoning and factual input, likely influenced by its reinforcement learning from human feedback design during training.

Overall, the results indicate that ChatGPT outperforms several established models in both arithmetic reasoning and natural language tasks, showcasing its strengths in reasoning capabilities and factual comprehension.

The paper presents examples of commonsense reasoning using the COPA framework, focusing on identifying causes and effects based on given premises. Each example consists of a premise followed by two choices, with a step-by-step analysis to determine the most logical conclusion.

1. In the first example, the premise indicates that a boy threw a temper tantrum. The analysis concludes that the cause is unclear between the two choices, as neither is confirmed by the premise.

2. The second example involves a woman reading a newspaper, where the analysis identifies that the effect is that she discovered the election outcome, as it directly relates to the act of reading.

3. The third premise describes a woman banishing children from her property. The analysis finds that the cause remains unknown, as both choices are potential causes but not definitive.

4. In the fourth example, the premise states that a woman retired. The analysis concludes that the effect is that she received her pension, as it directly correlates with retirement.

5. The final example discusses the cost of fixing a computer. The analysis indicates that both choices lead to having a functioning computer but highlights the financial implications of each option.

Overall, the paper illustrates the process of commonsense reasoning by evaluating premises and choices to derive logical conclusions about causes and effects.

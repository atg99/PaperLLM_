The paper presents a series of examples demonstrating how to determine dates using logical reasoning. Each question outlines a scenario involving a specific date, and the answers are derived through step-by-step analysis.

1. In the first example, Christmas Eve of 1937 leads to the conclusion that the following day is Christmas Day, resulting in the answer 12/25/1937.
2. The second question establishes that if the day before yesterday was 11/23/1933, then yesterday would be 11/24/1933.
3. A concert scheduled for 06/01/1943 was delayed by a day, meaning ten days prior would be calculated as 05/21/1943.
4. Jane's egg consumption scenario illustrates that if she bought 40 eggs and ate one per day until she ran out, ten days ago would be 06/08/2017.
5. The same egg scenario is revisited to determine the date tomorrow after running out of eggs, which is 06/19/2017.
6. Lastly, Jane's 16th birthday, falling on February 29, 2016, indicates that ten days prior would be 02/19/2016.

Through these examples, the paper emphasizes the importance of logical reasoning in processing date-related queries, resulting in accurate answers based on the provided information.

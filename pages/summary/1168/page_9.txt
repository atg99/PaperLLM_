The paper discusses various clustering algorithms, specifically focusing on their performance with small and large data sets. It highlights that while four methods are suitable for small datasets, the Canopy algorithm proves to be the most effective for larger and more complex datasets due to its enhanced fault tolerance and noise immunity. The authors plan to conduct experimental verifications using real-world multidimensional data to further assess the strengths and weaknesses of each algorithm and explore potential performance improvements. 

The contributions of the authors are delineated, with Chunhui Yuan responsible for methodology, software, validation, and data curation, while Haitao Yang focused on formal analysis, writing, review, and supervision. The research did not receive external funding, and the authors have declared no conflicts of interest. 

The references cited include various studies on clustering algorithms, including improvements to the k-means algorithm and methods for handling categorical data, underscoring the breadth of research in clustering methodologies.

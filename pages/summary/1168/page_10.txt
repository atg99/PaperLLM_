The paper discusses the use of clustering algorithms, specifically K-means and fuzzy c-means, for analyzing Wikipedia's latest articles in a cloud computing environment. Esteves and Rong present their findings at the 2011 IEEE International Conference, comparing the effectiveness of these algorithms. Additionally, Yu and Zhang explore an FCM algorithm enhanced by a canopy clustering method tailored for cloud settings, as detailed in their 2014 publication. Mccallum et al. contribute to the discourse by addressing efficient clustering of high-dimensional datasets, particularly in the context of reference matching, presented at the ACM SIUKDD Conference in 2000.

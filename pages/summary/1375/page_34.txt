The paper discusses the integration of logic-based technologies within Multi-Agent Systems (MAS), emphasizing their role in fostering intelligence in knowledge-intensive systems. A cloud of keywords highlights central themes such as information, reasoning, and intelligence, which reflect the primary objectives of logic-based MAS technologies. The social aspect of MAS is also underscored through keywords like social, interaction, protocol, and communication, indicating the importance of collaboration and communication in these systems.

Furthermore, the analysis of selected technologies from a MAS and logic perspective reveals that logic is predominantly used in agent programming and their reasoning and planning capabilities. Logic-based languages are identified as effective tools for developing intelligent components due to their inherent features, such as treating programs as logic theories and facilitating computation via deduction. The paper also notes that logic-based technologies are particularly valuable in the verification of agent behavior and social interactions within MAS. This suggests a significant intersection between logic and the operational aspects of MAS, addressing key research questions related to the deployment and effectiveness of these technologies in enhancing agent functionality.

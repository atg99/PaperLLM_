This document consists of a series of references related to the field of Autonomous Agents and Multi-Agent Systems, highlighting various contributions to agent programming languages, model checking, and the integration of social structures and norms in agent organizations. Key works include Dennis and Farwer's introduction of the Gwendolen BDI language for verifiable agents, as well as Dennis et al.'s exploration of model checking in agent programming languages. Denti et al. discuss tuProlog for Internet applications, while Di Stefano and Santoro focus on using Erlang for multi-agent systems. Dignum and colleagues introduce the OMNI framework, enhancing agent organizations with social structures. The dMARS architecture is presented by d’Inverno et al., specifying a distributed multi-agent reasoning system. Various papers also investigate the role of argumentation frameworks, commitment protocols, and electronic institutions within multi-agent systems, showcasing the diversity of approaches and methodologies in this domain. Each reference contributes to the understanding and development of intelligent agents and their interactions within complex environments.

The paper discusses the significance of logic-based techniques in the field of Multi-Agent Systems (MAS), highlighting their historical importance and potential for enhancing human understanding of intelligent systems. Logic serves as a foundation for rational processes, knowledge representation, communication, and coordination among cognitive agents in MAS. The authors argue that these techniques are promising for developing explainable and understandable intelligent systems.

Despite previous surveys on MAS technologies and logic-based approaches, the authors note a lack of systematic reviews that comprehensively cover these topics. This gap is particularly relevant given the increasing public interest and scrutiny in AI research. The paper aims to fill this void by conducting a systematic literature review (SLR) to present a thorough overview of available logic-based technologies in MAS.

The structure of the paper is outlined as follows: Section 2 details the methodology for the SLR, including research questions and technical operations. Section 3 categorizes the identified technologies from a MAS perspective, providing brief descriptions. Section 4 organizes the collected data into tables and word clouds for clarity. Section 5 offers an analysis of the current state and future perspectives of logic-based technologies in MAS. Finally, Section 6 concludes with reflections on the findings.

The authors emphasize the importance of clearly defining key concepts to ensure the review's reproducibility and rigor, adhering to established definitions of agents and MAS to guide their inclusion or exclusion criteria in the survey.

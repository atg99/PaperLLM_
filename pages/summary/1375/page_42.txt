The paper presents statistics on selected technologies within the domain of logic-based multi-agent systems (MAS), highlighting key characteristics and trends. A total of 47 technologies were analyzed, with 25.53% maintained since 2019 and 42.55% since 2018, indicating a significant portion of technologies are relatively recent. Open-source technologies comprise 55.32% of the total, while 40.43% are unlicensed, suggesting a notable prevalence of unregulated software. 

The majority of the technologies, specifically 63.83%, are JVM-based, indicating a strong preference for this platform. Despite the high success rate in running these technologies (65.96%), only 34.04% have a codebase quality rated above 3, and merely 19.15% exhibit similar documentation quality, reflecting a lack of attention to code and documentation standards.

Additionally, the paper outlines the criteria for including technologies in the systematic literature review (SLR), emphasizing that eligible technologies must be clearly defined within the MAS framework, adhere to identifiable logic, and be both scientifically recognized and technically available. A summary of rejected technologies is also provided, detailing the reasons for their exclusion based on these criteria.

This section of the paper outlines the fundamental research questions that guide a systematic literature review (SLR) focused on the role of logic-based technologies in multi-agent systems (MAS). The primary research question is framed as an inquiry into the current role of logic-based technologies in MAS. To facilitate a thorough investigation, the authors articulate several secondary research questions: 

1. Identification of available logic-based technologies for MAS.
2. Examination of how logic-based technologies impact various aspects of MAS technologies and the degree of this impact.
3. Exploration of the MAS abstractions, issues, and features addressed by logic-based technologies.
4. Analysis of the types of logics utilized to develop logic-based MAS technologies.
5. Assessment of the practical applications of logic-based technologies in real-world domains.

The methodology for the SLR involves utilizing two types of electronic sources: general bibliographic databases and specific sources such as conferences and workshops focused on MAS and logic. The authors emphasize the importance of an extensive search through specialized workshops, as these often yield insights not captured in major bibliographic databases. They specifically mention several workshops and events relevant to their research, indicating a comprehensive approach to gathering literature on the topic.

This section of the paper discusses various programming languages and technologies designed for autonomous agents within multi-agent systems (MAS), categorized under "Programming, reasoning, and planning." It highlights languages that incorporate mentalistic notions such as beliefs, desires, goals, and commitments, including ASTRA, AGENT-0, AgentSpeak(L), and others. The text also mentions languages focused on deliberation (e.g., SHOP, AgentSpeak(ER)) and those extending logic programming with additional features like communication and concurrency (e.g., CaseLP/DCaseLP, DALI).

Furthermore, the paper emphasizes the necessity for dependable computational systems in MAS, particularly regarding their verifiability. It introduces the "Agent reliability & verification" sub-category, which discusses MCAPL, a technology oriented towards model checking individual agents.

From a logic perspective, the paper notes that many technologies in the "Agent" category leverage logic programming (LP) to specify agent behaviors and beliefs. The connection between agents and LP has been recognized in the literature, with foundational works establishing LP as a suitable method for specifying, implementing, and verifying rational agents.

The section also provides a brief overview of AGENT-0, a framework for programming MAS, where agents are defined by their commitment rules. These rules dictate actions based on the agent's mental state and communication through message passing, though it does not support complex mental categories like desires or plans.

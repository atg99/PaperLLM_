The paper titled "Logic-based technologies for multi-agent systems: a systematic literature review" by Roberta Calegari, Giovanni Ciatto, Viviana Mascardi, and Andrea Omicini, published in 2020, explores the growing interest in symbolic approaches to artificial intelligence amid the dominance of sub-symbolic techniques. The authors argue that logic-based technologies, often associated with classical AI, could enhance the explainability of intelligent systems, particularly in the context of agents and multi-agent systems (MAS).

The paper emphasizes the historical significance of logic in the development of MAS and posits that revisiting these technologies may lead to advancements in creating more interpretable AI systems. To provide a thorough understanding of the current landscape of logic-based technologies for MAS, the authors conduct a systematic literature review. They assess and discuss the identified technologies from both MAS and logic-based perspectives, highlighting their relevance and potential applications.

Overall, the review serves to underscore the importance of integrating logic-based approaches into the design of intelligent systems, especially as the field of AI continues to evolve.

The paper discusses the integration of symbolic and sub-symbolic techniques in Autonomous Agents and Multi-Agent Systems (MAS), highlighting that while the potential for these technologies is significant, they have not yet been fully explored or implemented. Only a small percentage of surveyed technologies are deemed ready for practical application, primarily due to a lack of testing in real-world scenarios and insufficient integration with sub-symbolic techniques. 

The authors advocate for the need for further research to overcome technological barriers and emphasize the importance of logic-based technologies in MAS. They note that a systematic literature review (SLR) approach, which originated in medical research, can enhance the coherence and reproducibility of surveys in the field of AI. The paper presents an SLR focused on logic-based technologies for MAS, detailing a methodical retrieval and analysis process that resulted in a collection of 271 documents. This approach aims to provide a more reliable and unbiased overview of the current state of MAS technologies, underlining the growing importance of logic-based systems in developing complex intelligent systems.

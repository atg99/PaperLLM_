The paper "Unsupervised Learning of Debiased Representations with Pseudo-Attributes" by Seonguk Seo, Joon-Young Lee, and Bohyung Han addresses the challenge of dataset bias in machine learning, which often leads to models capturing unintended decision rules due to spurious correlations. Traditional approaches typically rely on human supervision for bias mitigation, which is not always feasible.

The authors propose an unsupervised debiasing technique that involves identifying pseudo-attributes through clustering in the feature embedding space without explicit bias attribute supervision. They introduce a novel cluster-wise reweighting scheme that emphasizes minority groups during the learning process, thus improving worst-case generalization performance.

Extensive experiments demonstrate that their method achieves competitive accuracy compared to supervised approaches across multiple benchmarks. The proposed technique effectively enhances unbiased accuracy and reduces reliance on biased correlations, leading to better overall model performance, particularly in scenarios where bias information is not available. The source code for their method is accessible online.

The paper discusses the evaluation of algorithmic bias on the CelebA dataset through various models, focusing on unbiased and worst-group accuracies. It compares the performance of the proposed LfF*BPA method against baseline and Group DRO approaches across multiple target attributes such as attractiveness, smiling, and more. Results show that LfF*BPA consistently achieves higher unbiased accuracy and better handles multiple bias attributes without requiring separate model training, unlike Group DRO.

In ablation studies, the importance of a weighting scheme is highlighted, revealing that both scale and loss factors are crucial for optimizing performance, with the scale factor being particularly significant. The paper also addresses robustness under unspecified group shifts, indicating that the proposed method maintains effectiveness across diverse bias scenarios, further solidifying its utility in mitigating algorithmic bias in machine learning applications.

The referenced works focus on addressing biases in deep learning, particularly in image representation. Wang et al. emphasize that balanced datasets alone do not eliminate gender bias, proposing methods for estimating and mitigating this issue. Zemel et al. discuss the concept of fair representations within machine learning, while Zhang et al. introduce adaptive risk minimization as a meta-learning strategy to handle group shifts. Additionally, Zhang and Sang tackle the accuracy-fairness paradox through adversarial example-based data augmentation for visual debiasing. Zhou et al. provide a substantial dataset, Places, for scene recognition, which can be instrumental in training models that may be affected by biases.

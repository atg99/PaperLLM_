The paper by Zhang et al. (2018) presents a novel approach to style transfer, framing it as an unsupervised machine translation problem. The authors argue that by treating style transfer in this manner, they can leverage existing translation frameworks to effectively transform text while preserving its content. They propose a method that utilizes a dual learning framework to learn mappings between different styles without the need for parallel datasets. 

In contrast, the work by Zhu et al. (2019) introduces the concept of text infilling, which focuses on generating coherent text by filling in gaps within sentences or paragraphs. This method aims to enhance the quality and fluency of text generation by understanding context and maintaining semantic integrity. 

Both papers contribute to the field of natural language processing by exploring innovative techniques for manipulating and generating text, highlighting the potential of unsupervised learning approaches in these domains.

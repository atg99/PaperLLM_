This enhancement allows the model to estimate insertion probabilities without making implicit length assumptions about the sequence being inserted. Traditional autoregressive models struggle to utilize both left and right contexts simultaneously, which the Seq2Seq formulation addresses by treating both contexts as the source sequence and the inserted sequence as the target.

Existing methods, such as those by Ippolito et al. and Zhu et al., have attempted to address these issues but often treat the left context and inserted tokens differently, which affects the accuracy of their predictions. Additionally, their focus has primarily been on generating the inserted sequences rather than enabling post-editing operations.

In the proposed XL-Editor framework, the attention mechanism is modified to compute relative positional encodings uniformly, regardless of the length of the inserted sequence. By treating the remaining tokens as having a length of one, the model can maintain consistent encoding of positional relationships. This approach is expected to enhance the model's performance in post-editing tasks by providing more accurate probability estimations for variable-length insertions. Comparisons between XL-Editor and XLNet on various post-editing tasks are presented to validate the effectiveness of this enhancement.

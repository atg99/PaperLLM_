This paper presents an ablation study on DiffusionDet, a detection model evaluated on the COCO dataset. The experiments focus on several aspects of the model, including signal scaling, ground truth box padding strategies, sampling strategies, and the relationship between training and evaluation box numbers.

1. **Signal Scaling**: The study reveals that a scaling factor of 2.0 yields optimal average precision (AP) performance, surpassing lower scaling factors like 1.0 and 0.1. This finding suggests that a higher signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) is beneficial for the box representation, which is inherently more fragile than dense representations.

2. **Ground Truth Boxes Padding**: Different strategies for padding additional boxes to the original ground truth were tested. The results indicate that concatenating Gaussian random boxes is the most effective method for improving performance, which is adopted as the default strategy.

3. **Sampling Strategy**: The paper compares various sampling strategies and finds that using both DDIM (Denoising Diffusion Implicit Models) and box renewal during the sampling step significantly enhances performance. Without these strategies, the model's accuracy declines with increased iteration steps.

4. **Matching Training and Evaluation Box Numbers**: The study demonstrates that DiffusionDet can effectively evaluate with a flexible number of random boxes. The results show a consistent increase in accuracy with more evaluation boxes, peaking around 2000 boxes. Additionally, the model performs best when the number of training boxes matches that of the evaluation boxes.

5. **Running Time vs. Accuracy**: The evaluation of DiffusionDet's running time indicates that it operates efficiently, achieving comparable speeds to Sparse R-CNN while demonstrating superior performance in zero-shot transfer scenarios.

Overall, the experiments provide insights into optimizing DiffusionDet's performance through careful consideration of scaling factors, padding strategies, and sampling techniques, while also highlighting its efficiency in terms of running time.

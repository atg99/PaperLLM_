The paper discusses the transformation of matrices associated with recurrent neural networks, particularly focusing on the handling of complex eigenvalues and their corresponding eigenvectors. It demonstrates that by applying a specific change of basis, one can simplify the representation of non-symmetric matrices into a real domain format. This transformation results in a structured 2x2 matrix form that mirrors the multiplication of complex numbers, allowing for a more manageable approach to matrix operations. The advantage of using this method lies in its ability to represent complex eigenvalues and eigenvectors succinctly while retaining the necessary mathematical properties for analyzing long sequences in recurrent neural networks. The findings suggest that complex numbers, while not essential, provide a convenient framework for simplifying calculations in this context.

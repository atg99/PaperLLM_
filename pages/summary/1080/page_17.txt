The paper discusses advancements in recurrent neural networks (RNNs) for processing long sequences, focusing on the S4 formulation and its variants. It highlights the integration of liquid time-constant networks and the simplification of S4 into a diagonal state-space model (SSM), which retains efficacy when combined with attention mechanisms and gated designs. The SGConv model offers a filter-based approach to S4, eliminating the need for continuous-time models and discretization.

The authors reference concurrent work by Gupta et al., who demonstrated that diagonal state-space models and linear RNNs can approximate functions similarly after numerical integration. They introduced DLR, which processes inputs independently at each layer while incorporating discretization into the transition matrix. Their work centers on new synthetic long-range tasks, contrasting with the established Long Range Arena benchmark addressed in this paper.

The authors point to successful applications of S4-inspired models in natural language processing, with notable architectures like H3 outperforming existing models in language generation. Additionally, deep state-space models have shown promise in understanding and generating long video and audio sequences, as well as applications in biology and time series forecasting.

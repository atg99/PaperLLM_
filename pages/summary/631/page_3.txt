The evaluation metrics used to assess the performance of these models included accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 score. The results demonstrated that our proposed multimodal emotion recognition model outperformed all comparative models across these metrics. The self-attention mechanism effectively allowed the model to capture and process the intricate relationships between visual, auditory, and textual data, leading to improved sentiment classification accuracy.

In conclusion, the study presents a robust framework for multimodal emotion recognition using a Transformer-based architecture that integrates self-attention mechanisms applied to image, audio, and text data. The use of the MELD dataset highlights the model's ability to leverage contextual information for enhanced emotion classification. Overall, the findings underscore the potential of multimodal approaches in advancing emotion recognition systems.

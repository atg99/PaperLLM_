The paper discusses the integration of emotion-aware interaction design in intelligent user interfaces (UIs) using multi-modal deep learning techniques. It emphasizes the critical role of effective UI design in enhancing user engagement and accessibility. The research introduces an advanced emotion recognition system that utilizes a multi-branch Transformer model to analyze facial expressions, speech, and textual data in real-time, thereby enabling UIs to respond empathetically to users’ emotional states.

Validation using the MELD dataset shows that the proposed model significantly improves emotion recognition accuracy and F1 scores compared to traditional methods. The findings highlight the importance of sophisticated emotion recognition in creating UIs that are more attuned to user needs, fostering deeper connections between users and technology.

The paper outlines various applications of emotion recognition technology, including health monitoring, education, and customer service, illustrating its potential to enhance user experiences by dynamically adapting interactions based on emotional cues. Ultimately, the research underscores that improving emotional intelligence in UIs not only involves technical advancements but also aims to create meaningful interactions between users and digital platforms.

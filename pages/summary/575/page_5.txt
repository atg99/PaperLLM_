The paper discusses various approaches in Deep Transfer Learning (DTL), highlighting their effectiveness and popularity in different applications. The most prevalent method involves fine-tuning a pre-trained model on a related dataset to adapt it for target data, utilized in 21 out of 38 selected studies. This technique is favored for its simplicity and ability to reduce training costs while addressing the limitation of requiring extensive target datasets. However, it is susceptible to catastrophic forgetting.

Another common approach involves freezing the convolutional neural network (CNN) layers of a pre-trained model, fine-tuning only the fully connected layers for classification tasks. This method leverages the feature extraction capability of CNN layers while adapting to new tasks.

The paper also includes a table listing recent DTL publications across various fields, such as medical imaging and malware classification, showcasing the diverse applications of these methods. Key models used in these works include YOLO, VGG16, and Res-Net, among others. The findings emphasize the versatility and effectiveness of DTL techniques in enhancing model performance across multiple domains.

The paper discusses various applications of deep transfer learning (DTL) techniques across different data types, including images and tabular data, as summarized in Table 1. The focus is on methods incorporating convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and their extensions to other deep learning architectures. Notable models like VGG-Net, Alex-Net, and Res-Net are highlighted for their effectiveness, as they are pre-trained on ImageNet datasets, which allows researchers to bypass extensive training times.

Several studies demonstrate the advantages of using progressive neural networks (PNNs) in DTL. For instance, the work referenced in [44] evaluates PNNs for natural language processing tasks, showing improvements in accuracy by mitigating issues like catastrophic forgetting during fine-tuning. Other studies, such as [45] and [46], also report significant enhancements in image and audio classification tasks through PNNs.

The paper further explores adversarial-based approaches, such as those in [47], where conditional generative adversarial networks (CGAN) are employed to augment data for COVID-19 detection from chest X-ray images. Additionally, reference [48] discusses domain adversarial training to extract shared features from multiple datasets.

Lastly, the paper mentions tailored DTL methods for specific tasks, such as the approach described in [49], which utilizes a three-layer sparse auto-encoder for fault diagnosis in mechanical systems. Overall, the research highlights the versatility and effectiveness of DTL methodologies across various domains and data types.

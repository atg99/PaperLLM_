The paper reviews various frameworks for video generation using Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), particularly focusing on models conditioned on semantic maps or addressing missing regions in videos. Table 4 summarizes key publications, specifying the conditional information and main tasks of each model. 

Vid2vid is a foundational framework that generates videos from semantic images, like segmentation masks. Few-shot-vid2vid enhances this by generating videos based on both a target image and semantic information. Other models, such as those by Pan et al. and Chang et al., focus on generating complete videos from single semantic maps or by addressing missing regions using advanced techniques like 3D gated convolutions and self-attention mechanisms.

The paper contrasts different approaches to incorporating conditions in GAN frameworks. Traditional Conditional GANs (CGANs) use concatenation of conditions with noise vectors, while newer frameworks like TFGAN utilize multi-scale text conditioning to create convolution filters that improve the association between text and generated videos. 

StoryGAN is highlighted as a unique model that visualizes narratives based on multiple sentences rather than a single condition. It employs a context encoder and a story encoder to maintain a cohesive storyline, focusing more on global story consistency rather than motion continuity.

Overall, the section emphasizes the evolution of video synthesis techniques, particularly the integration of semantic information and the handling of incomplete data in video generation processes.

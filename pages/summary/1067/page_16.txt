The paper discusses various approaches to video retargeting and pose generation using advanced techniques such as Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and motion prediction networks. Chan et al. introduce a pose detector network that feeds into a GAN framework to enhance realism, while Yang et al. focus on disentangling video representations to create retargeted content. Limitations of datasets are noted, particularly in studies by Chan et al. and Zhou et al., with the former's methods allowing for easier pose translation.

Siarohin et al. and Liu et al. aim to generate videos that replicate actions from input sources, expanding beyond dance movements. A distinction is made between pre-trained and self-supervised frameworks for pose extraction, with Monkey-net utilizing a three-stage process for key point extraction and image synthesis. Liu et al. employ 3D reconstruction for pose generation.

The vid2Game framework employs joystick movements to control human subjects in synthesized images, highlighting the complexity of working with synthesized poses compared to real images. The paper also differentiates between methods focused on individual body motion retargeting and those concentrating on facial expressions and head movements.

Deep Video Portraits and ReenactGAN are highlighted for their ability to modify transferred motion through adjustable parameters, with ReenactGAN noted for its precision in capturing subtle facial changes. Recycle-GAN offers a unique approach by converting frame sequences from one domain to another while preserving the style of the target domain, showcasing the diversity of strategies employed in video retargeting.

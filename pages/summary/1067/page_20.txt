The paper by N. Aldausari et al. discusses the challenges in evaluating and comparing video GANs (Generative Adversarial Networks) due to the variability in input signals and the datasets used across different models. The authors argue that comparing performance is impractical because different GANs may utilize distinct input types, such as images, audio, or additional features like lip landmarks, particularly in applications like speech synchronization. Furthermore, the use of different datasets, including subsets of larger datasets, complicates direct comparisons. The authors highlight the absence of benchmark datasets for video synthesis, suggesting that the establishment of such benchmarks could be a crucial future direction for research in this field.

The analysis presented in the paper evaluates the performance of various machine learning models, particularly focusing on a proposed model, as data volume increases. The results indicate that the Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) model maintains stable accuracy once the data volume reaches 10,000, while the Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) model exhibits inconsistent accuracy, fluctuating between 0.70 and 0.75 regardless of data size. 

Table 3 summarizes the highest accuracy, F1 score, epoch values, and training data for each model, with a fixed data size of 20,000 and varying epochs. The proposed model achieved the highest accuracy of 0.9141 and an average accuracy of 0.9026, outperforming other models in the following order: hybrid, LSTM, CNN, and MLP. The optimal epoch values for achieving the best accuracy were 10 for CNN, 14 for LSTM, 5 for MLP, and 15 for the hybrid model, which required the longest training time. The proposed model also excelled in F1 score, reaching 0.9018, again surpassing the other models.

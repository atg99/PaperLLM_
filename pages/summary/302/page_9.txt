The paper analyzes the performance of various models, specifically focusing on accuracy and F1 score as the number of training epochs increases. The results indicate that the Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) model achieves stable accuracy after 10,000 epochs, while the Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) model maintains an accuracy range of 0.70 to 0.75 regardless of data size. Additionally, the F1 score trends for models other than MLP mirror the accuracy patterns, whereas the MLP model exhibits significant fluctuations without reaching a stable value. This highlights the differences in performance stability between LSTM and MLP models during training.

The paper proposes a hybrid model combining Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) and Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory (Bi-LSTM) networks with an attention mechanism for sentiment classification. The model aims to improve classification accuracy by capturing complex word associations. 

In the sequence embedding layer, the text is preprocessed to create a meaningful sequence of words, and Word2Vec's skip-gram model is utilized for embedding. The 1D CNN extracts low-level semantic features from the preprocessed text, employing convolution kernels of varying sizes to identify unigram, bigram, and trigram features. The convolution process, defined mathematically, captures the relationships between adjacent words while reducing the number of parameters through pooling techniques.

Overall, the study highlights the advantages of integrating CNN for feature extraction with Bi-LSTM for sequential data processing, aiming for enhanced sentiment analysis performance.

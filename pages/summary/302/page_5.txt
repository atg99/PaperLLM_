the authors propose a hybrid model that integrates Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks with one-dimensional Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) to enhance sentiment classification accuracy. The existing LSTM models, while effective in tracking long-term dependencies, struggle with extracting context from future tokens and local context information. This limitation can affect the model's overall accuracy, particularly in recognizing relationships between different parts of a document.

To address these challenges, the proposed hybrid approach leverages the strengths of both LSTM and CNN. The CNN component is utilized to capture local features from the input sequences, while the LSTM component is responsible for managing the sequential nature of the data, thereby improving the model's ability to understand context over longer sequences. This combination aims to overcome the shortcomings of traditional LSTM models and improve classification performance.

The paper discusses various studies that have explored similar hybrid models, highlighting their effectiveness in different natural language processing tasks. Notably, the hybrid CNN-LSTM models have demonstrated superior results compared to conventional deep learning and machine learning methods in terms of precision, recall, F1-score, and accuracy. The authors also reference the emergence of attention mechanisms in recent models, which have further enhanced classification accuracy by allowing the model to focus on relevant parts of the input data.

In summary, the proposed model seeks to improve sentiment classification by integrating CNN and LSTM, addressing the limitations of existing LSTM architectures, and building on the advances made by recent hybrid and attention-based models in the field of natural language processing.

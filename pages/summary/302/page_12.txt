In this paper, a Bi-LSTM + CNN attention hybrid model for text classification is proposed. The model was evaluated using IMDB movie review data and demonstrated superior performance compared to existing models such as CNN, LSTM, and MLP. The results indicated that the proposed model's accuracy improved with the size of the training data and the number of training epochs, addressing long-term dependency issues and mitigating data loss associated with larger datasets. The authors highlight a shift in text classification research towards extracting relevant semantics and features from specialized domains rather than merely focusing on accuracy enhancement. Future work will involve developing new techniques or architectures to overcome the limitations of the current model.

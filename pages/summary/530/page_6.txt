The paper presents results from linear classification experiments using AlexNet and ResNet-50 on two datasets: ImageNet and Places. The performance is measured in terms of top-1 center-crop accuracy, with the highest and second highest results highlighted in bold and underlined, respectively.

For AlexNet, the table shows various methods with their corresponding accuracy across different convolutional layers for both datasets. Notably, the proposed method ODC achieves competitive results, particularly in the conv1 and conv4 layers, indicating strengths in initial feature extraction and deeper representations.

In the ResNet-50 results, ODC also demonstrates strong performance, particularly in the latter convolutional layers, where it surpasses many other methods. The reported mean Average Precision (mAP) for ODC is 78.2%, which is significantly higher than DeepCluster (DC) but still shows a gap when compared to supervised models pre-trained with ImageNet labels.

Additionally, the study explores a low-shot classification setting using VOC2007, where ODC consistently outperforms DC across varying numbers of training samples per class. This suggests that ODC provides robust feature representations even with limited training data, reinforcing its potential for practical applications in scenarios with few labeled examples. The findings highlight the effectiveness of the ODC method in enhancing classification performance across different architectures and datasets.

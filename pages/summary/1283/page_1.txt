The paper emphasizes the significance of feature selection as a crucial step in the dimensionality reduction process. It discusses how the rapid increase in data generated through IoT and web applications presents challenges due to the dynamic nature of data, leading to potential noise and scalability issues in existing feature selection methods. The authors argue that traditional feature selection methods predominantly focus on static data and may not effectively handle the complexities of dynamic datasets, which can hinder algorithm performance and increase the risk of overfitting.

The review outlines various feature selection techniques, highlighting their strengths and limitations in addressing the challenges posed by high-dimensional and dynamic data. The authors conclude that while feature selection can significantly reduce the computational burden and improve model performance, there is a pressing need for the development of more adaptive and scalable feature selection methods that can accommodate the evolving nature of data in the contemporary digital landscape.

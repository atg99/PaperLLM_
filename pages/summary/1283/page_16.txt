Hyperspectral images contain hundreds of spectral bands, allowing for detailed object characterization, unlike standard RGB images. However, the abundance of bands can lead to redundancy, necessitating feature selection methods to eliminate non-essential bands. Gabor wavelet transformation enhances classifier performance but generates excessive features, increasing computational burden. To address this, a multi-task joint sparse representation framework for Gabor cube feature selection has been proposed.

In the realm of intrusion detection systems (IDS), the rapid growth of network technologies has heightened the need for robust security measures. IDS must manage high-dimensional data that is often noisy and redundant, which can impair detection rates and increase computational time. Feature selection methods are crucial for improving detection efficiency. Amir et al. introduced a mutual information-based filter method for IDS, while Chen et al. discussed various feature selection techniques. Literature suggests that hybrid methods are more effective than wrapper methods, particularly for larger datasets, whereas filter methods offer speed but lower accuracy.

Microarray data presents its own challenges, consisting of numerous features with few samples, complicating learning models. Ang et al. reviewed gene selection methods applicable to various learning models, while Mandal and Mukhopadhyay proposed an improved minimum Redundancy Maximum Relevance (mRMR) method that concurrently considers redundancy and relevance. Notably, semi-supervised and unsupervised feature selection methods have shown superior performance compared to supervised models in gene feature selection.

4. Razavi et al. (2019) developed VQ-VAE-2 for generating diverse and high-fidelity images.

16. Vahdat and Kautz (2020) introduced NVAE, a hierarchical variational autoencoder.
17. van den Oord et al. (2016) and subsequent works developed models for audio and image generation.
18. Vaswani et al. (2017) presented the Transformer architecture, emphasizing attention mechanisms.
19. Vondrick et al. (2016) explored generating videos with scene dynamics.
20. Wallace (1992) discussed the JPEG compression standard.
21. Wang et al. (2018) proposed video-to-video synthesis techniques.
22. Wang et al. (2017) introduced non-local neural networks for improved feature learning.
23. Weissenborn et al. (2019) focused on scaling autoregressive video models.

These studies collectively contribute to the understanding and capabilities of generative models, particularly in video and image synthesis, showcasing advancements in architecture, training methodologies, and application domains.

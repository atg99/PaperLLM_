The paper introduces VideoGPT, a novel architecture for video generation that leverages VQ-VAE and Transformer models, traditionally used for image generation, with minimal modifications. VideoGPT demonstrates competitive performance against state-of-the-art GAN-based video generation models. The authors provide detailed ablation studies on critical design choices within VideoGPT, aiming to inform future developments in video generation architectures.

The paper presents the architecture details and hyperparameters for the VideoGPT model, focusing on the VQ-VAE encoder and decoder, as well as prior networks for various datasets. 

In the VQ-VAE section, key hyperparameters include input sizes, latent sizes, commitment loss coefficients, batch sizes, learning rates, hidden units, residual layers, attention usage, codebook sizes, and training steps. The models are tailored for datasets such as Moving MNIST, BAIR, RoboNet, ViZDoom, UCF-101, and TGIF, with specific configurations like 16x64x64 input size for Moving MNIST and 8x32x32 for others.

For the prior networks, similar parameters are outlined, including input sizes, conditional sizes, vocabulary sizes, attention heads, layers, embedding sizes, feedforward hidden sizes, and dropout rates. The training steps for these networks vary across datasets, with BAIR and RoboNet requiring 150K and UCF-101 needing up to 600K.

Overall, the paper details a structured approach to video generation using a combination of VQ-VAE and prior networks, optimizing for different datasets through specific hyperparameter settings.

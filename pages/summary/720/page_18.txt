The paper discusses the analysis of expectations in a certain iterative process, focusing on the performance difference lemma. The first expectation leads to an equality involving value functions, while the second expectation also utilizes the performance difference lemma to derive a relationship between two value functions. By substituting these results into a broader equation, the authors establish a bound on the difference between the value function at iteration k and a target value function. They conclude that the number of iterations required to reduce this difference to a specified threshold is proportional to a constant factor, independent of the dimensions of the problem. Specifically, the result indicates that the convergence rate is determined by parameters related to the process rather than the size of the state and action spaces.

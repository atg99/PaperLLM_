The paper discusses the convergence rates of policy gradient methods, focusing on the inexact Proximal Method of Descent (PMD). It begins with Lemma 13, which establishes conditions for a nonnegative sequence to converge under specific constraints. The main result is Theorem 14, which characterizes the convergence of the inexact PMD method given certain assumptions and step size conditions. It demonstrates that the difference in value functions decreases at a rate proportional to the step sizes and other parameters, ultimately leading to a bound on the convergence rate. The proof utilizes the performance difference lemma and properties of expectations to derive inequalities that support the convergence claims. Overall, the results provide insights into the efficiency and reliability of policy gradient methods in optimization contexts.

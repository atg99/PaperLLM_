The paper discusses properties of a proper, closed convex function and its implications in optimization, specifically within the context of the PMD (Proximal Method of Descent) algorithm. It establishes a lemma regarding a three-point descent property under the conditions of essential smoothness and strict convexity of the function h, which is of Legendre type. 

Key concepts include:

1. **Essential Smoothness**: The function h is differentiable and its gradient norm approaches infinity as sequences approach the boundary of its domain.
2. **Bregman Divergence**: The divergence D is generated by the Legendre type function h, facilitating the analysis of descent properties.
3. **Descent Lemma**: For any point in the relative interior of the domain of h, the minimization of a combination of the function and Bregman divergence yields a new point that remains within the relative interior of the intersection of the domain and a closed convex set.
4. **Application to PMD**: The PMD method generates sequences that demonstrate descent properties, ensuring that the value of the function decreases with each iteration under certain conditions.

The proof provided for the descent property of PMD reinforces the findings of earlier works and outlines the conditions under which the sequences generated by the PMD method converge effectively, maintaining their positions within the defined domains.

This paper presents a performance comparison of various language models, specifically focusing on their instruction-following capabilities. The evaluation involves multiple chatbots, including LLaMA, Alpaca, Vicuna, Bard, ChatGPT, and GPT-4, using a set of 80 unseen questions. The models are rated by GPT-4 on a scale from 1 to 10 based on the quality of their responses.

The results are visually represented in several figures, where relative scores are calculated against strong competitor models like ChatGPT and GPT-4. Notably, the LLaMA model tuned with GPT-4 is assessed under two decoding strategies: one response per question as a baseline and five responses per question, which are then ranked using a reward model.

The findings indicate that models leveraging instruction-tuning with GPT-4 demonstrate competitive performance in unseen tasks, suggesting a viable path for developing advanced instruction-following large language models. The paper emphasizes the consistency of evaluation results across different models, highlighting the effectiveness of automatic evaluation using GPT-4 as a benchmark.

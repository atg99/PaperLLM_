The paper outlines the implementation of human evaluation for model-generated responses using the HHH alignment criteria established by Askell et al. in 2021. The evaluation was conducted through Amazon Mechanical Turk, where participants were presented with two responses from different assistants and asked to assess them based on three criteria: Helpfulness, Honesty, and Harmlessness.

Helpfulness evaluates which response better serves the human's interests, while Honesty assesses the accuracy of the information provided. Harmlessness focuses on the social safety of the responses and their potential to avoid harm to humans. The evaluation interface allows respondents to choose from five options for each question, with the first two and last two options combined for analytical simplicity. This structured approach aims to gather insights on the effectiveness of the model in aligning with human values.

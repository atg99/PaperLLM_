Jocelyn Chanussot is a prominent figure in the field of signal and image processing, holding a M.Sc. degree from the Grenoble Institute of Technology and a Ph.D. from the Université de Savoie. Since 1999, he has been a professor at Grenoble INP, specializing in image analysis, hyperspectral remote sensing, data fusion, machine learning, and artificial intelligence. His academic contributions include visiting scholar positions at esteemed institutions such as Stanford University, KTH, and NUS, and he serves as an Adjunct Professor at the University of Iceland and the Chinese Academy of Sciences.

Chanussot has held significant roles within the IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Society, including founding president of the French chapter and vice-president responsible for meetings and symposia. He has organized key workshops and served as chair or co-chair of various technical committees. His editorial work includes roles as Associate Editor for several IEEE publications and Editor-in-Chief for the IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and Remote Sensing. Recognized as a Fellow of the IEEE and a Highly Cited Researcher, he has received numerous awards for his outstanding papers and contributions to the field.

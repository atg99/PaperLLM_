This design allows for better preservation of features at various scales while mitigating the loss of detail often encountered in traditional U-Net architectures.

The proposed UIU-Net architecture leverages the RM-DS module to address the challenges in infrared small object detection, particularly the low contrast and small size of objects. By maintaining resolution throughout the network, UIU-Net enhances the ability to discern between objects and backgrounds. The interactive-cross attention module further refines this process by focusing on local features, thereby improving the discriminability of infrared small objects.

In summary, the paper presents a novel approach for infrared small object detection through the development of UIU-Net, which integrates a resolution-maintenance deep supervision network and an interactive-cross attention mechanism. This design not only enhances feature representation at multiple scales but also effectively addresses the limitations of existing segmentation methods, resulting in improved detection performance.

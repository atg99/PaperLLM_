IEEE Transactions on Image Processing discusses advancements in small object detection using multi-source data, particularly focusing on the integration of visible and infrared data. The authors highlight the complementary nature of attributes found in these data sources, suggesting that future research will delve into deep learning methods for multi-source data fusion to enhance detection performance. They also intend to investigate factors affecting the model's adaptability to different scenarios, aiming to improve generalization to unknown conditions.

Acknowledgments are extended to various universities for providing specific datasets used in the research. The paper references numerous studies that contribute to the field of infrared image processing and small target detection, emphasizing the ongoing development of techniques such as conditional GANs, tensor decomposition, and convolutional neural networks to tackle the challenges posed by cluttered environments and varying target characteristics.

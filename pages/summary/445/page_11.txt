This paper introduces the UIU-Net, an advanced interactive-cross attention-nested U-Net architecture designed to enhance video object detection capabilities, particularly for small infrared objects. The UIU-Net architecture increases network depth while preserving object resolution and mitigates information loss from small objects during downsampling by not relying on a classification backbone.

The paper highlights the challenges of detecting unknown scenes and objects, particularly in the context of the ATR ground/air dataset, which features diverse backgrounds and varying object sizes and characteristics. Existing methods such as background subtraction and spatiotemporal feature learning have shown promise, but the UIU-Net demonstrates superior robustness and generalization in unseen scenarios. 

Quantitative experiments conducted on both SIRST and synthetic datasets, along with generalization studies on the ATR dataset, confirm the efficiency and effectiveness of the UIU-Net. The findings suggest that UIU-Net offers a novel approach to infrared small object detection, making it suitable for real-world applications. Future research will focus on refining the network to further enhance detection accuracy and efficiency, particularly in complex video sequences.

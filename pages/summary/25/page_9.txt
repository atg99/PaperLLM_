The paper discusses the importance of diversifying classification categories to identify different bone types and tissue materials, particularly in the context of detecting tissue abnormalities using X-ray images. This differentiation is crucial for the medical field, as tissue abnormality detection presents significant challenges. 

In the acknowledgements section, the authors express gratitude to various organizations and individuals who supported their research, including the Centre for Doctoral Training in Data Intensive Science at Durham University and IBEX Innovations Ltd. They also acknowledge the use of specific computational facilities and funding sources that facilitated their work.

The references list includes a range of studies focused on image segmentation and analysis in medical imaging, highlighting advancements in techniques for bone and tissue identification through various methodologies. These references underscore the ongoing research efforts aimed at improving diagnostic accuracy in radiographic imaging.

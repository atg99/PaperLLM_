Asgari et al. introduce ALRESCHA, a lightweight reconfigurable accelerator designed for sparse computations, highlighting its efficiency (2020). Song et al. present GraphR, which utilizes ReRAM to accelerate graph processing, showcasing improvements in processing speed (2018). Zhang et al. propose GraphP, which enhances data partitioning in processing-in-memory (PIM) systems to reduce communication overhead (2018). 

Ham et al. discuss Graphicionado, an energy-efficient accelerator specifically tailored for graph analytics, emphasizing its high performance (2016). Ozdal et al. explore energy-efficient architectures for graph analytics accelerators, contributing to sustainability in computing (2016). 

Other notable works include Greathouse and Daga's efficient sparse matrix-vector multiplication on GPUs (2014), Liu and Vinter's approach to sparse matrix-matrix multiplication (2014), and Ashari et al.'s fast matrix-vector multiplication techniques (2014). Bell and Garland's reports on CUDA implementations for sparse matrix operations (2008, 2009) further signify the evolution of techniques suited for throughput-oriented processors. 

Finally, Hamilton et al. examine inductive representation learning on large graphs, while Xu et al. analyze the power of graph neural networks, contributing to the understanding of machine learning applications in graph structures (2017, 2018).

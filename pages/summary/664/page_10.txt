The paper evaluates the performance and hardware utilization of the 1K-PE Adaptive Weighting GCN (AWB-GCN) across five design variations. The designs include different combinations of distribution smoothing and techniques like remote switching and row remapping, specifically tailored for various datasets such as Cora, Citeseer, Pubmed, Nell, and Reddit. 

Figures 14 and 15 present the overall performance and per-SpMM performance of these designs, highlighting the execution latency and PE utilization metrics. The evaluation compares the AWB-GCN designs against a baseline design without rebalancing techniques. The hardware consumption and operating frequency data were gathered using Quartus Pro 19.4, while cross-platform comparisons were made with implementations on Intel Xeon and NVIDIA RTX 8000.

The results indicate that Design(B), which employs 2-hop distribution smoothing, significantly improves PE utilization compared to the baseline across all datasets. The paper also discusses the breakdown of execution cycles and highlights the impact of various design choices on performance. Overall, the findings suggest that the proposed design choices enhance the efficiency of GCN implementations in terms of both latency and resource consumption.

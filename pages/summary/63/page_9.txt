The paper discusses two models, CNN-RNN(W) and CNN-RNN(S), for predicting crop yield based solely on different types of data. The CNN-RNN(W) model utilizes weather data to capture both linear and nonlinear effects on yield, while the CNN-RNN(S) model focuses on soil data, similarly capturing linear and nonlinear effects, without incorporating weather or management data. Additionally, the paper presents a bar plot (Figure 6) illustrating the estimated effects of soil conditions at various depths and at the surface on corn yield. The vertical axes of the plot are normalized to facilitate comparison across different soil conditions, and it references the national commodity crop productivity index for corn and all crops. The study emphasizes the distinct contributions of weather and soil data to yield prediction.

The paper presents a bar plot illustrating the estimated effects of varying soil conditions on soybean growth, with measurements taken from different soil depths and the surface. To facilitate comparison, the vertical axes were normalized separately for conditions at different depths and those at the surface. The study also references the National Commodity Crop Productivity Index for corn and for all crops, denoted as NCCPIcorn and NCCPIA, respectively. This framework aims to enhance yield prediction through a CNN-RNN approach as detailed in the work by Khaki et al.

The paper discusses the derivation of derivatives related to the optimization problem involving a function Ω(p), which is twice differentiable on the interval (0,1]. The central result is the expression for the gradient of the optimal value function with respect to a variable z, particularly focusing on the support S of the variables. The authors establish that the derivatives outside the support are zero and derive the necessary conditions for the optimality within the support.

The paper further elaborates on the relationship between the Hessian matrix H and the derivatives of the function g, which is linked to the second derivative of Ω. It highlights the importance of the convexity of Ω in ensuring that H is invertible, leading to a specific expression for the derivatives. Notably, the authors point out that their findings are simpler than those in previous works, such as Niculae and Blondel, especially in the case of separable functions, and they extend applicability to scenarios where the second derivative may not exist at certain points.

Additionally, the paper introduces algorithms for computing the α-entmax function, providing a unique threshold τ⋆ that relates to the α-entmax transformation of a vector z. The authors demonstrate the connection between the α-entmax and the regularized prediction functions, establishing a one-to-one mapping between the optimal solution and τ⋆. A specific corollary for α = 1.5 is also provided, confirming the uniqueness of τ⋆ for that case.

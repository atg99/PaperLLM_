The paper discusses the implications of strong morphological inflection results, suggesting their potential utility in various applications, particularly in speech recognition tasks where probability is concentrated in a limited set of hypotheses. The research received support from the European Research Council and the Fundação para a Ciência e Tecnologia, acknowledging contributions from various individuals for their discussions and feedback. 

Key references include works on morphological inflection generation, attention-based models for machine translation, and structured prediction losses for sequence learning. The paper highlights the relevance of attention mechanisms in neural networks, the importance of data augmentation for low-resource tasks, and the advancements in multilingual neural machine translation systems. 

Overall, the findings emphasize the significance of morphological analysis in enhancing computational models across different language processing tasks.

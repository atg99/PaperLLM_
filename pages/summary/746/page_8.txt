The paper highlights the significance of sparse attention mechanisms in improving model performance and interpretability, particularly in sequence-to-sequence frameworks for tasks like machine translation. It discusses the limitations of dense attention and presents entmax as a differentiable alternative to softmax, facilitating easier implementation in training. The authors emphasize the advantages of sparse outputs, which contribute to more precise predictions without the exhaustive search typically required in beam search. Future work is suggested to investigate the integration of entmax in self-attention models and further exploration of sparse attention methodologies. Overall, the study contributes to the ongoing discourse on enhancing NLP models through innovative attention strategies.

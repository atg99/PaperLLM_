The paper presents a mathematical derivation that shows the relationship between two variables, τz(ρ) and τz(ρ+1). By plugging a specific value, x⋆, into the objective function, it is established that the expression /tildewideτ(x⋆) equals τz(ρ). The inequality /tildewideτ(x) ≥ τz(ρ) is demonstrated for any valid x, leading to the conclusion that τz(ρ) is less than or equal to τz(ρ+1). This result provides a foundational proof for the comparison of τz values across different parameters.

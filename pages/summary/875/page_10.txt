This collection of references highlights significant advancements in deep learning and computer vision, particularly focusing on convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and transformers. Key contributions include Krizhevsky et al.'s pioneering work on CNNs for image classification, which laid the groundwork for many subsequent models. LeCun et al.'s early application of backpropagation to handwritten digit recognition showcases foundational techniques in neural networks.

Recent developments such as the introduction of selective kernel networks, focal loss for object detection, and the Swin transformer emphasize the evolution of architectures designed for specific tasks in computer vision. The Microsoft COCO dataset has become a standard benchmark for evaluating object detection algorithms, while various transformer models, including Conformer and Pyramid Vision Transformer, illustrate the integration of attention mechanisms into visual tasks.

The references also discuss innovative approaches like the Faster R-CNN for real-time detection, the Bottleneck Attention Module, and methods for enhancing the performance of vision transformers through the incorporation of convolutions. Notably, the exploration of self-attention in image recognition and the combination of convolutional and transformer architectures illustrate ongoing research aimed at improving efficiency and accuracy in visual recognition tasks. 

Overall, these works reflect a dynamic landscape in computer vision, where novel architectures and techniques continue to push the boundaries of what's possible in visual understanding.

In summary, the paper presents a comprehensive evaluation of RandAugment across several datasets, including CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, SVHN, and ImageNet, demonstrating its competitive or superior performance compared to existing data augmentation techniques. Specifically, RandAugment achieves state-of-the-art results on CIFAR-10 and SVHN while also showing promising results on larger datasets like ImageNet without increasing computational costs during inference. The findings suggest that RandAugment is a robust and efficient solution for improving model performance across diverse image classification and object detection tasks.

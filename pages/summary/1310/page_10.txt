The cited works focus on various aspects of data augmentation and deep learning techniques, primarily in the context of image classification and speech recognition. 

Key contributions include:

1. **SpecAugment**: Introduced by Park et al., this method enhances automatic speech recognition through simple data augmentation techniques.
2. **Effectiveness of Data Augmentation**: Perez and Wang examine how data augmentation improves image classification accuracy using deep learning.
3. **Domain-Specific Transformations**: Ratner et al. propose a method for learning transformations tailored to specific domains for enhanced data augmentation.
4. **Generative Models**: Ravuri and Vinyals discuss accuracy metrics for conditional generative models, contributing to the understanding of model performance.
5. **Generalization of Classifiers**: Recht et al. investigate whether classifiers trained on ImageNet generalize well to the dataset itself.
6. **Augmented Pattern Classification**: Sato et al. present a neural network approach for augmented pattern classification.
7. **Best Practices for CNNs**: Simard et al. outline effective strategies for applying convolutional neural networks to visual document analysis.
8. **Data Generation Techniques**: Sixt et al. introduce RenderGAN for generating realistic labeled data, while other works explore regularization methods and new architectures.
9. **Unsupervised Data Augmentation**: Xie et al. propose a method that utilizes unsupervised data for augmentation, enhancing model robustness.
10. **Mixup and Random Erasing**: Zhang et al. and Zhong et al. present innovative augmentation techniques that blend or erase parts of images to improve model training.

Overall, these studies highlight the significance of data augmentation in deep learning, presenting various methodologies and their impact on model performance across different tasks.

The paper presents an analysis of the impact of various hyperparameters on the performance of detection models using the NSL-KDD and CSE-CIC-IDS2018 datasets. It includes visual representations (Figures 4 and 5) that illustrate the effects of learning rate, number of hidden layers, and batch size on the model's detection rates. The findings indicate that increasing the number of hidden layers generally enhances model performance, with a notable improvement observed as the layers increase from two to four. However, the performance gains tend to diminish with further additions, suggesting a point of diminishing returns in layer depth. This analysis provides insights into optimizing hyperparameters for better detection outcomes in cybersecurity applications.

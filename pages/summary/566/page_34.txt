The Journal of Information Security and Applications focuses on a broad range of topics within the field of information security. It publishes research articles, reviews, and case studies that address current challenges, innovations, and methodologies in securing information systems and applications. The journal emphasizes the importance of practical relevance and aims to bridge the gap between theoretical advancements and their real-world applications. It serves as a platform for researchers and practitioners to share insights, findings, and best practices in the evolving landscape of information security.

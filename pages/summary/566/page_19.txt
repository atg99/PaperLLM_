The Journal of Information Security and Applications published in May 2021 includes various studies and articles focused on advancements in information security. The papers explore topics such as cybersecurity threats, data protection techniques, encryption methods, and the application of artificial intelligence in security measures. Key concepts include the importance of robust security protocols in safeguarding sensitive information, the evolving nature of cyber threats, and innovative approaches to enhance security infrastructure. The research emphasizes the need for continuous adaptation and improvement in security strategies to address emerging challenges in the digital landscape. Overall, the journal serves as a critical resource for researchers, practitioners, and policymakers in the field of information security.

The Journal of Information Security and Applications published in May 2021 focuses on various aspects of information security, including emerging threats, security protocols, and application vulnerabilities. The articles in this volume explore the latest research and developments in the field, providing insights into effective security measures and strategies for protecting sensitive information. Key topics include the impact of new technologies on security practices, the importance of robust encryption methods, and the role of user awareness in preventing security breaches. The journal serves as a valuable resource for researchers, practitioners, and policymakers aiming to enhance information security frameworks and address contemporary challenges in the digital landscape.

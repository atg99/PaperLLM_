The paper discusses the importance of hyperparameter optimization (HPO) in deep learning (DL) models, particularly in the context of intrusion detection systems (IDS). It highlights the challenges of manual hyperparameter tuning, which is often time-consuming and requires expertise. To address this, the authors propose an automated approach that combines grid search and random search techniques to enhance the HPO process. This method aims to efficiently identify optimal hyperparameters, thereby improving the classification performance of DL algorithms in IDS.

The research explores various existing techniques in deep learning for IDS, noting that DL has demonstrated superior performance compared to traditional machine learning methods, especially on large datasets. The paper reviews studies that have modified DL network architectures and employed techniques like autoencoders for feature learning, which have shown promising results in attack detection.

Despite the advancements, the authors point out that many studies still rely on manual tuning of hyperparameters, which can limit the performance of DL models. The paper emphasizes the need for a systematic approach to HPO that can handle both numerical and categorical parameters effectively, ultimately leading to better detection rates in attack classification.

The proposed solution is a novel combination of grid and random search methods for automating HPO, which has not been previously explored in the context of deep learning models with deep autoencoder features. The research aims to provide a more efficient framework for selecting DL structures and optimizing hyperparameters to enhance the performance of IDS.

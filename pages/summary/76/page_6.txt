The paper discusses the predictions of PM2.5 concentration using an LSTM-FC neural network, showcasing results from February 1 to February 28, 2015. The first six subfigures present exact predictions for the next six hours, while the last three illustrate maximum and minimum predictions. The findings indicate significant fluctuations in PM2.5 levels, with a notable drop of nearly 150 mg/m3 in just one hour. Despite these variations, the LSTM-FC model yields satisfactory prediction results, with actual measurements typically lying between the predicted maximum and minimum values. The study also highlights the effectiveness of linear interpolation for managing missing data in the dataset, ensuring that prediction accuracy remains intact. A marked straight line observed around February 27 indicates missing data, which does not affect the LSTM-FC's predictive capabilities. Additionally, prediction errors across various monitoring stations are analyzed, reinforcing the reliability of the model in forecasting air quality.

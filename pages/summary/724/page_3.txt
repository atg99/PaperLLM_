The paper discusses robust policy optimization in Markov decision processes (MDPs), focusing on s-rectangular uncertainty sets, in contrast to previous work that addressed (s, a)-rectangular cases. The authors point out that the existing robust Bellman operator cannot be applied to s-rectangular sets, hindering the transposition of prior analyses. They reference recent developments in robust policy gradient methods (RPG) for general uncertainty sets, which exhibit higher complexity than non-robust methods. Both previous works assume access to an oracle gradient of robust returns, which complicates scalability when this assumption is lifted.

The main contribution of this study is the development of an RPG method that generalizes existing results while being efficient for s-rectangular robust MDPs. The authors provide a comparative analysis of the time complexity associated with various uncertainty sets, showing that their method maintains the same complexity as non-robust policy gradient methods, thus enhancing efficiency.

In the preliminaries, the paper establishes foundational concepts such as notation for finite sets, inner products, norms, and the definition of an MDP. It outlines the components of an MDP, including state and action spaces, transition kernels, reward functions, and policies, setting the stage for the subsequent discussion on robust policy optimization techniques.

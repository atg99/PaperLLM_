The referenced works focus on advancements in optimization techniques, particularly in the context of stochastic gradient descent (SGD) and policy gradient methods. Key contributions include variance reduction strategies to enhance convergence rates for non-convex optimization problems. 

Johnson and Zhang propose predictive variance reduction to accelerate SGD, while Allen-Zhu and Hazan explore variance reduction specifically for non-convex scenarios. Reddi et al. extend these ideas to stochastic variance reduction for nonconvex optimization. The SAGA method introduced by Defazio et al. offers a fast incremental gradient approach for non-strongly convex objectives.

Subsequent studies, such as those by Xu et al. and Papini et al., apply stochastic variance reduction to policy gradient estimation and present improved convergence analyses. Other works investigate global convergence of policy gradient methods, addressing conditions under which these methods can achieve optimality. Notably, Agarwal et al. delve into the theoretical aspects of policy gradient methods, emphasizing optimality and distribution shifts.

The exploration of adaptive trust region methods by Shani et al. and the improvements in sample complexity for actor-critic algorithms by Xu et al. further illustrate the ongoing research aimed at refining the efficiency and effectiveness of reinforcement learning techniques.

Overall, these contributions collectively enhance our understanding of optimization in machine learning, particularly in achieving faster convergence and global optimality in policy gradient methods.

The cited works focus on various aspects of optimization and learning in the context of machine learning. Sutton et al. (2009) explore fast gradient-descent methods for temporal-difference learning with linear function approximation, highlighting efficiency in reinforcement learning. Amari (1998) introduces the natural gradient method, demonstrating its effectiveness in learning processes, while Martens (2014) provides new insights into this method. Bach and Moulines (2013) present a convergence rate for non-strongly-convex smooth stochastic approximation, contributing to the understanding of stochastic optimization. Nemirovski et al. (2009) discuss a robust stochastic approximation approach for stochastic programming, emphasizing its reliability.

Ghadimi and Lan (2012) propose optimal algorithms for strongly convex stochastic composite optimization, establishing a generic framework. Pirotta et al. (2013) address adaptive step-size strategies for policy gradient methods, enhancing performance in reinforcement learning. Cortes et al. (2010) analyze learning bounds for importance weighting, which is crucial for various learning tasks. Kakade and Langford (2002) tackle approximate reinforcement learning, presenting strategies for achieving near-optimal performance.

DasGupta (2011) discusses the exponential family and its statistical applications, while Kullback (1997) provides foundational insights into information theory and statistics. Finally, Duchi et al. (2011) introduce adaptive subgradient methods for online learning, and Kingma and Ba (2014) present the Adam optimization algorithm, both of which have become widely adopted techniques in machine learning. Collectively, these works contribute significantly to the theoretical and practical advancements in optimization and learning algorithms.

The references section includes a comprehensive list of significant contributions to the field of image processing, particularly focusing on object recognition, style transfer, and texture synthesis. Notable works include Boykov and Huttenlocher's Bayesian framework for object recognition, and Gatys et al.'s pioneering methods in image style transfer using convolutional neural networks. Various techniques for fast style transfer, such as those by Huang and Belongie, and adaptive normalization methods, are highlighted. The references also cover advancements in energy minimization via graph cuts, texture synthesis approaches, and perceptual loss functions for real-time applications. This compilation serves as a foundational resource for understanding the evolution and current trends in image processing methodologies.

The paper presents a comparison of various style transfer methods, focusing on the efficiency of the proposed Multi-Scale Transfer (MST) approach. The visual comparison in Figure 10 highlights the performance of MST with K=3 alongside other established methods such as Gatys, AdaIN, WCT, and DFR. Table 2 details the running times of each method when tested on 100 image pairs of size 512x512, using a specific hardware setup (Intel i7-6850K CPU and Titan Xp GPU). 

The results reveal that MST outperforms Gatys and DFR in terms of speed. Even with the use of Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) on the CPU, MST-1 is quicker than AvatarNet and WCT, despite WCT's multi-level stylization requiring more processing time. However, as the value of K increases in MST, the processing time also increases significantly due to the computational demands of the cluster operation on the CPU. The paper concludes that while larger K values yield more robust stylized results, there is no need to select excessively large K, as the trade-off in efficiency may not be justified. Further details on the effects of varying K are provided in subsequent sections of the paper.

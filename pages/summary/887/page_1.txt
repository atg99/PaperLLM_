In the paper "Multimodal Style Transfer via Graph Cuts," the authors Yulun Zhang et al. present a novel approach to image style transfer called Multimodal Style Transfer (MST). Traditional methods often rely on global statistics of deep features, like Gram or covariance matrices, to represent styles. However, these techniques can struggle with complex styles and may fail to accurately capture the nuances of style patterns.

MST addresses these limitations by focusing on the semantic patterns of both content and style images. The authors propose a method that clusters style image features into sub-style components, which are then matched with local content features using a graph cut formulation. This approach allows for a more flexible and precise transfer of style, as each sub-style is effectively rendered onto the content image through a trained reconstruction network.

The paper emphasizes the robustness and effectiveness of MST compared to existing methods, demonstrating its ability to produce superior stylized results. The authors also indicate that MST can enhance the performance of some current style transfer techniques. Overall, the study showcases a significant advancement in the field of image style transfer, offering a more nuanced and effective solution for rendering complex styles.

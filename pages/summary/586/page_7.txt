The training procedure outlined in the paper involves setting a maximum of 30 epochs and using mini-batches containing between 16 to 128 observations, depending on the neural network architecture, with a fixed learning rate of 0.001. Notably, the authors chose not to implement data augmentation for fine-tuning, as their experiments did not show significant performance improvements from this technique.

For image preprocessing, four methods are highlighted as effective prior to feeding images into convolutional neural networks (CNNs): gradient, orientation, Local Binary Patterns (LBP), and Local Ternary Patterns (LTP). The gradient preprocessing involves calculating the gradient image based on local intensity gradients, while orientation preprocessing captures edge directions discretized over the range of 0 to π. 

The LBP method creates a 3D image by combining the original image with two LBP-processed images using specific parameters. In contrast, the LTP approach is a novel transformation that maps LBP images to a three-channel space, designed to be invariant to monotonic photometric transformations. The authors opted to use LTP encoding, resulting in two output images named LTP+ and LTP-. 

Visual representations of the preprocessing methods are provided, showcasing a sample image alongside its processed versions.

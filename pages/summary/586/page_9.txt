In this section, the paper details the training and testing datasets used for experiments involving deep features and CNNs on three Plankton datasets. The datasets include:

1. **WHOI**: Comprising 6600 manually categorized images from Woods Hole Harbor, this dataset is split evenly into training and testing sets, with 22 categories and 150 samples per category.

2. **ZooScan**: This dataset contains 3771 images collected from the Bay of Villefranche-sur-mer, categorized into 20 classes with varying sample sizes. Due to the presence of artifacts, images were cropped before classification, and 2-fold cross-validation was applied.

3. **Kaggle**: A subset from the Straits of Florida with 14374 images across 38 categories. The distribution of samples per class is uneven, and 5-fold cross-validation was utilized.

Additionally, the authors created a further training dataset by combining images from the National Data Science Bowl dataset and the "Esmeraldo" dataset, resulting in 15962 images from 83 classes.

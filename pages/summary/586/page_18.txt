The referenced works focus on advancements in plankton classification through various machine learning and deep learning techniques, particularly utilizing convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and transfer learning. Key studies include the application of deep learning methods to large-scale imbalanced datasets for effective plankton classification, as highlighted by Lee et al. and Dai et al. Other research emphasizes the integration of multiple feature extraction techniques and kernel learning for improved classification accuracy, as seen in Zheng et al.'s work.

Several papers discuss the evolution of CNN architectures and their adaptations for specific tasks in marine biology, including the incorporation of texture and shape information for enhanced classification performance. Notably, Bochinski et al. introduced deep active learning approaches to facilitate in situ plankton classification, showcasing the potential for real-time applications.

The literature also reviews the effectiveness of transfer learning in various scenarios, suggesting it as a viable strategy for addressing challenges in image recognition tasks. Overall, these contributions illustrate a significant shift towards automated and efficient methods for plankton classification, leveraging the power of deep learning frameworks to process complex biological data.

The paper contributes a comprehensive collection of classification models, detailed performance evaluations, and methodologies for combining multiple CNNs to elevate classification outcomes.

Overall, the study underscores the critical role of automated plankton classification in monitoring ocean health and the effectiveness of advanced deep learning techniques in this domain.

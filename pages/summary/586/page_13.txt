The paper discusses the combination of results from various experiments to develop a method that maximizes performance in convolutional neural networks (CNNs). It emphasizes the importance of diversity in training strategies, where training multiple CNN learners with different approaches can lead to decorrelated errors, enhancing overall predictions. The ensemble method Fus_2R + Fus_1R is highlighted as the top-performing approach across three datasets, demonstrating superior results compared to other state-of-the-art methods. Notably, this method does not require specific parameter optimization for each dataset, making it practical for newcomers to the field.

The analysis indicates that while combining different resizing and training strategies can improve performance over a single CNN, fusing all seven models in the study does not yield significant gains, possibly due to insufficient independence among classifiers. Furthermore, fusions involving transfer learning and preprocessing methods did not outperform the Fus_2R + Fus_1R combination. The paper includes a comparative table (Table 6) showing the F-measure results for various methods across different datasets, with Fus_2R + Fus_1R achieving the highest rank. Confusion matrices for this method are presented in subsequent figures for further evaluation.

This paper investigates the use of deep learning techniques and transfer learning to create an ensemble of classifiers aimed at improving classification performance in plankton image datasets. The authors focus on fine-tuning various convolutional neural network (CNN) architectures trained with different strategies, ultimately demonstrating that combining these networks into a heterogeneous ensemble yields better results than individual models. The study evaluates multiple CNN models and training methods, identifying DenseNet as the most effective for the classification task at hand. 

The ensemble approach is validated across three established plankton datasets, showing superior performance compared to existing feature selection and classification methods. The proposed system is noted for its robustness, functioning effectively across different datasets with minimal parameter tuning required. Future work will explore different strategies for layer selection in transfer learning, additional preprocessing methods, and diversity-based classifier selection techniques.

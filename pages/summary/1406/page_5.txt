The paper discusses the architecture and methodology of RT-DETR, which incorporates an efficient hybrid encoder and an uncertainty-minimal query selection mechanism to improve object detection performance. The architecture leverages features from the backbone's last three stages, utilizing Attention-based Intra-scale Feature Interaction (AIFI) and CNN-based Cross-scale Feature Fusion (CCFF) to transform multi-scale features into a sequence of image features.

The uncertainty-minimal query selection addresses the limitations of previous query selection methods that relied on confidence scores to select top K features, which led to suboptimal initialization for the decoder due to high uncertainty in the selected features. This paper proposes a new approach that models the joint latent variable of encoder features, optimizing epistemic uncertainty to enhance the quality of the queries provided to the decoder.

The feature uncertainty is defined as the discrepancy between predicted distributions of localization and classification. The loss function integrates this uncertainty to guide gradient-based optimization, thereby minimizing the uncertainty during query selection. 

The effectiveness of this method is supported by visual analyses of classification and IoU scores on the COCO val2017 dataset, demonstrating improved performance in terms of accuracy and reduced latency. The proposed CCFF significantly enhances both speed and accuracy compared to previous methods, making RT-DETR a robust approach for object detection tasks.

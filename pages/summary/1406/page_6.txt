The paper presents RT-DETR, a real-time object detection model that outperforms existing state-of-the-art (SOTA) models, particularly YOLO detectors, in both speed and accuracy. A key feature of RT-DETR is its effective query selection method, termed uncertainty-minimal query selection, which yields higher quality encoder features. This is demonstrated through a scatterplot analysis, where features from this method (represented by purple dots) show better classification and location accuracy compared to traditional methods (green dots). 

The model supports flexible scaling by adjusting various parameters such as embedding dimensions, number of channels, and Transformer layers, allowing it to adapt to different operational scenarios without significantly compromising accuracy. In experiments, RT-DETR-R50 and RT-DETR-R101 achieve 53.1% and 54.3% average precision (AP) with 108 FPS and 74 FPS respectively, surpassing the performance of similar YOLO models and other DETRs.

The comparisons with YOLO models highlight RT-DETR's advantages, showing significant improvements in accuracy and frame rates while also reducing the number of parameters. Overall, the results indicate that RT-DETR is a robust solution for real-time object detection, combining high efficiency and accuracy.

The paper reviews advancements in attention mechanisms within deep learning, targeting researchers familiar with the field. It highlights differentiable attention, where attention weights are learned alongside model parameters. The authors discuss the application of deep learning in modeling dynamical and complex systems, emphasizing the necessity for attention mechanisms.

Section 2 covers traditional deep learning techniques, noting how deep neural networks combined with GPU capabilities have significantly enhanced tasks like image recognition and machine translation. The paper outlines the structure of feedforward neural networks (FNNs) and the learning process involving gradient-based optimization to adapt model parameters.

In Section 3, the authors detail various attention mechanisms, including seq2seq models, self-attention, and memory networks, underscoring their roles in improving machine learning performance. They provide examples of how these techniques can effectively model complex systems and conclude with a discussion on their implications. The paper also includes a list of abbreviations for reader convenience.

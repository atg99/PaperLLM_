The paper discusses various attention techniques in complex systems, highlighting their operations, use cases, costs, and applications. Key techniques include one-stage attention, several stages of attention, memory networks, self-attention, and combinations of these methods. Each technique aims to integrate components, manage long-term dependencies, and enhance modeling complexity and prediction accuracy. Despite the theoretical benefits of attention mechanisms over traditional neural networks, the authors note that further research is necessary to validate these advantages in complex systems.

The authors, A.H. and J.M.A., contributed to the writing and final version of the manuscript, which was supported by the Spanish Ministry of Science and Innovation. There are no conflicts of interest declared. The paper also includes a list of abbreviations relevant to the discussed concepts, such as DNC for Differentiable Neural Computers and LSTM for Long Short-Term Memory. 

Overall, the study emphasizes the need for continued exploration of attention mechanisms to fully understand their effectiveness in complex system applications.

The paper presents a compilation of significant contributions to the field of semi-supervised learning, highlighting various methodologies and theoretical frameworks that have advanced the understanding of this area. Key works include Zhu and Ghahramani's exploration of label propagation and Markov random fields, which emphasize the integration of labeled and unlabeled data. The literature survey by Zhu consolidates foundational theories and applications, while Zhou and Li introduce a unique perspective through the concept of learning by disagreement. Additionally, the work on harmonic mixtures demonstrates innovative approaches to combining mixture models with graph-based methods. The collection of these studies reflects the evolving landscape of semi-supervised learning, showcasing its practical implications in machine learning and artificial intelligence.

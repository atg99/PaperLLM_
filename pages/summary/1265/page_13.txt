The paper evaluates the performance of five ensemble learning approaches—RandomForest, Extra-Trees, XGBoost, LightGBM, and BoostForest—across 30 datasets, focusing on classification accuracy and regression RMSE. 

In classification tasks, BoostForest consistently outperforms the other methods, achieving the highest accuracy on multiple datasets, with an average accuracy of 0.856 and the top rank of 1.000. Statistically significant wins for BoostForest are marked in the results, highlighting its superior performance in several cases, such as on the SEE and BCD datasets.

For regression tasks, BoostForest again shows strong results, with the lowest average RMSE of 0.483. It outperforms its competitors, which includes significant improvements over RandomForest and Extra-Trees. The average ranks also reflect BoostForest's dominance in regression, with an average rank of 1.600.

Overall, BoostForest demonstrates both high classification accuracy and low regression RMSE, establishing it as a leading ensemble learning method in this study.

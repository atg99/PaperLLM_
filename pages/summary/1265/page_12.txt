The list of figures includes two primary visual representations related to the BoostTree and BoostForest algorithms. 

Figure 1 illustrates the structure of BoostTree and BoostForest. It depicts a BoostTree with four leaves, where the model uses Gradient Boosting Machine (GBM) to train functions at each node. The process involves sorting an input to a specific leaf and calculating the final prediction by summing outputs from node models along the path to that leaf. In contrast, BoostForest utilizes K replicas of the training set through bootstrap sampling to create multiple BoostTrees.

Figure 2 presents generalization performance metrics concerning the number of base learners and their model complexity, averaged from five repetitions of 2-fold cross-validation. It includes average classification accuracies and root mean square errors (RMSEs) across various datasets, with additional details provided in supplementary figures for further insight into the performance across different configurations.

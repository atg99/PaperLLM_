The paper presents a derivation of the loss function for Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), emphasizing its nature as a gain function rather than a loss function during training. The authors detail the process of deriving the VAE loss function and highlight the principles of variational inference, particularly in the context of Gaussian latent variables.

The paper discusses the challenges faced by the pharmaceutical and life sciences industry, particularly the high rate of drug trial failures, which can lead to financial losses and operational downsizing. To address these issues, the industry is increasingly adopting AI technologies to enhance drug research and development. AI aims to identify reasons behind high failure rates and improve the efficiency of discovering new therapies from existing medications, thereby accelerating the development and market introduction of new drugs. This is particularly crucial during pandemics, where rapid drug availability can save lives.

The paper also highlights the role of AI in clinical trials, particularly in patient stratification, which helps identify groups most likely to benefit from specific drugs, improving trial outcomes. 

Additionally, the paper explores the emerging field of 3-D printing in healthcare, emphasizing its potential to disrupt traditional practices. 3-D printing, or additive manufacturing, allows for precise modeling by building objects layer by layer. Although still in early stages, its future applications could include integrating robotics and bioprinted materials into human bodies, showcasing the transformative possibilities of these technologies in healthcare.

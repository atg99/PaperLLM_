The paper discusses the impact of technology, particularly augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR), on various aspects of healthcare. AR gained significant attention with the success of the Pokemon Go app, which showcased the potential of overlaying digital content in real-world environments. Merged reality enhances this experience by allowing users to interact with both digital and real-world objects through headsets and gesture tracking.

In pain management, research indicates that VR can effectively reduce pain perception by immersing patients in virtual environments. This technique is particularly beneficial during surgeries and for managing phantom limb pain in amputees. Additionally, VR serves as a distraction method, as demonstrated by pilot programs where patients use VR headsets during injections, significantly reducing their awareness of the procedure. Overall, the integration of AR and VR in healthcare presents innovative approaches to pain management and patient care.

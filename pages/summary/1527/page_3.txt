The book "Machine Learning and AI for Healthcare" by Arjun Panesar explores the application of machine learning and artificial intelligence in the healthcare sector. It discusses various methodologies and tools that leverage these technologies to improve patient care, enhance diagnostic accuracy, and streamline healthcare operations. Key concepts include data analysis, predictive modeling, and the integration of AI systems in clinical settings. The author emphasizes the potential benefits and challenges of implementing these technologies, including ethical considerations and the need for regulatory frameworks. The book serves as a resource for professionals seeking to understand and utilize machine learning and AI in healthcare effectively.

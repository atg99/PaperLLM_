The paper discusses various connected medicine devices and their applications. Key examples include:

1. **Stress Bands**: These wearables monitor breathing and heart rate to detect tension, promoting mindfulness and relaxation. They include features like fitness trackers and headbands that deliver calming waveforms to the brain.

2. **UV Sensors**: Developed by Northwestern University, these small, wafer-thin sensors monitor UV light exposure, helping to prevent overexposure and related health issues like heatstroke and melanoma.

3. **Smart Tattoos**: Researchers at MIT and Harvard are advancing smart tattoos that use ink reacting to the biochemical composition of interstitial fluid to provide health status indicators.

These devices illustrate the growing integration of technology in health monitoring and management, emphasizing their potential benefits in improving well-being and preventing health complications.

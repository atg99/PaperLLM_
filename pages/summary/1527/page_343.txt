The study highlights the effectiveness of an online program focused on a carbohydrate-reduced diet for adults with type 2 diabetes. It reports an increase in individuals achieving HbA1c levels below 6.5% from 26% to 50%. However, pharmacotherapy for glycemic control may lead to weight gain and hypoglycemic risks. The ACCORD study indicated that intensive hypoglycemic therapy could increase mortality without significantly reducing major cardiovascular events. A limitation of the program was that only 52.8% completed all modules, though 70.8% reported 12-month outcomes, potentially influenced by seasonal factors. Overall, for those who fully engaged, the program was effective in improving glycemic control, facilitating weight loss, and decreasing reliance on hypoglycemic medications.

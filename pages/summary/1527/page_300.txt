The paper discusses the critical issue of medication adherence, highlighting that over 50% of prescribed medications are not taken as directed, leading to serious health consequences. A study indicates that improved adherence could prevent approximately 125,000 deaths annually in the U.S. To address this, technology such as medication reminder apps and smart pills with ingestible sensors has emerged. These smart pills notify healthcare providers when a medication is consumed, allowing for better monitoring of treatment effectiveness.

Additionally, the paper emphasizes the growing importance of accessible diagnostic tests for individuals with chronic conditions and the elderly. Portable devices equipped with smart sensors enable users to conduct tests at home, with results shared wirelessly with healthcare teams. This innovation facilitates quicker treatment and reduces healthcare costs. The paper also mentions advancements in wearables, such as the Apple Watch, which can predict conditions like Type 2 diabetes through heart rate variability. Overall, these technological advancements aim to enhance patient care and optimize health outcomes.

The paper discusses various applications of wearable technology across different domains. Key categories include immersive technology, military apparel, and health monitoring devices. 

Immersive technology is used in mixed reality for education and behavior change. Military apparel includes helmets designed for enhanced intelligence communication. Health-related wearables encompass smart contact lenses for monitoring blood glucose, hearing aids, and sound-tracking headphones. Other innovations include odor detection devices and smart tattoos that function as trackers or patches.

Smartwatches serve multiple health purposes, tracking blood glucose, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, and ketone levels, and are utilized in education and rehabilitation. Clothing equipped with chest straps and implantables aids in health tracking, while exoskeletons provide body rehabilitation and protection. Embedded footwear focuses on health metrics and posture correction, further emphasizing the role of wearable technology in rehabilitation.

The paper discusses the potential of utilizing blockchain technology in healthcare to create a token-based reward system for positive health behaviors. Patients could earn tokens for activities such as exercising, attending educational sessions, and adhering to treatment plans, which could be used as a tradable currency or saved in health token accounts for transactions within healthcare facilities.

Additionally, the paper highlights the growing demand from patients for access to their health records and the challenges associated with sharing sensitive medical data securely. Implementing blockchain can address these challenges by providing cryptographic assurance of data integrity and privacy. Each health record transaction would be secured with digital signatures and timestamps, allowing for a comprehensive and immutable patient health record. This decentralized approach ensures that all stakeholders can access and verify data without concerns over data manipulation, while maintaining an auditable trail of all transactions.

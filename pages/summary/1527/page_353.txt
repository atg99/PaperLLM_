The Diabetes.co.uk community forum is the largest global support network for individuals with diabetes, including patients, caregivers, and healthcare professionals. It facilitates discussions where members share personal experiences, resulting in high patient engagement, with over 2 million years of cumulative experience and an average visit duration of over 17 minutes. The forum serves as a vital resource for insights into diabetes management, with data shared among various stakeholders.

A study conducted by Royal Holloway, University of London in 2017 explored the impact of online health communities on patient self-management. It focused on how patients utilize knowledge from these communities and how healthcare professionals perceive this information for clinical innovation. The study found that the Diabetes.co.uk forum acts as a catalyst for innovation and empowers patients by fostering strong relationships with healthcare professionals, enhancing self-efficacy in managing diabetes, and reducing emotional burdens.

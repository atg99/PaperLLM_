The paper discusses the development of a machine learning-based system for the automatic detection of diabetic foot concerns, aimed at improving patient outcomes and communication with healthcare teams. Utilizing image recognition technology, the system can accurately identify early signs of foot issues, such as bruising and cracked heels, which may lead to ulceration if not addressed promptly. 

A cloud-based platform allows users to upload images through any internet-enabled device, generating a list of detected concerns along with suitable referral pathways and educational resources. The model continuously improves through a machine learning feedback loop that incorporates new data from users.

The project's objectives include further refinement and validation of the technology, particularly the deep learning neural networks that assess foot health risks. The emphasis is on achieving high accuracy and reducing errors, supported by grading schemes and standardized protocols. Ultimately, the aim is to enhance early diagnosis and treatment of foot concerns, potentially reducing ulceration healing times and the risk of amputations.

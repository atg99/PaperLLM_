The paper discusses the emergence of smart implantable devices that connect with smartphones and healthcare networks to monitor and treat diseases in real time. These devices, along with wearables, can collect vital biological metrics, enhancing patient care. It also highlights the evolution of telemedicine, which dates back to the 1900s, into digital health and therapeutics. This modern approach integrates digital and genomic technologies to provide personalized medicine, improving healthcare efficiency.

The proliferation of health and wellness apps, with over 150,000 available and downloaded by more than 50 million users, empowers patients to actively manage their health. As healthcare increasingly focuses on chronic diseases, behavioral therapy is becoming a preferred first-line treatment over pharmaceuticals. Digital health tools are applied in various conditions, enabling rapid changes in healthcare delivery. Examples include digital health apps for weight loss prior to bariatric surgery, connected inhalers for asthma management, and seizure management apps for epilepsy patients.

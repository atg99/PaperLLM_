The paper discusses a behavior change program designed to help participants achieve self-selected health goals through an online platform. Participants are encouraged to set "Action Points" at the end of each module, which are supported by downloadable resources such as information sheets and recipes. The platform includes digital tools for self-monitoring various health metrics, including blood glucose, blood pressure, mood, sleep, food intake, and body weight. Weekly automated feedback is provided via email, and participants are notified when new modules are available.

The program emphasizes regular communication with healthcare providers for medication adjustments during the first ten weeks. After completing the modules, participants retain access to educational content and tracking tools. The program is grounded in research, particularly the effectiveness of goal setting in behavior change, and prompts participants to reflect on their health improvements as they work towards their goals. Integrated tracking encourages users to monitor their progress, aligning with control theory principles that highlight the importance of evaluating goal attainment.

The paper discusses the role of telemedicine and digital health in modern healthcare, highlighting various applications such as video conferencing, electronic health records, and remote monitoring. These tools primarily address minor primary care issues, allowing healthcare professionals to proactively manage health concerns before symptoms arise. Performance metrics for digital health focus on patient convenience, health outcomes, quality of care, and long-term user engagement.

Digital health also presents a significant opportunity for patient education, particularly for chronic conditions like diabetes, where traditional education methods see limited engagement. Research indicates that offline education benefits diminish shortly after interventions end, necessitating ongoing support for lifestyle changes. In contrast, digital education is accessible, engaging, and can be personalized through real-time data exchange, fostering a patient-centered approach. Digital therapeutics empower patients by equipping them with the necessary knowledge and skills to manage their health effectively.

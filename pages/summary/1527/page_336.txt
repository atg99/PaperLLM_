The paper discusses the governance and ethical considerations in a project utilizing machine learning for healthcare, specifically in predicting foot ulceration risks. It highlights the importance of maintaining a governance trail for decision-making, ensuring data integrity, and facilitating reflective learning. The project faced challenges in balancing model performance with transparency, as complex neural networks often yield unexplainable outcomes. Ethical governance procedures were established to address data ethics, focusing on the implications of false positives and true negatives, which are critical in healthcare settings. The paper emphasizes the necessity of informing healthcare professionals about the model's limitations and the reliance on validation datasets to support informed decision-making.

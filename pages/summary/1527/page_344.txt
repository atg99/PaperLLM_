The case study discusses the development of a scalable and engaging digital therapy for epilepsy, led by Charlotte Summers from Diabetes Digital Media. Epilepsy is a complex neurological condition characterized by seizures, often managed through antiepileptic drugs (AEDs), which may have side effects. While epilepsy is typically not curable, many individuals can control their seizures effectively.

The paper highlights the ketogenic diet (KD) as a significant alternative treatment. The KD is a high-fat, low-carbohydrate diet that has been shown to have positive therapeutic effects on human physiology, particularly in managing epilepsy. The origins of the KD's metabolic effects date back to the 1960s, while its therapeutic use can be traced to the 1920s. This evidence base supports the implementation of digital therapies that leverage dietary interventions for epilepsy management.

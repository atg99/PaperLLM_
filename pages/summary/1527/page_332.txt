Cognitive vision, or image recognition, is a machine learning approach that emulates human visual processing. It involves training models on large datasets to identify visual components and classify images. Neural networks are commonly used for this purpose, but they require significant computational resources, especially for larger images. For example, a 1,000-pixel by 1,000-pixel image generates a vast number of inputs, making processing expensive.

Convolutional neural networks (CNNs) address this challenge by leveraging the spatial relationships between nearby pixels, which reduces the number of connections and computational load compared to traditional neural networks. This method enhances efficiency in image recognition tasks.

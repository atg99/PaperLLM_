The paper evaluates case studies focused on precision medicine, emphasizing machine learning and data-driven models that are currently in production. The criteria for selection included the project's focus on precision medicine, existing deployment, learnings from the projects, and adequate information on their impact. The chosen case studies illustrate real applications in healthcare, including:

1. AI for Imaging of Diabetic Foot Concerns aimed at improving morbidity and mortality.
2. A longitudinal study on a digitally delivered low-carbohydrate self-management program for Type 2 diabetes.
3. A scalable digital therapy for epilepsy.
4. The use of augmented and virtual reality to enhance learning outcomes for junior doctors.
5. An exploration of patient risk diagnosis using big data, highlighting ethical considerations.

These studies provide insights into organizational, business, clinical, and financial impacts of implementing advanced technologies in healthcare.

The adoption strategy for the project emphasized stakeholder engagement through workshops that addressed concerns and showcased the efficacy of a cloud-based predictive system for detecting diabetic foot issues. This system integrates diverse data types, including clinical, demographic, behavioral, and genomic information, to create a precision-medicine framework. Healthcare professionals reported increased confidence in diagnosing foot concerns when using the digital tool, which also facilitated better decision-making.

The findings suggest that having extensive data is more critical than the algorithm used. Challenges remain in text digitalization and label recognition, particularly due to handwriting and human error, which can lead to false recall. The next step involves conducting a large-scale clinical trial, pending funding approval, to further assess the project's feasibility.

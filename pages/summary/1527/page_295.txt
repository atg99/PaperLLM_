The 272smart insulin patch is an innovative device designed for individuals with type 1 diabetes. It utilizes micro-needles to automatically detect elevated blood glucose levels and administer insulin using live beta cells stored externally, eliminating the risk of immune rejection. 

The paper also discusses the evolving role of patients in healthcare, highlighting the trend of individuals turning to online resources for health information. While this has led to more informed patients who engage actively in their healthcare, it can also result in anxiety and hypochondria. The importance of professional healthcare assessments is emphasized, as qualified providers offer comprehensive evaluations based on current evidence. 

A survey by Diabetes.co.uk in 2018 revealed that 87% of patients felt their relationship with healthcare professionals improved due to increased engagement, and 75% reported a better understanding of their condition. This marks a significant improvement from a 2015 survey, where only 20% noted enhanced relationships with their healthcare team.

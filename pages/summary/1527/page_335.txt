The paper discusses challenges encountered during a project related to machine learning model development, focusing on data quality and governance. 

Key challenges identified include:

1. **Quality of Data**: The effectiveness of any machine learning model is heavily reliant on the quality of the data used. Issues such as unlabelled data, illegible handwritten notes, and incomplete medical documentation hindered the data validation process. These challenges prompted the establishment of robust data governance processes to ensure the dataset could serve as a reliable source of truth.

2. **Data Governance**: The project required adherence to national and international regulatory standards, necessitating transparent data governance. This involved creating standards and best practices for data architecture, management, privacy, security, and validation. A centralized repository was implemented to manage all data and metadata, with access restricted to authorized users to ensure proper data management and compliance.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the critical importance of data quality and governance in the successful development of machine learning algorithms.

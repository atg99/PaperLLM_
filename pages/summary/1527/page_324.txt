The paper discusses the evolution of smart homes and their potential to enhance patient and population health through real-time monitoring and personalized healthcare solutions. It illustrates various examples of smart devices that can assist individuals in managing their health, such as wearable sleep monitors, smart toothbrushes that assess hydration, and insulin-dosing coffee makers. Other innovations include smart fridges that track food safety and allergens, smartphone apps for nutritional information, and 3-D printers for at-home pharmaceuticals. Virtual assistants and smart mirrors can monitor mental health and physical symptoms, facilitating timely medical interventions. The paper emphasizes the importance of context and local activity when scaling smart technologies. An example from Bolzano, Italy, highlights a project utilizing sensors to ensure the safety of aging individuals at home, focusing on monitoring environmental factors. Overall, the paper presents a vision of a future where integrated smart technologies significantly improve health management and patient care.

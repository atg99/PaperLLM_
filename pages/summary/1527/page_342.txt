The study examined the impact of a program designed to improve health outcomes for individuals with type 2 diabetes through enhanced progress monitoring. Out of 1,000 participants, 708 reported outcomes after 12 months, with 672 completing at least 40% of the lessons and 528 completing all lessons. Among participants with an initial HbA1c at or above 6.5%, 26.2% achieved a reduction below this threshold without using glucose-lowering medications. Additionally, 40.4% of those on hypoglycemic medications reduced their usage, and 46.4% of all participants lost at least 5% of their body weight. Significant improvements were noted for those completing all 10 modules, particularly for participants with higher baseline HbA1c, who saw reductions from 9.2% to 7.1% and an average weight loss of 6.9%. The study noted that the engagement platform was web-based only, suggesting that a mobile app could enhance participant engagement. Inclusion criteria required participants to be over 18 and English speakers.

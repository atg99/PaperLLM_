The paper discusses the emerging applications of virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR) in healthcare. It highlights the potential for surgeons to use AR to overlay CT scans on patients, medical students to explore the heart in VR, and burn victims to experience virtual environments for pain relief. While VR has primarily been associated with gaming, it is now being integrated into medical practices, suggesting a transformative impact on healthcare delivery. VR immerses users in a fully digital environment, typically through headsets and sensors, while AR enhances the real-world experience by overlaying digital information onto it. Both technologies are increasingly being adopted in various medical applications, indicating a significant shift in healthcare services.

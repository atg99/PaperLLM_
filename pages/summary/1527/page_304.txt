Wellness programs have evolved from encouraging basic healthy behaviors to offering immersive, digitally accessible tools that empower individuals to make significant lifestyle changes for better health outcomes. These programs now incorporate therapy, coaching, and biometric feedback, making wellness more patient-centered. 

Artificial intelligence (AI) has begun to play a crucial role in healthcare, with applications in disease prediction, cost reduction, and efficiency improvements. AI aims to enhance personal health management and is increasingly capable as data volume and diversity grow, although it raises ethical concerns.

Mining healthcare information systems presents an opportunity to leverage extensive patient data to enhance healthcare quality, reduce costs, and minimize errors. This data-driven approach can democratize healthcare access and improve overall patient outcomes.

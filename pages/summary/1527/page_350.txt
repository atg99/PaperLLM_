The paper discusses the integration of virtual reality (VR) simulations into medical education, highlighting their effectiveness in providing a safe clinical environment for junior doctors. Using 360-degree videography, short simulations featuring decision-making moments were created, allowing students to engage actively with clinical scenarios, such as managing cardiac arrest and handling psychiatric emergencies. The conclusions emphasize that VR technology facilitates learning anytime and anywhere, enhances students' focus on mistakes, promotes active decision-making during lectures, and encourages collaborative discussions among peers. Overall, VR simulations are accessible to all medical students with the necessary hardware, unifying educational approaches across the cohort.

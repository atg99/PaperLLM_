The paper evaluates the 1-year outcomes of a digitally delivered Low-Carb Program (LCP) aimed at glycemic control and weight loss in adults with type 2 diabetes. The LCP is a 10-session educational intervention that emphasizes carbohydrate restriction through behavioral techniques like goal setting, peer support, and self-monitoring. The study utilized a quasi-experimental design with a sample of 1,000 participants, randomly selected from those who completed baseline assessments. Key demographics included a mean age of 56.1 years, with 59% women, an average HbA1c of 7.8%, and an average body weight of 89.6 kg. The program is fully automated, providing weekly nutrition-focused modules that guide participants in reducing their carbohydrate intake to under 130 grams. The findings suggest that online programs can effectively support dietary interventions for individuals with type 2 diabetes.

The paper discusses the transformative impact of data on artificial intelligence (AI), particularly in healthcare over the past five years. AI applications have evolved to not only predict and diagnose diseases but also to manage and prevent them, leading to the development of digital therapeutics. Advances in image recognition, particularly through deep neural networks, have shown significant success, often surpassing human capabilities in visual tasks. The concept of machine learning, introduced by Arthur Samuel in 1959, is highlighted as a subset of AI that allows computers to learn from data without explicit programming. This learning is rooted in pattern recognition and includes various techniques like Bayesian methods and neural networks.

The paper discusses the challenges of knowledge discovery in data systems due to variations in complexity and standardization. It highlights the role of data mining in extracting relevant information from both textual and image-based archives, enabling the discovery of patterns for predictive modeling. Unsupervised learning is emphasized for feature extraction, while supervised learning is suited for predictive tasks. Mining health records can identify high-risk patients and inform personalized interventions, helping providers optimize treatment practices and reduce hospital admissions.

Additionally, the paper covers the emergence of conversational AI, which allows users to interact with systems through voice rather than text. This technology is gaining traction, with significant adoption rates among consumers. The development of AI chatbots is leading to their use as healthcare assistants, particularly benefiting individuals with disabilities who can perform tasks using voice commands. Overall, conversational AI has the potential to enhance patient engagement and streamline healthcare interactions.

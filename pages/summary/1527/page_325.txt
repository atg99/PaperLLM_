The paper discusses the concept of smart hospitals, which leverage technology to enhance clinical excellence, supply chain efficiency, and patient experience. Smart hospitals utilize a continuous learning ecosystem that incorporates electronic health records, digital technology, robotics, 3-D printing, and advanced analytics. This technological integration allows for greater patient involvement in treatment decisions, with non-emergency consultations conducted online and AI facilitating the matching of patients to appropriately skilled doctors.

Upon hospital admission, patients are equipped with clinical-grade wearables that monitor vital signs, with data transmitted to a medical team dashboard for real-time monitoring and anomaly detection. The paper highlights the potential for hospitals to act as data hubs, collaborating with businesses to improve operational efficiency and treatment outcomes. Furthermore, healthcare is envisioned as a service, with patients encouraged to adopt healthy lifestyles through cryptocurrency incentives. Data collected is anonymized and shared with digital health partners for ongoing learning and improvement in patient care.

The paper discusses the significant cost-saving potential of digital health innovations for the NHS, particularly in managing epilepsy through a Ketogenic Diet (KD). Empowering patients to manage their condition leads to improved health outcomes, reduced medication use, and fewer hospital admissions, ultimately benefiting the UK economy and healthcare budget. The project aims to assess the one-year outcomes of a digitally delivered Ketogenic Program, consisting of 16 educational sessions designed to help patients control epilepsy. The anticipated benefits extend beyond individual patients to positively impact communities, businesses, and the healthcare system by reducing absenteeism and improving overall quality of life.

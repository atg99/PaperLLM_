The platform serves as a comprehensive data collection tool, gathering extensive metrics on patient behavior and engagement from the moment they access it. Patients register by providing over 120 variables related to their diabetes and health status. The platform collects both unstructured data, such as conversations and interactions, and structured data from self-reports, wearable devices, and clinical records. It offers various services to patients and bill payers.

A key focus is on medication adherence, efficacy, and burden. Patients with diabetes typically enter the platform on at least one medication and regularly evaluate their medications, sharing insights on real-world usage, side effects, adherence, and overall burden. This information is geographically mapped and aggregated, allowing pharmacological and academic partners to use it for research on drug interactions, adherence, and adverse effects. Additionally, the platform enhances patient safety by alerting users about issues or recalls related to their medications or devices.

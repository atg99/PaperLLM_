Data science is defined as a discipline focused on data cleansing, extraction, preparation, and analysis, aimed at deriving insights from data. Coined by William Cleveland in 2001, the term emphasizes the integration of statistics and computer science. AI projects invariably involve data, and in scenarios with big data, real-time analytics become crucial. A data scientist or engineer plays a vital role in managing and interpreting data, building models, and communicating insights to stakeholders. While data science is essential for big data, it can also be applied to smaller datasets.

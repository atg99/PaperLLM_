The paper discusses the transformative role of artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning in healthcare, emphasizing that while AI will not replace healthcare professionals, it significantly enhances diagnostics, outcome predictions, and personalized care. Key applications include the integration of AI in clinical decision-making, where doctors can access the latest evidence-based information to aid in diagnosing and treating conditions. Additionally, AI assists radiologists by identifying subtle issues in imaging that may be overlooked. Wearable technology also plays a role by continuously monitoring vital signs, contributing to a more comprehensive understanding of patient health. Overall, AI is reshaping the patient-healthcare professional relationship through data-driven insights.

The paper discusses the advancements in inpatient care and companionship through robotics and drones. It highlights the potential of robots to automate tasks like mail collection, blood delivery, and room disinfection, thereby enhancing efficiency and minimizing human risk. Future developments may include ingestible agents and robotic nurses capable of performing medical tasks with greater accuracy than humans.

Additionally, the paper emphasizes the role of virtual assistants in providing companionship, particularly for the elderly and those with mental health issues. As robots evolve, they will take on more healthcare responsibilities, including patient monitoring through IoT devices.

Drones are also presented as a transformative tool in healthcare delivery, particularly in remote or conflict-affected areas. They can transport medications, vaccines, and diagnostic tools efficiently, addressing the needs of hard-to-reach populations. Overall, the integration of these technologies is poised to significantly improve patient care and healthcare accessibility.

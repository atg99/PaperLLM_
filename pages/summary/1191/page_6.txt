The paper presents an evaluation of CoMatch, a semi-supervised learning method, alongside various baseline methods on two datasets: CIFAR-10 and STL-10, as well as on ImageNet. The results, summarized in tables, indicate that CoMatch consistently outperforms other methods across different label fractions.

In the CIFAR-10 and STL-10 evaluations, CoMatch demonstrates significant improvements over FixMatch, particularly achieving an accuracy of 87.67% on 20 labeled examples and 93.97% on 80 labeled examples. Notably, it improves FixMatch with data augmentation by 13.27% on STL-10.

For the ImageNet dataset, CoMatch is tested with 1% and 10% of labeled images, achieving a top-1 accuracy of 66.0% with 1% of labels, outperforming baseline methods such as FixMatch and self-supervised pre-training techniques. The implementation details are provided, including the use of a ResNet-50 model and specifics on training parameters.

Overall, CoMatch not only demonstrates superior performance but also does so with a reasonable number of model parameters compared to other approaches, highlighting its efficacy in semi-supervised learning tasks.

The references cited in this paper encompass a diverse range of studies focused on time series classification and forecasting, utilizing various methodologies including deep learning and convolutional neural networks. Key contributions include:

1. Baydogan (2019) provides datasets for multivariate time series classification.
2. Cai et al. (2020) introduce the Traffic Transformer, aimed at capturing continuity and periodicity in time series for traffic forecasting.
3. Cui et al. (2016) explore multi-scale convolutional neural networks for time series classification.
4. Devlin et al. (2018) present BERT, which pre-trains deep bidirectional transformers for language understanding, applicable to time series data.
5. Ismail Fawaz et al. (2019) conduct a comprehensive review of deep learning techniques for time series classification.
6. Karim et al. (2017) develop LSTM fully convolutional networks for this purpose.
7. Keogh (2002) discusses exact indexing methods for dynamic time warping.
8. Le Guennec et al. (2016) focus on data augmentation techniques using convolutional neural networks.
9. Li et al. (2019) enhance locality and address memory bottlenecks in transformers for forecasting tasks.
10. Liu and Wang (2016) investigate the encoding of temporal Markov dynamics in graphs for time series analysis.
11. Maaten and Hinton (2008) provide a method for visualizing data using t-SNE.
12. Oh et al. (2018) utilize sequence transformer networks to exploit invariances in clinical time series data.
13. Rußwurm and Körer (2020) apply self-attention mechanisms for classifying raw optical satellite time series.
14. Serrà et al. (2018) work towards a universal neural network encoder for time series.
15. Tanisaro and Heidemann (2016) propose time warping invariant echo state networks for classification.
16. Vaswani et al. (2017) introduce the attention mechanism foundational to many modern models.
17. Wang et al. (2017) present a strong baseline for time series classification using deep neural networks.
18. Yang et al. (2015) apply deep convolutional neural networks to multichannel time series for human activity recognition.
19. Zhao et al. (2017) explore convolutional neural networks for time series classification.
20. Zheng et al. (2016) exploit multi-channel deep convolutional networks for multivariate time series classification.

These references collectively highlight advancements in methodologies and applications for time series analysis, emphasizing the impact of deep learning techniques.

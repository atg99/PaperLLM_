The paper presents Gated Transformer Networks (GTN), a novel approach for multivariate time series classification, leveraging the strengths of Transformer Networks. Traditional deep learning methods, such as convolutional networks and LSTMs, have been widely applied in time series analysis across various fields like healthcare and finance. However, multivariate time series data, which contains richer information through correlations among multiple channels, poses unique challenges that have not been fully addressed by existing models.

GTN introduces a gating mechanism that integrates two Transformer towers, targeting channel-wise and step-wise correlations separately. This design allows GTN to effectively capture the complex relationships inherent in multivariate time series data. The authors conducted extensive experiments on thirteen datasets, demonstrating that GTN competes favorably with state-of-the-art deep learning models while providing interpretability through attention maps.

The study highlights the potential of Transformer Networks in multivariate time series classification, establishing a strong baseline for future research in this area. The contributions of the paper include the introduction of GTN and a comprehensive evaluation of its performance compared to traditional and contemporary methods in the field.

In summary, the Gated Transformer Network (GTN) shows competitive performance compared to existing models, with specific enhancements through its two-tower architecture and masking techniques. The ablation study highlights the importance of both channel-wise and step-wise processing, suggesting that their combination, when optimized with gating mechanisms, significantly improves model performance. The attention visualization further supports the effectiveness of GTN in capturing relevant temporal and channel correlations in various datasets.

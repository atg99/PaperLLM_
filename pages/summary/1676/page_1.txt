This method first learns a context latent vector that encapsulates local dynamics, followed by predicting the next state based on this vector. A unique loss function is introduced to enhance the utility of the context latent vector for both forward and backward dynamics predictions. The proposed context-aware dynamics model (CaDM) demonstrates improved generalization across various simulated robotics and control tasks compared to existing reinforcement learning methods. The research addresses the common challenge of generalizing to environments with different dynamics, highlighting the limitations of traditional model-based RL approaches and emphasizing the importance of capturing rich contextual information for effective learning.

The paper presents a context-aware dynamics model (CaDM) designed to enhance generalization in model-based reinforcement learning (RL). It evaluates various model-based RL methods, including Vanilla DM, Stacked DM, and PE-TS, across multiple environments such as HalfCheetah, Ant, CrippledHalfCheetah, and SlimHumanoid. The results, summarized in a series of performance metrics (average returns), demonstrate that integrating CaDM significantly improves both training and test performance, particularly in complex environments.

The study highlights that while stacking input transitions can sometimes hinder performance, the separation of context encoding and transition inference in CaDM leads to better approximation of true context. This approach is validated by improved average returns in moderate and extreme test conditions. For instance, when combined with PE-TS, CaDM boosts the average return from 2019.6 to 7087.2 in the HalfCheetah environment. Overall, the findings suggest that context-aware dynamics modeling is a promising avenue for enhancing model-based RL across various tasks.

In this section, the authors evaluate the performance of their Context-aware Dynamics Model (CaDM) in model-based reinforcement learning (RL) through a series of experiments. The objectives include assessing the robustness of CaDM to changes in dynamics compared to other methods, its ability to enhance model-free RL generalization, the effectiveness of a proposed prediction loss, prediction accuracy, and the extraction of meaningful contextual information.

The experiments utilize simulated environments with various robotic and control tasks, including HalfCheetah, Ant, CartPole, and Pendulum, using the MuJoCo physics engine. The authors modify environment parameters to test generalization performance across moderate and extreme regimes, measuring how well models perform in unseen environments.

Baseline models compared to CaDM include Vanilla Dynamics Model, Stacked Dynamics Model, Gradient-Based Adaptive Learner, Recurrence-Based Adaptive Learner, and Probabilistic Ensemble Dynamics Model. The results indicate that CaDM can be effectively combined with these baseline methods to improve performance, particularly with Vanilla DM and PE-TS. The study provides insights into the advantages of incorporating a context encoder and demonstrates the flexibility of CaDM in enhancing various model-based RL approaches. Further details on experimental setups, model architecture, and hyperparameters are available in supplementary materials.

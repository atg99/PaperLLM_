The paper presents a context-aware dynamics model (CaDM) designed to enhance generalization in model-based reinforcement learning (RL). It compares the effectiveness of various policy optimization methods, including Vanilla Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), Stacked PPO, and several variants integrating context-aware strategies like PPO with Probabilistic Context (PPO + PC) and Environment Probing (PPO + EP). 

The results indicate that the proposed PPO + CaDM outperforms other methods across various control tasks, particularly in environments with changing dynamics. This method demonstrated superior stability compared to existing model-based meta-RL techniques, such as GrBAL and ReBAL. 

Performance metrics, including average returns, were evaluated across training and unseen test environments, revealing that PPO + CaDM consistently achieved higher average returns than its counterparts in complex environments like HalfCheetah and Ant. The findings suggest that incorporating a learned context latent vector significantly improves the generalization capabilities of model-free RL methods, making PPO + CaDM a promising approach for diverse RL applications. 

Overall, the study emphasizes the importance of context in enhancing the adaptability and performance of RL agents in variable environments.

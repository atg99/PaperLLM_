The paper discusses the architecture and training settings of STAR-CAPS and EMCaps models for image classification tasks, particularly on the MNIST dataset. Both models consist of convolutional layers and capsule layers, with specific configurations for kernel sizes and the number of capsules. The Adam optimizer is employed for training, with an initial learning rate of 0.01 and a batch size of 128.

Evaluation on the MNIST dataset, which includes 60,000 training and 10,000 testing images, reveals that STAR-CAPS models outperform EMCaps in terms of classification accuracy, training time, and testing time. The STAR-CAPS models demonstrate better stability and performance, especially when compared to EMCaps models, which struggle with larger parameter configurations and are prone to overfitting.

The results show that the performance of EMCaps is sensitive to the number of capsules defined in each layer and their initializations. In contrast, STAR-CAPS effectively addresses these issues by adjusting the connections among capsules during the routing process. The paper includes detailed comparisons of model performance, highlighting the superior accuracy and efficiency of STAR-CAPS models across various configurations.

The paper introduces STAR-CAPS, a novel capsule-based network designed to enhance efficiency through a straight-through attentive routing mechanism. The performance of STAR-CAPS is compared with EMCaps across several datasets, including SmallNorb, CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and ImageNet.

In the evaluation on SmallNorb, STAR-CAPS demonstrated competitive performance against EMCaps, achieving 98.0% accuracy compared to EMCaps' 97.8% for models with similar parameters. In detecting novel viewpoints, STAR-CAPS outperformed the CNN model by 6.3% and closely matched EMCaps' performance, indicating its robustness in handling unfamiliar data.

For CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100, STAR-CAPS achieved a test accuracy of 91.23% and 67.66%, respectively, outperforming EMCaps and other relevant models. Notably, STAR-CAPS excelled in CIFAR-100, where EMCaps failed to converge.

In the ImageNet evaluation, STAR-CAPS achieved a Top-1 validation accuracy of 60.07% and a Top-5 accuracy of 85.66%, marking a significant advancement as previous capsule models struggled with this dataset.

The study concludes that STAR-CAPS effectively addresses the computational challenges of capsule networks through a double-attention routing mechanism, showcasing superior performance compared to traditional models.

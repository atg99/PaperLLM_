The paper introduces STAR-CAPS, a capsule-based architecture that employs a straight-through attentive routing mechanism. Unlike previous methods that utilize Gumbel-Softmax for layer selection in transfer learning, STAR-CAPS formulates each capsule as a matrix to reduce parameter usage. The architecture processes pose features from lower-level capsules by transforming them through shared trainable weight matrices, termed pre-votes.

Routing between lower-level and higher-level capsules is facilitated by two components: the Attention Estimator and the Straight-through Router. The Attention Estimator generates an attentive coefficient matrix that serves as a soft relevance signal, while the Straight-through Router acts as a differentiable binary gate, determining the connectivity between capsules. This dual attention mechanism—soft and hard—creates a comprehensive routing structure that learns connectivity in a stochastic yet differentiable manner.

The overall architecture begins with a convolutional layer followed by a series of capsule layers, including PrimaryCaps and ConvCaps, leading to a ClassCaps layer where each class corresponds to a capsule. Each capsule is defined by a pose matrix, with activation encoded implicitly. The design aims to enhance connectivity search in capsule networks through attention-based methods.

The paper introduces STAR-CAPS, an innovative capsule network designed to enhance image classification performance while addressing the computational challenges associated with traditional capsule networks. Capsule networks are recognized for their ability to capture viewpoint variations and represent part-whole relationships of objects. However, their recurrent dynamic routing leads to high computational complexity, making them less suitable for large-scale applications.

STAR-CAPS improves upon this by implementing a straight-through attentive routing mechanism that eliminates the need for recurrence. This is achieved through attention modules combined with differentiable binary routers, which efficiently estimate routing coefficients between capsules. The routers employ straight-through estimators to facilitate binary decision-making on connecting or disconnecting routes, resulting in more stable and faster performance.

Experimental results demonstrate that STAR-CAPS outperforms baseline capsule networks across various image classification datasets, including MNIST, SmallNorb, CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and ImageNet. The paper highlights the trade-off between computational efficiency and the ability to detect viewpoint variations in neural network architectures, positioning STAR-CAPS as a viable solution for large-scale image classification tasks.

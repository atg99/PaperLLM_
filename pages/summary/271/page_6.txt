The Straight-Through Router is a differentiable hard attention module designed to estimate binary decision signals for connectivity between capsules based on an attentive matrix Aij. It consists of two main components: the Decision-Learner and the Decision-Maker.

The Decision-Learner generates decision scores by first applying global average pooling to A to capture confidence maps and reduce computational complexity. It then utilizes Conv2D operations, followed by BatchNorm and LeakyRelu activations, to produce unnormalized decision scores. This architecture ensures efficient estimation of decision scores, which is crucial for minimizing computational overhead in routing processes.

The Decision-Maker processes the real-valued scores to estimate a binary decision parameter indicating whether to connect or disconnect routes between capsules. While a deterministic approach could select the maximum score, it is not differentiable. Instead, the paper adopts a straight-through estimator based on the Gumbel-Softmax method, which allows for sampling from a discrete categorical distribution while maintaining differentiability. This involves using the Gumbel-Max trick to introduce stochasticity and approximating gradients through a softmax function during the backward pass.

The proposed method was evaluated on various image classification tasks using datasets such as MNIST, SmallNorb, CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and ImageNet. The baseline models were based on EMCaps, which showed better performance compared to other approaches.

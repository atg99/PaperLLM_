The paper presents a detailed architecture for capsule networks, focusing on the ConvCaps layer, which is crucial for routing between capsules. The ConvCaps layer processes input pose matrices generated by lower-level capsules and outputs pose matrices for higher-level capsules. Each input pose matrix is transformed using a trainable matrix, resulting in a pre-vote representation. 

An Attention Estimator is implemented to create distinct attentive matrices for each capsule, allowing the model to focus on relevant features. The routing mechanism employs a Straight-Through Router that makes binary decisions to connect or disconnect capsules based on the attentive matrices. 

The output pose matrix for each higher-level capsule is computed by aggregating the pre-votes and attentive matrices from lower-level capsules, utilizing element-wise operations. The ClassCaps layer then outputs activation probabilities indicating the presence of object classes, calculated through a global average pooling followed by a sigmoid function.

To optimize the Attention Estimator, the authors propose an efficient bottleneck architecture consisting of three convolutional layers, including a lightweight depth-wise separable convolution. The paper highlights the innovative routing and attention mechanisms that enhance the performance of capsule networks in recognizing and classifying objects.

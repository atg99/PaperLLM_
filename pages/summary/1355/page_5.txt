The paper discusses the development of an autonomous driving (AD) system that closely aligns with human-like preferences and behaviors. The main objective is to create a human-centric AD system that enhances user trust and acceptance. The authors express gratitude towards RPL members for their constructive feedback and acknowledge the funding from Vinnova and the Wallenberg AI, Autonomous Systems and Software Program in Sweden, as well as the computational resources provided by the National Supercomputer Centre at Linköping University.

The references cited in the paper cover a range of topics within autonomous driving, including datasets, perception scalability, and the integration of language models for human-like interaction in vehicles. Notable contributions include exploring natural language applications in autonomous driving and the implications of large language models on driving behaviors. The paper emphasizes the importance of rethinking autonomous driving paradigms to improve user interaction and overall system performance.

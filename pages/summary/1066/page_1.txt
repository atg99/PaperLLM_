The paper "Generative Adversarial Networks in Computer Vision: A Survey and Taxonomy" by Zhengwei Wang, Qi She, and Tomás E. Ward provides a comprehensive overview of the advancements and challenges associated with Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) in the field of computer vision. It highlights the significant impact of GANs in various areas, including image generation, image-to-image translation, and facial attribute manipulation. 

The authors identify three primary challenges in applying GANs to real-world problems: generating high-quality images, ensuring diversity in image generation, and stabilizing the training process. The survey categorizes existing research using a taxonomy based on variations in GAN architectures and loss functions, offering a critical analysis of popular GAN technologies in relation to these challenges.

The paper aims to assess the current state of GAN research, focusing on practical applications in computer vision and suggesting future research directions. The authors emphasize that this review differs from previous ones by concentrating specifically on the progress made in addressing real-world challenges. Additionally, the paper includes a summary of related code available on GitHub, providing resources for further exploration in the field.

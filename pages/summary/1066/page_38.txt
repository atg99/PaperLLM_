This collection of references presents significant contributions to the fields of computer vision and deep learning, focusing on various methodologies and advancements in image processing and generative models. 

Key highlights include:
- The development of fully convolutional networks for object contour detection, emphasizing encoder-decoder architectures.
- The introduction of semi-supervised approaches to question answering using generative domain-adaptive networks, enhancing performance with limited labeled data.
- Innovations in semantic image inpainting using deep generative models, showcasing the integration of context in image restoration.
- The proposal of DualGAN for unsupervised image-to-image translation, leveraging dual learning frameworks.
- The application of spectral norm regularization to improve the generalizability of deep learning models.
- The construction of a large-scale image dataset, LSUN, facilitated by deep learning techniques with human involvement.
- Advances in generative adversarial networks (GANs), including self-attention mechanisms and stacked architectures for synthesizing photo-realistic images from text descriptions.

The references span various conferences and publications, indicating a broad and ongoing research effort to enhance the capabilities of generative models and their applications in image processing. The work collectively underpins the evolution of techniques that improve the accuracy and efficiency of deep learning systems in visual tasks.

However, while BERT and its domain-specific variants excel in certain areas, they often struggle with the computational demands of pretraining on specialized datasets, which may limit their adaptability to broader tasks.

To address these challenges, the authors propose XLNet-CNN, which integrates the strengths of XLNet, an autoregressive transformer model that employs permutation-based training to capture long-range dependencies, with a convolutional neural network (CNN) layer focused on local context. This hybrid approach allows XLNet-CNN to effectively recognize important phrases and patterns essential for multi-label text classification.

The paper presents experimental results demonstrating that XLNet-CNN outperforms both XLNet and various domain-specific BERT models across multiple datasets, including medical abstracts, anti-COVID vaccine tweets, and cyberbullying detection. The findings highlight the model's improved performance, measured through F1-scores, indicating its efficacy in handling the complexities of multi-label classification tasks.

In conclusion, XLNet-CNN represents a significant advancement in multi-label text classification, combining global context understanding with local context capture, ultimately enhancing classification accuracy in challenging scenarios. The authors provide their code and preprocessed data for further research and application.

The paper provides a comprehensive survey of pre-trained models (PTMs) in natural language processing (NLP), addressing various aspects such as model architecture, pre-training tasks, extensions, adaptation approaches, resources, and applications. It introduces a new taxonomy of PTMs from four perspectives, highlighting their vulnerabilities and the potential of adversarial defenses to enhance their robustness. The interpretability and reliability of PTMs are emphasized as areas needing further exploration to improve understanding and usage. The authors propose future research directions to advance the field of PTMs. Acknowledgments are given to contributors and funding sources for the research.

The paper provides a comprehensive survey of pre-trained models in natural language processing (NLP), highlighting various advancements and methodologies from 2019 to early 2021. Key contributions include models like ERNIE, K-BERT, and BioBERT, which integrate knowledge graphs and contextual representations to enhance language understanding. The authors discuss the evolution of models such as BERT and its adaptations for different languages, including Dutch, French, and Chinese. They also explore cross-modal models like ViLBERT and LXMERT, which combine visual and linguistic representations. The survey emphasizes the significance of pre-training techniques in improving model performance across diverse NLP tasks, underscoring the growing trend towards knowledge-enhanced and multi-lingual representations in the field.

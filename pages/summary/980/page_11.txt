The paper by QIU XP et al. surveys pre-trained models (PTMs) for natural language processing, highlighting advancements in knowledge-enriched PTMs and multilingual PTMs. It discusses the limitations of existing methods like LAMA in measuring language models' knowledge and suggests more sophisticated query generation techniques.

Knowledge-enriched PTMs aim to integrate external domain-specific knowledge into language models, enhancing their capabilities. Various approaches are explored, including injecting knowledge during pre-training through auxiliary tasks, such as LIBERT, which incorporates linguistic constraints, and SentiLR, which integrates sentiment polarity. Other models like SenseBERT and ERNIE enhance representations by incorporating knowledge graph embeddings. K-Adapter addresses catastrophic forgetting by training independent adapters for different knowledge types.

The paper also examines multilingual PTMs, emphasizing the importance of shared multilingual representations in cross-lingual tasks. Early methods focused on multilingual word embeddings, while models like mBERT and XLM utilize masked language modeling and translation tasks to improve cross-lingual performance. Overall, the survey underscores the significance of infusing both knowledge and multilingual capabilities into PTMs to better serve diverse NLP applications.

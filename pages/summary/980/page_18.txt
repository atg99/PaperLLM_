The paper provides an extensive survey of pre-trained models (PTMs) for natural language processing (NLP), detailing various resources and frameworks available for implementation and evaluation. It lists several open-source implementations, including popular models such as word2vec, GloVe, FastText, and transformer-based models like BERT, GPT-2, and RoBERTa, along with their respective URLs for access.

The survey emphasizes the importance of general evaluation benchmarks for PTMs, highlighting the General Language Understanding Evaluation (GLUE) benchmark, which encompasses multiple natural language understanding tasks. GLUE is designed to assess model robustness and generalization but has been succeeded by the more challenging SuperGLUE benchmark due to diminishing returns on GLUE.

Additionally, the paper discusses question answering (QA) as a significant NLP application, categorizing it into three types: single-round extractive QA, multi-round generative QA, and multi-hop QA. It notes that BERT has innovatively transformed extractive QA into a span prediction task, establishing a strong baseline for this type of problem.

Overall, the paper serves as a comprehensive resource for understanding PTMs in NLP, their evaluation benchmarks, and specific applications like question answering.

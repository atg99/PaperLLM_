(3) Distillation to other structures involves adapting the knowledge from the teacher model to a different architecture for the student model. This can include transferring knowledge from a large model to a smaller one or from a complex model to a simpler framework, enhancing flexibility in deployment.

In summary, the paper discusses the development of domain-specific and task-specific pre-trained models (PTMs) to enhance the applicability of natural language processing applications. It highlights the challenges posed by the size of PTMs, which necessitates model compression techniques to facilitate deployment on resource-constrained devices. Several methods for model compression are outlined, including model pruning, quantization, parameter sharing, and knowledge distillation, each with its own advantages and considerations for implementation. These strategies aim to maintain model performance while reducing computational demands, thereby broadening the accessibility and usability of advanced NLP technologies.

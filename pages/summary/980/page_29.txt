The paper presents a comprehensive survey of pre-trained models for natural language processing (NLP), detailing their evolution, methodologies, and applications. It emphasizes the significance of transfer learning, which allows models to leverage knowledge from previous tasks to enhance performance on new tasks. Key concepts discussed include the role of models like BERT in various NLP tasks, fine-tuning techniques, and the exploration of effective neural extractive summarization. The authors also address the challenges of model adaptation and the importance of understanding what knowledge language models possess. Various studies are cited to illustrate advancements in model training, including strategies for improving fine-tuning, the integration of auxiliary sentences for sentiment analysis, and the use of visual tools for analyzing model representations. The paper concludes by highlighting the ongoing innovation in pre-trained models and their impact on the field of NLP.

The paper presents a processing pipeline for deep learning on an FPGA, specifically focusing on feature aggregation and weight transformation. It addresses potential read conflicts when accessing the X buffer during operations on self weights, proposing a tile buffer to mitigate these conflicts. The tile buffer allows for efficient data handling while minimizing performance impact during the forward pass. 

The scheduling mechanism is detailed, emphasizing the interaction between the CPU and FPGA. The CPU prepares subgraphs for the next mini-batch while the FPGA processes the current one, enabling parallelism through multiple CPU cores. The scheduling of operations within the FPGA is also outlined, ensuring that computations on neighbor weights and self weights are overlapped to optimize efficiency.

The performance analysis simplifies notation for weight matrices and discusses the implications for DSP utilization. Overall, the proposed system enhances computational efficiency in deep learning tasks by leveraging the parallel processing capabilities of both the CPU and FPGA, while carefully managing data flow and potential conflicts.

The paper discusses the efficiency of the Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) compared to Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks in time series forecasting. It highlights that GRU can perform approximately 18.16% faster than LSTM without sacrificing accuracy. The authors reference a study by Rahman et al., which applied the GRU model to predict stock market prices using real-time data from Yahoo Finance. The data was processed using Pandas, scaled, and reshaped for training and testing. The results showed Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) values for various companies, demonstrating GRU's effectiveness in time series prediction.

Additionally, the paper cites research by Le et al., which utilized 18 years of water level data to validate the GRU's performance. The study involved splitting the data into training, validation, and testing sets, with training conducted on 17 years of data. The results indicated a Mean Absolute Error (MAE) ranging from 0.087 to 0.106, supporting the claim that GRU can achieve competitive results with a simpler architecture.

The research methodology outlined in the paper consists of seven steps: data collection, data cleaning, data division, RNN-GRU construction, training of RNN-GRU, and implementation of update gates. The data used was sourced from oil and gas organizations, amounting to approximately 55 GB. The methodology emphasizes normalizing the data, setting appropriate parameters, and utilizing the ADAM optimizer to enhance network performance. Overall, the findings underscore the effectiveness of GRU in time series forecasting with fewer computational demands compared to LSTM.

The paper investigates the performance of Recurrent Neural Networks with Gated Recurrent Units (RNN-GRU) compared to Long Short-Term Memory networks (RNN-LSTM) within the context of sensor data analysis in the oil and gas sector. Figure 5 illustrates sensor values, emphasizing that measurements should remain between 25 kPa and 400 kPa to avoid failure. 

Table 1 presents the performance metrics of RNN-GRU, showcasing the Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) values for different configurations. The results indicate that increasing the number of neurons generally improves accuracy, with the highest testing and training accuracies recorded at 13.13 and 4.58, respectively, for six neurons. However, as the number of neurons decreases, accuracy also declines.

In comparison, Table 2 details the performance of RNN-LSTM under similar conditions. The RMSE values indicate that RNN-LSTM requires more time for training while producing slightly higher RMSE values than RNN-GRU. Notably, RNN-GRU demonstrates faster execution times due to its simpler architecture, containing one less gate than LSTM, while maintaining comparable accuracy levels. 

Overall, the findings suggest that RNN-GRU is a more efficient choice for processing sensor data in the oil and gas industry without significantly sacrificing accuracy.

The paper discusses the challenges of handling time series data, particularly in the context of predicting machine failures in industrial settings. It highlights the significance of machine learning algorithms in managing large datasets and performing accurate forecasting to mitigate potential losses from unexpected machine breakdowns. The study specifically employs a recurrent neural network with a gated recurrent unit (RNN-GRU) for forecasting machine conditions using data from an oil and gas company.

Key aspects of the RNN-GRU method are examined, including various parameters such as learning rate, number of epochs, neurons in the hidden layer, and batch size, all of which influence the model's performance. The experimental results indicate that the RNN-GRU model achieves a low root mean square error (RMSE) and an accuracy rate of up to 87%. It is noted that while GRU provides similar accuracy to long short-term memory (LSTM) networks, it processes data more efficiently.

In conclusion, the paper emphasizes the effectiveness of the RNN-GRU approach in predicting machine reliability, thereby contributing to improved operational efficiency in industries reliant on machinery.

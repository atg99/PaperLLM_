This paper presents a deep learning approach using the Recurrent Neural Network - Gated Recurrent Unit (RNN-GRU) to forecast machine failures through time series data generated by sensors in the oil and gas sector. The RNN-GRU model is specifically designed to handle sequential data, utilizing update and reset gates to manage information retention effectively. This structure simplifies the more complex Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks while achieving a commendable prediction accuracy of 87%.

The study highlights the challenges posed by the high dimensionality of time series data and the necessity for advanced machine learning techniques to interpret and forecast future machine states. By leveraging RNN-GRU, the research aims to provide a reliable solution to mitigate the risks associated with sudden machine breakdowns, ultimately contributing to cost savings and operational efficiency in the industry. 

Keywords associated with the study include Gated Recurrent Unit, machine failure, machine learning, prediction, and time series data, underscoring the focus on utilizing advanced algorithms to enhance predictive maintenance in industrial applications.

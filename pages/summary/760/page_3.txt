The decoder consists of a series of identical decoder-blocks, each having three sub-layers: masked multi-head scaled dot-product attention, multi-head scaled dot-product attention (which attends to the encoder outputs), and a position-wise fully connected network. The masked attention mechanism ensures that the decoder only processes the current and past tokens, maintaining the autoregressive property necessary for text generation.

In summary, the architecture of the NRTR (Non-Recurrent Text Recognition) model is characterized by a modality-transform block that processes input images through a series of convolutional layers, followed by an encoder that employs multi-head attention for feature extraction, and a decoder that generates corresponding text sequences based on the encoded representations. The innovative use of positional encoding and residual connections throughout the model enhances its ability to learn complex patterns effectively.

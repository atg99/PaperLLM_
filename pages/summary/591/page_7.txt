This can be achieved by employing deep autoencoders that capture the essential characteristics of the data while disregarding domain-specific noise.

4.3.3 Adversarial-based
Adversarial-based adaptation methods leverage the principles of generative adversarial networks (GANs) to create a more robust model that can generalize across domains. These methods involve training a generator to produce data that mimics the target domain while simultaneously training a discriminator to distinguish between the source and target data. This adversarial training process encourages the model to learn features that are invariant to the domain, thus improving its performance on the target domain.

In conclusion, the paper discusses various strategies for domain adaptation, focusing on minimizing objective functions to enhance the transfer of knowledge from source to target domains. Techniques such as Low-Rank Transfer Subspace Learning, deep neural networks, and different adaptation methods (discrepancy-based, reconstruction-based, and adversarial-based) are explored to address the challenges posed by domain shifts and the lack of labeled data in target domains. Each method presents its advantages and limitations, contributing to the ongoing research in effective domain adaptation strategies.

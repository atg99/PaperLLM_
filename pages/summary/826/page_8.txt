The paper presents a low-bit precision quantization framework for neural networks, focusing on MSE-based techniques to optimize the accuracy-compression ratio. The study evaluates various architectures, including AlexNet, VGG16bn, ResNet (18, 50, 101), Inception v3, SqueezeNet, and DenseNet, under INT4 quantization scenarios, both with and without offset. Results indicate that the proposed method achieves significant performance while maintaining a balance between compression ratios and accuracy.

The study emphasizes the sensitivity of models like SqueezeNet and DenseNet to quantization, which is often overlooked in existing literature. 

The framework includes a detailed methodology for quantizing weights and activations, using a calibration set for optimizing scaling factors. The MSE grid search process and the impact of modifying the MSE threshold on accuracy-compression trade-offs are discussed, as seen in Table 7. 

In conclusion, the authors introduce a novel and effective quantization strategy that leverages hardware-aware techniques and residual quantization for sensitive layers, enabling efficient deployment of pretrained models with state-of-the-art INT4 inference results. The framework is adaptable for various precision requirements, addressing the complexities of compression in neural network applications.

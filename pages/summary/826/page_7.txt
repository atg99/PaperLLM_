The paper presents an evaluation of various deep learning architectures, focusing on their performance with different quantization techniques. The architectures assessed include AlexNet, VGG16bn, Inception v3, ResNet (18, 50, 101), SqueezeNet, and DenseNet, with comparisons made between original baselines and various quantization methods, such as KLD, OMSE, and dual MSE.

Key findings indicate that the proposed methods, OMSE and Dual, enhance accuracy across several models when compared to traditional quantization approaches. For instance, ResNet50 with OMSE achieved a top-1 accuracy of 73.252%, demonstrating significant improvements over the baseline. The paper also details a refinement procedure that can boost accuracy by up to 23%, particularly beneficial in low compression ratio scenarios.

The authors emphasize the importance of activation quantization, which differs from weights quantization as it is performed dynamically on each image. They propose a method for approximating optimal scaling factors using a small calibration set, which mitigates issues related to saturation and the influence of outliers. 

Overall, the experiments validate the robustness of the proposed framework across popular architectures, showcasing its effectiveness in enhancing both deployment speed and generalization capabilities.

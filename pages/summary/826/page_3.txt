The paper discusses challenges in deploying quantized neural networks (NNs) on constrained hardware, such as mobile and IoT devices, due to the limitations of existing quantization methods. It introduces a proposed Minimum Mean Squared Error (MMSE) quantization method aimed at minimizing quantization noise, which can significantly impact model performance.

The quantization process is described for a multi-layer NN architecture, where weights and activations are approximated as quantized tensors. The paper explains the linear approximation of these tensors, emphasizing the importance of scaling factors to facilitate low precision tensor operations. It outlines a uniform quantization scheme and highlights the potential complexities introduced by using offsets for non-symmetric distributions.

The analysis further delves into the Mean Squared Error (MSE) associated with quantization, providing a mathematical framework to understand how quantization noise affects the output of the NN. It derives expectations for the MSE between the original and quantized outputs, illustrating the relationships between the quantization noise of weights and activations and the overall model accuracy.

In summary, the proposed MMSE quantization method aims to optimize the quantization process by focusing on minimizing the noise introduced during the conversion of full precision NNs to fixed-point representations, thereby enhancing the deployment of NNs on hardware with stringent resource constraints.

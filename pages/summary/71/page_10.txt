In this section of the paper, Laghrissi et al. discuss the performance of their model in binary and multiclass classification tasks, achieving over 81% accuracy for binary classification and 87% for multiclass classification. They subsequently tested the model using a reduced dataset that included two and three components.

The authors explain the concept of Mutual Information (MI), which quantifies the statistical dependence between two variables. They computed MI scores for various features to determine their impact on classification outcomes. For binary classification, four features—feature 21, feature 3, feature 2, and feature 4—exhibited the highest MI scores, indicating significant influence on the results. Conversely, ten features demonstrated relatively good scores, while others had minimal impact on classification performance. The findings are illustrated in the figures provided.

In this section of the paper by Laghrissi et al., the authors discuss their methodology for sampling data in the R2L category, outlining the sizes of the records before and after sampling. They sampled 120,000 records for normal data, and 100,000 records each for DoS and R2L categories. 

Additionally, the authors explain the use of Principal Component Analysis (PCA) as a technique for dimensionality reduction in their dataset. They present the percentage of explained variances for both binary and multiclass classifications. The results indicate that the first two principal components account for over 71% of the variance in binary classification and 74% in multiclass classification. This suggests that incorporating a third component may enhance data representation, although the specific impact is not detailed in this excerpt. The findings are visually supported by figures illustrating PCA calculations.

The cell state is protected by gates that regulate the addition or removal of information, utilizing a sigmoid function where a value of one permits modification and zero denies it. 1. **Forget Gate Layer**: This gate assesses the input data and the previous hidden layer's data to determine what information to erase from the cell state. It is formulated as ft=σ(Wf.[ht−1,xt]+b f).

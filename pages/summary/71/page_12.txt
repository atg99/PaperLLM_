In this study, Laghrissi et al. examined the performance of LSTM for binary and multi-class classification tasks, utilizing PCA and Mutual Information for dimensionality reduction. The results demonstrated that LSTM-PCA yielded the best performance for both binary and multi-class classifications, particularly when using only two components. This effectiveness is attributed to PCA's ability to minimize noise sensitivity and eliminate correlated features, facilitating easier learning for the classifier. 

For binary classification, LSTM performed well, though its efficiency decreased in multi-class scenarios, likely due to the noisy dataset. The study also highlighted that Mutual Information was more effective with four features compared to ten. Processing times for training varied significantly; LSTM-PCA models required 1397.74 seconds for multi-class and 876.68 seconds for binary classification with four features, while using all features without dimensionality reduction took considerably longer, at 11,629.69 seconds for multi-class and 18,802.28 seconds for binary classification. The findings underscore the importance of dimensionality reduction techniques in improving classification accuracy and reducing processing time.

In this study, Laghrissi et al. conducted experiments to evaluate their model using 4 and 10 feature scores for both binary and multiclass classification. The implementation was carried out on Google Colab utilizing the Keras library, with the dataset split into 60% for training, 20% for validation, and 20% for testing. The LSTM model was configured according to specified parameters.

To assess the model's performance, a confusion matrix was utilized, which delineates the relationship between predicted and actual classes. Key metrics calculated from the confusion matrix included true positives, true negatives, false positives, and false negatives. The study emphasized the importance of minimizing false positives and false negatives, particularly the latter due to their significant impact on information systems.

The evaluation metrics employed in the research were accuracy, sensitivity (recall), precision, and F1 score, defined as follows: 
- Accuracy is the ratio of correct predictions to total predictions.
- Sensitivity (Recall) measures the proportion of actual positives correctly identified.
- Precision indicates the proportion of true positives among all predicted positives.
- F1 Score is the harmonic mean of precision and sensitivity.

The parameters for the proposed LSTM model included specific settings for activation functions, loss functions, optimizers, learning rates, and dropout rates for both binary and multiclass scenarios, with all configurations set for 50 epochs.

In this paper, Laghrissi et al. present a comparative analysis of intrusion detection systems utilizing various deep learning models, specifically focusing on Long-Short Term Memory (LSTM) networks. The authors employ Principal Component Analysis and Mutual Information for dimensionality reduction and evaluate the performance and processing times of the proposed approaches.

The findings indicate that the proposed architecture demonstrates superior efficiency compared to other deep learning models, highlighting its enhanced generalization performance and robustness. The performance metrics, including accuracy and sensitivity, of different models are compared, showing that the proposed LSTM-PCA model achieves an accuracy of 99.49% and a sensitivity of 99.15%. This positions it favorably against previously established models, such as the Improved CNN and Prunning VELM, underscoring the potential for future advancements in intrusion detection systems.

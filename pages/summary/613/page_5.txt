The paper describes the training and evaluation of deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) using VGG-16 and VGG-19 architectures across 12 experimental groups, focusing on the degree of fine-tuning. The experiments were conducted on an NVIDIA DGX Station equipped with a robust hardware setup, including four NVIDIA Tesla V100 GPUs. The training process utilized the Keras library with TensorFlow as the backend engine, employing an initial learning rate of 0.00001. The ReduceLROn-Plateau method was applied to adjust the learning rate when training performance plateaued, while the RMSprop algorithm served as the optimization solver. This structured approach ensures effective model training and evaluation across multiple configurations.

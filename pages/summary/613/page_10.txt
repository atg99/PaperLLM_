The paper discusses the performance of the VGG16-FT2 classification model in analyzing images, specifically focusing on its misclassifications and correct classifications. Figures 9 and 10 illustrate sample images that the model either misclassified for incorrect reasons or classified correctly for incorrect reasons. The use of Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping) is highlighted to visualize the model's decision-making process, showcasing areas of the images that influenced the model's predictions. This analysis is crucial for understanding the model's limitations and improving its accuracy in future applications.

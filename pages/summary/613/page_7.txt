The performance metrics for the VGG-16 model fine-tuned with two blocks showed an accuracy of 0.959 for COVID-19, along with a specificity of 0.975 and a sensitivity of 0.925. The AUC was recorded at 0.950, indicating strong predictive capability. In contrast, the performance for pneumonia and normal classes was also noteworthy, with accuracies of 0.909 and 0.901, respectively. Other configurations of the VGG-16 and VGG-19 models exhibited varying degrees of accuracy, specificity, and sensitivity, but none surpassed the VGG16-FT2 model for COVID-19 classification. This analysis highlights the effectiveness of fine-tuning deep learning models for improved diagnostic performance in distinguishing between normal, pneumonia, and COVID-19 cases.

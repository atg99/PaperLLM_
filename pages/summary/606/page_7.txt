The analysis indicates that the generalist initialization is the least effective among the tested methods, primarily influenced by the outlier performance of the sign specialist network. In contrast, other specialist initializations consistently outperform the generalist model across various datasets. Notably, when the results from the sign dataset are excluded, the high-fan networks emerge as the superior initialization method, while the generalist continues to underperform. The paper includes visual data representations, showcasing the performance of the first five specialist networks—bird, cat, flower, plane, and vegetable—alongside sample images from each dataset to illustrate their respective categories.

This paper provides a comprehensive survey on the integration of machine learning (ML) techniques with constrained optimization (CO) problems, emphasizing the recent advancements in end-to-end CO learning. The authors highlight the significance of CO across various fields such as transportation, supply chains, and resource allocation, noting the complexity of combinatorial optimization problems, which are often NP-Hard. Despite the challenges, existing algorithms have been successfully applied to solve many CO problems, yet their complexity limits their use in real-time applications or scenarios that involve extensive empirical data.

The survey categorizes current research into two main areas: ML-augmented CO, which enhances optimization algorithms with ML, and end-to-end CO learning, which integrates ML and CO to predict solutions directly. The paper distinguishes two primary application domains: the rapid approximation of CO solutions and the incorporation of data-driven inference into CO solvers.

The authors aim to fill a gap in the literature regarding end-to-end CO learning by providing a critical analysis of existing work and identifying open questions and future research directions. The paper also discusses the foundational concepts of constrained optimization, including the definition of feasible and optimal solutions, and outlines various types of optimization problems, such as linear and quadratic programming, which are prevalent in practical applications.

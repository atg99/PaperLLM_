Reinforcement Learning
In contrast, reinforcement learning approaches leverage the inherent structure of optimization problems to derive solutions. These methods treat the optimization process as a sequential decision-making task, utilizing the optimization problem's objective function as a reward signal. This allows the model to learn from its actions over time, refining its strategy based on the feedback received from the environment.

Overall, the paper discusses the evolution of machine learning techniques applied to constrained optimization, highlighting two main categories: learning with constraints, which focuses on predicting feasible solutions, and learning solutions on graphs, which leverages deep learning architectures for combinatorial problems. The survey emphasizes the shift from traditional methods to modern machine learning frameworks, showcasing their potential to enhance the efficiency and accuracy of constrained optimization solutions.

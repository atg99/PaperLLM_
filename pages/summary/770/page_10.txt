This collection of references encompasses a wide array of topics in deep learning, combinatorial optimization, and reinforcement learning, highlighting significant advancements and methodologies in these fields. 

LeCun, Bengio, and Hinton's seminal work on deep learning outlines foundational concepts and applications, while Lodi and Zarpellon provide a comprehensive survey on learning and branching techniques in optimization. Ma et al. introduce graph pointer networks combined with hierarchical reinforcement learning for combinatorial optimization, showcasing innovative problem-solving frameworks.

Mandi and Guns focus on interior point methods for linear programming, advancing prediction and optimization techniques. Their follow-up work with Stuckey et al. presents smart predict-and-optimize strategies for complex combinatorial problems. Nazari et al. explore reinforcement learning applications in vehicle routing, contributing to the integration of machine learning with operational challenges.

Ndiaye et al. discuss gap-safe screening rules for sparsity in penalties, while Ng et al. apply statistical learning to DC optimal power flow, demonstrating practical applications of these methods in power systems. Nocedal and Wright's book on numerical optimization remains a key reference for optimization techniques.

The references also highlight advancements in graph neural networks (Scarselli et al., Veličković et al.), indicating their growing importance in machine learning. Wang et al.'s work on low-rank coordinate descent and differentiable satisfiability solvers bridges deep learning with logical reasoning, further emphasizing the interdisciplinary nature of recent research.

The contributions of Sutton and Barto on reinforcement learning fundamentals and the introduction of decision-focused learning by Wilder et al. illustrate the ongoing evolution of learning algorithms in addressing complex decision-making problems. Overall, these works collectively advance the understanding and application of deep learning and optimization in various domains, reflecting the dynamic nature of research in artificial intelligence.

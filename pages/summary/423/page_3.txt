This pipeline effectively combines advanced deep learning techniques with traditional object tracking methods to enhance accuracy and robustness.

The paper discusses various strategies for object detection and tracking, including the use of YOLOv2 for vehicle tracking and the application of R-CNN for UAV-based tracking. It highlights the challenges posed by UAV perspectives and the need for specialized approaches, such as the multi-level prediction Siamese Network and the Hierarchical Deep High-Resolution Network for multi-object tracking. The proposed method leverages a Multi-Stream CNN to extract multi-scale features, which are then used to generate bounding boxes and track objects across video frames.

Overall, the work contributes to the field of object tracking by integrating state-of-the-art deep learning models with established tracking methodologies, aiming to improve performance in complex scenarios.

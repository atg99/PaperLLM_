4.2. Implementation Details
The implementation of the proposed MS model involved configuring the algorithm to optimize performance in object detection and tracking. 4.3. Results
The experimental results demonstrated the effectiveness of the MS model across various tasks in the datasets. Performance metrics indicated significant advancements in object detection and tracking capabilities, particularly in challenging scenarios characterized by occlusion, varying altitudes, and camera motion. The findings suggest that the proposed model offers robust solutions for UAV-based applications in computer vision.

In summary, the paper presents a comprehensive evaluation of the proposed MS model, utilizing established UAV datasets to validate its performance in object detection and tracking under diverse conditions. The results indicate that the model significantly enhances performance, addressing critical challenges in the field.

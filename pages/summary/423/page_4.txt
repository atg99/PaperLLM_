The object tracking pipeline proposed in the study utilizes the Faster R-CNN object detector, which leverages the feature maps generated by the multi-stream CNN for robust object detection across video frames. The methodology incorporates the Deep SORT tracking algorithm to maintain continuity of detected objects through the video sequence, enabling accurate tracking despite potential occlusions or variations in object appearance.

In summary, the paper presents a novel multi-stream CNN architecture that enhances feature extraction by employing parallel convolutional streams with varying kernel sizes. This approach allows for a more comprehensive analysis of object features, ultimately improving tracking performance in dynamic video environments. The integration of Faster R-CNN and Deep SORT further solidifies the effectiveness of the proposed object tracking pipeline.

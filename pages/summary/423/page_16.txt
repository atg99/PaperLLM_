The paper discusses contributions from multiple authors in the fields of computer science and robotics, particularly focusing on the development of technologies for immersive neurocognitive rehabilitation and various applications of virtual and augmented reality. The authors, including D.A., A.F., and D.P., were involved in conceptualization, methodology, formal analysis, and writing of the manuscript, with all authors agreeing on the final version. The research did not receive external funding but was partially supported by ONRG and MIUR projects. There are no conflicts of interest declared by the authors.

The references cited in the paper cover a wide range of topics, including the design of 3D platforms for rehabilitation, the impact of serious games on cognitive impairment in older adults, and advancements in virtual reality systems for biomedical applications. The references also address various methodologies for deception detection using computer vision and machine learning, as well as applications in navigation for unmanned vehicles. Overall, the paper emphasizes the interdisciplinary nature of the research, integrating concepts from robotics, artificial intelligence, and immersive technologies.

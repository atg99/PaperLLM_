The paper discusses the organization of videos for UAV tracking based on nine attributes: aspect ratio change, background clutter, camera motion, fast motion, full occlusion, illumination variation, low resolution, out-of-view, partial occlusion, similar object, scale variation, and viewpoint change. It introduces the UMCD dataset, which consists of 50 videos captured at low altitudes (6 to 15 meters) across various environments like dirt, countryside, and urban settings. This dataset includes diverse objects such as individuals, vehicles, and luggage, making it suitable for testing UAV object detection algorithms. The paper summarizes the characteristics of the utilized datasets in a table and provides visual examples from each dataset to illustrate their unique features, facilitating comprehensive testing of UAV tracking algorithms.

The paper discusses the implications of using latent neurons in the representation of qubits, highlighting a minor enhancement observed when transitioning from two to three latent neurons for a single qubit. This improvement is attributed to the limitations of continuous encoding in representing cyclic parameters inherent in models like the Bloch sphere. The authors suggest that a similar challenge arises when extending the representation to two qubits, where moving from six to seven latent neurons may not significantly enhance interpretability. The text references various works and acknowledges the complexity in interpreting learned representations due to these restrictions.

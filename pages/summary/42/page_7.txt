This reference list includes a variety of scholarly works primarily focused on named entity recognition (NER) and related natural language processing (NLP) techniques. Key contributions include:

1. **Dependency Kernels for Relation Extraction**: Bunescu and Mooney (2005) introduced a shortest path dependency kernel for enhancing relation extraction capabilities.

2. **Bilingual Constraints in NER**: Che et al. (2013) explored named entity recognition using bilingual constraints, showcasing effective techniques in a multilingual context.

3. **Conditional Models for Chinese NER**: Chen et al. (2006) presented probabilistic models specifically for Chinese named entity recognition.

4. **Dynamic Multi-Pooling CNNs**: Chen et al. (2015) utilized dynamic multi-pooling convolutional neural networks for event extraction, advancing the understanding of neural architectures in NLP.

5. **Generalized Pooling for Sentence Embedding**: In 2018, Chen et al. enhanced sentence embeddings through generalized pooling methods.

6. **Survey of QA Systems**: Diefenbach et al. (2018) provided a comprehensive survey of core techniques in question answering systems over knowledge bases.

7. **Character-based Models for Chinese NER**: Dong et al. (2016) developed character-based LSTM-CRF models that incorporated radical-level features for Chinese NER, illustrating the importance of character-level understanding.

8. **Max Margin Neural Networks**: He and Sun (2016, 2017) proposed a max margin neural network approach for NER, focusing on social media contexts.

9. **Long Short-Term Memory Networks**: Hochreiter and Schmidhuber (1997) introduced LSTM, a key architecture that has significantly influenced subsequent NER methods.

10. **End-to-End Sequence Labeling**: Ma and Hovy (2016) presented a bi-directional LSTM-CRF model for sequence labeling, which has been widely adopted in NER tasks.

11. **Lexicon Infused Phrase Embeddings**: Passos et al. (2014) integrated lexicon knowledge into phrase embeddings to improve named entity resolution.

12. **Iterated Dilated Convolutions**: Strubell et al. (2017) developed a method for fast and accurate entity recognition utilizing iterated dilated convolutions.

13. **Lattice LSTM for Chinese NER**: Zhang and Yang (2018) applied lattice LSTM architectures to enhance Chinese NER, demonstrating the effectiveness of integrating linguistic structures.

This collection of references underscores the evolution of techniques and models in NER, particularly within the context of Chinese language processing, offering insights into both theoretical and practical advancements in the field.

The paper presents an overview of various natural language processing (NLP) tasks categorized into single-sentence tasks, sentence pair tasks, and machine reading comprehension tasks, along with their respective datasets and metrics. 

In single-sentence tasks, the TNEWS dataset contains 53.3k samples for short text classification with 15 classes, while the IFLYTEK dataset includes 12.1k app descriptions classified into 119 categories. The CLUEWSC2020 dataset is focused on coreference resolution, featuring 1,244 samples derived from contemporary Chinese fiction.

The sentence pair tasks include the AFQMC, which assesses semantic similarity through binary classification of sentence pairs, and the CSL dataset that evaluates keyword recognition from Chinese scientific literature. The OCNLI dataset is designed for natural language inference, containing 50k pairs from various genres in Chinese, ensuring that it remains contextually relevant.

For machine reading comprehension, the CMRC 2018 dataset involves answer span extraction from Wikipedia, while ChID focuses on multiple-choice questions related to idioms across different texts. Lastly, another dataset C311 features a mix of multiple-choice and free-form tasks, emphasizing the diverse applications of NLP in understanding and processing language data.

The paper also discusses the methodology of dataset filtering and random shuffling used to enhance model performance and reduce easy examples, ensuring a robust evaluation framework across the discussed tasks.

The paper presents a structured overview of dataset samples, hyperparameters for pre-training and fine-tuning, and additional baseline details for various tasks. 

In the section on dataset samples, examples are compiled in Table 5, with a note that some samples were omitted due to excessive length. Complete datasets can be found in referenced papers, with a download link promised in the final version of the paper.

Hyperparameters for pre-training are provided in Table 6, though most models were not trained by the authors. Table 7 contains hyperparameters for fine-tuning, highlighting specific behaviors observed during experiments. For the CSL task, it was noted that negative samples were generated by replacing one real keyword with a fake one, with larger models requiring very small learning rates for convergence.

The IFLYTEK dataset, which originally has 126 categories, was filtered to exclude classes with fewer than 10 examples to facilitate effective cross-validation. Additionally, it was observed that the ALBERT-tiny model required more epochs to converge compared to others, and that most critical information in IFLYTEK sentences is found at the beginning, leading to a maximum length of 128 for processing.

Lastly, the dataset filtering process aimed to enhance model differentiation and increase dataset difficulty. Four-fold cross-validation was applied to the IFLYTEK and TNEWS datasets, using three parts for fine-tuning ALBERT-tiny and subsequently filtering out easier examples from the remaining set.

The paper introduces the Chinese Language Understanding Evaluation (CLUE) benchmark, a significant advancement in the field of natural language understanding (NLU) for the Chinese language. Existing benchmarks like GLUE and SuperGLUE predominantly focus on English, which limits the ability to replicate successful NLU models for other languages, including Chinese, spoken by a substantial portion of the global population.

CLUE is a comprehensive, community-driven initiative that encompasses nine diverse tasks related to NLU, including semantic similarity, natural language inference, and various classification and machine reading comprehension tasks, all utilizing original Chinese text. The benchmark aims to facilitate research and development in Chinese NLU by providing a structured evaluation framework.

The paper evaluates these tasks using nine state-of-the-art pre-trained Chinese models, revealing varying levels of difficulty and performance discrepancies between human and machine results. The authors also highlight the lack of large-scale open-source pre-training datasets for Chinese, which contrasts with the availability of such resources in English. CLUE aims to address these challenges, supporting further advancements in Chinese NLU and providing a foundation for future research in the field. The benchmark is accessible online at CLUEbenchmarks.com.

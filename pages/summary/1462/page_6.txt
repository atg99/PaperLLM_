The paper presents a comparative analysis of various pre-trained transformer models on the CLUE benchmark, focusing on their performance across multiple tasks. The models evaluated include BERT-base, BERT-wwm-ext-base, ALBERT-tiny, ALBERT-xxlarge, ERNIE-base, XLNet-mid, RoBERTa-large, RoBERTa-wwm-ext-base, and RoBERTa-wwm-ext-large. Results indicate that RoBERTa-wwm-ext-large achieved the highest average performance across the tasks, while human performance set a benchmark significantly above all models. The experiments utilized a variety of architectures, including single-sentence and sentence-pair tasks, with specific configurations for machine reading comprehension tasks. The models were implemented using TensorFlow and PyTorch, and the study emphasizes the importance of the CLUEOSCAR corpus, which is a large multilingual dataset derived from the Common Crawl corpus, for the training of these models.

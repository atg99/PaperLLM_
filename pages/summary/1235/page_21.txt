The paper discusses the performance of different models in predicting daily temperature, specifically focusing on the results from a synthetic case study conducted on February 2, 2008. Among the models tested, Ordinary Kriging (OK) demonstrated the best performance, as it was aligned with the realities created using its own simulation method. However, the authors caution that the accuracy of OK may have been overestimated due to the exclusion of semivariogram estimation error, which can significantly impact results, particularly with small sample sizes. In comparison, Inverse Distance Weighting (IDW), Random Forest Spatial (RFsp), and Random Forest Spatial Interpolation (RFSI) showed similar performance, slightly lagging behind OK. The findings align with previous studies that also reported inferior performance of IDW relative to OK in synthetic case studies.

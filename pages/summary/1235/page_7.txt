The study focuses on predicting precipitation using various datasets and covariates. It specifically avoids using the final run of the GHCN-daily station precipitation data, as this is the dependent variable of interest. Instead, it utilizes IMERG estimates, which provide space-time covariates with a spatial resolution of 10 km and a temporal resolution of one day. The analysis also incorporates daily temperature data, with maximum (TMAX) and minimum (TMIN) temperatures estimated using models referenced from Kilibarda et al. The inclusion of digital elevation model (DEM) data was considered but did not enhance model accuracy, likely due to the effects of elevation being captured by other covariates.

Additionally, the Croatian temperature dataset, consisting of 57,282 observations from 159 stations in 2008, is introduced, with temperature readings ranging from -14.1°C to 32.6°C. Key covariates for modeling mean daily temperature include geographic factors (latitude, longitude, distance to coastline), elevation, seasonal variations, insolation, and MODIS land surface temperature images. The spatial distribution of the station locations in Croatia is illustrated on a DEM, providing context for the study area.

The paper presents an analysis of covariate importance and accuracy assessment for various predictive models in a temperature case study. Figure 14 illustrates the importance of environmental covariates for three models: RF, RFsp, and RFSI, with the importance index scaled to a maximum of one. The accuracy metrics for six models are summarized in Table 7. The STRK model exhibited the poorest performance, attributed to its restrictive separable structure. In contrast, the RF models demonstrated improved accuracy due to the integration of covariates, with RFSI outperforming all other methods by leveraging nearest observation covariates effectively. Predictions made at a 1 km spatial resolution for February 2, 2008, reveal that IDW predictions were the smoothest, while RF methods exhibited similar influences from key covariates like seasonal fluctuation, MODIS LST images, and insolation. Overall, RFSI achieved the highest accuracy metrics, indicating its robustness in temperature prediction.

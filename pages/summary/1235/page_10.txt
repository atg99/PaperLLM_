The paper presents a comparative analysis of various interpolation methods, specifically focusing on the mean absolute error (MAE) and computational efficiency of RFsp and RFSI under different semivariogram conditions. The results indicate that RFSI outperforms RFsp in terms of speed, particularly with larger datasets, due to its method of calculating distances to the nearest locations instead of generating a covariate raster. This efficiency is crucial as RFsp can be memory-intensive and may lead to significant slowdowns when handling large sample sizes. The findings include prediction maps from a simulation with a 0.25 nugget-to-sill ratio and 500 sample locations, illustrating that while TS yields a smooth surface, other methods like IDW, OK, RFsp, and RFSI produce similar patterns with varying levels of noise. Overall, the study highlights the advantages of RFSI in both time efficiency and computational resource management.

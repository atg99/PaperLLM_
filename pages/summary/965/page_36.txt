A Markov Decision Process (MDP) is characterized by a state space S, an action space A, transition probabilities p(s′|s, a) for all states s, s′ in S and actions a in A, along with a reward function r(s, a). The transition probabilities describe the likelihood of moving to state s′ after taking action a in state s. The goal in studying MDPs is to identify decision policies that maximize the cumulative reward signal γtr(st, at) over an infinite time horizon. MDPs represent a specific form of discrete-time optimal control problems.

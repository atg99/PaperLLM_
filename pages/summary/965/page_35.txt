This paper discusses constructive algorithms for designing an optimal policy in reinforcement learning when dealing with finite state and action spaces. It emphasizes that the existence of numerous effective reinforcement learning algorithms is contingent on the finiteness of these spaces. The paper initially explores algorithms that separately address the two critical steps of policy design before progressing to those that integrate both steps. The algorithms proposed are designed to infer the optimal policy from historical data. Additionally, they can be easily adapted for episode-based reinforcement learning, where the optimal policy must be inferred from multiple trajectories.

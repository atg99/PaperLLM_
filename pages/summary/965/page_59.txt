The paper introduces a noise factor η and a parameter α within the context of an algorithm that updates a selected element e from a set E at each stage k. The update rule for the value R(e) is formulated as an equation that combines the previous value of R(e) with a weighted average of a function GR applied to e and the noise factor η. The notation is streamlined to denote the selected element at stage k as ek, the noise at this stage as ηk, and the value of R at stage k as Rk. The parameter α is specified for the selected element, and the update equation is reformulated to highlight the dependence of R on both the previous value and the newly computed values influenced by the noise. This structured approach allows for clear tracking of how R evolves over time with the introduction of noise and the selection mechanism.

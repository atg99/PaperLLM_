Intelligent agents are utilized across multiple domains, including project management, electronic commerce, robotics, information retrieval, military applications, networking, and planning and scheduling. 

Key examples include:

- A predictive maintenance agent that analyzes sensor data from industrial equipment to forecast potential failures, enabling maintenance to be scheduled only when necessary, thereby minimizing downtime and costs (Leroy et al. 2023).
- An autonomous delivery drone system that enhances delivery efficiency by optimizing routes and schedules based on real-time traffic, weather, and customer availability, while also learning from past deliveries to improve future performance.
- An alignment agent that refines large language models like ChatGPT by adapting to user intent through feedback, which enhances the interpretation of questions and ensures relevant responses, as discussed in lecture 11 on reinforcement learning and large language models.
- A robotic harvesting assistant equipped with visual recognition capabilities to identify ripe produce in orchards. It carefully picks fruits and vegetables while learning from each harvest to improve yield and quality, thereby assisting farmers in optimizing their picking schedules, as explored in lecture 10 on robotic reinforcement learning. 

These applications illustrate the versatility and effectiveness of intelligent agents in improving operational efficiency and user satisfaction across various sectors.

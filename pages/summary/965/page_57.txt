In this paper, a fixed point R∗ of a mapping G from B(E) to B(E) is defined as the point satisfying the equation GR∗ = R∗. It is established that if G is a contraction mapping, there exists a unique fixed point R∗. Additionally, for any R in B(E), it is shown that the limit of the norm of the difference between G raised to the power k applied to R and the fixed point R∗ approaches zero as k approaches infinity. The paper operates under the assumptions that E is finite with n elements, G is a contraction mapping with a fixed point R∗, and R is an element of B(E).

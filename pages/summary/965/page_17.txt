The concept of cumulative reward signal is defined through the trajectory ht, which encompasses the sequence of states, actions, and rewards from time 0 to t. The stochastic policy π maps the combined state and action spaces to probabilities, guiding the agent's decisions. The expected return of the policy, denoted as Jπ(s), represents the anticipated cumulative reward when starting from a specific state s0. This is calculated as the limit of the expected sum of discounted rewards over time, factoring in the policy's actions and the inherent uncertainty captured in the trajectory ht. Notably, the agent operates under the constraint of limited knowledge about the underlying system dynamics and reward functions, relying solely on the information derived from its trajectory.

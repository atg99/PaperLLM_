In this section, the computation of the estimated optimal policy ˆµ∗ is achieved by learning the structure of the equivalent Markov Decision Process (MDP). The ˆQN-functions are computed using the recurrence equation, which incorporates the estimated reward ˆr and the estimated transition probabilities ˆp. Specifically, the equation is defined as:

ˆQN(s, a) = ˆr(s, a) + γ ∑s′∈S ˆp(s′|s, a) max a′∈A ˆQN−1(s′, a′)

with the initial condition ˆQ0(s, a) set to 0. The optimal policy approximation is obtained by maximizing the ˆQN-function for a sufficiently large N, ensuring that the right-hand side of a specified inequality falls below a small threshold ε. 

The paper establishes that if the estimated MDP structure is within an ε-neighborhood of the true structure, then the value of the computed policy Jˆµ∗ will be within an O(ε)-neighborhood of the true optimal policy Jµ∗. This indicates that the convergence of the estimated policy to the optimal policy is contingent upon the accuracy of the estimated MDP structure.

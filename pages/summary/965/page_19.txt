The solution for Exercise 1 involves calculating the expected return Jπ(1) using the formula for the limit of a discounted sum. The expression starts with the limit as T approaches infinity of a sum involving rewards at different time steps, specifically r(1,1), r(2,1), and so forth. The rewards are discounted by the factor γ raised to the power of the time step.

Through simplification, the solution shows that the sum converges to γ^2 multiplied by a limit that involves a geometric series. The key result from the limit of the geometric series is applied, leading to the final expression for the expected return:

Jπ(1) = γ^2 / (1 - γ)

This result is valid under the condition that the discount factor γ is less than 1, which ensures the convergence of the series.

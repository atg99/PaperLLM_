The Q-learning algorithm updates the Q-function at each stage k using a specific formula. The update rule is given by:

Qk+1(sk, ak) = (1 - αk)Qk(sk, ak) + αk(r(sk, ak, wk) + γmaxa∈AQk(f(sk, ak, wk), a))

In this context, Qk represents the estimate of the Q-function at stage k, where wk is drawn independently according to the probability distribution Pw(·|sk, ak). The formula combines the previous Q-value with a new estimate based on the reward function r and the maximum expected future reward, thereby incrementally refining the Q-function. The convergence of this algorithm relies on the appropriate selection of the learning rate αk and the exploration of the state-action space, ensuring that all state-action pairs are visited sufficiently over time.

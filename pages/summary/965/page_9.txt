The paper discusses the interactions between a reinforcement learning (RL) agent and its environment. It emphasizes the importance of these interactions in learning optimal policies for decision-making. The authors explore various algorithms and methodologies that enhance the agent's ability to adapt and respond to dynamic environments. Key concepts include the agent's exploration-exploitation trade-off, reward structures, and the impact of environmental feedback on the learning process. Overall, the study provides insights into improving RL agent performance through effective environmental engagement.

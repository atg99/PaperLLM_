To compute the cumulative reward signal \( J_\pi(1) \) with the given policy \( \pi(s) = 1 \) for all states \( s \in S \), we first need to understand the reward function and the dynamics of the system.

### State and Action Spaces:
- **State Space**: \( S = \{1, 2, 3, 4\} \)
- **Action Space**: \( A = \{-1, 1\} \)

### Reward Function:
The reward function \( r(s, a) \) is defined as follows:
- \( r(s, a) = 1 \) if \( f(s, a) = 4 \)
- \( r(s, a) = 0 \) otherwise

### Dynamics Function:
The dynamics function \( f(s, a) \) is given by:
\[ 
f(s, a) = \min(\max(s + a, 1), 4) 
\]

### Cumulative Reward Calculation:
We will calculate \( J_\pi(1) \) by evaluating the reward for each state when the policy \( \pi \) always selects action \( a = 1 \).

1. **For \( s = 1 \)**:
   - Action \( a = 1 \): 
   \[
   f(1, 1) = \min(\max(1 + 1, 1), 4) = \min(2, 4) = 2 
   \]
   - Reward: \( r(1, 1) = 0 \) (since \( f(1, 1) \neq 4 \))

2. **For \( s = 2 \)**:
   - Action \( a = 1 \):
   \[
   f(2, 1) = \min(\max(2 + 1, 1), 4) = \min(3, 4) = 3 
   \]
   - Reward: \( r(2, 1) = 0 \) (since \( f(2, 1) \neq 4 \))

3. **For \( s = 3 \)**:
   - Action \( a = 1 \):
   \[
   f(3, 1) = \min(\max(3 + 1, 1), 4) = \min(4, 4) = 4 
   \]
   - Reward: \( r(3, 1) = 1 \) (since \( f(3, 1) = 4 \))

4. **For \( s = 4 \)**:
   - Action \( a = 1 \):
   \[
   f(4, 1) = \min(\max(4 + 1, 1), 4) = \min(5, 4) = 4 
   \]
   - Reward: \( r(4, 1) = 1 \) (since \( f(4, 1) = 4 \))

### Cumulative Reward:
Now we sum the rewards obtained from each state:
\[
J_\pi(1) = r(1, 1) + r(2, 1) + r(3, 1) + r(4, 1) = 0 + 0 + 1 + 1 = 2
\]

### Conclusion:
The cumulative reward signal \( J_\pi(1) \) is \( 2 \).

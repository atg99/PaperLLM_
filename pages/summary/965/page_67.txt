This section lists key references and additional readings related to reinforcement learning and its applications in various domains. The first reference is a 2023 paper by Pascal Leroy et al. on IMP-MARL, which focuses on environments for large-scale infrastructure management planning. The second reference, by Elia Kaufmann et al., discusses the use of deep reinforcement learning in achieving champion-level performance in drone racing, published in Nature in August 2023. 

Further foundational texts include "Reinforcement Learning and Dynamic Programming Using Function Approximators" by Lucian Busoniu et al., published in 2017, and "Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction" by Richard Sutton and Andrew Barto, released by MIT Press in 2018. Dimitri Bertsekas's "Dynamic Programming and Optimal Control" is a comprehensive resource spanning multiple volumes, with Volume I published in 2012. Lastly, Csaba Szepesvári's "Algorithms for Reinforcement Learning" from 2022 provides insights into algorithmic approaches in the field.

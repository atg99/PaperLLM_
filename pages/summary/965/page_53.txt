The paper discusses a significant challenge in reinforcement learning known as the curse of dimensionality. This issue arises because the computational requirements to determine near-optimal policies increase exponentially as the size of the state-action space grows. As a result, traditional reinforcement learning algorithms become impractical for larger dimensions. Researchers in the fields of reinforcement learning, dynamic programming, and optimal control are actively seeking algorithms that can overcome this limitation. The paper highlights the potential of deep neural networks to address certain classes of problems related to this challenge.

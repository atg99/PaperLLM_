This paper discusses various methodologies and advancements in the quantization of deep neural networks, aiming to reduce the model size and improve efficiency for deployment in resource-constrained environments. Key contributions in the field include loss-aware binarization techniques, efficient architectures like MobileNets, and strategies for compressing networks through pruning and quantization. The authors highlight the significance of techniques such as binarized neural networks, hierarchical filter groups, and batch normalization to enhance training and inference performance. Several studies emphasize the use of integer-arithmetic-only inference and efficient execution methods, including compiler approaches and adaptive structures for handling diverse datasets. Overall, the paper presents a comprehensive overview of the ongoing research and developments in deep network quantization, aiming to balance accuracy with computational efficiency.

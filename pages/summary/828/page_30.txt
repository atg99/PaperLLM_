This collection of references focuses on advancements in neural network architectures, quantization techniques, and their applications in computer vision and machine learning. Key contributions include:

1. **Quantization Techniques**: Several papers explore methods for quantizing neural networks to reduce model size and improve efficiency without significantly compromising accuracy. Notable works include "Degree-quant" which addresses quantization-aware training for graph neural networks and "Clip-q" which proposes a method for network compression through pruning and quantization.

2. **Neural Architecture Search**: The development of efficient architectures like MnasNet and EfficientNet highlights strategies for optimizing model performance based on specific platforms and scaling models effectively for convolutional neural networks.

3. **Semi-Supervised Learning**: The introduction of mean teacher models demonstrates improvements in semi-supervised learning by using weight-averaged consistency targets as role models.

4. **Bayesian Approaches**: The integration of Bayesian techniques in quantization and pruning illustrates a unified framework that enhances model performance while maintaining resource efficiency.

5. **Hardware-Aware Solutions**: Papers such as HAQ and APQ emphasize the importance of hardware considerations in the design of quantization methods, ensuring that neural networks are optimized for real-world deployment.

Overall, these references reflect ongoing research aimed at enhancing the efficiency, scalability, and practicality of deep learning models, particularly in the context of mobile and embedded systems.

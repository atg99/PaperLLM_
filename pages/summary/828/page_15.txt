The paper discusses two main topics: knowledge distillation and extreme quantization techniques in neural networks. 

Knowledge distillation involves transferring knowledge from a teacher model to a student model. Various methods have been explored, focusing on different knowledge sources such as logits and intermediate layers. Some studies utilize multiple teacher models, while others employ self-distillation without an additional teacher.

Extreme quantization, particularly binarization and ternarization, significantly reduces memory usage and enhances computational efficiency. Binarization constrains values to a 1-bit representation, leading to substantial memory savings and acceleration in operations. However, naive approaches often result in accuracy loss. Notable techniques to mitigate this include BinaryConnect, which binarizes weights during training while maintaining real values, and Binarized Neural Networks that binarize both weights and activations for improved latency. 

Ternarization introduces a third value (0) alongside +1 and -1, allowing for further efficiency gains. The paper categorizes solutions to address accuracy degradation in extreme quantization into three branches: minimizing quantization error, improving loss functions, and leveraging knowledge distillation from full-precision models. Each branch presents distinct strategies to balance accuracy and computational efficiency, with several key works referenced for further reading.

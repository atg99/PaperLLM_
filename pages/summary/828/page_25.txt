The referenced documents cover a wide range of topics related to neural network quantization and optimization techniques. Key concepts include the distribution and sparsity of attention values in transformers, efficient verification of binarized neural networks, and the integration of knowledge distillation with quantization in models like BERT. Various methodologies for optimizing quantization intervals, adaptive bit-widths, and structured compression are discussed, highlighting approaches like Fisher information regularization and weight encryption. The papers also explore the implications of low-precision computations in deep learning and propose frameworks for mixed hardware and software co-design for deploying deep neural networks on edge devices. Overall, the collection emphasizes advancements in making neural networks more efficient while maintaining performance.

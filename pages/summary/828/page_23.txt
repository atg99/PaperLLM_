3. **Model Compression**: Techniques like deep compression integrate pruning, quantization, and coding to compress deep neural networks effectively. Data-free model compression approaches also aim to reduce model size without extensive retraining.

4. **Learning with Limited Data**: Several studies address the challenge of learning compression techniques from limited unlabeled data, emphasizing the need for efficient learning strategies in resource-constrained environments.

5. **Emerging Techniques**: Innovations such as generative zero-shot network quantization and automated machine learning (AutoML) for model compression highlight ongoing research efforts to enhance the performance and efficiency of neural networks.

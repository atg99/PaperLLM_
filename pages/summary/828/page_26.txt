This paper presents a comprehensive list of references focusing on advancements in neural network quantization, pruning, and optimization techniques. The references span various methods, including fractional quantization, rapid deployment strategies, single-shot network pruning based on connection sensitivity, and low-bit neural networks. Notable works include the introduction of ternary weight networks, fully quantized networks for object detection, and efficient non-uniform discretization methods. The references also discuss the challenges posed by noisy labels and the implementation of high-order residual quantization for network acceleration. Further, surveys on pruning and quantization techniques are highlighted, along with innovative approaches like differentiable architecture search and layer importance estimation for effective quantization. Overall, the citations reflect a rich landscape of research aimed at enhancing the efficiency and performance of deep learning models through quantization and related methodologies.

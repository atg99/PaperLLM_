The paper discusses recent advancements in quantization techniques for neural networks (NNs), highlighting their importance in enhancing computational efficiency, particularly for edge-deployment scenarios. It emphasizes that INT4 inference of models like ResNet50 can achieve significant speedups of 50-60% compared to INT8 on GPUs such as NVIDIA T4 and RTX. Additionally, it mentions a 23% speedup using mixed-precision quantization without sacrificing accuracy and further enhancements for models like BERT.

The authors categorize different quantization methods, including uniform, non-uniform, symmetric, and asymmetric quantization, and delve into more specific strategies like layerwise and channelwise quantization. They also explore the interplay between training and quantization, discussing the benefits of quantization-aware training versus post-training quantization, particularly in scenarios where training data may not be available, leading to the concept of zero-shot quantization.

The paper addresses unique challenges associated with deploying efficient NNs on low-end microprocessors, including the potential for using fewer than 8 bits for parameters and integer-only quantization. The authors aim to provide a structured overview of current research in NN quantization to facilitate ongoing evaluation and future developments in this dynamic field.

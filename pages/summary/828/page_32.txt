This compilation of references includes significant works in the field of neural network optimization, focusing on knowledge transfer, quantization, pruning, and self-distillation techniques. 

Key contributions include:

1. **Data-Free Knowledge Transfer**: The work by Hongxu Yin et al. introduces a method called "DeepInversion" for knowledge transfer without using data, emphasizing its efficacy in model distillation.

2. **Quantization Techniques**: Several studies, including those by Penghang Yin et al. and Dongqing Zhang et al., explore methods for quantizing neural networks to reduce model size and improve computational efficiency while maintaining accuracy.

3. **Pruning Strategies**: Research by Ruichi Yu et al. and others focuses on neuron importance score propagation and Hessian-aware pruning, which optimize neural networks by removing less important neurons.

4. **Self-Distillation**: The approach proposed by Linfeng Zhang et al. allows convolutional neural networks to enhance their performance through self-teaching mechanisms.

5. **Bit-width Optimization**: Various papers, including those by Aojun Zhou et al. and Shuchang Zhou et al., address techniques for low-bitwidth training and quantization, aiming for minimal loss in performance while achieving efficient models.

Overall, these works contribute to advancing the efficiency and effectiveness of neural networks through innovative methods in knowledge transfer, quantization, and pruning.

The referenced works span various topics in machine learning, neural networks, and quantization techniques. Key contributions include:

1. FitNets propose hints for training thin deep networks to improve performance without increasing model size (Romero et al., 2014).
2. Deterministic annealing methods for clustering are introduced, enhancing the efficiency of clustering algorithms (Rose et al., 1990).
3. The perceptron model by Rosenblatt laid foundational principles for neural networks, further explored in his work on neurodynamics (Rosenblatt, 1957; 1961).
4. Advances in low-precision quantization for edge microcontrollers aim to optimize model performance while reducing computational demands (Rusci et al., 2020).
5. Low-rank matrix factorization techniques are applied to train deep neural networks with high-dimensional outputs, improving computational efficiency (Sainath et al., 2013).
6. Innovations in quantization methods, such as Int4 precision and post-training sparsity-aware quantization, focus on enhancing AI inference capabilities (Salvator et al., 2019; Shomron et al., 2021).
7. The development of MobileNetV2 highlights the use of inverted residuals and linear bottlenecks to optimize mobile network architectures (Sandler et al., 2018).
8. Research on quantization noise during training supports extreme model compression while maintaining performance (Stock et al., 2021).
9. The history of statistics and its measurement of uncertainty is discussed, providing context for the evolution of data analysis techniques (Stigler, 1986).

These contributions reflect ongoing efforts to enhance neural network efficiency, robustness, and applicability across various computational environments.

Abbass discusses the increasing integration of AI into society and examines how human cognitive intelligence interfaces with artificial cognitive intelligence. 

The exploration takes a multi-disciplinary approach, tracing the development of technology over the past 50 years to understand the dynamics of the Human-AI relationship. It emphasizes the importance of trust in this interaction and identifies both risks and opportunities at the human-AI interface. The analysis reveals that despite the sophistication of AI, human involvement in decision-making processes remains crucial, with individuals participating either in-the-loop or on-the-loop, influencing AI operations.

The paper outlines the historical context of technology as an extension of human capabilities, from simple tools to advanced digital calculators and computers. It highlights the role of modern technology in augmenting human cognition and capabilities, such as planning, memory extension, and sensory enhancements. The conclusion stresses that the successful social integration of AI requires a collaborative relationship between humans and machines, underscoring the enduring necessity for human agency in AI-driven decision-making.

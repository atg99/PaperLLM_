This article explores the intricate relationship between humans and artificial intelligence (AI), emphasizing that the interaction is multifaceted and involves various forms of functional interactions. The author discusses the concept of E-cookies, which serve as smart watchdogs to ensure AI systems operate correctly. However, the design of E-cookies presents significant challenges, particularly in assessing the safety of evolving AI behaviors.

The paper stresses that the human-AI relationship should not be viewed solely through the lens of use, as this could lead to distrust. The allocation agent, an AI that reallocates functions between humans and AI, plays a critical role in maintaining trust. If not designed effectively, it may cause humans to distrust the AI system, especially in contexts like autonomous vehicles where various interactions occur.

The author argues for the necessity of social integration of AI within human systems, highlighting that humans will always play a role in decision-making processes that influence AI operations. Even highly autonomous AIs will need to interact with humans and other AI systems, making social integration essential for effective functioning.

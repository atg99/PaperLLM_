This section references several key works in the field of image processing and computer vision, particularly focusing on techniques related to face synthesis, style transfer, and artistic rendering. 

Chen et al. explore face sketch synthesis utilizing pyramid column features, highlighting advancements in style transfer methods. Korshunova et al. present a fast face-swapping technique leveraging convolutional neural networks, demonstrating efficient image manipulation. Zhang et al. and Gai and Wang focus on low-poly style rendering, with Zhang et al. addressing both images and video processing while Gai and Wang delve into artistic rendering specifically. Gerstner et al. introduce pixelated image abstraction, contributing to non-photorealistic rendering techniques. Lastly, Huang et al. discuss multi-modal unsupervised image-to-image translation, expanding the capabilities of image transformation without supervision.

These works collectively represent significant contributions to the advancement of image synthesis, manipulation, and artistic rendering in computational graphics.

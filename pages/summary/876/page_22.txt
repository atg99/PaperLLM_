The paper discusses advancements in 3D surface stylization, focusing on techniques that optimize and produce 3D objects for both photorealistic and non-photorealistic styles. It highlights a shift in neural style transfer (NST) from merely replicating human art to generating new forms of AI-created art guided by aesthetic principles. The authors note that current NST methods have begun to combine various styles, citing an example where Wang et al. successfully fused coarse texture distortions with fine brush strokes from different styles. The references provided cover a wide range of related topics, including non-photorealistic rendering, artistic stylization techniques, and the use of convolutional neural networks in image style transfer, indicating a comprehensive exploration of the field.

The paper discusses various methodologies for texture modeling and image reconstruction, particularly focusing on the use of Markov Random Fields (MRFs) and Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs). 

In texture modeling, the paper highlights the effectiveness of using pixel correlation to incorporate spatial arrangement information, which is beneficial for modeling textures with symmetric properties. Non-parametric texture modeling with MRFs is introduced, where each pixel's characteristics are derived from its spatial neighborhood. The work by Efros and Leung is noted for synthesizing pixels by searching for similar neighborhoods, while Wei and Levoy enhance this by employing a fixed neighborhood for faster matching.

The section on image reconstruction emphasizes two approaches: Image-Optimisation-Based Online Image Reconstruction (IOB-IR) and Model-Optimisation-Based Offline Image Reconstruction (MOB-IR). IOB-IR, as proposed by Mahendran and Vedaldi, iteratively optimizes an image starting from random noise to match a desired CNN representation, though this method is computationally intensive. Conversely, MOB-IR, introduced by Dosovitskiy and Brox, involves pre-training a feed-forward network to streamline the reconstruction process during testing, significantly improving efficiency.

The paper also covers Neural Style Transfer (NST) algorithms, categorizing them into Image-Optimisation-Based Online Neural Methods (IOB-NST) and Model-Optimisation-Based Offline Neural Methods (MOB-NST). IOB-NST methods, exemplified by DeepDream, rely on iterative optimization to achieve artistic image transformations, while MOB-NST methods optimize a generative model for faster stylization through a single forward pass. The work by Gatys et al. is highlighted as a foundational NST algorithm that combines content and style components by leveraging CNN representations and Gram-based summary statistics for style characterization.

Overall, the paper provides a structured overview of these techniques, discussing their strengths and limitations in the context of texture modeling and image reconstruction.

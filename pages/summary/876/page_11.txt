This paper discusses the evaluation methodologies for Neural Style Transfer (NST) algorithms, emphasizing the importance of both qualitative and quantitative assessments. The authors explore a range of style images representing various art movements, as detailed in a table listing notable artworks and their creators. The study includes a comparison of NST algorithms based on offline model optimization and highlights previous work in audio style transfer.

The evaluation methodology consists of two major types: qualitative evaluation, which relies on aesthetic judgments from observers, and quantitative evaluation, focused on measurable metrics such as time complexity and loss variation. The experimental setup includes ten style images and twenty content images, chosen from established benchmark datasets to ensure a diverse range of characteristics.

To ensure fairness in comparisons, the authors adhere to specific principles during their experiments, including using provided implementations from original studies and maintaining consistent content and style weights across algorithms. They also emphasize the importance of default parameters in assessing algorithm effectiveness and note additional implementation details relevant to the methodologies used.

Overall, the paper aims to provide a comprehensive evaluation of NST algorithms, contributing to the ongoing discussion on effective methods for assessing artistic stylization in images.

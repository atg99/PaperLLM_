In this paper section, the authors discuss the applications and impacts of Neural Style Transfer (NST) in social communication, user-assisted creation tools, and entertainment production. 

NST has gained significant attention due to its popularity on social media platforms, with applications like Prisma leading the way. These tools allow users to create and share stylized artworks, fostering new forms of social interaction. The sharing of these creations not only enhances connections among users but also provides valuable feedback to researchers, assisting in algorithm improvements. Furthermore, the demand for real-time NST in video processing has spurred advancements in deep learning systems, such as Caffe2Go.

The authors also highlight the potential of NST as a user-assisted creation tool for artists and designers, streamlining the process of generating artwork and design elements. While current applications in this area are limited, the authors see promising future opportunities.

In the entertainment industry, NST can significantly reduce production costs and time for animations and games by automating the stylization of frames. The paper cites several studies that explore NST applications in film and 3D rendering, illustrating the potential for NST to revolutionize creative processes in entertainment.

Lastly, the authors identify future challenges for NST, particularly in aesthetic evaluation methodologies. They emphasize the need for reliable criteria to assess the effectiveness of NST algorithms, as current evaluations often rely on subjective visual comparisons that can vary greatly among observers. Addressing these challenges is crucial as the field of NST evolves.

The paper discusses the results of a qualitative evaluation of the MSPM-MOB-NST model, as illustrated in Figures 7 and 8. It references various studies, including works by Dumoulin et al., Chen et al., Li et al., and Zhang and Dana, to categorize the results into different groups. Figure 7 presents example results of the model using content images from a benchmark dataset developed by Mould and Rosin, while Figure 8 shows saliency detection outcomes produced by employing a discriminative regional feature integration approach from Wang et al. The detailed information regarding the style images used is provided in Table 1, emphasizing the significance of both the content and style elements in the evaluation process.

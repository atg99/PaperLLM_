The paper also addresses training times for the algorithms, indicating that they vary significantly, with most MOB-NST algorithms requiring several hours to train a single model, while others like Chen and Schmidt’s can take days. Incremental learning methods are noted to reduce training time further.

Lastly, the paper touches on loss comparisons during training for algorithms sharing the same loss function, suggesting that analyzing training curves can aid researchers in evaluating the performance of different approaches.

In conclusion, the paper systematically evaluates the efficiency and effectiveness of NST algorithms, providing insights into their operational characteristics and implications for future research.

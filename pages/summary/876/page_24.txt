The paper references a wide array of studies and methodologies related to image stylization, style transfer, and various applications of neural networks in visual arts and computer vision. Key contributions include:

1. Li et al. propose a closed-form solution for photorealistic image stylization, enhancing the efficiency of style transfer techniques.
2. Zhang et al. focus on style transfer specifically for anime sketches using an enhanced residual U-Net and an auxiliary classifier GAN.
3. Liao et al. introduce a deep image analogy approach for visual attribute transfer, which emphasizes the role of deep learning in modifying visual characteristics.
4. Jiang and Fu present a fashion style generator, showcasing the intersection of fashion and artificial intelligence.
5. Several works explore neural style transfer in non-visual domains, such as audio spectrograms by Verma and Smith, and time-domain audio style transfer by Mital.
6. Mould and Rosin contribute to the evaluation of stylization methods through benchmark image sets.
7. Zhu et al. introduce cycle-consistent adversarial networks for unpaired image-to-image translation, highlighting advancements in generative adversarial networks.
8. Research on disentangled representations and generative models, such as those by Higgins et al. and Feng et al., reflects ongoing efforts to understand and manipulate complex visual concepts.

This collection of works illustrates the rapid advancement in the fields of non-photorealistic rendering, image manipulation, and machine learning, emphasizing the importance of innovative algorithms in enhancing visual creativity and realism in digital media.

In summary, the paper discusses various advancements in Neural Style Transfer (NST) across different media, including stereoscopic images, portraits, videos, characters, photorealistic images, attributes, fashion, and audio. Each category addresses specific challenges, such as preserving structural integrity in portraits, ensuring temporal consistency in videos, and achieving photorealism without distortions. Techniques like disparity loss, gain maps, temporal consistency losses, and GANs are employed to enhance the effectiveness of style transfer. Overall, the work illustrates the ongoing evolution and application of NST in diverse fields, driven by the need for high-quality artistic transformations.

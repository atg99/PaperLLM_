The paper discusses various approaches to neural style transfer (NST) and presents qualitative evaluations of the models IOB-NST and PSPM-MOB-NST. It references works by Gatys et al., Johnson et al., Ulyanov et al., and Li and Wand, highlighting their contributions to the field. Figures included in the paper illustrate the results of these models using content images from a benchmark dataset and style images sourced from the public domain. Additionally, the paper presents saliency detection results related to the qualitative evaluations, produced using a discriminative regional feature integration approach. The detailed information about the style images is provided in a referenced table, underscoring the systematic evaluation of the models’ performance.

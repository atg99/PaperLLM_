The paper discusses the performance comparison of various neural style transfer (NST) algorithms, specifically focusing on two popular mobile-based NST approaches. It presents training curves illustrating total loss, style loss, and content loss for these algorithms, highlighting their convergence speeds and effectiveness in minimizing losses. The results indicate that while both algorithms converge similarly, one outperforms the other in minimizing style loss.

Additionally, the paper addresses the scalability of style transfer models, noting that the ability to handle a wide range of styles is contingent upon the similarity of those styles. It provides statistics on the scalability of different models, ranging from 32 to 1000 styles, depending on the algorithm.

Lastly, the paper emphasizes the practical applications of NST, noting its commercial viability and various usage scenarios stemming from its ability to generate visually appealing stylized images. A summary of the advantages and disadvantages of the algorithms discussed is provided in a table for clarity.

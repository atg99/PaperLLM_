The paper presents the Relation-aware Graph Attention Network (ReGAT), designed to enhance visual question answering (VQA) by modeling multi-type object relations through a question-adaptive attention mechanism. Key findings are illustrated through attention maps, demonstrating how ReGAT captures interactions between objects and aligns image regions with questions effectively. The visualization of attention weights reveals that the graph attention mechanism improves the representation of object interactions, while the question-adaptive mechanism sharpens focus on relevant regions. 

Figures included in the paper show examples of how different relation types improve performance, highlighting semantic relations that resonate with keywords in questions, and spatial relations that depict geometric positions between objects. Implicit relations are also visualized, capturing both spatial and semantic interactions among regions.

The results indicate that ReGAT achieves state-of-the-art performance on the VQA 2.0 and VQA-CP v2 datasets and is compatible with existing VQA models, allowing for easy integration. The authors propose future work to explore more effective fusion of the identified relations to address specific question types.

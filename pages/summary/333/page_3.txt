The paper discusses the potential for artificial intelligence, specifically language models like GPT-3, to enhance patient triage in healthcare by prioritizing symptoms based on their severity. It emphasizes the importance of preparing for the integration of such technologies into clinical settings, while acknowledging the current limitations and risks associated with their use. The authors argue that rather than fearing advancements in AI, we should focus on harnessing them for the benefit of humanity. They caution that while AI can assist in medical decision-making, it is not yet ready to replace human providers in clinics. The paper is supported by various references that explore the capabilities, ethical considerations, and implications of AI in healthcare and other domains. The authors contributed equally to the manuscript and declare no competing interests. The work was funded by the National Human Genome Research Institute and the National Institutes of Health.

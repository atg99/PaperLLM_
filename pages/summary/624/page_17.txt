Figure 10 presents examples of fruit detection results that illustrate the impact of incorporating range-corrected signal intensity (S) and depth (D) information. Each sample, labeled a), b), and c), displays six variations of fruit detection outcomes based on different input data types. The first row shows results using RGB p, the second row uses S, the third row employs D, the fourth row combines multi-modal RGB p and S, the fifth row utilizes all modalities (RGB p, S, and D), and the last row features high-resolution images with all modalities (RGB hr, S, and D). True positive detections are indicated in green, false positives in red, and false negatives in blue, highlighting how the inclusion of additional data types enhances detection accuracy.

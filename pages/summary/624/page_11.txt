The results indicate that the choice of anchor scales and aspect ratios significantly impacts the detection performance of the model. The average precision, precision, recall, and F1-score were calculated for various configurations, revealing that certain combinations of anchor scales and aspect ratios yielded better performance metrics. Specifically, the 8-scale anchor configuration with aspect ratios of 1 showed promising results across different modalities.

In terms of image modalities, the analysis demonstrated that RGB images provided a solid foundation for fruit detection, while the combination of RGB with depth (D) images improved detection accuracy further. The use of 5-channel multi-modal images also showed potential, suggesting that integrating additional sensory information may enhance fruit detection capabilities.

Overall, the findings highlight the effectiveness of the proposed fruit detection methodology using Faster R-CNN and the importance of carefully selecting anchor scales and image modalities for optimal performance. The implementation of the model in the PyTorch framework facilitates future research and applications in automated fruit detection, contributing to advancements in agricultural technology.

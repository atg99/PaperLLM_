The paper evaluates model generalization and overfitting through training and validation loss assessments after each epoch. The dataset comprises 619 training images, 155 validation images, and 193 test images. Figure 7 illustrates the loss functions for different image modalities. It is observed that when using only color modality (RGB projected images), the model begins to overfit earlier compared to models that incorporate signal intensity (S) and depth (D) channels. The inclusion of S and D channels allows for extended training iterations without overfitting.

While the training loss was lower with just color images, the validation loss was minimized when combining all modalities, indicating that S and D channels enhance model generalization. The best validation results were achieved using five-channel multi-modal images. Furthermore, when comparing RGB projected images with high-resolution RGB images, a notable improvement in both training and validation losses was observed with high-resolution images. The performance enhancement was further increased when S and D channels were added, though not as significantly as with multi-modal images using RGB projected images. The findings suggest that utilizing higher resolution depth sensors in future RGB-D systems could further enhance detection performance.

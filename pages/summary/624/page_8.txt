The paper discusses the normalization of image data for a network to facilitate fast convergence, specifically using an RGB format where pixel values are normalized between 0 and 255. The RGB images are saved in 8-bit format, while depth and intensity images are stored in 64-bit format to prevent data precision loss. Ground truth fruit locations were annotated using the Pychet Labeller toolbox, identifying a total of 12,839 apples across the dataset. Given the high number of fruits per image and their relatively small size, each image (1600 x 1080 pixels) was divided into 9 sub-images of 548 x 373 pixels, with a 20-pixel overlap to prevent partial fruit segmentation at the boundaries. The dataset consists of 967 sub-images categorized into training, validation, and test sets. The KFuji RGB-DS dataset, along with annotations, is publicly available. The dataset configuration indicates that 64% of the sub-images are used for training, 16% for validation, and 20% for testing, maintaining a total of 12,839 labeled fruits.

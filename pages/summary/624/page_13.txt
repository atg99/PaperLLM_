Section 4.2 of the paper focuses on anchor optimization in the Faster R-CNN model when applied to multi-modal images of small spherical objects. The original anchor scales of 8, 16, and 32 from the Faster R-CNN paper were not tailored for the new dataset, prompting an evaluation of different anchor scales (2, 4, 8, 16, and 32) and aspect ratios (1:2, 1:1, and 2:1). 

Results in Table 3 indicate that anchor scales of 16 and 32 resulted in the poorest performance, while configurations with scales of 4 and aspect ratio 1:1 yielded the highest average precision (AP) at 94.8%. Squared anchors (1:1) consistently produced the best results, aligning with the spherical nature of the objects being detected. 

While combining various anchor scales and aspect ratios slightly reduced the frame rate due to increased convolutional operations, the overall computational efficiency remained stable, as the number of object proposals was capped at 100. Based on these findings, the study selects anchor scales of 4 and aspect ratios of 1:1 for subsequent sections, as this configuration demonstrated optimal performance.

The data preparation stage involves generating a 3D point cloud that incorporates RGB and backscattered intensity data, alongside the raw RGB images. Each capture is processed independently to ensure accurate representation of the scene. 

The Kinect v2 sensor, employed for this study, operates with specific specifications that impact its performance, particularly under varying lighting conditions. The RGB channel features a resolution of 1920 x 1080 pixels with a field-of-view of 84.1 degrees by 53.8 degrees, while the IR and depth channels have a lower resolution of 512 x 424 pixels and a field-of-view of 70 degrees by 60 degrees. The working range of the sensor extends from 0.5 to 8 meters, and it is mounted on a mobile platform developed by the GRAP-UdL-AT research group, which allows for adjustable forward speeds up to 0.5 km/h and sensor height adjustments between 1 and 3 meters. 

It is noted that the performance of the depth sensor diminishes in direct sunlight, highlighting the importance of considering environmental factors during data collection.

The paper discusses the use of the Faster R-CNN object detection network for fruit detection, specifically focusing on its application to a dataset of multi-modal images. Figure 4 presents samples of these images along with their corresponding fruit location ground truth indicated by red bounding boxes. The methodology leverages Faster R-CNN, allowing for performance comparisons with previous studies that utilized the same network for detecting fruits. Originally developed for color images, Faster R-CNN has shown significant results on standard datasets, achieving mean average precision scores of 78.8% on PASCAL VOC and 42.7% on COCO datasets. 

The architecture consists of two key modules: a region proposal network (RPN) that identifies regions of interest likely to contain objects, and a classification network that classifies these regions. The RPN uses feature maps from initial convolutional layers to generate promising ROIs through convolutional and fully connected layers. These regions are then cropped from feature maps via crop pooling and processed by the classification network and a regressor to determine the likelihood of a region containing an apple or being background, while also refining the bounding box.

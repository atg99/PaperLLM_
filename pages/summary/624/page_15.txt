The paper presents the results of fruit detection using various input image modalities evaluated through the Faster R-CNN model. The performance metrics include Precision, Recall, F1-score, Average Precision (AP), and frames per second, with results summarized in Table 4. A confidence threshold of 0.85 was determined from the Precision and Recall curves. The number of training epochs was selected based on loss curves to avoid overfitting.

The results indicate that the combination of RGB p, S, and D modalities yielded the highest F1-score and AP at 0.866 and 91.2%, respectively, after 10 epochs. Notably, RGB hr+S+D achieved the best performance metrics overall, with a Precision of 0.897, Recall of 0.899, F1-score of 0.898, and AP of 94.8%.

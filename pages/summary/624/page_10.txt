The paper discusses modifications made to the Faster R-CNN architecture to enhance its performance for a specific dataset. Key changes include the implementation of multi-modal input and adaptation of anchor scales. The VGG-16 model, pre-trained on the ImageNet dataset, serves as the backbone for the initial convolutional layers. The input layer was adjusted to accommodate 5-channel images, requiring an increase in the depth of filters from 3 to 5 and the random initialization of additional weights for the new channels.

For region proposal generation, the Region Proposal Network (RPN) evaluates boxes at each image position with a stride of 16 pixels. The original Faster R-CNN utilized three anchor scales (8, 16, and 32) and three aspect ratios (1:1, 1:2, and 2:1). However, since the dataset in question contains smaller objects, the study explored optimal anchor scales, including smaller ones (2 and 4), while maintaining the original aspect ratios. Additionally, two configurations were tested: one using only the 1:1 aspect ratio and another combining all three aspect ratios. The network resizes input images to 600 pixels on the shortest side, despite the original image size being 548 x 373 pixels.

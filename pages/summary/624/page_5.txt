The study focuses on the relationship between emitted power and received intensity in the context of 3D data acquisition using RGB-D sensors, particularly the Kinect v2. The emitted power is influenced by various factors such as the receiving area, object reflectance, and optical efficiency. The received power is range-corrected to facilitate comparisons from different distances. The research establishes a linear correlation between the digitized intensity from the Kinect and the received power, emphasizing the dependence of the range-corrected signal on reflectance.

Data acquisition was performed in a Fuji apple orchard in Catalonia, Spain, shortly before harvesting. Two Kinect v2 cameras were utilized at different heights to capture comprehensive data across the tree's height. The sensors collected color images, depth images for 3D point cloud generation, and IR backscattered intensity. Custom software was developed to automate data collection and ensure accurate synchronization of RGB and intensity data, with all data captured under controlled nighttime conditions.

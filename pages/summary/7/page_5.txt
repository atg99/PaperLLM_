The paper investigates the impact of importance weighting in deep learning models, specifically focusing on logistic regression and multilayer perceptrons (MLPs). It presents various scenarios where these models are trained with different regularization techniques, such as L2 regularization and dropout.

In Figure 2, the authors compare the performance of logistic regression and MLPs when both are subjected to L2 regularization, highlighting how this technique influences the models' ability to generalize and manage overfitting. 

Figure 3 further explores the scenario by showcasing the MLP trained with dropout, a method aimed at preventing overfitting by randomly omitting neurons during training. This figure emphasizes the role of dropout in enhancing the robustness of the MLP compared to the previous figures.

Overall, the paper underscores the significance of importance weighting and regularization techniques in optimizing deep learning models, suggesting that these methods can substantially affect model performance and generalization capabilities.

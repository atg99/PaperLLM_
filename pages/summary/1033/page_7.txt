The paper discusses the role of various stakeholders in ensuring ethical practices in artificial intelligence (AI) development and implementation. It emphasizes the necessity of robust mechanisms and adequate resources for relevant agencies to enforce compliance with ethical standards. Additionally, it highlights the influence of minor actors, such as professional societies and insurance companies, through codes of conduct and risk assessments.

The author argues that these approaches extend beyond mere ethical principles, advocating for a thorough and critical exploration of the implications of AI technologies. This includes recognizing both the potential benefits and risks associated with their use.

The acknowledgments section credits the reviewers for their constructive feedback, which prompted an expansion of the alternatives section and clarification of key arguments. The paper is a solo-authored work, and the author has declared no conflicts of interest. It is published under a Creative Commons license, allowing for use and distribution with proper attribution. 

Overall, the paper contributes to the ongoing discourse on AI ethics by proposing a framework that includes both regulatory and societal dimensions to address the complexities of AI's impact.

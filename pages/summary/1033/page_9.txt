The paper discusses the ethical considerations surrounding artificial intelligence (AI) and emphasizes the importance of addressing algorithmic bias and accountability. It highlights a growing concern among experts regarding the lack of ethics education for computer programmers, which can lead to unexamined biases in AI systems. The authors advocate for a comprehensive framework for internal algorithmic auditing to close the AI accountability gap. Various studies and recommendations are referenced, including calls for context-sensitive ethical frameworks and guidelines for creating trustworthy, human-centered AI systems. The importance of interdisciplinary approaches to fairness in machine learning is also emphasized, as well as the potential pitfalls of "ethics-washing" and "transparency-washing" in the tech industry. Overall, the paper calls for a more robust integration of ethical considerations into AI development and governance to ensure responsible and equitable outcomes.

The referenced works focus on advancements in the classification and detection of lung cancer using various deep learning techniques, particularly convolutional neural networks (CNNs). Key contributions include the application of models like AlexNet and VGG-16 for improving accuracy in lung nodule classification from CT images. Studies highlight methods such as segmentation, texture and shape feature fusion, and transfer learning to enhance diagnostic capabilities. Notable research includes the development of algorithms for automated classification and the integration of statistical and shape-based features for lung nodule detection. The collective findings emphasize the potential of deep learning to significantly improve early detection and diagnosis of lung cancer, showcasing a range of innovative approaches and architectures in this critical area of medical imaging.

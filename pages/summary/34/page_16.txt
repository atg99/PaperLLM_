This paper presents a comprehensive review of various intelligent systems and algorithms designed for the detection and classification of cancers, particularly breast and lung cancers, utilizing deep learning and machine learning techniques. 

Key studies highlighted include Siddiqui et al.'s work on breast cancer prediction using fusion and deep learning methods, and their subsequent research on cloud-based intelligent prediction of breast cancer stages. Other notable contributions include Carcagnì et al. who conducted a systematic investigation of deep architectures for skin lesion classification, and Zhang et al. who developed an optimized convolutional neural network for skin cancer diagnosis.

The paper also discusses the LUNA16 challenge, which focused on the automatic detection of pulmonary nodules in CT images, emphasizing the validation and comparison of different algorithms. Furthermore, it references the ACR-STR practice parameter for lung cancer screening, and highlights several innovative approaches such as the intelligent immune clonal optimization algorithm for pulmonary nodule classification and the application of machine learning in clinical routines for lung cancer.

Overall, the review underscores the evolving landscape of cancer detection technologies, emphasizing the need for continued research and development in intelligent systems to enhance diagnostic accuracy and clinical outcomes.

In the study, the performance of various deep learning models including LeNet, AlexNet, VGG16, ResNet-50, and Inception-V1 was evaluated on the LUNA16 dataset using different optimizers: SGD, Adam, and RMSprop. 

With the SGD optimizer, AlexNet achieved the highest accuracy at 99.09%, followed by ResNet-50 at 98.05%, Inception-V1 at 97.99%, LeNet at 97.47%, and VGG16 at 94.07%. 

When the Adam optimizer was applied, LeNet again led with 97.26% accuracy, while AlexNet decreased to 96.13%. VGG16 recorded the lowest at 93.71%, with ResNet-50 and Inception-V1 achieving 97.24% and 96.12% respectively.

Lastly, using the RMSprop optimizer, LeNet obtained 95.27%, AlexNet 95.54%, while the results for VGG16 were not fully detailed. This evaluation highlights the varying effectiveness of different optimizers across multiple deep learning architectures in medical image detection tasks.

The paper presents a validation statistical analysis of various Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) architectures, specifically LeNet, AlexNet, VGG16, and ResNet 50, utilizing different optimizers (RMSprop, Adam, and SGD). 

For the LeNet model, accuracy ranged from 95.06% to 95.92% across different optimizers, with the SGD optimizer yielding the highest accuracy of 95.92%. The respective sensitivity and specificity values were also reported, indicating strong performance metrics.

In contrast, the AlexNet model demonstrated superior performance, especially with the SGD optimizer, achieving an accuracy of 97.42%. Its sensitivity, specificity, and other statistical parameters were consistently high, confirming its robustness compared to the other optimizers.

The VGG16 model, while slightly lower in overall accuracy at 93.56% with the SGD optimizer, still exhibited commendable sensitivity and specificity metrics. The performance across different optimizers was assessed, showing that SGD outperformed the other two optimizers.

The paper underscores the effectiveness of optimizer choice in enhancing the performance of CNN architectures for validation tasks, with SGD generally yielding the best results across the models analyzed.

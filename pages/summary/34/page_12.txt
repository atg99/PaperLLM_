The paper evaluates the performance of various convolutional neural network (CNN) architectures—LeNet, AlexNet, VGG16, ResNet-50, and Inception-V1—using different optimizers including RMSprop, SGD, and Adam on the LUNA16 dataset. Results indicate that AlexNet with the SGD optimizer achieved the highest accuracy of 97.42%, while ResNet-50 and Inception-V1 also showed strong performance with 96.35% and 95.06% validation accuracy respectively when using SGD. 

In the analysis, RMSprop yielded lower accuracy compared to SGD, with the best results being 95.53% for ResNet-50. The Adam optimizer produced mixed results, with AlexNet achieving 95.71% accuracy. The study also implemented a five-fold cross-validation method, where AlexNet with SGD demonstrated superior performance across several metrics, including sensitivity, specificity, and F1-score, achieving a maximum accuracy of 95.73% and a 4.27% misclassification rate.

Overall, the findings underscore the efficacy of AlexNet with the SGD optimizer in lung cancer detection compared to other architectures and optimization methods, establishing it as a leading model in the field based on the evaluated metrics.

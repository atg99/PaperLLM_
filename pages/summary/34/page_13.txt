The study compared five CNN models—LeNet, AlexNet, VGG16, ResNet-50, and Inception-V1—using the SGD optimizer and other optimizers like RMSprop and Adam. The validation statistical analysis indicated that AlexNet with SGD achieved the highest accuracy of 97.42% and a low misclassification rate of 2.58%. Other architectures showed varying performance, with ResNet-50 following closely at 95.28% accuracy. The paper emphasizes the importance of early lung cancer detection and suggests that deep learning models, particularly AlexNet, can significantly aid in improving diagnostic accuracy, thus enhancing survival rates. The results underline the effectiveness of using advanced CNN architectures in medical image analysis.

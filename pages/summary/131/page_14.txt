The paper discusses the growing concerns surrounding the ethical use of artificial intelligence (AI) as it becomes more influential in society. In response to these concerns, five major tech companies—Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Facebook, and IBM—established the Partnership on AI to address ethical issues and public perceptions related to AI technology. New trustees, including Dario Amodei from OpenAI and Carol Rose from the ACLU, have joined this initiative. The paper highlights potential challenges the AI industry may face, such as the displacement of white-collar jobs, the erosion of trust in public media, and the integration of AI into public institutions like courts and hospitals.

Furthermore, the paper details the construction of a dataset using GPT-4 to generate prompt-answer pairs for instruction fine-tuning and evaluation. This dataset includes specific prompts designed to assess various aspects of text and includes general prompts for tasks like summarization, keyword extraction, and continuation writing. The evaluation methodology for GPT-4 is also outlined, emphasizing a holistic approach to comparing AI-generated responses while mitigating bias in the assessment process. The results indicate that the GPT-4 model may favor longer responses, which could affect performance evaluations based on the dataset generated from shorter responses.

The paper discusses the evaluation of two types of Information-Centric AI Engines (ICAE) in generating summaries based on provided prompts. It showcases examples where a pretrained ICAE and a non-pretrained ICAE were asked to respond to specific prompts derived from passages. The evaluations highlight the accuracy of responses, with the pretrained ICAE providing correct information while the non-pretrained ICAE often yielded incorrect data. The examples illustrate the importance of context and accurate information retrieval in AI-generated responses, emphasizing the role of pretrained models in enhancing performance in summarization tasks.

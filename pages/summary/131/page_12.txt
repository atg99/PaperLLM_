The paper presents the training configuration for the ICAE model, detailing the pretraining and fine-tuning processes using a text continuation objective. The model is trained on 8 Nvidia A100 GPUs with a focus on optimizing performance through specific hyperparameters. Key hyperparameters include the AdamW optimizer, a learning rate of 1e-4 for pretraining and 5e-5 for fine-tuning, a batch size of 256, a warmup period of 300, and a total of 200k updates for pretraining and 30k for fine-tuning. The model employs bf16 precision and utilizes a gradient clipping norm of 2.0 to improve stability during training. The configurations aim to enhance the model's capabilities, aligning with advancements in large language models as discussed in the referenced literature.

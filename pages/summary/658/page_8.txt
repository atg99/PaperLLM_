This paper presents Grid-GCN, a novel approach for efficient and scalable point cloud learning. The method leverages Coverage-Aware Grid Query (CAGQ) for effective data structuring and computation by employing voxelization, which significantly reduces the cost associated with data structuring while ensuring comprehensive coverage of the point cloud. Additionally, a graph convolution module known as Grid Context Aggregation (GCA) is introduced to integrate context features and coverage information during processing.

The experiments conducted on various datasets, including S3DIS and ModelNet, demonstrate that Grid-GCN outperforms existing models in terms of accuracy and speed. Specifically, it shows an average speed-up of up to 8 times compared to other models, particularly excelling in handling large-scale point clouds with increased input sizes. Ablation studies further reveal that reducing output channels from GridConv layers can enhance speed without sacrificing accuracy, indicating the efficiency of the proposed architecture.

Overall, Grid-GCN represents a significant advancement in point cloud processing, achieving state-of-the-art results while maintaining high efficiency, making it suitable for real-time applications in large-scale environments.

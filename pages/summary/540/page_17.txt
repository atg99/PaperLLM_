The paper presents a comparative analysis of K-means clustering performance using various feature representations on text and image datasets. In Table 12, the Normalized Mutual Information (NMI) and F-score for K-means clustering on the R8 and 20news datasets are reported, showcasing the effectiveness of different feature representations: Doc2Vec, RDP, and a combination of Doc2Vec+RDP. The results indicate that RDP significantly enhances NMI performance on the R8 dataset compared to Doc2Vec and Doc2Vec+RDP. For the 20news dataset, RDP shows a slight improvement over Doc2Vec but limited overall performance.

In the analysis of the Olivetti image dataset, the paper compares the RDP method with the advanced representation learning technique RotNet and its variants. Notably, RDP demonstrates superior performance in both NMI and F-score metrics compared to RotNet and its simplified versions. The original RotNet architecture struggles due to the small sample size of the Olivetti dataset, leading to suboptimal representation learning. The results emphasize that RDP can provide more expressive representations than RotNet, particularly when applied to lower-dimensional spaces.

Overall, the findings suggest that RDP is a robust feature representation method that outperforms advanced techniques like RotNet in specific contexts, particularly when data availability is limited.

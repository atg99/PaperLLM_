The paper presents an analysis of the representation dimensions and their impact on the performance of a method called RDP across 14 datasets, evaluated through AUC-ROC and AUC-PR metrics. Figures illustrate how varying representation dimensions from 25 to 100 affect the AUC-ROC and AUC-PR results, with a general recommendation for a representation dimension of 1,024 for clustering tasks due to its higher information requirement compared to anomaly detection.

Additionally, the study examines the computational efficiency of RDP by comparing its runtime with competing methods during testing, noting that training times can vary significantly based on different strategies used in deep learning. The experiments were conducted on a high-performance computing server to ensure accurate comparisons. Overall, the findings suggest that while RDP shows promising results, the choice of representation dimension is crucial for optimal performance in different tasks.

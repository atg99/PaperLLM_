The paper examines the performance of various representation learning methods, particularly focusing on RotNet variants and the impact of incorporating a random distance prediction loss (RDP) in classification tasks. The evaluation utilized a feed-forward three-layer neural network model, applying the same datasets as in previous clustering tasks. The study compared RDP against other methods including HLLE, SRP, AE, and COP, using F-score as the performance metric.

Results indicated that the model utilizing only the random distance prediction loss (RDPnLclu aux) consistently performed well across all five datasets. The addition of the clustering loss (nLclu aux) in the RDP model provided performance enhancements on certain datasets like 20news, although it could negatively impact results on others. The findings suggest that introducing an additional hyperparameter to balance the importance of these losses could optimize performance further. The F-score results across datasets are summarized in a table, highlighting the competitive performance of RDP compared to other methods.

The paper presents quantitative experiments on learned transformations utilizing the StyleGAN FFHQ face generator. It explores various transformations such as shifting in the X and Y directions, zooming, and luminance adjustments. The results indicate that certain zoom levels do not yield detectable faces, which are represented as zeros in the distribution for those values. The dlib face detector is employed to obtain bounding box coordinates for the analysis. The findings highlight the effectiveness of the model in handling transformations and the limitations encountered with specific zoom operations. Overall, the experiments contribute to understanding the capabilities and constraints of the StyleGAN in generating and manipulating facial images.

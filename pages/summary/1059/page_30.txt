The paper presents quantitative experiments on the steerability of a Deep Convolutional Generative Adversarial Network (DCGAN) trained on the MNIST dataset, specifically focusing on transformations such as Zoom and Shift X. Figures 31 and 32 illustrate the results, where odd rows display the target images and even rows show the transformations learned by the model. The experiments aim to demonstrate the effectiveness of the DCGAN in generating desired transformations based on input conditions. The approach utilizes an optimization framework that combines multiple argmin operations to achieve the desired results in image generation and transformation.

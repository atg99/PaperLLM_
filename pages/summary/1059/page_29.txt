The paper discusses the optimization of color transformations within the StyleGAN framework, comparing the effects of manipulating the w and z latent spaces. Figures 28 and 29 illustrate qualitative results from optimizing color transformations and contrast adjustments, showcasing the differences in outcomes when using StyleGAN versus BigGAN. Additionally, Figure 30 presents examples of linear transformations that combine zoom and shift operations, highlighting the effectiveness of jointly learned transformations compared to separately trained ones. The results emphasize the potential of StyleGAN in generating varied visual outputs through targeted latent space manipulations.

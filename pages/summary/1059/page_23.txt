The paper presents a series of quantitative experiments focused on learned transformations in the context of the StyleGAN car generator. Key transformations analyzed include luminance adjustments, shifts in the X and Y axes, and zoom effects. Each transformation is evaluated across a range of parameters represented by alpha (α) values, demonstrating the impact on the generated images. The results are illustrated through probability density functions (PDFs) and intersections that highlight differences between the data and model outputs across various settings. The findings contribute to understanding the effectiveness of StyleGAN in generating diverse and high-quality images through controlled transformations. This work emphasizes the model's ability to manipulate image characteristics systematically, showcasing its potential in generative modeling.

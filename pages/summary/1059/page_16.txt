This paper, presented at ICLR 2020, investigates the distribution of generated images compared to the training dataset across various transformations such as luminance, zoom, and shifts in bounding box centers. Figures illustrate the statistical comparisons, highlighting how the model-generated images align or deviate from the original dataset.

The analysis includes perceptual distances between images produced by consecutive transformations using BigGAN. A sample of 1000 images from different categories was transformed along a learned linear trajectory, with perceptual distance metrics (LPIPS) plotted to assess the visual differences. The results indicate that certain transformations, particularly the 3D rotation, show more pronounced effects when their magnitude is increased.

Additionally, the paper features bounding boxes for randomly selected classes from ImageNet training images, providing a visual context for the transformations discussed. Overall, the findings contribute to understanding the effectiveness of generative models in producing visually coherent images that reflect the characteristics of the training data.

The paper presented at ICLR 2020 discusses the capabilities of BigGAN in generating high-quality images through various transformations. It highlights qualitative examples that illustrate how different manipulations, such as rotating, shifting, and zooming in 2D and 3D spaces, can affect the output. The use of a linear trajectory and the LPIPS objective for assessing image quality is emphasized, demonstrating the effectiveness of these transformations in producing diverse and visually appealing results across randomly selected categories.

The paper presents findings from experiments conducted using BigGAN, focusing on various image transformation techniques. Figure 13 illustrates bounding boxes for randomly selected classes, showcasing the effects of model-generated images subjected to zoom and horizontal and vertical shift transformations with random values. Figure 14 displays linear walks in BigGAN, specifically trained to minimize LPIPS loss, and compares these samples to those obtained using a linear walk with L2 loss as shown in Figure 1. Figure 15 highlights nonlinear walks in BigGAN, which are trained to minimize L2 loss for color and LPIPS loss for other transformations. This figure also provides a comparison to the samples in Figure 1, where the linear walk vector is replaced with a nonlinear walk to demonstrate different transformation effects. These illustrations collectively emphasize the impact of different loss functions and transformation strategies in generating high-quality images.

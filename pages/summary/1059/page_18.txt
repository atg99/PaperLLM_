This conference paper presented at ICLR 2020 discusses quantitative experiments involving nonlinear walks in BigGAN, a generative adversarial network. The authors analyze the attributes of images generated from the raw model output G(z) and compare these with distributions from a learned transformation model. Key metrics include the intersection area between G(z) and the transformation model, the Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) score for transformed images, and scatterplots that illustrate the relationship between dataset variability and model transformation extent. The experiments are aimed at understanding how well the model can capture and transform image characteristics, highlighting the effectiveness of the learned transformations in improving image quality and diversity.

The paper discusses the use of BigGAN for generating high-quality images, showcasing qualitative examples of various categories. It emphasizes the effectiveness of applying transformations such as color adjustments, zooming, shifting in both X and Y directions, and rotating both in 2D and 3D. These transformations were evaluated using a linear trajectory and an L2 objective to assess their impact on the generated images. The results illustrate the model's ability to produce diverse and visually appealing outputs across different categories.

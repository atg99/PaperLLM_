The paper presents a study on BigGAN, showcasing the capabilities of the model through qualitative examples. It highlights the effects of various transformations, including rotation in 2D and 3D, shifts along the X and Y axes, and changes in zoom and color. Figure 19 illustrates these transformations applied to randomly selected categories, emphasizing the model's ability to generate diverse and high-quality images based on nonlinear trajectories. The findings underscore the flexibility and robustness of BigGAN in handling complex image generation tasks.

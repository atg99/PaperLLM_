The references listed encompass a range of studies and papers that contribute to the understanding of dropout, regularization techniques, and collaborative filtering in deep learning and neural networks. 

Key concepts include:

1. **Dropout and Regularization**: Helmbold and Long discuss the fundamental differences between dropout and weight decay, emphasizing their distinct roles in preventing overfitting. Hinton et al. highlight dropout as a method to prevent co-adaptation of feature detectors, which is crucial for improving neural network performance.

2. **Collaborative Filtering**: Various works, including those by Hu, Koren, and Volinsky, as well as Liang et al., explore collaborative filtering techniques for implicit feedback datasets and the use of variational autoencoders, respectively.

3. **Loss Landscapes and Dynamics**: Studies by Kunin et al. and Mehta et al. analyze the loss landscapes of regularized models, providing insights into the geometry of optimization in machine learning. Saxe et al. offer exact solutions to the nonlinear dynamics of learning in deep linear networks.

4. **Denoising Autoencoders**: Vincent and others delve into the theory of denoising autoencoders, discussing their ability to extract robust features and their connection to score matching.

5. **Adaptive Regularization**: Wager et al. and Wang et al. examine dropout training as a form of adaptive regularization, demonstrating its effectiveness in various settings.

Overall, these references collectively contribute to a deeper understanding of how dropout, autoencoders, and collaborative filtering techniques can be utilized to enhance model performance and robustness in machine learning applications.

The paper presents empirical results on the prediction accuracies of a linear autoencoder with a model rank of 1,000, trained using various regularization techniques on three datasets: ML-20M, Netflix, and MSD. The results, summarized in Table 1, highlight the effectiveness of different regularization strategies, particularly in terms of Recall and nDCG scores.

The findings indicate that standard L2-norm regularization (weight decay) yields the lowest accuracy across all datasets. In contrast, replacing weight decay with dropout-style L2-regularization significantly improves performance. Further enhancements are observed when item-specific scaling of L2-regularization is implemented, demonstrating the benefits of this approach.

The results also show that the dropout-based method (DLAE) and the enhanced dropout approach (EDLAE) yield comparable outcomes, affirming their equivalence in preventing overfitting towards the identity matrix. EDLAE outperforms DLAE, underscoring the importance of avoiding overfitting for accuracy gains.

The paper compares the performance of linear autoencoders with various deep non-linear models, revealing that well-regularized linear models can achieve competitive results. Notably, the differences in performance among regularization types are substantial, suggesting that they can be more significant than those between different model classes.

Figures illustrate that EDLAE consistently achieves the highest accuracy across different matrix ranks. A rank of approximately 1,000 is sufficient for optimal performance, validating the analytic derivation that near-orthogonality constraints provide an effective approximation for large model ranks. Conversely, when the rank is very low, prediction accuracy suffers due to limited model capacity.

As the model rank increases, the study highlights that a low-rank model can begin to overfit towards the identity even at relatively small ranks, emphasizing the need for appropriate regularization in training low-rank models. Overall, the results underscore the critical role of regularization methods in enhancing the performance of linear autoencoders in recommendation systems.

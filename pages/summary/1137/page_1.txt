The paper discusses the development of an intelligent medical diagnostic system for the early detection of renal cancer using fuzzy and neuro-fuzzy logic techniques. Renal cancer, which primarily affects older adults, poses significant health risks due to its often asymptomatic nature and the absence of reliable early detection tests. The proposed system employs a two-layer fuzzy inference model to assess whether a patient has renal cancer and to determine the current stage of the disease. The neuro-fuzzy approach utilizes Gaussian membership functions for the input variables, enhancing diagnostic precision. A comparative analysis of the system's performance indicates that the neuro-fuzzy model outperforms existing diagnostic methods, providing more accurate outcomes. The research highlights the critical need for early diagnosis to improve patient prognosis, particularly given the disease's potential to spread to surrounding organs.

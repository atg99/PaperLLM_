The paper discusses the development of an intelligent system utilizing Gaussian membership functions for various input and output variables. Specifically, it employs four Gaussian membership functions for input1, three for input2, input3, and input7, and two for inputs 4, 5, and 6. An example provided is the categorization of the tumor size input variable into two groups: smaller and larger. The adaptive neuro-fuzzy technique facilitates the automatic creation of these membership function groups during the system's training phase, which is based on the provided dataset. The paper includes graphical representations of the membership functions for inputs 1, 2, and 3.

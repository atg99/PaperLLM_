The paper presents a multi-layered inference system developed using MATLAB and the Mamdani fuzzy model of fuzzy logic. The system consists of two layers of inputs. The first layer includes factors such as smoking, dialysis, occupational exposure, and genetic or hereditary influences. The second layer comprises clinical indicators, including blood in urine, red blood cell count, flank pain, tumor size, the Von Hippel-Lindau gene, high blood pressure, and exposure to trichloroethylene. Input variables for both layers are detailed in Tables 1 to 3, which outline the ranges and semantic significance of each parameter. This structured methodology aims to improve the inference process in the context of health assessments related to the specified factors.

The paper discusses the use of Gaussian membership functions in a multi-layered inference system designed for the diagnosis of renal cancer. It details the membership functions for input variables, specifically smoking and haematuria, and presents their graphical representations. The output variables for layer 2 are also illustrated. 

Mathematical functions represent the statistical values for these input and output variables. The specific mathematical representation for the smoking variable is provided, highlighting its Gaussian nature. The equations reflect the relationship between the input variables and their respective membership functions, underscoring the structure and methodology used in the inference system for cancer diagnosis. Overall, the paper emphasizes the significance of these membership functions in enhancing diagnostic accuracy.

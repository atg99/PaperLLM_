The papers referenced focus on advancements in 3D object reconstruction using machine learning techniques. 

The first paper by Yan et al. introduces Perspective Transformer Nets, which enable single-view 3D object reconstruction without requiring 3D supervision. This approach leverages perspective transformations to learn the underlying structure of objects from 2D images, thus addressing the challenge of reconstructing 3D shapes from limited visual information.

The second paper by Zhang et al. explores the ability to reconstruct shapes from unseen classes. This research emphasizes the importance of generalization in 3D shape reconstruction, showcasing methods that allow models to learn and infer shapes that they have not been explicitly trained on. 

Both studies contribute to the field by enhancing the understanding of how to effectively reconstruct 3D objects from 2D representations, paving the way for more robust applications in computer vision and graphics.

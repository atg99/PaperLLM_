The paper references a variety of significant works related to 3D surface construction, point cloud reconstruction, and object detection. Key contributions include the Marching Cubes algorithm by Lorensen and Cline, which is foundational for high-resolution 3D surface construction. The 3D-LMNet framework by Mandikal et al. focuses on reconstructing 3D point clouds from single images using latent embedding matching. Additionally, Mescheder et al.'s Occupancy Networks present a novel approach to learning 3D reconstruction in function space.

Further advancements in object pose estimation are highlighted by Pavlakos et al., who utilize semantic keypoints for 6-DOF object pose determination. The evolution of deep learning techniques is evident in works like PointNet++ by Qi et al., which enhances feature learning on point sets, and Faster R-CNN by Ren et al., which aims for real-time object detection.

The paper also discusses various methods for 3D shape modeling and representation, such as the use of geometric structures in GEOMetrics by Smith et al., and the approach of learning shape abstractions through volumetric primitives by Tulsiani et al. The integration of self-supervised learning techniques for dense correspondence is explored by Schmidt et al.

Notably, the PIX3D dataset introduced by Sun et al. provides a benchmark for single-image 3D shape modeling, while Wang et al.'s Pixel2Mesh generates 3D mesh models from RGB images. The contributions of these works collectively advance the field of 3D reconstruction and object detection, showcasing a range of methodologies from traditional algorithms to deep learning frameworks.

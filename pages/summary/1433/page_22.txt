In summary, while end-to-end models have made significant strides in surpassing traditional HMM-GMM models, they still lag behind the HMM-DNN model in performance. This is attributed to their limited capacity for language knowledge acquisition compared to HMM-DNN's extensive linguistic framework built from large corpora. Key areas for improvement include reducing model latency and enhancing the learning of language knowledge to fully realize the potential of end-to-end architectures in large vocabulary continuous speech recognition (LVCSR).

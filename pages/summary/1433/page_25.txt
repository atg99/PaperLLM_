The references listed pertain to advancements in automatic speech recognition (ASR) using deep learning techniques. Key contributions include the development of end-to-end neural networks for ASR, notably by Song and Cai, and the introduction of models like Deep Speech 2 by Amodei et al., which focus on both English and Mandarin. Significant research has been conducted on acoustic-to-word models, highlighted by works from Audhkhasi et al. and Li et al., which aim to improve recognition accuracy in conversational speech.

Innovations in sequence-to-sequence models for speech recognition are discussed, particularly by Prabhavalkar et al. and Hwang and Sung, who explore character-level recognition using recurrent neural networks. The evolution of recurrent neural networks in speech recognition is also addressed, with Graves and others contributing foundational methods that leverage deep learning for improved performance.

Further, attention-based models have gained traction, as evidenced by the work of Bahdanau et al. and Chorowski et al., which enhance the alignment and translation capabilities in ASR systems. The references collectively illustrate a significant shift towards using neural architectures for complex speech recognition tasks, emphasizing the integration of various models and approaches to achieve competitive results in the field.

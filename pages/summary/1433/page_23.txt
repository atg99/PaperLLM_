The paper discusses the challenges faced by end-to-end models in automatic speech recognition (ASR), particularly in contexts with significant linguistic diversity. It emphasizes the necessity for these models to enhance their language knowledge while preserving their end-to-end architecture. The authors include D.W., X.W., and S.L., who contributed to various aspects of the research, including conceptualization, methodology, and project supervision. The study was supported by funding from the Fund of Science and Technology on Parallel and Distributed Processing Laboratory. 

The manuscript also provides a list of abbreviations relevant to the research, including terms such as LPC (Linear predictive coding), DNN (Deep neural network), and ASR (Automatic speech recognition). A comprehensive reference section is included, citing foundational works in the field of speech recognition. The authors declare no conflicts of interest, ensuring the integrity of their findings.

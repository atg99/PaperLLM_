To address these problems, various strategies have been employed, including the enhancement of attention mechanisms to better align the input and output sequences.

The LAS model introduced a pyramid structure in its encoder, reducing the loop steps of the RNN and subsequently the length of the encoding result sequence. This method, along with others that utilize subsampling techniques, serves to alleviate issues related to model delay and information redundancy in attention mechanisms. As the complexity of encoder structures has evolved, with deeper networks yielding improved performance, models have been developed that range from three to as many as fifteen layers, incorporating advanced techniques such as Batch normalization and convolutional LSTM.

Attention mechanisms have been classified into three types: content-based, location-based, and hybrid. Each type employs different strategies for calculating attention weights, with hybrid mechanisms offering a combination of the strengths of the other two. Despite advancements, challenges such as the continuity problem persist, necessitating ongoing research and innovation in speech recognition methodologies.

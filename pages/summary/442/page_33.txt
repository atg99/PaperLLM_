The paper discusses the evaluation of U-Net variants for medical image segmentation, focusing on their accuracy, complexity, and the trade-offs between performance and efficiency. Section 3.6 addresses modeling uncertainty in medical data annotations. The experimental results show that transformer variants, specifically UCTransNet and MISSFormer, outperform traditional CNN extensions in terms of dice scores across various datasets. For instance, MISSFormer achieved a DSC score of 0.81 with 42 million parameters, while U-Net, with 1.9 million parameters, scored 0.79. This highlights the importance of considering both accuracy and computational resource requirements when selecting a model for specific tasks in clinical and industrial applications. The study aims to guide researchers in further enhancing the U-Net architecture for diverse medical applications.

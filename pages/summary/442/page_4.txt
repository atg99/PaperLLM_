The paper presents a comprehensive taxonomy of U-Net extensions, categorizing various models based on their design enhancements. The taxonomy highlights improvements in modular design elements of U-Net, such as skip connections, which facilitate better feature map processing. Key enhancements discussed include Residual U-Net, V-Net, Attention U-Net, and several others, each contributing unique features to the U-Net framework. The paper also addresses advancements in multi-scale and multi-modality fusion techniques, as well as probabilistic designs incorporating graphical models and variational autoencoders.

Additionally, the paper explores the evolution from 2D to 3D U-Net architectures, emphasizing the advantages of 3D models in providing richer tissue information for medical image segmentation, albeit at a higher computational cost. The results from the Kidney Tumor Segmentation challenge are cited to illustrate the effectiveness of 3D U-Net implementations compared to their 2D counterparts. Overall, the paper aims to clarify the landscape of U-Net variations and their respective contributions to the field of semantic segmentation.

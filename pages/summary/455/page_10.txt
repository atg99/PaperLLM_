The paper presents the BU-Net architecture, a brain lesion segmentation prediction framework that utilizes a Residual Block (RES) to enhance performance compared to the baseline U-Net and other existing segmentation models. Evaluated on the BraTS 2017 and 2018 datasets, BU-Net demonstrated significant improvements in the accuracy of brain lesion segmentation. However, the 2D U-Net version has limitations in retaining local details and contextual information across slices, which the authors acknowledge. Future work will focus on exploring 3D-based networks to further enhance segmentation performance. 

The authors contributed to various aspects of the study, including conceptualization, methodology, software development, validation, and writing.

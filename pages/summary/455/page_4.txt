The proposed methodology emphasizes the importance of image preprocessing, particularly the use of the N4ITK algorithm for bias correction in MRI data. This step is crucial for enhancing the model's robustness against noise, ensuring that the input images are homogeneous. Additionally, the architecture of the BU-Net is designed to facilitate better feature extraction through the integration of RES blocks and a WC block, which create a bridge between shallow and deep layers.

The BU-Net model operates on images of size 256x256, maintaining the same dimensions for the output. The encoder consists of multiple blocks that employ convolution layers, max-pooling, and dropout, while the decoder utilizes Conv2DTranspose layers concatenated with outputs from the corresponding RES blocks. This structure not only aids in the contraction and expansion processes but also improves the model's performance in accurately segmenting different tumor classes represented in the ground truth images. 

Overall, the methodology presented in this paper contributes significantly to the field of medical image analysis, particularly in the context of brain tumor segmentation, by combining effective preprocessing techniques with a robust neural network architecture.

The paper presents BU-Net, a novel architecture for brain tumor segmentation in MRI scans, addressing the challenges posed by the complexity of these images. BU-Net improves upon traditional U-Net by incorporating two new blocks: residual extended skip (RES) and wide context (WC). These modifications enhance the model's ability to capture contextual features, which are crucial for accurately segmenting tumor regions.

The effectiveness of BU-Net is demonstrated through comparative results on the BraTS 2018 validation dataset, where it outperforms other architectures in terms of segmentation accuracy. Specifically, BU-Net achieves scores of 0.901 for whole tumor segmentation, 0.837 for core tumor segmentation, and 0.788 for enhancing tumor segmentation. This high resemblance between the predicted and ground truth regions indicates the quality of the proposed architecture.

The qualitative analysis further illustrates the distinctions between tumor classes, with color coding used to represent necrosis and non-enhancing regions, edema, and enhancing tumors. The conclusions underscore the importance of contextual features in MRI scans and highlight BU-Net's potential as an effective tool for brain tumor segmentation.

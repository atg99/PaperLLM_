The paper by Sharma, Granmo, and Goodwin presents a method for environmental sound classification utilizing multiple feature channels and an attention-based deep convolutional neural network (CNN). The authors aim to improve the accuracy of sound classification by leveraging various audio features and incorporating an attention mechanism that enhances the model's focus on relevant parts of the input data. The study demonstrates the effectiveness of their approach through experiments and results, showing that their model outperforms existing methods in classifying environmental sounds. The findings contribute to the field of sound recognition, emphasizing the importance of feature selection and attention in deep learning applications.

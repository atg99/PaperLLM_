This paper presents a novel model for Environment Sound Classification (ESC) that integrates multiple feature channels using a Deep Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) enhanced with an attention mechanism. The model utilizes diverse audio feature extraction techniques, specifically Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCC), Gammatone Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (GFCC), Constant Q-transform (CQT), and Chromagram, to enrich the input data.

The architecture employs a deeper CNN that leverages spatially separable convolutions, allowing for separate processing of time and feature domains before merging them. Additionally, an innovative attention module is introduced, which simultaneously applies spatial and channel attention, optimizing the model's focus on relevant features. The use of mix-up data augmentation further enhances the model's robustness and performance.

Results show that the proposed model achieves state-of-the-art accuracy on three benchmark datasets: UrbanSound8K (97.52%), ESC-10 (94.75%), and ESC-50 (87.45%). This work not only demonstrates the effectiveness of combining multiple feature channels but also highlights the advantages of attention mechanisms in deep learning for audio classification tasks.

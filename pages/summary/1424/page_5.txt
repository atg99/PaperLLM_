The paper presents an exploration of a dataset designed for Visual Question Answering (VQA) that focuses on rephrasings of questions to enhance model robustness. It details the process of generating rephrasings from original questions in the VQA v2.0 validation set, resulting in a comprehensive dataset comprising 162,016 questions across 40,504 images. The methodology includes two stages: first, collecting rephrasings and second, validating their semantic and grammatical accuracy. The study introduces a Consensus Score (CS) to measure a VQA model's consistency in answering the same question posed in different forms. A higher CS indicates better robustness to linguistic variations.

The paper benchmarks various existing VQA models, such as MUTAN and Bottom-Up Top-Down Attention (BUTD), analyzing their performance on the new dataset. It highlights that MUTAN uniquely employs sentence embeddings for question encoding, achieving an accuracy of 63.20% on the VQA v2.0 test-dev. The findings underscore the significance of consistency across rephrased questions in improving VQA model reliability. The dataset and evaluation metrics proposed aim to contribute to advancements in VQA systems by emphasizing the need for models to handle linguistic diversity effectively.

The appendix of the paper on the Dirichlet Variational Autoencoder provides detailed derivations and experimental settings relevant to the main content. 

It begins with Proposition A.1, which presents a Monte Carlo estimation of the marginal log-likelihood, expressed as p(x) approximated by the sum over the posterior distribution of a latent variable z. The proof outlines the transformation of the marginal likelihood into an integral form using the posterior distribution q(z).

Next, Proposition B.1 addresses the Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence between two MultiGamma distributions. It defines a vector of K independent Gamma random variables and derives the KL divergence formula, which incorporates the digamma function. The proof involves detailed calculations using the properties of Gamma functions and integrals to arrive at the final expression for the KL divergence.

These mathematical derivations form the theoretical backbone of the proposed model, enhancing the understanding of its statistical properties and performance.

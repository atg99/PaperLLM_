The paper presents visualizations of latent document embeddings from the 20Newsgroups dataset using t-SNE. Three model configurations are explored: ProdLDA, NVDM, and GSM, each evaluated with two different priors: Dirichlet and Stick-Breaking. Figures 7 and 8 illustrate the embeddings generated by combining these models with the respective priors, highlighting differences in the clustering and structure of the embeddings. Figure 9 displays the original models without the variational autoencoder component, providing a comparative baseline for understanding the impact of the variational approaches on document representation. The visualizations aim to demonstrate how different model architectures and priors affect the quality and interpretability of latent document embeddings.

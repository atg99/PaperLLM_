This paper proposes a hyper-parameter learning strategy using the method of moment estimator (MME) to update the Dirichlet prior parameter. The authors outline a process where a set of proportions sampled from a Dirichlet distribution is utilized to derive the MME update rule for the hyper-parameter. The update is represented mathematically, and the rationale for choosing the MME is its closed form and consistency, as emphasized by Minka in 2000.

The paper details the calculations involved in deriving the MME for the hyper-parameter, including definitions for moments of the Dirichlet distribution and their relationships. A proof is provided, demonstrating how the numerator and denominator of the MME formula are derived based on the moments of the Dirichlet distribution, ultimately leading to the conclusion that the hyper-parameter can be effectively updated using the sampled latent values during training.

The authors also discuss the importance of a burn-in period for stabilizing neural network parameters before employing the MME for hyper-parameter learning. The usefulness of this approach is further elaborated in an appendix, indicating its practical implications in the context of neural networks.

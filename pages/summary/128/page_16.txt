This paper presents the results of augmenting topic modeling techniques using Dirichlet Variational Autoencoder (DirV AE) across different datasets, specifically 20Newsgroups and RCV1-v2. In Table 8, the paper provides examples of the top ten high-probability words for each topic derived from the 20Newsgroups dataset after applying DirV AE augmentation. For ProdLDA with DirV AE, diverse topics ranging from political discussions to technological terms are identified, such as topics related to the Armenian genocide, Christian doctrine, and NHL hockey. Similarly, for NVDM and GSM, the learned topics reflect a wide array of subjects, including historical conflicts, religious beliefs, and various technological aspects.

The findings indicate that DirV AE effectively enhances the topic modeling capabilities of the base models, leading to more coherent and contextually relevant topics across different datasets. This augmentation approach demonstrates its potential for improving the interpretability of topics generated by various models in natural language processing tasks.

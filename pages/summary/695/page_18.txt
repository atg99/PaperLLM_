The paper discusses a Deep Q-Network (DQN)-based approach to optimize the decision-making process regarding the use of Multi-Connectivity (MC) in network environments. The study demonstrates that applying this algorithm significantly enhances performance for MC-capable users in the same evaluation setting, confirming the importance of optimizing MC configurations. The authors suggest potential future research directions, including the extension of their solution to handle diverse traffic types beyond the bit rate, such as Ultra Reliable and Low Latency Communications (URLLC), which could improve reliability through packet duplication across multiple cells. They also propose exploring the use of other user equipment (UE) acting as relays in various scenarios.

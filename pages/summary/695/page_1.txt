The paper by Hernández-Carlón et al. presents a Deep Q-Network (DQN)-based algorithm aimed at optimizing multi-connectivity in heterogeneous cellular networks. As data traffic continues to grow and new high-speed services emerge, Mobile Network Operators are deploying 5G networks alongside existing technologies like LTE, creating a complex environment with multiple radio access technologies. Multi-connectivity allows devices to connect to multiple cells simultaneously, which can enhance network performance. 

The authors address the challenge of effectively distributing traffic across these cells based on current conditions. They propose a deep reinforcement learning approach using DQN to determine the optimal traffic distribution. The results indicate that the DQN-based method achieves near-optimal performance, with only a 3.9% average difference in reward compared to the optimal strategy. Additionally, it significantly outperforms a traditional scheme based on Signal to Interference Noise Ratio (SINR), showing improvements of up to 50% in reward and 166% in throughput in certain scenarios.

Overall, the study highlights the potential of DQN for multi-connectivity optimization and suggests avenues for future research in this domain, emphasizing its relevance for radio access network challenges.

the paper focuses on optimizing traffic distribution across multiple Radio Access Technologies (RATs) and cells in a multi-connectivity (MC) scenario. The objective is to create a policy that ensures quality of service (QoS) requirements are met, while minimizing resource consumption and preventing overload in the involved cells. 

The authors propose a novel approach using Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL), specifically the Deep Q-Network (DQN) technique, to learn the optimal traffic split policy on a per-user equipment (UE) basis. This method contrasts with prior works that utilized algorithmic solutions such as linear programming, Lagrangian multipliers, and heuristic algorithms with different optimization targets. The motivation for employing DRL is its effectiveness in addressing dynamic decision-making problems where mathematical models may be lacking, as well as its ability to generalize from past experiences in complex environments.

The paper builds on earlier versions of the DQN model and introduces a comprehensive formulation and algorithmic solution, along with an architectural framework based on the open RAN (O-RAN) architecture to support the proposed approach. The assessment of the algorithm's performance across various evaluation conditions is also a significant contribution. 

The structure of the paper includes a system model and problem formulation in Section 2, the DQN-based solution in Section 3, performance results in Section 4, and conclusions in Section 5.

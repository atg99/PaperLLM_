The paper investigates the performance of a deep reinforcement learning (DQN) based algorithm for optimizing multi-connectivity (MC) configurations in a network environment featuring both LTE and 5G NR cells. The evaluation scenario includes two LTE cells and four 5G NR cells, allowing for five possible connection modes. Analysis shows that approximately 56% of the time, the algorithm opted for single connections, while 44% of the time, it employed MC, with the LTE-NR combination being the most favored. This approach is justified by the LTE cells' broader coverage area, which can meet user equipment (UE) data rate requirements more effectively while on the move.

The DQN algorithm demonstrated superior performance compared to a traditional SINR-based method, as highlighted by the reward metrics. Additionally, the study evaluates a scenario where users remain stationary, analyzing performance across 361 fixed positions within a defined area. The results indicate that the DQN-based strategy closely approaches optimal performance and significantly outperforms the SINR-based strategy in terms of throughput and reward, achieving up to a 65% improvement in specific cases. The findings underscore the significance of selecting the appropriate connection mode and MC configuration to enhance network performance under varying conditions.

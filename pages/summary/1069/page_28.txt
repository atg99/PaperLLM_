The paper discusses the challenges and solutions associated with the cost of using Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs). It highlights that while CNNs can provide significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency over traditional machine learning algorithms, they often require substantial training time, hardware resources, and data preprocessing, leading to increased costs.

To mitigate these costs, the paper suggests leveraging cloud-based services, which offer scalable and flexible access to computing resources. This approach reduces the need for upfront hardware investments and allows developers to adjust resources based on specific tasks. Additionally, renting hardware from cloud providers is presented as a cost-effective solution, providing access to high-performance computing without the need for significant capital investment.

The potential benefits of using CNNs are emphasized, including enhanced accuracy in applications such as image classification, object detection, and natural language processing. CNNs can uncover complex patterns in data, leading to new insights and innovations across various fields, while also automating tasks that reduce labor costs and improve productivity.

In conclusion, although the costs associated with CNNs can be considerable, the potential advantages, combined with strategies like utilizing cloud services or hardware rental, can make CNNs a valuable investment for diverse applications. The paper also notes ongoing research and development in CNNs, which continues to advance their performance and efficiency.

The paper discusses various optimization algorithms and regularization techniques used in training neural networks, particularly convolutional neural networks (CNNs). 

In Section 2.8, several optimization algorithms are summarized, including Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD), momentum, AdaGrad, RMSProp, and Adam. Each algorithm has distinct characteristics affecting the performance of the network. SGD updates weights based on the gradient of the loss function, momentum smooths updates to accelerate convergence, AdaGrad adapts the learning rate for each weight based on past gradients, RMSProp uses a moving average of squared gradients for adaptive learning rates, and Adam combines momentum and AdaGrad for improved performance.

Section 2.9 focuses on regularization techniques to combat overfitting, a common problem where models perform well on training data but poorly on unseen data. Regularization methods add penalty terms to the loss function to encourage simpler models. The paper outlines three primary techniques:

1. **L1 Regularization**: Adds a penalty proportional to the absolute value of weights, promoting sparsity and feature selection.
2. **L2 Regularization**: Imposes a penalty based on the square of weights, encouraging smaller weights to help prevent overfitting.
3. **Dropout**: Randomly drops neurons during training to reduce reliance on specific features, enhancing generalization performance.

These techniques are essential for improving the robustness and effectiveness of neural network models during training.

CNNs excel at recognizing complex patterns through hierarchical learning, making them effective for visual and sequential data analysis. Their translation-invariant feature learning enables effective handling of varying input sizes. However, they necessitate substantial labeled datasets to mitigate overfitting risks and can be adversely affected by noisy or biased data. Despite their computational demands, advancements in technology offer solutions to optimize training efficiency. It is crucial to assess the specific task requirements, as alternatives like graph neural networks or rule-based systems may be better suited for different data structures or reasoning needs.

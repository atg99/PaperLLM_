Another study explored the application of CNNs for lip reading, where the model achieved high accuracy rates in recognizing spoken words based on visual input alone. This has potential implications for improving communication technology, particularly in noisy environments.

In the agricultural sector, CNNs have been used for olive leaf disease detection, demonstrating effective disease classification and offering a tool for farmers to monitor crop health. The model's performance indicates its applicability in precision agriculture.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, CNNs have been employed for mask recognition, allowing for automated monitoring of compliance in public spaces. The accuracy rates achieved by these models support their use in enhancing public health measures.

Additionally, CNNs have been applied to visual spam classification, where they successfully distinguish between legitimate content and spam, improving user experience on digital platforms. 

Lastly, driver sleepiness detection using CNNs showcases their potential in enhancing road safety by monitoring driver behavior and alertness levels.

Overall, these examples underscore the versatility and effectiveness of CNNs across various fields, highlighting their capability to improve accuracy and efficiency in tasks traditionally reliant on human expertise. Each application illustrates the growing importance of deep learning techniques in solving complex real-world problems.

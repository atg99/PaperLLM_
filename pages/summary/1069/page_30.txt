In summary, adversarial training enhances CNN robustness against adversarial attacks by incorporating both original and adversarial examples during training. Quantization and compression techniques reduce the memory and computational demands of CNNs, facilitating deployment on resource-constrained devices. Furthermore, formal methods offer a rigorous framework for designing and verifying CNNs, ensuring adherence to specifications such as accuracy and efficiency. These methods also aid in identifying vulnerabilities and optimizing hyperparameters, ultimately improving the reliability, security, and performance of CNN systems across various applications.

The paper provides a comprehensive guide to Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), addressing their fundamentals, architectures, recent advancements, benefits and drawbacks, and practical recommendations for developers and data scientists. It emphasizes the importance of understanding model architecture, training processes, and performance evaluation to enhance the accuracy, efficiency, and robustness of CNNs, which are expected to play a crucial role in advancing artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML).

Future research directions include improving CNN interpretability and explainability, enhancing robustness against adversarial attacks, and developing CNNs for complex tasks such as video recognition and natural language processing. The paper acknowledges a limitation in its focus on CNNs, suggesting that future studies should explore other machine learning models like recurrent neural networks, decision trees, and support vector machines, as well as the integration of CNNs with techniques such as generative adversarial networks and reinforcement learning.

The research received no external funding, all data are presented in the text, and there are no conflicts of interest declared. Key abbreviations used in the paper include AI, ML, CNN, and others relevant to the discussion of CNNs.

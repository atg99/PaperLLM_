The referenced works focus on the application and optimization of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) across various domains, particularly in natural language processing and image analysis. Key studies include:

1. Wang and Gang's exploration of CNNs in natural language processing, presented at the 2018 ICISCAE conference.
2. Li et al.'s similar application at the ICCWAMTIP conference, emphasizing the utility of CNNs for text-based tasks.
3. Gimenez et al. introduced semantic-based padding to enhance CNN performance in sentiment analysis within their Neurocomputing publication.
4. Other notable contributions include deep feature extraction methods for hyperspectral images by Chen et al., and architecture innovations by Tu et al. focusing on reconfigurable computation patterns.
5. Several studies, such as those by Dogo et al. and Ren and Cheng, reviewed optimization techniques for CNNs, highlighting the importance of training methodologies.
6. Applications extend to practical scenarios, like vehicle detection and classification, as demonstrated by Alam et al., Zhong et al., and Dong et al. through various CNN adaptations.
7. Recent advancements also include the development of specialized CNN layers, like dynamic pooling and activation layers, as discussed by Jie et al. and Ouyang et al.

Overall, these works collectively contribute to a comprehensive understanding of CNNs, showcasing their versatility and the ongoing efforts to enhance their functionality in diverse applications.

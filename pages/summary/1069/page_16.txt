The paper discusses various convolutional neural network (CNN) architectures, focusing on their structural components and performance comparisons. 

1. **Residual Blocks**: The architecture features multiple residual blocks characterized by:
   - Convolutional layers (Conv_Lay) with 256 or 512 filters of size 3x3, followed by 2x2 max pooling layers.
   - Each block includes a shortcut connection that adds the original input to the output, enhancing gradient flow and improving training efficiency.

2. **InceptionNet**: Introduced by Christian Szegedy et al. in 2014, InceptionNet (or GoogLeNet) utilizes inception modules to optimize computational resources. Key components include:
   - Initial Conv_Lay with 64 filters of size 7x7 and subsequent layers with various configurations of convolutional filters (1x1, 3x3, and 5x5) and pooling layers.
   - The architecture incorporates multiple inception modules that concatenate outputs from different convolutional paths, along with auxiliary classifiers for improved training and regularization.

3. **Comparison of CNN Architectures**: The paper aims to compare key aspects and performances of prominent CNN architectures, including LeNet, AlexNet, VGG, ResNet, and InceptionNet.

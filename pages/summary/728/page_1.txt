The paper titled "Policy Gradient Assisted MAP-Elites" by Olle Nilsson and Antoine Cully discusses an innovative approach that integrates policy gradient methods with the MAP-Elites framework. MAP-Elites is a popular algorithm used in evolutionary computation for exploring and exploiting diverse solutions across a range of objectives. The authors propose enhancing MAP-Elites by incorporating policy gradients, which are typically used in reinforcement learning to optimize policies.

The key argument of the paper is that this integration allows for more efficient exploration of the solution space and improves the quality of the generated solutions. The authors present experimental results demonstrating that their approach outperforms traditional MAP-Elites in various scenarios, particularly in environments with complex reward structures.

Overall, the paper contributes to the fields of evolutionary computation and reinforcement learning by providing a novel method that leverages the strengths of both MAP-Elites and policy gradient techniques, aiming for more effective solution generation in multi-objective optimization problems.

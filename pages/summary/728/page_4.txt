The paper discusses the integration of Policy Gradient methods with MAP-Elites to enhance data efficiency in sequential decision-making tasks, particularly in robotics. MAP-Elites, which organizes solutions in a behavioral space, can be resource-intensive due to the high cost of simulations. To address this, the paper introduces Centroidal Voronoi Tesselation MAP-Elites (CVT-MAP-Elites), which automates the archive creation process.

The framework of Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) is outlined as a Markov Decision Process (MDP), where a robot interacts with its environment through a policy defined by a deep neural network (DNN). The goal is to maximize expected returns over time. The paper details the TD3 algorithm, a prominent DRL method that employs an actor-critic architecture to learn policies indirectly by maximizing an action-value function. TD3 utilizes experience replay and deterministic policies, with mechanisms to stabilize learning, such as target networks and noise addition for exploration.

The authors also highlight related work that combines DRL with evolutionary computation methods, noting the success of algorithms like CEM-RL and MAP-Elites-ES in improving performance in robotics applications. This synthesis of approaches aims to leverage the strengths of both DRL and evolutionary strategies to tackle complex decision-making challenges efficiently.

In this paper, the authors propose the Policy Gradient Assisted MAP-Elites (PGA-MAP-Elites) algorithm as an enhancement to the traditional MAP-Elites approach. By integrating gradient-based optimization from the Twin Delayed Deep Deterministic (TD3) policy gradient method, PGA-MAP-Elites improves the search for diverse and high-performing solutions in complex robotic control tasks. The results demonstrate that PGA-MAP-Elites can effectively explore the behavioral space, leading to the discovery of robust solutions that perform competitively with state-of-the-art deep reinforcement learning algorithms.

The evaluation of the PGA-MAP-Elites algorithm is conducted on a variety of stochastic behavior generation tasks, highlighting its capability to scale across different domains where conventional MAP-Elites may struggle. The authors provide access to their benchmark tasks through the open-source QDgym module, facilitating further research in quality-diversity optimization without reliance on proprietary software.

Overall, the introduction of PGA-MAP-Elites represents a significant advancement in quality-diversity optimization, particularly for applications requiring complex behavioral strategies in robotics. The paper underscores the importance of combining gradient-based optimization with quality-diversity approaches to enhance the efficacy of behavioral repertoire generation.

In contrast, the paper introduces Policy Gradient Assisted MAP-Elites (PGA-MAP-Elites), a novel algorithm designed to enhance the traditional MAP-Elites framework by incorporating a gradient-based variation operator. This operator is inspired by Deep Reinforcement Learning and utilizes a critic neural network to derive performance gradients efficiently. By combining this directed performance improvement with a traditional genetic algorithm approach for diverse search, PGA-MAP-Elites aims to overcome the limitations of existing MAP-Elites implementations, particularly in robotics where complex neural network controllers are often required.

The methodology involves a dual variation strategy during the MAP-Elites optimization loop, where the policy gradient variation targets performance enhancement while the genetic variation ensures exploration of diverse solutions. This synergy not only accelerates the discovery of high-performing solutions but also maintains a broad behavioral repertoire.

Evaluation of the PGA-MAP-Elites algorithm is conducted across four different tasks involving deep neural network controllers. The results demonstrate a significant improvement in the quality of the generated behavioral repertoires compared to traditional methods, highlighting the effectiveness of the proposed approach in achieving both diversity and high performance.

In summary, the paper presents a significant advancement in Quality-Diversity optimization by integrating policy gradient techniques into the MAP-Elites framework, providing a powerful tool for evolving complex behaviors in robotics and beyond.

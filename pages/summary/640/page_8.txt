The paper introduces Cluster-GCN, a novel graph convolutional network (GCN) training algorithm designed to be fast and memory efficient. Experimental results demonstrate that Cluster-GCN can effectively train deep GCNs on large-scale graphs, such as Amazon2M, achieving notable performance with minimal resource consumption. Specifically, when training a 4-layer GCN on Amazon2M, it records a training time of 337 seconds, memory usage of 7476 MB, and an F1 score of 90.41, while a comparable Cluster-GCN configuration uses significantly less memory and achieves a similar accuracy.

The study highlights the impact of layer depth on model performance, noting that accuracy improves with the addition of layers up to 5, but fails to converge effectively at depths of 7 or 8 due to optimization difficulties. The proposed diagonal enhancement technique notably improves convergence rates. 

Cluster-GCN achieves state-of-the-art results on benchmark datasets, including PPI and Reddit, outperforming existing methods by leveraging its design and normalization approach. The results confirm the potential of deeper GCNs for enhanced accuracy, with Cluster-GCN yielding an F1 score of 99.36 on PPI with a 5-layer model and 96.60 on Reddit with a 4-layer model.

In conclusion, Cluster-GCN is presented as a breakthrough in GCN training, showing that it is possible to train deeper networks efficiently while achieving high accuracy on large datasets.

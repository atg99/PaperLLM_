This section provides detailed information about the experiments conducted to ensure reproducibility. 

Datasets and Software Versions: The datasets used include PPI, Reddit, and Amazon, which were downloaded from specified websites. The Amazon dataset was used in an inductive setting, differing from the transductive setting used in prior work. The software environment consisted of CUDA 10.0, cuDNN 7.0, TensorFlow 1.12.0, and PyTorch 1.0.0, with METIS 5.1.0 for graph partitioning.

Implementation Details: The study adopted a strategy from previous works to pre-compute the multiplication of AX in the first GCN layer, which allowed for efficient use of the 1-hop neighborhood and reduced computational costs. When multiple clusters were selected, between-cluster links were reintroduced, necessitating the re-normalization of the adjacency matrix to maintain the numerical stability of the embedding matrix. In the inductive setting, testing nodes were kept hidden during training, and separate adjacency matrices were constructed for training and testing.

Running Time of Graph Clustering Algorithm and Data Preprocessing: The experiments highlighted the training time for different GCN methods without detailing preprocessing times. The graph clustering algorithm, implemented via METIS, was found to require only a small fraction of the total preprocessing time, demonstrating efficiency and scalability, especially as it only needed to be performed once for the node partitions, which could be reused in subsequent training phases.

Tables included outline the data splits for each dataset and the running times for the graph clustering and preprocessing steps, showing a range of times across different datasets.

The publication titled "A Review of Personality in Human–Robot Interactions" appears in Foundations and Trends® in Information Systems, authored by L. P. Robert Jr., R. Alahmad, C. Esterwood, S. Kim, S. You, and Q. Zhang. This review discusses the significance of personality traits in the context of interactions between humans and robots, providing insights into how these traits influence user experience and engagement. The paper spans pages 107 to 212 of volume 4, issue 2, published in 2020.

The authors explore various dimensions of personality, examining how they can be integrated into robotic systems to enhance interaction quality. They argue that understanding and modeling personality can lead to more effective and relatable robots, thereby improving user satisfaction.

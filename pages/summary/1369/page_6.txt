Foundations and Trends® in Information Systems publishes survey and tutorial articles across various topics related to information systems. These include the interaction of information systems with individuals, groups, organizations, industries, and society. The journal also covers areas such as information systems development, economics, management, and research methods. It is available in a print version with ISSN 2331-1231 and an online version with ISSN 2331-124X. The journal provides full-text access through its DOI link.

The paper titled "A Review of Personality in Human–Robot Interactions" by Lionel P. Robert Jr. and colleagues examines the significance of personality in enhancing the quality of interactions between humans and robots. Despite the recognized importance of personality, the research in this field is fragmented and lacks a comprehensive framework, creating challenges in understanding existing knowledge and identifying gaps. To address this, the authors review 83 articles and 84 studies to evaluate the current landscape of human-robot personality research. The review emphasizes major thematic areas of research, aiming to provide clarity and direction for future studies in this evolving domain. The findings highlight the need for a more structured approach to better align research with the increasing integration of robots in various societal and organizational contexts.

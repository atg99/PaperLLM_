The introduction discusses the evolving role of robots in various domains, emphasizing their capacity to interact with both employees and customers. This interaction necessitates an understanding of human-robot dynamics, particularly the significance of personality in these relationships. Personality, defined as the amalgamation of past behaviors, cognitions, and emotions influenced by biological and social factors, is highlighted as a crucial element in enhancing the quality of human-robot interactions. The paper aims to draw insights from organizational behavior and social psychology literature to explore the implications of personality in this context, while maintaining a focused discussion on its relevance to human-robot interaction.

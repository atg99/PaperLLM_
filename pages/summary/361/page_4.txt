This study introduces a labeling convention for entity recognition using the BIOES format, where B denotes the beginning, E the end, I the inside, and S a single word entity position. To improve entity recognition, an "Entity Type" suffix was added to the labels. For example, the term ‘黄无座盘菌’ is identified as the Chinese name of the fungus (CNF) and ‘直径0.5~1.5mm’ as the size of the cystic disk (SICD). The annotation process applies the BIOES method to label each character accordingly.

The methodology section details the XLNet-IDCNN-CRF model structure, which consists of four layers: embedding, XLNet, IDCNN, and CRF. This model builds on previous research by incorporating the XLNet model instead of BERT, along with an IDCNN to enhance feature extraction. The study aims to refine the recognition of various fungal-related entities, as illustrated in the provided tables that categorize different entity types and their respective labels.

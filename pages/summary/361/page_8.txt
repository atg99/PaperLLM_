In this study, the epoch parameter was set to 50 for all simulations, resulting in notable improvements in performance metrics after training. Specifically, the precision (P), recall (R), and F1-scores reached 0.971, 0.986, and 0.979, marking significant enhancements over the XLNet-IDCNN model by 0.093, 0.025, and 0.062, respectively. Comparative analysis demonstrated that the XIC model outperformed XLNet-IDCNN across various entities, particularly in recognizing 22 specific types of entities. This improvement is largely attributed to the integration of a Conditional Random Field (CRF) layer, which enhances the model's ability to capture dependencies between consecutive and interrelated entities. The CRF layer optimizes label predictions by leveraging contextual information and transfer probabilities, ensuring a globally optimal sequence of predicted labels. This methodology significantly increases the accuracy of entity recognition tasks.

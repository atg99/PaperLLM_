The introduction highlights the significance of edible fungus as a vital health food and raw material for various industries, particularly in China, which has a rich history of cultivating and utilizing these fungi. Since 2012, edible fungus has become a major agricultural product, ranking fifth in importance. The complexity and diversity of edible fungus species pose challenges for research and information dissemination, necessitating efficient methods for acquiring knowledge in this area.

Named entity recognition (NER) systems, which are still developing in agriculture, can address these challenges by identifying entities related to edible fungus. Traditional NER methods have limitations, such as reliance on predefined dictionaries and manual feature selection, while deep learning models, like BERT, have shown improved performance in various tasks. Recent advancements include the integration of models like BiLSTM and CRF, as well as IDCNN, which enhance entity recognition capabilities.

Despite the advancements in NER, the application to edible fungus remains unexplored, with existing literature primarily focusing on cultivation and related fields. The paper identifies two key challenges in NER for edible fungus: the lack of publicly available Chinese datasets tailored for NER tasks and the need for further research in this area. To overcome these challenges, the authors propose combining XLNet with IDCNN and CRF for improved entity recognition in edible fungus research.

The XIC model demonstrates significant advancements in the Named Entity Recognition (NER) task concerning edible mushrooms. By incorporating transfer probability between labels, the model ensures logical consistency in predicted sequences, enhancing its robustness and accuracy. The introduction of an IDCNN layer allows for improved contextual information capture, resulting in more precise predictions and better interpretability. Additionally, the integration of a Conditional Random Field (CRF) layer facilitates global optimality in label sequence predictions, reducing the likelihood of logical labeling errors and further stabilizing the model's outputs.

This study offers practical contributions to the research and understanding of edible fungi by providing an efficient method for extracting key entities from large, non-standardized datasets, which enhances the extraction process compared to traditional methods. The research lays the groundwork for constructing a knowledge graph specific to edible fungi, enabling a structured representation of knowledge that enhances understanding of entity relationships. Future work aims to extend the NER model to include relationship extraction, ultimately creating an intelligent Q&A system that provides user-friendly insights regarding edible fungi.

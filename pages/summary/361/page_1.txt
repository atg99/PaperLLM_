The paper presents an improved method for Chinese named entity recognition (NER) focusing on edible fungi, addressing the challenges posed by the diversity of species and insufficient mycological knowledge. It emphasizes the need for a fast and accurate information acquisition system, proposing the integration of an intelligent Q&A system supported by NER. The authors established a Chinese corpus specific to edible fungi, highlighting the limitations of conventional methods in capturing long-distance dependencies.

The proposed NER method utilizes XLNet and conditional random fields (CRFs), incorporating an iterated dilated convolutional neural network (IDCNN) to enhance feature extraction across utterances. The IDCNN layer extends the receptive field of the convolutional kernel, and its output is processed through a CRF layer to optimize labeling accuracy.

Experimental results indicate that the model achieves a precision of 0.971, recall of 0.986, and an F1-score of 0.979, surpassing existing methods. The findings suggest that the model effectively identifies entities related to edible fungi, providing valuable support for constructing knowledge graphs in this domain.

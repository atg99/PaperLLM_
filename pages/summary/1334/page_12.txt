The paper discusses the integration of ethics into the governance of autonomous systems, particularly within the context of the European Union. It highlights the need for a comprehensive approach to ethical inquiry that extends beyond EU policymaking to include various governance contexts, such as member states and intergovernmental organizations. The author emphasizes the importance of developing new insights into ethics implementation, particularly as ethical considerations gain prominence in discussions surrounding technology and robotics. The narrative policy framework is utilized to analyze the construction of public problems and the role of narratives in shaping policy agendas. The paper aims to provide pathways for future ethical inquiries in the field of robotics and artificial intelligence. It is supported by the TrustRobots Doctoral College at TU Wien, and the datasets used in the study are available upon request. The author declares no conflicts of interest and notes that the article is open access under a Creative Commons license.

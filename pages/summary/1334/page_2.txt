The paper discusses the critical intersection of human-robot interaction (HRI) research and ethical governance within the context of European Union (EU) robotics and artificial intelligence (AI) policy-making. The main argument posits that while policy narratives are essential for fostering inclusive and trustworthy technologies, there is an urgent need for enhanced ethical implementation in HRI and social robotics research. This dual focus aims to deepen and broaden the ethical discourse surrounding these technologies.

The paper is structured as follows: Section 2 outlines the current role of the ethics narrative in EU robotics and AI strategy, drawing insights from various policy documents. Section 3 critically examines the uptake of the ethics narrative in policy-making, emphasizing its evolution within the governance of emerging technologies and advocating for a collaborative approach between policymakers and technical experts. Section 4 proposes four pathways to advance ethical implementation in robotics and HRI research. The conclusion reflects on the future development of ethical approaches and their significance in shaping societal interactions with autonomous systems.

The narrative analysis approach highlights that the emergence of ethical considerations in EU policy is a dynamic process influenced by ongoing negotiations among stakeholders, including lobbyists and experts. The paper argues that understanding these narratives is crucial for monitoring and controlling the policy landscape as it relates to the evolving definitions of policy problems associated with autonomous systems. Overall, the work underscores the importance of integrating ethical deliberations into HRI research and policy-making to ensure responsible development and deployment of robotics and AI technologies.

The paper discusses the integration of ethical considerations into Human-Robot Interaction (HRI) research and European Union (EU) governance, emphasizing the importance of an interdisciplinary approach. It highlights the need for collaboration between robot and AI ethicists and HRI researchers to develop comprehensive frameworks that can inform policy-making and enhance public trust in technology. The ethics narrative within EU governance is portrayed as a critical component that can guide the implementation of ethical principles in the development of autonomous social robots, addressing issues such as human rights and democratic values.

The author argues that the emerging narrative presents an opportunity for significant societal change, necessitating a swift realization of new ethical ideas to effectively navigate the implications of advanced robotics. The paper advocates for a shift in focus from merely discussing what robots can do to exploring what they should do within sociotechnical systems. This involves adopting methodologies that reflect real-world interactions, as current HRI research often overlooks these complexities.

The conclusion reiterates the importance of ethical frameworks in shaping the future of robotics and AI, suggesting that these frameworks can lead to innovative practices while fostering a culture that prioritizes responsible technology development. The paper ultimately calls for a cohesive understanding of policy-making and technical expertise to ensure the ethical integration of robots into society.

The paper presents a comprehensive survey of various advancements in deep learning, specifically focusing on techniques for improving efficiency and performance in neural networks. Key contributions discussed include network pruning methods aimed at reducing model size and computational demands while maintaining accuracy. Notable techniques such as Thinet, which prunes filters at a granular level, and NISP, which utilizes neuron importance scores, highlight the emphasis on structured sparsity. The paper also covers innovative architectures like MobileNet and EfficientNet that leverage depthwise separable convolutions and model scaling for optimized performance in resource-constrained environments. Additionally, the exploration of low-bitwidth networks, such as Dorefa-Net, underscores the trend toward quantization for efficient inference. Overall, the paper encapsulates the evolution of strategies to enhance deep learning models, addressing challenges in computation and memory usage, thus paving the way for their deployment in real-world applications.

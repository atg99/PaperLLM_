The paper presents a performance comparison of various pruning methods on ILSVRC-12, focusing on the Top-1 and Top-5 error rates of pruned models relative to baseline models. Table 2 summarizes the results of pruned models, indicating that most methods incur a slight degradation in accuracy, with DCP showing competitive performance. For instance, DCP applied to ResNet-50 results in only a 1.02% increase in Top-1 error compared to WM (Scratch-B), which has a 1.45% increase. The proposed Adapt-DCP method demonstrates improved efficiency, achieving better accuracy than DCP with fewer parameters and FLOPs.

The paper also evaluates the inference acceleration of pruned models on mobile devices, highlighting that MobileNetV1 and MobileNetV2 benefit significantly from pruning, achieving speedups of nearly two times in execution time. The results indicate that DCP and Adapt-DCP outperform several state-of-the-art methods, such as ThiNet and AMC, in both accuracy and model compression.

Overall, the findings underscore the effectiveness of DCP and Adapt-DCP in enhancing the performance of pruned models while significantly reducing their computational requirements, making them suitable for deployment on mobile devices.

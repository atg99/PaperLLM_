6.2.2 Datasets and Implementation Details
The proposed DCP method is evaluated on four face recognition benchmark datasets: LFW, CFP-FP, AgeDB-30, and MegaFace. These datasets consist of numerous face images across various identities, with MegaFace presenting a substantial challenge due to its scale. The models are trained using the refined MS-Celeb-1M dataset, which includes millions of face images. The training process involves initial training of LResNet34E-IR and MobileFaceNet from scratch, followed by the application of DCP for model compression.

6.2.3 Performance Comparisons
The results indicate that pruned models maintain performance close to that of the pre-trained models, with some pruned models even outperforming their pre-trained counterparts. Specifically, LResNet34E-IR with a 25% pruning rate shows improved performance on certain datasets. MobileFaceNet demonstrates efficiency with significantly fewer parameters while maintaining performance, making it suitable for resource-constrained environments. The proposed DCP approach proves effective in enhancing face recognition performance while reducing model complexity compared to existing methods like SphereFace and CosFace.

6.3 Effectiveness of DKP
The effectiveness of DKP is assessed through comparisons with various methods, including DCP and Adapt-DCP. The evaluation utilizes CIFAR-10 and ILSVRC-12 datasets, focusing on kernel selection in conjunction with the previously mentioned additional losses. The results illustrate the impact of different pruning strategies on model performance, showcasing the potential improvements achieved through the integration of DKP with DCP. 

Overall, the paper highlights the efficiency and effectiveness of the proposed DCP and DKP methods in enhancing face recognition models while reducing computational requirements, making them viable options for deployment in practical applications.

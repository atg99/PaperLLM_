The results demonstrate that the DCP method can successfully prune channels in various architectures, such as ResNet-56 and MobileNetV1, while maintaining or even enhancing accuracy. Visualization of feature maps indicates that pruned channels are less informative compared to selected ones, confirming the effectiveness of DCP in enhancing the network's discriminative capabilities.

Additionally, the study explores the impact of incorporating additional losses during the pruning process, revealing that a moderate number of additional losses can lead to improved performance, although excessive losses can increase computational costs without significant gains. The findings suggest that strategically adding losses at intervals of 5-10 blocks strikes a balance between accuracy and efficiency.

Overall, the DCP method represents a promising approach for deep neural network compression, facilitating efficient model deployment without compromising performance.

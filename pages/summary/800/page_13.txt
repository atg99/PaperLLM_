The paper discusses a method for channel pruning in neural networks, proposing a greedy algorithm to optimize the selection of informative channels as a sparsity-induced optimization problem. The authors introduce techniques to accelerate this channel pruning process and present a discrimination-aware kernel pruning (DKP) method for model compression at the kernel level. The effectiveness of these methods is validated through extensive experiments on image classification and face recognition tasks.

Future work aims to enhance the proposed methods by considering multiple layers for channel/kernel selection to avoid suboptimal performance associated with the current layer-wise approach. Additionally, the authors plan to extend the DCP framework to incorporate model quantization, allowing simultaneous channel/kernel pruning and quantization, which may improve overall efficiency.

The research is supported by various funding programs, and references are provided to substantiate the work and its context within existing literature on neural network optimization and compression techniques.

The proposed modified residual block enhances the original design by incorporating additional features that specifically target the challenges of segmenting small objects in water area remote sensing images. By maintaining the integrity of the input information and allowing for direct connections, this approach effectively reduces training errors associated with deeper networks.

The SR-SegNet v2 architecture consists of a down-sampling network followed by an up-sampling network, with detailed specifications for each layer provided in Table 1. The down-sampling network progressively reduces the spatial dimensions while increasing the depth of feature maps through convolutional layers and pooling operations. The up-sampling network reverses this process, gradually reconstructing the output to match the original input dimensions, culminating in an output layer that produces a segmented map of the input image.

Overall, the integration of modified residual blocks within the SR-SegNet framework aims to enhance segmentation performance, particularly for small and challenging objects, while leveraging the benefits of residual learning to optimize training efficiency and accuracy.

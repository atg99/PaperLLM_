The paper presents a comparison of various segmentation models applied to lake identification, including FCN32s, FCN16s, FCN8s, DeconvNet, SegNet, and two versions of SR-SegNet. The segmentation metrics, including accuracy (Ac), Dice coefficient, F1 score, and mean intersection over union (Miou), are reported for each model, with the highest values highlighted. 

SR-SegNet v1 and v2 demonstrate the best performance across all metrics, particularly excelling in lake boundary extraction compared to simpler models like FCN and DeconvNet, which struggle with detail and often misidentify non-lake areas. SegNet shows improved segmentation over these simpler models but still fails to accurately extract lake details.

Figure 8 illustrates the performance of the models on different lakes, highlighting SR-SegNet's effectiveness in addressing network degradation and accurately recognizing small lakes. Additionally, Figure 9 compares the training curves of SR-SegNet v2 and SegNet, showing that SR-SegNet v2 is more stable and performs better due to the incorporation of modified residual blocks. Overall, the results indicate that SR-SegNet models significantly enhance lake segmentation performance compared to traditional methods.

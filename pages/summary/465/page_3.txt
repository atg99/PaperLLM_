The proposed SR-SegNet aims to address the limitations of classical SegNet, particularly in remote sensing image segmentation, where factors such as spectrum and resolution affect the accuracy of lake and river segmentation. By integrating high-level and low-level features through cascading, SR-SegNet enhances segmentation accuracy while significantly reducing training parameters and prediction time. Experimental results demonstrate that SR-SegNet outperforms existing models like FCN, SegNet, and DeconvNet, achieving higher F1-scores, mean intersection over union (Miou), and recall rates.

The paper is structured as follows: Section 2 delves into the SR-SegNet architecture, Section 3 outlines the experimental setup using the Lake and River dataset, and Section 4 concludes with future research directions.

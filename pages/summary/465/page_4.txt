The paper presents the development of SR-SegNet, a modified version of the classical SegNet designed for water body segmentation in remote sensing images. Key modifications include reducing the number of upsampling layers from five to four and eliminating the last five convolution layers in the encoding stage to streamline the network. Additionally, a cascade method is implemented to fuse deep and shallow features, enhancing the capture of detailed location information of water bodies. 

To further simplify the network and reduce computational load, depthwise separable convolutions are introduced. However, the replacement of some 3x3 convolution kernels with 2x2 kernels in the modified residual block leads to suboptimal boundary extraction of water bodies. To address this issue, the authors propose SR-SegNet v2, which incorporates dilated convolutions. Experimental results demonstrate that SR-SegNet v2 effectively improves segmentation results. The input for the model consists of three-channel remote sensing images, and the output is a binary segmentation map indicating water bodies and background.

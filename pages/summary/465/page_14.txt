The paper discusses the performance of different segmentation methods on a dataset containing images of lakes and rivers. It highlights the challenges faced by the classical SegNet model in accurately segmenting rivers compared to lakes. The authors attribute this difficulty to two main factors: the lower proportion of positive pixels in river images relative to negative pixels, and the inherent complexity in extracting river features compared to lake features. 

To address these challenges, the authors introduce two versions of a modified segmentation model, SR-SegNet v1 and SR-SegNet v2. These models utilize depth separable convolutions to reduce upsampling time and the number of training parameters while maintaining effective feature extraction. Additionally, a modified residual block is incorporated in the encoding stage to mitigate network degradation issues, allowing for better information extraction.

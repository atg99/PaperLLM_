The paper discusses various methodologies and algorithms for path planning and navigation of autonomous maritime vessels, highlighting the integration of modern computational techniques. Key studies cited include the use of genetic algorithms, artificial potential fields, and deep reinforcement learning to enhance decision-making in navigation while adhering to maritime regulations such as COLREGS. Significant contributions from researchers are noted, including reactive path planning, cooperative navigation, and energy-efficient control approaches, showcasing a range of strategies from heuristic methods to advanced machine learning techniques. The importance of safety and collision avoidance in autonomous navigation is emphasized, alongside the need for efficient data management and trajectory optimization. Overall, the cited works collectively contribute to the advancement of autonomous navigation technologies in maritime environments.

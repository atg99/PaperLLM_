The referenced papers address various aspects of image generation and manipulation, particularly focusing on the detection of fake images and the underlying technologies used in their creation. 

Key contributions include:

1. Wang et al. (2019) explore methods for detecting photoshopped faces through scripting in Photoshop, highlighting the challenges in identifying manipulated images.
2. Yu et al. (2015) discuss the LSUN dataset, which utilizes deep learning and human input for large-scale image dataset construction, enhancing the quality of training data for generative models.
3. Ning et al. (2019) analyze the unique fingerprints left by Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) in generated images, providing insights into attribution and detection.
4. Zhang et al. (2019) introduce self-attention mechanisms in GANs to improve the quality of generated images by allowing the network to focus on relevant features.
5. Zhou et al. (2016) and Peng et al. (2018) contribute to the understanding of feature learning for image manipulation detection, emphasizing the importance of rich feature extraction in identifying altered images.
6. Zhu et al. (2015 and 2017) present methodologies for realism perception in composite images and unpaired image-to-image translation, respectively, showcasing advancements in the capabilities of GANs.

Overall, these works collectively advance the field of image synthesis and manipulation detection, underscoring the ongoing battle between creation and detection of artificial images.

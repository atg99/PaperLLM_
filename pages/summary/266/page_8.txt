The cited works focus on the application of capsule networks with dynamic routing in natural language processing tasks, specifically relation extraction and text classification. Ningyu Zhang et al. (2018) propose an attention-based capsule network for efficiently extracting relationships from text, highlighting the advantages of capsule networks in capturing spatial hierarchies and improving performance over traditional methods. Wei Zhao et al. (2018) investigate the effectiveness of capsule networks for text classification, demonstrating their ability to outperform conventional models by leveraging the dynamic routing mechanism to better understand the context and relationships within text. Both studies contribute to advancing the capabilities of neural network architectures in processing and understanding natural language.

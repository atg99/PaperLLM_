The article from Nature Immunology presents findings on the interactions between pentraxin 3 (PTX3) and various proteins of the SARS-CoV-2 virus. It details several experiments demonstrating the binding affinities of PTX3, Ficolin-2, CL-K1, and MBL to the nucleocapsid and spike proteins of the virus. Specifically, it shows that PTX3 binds to captured SARS-CoV-2 nucleocapsid proteins from different sources, while Ficolin-2 and CL-K1 also exhibit binding to the nucleocapsid. Additionally, MBL, Ficolin-2, and CL-K1 are shown to bind to the spike protein, with MBL and CL-L1/CL-K1 heterocomplexes binding to the spike protein-coated plates as well. The data is presented as mean ± SEM, with sample sizes noted for each experiment.

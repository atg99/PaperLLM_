The article discusses interactions involving the nucleocapsid protein of SARS-CoV-2 and various immune components, particularly focusing on the roles of mannose-binding lectin (MBL) and surfactant protein D (SP-D). It confirms previous findings regarding MBL's association with the nucleocapsid protein, while noting that MBL's interaction with the nucleocapsid was absent in the current study. Additionally, it references research by Hsieh et al. that demonstrated an interaction between SP-D and the spike protein of SARS-CoV-2. The study employs advanced imaging techniques to analyze the colocalization of MBL with the spike protein, indicating potential pathways for immune response modulation against SARS-CoV-2. Overall, the findings contribute to the understanding of how specific proteins in the immune system engage with viral components, which could inform therapeutic strategies.

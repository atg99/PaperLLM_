The paper outlines the statistical methodologies and analyses employed in a study investigating genetic associations related to immune response, particularly focusing on mannose-binding lectin (MBL) and its genetic variants in the context of COVID-19. Key statistical tools utilized include GraphPad Prism software for ANOVA comparisons, logistic regression using PLINK for genetic studies, and various tests to identify outliers and normalize data. The analysis involved case-control allele-dose association tests, stratification of individuals by genotype, and haplotype assessments using both PLINK and Beagle software. 

The study examined specific single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) linked to MBL functionality, assessing their distribution among infected and healthy individuals. It also included a meta-analysis utilizing data from the Regeneron Genetic Center to explore the role of rare genetic variants in severe COVID-19 outcomes. The authors acknowledged the support from various organizations and funding bodies, emphasizing the collaborative effort in the research. Overall, the paper details robust statistical frameworks aimed at elucidating the genetic factors influencing immune responses during the COVID-19 pandemic.

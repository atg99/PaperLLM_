The article presents a model for the Omicron Spike-MBL complex, highlighting the glycosylation patterns of the spike protein. Glycosylation sites are color-coded based on their oligomannose content, with gold indicating less than 60%, purple representing greater than 80% up to the S2’ region, and blue signifying over 80% in the S2’ region. Additionally, mutations specific to the Omicron variant are marked in magenta, emphasizing the structural changes associated with this variant.

The study investigates the interaction between mannose-binding lectin (MBL) and the spike protein of SARS-CoV-2 in human bronchial epithelial cells (HBECs). MBL treatment was found to inhibit the production of interleukin-8 (IL-8) and CXCL5, which are key chemokines that facilitate myeloid cell recruitment and activation. Using confocal microscopy, researchers observed that MBL colocalized with the SARS-CoV-2 spike protein in infected HBECs, particularly at the apical side of cytokeratin 14-positive cells. Super-resolution microscopy further confirmed the interaction at a molecular scale. The study also explored various spike protein variants and their glycosylation patterns, highlighting the complex relationship between viral proteins and host immune responses. Overall, the findings suggest that MBL may play a significant role in modulating the immune response to SARS-CoV-2 infection.

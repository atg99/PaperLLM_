The article presents an analysis of the interaction between recombinant receptor-binding domain (RBD) and immobilized mannose-binding lectin (MBL) using Surface Plasmon Resonance (SPR). The study evaluates how the presence or absence of calcium influences this interaction. SPR analysis is a powerful tool for assessing molecular interactions, providing insights into the binding dynamics and affinities of the RBD-MBL complex, which is relevant for understanding immune responses.

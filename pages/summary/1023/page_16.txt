The article discusses various contributions from multiple authors in the fields of genetic analysis, imaging, SPR analysis, and bioinformatics related to immune response and complement activation. It highlights the roles of E.M.P., supervised by S.D. and R.A., in genetic analysis, while A.D. focused on imaging. The SPR analysis and modeling were conducted by M.P., L.V., M.M., and A.C., and S.N.M. performed bioinformatic analysis. Essential tools and materials were provided by M.U., R.R., P.I., R.B.-O., and P. Garred, with additional experiments carried out by F.S., M. Sironi, C.P., D.C., P. Gallina, N.P., V.C., N.C., and N.M. 

The paper also discloses competing interests, noting that A.M., C.G., and B.B. hold patents related to PTX3 and MBL, while R.R. is affiliated with GSK. Other authors declare no competing interests. Extended data and supplementary materials are accessible through specified links. The peer review process is acknowledged, with thanks to reviewers and editors involved.

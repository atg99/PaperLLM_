The paper introduces BioGPT, a generative pre-trained Transformer language model specifically designed for biomedical text generation and mining. Inspired by the success of pre-trained language models in general natural language processing, BioGPT addresses the limitations of existing BERT-like models, which excel in understanding tasks but lack generative capabilities. 

BioGPT is pre-trained on a large corpus of biomedical literature, specifically 15 million PubMed abstracts, and is evaluated across six biomedical NLP tasks, including end-to-end relation extraction, question answering, document classification, and text generation. The results demonstrate that BioGPT outperforms previous models, achieving notable F1 scores of 44.98% on BC5CDR, 38.42% on KD-DTI, and 40.76% on DDI relation extraction tasks, along with a record accuracy of 78.2% on PubMedQA.

The paper emphasizes the importance of adapting pre-training techniques to the biomedical domain to overcome performance issues caused by domain shift. BioGPT's architecture and pre-training approach are tailored to enhance its effectiveness for biomedical applications, showcasing its potential in generating fluent descriptions of biomedical terms and facilitating knowledge discovery in the field. The code for BioGPT is made publicly available, promoting further research and application in biomedical NLP.

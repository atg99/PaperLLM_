The study explored various target sequence formats for relation extraction, revealing that natural language formats, especially the rel-is format, outperform structured formats that use special tokens. This highlights the importance of format consistency between inputs and outputs for effective learning and generation in BioGPT. 

Additionally, an ablation study on prompt design showed that continuous embedding soft prompts generally yield better results than manually designed hard prompts. The optimal performance was achieved with a prompt length of 13 virtual tokens, emphasizing the need for informative prompts in enhancing model performance.

In conclusion, BioGPT represents a significant advancement in biomedical text generation and mining, achieving state-of-the-art results in several tasks while offering insights into the effectiveness of prompt design and target sequence formatting.

The paper introduces BioGPT, a generative pre-trained Transformer language model designed specifically for the biomedical domain. BioGPT demonstrates state-of-the-art performance on various tasks, including end-to-end relation extraction and question answering, achieving top results on four benchmarks: BC5CDR, KD-DTI, DDI, and PubMedQA. Additionally, it outperforms GPT-2 in biomedical text generation.

Key contributions of the study include the development of BioGPT for biomedical literature generation and mining, and the exploration of prompt and target sequence designs that enhance performance on downstream tasks. The authors found that using natural language semantics for target sequences yields better results than previously used structured prompts.

The paper also discusses the scaling of BioGPT to a larger version, BioGPT-Large, which employs the GPT-2 XL architecture with 1.5 billion parameters. The performance metrics for this larger model on various tasks are summarized, showcasing its effectiveness in the biomedical field.

Future work will focus on training even larger models on extensive biomedical datasets and applying BioGPT to additional tasks.

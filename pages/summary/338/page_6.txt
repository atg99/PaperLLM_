The paper presents results from various tasks involving drug-target interaction extraction, drug-drug interaction extraction, question answering, document classification, and text generation, utilizing models like BioGPT, GPT-2 medium, and REBEL. 

In the KD-DTI drug-target interaction extraction task, BioGPT outperforms all other models with an F1 score of 38.42%, significantly improving upon GPT-2 medium and REBEL. For the DDI drug-drug interaction extraction task, BioGPT achieves an F1 score of 40.76%, surpassing both GPT-2 medium and REBEL models. 

In the PubMedQA question answering task, BioGPT reaches a new state-of-the-art accuracy of 78.2%, showing a 6.0% improvement over the previous best performance. Additionally, in document classification using the Hallmarks of Cancers corpus, BioGPT records an F1 score of 85.12%, outperforming other models including BioBERT and PubMedBERT.

The text generation capabilities of BioGPT are also evaluated, revealing that it generates more specific and professional biomedical descriptions compared to GPT-2, particularly for uncommon drug names, indicating its effectiveness in producing contextually relevant biomedical text.

Overall, BioGPT demonstrates superior performance across various biomedical NLP tasks, highlighting its utility in the field.

The paper presents BioGPT, a model fine-tuned for relation extraction tasks, specifically focusing on the BC5CDR and KD-DTI datasets. For BC5CDR, the authors fine-tune both GPT-2 medium and BioGPT for 100 epochs, utilizing a peak learning rate of 10^-5 and 100 warm-up steps. The study employs continuous embeddings as prompts and a specific target sequence format to identify binary relations between entities. Evaluation is based on the micro-F1 score, with BioGPT outperforming other models, achieving a score of 44.98%, which is a significant improvement over REBEL and seq2rel. Notably, the pipeline-based method GLRE shows diminished performance when using open-source NER tools instead of ground truth annotations, highlighting the challenges in practical applications.

For the KD-DTI dataset, which includes 12k training documents, BioGPT is fine-tuned for 30 epochs under similar conditions. The results demonstrate that BioGPT achieves an F1 score of 38.42%, surpassing other models such as Transformer + PubMedBERT-attn, GPT-2 medium, and REBEL by notable margins. This indicates that BioGPT effectively enhances performance in drug-target interaction tasks without requiring extensive pre-training on large datasets.

Overall, the findings underscore BioGPT's efficacy in relation extraction tasks compared to existing models, particularly in scenarios where high-quality NER annotations are not available.

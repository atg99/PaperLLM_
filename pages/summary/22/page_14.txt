The study evaluates the effectiveness of a computer-aided diagnostic (CAD) tool, specifically DenseNet201, in classifying pneumonia from chest X-ray images. The tool demonstrated high accuracy, precision, and recall rates for distinguishing between normal, bacterial, and viral pneumonia images, achieving scores of 98%, 97%, and 99% for normal images, and lower but significant scores for pneumonia types. Given the high mortality rates among children from pneumonia, the authors emphasize the importance of timely diagnosis and intervention, particularly in resource-limited settings where doctors face time constraints. The CAD tool is proposed to assist radiologists in quickly identifying pneumonia types, potentially enhancing clinical outcomes and facilitating applications in areas like airport screenings.

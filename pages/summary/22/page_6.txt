The paper discusses the methodology employed in a study that utilizes the Kaggle chest X-ray pneumonia database, which consists of 5247 chest X-ray images with resolutions ranging from 400p to 2000p. Among these images, 3906 are associated with pneumonia cases—2561 for bacterial pneumonia and 1345 for viral pneumonia—while the remaining 1341 images are from normal subjects. Notably, the dataset does not include instances of mixed viral and bacterial infections. The dataset was divided into training and test sets for analysis. The details of the dataset are summarized in a table, which categorizes the number of images by type.

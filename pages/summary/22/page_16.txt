The referenced literature predominantly explores the application of deep learning techniques in medical imaging, particularly in the classification and diagnosis of various conditions using chest radiography and CT scans. Key studies highlight the effectiveness of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) in distinguishing between bacterial and viral pneumonia, as well as in identifying pulmonary tuberculosis and lung nodules. The importance of transfer learning is emphasized, showcasing its utility in enhancing model performance for medical image analysis. Additionally, various neural network architectures such as ResNet, AlexNet, and Inception are discussed, providing insights into their respective strengths and applications in the field. The papers collectively illustrate the growing impact of AI research on medical diagnostics, underscoring the potential for improved accuracy and efficiency in disease detection through advanced computational methods.

This study investigates the application of various algorithms for the classification of chest X-ray images, focusing on normal cases and different types of pneumonia (bacterial and viral). The dataset comprises a total of 5247 X-ray images, with specific training and testing sets detailed in Table 2. The training sets utilize data augmentation to enhance the model's learning capability, with 4500 images for each category in the training set.

Four pre-trained convolutional neural network (CNN) models—AlexNet, ResNet18, DenseNet201, and SqueezeNet—were employed for training and evaluation. The training was conducted using MATLAB 2019a on a system equipped with an Intel i7 processor and a GPU, ensuring efficient processing.

The paper discusses estimation methods for parameters in nonlinear regression models, focusing on the least squares approach. The objective is to minimize the sum of squared residuals, represented mathematically as S(θ)=Σ(yi−f(xi,θ))², to find the least squares estimators denoted as bθ. An example provided is the Beverton-Holt model, where the parameters α and β are estimated by minimizing S(α,β)=Σ(yi−αxi/(1+xi/β))². 

The paper derives the gradients (gα, gβ) and the Hessian matrix elements (Hα,α, Hα,β, Hβ,α, Hβ,β) needed for optimization. It highlights the use of iterative methods such as the Gauss-Newton and Newton-Raphson methods for estimating the parameters, with the Gauss-Newton method applying an approximation to the Hessian matrix using the Jacobian.

Additionally, when the function is twice differentiable, the gradient and Hessian can be expressed in terms of the residuals and their derivatives. The least squares solution is found by solving the normal equations, where the gradients are set to zero. This systematic approach ensures that the estimators are derived efficiently and accurately for nonlinear regression models.

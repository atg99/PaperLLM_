It is essential to consider the curvature of the parameter space as it can impact the convergence behavior of iterative estimation techniques. Nonlinear models may exhibit varying degrees of curvature, which affects the local behavior of the likelihood function or the least squares criterion near the true parameter values. 

In conclusion, the paper highlights the equivalence of maximum likelihood and least squares estimators under normal noise conditions, the asymptotic properties of these estimators in nonlinear regression models, and the implications of parameterization and curvature on estimation procedures and inference. The discussion emphasizes the importance of understanding the relationship between different estimation methods and the statistical properties of the resulting confidence regions, particularly in the context of finite sample sizes versus asymptotic results.

The paper presents an analysis of hypergraph datasets and introduces a method called FastHyperGCN for semi-supervised learning. The datasets analyzed include DBLP, Pubmed, Cora, Cora Citeseer, and Citeseer, characterized by their hypernode and hyperedge counts, average hyperedge sizes, feature dimensions, and class distributions. The study reveals a strong positive skewness in the distribution of hyperedge sizes.

FastHyperGCN is designed to construct the hypergraph Laplacian matrix using only initial features, significantly reducing training time as the matrix is computed once prior to training. The method is evaluated alongside other models, including HyperGCN, 1-HyperGCN, hypergraph neural networks (HGNN), multi-layer perceptrons (MLP), and a confidence interval-based method (CI).

Experiments involve predicting document topics through multi-class classification. Furthermore, FastHyperGCN also surpasses 1-HyperGCN, likely due to the handling of larger hyperedges. The paper emphasizes the effectiveness of FastHyperGCN in leveraging hypergraph structures for improved performance in semi-supervised learning tasks.

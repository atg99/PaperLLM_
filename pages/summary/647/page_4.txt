The paper presents HyperGCN, a Hypergraph Convolutional Network designed for semi-supervised learning (SSL) focused on hypernode classification within undirected hypergraphs. The objective is to predict labels for unlabelled hypernodes based on a small set of labelled examples. Each hypernode is linked to a feature vector, and the method leverages the inherent similarity among hypernodes within the same hyperedge to enhance classification accuracy.

The training process involves minimizing cross-entropy loss through gradient descent, utilizing a defined Laplacian for the hypergraph as an implicit regularizer. This approach allows for the representation of hypernodes to be close together in feature space if they belong to the same hyperedge. The paper introduces the hypergraph Laplacian, which is crucial for applying Graph Convolutional Networks (GCN) to hypergraphs.

The authors detail the computation of the hypergraph Laplacian and highlight the significance of maintaining close representations for hypernodes connected by hyperedges. They propose an approach termed 1-HyperGCN that approximates hyperedges with pairwise edges to facilitate the application of GCN techniques.

Overall, HyperGCN aims to improve the classification of hypernodes by utilizing the structural properties of hypergraphs and the relationships among connected hypernodes.

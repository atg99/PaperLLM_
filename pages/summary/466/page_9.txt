The paper presents Duo-SegNet, an adversarial dual-view learning approach designed for semi-supervised medical image segmentation. The study's effectiveness is validated through extensive experiments on three publicly available medical datasets. Key findings indicate that the implementation of a min-max paradigm within a multi-view learning framework enhances the delineation of boundaries in prediction masks, achieving performance levels comparable to full supervision despite limited annotations. The ablation study reveals that Duo-SegNet outperforms variations such as the exclusion of the critic and unlabeled data, as well as a single segmentation network. Additionally, the analysis of hyper-parameters indicates that the optimal value for the parameter ϕu is 0.3, corresponding to the highest DICE score. Future work will explore self-tuning mechanisms to further improve dual-view training.

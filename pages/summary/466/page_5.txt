Duo-SegNet introduces a novel approach to semi-supervised segmentation by utilizing a critic to generate pixel-wise uncertainty maps or confidence maps. This mechanism ensures a higher-order consistency between the predicted masks and the ground truth, identifying areas where predictions align closely with the true distribution. The critic loss is formulated as a combination of two adversarial losses, aiming to enhance the confidence of the segmentation predictions against the ground truth.

The training process involves co-training two segmentation networks that strive to outsmart the critic, effectively maximizing the reliability of their predictions. The critic is trained using labeled predictions alongside the corresponding ground truth segmentation masks, utilizing a normalized loss function.

The training algorithm is structured to iteratively update the network parameters for both segmentation networks and the critic over multiple epochs and batches. Segmentation predictions are generated for both labeled and unlabeled data, and confidence maps are computed to inform the loss calculations. The overall optimization strategy follows a min-max approach, balancing the updates between the segmentation networks and the critic.

In the related work section, the paper also highlights other semi-supervised learning methods, particularly those that utilize pseudo-labeling, which relates closely to the proposed Duo-SegNet framework. This provides context for the innovations introduced in the study.

The paper introduces Duo-SegNet 3, an innovative framework for image segmentation that utilizes a dual view segmentation network alongside a critic network. The primary aim is to enhance segmentation performance by learning from both labeled and unlabeled data. The dataset comprises pairs of images and their corresponding ground-truth masks, while the unlabeled images serve to improve the model's learning capacity.

Duo-SegNet consists of two main components: dual view segmentation networks (F1 and F2) designed as UNet architectures and a critic network that evaluates the quality of the segmentation masks against the ground truth. The model employs a min-max optimization strategy where the segmentation networks strive to produce masks that can deceive the critic, thus ensuring they closely resemble realistic segmentations.

To facilitate training, the authors propose a multitask loss function that incorporates three distinct loss components: supervised loss (Ls), unsupervised loss (Lu), and critic loss (Lc). Each loss term contributes differently to the overall loss function, with hyper-parameters (s, u, c) controlling their impact. The approach allows for collaborative training of the dual view networks, ultimately leading to improved segmentation accuracy.

The paper highlights the effectiveness of this method and notes that tuning the network's hyper-parameters is manageable, paving the way for practical applications in image segmentation tasks.

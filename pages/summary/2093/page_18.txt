The paper discusses the implementation of a motion planning algorithm for a mobile robot, specifically a Turtlebot, navigating through obstacles in a symmetric layout. It emphasizes the importance of an obstacle collision mitigation method to ensure safe navigation. The study includes two experiments, each consisting of three sub-experiments, repeated ten times to average the results. The dimensions of the obstacles range from 21.5cm to 93.0cm, indicating varied sizes that the robot must navigate around effectively.

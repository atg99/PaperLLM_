The paper presents a symmetric environment layout for a robot navigation system, featuring obstacles of various dimensions and shapes. The layout includes starting areas (S1, S2) and destination areas (D1, D2). The robot is equipped with two bumpers that help mitigate the impact of collisions by immediately reducing speed upon detecting nearby objects with infrared sensors. In the event of a collision, the robot executes a controlled response to avoid odometry errors, which involves stopping, reversing, and selecting a temporary location away from the obstacle. The recovery process includes rotating towards this temporary location, moving for a brief period, and then returning to the motion planning algorithm to determine a new path to the destination. The paper notes that, apart from Dijkstra's algorithm, there is no further information provided on navigation strategies.

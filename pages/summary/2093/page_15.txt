The paper describes a maze environment designed for robot navigation, with specific dimensions and obstacle placements. The layout measures 8m by 5m, featuring start (S) and destination (D) points marked in blue and red, respectively. Obstacles, represented as black rectangles, are categorized into two types: A and B, with dimensions of 79cm by 60cm and 92cm by 60cm, respectively. The spacing between obstacles is intentionally set to less than twice the robot's diameter (55cm), ensuring navigability. Gaps between obstacles and walls measure 84cm on the right and 63cm on the left. Additionally, Figure 2 provides various perspectives of the maze to illustrate obstacle density.

The paper outlines the settings for various algorithms used in motion planning experiments, focusing on both classical and meta-heuristic methods. Key algorithms discussed include Potential Field (PF), Dijkstra's Algorithm (DA), Rapidly-exploring Random Tree (RRT), and Probabilistic Road Map (PRM), with DA receiving the full layout of the environment while others rely on real-time sensory input. The settings for each algorithm are specified, such as minimum range for PF, randomness point for RRT, and population size for Genetic Algorithm (GA) and Differential Evolution (DE). The performance of these algorithms is evaluated against DA, which serves as the benchmark. Seven performance factors are used for comparison, with parameter settings for meta-heuristic methods drawn from prior research. The results aim to assess the effectiveness of each algorithm in navigating from a fixed starting point to a destination.

The paper presents a comparative review of mobile robot path planning methods, focusing on classical techniques versus meta-heuristic approaches. It analyzes the strengths and weaknesses of each category, highlighting how classical methods provide optimal solutions in structured environments, while meta-heuristic methods offer flexibility and adaptability in complex and dynamic settings. The review emphasizes the importance of selecting appropriate path planning strategies based on specific application requirements and environmental conditions. Key concepts include algorithm efficiency, computational complexity, and practical implementation challenges in real-world scenarios. Overall, the paper aims to guide researchers and practitioners in choosing the most suitable path planning method for their mobile robotics applications.

The paper describes two experiments involving different layout designs for robotic navigation. Experiment I features a maze layout, while Experiment II employs a symmetric layout, which is 6 meters long and 5 meters wide. The symmetric layout includes irregular obstacles and two starting points (S1 and S2) and two destination points (D1 and D2). Three sub-experiments are conducted, focusing on routes S1 to D1, S2 to D1, and S2 to D2, with the S2 to D1 route excluded due to redundancy. The Turtlebot, measuring 30 cm in width and equipped with six infrared sensors, is utilized alongside the Robotic Operating System (ROS) to perform the experiments. Key details about obstacle dimensions are provided in a table, and figures illustrate the layout from various angles.

In summary, the discussed algorithms for cluster head (CH) selection in wireless sensor networks (WSNs) utilize various optimization techniques, including Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO), Ant Colony Optimization (ACO), and Artificial Bee Colony (ABC). These methods aim to enhance energy efficiency, network lifespan, and data delivery performance by considering factors like node energy levels, distances, and cluster formation strategies. The hybrid approaches, such as Tabu-PSO and FAMACROW, show promise in overcoming limitations of traditional algorithms by improving node selection diversity and reliability through intelligent parameter integration and optimization techniques.

The paper discusses the application of swarm intelligence (SI) algorithms in the Internet of Things (IoT), emphasizing their relevance in wireless sensor networks (WSN) and UAV-assisted communications. It highlights the importance of node localization as a complex optimization problem, solvable through SI algorithms. UAVs are recognized for their dynamic deployment and autonomy, with three primary use cases: coverage enhancement, relay functions for remote communication, and data collection in smart environments.

The challenges in UAV networks are noted, including the need for indirect link establishment due to dynamic topologies and energy efficiency. The paper reviews existing literature on SI algorithms in WSN, noting gaps in previous studies, particularly regarding the limited focus on certain algorithms like particle swarm optimization (PSO) and the lack of comprehensive analyses across various SI applications.

To address these gaps, the paper classifies SI applications in IoT into three categories: SI in WSN, UAV-aided wireless networks, and other IoT applications. It further categorizes UAV roles into base stations, relays, and information collectors, while summarizing key SI algorithms such as PSO, ant colony optimization (ACO), and artificial bee colony (ABC). 

The main contributions include a detailed overview of over 40 SI applications in IoT, a comparison of 11 SI-based cluster head selection algorithms and 13 routing protocols in WSNs, and an analysis of validation platforms and parameter selection, highlighting the strengths and weaknesses of each method.

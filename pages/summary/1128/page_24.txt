The paper discusses the application of Swarm Intelligence (SI) algorithms in enhancing wireless security, particularly in the context of Internet of Things (IoT) and Wireless Sensor Networks (WSN). SI algorithms are currently utilized for intrusion detection, cryptography, and computational efficiency in cryptographic tasks. The authors emphasize the need for improvements in the global optimization capabilities and convergence speed of these algorithms to reduce false alarm rates in intrusion detection systems.

As IoT technologies advance, the proliferation of IoT devices presents challenges that 5G networks must address, including low latency and high capacity. WSNs are pivotal for monitoring large areas, yet face challenges due to the limited energy of nodes. The paper argues that SI algorithms can effectively tackle complex optimization problems within WSN applications, such as Cluster Head (CH) selection and node collaboration.

The review categorizes UAV-assisted wireless networks into three types based on their principles and analyzes their applications in conjunction with SI algorithms. The authors highlight the synergy between UAVs and other technologies, including 5G and mobile edge computing, as a promising direction for future development in IoT.

The research was supported by various funding initiatives and acknowledges contributions from all authors involved in the study. The paper concludes by underscoring the potential of SI algorithms in solving optimization issues in wireless networks and their wide applicability in WSNs.

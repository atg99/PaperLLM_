Additionally, the paper discusses various approaches in sensor deployment and localization, highlighting the use of algorithms such as fuzzy clustering combined with Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) to enhance network connectivity and reduce energy use. The hybrid FCM-PSO algorithm optimally determines sensor topology, leading to significant improvements over traditional methods. A PSO-based coverage algorithm is also described, which effectively partitions networks into grids while optimizing sensor deployment to achieve a high coverage rate, albeit with limitations in obstacle-affected environments.

The paper further explores node localization using dimension-based PSO and butterfly optimization algorithms, both of which enhance positioning accuracy in challenging environments. These approaches outperform conventional methods by offering better accuracy and speed in node positioning.

Overall, the integration of swarm intelligence in various aspects of wireless sensor networks, including deployment, localization, and UAV assistance, showcases significant advancements in efficiency, accuracy, and network performance.

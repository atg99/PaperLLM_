This paper presents a comprehensive review of the application of swarm intelligence (SI) algorithms in the Internet of Things (IoT), highlighting the synergy between advancing technologies and collaborative behaviors observed in nature. The authors discuss how SI, characterized by collective behaviors of groups with limited individual intelligence, can effectively tackle complex optimization problems within IoT frameworks, particularly in wireless sensor networks (WSN).

The paper provides an overview of various SI algorithms, including ant colony optimization, particle swarm optimization, and artificial bee colony algorithms, detailing their functionality and applications. It emphasizes SI's role in enhancing WSN performance and addresses pertinent research challenges within this domain. Moreover, the review extends to other IoT applications, such as using SI in unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) networks, illustrating the versatility of these algorithms across different contexts.

In conclusion, the authors propose future research directions and trends, advocating for the continued exploration of SI algorithms to optimize IoT systems and improve information sharing and decision-making processes. The review serves as a foundational resource for researchers and practitioners interested in the intersection of swarm intelligence and IoT technologies.

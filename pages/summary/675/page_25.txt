The paper evaluates the performance of the Time-Continuous Delay Function (TCDF) in causal discovery from time series data. It highlights that when TCDF is applied to time series with at least 1000 time steps, it outperforms other methods in identifying time delays, achieving a correct discovery rate of approximately 95-97%. The study also demonstrates TCDF's capability to identify hidden confounders in a small case study. Future research will focus on hyperparameter optimization and testing TCDF across diverse datasets with varying noise levels, stationarity, and time delays. Enhancements may include improving attention interpretation and exploring additional causal validation methods.

The results indicate that TCDF, despite its minor inaccuracies, outperformed other methods in terms of discovering hidden confounders. Specifically, TCDF identified three hidden confounders, while PCMCI, tsFCI, and TiMINo failed to locate any. Notably, TCDF's attention mechanism effectively discerned potential causal relationships, though it missed some due to weak correlations arising from unequal delays. This highlights the method's robustness and strictness in causal interpretation.

In summary, the findings suggest that TCDF is a valuable tool for causal discovery, particularly in scenarios involving hidden confounders, outperforming existing methods in discovering both relationships and confounders, despite some limitations in accuracy.

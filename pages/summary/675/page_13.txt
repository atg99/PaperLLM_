This validation process allows TCDF to effectively identify true causal relationships within time series data, despite the challenges posed by the inability to conduct controlled experiments.

The paper emphasizes the importance of using data-driven methods for causal validation when traditional experimental designs are impractical. By employing Permutation Importance as a causal validation technique, the authors argue that it offers a more reliable means of establishing causation compared to simply removing variables from the dataset. The method ensures that the chronological nature of time series data is respected, thereby providing a clearer understanding of the causal dynamics at play.

In summary, the TCDF framework utilizes a systematic approach to validate potential causal relationships in time series data by comparing the performance of predictive models on original and intervened datasets. This method not only enhances the accuracy of causal inference but also contributes to a deeper understanding of the underlying mechanisms driving the observed phenomena.

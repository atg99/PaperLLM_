The paper presents a study on causal graph structures using datasets from financial markets, specifically focusing on the FINANCE benchmark, which comprises ten different causal structures. The datasets are generated based on the Fama-French Three-Factor Model, which relates stock returns to three factors: volatility, size, and value, along with a portfolio-specific error term. 

To assess the detection of hidden confounders, the authors created a new benchmark, FINANCE HIDDEN, where one time series from selected datasets is masked by replacing its values with zeros. This setup allows for testing the TCDF method's ability to identify hidden confounders with both equal and unequal delays in their effects. 

Additionally, the authors established training and testing datasets, referred to as FINANCE TRAIN/TEST, using the first 80% of the data for training and the remaining 20% for testing to evaluate the predictive capabilities of the TCDF method. The paper includes visual representations of the datasets and causal graphs to illustrate the underlying structures and the impact of hidden confounders on the analysis.

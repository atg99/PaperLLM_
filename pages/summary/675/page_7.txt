The paper presents the Temporal Causal Discovery Framework (TCDF), which is designed to analyze time series data and construct a temporal causal graph. TCDF operates through a series of steps utilizing independent attention-based convolutional neural networks (CNNs), each targeting a different time series while maintaining the same architecture. 

Each network, denoted as Nj, aims to predict its respective target time series Xj by minimizing the loss between actual and predicted values. The input for each network consists of a dataset of N equal-sized time series of length T, where the target time series is represented by row Xj and the other rows are exogenous time series (X¬j).

As each network Nj predicts Xj, it generates attention scores (aj) that indicate which time series the network focuses on for its predictions. These scores suggest that the attended time series may hold relevant information for predicting Xj, thereby hinting at a potential causal relationship. Following the prediction, the framework conducts attention interpretation, causal validation, and delay discovery to ultimately construct the temporal causal graph. The TCDF thus provides a structured approach to uncovering causal relationships in time series data through the application of CNNs and attention mechanisms.

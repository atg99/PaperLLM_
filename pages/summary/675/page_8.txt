The proposed architecture, Attention-based Dilated Depthwise Separable Temporal Convolutional Networks (AD-DSTCNs), integrates an attention mechanism with dilated convolutions to enhance the capability of detecting causal relationships and their respective delays in time series predictions. The architecture builds on the foundational Temporal Convolutional Network (TCN) model, which utilizes causal convolutions to prevent information leakage from future time steps.

In summary, AD-DSTCNs leverage a unique combination of attention mechanisms and dilated convolutions to effectively model and predict time series data while identifying potential causal relationships. The methodology includes a detailed algorithm for cause detection and a Permutation Importance Validation Method (PIVM) for validating these causes. The overall approach culminates in the construction of a Temporal Causal Graph, illustrating the discovered causal relationships and their corresponding delays.

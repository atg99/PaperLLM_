The paper by OKSUZ et al. reviews imbalance problems in object detection, highlighting the complexities that arise in labeling examples and the resulting challenges for model training. It illustrates how ambiguities in labeling can mislead the detection process, as seen with misclassified positive boxes that either occlude other objects or lack descriptive features. 

The authors also address the unique challenges faced by bottom-up object detectors. While some methods like CornerNet and ExtremeNet have attempted to tackle foreground-background class imbalance using focal loss, there remains a gap in understanding specific imbalance issues pertinent to bottom-up approaches. The paper emphasizes the need for further research to explore these challenges and develop effective solutions.

In conclusion, the review provides a comprehensive taxonomy of imbalance problems and their solutions, while identifying open issues that require attention in the field of object detection. The authors advocate for a collective effort within the research community to navigate these challenges and improve detection methodologies.

The paper by Oksuz et al. reviews imbalance problems in object detection, a significant concern in machine learning and computer vision. The authors highlight how class imbalance affects the performance of detection systems and discuss various strategies to address these challenges.

Key concepts include the exploration of techniques such as class rectification hard mining, active learning, and generative adversarial networks (GANs) to enhance model training with imbalanced data. The review synthesizes findings from numerous studies, emphasizing methods that improve the representation of minority classes, such as diversified subset selection and data augmentation.

The authors also discuss the implications of these imbalance issues on model accuracy and the importance of focusing on high-variance samples. Additionally, they examine the use of deep metric learning approaches for better feature representation and the potential of multi-task learning frameworks to balance losses effectively during training.

Overall, the paper underscores the critical need for innovative solutions to tackle imbalance in object detection to improve model robustness and performance in real-world applications.

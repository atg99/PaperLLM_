The paper by Oksuz et al. presents a comprehensive review of imbalance problems in object detection, highlighting the challenges posed by class imbalance in datasets. The authors emphasize that traditional object detection models often struggle with underrepresented classes, leading to suboptimal performance. They explore various strategies for addressing these imbalances, including data augmentation techniques, focal loss, and the use of synthetic data generation methods like SMOTE. The review also discusses the importance of performance metrics that can better reflect the model's ability to detect minority classes, proposing new metrics such as Localization Recall Precision (LRP). The authors conclude that addressing class imbalance is crucial for improving the robustness and accuracy of object detection systems, suggesting that future research should focus on developing more effective techniques tailored to this issue.

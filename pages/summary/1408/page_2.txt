OKSUZ et al. present a comprehensive review of imbalance problems in object detection, emphasizing the impact of various imbalances on performance. The paper categorizes imbalance issues into several types, including class imbalances (foreground-background and foreground-foreground), scale imbalances at both object and feature levels, spatial imbalances in regression loss, IoU distribution imbalances, and objective imbalances related to task contributions.

The authors aim to systematically define and categorize these imbalance problems, providing a critical literature review that summarizes existing approaches and solutions. They also discuss open issues in the field and explore imbalance problems in other domains, highlighting the adaptability of various methods to the object detection pipeline. An accompanying webpage serves as a repository for ongoing research on these topics.

In contrast to previous reviews, which primarily focus on class imbalance and specific categories of object detection, this paper offers a broader classification of imbalance issues and a detailed analysis of methods addressing them. The review identifies gaps in existing literature, particularly regarding the comprehensive treatment of imbalances beyond just class issues, and emphasizes the need for a unified approach to tackle these challenges in the context of deep learning-based object detection.

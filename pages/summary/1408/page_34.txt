The paper by Okusz et al. addresses the challenges posed by imbalance problems in object detection, providing a comprehensive review of the existing literature. It highlights how class imbalance can significantly affect the performance of object detection systems, leading to biased predictions towards the more frequently represented classes. The authors discuss various strategies employed to mitigate these issues, including data augmentation, loss function adjustments, and algorithmic modifications. The review emphasizes the importance of developing robust methodologies to ensure fair and accurate detection across all classes, thus enhancing the overall effectiveness of object detection systems.

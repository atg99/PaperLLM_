In the paper "Imbalance Problems in Object Detection: A Review," Oksuz et al. provide a thorough examination of the imbalance issues faced in object detection (OD) within the context of deep learning. They introduce a problem-based taxonomy that categorizes imbalance problems into four main types: class imbalance, scale imbalance, spatial imbalance, and objective imbalance. Class imbalance highlights the disparity between the number of positive and negative examples, with foreground-to-background imbalance being a notable example. Scale imbalance addresses variations in object sizes and their representation in datasets, while spatial imbalance relates to the characteristics of bounding box placements. Objective imbalance involves the challenges posed by multiple loss functions during training.

The authors emphasize the significance of addressing these imbalance issues, as they can severely impact detection performance. They also identify eight specific imbalance problems prevalent in the literature and discuss existing solutions critically. To foster ongoing research, they provide an accompanying webpage that catalogs relevant studies according to their taxonomy, allowing researchers to stay updated on the field's developments. Overall, the paper aims to consolidate knowledge on imbalance problems in OD and highlight open issues for further investigation, making it a valuable resource for researchers in the domain.

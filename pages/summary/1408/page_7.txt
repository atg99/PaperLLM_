The paper by OKSUZ et al. addresses class imbalance issues in object detection, particularly focusing on the distribution of anchors for background and foreground classes in datasets like MS-COCO. The figures presented illustrate a significant imbalance, highlighting the need for effective solutions.

The authors categorize solutions to foreground-background class imbalance into four main groups: hard sampling methods, soft sampling methods, sampling-free methods, and generative methods. In sampling methods, the contribution of a bounding box to the loss function is adjusted based on a weight factor. Hard sampling methods utilize a binary weighting system, selecting a specific number of positive and negative examples based on heuristics, while soft sampling allows for a range of contributions.

Hard sampling is a prevalent technique that simplifies the selection process by focusing on a fixed number of examples. Random sampling, a straightforward approach, is employed in various detector families like R-CNN, where a specified number of positive and negative examples are randomly selected. However, more sophisticated strategies such as hard-example mining have emerged, which prioritize training on examples with higher loss values, thereby enhancing detector performance.

Deep learning-based methods have adopted variations of hard-example mining, with Online Hard Example Mining (OHEM) being notable for its systematic approach that considers loss values. Despite its effectiveness, OHEM presents challenges related to memory usage and training speed. As an alternative, IoU-based sampling has been proposed, which organizes negative samples into intervals based on their Intersection over Union (IoU) scores, allowing for targeted sampling that emphasizes harder examples.

To further optimize mining, the paper discusses two-stage object detectors that refine the search for bounding boxes, focusing on the most promising regions before applying additional sampling methods. Overall, the review provides a comprehensive overview of techniques to mitigate imbalance problems in object detection, emphasizing the importance of sample selection strategies in improving model performance.

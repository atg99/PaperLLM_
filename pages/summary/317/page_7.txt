4.3. Ablation Studies
To further validate the effectiveness of the integer-only approximation methods, we conduct ablation studies. These experiments assess the impact of individual components of the I-BERT framework on overall accuracy and performance. By systematically enabling and disabling specific integer operations, we can analyze their contributions. The findings reveal that the integer quantization methods employed in I-BERT not only maintain high accuracy but also enhance computational efficiency, reinforcing the potential of integer-only approaches in natural language processing tasks.

In conclusion, I-BERT presents a robust integer-only quantization method for BERT models, achieving competitive accuracy while significantly improving inference speed. The results affirm the viability of deploying integer-based models in various hardware environments, paving the way for more efficient natural language processing solutions.

I-BERT introduces an innovative integer-only quantization approach for BERT-like models, addressing the challenges posed by non-linear operations in Transformer architectures. Traditional integer-only inference has been effective in computer vision but struggles with the complexities of Transformer models due to operations like GELU, Softmax, and Layer Normalization, which cannot be efficiently computed with integer arithmetic.

The key contributions of I-BERT include the development of new kernels for accurate integer-only computation of GELU and Softmax through lightweight polynomial approximations, achieving minimal approximation errors. For LayerNorm, I-BERT utilizes an algorithm for integer square root computation, enabling integer-only processing. The model processes embeddings and matrix multiplications using INT8 arithmetic and re-quantizes results back from INT32 to INT8, maintaining integer representation throughout the computational graph.

I-BERT has been applied to RoBERTa-Base and RoBERTa-Large, achieving comparable accuracy to full-precision models on GLUE downstream tasks, with slight performance improvements. Additionally, deploying these models on T4 GPUs with TensorRT demonstrated significant speedups in inference times compared to FP32.

The paper also situates I-BERT within the broader context of neural network efficiency, discussing various techniques including pruning, knowledge distillation, and efficient architecture design, while highlighting the specific advancements in quantization applicable to Transformer-based models.

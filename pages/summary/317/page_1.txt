This paper introduces I-BERT, a quantization scheme specifically designed for Transformer-based models like BERT that utilizes integer-only arithmetic for the entire inference process. Unlike previous methods that still rely on floating-point calculations for certain operations, I-BERT implements lightweight integer-only approximations for nonlinear operations such as GELU, Softmax, and Layer Normalization.

The authors evaluate I-BERT on various GLUE downstream tasks using RoBERTa-Base and RoBERTa-Large models, demonstrating that I-BERT achieves comparable or slightly higher accuracy compared to full-precision baselines. Additionally, I-BERT shows significant performance improvements, achieving speedups of 2.4 to 4.0 times for INT8 inference on a T4 GPU compared to FP32 inference. The framework has been developed in PyTorch and is open-sourced, making it accessible for further research and application in efficient NLP model deployment. Overall, I-BERT addresses the challenges of deploying large Transformer models by enabling efficient integer-only inference without sacrificing accuracy.

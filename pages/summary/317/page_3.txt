The paper introduces I-BERT, a novel approach for integer-only quantization of BERT models, enhancing efficiency during inference by eliminating the need for floating point arithmetic. Prior quantization methods, including those by Shen et al. and Zadeh et al., typically use simulated quantization, where some operations remain in floating point, necessitating dequantization. In contrast, I-BERT employs a fully integer-based process throughout the inference phase, enabling faster execution and reduced energy consumption on specialized hardware.

I-BERT's approach is particularly significant as it overcomes limitations of existing quantization techniques primarily designed for convolutional neural networks (CNNs), which cannot handle the non-linear operations present in Transformer models, such as GELU and Softmax. The methodology is based on uniform symmetric quantization, where real numbers are mapped to integers using a defined quantization operator that incorporates clipping parameters and scaling factors.

Overall, I-BERT not only maintains accuracy without requiring floating point computations but also complements existing quantization strategies by facilitating deployment in mixed and low-precision settings. This advancement is poised to streamline the use of Transformer models in resource-constrained environments.

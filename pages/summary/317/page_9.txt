In the paper, the authors introduce I-BERT, an innovative integer-only quantization method for Transformers that executes inference using pure integer arithmetic. The core of I-BERT lies in its approximation techniques for nonlinear operations like GELU, Softmax, and LayerNorm, allowing these functions to be computed using integer arithmetic. Empirical evaluations on RoBERTa-Base and RoBERTa-Large models demonstrate that I-BERT enhances the average GLUE score by 0.3 and 0.5 points, respectively, compared to the baseline. Additionally, the quantized models exhibit an end-to-end inference latency improvement, achieving up to a 4.00x speedup on a Tesla T4 GPU relative to floating-point baselines. The authors suggest potential future work, such as utilizing the approximation methods to enhance training speed, including the exploration of replacing GELU with i-GELU during training.

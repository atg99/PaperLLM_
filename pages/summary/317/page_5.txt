The paper discusses the integer-only quantization of the GELU activation function used in Transformer models. The challenge lies in approximating non-linear functions effectively while maintaining integer arithmetic to prevent overflow. The authors introduce i-GELU, an integer-only approximation for GELU, which utilizes a second-order polynomial to achieve a close approximation.

Initially, the paper highlights the limitations of existing methods, such as using the hard Sigmoid to approximate GELU, which still involves non-linear functions requiring floating point calculations. This led to a significant accuracy drop when implemented in Transformers.

To improve upon this, the authors propose a polynomial optimization approach to approximate the error function integral in GELU. They restrict the optimization to a limited range where the error function behaves predictably, ultimately leading to the formulation of the i-GELU function. The polynomial used for the approximation is defined with specific coefficients, ensuring that i-GELU closely follows the behavior of GELU, especially near the origin.

The results demonstrate that i-GELU significantly outperforms the hard Sigmoid approximation (h-GELU) in terms of accuracy, with substantially lower approximation errors. The authors conclude that the integer-only nature of i-GELU allows for efficient computation without sacrificing performance, ultimately yielding improved accuracy in Transformer models over previous integer-only methods.

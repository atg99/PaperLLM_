In summary, the paper presents a novel single timescale actor-critic algorithm designed to effectively address the Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR) problem. By employing an LSTD method for the critic and a natural policy gradient method for the actor, the authors derive a convergence proof with a sample complexity of O(ε⁻¹ log(ε⁻¹)²) for achieving an ε-optimal solution. The primary challenge addressed is the interdependence of the actor and critic components, necessitating simultaneous analysis of both to ensure convergence. The work not only resolves the LQR problem but also contributes to the broader understanding of convergence in single timescale bilevel optimization, utilizing a Lyapunov function that captures the dynamics of both actor loss and critic error.

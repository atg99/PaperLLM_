The paper by Sihan Zeng, Thinh T Doan, and Justin Romberg presents a two-time-scale stochastic optimization framework applicable to control and reinforcement learning. This framework addresses the challenges associated with optimizing systems that operate on different time scales, enabling more efficient learning and control. The authors discuss the theoretical foundations of the approach, demonstrating its effectiveness through various applications.

Additionally, Mo Zhou introduces a single time-scale actor-critic method specifically designed to solve the linear quadratic regulator problem. The paper provides a convergence proof for this method, ensuring its reliability in achieving optimal control solutions. The implementation is available through a GitHub repository, offering practical resources for further exploration of the proposed techniques.

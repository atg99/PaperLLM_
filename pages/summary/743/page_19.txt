The paper presents a detailed analysis of the Single Timescale Actor-Critic Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR) framework. The discussion begins with foundational equations, including the expression for DKt involving the system dynamics and the control matrix. The author demonstrates that DKt can be expressed in a series form, which is essential for deriving further results.

The proof of Lemma 1 establishes the relationship between DKt and its bounds, utilizing properties of the matrix (A - BKt). The argument is supported by showing that the spectral radius of the matrix is less than one, which allows for the convergence of the series. The constants involved are shown to depend on system parameters, ensuring that the inequalities presented hold.

The second part of the proofs focuses on the critic, specifically Lemma 2, which aims to demonstrate the behavior of the expected value of the trace of a product involving the state-action pair (x,u) and a matrix M. The proof involves deriving bounds on the expected value, leveraging properties of matrix norms.

Overall, the paper rigorously establishes the theoretical underpinnings of the Single Timescale Actor-Critic LQR approach, providing necessary proofs and inequalities that form the foundation for further applications and analyses in control theory and reinforcement learning.

In this paper, Zhou and Lu demonstrate that under specific conditions (33) and (34), the Lyapunov function decreases at a defined rate (70) provided that the expected value of the norm of the error is below a threshold. If these conditions are not met, the Lyapunov function remains less than a predetermined constant. The analysis leads to the conclusion that the expected value of the Lyapunov function at time T is bounded above by this constant, confirming that both components contributing to this expectation are non-negative and less than the threshold. This establishes a foundation for further exploration of stability in control systems. 

The paper references significant works in optimal control and reinforcement learning, including foundational texts and recent advancements in the field, which contextualize the findings within the broader literature.

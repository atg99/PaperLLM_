The paper discusses Single Timescale Actor-Critic methods applied to the Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR) problem, focusing on optimal control strategies for direct current motors. It references foundational works in reinforcement learning and actor-critic algorithms, including the natural policy gradient and various enhancements in policy gradient methods. Key contributions from prior research are highlighted, such as certainty equivalence in LQR control, convergence properties of gradient methods, and the significance of least-squares temporal difference learning in achieving optimal control outcomes.

The authors delve into the efficiency of these methods and their theoretical underpinnings, emphasizing the importance of both sample complexity and convergence in the context of model-free control. The survey of literature spans various methodologies in reinforcement learning, including multi-agent systems and bilevel optimization, showcasing a comprehensive understanding of the field. The paper ultimately aims to consolidate knowledge on actor-critic approaches, particularly their application to LQR, while addressing the challenges and advancements in achieving efficient control solutions.

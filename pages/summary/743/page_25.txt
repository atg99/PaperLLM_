The paper discusses a single timescale actor-critic approach using linear quadratic regulator (LQR) techniques. It presents mathematical expressions related to the integration of certain terms and the properties of matrices involved in the actor-critic framework.

The derivation begins with a specific integral that yields a relationship between expected values under different control policies. By analyzing the differences in performance metrics, the authors seek to provide bounds on certain matrix norms, specifically the Frobenius norm of the difference between two matrices related to the control policies.

The proof of Lemma 4 illustrates the relationship between the matrices representing the control policies and the corresponding value functions. It establishes that the difference in value functions can be expressed in terms of the differences in control matrices and system dynamics. The ultimate goal is to bound the Frobenius norm of the difference in value functions by the norm of the difference in control matrices.

The paper employs rigorous mathematical techniques to derive these results, ensuring that the bounds are valid under the conditions of the actor-critic framework. The findings contribute to understanding the stability and performance of LQR-based actor-critic methods in control systems.

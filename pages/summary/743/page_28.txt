The paper by Zhou and Lu presents an analysis of a specific mathematical expression related to matrix operations and expectations. The authors derive a relationship between two functions J(K) and J(K') by employing properties of expected values and matrix dynamics. 

Starting with the expression for J(K) - J(K'), they express it in terms of the expectation ED;K' and a summation over terms involving the matrices A, B, and K. The expectation is evaluated to yield DK', where DK' is the solution to a Lyapunov equation involving the matrices A and BK'. 

The authors then analyze the second term in the derived expression, showing through direct computation that it can be expressed in terms of the difference (K - K') and a matrix G, which is derived from the original matrices. The results indicate that the difference between the two matrices is positive semi-definite, leading to a conclusion that relates the trace of DK' and G.

Ultimately, the paper concludes that the difference J(K) - J(K') is bounded by a term involving the trace of matrices G and K, establishing a crucial relationship that may have implications for optimization problems in control theory and related fields. The main arguments are supported by detailed mathematical derivations and properties of the matrices involved.

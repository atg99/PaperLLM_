This table summarizes various studies on trust in autopilot systems, highlighting approaches, methodologies, and concentration areas. 

1. **Automatic vs. Manual Failures**: A simulation experiment found that automatic failures significantly diminish trust more than manual failures.
2. **Air Traffic Management Trust**: A survey provided a procedural framework for enhancing trust in air traffic management systems.
3. **False Alarms**: Experiments revealed that continuous false alarms lead to a greater decline in trust in autonomous systems.
4. **Human Judgment in Airplanes**: A simulation assessed how participants rated airplanes with autopilot features as friendly or hostile based on various factors.
5. **Cultural Impact on Trust**: An internet survey indicated that cultural differences influence trust in autonomous and semi-autonomous autopilot systems.

Additional references cover topics such as trust dynamics in human-automation interactions, the effects of robot appearance on trust, and public perceptions of self-driving cars. Key findings emphasize that trust is influenced by system performance, user experience, cultural factors, and the nature of automation errors.

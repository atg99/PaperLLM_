In summary, trust in human-robot interaction and self-driving cars is influenced by various factors including past performance, emotional responses, and user knowledge. Studies have shown that real-time psychophysiological measurements can be used to gauge human trust, although their effectiveness may vary among individuals. The performance of caregiving robots and the provision of information regarding the capabilities of autonomous vehicles are also critical in shaping user trust. Overall, understanding these trust dynamics is essential for the successful integration of robotic systems and autonomous vehicles into everyday life.

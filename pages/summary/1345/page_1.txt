In the context of autonomous systems, trust is crucial as it influences how humans interact with and rely on these technologies.

III. LITERATURE REVIEW AND EXPERIMENTAL TECHNIQUES
The survey examines a multitude of studies on trust in autonomous systems, focusing on various experimental techniques used to gauge human trust levels. These studies reveal that trust is often low when humans interact with semi-autonomous and fully autonomous systems. Factors affecting trust include system reliability, transparency, and user experience. The review highlights the need for improved trust management frameworks to enhance user confidence and facilitate better human-autonomy collaboration.

IV. LIMITATIONS AND CONCLUSIONS
Despite the wealth of literature on trust in autonomous systems, significant limitations remain. Many studies lack a standardized approach to measuring trust, leading to inconsistencies in findings. Additionally, there is a need for more comprehensive research that addresses the nuances of trust in diverse contexts and user populations. The paper concludes by emphasizing the importance of trust management in advancing autonomous technologies and suggests avenues for future research to address existing gaps and challenges. 

In summary, the paper underscores the critical role of trust in the successful integration of autonomous systems into daily life, advocating for enhanced understanding and management of trust to optimize human-autonomy interactions.

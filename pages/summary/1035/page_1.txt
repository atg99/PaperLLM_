The paper "The Ethics of AI Ethics: A Constructive Critique" by Jan-Christoph Heilinger provides a critical examination of current practices in AI ethics. It highlights several conceptual, substantive, and procedural challenges that exist within the field. The author proposes strategies to address these challenges, emphasizing the need to counteract the hype surrounding AI and to recognize AI as a pervasive infrastructure that includes often-overlooked issues of ethics and justice, particularly structural background injustices.

Heilinger advocates for a more inclusive and well-informed approach to AI ethics, integrating the concept of AI justice. This integration aims to enhance the normative guidance provided for the development and use of AI technologies, ensuring they genuinely improve human lives and societal interactions. The paper underscores the importance of addressing ethical considerations as AI continues to become increasingly embedded in everyday life, from navigation tools to law enforcement applications. By doing so, it seeks to strengthen the ethical framework surrounding AI and promote responsible practices that prioritize human welfare.

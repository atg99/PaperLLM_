The paper discusses the urgent need for normative reflection on AI ethics as AI technology continues to evolve and influence human life. It identifies several challenges within the current AI ethics discourse, including a narrow understanding of AI's role in society, neglect of non-AI related issues, a technosolutionist approach that overly relies on technology to solve diverse problems, and the significant impact of commercial and political interests on ethical discussions. Additionally, it highlights the lack of integration of critical philosophical and ethical expertise in the field.

The author proposes a reformed approach to AI ethics that encompasses broader relational ethics and social justice considerations, advocating for more inclusive deliberative processes that represent diverse perspectives. The main conclusion stresses the necessity of ethical reflection to ensure AI technologies are used for the common good, addressing structural injustices rather than exacerbating them. The paper warns that without these advancements, AI ethics risks becoming an inadequate framework that fails to address existing inequalities.

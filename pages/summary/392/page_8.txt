This paper provides an overview of performance metrics used in model evaluation, focusing on various iterations of the YOLO (You Only Look Once) object detection framework. Key performance metrics include Precision, Recall, F1 Score, Intersection over Union (IoU), Frames Per Second (FPS), and Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS). Each metric is defined through its equation and described in terms of its significance in evaluating model performance.

The paper adopts a reverse-chronological analysis of YOLO's evolution, starting from the latest version, YOLOv11, and moving backward to earlier versions. It is structured into six subsections: 
1. The latest iterations (YOLOv11).
2. YOLOv10, YOLOv9, and YOLOv8, focusing on their architectural advancements.
3. YOLOv7, YOLOv6, and YOLOv5, tracing evolutionary enhancements.
4. Earlier YOLO versions, providing a historical perspective on foundational technologies.
5. Alternative versions derived from YOLO.

Finally, the paper discusses the application of YOLO models in five key real-world domains: autonomous vehicles and traffic safety, healthcare and medical image analysis, surveillance and security, among others. This structured analysis showcases the progression and impact of the YOLO framework in the dynamic field of object detection technology.

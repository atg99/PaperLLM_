This paper provides a comprehensive overview of the YOLO (You Only Look Once) object detection framework, detailing its evolution through various versions and their applications. It highlights the advancements and challenges associated with each YOLO iteration, emphasizing their significance in fields such as autonomous vehicles and security.

The discussion begins with YOLOv6, which presents three variants: the standard version for general detection tasks, YOLOv6-Nano optimized for real-time applications on edge devices, and YOLOv6-Tiny for faster inference on low-resource hardware with some accuracy trade-offs. YOLOv7 introduces innovative techniques such as trainable bag-of-freebies and dynamic label assignment, offering three variants that balance performance, speed, and computational needs.

YOLOv8, released in 2023 by Ultralytics, features an efficient architecture and enhanced training techniques, with four variants catering to different needs from fast inference to high accuracy. YOLOv9 introduces the concept of programmable-gradient information (PGI) and a new lightweight architecture called Generalized Efficient Layer Aggregation Network (GELAN), which improves performance on lightweight models.

Finally, YOLOv10, developed by Tsinghua University, addresses limitations of previous versions by optimizing model architecture and eliminating non-maximum suppression, resulting in improved efficiency and performance. It offers six variants, with YOLOv10-N and YOLOv10-S achieving the lowest latencies, making them ideal for low-latency applications.

Overall, the paper not only compares the technical aspects of each YOLO version but also reflects on their future impact on industry and society, indicating a trajectory towards enhanced performance and broader applicability.

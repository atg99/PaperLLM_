The paper presents the YOLOv11 architecture, which is designed for efficient object detection, instance segmentation, pose/keypoint detection, oriented detection, and classification. The architecture features a resolution of 640x640x3 and incorporates multiple scales, enhancing feature extraction through an improved backbone. Key components include C3K2 blocks that replace C2F, SPPF for multi-scale feature extraction, and C2PSA for an attention mechanism. The architecture allows for efficient feature processing, culminating in a detection head optimized for multi-class object detection and localization.

Additionally, the paper includes simplified architecture diagrams for previous YOLO versions, from YOLOv1 to YOLOv10, illustrating the evolution of the model's design. The diagrams emphasize the progressive enhancements made in each version, highlighting the advancements leading to YOLOv11. Overall, the paper emphasizes the improvements in performance and efficiency achieved in the YOLOv11 architecture compared to its predecessors.

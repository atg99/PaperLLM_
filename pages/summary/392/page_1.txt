This paper provides a comprehensive review of the You Only Look Once (YOLO) series of object detection algorithms, tracing their development from YOLOv1 to the latest YOLO11. The authors utilize a reverse chronological analysis to highlight the advancements made in each version, focusing on improvements in speed, detection accuracy, and computational efficiency for real-time object detection.

The review details the technological enhancements introduced in each YOLO iteration, documenting both achievements and challenges faced by earlier versions. The authors emphasize the significance of these developments in the context of integrating YOLO with multimodal, context-aware systems and the potential for future applications in Artificial General Intelligence (AGI).

Key applications discussed include healthcare, autonomous vehicles, traffic safety, industrial manufacturing, surveillance, and agriculture. Overall, the paper outlines the evolutionary trajectory of YOLO, suggesting that continued advancements will have substantial implications for the future of AI-driven technologies.

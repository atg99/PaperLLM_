The paper discusses the evolution of the YOLO (You Only Look Once) series of object detection models, highlighting the advancements from YOLOv1 to YOLOv10. YOLOv8, introduced in January 2023, features a refined architecture with a modified CSPDarknet53 backbone and the innovative C2f module, enhancing detection accuracy. YOLOv8 is available in multiple scaled versions (nano to extra-large) tailored to various applications, including object detection, segmentation, and pose estimation.

The architecture processes input images through convolutional layers, creating a feature pyramid that improves detection at different scales. YOLOv9 introduced significant advancements with the Programmable Gradient Information (PGI) and Generalized Efficient Layer Aggregation Network (GELAN) to address efficiency and accuracy challenges, particularly concerning information loss. YOLOv10 further builds on these innovations by optimizing the model architecture to eliminate the need for Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS), enhancing response time without sacrificing accuracy.

The paper emphasizes the incremental improvements in mAP (mean Average Precision) scores across the YOLO series, with YOLOv10 achieving superior efficiency compared to its predecessors. YOLOv9 and YOLOv8 show competitive mAP scores but with higher latency. The enhancements across the YOLO series have expanded their practical applications in fields such as autonomous driving and surveillance, showcasing significant progress in real-time object detection capabilities.

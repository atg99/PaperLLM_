This collection of references focuses on advancements in the YOLO (You Only Look Once) algorithm, which is widely used for real-time object detection across various applications. The papers span from the development of YOLOv1 to the latest iterations like YOLOv10, highlighting improvements in accuracy, speed, and efficiency.

Key themes include the application of YOLO in diverse fields such as medical imaging, agriculture for crop and pest detection, and remote sensing for environmental monitoring. Notable contributions include YOLOv5 for medical object detection and YOLOv6 for industrial applications. The progression of the YOLO framework also emphasizes enhancements in model architecture, such as the integration of attention mechanisms, spatial pyramid pooling, and lightweight designs for deployment on mobile and edge devices.

Several studies compare different YOLO versions, assessing their performance in specific tasks like vehicle detection, landslide detection from satellite images, and autonomous landing spot detection for UAVs. The references also explore the utilization of synthetic datasets for training these models, which has implications for improving detection capabilities in real-world scenarios.

Overall, the body of work reflects the ongoing evolution of YOLO as a leading framework in the field of computer vision, addressing both theoretical advancements and practical applications.

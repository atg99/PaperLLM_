The evolution of object detection has transitioned from traditional methods reliant on handcrafted features to advanced deep learning techniques such as CNNs and the YOLO framework. The development of CNNs marked a significant shift in feature extraction, allowing for hierarchical learning and improved robustness in various conditions. YOLO, introduced in 2016, further streamlined the detection process by integrating object localization and classification, resulting in faster and more efficient real-time applications across multiple domains.

In summary, the paper outlines the historical progression of object detection methods, emphasizing the pivotal role of CNNs and the YOLO algorithm in transforming the field. It highlights the strengths of these modern approaches in various applications, showcasing their impact on real-time object detection and the advancements they have brought to computer vision.

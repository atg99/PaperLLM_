This document references a collection of significant papers related to advancements in deep learning and computer vision. Key contributions include Faster R-CNN for efficient object detection, weight normalization for accelerating training, and various techniques for enhancing neural network architectures such as batch normalization, instance normalization, and residual networks. Notable methods like Grad-CAM provide visual explanations for deep networks, while attention mechanisms like CBAM enhance feature representation. The exploration of adversarial training and properties of neural networks is addressed, alongside innovations in convolutional networks aimed at improving performance and understanding. These works collectively contribute to the evolution of deep learning methodologies and their applications in image recognition and processing.

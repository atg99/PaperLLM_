This innovation aims to enhance the learning capabilities of deep residual networks by recalibrating feature-maps based not only on their residual contributions but also on their overall relevance to class discrimination. The SE-block serves to adaptively adjust the importance of each feature-map, utilizing transformation weight vectors and regulating factors to manage model complexity and improve generalization. The integration of the sigmoid activation function in the gating mechanism further allows for a nuanced modeling of non-linear responses, ultimately enhancing the effectiveness of the recalibration process. This advancement addresses the limitations of traditional SE-Nets, ensuring that the recalibration process is more reflective of the feature-maps' contributions beyond mere residual information.

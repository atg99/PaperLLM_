Khan et al. proposed extending the channel boosting technique by adding auxiliary channels at various layers within deep architectures. This method, referred to as Channel Boosted CNN (CB-CNN), has shown promising results, particularly in medical imaging. For instance, the evaluation on a mitosis dataset revealed that CB-CNN outperformed traditional CNN architectures, achieving an F-score of 0.53 compared to 0.47 for the standard 26-layer deep CNN and 0.71 for the CB-VGG architecture.

The paper highlights the importance of attention mechanisms in CNNs, emphasizing their role in enhancing discrimination power by allowing networks to learn multiple levels of abstraction while focusing on contextually relevant features. This mirrors the human visual system's approach to scene analysis and recognition, thereby improving performance in tasks such as image localization and classification.

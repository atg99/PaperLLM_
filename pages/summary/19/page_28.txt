In the paper published in Artificial Intelligence Review, the authors discuss advancements in deep learning architectures, particularly focusing on Inception-V3, V4, and Inception-ResNet. These models build upon earlier versions (Inception-V1 and V2) with the primary goal of reducing computational costs while maintaining generalization performance. Inception-V3 implements small and asymmetric filters (1x7 and 1x5) in place of larger filters (5x5 and 7x7), complemented by a 1x1 convolutional layer that serves as a bottleneck. This design enhances the convolution process by allowing for cross-channel correlation, as previously explored by Lin et al. in their Network in Network architecture. The Inception-V3 model utilizes 1x1 convolutions to project input data into smaller spaces, facilitating efficient correlation mapping through standard convolutions. Furthermore, Inception-ResNet merges the principles of residual learning with the inception block, optimizing filter concatenation to improve model performance.

The paper published in Artificial Intelligence Review discusses recent advancements in deep vision systems, emphasizing the application of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) across various domains, including speech recognition, structural damage detection, and medical diagnosis. It reviews the methodologies and optimization techniques employed to enhance the performance of CNNs, highlighting their effectiveness in real-time applications. The authors also reference significant contributions in the field, including techniques for modularization in neural networks and the development of deep learning architectures for image segmentation and classification tasks. The review serves as a comprehensive resource for understanding the current landscape and challenges in deep learning, particularly in the context of visual recognition and related applications.

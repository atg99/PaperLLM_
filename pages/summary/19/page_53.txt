The paper discusses the implementation challenges associated with various CNN architectures, particularly focusing on Channel Boosting and Attention-based networks. Channel Boosting enhances the representational capacity of CNNs by increasing input channels through auxiliary learners, which can lead to increased computational load. Attention networks, such as the Residual Attention Neural Network and Convolutional Block Attention Module (CBAM), allow for the selection of important image patches, improving feature representation but also potentially increasing computational demands.

The challenges faced by deep CNNs include their black-box nature, which complicates interpretation and verification, and their vulnerability to noisy data, which can lead to misclassification errors. Additionally, while CNNs are designed to extract task-specific features automatically, understanding the input data is crucial for effective architecture design, as some tasks require specific knowledge about the data characteristics. Overall, the paper highlights the complexities and considerations necessary for optimizing CNN performance in various applications.

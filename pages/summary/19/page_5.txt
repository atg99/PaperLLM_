This paper, published in Artificial Intelligence Review, provides a comprehensive survey of deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) from 2012 to 2020. It highlights the evolution of CNN architectures and introduces a taxonomy that categorizes these architectures into seven main types: spatial exploitation, depth, multi-path, width, feature-map exploitation, channel boosting, and attention-based CNNs. 

The authors trace the historical development of CNNs, linking their structure to the primate visual cortex and discussing their contributions to machine vision. The survey reviews foundational components of CNNs and outlines significant architectural innovations, offering insights into design principles that may inspire future advancements in CNN architecture. 

The paper is organized into distinct sections: the first section establishes a foundational understanding of CNNs; the second reviews essential components; the third details architectural evolution; the fourth categorizes recent innovations; the fifth and sixth sections address applications and challenges; and the final section discusses future directions and concludes the survey. Overall, this work aims to enhance theoretical understanding and promote further architectural innovations in CNNs.

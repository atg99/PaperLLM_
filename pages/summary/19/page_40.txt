The paper discusses the development of a Channel-wise Multi-Perspective Squeeze and Excitation (CMPE-SE) block within Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) to enhance feature representation. It introduces a method for generating global representations of feature maps through global average pooling and evaluates the relevance of these feature maps by comparing residual and identity mappings. This process, referred to as inner imaging, establishes a competitive relationship between the feature descriptors of these mappings.

The mathematical framework for the CMPE-SE block is provided through several equations that illustrate the operations involved, including squeeze and excitation processes. The output masks from the excitation operation are utilized to amplify the importance of residual information, allowing for optimized backpropagation that balances the competition between identity and residual feature maps.

Moreover, the paper emphasizes the significance of effective image representation in improving the performance of image processing algorithms, both conventional and deep learning-based. It highlights that a well-defined representation can capture salient features of an image, which is crucial for tasks involving multiple views (MV). The paper also notes that while CNNs are powerful feature learners, their performance is heavily dependent on the quality and diversity of input representations.

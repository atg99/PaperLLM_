The paper published in Artificial Intelligence Review discusses the evolutionary history of deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs), highlighting key architectural innovations from the inception of ConvNet to contemporary architectures. It outlines the progression of CNN designs, emphasizing significant milestones and improvements that have shaped the field of computer vision. The analysis provides insights into how these innovations have contributed to advancements in performance and efficiency in various applications. The paper serves as a comprehensive overview of the development of deep learning architectures, underlining the importance of each evolution in the context of artificial intelligence.

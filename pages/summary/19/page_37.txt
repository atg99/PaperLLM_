the effective exploitation of feature-maps in Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) is crucial for improving performance in multi-view (MV) tasks. CNNs are favored for their hierarchical learning and automatic feature extraction capabilities, which allow for dynamic feature selection through weight tuning associated with kernels. However, not all feature-maps contribute significantly to object discrimination, leading to potential noise and overfitting due to the large number of features. This underscores the importance of not only network architecture but also the strategic selection and utilization of feature-maps to enhance classification, segmentation, and detection outcomes in CNN applications.

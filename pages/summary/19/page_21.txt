The paper published in the Artificial Intelligence Review presents a comprehensive overview of various online resources available for deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs). It categorizes these resources into five main groups:

1. **Cloud-Based Platforms**: These platforms offer free online access to GPUs and other deep learning accelerators. Notable examples include Google Colab, CoCalc, FloydHub, Amazon SageMaker, Microsoft Azure ML Services, Google Cloud, and IBM Watson Studio.

2. **Deep Learning Libraries**: This section highlights libraries that provide built-in classes for neural networks, fast numerical computation, and automated gradient estimation. Key libraries mentioned are PyTorch, TensorFlow, MatConvNet, Keras, Theano, Caffe, and Julia.

3. **Lecture Series**: The paper lists freely accessible online courses focused on deep learning, including Stanford’s Lecture Series, courses on Udacity, Udemy, and Coursera.

4. **Vision Datasets**: It identifies various freely accessible datasets containing annotated images, such as ImageNet, COCO, Visual Genome, Open Images, and others including CIFAR10 and Fashion MNIST.

5. **Deep Learning Accelerators**: This category discusses energy-efficient and computationally efficient accelerators for deep learning, featuring NVIDIA, FPGA, Eyeriss, and Google’s TPU.

Overall, the paper serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners in the field of deep learning, providing key links and descriptions for each category.

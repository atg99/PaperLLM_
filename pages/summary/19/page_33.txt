This approach aims to address the limitations of traditional deep CNN architectures, where an increase in feature-map depth often leads to a corresponding loss of spatial information. The authors argue that this loss restricts the learning capability of CNNs. 

Wide ResNet is highlighted for its effectiveness in improving performance by widening layers rather than deepening networks. It incorporates dropout between convolutional layers to enhance learning without the drawbacks associated with deep architectures, such as gradient vanishing and exploding. Empirical studies indicate that Wide ResNet, despite having more parameters than standard ResNet, can be trained more effectively.

Additionally, stochastic ResNet is mentioned as a method to mitigate performance degradation caused by the removal of residual blocks. The Pyramidal Net's design contrasts with ResNet's approach by preserving spatial dimensions, thereby enhancing the overall learning ability of the network. This paper emphasizes the significance of architectural modifications in improving the efficiency and effectiveness of deep learning models in image classification tasks.

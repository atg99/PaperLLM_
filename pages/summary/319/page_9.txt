The references list includes a diverse range of studies and papers that contribute to the fields of natural language processing, entity linking, and knowledge representation. Key contributions include:

1. Broscheit (2019) investigates entity knowledge in BERT through neural entity linking.
2. Chen et al. (2019) present YELM, an end-to-end contextualized entity linking model.
3. Das et al. (2017) explore question answering using universal schema and memory networks.
4. Davison et al. (2019) focus on mining commonsense knowledge from pretrained models.
5. Devlin et al. (2019) introduce BERT, a model for deep bidirectional language understanding.
6. Dodge et al. (2019) emphasize improved reporting of experimental results.
7. Elsahar et al. (2018) discuss T-REx, which aligns natural language with knowledge base triples.
8. Ghazvininejad et al. (2019) propose Mask-Predict for parallel decoding in masked language models.
9. Hoffart et al. (2011) address robust disambiguation of named entities in text.
10. Jiang et al. (2019) investigate what language models know.
11. Kolitsas et al. (2018) present an end-to-end neural entity linking approach.
12. Liu et al. (2019) introduce RoBERTa, an optimized BERT pretraining method.
13. Mikolov et al. (2013) contribute to word representation estimation and cross-lingual similarities.
14. Peters et al. (2019) discuss knowledge-enhanced contextual word representations.
15. Petroni et al. (2019) explore the role of language models as knowledge bases.
16. Radford et al. (2019) describe language models as unsupervised multitask learners.
17. Schick and Schütze (2019) analyze the impact of improved word embeddings on model performance.
18. Sun et al. (2018) focus on open domain question answering using knowledge bases and text.
19. Vaswani et al. (2017) introduce the transformer architecture, crucial for many modern NLP tasks.
20. Wang et al. (2019) discuss enhancing multilingual models through vocabulary expansion.

These works collectively advance understanding and methodologies in language processing, entity linking, and knowledge integration.

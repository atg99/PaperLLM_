The paper presents an analysis of hyperparameter configurations for the E-BERT-MLM model. It evaluates various batch sizes (16, 32, 64, 128) and learning rates (2e-5, 3e-5, 5e-5), with the optimal configuration highlighted in bold. Figures 6 and 7 illustrate the expected maximum performance in terms of macro and micro F1 scores, respectively, based on the number of evaluated configurations. 

In terms of dataset statistics, for relation classification, there are 8000 training samples, with 16000 samples each for development and testing, encompassing 80 relations and 54648 unique entities. For the entity linking task using the AIDA dataset, there are 946 training documents, 216 development documents, and 231 test documents, resulting in a total of 18454 gold entities across the datasets. 

Overall, the findings indicate the importance of hyperparameter tuning in enhancing model performance, as evidenced by the variations in F1 scores across different configurations.

The table presents the mean Hits@1 scores across different models (original E-BERT, E-BERT-replace, E-BERT-concat, etc.) for various relations from the T-REx and Google-RE datasets. Each row corresponds to a specific relation and indicates performance under three different conditions: the original LAMA dataset, after applying a string match filter (Heuristic 1), and after applying both Heuristic 1 and a second heuristic (LAMA-UHN). 

Key observations include:

- The T-REx:P463 relation consistently shows high performance across all models, particularly in the original LAMA condition with a score of 67.1.
- The T-REx:P495 relation exhibits lower performance, with scores ranging from 15.0 to 16.5, indicating challenges in this relation.
- The Google-RE relations, such as date of birth and place of birth, demonstrate varied performance, with some models performing significantly better than others.
- The number of questions per relation varies widely, impacting the overall mean scores, particularly in relations with fewer questions.

The data indicates that the model's performance can be significantly affected by the dataset and the applied heuristics, emphasizing the importance of data quality and preprocessing in relation extraction tasks.

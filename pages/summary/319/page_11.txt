The appendix of the paper "E-BERT: Efficient-Yet-Effective Entity Embeddings for BERT" details the methodologies utilized for data preparation and processing in various tasks, specifically focusing on unsupervised question-answering, relation classification, and entity linking.

For the unsupervised QA task using the LAMA dataset, the authors retrieved data from specific relations designed to assess factual knowledge. They employed the Wikidata query API to map surface forms to entity IDs, excluding the use of LAMA's oracle entity IDs. The preprocessing included selecting the lowest returned Wikidata ID and subsequently mapping these to Wikipedia URLs.

In relation classification, the authors utilized a subset of the FewRel corpus, preprocessing the dataset by tokenizing sentences with the BERT wordpiece tokenizer and marking entity mentions with special tokens. They maintained the use of oracle entity IDs for training, and hyperparameters were optimized based on peak learning rate and the number of epochs, with the best configurations highlighted.

For entity linking, the AIDA dataset was similarly processed, with documents containing annotated entity spans. The authors tokenized these documents and handled cases where document lengths exceeded BERT's input limits by recursively splitting them into manageable chunks. Hyperparameters for this task were fine-tuned based on strong match micro F1 metrics.

Overall, the appendix provides a comprehensive overview of the data sources, preprocessing steps, and hyperparameter tuning strategies employed for the various tasks in the study, ensuring clarity and reproducibility in the methodology.

The paper introduces E-BERT, a novel approach to enhance the BERT model with entity knowledge by aligning Wikipedia2Vec entity vectors with BERT's wordpiece vector space. This method allows the use of these aligned entity vectors as wordpiece vectors without requiring additional pretraining of the BERT encoder, distinguishing E-BERT from other entity-enhanced models like ERNIE and KnowBert.

E-BERT is evaluated across three key tasks: unsupervised question answering (QA), supervised relation classification (RC), and entity linking (EL), demonstrating superior performance compared to BERT and other baseline models. Notably, the authors identify that the original BERT model tends to rely heavily on the surface forms of entity names, leading to inaccuracies, a limitation that E-BERT addresses effectively.

Key contributions of the paper include the introduction of E-BERT, the establishment of a new state-of-the-art on the LAMA unsupervised QA benchmark, the creation of a more challenging version of the LAMA benchmark (LAMA-UHN) that removes overly helpful entity names, and successful evaluations in relation classification and entity linking tasks. The authors plan to release the LAMA-UHN dataset and E-BERT models upon publication.

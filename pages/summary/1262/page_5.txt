The paper presents a Dynamic Ensemble Learning (DEL) framework designed to optimize the architecture of neural network ensembles through a systematic process of adding or deleting hidden neurons and networks. The procedure comprises eight steps. Initially, a minimum ensemble architecture is created with two neural networks (NNs) using a constructive algorithm and a pruning algorithm for training. Training sets are generated with varied examples to ensure that individual NNs learn from different parts of the data distribution.

Key steps include checking the neuron addition and deletion criteria after a specified number of training epochs. If the criteria are not met, a new NN is added to the ensemble to enhance performance. The core of DEL lies in the hybrid strategy of combining constructive and pruning algorithms, which helps to diversify learning among the NNs in the ensemble.

The addition of NNs continues until a minimum ensemble error criterion is achieved. The paper emphasizes the importance of varied training sets, ensuring that each NN gains sufficient information for effective learning. The algorithm is designed to balance accuracy and diversity within the ensemble, ultimately leading to improved performance in neural network applications.

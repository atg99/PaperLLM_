The referenced papers focus on various aspects of neural network ensembles and their applications in machine learning. Key contributions include pruning algorithms for training cooperative neural network ensembles, the development of dynamic ensembles for non-stationary data, and frameworks using meta-learning for dynamic ensemble selection. Regularization techniques such as negative correlation learning enhance ensemble performance, while semi-supervised learning approaches address challenges with limited labeled data. The papers also review dynamic classifier selection, emphasizing the importance of classifier diversity and ensemble accuracy. Additionally, advancements in constructive algorithms for feedforward neural networks and early stopping criteria in training are discussed. Overall, these studies contribute to the understanding and improvement of ensemble methods in machine learning.

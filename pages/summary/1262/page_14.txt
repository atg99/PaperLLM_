The paper introduces the DEL algorithm, a novel approach for designing and training neural network (NN) ensembles. Unlike traditional methods that rely on manual trial-and-error, DEL automates the creation of ensemble architectures, which enhances efficiency. The algorithm employs a single correlation strength parameter (k) and utilizes an incremental training approach to optimize both the learning rate and k value. 

DEL emphasizes the balance between accuracy and diversity among individual networks, using a one-stage design process that avoids the need for separate gating blocks. It generates uncorrelated networks within the ensemble, which promotes diversity. The algorithm incorporates both simple averaging and majority voting methods for combining results, with performance varying based on the specific problem.

The findings demonstrate that DEL outperforms existing ensemble methods across benchmark problems, showcasing its effectiveness in preserving accuracy and diversity. However, further evaluation is needed for its application in regression and time series tasks.

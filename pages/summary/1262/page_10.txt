Table 5 presents the test set error rates for various datasets, comparing the performance of the proposed DEL method against several existing classification approaches. The methods compared include a single neural network classifier (Stan), ensembles created through varying random initial weights (Simp), Bagging, Arcing, Ada with different percentages of labeled data, a semi-supervised ensemble learning algorithm (SemiNCL), and a cluster-based boosting (CBoost) ensemble in two variations (CBoost-Sup and CBoost-Semi).

For each dataset, the error rates are reported for DEL alongside the other methods, highlighting the conditions under which each method was evaluated, particularly the percentage of labeled data for Ada, SemiNCL, and CBoost variants. The results indicate that DEL consistently outperforms most of the traditional methods across several datasets, particularly in the Cancer and Thyroid datasets where DEL achieved significantly lower error rates. In contrast, some methods like Bagging and Arcing showed competitive performances but were generally less effective than DEL. 

The dataset results suggest varying effectiveness across different algorithms, with DEL showing robustness in handling diverse data distributions.

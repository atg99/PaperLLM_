The paper presents the Dual Self-Attention Network (DSANet) for multivariate time series forecasting, incorporating both autoregressive and self-attention components. The architecture leverages scaled dot product self-attention, allowing for the integration of information from various representation subspaces through multi-head attention. The model includes position-wise feed-forward layers and employs residual connections and layer normalization to enhance training efficacy.

The autoregressive component addresses the nonlinearity of the convolutional and self-attention components by combining a linear component, modeled by a classical AR model, with the non-linear output of DSANet. The final prediction is derived from the sum of the self-attention-based prediction and the AR prediction.

The experimental section utilizes a dataset from a gas station service company, covering daily revenue data from five geographically close stations over three years. The dataset is divided into training, validation, and test sets, and multiple baselines are compared, including VAR, LRidge, and GRU, among others. The evaluation metrics include root relative squared error (RRSE), mean absolute error (MAE), and empirical correlation coefficient (CORR).

Results demonstrate DSANet's effectiveness in forecasting multivariate time series, with detailed evaluations provided in the paper. The implementation is executed using Python and PyTorch, and additional experimental results and code are available online.

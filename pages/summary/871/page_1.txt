The paper introduces DSANet, a novel dual self-attention network designed for multivariate time series forecasting, particularly effective for dynamic-period and nonperiodic patterns. Traditional forecasting methods struggle to accurately capture complex non-linear dependencies among time steps and variables, which DSANet aims to address.

DSANet eliminates recurrence by employing two parallel convolutional components: global temporal convolution and local temporal convolution. These components help capture a mixture of global and local temporal patterns. Additionally, a self-attention module is utilized to model interdependencies among multiple time series, enhancing the model's ability to understand the intricate relationships within the data.

To improve robustness, DSANet integrates a traditional autoregressive linear model alongside the non-linear neural network approach. Experiments with real-world multivariate time series data demonstrate that DSANet outperforms existing baseline models, confirming its effectiveness in forecasting tasks.

In summary, DSANet represents a significant advancement in multivariate time series forecasting, leveraging dual self-attention mechanisms and convolutional architectures to better capture the complexities of time series data.

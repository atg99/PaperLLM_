This paper presents a novel approach to time series forecasting through the development of DSANet, which integrates an autoregressive linear model alongside a non-linear attention network. This dual-branch architecture is the first of its kind to employ a self-attention mechanism specifically for time series forecasting. The authors demonstrate the model's effectiveness and robustness through experiments on real-world data sets.

The related work section discusses existing statistical linear methods for multivariate time series, highlighting the vector autoregression (VAR) model as a baseline. It also addresses the limitations of various autoregressive models in capturing non-linear relationships. The paper notes that while neural networks, particularly recurrent neural networks (RNNs), have been utilized for non-linear forecasting, they often struggle with dynamic or non-periodic data.

The methodology section outlines the structure of DSANet, which comprises two convolutional components: global temporal convolution and local temporal convolution. Global temporal convolution captures time-invariant patterns across all time steps, while local temporal convolution focuses on local dependencies, enhancing the model's predictive capabilities. The output of these convolutional structures is processed through a self-attention module, inspired by the Transformer architecture, which learns the relationships between different time series.

In summary, DSANet demonstrates a significant advancement in time series forecasting by effectively combining linear and non-linear modeling techniques, thereby achieving improved accuracy and robustness in predictions.

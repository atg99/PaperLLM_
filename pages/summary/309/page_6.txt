In summary, various methods exist for evaluating text quality, ranging from edit distance-based approaches to modern metrics leveraging machine learning and word embeddings, notably BERT. BERTScore stands out as a leading metric due to its advanced capability to assess the semantic similarity of sentences, utilizing cosine distance calculations and the IDF metric to weight rarer words. Despite its computational demands, BERTScore demonstrates superior correlation with human evaluations compared to conventional methods, making it a valuable tool for text generation assessment.

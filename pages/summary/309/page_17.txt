The references provided include a variety of papers focusing on advancements in natural language processing, machine translation, image description evaluation, and text summarization. 

Key highlights include:

1. **CIDEr**: A method for evaluating image descriptions based on consensus among multiple human-written captions, proposed by Ramakrishna Vedantam et al.

2. **Unsupervised Multilingual Word Embedding**: Takashi Wada and colleagues discuss a neural language model approach for creating multilingual embeddings with limited resources.

3. **GLUE Benchmark**: Introduced by Alex Wang et al., GLUE serves as a multi-task benchmark for evaluating natural language understanding models.

4. **Translation Edit Rate**: Weiyue Wang and collaborators present CharacTer, a metric for assessing translation accuracy at the character level.

5. **Google's Neural Machine Translation**: A comprehensive system that enhances machine translation quality, bridging the gap to human performance.

6. **XLNet**: A generalized autoregressive pretraining method for language understanding developed by Zhilin Yang et al., which improves upon existing models.

7. **Hierarchical Attention Networks**: Proposed by Zichao Yang and team, this method enhances document classification by employing a hierarchical attention mechanism.

8. **CollaboNet**: A framework for biomedical named entity recognition developed by Wonjin Yoon and others, focusing on collaboration among deep neural networks.

9. **BERTScore**: A metric for evaluating text generation using BERT embeddings, introduced by Tianyi Zhang and colleagues.

10. **MoverScore**: A novel evaluation metric that uses contextualized embeddings and the Earth Mover's Distance for assessing text generation, as presented by Wei Zhao et al.

These works collectively contribute to the fields of NLP and machine learning, emphasizing the importance of evaluation metrics, model architectures, and cross-linguistic applications.

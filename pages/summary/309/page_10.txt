In summary, the paper discusses advancements in machine translation and knowledge extraction through specialized text models based on BERT. The TLM learning procedure enhances translation accuracy by an average of 4.9%. Given the surge in scientific publications, automated word processing tools are essential, but traditional neural networks require extensive pre-mapped text, which is challenging for specialized domains. Models like BioBERT and SciBERT illustrate the benefits of using unsupervised learning on domain-specific corpora. BioBERT focuses on biomedical texts and demonstrates superior performance in tasks like named entity recognition and relation extraction. SciBERT, trained on scientific articles, also shows improved results by employing a specialized vocabulary. Both models highlight the importance of tailored approaches in extracting knowledge from specific fields, suggesting that domain-specific training can significantly enhance productivity and efficiency in text processing tasks.

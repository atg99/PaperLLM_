The paper discusses enhancements to the AutoML approach, particularly focusing on the CASH problem. It highlights the use of the SMAC method, which leverages random forests for Bayesian optimization and improves efficiency through fast cross-validation. Two key improvements are introduced: the integration of a meta-learning step to warm-start the Bayesian optimization process, significantly boosting efficiency, and the implementation of automated ensemble construction, which utilizes all classifiers identified during optimization. The authors emphasize that these enhancements are expected to be more effective in flexible machine learning frameworks with numerous algorithms, hyperparameters, and preprocessing methods. Meta-learning is applied to select promising instantiations of the machine learning framework based on performance data collected from various datasets, mimicking the knowledge domain experts gain from previous tasks. The overall workflow of the improved AutoML system is summarized, showcasing the new components that contribute to its effectiveness.

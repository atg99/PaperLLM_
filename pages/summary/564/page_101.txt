This paper presents Auto-WEKA, an automated tool designed to address the combined algorithm selection and hyperparameter optimization (CASH) problem in machine learning. Utilizing recent advancements in Bayesian optimization, Auto-WEKA systematically builds models of the algorithm and hyperparameter landscape, allowing it to effectively identify promising configurations for investigation. The tool integrates a wide range of learning algorithms available in WEKA, making it accessible for non-experts to create high-quality classifiers tailored to specific applications.

The empirical evaluation conducted on 21 notable datasets demonstrates that Auto-WEKA frequently surpasses traditional methods for algorithm selection and hyperparameter tuning, particularly with larger datasets. Since its launch, Auto-WEKA has gained significant traction in both academic and industrial circles, with over 30,000 downloads of its 2.0 version, which integrates with the WEKA package manager. The tool remains under active development, with ongoing enhancements being implemented.

In summary, Auto-WEKA represents a significant advancement in the field of machine learning by providing an efficient, user-friendly solution for model selection and optimization, bolstered by the effectiveness of Bayesian optimization techniques.

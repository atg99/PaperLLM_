In the baseline experiment, the performance of vanilla Auto-sklearn was assessed using the same experimental setup as Auto-WEKA, employing 21 datasets with specific train/test splits, a walltime limit of 30 hours, and 10-fold cross-validation. The results indicated that Auto-sklearn outperformed Auto-WEKA in 6 out of 21 cases and tied in 12 cases, while it lost in 3 instances. Notably, in the cases where Auto-WEKA excelled, the best classifiers selected were not available in scikit-learn, highlighting a limitation in the latter's implementation.

Hyperopt-Sklearn was noted to function more as a proof-of-concept, with significant limitations such as crashing on sparse data and certain datasets like Cifar-10 due to memory restrictions. Despite these issues, it statistically tied with the leading AutoML system in 9 cases out of 16 datasets where it was operational.

Overall, the evaluation underscored the effectiveness of the proposed enhancements in Auto-sklearn, demonstrating its robustness across a diverse array of datasets while also identifying areas for further development in competing systems like Hyperopt-Sklearn.

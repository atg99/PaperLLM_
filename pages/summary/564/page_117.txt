This chapter presents Hyperopt-sklearn, a Python package designed for the automated configuration of machine learning algorithms within the Scikit-Learn framework. The package offers a cohesive interface to a broad array of Scikit-Learn algorithms and leverages Hyperopt's optimization capabilities to achieve performance that can match or exceed that of human experts in the configuration of these algorithms. The authors anticipate that Hyperopt-sklearn will serve as a valuable resource for practitioners developing machine learning systems and provide benchmarks for researchers in the field of automated machine learning.

The research received support from various programs, including the NSERC Banting Fellowship and the NSERC Engage Program, with acknowledgments given to contributors for their early work on bridging Hyperopt and Scikit-Learn. 

The bibliography includes key references that support the development and evaluation of hyperparameter optimization techniques, deep learning architectures, and machine learning frameworks, underscoring the foundational work in this area.

In terms of configuration space, Auto-Net 2.0 expands significantly on its predecessor by incorporating contemporary deep learning techniques and architectures, such as ResNets. This enhancement allows for a more comprehensive exploration of potential model configurations, enabling the optimization process to leverage modern advancements in neural network design.

Furthermore, Auto-Net 2.0 transitions from using the SMAC optimization method to BOHB (Bayesian Optimization and Hyperband). This switch aims to improve the efficiency of finding high-performing neural networks by combining the strengths of both Bayesian optimization and bandit-based approaches, which can effectively allocate resources to promising configurations.

Overall, these updates in Auto-Net 2.0—adopting PyTorch, expanding the configuration space, and applying BOHB—aim to enhance the automatic tuning capabilities of deep neural networks, making the process more efficient and aligned with current best practices in the field.

The analysis discusses the use of hyper-parameter optimization in the AutoML Challenge Series from 2015 to 2018, focusing on the auto-sklearn software developed by the AAD Freiburg team. The study compares the performance of four basic machine learning methods—k-nearest neighbors, naive Bayes, Random Forest, and a linear model trained with stochastic gradient descent—using both default hyper-parameters from scikit-learn and those optimized with auto-sklearn. Results indicate that hyper-parameter optimization generally enhances performance, primarily due to its adaptability to the specific optimization metric of the task and its ability to identify suitable hyper-parameters for the dataset in question. However, there are instances where optimization could not be completed within the time constraints due to dataset size, leading to a score of zero. The findings underscore the need for further research on efficient optimization strategies under time limitations.

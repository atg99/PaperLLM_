In the paper, the authors present a comparison of four variants of Auto-sklearn based on their performance measured through average rank, specifically focusing on balanced test error rate (BER) across 140 datasets. The analysis indicates that while the standard Auto-sklearn (vanilla) variant initially achieves the best rank within the first 10 seconds due to its faster predictions, the implementation of meta-learning significantly enhances performance over time. The authors limited CPU time for each run to one hour and set a six-minute cap for evaluating a single model, ensuring a fair assessment of each variant. To avoid bias from datasets used in meta-learning, they adopted a leave-one-dataset-out validation approach. The results, illustrated in Fig. 6.3, demonstrate that both new methods, especially meta-learning, lead to substantial improvements in ranking compared to the vanilla version, highlighting the effectiveness of incorporating ensemble techniques and meta-learning strategies in Auto-sklearn.

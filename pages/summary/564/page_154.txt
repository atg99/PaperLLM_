The references listed include significant contributions to the fields of machine learning and optimization. Key works include the design of the 2015 AutoML challenge by Guyon et al., which emphasizes the importance of automated machine learning. The paper by Hutter et al. discusses sequential model-based optimization, essential for algorithm configuration. Independent component analysis is covered by Hyvärinen and Oja, while the Adam optimization method by Kingma and Ba has become a standard in stochastic optimization. Krizhevsky et al.'s work on deep convolutional neural networks highlights advancements in image classification. Hyperband, proposed by Li et al., introduces a novel approach to hyperparameter optimization. Additionally, Mendoza et al. address the trend towards automatically-tuned neural networks, showcasing the shift towards automation in machine learning. Other notable contributions include Gaussian processes for machine learning by Rasmussen and Williams, and the introduction of automatic differentiation in PyTorch by Paszke et al. These references collectively illustrate the evolution and current state of research in machine learning methodologies and practices.

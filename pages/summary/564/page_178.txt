The paper presents a collection of references relevant to machine learning and statistical analysis, focusing on various methodologies and algorithms. Key contributions include Gaussian processes for machine learning, symbolic regression, and techniques for missing value estimation in datasets. Notable works by Schmidt and Lipson discuss the extraction of natural laws from data and the symbolic regression of implicit equations. The use of pattern alternating maximization and non-parametric imputation methods for high-dimensional data is highlighted, along with advancements in Bayesian optimization and automated model selection.

Chapter 3 of the paper by Thomas Elsken, Jan Hendrik Metzen, and Frank Hutter discusses Neural Architecture Search (NAS), which has gained attention due to its potential to automate the design of neural networks. The authors highlight that the manual development of neural architectures is labor-intensive and prone to errors, leading to increased interest in automated methods. They categorize existing NAS approaches based on three dimensions: search space, search strategy, and performance estimation strategy. The introduction emphasizes that deep learning's success in tasks like image and speech recognition stems from automating feature extraction, contrasting it with traditional manual feature engineering. The chapter aims to provide a comprehensive overview of the current state of research in NAS.

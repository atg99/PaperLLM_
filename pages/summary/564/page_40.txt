This section lists several key references pertinent to advancements in optimization techniques, particularly in the context of Bayesian optimization and hyperparameter tuning for machine learning algorithms. Hernández-Lobato et al. discuss parallel and distributed Thompson sampling for exploring chemical space, while their work on constrained Bayesian optimization provides a framework for information-based search strategies. Hoeting et al. offer a tutorial on Bayesian model averaging, which is foundational for understanding model uncertainty.

Hutter's contributions include a series of papers on automated configuration of algorithms, emphasizing sequential model-based optimization and parallel algorithm configuration. These works highlight the significance of efficient hyperparameter tuning, including time-bounded optimization and assessing hyperparameter importance.

Additionally, the references explore multi-objective optimization approaches, such as those by Igel and Kandasamy et al., focusing on model selection and Gaussian process bandit optimization with multi-fidelity evaluations. The overarching theme in these works is the continuous improvement of optimization processes for complex machine learning tasks, underscoring the importance of sophisticated methods to enhance algorithm performance.

This chapter presents a comprehensive review of advancements in deep neural networks, emphasizing the trend towards automatic tuning and optimization techniques. It highlights significant contributions to the field, including Bayesian optimization methods that remove human intervention and enhance model performance. Notable works discussed include the application of deep learning in complex tasks such as mastering games, face verification, and improving computational efficiency through frameworks like Theano. The chapter also covers regularization techniques like dropout and mixup that help prevent overfitting, alongside learning rate adaptations such as cyclical learning rates and ADADELTA. Overall, the chapter underscores the evolution of deep learning methodologies and the importance of hyperparameter optimization in achieving superior model accuracy and efficiency.

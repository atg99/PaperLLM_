The paper discusses advancements in Neural Architecture Search (NAS) and its integration with hyperparameter optimization and data augmentation pipelines, highlighting the need for a more comprehensive approach that combines these elements within an open-source AutoML system. While NAS has shown impressive results, it lacks insights into the reasons behind the success of specific architectures and the consistency of architectures across different runs. The authors emphasize the importance of identifying common motifs in architectures, understanding their significance for high performance, and exploring their generalizability across various problems. The acknowledgments section credits contributors for their feedback and mentions support from the European Research Council under the Horizon 2020 program. The bibliography lists various works related to NAS and hyperparameter optimization, showcasing the breadth of research in this field.

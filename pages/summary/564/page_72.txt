This section discusses various contributions to the field of meta-learning, particularly focusing on methods for automatic machine learning (AutoML) and hyperparameter tuning. Notable works include Yang et al.'s OBOE, which employs collaborative filtering techniques for initializing AutoML processes, presented at the NeurIPS 2018 Workshop and also available as a preprint. Additionally, Yogatama and Mann propose an efficient transfer learning method aimed at automatic hyperparameter tuning, presented at the AI and Statistics conference in 2014. Yosinski et al. investigate the transferability of features in deep neural networks, contributing to understanding how knowledge can be leveraged across different tasks.

The author emphasizes the significance of automating various aspects of the machine learning pipeline, including data collection, cleaning, feature selection, model discovery, and monitoring. While the ultimate goal is full automation, the author acknowledges that semi-automation will likely be more practical in many cases. This approach not only aims to enhance the systematic nature of machine learning practices but also to increase efficiency. The text argues that current AutoML methods have already outperformed human experts in specific tasks, and as computational resources become more accessible, the relevance of AutoML will continue to grow. The book serves as a comprehensive resource for those looking to engage with AutoML, featuring up-to-date discussions on essential techniques and existing systems.

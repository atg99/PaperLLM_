The paper discusses hyperparameter optimization, highlighting a novel pipeline creation method through hierarchical planning exemplified by ML-Plan. This approach employs hierarchical task networks and demonstrates competitive performance against established AutoML systems such as Auto-WEKA and Auto-sklearn. While current methods do not consistently surpass AutoML systems with fixed pipeline lengths, it is suggested that larger pipelines could yield significant improvements. Additionally, the paper references neural architecture search and its complex configuration spaces, indicating a more detailed exploration of this topic in Chapter 3. The authors express gratitude to several individuals for their feedback on the manuscript. The bibliography lists key references related to machine learning and optimization techniques.

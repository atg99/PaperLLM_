The discussion advances to bandit-based methods, notably successive halving and Hyperband, which have demonstrated strong efficacy in optimizing deep learning models. The Freeze-Thaw Bayesian optimization technique integrates learning curves into the Bayesian optimization framework, allowing for iterative training of machine learning models before deciding to continue training or to explore new configurations. This approach leverages Gaussian processes to model the performance of algorithms and incorporates a covariance function tailored for learning curves.

Overall, while the optimization methods discussed exhibit varying degrees of effectiveness in hyperparameter tuning and algorithm selection, the ongoing exploration of learning curves and adaptive strategies shows promise for improving the performance and efficiency of machine learning models.

In Chapter 1 of their work, Matthias Feurer and Frank Hutter explore the field of hyperparameter optimization (HPO), highlighting its significance in the context of complex machine learning models, particularly in automated machine learning (AutoML) and deep neural networks. The chapter begins with an overview of various HPO approaches, starting with blackbox function optimization methods, which include model-free techniques and Bayesian optimization. Due to the high computational costs associated with these methods, the authors shift focus to modern multi-fidelity methods that leverage cheaper alternatives to evaluate hyperparameter settings more efficiently.

The introduction emphasizes the critical role of hyperparameters in machine learning systems, noting that optimizing these parameters is essential for enhancing model performance. Automated HPO is particularly beneficial as it minimizes the human effort required for machine learning applications, making it a crucial component of AutoML. The chapter concludes by identifying open problems and potential future research directions in the field of hyperparameter optimization.

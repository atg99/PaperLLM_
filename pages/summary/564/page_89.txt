Chapter 4 discusses Auto-WEKA, a system designed for automatic model selection and hyperparameter optimization within the WEKA framework. The authors, Lars Kotthoff and colleagues, address the complexity of choosing the right machine learning algorithm and its associated hyperparameters, highlighting the vast number of potential combinations. They propose a fully automated solution that utilizes advancements in Bayesian optimization, making the process more accessible for non-expert users.

The chapter outlines the integration of various feature selection techniques and machine learning methods available in WEKA, which includes 2 ensemble methods, 10 meta-methods, and 28 base learners. The performance of Auto-WEKA is evaluated across 21 well-known datasets, demonstrating that its results frequently surpass those achieved through traditional selection and optimization methods. The authors aim for their approach to facilitate better algorithm identification and hyperparameter tuning, ultimately enhancing performance for users with limited expertise in machine learning.

The paper discusses the performance of Auto-Net in the AutoML challenge, specifically in the human expert track across multiple datasets. Figures show the errors from various methods on the validation sets of the newsgroups and dorothea datasets, presenting mean error with standard deviations over ten runs. The results highlight that Auto-sklearn, being more developed and robust, outperformed Auto-Net in most datasets during the competition's later phases. However, Auto-Net excelled in the alexis dataset, achieving an AUC score of 90% with an ensemble of 39 models, surpassing the best human competitor's score of 80%. This marks a significant milestone, as it is the first instance where an automatically-constructed neural network won a competition dataset. The yolanda and tania datasets were also tackled by Auto-Net, with extensive computational resources utilized to optimize performance.

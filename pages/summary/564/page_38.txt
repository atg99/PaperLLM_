The paper discusses various methods and frameworks for hyperparameter optimization in machine learning, specifically focusing on deep neural networks and model selection. It references several key works in the field, including techniques such as Hyperband combined with Bayesian model selection, resampling methods for meta-model validation, and the use of Gaussian process bandit optimization. The authors highlight the importance of addressing overfitting in model selection and the biases that can arise in performance evaluation. Additionally, they explore strategies to accelerate hyperparameter optimization by utilizing learning curve extrapolation. The paper emphasizes the need for empirical foundations to assess the effectiveness of Bayesian optimization methods and discusses the comparative advantages of genetic algorithms and particle swarm optimization in model selection tasks. Overall, the work presents a comprehensive overview of contemporary approaches to optimizing machine learning models, underscoring the significance of methodological rigor in achieving reliable performance outcomes.

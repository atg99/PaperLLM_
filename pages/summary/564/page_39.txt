The references listed focus on various methodologies and advancements in hyperparameter optimization, a critical aspect of machine learning model performance. Key contributions include:

1. **BOHB** (Falkner et al.) presents a robust and efficient approach for hyperparameter optimization at scale, combining Bayesian optimization with Hyperband for resource allocation.
2. **Automated Machine Learning** (Feurer et al.) discusses effective and robust strategies for automating machine learning processes, enhancing efficiency.
3. **Gradient-Based Optimization** (Franceschi et al.) explores forward and reverse gradient methods for hyperparameter tuning, emphasizing the importance of gradient information.
4. **Bilevel Programming** (Franceschi et al.) introduces a framework for both hyperparameter optimization and meta-learning, highlighting the interaction between model training and hyperparameter selection.
5. **Evolutionary Tuning** (Friedrichs and Igel) and **Model-Based Optimization** (Frohlich and Zell) focus on optimizing parameters of support vector machines using evolutionary strategies and global optimization methods, respectively.
6. **Bayesian Optimization** (Gardner et al., Gelbart et al.) emphasizes the application of Bayesian techniques in optimization problems, including those with constraints and integer-valued variables.
7. **Google Vizier** (Golovin et al.) presents a service designed for black-box optimization, showcasing practical applications in large-scale settings.

These works collectively contribute to the understanding and development of efficient strategies for hyperparameter optimization, which is essential for improving machine learning model performance.

The paper discusses future work aimed at extending Auto-Net to encompass a wider range of neural network architectures, specifically convolutional and recurrent neural networks. It acknowledges support from the European Research Council under the Horizon 2020 program. The bibliography includes significant references related to machine learning, hyper-parameter optimization, and automated machine learning techniques, showcasing foundational algorithms and methodologies in the field. Key contributions from various authors are highlighted, providing a comprehensive overview of the advancements in optimizing deep neural networks and enhancing their performance through techniques such as Bayesian optimization and ensemble selection.

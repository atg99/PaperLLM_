The paper discusses alternatives to traditional optimization methods in hyperparameter optimization (HPO), primarily focusing on ensembling and marginalization. Instead of selecting a single hyperparameter configuration, the authors highlight the benefits of constructing ensembles, which can enhance model performance by combining multiple good configurations, particularly in complex AutoML systems. They also mention the concept of Automatic Frankensteining, where HPO is used to create a stacking model that combines outputs from various models using traditional ensembling techniques.

Furthermore, the paper addresses the optimization of multiple objectives, which is often necessary in practical applications. Two primary approaches are suggested: formulating a constrained optimization problem when a limit on a secondary performance metric is known, and applying multi-objective optimization to identify the Pareto front. The Pareto front represents optimal trade-offs between different objectives, allowing users to select configurations that best meet their needs. The authors reference additional literature for readers interested in exploring these topics further.

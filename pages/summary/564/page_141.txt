In Chapter 7, the authors discuss advancements in AutoML, specifically through the introduction of Auto-Net, which enables the automatic tuning of deep neural networks without human intervention. The chapter presents two iterations: Auto-Net 1.0, which utilizes Bayesian Optimization with the SMAC method and employs the Lasagne deep learning library, and Auto-Net 2.0, which improves on this by integrating Bayesian Optimization with HyperBand (BOHB) and using PyTorch.

Auto-Net 1.0 is notable for being the first automatically-tuned neural network to outperform human experts in competition settings, demonstrating its effectiveness as part of the initial AutoML challenge. The authors provide empirical evidence that combining Auto-Net 1.0 with Auto-sklearn yields superior performance compared to either method individually, while Auto-Net 2.0 achieves even greater results.

The introduction highlights the challenges of using neural networks, particularly for non-experts, due to the complexity of hyperparameter settings and architectural decisions. The chapter emphasizes the significance of these developments in making deep learning more accessible and effective.

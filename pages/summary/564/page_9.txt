The preface outlines the rapid growth of machine learning research, particularly highlighting the advancements in deep learning across various domains like computer vision and speech processing. It emphasizes the challenges faced by users, especially newcomers, due to the intricate design decisions required for optimal performance in machine learning applications. These challenges often lead to extensive trial and error, even among experts.

Automated machine learning (AutoML) is introduced as a solution to streamline this process, allowing users to provide data while the AutoML system autonomously determines the best approach for the specific application. This advancement is portrayed as a means to democratize machine learning, making sophisticated techniques accessible to those without extensive technical knowledge.

The preface asserts that AutoML systems have reached a level of maturity that can match or exceed the performance of human experts, leading to significant time and cost savings. It also notes the increasing commercial interest in AutoML, with major tech companies developing their own systems. However, the authors advocate for open-source AutoML solutions over proprietary services to better serve the goal of making machine learning accessible to all.

Finally, the preface clarifies that while the current focus in the community may lean towards neural architecture search (NAS), AutoML encompasses a broader range of techniques and methodologies.

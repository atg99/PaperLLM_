The section discusses various approaches to hyperparameter optimization, highlighting significant contributions in the field. Zabinsky's work focuses on pure random and adaptive search methods for global optimization, while Zeng and Luo propose a progressive sampling-based Bayesian optimization technique aimed at improving efficiency in automatic machine learning model selection. Zhang et al. present FLASH, a fast Bayesian optimization framework designed for data analytic pipelines. The chapter emphasizes the importance of these methods in enhancing machine learning performance.

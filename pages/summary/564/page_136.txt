The paper discusses the advancements and applications of Auto-sklearn, a system designed for automated machine learning. Auto-sklearn has established itself as a standard benchmark for various automated machine learning methods since its introduction. It serves both novices and experts, with features that allow it to seamlessly replace any scikit-learn classifier or regressor. The package supports various loss functions and resampling strategies, and has recently incorporated regression capabilities.

The authors also highlight the development of PoSH Auto-sklearn, an extension of the original framework aimed at handling larger datasets effectively. This version proved successful in the AutoML 2 challenge by leveraging a multi-fidelity approach and incorporating extreme gradient boosting methods. Overall, the paper emphasizes the versatility and improved performance of Auto-sklearn and its extensions in the evolving field of automated machine learning.

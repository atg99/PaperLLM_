The paper presents a comparison of the performance of various classifiers against Auto-sklearn over time, specifically using the MNIST and Promise pc4 datasets. The results indicate that while Auto-sklearn initially shows inferior performance, it ultimately approaches the best-performing method by the end of the optimization process. The analysis includes median test error rates as well as the fifth and 95th percentiles, highlighting the effectiveness of optimizing classifiers both separately and within a joint space. Further details and a comprehensive comparison of all classifiers are available in the supplementary material.

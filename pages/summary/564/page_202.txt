This section of the paper analyzes the performance distribution of participants in the AutoML Challenge Series from 2015 to 2018, focusing on the results from the AutoML and Tweakathon phases. The performance on various datasets is visualized through violin plots, which illustrate the median and quartiles of participant performances. Notably, the first five datasets were used solely for development, while the last five were reserved for final blind testing during the Tweakathon phase. Issues arose in Round 3, where many participants faced computational limits, highlighting the challenges of resource allocation among tied teams.

The analysis also addresses the difficulty of the datasets and tasks presented in the challenges. It notes that the 2015/2016 challenge featured few categorical variables and missing data, which did not pose significant challenges. However, the dataset ALBERT emerged as particularly difficult due to its size. In contrast, the 2018 challenge saw a shift, with five out of ten datasets incorporating categorical variables and missing values, indicating an increase in complexity and difficulty in the tasks participants had to tackle.

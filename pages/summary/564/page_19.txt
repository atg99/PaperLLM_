The section on blackbox hyperparameter optimization (HPO) discusses methods for optimizing hyperparameters in a non-convex space, emphasizing the need for global optimization algorithms while also recognizing the benefits of local exploration due to limited function evaluations. 

Model-free blackbox optimization methods are introduced, starting with grid search, which evaluates all combinations of specified hyperparameter values. While straightforward, grid search is hindered by the curse of dimensionality, as the number of required evaluations increases exponentially with the number of hyperparameters. Additionally, refining the discretization leads to significantly more evaluations.

In contrast, random search offers a more efficient alternative by randomly sampling configurations within a predefined budget. This approach is particularly advantageous when certain hyperparameters have a greater impact than others, as it enables broader exploration of the hyperparameter space. The text illustrates that with a fixed budget, random search can evaluate more distinct values for each hyperparameter compared to grid search, making it a preferable choice in many scenarios.

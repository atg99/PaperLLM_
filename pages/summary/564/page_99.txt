The paper discusses the performance of Auto-WEKA, an automated machine learning framework, in comparison to traditional methods such as Ex-Def and Grid Search. The evaluation is based on various datasets, measuring misclassification rates through 10-fold cross-validation and test performance.

Key findings include:

- Auto-WEKA consistently produces competitive results across different datasets, often achieving lower misclassification rates compared to Ex-Def and Grid Search.
- For instance, in the Dexter dataset, Auto-WEKA achieved a test performance of 5.00%, outperforming Grid Search at 7.49% and Ex-Def at 10.20%.
- The GermanCredit dataset saw Auto-WEKA at 28.24% test performance, better than both Ex-Def and Grid Search.
- Other datasets such as Yeast and Amazon also demonstrated Auto-WEKA's effectiveness, with notable performance improvements over traditional methods.
- The results are statistically significant, with bolded values indicating the lowest error rates within comparable methods.

Overall, Auto-WEKA shows promise as an effective tool for automatic model selection and hyperparameter tuning, demonstrating superior performance on several datasets compared to established methods.

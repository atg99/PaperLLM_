The preface outlines the key components of the book, particularly focusing on advancements in Automated Machine Learning (AutoML). Chapter 8 discusses the TPOT system, which automates the construction and optimization of tree-based machine learning pipelines, offering greater flexibility than fixed component approaches. Chapter 9 introduces the Automatic Statistician, which automates data science reporting by generating analyses, predictive models, and performance comparisons, while also providing natural-language descriptions for accessibility to non-experts. Part III and Chapter 10 review AutoML challenges initiated in 2015, aimed at promoting effective approaches to practical problems and identifying the best solutions among submissions. This book serves as a comprehensive resource on AutoML, covering its methods, practical systems, and evaluation challenges. It aims to equip practitioners with knowledge and tools for developing AutoML systems and highlights existing state-of-the-art solutions for various machine learning tasks.

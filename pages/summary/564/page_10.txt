This book serves as a comprehensive resource on AutoML, highlighting its breadth beyond Neural Architecture Search (NAS). It is structured into three parts: an overview of AutoML methods, practical applications through systems, and a state-of-the-art discussion for researchers.

Part I covers foundational AutoML methods. Chapter 1 addresses hyperparameter optimization, detailing various approaches with a focus on efficiency. Chapter 2 introduces "learning to learn," which leverages prior experiences in machine learning to enhance performance on new tasks, mirroring the human learning process. Chapter 3 provides an in-depth look at NAS, emphasizing its complexity and the rapid advancements in this area.

Part II focuses on user-friendly AutoML systems. Chapter 4 discusses Auto-WEKA, an early AutoML system leveraging the WEKA toolkit for classification and regression without coding. Chapter 5 reviews Hyperopt-Sklearn, built on scikit-learn, offering practical examples for users. Chapter 6 examines Auto-sklearn, which incorporates meta-learning and ensembling techniques, demonstrating superior performance in competitions compared to Auto-WEKA and Hyperopt-Sklearn. Lastly, Chapter 7 presents Auto-Net, an automated deep learning system that optimizes both architecture and hyperparameters, achieving notable success against human experts.

Overall, the book provides essential insights for researchers and practitioners in the evolving field of AutoML.

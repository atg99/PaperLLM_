The section on Meta-Learning presents a variety of studies and contributions to the field, highlighting the evolution of algorithms and methodologies aimed at improving machine learning processes. Key works include:

1. Runarsson and Jonsson's exploration of distributed learning rules, emphasizing the design aspects of evolutionary computation in conjunction with neural networks.
2. Salama et al.'s investigation into integrating neural networks with rough set theory to enhance meta-learning approaches.
3. Sanders and Giraud-Carrier's work on hyperparameter optimization informed by meta-learning principles, showcasing the practical applications of meta-learning in model tuning.
4. Santoro et al.'s research on memory-augmented neural networks, contributing to advancements in one-shot learning and memory utilization in neural architectures.
5. Schilling et al.'s focus on hyperparameter optimization using factorized multilayer perceptrons, indicating a shift towards more efficient model training techniques.

Other notable mentions include Schmidhuber’s foundational work on dynamic memory systems, the development of feature selection methods for high-dimensional data by Schoenfeld et al., and the introduction of prototypical networks by Snell et al. for few-shot learning scenarios. The overarching theme is the continuous improvement and adaptation of models through meta-learning strategies, enhancing algorithm selection, model performance, and data analysis capabilities.

The paper presents a compilation of significant works related to machine learning, statistical analysis, and model selection. It references various studies and theses that explore topics such as nonparametric regression, automated machine learning, Bayesian data analysis, and structure discovery in probabilistic models. Key contributions include the development of the Automatic Statistician, which focuses on scalable structure discovery, and Auto-WEKA, which automates model selection and hyperparameter optimization. The cited works also highlight advancements in Gaussian processes and their applications in time series data, indicating a trend towards integrating probabilistic methods with automated systems for enhanced data analysis and model generation. Overall, these references underscore the evolving landscape of machine learning methodologies aimed at improving efficiency and robustness in data interpretation and model construction.

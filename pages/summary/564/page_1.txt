The book "Automated Machine Learning: Methods, Systems, Challenges," edited by Frank Hutter, Lars Kotthoff, and Joaquin Vanschoren, is part of the Springer Series on Challenges in Machine Learning. It addresses the growing need for automation in machine learning processes, highlighting various methods and systems that facilitate this automation. The work discusses the challenges faced in implementing automated machine learning, including issues related to model selection, hyperparameter tuning, and the integration of domain knowledge. Through a comprehensive exploration of current advancements and methodologies, the editors aim to provide insights into the future of machine learning automation and its implications for researchers and practitioners in the field.

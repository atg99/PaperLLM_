Chapter 10 of the book titled "Analysis of the AutoML Challenge Series 2015–2018" provides a comprehensive overview of the ChaLearn AutoML Challenge, which took place from NIPS 2015 to ICML 2016. The competition comprised six rounds that increased in difficulty while participants faced constraints on computational resources. The authors, led by Isabelle Guyon, include contributions from various experts in machine learning and related fields. The chapter highlights the structure of the challenge, the methodologies employed by participants, and the outcomes of the competition, offering insights into the advancements in automated machine learning during this period. The collaborative efforts of the authors reflect a diverse range of expertise, underscoring the significance of the challenge in driving innovation in the field.

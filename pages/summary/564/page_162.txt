The paper presents TPOT, a Tree-Based Pipeline Optimization Tool designed to automate the machine learning process. It highlights the performance of TPOT compared to a Random Forest model with 500 trees across 25 benchmarks where a significant difference in median accuracy was observed. The results are visualized through box plots that illustrate the distribution of balanced accuracies. Each box plot includes 30 replicates, indicating the median with an inner line, showcasing the bootstrapped 95% confidence interval of the median through notches, and marking the first and third quartiles at the ends of the box. Outliers are represented by dots, emphasizing the robustness and effectiveness of TPOT in optimizing machine learning pipelines.

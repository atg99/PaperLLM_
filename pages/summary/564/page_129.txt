The paper evaluates the performance of Auto-sklearn (AS) compared to Auto-WEKA (AW) and HYPEROPT-SKLEARN (HS) across various datasets. It presents the median percent test error rates derived from 100,000 bootstrap samples, simulating parallel runs and selecting the best based on cross-validation performance. The results indicate that AS performs competitively, with several bolded figures representing the best outcomes. The datasets used for evaluation encompass a broad range of applications, ensuring robust performance assessments. 

Data was split into training and test sets, with AS utilizing a further division of the training set for validation. The study emphasizes the use of balanced classification error rate (BER) to account for class imbalances, contrasting it with standard classification error. This approach aligns with practices in machine learning competitions, highlighting its relevance. The experiments included multiple runs of AS with variations in meta-learning and ensemble building to analyze their effects on performance. Overall, the findings suggest that Auto-sklearn is an efficient and robust automated machine learning tool.

This chapter discusses the one-round AutoML challenge held at PAKDD 2018, which differs from earlier model selection challenges by emphasizing the development of fully automated and computationally efficient systems that require no human intervention for training and testing. The chapter analyzes competition results, details datasets that were kept hidden from participants, and benchmarks the winning solutions against canonical machine learning algorithms available in scikit-learn. All relevant materials, including data and code, are publicly accessible.

The introduction highlights the evolution of machine learning from a niche field to a widely recognized discipline, driven by large corporations and increased data availability. It notes the transition from a "seller's market" for machine learning experts to a "buyer's market," where the demand for learning machines is high. Despite this progress, the chapter emphasizes that full automation remains a challenge due to the complexities of software application, data fitting, and hyperparameter selection. The ChaLearn AutoML challenge series aims to progressively minimize human intervention in machine learning applications, although full automation faces ongoing challenges due to the emergence of novel problem settings. The initial AutoML challenges focused on supervised learning, feature vector representations, and homogeneous datasets.

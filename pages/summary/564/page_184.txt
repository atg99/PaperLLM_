This chapter provides an analysis of the AutoML Challenge Series held from 2015 to 2018, summarizing results from the ICML AutoML workshops and a subsequent event at the PAKDD conference. The challenges involved multiple rounds with numerous datasets, focusing on supervised learning tasks, specifically classification and regression, without human intervention. 

The chapter presents systematic studies of the winning solutions across all datasets and compares them with commonly used machine learning models from scikit-learn. It emphasizes the challenges posed by data complexity, including class imbalance, sparsity, missing values, and categorical variables, while noting that the tasks were preformatted into fixed-length vector representations to streamline the process.

The goal of the challenge was to develop automated systems capable of effectively handling these complexities, thereby reducing the need for extensive human input in model selection and hyper-parameter tuning. The chapter includes unpublished details and reflective analyses, supported by a comprehensive online appendix for further reference.

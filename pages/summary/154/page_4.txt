This paper presents a VAE-LSTM hybrid model designed for unsupervised anomaly detection in time series data. The model combines a Variational Autoencoder (VAE) for capturing local features and a Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) network for understanding long-term dependencies. The authors demonstrate the model's effectiveness across five real-world datasets, showing that it achieves 100% recall without missing any anomalies while maintaining a low false positive rate, leading to a high F1 score compared to other methods like ARMA, LSTM-AD, and VAE. While the VAE-LSTM model excels in precision and recall, it does exhibit a delay in detecting some anomalies, particularly noted in the EC2 CPU utilization data. The authors suggest that this limitation could be mitigated with the use of multiple scale windows in future research.

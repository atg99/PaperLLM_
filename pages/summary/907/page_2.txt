In summary, we propose the Attentive CutMix method, which improves upon existing data augmentation strategies like Mixup, Cutout, and CutMix. By utilizing attention maps, our method focuses on the most significant parts of objects during the training process, leading to a more robust network with enhanced recognition performance without additional testing costs. Extensive experiments conducted on CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100 demonstrate that Attentive CutMix consistently boosts accuracy across various network architectures, achieving a notable improvement of 2.16% on CIFAR-100 with ResNet-152 compared to the baseline CutMix. This work highlights the importance of attention mechanisms in augmenting training data effectively, enabling better model generalization in recognizing complex natural objects.

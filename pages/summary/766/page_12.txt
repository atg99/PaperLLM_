Zhenbo Ren completed his BS at Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2011, his MS at Tsinghua University in 2014, and his PhD at the University of Hong Kong in 2018. His research focuses on digital holography and optical imaging, and he is a student member of SPIE. Zhimin Xu earned both his BS and MS degrees in electronic engineering from Fudan University and obtained his PhD from the University of Hong Kong in 2012. His research interests lie in artificial intelligence and imaging. Edmund Y. Lam holds BS, MS, and PhD degrees in electrical engineering from Stanford University. He is a professor at the University of Hong Kong, directing the Imaging Systems Laboratory, with research centered on computational optics and imaging. Lam has published over 300 papers and received the IBM faculty award. He is a fellow of several prestigious organizations, including SPIE, OSA, IEEE, IS&T, and HKIE. The paper discusses an end-to-end deep learning framework related to their research interests.

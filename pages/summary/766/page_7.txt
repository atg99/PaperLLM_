In this section, the authors explore the capabilities of a proposed framework using a two-sectional holographic sample. A total of 2000 holograms were collected by spatially shifting the object, with specific examples illustrated in the figures. The two objects, a triangle and a rectangle, are positioned at varying distances, which are crucial for achieving effective 3-D imaging. The authors aim to eliminate defocus noise and produce all-in-focus images through two reconstruction modalities: Extended Focus Imaging (EFI) and Digital Microscopy (DM), which are realized using HRNet.

The methodology for generating ground-truth images for EFI and DM is similar to that described in a previous section. The reconstruction performance of HRNet is compared with two other methods, PCA and DE, showcasing HRNet's superior results in terms of Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (PSNR) and Structural Similarity Index (SSIM). The performance metrics indicate that HRNet significantly outperforms the other methods in both validation and test datasets, with the fastest processing time among the three. The findings highlight the effectiveness of the proposed framework in multi-sectional holography.

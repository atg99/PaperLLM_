The paper provides a concise overview of Explainable Artificial Intelligence (xAI), highlighting its importance in understanding complex machine learning models, particularly deep neural networks. The authors introduce 17 notable xAI methods, including LIME, SHAP, and Integrated Gradients, aimed at offering application engineers and data scientists a foundational understanding of current techniques in the field.

The introduction emphasizes the resurgence of AI driven by machine learning advancements, noting that while these methods have achieved remarkable success, they also pose challenges in terms of interpretability. The paper addresses the need for explainability to ensure that AI systems can be understood and trusted by users.

The selected methods discussed in the paper provide a range of approaches for interpreting model predictions, allowing practitioners to gain insights into how models arrive at their conclusions. This overview serves as a valuable resource for those new to the field, facilitating a better grasp of xAI and its essential role in responsible AI deployment.

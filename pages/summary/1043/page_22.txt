This paper compiles a comprehensive list of references related to various aspects of explainable artificial intelligence (AI) and deep learning methodologies. It covers significant contributions in the field, including discussions on the challenges of gradient propagation in deep networks, the development of automatic evaluation metrics for machine translation, and frameworks for model interpretability. Key works include Balduzzi et al.'s exploration of the shattered gradients problem, Banerjee and Lavie's introduction of the METEOR metric for machine translation evaluation, and Becking et al.'s focus on explainability-driven quantization for deep neural networks. The references also highlight methodologies for providing interpretability in predictive models, such as DALEX and layer-wise relevance propagation techniques. Additionally, the paper addresses the integration of expert knowledge with deep learning through neural-symbolic approaches and emphasizes the importance of causal knowledge in model explanations. Overall, these contributions underscore the ongoing efforts to enhance the transparency and accountability of AI systems.

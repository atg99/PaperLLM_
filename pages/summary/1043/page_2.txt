The paper discusses the challenges associated with the lack of transparency in black-box machine learning approaches, which hinder trust and acceptance of artificial intelligence (AI). This issue is exacerbated by legal and data protection regulations, notably the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), particularly in sensitive areas like healthcare. The concept of explainable AI (xAI), popularized by DARPA, represents a longstanding scientific pursuit aimed at elucidating the reasons behind AI decisions. By providing insights into the explanatory factors, xAI promotes ethical and responsible AI use, as well as transparent machine learning in decision-making processes. 

The authors present a concise overview of various xAI methods and tools to assist engineers and students in selecting appropriate techniques for model explanation. They highlight the popularity of different xAI toolboxes, illustrated by a figure showing the growth in GitHub stars for various repositories. The paper aims to guide practitioners in choosing effective methods for explaining complex models, thereby enhancing the understanding and acceptance of AI technologies.

By integrating structured knowledge and logical reasoning with deep learning, this approach aims to create models that are not only performant but also interpretable. 

The key advantage of X-NeSyL is its ability to leverage existing knowledge representations, enabling the model to reason about data in a way that aligns with human understanding. This method addresses the challenge of explainability in deep learning by grounding model predictions in symbolic logic, which can be more easily understood by users.

However, the integration of neural and symbolic components may introduce complexity in model training and inference. The authors suggest that further research is needed to optimize the synergy between these two paradigms to achieve both high performance and clarity in explanations.

In conclusion, both Meaningful Perturbations and X-NeSyL represent significant strides in the quest for explainable AI, each offering unique methodologies to enhance model transparency and user trust in AI systems.

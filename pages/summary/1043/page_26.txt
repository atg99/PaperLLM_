This section references various studies and contributions in the field of machine learning and neural networks. Key works include Williams' exploration of statistical gradient-following algorithms for reinforcement learning, and Xu et al.'s investigation into explainability-based backdoor attacks on graph neural networks. Yeom et al. propose a novel criterion for pruning deep neural networks by utilizing explanations, while Yuan et al. introduce XGNN, focusing on model-level explanations for graph neural networks. Zeiler and Fergus contribute to the understanding of convolutional networks through visualization techniques. Additionally, Zintgraf et al. present a method for visualizing decisions made by deep neural networks through prediction difference analysis.

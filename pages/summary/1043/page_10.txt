The methodology starts by defining concept activation vectors (CAVs) through the collection of two datasets: one representing the concept of interest and another lacking it. A logistic regression model is trained on these datasets, using neural network activations as features to derive CAVs. These vectors quantify the influence of specific concepts on classification outcomes.

TCAV extends the analysis by aggregating CAVs across a dataset, providing a broader view of concept influence on classifications. It calculates the ratio of images with positive conceptual sensitivity, indicating the extent of concept contribution to the classification. Statistical testing, specifically a two-tailed Student t-test, is employed to assess the significance of these contributions.

In summary, TCAV serves as a powerful tool for assessing concept sensitivity in image classifiers, promoting interpretability and enabling users without deep machine learning knowledge to explore how various concepts impact model predictions. It can also facilitate discussions around fairness in AI by examining the influence of sensitive attributes on classification decisions.

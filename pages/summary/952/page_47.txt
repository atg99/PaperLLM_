The paper "S1559 A Survey of Uncertainty in Deep Neural Networks" discusses methods for addressing uncertainty in deep learning models, particularly during inference when model uncertainty cannot be obtained. One prominent technique highlighted is label smoothing, which modifies training labels by redistributing a portion of the true class probability to incorrect classes. This approach mitigates the issue of hard labels leading to overconfident predictions, ultimately decreasing calibration error and often improving accuracy.

Seo et al. (2019) expanded on label smoothing by introducing a method to reduce model uncertainty during training. They sampled multiple forward passes through a stochastic neural network and derived a normalized model variance based on the predictions. This variance informed a variance-weighted confidence-integrated loss function, which combined cross-entropy loss with a KL-divergence penalty. The adaptive smoothing parameter guided predictions of uncertain samples towards a uniform distribution while enhancing the scores for certain predictions, allowing for a single forward pass during inference.

Pereyra et al. (2017) addressed the issue of overconfidence in model predictions by incorporating negative entropy into the loss function, creating an entropy-based objective that penalizes high confidence predictions. This adjustment encourages more calibrated outputs from the network.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the importance of uncertainty estimation in deep neural networks and presents various methodologies to enhance model calibration and reliability.

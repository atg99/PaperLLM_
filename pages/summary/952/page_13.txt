The paper surveys the various methods for quantifying uncertainty in deep neural networks (DNNs). It identifies four primary types of methods: 

1. **Bayesian Methods**: These encompass a range of stochastic DNNs, where multiple forward passes with the same input yield different results due to inherent randomness.
  
2. **Ensemble Methods**: These involve aggregating predictions from several distinct deterministic networks during inference to enhance reliability.

3. **Test-Time Augmentation**: This approach employs a single deterministic network but modifies the input data during testing to produce multiple predictions, which helps evaluate the certainty of the outcomes.

The paper further elaborates on the core concepts and potential extensions of these methods, discussing their key properties, including complexity, computational demands, and flexibility. Visual aids like figures and tables provide a comprehensive overview of the methods and their characteristics, facilitating a clearer understanding of uncertainty quantification in DNNs.

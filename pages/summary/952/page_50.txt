Wenger et al. (2020) introduced a Gaussian process-based method for calibrating multi-class classifiers that output confidence values, specifically applied to neural networks. Their approach involves learning a calibration map using a Gaussian process trained on the confidence predictions and corresponding true labels from a calibration set. However, this method does not guarantee the preservation of predictions.

The discussion on calibration with uncertainty estimation highlights the importance of reducing model uncertainty for accurate data uncertainty estimations, leading to well-calibrated predictors. Research by Lakshminarayanan et al. (2017) and Mehrtash et al. (2020) indicates that deep ensembles improve calibration compared to single networks. Conversely, Rahaman et al. (2021) found that certain configurations, like mixup-regularization, may increase calibration errors, while temperature scaling on averaged predictions can enhance calibration.

Bayesian approaches show promise as well, with Kristiadi et al. (2020) demonstrating that confining Bayesian approximations to the weights of the final layer of a deep neural network can significantly improve calibration. However, Zhang et al. (2019) and Laves et al. (2019) noted that confidence estimates from Monte Carlo dropout may be poorly calibrated, leading Zhang et al. to propose structured dropout to enhance model diversity and reduce calibration errors.

Calibration quality evaluation involves assessing the consistency between predictive distributions and observations. For classification tasks, calibration measures often use binning, where predictions are grouped by predicted confidence, and the average bin confidence is compared to average bin accuracy. Guo et al. (2017) emphasized that confidence is well-calibrated when the average accuracy matches the average confidence for each bin. The reliability diagram, introduced by DeGroot and Fienberg (1983), is a common visual tool for assessing calibration, plotting confidence against accuracy; a well-calibrated model's plot should align closely with the diagonal. To enhance interpretability, modifications to the reliability diagram can distinguish between different classes.

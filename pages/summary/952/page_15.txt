The paper presents a survey on the uncertainty in deep neural networks, categorizing various approaches to manage and understand this uncertainty. It evaluates single deterministic networks, Bayesian methods, and ensemble methods, highlighting their application scenarios in fields such as medical imaging, remote sensing, and machinery fault detection. The authors reference specific studies to illustrate the effectiveness of these methods in different contexts, emphasizing the importance of uncertainty quantification in enhancing the reliability of deep learning models. Tables are provided for a detailed comparison of the approaches and their applications.

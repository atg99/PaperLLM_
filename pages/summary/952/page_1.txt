This paper provides a comprehensive survey of uncertainty estimation in deep neural networks, addressing the growing importance of confidence in predictions as neural networks become prevalent across various scientific fields and real-world applications. The authors highlight that conventional neural networks often lack proper certainty estimates and can exhibit issues such as over- or under-confidence, indicating poor calibration. 

To address these issues, the paper categorizes different types and sources of uncertainty, distinguishing between reducible model uncertainty and irreducible data uncertainty. It reviews various modeling approaches, including deterministic neural networks, Bayesian neural networks, ensembles of neural networks, and test-time data augmentation strategies. The authors discuss advancements in these areas, current challenges, and potential research opportunities.

Practical applications of uncertainty estimation are examined, including measures of uncertainty, calibration techniques, and existing baselines. The paper also illustrates the relevance of uncertainty quantification in fields such as medical image analysis, robotics, and earth observation. Furthermore, it addresses the practical limitations of these methods in mission- and safety-critical applications and outlines future directions for broader implementation of uncertainty quantification in neural networks.

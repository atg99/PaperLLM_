The paper presents a survey on uncertainty in deep neural networks, focusing on the comparison of internal and external deterministic single network methods for estimating uncertainty. 

Internal methods estimate uncertainty using a single network evaluation without external components, resulting in a relatively low implementation effort. However, these methods do not allow for the separation of prediction and uncertainty estimation and can be applied to already trained networks. They are applicable in scenarios such as tumor segmentation, molecular property prediction, and remote sensing.

In contrast, external methods also use a single network evaluation but rely on additional external components. These methods allow for the separation of prediction and uncertainty estimation and can be applied to already trained networks as well. They are particularly relevant in applications like eye disease detection.

The paper emphasizes the differences in implementation efforts, application scenarios, and the ability to separate prediction from uncertainty estimation between these two approaches.

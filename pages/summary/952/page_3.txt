The paper provides a comprehensive survey on uncertainty in deep neural networks (DNNs), emphasizing the importance of reliable uncertainty estimates for safe decision-making. It explores the calibration of DNNs and discusses various re-calibration methods that have been proposed to enhance the reliability of these estimates. The authors reference several foundational works in the field, including handbooks and theses that cover uncertainty quantification, Bayesian neural networks, and specific approaches like Monte Carlo Dropout.

Key contributions from prior studies are highlighted, including frameworks for uncertainty quantification in real-world applications, distinctions between aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty, and comparisons of methodologies such as MC Dropout and ensemble methods. The paper aims to provide a clear understanding of the sources of uncertainty, the recent advancements in estimating uncertainty in DNNs, and the measures for assessing the quality of these estimates. 

Furthermore, it identifies the limitations of existing approaches and outlines future challenges to be addressed. The survey is intended for both newcomers and practitioners in deep learning who wish to incorporate uncertainty estimation into their work. Overall, it encompasses a detailed discussion on the types and sources of uncertainty, recent methodologies for estimation, evaluation of uncertainty measures, and calibration techniques for DNNs.

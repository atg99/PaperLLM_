The paper discusses the quantification of uncertainties in deep neural networks (DNNs) and its practical applications, emphasizing the importance of making confident decisions based on predictions. It highlights applications in active learning and reinforcement learning, as well as in fields such as medical image analysis, robotics, and earth observation, suggesting that the challenges and concepts can be adapted to various domains.

In active learning, the paper outlines a framework where DNNs are trained sequentially on increasingly larger labeled datasets. This process aims to minimize the labor-intensive and costly nature of data labeling. Uncertainty sampling is a prominent strategy within this framework, where samples with the highest predictive uncertainty are prioritized for labeling. Several studies have successfully implemented uncertainty-based active learning strategies.

In reinforcement learning, uncertainty estimates help address the exploration-exploitation dilemma, allowing for a more effective balance between exploring new actions and exploiting known information. This enhances the learning process by informing decisions based on the level of uncertainty associated with different actions. Overall, the paper emphasizes the critical role of uncertainty quantification across various applications in enhancing DNN performance and decision-making capabilities.

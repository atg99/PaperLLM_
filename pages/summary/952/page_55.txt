The paper discusses the significance of uncertainty in deep neural networks, particularly in the context of exploring unknown environments while leveraging existing knowledge. It highlights methods for estimating uncertainty, such as using ensembles of bootstrapped models and dropout sampling, as demonstrated by researchers like Huang et al., Gal and Ghahramani, Kahn et al., and Lütjens et al.

The importance of quantifying and managing uncertainty is emphasized across various fields, including robotics, medical analysis, and earth observation. In medical analysis, for instance, the presence of significant uncertainty in medical data necessitates reliable predictions and confidence values from machine learning models. These models must adapt to distribution shifts due to the confidential nature of medical information and the variability in training data sources.

The paper illustrates how uncertainty can guide decision-making in reinforcement learning frameworks, where an agent's actions and their associated predicted uncertainties influence its behavior and subsequent actions. The discussion underscores the critical role of uncertainty quantification in enhancing trust and minimizing risk in real-world applications of deep learning.

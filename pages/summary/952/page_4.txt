The paper by J. Gawlikowski et al. provides a comprehensive overview of uncertainty in deep neural networks (DNNs), focusing on various aspects of evaluation, real-world applications, and future research directions. The authors categorize their discussion into several key sections, including a review of commonly used evaluation datasets and benchmarks, applications of uncertainty estimates in practical scenarios, and ongoing challenges within the field.

The authors explain that uncertainty representations in neural networks are broadly applicable across different problems, such as classification, regression, and segmentation, with methods like Bayesian Neural Networks (BNNs) and ensembles being versatile tools. They emphasize four crucial steps that contribute to a DNN's prediction with quantified uncertainties: data acquisition, DNN building, inference model application, and the uncertainty modeling process.

Furthermore, the paper identifies five primary sources of uncertainty affecting predictions in DNNs: variability in real-world situations, measurement system errors, errors in DNN architecture specification, and other related factors. This structured approach highlights the complexity of uncertainty in neural network predictions and underscores the need for continued exploration in this area. The authors also provide references for further reading and practical applications of uncertainty estimation methods.

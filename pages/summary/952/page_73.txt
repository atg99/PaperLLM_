The paper presents a comprehensive survey of uncertainty in deep neural networks, emphasizing the significance of understanding and quantifying uncertainty in various applications. It discusses different types of uncertainty, including epistemic and aleatoric uncertainty, and their implications for model performance and decision-making. The authors explore various methods for uncertainty estimation, such as Bayesian approaches, ensemble learning, and Monte Carlo techniques, highlighting their effectiveness in improving model robustness and interpretability.

Furthermore, the paper reviews key literature in the field, detailing advancements in methodologies for uncertainty quantification across diverse domains including medical imaging, remote sensing, and toxicology prediction. The survey underscores the importance of model uncertainty in enhancing the reliability of deep learning systems, particularly in high-stakes environments where mispredictions can have significant consequences.

Overall, the paper serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners aiming to incorporate uncertainty estimation into deep learning frameworks, providing insights into existing techniques and future research directions.

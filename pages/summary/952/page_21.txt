In summary, various methods have been developed to address uncertainty in deep neural networks, both through modifications to the network architecture and through external frameworks. Techniques such as the inhibited softmax function, Radial Basis Function networks, interval neural networks, and quantile regression loss functions have shown promise in improving accuracy and uncertainty estimation. Additionally, external approaches that separate uncertainty quantification from prediction tasks have been proposed, emphasizing the importance of unbiased uncertainty estimation. Overall, these advancements contribute to better handling of uncertainty in neural networks, particularly in challenging scenarios such as out-of-distribution detection and regression tasks.

The paper presents a comprehensive survey on uncertainty in deep neural networks, specifically focusing on Bayesian neural networks (BNNs), ensembles, single deterministic models, and test-time data augmentation methods. It highlights the comparative performance of these approaches for various tasks such as regression, calibration, out-of-distribution (OOD) detection, adversarial attacks, active learning, continual learning, and reinforcement learning. 

The authors note that BNNs are evaluated across a broader range of tasks but lack extensive testing on large datasets like ImageNet. A table is provided that organizes the benchmark approaches, the tasks they are evaluated on, the datasets used, and the common baselines employed in the studies. This detailed overview aims to clarify the landscape of existing methods and their effectiveness in handling uncertainty in deep learning tasks.

Key methodologies discussed include softmax, Monte Carlo dropout, deep ensembles, and various Bayesian approaches. The survey emphasizes the importance of understanding uncertainty in neural networks to improve model reliability and robustness in real-world applications.

The paper discusses the influence of the validation dataset's distribution and size on the calibration results of neural networks. It emphasizes that neural network uncertainty estimation methods can improve confidence predictions by reducing model uncertainty, thus yielding better-calibrated predictors. These methods include Bayesian approaches and deep ensembles.

The paper elaborates on three types of calibration methods, starting with regularization methods. These methods adjust the training of deep neural networks (DNNs) by altering the objective function or augmenting the training dataset. The aim is to minimize model uncertainty, aligning with earlier methods that quantify model and data uncertainty separately during a single forward pass. The distinction lies in the focus of calibration methods on regularization to enhance prediction accuracy.

Overall, the paper provides a comprehensive overview of calibration techniques essential for improving the reliability of neural network predictions.

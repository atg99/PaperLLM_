The paper presents a compilation of references related to advancements in deep learning, uncertainty quantification, and Bayesian methods within various applications, including computer vision and remote sensing. Key contributions are highlighted, such as adaptive learning rate scheduling, monocular visual odometry, and methods for uncertainty calibration in neural networks. The works cited explore innovative approaches like auto-ensemble techniques, stochastic Hamiltonian Monte Carlo, and maximum classifier discrepancy for out-of-distribution detection. The authors, affiliated with institutions like the German Aerospace Center and the Technical University of Munich, emphasize the importance of these methodologies in enhancing model reliability and accuracy in predictive tasks. The paper maintains a focus on the relevance of uncertainty estimation in deep learning systems, suggesting a growing interest in improving model robustness and interpretability.

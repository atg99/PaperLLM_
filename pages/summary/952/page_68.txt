The referenced paper by Gawlikowski et al. provides a comprehensive overview of various advancements in deep learning, specifically focusing on methods that enhance predictive accuracy and uncertainty estimation in neural networks. Key contributions from multiple studies are highlighted, emphasizing techniques such as Bayesian neural networks, attention-based ensembles, and variational dropout.

The paper also discusses strategies to mitigate overconfidence in neural networks and the challenges posed by distribution shifts in real-world applications.

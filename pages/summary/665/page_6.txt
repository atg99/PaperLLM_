The paper discusses the performance of the Inception Recurrent Convolutional Neural Network (IRCNN) on the CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100 datasets. The experiments utilized both data augmentation and various optimization techniques, specifically Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) combined with Layer-sequential Unfolding of Variances (LSUV) and the EVE optimization method.

For CIFAR-10, the IRCNN achieved an error rate of 8.41% without data augmentation and 7.37% with data augmentation using SGD. When employing LSUV and EVE, the error rates improved to 8.17% and 7.11%, respectively. These results surpassed those of many existing deep convolutional neural network (DCNN) models, demonstrating the effectiveness of the proposed approach.

In the CIFAR-100 experiments, the IRCNN also exhibited strong performance, with testing errors of 34.13% without data augmentation and 31.22% with data augmentation using SGD. When using LSUV and EVE, the errors dropped to 30.87% and 28.24% on the augmented dataset. The model achieved a recognition accuracy of 71.76% with LSUV and EVE, marking a 3.51% improvement over previous models like RCNN.

Overall, the results indicate that the proposed IRCNN model outperforms various advanced and hybrid models in both datasets, highlighting its potential for object classification tasks. The paper includes figures illustrating training and validation accuracy and loss for both datasets, reinforcing the model's effectiveness.

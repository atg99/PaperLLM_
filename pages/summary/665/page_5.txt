4.2.3. CIFAR-100
CIFAR-100, an extension of CIFAR-10, contains 60,000 32x32 color images across 100 classes. The IRCNN was trained similarly, resulting in a test error of around 27.12% with SGD, which improved to about 25.46% when utilizing the LSUV and EVE methods. 

4.2.4. SVHN
The Street View House Numbers (SVHN) dataset consists of 10 classes of digit recognition from real-world images. The IRCNN attained a test error of 2.23% with SGD and further decreased to 2.01% using the LSUV and EVE optimization approach.

In summary, the proposed Inception Recurrent Convolutional Neural Network (IRCNN) demonstrates strong performance across various benchmark datasets, outperforming traditional models and showcasing the effectiveness of advanced training techniques. The results indicate that proper weight initialization and optimization strategies significantly enhance the accuracy of deep learning models.

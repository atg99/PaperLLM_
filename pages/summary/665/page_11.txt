The paper discusses advancements in deep learning architectures, particularly focusing on the Inception Recurrent Convolutional Neural Network (IR-CNN). This model integrates the strengths of both Inception modules and recurrent neural networks to enhance visual tracking and captioning tasks. The authors reference significant prior works that contributed to the development of robust feature hierarchies, such as Wang et al.'s exploration of visual tracking and Xu et al.'s multimodal approaches to deep captioning. The paper also highlights innovations like wide residual networks by Zagoruyko and Komodakis, and stochastic pooling techniques introduced by Zeiler and Fergus, which help in regularizing deep networks. Furthermore, it draws on insights from neuroscience, particularly Zoccolan et al.'s findings on how the brain processes visual object recognition, suggesting that understanding biological systems can inform better artificial neural network designs. Overall, the IR-CNN aims to improve performance in complex visual tasks by leveraging these diverse methodologies.

The paper presents the Inception Recurrent Convolutional Neural Network (IRCNN), which improves upon the Recurrent Convolutional Neural Network (RCNN) architecture proposed by Liang and Hu in 2015. The IRCNN enhances the training process, addressing concerns related to the efficiency of training large-scale deep learning models. Empirical evaluations demonstrate that the IRCNN outperforms both the standard Inception Networks and Inception-Residual Networks. Future work aims to further enhance the model through large-scale implementations using the teacher-student paradigm, known as Net2Net, specifically on the ImageNet dataset. Additionally, the IRCNN will be tested with advanced activation functions like Exponential Linear Units (ELUs). The authors also note the potential of the new architecture to model contextual information in input videos, suggesting a promising direction for future research.

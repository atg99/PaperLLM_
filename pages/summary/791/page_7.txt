The paper discusses parameter quantization, which establishes a data-mapping relationship between fixed-point and floating-point data to enhance performance while minimizing accuracy loss. The quantization process is described through several equations, where S represents the scale factor, Z denotes the zero floating-point value in fixed-point representation, R is the real floating-point number, and Q is the quantized fixed-point value. The key equations include the quantization from floating-point to fixed-point (Q = R/S + Z) and the inverse quantization (R = (Q - Z) * S).

The paper highlights two approaches to quantization: quantization-aware training (QAT) and post-training quantization (PTQ). QAT involves retraining a pre-trained model using training data to adjust parameters and recover from potential accuracy degradation, while PTQ utilizes calibration data to determine the scaling factor and shear range for quantization without retraining.

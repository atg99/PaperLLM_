Anwar et al. address structured pruning, while Ioffe and Szegedy contribute to batch normalization, enhancing training speed.

Han et al. discuss deep compression techniques that combine pruning, quantization, and Huffman coding, while Molchanov et al. propose importance estimation for pruning.

Dynamic pruning strategies are examined by Guo et al., and the lottery ticket hypothesis by Frankle and Carbin investigates sparse, trainable networks. Recent advancements include lightweight object detection networks and white papers on neural network quantization by Nagel et al. and Krishnamoorthi. Techniques for quantized CNNs for mobile devices are presented by Wu et al., while binary neural networks are explored by Courbariaux et al. and Hubara et al., emphasizing resource-efficient inference.

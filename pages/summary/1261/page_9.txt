The table summarizes various machine learning techniques applied to heart disease prediction, highlighting the base learner models, ensemble models, data types, preprocessing techniques, case distributions, dataset attributes, and accuracies of different models.

Key findings include:

2. Ensemble methods like Gradient Boosting, AdaBoost, and Stacking were prominent, with Stacking often yielding the highest accuracy.
3. Data preprocessing techniques varied, including feature selection, random oversampling, and handling missing values.
4. The UCI Cleveland Heart Disease dataset was frequently used, with varying case distributions and instances across studies.
5. The highest reported accuracy was 99% with a Stacking model on the Kaggle Chronic Heart Disease dataset, while other models achieved notable accuracies ranging from 64% to 98.18% depending on the technique used.

Overall, the results indicate that ensemble methods, particularly Stacking, along with effective preprocessing, significantly enhance predictive performance in heart disease classification tasks.

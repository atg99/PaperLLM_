The paper discusses the challenges of disease diagnosis, emphasizing the importance of accurately identifying diseases based on symptoms for effective treatment. It highlights how machine learning can enhance disease prediction through models trained on extensive datasets. The study focuses on common diseases such as diabetes, skin cancer, kidney disease, liver disease, and heart conditions, aiming to explore existing literature on machine learning-based disease prediction models.

The authors note a lack of comprehensive research on ensemble learning techniques—such as bagging, boosting, stacking, and voting—in disease prediction. The study seeks to identify trends in the use of these ensemble approaches, their performance accuracy, and the diseases being examined. By summarizing the advantages and disadvantages of various ensemble methods, the research aims to guide future studies in the field.

Subsequent sections of the paper outline the ensemble methods, analyze scientific materials from 2016 to 2023, and summarize articles that employed at least two ensemble techniques for chronic diseases along with their performance metrics. The findings are intended to provide insights into current trends in disease prediction models using ensemble learning and help set research priorities in this area. Overall, the paper underscores the growing relevance of ensemble learning in improving disease prediction accuracy.

The paper discusses the Voting Classifier, an ensemble method in machine learning that enhances predictive performance by integrating multiple models with varying strengths and weaknesses. This approach increases accuracy, robustness, and model diversity while minimizing bias and variance in predictions. The Voting Classifier employs different voting mechanisms, such as hard voting, soft voting, and weighted voting, allowing customization for specific tasks. It also improves interpretability by analyzing model contributions, aiding in understanding decision-making processes.

The study reviews scientific literature related to five major chronic diseases—heart disease, skin disease, liver disease, kidney disease, and diabetes—examining the algorithms utilized for predictive analytics from 2016 to 2023. The significance of these diseases is emphasized due to their substantial health burden, with statistics indicating high prevalence and mortality rates associated with them. The World Health Organization labels these chronic diseases as an "invisible epidemic," impacting economic development globally. The research involved a thorough literature search across several reputable databases, focusing on publications that utilized classical ensemble methods for disease prediction.

This list presents a collection of references related to advancements in domain adaptation and transfer learning within the field of computer vision and artificial intelligence. Key contributions include methods for adversarial domain adaptation, simultaneous transfer across domains and tasks, and techniques for handling heterogeneous domains. Notable works include Eric Tzeng's research on adversarial discriminative domain adaptation, Chang Wang's approach utilizing manifold alignment, and studies on generative adversarial networks for bridging domain gaps. Various surveys and empirical studies are also included, highlighting the evolving landscape of domain adaptation methodologies. The references span significant conferences and journals, underscoring the collaborative and rapidly advancing nature of this research area.

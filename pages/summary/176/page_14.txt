The referenced papers discuss advancements in generative adversarial networks (GANs) and their applications in various domains. Vondrick and Torralba focus on future prediction using adversarial transformers. Wang et al. introduce tag disentangled GANs for object image re-rendering, enhancing image fidelity by separating tags. Wang et al. also explore shape inpainting through 3D GANs combined with recurrent convolutional networks. 

Wang et al. propose a method for evolutionary compression, while Yao discusses the evolution of artificial neural networks. Young et al. optimize deep learning hyper-parameters using evolutionary algorithms, demonstrating the intersection of evolutionary strategies and deep learning. Yu et al. present a large-scale image dataset construction method that incorporates human feedback in the deep learning process.

Zhang et al. introduce StackGAN for synthesizing photo-realistic images from text descriptions using stacked GANs. Zhang, Gan, and Carin explore text generation through adversarial training, while Zhao et al. propose an energy-based GAN approach. Zhu et al. present cycle-consistent GANs for unpaired image-to-image translation, showcasing the versatility of GANs in generating and transforming visual content.

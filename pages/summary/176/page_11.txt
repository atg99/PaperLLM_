The paper presents an evolutionary GAN framework (E-GAN) aimed at enhancing the training of deep generative models, particularly for generating high-quality human face images. It addresses the challenges of generating realistic facial images by demonstrating that the generator can interpolate between different attributes such as gender and age, indicating a meaningful mapping from latent space to image space. This capability suggests that E-GAN avoids mode collapse and maintains space continuity in generated samples. The proposed evolutionary algorithm allows the population of generators to adapt to the evolving discriminator, improving training stability and overall generative performance compared to traditional GANs. Future research will focus on further exploring the interaction between the discriminator and the generator population to enhance generative outcomes.

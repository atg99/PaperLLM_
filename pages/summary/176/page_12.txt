The paper discusses the process of interpolating in latent space using a well-trained E-GAN model. It involves selecting pairs of generated images, represented by their latent vectors z1 and z2. Linear interpolation between these vectors generates samples that blend the features of the original images. The references cited provide foundational concepts and advancements in generative adversarial networks (GANs) and related fields, including Wasserstein GANs, information maximizing GANs, and evolutionary computation, highlighting the evolution and applications of these techniques in machine learning.

This section discusses the functionality of gate signals in a recurrent neural network (RNN) system, specifically focusing on how they control the blending of state and update signals at each step. The state signal from the previous step, represented as ⃗Fs(⃗ s[n−1]), and the update information, represented as ⃗Fu(⃗ r[n−1], ⃗ x[n]), are combined in equal proportions to form the current state signal ⃗ s[n]. The elements of these gate signals are constrained between 0 and 1, allowing for fine-tuned control over the contributions to the state signal.

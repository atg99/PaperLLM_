The paper discusses the partial derivatives of the objective function E with respect to various parameters of the Augmented LSTM cell, as defined in specific equations. The derivatives are computed for different weights and biases associated with the update, forget, and input gates, as well as the cell state and output. Each parameter's derivative is expressed in terms of the corresponding activation vector α and the transposed input, state, or output vectors.

The total partial derivative of E with respect to the model parameters, denoted as Θ, is organized systematically to reflect the structure of the parameters defined earlier. This arrangement facilitates the understanding of how each parameter contributes to the overall error in the model, which is crucial for optimizing the LSTM cell during training.

The equations illustrate the relationships between the parameters and the gradients, highlighting the complexity of the LSTM architecture and the importance of these gradients for effective learning in neural networks.

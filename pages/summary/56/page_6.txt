This paper discusses the numerical evaluation of nonlinear first-order non-homogeneous delay differential equations (DDEs), specifically focusing on Equations 11, 12, and 14. The approach involves discretizing the equations in time to compute the values of input and state signals through numerical integration. 

Using the backward Euler discretization method, the authors establish a relationship between the state vector s(t) at discrete time samples. The time step is denoted as △T, and the equations are reformulated into a dimensionless form where time is represented by discrete indices (n). This results in a nonlinear first-order non-homogeneous difference equation.

The paper details the transformation of the DDE into a difference equation through the discretization process. It derives a key equation relating the state at the next time sample to the current and previous states, as well as input signals. The authors define a matrix Ws, which plays a crucial role in the transformation of the state space, leading to a recursive relationship for the state vector.

Additionally, the paper notes that the backward Euler method is a stable numerical technique suitable for solving such equations and mentions that it can be extended to include time delay terms without losing generality. Overall, the paper provides a systematic approach to discretizing and solving nonlinear DDEs, laying the groundwork for further analysis and applications.

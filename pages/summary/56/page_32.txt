The paper discusses the advantages of Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks over standard Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) regarding the Gradient Descent training procedure. Section V highlights that the gates in LSTM networks contribute to more stable gradient flow, which is essential for effective training. The paper emphasizes that for gradients to behave well numerically, intermediate and border gradient sequences must maintain a consistent flow of information over time.

Equations presented in the paper illustrate the relationships between different gradient sequences and their dependencies. Specifically, the analysis of how the gradient sequences depend on one another allows for a deeper understanding of how LSTM systems can mitigate gradient decay. The paper derives expressions that show the sensitivity of these sequences to various factors, ultimately leading to insights on how to optimize the training process of LSTM networks. 

The derivations rely on the chain rule and involve calculating the impact of changes in one gradient on another, showcasing the interconnectedness of the components in the LSTM architecture. The results underscore the importance of maintaining well-behaved gradients to enhance the performance of LSTM networks during training.

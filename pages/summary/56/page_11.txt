The paper discusses the sampling of RNN sequences using a defined window of length N, represented by the equation w[n] = u[n] - u[n-N]. The sequence is expressed as a sum of terms involving j(m) and a series of delta functions δ[n-k]. The transformation of indices leads to a rectangular window representation for wm[n], showing that wm[n] consists of K samples. 

The extraction of a segment from the ground truth output sequence v[n] is performed by multiplying v[n] by wm[n], resulting in a subsequence vm[n] that retains non-zero values only within the specified range of indices defined by j(m). This aligns with previously established equations. 

Furthermore, the paper introduces an invertible map Q that relates the readout signals of the RNN system to observable output signals y[n]. An objective function L is defined to quantify the deviation of the observable output from the desired ground truth outputs over the entire range of indices. Lastly, all parameters of the standard RNN system are concisely denoted by a single symbol, Θ, which encompasses weights and biases.

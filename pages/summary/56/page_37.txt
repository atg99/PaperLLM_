The paper discusses the Augmented LSTM cell, detailing its parameters and structure through a series of equations. The parameters are consolidated into a comprehensive set denoted as Θ, which includes various weight matrices and bias vectors. The weights are categorized based on their respective functionalities, such as updating, controlling, and forgetting mechanisms within the LSTM framework.

The Augmented LSTM cell's inference phase, or forward pass, is defined through these parameters, which are critical for the cell's operation. A note is made on the omission of bias parameters in the accompanying figure for simplicity; however, it is clarified that these can be incorporated into the model by modifying the input signal vector. 

Additionally, the paper illustrates the non-causal context windows utilized in the Augmented LSTM, which are executed through convolutions of the input signal with matrix-valued linear filters. This approach allows for the computation of specific quantities essential for the model's performance.

The paper effectively combines theoretical and practical elements to present a clear understanding of the Augmented LSTM cell's design and functionality.

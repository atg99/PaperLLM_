The section discusses the process of unrolling or unfolding Recurrent Neural Network (RNN) cells as defined by specific equations. Initially, the state signal is not computed, and by setting initial conditions, the RNN can be numerically evaluated over a range of discrete steps. This unfolding process allows the RNN to incorporate contributions from previous states and inputs, making it an Infinite Impulse Response (IIR) system. 

Additionally, the text introduces vector-valued functions: the unit step function and the unit sample function. The unit step function is defined for non-negative indices and zero for negative indices, while the unit sample function serves as a discrete impulse, being one at n equals zero and zero otherwise. These functions are visually represented to aid understanding.

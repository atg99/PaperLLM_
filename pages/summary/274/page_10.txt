The cited works span various advancements in video understanding, object detection, face recognition, and deep learning architectures. 

1. "Moments in Time Dataset" by Fan et al. presents a dataset of one million videos aimed at enhancing event understanding in video analysis.
2. Paszke et al. discuss the implementation of automatic differentiation in PyTorch, a crucial feature for developing deep learning models.
3. Ren et al. introduce Faster R-CNN, which significantly improves real-time object detection through the use of region proposal networks.
4. Ryoo et al. propose Assemblenet for optimizing multi-stream neural connectivity in video architectures.
5. Schroff et al. present FaceNet, which offers a unified embedding approach for face recognition and clustering.
6. Sevilla-Lara et al. explore methods for discovering temporal data to improve temporal modeling in computer vision.
7. Simonyan and Zisserman detail the architecture of very deep convolutional networks for large-scale image recognition.
8. Taigman et al. describe DeepFace, a system that achieves near-human-level performance in face verification tasks.
9. Tan and Le introduce EfficientNet, focusing on model scaling for convolutional neural networks.
10. Touvron et al. discuss techniques for training data-efficient image transformers through attention.
11. Tran et al. demonstrate the effectiveness of 3D convolutional networks in learning spatiotemporal features.
12. Vaswani et al. present the transformer model, emphasizing the importance of attention mechanisms in deep learning.
13. Wang et al. propose Temporal Segment Networks for improving deep action recognition practices.
14. Wang et al. investigate non-local neural networks for enhanced feature representation.
15. Wu et al. detail the use of long-term feature banks for comprehensive video understanding.
16. Zhou et al. present an end-to-end approach for dense video captioning using a masked transformer.

These contributions collectively enhance the fields of computer vision and machine learning, particularly in video analysis and recognition tasks.

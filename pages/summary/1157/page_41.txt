The paper provides a comprehensive review of various strategies for selecting training sets for support vector machines (SVMs), emphasizing the importance of effective data selection in improving model performance. It discusses a range of methodologies, including genetic algorithms, clustering techniques, and convex hull approaches, that have been proposed to optimize the selection process. Key contributions from the literature are highlighted, including works by Lessmann et al. on genetic algorithms for model selection, and Li et al. on critical pattern selection based on geometrical and statistical information. The review also examines advanced techniques like hierarchical classification and large-scale image classification, showcasing the evolution of SVM training methods. The paper concludes by noting the ongoing research efforts aimed at refining training set selection, underlining the critical role it plays in enhancing SVM efficiency and accuracy.

This paper reviews methods for selecting training sets for support vector machines (SVMs), a widely used supervised classifier in various applications. Despite their effectiveness, SVMs face significant challenges due to high training time and memory complexity, which are exacerbated by the increasing volume of data generated in many domains. The authors categorize existing data selection techniques, providing insights into their underlying principles, which can inform the development of new methods to enhance practical SVM utilization. Additionally, the review discusses potential future research directions to improve SVM applicability in the context of big data, addressing issues related to data variety, volume, velocity, and veracity.

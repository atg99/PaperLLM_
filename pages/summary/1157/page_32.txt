The paper discusses the use of various benchmark datasets for evaluating algorithms, specifically in the context of training set selection. It presents a summary of frequently used datasets, detailing their features, types, and class distributions. Notable datasets include Adult, Breast, Bupa, German, Ionosphere, Iris, Mushroom, Sonar, Wisconsin, and Yeast, each with a varying number of features and classes. The authors highlight that while many benchmark datasets are utilized for practical applications, their sizes are generally smaller than those encountered in real-world situations. The focus on 2D datasets, including artificially generated chessboard examples, showcases the diversity of data used in algorithm testing.

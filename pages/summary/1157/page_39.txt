The paper provides a comprehensive review of methods for selecting training sets in support vector machines (SVMs), emphasizing the importance of training set quality in enhancing model performance. It discusses various strategies and algorithms, including genetic algorithms, for optimizing parameters and improving classification accuracy. The review highlights key studies that explore diverse applications, such as image segmentation, dispute classification, and handling imbalanced data. 

Notable contributions include the use of evolutionary approaches for parameter tuning and sampling strategies aimed at minimizing label noise. The authors reference significant works that introduce innovative techniques like randomized sampling and active learning strategies, which help in efficiently managing large datasets. 

The paper underscores the evolving nature of SVM applications across different domains and the ongoing efforts to refine training set selection processes to achieve better predictive outcomes. Overall, it serves as a critical resource for researchers and practitioners aiming to enhance SVM performance through effective training set selection.

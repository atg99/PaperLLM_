This paper provides a comprehensive review of the various methodologies employed in selecting training sets for support vector machines (SVMs). It discusses the significance of training set selection as a critical factor influencing the performance of SVM classifiers. The review synthesizes findings from multiple studies, highlighting techniques such as neuro-fuzzy systems, evolutionary algorithms, and model selection strategies aimed at improving SVM efficiency and accuracy.

Key concepts include the use of Gaussian kernel functions, active learning applications in image and text classification, and the importance of optimizing training data to enhance predictive performance. The paper also addresses the challenges posed by outliers and label contamination, suggesting robust methods for mitigating these issues.

Overall, the review emphasizes the necessity of effective training set selection in the context of SVMs, providing insights into various approaches and their experimental evaluations. It serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners seeking to improve SVM classification through informed training set selection strategies.

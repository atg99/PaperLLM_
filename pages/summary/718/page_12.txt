The paper presents an optimization approach for the Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) algorithm, focusing specifically on scaling observations to improve performance. The key procedure outlined is INITIALIZE-SCALING, which initializes the running statistics to track the mean and standard deviation of rewards. The SCALE-OBSERVATION procedure takes a reward input and computes a discounted reward that is then added to the running statistics. Finally, it returns the scaled reward based on the standard deviation calculated from the running statistics. This optimization aims to enhance the stability and efficiency of the PPO algorithm during training.

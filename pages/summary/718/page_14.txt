The paper presents a comparative analysis of reinforcement learning algorithms, specifically Trust Region Policy Optimization (TRPO), Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), and a modified version of PPO (PPO-M), using the MuJoCo physics engine tasks: Humanoid-v2, Walker2d-v2, and Hopper-v2. 

Key findings include the performance metrics such as mean reward, maximum ratio, and KL divergence. The results illustrate that the mean reward for the algorithms varies across iterations, with PPO and PPO-M generally demonstrating competitive or superior performance compared to TRPO. The maximum ratio constraint in PPO is illustrated by a black dotted line, indicating adherence to the desired limits. The mean KL divergence for TRPO remains consistently within the prescribed trust region, showcasing its stability in maintaining policy updates.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the effectiveness of PPO and its modifications in achieving high performance while managing constraints, suggesting their potential advantages in practical applications of reinforcement learning.

In this paper, the authors examine the significant role of code-level optimizations in deep reinforcement learning algorithms, specifically focusing on Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) and Trust Region Policy Optimization (TRPO). They argue that these optimizations, often perceived as minor implementation details, substantially influence agent performance, particularly in PPO's cumulative reward compared to TRPO. The study reveals that these optimizations are responsible for a notable portion of PPO's success and alter the operational dynamics of reinforcement learning methods. The authors emphasize the need for a deeper understanding of how various components of deep RL algorithms affect training outcomes, as existing methods demonstrate issues related to reliability and reproducibility. Through a case study and ablation experiments, the paper highlights the importance of these optimizations, advocating for a reevaluation of algorithmic structures in deep RL to enhance performance consistency.

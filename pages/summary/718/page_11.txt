The paper presents hyperparameter settings for various reinforcement learning algorithms applied to the Humanoid-v2 and Hopper-v2 environments. For both environments, the algorithms compared include Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), Trust Region Policy Optimization (TRPO), and their variants.

For the Humanoid-v2 environment, key hyperparameters include a discount factor of 0.99, GAE discount values ranging from 0.85 to 0.95, and a consistent number of timesteps per iteration set at 2048 across all algorithms. The learning rates for the value network vary slightly between the algorithms, with values like 0.0001 for PPO and 0.0003 for TRPO. The policy network and value network both utilize two hidden layers of 64 units. Notably, TRPO employs KL constraints and conjugate gradient methods, while PPO uses clipping with a parameter of 0.2.

In the Hopper-v2 environment, similar settings are observed, with a consistent discount factor and timesteps per iteration. Adjustments are made in the GAE discount and learning rates for the value network, which are 0.00025 for PPO and 0.0002 for TRPO, among others. The paper also highlights differences in reward clipping ranges and gradient clipping norms between the algorithms.

Overall, the paper provides a comprehensive overview of the hyperparameters utilized for effective training of reinforcement learning models in these environments, emphasizing the importance of careful tuning to achieve optimal performance.

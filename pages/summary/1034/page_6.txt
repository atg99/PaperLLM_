The paper discusses the inherent tensions within AI ethics and emphasizes the need for frameworks that recognize and address these conflicts. It argues that effective AI ethics must provide guidance on reconciling differing high-level goals and accommodate diverse perspectives to reflect genuine consensus. By focusing on these tensions, researchers can better identify ambiguities and gaps in the application of AI, thereby directing future research priorities. The paper suggests that reframing ethical inquiries to concentrate on resolving these tensions is crucial for developing practical solutions that enhance the beneficial use of AI in society.

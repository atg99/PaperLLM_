The paper presents a novel approach for Arabic Sign Language (ArASL) recognition using deep neural networks, specifically through the fine-tuning of well-known convolutional neural networks (CNNs). The study utilizes the ArASL dataset, applying random under-sampling to address class imbalance, effectively reducing the dataset size from 54,049 to 25,600 images. This method achieved impressive validation accuracies of 99.4% with VGG16 and 99.6% with ResNet152.

The authors emphasize the significant potential of leveraging Arabic sign language imaging for classification and highlight the innovative aspect of their work, as it is the first known instance of employing fine-tuning techniques in the context of Arabic Sign Language recognition. The results indicate high accuracy, suggesting that the approach is reliable for practical applications in this field. Overall, the paper contributes to the advancement of deep learning in sign language recognition, particularly within the Arabic context.

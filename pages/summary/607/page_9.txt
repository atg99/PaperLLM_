The paper discusses the recognition of Arabic Sign Language using deep neural networks, specifically focusing on the fine-tuning of the ResNet152 and VGG16 models. The experimentation involved training both models over 100 epochs, with detailed outputs provided for each epoch. The ResNet152 model achieved a validation accuracy of 99% by the 20th epoch, ultimately reaching a peak accuracy of 99.65% by the 95th epoch.

The paper "Arabic Sign Language Recognition Through Deep Neural Networks Fine-Tuning" explores the application of deep learning techniques to recognize Arabic sign language. The authors focus on fine-tuning deep neural networks to enhance the accuracy of sign language recognition systems. They discuss the importance of utilizing advanced machine learning methodologies to improve performance in this field, emphasizing the potential for these systems to aid communication for the hearing-impaired community.

The authors reference relevant literature that contributes to their research, including works on Bengali sign language recognition and deep residual learning for image recognition. They highlight the significance of data augmentation in training deep learning models, which is a critical factor in achieving robust performance.

The paper is authored by Yaser Saleh, an Assistant Professor at the University of Petra with a background in artificial intelligence and image processing, and Ghassan Issa, a Professor of Computer Science at the same institution with expertise in machine learning and e-learning systems. The article was submitted in January 2020, with subsequent revisions leading to its final acceptance in March 2020. 

Overall, the paper contributes to the growing body of research on sign language recognition and the application of deep learning technologies in enhancing accessibility for individuals with hearing impairments.

This paper discusses a model designed for recognizing Arabic Sign Language using deep neural networks, specifically fine-tuning pre-trained models like VGG-16 and ResNet152. The results show that ResNet152 consistently outperformed VGG-16 across various epochs, achieving a validation accuracy of 99.57% compared to VGG-16's 99.26% by the 100th epoch. 

The study highlights the effectiveness of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) in image recognition, emphasizing their ability to achieve high accuracy while reducing the dataset size needed for training. The authors suggest that this model could have broader applications, including in sign language translation and sound techniques, thereby contributing to advancements in related research areas. Overall, the findings illustrate the potential of deep learning in enhancing Arabic Sign Language recognition.

The paper discusses the recognition of Arabic Sign Language using deep neural networks, focusing on the ResNet152 model structure. The dataset employed for evaluation is the ArASL, which consists of 54,049 images across 32 classes of Arabic signs, standardized to dimensions of 64 x 64 pixels. However, the dataset suffers from class imbalance, with varying numbers of images per class. To address this issue, an under-sampling technique is utilized to reduce the size of the majority classes, resulting in an evenly distributed dataset of 25,600 images, with 800 images per class.

Additionally, the paper highlights the importance of data augmentation, which generates new data sets from existing images to enhance diversity during training. This process not only aids in building a more robust classification network but also mitigates the risk of overfitting, particularly when the training dataset is limited. The authors reference research showing that data augmentation can significantly improve the accuracy of deep convolutional neural networks by applying various augmentation techniques.

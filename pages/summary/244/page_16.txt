The paper discusses the performance evaluation of a proposed DenseNet-based malware detection model using various metrics, including Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC) curves. The ROC curves, which illustrate the relationship between the True Positive Rate (TPR) and the False Positive Rate (FPR) at different classification thresholds, are presented for three training malware datasets, notably including the Malimg dataset with 26 classes. Each curve represents the classification performance of one class against all others, showing that TPR approaches one and FPR remains near zero across the classes. Furthermore, the area under the ROC curve is significantly higher for the Malimg and BIG2015 datasets compared to the MaleVis dataset, indicating superior detection efficiency of the proposed model. Additionally, a confusion matrix for the Malimg dataset is provided to further analyze classification performance.

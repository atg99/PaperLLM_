The paper presents an evaluation of a DenseNet-based malware detection system using three binary classification datasets: Malimg, BIG2015, and MaleVis. Each dataset comprised 1000 malware samples and 1043 cleanware samples. The results indicate that the system achieved the highest detection accuracy on the BIG2015 dataset at 97.72%, followed by 97.55% for Malimg and 96.81% for MaleVis. Additionally, metrics such as precision, recall, and F-score were notably higher for the BIG2015 dataset compared to the others. The paper includes Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC) curves to visualize the performance across these datasets.

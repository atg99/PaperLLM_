The paper presents a comprehensive evaluation of scene parsing results on the Pascal Context and ADE20K validation sets, adhering to standard benchmarks by excluding background pixels for metric calculations. It discusses the use of the Pascal VOC and Cityscapes datasets for semantic segmentation, utilizing public evaluation servers.

In the section on ImageNet pretraining, the authors modify the standard ResNet architecture by replacing the initial convolution with three 3x3 convolutions. They report Top-1 and Top-5 accuracy metrics on the ImageNet validation set and indicate that pretrained models will be available in their public code system.

An ablation study on the Pascal Context dataset, which contains semantic labels for 4,998 training and 5,105 validation images, is detailed. The study examines the performance of CFNet compared to the baseline Fully Convolutional Network (FCN). The CFNet shows improvements in pixel accuracy and mean Intersection over Union (mIoU) through the addition of the ACF Module and global pooling features. The optimal configuration for the Aggregated Co-occurrent Feature Module is explored, identifying that a mixture count of K=2 and multi-head count of H=4 yields the best performance.

State-of-the-art comparisons reveal that CFNet outperforms many previous methods, achieving a mIoU of 54.0% on the validation set using ResNet-101, even without deeper networks or COCO pretraining. 

Results from the Pascal VOC 2012 dataset indicate that CFNet trained on augmented data achieves an mIoU of 84.2% on the test set, surpassing prior methods without COCO pretraining. Further pretraining on the COCO dataset allows CFNet to reach an mIoU of 87.2%.

In the ADE20K dataset evaluation, CFNet improves upon the baseline FCN, achieving a mIoU of 39.28% and demonstrating its effectiveness in large-scale scene parsing tasks. Overall, the findings underscore CFNet's strong performance in semantic segmentation across multiple datasets and configurations.

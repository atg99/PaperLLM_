The paper presents a novel Co-occurrent Feature Model (CFNet) aimed at enhancing semantic segmentation performance by effectively capturing and utilizing co-occurrent features across an image. The model is evaluated on multiple datasets, including PASCAL VOC 2012, ADE20K, and Cityscapes, demonstrating superior results compared to existing methods.

In the PASCAL VOC 2012 testing set, CFNet achieves a mean Intersection over Union (mIoU) of 84.2% without pre-training and 87.2% with pre-training on the COCO dataset, outperforming other approaches. Notable models compared include DeepLabv2, CRF-RNN, and PSPNet, with CFNet consistently achieving higher mIoU scores across various classes.

For the ADE20K validation set, CFNet also excels, achieving 44.89% mIoU, surpassing all previous methods using the same base network. The results indicate that CFNet captures semantic dependencies more effectively than traditional models like FCN, which rely on local feature representations.

In the Cityscapes dataset evaluation, CFNet achieves 79.60% mIoU on the test set using only fine-label data, indicating its robustness in high-resolution scene parsing.

The paper concludes that the proposed Co-occurrent Feature Model and the Aggregated Co-occurrent Feature Module significantly improve semantic segmentation by leveraging contextual information, setting a new benchmark for future research in the field.

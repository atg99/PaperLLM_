The paper presents wav2vec, an innovative approach to unsupervised pre-training for speech recognition utilizing a fully convolutional model. The results indicate a word error rate (WER) of 2.43% on the WSJ test set, surpassing the performance of existing character-based models, specifically the one by Amodei et al. in 2016, while requiring significantly less transcribed training data. The study demonstrates that increasing the amount of data for pre-training enhances the model's performance, benefiting both resource-limited scenarios and those utilizing the complete WSJ training dataset. The authors express intentions to explore different architectures to further boost performance in future work. Acknowledgments are given to the Speech team at FAIR for their contributions to the wav2letter++ experiments and for providing convolutional language models. The paper cites several related works that inform the development and context of wav2vec.

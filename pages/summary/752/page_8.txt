The paper discusses advancements in handwritten text recognition (HTR) through the proposed model that integrates language resources and external memory modules. This model aims to enhance online adaptation and manage rare training examples effectively. It suggests that incorporating an expandable external memory module could improve model performance without increasing the number of trained parameters. Additionally, the Seq2Seq model could be combined with a pretrained language model or the decoder could be pretrained separately before integration with an encoder. However, the effectiveness of directly integrating a language model into the decoder compared to using Connectionist Temporal Classification (CTC) and a conventional language model is yet to be determined.

The acknowledgments indicate that the research was partially funded by the European Union’s Horizon 2020 program under specific grant agreements related to the recognition and enrichment of archival documents and news analysis. The references list includes a comprehensive range of studies and methodologies relevant to HTR, neural networks, and attention-based models, highlighting significant contributions to the field.

The paper discusses optimization studies for the I-V characteristics of monocrystalline Shell SQ85 and thin film Shell ST40 solar cells using a single-diode model. The optimized parameters for the SQ85 cell are detailed in Appendix Table 5 A.1, revealing that multiple runs produced optimal parameters leading to zero error at three critical I-V points. Figure 6 illustrates the 30 I-V characteristics, all of which intersect the defined points. The scatter of optimal solutions is depicted in Figure 7, showing the diode ideality factor varying between 1 and 1.9, and the parallel resistance ranging from 75 to 160 ohms. For the ST40 thin film cell, optimal solutions are listed in Appendix Table A.2, with corresponding I-V characteristics displayed in Figure 8 and a 3D scatter of solutions in Figure 9. The I-V characteristics for the ST40 cell demonstrate a broader spread, attributed to its limited current capability compared to the voltage capabilities of series-connected cells.

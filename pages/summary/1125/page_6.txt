The paper presents a detailed analysis of photovoltaic (PV) cell performance through mathematical equations and numerical data. It begins with equations (14) to (19) that describe the relationship between various electrical parameters, such as the short circuit current (ISC), open circuit voltage (VOC), and maximum power point (VMPP, IMPP) under standard test conditions (STC). The equations highlight the role of reverse saturation currents (IO1, IO2) and resistances (RS, RP) in determining the overall current output of the PV cells.

Numerical data is provided for three types of solar cell modules: polycrystalline, monocrystalline, and thin film, with specific parameters like VOC, ISC, VMPP, IMPP, and temperature coefficients listed in Table 1. These parameters are crucial for understanding the efficiency and performance of different solar cell technologies under set conditions of temperature and irradiance.

Furthermore, the paper introduces the Linear Population Reduction Technique of Success History Based Adaptive Differential Evolution (L-SHADE) algorithm. This section emphasizes the advantages of using adaptive control parameters in differential evolution for optimization tasks, which can significantly enhance performance compared to fixed parameter settings. The method relies on historical success rates to adjust parameters dynamically, improving the algorithm's effectiveness in solving complex optimization problems. 

Overall, the paper combines theoretical equations with empirical data to explore PV cell performance and optimization techniques, aiming to enhance the efficiency and reliability of solar energy systems.

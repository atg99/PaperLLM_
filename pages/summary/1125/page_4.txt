The paper is organized into several sections, starting with circuit analyses of single-diode and double-diode models of photovoltaic (PV) modules in Section 2. Section 3 introduces the L-SHADE algorithm, while Section 4 describes its objective function and applications. Section 5 presents simulation results and detailed analyses, followed by a comparison with other metaheuristic algorithms in Section 6. The paper concludes with remarks in Section 7.

In Section 2, the mathematical model and numerical data are discussed, focusing on the equivalent circuits of ideal PV cells and modules. The ideal PV cell model is represented by an output current equation that incorporates the photovoltaic current and diode current. The diode current is defined by the Shockley diode equation, leading to the I-V characteristic of the ideal cell. Additionally, the section explores the single-diode model of PV modules, detailing the I-V characteristic for a module composed of multiple series-connected cells.

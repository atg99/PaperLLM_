The paper discusses the variability in I-V characteristics of solar cells, attributing differences to the series resistance (RS), parallel resistance (RP), and the diode ideality factor (a). It presents data showing a wide range of values for these parameters across 30 optimal solutions, each capable of achieving zero error at key operational points: open circuit, short circuit, and maximum power. The findings suggest that while there is a defined I-V characteristic for a solar cell linked to a specific set of parameters, it can appear non-unique when only these three points are considered. The paper raises the question of whether multiple I-V characteristics exist for a solar cell, concluding that a single characteristic can be identified only with sufficient experimental data beyond the three characteristic points. The accuracy of parameter estimation improves with more data points, indicating that for precise modeling, additional I-V data is crucial, rather than relying solely on the three key points typically provided in datasheets.

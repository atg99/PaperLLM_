The tables presented provide optimal parameters for different photovoltaic (PV) cell models, including thin-film and monocrystalline cells. 

Table A.2 details the optimal parameters for a single-diode model of the thin-film PV cell, Shell ST40. It includes runs from 1 to 30, with variables such as series resistance (RS), parallel resistance (RP), reverse saturation current (IO), and the current under photovoltaic conditions (IPV). The error (ERR) values are also noted, with several runs showing negligible error.

Table A.3 outlines the optimal parameters for a double-diode model of the monocrystalline PV cell, Shell SQ85. This table also lists runs from 1 to 4, detailing variables such as the two ideality factors (a1 and a2), series resistance (RS), parallel resistance (RP), and the two reverse saturation currents (IO1 and IO2), along with the photovoltaic current (IPV). 

Both tables emphasize the calculated optimal variables and their respective errors, illustrating the performance characteristics of the PV cells under different configurations. The data suggests a focus on minimizing error for improved efficiency in PV cell designs.

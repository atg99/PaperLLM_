The paper discusses the limitations of estimating the I-V characteristics of polycrystalline photovoltaic (PV) cells using only short circuit, open circuit, and maximum power points. It highlights that such optimization can yield varying characteristics across different trials. 

A table is presented with data on optimal parameters for the double-diode model of the Kyocera KC200GT PV cell. The table includes run numbers, optimized variables (a1, a2, RS, RP, I01, I02), calculated optimal variables, and the error (ERR) for each run. 

The results show a range of optimized variables across 30 runs, with errors consistently reported as zero, indicating precise calculations. Figure 10 illustrates 30 probable I-V characteristics derived from the double-diode model for the KC200GT cell, emphasizing the variability that can arise from different optimization trials. 

Overall, the findings suggest that while the three-point estimation method is useful, it does not guarantee a unique I-V characteristic for the PV cells.

The paper presents a qualitative evaluation of various generative models, including Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), Wasserstein Autoencoders (WAEs), and Robust Autoencoders (RAEs), on the MNIST dataset. The evaluation includes three main aspects: reconstructed samples, randomly generated samples, and spherical interpolations between two images. The results are displayed in a figure where the top row shows the ground truth images, followed by the reconstructions from each model, random samples generated by the models, and interpolations. This comparison highlights the differences in sample quality and reconstruction fidelity among the different generative approaches, providing insights into their performance.

The paper evaluates various generative models using Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) to assess reconstruction quality and generative capabilities. The models include Variational Autoencoders (VAE), Conditional VAE (CV-VAE), Wasserstein Autoencoders (WAE), and Regularized Autoencoders (RAE) with different configurations. The evaluation setup involves computing FID scores between reconstructions from a validation set and a test set, as well as between test data and samples from different Gaussian distributions. 

For precision and recall evaluation, the models are assessed across several datasets: MNIST, CIFAR-10, and CelebA, with results indicating that the proposed ex-post density estimation enhances both precision and recall metrics. Notably, the Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM) consistently yields the best results across all datasets. The findings highlight that while some models exhibit low precision, they maintain high recall, particularly on CIFAR-10, suggesting a trade-off in performance that warrants further investigation. Overall, the study emphasizes the importance of using GMMs for improved evaluation of generative model quality.

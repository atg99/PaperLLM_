The paper presents a comparison of various generative models applied to the MNIST dataset, including Variational Autoencoders (VAE), Conditional Variational Autoencoders (CV-VAE), Wasserstein Autoencoders (WAE), and several variants of Regularized Autoencoders (RAE) such as RAE with Gaussian Process (RAE-GP), RAE with L2 regularization (RAE-L2), and RAE with Spectral Normalization (RAE-SN), alongside traditional Autoencoders (AE). 

Figure 5 illustrates the Precision-Recall Density (PRD) curves for these methods, highlighting the performance when utilizing both fixed and fitted density estimation through ex-post density estimation (XPDE). The results indicate that XPDE significantly enhances both precision and recall across all models, demonstrating its effectiveness in improving generative model performance on image data.

The paper presents experimental results on the CIFAR10 dataset, comparing various generative models including Variational Autoencoders (VAE), Conditional Variational Autoencoders (CV-VAE), Wasserstein Autoencoders (WAE), and different variants of Regularized Autoencoders (RAE), specifically RAE-GP, RAE-L2, and RAE-SN. The key findings are illustrated through Precision-Recall Density (PRD) curves, which indicate the performance of each method. The results show that employing ex-post density estimation (XPDE) significantly enhances both precision and recall across all models tested, highlighting the effectiveness of this approach in improving generative model performance.

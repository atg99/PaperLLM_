The references provided in the conference paper at ICLR 2020 cover a wide array of topics central to machine learning, particularly focusing on variational inference, generative models, and adversarial training. Key contributions include the exploration of generative adversarial networks (GANs) and their enhancements, such as the Wasserstein GAN and improvements in training methodologies. The works of Alemi et al. on fixing the evidence lower bound (ELBO) and the introduction of resampled priors for variational autoencoders by Bauer and Mnih are notable for their advancements in variational inference techniques. Other significant studies include the application of autoencoders for generative modeling and the development of methods to resist adversarial attacks using Gaussian mixture variational autoencoders. The references also highlight the importance of diagnostic tools for Markov chain Monte Carlo convergence and the application of deep learning in chemical design. Overall, these references collectively underscore the ongoing advancements and research in the field of generative modeling and variational inference.

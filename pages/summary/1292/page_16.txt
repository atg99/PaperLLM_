The paper presents a comparison of various models in terms of their performance metrics, specifically focusing on Precision-Recall Distribution (PRD) curves and Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) scores. The analysis includes different versions of the Robust Autoencoder (RAE), traditional Variational Autoencoders (VAE), and Wasserstein Autoencoders (WAE), including their Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM) variants.

Key findings indicate that RAE-SN outperforms other RAE methods in both precision and recall, as reflected in the PRD curves. Traditional VAE variants do not show a definitive winner, similar to the FID score results. The WAE model, particularly when enhanced with GMM for ex-post density estimation, exhibits higher recall but lower precision compared to RAE+SN-GMM, despite achieving similar FID scores. This highlights the importance of ex-post density estimation in improving the performance of the WAE model across both PRD and FID metrics.

The paper presents a comparative analysis of various models for density estimation in a 16-dimensional latent space learned from the MNIST dataset. It includes models such as Variational Autoencoder (VAE), Conditional Variational Autoencoder (CV-VAE), Wasserstein Autoencoder (WAE), and several versions of the Regularized Autoencoder (RAE), among others. The results are visualized using T-SNE projections, with blue points representing 2000 test samples and orange points indicating estimates from different density estimators: isotropic Gaussian, multivariate Gaussian, and a 10-component Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM). The findings suggest that post-hoc density estimation significantly enhances the ability to sample from the latent space, indicating the potential for improved generative modeling.

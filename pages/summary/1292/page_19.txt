The paper presents a comparison of various generative models applied to image data experiments on the CELEBA dataset, specifically analyzing the performance of different methods such as VAE, CV-VAE, WAE, and RAE with different configurations (GP, L2, SN). The results are illustrated through PRD curves demonstrating that the use of ex-post density estimation (XPDE) significantly enhances both precision and recall across all models tested. This finding highlights the effectiveness of XPDE in improving model performance in generative tasks.

The paper discusses the effectiveness of ex-post density estimation in reducing the mismatch between the aggregated posterior and the prior in variational autoencoders (VAEs). Using a VAE trained on the MNIST dataset with a two-dimensional latent space, the authors visualize the density of test samples in the latent space through scatterplots. The results, illustrated in figure 11, show that an expressive density estimator significantly improves sample quality by correcting the mismatch observed with traditional isotropic Gaussian priors. The paper presents various density estimators, including isotropic Gaussian, multivariate Gaussian, and a 10-component Gaussian mixture model (GMM), highlighting the limitations of the isotropic Gaussian prior. In figure 12, the authors illustrate similar findings across multiple models trained on MNIST, further emphasizing the advantages of ex-post density estimation in addressing the discrepancy between the aggregate posterior and prior distributions.

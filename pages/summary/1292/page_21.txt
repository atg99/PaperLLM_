The paper presents a qualitative evaluation of various generative models, specifically Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), Wasserstein Autoencoders (WAEs), and Robust Autoencoders (RAEs), on the CIFAR-10 dataset. The evaluation includes three aspects: reconstructed samples, randomly generated samples, and spherical interpolations between two images. The left section of the figure shows the reconstructed samples with the ground truth images on the top row. The middle section displays randomly generated samples, while the right section illustrates spherical interpolations between the first and last images. This comparative analysis aims to assess the sample quality and the effectiveness of each model in generating realistic outputs.

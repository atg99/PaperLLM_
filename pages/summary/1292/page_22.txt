The paper presented at ICLR 2020 investigates overfitting in various generative models, including VAE, CV-VAE, WAE, and RAE variants, using datasets such as MNIST, CIFAR-10, and CelebA. The findings indicate that these models demonstrate good generalization capabilities, particularly when fitting only 10 Gaussians to the latent space, which effectively mitigates overfitting. The analysis includes visualizations of nearest neighbors to generated samples, suggesting that the models are able to learn meaningful representations without succumbing to overfitting.

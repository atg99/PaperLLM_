The paper emphasizes the importance of automated medical image segmentation due to the complexity and variability of medical images. Traditional rule-based methods have largely been replaced by more sophisticated algorithms that incorporate deep learning techniques, particularly Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs). These advancements have significantly improved the segmentation of important regions in medical images, facilitating better diagnosis and analysis. Notably, U-Net has emerged as a leading architecture in the field, demonstrating efficacy in handling the challenges associated with medical image data. The paper outlines the evolution of segmentation methods, highlighting the shift from simple approaches to complex neural networks, and underscores the ongoing need for effective tools in medical image analysis.

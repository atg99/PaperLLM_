This paper presents the architecture details of MultiResUNet, focusing on its multi-resolution and residual pathways. The architecture comprises multiple blocks, each with specific convolutional layers, filter sizes, and filter counts. The first multi-resolution block features layers with filter sizes of 3x3 and 1x1, starting with 8 filters and increasing to 51 filters across subsequent layers. This pattern continues through multiple blocks, with each block generally increasing the number of filters, especially in the residual paths.

The study also discusses the datasets used for evaluating the MultiResUNet architecture. For endoscopy images, the CVC-ClinicDB database was utilized, containing 612 colonoscopy images resized from their original resolution of 384x288 to 256x192. For 3D medical images, the BraTS17 competition database was employed, comprising 210 glioblastoma and 75 lower-grade glioma MRI scans. These scans include various modalities and were resized from 240x240x155 to 80x80x48 for consistency in evaluation.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the architecture's design and the datasets' relevance in assessing its performance in medical imaging tasks.

The paper discusses various open-access datasets utilized in bio/medical imaging, focusing on their applications in disease detection, diagnosis, and neuroimaging. The ADHD 200 dataset aims to classify imaging-based diagnostics for ADHD. The Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI) provides neuroimaging data for Alzheimer's disease and related conditions, while the Open Access Series of Imaging Studies (OASIS) offers MRI datasets for studying Alzheimer's. Neurosynth includes fMRI literature and datasets for analyzing brain structures and functions. The Open Neuroimaging (Open NI) dataset supports research on decision support systems for disease identification.

The COVID-19 pandemic has led to the creation of the NIH nCOV chest X-ray database, which contains chest X-ray and CT images for distinguishing between bacterial, viral, and COVID-19 pneumonia. Additional datasets on chest X-rays are available on platforms like Kaggle.

The paper categorizes various open-access data repositories based on their applications, including bio/medical image processing, disease detection, and neuroimaging. Notable datasets include the Autism Brain Imaging Data Exchange (ABIDE), the Multimodal Brain Imaging of Autism (ADHD-200), and various cancer imaging datasets. The paper concludes by highlighting the significance of these datasets in advancing research and improving diagnostic methods in the medical field.

The paper discusses various biological databases that facilitate research in genomics, proteomics, and drug design. 

1. **PubChem**: A comprehensive database maintained by the National Center for Biotechnology Information, it provides millions of chemical compound structures and their biological activity datasets, supporting studies in genomics, proteomics, and drug design.

2. **Cancer Gene Expression (GE) and TCGA**: These repositories contain datasets for cancer detection and genomic studies, respectively. TCGA includes extensive genomic data for 33 cancer types, aiding in the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of cancer.

3. **Indian Genetic Disease Database (IGDD)**: Focuses on tracking genetic mutations related to diseases in India.

4. **Nucleic Acid Research**: 
   - **BDTNP**: Offers datasets for gene expression and DNA-binding studies.
   - **ENCODE**: Curates a wide range of functional genomics datasets across various organisms.
   - **ESP**: Focuses on genome datasets for lung and blood disorder research.
   - **GEO**: An open-access repository for functional genomic studies.
   - **gnomAD**: Provides large-scale exome and genome sequencing data for genetic studies.
   - **GTEx**: Contains gene expression data from healthy tissues.

5. **Harmonizome**: Compiles extensive gene and protein data from various resources.

6. **International Nucleotide Sequence Database (INSDC)**: Integrates data from major nucleotide databases to provide comprehensive biological data.

7. **IGSR**: Contains genome sequencing data from diverse populations to aid in genetic studies.

8. **JASPAR**: A database for transcription factor DNA-binding profiles across multiple taxonomic groups.

9. **NIH Roadmap Epigenomics Mapping repository (NIHREM)**: Offers datasets related to epigenomic modifications, useful for studying human diseases.

10. **Nature Scientific Data (NSD)**: Includes diverse datasets for various aspects of genomics.

11. **Protein Data Bank (PDB)**: Hosts 3D structural data for proteins and nucleic acids, obtained through advanced imaging techniques.

These databases collectively enhance our understanding of biological processes and support advancements in medical research and treatment strategies.

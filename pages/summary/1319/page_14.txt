The paper discusses various applications in the fields of anomaly detection, human-machine interfacing, and emotion/affective state detection, focusing on the utilization of different physiological signals such as EEG, ECG, and EMG.

In anomaly detection, several open access datasets are highlighted. The SAD mc-EEG dataset contains 32-channel EEG signals from subjects engaged in virtual reality driving, while the TUH EEG corpus provides clinical EEG data for various conditions including seizure detection. The MIT-BIH arrhythmia database and the PTB diagnostic ECG database are significant for studying ECG anomalies, with the former focusing on arrhythmia and the latter encompassing a broader range of ECG recordings. Additionally, the TELE-ECG dataset includes annotated ECG records and algorithms for QRS detection.

Human-machine interfacing applications leverage EMG and EEG signals, with the BNCI Horizon 2020 database offering over 25 datasets for brain-computer interface development. This includes datasets for motor imagery and neuroprosthetic control. The EMG Datasets Repository provides data on finger movements and upper-limb prostheses, while facial sEMG datasets are used for rehabilitation research. The NinaPro database includes both kinematic and sEMG data for studying biorobotics.

Emotion and affective state detection is underscored as a vibrant research area utilizing multimodal datasets. The DEAP database offers EEG and sEMG signals from participants watching music videos, while the DECAF dataset combines various physiological signals to analyze responses to multimedia content. The MAHNOB-HCI dataset synchronizes multiple physiological signals during emotional stimuli. Other datasets like Imagined Emotion and the SJTU Emotion EEG Dataset focus on EEG recordings while subjects engage with different emotional stimuli, further contributing to the understanding of emotional states.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the importance of these datasets in advancing research and applications in anomaly detection, human-machine interfacing, and emotion detection.

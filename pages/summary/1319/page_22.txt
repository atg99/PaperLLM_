The paper presents an analysis of deep learning tools based on search data, academic mentions, community involvement, and interoperability. It begins by examining Google Trends data from January 2015 to December 2019, highlighting a significant increase in searches for TensorFlow since its release, followed by Keras. The authors then analyze around 2,000 papers submitted to arXiv in early 2020, identifying the top six tools mentioned: PyTorch, TensorFlow, Keras, Caffe, MXNet, and Theano.

Community engagement is assessed through GitHub metrics, with a bubble plot illustrating that TensorFlow attracts the most community effort, followed by Keras and Caffe. Interoperability is emphasized, with Keras noted as the most flexible tool, capable of functioning with TensorFlow and Theano, among others. The paper also addresses hardware scalability, indicating that while most tools support CPU and GPU execution, FPGA and ASIC primarily run pretrained models. Overall, the findings underline the popularity and community support for TensorFlow and Keras within the deep learning landscape.

This paper discusses the integration of advanced machine learning (ML) techniques, particularly deep learning (DL), in analyzing various biological data types, including images, signals, and sequences. It highlights the significant contributions of ML methods to diverse biological applications, such as anomaly detection, disease management, and natural language processing, while noting the lack of a comprehensive review focused on pattern recognition in biological data.

The authors argue that understanding the entire ecosystem of modern data analysis—encompassing open access data sources and open-source libraries—is crucial for effectively applying ML techniques. They aim to provide a thorough overview of DL concepts, applications to biological data, available data repositories, and the capabilities of various open-source libraries, along with performance comparisons.

The paper is structured into sections that cover the foundational theory of DL, its applications, and a list of open-source tools. Additionally, it identifies current challenges and offers speculative insights into future developments in this field. The novelty of this review lies in its comprehensive approach to mapping the complete ecosystem of contemporary data analysis using advanced ML techniques.

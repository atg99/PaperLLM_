The paper presents results from experiments on abstractive summarization using the XSum dataset and English-Romanian translation evaluated through BLEU scores. Various methods of tuning models are compared, including Preﬁx Tuning, LoRA, and Sequential and Parallel Adapters. 

Key findings include:

- For Preﬁx Tuning, the model with r=200 achieved 43.40 for R-1, 20.46 for R-2, and 35.51 for the length score, with a BLEU score of 35.6. 
- LoRA with r=102 showed the best performance in the modified representation with FFN, yielding 44.59 for R-1, 21.31 for R-2, and 36.25 for the length score, along with a BLEU score of 36.5.
- Overall, Parallel Adapters with r=1024 had the highest performance in the FFN category, achieving 44.53 for R-1, 21.24 for R-2, and 36.23 for length, with a BLEU score of 37.3.

These results indicate that different tuning strategies and parameter settings significantly impact model performance in summarization and translation tasks.

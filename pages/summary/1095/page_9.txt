The paper presents a comprehensive comparison of various parameter-efficient tuning methods, including a novel approach termed the Mix-And-Match (MAM) Adapter. The results indicate that traditional full fine-tuning, while effective, requires substantial parameter updates, whereas the proposed MAM Adapter achieves comparable performance with only 6.7% of the pre-trained parameters modified.

Table 6 summarizes the performance of several tuning methods on tasks like XSum and machine translation, highlighting that the MAM Adapter outperforms other methods such as LoRA and various combinations of adapters. The findings suggest that the scaling composition function used in the MAM Adapter enhances performance, particularly when combined with a small bottleneck dimension in pre-existing architectures.

The discussion emphasizes the framework's potential to unify and improve upon existing parameter-efficient tuning strategies, thereby enabling effective model performance without extensive parameter adjustments. The authors advocate that their approach can serve as a foundation for future research in parameter-efficient tuning, providing a pathway to more efficient model training methodologies.

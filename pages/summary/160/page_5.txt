The paper evaluates NeRF-VAE against several datasets: GQN, CLEVR, and a custom raytraced dataset called Jaytracer. The GQN dataset consists of scenes without reflections, while CLEVR includes higher visual quality with reflections. The Jaytracer dataset allows for more controlled variations in scene complexity and view distribution. Each dataset comprises 64x64 colored images with associated camera parameters.

NeRF-VAE’s architecture is based on NeRF but incorporates a latent variable for scene representation, enabling it to infer scene structures without retraining. The model employs hierarchical volume sampling for color approximation and uses Adam optimization with KL term annealing.

Results indicate that NeRF-VAE outperforms NeRF in terms of reconstruction errors, particularly when trained with fewer views. The analysis highlights the advantages of parameter sharing and the efficiency of using a latent variable, establishing NeRF-VAE as a robust model for 3D scene generation.

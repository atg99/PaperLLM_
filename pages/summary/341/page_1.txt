The paper investigates the selection of in-context examples to enhance the performance of GPT-3, particularly its few-shot learning capabilities. Despite GPT-3's impressive abilities, the authors note that its performance is sensitive to the choice of in-context examples, with significant variability observed in results based on different selections. 

The study proposes a retrieval-based approach to select semantically similar examples relative to a test sample, aiming to provide more informative inputs for GPT-3. This method is evaluated across various natural language understanding and generation benchmarks, demonstrating consistent improvements over random example selection. Notably, fine-tuning sentence encoders on task-specific datasets further enhances retrieval effectiveness.

Significant performance gains were reported in specific tasks, such as a 41.9% improvement in table-to-text generation on the ToTTo dataset and a 45.5% improvement in open-domain question answering on the NQ dataset. The authors hope that their findings will contribute to a better understanding of GPT-3 and improve the few-shot capabilities of large-scale pre-trained language models.

The paper discusses the selection of in-context examples for enhancing the performance of the GPT-3 model. It highlights the significance of choosing relevant examples based on their proximity in an embedding space, specifically using the k-nearest neighbors (kNN) algorithm. 

The study compares two strategies for selecting in-context examples from the Natural Questions dataset: one that uses the ten farthest training instances and another that employs the ten closest neighbors. The results indicate that using the closest neighbors significantly improves the model's performance, as evidenced by a higher average Exact Match score of 46.0 compared to 31.0 for the farthest examples.

Building on these findings, the paper introduces KATE, a method for selecting effective in-context examples for in-context learning. KATE involves encoding both training and test sources into vector representations and retrieving the nearest neighbors for each test source. This process facilitates the construction of a context that combines the neighbors with their corresponding targets, which is then provided to GPT-3 for predictions. The paper emphasizes the efficacy of pre-trained RoBERTa embeddings for this retrieval process, demonstrating that the choice of in-context examples plays a crucial role in the model's accuracy.

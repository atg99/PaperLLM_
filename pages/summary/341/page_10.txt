The paper discusses the behaviors of GPT-3 and aims to enhance its few-shot learning capabilities. It draws upon various research works that contribute to understanding and improving language models, particularly in the context of semantic parsing, dialogue generation, and retrieval-augmented generation techniques. Key references include foundational studies in language modeling and machine learning, which highlight advancements in understanding generalization, categorization, and open-domain question answering. The authors hope that their insights will facilitate further research and improvements in natural language processing, particularly in making language models more effective in few-shot scenarios.

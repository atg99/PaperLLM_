In summary, the findings demonstrate that KATE, particularly when fine-tuned on related tasks, achieves superior performance compared to traditional kNN methods and random guessing. The retrieval module significantly enhances GPT-3's capabilities, facilitating better outcomes across various tasks, including table-to-text generation and open-domain question answering. The results affirm that fine-tuning on similar datasets is beneficial, while fine-tuning on dissimilar tasks can detrimentally affect performance. Overall, KATE's approach underscores the importance of combining retrieval techniques with advanced language models to improve accuracy and reduce errors in generated responses.

The paper presents a qualitative comparison of various super-resolution techniques on real-world images from the DPED dataset. The authors highlight that their approach demonstrates superior perceptual quality compared to other methods, including Ours, ZSSR, EDSR, ESRGAN, and ESRGAN FT. It is important to note that since the dataset consists of real-world images, a ground-truth reference for super-resolution is not available, making the evaluation of perceptual quality crucial.

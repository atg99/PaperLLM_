The supplementary material provides additional insights and results related to the paper's main findings. Sections S1 and S2 detail experiments conducted on the AIM 2019 and NTIRE 2018 challenge datasets, respectively. In Section S3, further illustrations and specifics regarding the analyzed approaches in the ablative experiments are included. Section S4 elaborates on the real-world super-resolution benchmarking procedure, while Section S5 showcases example output images of the learned domain transfer mapping. Section S6 presents additional qualitative visual results, and Section S7 discusses failure cases encountered during the experiments.

In Section S1, the real-world SR benchmark from the AIM 2019 challenge is introduced. The input domain training images were obtained by adding complex degradation to high-resolution Flickr2K images, with the clean DIV2K dataset used for the target domain. Results indicate that the proposed approach achieved superior LPIPS scores compared to state-of-the-art methods, with detailed comparisons presented in Tables S1 and S2.

Section S2 focuses on the NTIRE 2018 challenge, specifically in an unsupervised scenario with realistic mild adverse conditions. The approach was trained using unpaired low-resolution and high-resolution images, leading to improved perceptual results as indicated by the LPIPS metric. Although bicubic upscaling achieved the best PSNR, the proposed method outperformed others in terms of perceptual quality, achieving an LPIPS score of 0.1858.

Section S3 provides additional details on the baseline approaches evaluated, including various methods and their respective performance metrics, reinforcing the findings from the main paper. Overall, the supplementary material enhances the understanding of the experiments conducted and the effectiveness of the proposed methods in real-world super-resolution tasks.

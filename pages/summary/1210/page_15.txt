The paper presents a qualitative comparison of various image super-resolution techniques, specifically GT, Bicubic, ZSSR, EDSR, ESRGAN, and the proposed method, referred to as "Ours." The comparison is conducted on the NTIRE2018 Track 2 challenge dataset, showcasing the performance of each model in enhancing low-resolution images. The emphasis is on the effectiveness of the proposed method relative to existing approaches, highlighting its ability to clean and improve input images.

The section presents visual results from the NTIRE2018 challenge and additional examples from the real-world DPED iPhone dataset. It highlights the challenges faced during the development of the method, particularly in matching the natural input domain for real-world datasets that contain significant noise or JPEG artifacts. The necessity for domain distribution learning is noted, which can introduce artifacts, as illustrated in the visual examples. The method appears to produce slightly more artifacts when trained on clean, high-quality data due to the effects of domain transfer learning, particularly when bicubic downsampling is applied.

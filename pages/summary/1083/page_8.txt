The paper further explores the perfect rebound problem, which serves as an example of a stiff ordinary differential equation (ODE) in a physical system. The scenario involves a billiard bouncing in a two-dimensional domain, where it rebounds without energy loss upon hitting walls. The authors propose a method to model rebound events within a leapfrog-based Stochastic Recurrent Neural Network (SRNN) by incorporating a momentum reversal operation. This approach addresses the challenges of simulating such events accurately within Hamiltonian dynamics.

Key to this model is the introduction of a scalar parameter that determines the timing of the rebound event, allowing for precise adjustments between time steps. The augmented leapfrog schema is designed to capture the dynamics of the billiard's trajectory effectively, ensuring that the rebound event is modeled accurately while considering potential energy dissipation. This innovative approach aims to enhance the simulation of stiff ODEs in complex physical systems.

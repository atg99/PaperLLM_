The paper presented at ICLR 2020 discusses the development and performance of recurrent neural network architectures, specifically focusing on Recurrent L-L O-NET and Recurrent E-E O-NET with ISO under noiseless dynamic observations. The figures illustrate the position over time for different models, highlighting their ability to process and predict sequences effectively. The results indicate that these models can capture dynamic patterns in the data, showcasing their potential applications in various sequential tasks. The emphasis on recurrent structures suggests a focus on improving temporal dependencies in modeling.

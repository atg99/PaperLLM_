This section of the paper presents additional plots related to the spring-chain experiments discussed in the main text. Specifically, it focuses on noiseless data as outlined in section 4.1. The provided figures illustrate the positions of ten masses over time steps, showcasing the dynamics of the spring-chain system. Each mass is represented in separate plots, with the first mass positioned closest to one end and the tenth mass located at the center of the chain. The plots include various neural network models such as Single-step E-E H-NET, Single-step E-L H-NET, and Single-step L-L H-NET, allowing for comparisons of their performance in predicting the positions of the masses over time. The observations derived from these plots contribute to the understanding of the system's behavior under controlled conditions.

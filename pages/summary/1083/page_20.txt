The paper presents a comparison between Vanilla RNNs and LSTMs in the context of processing noiseless dynamic observations over time steps. Figures 18 and 19 illustrate the performance of each model in tracking the position over time. The results indicate that LSTMs outperform Vanilla RNNs in this task, showcasing their ability to handle long-range dependencies more effectively. This highlights the advantages of using LSTMs for sequential data processing in comparison to traditional RNNs.

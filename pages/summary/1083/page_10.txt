In this paper, the authors introduce the Symplectic Recurrent Neural Network (SRNN), designed to learn the dynamics of Hamiltonian systems based on data. The SRNN utilizes symplectic integration, multi-step training, and initial state optimization, which collectively enhance its predictive capabilities for complex and noisy Hamiltonian systems, such as spring-chain and three-body systems. Remarkably, the SRNN demonstrates superior performance compared to traditional simulation methods, potentially compensating for numerical discretization errors inherent in exact equations. Additionally, the authors extend the SRNN's functionality to learn perfect rebound dynamics, which may enable the handling of stiff systems using ordinary differential equation-based learning algorithms.

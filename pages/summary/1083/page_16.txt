The paper presented at ICLR 2020 discusses the impact of noisy data on the performance of various neural network architectures, specifically within the context of dynamic observations. Section 4.2 focuses on the analysis of single-step models, including E-E O-NET, H-NET, and L-L O-NET. Figures 6, 7, and 8 illustrate the position predictions over time steps for these models under noiseless dynamic conditions. The results highlight the strengths and weaknesses of each architecture in handling dynamic data, emphasizing the importance of model selection based on the nature of the input data. Overall, the findings contribute to a deeper understanding of how different neural networks respond to varying data quality in dynamic environments.

The paper presents additional plots from three-body experiments, showcasing the performance of various prediction methods. In the figures, the ground truth trajectories of the three masses are represented by dash-dot curves, while the predictions from different methods are shown as sequences of dots. Specifically, Figures 20 and 21 compare single-step-trained methods and recurrently trained methods, respectively, including H-NET, Euler-Euler, O-NET, Euler-Leapfrog, and Leapfrog-Leapfrog. Figure 22 contrasts the actual trajectory with those simulated by the leapfrog integrator at two different time steps, illustrating the impact of time resolution on trajectory accuracy.

The conference paper presented at ICLR 2020 discusses various network architectures for modeling dynamic observations in noiseless environments. The figures illustrate the performance of different models, specifically Single-step L-L H-NET, Recurrent E-E O-NET, and Recurrent E-E H-NET, across multiple time steps. Each model's output is visualized in terms of position over time, indicating their ability to capture dynamic changes effectively. The findings contribute to the understanding of how different neural network configurations can influence the representation and prediction of dynamic data.

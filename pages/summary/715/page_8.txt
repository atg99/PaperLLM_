This document references several key papers in the field of reinforcement learning, particularly focusing on policy optimization techniques. 

1. Schulman et al. (2015) introduced Generalized Advantage Estimation, which provides a way to estimate advantages in high-dimensional continuous control tasks, improving the stability and performance of policy gradient methods.

2. In 2017, Schulman et al. further developed Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) algorithms, which emphasize maintaining a balance between exploration and exploitation by using a trust region approach to update policies.

3. Song et al. (2019) proposed V-MPO, an on-policy method that optimizes policies using maximum a posteriori principles, applicable to both discrete and continuous control environments.

4. Vinyals et al. (2019) demonstrated the capabilities of multi-agent reinforcement learning by achieving Grandmaster level performance in StarCraft II, showcasing the potential of these techniques in complex environments.

5. Wang et al. (2016) presented a sample-efficient actor-critic method that incorporates experience replay, enhancing the learning process by reusing past experiences.

6. Wu et al. (2017) introduced a scalable trust-region method for deep reinforcement learning, leveraging Kronecker-factored approximations to improve computational efficiency and model performance.

These works collectively contribute to advancing the methodologies and applications of reinforcement learning, particularly in high-dimensional and complex control tasks.

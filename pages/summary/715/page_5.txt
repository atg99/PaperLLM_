In conclusion, the paper presents insights into the performance of PPG with varying auxiliary phase frequencies, the comparative effectiveness of a fixed KL penalty versus clipping, and the potential of employing a single-network architecture to optimize computational efficiency. The findings suggest that infrequent auxiliary phases are crucial for success, and the fixed KL penalty approach performs comparably to clipping while being easier to analyze. The single-network strategy effectively mitigates the increased computational cost associated with PPG, demonstrating that it is possible to achieve similar performance with reduced resource requirements.

The referenced works in this section highlight the application of artificial intelligence and machine learning in clinical laboratory diagnostics. Various studies demonstrate the efficacy of these technologies in predicting conditions such as iron deficiency anemia, asthma, chronic kidney disease progression, and COVID-19 outcomes, often utilizing routine laboratory data. Machine learning models have been shown to outperform traditional methods in diagnosing conditions like brain tumors and lung cancer, as well as in identifying mislabeled samples and improving diagnostic workflows in flow cytometry for chronic lymphocytic leukemia. Additionally, advancements in data mining techniques for establishing reference intervals and optimizing preanalytic practices are discussed. The overarching theme emphasizes that artificial intelligence is transforming laboratory medicine, enhancing diagnostic accuracy, and streamlining workflows while addressing the challenges posed by increasing diagnostic workloads.

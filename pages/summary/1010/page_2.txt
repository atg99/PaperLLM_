The paper by Wen et al. discusses the importance of individual differences in disease management through the analysis of the genome and transcriptome, which can enhance clinical practices. It highlights advancements in proteomics that focus on identifying disease-associated proteins to establish better disease markers and therapeutic targets. The authors also emphasize the growth of clinical omics, particularly the use of radiomics, which utilizes high-throughput methods to analyze radiographic images for developing diagnostic and prognostic models. Additionally, the role of clinical laboratories in providing crucial test results through various examination methods is outlined. The paper illustrates how different omics technologies can improve disease prediction and treatment strategies through machine learning and statistical analysis, while also noting the differences in data structures among these omics.

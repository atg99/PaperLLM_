The paper by Wen et al. published in BMC Bioinformatics discusses several key aspects of the submission and publication process for research articles, emphasizing the benefits of choosing BMC for researchers. Highlighted features include fast online submission, comprehensive peer review by experts, rapid publication upon acceptance, and support for various types of complex research data. The paper also underscores the advantages of gold Open Access, which enhances visibility and citation rates, with BMC experiencing over 100 million website views annually. 

The references listed cover a range of studies related to artificial intelligence in laboratory medicine, including reviews of its value, employee attitudes, the importance of external validation of machine learning models, and ethical considerations. The references collectively illustrate the ongoing discourse on integrating AI into laboratory practices, highlighting both opportunities and the necessity for ethical oversight. The paper concludes with a note on the neutrality of Springer Nature regarding jurisdictional claims in published materials.

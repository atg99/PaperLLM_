Integration of AI and data analysis
The rising volume of clinical data necessitates the application of artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning techniques to improve data analysis and interpretation. The growing interest in AI within the medical field is evidenced by a substantial increase in related publications, indicating a shift towards leveraging technology for better clinical outcomes. The review of literature from 2010 to 2022 reveals a focused effort on merging AI with clinical laboratory practices, highlighting the potential of these technologies to optimize laboratory processes and enhance diagnostic accuracy.

Emergence of Clinlabomics
Clinlabomics, the proposed model integrating clinical laboratory data with other omics data, aims to bridge the gap between traditional laboratory practices and modern data-driven approaches. Although the current clinical laboratory dataset dimensions are lower compared to other omics, the quantitative nature of clinical data presents a unique opportunity to develop comprehensive models that can inform medical decision-making and improve patient care.

Advancements in laboratory technology
The evolution of laboratory technology over the past decade has significantly transformed clinical laboratory medicine. From manual testing to the automation of processes, the development of sophisticated medical equipment has expanded the range of tests available and improved efficiency. This technological advancement is crucial for the establishment of Clinlabomics, as it facilitates the integration and analysis of diverse data types.

In conclusion, the integration of AI with clinical laboratory practices, supported by advancements in laboratory technology, holds promise for the future of medical diagnostics and treatment. By developing Clinlabomics models, the potential exists to enhance the understanding of diseases and improve patient outcomes through more informed clinical decisions.

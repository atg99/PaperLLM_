The paper discusses the importance of the ISO15189 standard in laboratory medicine, emphasizing its role in ensuring quality management and comparability of clinical laboratory test results. Accurate test results are critical for correct diagnosis and treatment decisions. 

It highlights the evolving clinical significance of traditional laboratory tests, noting that certain tests have undiscovered implications. For instance, platelets, traditionally associated with hemostasis, are now recognized for their roles in immune response and cancer progression. Prealbumin, long used to assess nutritional status, has been identified as a prognostic marker for various cancers, including lung and esophageal cancers.

The paper also addresses the challenges faced during the COVID-19 pandemic, where researchers turned to routine blood tests to identify potential biomarkers. Studies identified interleukins and serum ferritin as indicators for severe cases of COVID-19, alongside notable changes in other blood parameters that aid in diagnosis and prognosis.

Furthermore, the paper notes the trend of using combinations of laboratory test data for clinical evaluations. For example, the AST/ALT ratio has been linked to various diseases, including prostate cancer and oropharyngeal squamous cell carcinoma, indicating its potential as a valuable diagnostic tool.

The paper introduces Clinlabomics, a novel concept focused on leveraging routine laboratory test data for disease prediction, diagnosis, and monitoring, while also enhancing laboratory management. Despite the initial stage of research combining artificial intelligence (AI) with clinical laboratory data, existing studies have primarily concentrated on blood test items, yielding promising results in diagnosing and evaluating clinical diseases. However, a lack of extensive clinical trials and standardized practices remains a significant challenge. Additionally, there is limited research on integrating AI with body fluid-related tests, such as urine and cerebrospinal fluid, indicating potential for future exploration using deep learning methods.

The paper discusses various models and algorithms used in the integration of AI with clinical laboratory data, emphasizing that the choice of model can significantly impact disease prediction, diagnosis, and laboratory management. Logistic regression (LR) is noted for its simplicity and interpretability, though it may struggle with data accuracy due to its linear nature. Random Forest (RF) is highlighted for its high accuracy and interpretability, making it suitable for disease diagnosis applications. Support Vector Machines (SVM) are recognized for their robust mathematical framework and effectiveness in medical diagnostics. Neural network models (NN), including artificial neural networks (ANNs) and deep neural networks (DNNs), are acknowledged for their capabilities in pattern recognition and require substantial training resources.

In summary, the integration of AI with clinical laboratory data presents significant opportunities for improving diagnostic accuracy and reliability, although challenges in standardization and data diversity persist.

The paper discusses the importance of renewing laboratory equipment to enhance the efficiency of clinical tests, thereby increasing the volume of data obtained from clinical laboratory testing. It emphasizes that while the quantity of data is significant, the quality is equally critical. Historically, clinical laboratory tests were performed by individual medical institutions without a unified quality assurance framework until the adoption of the ISO15189 standard. This shift has facilitated the establishment of Clinlabomics, which leverages advancements in clinical laboratories and the emergence of big data. The workflow for searching and filtering articles is illustrated, highlighting the development of the field over time.

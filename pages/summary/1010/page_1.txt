The paper introduces a new concept termed "Clinlabomics," which integrates clinical laboratory data with artificial intelligence (AI) to enhance disease prediction, understanding, and treatment formulation. The authors highlight the growing significance of big data and AI in medicine, particularly in areas like disease diagnosis, surveillance, and targeted interventions. Clinlabomics aims to leverage high-throughput methods to extract extensive feature data from various clinical samples, employing data statistics and machine learning techniques to uncover previously undiscovered insights. The review summarizes applications of AI in clinical laboratory data across medical fields, emphasizing that while Clinlabomics holds promise for transforming healthcare, further validation in multi-center environments is necessary. The authors assert that this approach could significantly assist various medical domains.

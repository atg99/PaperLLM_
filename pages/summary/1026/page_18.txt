In summary, this paper outlines a scoping review conducted to synthesize and map existing gray literature on ethical principles and guidelines for artificial intelligence (AI). The methodology employed is particularly suitable for complex research areas due to the lack of a unified database for AI ethics guidelines. A protocol was developed based on the PRISMA framework and was pilot-tested prior to data collection. The review process involved a comprehensive multi-stage screening strategy that included manual searches of link hubs and keyword-based searches on Google, ensuring systematic retrieval of relevant documents. The results from these strategies were supplemented by citation chaining to identify additional sources. Continuous literature monitoring was also implemented to capture relevant documents published after the initial search, highlighting the evolving nature of the field.

The paper emphasizes the importance of justice in AI systems through various proposed methods, including the establishment of technical standards and normative guidelines, enhancing transparency by providing information and raising public awareness, and implementing rigorous testing and monitoring practices favored by data protection offices. 

It advocates for strengthening the rule of law and ensuring the right to appeal and redress, as well as promoting systemic changes that involve diverse workforces and active civil society engagement. The paper also highlights the principle of non-maleficence, stressing the need for AI to avoid causing harm, whether intentional or unintentional, with particular focus on preventing discrimination, privacy violations, and other forms of harm.

Harm-prevention strategies are centered on technical measures and governance approaches, which include interventions during AI research and design, and the promotion of security and privacy standards. The paper calls for interdisciplinary cooperation, compliance with legislation, and the establishment of oversight mechanisms to ensure accountability in AI deployment. Overall, it presents a comprehensive framework for pursuing justice and preventing harm in AI systems.

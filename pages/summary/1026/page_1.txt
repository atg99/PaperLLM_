The paper by Anna Jobin, Marcello Ienca, and Effy Vayena explores the evolving landscape of ethical guidelines for artificial intelligence (AI) over the past five years. The authors identify a growing global consensus around five key ethical principles: transparency, justice and fairness, non-maleficence, responsibility, and privacy. However, they note significant differences in the interpretation, importance, applicability, and implementation of these principles among various stakeholders, including private companies, research institutions, and public organizations. The study emphasizes the need for a cohesive approach that combines the development of guidelines with thorough ethical analysis and effective strategies for implementation to ensure the realization of ethical AI.

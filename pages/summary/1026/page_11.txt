The concept of beneficence in the context of artificial intelligence (AI) is often referenced but lacks a clear definition. It encompasses the enhancement of human senses, the promotion of well-being, peace, happiness, socio-economic opportunities, and economic prosperity. There is ambiguity regarding who should benefit from AI, with private sector entities typically focusing on customer benefits while many sources advocate for broader societal benefits that include humanity, society, and the environment. Strategies to promote good involve aligning AI with human values, advancing scientific understanding, minimizing power concentration, and engaging with affected communities. 

The discussion on freedom and autonomy highlights both positive and negative aspects. Positive freedom encompasses the ability to flourish, self-determination, and the right to form relationships, while negative freedom pertains to protection from technological manipulation and surveillance. Promoting freedom and autonomy is linked to transparency in AI systems, ensuring informed consent, and enhancing public knowledge about AI, thereby empowering individuals and protecting their rights.

The paper discusses the enhancement of an entailment classifier's performance by integrating the title of the text to improve topic awareness and reduce irrelevant retrievals. It suggests that the classifier should have access to dynamic retrievals that allow it to seek additional information about keywords encountered during its analysis. This approach aims to create higher quality and more abundant multi-evidence claims, thereby improving the overall effectiveness of the system. The references cited provide foundational work in natural language processing and inference, indicating the research's grounding in existing literature and methodologies.

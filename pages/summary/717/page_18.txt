The paper presents a convergence analysis of a modified policy gradient (PG) algorithm under the Coordinate Non-Convex (CNC) condition. It outlines the key parameters and constraints used in the analysis, summarized in Table 1, which details values for parameters such as beta, Jthre, alpha, kthre, and delta, along with their corresponding orders and constraints. The analysis emphasizes the importance of the variance of the stochastic gradient and its relationship with the curvature of the objective function, which is influenced by the Hessian matrix.

The core argument is that the CNC condition ensures that the perturbations from the stochastic gradient maintain a positive curvature, aiding convergence without the need for artificial isotropic noise, which can be detrimental in reinforcement learning (RL) contexts. The paper contrasts this approach with existing methods in nonconvex optimization that require such noise.

Theorem 4.8 formalizes the improved convergence guarantees of the proposed algorithm, stating that it achieves a specified level of convergence under certain assumptions. The results are significant for enhancing the efficiency and reliability of policy gradient methods in RL, particularly in dealing with the inherent noise in policy evaluations. The paper concludes with a note on the implications of these findings for future research and applications in the field.

The paper presents two algorithms, EvalPG and MRPG, for calculating stochastic policy gradients in reinforcement learning. 

EvalPG is designed to compute three types of stochastic policy gradients based on the input parameters: state (s), action (a), policy parameters (θ), and gradient type (diamondsuit). The algorithm estimates the action-value function (Q) and the state value function (V) as needed, depending on the specified gradient type. It uses these estimates to calculate the stochastic gradient, which is returned as gθ.

MRPG, or Modified Random-horizon Policy Gradient Algorithm, leverages EvalPG to update the policy parameters through a series of iterations. It initializes with a starting state and policy parameters, then repeatedly samples the length of the trajectory and actions. The stochastic gradient calculated by EvalPG is used to update the policy parameters, with different update rules based on the iteration count. The process continues until convergence, after which the algorithm returns a randomly selected set of parameters.

The detailed execution of both algorithms facilitates the optimization of policy gradients in reinforcement learning tasks, enhancing the decision-making process.

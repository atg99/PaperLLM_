The paper discusses the convergence properties of the Modified Randomized Policy Gradient (MRPG) algorithm, which aims to approximate second-order stationary points (SOSPs) in optimization problems. The MRPG algorithm, as detailed in Algorithm 3.5, utilizes constant step sizes \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\), with \(\beta\) periodically increased to enhance the update direction, thereby aiding in escaping saddle points. This approach builds on previous work in stochastic gradient methods, correcting flawed proofs to ensure the convergence of MRPG.

Key results include the definition of an (\(\epsilon g\), \(\epsilon h\))-approximate second-order stationary point and the establishment of Lipschitz continuity for the policy gradient under specific assumptions. The paper proves that stochastic policy gradient estimates are unbiased and bounded, which is critical for the analysis of the MRPG algorithm. The findings contribute to understanding the convergence of modified policy gradient algorithms, emphasizing the equivalence between converging to SOSPs and local maxima under certain conditions.

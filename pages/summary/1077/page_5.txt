The paper discusses techniques to enhance the stability of INT8 training in MobileNetV2 through Direction Sensitive Gradient Clipping and Deviation Counteractive Learning Rate Scaling. The analysis begins with the Kolmogorov-Smirnov (KS) test, which reveals that gradients do not conform to a common distribution, undermining previous assumptions that relied on Gaussian distributions for optimal clipping values. The findings are presented in Table 1, highlighting KS-statistics for different layers.

To address quantization errors, the paper emphasizes the significance of gradient characteristics, advocating for the use of cosine distance to optimize clipping values instead of mean-square error (MSE), which is ineffective due to the magnitude fluctuations of gradients. Furthermore, the necessity of scaling down the learning rate during quantized training is established, as shown in Figure 4(b), where a reduced learning rate mitigates training crashes. The Deviation Counteractive Learning Rate Scaling method is introduced to adaptively adjust the learning rate based on direction deviations, formulated in Equation (8). This layer-wise scaling effectively counters gradient deviations and supports stable training.

Overall, the proposed methods aim to minimize quantization errors and enhance training stability, thereby addressing challenges associated with INT8 training in deep neural networks.

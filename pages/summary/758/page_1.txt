The paper introduces BARTpho, the first public large-scale monolingual sequence-to-sequence (seq2seq) models pre-trained specifically for Vietnamese, available in two versions: BARTpho syllable and BARTpho word. Utilizing the architecture and pre-training scheme of the BART model, BARTpho is designed for generative natural language processing (NLP) tasks. The authors conduct experiments comparing BARTpho with the multilingual model mBART on Vietnamese text summarization and find that BARTpho consistently outperforms mBART in both automatic and human evaluations, achieving state-of-the-art results. 

Additionally, BARTpho is evaluated on capitalization and punctuation restoration tasks, where it also demonstrates superior performance compared to mBART. The paper highlights the influence of word segmentation in Vietnamese, showing that the word-level model (BARTpho word) performs better than the syllable-level model (BARTpho syllable). The authors emphasize the importance of dedicated language-specific models over multilingual ones, particularly for languages like Vietnamese, which have unique linguistic characteristics. 

BARTpho is publicly released to support future research and applications in generative Vietnamese NLP tasks, serving as a strong baseline for the field. The paper also reviews related works, including PhoBERT, which is another significant pre-trained language model for Vietnamese, and discusses the advantages of using BART for seq2seq tasks.

The paper discusses the importance of capitalization and punctuation restoration in Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) systems, which typically generate lowercased and unpunctuated text, affecting readability and performance in downstream tasks. The authors present two models, BARTpho syllable and BARTpho word, as pre-trained sequence-to-sequence models for Vietnamese, demonstrating their effectiveness compared to the mBART model.

Experimental results show that BARTpho models outperform mBART in both capitalization and punctuation restoration tasks, with notable improvements in F1 scores for specific punctuation types. The study employed an automatically generated dataset from the PhoST dataset to evaluate model performance, highlighting the significance of proper formatting in enhancing the usability of ASR outputs.

In conclusion, BARTpho models are shown to achieve state-of-the-art results in Vietnamese text summarization, capitalization, and punctuation restoration, contributing to advancements in generative Vietnamese NLP tasks. The authors hope that their work will encourage further research and applications in this area.

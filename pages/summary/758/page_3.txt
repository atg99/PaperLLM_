The paper presents a comparative analysis of ROUGE scores for different models in the context of Vietnamese text summarization, focusing on the performance of BARTpho variants against the baseline mBART. The results, detailed in Tables 1 and 2, show that both BARTpho versions significantly outperform mBART on validation and test sets, with statistically significant differences (p-value < 0.05). 

In the context of duplicate article removal, BARTpho word achieves the highest ROUGE scores among the tested models, with scores of 60.55 for ROUGE-1, 29.89 for ROUGE-2, and 39.73 for ROUGE-L on the test set. A human evaluation further supports these findings, as BARTpho models received better assessments compared to mBART, with a Cohen’s kappa coefficient of 0.61 indicating good inter-annotator agreement.

When tested on the original dataset without duplicate removal, BARTpho still outperforms previous models, including PhoBERT2PhoBERT, confirming its state-of-the-art status in this task. The study highlights the effectiveness of large-scale, language-specific BART-based models for Vietnamese, emphasizing the advantage of word segmentation in improving performance over syllable-based approaches. Despite mBART and mT5 being trained on significantly larger datasets, BARTpho demonstrates superior results, reaffirming the benefits of dedicated models for specific languages.

The VAE model is noted for its applicability in recognizing patterns in images or video frames, facilitating automatic recognition of player sketches in gaming contexts. Additionally, it supports data augmentation, which is beneficial for puzzle games that require players to draw or write answers. When a player's input matches the model's expectations, they receive rewards or progress in the game.

The paper also presents insights from case studies, indicating that the average loss associated with VAE modeling stabilizes around a limiting value when training with 50 epochs, suggesting the parameters used are generally effective. However, training time is directly proportional to the number of epochs, highlighting a trade-off between accuracy and computational efficiency.

This paper emphasizes the need for a standardized, indicator-based methodology to quantify resilience effectively, acknowledging the challenges posed by uncertainty and interdependencies in resilience modeling. It argues for the preference of probabilistic methods over deterministic ones, as they offer a more realistic representation of complex systems. The integration of Fuzzy analysis and Bayesian Networks is proposed as a promising approach for modeling resilience, particularly in scenarios where historical data is lacking. The paper aims to contribute to the ongoing discourse on disaster resilience by exploring these advanced methodologies and their applications in engineering systems.

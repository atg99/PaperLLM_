The paper outlines future research directions aimed at constructing detailed networks for damage and recovery variables. This approach will enhance the system's expression of interdependencies among variables and their respective weighting factors. Weighting indicators is pivotal for determining conditional probabilities of key nodes. The Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) will be employed in future studies to analyze these complex decisions, as it has been effectively utilized in similar contexts.

The research received funding from the European Research Council under Grant Agreement ERC_IDEAL RESCUE_637842, focusing on the Integrated Design and Control of Sustainable Communities during Emergencies. Additional support came from the MAE Center at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. The authors' views may not reflect the official stance of the sponsors.

An appendix presents scores of resilience indicators for 37 countries as assessed by the United Nations, providing valuable data for comparative analysis.

The paper presents performance results from two scenarios of a simulation assessing system resilience under varying conditions of damage and recoverability. Figure 15 illustrates the system's performance when faced with high damage and high recoverability, indicating how effectively the system can recover under these circumstances. Conversely, Figure 16 displays the performance outcomes for a scenario characterized by high damage but low recoverability, highlighting the challenges and limitations the system encounters in this situation. These results underscore the importance of recoverability in enhancing system resilience following significant damage.

The paper presents a Bayesian Network (BN) analysis focused on Brazil, highlighting the country's resilience in various aspects. Figure 6 illustrates the BN analysis and the resulting resilience metrics, showcasing key factors that influence Brazil's ability to withstand and recover from challenges. The analysis integrates multiple variables to assess the overall resilience, providing insights into the strengths and vulnerabilities within the country's socio-economic and environmental contexts. The findings emphasize the importance of understanding these dynamics to enhance Brazil's resilience strategies.

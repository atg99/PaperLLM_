The section discusses the construction of Conditional Probability Tables (CPTs) for a Bayesian network designed to compute the resilience index of a static system. It emphasizes the importance of defining connections between resilience pillars (son nodes) and indicators (father nodes) using expert knowledge or available data to establish the states of the indicators.

Each resilience pillar is assigned three states (High, Medium, Low) to simplify the network's complexity, while the indicators have five states (High, Good, Medium, Low, Vulnerable) with corresponding numerical values of 4, 3, 2, 1, and 0. The resilience index for each son node is calculated by summing the values of the father nodes and normalizing this sum to produce a relative value x ranging from 0 to 1.

The paper illustrates the calculation process with an example involving three father nodes, showing how different combinations of their states contribute to the overall resilience index. The computed value x is then used to determine the distribution of the son node among its three levels, ensuring a comprehensive representation of the resilience state.

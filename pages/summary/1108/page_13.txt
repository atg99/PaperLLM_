The document outlines five key priorities for disaster risk reduction (DRR) along with a detailed framework for assessing and monitoring these priorities through specific indicators. The priorities include ensuring DRR is a national and local priority, identifying and assessing disaster risks, utilizing resources to promote safety and resilience, reducing underlying risk factors, and strengthening disaster preparedness for effective response. Each priority is associated with four to six indicators, totaling twenty-two indicators that guide the implementation of activities aimed at risk reduction, preparedness, and recovery.

To establish a network linking these indicators to resilience pillars, the document categorizes each indicator based on its focus: those related to regulatory actions for risk reduction are assigned to Priority 1 (P1), indicators related to building resilience capabilities fall under Priority 2 (P2), and those concerning emergency response and recovery practices are categorized under Priority 3 (P3).

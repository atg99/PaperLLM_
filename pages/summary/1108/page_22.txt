The model is structured with resilience as the top level, further divided into seven dimensions: Physical infrastructure, User's behavior, Resources, Plan, Organization and management, Social-economic characteristics, and Environment and climate. Each dimension is broken down into components and indicators, which are evaluated through specific measures. A total of 21 components and 78 indicators are identified, ensuring comprehensive coverage of the transportation infrastructure while minimizing redundancy through expert consultation.

The first dimension, Physical infrastructure, is elaborated upon with its components and indicators, which are assigned importance factors to prioritize their influence on resilience. The paper emphasizes the systematic approach to modeling, highlighting the importance of detailed descriptions for effective resilience evaluation in transportation systems.

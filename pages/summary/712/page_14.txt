The appendix of the paper discusses the robustness of the policy gradient method in reinforcement learning, specifically focusing on the Lipschitz continuity of the robust value function. The robust value function, denoted as \( V_{\pi_{\theta}}(s) \), is shown to satisfy a Lipschitz condition with respect to the policy parameters \( \theta \). 

The lemma presented establishes that for any two policy parameters \( \theta_1 \) and \( \theta_2 \), the difference in the robust value function at a state \( s \) is bounded by a constant \( L_V \) multiplied by the norm of the difference between the parameters. This is expressed mathematically as:

\[
|V_{\pi_{\theta_1}}(s) - V_{\pi_{\theta_2}}(s)| \leq L_V \| \theta_1 - \theta_2 \|
\]

The proof involves analyzing the difference \( V_{\pi_{\theta_1}}(s) - V_{\pi_{\theta_2}}(s) \) through the robust Bellman operator and applying recursive relationships to decompose the differences in expected values over actions and subsequent states. The key steps include using properties of the action-value function \( Q_{\pi} \) and the transition dynamics to establish bounds on the differences in value functions.

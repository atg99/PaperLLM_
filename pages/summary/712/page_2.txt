This paper presents a robust policy gradient method tailored for reinforcement learning under uncertainty, particularly focusing on Markov Decision Processes (MDPs). The authors first tackle the challenge posed by the non-differentiability of the robust value function, which involves a maximization over an uncertainty set. They derive the Fréchet sub-gradient of the robust value function, demonstrating that it is almost everywhere differentiable. This robust policy gradient is applicable to any differentiable and Lipschitz policy class.

The authors explore the potential for achieving global optimality with their robust policy gradient method, noting the complex landscape of the robust value function compared to traditional methods. They prove that the robust value function satisfies the Polyak-Łojasiewicz condition, ensuring that their robust policy gradient method converges to a global optimum with high probability.

To handle the non-differentiability of the robust value function during convergence rate analysis, the authors propose a smoothed robust policy gradient method that approximates the original function, ensuring differentiability. This method is shown to converge to an ε-global optimum with a complexity of O(ε−3).

The paper also addresses practical applications, focusing on a model-free setting where only samples from the centroid of the uncertainty set are available. The authors develop a robust actor-critic algorithm capable of operating with any differentiable policy class and value function approximation. They establish the global optimality of this robust actor-critic method in a tabular setting.

In relation to existing literature, the authors discuss the limitations of previous policy gradient methods, particularly in non-robust settings, and highlight the unique challenges presented by robust reinforcement learning. They reference earlier works on robust MDPs and the distinction between direct policy search methods and value-based approaches, emphasizing the novelty and applicability of their proposed robust policy gradient method in addressing uncertain transition kernels.

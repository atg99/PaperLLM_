The authors first present the concept of a robust policy (sub-)gradient, applicable to any differentiable parametric policy class. They demonstrate that their proposed robust policy gradient method converges asymptotically to a global optimum when employing direct policy parameterization. Furthermore, they introduce a smoothed robust policy gradient method, which achieves an epsilon-global optimum with a complexity of O(epsilon^-3).

The methodology is extended to a general model-free setting, resulting in the robust actor-critic method that utilizes a differentiable parametric policy class and value function. The authors provide a thorough analysis of its asymptotic convergence and sample complexity in a tabular setting.

Simulation results are included to showcase the robustness of the proposed methods against model mismatch. The work emphasizes the importance of developing a robust reinforcement learning framework that ensures optimal performance even in the presence of uncertainties and deviations between simulated and real-world environments. This contribution fills a significant gap in the existing literature by offering a theoretically grounded approach to robust policy gradient methods.

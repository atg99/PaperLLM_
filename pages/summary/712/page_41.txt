The paper discusses a policy gradient method aimed at enhancing the robustness of reinforcement learning. It establishes specific conditions for parameters related to the performance of the method, ensuring that certain inequalities hold true to maintain the quality of the policy being learned. The authors derive bounds on the expected performance difference between the current policy and an optimal policy, suggesting that these bounds can be kept within acceptable limits under certain conditions.

Additionally, the paper includes a lemma that outlines the relationship between the gradients of individual functions and the gradient of their maximum. This lemma provides a foundation for understanding how the optimization landscape behaves in the context of policy gradients.

The paper also defines several constants that play crucial roles in the analysis, including parameters related to the discount factor, action space, and other performance metrics. These constants are critical for ensuring that the derived inequalities are satisfied, ultimately contributing to the robustness of the reinforcement learning approach proposed. Overall, the work aims to improve the reliability of policy gradient methods in various environments.

The paper discusses a robust reinforcement learning approach utilizing policy gradient methods, specifically focusing on two algorithms: Smoothed Robust TD and Robust Smoothed Actor-Critic in a tabular setting. 

Algorithm 5, Smoothed Robust TD, initializes a state-action value function (Q0) and iteratively updates this function based on observed rewards and the next state. The update rule incorporates a smoothed version of the value function, ensuring that the updates converge to a fixed point Qπσ almost surely, as shown through the contraction property of the operator involved. The proof for the convergence of the non-smoothed version is noted to be similar and thus omitted.

Algorithm 6 outlines the Robust Smoothed Actor-Critic method, which builds on the previous algorithm. It initializes the policy parameters and runs the smoothed TD algorithm for a specified number of iterations until the difference between the smoothed value function and the policy's value function is sufficiently small. The algorithm samples trajectories and computes unbiased estimates of the policy gradient, ensuring that the updates to the policy parameters converge towards an optimal policy.

Overall, the paper presents a structured approach to enhancing reinforcement learning robustness through policy gradient methods, demonstrating convergence properties and the effectiveness of the proposed algorithms in a tabular setting.

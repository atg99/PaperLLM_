The paper evaluates the performance of a robust policy gradient method in reinforcement learning by comparing it to non-robust variants and the Adaptive Robust Policy Learning (ARPL) method. In the context of the Garnet problem, the robust Actor-Critic approach demonstrates superior performance under varying reward conditions, specifically at R=0.15 and R=0.2. The results indicate that the robust method consistently outperforms the non-robust counterpart.

Further analysis is conducted on the Frozen-Lake problem, where the robust policy gradient method is compared to ARPL. The findings reveal that the robust policy gradient method achieves better discounted accumulative rewards under worst-case scenarios across multiple iterations, confirming its enhanced robustness relative to ARPL. Overall, the study highlights the effectiveness of robust policy gradient methods in improving reinforcement learning outcomes in challenging environments.

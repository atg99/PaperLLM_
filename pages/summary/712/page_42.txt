In this section of the paper, additional experiments are presented to validate the theoretical results regarding robust reinforcement learning. The focus is on comparing robust policy gradient methods with non-robust counterparts. The experiments conducted on various Garnet problems (G(12,6) and G(20,10)) reveal that robust policy gradients consistently yield higher accumulated discounted rewards under the worst-case transition kernel. 

Figures 8 and 9 illustrate these comparisons, showing the performance across different reward settings. Furthermore, the section discusses robust actor-critic methods, contrasting them with vanilla actor-critic approaches on the Garnet problem G(40,15). The results, depicted in Figure 10, indicate that the robust actor-critic method demonstrates greater resilience to model mismatches. 

Additionally, Figure 11 presents findings from another Garnet problem (G(30,10)) using direct policy parameterization with a two-layer neural network for approximating robust value functions. The robust actor-critic algorithm outperforms the vanilla version, achieving higher accumulated discounted rewards under worst-case scenarios. Overall, the experiments substantiate the advantages of robust methods in reinforcement learning.

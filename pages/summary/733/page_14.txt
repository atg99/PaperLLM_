The collection of references highlights significant contributions in the field of reinforcement learning, particularly focusing on off-policy evaluation and bias reduction techniques. Key works include Tang et al. (2019), which discusses doubly robust methods for bias reduction in infinite horizon settings, and Thomas and Brunskill (2016), which emphasizes data-efficient policy evaluation strategies. 

Thomas et al. (2015) introduces high confidence policy improvement methods, while their later work in 2019 addresses the mitigation of undesirable behaviors in intelligent systems. Uehara et al. (2020) and Uehara and Sun (2021) delve into minimax learning and pessimistic model-based approaches, contributing to the theoretical foundations of offline reinforcement learning.

Voloshin et al. (2021) and Wang et al. (2019) explore minimax model learning and neural policy gradient methods, respectively, focusing on global optimality and convergence rates. Wu et al. (2019, 2021) investigate behavior regularization and uncertainty weighting in offline reinforcement learning contexts.

Xie and Jiang's works (2020a, 2020b) contribute to value-function approximation and comparison of Q* approximation schemes, while Yang et al. (2020) discuss reinforcement learning in feature space. The references collectively underscore advancements in statistical methodologies and theoretical frameworks that enhance the understanding and application of off-policy evaluation in reinforcement learning.

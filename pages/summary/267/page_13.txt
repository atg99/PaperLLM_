The paper discusses the significant challenge of diabetic retinopathy, a condition leading to vision impairment due to damage to the retina's blood vessels in diabetic patients. Early detection is crucial to prevent severe consequences, but traditional methods often lack efficiency and accuracy, especially in the early stages of the disease. This research introduces a capsule network (CapsNet) designed to enhance detection accuracy for diabetic retinopathy across four stages. The CapsNet architecture comprises a convolution layer for feature extraction, a primary capsule layer, and a class capsule layer for class probability identification. The proposed model achieved an impressive accuracy of 97.98% when tested on the Messidor dataset, outperforming the modified AlexNet model. Future work aims to train the CapsNet on additional datasets that classify diabetic retinopathy into five stages. Overall, the study underscores the potential of CapsNet for early and accurate diagnosis, contributing to better management of diabetic retinopathy and prevention of vision loss.

The paper presents a comparative analysis of a proposed reformed CapsNet model against a modified AlexNet architecture. The evaluation focuses on performance metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 score across four classes of the Messidor dataset. The reformed CapsNet achieves a notable maximum accuracy of 98.64% for Grade 3 images and a 97.19% F1 score for Grade 0 images, with a perfect recall of 100% for Grade 3. 

In contrast, the modified AlexNet records lower accuracy rates of 96.24% to 96.57% across the same classes. Precision metrics show the proposed CapsNet outperforming AlexNet with figures of 97.19% for Grade 0 compared to 95.32% for AlexNet. Similarly, recall values for the CapsNet also exceed those of AlexNet, particularly with a 100% recall for Grade 3. 

Overall, the reformed CapsNet achieves an average performance of 97.98% accuracy, 95.62% precision, 96.11% recall, and 96.36% F1 score, demonstrating superior performance across all evaluated metrics when compared to the existing modified AlexNet model. The results are visually represented in figures comparing the various performance measures, confirming the efficacy of the proposed model.

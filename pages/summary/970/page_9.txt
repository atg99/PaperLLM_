The paper discusses the various challenges associated with applying reinforcement learning (RL) in real-world scenarios. It highlights model-based RL's potential to enhance sample efficiency and tackle issues like off-policy evaluation, robustness, and explainability. The use of ensembles is emphasized as a strategy for improving both sample efficiency and robustness. A critical aspect of deploying RL systems involves collaboration with product owners and human experts to establish appropriate reward objectives and safety constraints, obtain expert demonstrations for initial learning, and ensure the algorithm's actions are understandable to foster confidence in the system.

The paper suggests that addressing these challenges will lead to research advancements that can facilitate the deployment of RL in practical systems and products. The references cited provide a foundation for further exploration of the methodologies and techniques relevant to overcoming the identified challenges in real-world RL applications.

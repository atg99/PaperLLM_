The appendix of the paper "Challenges of Real-World Reinforcement Learning" outlines various safety constraints and perturbations relevant to different control environments. It details the constraints for four specific tasks: Cart-Pole, Walker, Manipulator, and Humanoid, categorized under static, kinematic, and dynamic limits.

For the Cart-Pole task, constraints include limits on position, velocity near the goal, and cart acceleration. The Walker task emphasizes joint angles, uprightness, joint velocities, and foot contact forces. The Manipulator task focuses on joint angles, avoidance of dynamic obstacles and self-contact, joint velocities, and end effector forces. Lastly, the Humanoid task includes joint angles, uprightness enforcement, joint velocities, foot contact forces, and encourages falls on the posterior.

The paper also discusses robustness by highlighting potential environmental noise and non-stationarity. Examples of perturbations for each environment, such as actuator delays and varying payloads, illustrate the challenges posed by real-world conditions. The authors aim to drive research in real-world reinforcement learning and plan to develop an open-source benchmark to address these safety constraints, thereby enhancing the evaluation of candidate algorithms.

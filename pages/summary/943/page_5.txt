In summary, the training of machine learning models involves adjusting parameters to enhance performance, monitored through training, validation, and testing datasets. Overfitting and underfitting are key issues that affect model accuracy, with overfitting resulting from excessive parameterization and underfitting stemming from inadequate model complexity. The inductive bias inherent in a model influences its learning behavior and suitability for specific data types, while the bias-variance tradeoff emphasizes the need for a balanced approach to achieve optimal model performance.

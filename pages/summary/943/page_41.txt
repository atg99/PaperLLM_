This collection of references highlights various advancements in computational biology and machine learning applications. Pires et al. (2014) introduced DUET, a server that predicts mutation effects on protein stability. Yuan and Bar-Joseph (2019) explored deep learning to infer gene relationships from single-cell expression data. Chen et al. (2016) utilized an autoencoder model for hierarchical representation of yeast transcriptomics. Kantz et al. (2019) applied deep neural networks for classifying LC-MS spectral peaks.

Dührkop et al. (2019) developed SIRIUS 4 for interpreting tandem mass spectra, while Liebal et al. (2020) discussed machine learning in mass spectrometry-based metabolomics. Zhong et al. (2021) presented CryoDRGN, a neural network approach for reconstructing cryo-EM structures. Schmauch et al. (2020) introduced a deep learning model predicting RNA-Seq expression from whole slide images.

Das et al. (2021) accelerated antimicrobial discovery through deep generative models, and Gligorijevic et al. (2018) focused on deep network fusion for protein function prediction. Karpathy (2019) and Bengio (2012) provided insights into training neural networks, while Roberts et al. (2021) reviewed machine learning studies on COVID-19 detection, highlighting the clinical utility challenges. Lastly, List et al. (2017) and Sonnenburg et al. (2007) emphasized the importance of usable software and open-source contributions in machine learning, respectively.

This list of references highlights significant contributions to the fields of deep learning, machine learning, and bioinformatics. Key works include a comprehensive review of deep learning by prominent figures LeCun, Bengio, and Hinton, which outlines foundational concepts and advancements. The book "The Elements of Statistical Learning" by Hastie, Tibshirani, and Friedman serves as a critical resource for statistical methods in data mining and prediction.

Several studies focus on specific methodologies, such as Adebayo et al.'s examination of saliency maps, Gal and Ghahramani's work on dropout as a Bayesian approximation, and the comparison of traditional machine learning approaches against deep learning for phenotype prediction by Smith et al. Notable statistical techniques like the lasso and elastic net for regression are discussed by Tibshirani and Zou & Hastie, respectively.

Support vector machines are introduced by Noble and further explored by Ben-Hur and colleagues, emphasizing their applications in computational biology. Other significant contributions include a framework for assessing genetic variant pathogenicity by Kircher et al., automated detection of cellular motifs by Driscoll et al., and a review of supervised machine learning methods by Bzdok et al.

The references also cover advancements in protein-ligand interactions, multi-omic clustering algorithms, and sensitive protein sequence searching, illustrating the integration of machine learning in various biological contexts. Overall, these works collectively advance the understanding and application of computational methods in bioinformatics and related fields.

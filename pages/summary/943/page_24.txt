The paper presents a flowchart designed to assist researchers in selecting and training a machine learning model. It outlines a general procedure for training and emphasizes that the flowchart serves as a visual guide, but it cannot encompass every scenario. Notably, the applicability of machine learning is influenced by the number of data points relative to the features available; more features necessitate a larger dataset. Additionally, the paper highlights the existence of deep learning models capable of operating on unlabelled data, indicating a complexity in model selection that goes beyond simple guidelines.

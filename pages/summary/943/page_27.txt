The encoder compresses the input data into a more manageable form, while the decoder reconstructs the input from this compressed representation. Each of these neural network methods—multilayer perceptrons, convolutional neural networks, recurrent neural networks, graph convolutional networks, and autoencoders—plays a significant role in processing complex biological data, enabling predictions and insights into molecular properties, biological sequences, and interactions within networks. The flexibility and adaptability of these architectures allow researchers to tailor their approaches to the specific characteristics of their data, thereby enhancing the efficacy of their analyses in fields such as drug discovery and genomics.

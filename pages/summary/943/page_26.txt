The paper discusses traditional machine learning methods, highlighting various techniques and their applications. 

1. **Regression**: This method identifies the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables, such as predicting a person's weight based on their height.

2. **Support Vector Machine (SVM)**: SVM transforms input data to create a latent representation, ensuring that data from different categories are separated by the widest possible gap. An example provided is predicting whether a protein is ordered or disordered based on this transformation.

3. **Gradient Boosting**: This technique utilizes an ensemble of weak prediction models, commonly decision trees, to enhance prediction accuracy. It can predict active drugs from molecular descriptors like molecular weight and specific chemical groups, combining individual predictors in a stage-wise manner.

4. **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**: PCA identifies combinations of features that best describe the data while remaining orthogonal. It is often used for dimensionality reduction, illustrating how height and weight correlate, while also allowing for the examination of additional variables like body fat percentage.

5. **Clustering**: This method groups similar objects using various algorithms, such as categorizing cell types based on gene expression profiles.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the utility of these methods in predictive modeling and data analysis.

This paper provides a comprehensive introduction to machine learning for biologists, highlighting its growing importance due to the increasing complexity and scale of biological data. The authors aim to demystify various machine learning techniques, particularly focusing on deep neural networks, and how they can be applied to biological data. The discussion includes the suitability of different methodologies for specific types of data, best practices for conducting machine learning experiments, and emerging trends in the field. By offering these insights, the paper serves as a valuable resource for biologists looking to leverage machine learning in their research.

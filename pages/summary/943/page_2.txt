The introduction highlights the parallels between human learning and machine learning, illustrating how both processes involve building and refining models based on observation and experience. The paper underlines the growing relevance of machine learning in biology due to the increasing size and complexity of biological datasets, which surpass human analytical capabilities. It emphasizes the need for biologists to understand and effectively utilize machine learning methods, while avoiding common pitfalls that can compromise study validity.

The review aims to connect machine learning techniques to various biological data types, offering practical guidance on model training and improvement. It discusses both traditional machine learning methods and the rising prominence of deep learning, providing examples of their applications and interpretations. Ultimately, the article seeks to equip biologists with the knowledge to leverage machine learning in their research, enhancing their ability to analyze and interpret biological data.

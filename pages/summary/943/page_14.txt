In summary, the successful application of machine learning in biology requires careful consideration of data availability and the potential for data leakage. The choice of model and training data should align with the specific research questions, and the amount of data available influences the techniques employed. Smaller datasets necessitate traditional machine learning methods, while larger datasets allow for more complex models. Additionally, researchers must rigorously validate model performance to avoid overestimating accuracy due to data leakage, especially in contexts where related samples might be inadvertently included in both training and test sets. Addressing these challenges is crucial for ensuring robust and generalizable predictions in biological research.

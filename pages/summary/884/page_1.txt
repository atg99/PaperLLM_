Our findings reveal that the key difference lies in the presence of residual connections in ResNet architectures, which result in feature maps with low entropy, making them unsuitable for effective style transfer. We propose a solution involving a softmax transformation applied to the feature activations, which enhances their entropy and significantly improves stylization results. 

Our experiments demonstrate that this transformation can enhance the quality of style transfer even when using networks initialized with random weights, indicating that the architecture's design for feature extraction plays a more critical role in style transfer efficacy than the learned weights. This work not only addresses the performance gap between VGG and other networks but also offers a pathway to improve the robustness of image style transfer methods across various architectures.

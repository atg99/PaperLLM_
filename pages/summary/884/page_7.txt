For instance, the pre-trained ResNet (p-R) shows an impressive increase in preference from 17.0% to 83.0%, while the pre-trained Wide ResNet (p-W) also demonstrates substantial improvement from 34.7% to 65.3%. The Inception model (p-I) exhibits a smaller enhancement, possibly due to its unique structure with parallel connections that complicate stylization optimization.

The comparison of SWAG with other stylization algorithms indicates that SWAG enhances their performance across various networks. Notably, the state-of-the-art universal stylization method struggles with the pre-trained ResNet, yielding poor results, but SWAG elevates its output to a level comparable to VGG.

The study emphasizes the subjective nature of stylization quality, advocating for user studies as a valid quantitative evaluation approach. In this study, participants from Amazon Mechanical Turk were tasked with selecting images that best matched a given style from pairs synthesized by networks with and without SWAG. The results reveal that SWAG is generally advantageous for stylization, underscoring its effectiveness across different architectures and algorithms.

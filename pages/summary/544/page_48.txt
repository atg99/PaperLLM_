The paper presents a collection of references that contribute to the field of machine learning, specifically focusing on meta-learning, neural architecture search, and classifier selection techniques. Key contributions include:

1. Wistuba et al. (2000) introduce a meta-learning approach that involves landmarking various learning algorithms, providing insights into algorithm selection.
2. Pham et al. (2018) discuss an efficient method for neural architecture search that leverages parameter sharing, enhancing the search process.
3. Ramachandran et al. (2018) explore the search for optimal activation functions, which is critical for improving neural network performance.
4. Rasmussen and Williams (2006) provide a comprehensive guide to Gaussian processes, a fundamental concept in probabilistic modeling.
5. Real et al. (2017) detail the large-scale evolution of image classifiers, showcasing advancements in automated model generation.
6. The work on aging evolution for image classifier architecture search (Real et al., 2019) emphasizes the importance of adaptive architectures.
7. Reif et al. (2014) present automatic classifier selection methods aimed at non-expert users, simplifying the application of machine learning.
8. Rummery and Niranjan (1994) address online Q-learning using connectionist systems, contributing to reinforcement learning methodologies.
9. Russakovsky et al. (2015) discuss the ImageNet challenge, a pivotal event in computer vision that spurred significant advancements in deep learning.
10. Sabharwal et al. (2016) focus on selecting near-optimal learners through incremental data allocation, which is crucial for efficient learning.

These works collectively advance the understanding of algorithm selection, architecture optimization, and automated learning processes in machine learning.

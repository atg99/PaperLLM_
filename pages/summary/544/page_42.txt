Key contributions include:

1. **SMASH**: Introduced by Wistuba et al., this work discusses one-shot model architecture search using hypernetworks, providing a framework for efficient architecture exploration.
   
2. **Efficient Architecture Search by Network Transformation**: Cai et al. present a method that transforms existing networks to search for optimal architectures, emphasizing efficiency in the search process.

3. **Path-level Network Transformation**: This follows from the previous work by Cai et al., focusing on transforming networks at the path level to enhance architecture search efficiency.

4. **ProxylessNAS**: In this work, Cai, Zhu, and Han introduce a direct approach to neural architecture search that targets specific tasks and hardware without using proxy tasks.

5. **Learnable Embedding Space**: Cao et al. propose a method for efficient neural architecture compression by utilizing a learnable embedding space.

6. **Probabilistic Neural Architecture Search**: Casale et al. explore a probabilistic approach to NAS, aiming to enhance the robustness and effectiveness of the search process.

7. **Extrapolation of Learning Curves**: Chandrashekaran and Lane address hyper-parameter optimization, suggesting methods to speed up the process by leveraging insights from previous builds.

8. **Net2Net**: Chen et al. present a technique for accelerating learning through knowledge transfer between networks, facilitating faster convergence.

9. **Dual Path Networks**: Proposed by Chen et al., this work introduces a novel architecture that effectively combines features from different pathways, enhancing model performance.

These references collectively contribute to the evolving landscape of neural architecture search and optimization, highlighting innovative strategies to improve efficiency and effectiveness in deep learning model design.

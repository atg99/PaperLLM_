The paper provides an overview of Neural Architecture Search (NAS), highlighting its early-stage development and the ongoing challenges in automating deep learning processes. It emphasizes the potential of NAS to enhance practical applications in the field. The references cited include significant works that contribute to the understanding and advancement of NAS, such as reinforcement learning approaches for designing neural networks, performance prediction techniques to accelerate NAS, and various optimization strategies. The authors suggest that future research will further illuminate the relevance of NAS in real-world scenarios, indicating a promising direction for the field.

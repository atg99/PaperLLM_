This paper presents a comprehensive survey on Neural Architecture Search (NAS), highlighting the growing automation in machine learning and deep learning. The authors emphasize the critical role of network architecture in enhancing deep learning performance, noting that traditional methods require extensive computational resources and domain expertise. The survey categorizes existing NAS methods and provides an in-depth analysis, discussing various architecture search spaces and optimization algorithms, particularly those based on reinforcement learning and evolutionary strategies. It also covers innovative approaches involving surrogate and one-shot models. Furthermore, the paper explores emerging research directions, including constrained and multi-objective architecture search, as well as automated searches for data augmentation, optimizers, and activation functions. The survey aims to make deep learning more accessible by partially automating the architecture selection process, thereby reducing the complexity associated with manual feature engineering.

The table presents the results of various neural architecture search methods and human-designed architectures evaluated on the ImageNet dataset. Key metrics include Top 1 and Top 5 accuracy percentages, the number of parameters in millions, image size, and GPU days required for training.

- Comparatively, human-designed architectures, such as those from Howard et al. and Zhang et al., achieved lower accuracies, with the highest being 81.5%.

Overall, architectures discovered through neural architecture search methods generally outperformed traditional human-designed models in terms of accuracy, albeit often at the cost of increased computational resources.

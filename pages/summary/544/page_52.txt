This document references a collection of significant works in the field of neural networks and machine learning, highlighting various approaches to enhancing model performance and architecture. 

Key contributions include:

1. **Shakedrop Regularization** by Wistuba et al., which introduces a novel method for regularizing deep networks.
2. **Wide Residual Networks** by Zagoruyko and Komodakis, proposing wider architectures to improve training efficiency and performance.
3. **Graph Hypernetworks** by Zhang et al., focusing on optimizing neural architecture search using hypernetworks.
4. **Mixup** by Zhang et al., presenting a technique that goes beyond traditional empirical risk minimization to improve generalization.
5. **ShuffleNet** by Zhang et al., which is designed for efficient computation on mobile devices through a novel architecture.
6. **Polynet** by Zhang et al., which emphasizes structural diversity in very deep networks.
7. **Block-Wise Neural Network Architecture Generation** by Zhong et al., exploring practical methods for generating neural network architectures.
8. **Resource-Efficient Neural Architect** by Zhou et al., aiming for efficient design in neural architectures.
9. **Neural Architecture Search with Reinforcement Learning** by Zoph and Le, introducing a framework for discovering optimal architectures.
10. **Learning Transferable Architectures** by Zoph et al., which focuses on scalable image recognition through transferable architecture designs.

These works collectively contribute to advancing the understanding and capabilities of neural network architectures, emphasizing efficiency, generalization, and innovative training techniques.

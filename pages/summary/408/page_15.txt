The advancements in YOLO technology have significantly enhanced the capabilities of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) in both transportation and agriculture sectors. In transportation, various studies have focused on road safety inspections, vehicle detection, and traffic management, demonstrating high metrics such as mean Average Precision (mAP) and accuracy. The use of YOLO models has proven to decrease costs associated with traffic video acquisition and processing while improving the spatial flexibility of traffic supervision.

In agriculture, the application of YBUT (You Only Look But You Transform) technology addresses critical issues like pest and disease detection, which can prevent economic losses and reduce pesticide use. Drones utilizing YOLO algorithms can efficiently identify pests, diseases, and valuable plant species. This technology is also being applied to monitor various agricultural tasks, such as detecting dead trees, monitoring plant health, and counting moving targets like animals. Overall, the integration of YOLO-based UAV technology in these fields shows substantial potential for improving efficiency, safety, and productivity.

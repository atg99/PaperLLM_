The referenced papers focus on the application of deep learning and computer vision techniques in drone imagery for various agricultural and ecological tasks. Key studies include the detection of date palm trees, oil palm fruits, and weeds using advanced models such as YOLO and deep neural networks. Research highlights the automation of identifying crops and animals, including a system for recognizing big mammals and classifying cattle through hybrid algorithms. Other notable works involve the detection of vegetation, snow breakage, and even litter using UAV imagery, showcasing the versatility of drones in environmental monitoring. Additionally, some studies explore pedestrian tracking and obstacle detection in urban settings, emphasizing real-time applications of UAV technology. Overall, the integration of deep learning with drone imagery presents significant advancements in precision agriculture, wildlife monitoring, and environmental management.

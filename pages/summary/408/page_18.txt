The paper discusses the applications of YOLO-based Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) in agriculture and automation, highlighting their potential in developing smart agricultural practices. Despite being in the early stages, challenges such as dataset collection and algorithm stability are acknowledged. The integration of computer technology and YOLO's object detection capabilities aims to reduce human labor and enhance productivity in various industries.

In automation, significant advancements have been made in detecting, tracking, and avoiding specific targets, with successful applications noted in litter detection and management using drones. For instance, YOLOv4 has been utilized to locate litter in parks, while an improved YOLO algorithm was employed for marine litter detection. The paper presents a comprehensive overview of research in this area, detailing various YOLO models and their purposes.

Furthermore, the paper explores innovative uses of YBUT in diverse fields, including GPS-limited target tracking and disaster damage assessment. The successful applications of YBUT indicate a promising future for unmanned automation, emphasizing the need for further research to expand operational capabilities, develop specialized drones, and create efficient control systems. Overall, the findings underscore the potential of YBUT to alleviate labor demands and boost productivity across multiple sectors.

The paper discusses the application of YBUT technology in urban traffic and road management, emphasizing its role in enhancing intelligent traffic management systems. It highlights how automatic detection and tracking of vehicles can enforce road management rules and facilitate the punishment of traffic violations, contributing to the development of civilized urban environments.

Key research contributions mentioned include the use of various YOLO models for specific applications, such as Silva et al.'s distributed UAV platform utilizing YOLOv4 for road damage detection, and Zhao et al.'s YOLOv3-based algorithm for detecting highway center marks. Ma et al.'s recent method for road damage detection demonstrated superior performance compared to earlier studies, indicating advancements in road management technology.

The paper underscores that effective urban traffic management encompasses not only vehicles but also the condition and management of urban roads. The proliferation of YBUT technology significantly advances intelligent traffic management and improves the quality of life for residents. A summary table provides an overview of various studies employing YOLO models in transportation, detailing their objectives, metrics, and the sensors used, highlighting the breadth of research in this domain.

The paper presents an overview of various research fields related to drone technology, as evidenced by the screened articles organized in a detailed table. This table categorizes subjects into 41 fields, including Engineering, Computer Science, Environmental Sciences, and more, highlighting the interdisciplinary nature of drone research. The screening process was rigorous, eliminating irrelevant articles and focusing on those that contributed significant insights into the topic, particularly those with innovative algorithmic advancements.

The methodology employed for literature review involved thorough checks on the content and datasets used in the articles, ensuring that only those with substantial relevance were included. Additionally, the research utilized both English and Chinese databases, such as Web of Science and China National Knowledge Infrastructure, to identify key research themes across different languages.

A notable focus of the research is on the development of computer vision technologies, particularly the YOLO algorithm, which was integrated with UAVs starting in 2017. This integration has led to a notable increase in research and applications, transitioning from experimental phases to becoming a significant academic focus, as depicted in the accompanying figures. The paper emphasizes the growing importance of such technologies in advancing the drone industry.

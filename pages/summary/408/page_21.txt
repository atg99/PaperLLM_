Firstly, it emphasizes the need for professional, stable, and reliable UAVs. This involves improving the overall design of UAVs to ensure adaptability and stability in various operational environments. Key components such as power systems and onboard equipment, including multisensor fusion technology, should be developed to enhance practicality and safety. The establishment of robust safety flight algorithms and self-protection systems is crucial to prevent loss of control and data leakage.

Secondly, the paper highlights the importance of enhancing security measures within the YBUT framework. As UAVs heavily rely on wireless communication, they are vulnerable to interference and hacking, posing risks not only to the UAVs themselves but also to surrounding environments and operators. Therefore, strengthening data security during storage and transmission is paramount.

Lastly, the paper calls for the popularization of UAV and YOLO algorithm knowledge through education and training, alongside the establishment of relevant laws and regulations. This is vital for cultivating skilled personnel who can effectively operate UAVs and utilize YOLO algorithms, ensuring the responsible and legal use of UAV technology.

In conclusion, the rapid development of UAV technology necessitates a comprehensive approach that integrates advanced productivity, security, education, and regulation to foster sustainable growth in this emerging field.

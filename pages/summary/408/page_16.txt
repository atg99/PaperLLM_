The paper discusses the advancements in YOLO-based UAV (drone) applications, particularly in the transport and agricultural sectors. It highlights significant research progress in traffic management, where YOLO algorithms have achieved high accuracy metrics, thus reducing costs and enhancing the flexibility of traffic supervision. Despite limited experimentation, the potential for broader applications beyond vehicle inspection is acknowledged.

In agriculture, the paper emphasizes the importance of early pest and disease detection to prevent economic losses. The integration of drones with YOLO algorithms facilitates efficient agricultural practices, including the detection of various plant species, pests, and moving targets like animals, which can aid in effective management and monitoring. Examples include detecting diseased trees, weeds in crops, and monitoring wildlife, showcasing the versatility of YBUT (YOLO-based UAV technology).

While the applications of YBUT in agriculture are still in the early stages and face challenges such as data collection and algorithm stability, the authors are optimistic about its potential to contribute to smart agriculture and expand its usage in agricultural practices. The paper concludes by underscoring the broad development opportunities for YOLO-based applications in the agricultural sector.

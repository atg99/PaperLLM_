The references listed encompass a range of studies focused on advancements in real-time object detection and classification using Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) and various algorithms, particularly those based on the YOLO (You Only Look Once) framework. Key contributions include the use of synthetic training images to enhance detection capabilities, the evaluation of image quality and super-resolution effects on object recognition, and the development of compact and efficient object detection networks. Several studies explore specific applications such as wildfire detection, vehicle detection, and the localization of UAVs using visual methods.

Noteworthy methodologies include the implementation of ensemble CNNs for smoke detection, the adaptation of YOLO models for thermal infrared imagery, and improvements in detection speed with fine-tuning of YOLOv3. The works also highlight the importance of model pruning and layered object generation for better anchor creation in aerial images. Additionally, there is a focus on automated detection of aviation obstacles and the perception of fast-moving objects, showcasing the growing significance of UAVs in various fields, including agriculture and security.

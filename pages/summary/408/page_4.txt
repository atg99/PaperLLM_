The paper presents an overview of the history and application of YBUT (a technology related to aerial robots or UAVs) across various industry sectors. It aims to enhance understanding for researchers, beginners, and consumers, while also serving as a reference for future research and application. The authors discuss the current state of YBUT, its case studies, and offer insights into its future directions.

In the methodology section, the authors outline their approach to selecting relevant literature on YBUT. They implemented a systematic screening process to efficiently identify high-impact research papers that utilize deep learning YOLO models and algorithms. The keywords for the search were sourced from reputable journals and databases, and these were organized into groups for comprehensive literature searches.

The search yielded 243 articles from 41 research fields as of February 2023. The authors filtered these results to exclude irrelevant topics and rigorously reviewed the content to ensure relevance to their research focus. They analyzed the articles for algorithmic advancements and selected those deemed progressive or applicable to industry development. The methodology for this screening process is detailed and visually represented in the paper.

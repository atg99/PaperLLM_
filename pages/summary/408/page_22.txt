This literature review explores the integration of deep learning YOLO algorithms with UAV technology, highlighting its potential for practical applications that meet societal needs. The paper details the evolution of YOLO-based UAV technology (YBUT) and emphasizes its promising utility in enhancing productivity and convenience across various fields, particularly engineering, transportation, agriculture, and automation.

The review indicates that while UAV technology and YOLO algorithms are established in their domains, their combination is increasingly significant for developing new technological solutions. The authors note a strong complementary relationship between UAV platforms and YOLO algorithms for object detection. However, challenges remain in application methodology and performance, particularly in the need for higher quality datasets for training UAV-based detection models.

Future advancements in YBUT should focus on specific use-case drones, the optimization of YOLO algorithms for mobile processors, and the cultivation of talent in this area to facilitate rapid technology diffusion. The authors argue that addressing emerging problems and needs during this diffusion process will further drive technological progress.

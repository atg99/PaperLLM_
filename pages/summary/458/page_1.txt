This paper presents a comprehensive survey on modality-specific U-Net variants for biomedical image segmentation, highlighting the advancements in deep learning techniques that have enhanced the automation of identifying target regions in medical imaging. U-Net architectures have emerged as a leading approach in this field, demonstrating exceptional performance in applications related to early diagnosis and treatment of various diseases, including brain tumors, lung cancer, Alzheimer's, and breast cancer.

The authors delve into the U-Net framework and categorize its variants through inter-modality and intra-modality analyses, shedding light on the challenges and solutions associated with biomedical image segmentation. They also discuss the role of U-Net frameworks in addressing the COVID-19 pandemic. The paper concludes by examining the strengths and similarities of U-Net variants, along with the challenges faced in the field, and proposes promising directions for future research in biomedical image segmentation.

In summary, recent advancements in brain tumor segmentation have leveraged models like U-Net and its variants, such as U-SegNet and modified U-Net, which incorporate multi-modality fusion to enhance segmentation accuracy. The integration of residual blocks and strided convolutions has further refined these models, although some studies noted limitations in 3D volumetric analysis. Attention mechanisms, as seen in the Deep Attention U-Net and USE-Net, have also been employed to improve segmentation performance by enhancing feature extraction and generalization across diverse datasets. These innovations collectively represent a significant leap forward in the automation of medical image analysis, particularly for brain and prostate cancer diagnostics.

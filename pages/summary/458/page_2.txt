The paper discusses the advancements in medical imaging and the role of deep learning, specifically U-Net based frameworks, in enhancing biomedical image segmentation. It highlights the complexity and time-consuming nature of analyzing various imaging modalities such as MRI, CT, and PET and emphasizes the need for automated assistance in diagnostics. The authors categorize segmentation into semantic and instance segmentation, detailing how U-Net's encoder-decoder structure excels in state-of-the-art performance.

The motivation behind the study is to provide a comprehensive analysis of modality-specific U-Net approaches, distinguishing it from existing reviews that primarily focus on general methods. The paper aims to facilitate a deeper understanding of the mechanics behind successful segmentation systems, offering insights into both general and modality-specific challenges faced in biomedical image segmentation.

The review process for the survey includes only those articles that present U-Net based segmentation research with significant advancements, excluding purely discussion-based articles. This ensures a focused and relevant examination of the current landscape in biomedical image analysis using deep learning techniques.

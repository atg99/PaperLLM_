Attention U-Nets focus on enhancing feature discrimination through preprocessing techniques and advanced segmentation methods, while Inception U-Nets utilize dual encoder networks to extract high-level features and expand the network's capacity through dilated convolutions. Ensemble U-Nets like CardioXNet demonstrate the effectiveness of combining multiple models to tackle specific medical imaging challenges, ultimately leading to enhanced performance in tasks such as pneumonia detection and cardiomegaly localization.

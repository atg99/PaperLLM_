In another approach, the ensemble U-Net models utilize multiple U-Net architectures to enhance segmentation performance by combining the outputs of different models, thereby leveraging their individual strengths. This method has shown to be effective in various biomedical imaging tasks, particularly in scenarios where the variability of structures is high, leading to more robust segmentation results.

In summary, recent advancements in U-Net variants for biomedical image segmentation have focused on integrating various techniques such as deformable convolutions, multi-scale attention mechanisms, spatiotemporal separable convolutions, and ensemble strategies. These innovations aim to improve segmentation accuracy, efficiency, and the ability to handle complex anatomical variations in medical images, thereby enhancing the overall performance of segmentation tasks in the biomedical field.

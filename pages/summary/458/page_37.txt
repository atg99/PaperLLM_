The paper discusses the advancements in biomedical image segmentation through various U-Net variants, emphasizing the model's potential for clinical applications. U-Net, a significant breakthrough in deep learning, serves as a foundation for developing novel architectures aimed at identifying and localizing target regions in biomedical images. The authors explore different U-Net variants tailored to address challenges posed by various imaging modalities. They argue that due to their high utility, U-Net-based models are expected to play a crucial role in tackling complex issues in biomedical image segmentation, thereby contributing to the development of effective computer-aided diagnosis systems.

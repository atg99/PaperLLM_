The paper discusses various modalities of U-Net variants tailored for biomedical image analysis, emphasizing the importance of different datasets available for training and evaluation. It provides a comprehensive summary of popular biomedical image segmentation datasets, detailing their descriptions and availability. Key datasets include ISBI 2012 for cell segmentation, CVC-ClinicDB for polyp detection, and BraTS for brain tumor segmentation, among others. Each dataset is linked to its respective source for accessibility. The paper highlights the significance of these datasets in advancing research and development in biomedical imaging, facilitating the training of models for specific tasks such as tumor and lesion segmentation across various imaging modalities.

The paper presents a comparison of various quantization methods on ImageNet, specifically focusing on their performance in terms of Top1 and Top5 accuracy as well as the degradation in Top1 accuracy. The methods evaluated include LQ-Nets, RQ, RQ+ST, and the authors' proposed methods LLSQ and LLSQF across different models such as ResNet18, ResNet34, and AlexNet.

Key findings include:

- For ResNet18, LLSQ achieved a Top1 accuracy of 69.84% with 4 bits per weight and minimal degradation of 0.08%. In contrast, RQ with the same bit configuration showed a significant degradation of 8.02%.
- LLSQF demonstrated competitive results with 69.40% Top1 accuracy at 4 bits for ResNet34, with a degradation of 0.36%.
- On AlexNet, LLSQ yielded a Top1 accuracy of 56.57% with 4 bits, showing negligible degradation.
- In the case of MobileNet(v2), LLSQ maintained a Top1 accuracy of 71.20% at 6 bits, with a degradation of only 0.60%.

Overall, LLSQ and LLSQF methods consistently show strong performance, with low degradation across various models and configurations, indicating their effectiveness in quantization tasks.

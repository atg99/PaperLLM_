The paper presents a method for training neural networks with quantized weights and activations, focusing on efficient gradient simulation and backpropagation. The process begins by quantizing weights and biases according to specified equations, followed by generating simulated gradients for these quantized parameters. The method utilizes a ReLU activation function and convolution operations to compute activations, which are also quantized. During the backward pass, gradients for weights, biases, and activations are generated using established equations and the backpropagation algorithm. The training iteration continues until a predetermined maximum number of iterations is reached, typically set to the length of the dataset divided by the batch size. This approach aims to optimize neural network training while maintaining performance despite the quantization of parameters.

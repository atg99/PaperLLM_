The paper presents a detailed analysis of the training times for different quantization methods applied to neural networks, specifically ResNet18 and AlexNet. It highlights the efficiency of the proposed Low-Latency Simulated Quantization (LLSQ) method, which requires approximately two-thirds of the training epochs compared to traditional floating-point network training. 

Key findings include that the training time for quantized versions of ResNet18 is about 70% of the baseline, indicating significant time savings when using LLSQ. The methodology involves quantizing weights and biases according to specified algorithms, with the quantization process being less time-consuming than training from scratch. 

The paper also emphasizes the importance of maintaining Dropout layers during quantization to prevent overfitting, and it reports that test accuracy reaches acceptable levels after just one epoch of training with pre-trained full-precision weights. 

Overall, the results demonstrate that the LLSQ algorithm is an effective approach for efficiently training quantized neural networks while achieving competitive accuracy on benchmark datasets like ImageNet.

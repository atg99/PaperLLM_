The paper presents a method for quantization in neural networks, specifically focusing on a technique called Layer-wise Learning Rate Scaling Quantization (LLSQ). The quantization process involves minimizing the differences between the original and quantized weights and activations across multiple layers. The method employs bit-shift quantization, which is mathematically defined to clamp and round the scaling factors based on a specified bitwidth.

The algorithm begins with the input dataset and pre-trained full-precision parameters, including weights and biases. The primary goal is to produce quantized scaling factors and weights. The process is divided into two main steps: 

1. **Quantization and Re-training**: This step involves quantizing the weights and activations of the neural network, leveraging Batch Normalization (BN) layers to facilitate faster convergence and improved accuracy. The forward pass includes quantization of weights and activations, while the backward pass focuses on generating gradients and updating parameters through backpropagation.

2. **Bias and Scaling Factor Quantization**: After fusing the Batch Normalization parameters, the algorithm quantizes the biases and scaling factors. This step also includes a forward pass for quantization followed by the appropriate updates to the model parameters.

The algorithm iterates through these steps until a maximum number of iterations is reached, ensuring that the quantized model retains performance comparable to its full-precision counterpart. The paper highlights the efficiency and effectiveness of the proposed quantization method, making it suitable for deploying neural networks in resource-constrained environments.

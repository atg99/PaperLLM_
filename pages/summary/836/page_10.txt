The paper presents a novel approach to quantizing neural networks using learned linear symmetric quantization (LLSQ) to optimize network performance while minimizing resource requirements. It details the implementation of low-precision integer neural network processors, specifically focusing on 8-bit, 4-bit, and 2-bit configurations synthesized using Synopsys Design Compiler under 40nm technology. The study demonstrates that the 4-bit and 2-bit processors achieve significant reductions in silicon area and power consumption when compared to the 8-bit baseline, with 2.56 times lower silicon area and 5.56 times lower power for the 4/2-bit implementations.

The research emphasizes the effectiveness of quantization in maintaining output accuracy with minimal degradation, facilitated by linear symmetric quantization, which allows straightforward multiply-accumulate operations. The authors validate their approach across multiple datasets, including Cifar10, ImageNet, and Pascal VOC, highlighting that linear symmetric quantization can outperform asymmetric or non-linear methods in 4-bit networks.

The paper concludes with an acknowledgment of the varying sensitivity of different network layers to quantization bitwidth and expresses intent to explore frameworks for more flexible bitwidth configurations in future work.

In contrast, this survey by Wang et al. systematically categorizes deep reinforcement learning algorithms and applications. The authors divide the algorithms into three main categories: model-based methods, model-free methods, and advanced RL methods. 

They analyze significant advances in the field, including exploration techniques, inverse reinforcement learning, and transfer learning. The paper highlights the versatility of deep RL, showcasing its applications in various domains such as robotics, recommendation systems, and natural language dialogues. 

Additionally, the authors identify four open problems that warrant further research, emphasizing the need for continued innovation in deep RL to address these challenges. Overall, the survey serves as a comprehensive resource for understanding the current landscape and future directions of deep reinforcement learning.

The paper presents a comprehensive overview of advancements in deep reinforcement learning (DRL), emphasizing its evolution and key methodologies. It highlights significant contributions from various researchers, including Mnih et al.'s work on human-level control through DRL and the development of asynchronous methods for enhancing learning efficiency. The paper discusses various approaches, such as value and policy-based reinforcement learning, meta-reinforcement learning, and model-based techniques. It also covers innovative exploration strategies, including curiosity-driven exploration and the use of imagination-augmented agents to improve agent performance in complex environments. The references cited illustrate a breadth of methodologies and applications in the field, showcasing the ongoing research aimed at optimizing DRL algorithms and their practical implementations in areas like robotics and speech synthesis.

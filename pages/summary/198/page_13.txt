5.3 Computer Systems
In computer systems, RL has been utilized for optimizing resource management and enhancing system performance. For instance, RL algorithms can dynamically allocate resources in cloud computing environments, ensuring efficient utilization and reducing costs. Furthermore, RL has been employed in system security, where it can adaptively respond to threats by learning from previous attacks and improving defense mechanisms.

Overall, the applications of RL are diverse and impactful, spanning across gaming, robotics, natural language processing, and computer systems. The integration of RL into these fields has not only advanced theoretical understanding but also provided practical solutions to complex real-world problems.

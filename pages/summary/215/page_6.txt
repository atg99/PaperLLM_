The paper presents a comparison of training loss over epochs for a ResNet architecture, referred to as ResNet Net 1, alongside its equivalent convolutional network (ConvNet). The analysis is grounded in existing literature on deep learning frameworks, including foundational works on very deep convolutional networks, highway networks, and techniques for visualizing convolutional networks. These references provide context and support for the methodologies employed in the study, highlighting advancements in image recognition and network architecture understanding.

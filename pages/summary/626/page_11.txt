The paper discusses the challenges posed by the greedy nature of learning in multi-modal deep neural networks and proposes methods to enhance their ability to integrate information from various modalities. The authors emphasize the importance of effectively utilizing diverse data sources, particularly in applications like breast cancer screening, where multi-view information can significantly improve diagnostic accuracy. They reference previous works, including Wu et al. on volumetric shapes and Zhang et al. on weight decay regularization, to illustrate the underlying principles and mechanisms that can be leveraged to overcome the limitations of greedy learning. The core argument revolves around the necessity for deep networks to adopt more holistic approaches to learning that account for the interdependencies among different data views, thereby improving performance in complex tasks.

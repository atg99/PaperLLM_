The paper presents a study on multi-modal deep neural networks, focusing on the challenges posed by the greedy nature of learning. In the data preparation section, three datasets are utilized: Colored MNIST, ModelNet40, and NVGesture. For both Colored MNIST and ModelNet40, the original datasets were used without additional preprocessing. The NVGesture dataset consists of videos with a resolution of 240x320 and 80 frames, where RGB and depth modalities have unmatched starting indices. The authors selected the starting frame index from the RGB videos and randomly picked 64 consecutive frames, applying zero-padding if necessary. All frames were resized to 256x256 and cropped to 224x224 for input, ensuring consistency across modalities. The Colored MNIST dataset includes monochromatic and gray-scale images, ModelNet40 features 2D representations from various viewpoints, and NVGesture incorporates depth and RGB channels.

To address this issue, the paper introduces the concept of conditional learning speed, a proxy for conditional utilization rate that quantifies how quickly a model learns from each modality. The authors propose an algorithm aimed at balancing the conditional learning speeds across modalities during training. This approach is designed to mitigate the greedy learning behavior identified in multi-modal deep neural networks.

Empirical results demonstrate that the proposed algorithm effectively improves model generalization across three distinct datasets: Colored MNIST, ModelNet40, and NVIDIA Dynamic Hand Gesture. The findings underscore the importance of addressing the greedy nature of learning in multi-modal frameworks to enhance their performance in real-world applications where multiple modalities are present.

The paper introduces a novel approach to feature extraction in network-level traffic state analysis using capsule networks, where the squashing activation function effectively normalizes output vectors to reflect the presence probabilities of local features. The dynamic routing algorithm plays a crucial role in establishing connections between primary and advanced capsules, allowing for the identification of features that significantly influence traffic conditions, such as congestion. Each step of the routing process is designed to iteratively refine the coupling coefficients, ensuring that the model accurately captures the relationships among distant links and improves its predictive capabilities in traffic scenarios.

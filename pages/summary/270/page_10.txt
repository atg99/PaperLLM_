The paper by X. Ma et al. presents a prediction model for traffic data using a combination of Capsule Networks (CapsNet) and Neural Long Short-Term Memory (NLSTM) networks. The architecture includes fully connected layers (FC) that facilitate the prediction of future traffic states based on historical data.

In the empirical study, the authors describe the dataset collected from GPS devices on floating vehicles, with data upload intervals ranging from 10 seconds to 1 minute. To improve prediction accuracy, the data was aggregated to 2-minute intervals, capturing traffic variations effectively. The dataset was split into training and testing subsets, with training data from June 2015 and testing data from August 2015. The study focused on a roadway network in Beijing, comprising 278 links. After processing, the traffic states were represented as images sized 164 × 148 for each 2-minute interval.

The model utilized a time lag of 15, indicating that the previous 30 minutes of traffic data were used to predict speeds for the next 2, 10, and 20 minutes, corresponding to specific time lags. The implementation of the deep learning model was carried out using Python Keras on a powerful server equipped with 8 NVIDIA GeForce Titan X GPUs, ensuring effective processing of the model parameters.

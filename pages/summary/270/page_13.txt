The study by X. Ma et al. demonstrates that the CapsNet+NLSTM model outperforms other models, specifically CNN+LSTM, in traffic prediction accuracy, achieving lower Mean Squared Error (MSE) and Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE). The CapsNet model shows a marked improvement over CNN, with reductions in MSE of 16.6%, 9.8%, and 8.4%, highlighting its superior capability in extracting spatial features. However, the prediction error increases with the prediction horizon, indicating that temporal features become increasingly important as the prediction step size grows.

In the evaluation of CapsNet, the authors visualized prediction results, showing that the CapsNet+NLSTM model significantly reduces the number of inaccurate predictions compared to CNN+LSTM, particularly in complex road networks. While the CNN+LSTM model highlighted 83 inaccurate links, the CapsNet+NLSTM model only identified 17, demonstrating its effectiveness in handling overlapping areas and links that are not spatially connected.

Furthermore, the performance of NLSTM was assessed against LSTM using 40-minute historical traffic speeds as input. Overall, the findings suggest that the CapsNet+NLSTM model not only requires fewer parameters than CNN+LSTM but also offers superior performance in traffic state prediction, indicating its potential for practical applications.

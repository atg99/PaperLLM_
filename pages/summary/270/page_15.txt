The paper discusses potential improvements to the dynamic routing algorithm within Capsule Networks (CapsNet) to enhance prediction accuracy and model efficiency. This algorithm is identified as a critical component of CapsNet. Additionally, the authors plan to explore the interpretation of spatiotemporal features learned by CapsNet and Neural Long Short-Term Memory (NLSTM) networks in future research.

The research is supported by several funding bodies, including the National Natural Science Foundation of China and the Beijing Natural Science Foundation. Acknowledgments are given to the Future Transportation and Urban Computing Joint Lab and AutoNavi company for providing data support.

The references cited encompass various studies related to traffic prediction, including the use of neural networks and other methodologies in transportation engineering, indicating a broad foundation of prior research in this domain.

The paper presents a CapsNet+NLSTM deep learning model, detailing its structure and training methodology. The model accepts input images with three dimensions, specifically the resolution and channel count. It employs the RMSprop optimizer with a learning rate of 0.001 and a decay parameter of 0.5 every 20 epochs, using a batch size of 32. To mitigate overfitting, a dropout layer is incorporated, and fivefold cross-validation is utilized to optimize model parameters by dividing the training set into subsets for validation.

The model structure includes several layers: an input layer, convolutional layers, primary capsules, reshaping layers, traffic capsules, NLSTM layers, and fully connected layers, totaling 28,477,526 parameters. 

To evaluate the predictive performance of the CapsNet+NLSTM model, it was compared against five baseline deep neural network models: LSTMs, NLSTM, DCNNs, CapsNet, and CNN+LSTMs. The CNN+LSTM model had approximately half the parameters of the CapsNet+NLSTM model, while the LSTM and NLSTM models maintained the same structure with 800 hidden units. The DCNN and CapsNet models included a flattening layer for integrating outputs across time steps for prediction.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the innovative aspects of the CapsNet+NLSTM model and its comparative performance within the context of existing models.

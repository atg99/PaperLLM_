The collection of studies presented focuses on various methodologies and models for forecasting traffic flow. 

Ma et al. emphasize the development of models tailored for traffic flow prediction, while Williams (2001) evaluates the ARIMAX model's effectiveness in multivariate vehicular traffic flow prediction. Williams et al. (2003) expand on this by applying a seasonal ARIMA process, providing both theoretical underpinnings and empirical results for traffic forecasting.

In another study, Williams et al. (1998) explore urban freeway traffic flow prediction using seasonal autoregressive integrated moving average and exponential smoothing models. Wu et al. (2004) introduce support vector regression for travel-time prediction, highlighting advancements in intelligent transportation systems.

Wu and Tan (2016) propose a hybrid deep learning framework that incorporates spatial-temporal correlation for short-term traffic flow forecasting. Yin et al. (2002) utilize a fuzzy-neural approach for urban traffic flow prediction, showcasing the integration of fuzzy logic with neural networks.

Yu et al. (2017) present spatiotemporal recurrent convolutional networks as a solution for traffic prediction in transportation networks. Zhang et al. (2016) focus on deep spatio-temporal residual networks for predicting citywide crowd flows, indicating a trend towards deep learning techniques.

Zhang et al. (2014) introduce a hybrid forecasting method that combines spectral analysis with a statistical volatility model for short-term traffic flow predictions. Zheng and Su (2014) enhance the k-nearest neighbor approach for short-term traffic volume forecasting using a constrained linearly sewing principle component algorithm.

Finally, Zhu et al. (2014) investigate traffic volume forecasting through a radial basis function neural network, taking into account traffic flows at adjacent intersections. Collectively, these studies illustrate the evolution of traffic forecasting models, incorporating statistical methods, machine learning, and deep learning techniques to improve prediction accuracy.

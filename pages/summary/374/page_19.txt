The paper presents results from the LAMA and CAT evaluations, focusing on performance across various tasks related to factual knowledge and commonsense reasoning. The figures illustrate the average performance of different models, including original RoBERTa, across multiple datasets such as Google RE, SQuAD, T-REx, and ConceptNet. Performance metrics are depicted as accuracy rates against the number of pretraining steps, with data smoothed using an exponential moving average for clarity. Notably, the results indicate variations in effectiveness depending on the training dataset, with specific tasks like Conjunction Acceptability and Winograd showing distinct accuracy levels. Overall, the study emphasizes the influence of pretraining on model performance in handling commonsense reasoning and factual accuracy across diverse domains.

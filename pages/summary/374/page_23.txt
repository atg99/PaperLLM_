The paper presents performance results of the OLM PICS model on various reasoning tasks across different corpus sizes. The results indicate varying accuracy levels for tasks such as taxonomy conjunction, antonym negation, objects comparison, and others. Notable performance metrics include a peak accuracy of 0.65 for objects comparison and 0.4 for always never. The analysis also shows the impact of pretraining steps on model performance, with a general trend of improving accuracy as the corpus size increases. The average performance across all tasks is calculated and smoothed using an exponential moving average with a coefficient of 0.5, highlighting the effectiveness of the model in reasoning tasks based on the size of the training data.

The paper presents a series of performance evaluations of various models on commonsense reasoning tasks using metrics such as accuracy across different datasets. Key findings include the effectiveness of the checkpoints compared to the original RoBERTaBASE model, highlighting improvements in accuracy for tasks like sense making, argument evaluation, and conjunction acceptability. Figures illustrate learning progress over pretraining steps, showing the performance trajectory as models are trained further. The results indicate that as the number of pretraining steps increases, the models generally achieve higher accuracy, demonstrating the importance of extensive training for enhancing reasoning capabilities in AI systems.

The paper presents the results of pretraining RoBERTa on various domains, highlighting the number of saved checkpoints for each dataset. The datasets include NEWS+WIKI+STORIES, NEWS, STORIES, WIKI, and Small WIKI, with respective checkpoint counts of 65, 56, 52, 71, and 52. Additionally, the paper discusses the reproduction of a model based on Liu et al., 2019b, which yielded 62 checkpoints.

Furthermore, the paper outlines the important hyperparameters used during the fine-tuning experiments across different tasks such as CoLA, SST-2, MRPC, WNLI, MNLI, SQuAD, WSC, and ReCoRD. The batch size was consistently set at 32 for most tasks, with the exception of SQuAD, which used a batch size of 12. The number of epochs varied between 2 and 10, and the learning rates ranged from 1e-5 to 3e-5, ensuring reproducibility of the experiments.

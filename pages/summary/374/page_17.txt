The paper presents results of the Linguistics Knowledge Transfer (LKT) across different domains, showcasing performance metrics for various tasks such as Part-of-Speech (POS) tagging, syntactic chunking, Named Entity Recognition (NER), and syntactic arc prediction and classification. The results indicate that as the number of pretraining steps increases, performance metrics such as accuracy and F1 score improve across tasks. The plots illustrate that models trained on diverse datasets like WIKI, STORIES, and NEWS yield varying performance, with the original RoBERTaBASE model outperforming simpler models like Random Vector combined with Linear Classifiers and GloVe with Linear Classifiers. Performance is smoothed using an exponential moving average for clarity, and average performance metrics are derived from all tasks included in the analysis.

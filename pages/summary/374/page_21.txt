The paper presents a detailed analysis of the performance of various linguistic tasks evaluated using different corpus sizes in the BLIMP framework. The results indicate varying accuracy across tasks such as Island Effects, Filler-Gap, Irregular Forms, and others, with performance measured on a scale from 0 to 1. The study highlights how the number of pretraining steps influences model accuracy, with specific attention given to the performance of the RoBERTa model across different datasets including Small WIKI, WIKI, and NEWS combined with WIKI and STORIES. The findings suggest a correlation between corpus size and task performance, evidenced by the smoothed average accuracy depicted in the graphical representation. Overall, this analysis provides insights into the effectiveness of pretraining on linguistic capabilities of models.

In summary, the paper categorizes DNN compression techniques into five primary strategies: network pruning, sparse representation, bits precision, knowledge distillation, and miscellaneous methods. Each category addresses different aspects of model optimization, aiming to reduce storage and computation requirements while maintaining accuracy. The paper provides a comprehensive overview of each technique, including subcategories and existing literature, and concludes with discussions on future directions for DNN compression research.

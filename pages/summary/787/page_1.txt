The paper "Trends in audio signal feature extraction methods" by Garima Sharma, Kartikeyan Umapathy, and Sridhar Krishnan provides a comprehensive overview of audio signal processing, focusing specifically on feature extraction techniques. Audio signal processing is crucial for analyzing various types of audio signals, including music, speech, and environmental sounds. The integration of modern machine learning (ML) algorithms has significantly advanced audio signal analysis and classification.

The authors emphasize that the performance of any ML algorithm is heavily dependent on the quality of the features used for training and testing. Feature extraction is, therefore, a critical component of the ML process, serving as a means of reducing the dimensionality of audio signals while retaining their essential characteristics. This reduction not only helps in managing computational complexity but also enhances the efficiency of ML algorithms for real-time applications.

The paper discusses various domains for feature extraction, including temporal, frequency, cepstral, wavelet, and time-frequency domains. The authors highlight the importance of selecting robust and discriminatory features that enable machines to classify sounds accurately, akin to human perception. They also mention practical applications of speech signals, such as speech recognition, speaker recognition, and human-computer interaction.

In summary, the paper underscores the significance of effective feature extraction methods in audio signal processing and their role in advancing machine hearing capabilities through ML techniques.

This paper provides a comprehensive overview of the thresholds of the human auditory system and the classification of sounds into categories such as speech, music, and environmental sounds. It traces the evolution of audio features from the early 1950s to the present, examining various domains including time, frequency, time-frequency, cepstral, phase, sparse, eigen, wavelet, and image/texture-based features. The review emphasizes the importance of these features for applications in spooﬁng detection and speaker recognition. Additionally, the authors relate current audio features to their extraction algorithms, highlighting their suitability for machine learning and machine hearing. The paper includes benchmarking of the discussed audio features and summarizes their applications, with emphasis on the most prominent features.

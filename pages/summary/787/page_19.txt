This paper discusses various audio types, including speech, music, and environmental sounds, along with their applications and relevant audio features. The authors present algorithms and MATLAB commands for extracting audio features, emphasizing the importance of selecting suitable features for specific applications. Key application areas identified include human speech recognition, music analysis, and environmental sound classification.

For speech applications, significant research areas include speaker and gender recognition, emotion recognition, and speech enhancement, with features like shimmer, fundamental frequency, and MFCCs being highlighted as effective for machine learning. In music applications, the focus is on music segmentation and genre classification, while environmental sound research primarily targets acoustic scene classification and audio fingerprinting.

The paper concludes by noting that not all audio features yield optimal results across applications, urging researchers to identify the most effective features for their specific needs. Future directions include a comprehensive analysis of audio signals and emerging trends in feature extraction methods for machine hearing.

This paper discusses algorithms for analyzing audio signals, specifically focusing on measuring loudness and temporal centroid, as well as exploring auto-correlation based features. 

Algorithm 5 calculates the loudness of an audio signal using MATLAB's built-in function, which takes an audio signal and its sampling frequency as inputs. The result is the integrated loudness of the audio.

Algorithm 6 outlines the process for determining the temporal centroid (TC) of an audio signal. It begins by finding the energy envelope of the signal, multiplying it by the signal itself, and then summing the products. The TC is computed by dividing this sum by the total energy envelope.

Additionally, the paper addresses auto-correlation, which measures the self-similarity of a signal over time. It explains that auto-correlation values range from +1, indicating strong positive correlation, to -1, indicating negative correlation, with a value of 0 showing no correlation. The auto-correlation function is particularly useful for determining periodicity in signals, such as analyzing musical beats. The paper includes figures that illustrate amplitude-based envelope features and auto-correlation values of a speech signal at various time lags.

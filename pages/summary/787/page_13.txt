The paper discusses various algorithms for extracting audio signal features, specifically focusing on OBSC (Octave Band Spectral Contrast) features and cepstral domain features. 

**OBSC Extraction Algorithm**: This algorithm involves framing the audio signal, applying FFT to each frame, dividing the transformed signal into octave-scaled sub-bands, and calculating peak and valley values to derive the OBSC feature set.

**Cepstral Domain Features**: A cepstrum is obtained through the inverse Fourier transform of the logarithm of the signal's spectrum, with power cepstrum being most relevant in speech processing. Cepstral features are utilized in applications like pitch detection, speech recognition, and speech enhancement.

1. **Mel Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCCs)**: MFCCs represent the short-time power spectrum based on a discrete cosine transform of the log power spectrum on a mel scale, closely mimicking human auditory perception. They are widely used in various applications including speech and speaker recognition.

2. **Linear Prediction Cepstral Coefficients (LPCCs)**: LPCCs are derived from linear prediction coefficients (LPCs) and possess advantages such as robustness and compactness, making them suitable for machine learning applications in speech recognition and analysis.

3. **Perceptual Linear Prediction (PLP) Cepstral Coefficients**: PLP coefficients incorporate perceptual processing into the LPC framework, making them effective for applications like emotion recognition and animal sound analysis.

4. **Relative-Spectral PLP (RASTA-PLP) Features**: RASTA-PLP applies a band-pass filter to smooth short-term noise variations and enhance robustness against spectral coloration, making it useful in speech recognition and speaker verification.

Each algorithm includes specific steps for processing the audio signal, ultimately extracting relevant features for various applications in audio signal processing.

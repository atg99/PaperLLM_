The Combination Engine, on the other hand, focuses on performing matrix-vector multiplications (MVMs) effectively. It utilizes shared parameters to optimize the computation and enhance performance. The architecture facilitates communication between the Aggregation and Combination engines through a Coordinator, which ensures smooth data flow and minimizes interference, thereby maintaining high throughput.

In summary, the proposed HyGCN architecture leverages edge- and MVM-centric programming models to enhance performance in GCN execution. By introducing specialized engines for different phases of GCN processing, employing edge-level parallelism, and optimizing memory access, HyGCN aims to significantly improve the efficiency and speed of GCN computations. This design strategy addresses the limitations of existing architectures, paving the way for advanced GCN applications.

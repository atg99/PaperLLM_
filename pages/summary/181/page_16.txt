The references listed in this document primarily focus on advancements in generative models, particularly Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), and their applications across various domains such as image generation, medical imaging, and semantic manipulation. Key contributions include methods for detecting out-of-distribution samples, domain adaptation techniques, and approaches for controlling GAN outputs through latent space manipulation. Notable studies explore the efficacy of rectifier nonlinearities in neural networks, the use of likelihood ratios for anomaly detection, and advancements in artistic generation from images. Additionally, several papers address the challenges of image synthesis from sketches and the exploration of disentangled representations within GANs. Overall, this collection of works illustrates the dynamic progress in generative modeling techniques and their broad applicability in real-world scenarios.

It highlights key properties of these methods, such as semantic awareness, layerwise inversion, and supported resolution, along with the specific GAN models and datasets used for inversion.

Additionally, the paper reviews evaluation metrics for assessing the quality of generated images from GANs, including Inception Score (IS), Fréchet Inception Distance (FID), and Learned Perceptual Image Patch Similarity (LPIPS), which measure diversity, perceptual quality, and similarity to real images, respectively. It also delves into faithfulness, which assesses the similarity between real and generated images using metrics like Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (PSNR) and Structural Similarity Index (SSIM).

Finally, the editability of inverted latent codes is examined, focusing on the flexibility to modify specific attributes in the generated images. The paper emphasizes the challenges in evaluating editability and discusses methods that utilize cosine similarity and Euclidean distance to assess the preservation of attributes such as facial expressions and poses.

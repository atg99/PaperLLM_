The paper discusses neural abstractive text summarization using sequence-to-sequence models. It references various studies in the field, highlighting significant advances and methodologies in automatic summarization. Key contributions include approaches that treat language as a latent variable, end-to-end speech recognition, and the application of deep recurrent neural networks for summarization tasks. The paper also emphasizes the evolution of models such as SummaRuNNer for extractive summarization and the impact of attention mechanisms in generating coherent summaries. Noteworthy works, including those on minimum error rate training and evaluation metrics like BLEU, provide a foundational understanding of the challenges and solutions in summarization. Overall, the paper reflects on the progress made in the area of neural networks for abstractive summarization and outlines future directions for research in this dynamic field.

The paper discusses the REINFORCE algorithm, which aims to optimize parameters to maximize expected rewards in the context of sequential decision-making, particularly in abstractive text summarization. The loss function is defined as the negative expected reward. The optimization process involves taking the derivative of the loss function to derive the gradient, which is essential for updating model parameters.

In the context of summarization, the policy is expressed as the probability of generating a sequence given an input. The gradient computation typically requires sampling all possible sequences, which is impractical due to the exponential growth of possibilities. Instead, REINFORCE approximates the expectation using a single sample, leading to a high variance in the gradient estimator.

To mitigate this high variance, the paper suggests the introduction of a baseline reward, which adjusts the gradient calculation. This adjustment helps stabilize the training and improve the efficiency of the model's learning process. The update rule for model parameters incorporates this gradient, along with a learning rate, to refine the policy iteratively. Overall, the approach highlights the challenges and solutions in using REINFORCE for effective training in complex tasks like text summarization.

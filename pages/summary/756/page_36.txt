The cited works in this paper cover a broad range of topics related to automatic text summarization and neural machine translation. Key contributions include the exploration of historical and contemporary approaches to summarization, such as the development of pointer-generator networks and attention mechanisms. Notable datasets like BIGPATENT are highlighted for enabling advancements in abstractive summarization. Techniques such as minimum risk training and diverse beam search are discussed for improving model performance. The paper also references significant frameworks like reinforcement learning and sequence-to-sequence models, emphasizing their relevance in enhancing the quality of generated summaries. Overall, these studies collectively contribute to the ongoing evolution of summarization methodologies and neural translation systems.

The paper discusses the development of an interoperability architecture specifically designed for blockchain autonomous systems. It emphasizes the need for seamless integration among various blockchain platforms to enhance collaboration and functionality. The authors argue that interoperability is crucial for the scalability and effectiveness of blockchain applications, particularly in environments where multiple systems must interact. The proposed architecture aims to address the challenges of data exchange, consistency, and security across different blockchain networks. By establishing standardized protocols and interfaces, this framework seeks to facilitate greater cooperation and innovation within the blockchain ecosystem. Overall, the paper highlights the importance of interoperability in realizing the full potential of blockchain technology in autonomous systems.

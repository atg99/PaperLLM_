This paper discusses the computation of derivatives in recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and introduces an incremental learning approach that circumvents the need for back-propagation through time. The derivative pk_uv(t+1) is derived in relation to pk_uv(t), allowing for real-time learning as new inputs are received. The Kronecker delta is utilized to simplify calculations, indicating that the initial state of the network does not depend on the weights. The paper emphasizes that, by using the initial values from time t0, it is possible to recursively compute pk_uv for all subsequent time steps. This method effectively addresses the vanishing error problem, a common issue in standard RNNs that limits their ability to learn from sequences longer than 5 to 10 time steps. The paper references additional resources for a more comprehensive understanding of the Real-Time Recurrent Learning (RTRL) algorithm, which forms the basis of this innovative approach.

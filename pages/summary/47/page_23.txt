The paper presents a three-cell Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) memory block featuring recurrent self-connections. This architecture enhances the ability of LSTMs to retain and process information over long sequences, addressing issues related to vanishing and exploding gradients. The recurrent self-connections allow each cell to maintain a dynamic memory state, facilitating improved learning of temporal dependencies in sequential data. The design emphasizes the importance of structure in neural networks to optimize performance in tasks involving time-series or sequential input.

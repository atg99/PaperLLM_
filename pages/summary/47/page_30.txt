Grid LSTM is a novel extension of LSTM networks, designed to harness the strengths of LSTMs, such as input selection and modulation, within a multi-dimensional architecture. The Grid LSTM, or N-LSTM, organizes LSTM cells in an N-dimensional grid, facilitating communication across layers in all dimensions, including depth. This setup enhances the model's stability compared to traditional Multidimensional LSTMs by allowing better interaction modulation among layers.

In a trained LSTM network, the hidden and memory cells output vectors that change in response to input signals. The transformation of these signals can be mathematically represented and involves a projection matrix to concatenate new input and recurrent signals. 

The paper also discusses Stacked LSTM and Multidimensional LSTM architectures. Stacked LSTMs increase capacity by layering LSTM networks, where the input of each subsequent layer consists of the hidden signals from the previous layer. In contrast, Multidimensional LSTMs are structured to process inputs organized in N-dimensional grids, enhancing their capability to handle complex data structures by increasing the number of recurrent connections to match the input dimensions. 

Overall, Grid LSTM represents a significant advancement in LSTM architecture, allowing for more complex data representations and improved stability in training deep networks.

The paper discusses the weight update mechanism in a neural network using backpropagation. The weight update for a connection between units \( u \) and \( v \) is defined by the formula:

\[
\Delta W[u;v] = -\eta \frac{\partial E}{\partial W[u;v]}
\]

where \( \eta \) is the learning rate. The paper highlights the need to calculate the weight update by deriving the error with respect to the activation and the activation in terms of the state. The formula is expanded to incorporate these derivatives, leading to:

\[
\Delta W[u;v] = -\eta \frac{\partial E}{\partial y_u} \frac{\partial y_u}{\partial s_u} \frac{\partial s_u}{\partial W[u;v]}
\]

For output units, the derivative of the error with respect to the activation is given by:

\[
\frac{\partial E}{\partial y_o} = (d_o - y_o)
\]

and the derivative of the activation with respect to the state is:

\[
\frac{\partial y_o}{\partial s_o} = y_o(1 - y_o)
\]

Additionally, the derivative of the state with respect to a weight connecting a hidden unit \( h \) to an output unit \( o \) is expressed as:

\[
\frac{\partial s_u}{\partial W[u;v]} = y_h
\]

The error signal for an output unit \( o \) is defined as:

\[
\delta_o = -\frac{\partial E}{\partial y_o} \frac{\partial y_o}{\partial s_o}
\]

Resulting in:

\[
\delta_o = (d_o - y_o) y_o (1 - y_o)
\]

This allows for the weight update between the hidden unit \( h \) and the output unit \( o \):

\[
\Delta W[h;o] = -\eta \delta_o y_h
\]

For hidden units, the error signal is backpropagated from the output units. The weight update for the connection between an input unit \( i \) and a hidden unit \( h \) is expressed as:

\[
\Delta W[i;h] = -\eta \sum_{o} \delta_o W[h;o] \frac{\partial y_h}{\partial s_h} \frac{\partial s_h}{\partial W[i;h]}
\]

where \( Suc(h) \) is the set of units that receive output from \( h \). After solving the partial derivatives, the final expression for the weight update becomes:

\[
\Delta W[i;h] = -\eta \sum_{o} \delta_o W[h;o] y_h(1 - y_h) y_i
\]

This framework facilitates the adjustment of weights in a neural network through systematic error correction, demonstrating how the contributions of each unit can be quantified and propagated backward through the network.

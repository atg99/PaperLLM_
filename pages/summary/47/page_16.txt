The paper discusses the calculation of weight changes in a neural network, specifically focusing on the weight change for W[u;v] over time. The overall weight change is expressed as a sum of incremental changes across time steps. To derive the weight changes, the gradient of the error E with respect to the weight W[u;v] is computed at each time step using gradient descent.

The equation for weight change is expanded to show that it involves the error ek, which is the difference between the desired output dk and the actual output yk. The paper introduces the sensitivity measure pk_uv, which quantifies how a small change in weight W[u;v] affects the output of unit k over the entire trajectory of the network from time t0 to t. This indicates a non-local property of the algorithm, meaning that changes in one part of the network can impact outputs in other areas.

The paper also details the forward propagation of gradient information, where the output at the next time step is determined by a weighted sum of inputs from connected units. By differentiating the relevant equations, the authors aim to calculate the necessary results for understanding the dynamics of weight changes within the network.

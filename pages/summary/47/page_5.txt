The paper discusses the differences between the Boolean functions OR and XOR, focusing on their linear separability. It illustrates that the OR function can be represented in a way that allows it to be separated by a linear boundary, making it linearly separable. In contrast, the XOR function cannot be separated by a linear boundary, indicating that it is not linearly separable. The figures provided in the paper visually represent these concepts, clearly showing the distinctions in their respective input-output mappings. This analysis is fundamental in understanding the capabilities and limitations of linear classifiers in machine learning.

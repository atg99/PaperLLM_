The paper discusses experiments aimed at evaluating the robustness of the CapsNet architecture against transformations using various datasets including MNIST, Fashion-MNIST, SVHN, and CIFAR10. The training was conducted on the original datasets, while testing involved afﬁne-deformed versions, with examples illustrated in Figure 5. 

To establish a performance baseline, the authors compared CapsNet to a modified version of AlexNet, which is known for its efficiency in training CNNs across multiple GPUs. This modified AlexNet retains the original five layers of convolution, ReLU activations, and max-pooling but simplifies the final three fully connected layers into a single layer with 256 neurons. This adjustment allows the model to achieve performance close to state-of-the-art results while maintaining a similar complexity in both parameters and runtime.

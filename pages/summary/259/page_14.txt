The paper presents visualizations of embedding vector outputs using 3D sphereized PCA and t-SNE across different datasets after dynamic routing iterations. Figure 19 illustrates the results of 3D sphereized PCA for the MNIST, Fashion-MNIST, SVHN, and CIFAR10 datasets at epoch 50, highlighting the distribution of embeddings. Figure 20 shows t-SNE visualizations for 10,000 embeddings after 50 epochs and the third dynamic routing iteration, further emphasizing the effectiveness of the embedding techniques in capturing the underlying structures of the data across various datasets.

The paper discusses the perturbation of embedding vectors from CIFAR10 samples at various epochs, specifically focusing on epochs 34, 38, and 50. It highlights that during the dynamic routing process, the second iteration's outputs tend to spread, but by the third iteration, this spread is typically reduced as the embeddings converge towards final class representations. This convergence suggests that two iterations are adequate to achieve the accuracy recommended by previous work, specifically Sabour et al. (2017), which suggested three iterations.

Additionally, the paper explores the application of t-SNE for visualizing embeddings, projecting them into 2D or 3D space to reveal the relationships between similar vectors based on Euclidean distance. This visualization is further enhanced by incorporating ground truth class labels, allowing for a clearer understanding of how embeddings of the same class are positioned relative to one another. Various datasets are visualized, with detailed representations provided in the associated figures.

The references listed provide a comprehensive collection of works related to neural networks, specifically focusing on auto-encoders, capsule networks, and related machine learning techniques. Key contributions include the foundational paper by Hinton et al. on transforming auto-encoders, critical implementations of capsule networks by Iwasaki and Keselj, and innovative approaches to convolutional neural networks by Krizhevsky. The MNIST database by LeCun and Cortes is a widely used resource for benchmarking, while the Fashion-MNIST dataset offers a modern alternative for evaluating machine learning algorithms. The dynamic routing mechanism between capsules introduced by Sabour, Frosst, and Hinton represents a significant advancement in network architecture. Each reference contributes to the evolving landscape of deep learning and neural network research.

The paper discusses the integration of batch covariates in the generalized linear model (GLM) framework for analyzing single-cell RNA sequencing data. It highlights the use of Principal Component Analysis (PCA) on all genes to compute a UMAP embedding, illustrating how to manage nuisance parameters in analysis. The authors emphasize the necessity of non-linear batch effect correction strategies when cell-type-specific batch effects or shifts in cell type percentages occur across experiments. 

The supplementary information includes additional datasets, figures, and a guide for using the sctransform R package, which is an open-source tool developed for this analysis. The methods and findings are supported by various funding sources, including the National Institutes of Health and the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative. The authors express gratitude to contributors and declare no competing interests. The paper was received on March 17, 2019, and accepted on October 30, 2019. 

The references listed include significant works related to normalization, computational challenges, and methodologies in single-cell transcriptomics, underscoring the importance of addressing technical variability and batch effects in such analyses.

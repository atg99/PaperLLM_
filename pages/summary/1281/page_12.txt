The results indicated that our approach is robust in detecting variable genes across a range of conditions. Specifically, we found that even with subtle expression shifts, our method maintained a high true positive rate for identifying variable genes, demonstrating its sensitivity and reliability. 

In summary, the paper presents a detailed analysis of the sctransform method for normalizing single-cell RNA sequencing data, emphasizing computational efficiency and model parameter stability. The authors demonstrate that using a subset of genes for initial parameter estimation can yield similar results to using the full transcriptome, thus optimizing processing time. They also provide insights into model parameter uncertainty through bootstrapping and confidence interval analysis. Finally, the simulation study confirms the method's efficacy in detecting variable genes, highlighting its applicability in various biological contexts.

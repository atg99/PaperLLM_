The paper investigates the performance of CapsNets and Aff-CapsNets on the expanded MNIST and AffNIST test sets, focusing on their accuracy with and without a dynamic routing procedure. Results indicate that Aff-CapsNets consistently outperform CapsNets on the AffNIST dataset, particularly in terms of affine robustness. 

To ensure a fair comparison, models were trained until they reached similar performance levels on untransformed examples. The findings reveal that while CapsNets demonstrate high affine robustness, this characteristic cannot be ascribed to the dynamic routing procedure. Instead, it is the architectural design of CapsNets that enhances their robustness to affine transformations.

The experiments further highlight that Aff-CapsNets maintain effective performance even with dynamic routing, while CapsNets with dynamic routing exhibit signs of overfitting and instability during training. The results from various models are summarized in a table, showcasing that Aff-CapsNets achieve state-of-the-art performance on the AffNIST test dataset without prior training on it, confirming their robustness to affine transformations.

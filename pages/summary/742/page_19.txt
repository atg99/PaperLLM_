The paper presents hyperparameters for two reinforcement learning algorithms: Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (DDPG) and Deep Q-Network (DQN), applied to two environments: Path Following and Mujoco.

For DDPG, the architecture consists of hidden layers structured as [64;64] for Path Following and [64;64;64] for Mujoco. The exploration noise is modeled as a Gaussian distribution, with N(0, 0.3) for Path Following and N(0, 0.1) for Mujoco. The target update rates are set at 0.01 and 0.001 respectively, with a discount factor of 0.99. Other parameters include dropout rates of 10% and keep probabilities of 0.8 for Path Following and 0.9 for Mujoco. The model uses 50 Monte Carlo samples and a batch size of 128. Actor L2 losses are 0.0 for Path Following and 0.1 for Mujoco.

For DQN, the hidden layers are similarly structured as [64;64], with a learning rate of 5.0e-4. The exploration strategy finalizes at 0.02 after 100,000 timesteps. The buffer size is 100,000, and the discount factor is again set at 0.99. The training frequency is every 10 steps, and the target network update frequency is every 1,000 steps, with a batch size of 32. The reward scale remains at 1.

Overall, the paper details the specific configurations and parameters crucial for optimizing the performance of DDPG and DQN in their respective environments.

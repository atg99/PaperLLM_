In Section C.2, the paper presents experiments conducted in the Hook Sweep environment, showcasing the performance of the AC-Teach algorithm with various teacher configurations. The results, illustrated in Figure C.5, indicate that AC-Teach demonstrates robust performance across different teacher types, confirming its effectiveness in this task.

Section C.3 discusses an ablation analysis where the impact of various components of AC-Teach is evaluated, particularly time commitment and behavioral target. The findings reveal that time commitment enhances performance in the Pick and Place and Hook Sweep tasks but does not benefit the Path Following task. Conversely, the behavioral target proves significant in the Path Following task but is less relevant for the other two. This variation in effectiveness is attributed to the differing characteristics of each task, such as stochasticity and extrapolation error.

In Section C.4, the paper explores the sensitivity of the AC-Teach algorithm to various parameters and hyperparameters, using the Path Following task for a clearer analysis due to its simplified dynamics. This analysis aims to further understand the algorithm's performance under different conditions.

This paper introduces a novel policy learning framework called Actor-Critic with Teacher Ensembles (AC-Teach), designed to effectively integrate advice from multiple teachers in reinforcement learning settings. The proposed framework addresses the complexities of off-policy learning, particularly when teacher advice varies in utility across different state spaces and may even conflict. 

Key contributions include: 

1. A comprehensive collection of teacher attributes that characterize the quality of teachers in guiding agent training.
2. AC-Teach's mechanism for leveraging multiple teachers, which remains robust even when individual teachers provide useful advice only in specific states or contradict one another. The agent is also capable of learning from scratch in states without useful teacher guidance.
3. Experimental results showing that AC-Teach can successfully tackle multi-step tasks, greatly enhancing sample efficiency compared to existing baselines.
4. Demonstration that AC-Teach not only performs well with low-quality teacher sets but also outperforms baselines when higher quality teachers are involved, providing a unified algorithmic approach to diverse teacher attributes.

The framework employs a probabilistic posterior critic to quantify action value uncertainty, facilitating informed decision-making about which teacher advice to follow. Additionally, a commitment mechanism allows the behavioral policy to maintain actions from a single teacher for longer durations, promoting more meaningful experience collection despite potential contradictions among teachers. This coupling of the behavioral policy and critic mitigates the instability often associated with off-policy learning, resulting in a more effective training process.

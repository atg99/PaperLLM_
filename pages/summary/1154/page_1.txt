To bridge this gap, the paper introduces PiTree, a novel framework designed to convert sophisticated ABR algorithms into lightweight decision trees. This approach allows for a faithful replication of the original algorithms' decision-making capabilities while significantly reducing the computational burden required for deployment on client devices. The authors provide a theoretical upper bound on the optimization loss during this conversion process, ensuring that the performance degradation remains minimal.

Evaluation of PiTree against three representative ABR algorithms reveals that it achieves less than 3% average performance degradation while also offering substantial savings in operational costs for large content providers. The framework thus enables the practical implementation of advanced ABR strategies without compromising on user quality of experience.

In summary, PiTree presents a scalable and efficient solution for deploying adaptive bitrate algorithms in client-side video players, particularly on mobile devices, by leveraging decision trees to maintain performance while minimizing resource requirements.

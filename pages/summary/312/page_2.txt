4 Results
The results from the one-dimensional and two-dimensional loss visualizations demonstrate that fine-tuning BERT leads to a more efficient optimization path compared to training from scratch. The one-dimensional loss curve indicates that the initial loss for fine-tuning is lower, suggesting a better starting point due to pre-training. In the two-dimensional loss surface, the optimization trajectory shows that fine-tuning converges more rapidly to lower loss regions, highlighting the advantages of leveraging pre-trained parameters.

5 Conclusion
This study illustrates the effectiveness of fine-tuning pre-trained BERT models on downstream tasks through comprehensive visualization techniques. The analysis of loss landscapes and optimization trajectories provides insights into the benefits of pre-training, emphasizing how it facilitates faster convergence and improved performance compared to training models from random initialization. Future work may explore additional visualization methods and their implications for understanding other pre-trained models.

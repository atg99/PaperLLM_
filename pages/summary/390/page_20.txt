The paper presents a comprehensive evaluation of the classification performance of various EfficientNet models (EfficientNet-B0 to EfficientNet-B3) using confusion matrices. These matrices facilitate the analysis of the models' accuracy, precision, recall, and other relevant metrics.

Key findings include:

- **EfficientNet-B0**: Correctly classified 269 out of 280 images, with 11 misclassifications.
- **EfficientNet-B1**: Correctly classified 274 out of 280 images, with 6 misclassifications.
- **EfficientNet-B2**: Correctly classified 275 out of 280 images, with 5 misclassifications.
- **EfficientNet-B3**: Correctly classified 274 out of 280 images, with 6 misclassifications.

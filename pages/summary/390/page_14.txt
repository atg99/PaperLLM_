The paper discusses the performance of the EfficientNet-B3 and EfficientNet-B4 models in terms of validation accuracy and loss. For the EfficientNet-B3 model, graphs illustrate the trends in loss and accuracy, highlighting the model's training dynamics. The EfficientNet-B4 model initially achieved a validation accuracy of 85.07% and a loss of 0.3899 after 3 epochs of training. Following a tuning process and an additional 5 epochs of training, the model significantly improved, reaching a validation accuracy of 98.88% and a loss of 0.0433. Figures 15 and 16 provide visual representations of the model's performance metrics throughout the training epochs.

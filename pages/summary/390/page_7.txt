The paper discusses enhancements made to a dataset to improve its robustness and diversity for image processing applications. Various augmentation methods are employed, including pixel intensity adjustments, histogram equalization for better contrast and detail, image rotation at specific angles, and luminosity adjustments through intensity value modifications. The preprocessing techniques outlined are essential for manipulating and analyzing digital images in computer vision. This section emphasizes the importance of these methods in preparing images for further analysis and improving the overall performance of image processing tasks.

The paper presents an evaluation of the efficiency of various EfficientNet models (B3 to B7) based on their accuracy before and after certain modifications. The results indicate a notable improvement in accuracy across all models after the adjustments. For instance, EfficientNet-B3 improved from an accuracy of 77.61% to 90.41% after the modifications, while EfficientNet-B7 showed an increase from 78.72% to 93.0%. 

The evaluation methodology includes calculating accuracy using a formula that incorporates true positives (TP), true negatives (TN), false positives (FP), and false negatives (FN). The accuracy formula is given as: 

Accuracy = (TP + TN) / (TP + TN + FP + FN).

After training and validation, the models were tested on a dataset of 280 images, where the outputs were predicted and a Confusion Matrix (CM) was created. The CM is an essential tool for assessing the performance of classification algorithms, as it provides insights into the true and false classifications made by the model.

The findings underscore the effectiveness of the adjustments made to the EfficientNet models, significantly enhancing their predictive accuracy in the classification tasks evaluated.

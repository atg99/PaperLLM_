The paper presents experimental results evaluating the performance of the EfficientNet-B0 model under various parameter settings. Initially, the model was trained for 2 epochs, achieving a validation accuracy of 82.08% and a validation loss of 0.4411, indicating a reasonable alignment with actual labels. After tuning, the model underwent an additional 3 epochs of training, resulting in a significant improvement with a validation accuracy of 94.94% and a validation loss of 0.1314. Graphical representations of the model's performance across epochs are included to aid in understanding these results.

The paper discusses advancements in brain tumor (BT) detection systems and their potential impact on medical practices. It highlights several key areas of development:

1. **Real-time Detection**: The creation of devices for real-time brain tumor detection could revolutionize surgical procedures by allowing surgeons to make informed decisions during operations.

2. **Integration of Imaging Technologies**: Seamless integration of MRI and other imaging modalities could provide radiologists and medical professionals with immediate analysis and interpretation, enhancing diagnostic capabilities.

3. **Quantitative Evaluation**: Advanced systems may eventually offer numerical assessments of tumor size, growth rate, and treatment response, improving patient monitoring.

4. **Genetic Analysis**: Incorporating genetic data could lead to a better understanding of hereditary factors associated with brain tumors, potentially facilitating the development of targeted therapies.

5. **AI-Driven Drug Discovery**: Artificial intelligence models can analyze extensive molecular and clinical datasets to identify new drugs and treatments for brain tumors.

6. **Ethical Considerations**: The proliferation of these technologies necessitates ongoing attention to ethical issues, including patient data protection, reduction of biases, and ensuring transparency in AI applications.

The authors conclude that the future of brain tumor detection systems is set to significantly enhance patient care, improve medical decision-making, and deepen understanding of brain tumors. Continuous research and innovation are essential for realizing these advancements, benefiting both patients and the broader medical community. 

Acknowledgments are given to organizations that supported the research. The paper cites various references, including studies on brain tumors, their classification, and the application of AI in medical imaging.

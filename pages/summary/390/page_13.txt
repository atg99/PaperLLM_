The paper discusses the performance of the EfficientNet-B3 model in a study focusing on its training and optimization results. Initially, the model was trained for 3 epochs, achieving a validation accuracy of 81.97% and a validation loss of 0.4176. After tuning, an additional 5 epochs of training led to significant improvements, with the model reaching a validation accuracy of 97.72% and a validation loss of 0.0703. Figures 13 and 14 illustrate the trends in accuracy and loss throughout the training process, highlighting the effectiveness of the tuning applied to the EfficientNet-B3 model.

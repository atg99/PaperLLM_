The paper presents a dense CNN-based EfficientNet model designed for classifying brain MRI images into four categories: GT (glioma tumor), MT (meningioma tumor), PT (pituitary tumor), and no tumor. The proposed model achieves a remarkable testing accuracy of 98.78%, demonstrating its effectiveness even with a limited dataset. The methodology section outlines the steps involved in the study, including input, preprocessing, model training, tumor detection, and classification. The dataset consists of approximately 18,500 MRI images, with specific distributions for each tumor category. The dataset is noted for its quality, having undergone a thorough cleaning process that ensured the absence of duplicates and proper labeling, thereby minimizing the need for extensive preprocessing.

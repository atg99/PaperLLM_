The paper presents an analysis of the EfficientNet-B4 and EfficientNet-B5 models, focusing on their training results and performance metrics. For the EfficientNet-B4 model, graphs illustrate the trends in loss and accuracy over the training period. The EfficientNet-B5 model demonstrated a validation accuracy of 86.46% and a validation loss of 0.4150 after 3 epochs. After further tuning and training for an additional 5 epochs, the model achieved a notable validation accuracy of 98.92% with a significantly reduced validation loss of 0.0358. Visual representations of the performance metrics for both models are provided in the respective figures. Overall, the findings indicate substantial improvements in model accuracy with optimized training strategies.

The paper discusses the performance of the EfficientNet-B6 and EfficientNet-B7 models in terms of training and validation metrics. For the EfficientNet-B6 model, loss and accuracy trends are illustrated in Figures 19 and 20, indicating the model's performance before and after optimization. The EfficientNet-B7 model initially achieved a validation accuracy of 85.62% and a validation loss of 0.3780 after 3 epochs of training without tuning. Following a tuning phase where the model was trained for an additional 5 epochs, it reached a significantly improved validation accuracy of 98.99% and a validation loss of 0.0274. These results are visually represented in Figures 21 and 22, with a comparative summary of all model results provided in Table 2. The findings highlight the effectiveness of tuning in enhancing model performance.

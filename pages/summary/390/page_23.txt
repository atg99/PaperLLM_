The paper presents a robust brain tumor (BT) detection system utilizing the EfficientNet model, which incorporates the SoftMax function, ReLu activation, and the ADAM optimizer. It evaluates the performance of eight EfficientNet variants (B0 to B7) and reports their test accuracies, highlighting EfficientNet-B5 and EfficientNet-B7 as the most effective models with an accuracy of 99.64%. The study demonstrates the potential of the EfficientNet architecture in accurately differentiating and classifying BT types, marking significant progress in medical image analysis and deep learning applications in healthcare.

The results indicate that advanced deep learning models like EfficientNet can significantly improve early detection and patient care for life-threatening conditions such as BTs. The research suggests promising avenues for future developments, including enhanced accuracy through hyperparameter optimization, early detection initiatives, and multi-class classification capabilities for more tailored treatment approaches. Overall, the findings underscore the transformative impact of deep learning technologies in clinical settings, paving the way for improved patient outcomes and advancements in medical science.

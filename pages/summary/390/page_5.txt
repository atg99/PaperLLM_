The paper presents a comprehensive overview of deep learning techniques applied in brain tumor (BT) detection, emphasizing advancements in model accuracy and methodologies. It includes a comparative analysis of various studies, highlighting key contributions made by researchers in the field.

The summary of research efforts is organized in a table format, showcasing the authors, descriptions of their work, publication years, models used, and accuracy results. Notable contributions include:

1. Mishra et al. developed an efficient deep learning technique using EfficientNet, achieving 98.78% accuracy in classifying BTs into four categories.
2. Mahesh et al. introduced the CE-EEN-B0 model, which classified BTs based on their location with 97.24% accuracy.
3. Isunuri and Kakarla utilized a multi-path convolution and attention network with EfficientNet-B4, achieving an accuracy of 98.35% for BT grade categorization.
4. Goutham et al. implemented a GUI application with EfficientNet-B0 for BT detection, reaching 96.94% accuracy.
5. Mantha and Reddy's automated system with EfficientNet-B3 achieved a remarkable 99.35% accuracy in identifying different BT types.
6. Zulfiqar et al. compared multiple EfficientNet models, with the EfficientNet-B2 model achieving 98.86% accuracy while emphasizing the role of data augmentation.
7. Padmavathi et al. demonstrated the effectiveness of transfer learning with EfficientNet, achieving 99% accuracy in classifying BT types.
8. Du et al. created a web-based system using EfficientNet, achieving 98% accuracy in BT classification.
9. Shah et al. fine-tuned EfficientNet-B0, attaining 98.87% accuracy in classifying BTs through image enhancement and data augmentation.

Overall, the paper underscores the significant progress in deep learning techniques for BT detection, showcasing high accuracy rates and innovative approaches in model architecture and feature extraction.

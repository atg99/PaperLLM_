The paper discusses the performance of various EfficientNet models in classifying images, specifically focusing on misclassifications across different versions. In EfficientNet-B0, several misclassifications were noted: 2 ground truth (GT) images were misclassified as MT, 4 MT images as GT, and 5 MT images as PTs. EfficientNet-B1 showed similar issues, with 2 GT images misclassified as MT, 2 MT images as GT, 2 MT images as PTs, and 1 PT image as MT. EfficientNet-B2 had 1 GT image misclassified as PT, 1 MT image as GT, and 3 MT images as PTs. EfficientNet-B3 exhibited a significant issue with 6 MT images misclassified as GT.

The performance of EfficientNet-B4 to B7 was illustrated through confusion matrices, showing high accuracy with 277 correct predictions for B4, 279 for B5, 276 for B6, and 279 for B7 out of 280 images. However, there were also instances of incorrect predictions: 3 for B4, 1 for B5, 4 for B6, and 1 for B7, highlighted in the false positive and false negative sections of the confusion matrices. This analysis underscores the varying effectiveness of the EfficientNet models in image classification tasks.

The paper discusses the performance of the EfficientNet-B1 and EfficientNet-B2 models in terms of their training and validation metrics. For EfficientNet-B1, graphs illustrate the trends in loss and accuracy, showcasing the model's performance over the training process. In the case of EfficientNet-B2, the model initially trained for 2 epochs, achieving a validation accuracy of 85.90% and a validation loss of 0.3520. After further tuning and an additional 3 epochs of training, the model significantly improved its performance, reaching a validation accuracy of 96.58% and a validation loss of 0.0988. The results are visually represented in the respective figures and tables, highlighting the effectiveness of the tuning process on model performance.

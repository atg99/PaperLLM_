The paper discusses the performance of the EfficientNet-B6 model, highlighting the impact of tuning on its accuracy and loss metrics. Initially, the model was trained for 3 epochs, achieving a validation accuracy of 85.26% and a validation loss of 0.3794. After an additional 5 epochs of tuning, the model significantly improved, reaching a validation accuracy of 99.05% and a validation loss of 0.0354.

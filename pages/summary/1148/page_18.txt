The paper discusses the performance of a model on the CIFAR100 dataset, specifically using a WideResNet28x10 architecture. The authors detail their experimental setup, including a learning rate of 0.064, a decay rate of 97% every 2.4 epochs, and a weight decay of 10^-5, along with a drop-connect probability of 0.2 on 8 V100 GPUs. They report a baseline accuracy of 77.23%, aligning with results from EfficientNet authors. Additionally, the paper provides tree visualizations for models trained on CIFAR10 and CIFAR100, demonstrating that subtrees can be categorized by visual meanings, which may correspond to Wordnet attributes or contextual characteristics. This highlights the interpretability of the model's decision-making process in relation to visual data.

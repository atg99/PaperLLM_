The paper introduces the MHSA-Darknet architecture, which enhances the original CSPDarknet by integrating Multi-Head Self-Attention (MHSA) layers in place of the CSPDark blocks in the last processing stage. This modification aims to improve the transferability of learned features and effectively capture long-distance context information, addressing challenges such as severe occlusions, perturbations, and domain shifts that traditional CNNs struggle with.

The architecture is structured to operate on an input resolution of 1024x1024, specifically at the P7 stage, where the MHSA layers engage with an 8x8 feature map. The core innovation lies in implementing global self-attention over the two-dimensional feature map, allowing for more comprehensive feature extraction for detection tasks.

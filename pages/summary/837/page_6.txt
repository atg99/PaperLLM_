The paper discusses the impact of clipping levels on the quantization process in deep learning models, particularly focusing on MobileNet V1 when trained on ImageNet with varying bit-widths. It highlights that different bit-widths require distinct clipping levels for optimal performance. The authors observe that while higher bit-widths lead to larger clipping levels, the Vanilla AdaBits approach shares clipping levels across different bit-widths, which can disrupt the optimization process and increase quantization errors, especially at the lowest bit-width of 4 bits.

To address this issue, the authors introduce a method called Switchable Clipping Level (S-CL), which allows for independent clipping levels for each bit-width in every layer of the model. This approach prevents interference between clipping levels, thus reducing quantization errors and improving performance for lower bit-widths. The implementation of S-CL does not significantly increase model size or introduce additional runtime overhead, making it a practical solution for adaptive bit-width quantization in neural networks. The findings suggest that proper adjustment of clipping levels is critical for optimizing the performance of models using variable bit-widths.

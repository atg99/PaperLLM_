data2vec presents a comprehensive framework for self-supervised learning applicable to speech, vision, and language modalities. In the extended speech processing results, the performance of different models on the Librispeech dev/test sets is evaluated based on word error rates (WER) using varying amounts of labeled data. The data2vec Base model demonstrates superior performance compared to wav2vec 2.0 and Hubert across different labeled dataset sizes, particularly excelling with minimal labeled data, achieving a WER of 7.3 with 10 minutes of labeled data, compared to wav2vec 2.0's 8.9.

Additionally, the comparison of loss functions indicates that variations in loss function choice yield marginal differences in WER, with L2 loss achieving a score of 17.1, while Smooth L1 with different beta values showed similar results, ranging from 16.8 to 17.3. 

Furthermore, the paper discusses the impact of masking parameters, highlighting the necessity for distinct parameters across modalities. A masking rate of 15% is effective for text, while images require a higher masking rate for optimal performance. This reflects the varying semantic structures of inputs across different modalities, with the study confirming that tuning these parameters can enhance the model's effectiveness.

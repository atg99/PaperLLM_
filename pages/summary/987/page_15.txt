The paper presents a taxonomy of T-PTLMs, which stands for Transformer-based Pre-trained Language Models. This classification aims to categorize various models based on their architectural features, training methodologies, and application domains. The taxonomy highlights the evolution of T-PTLMs, showcasing the diversity in design and functionality that has emerged in the field of natural language processing. Key concepts include the differentiation between model types, their respective training techniques, and the specific tasks they are optimized for, which collectively contribute to understanding the landscape of T-PTLMs and their impact on language-related applications.

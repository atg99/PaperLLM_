Hybrid MTFT combines elements of both vanilla and iterative multi-task fine-tuning, leveraging the strengths of each approach while mitigating their weaknesses. It allows for simultaneous training on multiple tasks while also emphasizing the selection of relevant tasks to improve overall model performance.

In summary, the paper discusses various fine-tuning strategies for adapting pre-trained language models to specific tasks. Vanilla fine-tuning often leads to overfitting, particularly with small datasets. Intermediate fine-tuning, both domain and task adaptive, provides additional task-specific knowledge that improves performance on target datasets. Multi-task fine-tuning enhances learning through simultaneous exposure to multiple datasets, although it may not always yield better results. Iterative and hybrid approaches offer solutions to optimize task selection and improve model outcomes.

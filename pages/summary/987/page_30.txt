The table summarizes various language-based extrinsic benchmarks used for evaluating natural language understanding (NLU) and natural language generation (NLG) tasks across multiple languages. Key benchmarks include:

1. **XGLUE**: Covers eleven tasks across 19 languages, focusing on both NLU and NLG.
2. **LinCE**: Focuses on code-switching with five tasks across nine language pairs.
3. **GLUECoS**: Includes eleven datasets for six tasks related to code-switching in English-Hindi and English-Spanish.
4. **XTREME** and **XTREME-R**: Address cross-lingual tasks across diverse languages, with XTREME spanning 40 languages.
5. **RussianSuperGLUE**: Dedicated to nine NLU tasks in Russian.
6. **IndicGLUE**: Encompasses ten tasks across multiple Indian languages.
7. **CLUE**: Concentrates on nine Chinese language tasks.
8. **IndoNLU** and **IndoNLG**: Cover various Indonesian language tasks in NLU and NLG respectively.
9. **FLUE**: Focuses on French with six tasks, including cross-lingual ones.
10. **ArBench**: Involves six Arabic language tasks.
11. **KLEJ**: Encompasses seven NLU tasks in Polish.
12. **KLUE**: Addresses eight NLU tasks in Korean.
13. **GLUES**: Includes various NLU tasks in Spanish.
14. **ParsiNLU**: Features six NLU tasks in Persian.

Each benchmark is characterized by specific tasks such as text classification, sentiment analysis, machine translation, and question answering, showcasing the diversity and complexity of language processing tasks across different languages.

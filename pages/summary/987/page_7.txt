The paper discusses various language-based pretraining corpora used for training models in different languages, highlighting both monolingual and multilingual datasets. The monolingual corpora include collections like Indo4B for Indonesian, BrWaC for Brazilian Portuguese, CLUECorpus2020 and WuDaCorpora for Chinese, each with substantial text volumes. Multilingual datasets such as OSCAR, IndicCorp, and CC-100 encompass multiple languages and serve as extensive resources for training models like MuRIL and IndicBERT.

Additionally, the paper addresses parallel corpora, including PMINDIA and Multi-UN, which provide aligned sentence pairs across different languages, aiding in translation tasks. The largest of these, Samanantar, offers extensive pairs for 12 Indian languages.

The pretraining process typically involves initializing transformer layers randomly and refining them through tasks like Masked Language Modeling (MLM) and Next Sentence Prediction (NSP). Continual Pretraining (CPT) is also discussed, where existing pretrained models are further trained on specialized data, exemplified by models like BioBERT and infoXLM. This approach allows leveraging prior knowledge for enhanced performance on specific tasks.

This paper presents a comprehensive overview of advancements in transformer models, particularly their applications in vision and natural language processing. It highlights various surveys on visual transformers and their integration into deep learning frameworks. Key studies examine the effectiveness of unsupervised pre-training, the importance of deduplicating training data, and the development of multilingual models. Notable contributions include adaptations for specific languages, enhancements for abusive language detection, and innovations in tokenization methods. The work emphasizes the significance of large-scale pre-training and domain-specific adaptations, showcasing various models and their impact on tasks like sentiment analysis and cross-lingual representation learning. The references included reflect a broad spectrum of research, underscoring the ongoing evolution and refinement of transformer-based architectures in both language and vision tasks.

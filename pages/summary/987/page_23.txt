The paper discusses various advancements in Transformer-based pre-trained language models (T-PTLMs), emphasizing their architecture, scalability, and integration of external knowledge sources. 

1. **ByT5 and Charformer**: ByT5 features a 23-layer architecture with a deeper encoder compared to its decoder. Charformer introduces a gradient-based tokenizer that learns sub-words from characters, eliminating the need for a fixed vocabulary. It matches the performance of models like T5 while outperforming byte-level models.

2. **Large Scale T-PTLMs**: The paper highlights that the performance of T-PTLMs correlates more with model size than depth or width. Increasing the number of parameters, training data size, and training duration simultaneously is essential for optimal performance. This led to the creation of large models such as GPT-3, PANGU, and Switch-Transformers, which contain billions to trillions of parameters.

3. **Knowledge Enriched T-PTLMs**: These models enhance their capabilities by incorporating external knowledge sources like WordNet and UMLS during pretraining. Examples include CasualBERT, which integrates causal knowledge, and various biomedical models that utilize UMLS for domain-specific knowledge.

4. **Long-Sequence T-PTLMs**: Traditional self-attention mechanisms in transformers face limitations due to quadratic time complexity, restricting their application to long sequences. Variants like sparse and linearized self-attention have been developed to mitigate this issue, with models such as Longformer and Performer exemplifying these advancements.

5. **Efficient T-PTLMs**: Recent architectures allow for improved performance with less pretraining data and cost. DeBERTa employs a disentangled attention mechanism and an enhanced masked decoder, achieving superior efficiency compared to RoBERTa. Similarly, ConvBERT combines self-attention with dynamic convolution to model dependencies more effectively, outperforming other models with lower pretraining costs.

The paper concludes by noting that once a language model is pretrained, it can be adapted for various downstream tasks, further enhancing its applicability.

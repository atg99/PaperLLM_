For biomedical applications, benchmarks like BLUE, BLURB, and CBLUE evaluate various NLP tasks within the biomedical domain, covering aspects such as named entity recognition and relation extraction.

Similarly, XTREME-R expands on the XTREME benchmark by increasing the number of languages and introducing more complex NLU tasks. Overall, these benchmarks serve as critical tools for assessing and advancing the capabilities of natural language processing models across different domains and languages.

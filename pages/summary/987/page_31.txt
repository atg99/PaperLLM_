In summary, the paper discusses the evaluation of cross-lingual and monolingual models through various benchmarks tailored to specific languages and domains. It highlights the importance of libraries that facilitate the training and evaluation of transformer-based pre-trained language models (PTLMs) while also addressing the need for faster inference and model interpretability. Future directions in this field emphasize the development of better pretraining methods, sample-efficient tasks, and efficient models to minimize resource usage and improve performance. Additionally, there is a call for innovative position encoding mechanisms and enhancements to existing models like BERT and RoBERTa, which could further advance their capabilities in natural language processing tasks.

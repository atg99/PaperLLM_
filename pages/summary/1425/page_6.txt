The paper presents OCR-VQA, a large-scale dataset aimed at enhancing visual question answering (VQA) by incorporating text from images. The dataset includes 200,000 samples designed to address the challenges of reading text in images to answer questions, filling a gap in existing research. The authors benchmark the dataset using a baseline deep model that integrates advanced VQA and optical character recognition (OCR) techniques. 

Results indicate varying accuracy across question types, with binary questions achieving 58.2% accuracy, while book genre questions have the lowest at 22.0%. The authors highlight the dataset's potential to stimulate further research in document image analysis and VQA, advocating for improved text recognition methods. Future plans include extending the dataset to include scene text and digital images, and focusing on more complex VQA tasks that combine image and text comprehension. 

The work is supported by the Pratiksha Trust Young Investigator Award to Dr. Anirban Chakraborty, and references numerous studies in the fields of text recognition and visual question answering, underscoring the relevance and novelty of their contribution.

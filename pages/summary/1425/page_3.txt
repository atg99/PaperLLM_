The paper discusses the OCR-VQA-200K dataset designed for visual question answering (VQA) tasks related to book covers. It includes a variety of question types and answers, highlighting the diversity in the dataset. The authors detail the process of generating questions, where they utilize templates and paraphrased versions to create a comprehensive set of inquiries about the books.

The dataset is split into training, validation, and test sets, with 800K, 100K, and 100K question-answer pairs respectively. The authors identify specific challenges for both the document image analysis community, such as improving layout analysis and optical character recognition (OCR) for diverse book cover designs, and for the VQA community, which include managing large answer spaces and interpreting text in images.

The proposed approach to evaluate VQA performance involves four modules, starting with text block extraction from images. The authors compare three methods for text block extraction: Tesseract, EAST, and VGG text detector, ultimately selecting the most effective method for the task. The paper emphasizes the innovative aspects of the dataset and the importance of developing robust methods to address the unique challenges posed by OCR and VQA applications.

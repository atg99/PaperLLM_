The paper presents the OCR-VQA –200K dataset, which combines traditional OCR techniques with advancements in visual question answering (VQA). The dataset comprises 207,572 images and over 1 million question-answer pairs, aiming to bridge the gap in existing VQA literature that typically neglects textual information in images. The authors highlight that while OCR has seen significant progress, particularly with camera-captured and handwritten text, the integration of text into VQA tasks remains underexplored. 

The construction of the OCR-VQA –200K dataset involves three stages: first, acquiring book cover images from a pre-existing dataset; second, generating a set of template questions related to the images; and third, paraphrasing these questions to enhance variability. This novel dataset is expected to open new research avenues in both document image analysis and VQA. The authors express optimism that this contribution will fill existing gaps in VQA research, particularly in the context of text interpretation within images.

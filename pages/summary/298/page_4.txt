MobileNet V2 is recognized as an efficient lightweight model for image classification, utilizing a setup similar to ResNet with specific adjustments in learning rate and weight decay. The evaluation on the ImageNet-1K dataset demonstrates that the GAM (Global Attention Mechanism) consistently enhances performance across various neural architectures. Notably, for ResNet18, GAM surpasses ABN (Attention Block Network) in both parameter efficiency and overall performance.

The classification results reveal that ResNet18 with GAM achieves a Top-1 error of 29.34% and a Top-5 error of 10.23%, while maintaining fewer parameters compared to ABN. In the case of ResNet50, GAM also shows improved performance with a Top-1 error of 22.78%, despite a higher parameter count, indicating its effectiveness in leveraging attention mechanisms.

Ablation studies conducted on ResNet18 further clarify the contributions of spatial and channel attention. The experiments reveal that both attention types significantly enhance performance when activated, with their combination yielding the best results. Additionally, the impact of max-pooling on spatial attention was evaluated, suggesting that it can negatively influence performance depending on the architecture, such as ResNets.

Overall, the findings underscore the advantages of integrating GAM into existing architectures for improved image classification performance on the ImageNet-1K dataset.

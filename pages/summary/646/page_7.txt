The paper presents a comprehensive evaluation of the view-GCN method for 3D shape retrieval using the ShapeNet Core55 dataset and its performance on real multi-view image datasets. 

In the shape retrieval experiments, view-GCN is compared against several state-of-the-art methods, including voxel-based and multi-view approaches. The results displayed in Table 3 indicate that view-GCN achieves the highest accuracies across multiple metrics, including micro-averaged precision, recall, F1 score, mean average precision (mAP), and normalized discounted cumulative gain (NDCG). Notably, it surpasses the current leading method, RotationNet, in all metrics except for micro-averaged NDCG. View-GCN demonstrates a significant improvement over other methods such as MVCNN, achieving 4.9% higher in mAP for micro-averaged results, highlighting its effectiveness in aggregating multi-view features hierarchically on view-graphs.

Additionally, the paper reports on the classification accuracies of view-GCN on the RGBD dataset, showcasing its capability with real captured multi-view images. As shown in Table 4, view-GCN achieves the highest classification accuracy compared to other methods, outperforming MVCNN and RotationNet by notable margins. The use of different backbone networks, including AlexNet and ResNet variants, further enhances performance, with view-GCN (ResNet-18) achieving an impressive accuracy of 94.3%.

These results collectively affirm the robustness and superiority of view-GCN in both 3D shape retrieval and classification tasks, emphasizing its potential for real-world applications in shape analysis.

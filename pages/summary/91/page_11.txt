The results presented in Figure 6 indicate a comparison of performance metrics between the LEGW optimizer and the Adam solver across PTB and GNMT applications. The findings demonstrate that LEGW significantly outperforms Adam in both applications, even after extensively tuning the learning rate for the Adam optimizer, which still fails to surpass the performance of LEGW.

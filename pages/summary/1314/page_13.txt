The paper discusses a Dynamic Service Migration Mechanism, focusing on the convergence performance across different service resolutions and the delay comparison among various migration schemes. Figure 5 illustrates that as service resolution varies, convergence performance is affected. Figure 6 contrasts the delay of three migration strategies: an ECC-based scheme, a nearest migration scheme, and a no migration scheme. The ECC-based scheme demonstrates superior performance by adapting to user mobility, significantly reducing delay jitter compared to the nearest migration scheme, which triggers migration only upon user movement to a new access point. The analysis indicates that delays become more pronounced with increased service request times, particularly beyond 25, while the no migration scheme results in the longest delays. The findings emphasize the importance of proactive service migration based on user mobility predictions to enhance service efficiency and reduce latency.

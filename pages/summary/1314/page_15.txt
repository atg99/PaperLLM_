The paper presents a dynamic service migration mechanism designed to enhance performance in mobile edge clouds. It addresses the challenges associated with service migration in ultradense cellular networks, particularly in the context of mobility-aware caching and computation offloading. The authors integrate findings from various studies, including those on speech emotion recognition using deep learning techniques and medical image analysis with convolutional neural networks. They emphasize the significance of live service migration and provide a comprehensive survey of existing virtual machine migration mechanisms. The paper also highlights the importance of big data analytics in cloud and IoT contexts, underscoring the need for efficient migration strategies to support seamless service delivery. Overall, the research contributes to advancing the understanding of service migration mechanisms in mobile edge computing environments.

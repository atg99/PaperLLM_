This paper introduces an Edge Cognitive Computing (ECC) network architecture and addresses key issues related to it. The authors developed an ECC platform that enables dynamic service migration based on mobile user behavior cognition, which was experimentally tested. Results indicate that the proposed ECC architecture enhances Quality of Experience (QoE) compared to traditional edge-computing frameworks lacking cognitive engines for data and resource management. This approach effectively utilizes user behavior predictions to optimize service migration based on traffic data and network resources, demonstrating the potential of edge cognitive computing in achieving a human-centered resource distribution and optimization cycle.

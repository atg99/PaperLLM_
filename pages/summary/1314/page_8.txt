The paper discusses the challenges of emotion detection in mobile environments, emphasizing the need for dynamic adjustments in computing resources to maintain user quality of experience (QoE). It highlights the contradiction between achieving high accuracy and low latency in emotion recognition. As the accuracy of emotion detection increases, more computational resources are needed, which typically leads to higher latency. The authors present different service resolutions for emotion detection with corresponding accuracy and latency metrics in Table 2. For instance, the VGG algorithm achieves 66.3% accuracy with a latency of 103 ms, while a combination of AlexNet, VGG, and DBN improves accuracy to 79.1% but increases latency to 265.3 ms. The study suggests that when users prioritize interactive experience over accuracy, lower resolution options can be adopted without compromising overall QoE. The paper underscores the importance of elastic computing modes to adapt to fluctuating network statuses and user mobility, which were not adequately addressed in prior research.

The paper discusses the optimization of service migration in cloud computing, specifically focusing on video content processing and emotion detection services. It highlights the significance of computing and storage resources, emphasizing the data size involved in tasks such as video decoding. The migration cost is influenced by the size of the virtual server and available bandwidth, with higher service resolutions leading to increased costs.

The main goal of migration is to minimize service costs while enhancing the Quality of Experience (QoE) for users by adjusting service resolutions based on user demands, mobility, and network resources. The paper presents an optimization objective that seeks to maximize a score representing user experience minus the associated costs. This score is defined in relation to service resolution and user expectations, indicating that lower service resolutions may suffice when user expectations are not high, thus reducing energy consumption without compromising QoE.

The authors frame the problem as a reinforcement learning scenario, where the aim is to find an optimal migration policy that maximizes system rewards. The Q-learning algorithm is proposed as a method to achieve this, with the reward defined as the difference in scores before and after a migration action, adjusted for costs. Overall, the paper contributes to the understanding of service migration strategies in cloud environments, particularly in optimizing resource use while catering to user satisfaction.

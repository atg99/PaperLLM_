This paper introduces a dynamic service-migration mechanism within the context of edge cognitive computing (ECC), a novel computing paradigm that combines cognitive computing capabilities with edge computing. The authors, affiliated with various institutions, highlight the growing importance of ECC in enhancing user experiences and energy efficiency through intelligent service management at the network edge.

The paper begins by outlining the architecture of ECC and discussing key design considerations. The proposed dynamic service-migration mechanism leverages behavioral cognition of mobile users to optimize service delivery. An experimental platform was developed to evaluate this mechanism, demonstrating that the ECC architecture achieves ultra-low latency and enhances user experience while conserving computing resources and improving energy efficiency.

Key subjects addressed in the paper include cognitive computing, cloud computing, edge computing, mobile cloud computing, and service migration. The findings underscore the potential of ECC to significantly improve service quality and operational efficiency in edge computing environments.

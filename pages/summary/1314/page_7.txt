The paper introduces a Dynamic Cognitive Service Migration Mechanism (DCSMM) designed to enhance elastic and flexible computing resources in edge computing, specifically for applications related to artificial intelligence in the Internet of Things (IoT). This mechanism addresses the varying computational needs of applications such as emotion recognition, video streaming, and smart technologies by migrating services to the nearest edge device with adequate computational power. 

A key concept introduced is "service resolution," which evaluates user quality of experience (QoE) based on application-specific metrics. For emotion detection, the accuracy rate is prioritized, while for video streaming, the resolution of the video is emphasized. The paper presents a table illustrating service resolutions for different applications, showing how they adapt to user demands by offering low, medium, and high resolutions. 

For emotion detection, the DCSMM facilitates three levels of service resolution: low resolution focuses on basic facial emotion recognition using a deep neural network (VGG), medium resolution analyzes both facial expressions and speech emotion with decision fusion, and high resolution employs advanced algorithms and deep networks (DBN) for more accurate multi-modal recognition. Each level progressively requires more computing resources and yields higher accuracy in emotion recognition. 

Overall, the DCSMM aims to optimize resource allocation at the edge, reduce latency, and enhance user experiences by dynamically adjusting service resolutions based on user behavior and application requirements.

This document is the final peer-reviewed accepted manuscript of the paper by Elisa Prati, Margherita Peruzzini, Marcello Pellicciari, and Roberto Raffaeli titled "How to include User eXperience in the design of Human-Robot Interaction," published in Robotics and Computer-Integrated Manufacturing, Volume 68, 2021. The paper discusses the importance of integrating User Experience (UX) into the design processes of Human-Robot Interaction (HRI). It emphasizes that considering user perspectives can enhance the effectiveness and acceptance of robotic systems. The authors provide insights and methodologies for incorporating UX principles to improve HRI design. The final published version is accessible online via the provided DOI link.

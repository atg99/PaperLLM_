The paper presents a User/Task Matrix that outlines the interactions between users and tasks in a specific robotics and computer-integrated manufacturing use case. This matrix serves as a framework to identify the roles of different users and the tasks they perform, highlighting the efficiency and effectiveness of user-task assignments. The analysis aims to improve workflow and task allocation through a structured approach, ultimately enhancing productivity in manufacturing environments. The findings emphasize the importance of aligning user capabilities with task requirements to optimize operational performance.

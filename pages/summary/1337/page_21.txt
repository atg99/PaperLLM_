This paper discusses the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) into digital twins (DTs) within the context of Industry 4.0, focusing on their potential for sustainable development through the lens of FESG factors. The study identifies several key contributions:

1. A comprehensive overview of the development and application of AI methods in AI-driven DTs.
2. An exploration of the advantages of these DTs in promoting sustainability, allowing for quantitative assessments.
3. A discussion of the challenges and future prospects for AI-driven DTs in smart manufacturing and advanced robotics.
4. A proposed pathway for integrating AI into multiscale DTs, utilizing diverse data sources throughout the product lifecycle.

The paper emphasizes that while traditional production models have been effective, their application has been limited by real-time capabilities and adaptability. AI can enhance these models, especially in dynamic environments, by incorporating prior knowledge to mitigate the reliance on extensive datasets. Additionally, uncertainty quantification is highlighted as crucial for assessing the reliability of AI-driven DT results, impacting decision-making in high-risk engineering contexts.

The authors acknowledge that the advancement of AI algorithms is constrained by current infrastructure, necessitating interdisciplinary collaboration and domain-specific expertise. Future innovations in sensors, 5G communication, and data security will be pivotal for successful implementation. The paper concludes with a call for further exploration of AI-driven DT technologies in various fields, including renewable energy and healthcare, anticipating that these efforts will foster synergies and support sustainable advancements across industries.

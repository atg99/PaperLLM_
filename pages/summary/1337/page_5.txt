The paper discusses the integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in various aspects of manufacturing, including planning, scheduling, production monitoring, quality control, logistics, supply chain management, and remanufacturing. It emphasizes that the availability of industrial production data in connected systems enhances the importance of AI and data-driven methods, paving the way for optimizing innovative manufacturing systems, such as line-less mobile assembly systems. 

The primary goal of using AI in this context is to enhance the adaptability of Digital Twins (DTs) to rapidly changing conditions on the factory floor. The paper identifies specific application areas for AI-integrated DTs, including production planning, production control, and quality control. A summary table outlines various AI categories and key methods applied in these subfields, showcasing examples like supervised learning for material selection and reinforcement learning for dynamic scheduling in flexible manufacturing systems. This integration aims to improve efficiency and effectiveness in smart manufacturing environments.

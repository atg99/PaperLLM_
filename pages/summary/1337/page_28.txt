The referenced papers explore the concept of digital twins within various manufacturing and engineering contexts. Key themes include the role of digital twins in predictive maintenance, modeling, and performance monitoring of machine tools and equipment. Notable contributions include approaches to integrate digital twins for decision-making and cooperative awareness, as well as their application in fault diagnosis for rotating machinery. Several studies emphasize the importance of real-time data and multi-fidelity modeling for accurate wear estimation and energy consumption management. The use of advanced techniques such as convolutional neural networks and generative adversarial networks for predictive analytics and defect recognition in manufacturing processes is also highlighted. Overall, the research underscores the potential of digital twins to enhance operational efficiency, predictive maintenance, and overall process optimization in smart manufacturing environments.

The paper discusses various applications of artificial intelligence (AI) in production control and quality management, highlighting key methods and their respective applications. In production control, supervised learning techniques such as probabilistic graphical models (PGM), deep neural networks (DNN), and reinforcement learning methods like deep Q-networks (DQN) are utilized for optimizing resource allocation, workflow, and order dispatching. Specific applications include optimizing assembly commissioning processes and improving geometrical quality in manufacturing.

For quality control, methods such as artificial neural networks (ANN), support vector machines (SVM), and convolutional neural networks (CNN) are employed to predict welding quality, detect anomalies in manufacturing components, and recognize features in CAD models. 

The paper also references a maturity model for production planning and control, emphasizing the role of AI in enhancing key performance indicators and facilitating decision-making through techniques like decision trees and dynamic scheduling approaches using DQN and graph convolution networks. Metaheuristic methods, including genetic algorithms, are noted for their effectiveness in addressing scheduling challenges in production environments. Overall, the integration of AI in these domains aims to create smarter, more efficient manufacturing systems.

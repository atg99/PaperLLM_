This paper presents a comprehensive survey of AI-driven digital twin (DT) technologies in the context of Industry 4.0, particularly focusing on smart manufacturing and advanced robotics. The authors, Ziqi Huang and colleagues, analyze over 300 manuscripts from the past five years to summarize the advancements and current state of AI integration within these sectors. 

The concept of digital twins serves as a digital replica of physical entities, underpinned by infrastructure and data, with algorithms and models at its core, and applications manifested through software and services. The integration of AI into DT technologies is crucial for enhancing operational efficiency and sustainability in manufacturing. The study highlights the significance of domain-specific expertise for successful implementation.

The paper discusses various applications of AI-driven DTs, including traditional metal machining, industrial automation, 3D printing, and human-robot interaction. It also addresses the advantages of these technologies in promoting sustainable development by balancing financial, environmental, social, and governance factors.

Moreover, the authors identify practical challenges and future development prospects for AI-driven DTs, emphasizing the need for a systematic approach to AI integration across different scales and fidelity levels. This survey contributes to understanding how AI can transform traditional manufacturing paradigms in the era of Industry 4.0.

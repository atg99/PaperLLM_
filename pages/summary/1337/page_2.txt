This paper explores the integration of Digital Twins (DT) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) within the context of sustainability and the circular economy, highlighting a lack of comprehensive reviews in this area. The authors propose four research questions aimed at understanding current developments in DTs, the state of AI integration, the benefits of AI-enabled DTs for sustainability, and the practical challenges and future work needed in this field.

The study aims to bridge the gap in existing literature by analyzing the application of AI methods in various engineering scenarios involving DTs, detailing their role in sustainable development, and identifying practical challenges across different domains. Key contributions of the paper include:

1. A summary of the development and application cases of AI-driven DTs within Industry 4.0.
2. An elaboration on the advantages of AI-driven DTs concerning sustainability, using FESG factors for quantitative assessment.
3. A discussion on the challenges and future prospects for AI-driven DTs in smart manufacturing and advanced robotics, tailored to various operational levels.
4. An outline for the integration of AI in multiscale DTs, utilizing diverse data sources throughout the product lifecycle.

The paper also defines key concepts, starting with Digital Twin, characterized by its ability to simulate physical entities and processes using vast amounts of data. The concept of Digital Shadow is introduced as a less data-intensive representation that still enables real-time evaluation and data analytics in product lifecycle management.

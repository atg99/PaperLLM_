In the production control stage, various machine learning (ML) models, including deep neural networks (DNN), decision trees, and ensemble models like AdaBoost and XGBoost, are utilized in digital production twins to enhance resource allocation and manufacturing performance metrics. The complexity of multi-objective problems in manufacturing often renders them non-deterministic and polynomial-time hard. To tackle this, reinforcement learning (RL) methods, including deep Q-networks (DQN) and deep RL, are employed, typically framed as Markov decision processes (MDP), aiming to optimize economic and logistic key performance indicators (KPIs) in manufacturing environments. Additionally, the integration of human behavior forecasting into production control through RL models is proposed to enhance smart manufacturing.

In quality control, traditional supervised ML models such as artificial neural networks (ANN), decision trees, and support vector machines (SVM) are used to predict production defects. Deep learning models, especially convolutional neural networks, are applied for real-time quality assessment during production, contributing to improved assembly efficiency and allowing for informed decision-making based on historical production data. The concept of digital twins (DT) combined with model-based systems engineering (MBSE) is emphasized as a means for modular simulation-based optimization.

The interim summary highlights that business profitability is essential for sustainable operations, with digital twins enhancing productivity, resilience, and transparency across the value chain. AI-enabled digital twins are seen as service agents, fostering innovative services and subscription models that support long-term manufacturing solutions. This adaptability is especially crucial for small- and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) during global crises, facilitating comprehensive assessments of sustainability factors.

Finally, the efficiency of machinery and equipment is critical for sustainable production, directly impacting energy consumption and resource utilization. The degradation of machinery due to operational stresses and environmental conditions necessitates a focus on maintenance strategies to ensure optimal performance and sustainability in manufacturing processes.

Manchev and Spratling's study investigates the performance of TPTT and TPTT hx networks on the MNIST sequence of pixels problem. The results indicate that both networks achieved similar accuracy rates, with TPTT scoring 54.20% and TPTT hx slightly higher at 54.75%. A grid search confirmed that both networks utilized the same learning rates, suggesting their performances are nearly identical.

The analysis concludes that the TPTT hx network, which employs an alternative update rule using local targets, outperforms both BPTT and the traditional TPTT network that relies on global error for weight updates. Additionally, TPTT hx converges more quickly than BPTT and effectively models synthetic classification problems while maintaining comparable accuracy on the MNIST task.

The paper references several key works in the field, highlighting the challenges of learning long-term dependencies in neural networks and the potential of alternative training methods in enhancing network performance.

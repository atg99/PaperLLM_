1.2. Biological Plausibility
Recurrent Neural Networks have also faced criticism for their lack of biological realism. The learning mechanisms employed in RNNs, particularly backpropagation through time, do not align well with how learning occurs in biological systems. This discrepancy raises questions about the applicability of RNNs in modeling cognitive processes that are observed in the human brain.

In summary, while Recurrent Neural Networks are powerful tools for sequence prediction and have seen widespread application, they face significant challenges related to training efficiency and biological relevance. The issues of gradient instability and the divergence from natural learning processes highlight the need for further research and development in this area.

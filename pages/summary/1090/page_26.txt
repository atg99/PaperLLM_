In the paper by Manchev and Spratling, the authors compare the performance of two models, TPTT and TPTT hx, in solving various problems, specifically focusing on their maximal depth or sequence length capabilities as indicated in Table A1. The depth achieved by both models varies across different problems, with TPTT performing better in most cases.

The paper details the learning rates used for both models in Table A2, highlighting the specific parameters for each problem. Notably, the TPTT hx network was tested for its robustness against Gaussian noise, which resulted in a 6.7% accuracy increase in the adding problem with tmax=70. However, this improvement was insufficient for the network to meet the solving criteria.

A significant finding is that the grid search did not yield a common set of learning rates effective across the temporal order, 3-bit temporal order, and permutation problems, indicating a lack of a unified model. The convergence speed of the TPTT hx network matched that of the BPTT implementation, falling short of the faster convergence times demonstrated by the TPTT model. This led to an exploration of larger learning rates, resulting in a successful parameter set of i=0.1, f=0.2, g=0.01, which solved all three problems more efficiently than BPTT. The modified search also revealed additional parameter combinations that improved problem-solving times further, emphasizing the need for continued exploration in this area.

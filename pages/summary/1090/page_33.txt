Wiseman et al. propose a novel approach for training language models using target propagation, emphasizing an alternative to traditional backpropagation methods.

Xu et al. present a model for neural image caption generation that incorporates visual attention, showcasing the versatility of RNNs beyond text. Lastly, Yogatama et al. delve into generative and discriminative text classification methods using RNNs, further demonstrating their applicability in various classification tasks.

Overall, these works collectively underscore the evolution and potential of recurrent neural networks in both cognitive neuroscience and various applications in natural language processing.

The YOLOv4-tiny method is specifically designed to address the limitations of existing YOLO models by significantly reducing the number of parameters and enhancing computational efficiency. This is crucial for real-time applications, especially on devices with restricted processing power and memory, such as mobile and embedded systems. The method achieves an impressive detection speed of 371 frames per second on a 1080Ti GPU, while maintaining an accuracy level suitable for practical applications.

The architecture of YOLOv4-tiny includes several key innovations, including the use of the CSPDarknet53-tiny backbone, which replaces the more complex CSPDarknet53 from the original YOLOv4. The incorporation of the CSPBlock module allows for better gradient flow and enhances the network's ability to learn from data, thereby improving detection capabilities.

In conclusion, the YOLOv4-tiny method represents a significant advancement in lightweight object detection, making it more feasible for deployment on devices with limited computational resources.

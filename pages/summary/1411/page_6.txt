The key innovation of our approach lies in the integration of two ResBlock-D modules, which replace the CSPBlock modules in the original CSPNet53-tiny architecture. Additionally, we have designed an auxiliary network block that employs two 3x3 convolutional layers, channel attention, spatial attention, and concatenation operations to enhance global feature extraction. This auxiliary block is incorporated into the backbone network, as illustrated in figure 5.

For our experiments, we utilized the MS COCO dataset, a widely recognized benchmark in object detection, comprising 117,264 training images and 5,000 testing images across 80 classes. The testing environment included an Ubuntu 18.04 operating system, an Intel Xeon E5-2678 v3 CPU, and an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1080Ti GPU, with CUDA 10.1 and the corresponding cuDNN installed to optimize GPU utilization during training. All methods were evaluated under consistent parameters: a batch size of 16, 273 epochs, a learning rate of 0.001, momentum of 0.973, and a decay of 0.0005. The performance metrics analyzed included mean Average Precision (mAP), Frames Per Second (FPS), and GPU utilization.

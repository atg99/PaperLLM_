The paper presents an enhanced version of the YOLOv4-tiny object detection method by modifying its network structure to improve detection speed while maintaining accuracy. The authors replace two CSPBlock modules with two identical ResBlock-D modules to simplify the network, thereby reducing computation time. An auxiliary network block is introduced, utilizing two 3x3 convolutional networks along with channel and spatial attention mechanisms to extract global features. This modified auxiliary network is integrated into the backbone, creating a new backbone network that effectively combines deep and shallow network benefits. The results indicate that the proposed method achieves faster object detection speeds compared to YOLOv3-tiny and YOLOv4-tiny, while maintaining a similar mean average precision to YOLOv4-tiny, thus demonstrating its effectiveness in balancing speed and accuracy in object detection tasks.

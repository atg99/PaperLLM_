The paper discusses advancements in camera model identification and source camera identification through various methodologies. 

Swaminathan et al. (2007) introduced a nonintrusive component forensics approach using output images, emphasizing the importance of analyzing visual sensor data for identification purposes. Thai et al. (2014) focused on a heteroscedastic noise model to enhance camera model identification accuracy. 

Tuama et al. (2016) explored the application of deep convolutional neural networks for camera model identification, demonstrating the potential of machine learning techniques in this domain. Yang et al. (2017) proposed a content-adaptive fusion network for source camera identification, showcasing a novel approach that adapts to different content types. 

Finally, Yao et al. (2018) developed a robust multi-classifier based on convolutional neural networks, further advancing the capabilities of camera model identification. Each study contributes to the growing body of knowledge on forensic techniques for visual sensors, highlighting the integration of deep learning and statistical models in enhancing identification accuracy.

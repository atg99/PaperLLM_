The paper presents the StoryGAN model, which enhances story visualization by generating coherent image sequences based on narrative input. The study focuses on the sequential conditional generation of images that represent a given story, where the model incorporates both local and global contextual information through its Text2Gist component within a Context Encoder framework.

The evaluation of StoryGAN includes a character image classifier trained on real images, achieving an accuracy of 86% on the test set, indicating improved character consistency compared to baseline models. Human evaluations conducted via Amazon Mechanical Turk showed that StoryGAN significantly outperformed ImageGAN in terms of visual quality, consistency, and relevance, with 74.17% of workers preferring StoryGAN's visual quality and 79.15% noting better consistency.

Moreover, ranking-based evaluations confirmed StoryGAN's superior performance, achieving the highest average rank among compared models. The findings underscore the effectiveness of StoryGAN in generating visually appealing and contextually relevant images that accurately depict the input narrative, suggesting that advancements in image generation techniques will further enhance story visualization quality.

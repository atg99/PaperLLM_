The paper discusses two benchmarks for commonsense reasoning: Physical Question Answering (PiQA) and The Story Cloze Test (StoryCloze). PiQA requires selecting a solution to a practical goal from two options, exemplified by the task of removing smell from garbage disposal, where the target solution is to create vinegar ice cubes. The dataset includes 16,113 examples, with a split of 16,013 for training and 100 for validation, using a separate test set of 1,838 examples. StoryCloze involves predicting the ending of a four-sentence story, where the correct continuation is that Caroline opened the soda and drank it all in one gulp. The dataset has 1,871 examples, with a training set of 1,671 and a test set of 1,871. Both benchmarks aim to evaluate the ability of systems to perform commonsense reasoning in physical and narrative contexts.

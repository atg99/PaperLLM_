The paper discusses Natural Language Inference (NLI) and presents examples of tasks where a hypothesis is evaluated against a premise to determine if it is entailed, not entailed, or if it is impossible to say. Two examples illustrate this: 

1. In the first example, the premise about Joey Heindle indicates that he received negative reviews, leading to the conclusion that he was disliked on television. The target answer is "Yes," supporting the inference that he was highly disliked.

2. The second example features a conversation where one speaker expresses that they do not really enjoy watching TV while exercising. The conclusion drawn is that "she really enjoys it" is not supported, resulting in the target answer "No."

The paper emphasizes the use of adversarial examples in NLI tasks and describes the datasets used for training and testing models in this context.

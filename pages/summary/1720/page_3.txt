The paper discusses the creation of an instruction tuning dataset by transforming 62 publicly available text datasets into an instructional format. These datasets, categorized into twelve task clusters, encompass both language understanding and generation tasks. Each dataset is paired with ten unique templates that describe the task using natural language, enhancing diversity by including templates that reframe the task. The authors then instruction tune a pretrained language model on this aggregated dataset.

For evaluation, the paper introduces a conservative definition of unseen tasks, where a dataset is considered unseen if no datasets from its task cluster were included in the instruction tuning phase. This allows for a robust assessment of the model's performance on tasks not encountered during training. The methodology aims to evaluate the model's zero-shot capabilities across different task clusters, ensuring a comprehensive understanding of its generalization abilities.

The paper discusses two commonsense reasoning tasks: Choice of Plausible Alternatives (COPA) and Commonsense Sentence Completion (HellaSwag). COPA requires inferring the cause or effect from a premise with two options, exemplified by a situation where packing belongings indicates moving out of an apartment. The dataset consists of 400 examples, with 350 for training and 50 for development, using a TFDS validation set of 100 for testing.

HellaSwag involves predicting the most probable sentence completion from four options based on a given context. An example illustrates a scenario where a male is climbing a wall and the correct continuation involves him using hooks for safety. The HellaSwag dataset contains 39,905 examples, with 30,000 for training and 200 for development, and a TFDS validation set of 10,042 for testing. Both tasks emphasize the importance of commonsense knowledge in reasoning and understanding context.

The paper discusses various tasks and datasets used for evaluating models in natural language processing, particularly in reading comprehension, structured text generation, translation, summarization, and miscellaneous tasks. 

For reading comprehension with commonsense, the datasets utilized include CosmosQA and ReCoRD. The structured text generation task is evaluated using CommonGen, DART, E2ENLG, and WebNLG. Translation tasks involve datasets from WMT’14 and WMT’16, as well as Paracrawl. Summarization is addressed using a diverse set of datasets, including AESLC, CNN-DM, Gigaword, and others, totaling eleven datasets. 

Miscellaneous tasks include conversational question-answering (QuAC, CoQA), evaluating word meanings (WiC), question classification (TREC), linguistic acceptability (CoLA), and math questions. 

The authors limit the training examples per dataset to 30,000 to ensure balanced contributions during fine-tuning. They utilize TensorFlow Datasets (TFDS) for loading and processing the datasets, and when available, they use labeled test sets; otherwise, they resort to TFDS validation sets. The paper also includes comparisons with GPT-3 and provides supplementary material for evaluation templates.

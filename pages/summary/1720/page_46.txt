The paper discusses the task of reading comprehension with commonsense reasoning, illustrated through an example involving a documentary film titled "The Flat." The film follows the story of an elderly woman's apartment filled with personal belongings, where her grandchildren discover documents linking their grandparents to a notable Nazi figure, Leopold von Mildenstein. The narrative raises questions about the grandparents' connections despite their Zionist beliefs. The example provided highlights a cloze-style question from the ReCoRD dataset, where the goal is to fill in the missing entity (Leopold von Mildenstein) based on context. Additionally, the paper includes a translation task example, showcasing the translation of a geographical description from English to German. The dataset includes a substantial number of training examples, with specific sets allocated for training, development, and testing purposes.

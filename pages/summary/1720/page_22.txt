The paper presents comprehensive results for various datasets evaluated in the context of instruction tuning for language models, specifically FLAN and LaMDA-PT. It includes performance metrics for translation tasks and structural-to-text tasks, detailed in Tables 1 and 2. The authors compare the performance of FLAN across multiple instruction templates, selecting the best-performing template from a pool of up to ten. For LaMDA-PT, they utilized existing templates optimized for GPT-3 without additional prompt engineering.

Key distinctions are made regarding the definition of zero-shot performance, particularly in relation to the DROP and SQuADv2 datasets, where the authors clarify that their methodology differs from prior work. The results for several translation tasks show varying BLEU scores, while structural-to-text tasks are evaluated using ROUGE metrics. Notably, the paper emphasizes the limitations in evaluating summarization tasks due to input length constraints. Overall, the results indicate FLAN's competitive performance relative to existing models like GPT-3 and T5 across the evaluated tasks.

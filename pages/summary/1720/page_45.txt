The paper discusses coreference resolution through the lens of two challenges: the Adversarial Winograd Schema Challenge (Winogrande) and the Winograd Schema Challenge (WSC273). Winogrande consists of 40,400 examples, from which 30,000 are used for training and 200 for development, with a test set of 1,267 from the TFDS validation set. It requires models to fill in a masked token based on context, as illustrated by examples where the model must determine which character is relevant to the sentence's conclusion. WSC273, on the other hand, is a test-set only challenge with no training or development examples, focusing on similar coreference tasks. The paper highlights the importance of understanding entities in sentences for accurate coreference resolution.

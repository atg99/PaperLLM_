The paper discusses various question-answering datasets and their characteristics. It highlights three specific datasets: The AI2 Reasoning Challenge (ARC), Natural Questions (NQ), and Trivia Question Answering (TriviaQA). 

ARC involves grade-school level multiple-choice science questions, with a training set comprising 1,119 challenge examples and 2,251 easy examples. The training and validation splits are specified, along with the test sets used for evaluation.

NQ focuses on open-ended questions that can be answered using Wikipedia content. The dataset includes 87,925 examples, with a clear division for training and testing purposes.

TriviaQA consists of trivia question-answer pairs created by enthusiasts, with a similar training set structure as NQ, and utilizes examples from Wikipedia for testing.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the structure and purpose of these datasets in advancing the field of question answering.

The paper discusses a confidential document from the Vatican's doctrinal congregation stating that "sex-change" procedures do not alter a person's gender according to church beliefs. It presents an example of Recognizing Textual Entailment (RTE), where the conclusion that "Sex-change operations become more common" is determined to be false based on the initial statement. The study involves a dataset of 2490 examples, with a focus on training, development, and testing subsets for evaluating the entailment task.

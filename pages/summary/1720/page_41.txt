The paper presents a study on Openbook Question Answering (OBQA), focusing on a dataset that includes 4,957 training examples and a validation set of 200 out of 500 examples from the TensorFlow Datasets (TFDS). The study illustrates the OBQA task with a specific example where the input statement highlights soil as a renewable resource for plants, leading to the correct answer choice of "dirt" among other options. The research emphasizes the importance of factual knowledge in answering multiple-choice questions accurately. The dataset is structured to facilitate training and testing of models in the OBQA framework.

The paper discusses the capabilities of the FLAN model in generating new words and narratives, showcasing its performance in various tasks. It highlights FLAN's ability to create unique words that convey specific meanings, such as "pipedreamic" for "like a pipedream" and "Bananation" for "when bananas shower from the sky." Additionally, the paper presents a poignant story about carrots, illustrating emotional connections through a narrative about a son trying to care for his sick mother by buying carrots she grew. The model's strengths in open-ended generation tasks are noted, along with examples of its outputs. However, it also points out limitations, such as failing to accurately respond to straightforward queries. Overall, the paper emphasizes FLAN's versatility and the challenges it faces in certain tasks.

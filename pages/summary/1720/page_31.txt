The paper discusses the unexpected finding that scaling up the LaMDA-PT model does not consistently enhance zero-shot performance, particularly in reading comprehension and sentiment analysis tasks. This contrasts with earlier findings by Brown et al. (2020) that scaling generally improves performance across various tasks. The authors note that similar results were observed in the GPT-3 paper, where zero-shot performance on specific tasks, such as BoolQ and DROP, decreased when increasing parameters from 13B to 175B. This suggests a consistent artifact across different models that merits further investigation, though it is outside the primary focus of the study on instruction tuning.

Additionally, the paper presents qualitative examples of the FLAN model's responses to prompts. In sentiment analysis, FLAN correctly adjusts its answer based on the phrasing of the question. For instance, when asked if a critic liked a movie based on a positive review, FLAN indicates that the critic liked the movie. In another example, FLAN demonstrates its ability to provide answers in different languages when prompted, showcasing its versatility in question answering tasks. The outputs are generated using random sampling techniques to enhance variability.

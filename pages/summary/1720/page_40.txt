The paper discusses two reading comprehension tasks: Boolean Questions (BoolQ) and Multi-Sentence Reading Comprehension (MultiRC). 

In the BoolQ task, participants answer yes/no questions based on provided passages. An example is given regarding Canadian citizenship, where the correct answer is "no," since citizenship by descent is not automatically granted through a grandparent.

For the MultiRC task, participants respond to open-ended questions based on a paragraph. An example involves a passage about glaciers in Illinois, where the response "one mile" correctly answers the question about the size of the glaciers.

The paper includes details about the training and validation sets for both tasks, highlighting the number of examples used for training and testing.

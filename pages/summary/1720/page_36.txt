The appendix of the conference paper details the datasets used for various natural language processing tasks. The datasets are categorized into specific task clusters:

1. **Natural Language Inference**: This cluster assesses the relationship between two sentences, determining if the second is true, false, or possibly true based on the first sentence. The datasets include ANLI, CB, MNLI, QNLI, SNLI, WNLI, and RTE.

2. **Reading Comprehension**: This evaluates the ability to answer questions based on a provided passage containing the answer. The datasets in this category are BoolQ, DROP, MultiRC, OBQA, SQuADv1, and SQuADv2.

3. **Commonsense Reasoning**: This focuses on the ability to perform reasoning that requires common sense and scientific understanding. Datasets include COPA, HellaSwag, PiQA, and StoryCloze.

4. **Sentiment Analysis**: This classic NLP task aims to determine if a text expresses a positive or negative sentiment. The datasets used are IMDB, Sentiment140, SST-2, and Yelp.

5. **Closed-book Question Answering**: This task involves answering questions about the world without specific access to the information needed for the answers. The datasets include ARC, NQ, and TriviaQA.

6. **Paraphrase Detection**: This assesses whether two sentences are semantically equivalent. The datasets used are MRPC, QQP, and Paws Wiki.

7. **Coreference Resolution**: This tests the ability to identify expressions referring to the same entity within a text. The datasets in this category are DPR and Winogrande.

The paper presents the capabilities of the FLAN model in generating utterances and expanding queries in a zero-shot manner. It showcases FLAN's ability to produce relevant phrases for specific intents, such as asking about voting locations or obtaining a COVID vaccine, without prior training on those specific queries. Additionally, the model can suggest related search terms for topics like Novak Djokovic and watermelon. FLAN also demonstrates creative writing skills by rewriting sentences in a Shakespearean style and completing narratives with positive outcomes. The outputs are generated through random sampling techniques, enhancing the model's versatility in assisted writing and query generation tasks.

The paper presents the performance results of the FLAN 137B model across various natural language understanding (NLU) tasks, comparing it with other models such as GLaM, LaMDA-PT, and GPT-3. The evaluation metrics focus on accuracy or exact match, with specific considerations for tasks like DROP, MultiRC, and SQuAD, where F1 scores are reported. 

The results are categorized into different task clusters, including NLI, reading comprehension, closed-book QA, commonsense reasoning, sentiment analysis, paraphrase detection, coreference resolution, and reading comprehension with commonsense. Each task shows a range of performance based on zero-shot, one-shot, and few-shot settings, indicating the model's adaptability to different learning paradigms.

For example, in the ANLI tasks, FLAN shows competitive accuracy, while in sentiment analysis tasks like SST-2 and IMDB, it achieves high accuracy rates. The paper also notes the number of few-shot exemplars and templates used in evaluations, emphasizing the model's performance across various configurations. 

Overall, the findings highlight FLAN 137B's strong capabilities in NLU tasks, showcasing its effectiveness in both few-shot and zero-shot learning scenarios compared to other leading models.

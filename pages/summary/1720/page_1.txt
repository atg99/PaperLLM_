This paper presents a method to enhance the zero-shot learning capabilities of language models through instruction tuning. The authors fine-tune a 137 billion parameter pretrained language model on over 60 NLP datasets using natural language instruction templates, resulting in a model called FLAN. The evaluation shows that FLAN significantly outperforms its unmodified counterpart and surpasses the zero-shot performance of the 175 billion parameter GPT-3 on 20 out of 25 datasets. Additionally, FLAN outperforms few-shot GPT-3 on several tasks, including ANLI, RTE, BoolQ, AI2-ARC, OpenbookQA, and StoryCloze. Key factors for the success of instruction tuning identified in ablation studies include the number of fine-tuning datasets, model scale, and the use of natural language instructions. The paper illustrates the effectiveness of instruction tuning in improving performance on unseen tasks, demonstrating its potential for advancing language model capabilities.

In the paper "Zadeh’s Type-2 Fuzzy Logic Systems: Precision and High-Quality Prediction Intervals," the authors Yusuf Güven, Ata Köklü, and Tufan Kumbasar explore the application of General Type-2 (GT2) Fuzzy Logic Systems (FLSs) for effective uncertainty quantification, particularly in high-risk decision-making scenarios. By revisiting Zadeh's definition of GT2 Fuzzy Sets (FSs), the paper aims to develop GT2-FLSs that can not only provide precise predictions but also generate High-Quality Prediction Intervals (HQ-PIs).

The authors argue that the integration of Zadeh's GT2 FS with the α-plane representation enhances the design flexibility of GT2-FLSs, as it decouples the secondary membership function from the primary membership function. They address challenges associated with learning from high-dimensional data, such as the curse of dimensionality, by proposing methods to adjust the primary membership function in relation to input dimensions, and introducing parameterization strategies to facilitate learning with deep learning optimizers.

A deep learning framework is developed for training dual-focused Z-GT2-FLSs, demonstrating high performance in both point-wise predictions and HQ-PI generation. The results indicate that Z-GT2-FLSs outperform their Interval Type-2 (IT2) and Mendel & John (MJ) GT2 counterparts, despite having fewer learnable parameters. The statistical analysis underscores the potential of Z-GT2-FLSs in effectively quantifying uncertainty, making them valuable tools for applications requiring reliable decision-making under uncertainty.

The paper discusses two types of Generalized Type-2 Fuzzy Logic Systems (GT2-FLS): the Modified Jang GT2-FLS (MJ-GT2-FLS) and the Z-GT2-FLS. The key distinction between them lies in their parameter definitions, specifically θA, which includes different sets of parameters for each system. For MJ-GT2-FLS, parameters include c, σ, h, and δ, while Z-GT2-FLS uses c, σ, along with left and right standard deviations σl and σr. The MJ-GT2-FLS has a greater number of linear parameters (LPs) compared to Z-GT2-FLS, which simplifies the model despite its added complexity.

The paper introduces parameterization tricks for deep learning (DL) optimizers to manage the constraints of GT2-FLS parameters, particularly σl and σr. These tricks ensure that the learned parameters adhere to the required conditions of GT2-FLSs, allowing for an unbounded search space for optimization. Specifically, the transformations for σl and σr leverage the sigmoid function to maintain the constraints.

A dual-focused GT2-FLS framework is proposed to enhance both point-wise accuracy and high-quality prediction intervals (HQ-PI). The training process involves minimizing a loss function composed of an accuracy-focused component and an uncertainty-focused component. The accuracy component utilizes an empirical risk function, while the uncertainty component employs a pinball loss to define quantile levels for uncertainty estimation.

The performance of the proposed Z-GT2-FLS is evaluated against MJ-GT2-FLS and two Interval Type-2 FLSs using high-dimensional benchmark datasets, including White Wine, Parkinson Motor UPDRS, and AIDS. The experiments involve preprocessing the datasets and training the models under consistent hyperparameters, ensuring a controlled comparison of performance metrics, particularly focusing on prediction interval coverage.

In conclusion, the paper presents a comprehensive approach to optimizing GT2-FLSs through parameterization techniques and a dual-focused learning strategy, demonstrating its effectiveness through empirical analysis on benchmark datasets.

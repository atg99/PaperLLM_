The study emphasizes the importance of these parameters in the context of generalized type-2 fuzzy logic systems (GT2-FLSs), particularly in addressing challenges such as the curse of dimensionality. 

The paper outlines the representation of GT2 fuzzy sets (GT2-FSs) using Zadeh's framework, employing an α-plane approach to define the output of the Z-GT2-FLS. The authors derive the membership functions for the primary and secondary memberships, establishing a clear mathematical foundation for their methodology. They also address the issue of high-dimensional data, proposing a scaling method to maintain the effectiveness of the GT2-FLS despite increasing feature dimensions.

In conclusion, the study presents a structured approach to defining and learning the parameters of GT2-FLSs, highlighting the significance of the α-plane representation and addressing the challenges posed by high-dimensional datasets. The proposed methods aim to enhance the robustness and applicability of GT2-FLSs in various computational scenarios.

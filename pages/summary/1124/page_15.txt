The paper discusses the advantages of a proposed online knowledge transfer scheme for multitask optimization, emphasizing its effectiveness in complex controller design tasks. This method operates automatically, reducing the need for human intervention in identifying inter-task synergies, and consistently outperforms several state-of-the-art optimizers. The paper also highlights the potential of future technologies, such as cloud computing and the Internet of Things, to facilitate large-scale data sharing, enabling embedded optimization solvers to leverage knowledge from related tasks. The principles of multitasking are anticipated to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of solving multiple tasks by recognizing recurring patterns in real-world scenarios. This approach aligns with the concept of General Optimization Intelligence (GOI), which envisions machines capable of performing diverse tasks and learning from experience. The acknowledgments note support from the Singapore Institute of Manufacturing Technology and Nanyang Technological University. The references include significant works in the fields of transfer learning, evolutionary computation, and multitask optimization, underscoring the foundational research that informs the study.

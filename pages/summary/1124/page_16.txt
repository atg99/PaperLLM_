The references presented cover a variety of topics within evolutionary computation and optimization, emphasizing cooperative coevolution, evolutionary multitasking, and neuroevolution techniques. Key works include Potter and De Jong's cooperative coevolutionary methods for function optimization, as well as advancements in adaptive memetic algorithms and many-tasking frameworks. The importance of modular knowledge representation and resource allocation in evolutionary multitasking is highlighted, along with contributions to estimation of distribution algorithms and natural evolution strategies. Several studies focus on the convergence and efficiency of evolutionary algorithms in various domains, including real-parameter optimization and combinatorial problems. The references collectively illustrate the evolution of methodologies aimed at enhancing the performance and scalability of evolutionary algorithms in solving complex optimization tasks.

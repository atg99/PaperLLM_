The referenced papers explore various advancements in deep learning architectures and techniques for image processing tasks. 

Chen et al. focus on efficient multi-scale architectures for dense image prediction, emphasizing the importance of optimizing neural networks for better performance in tasks such as image segmentation. Nekrasov et al. present a method for fast neural architecture search aimed at creating compact models for semantic segmentation through the use of auxiliary cells, demonstrating a balance between efficiency and accuracy.

Wang et al. introduce a novel approach to optical flow estimation, utilizing displacement-invariant matching cost learning to enhance the accuracy of flow predictions. Lastly, Dai et al. propose MVS2, a deep unsupervised method for multi-view stereo, leveraging multi-view symmetry to improve depth estimation from multiple images.

Overall, these studies contribute to the ongoing development of more effective and efficient neural network architectures for complex visual tasks.

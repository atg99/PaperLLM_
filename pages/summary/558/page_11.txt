This collection of references primarily focuses on advancements in neural architecture search (NAS) and efficient model design for various computer vision tasks. Key contributions include:

1. **FBNet** (Wu et al. 2019) introduces a hardware-aware approach to efficient convolutional network design through differentiable NAS, optimizing for performance on specific hardware.
2. **Densely Connected Search Space** (Fang et al. 2019) presents a flexible NAS framework that allows for more versatile architecture exploration.
3. **EfficientDet** (Tan et al. 2019) discusses a scalable and efficient object detection framework that balances accuracy and computational efficiency.
4. **Auto-DeepLab** (Liu et al. 2019) employs hierarchical NAS specifically for semantic image segmentation, enhancing performance across various scales.
5. **Neural Architecture Search with Reinforcement Learning** (Zoph & Le 2016) and subsequent works (Zoph et al. 2018) explore transferable architectures, emphasizing the importance of scalable solutions in image recognition tasks.
6. **Stereo Matching** advancements (e.g., GA-Net by Zhang et al. 2019 and DeepPruner by Duggal et al. 2019) leverage differentiable methods to improve disparity estimation and enhance efficiency in stereo vision applications.

The references collectively highlight the trend towards more adaptive and efficient architectures in deep learning, particularly in computer vision, while addressing challenges such as real-time performance and scalability.

This paper presents a novel hierarchical Neural Architecture Search (NAS) framework for deep stereo matching, addressing the challenges of applying NAS to low-level geometric vision tasks. While NAS has shown success in high-level vision tasks, its application to existing large deep stereo matching networks is computationally prohibitive. The authors introduce an end-to-end framework that incorporates human knowledge specific to stereo matching, optimizing the architectures of the entire processing pipeline, which includes feature extraction, feature volume construction, and dense matching.

The experimental results demonstrate that the proposed NAS framework outperforms all existing deep stereo matching architectures, achieving top accuracy on the KITTI stereo 2012, 2015, and Middlebury benchmarks, as well as the SceneFlow dataset. Additionally, the framework significantly improves both the size of the network and the speed of inference. The code for the framework is made available under the name LEAStereo, contributing to advancements in deep stereo matching methodologies.

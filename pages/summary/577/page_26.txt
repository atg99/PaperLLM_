The paper discusses transfer learning from both data and model perspectives, highlighting its mechanisms, strategies, and applications in areas like object recognition and text categorization. It emphasizes the critical nature of selecting appropriate transfer learning models, which remains a complex issue in practical applications. Future research directions include expanding transfer learning techniques to more diverse applications, addressing knowledge transfer challenges while safeguarding user privacy, improving methods to measure transferability and mitigate negative transfer, and enhancing the interpretability of transfer learning models. The authors advocate for further theoretical studies to support the effectiveness and applicability of transfer learning, which offers advantages over traditional machine learning, such as reduced dependency on data and labels. The research is supported by various grants from Chinese institutions.

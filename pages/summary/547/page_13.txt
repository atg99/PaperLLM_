The paper discusses the initialization and training procedures for a neural network model. The He initialization method was used for the network's weights, while bias variables were set to zero. The final fully connected layer was initialized with a mean of 0 and a standard deviation of 0.01. An L2 regularization rate of 4 x 10^-5 was applied to all convolutional kernels, excluding the final fully connected layer. The models were trained on 224 x 224 input images using ResNet image preprocessing for either 90 or 360 epochs. A dropout rate of 0 was used before the final fully connected layer for 90 epochs, while a rate of 0.15 was used for 360 epochs.

The paper also highlights the discovery of frontier architectures on ImageNet, as presented in Table 2 and Figure 10. The predictor identified architectures utilizing inexpensive operations, such as small kernel sizes and expansion factors or skip operations in early layers, while incorporating diverse operations in later layers to optimize performance. Additionally, the authors noted a correction made to the default TensorFlow implementation of He initialization to address an overestimation issue concerning the fan-in of depthwise convolutions.

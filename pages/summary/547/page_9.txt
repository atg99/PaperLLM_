The choice of method for Neural Architecture Search (NAS) is influenced by the complexity of implementing the search space, particularly with weight sharing, and the available computational resources. This paper emphasizes that while regression models have been previously employed in NAS, the proposed approach is notably simpler compared to other methods that utilize more intricate setups, such as back-propagation through network architectures or Bayesian optimization. The authors acknowledge contributions from various experts that aided in the development of their ideas. The references cited provide a foundation for understanding the evolution and various methodologies in NAS, highlighting the diversity of approaches, from performance prediction to parameter sharing and evolutionary strategies.

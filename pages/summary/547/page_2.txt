The process begins with constructing a graph representation of the architecture, where the nodes represent operations and the edges signify connections between them.

The Neural Predictor operates in three main steps. First, it builds a predictor using a dataset of architecture and validation accuracy pairs, which is crucial to train a regression model that predicts validation accuracy based on architecture. In the second step, the predictor is utilized to efficiently evaluate a large number of random architectures, allowing for the selection of the top K architectures for further validation. The final step involves traditional validation of these top K models to determine the best architecture based on actual validation accuracy.

The paper emphasizes the efficiency of the Neural Predictor compared to existing methods. It demonstrates that the Neural Predictor significantly outperforms random search and is more sample-efficient than Regularized Evolution, the leading method in the NASBench-101 benchmark. Additionally, the approach is adaptable to different search spaces, as evidenced by its successful application to ProxylessNAS.

The authors discuss the hyper-parameter settings for the number of models trained (N) and the number selected for final validation (K). They note that increasing N improves the predictor's accuracy but requires a decrease in K to maintain a fixed computational budget. This trade-off is crucial, as the performance of the predictor can influence the selection of high-quality architectures.

Lastly, the paper introduces Graph Convolutional Networks (GCNs) as a method for modeling the architecture representations. GCNs are particularly suited for learning from graph-structured data, allowing for effective representation of neural network architectures. The discussion on GCNs is intended to highlight their potential in improving the predictive capabilities of the Neural Predictor. Overall, the proposed method combines efficiency and adaptability, making it a promising approach for neural architecture search.

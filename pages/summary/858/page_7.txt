The referenced works contribute to advancements in natural language processing, particularly focusing on self-attention mechanisms and their applications in various models. Wei Wu et al. (2018) introduced Phrase-level Self-Attention Networks aimed at enhancing universal sentence encoding. Yuxin Wu and Kaiming He (2018) proposed Group Normalization, which improves stability in training deep networks. Baosong Yang et al. (2019) developed Context-Aware Self-Attention Networks, emphasizing the importance of context in attention mechanisms. In their 2018 work, Yang et al. also explored modeling localness within self-attention networks, addressing the need for localized attention. Furthermore, Yang et al. (2017) focused on bidirectional hierarchical representations to enhance attention-based neural machine translation. Lastly, Yu et al. (2018) presented QANet, which combines local convolution with global self-attention to improve reading comprehension tasks. Collectively, these studies illustrate the evolution of self-attention techniques and their integration into various NLP applications.

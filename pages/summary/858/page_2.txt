this paper, we introduce Self-Attention Networks (SANs) which enhance representation learning by applying attention mechanisms to all token pairs in an input sequence, regardless of their distance. The model transforms the input sequence into queries, keys, and values, enabling it to capture diverse contextual features through multiple attention heads. 

We propose two novel approaches to improve SANs: 1D-Convolutional SANs and 2D-Convolutional SANs. The 1D-Convolutional SANs restrict attention to a local region, allowing the model to focus on neighboring tokens, which facilitates the capture of phrasal patterns and local dependencies. This is achieved by modifying the attention mechanism to only consider a fixed-size window of tokens around each query. 

Furthermore, 2D-Convolutional SANs enhance feature learning by allowing interactions among adjacent attention heads, thereby enriching the representation beyond individual subspaces. This dual focus on locality and inter-head interaction is expected to yield better performance in capturing linguistic properties across different contexts.

The implementation of these models is structured in layers, where lower layers focus on surface-level information through locality modeling, while higher layers capture more abstract semantic information. This design aligns with previous findings in the field, suggesting that such hierarchical learning benefits overall model performance.

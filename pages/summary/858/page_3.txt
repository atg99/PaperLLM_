The paper discusses advancements in Self-Attention Networks (SANs) by proposing a hierarchical learning approach that captures both distance-aware local information and distance-agnostic global information. The authors introduce a 2D convolution mechanism to enhance interaction among multi-head attention, allowing different heads to capture distinct linguistic properties across local contexts. This method aims to improve the performance of SANs by leveraging local features from multiple heads instead of aggregating them linearly, which limits abstraction.

The authors build on related work that has aimed to enhance SANs through complementary information and locality modeling. Past studies have incorporated recurrent modeling, convolutional layers, and parametric localness biases to improve SANs. However, the proposed method is noted for being lightweight, as it does not introduce new parameters, unlike some previous models.

Empirical results from experiments on machine translation tasks demonstrate that the proposed approach surpasses existing methods in both translation quality and training efficiency. Overall, the paper emphasizes the importance of modeling interactions among attention heads to better exploit rich local properties, ultimately enhancing the expressivity and effectiveness of SANs in various applications.

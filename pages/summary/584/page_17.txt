This paper discusses the application of transfer learning (TL) in improving the performance of deep learning models for medical image classification, specifically focusing on diabetic foot ulcers (DFU). The study highlights six key points: 

1. The challenge of insufficient training data has been addressed through TL.
2. A hybrid deep learning model has been proposed, integrating various structures, including traditional and parallel convolutional layers with residual links, enhancing feature representation.
3. A DFU dataset was compiled and annotated by experts for experimental purposes.
4. Four training scenarios were established, including training from scratch and scenarios utilizing TL from both the same and different domains.
5. TL from the same domain was found to be significantly more effective, demonstrating that fewer images from the same domain yield better results than a large dataset from a different domain.
6. The proposed model achieved notable performance metrics, with an F1-score of 97.6% when utilizing TL from the same domain, compared to 89.4% from a different domain and 86.6% from scratch.

The authors indicate plans to extend the application of same domain TL to other areas, emphasizing its potential benefits. The research was conducted without external funding, and all authors contributed equally to the project.

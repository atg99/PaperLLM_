In this paper, the authors focus on the visualization of learned features from a convolutional neural network (CNN) trained on two distinct classes: abnormal and normal. The visualization was accomplished by examining the first convolutional layer of the model, where the learned filters for both classes were displayed in Figures 9 and 10. The training utilized stochastic gradient descent with a momentum of 0.9, a mini-batch size of 64, and a maximum of 100 epochs, beginning with a learning rate of 0.001. The experiments were conducted using Matlab 2019 on an Intel Core i7-5829K CPU with 32 GB RAM and an 8 GB GPU.

To evaluate the model's performance, the authors calculated recall, precision, and F1-score using true positives (TP), false positives (FP), and false negatives (FN). The formulas for these metrics are provided, highlighting their importance in assessing the model's effectiveness.

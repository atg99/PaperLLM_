This section of the paper discusses two datasets used for training a model to detect diabetic foot ulcers (DFUs) and classify animal images. Dataset A, focused on DFUs, includes images of both normal and abnormal regions, with abnormal patches indicated in blue and normal patches in green. Dataset B contains 1490 images of cows and chickens, resized to a uniform dimension of 224x224 pixels for model compatibility. The authors ensured a balanced comparison by matching the quantities of data from both datasets.

Additionally, the paper introduces pre-training datasets, Dataset C and Dataset D, which are designed to enhance the model's performance on the target datasets. Dataset C consists of medical images similar to those in Dataset A, featuring various wound types, while Dataset D contains images analogous to those in Dataset B. The primary goal of these pre-training datasets is to improve transfer learning (TL) by utilizing images that share similar characteristics with the target datasets.

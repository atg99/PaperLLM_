The paper discusses the compilation of two extensive image datasets used for medical and animal classification tasks. 

The medical dataset, referred to as Dataset C, consists of a total of 50,103 images sourced from various collections, including 2,700 wound images, 1,000 clinical skin disease images, and 37,364 images of different skin cancers such as melanoma and basal cell carcinoma. All images were resized to 224 by 224 pixels, and some were further divided into multiple sub-images.

The large animal dataset, known as Dataset D, comprises 50,957 images across several animal classes. This dataset includes images from eight animal classes (e.g., dog, cat, horse) totaling 21,215 images, an additional 8,000 images of cats and dogs, 2,099 images of various birds, and images of other animals like chimpanzees and deer. Additionally, it contains 16,643 images of wild animals such as pandas and lions, with all images also resized to 224 by 224 pixels.

Figures showcasing sample images from both datasets are included to illustrate their diversity and scope.

2. **Overfitting Prevention**: Srivastava et al. introduce dropout as an effective method to mitigate overfitting in neural networks.
3. **Batch Normalization**: Ioffe and Szegedy highlight the role of batch normalization in accelerating training by addressing internal covariate shift.

5. **Ensemble Learning**: Veit et al. and Zhao et al. examine how residual networks can be interpreted as ensembles of shallower networks, while Wang et al. propose deeply-fused networks to enhance performance.
6. **Transfer Learning**: A range of papers (e.g., by Shin et al. and Tan et al.) survey the applications and methodologies of transfer learning, particularly in medical imaging and activity recognition.
7. **Data Augmentation**: Shorten and Khoshgoftaar provide a comprehensive overview of image data augmentation techniques, which are critical for enhancing model robustness.

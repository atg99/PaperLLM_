The section discusses real-life applications of Differential Evolution (DE) and Evolution Strategies (ES) in various domains. 

In the context of DE, Ramli et al. utilized a multi-objective Self-adaptive DE (SaDE) algorithm for optimizing the sizing of a hybrid microgrid system that incorporates photovoltaic, wind, and diesel energy sources, along with battery storage. Their approach assessed factors such as power supply reliability and cost-effectiveness through case studies in Yanbu, Saudi Arabia. Yao et al. applied a multi-objective DE algorithm to improve a combined cooling, heating, and power compressed air energy storage system, successfully identifying a balance between exergy efficiency and cost. Wang et al. focused on optimizing wind farm layouts using DE, achieving superior performance in power output and execution time compared to other methods. Another study by the authors of the paper examined linear dipole array synthesis using a dynamic DE algorithm to enhance power pattern shaping. Lastly, Tian et al. employed a hybrid DE-PSO approach for dual-objective scheduling of rescue vehicles in forest fire scenarios, demonstrating quick generation of satisfactory solutions.

In contrast, applications of ES are less frequent but notable. Hasancebi introduced an integrated parallel optimization algorithm for minimizing the weight of steel frames in multi-story buildings, providing insights for cost-effective design. Fadda et al. addressed energy distribution system uncertainties by proposing an intelligent battery that utilizes a customized ES to optimize its recharge strategy. Ogidan et al. presented an enhanced non-dominated sorting ES algorithm aimed at reducing sanitary sewer overflows while minimizing rehabilitation costs, achieving improved convergence rates. Finally, a study on wireless sensor fault diagnosis employed the CMA-ES algorithm to optimize parameters in a hierarchical model, showing effectiveness over traditional methods.

Overall, the applications reflect the versatility and effectiveness of DE and ES algorithms in solving complex real-world problems across various fields.

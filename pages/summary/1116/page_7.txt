The paper discusses the challenges and methodologies related to constraint handling in evolutionary algorithms (EAs). It emphasizes the importance of understanding the nature of constraints at the optimum and the implications of different types of constraints on the applicability of various methods. The authors note that common techniques such as penalty methods, feasibility rules, and stochastic ranking are favored in real-world applications due to their simplicity, despite the absence of a universal approach suitable for all problems.

The paper also highlights the increasing popularity of EAs, evidenced by a growing number of publications from 2000 to 2018 across several algorithms including Genetic Algorithms (GA), Genetic Programming (GP), Differential Evolution (DE), and Evolution Strategies (ES). Notably, the Evolutionary Programming (EP) algorithm has seen a decline in publications since 2013. The authors present data on the total number of articles and patents related to these algorithms, indicating significant industrial interest from companies such as Caterpillar, Yamaha, and IBM.

The authors conclude by suggesting that future research will likely focus on improving constraint handling techniques within EAs, reflecting the ongoing interest and open research problems in this area.

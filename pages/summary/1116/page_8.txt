The paper discusses the application of evolutionary algorithms (EAs) in real-life problems, highlighting the increasing demand for smart optimization methods across various business and engineering sectors. EAs are particularly effective for optimization, scheduling, planning, design, and management tasks, prevalent in fields like investments, production, and distribution. 

Analysis of the Web of Science (WoS) database reveals that EAs are widely utilized in areas such as engineering (specifically electrical and civil), computer science (particularly artificial intelligence and theory methods), automation control systems, and operations research management science. The paper identifies highly cited papers employing specific EA methods, including genetic algorithms (GA), genetic programming (GP), differential evolution (DE), evolution strategies (ES), and evolutionary programming (EP), across diverse industries such as energy fuels, water resources, construction, and software engineering.

The paper also presents data on the number of patents registered in the Google Patents database from 2000 to 2018, indicating a significant increase in patents for GA, GP, DE, ES, and EP, which reflects the growing interest and application of these methods in various industrial contexts. The focus of the paper is on the real-world applications of these EA methods in the aforementioned sectors, providing insights into their relevance and impact.

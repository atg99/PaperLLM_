This review article by Adam Slowik and Halina Kwasnicka explores the family of evolutionary algorithms (EAs) and their applications in engineering. The authors provide a detailed overview of several key algorithms, including genetic algorithms, genetic programming, differential evolution, evolution strategies, and evolutionary programming. Each algorithm is presented with pseudo-code to facilitate implementation in various programming languages.

The paper highlights the essential properties of these algorithms and illustrates their state-of-the-art applications across different industries. It emphasizes the growing interest in nature-inspired optimization techniques within the field of soft computing and positions EAs as a significant subset of evolutionary computation.

Furthermore, the authors indicate open research issues related to EAs, suggesting areas where further exploration is needed. The paper serves as a complement to previous work on swarm intelligence algorithms, focusing specifically on the practical applications of EAs in real-world settings.

In conclusion, the review provides a comprehensive summary of evolutionary algorithms and their relevance in engineering, while also identifying opportunities for future research in this area.

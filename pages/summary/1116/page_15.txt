This paper presents a comprehensive review of various evolutionary algorithms and their applications in optimization problems. It highlights advancements in differential evolution algorithms, such as self-adaptive differential evolution, multi-population strategies, and hybrid approaches that combine local search mechanisms. The effectiveness of these algorithms is demonstrated through their application in diverse fields, including numerical optimization, machine learning, and economic load dispatch. Key concepts include the importance of adaptive control parameters, the use of composite trial vector generation strategies, and the integration of memory-based techniques to enhance performance. The survey also discusses the evolution of covariance matrix adaptation strategies and their role in improving optimization efficiency. Overall, the paper underscores the continuous development and refinement of evolutionary algorithms to tackle complex optimization challenges effectively.

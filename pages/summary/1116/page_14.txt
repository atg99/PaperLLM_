The paper discusses the inherent stochastic nature of evolutionary algorithms (EAs), emphasizing the variability in results with each execution. It highlights the importance of repeatability in EA outcomes, particularly for industrial applications. The authors predict that the development of new evolutionary algorithms will continue to be a significant area of research.

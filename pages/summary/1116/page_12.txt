The paper discusses the use of the Covariance Matrix Adaptation Evolution Strategy (CMA-ES) algorithm for solving various optimization problems, particularly in the context of subsurface inverse profiling and cloud computing task scheduling. The first study demonstrates that CMA-ES outperforms other optimization techniques like Evolutionary Programming (EP) and Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) in imaging inhomogeneous buried dielectric targets. Emadi et al. highlight the effectiveness of CMA-ES in reducing task execution times in cloud computing compared to traditional scheduling algorithms.

Subsequent studies showcase the versatility of evolutionary programming (EP) in real-world applications. Yan et al. present a bi-subgroup self-adaptive EP algorithm for optimizing hybrid electric vehicles, achieving significant fuel savings. Gao introduces an evolutionary neural network based on an immunized EP algorithm for underground engineering, demonstrating high accuracy in back analysis. Jiang et al. apply EP to optimize traffic signal control, effectively re-adjusting signal frequencies based on predicted traffic flow. Additionally, a novel approach to navigating 3-D terrain using EP for the traveling salesman problem is proposed, streamlining scene tours.

The paper concludes with a discussion on selecting appropriate evolutionary algorithms for specific problems, emphasizing that familiarity with a method, problem definition, and parameter sensitivity are crucial for success. Despite a rich body of literature on evolutionary algorithms, there remains a notable gap between theoretical advancements and practical applications, particularly concerning issues like constraint handling, robustness, and multi-objective optimization.

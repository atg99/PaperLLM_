The results section highlights a significant advancement in the performance of GPT-4 compared to its predecessor, GPT-3.5, particularly on the USMLE exam questions. GPT-4 achieves over a 30 percentage point increase in performance across both exams, indicating a substantial improvement. The findings also demonstrate that GPT-4 outperforms ChatGPT, a variant of GPT-3.5 tailored for chat interactions, based on recent independent performance metrics.

While the performance of Med-PaLM and Flan-PaLM 540B is not available for comparison, previous results from the PaLM family of models are discussed in Section 4. According to the USMLE guidelines, a passing score requires approximately 60 percent correct answers. GPT-4 surpasses this threshold comfortably, unlike GPT-3.5, which was close to the passing mark.

Tables presented in the results detail the performance of GPT-4 and GPT-3.5 across various steps of the USMLE Self Assessment and Sample Exam. For the Self Assessment, GPT-4 scores an average of 86.65, compared to GPT-3.5's 53.61. In the Sample Exam, GPT-4 again excels with an overall average of 86.70, significantly outperforming both GPT-3.5 and independently reported ChatGPT scores, further underscoring the capabilities of GPT-4 in medical knowledge assessments.

The paper discusses the performance of the GPT-4 model, specifically its capabilities in handling questions that reference visual media in the USMLE Self Assessment and Sample Exam. Despite the model not being able to process visual elements such as graphs and photographs, it achieved notable accuracy rates on questions that included these media references. The Self Assessment featured 314 out of 2173 questions (14.4%) that referenced media, while the Sample Exam had 49 out of 376 questions (13.0%).

In a performance analysis presented in Table 3, GPT-4's accuracy was higher on text-only questions compared to those referencing media. For instance, in the Self Assessment, GPT-4 achieved 89.51% accuracy on text questions and 69.75% on media questions. Similarly, in the Sample Exam, accuracy on text questions was 87.77% and 79.59% for media questions. The model demonstrated strong performance, achieving 70-80% accuracy on media-related questions, indicating its ability to utilize logical reasoning and test-taking strategies to derive reasonable answers despite the lack of visual input. This finding highlights the model's adaptability and reasoning skills in language-centric challenges.

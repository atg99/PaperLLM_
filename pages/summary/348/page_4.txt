The paper discusses a prompt template designed for generating multiple choice questions in the medical field, with a focus on enhancing model performance in answering these questions. The template includes placeholders for specific question elements, such as examples and context, which are replaced with relevant information during prompt instantiation. 

An example is provided where a medical scenario is presented, followed by a question and multiple answer choices. The correct answer is highlighted, demonstrating the effectiveness of the prompting structure. The authors emphasize that their approach allows for efficient benchmarking, minimizing the use of context tokens while ensuring accurate generation of responses. They also utilize the logit_bias parameter in OpenAI's API to guide the model towards valid answer options, further optimizing the response generation process.

Overall, the paper outlines a systematic method for creating and evaluating multiple choice questions in medical knowledge, leveraging advanced AI techniques for improved accuracy and efficiency.

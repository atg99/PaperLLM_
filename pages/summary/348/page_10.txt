The results presented in Table 5 illustrate the performance of various models on the multiple-choice components of the MultiMedQA benchmark. GPT-4 consistently outperforms both GPT-3.5 and Flan-PaLM 540B across most datasets, with the exception of the PubMedQA dataset. Notably, both GPT-4 and GPT-3.5 were evaluated using zero-shot direct prompts.

The MedQA dataset, which includes questions from mainland China and Taiwan in multiple languages, shows that GPT-4 achieved scores of 78.63 and 75.31 in different prompting scenarios. The English version of MedQA is further divided into a five-option and a four-option variant, with GPT-4 scoring 82.25 and 86.10, respectively. 

In the PubMedQA dataset, GPT-4 scored 77.40 when reasoning was required. For the MedMCQA dataset, GPT-4 demonstrated strong performance as well, with scores around 73.66. The MMLU benchmark revealed high accuracy in clinical knowledge and medical genetics, with scores of 88.68 and 97.00 for GPT-4.

The evaluation also highlights that GPT-4 maintains robust performance on challenging questions in non-English languages. In comparison, previous studies involving InstructGPT and Codex yielded lower scores on the same datasets, with Codex achieving a maximum score of 78.2 on PubMedQA when using an ensemble of prompts.

Overall, the findings underscore GPT-4's superior performance relative to other models in various medical and clinical knowledge assessments, confirming its effectiveness in handling complex medical queries across diverse datasets.

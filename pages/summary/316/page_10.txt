The paper discusses the broader impacts of a proposed method for understanding longer texts, which draws inspiration from the theory of working memory in humans. It highlights a positive impact, suggesting that this method could lead to the development of more efficient mechanisms and architectures that enable sub-models to collaborate on complex tasks requiring rich context. Applications of this approach could extend to areas such as legal document analysis and public opinion monitoring.

Conversely, the paper notes a negative impact related to privacy concerns. Enhanced understanding of longer texts may enable social platforms to gain deeper insights into users by analyzing their posts, potentially leading to more accurate and coherent representations of individuals, thereby posing a privacy threat. 

Overall, the proposed method holds promise for advancing text understanding while also raising important ethical considerations regarding user privacy.

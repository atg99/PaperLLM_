The paper discusses the challenges of adapting transformer models, particularly BERT, for processing long texts. It highlights existing solutions like Transformer-XL, Compressive Transformer, and Longformer, but notes their limitations in terms of GPU compatibility and efficacy. The authors introduce a new methodology called CogLTX, based on the premise that a few key sentences in a long text are often sufficient for many natural language processing tasks. 

CogLTX operates by breaking long texts into manageable blocks and identifying key blocks that contain essential information. This is achieved through a process called MemRecall, which retrieves these key blocks to be processed by a reasoner, in this case, BERT. The paper also emphasizes the importance of joint training of two BERT models to improve efficiency and effectiveness in handling longer texts.

Overall, CogLTX aims to enhance the performance of BERT on longer documents by focusing on key information retrieval, thus streamlining the processing pipeline without losing essential context.

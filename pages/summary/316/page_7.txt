The section on multi-hop question answering discusses the use of the HotpotQA dataset, which consists of 112,779 questions that require answers based on multiple paragraphs. Traditional methods often utilize a graph structure to connect key entities across these paragraphs. However, the CogLTX approach simplifies this by concatenating all paragraphs for input into BERT models, allowing for effective handling of long texts.

The results presented in Table 2 demonstrate that CogLTX outperforms most previous methods and all BERT variants on the HotpotQA leaderboard. While the leading model, HGN, relies on additional hyperlink data, CogLTX achieves competitive results without such dependencies. The SAE model, which shares similarities with CogLTX, performs slightly better in terms of Joint F1 scores but does not address the memory limitations associated with longer paragraphs.

Ablation studies reveal that multi-step reasoning improves performance but is not critical, suggesting that many questions in the dataset are sufficiently linked to their follow-up sentences. Additionally, the absence of rehearsal for fine scores significantly decreases the performance metrics for supporting facts, highlighting the importance of mutual relevance among top sentences.

Overall, the findings indicate that CogLTX effectively addresses the multi-hop QA challenge, yielding state-of-the-art results while simplifying the process of identifying supporting facts.

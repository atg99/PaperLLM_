In summary, LSTM architecture effectively addresses the limitations of traditional RNNs by utilizing a complex gating mechanism that manages both short-term and long-term memory. It consists of forget gates, input gates, and output gates, which work together to retain relevant information and discard unnecessary data, thereby enhancing the model's ability to learn from time series data. This capability makes LSTM a powerful tool for various applications, particularly in fields requiring the analysis of sequential data.

This paper provides a tutorial and survey on Boltzmann Machines (BM), Restricted Boltzmann Machines (RBM), and Deep Belief Networks (DBN). It begins with foundational concepts and progresses to the structure of BM and RBM. Key topics include Gibbs sampling in RBM for generating visible and hidden variables, and the training of RBM using contrastive divergence. The paper also discusses various states of visible and hidden units, followed by a detailed explanation of Conditional RBM (CRBM) and DBN, including the architecture of a DBN autoencoder where encoder and decoder layers typically have equal numbers of neurons, with the coder layer serving as a low-dimensional data representation.

In conclusion, the authors express gratitude to Prof. Mehdi Molkaraie for his course material and acknowledge contributions from Prof. Ali Ghodsi’s YouTube videos. The references cited include significant works on Boltzmann Machines and related algorithms, providing a comprehensive background for readers interested in the theoretical underpinnings and practical applications of these models.

CRBM modifies the traditional RBM by adding directed links from previous time steps' visible units to the current time step's visible and hidden units. This setup allows CRBM to capture dependencies over time, addressing the limitation of RBM that is primarily suited for static data.

The paper describes how the conditional distributions for both visible and hidden units can be represented using normal and Poisson distributions, depending on whether the units are continuous or discrete. The use of Gibbs sampling in training these models is emphasized, with specific equations provided for calculating expectations and updating weights and biases.

Furthermore, the CRBM maintains the updating rules for weights and biases similar to those in the standard RBM, but it introduces additional dynamics by considering the visible units from previous time steps. This results in modified equations that reflect the influence of past data on the current model's parameters.

Overall, the CRBM framework expands the capabilities of RBMs by effectively integrating temporal aspects, making it applicable for tasks involving sequential data.

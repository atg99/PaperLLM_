This paper discusses various aspects of wetland ecosystems, focusing on their distribution, ecological risks, and classification methods using remote sensing technologies. It highlights the potential distribution and loss of global wetlands, as well as the significance of ecological sensitivity analysis in specific wetland areas like Huixian Karst Wetland in China. The research emphasizes the impact of land use changes on soil properties and the identification of heavy metal contamination in wetland soils.

Several studies are cited to illustrate advancements in wetland classification techniques, including the use of object-based algorithms, deep learning methods, and satellite imagery. Notable algorithms such as Random Forest and SegNet are discussed for their effectiveness in classifying vegetation communities and assessing ecological risks. The paper also addresses the role of microbial communities and fish diversity in wetland health.

Overall, the findings underscore the importance of monitoring and managing wetland ecosystems to mitigate environmental risks and promote biodiversity. The references provided range from ecological assessments to technological innovations in remote sensing, offering a comprehensive view of the current state of wetland research.

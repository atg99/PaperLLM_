This paper discusses the effectiveness of SegNet-based classifications for various karst vegetation types using different EPOCH values and texture features. The results indicate that using EPOCH 15 yielded a 0.78 F1-score for karst grass, which is an improvement of 0.07 over EPOCH 10. The inclusion of texture features significantly enhanced classification accuracy across all models, with the best performance being a multi-class classification achieving over 0.81 F1-score for all vegetation types. Notably, karst water received the highest accuracy with an F1-score of 0.92, while karst broad-leaved forest followed with 0.86. 

The combination of EPOCH 10 and texture features also led to a notable improvement in F1-score for karst broad-leaved forest, increasing it by 10% compared to EPOCH 10 alone. The model with EPOCH 15 and texture features improved the kappa coefficient to 0.76, an increase of 6% from EPOCH 10. Overall, the integration of texture features resulted in an 8% improvement in F1-score across all karst vegetation classes compared to the model using only EPOCH 10. The findings underscore the importance of both EPOCH settings and texture features in enhancing classification performance in karst vegetation analysis.

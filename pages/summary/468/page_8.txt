This study investigates the impact of varying EPOCH values on the performance of single-class SegNet models for karst vegetation classification. The findings reveal that increasing the EPOCH to 15 enhances the kappa coefficient by over 7% across all models, with Model-g showing a notable improvement of 14%. The results indicate that EPOCH values significantly influence the accuracy of karst vegetation classifications.

Furthermore, the research compares the effectiveness of single-class versus multi-class SegNet models, particularly at different EPOCH settings. It was observed that the fusion of single-class models yields better representations of karst broad-leaved forest and karst grass in specific areas compared to the multi-class model. For instance, the karst broad-leaved forest was more accurately depicted near karst rivers and ponds, avoiding confusion with karst grass and aquatic flora.

Classification accuracies for each single-class SegNet model at EPOCH values of 5, 10, and 15 are detailed, highlighting the improvements in precision, recall, F1-score, and kappa for various karst vegetation types. Overall, the study confirms that higher EPOCH values enhance model performance in karst vegetation classification tasks.

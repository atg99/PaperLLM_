The paper by T. Xu, S. Zou, and Y. Liang presents a non-asymptotic analysis of two time-scale off-policy temporal difference learning using Markovian samples. The authors investigate the convergence properties and performance of the algorithm in the context of reinforcement learning. They provide theoretical guarantees that demonstrate the effectiveness of their approach in achieving optimal policies under specific conditions.

In a complementary work, Z. Yang, Y. Chen, M. Hong, and Z. Wang focus on the global convergence of the actor-critic method, particularly in the framework of linear quadratic regulators with ergodic cost. Their findings confirm that under certain assumptions, the actor-critic algorithm can reliably converge to the global optimum, which is significant for applications in control and decision-making tasks.

Both papers contribute to the understanding of reinforcement learning algorithms, providing insights into their theoretical foundations and practical implications.

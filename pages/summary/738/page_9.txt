The paper discusses the local convergence of an abstract actor-critic framework, specifically applied to Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) and RUDDER algorithms under practical assumptions. It introduces a parameter, β, which influences the required greediness for optimal actions in policy gradients, although its exact value remains unknown as it varies with task characteristics and optimal Q-values. The authors aim to further their work by ensuring convergence to an optimal policy and exploring the relaxation of assumptions related to loss functions and convergence rates.

The acknowledgment section credits various organizations and projects that supported the research, including the ELLIS Unit Linz, the LIT AI Lab, and multiple industry partners.

The references include foundational works on gradient systems, reinforcement learning, and neuro-dynamic programming, highlighting the paper's grounding in established research.

This appendix provides detailed expressions for the minimization problems associated with Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) and RUDDER, along with insights into the causality assumption relevant to these methods.

**A.1 Further Details on PPO**  
The loss function for PPO is defined as Lh(θn,ωn,zn) which incorporates expected values over trajectories sampled from the policy π(θn,zn). The gradient expressions include terms that account for both the advantage function and the probability ratio ρ(τ,θn,zn). Additional components h(θn,ωn,zn) and LTD g(θn,ωn,zn) involve gradients related to the policy and value functions, emphasizing the importance of both policies and value estimations in the optimization process. The update rules for the parameters θ and ω are also specified.

**A.2 Further Details on RUDDER**  
Similar to PPO, RUDDER's minimization problem is articulated through its loss function Lh(θn,ωn,zn), which reflects the expected squared difference between the reward-to-go and the estimated action-value function. The gradients in h(θn,ωn,zn) and the loss terms Lg(θn,ωn,zn) and f(θn,ωn,zn) are detailed, highlighting the integration of reward signals and value estimations. Update rules for the parameters are provided, maintaining consistency with the PPO framework.

This section elaborates on the causality assumption that underpins the formulation for the loss function in PPO.

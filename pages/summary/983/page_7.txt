This paper compiles a comprehensive list of references pertinent to advancements in natural language processing (NLP) and machine translation. It includes works from notable researchers and institutions, highlighting key contributions to various NLP tasks such as named entity recognition, dependency parsing, and the development of language resources. 

Key references include:

- The FLAIR framework, which simplifies the implementation of state-of-the-art NLP models.
- Contextual string embeddings for sequence labeling, showcasing advancements in embedding techniques.
- The findings from the WMT19 conference, which detail progress in machine translation.
- The introduction of the NoSta-D named entity annotation guidelines for German.
- The billion-word benchmark established for measuring progress in statistical language modeling.

Additionally, the references touch upon significant contributions to multilingual parsing, the development of treebank collections, and the evolution of named entity recognition tasks across various languages. The works cited reflect a rich tapestry of collaborative research aimed at improving NLP methodologies and tools.

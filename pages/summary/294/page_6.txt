This paper presents a comparison of top-1 error rates for various deep learning models across several image classification datasets: CIFAR10, CIFAR100, SVHN, and ImageNet. The results are summarized in Table 2, which includes models such as VGGNet, ResNet, DenseNet, and others, along with enhancements like Class Activation Mapping (CAM) and Adaptive Batch Normalization (ABN).

For CIFAR10 and SVHN, the introduction of ABN leads to improved accuracy. For example, ResNet and DenseNet with ABN reduce top-1 errors from 6.43% to 4.91% and from 4.51% to 4.17%, respectively. On CIFAR100, all ResNet models show a decrease in top-1 errors by more than 0.6% with the inclusion of ABN.

In ImageNet evaluations, the paper highlights that while the use of CAM generally leads to performance decline for some models, ResNet with CAM maintains performance levels close to the baseline. ABN is shown to outperform both conventional VGGNet and ResNet with CAM, achieving a top-1 error reduction from 22.19% to 21.37%. Furthermore, when ABN is applied in tandem with SENet, it reduces the error from 22.19% to 20.77%, indicating a significant enhancement in accuracy.

Overall, the findings suggest that the integration of ABN with various architectures leads to notable improvements in image classification accuracy across different datasets. The paper emphasizes the effectiveness of these modifications in enhancing model performance, particularly in complex datasets like ImageNet.

The paper introduces DeepCaps, a deep capsule network architecture designed to handle more complex datasets than MNIST. The authors argue that localized routing is more effective than fully connected routing for capturing higher-level information. They build upon the work of Sabour et al. by proposing a class-independent decoder that enhances regularization and reduces overfitting, especially in deeper networks. This decoder ensures consistency in the physical properties represented by instantiation parameters across classes, improving controllability in practical applications.

Key contributions of the paper include the introduction of a novel architecture for capsule networks, termed DeepCaps, which incorporates a 3D-convolution-based dynamic routing algorithm to streamline the learning process and reduce parameters. The class-independent decoder acts as a robust regularization mechanism and offers greater control over instantiation parameters. The authors demonstrate that DeepCaps significantly outperforms existing state-of-the-art capsule networks on benchmark datasets, achieving a notable accuracy improvement of 3% on CIFAR10 while reducing the number of parameters by 68%.

The paper is organized into sections discussing related works, the DeepCaps architecture and routing algorithm, the class-independent decoder, results, and conclusions. It addresses challenges in deep networks, such as vanishing and exploding gradients, and reviews various models that have attempted to mitigate these issues. The authors emphasize their focus on enhancing performance in deeper capsule networks, particularly in relation to complex datasets, distinguishing their work from previous approaches.

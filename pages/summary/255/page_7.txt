The paper discusses the implementation and performance of DeepCaps, a capsule network model developed using Keras and TensorFlow. The training utilized the Adam optimizer with a learning rate that halves every 20 epochs, and models were trained on GTX-1080 and V100 GPUs. DeepCaps was evaluated on several benchmark datasets: CIFAR10, SVHN, Fashion-MNIST, and MNIST, showing competitive performance compared to existing capsule network architectures.

Results indicate that DeepCaps outperformed other capsule network models in CIFAR10, SVHN, and Fashion-MNIST, achieving classification accuracies of 92.74%, 97.56%, and 94.73%, respectively, while maintaining similar performance to state-of-the-art models on MNIST. Notably, DeepCaps utilizes only 7.22 million parameters for CIFAR10 compared to CapsNet's 22.48 million, yet achieves a higher accuracy. Additionally, the inference time for DeepCaps is significantly lower than CapsNet.

The paper introduces a class-independent decoder that enhances regularization and allows for the joint learning of inter-class reconstructions. This decoder enables the identification of specific instantiation parameters responsible for variations such as rotation and thickness, applicable across different classes. This capability facilitates the generation of data with desired attributes, which was not possible with previous models. Overall, DeepCaps demonstrates efficient performance and innovative features that improve upon existing capsule networks.

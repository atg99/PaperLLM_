The paper discusses the integration of large language models (LLMs) with knowledge graphs (KGs) to enhance various tasks, including KG embedding, completion, construction, text generation, and question answering. It summarizes key methodologies and representative works in these areas, highlighting the use of LLMs as text encoders, joint encoders, and decoders.

In KG embedding, LLMs are utilized to encode textual descriptions of entities and relations, improving the representation of KGs by incorporating semantic information. Methods like Pretrain-KGE and KEPLER exemplify this approach, where LLMs generate initial embeddings that are refined through standard KG embedding models.

For KG completion, various techniques leverage joint encoding or masked language modeling to fill in missing information in KGs. The paper also details KG construction methods that employ LLMs for tasks such as named entity recognition and entity linking, aiming to automate the creation and enhancement of KGs.

In the context of KG-to-text generation, LLMs are leveraged to produce coherent text from structured KG data, while KG question answering methods focus on extracting and reasoning over knowledge for effective query responses.

Overall, the integration of LLMs into KGs addresses the limitations of traditional KGs, particularly their incompleteness and lack of textual context, thereby enhancing performance across multiple downstream applications.

The proposed system emphasizes a lightweight mobile application designed to continuously monitor patient status during the COVID-19 pandemic. It comprises two main modules: person identification, where each user creates an account using their identification number, and patient tracking, which utilizes GPS geolocation to monitor suspected and infected individuals.

The application features an interface that allows patients to input their symptoms to generate an initial COVID-19 score based on WHO guidelines. If this score reaches six or higher, the patient's status is classified as suspected, prompting them to undergo laboratory tests. The system manages patients through five stages: normal, suspected, highly suspected, confirmed, and recovered, with specific protocols for each stage to ensure safety.

To enhance tracking, public buildings and transportation systems incorporate unique QR codes that individuals must scan before entry. This mechanism retrieves their health status from the backend database, allowing healthy individuals to pass normally while directing suspected cases through designated pathways to mitigate infection spread. Close contacts of confirmed cases are also traced to prevent further transmission.

Overall, the mobile application serves as a critical tool for early-stage disease management, ensuring efficient monitoring and response to potential COVID-19 cases.

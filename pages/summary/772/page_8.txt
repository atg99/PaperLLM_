The paper discusses the significance of Clinical Decision Support Systems (CDSSs) in enhancing healthcare monitoring, especially in remote areas. CDSSs offer comprehensive patient medical histories, assist non-expert physicians with clinical guidelines, and provide patients with useful tools like medication reminders. Their integration into various diseases, including chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, Alzheimer’s, and diabetes, highlights their versatility. For effective CDSS implementation, components such as electronic health records (EHR), a knowledge base, and clinical guidelines must be combined, with machine learning continuously updating the knowledge base.

The proposed framework aims to establish a complete health-monitoring system to limit the spread of COVID-19 by focusing on early detection and isolation. Unlike existing monitoring systems that primarily track vital signs or address data issues, this framework provides an end-to-end solution for continuous monitoring of suspected cases and patient isolation. It is structured into three main components: the patient side, the cloud side, and the hospital side, facilitating a systematic flow of data for effective healthcare management.

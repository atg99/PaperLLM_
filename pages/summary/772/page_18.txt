The paper presents an end-to-end (E2E) framework for real-time monitoring and diagnosis of COVID-19 patients, addressing challenges faced by healthcare systems during the pandemic. The proposed framework integrates mobile monitoring with hospital electronic health records (EHR) to facilitate early detection and management of infected individuals, enabling home isolation and contact tracking to prevent virus spread.

A comparison of classification models indicates that the proposed ResNet-50 architecture achieved an accuracy of 97.78% with a sample size of 622, positioning it competitively against other models like COVID-Net and SqueezeNet. The choice of ResNet over alternatives like VGG or GoogleNet is justified by its superior performance in image processing tasks, as supported by existing literature.

The paper acknowledges several limitations, including potential inaccuracies in identifying asymptomatic COVID-19 cases, power consumption issues with wireless sensors, and the complexity added by fog nodes in the network. Future work will focus on integrating data collection and further investigating the framework's real-world performance as more data becomes available. The framework is designed to enhance patient monitoring through deep learning-assisted image processing, specifically targeting chest X-ray analyses to classify patients as infected or normal, thereby aiding healthcare providers in decision-making processes.

The paper presents an end-to-end deep learning framework aimed at the detection and monitoring of COVID-19. It addresses the limitations of current healthcare systems, which often lack comprehensive management solutions for the disease. The proposed framework integrates wireless body area networks, cloud computing, fog computing, and clinical decision support systems to create a holistic model for real-time patient monitoring.

The framework comprises three layers: the patient layer, where wearable sensors and a mobile app track patients; the cloud layer, which utilizes a fog network architecture to enhance data storage and transmission; and the hospital layer, which employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) to analyze X-ray images for COVID-19 detection. The model demonstrated impressive performance, achieving an accuracy of 97.95% and specificity of 98.85%, outperforming existing methods.

The authors argue that this integrated approach could significantly impact COVID-19 management, leading to reduced proliferation of the virus and decreased healthcare costs. The study emphasizes the importance of real-time monitoring in improving healthcare interventions and patient outcomes.

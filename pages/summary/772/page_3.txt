The paper discusses a novel end-to-end deep-learning-assisted communication framework aimed at managing COVID-19 disease through smart devices that connect patients with healthcare professionals. Key contributions include:

1. The introduction of a monitoring system to continuously track suspected and infected patients, limiting the spread of infection.
2. The integration of fog and cloud computing to address challenges such as power consumption and data transmission.
3. A focus on creating comprehensive patient data to aid clinicians in diagnosis, enhancing existing methodologies.
4. Development of a classification model that analyzes chest X-ray images to detect and classify COVID-19 cases effectively.

The structure of the paper is organized into sections covering related work, challenges of remote patient monitoring systems, the proposed framework, dataset descriptions, the classification model, results, limitations, and conclusions.

The related work highlights the background of COVID-19, detailing its classification as a novel coronavirus responsible for a global pandemic, and summarizes research efforts on factors influencing disease progression, such as age and comorbidities. Additionally, it discusses studies that employ data analysis and forecasting techniques to predict infection trends and outcomes. Overall, the paper presents a comprehensive approach to addressing COVID-19 through technological and analytical innovations.

The paper presents a comparative analysis of a fog computing approach versus a traditional network in healthcare systems, demonstrating that the fog-enabled system significantly enhances data transmission efficiency, reducing bandwidth and power consumption. Central to this system is a backend cloud database built on Red Hat Linux 9, Apache, PHP, and MySQL, designed to store and manage patient data effectively. This database supports continuous updates from various sources, allowing for real-time monitoring and notification of potential health risks.

On the hospital side, the focus is on developing a classification model for X-ray images to identify COVID-19 cases, creating a comprehensive cloud-integrated electronic health record (EHR) system, and establishing a Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) based on distributed EHRs and WHO guidelines. The dataset utilized for model training comprises 622 cases, which were augmented to address class imbalance, ultimately yielding 750 images for analysis. This framework aims to enhance diagnostic processes and patient care through improved data management and analysis capabilities.

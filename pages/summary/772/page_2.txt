The paper discusses the development and significance of an IoT-based wireless body area network (WBAN) for real-time patient health monitoring. WBAN consists of wearable sensors that collect vital signs such as ECG, EEG, body temperature, blood pressure, and more. These sensors facilitate various applications, including in-hospital monitoring, remote diagnosis, and emergency services. The authors highlight the potential of WBAN in managing COVID-19, especially given the rapid spread of the virus and the overwhelming demand for healthcare resources, particularly ICU beds in developing countries.

The paper notes the critical need for continuous monitoring of COVID-19 patients, as timely identification of clinical deterioration can significantly affect patient outcomes. It emphasizes that many high-risk patients require frequent monitoring of vital signs to aid in decision-making regarding ICU admissions, which is crucial for reducing mortality rates.

To address these challenges, the authors propose a mobile clinical-decision-support system (CDSS) designed to continuously monitor patients both inside and outside hospitals. This system aims to enhance the quality of patient care by automatically collecting data and establishing a communication link between patients and healthcare professionals. The proposed system utilizes a variety of sensors to monitor key health parameters, thus aiming to improve patient management during the ongoing pandemic.

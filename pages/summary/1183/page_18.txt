Augmentation anchoring utilizes a set of augmentations for each unlabeled sample to produce pseudo-labels and ensure that these pseudo-labels remain stable across different augmentations. This method enhances the robustness of the model by aligning the predictions of augmented versions of the same sample.

In summary, several self-supervised learning (SSL) methods, such as EnAET and SimCLRv2, leverage various techniques including contrastive learning, pseudo-labeling, and entropy minimization to enhance model performance. Hybrid methods like Mixup, ICT, MixMatch, and ReMixMatch integrate these concepts, utilizing data augmentation strategies and consistency regularization to create more effective training frameworks. These approaches aim to improve the use of unlabeled data in training, ultimately leading to better generalization and performance in semi-supervised learning tasks.

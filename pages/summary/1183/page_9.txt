The model incorporates both observed and hidden variables, facilitating a nuanced understanding of how different aspects of the data relate to one another.

In summary, the paper discusses various VAE-based deep generative semi-supervised methods, emphasizing their structure and functionality. It introduces SSV AEs, which consist of generative and inference models, highlighting their ability to leverage latent variables for classification tasks. The paper also details the Auxiliary Deep Generative Models (ADGM), which enhance the expressiveness of the variational distribution through auxiliary variables. Additionally, it presents the Infinite VAE and Disentangled VAE, which utilize non-parametric Bayesian approaches and structured graphical models, respectively, to improve the adaptability and interpretability of latent representations. Each model employs variational principles to approximate posterior distributions, aiming to achieve a close alignment with true posterior probabilities.

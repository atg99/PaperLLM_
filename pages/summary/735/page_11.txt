This section of the paper discusses advancements in reinforcement learning, specifically focusing on TD-regularized proximal policy optimization (PPO) and GAE-regularization. PPO simplifies the optimization challenge posed by TRPO by eliminating the KL constraint and utilizing clipped importance weights along with a pessimistic bound on the advantage function. The update mechanism is characterized by a clipping range defined by ε, allowing for conservative policy updates that prevent significant deviations from the previous policy.

The proposed modification to TD-regularization integrates the advantages of importance clipping and the pessimistic bound, enhancing the policy update process. The gradients for the objective functions Jppo and Gppo are derived to facilitate the optimization. 

Furthermore, the paper introduces GAE-regularization, which connects the generalized advantage estimator (GAE) with the TD(λ) method. This approach is particularly advantageous for algorithms already employing GAE, as it introduces no additional computational overhead while maintaining relevance to various reinforcement learning techniques. Overall, these innovations aim to improve the stability and efficiency of policy optimization in reinforcement learning frameworks.

The paper presents results from using various reinforcement learning algorithms on pendulum swing-up tasks, specifically focusing on the Generalized Advantage Estimation with Regularization (GAE-REG) and Retrace methods. The findings highlight that GAE-REG combined with Retrace yields the best performance across both single and double pendulum tasks.

In the single-pendulum task, GAE-REG consistently converges to the optimal policy, demonstrating its significance. Conversely, the TD-REG method shows some improvement but fails to converge within 500 iterations. The absence of regularization leads to poorer outcomes, as evidenced by the inferior performance of the NO-REG configurations that utilized Retrace alone, which suffers from overestimation bias despite having low TD error estimates.

For the double-pendulum task, the importance of Retrace increases, as it significantly enhances performance when combined with GAE-REG. The paper notes the specific regularization coefficients used for each task and how they decay over time. Overall, the results emphasize that effective regularization techniques are crucial for improving learning efficiency and performance in reinforcement learning tasks.

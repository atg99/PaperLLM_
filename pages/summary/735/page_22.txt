The paper presents a comparative analysis of Trust Region Policy Optimization (TRPO) methods applied to various OpenAI Gym tasks using MuJoCo physics. It evaluates the performance of TRPO with Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE) and TD-regularization across multiple environments, including Ant-v2, HalfCheetah-v2, Walker-v2, Swimmer-v2, Hopper-v2, Reacher-v2, Humanoid-v2, and HumanoidStandup-v2. The results, averaged over five runs, highlight the expected return across iterations, with confidence intervals illustrated.

The study emphasizes the significance of tuning the regularization coefficient η for the success of TD-regularized algorithms, revealing that empirical evaluations suggest potential improvements in this area. Furthermore, the paper proposes further investigation into the convergence properties of actor-critic methods with TD-regularization, particularly under conditions where convergence guarantees can be established, such as in tabular and compatible function approximation scenarios.

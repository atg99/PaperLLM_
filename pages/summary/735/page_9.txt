The paper discusses enhancements to actor-critic methods in reinforcement learning, particularly focusing on TD(λ) error regularization for advantage learning techniques like Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE). It introduces a TD-regularized stochastic policy gradient (SPG) and deterministic policy gradient (DPG) that slightly increase computational demands but are manageable due to the ease of gradient computation through automatic differentiation.

For the TD-regularized SPG, the gradient is derived using the chain rule and log-likelihood ratio trick, resulting in two additional computations compared to standard SPG. The TD-regularized DPG, which learns a deterministic policy, modifies the state distribution to reflect the behavior policy, leading to similar expressions for gradients. The paper emphasizes that while DPG can offer lower variance advantages over SPG, careful selection of the behavior policy is crucial for effective learning.

Empirical comparisons of these methods are provided in a later section, affirming the practical applicability of the proposed regularization techniques. Overall, the proposed methods aim to improve the performance of actor-critic algorithms with manageable computational overhead.

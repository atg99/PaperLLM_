The paper discusses the application of TD- and GAE-regularization techniques to TRPO, comparing their performance against state-of-the-art methods such as Retrace and double-critic learning. The results highlight that SPG TD-REG outperforms other methods in a linear quadratic regulator (LQR) task with non-uniform noise, successfully learning the optimal policy without being influenced by noise. The paper explains that the TD error for REINFORCE is not applicable as it lacks a critic. 

Retrace modifies the advantage function estimator using n-step advantage estimators and importance sampling to stabilize learning from off-policy data. The importance sampling ratio is truncated to prevent variance issues. Double-critic learning aims to reduce overestimation bias by using two critics, with only one being updated at each iteration, allowing the TD targets to be computed from the estimates of the other critic.

The experimental results encompass 12 algorithms, incorporating variations like vanilla TRPO, TD-REG, GAE-REG, Retrace, and DOUBLE, with outcomes averaged over 50 trials. The actor and critic are implemented as linear functions with random Fourier features, using a minimal number of samples collected across multiple iterations. The study emphasizes the effectiveness of the proposed regularization techniques in improving learning stability and performance in reinforcement learning tasks.

In the paper "TD-regularized actor-critic methods," the authors address the instability issues commonly encountered in actor-critic reinforcement learning methods. These methods have demonstrated remarkable success in complex tasks but are often difficult to train due to the interaction between the actor and critic components. Specifically, inaccuracies in one component can negatively impact the other, leading to destabilization during the learning process.

To mitigate this problem, the authors propose a novel approach where the learning objective of the actor is regularized by penalizing the temporal difference (TD) error of the critic. This TD-regularized actor-critic method enhances stability by preventing significant updates to the actor when the critic's predictions are unreliable. The authors present this method as a straightforward plug-and-play solution that can be easily integrated into existing actor-critic frameworks to improve both stability and performance.

Empirical evaluations on standard benchmarks support the effectiveness of this approach. The source code for the proposed method is available online, facilitating further research and application in the field of reinforcement learning.

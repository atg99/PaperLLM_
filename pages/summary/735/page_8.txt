The paper presents a TD-regularized actor-critic method aimed at reducing bias in TD error estimates. The authors propose an objective function that incorporates a penalty term related to the TD error, allowing the actor to optimize its policy while minimizing the squared TD error. The final algorithm updates the actor's parameters by alternating between optimizing the expected returns and adjusting for the TD error, utilizing a variable penalty parameter η that can change with iterations.

The key components of the objective function are defined as J(θ), which represents the expected returns, and G(θ), which captures the TD error. The method emphasizes the importance of properly tuning the penalty parameter to balance maximizing returns and minimizing bias in the critic's estimates. The authors suggest that, unlike typical optimization problems, η can be decreased as the iterations progress, provided that the overall TD error decreases.

Furthermore, the TD-regularization method is adaptable and can be integrated into various existing actor-critic frameworks, such as DPG, TRPO, and PPO, enhancing their performance by stabilizing learning and mitigating issues associated with large TD errors.

The paper presents a study on the implementation of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) with TD-regularization in various OpenAI Gym tasks utilizing MuJoCo physics. It evaluates the effectiveness of PPO with different configurations, specifically comparing GAE-REG and TD-REG across different environments such as Ant-v2, HalfCheetah-v2, Walker-v2, Swimmer-v2, Hopper-v2, and Reacher-v2. The results, averaged over five runs, indicate that the proposed TD-regularization enhances the stability of policy updates, leading to quicker improvements and reduced divergence, complementing existing successful methods like Retrace.

The authors suggest that their method opens up new research avenues, particularly in exploring Bellman-constrained optimization and extending regularization to residual methods, which offer stronger convergence guarantees. Future research will also focus on alternative formulations of the constrained problem and techniques to solve it, such as incorporating slack variables or different penalty functions. This work contributes to the ongoing development of more robust reinforcement learning algorithms.

This section discusses the implementation of TD-regularized Trust-Region Policy Optimization (TRPO) using a V-function critic. The V-function is defined as the expected Q-function under a policy π, satisfying a specific Bellman equation. The TD error for the critic is derived from the expected reward and the estimated V-function for the next state. Unlike previous examples where the critic estimates the Q-function, the update process for the V-function critic simplifies the calculations.

The optimization problem for TD-regularized TRPO aims to maximize a specific objective function while adhering to a constraint on the Kullback–Leibler divergence between the new and old policies. The advantage function, essential for policy updates, is estimated using the V-function critic, and the TD error is incorporated to stabilize the learning process.

The gradients for the objective and regularization terms are derived, revealing that the TD-regularized TRPO operates similarly to standard TRPO but with an adjusted advantage function that incorporates the TD error. This adjustment simplifies the implementation, allowing for the use of natural gradient ascent, a method that updates parameters based on the Fisher information matrix and the gradient of the objective function. Overall, the TD-regularized approach enhances the stability of actor-critic methods that utilize a V-function critic.

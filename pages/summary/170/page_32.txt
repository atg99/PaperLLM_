The paper discusses various advancements in image generation and manipulation using Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs). It highlights notable works from 2016 to 2019, including methods for high-resolution anime generation, realistic image blending, image completion, face aging, and text-to-image synthesis. Key contributions include the use of progressive structure-conditional GANs for full-body anime generation, the development of models like StackGAN and AttnGAN for fine-grained text-to-image generation, and the application of GANs in steganography and saliency prediction. The references encompass a wide range of techniques, such as combining convolutional neural networks with Markov random fields, using dilated convolutions for multi-scale context aggregation, and employing conditional adversarial autoencoders for age progression. The paper emphasizes the growing versatility of GANs in addressing complex problems in image synthesis and manipulation, showcasing their impact on the field of computer vision.

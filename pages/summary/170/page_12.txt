The integration of these models allows for enhanced control over the editing process, enabling users to create high-quality realistic images tailored to their specifications.

The paper discusses several advanced GAN frameworks designed for various applications in image generation and manipulation. StackGAN is highlighted for its two-stage architecture that generates low-resolution images from text descriptions and then enhances their resolution. Other frameworks like AC-GAN and TAC-GAN utilize class labels and text embeddings to improve text-to-image generation.

SIS-GAN employs an encoder-decoder structure for text-to-image manipulation, combining feature representations of input images with text descriptions to produce synthesized images. In the realm of human pose synthesis, PG2 utilizes pose information to generate realistic images through a two-stage process, while Deformable GAN focuses on generating images conditioned on appearance and pose using innovative loss functions.

Steganography applications are also explored, with various GANs like S-GAN and Stegano-GAN designed to embed secret information within images. Stegano-GAN includes an encoder, decoder, and critic network to manage the embedding and recovery of data.

Interactive manipulation is addressed through IGAN, which allows users to sketch inputs for realistic image outputs, and TAGAN, which modifies specific visual attributes based on text descriptions. The Dissection GAN framework enhances user interaction by enabling detailed editing and manipulation of images without requiring artistic skills.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the versatility and potential of GANs in creating and manipulating high-quality images across different domains, highlighting their significance in modern computer vision applications.

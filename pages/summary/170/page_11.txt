The paper discusses various types of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and their applications. It highlights the Progressive Structure-Conditional GAN (PS-CGAN), which generates high-resolution full-body anime characters with specific pose sequences. The paper also covers image blending techniques, particularly the Gaussian Poisson GAN (GP-GAN), which integrates CGAN with traditional blending methods to produce high-resolution blended images while preserving important details. The Geometrically and Color Consistent GAN (GCC-GAN) further improves this by seamlessly combining images from different sources through a structured framework involving transformation, refinement, discrimination, and segmentation networks.

In the domain of image in-painting, the Exemplar GAN (Ex-GAN) leverages reference images to enhance quality, while the Contextual attention model improves efficiency by matching background patches. PGGAN enhances results by merging global and local information in its discriminator network. The paper also addresses face aging with models like Age-CGAN, which predicts future and past appearances, and the Identity-Preserved CGAN (IPC-CGAN), which maintains identity while simulating aging.

Finally, the paper discusses text-to-image synthesis, emphasizing the Attentional GAN (AttnGAN), a multi-stage model that generates high-quality images from textual descriptions through an attentional mechanism that focuses on relevant sub-regions. Overall, the paper outlines significant advancements in GAN technologies across various applications in computer vision.

This paper references a series of studies focused on the application of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) across various domains, particularly in object detection, 3D modeling, medical imaging, and image synthesis. Key highlights include:

1. **Small Object Detection**: Li et al. (2017) introduced perceptual GANs for detecting small objects, while Bai et al. (2018) proposed a multi-task GAN for similar purposes.

2. **3D Modeling**: Wu et al. (2016) and Gadelha et al. (2016) explored 3D shape generation from 2D views. Conditional GANs were utilized by Ongün and Temizel (2018) for paired 3D model generation.

3. **Medical Imaging**: Xue et al. (2018) applied GANs for medical image segmentation, and Choi et al. (2017) generated multi-label patient records using GANs.

4. **Image Enhancement**: Ledig et al. (2017) and Wang et al. (2018) contributed to single image super-resolution using GANs, with the latter enhancing the approach through ESRGAN.

5. **Texture Synthesis**: Various studies, including those by Gatys et al. (2015) and Xian et al. (2017), developed methods for texture synthesis using GANs, enabling controlled image generation based on textures or sketches.

Overall, the referenced works demonstrate the versatility and effectiveness of GANs in generating and transforming images across different applications, showcasing significant advancements in the field.

The cited works explore various applications and advancements of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) across different fields. 

Wiese et al. investigate the use of GANs for generating financial time series data, highlighting their potential in financial modeling. Zhou et al. focus on stock market prediction using high-frequency data with GANs, contributing to the understanding of market dynamics. Jetchev and Bergmann present the Conditional Analogy GAN, which allows for the swapping of fashion items in images, showcasing GANs in image manipulation.

Kato et al. apply GANs in clothing design, suggesting that a pattern maker can facilitate the design process. Kumar et al. introduce eCommerceGAN, focusing on enhancing e-commerce experiences through GANs. Xie et al. develop tempoGAN, aimed at generating temporally coherent volumetric fluid flow data, while Werhahn et al. propose a multi-pass GAN for super-resolution in fluid dynamics.

Larsen et al. present a method that combines autoencoders with GANs to go beyond pixel-level generation, termed VAE-GAN. Lastly, Kim et al. explore the use of transferred GANs for zero-day malware detection, indicating the versatility of GANs in cybersecurity applications. 

These studies collectively demonstrate the broad applicability of GANs in finance, fashion, e-commerce, fluid dynamics, and cybersecurity, highlighting their innovative potential in generating and analyzing complex data.

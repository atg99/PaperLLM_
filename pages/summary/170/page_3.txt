Generative models (GM) represent a significant area of advancement in computer vision, focusing on unsupervised learning. They aim to generate new data samples that resemble training data from an unknown distribution. The primary objective of GMs is to ensure that the generated data closely matches the training data, thereby addressing core challenges such as realistic sample generation, handling missing data, and facilitating latent representation inference. GMs also tackle the density estimation problem in unsupervised learning and enable new data generation for training without human intervention, making them crucial in modern AI.

The paper highlights various applications of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) across different domains, including image generation, handwritten font creation, anime character generation, image blending, in-painting, face aging, text synthesis, human pose synthesis, and medical applications. A comprehensive table summarizes these applications, listing specific models used for each task, such as DenseNet-CycleGAN for handwritten fonts and PG-GAN for face aging. Additionally, it covers applications in image manipulation, object detection, 3D image synthesis, facial makeup transfer, image super-resolution, texture synthesis, sketch synthesis, and audio and video generation. This extensive range of applications underscores the versatility and importance of generative models in contemporary AI research and development.

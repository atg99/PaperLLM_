The paper presents a comprehensive list of references related to advancements in generative models, particularly focusing on the use of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) across various applications. Key themes include unsupervised learning, low-shot learning for face recognition, and the synthesis of multi-view images. Notable contributions include "Deforming Autoencoders" for disentangling shape and appearance, and "Identity Preserving GANs" for cross-domain person re-identification. The references also explore innovative approaches like dual variational generation, adversarial ranking for language generation, and the use of GANs in enhancing speech recognition and generating poetry from images. Additionally, advancements in video generation techniques, such as decomposing motion and content, are highlighted, showcasing the versatility and impact of GANs in machine learning and computer vision.

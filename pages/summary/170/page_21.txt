This paper introduces a new surrogate objective function for updating parameters in Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), specifically through Equations 24 and 25. The updates for the generator and discriminator parameters are outlined, with a focus on the gradient concerning the generator parameters as expressed in Equation 26. This gradient captures both the GAN's response and the discriminator's reaction to changes in the generator, addressing the issue of mode collapse. The Unrolled GAN (UGAN) approach helps mitigate this problem by allowing the discriminator to increase the loss for the generator if it begins to collapse to a single mode.

Additionally, the paper discusses Mini-batch Discrimination (MBD), a technique aimed at further preventing mode collapse during GAN training. Instead of processing each data example independently, MBD allows the discriminator to analyze multiple data examples in mini-batches. The MBD process involves generating a matrix from the feature vectors of input data, calculating L1 distances between the rows of this matrix, and applying a negative exponential function to these distances. This culminates in a summation of distances that is concatenated with the feature vectors, enhancing the discriminator's ability to distinguish between modes and thus improving the stability of the GAN training process.

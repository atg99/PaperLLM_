This survey paper provides a comprehensive overview of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and their various modifications aimed at addressing training challenges. It highlights techniques such as smoothing, sampling GANs, proper optimization, normalization methods, noise addition, label usage, alternative loss functions, gradient penalties, and cycle-consistency loss as strategies to stabilize GAN training. 

The structure of the survey includes an introduction to GANs and classical variants, followed by a comparative analysis of GAN variants presented in Table 1. Section 3 discusses the application of GAN expansions in different artificial intelligence domains, summarized in Table 3. Section 4 addresses issues related to GAN training stability and potential solutions. The paper concludes in Section 5 with new research directions.

The survey also differentiates itself from other literature by focusing on classical models that have significantly influenced the development of GANs, contrasting with other surveys that cover more general modeling aspects. It serves as a valuable resource for practitioners, developers, and researchers by providing insights into key concepts, core techniques, and trends in GAN modeling, along with a detailed examination of training problems and solutions. The survey aims to enhance understanding of GAN variants and their practical applications, as well as to identify future research opportunities.

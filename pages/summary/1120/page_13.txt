The paper presents SparseEA, a novel multi-objective evolutionary algorithm (MOEA) designed to address large-scale sparse multi-objective problems (MOPs). Despite the prevalence of sparse Pareto optimal solutions in real-world applications, previous research has inadequately explored this area, primarily applying existing MOEAs without tailored strategies. SparseEA introduces a unique population initialization and genetic operators specifically aimed at generating sparse solutions, demonstrating superior performance compared to traditional MOEAs such as NSGA-II and SPEA2, as evidenced by lower error rates on test sets.

The empirical results show that networks optimized by SparseEA exhibit reduced overfitting compared to those derived from stochastic gradient descent (SGD) and other MOEAs. For instance, the neural network with the lowest test error rate within the SparseEA framework is notably sparse, containing only 13 weights. 

The paper concludes by highlighting the need for further research into SparseEA's application, suggesting potential enhancements through the integration of customized search strategies, alternative environmental selection methods, and strategies to account for decision variable interactions. Overall, SparseEA is positioned as a more effective solution for sparse MOPs, paving the way for future exploration in this area.

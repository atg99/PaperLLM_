This paper addresses the challenges faced by existing evolutionary algorithms in solving large-scale sparse multi-objective optimization problems (MOPs), where the optimal solutions exhibit sparsity, meaning that many decision variables are zero. Such problems arise in various applications, including feature selection, neural network structure optimization, and sparse regression. The authors propose a new evolutionary algorithm specifically designed to tackle these sparse MOPs by incorporating a novel population initialization strategy and genetic operators that emphasize the sparsity of solutions.

The research includes the development of a test suite to evaluate the performance of the proposed algorithm against seven existing algorithms. Experimental results demonstrate that the new algorithm outperforms these competitors in solving large-scale sparse MOPs, highlighting its effectiveness in generating sparse Pareto optimal solutions.

The study underscores the significance of tailored approaches in evolutionary computation for complex optimization scenarios, particularly in real-world applications requiring efficient feature selection and model complexity control.

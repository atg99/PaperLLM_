This paper addresses the challenges of solving large-scale sparse multi-objective optimization problems (MOPs) using multi-objective evolutionary algorithms (MOEAs). It highlights that traditional MOEAs struggle with sparse MOPs, particularly when decision variables are numerous, as they often fail to consider the sparse nature of the Pareto optimal solutions. The paper presents a new MOEA designed to effectively handle these sparse MOPs by incorporating a novel population initialization strategy and genetic operators that promote sparsity in the solutions.

Key contributions of the paper include: 

1. Introduction of an MOEA tailored for large-scale sparse MOPs, which effectively approximates sparse Pareto optimal solutions.
2. Development of a multi-objective test suite comprising eight benchmark problems to evaluate the proposed algorithm's performance, allowing for adjustable sparsity of solutions.
3. Experimental validation demonstrating that the proposed algorithm significantly outperforms seven existing MOEAs in solving large-scale sparse MOPs.

The paper is structured to first provide an overview of sparse MOPs and existing MOEAs, followed by detailed descriptions of the proposed algorithm and test suite, and concludes with experimental results and discussions. The findings underscore the necessity for specialized algorithms that account for the unique characteristics of sparse MOPs, particularly in computationally intensive fields such as machine learning and data mining.

The primary focus was on the effectiveness of SparseEA in scenarios where the Pareto optimal solutions exhibit varying levels of sparsity.

Figures presented in the paper demonstrate the performance of SparseEA compared to other MOEAs across multiple benchmark problems (SMOP1 to SMOP8) with 100 decision variables. The results indicate that SparseEA consistently produces sparser solutions than its competitors, as evidenced by the ratio of nonzero decision variables shown in Fig. 7.

Further analysis was conducted on SMOP5 with 500 decision variables, where the sparsity of Pareto optimal solutions was varied. The findings reveal that while NSGA-II, CMOPSO, and MOEA/D-DRA struggled with increased sparsity, SparseEA excelled, particularly when sparsity was below 0.7. This suggests that SparseEA is particularly effective for sparse MOPs but may not perform as well in scenarios lacking sparse solutions.

In addition to benchmark testing, SparseEA was also applied to practical applications including feature selection, pattern mining, critical node detection, and neural network training. The experimental settings were tailored to address combinatorial MOPs, highlighting the versatility and practical implications of SparseEA in real-world problems.

Overall, SparseEA demonstrates superior performance for sparse MOPs compared to traditional MOEAs, making it a valuable tool for optimization tasks where sparsity is a critical factor.

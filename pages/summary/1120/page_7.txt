The paper presents a novel approach, SparseEA, designed for feature selection in multi-objective optimization problems (MOPs), particularly focusing on sparse MOPs. The proposed method includes new genetic operators tailored for binary variables while adopting traditional operators for real variables. The genetic operators are structured to ensure the offspring maintain sparsity, flipping elements in binary vectors based on the scores of decision variables. 

Empirical assessments demonstrate that SparseEA outperforms existing multi-objective evolutionary algorithms (MOEAs) by generating offspring that can dominate both parents, confirming the effectiveness of the new genetic operators. The authors note that many existing multi-objective test suites lack sufficient sparse MOPs or present problems that are either too simple or too complex without adjustable sparsity. 

To address this, the paper introduces a new test suite containing eight challenging sparse MOPs with adjustable sparsity, enabling more effective performance evaluation of MOEAs. The formulation for these MOPs includes multiple objective functions and a decision vector divided into segments, allowing for a diverse range of optimization scenarios.

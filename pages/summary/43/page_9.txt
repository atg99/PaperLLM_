The proposed AE-CNN algorithm demonstrates superior performance in classification error compared to various state-of-the-art architectures, both manually designed and those developed through semi-automatic and completely automatic methods. Specifically, AE-CNN achieves a classification error of approximately 4.3% on CIFAR10 and 20.85% on CIFAR100, outperforming DenseNet, ResNet, and other competitors.

In terms of parameters, AE-CNN has a higher count than some models like DenseNet and ResNet but remains significantly lower than others such as ResNet at depth 1,202 and VGG. The computational complexity of the proposed algorithm, measured in GPU Days, indicates it operates efficiently, utilizing three Nvidia GeForce GTX 1080 Ti cards over nine days for the CIFAR10 dataset, resulting in a total of 27 GPU Days.

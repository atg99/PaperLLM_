The referenced papers and works in this document focus on advancements in question answering, visual question answering, and the embedding of knowledge bases. Key contributions include the development of subgraph embeddings for question answering, structured embeddings for knowledge bases, and the exploration of few-shot learning in language models. Notable works such as BERT and RoBERTa highlight improvements in language understanding through deep learning techniques. The dataset innovations, like WebQA and CLEVR, aim to enhance visual reasoning capabilities. Furthermore, the integration of visual understanding in question answering systems is emphasized, showcasing the importance of multimodal learning. Overall, this collection illustrates the ongoing efforts to bridge language and vision, enhancing the capabilities of AI in understanding and generating responses based on complex inputs.

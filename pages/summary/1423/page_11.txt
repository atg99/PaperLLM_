The paper introduces the A-OKVQA dataset, a new benchmark designed to evaluate the reasoning capabilities of vision and language models in visual question answering (VQA). The dataset focuses on questions that require commonsense and world knowledge, and each question is accompanied by rationales to guide models in understanding the reasoning process leading to the answers. 

An analysis of various state-of-the-art models, including VilBERT, LXMERT, KRISP, ClipCap, GR-GPT, and GPV-2, reveals that while these models exhibit strong performance, they still struggle with reasoning and knowledge required for certain questions, highlighting significant room for improvement. The pairwise comparison of model performance shows a lack of overlap in the questions answered correctly, indicating that different models have distinct strengths and weaknesses depending on the type of knowledge needed.

The findings suggest that to effectively tackle the A-OKVQA challenges and advance towards general multi-modal intelligence, it is essential to integrate diverse capabilities from multiple methods. The paper emphasizes the need for future research to enhance the reasoning abilities of visual and language models.

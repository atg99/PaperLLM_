The paper discusses the evaluation of various models in the context of image captioning and question answering, particularly focusing on the integration of image features and language models. The results are presented in two main tables. 

Table 3 highlights the performance of different models, including ClipCap, which utilizes CLIP image features fine-tuned through GPT-2 to generate answers instead of captions. The findings reveal biases in the dataset and demonstrate that the direct answer setting poses a significant challenge. Notably, the performance of GPT-3 is comparable to that of the fine-tuned CLIP models, emphasizing the necessity of training for effective repurposing of CLIP text encodings in language tasks. 

Table 4 explores the impact of generating rationales on GPT-3's performance. The authors fine-tune ClipCap to produce rationales and observe that using ground-truth rationales significantly enhances GPT-3's performance compared to using generated captions. While generated rationales yield some improvement over generated captions, the authors note that further advancements in rationale generation are needed.

Additionally, the paper evaluates specialized models trained on knowledge-based visual question answering (VQA), including Pythia, VilBERT, LXMERT, KRISP, and GPV-2, establishing a baseline for comparison. The results indicate that while some models excel in traditional VQA tasks, the integration of rationales into the question-answering process presents opportunities for enhanced performance.

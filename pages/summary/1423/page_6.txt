The paper presents an analysis of the A-OKVQA dataset, focusing on question and rationale diversity compared to other datasets such as FVQA, VCR, and VQAv2. Table 2 highlights mean pairwise cosine distances, indicating that A-OKVQA exhibits the highest diversity in questions and rationales. The dataset's questions primarily address commonsense and visual knowledge, often overlapping in category. 

To assess question diversity, the authors compute average pairwise cosine distances using a sentence transformer. The results show that A-OKVQA outperforms VCR, which has many similar questions, in terms of diversity. Additionally, the rationales in A-OKVQA are found to be more varied compared to those in VCR and VQAv2, attributed to the dataset's foundation in the visually diverse COCO dataset.

The paper also compares the similarity of questions between A-OKVQA and OK-VQA, revealing a significant difference in their question sets based on minimum pairwise distances. 

In the experiments section, A-OKVQA is benchmarked against various models, categorized into large-scale pre-trained models, rationale-generating models, and specialized knowledge-based VQA models. Evaluation metrics include accuracy in multiple-choice settings and standard VQA evaluation for direct answer settings. The analysis of large-scale pre-trained models across different input configurations is also discussed, providing insights into their performance with the A-OKVQA dataset.

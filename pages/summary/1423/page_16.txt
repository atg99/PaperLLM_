The paper discusses the training methodologies for different types of models used in the context of question collection. 

In the section on discriminative models, all models are trained for 500 epochs with a learning rate of 0.01 and a batch size of 128, except for the model using ResNet input features, which is trained with a lower learning rate of 0.001.

For contrastive models, the CLIP zero-shot setting does not require any training. In the trained setting, a linear layer is trained for 500 epochs with a learning rate of 0.01 and the same batch size of 128. The paper describes the use of a "CLIP-style contrastive loss," where CLIP representations of questions and images are passed through a linear layer to create a 512-dimensional embedding. A cosine similarity matrix is produced between these embeddings and their corresponding answers, followed by the application of softmax to calculate matching probabilities, which are used to compute a cross-entropy loss to enhance embedding-answer similarity.

In the generative models section, the modified ClipCap model is illustrated. It utilizes CLIP image representations that are fed into a mapping network to generate prefix tokens for GPT-2. Questions and ground-truth answers are tokenized, with an end-of-sequence string included. In the multiple-choice setting, the question string is prefixed with "Choices: ...".

Overall, the paper outlines the specific configurations and methodologies employed in training discriminative, contrastive, and generative models, emphasizing the integration of CLIP representations and the mechanisms for optimizing model performance.

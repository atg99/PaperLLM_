The paper introduces the Augmented OK-VQA (A-OKVQA) dataset, which expands upon previous Visual Question Answering (VQA) datasets by requiring diverse sources of knowledge and reasoning. A-OKVQA consists of approximately 25,000 questions paired with both multiple-choice answer options and free-form answers, allowing for both multiple-choice and direct answer evaluations. Each question is accompanied by a rationale that provides the reasoning or knowledge necessary to arrive at the correct answer, highlighting the need for commonsense, world knowledge, and visual understanding in answering the questions.

The authors argue that the inclusion of rationales enhances model training by promoting reasoning and knowledge retrieval, ultimately leading to more explainable VQA systems. The dataset aims to address the limitations of previous VQA datasets that primarily focused on recognition tasks and lacked complexity in reasoning requirements.

Key contributions of the paper include the establishment of a new benchmark that necessitates diverse knowledge sources, a comprehensive analysis of the dataset's challenges and diversity, an evaluation of current baseline approaches against A-OKVQA's difficult questions, and insights into model performance across various answer distributions. The authors plan to release the dataset publicly to foster further research in this area. 

In the related work section, the paper discusses existing VQA datasets, noting that many do not require significant reasoning or outside knowledge. It also references earlier knowledge-based VQA datasets, which, while requiring knowledge, were limited to closed knowledge forms.

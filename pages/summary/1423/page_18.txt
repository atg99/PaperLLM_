The paper discusses a model architecture that combines CLIP and GPT-2 for visual question answering tasks. It outlines the use of contrastive loss to align embeddings of questions and images with text encodings of answers. The model is fine-tuned on training data to optimize performance, as indicated by BLEU and METEOR scores. Additionally, it includes a mapping network to enhance the model's capabilities. The paper emphasizes the significance of maintaining consistent training hyperparameters across different models and provides examples of generated outputs to illustrate the model's rationale generation.

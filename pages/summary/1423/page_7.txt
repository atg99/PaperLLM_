The paper presents an evaluation of various large-scale pre-trained models for answering questions using different input modalities: multiple-choice (MC), direct answer (DA), and combinations of questions with images. The models assessed include BERT, CLIP, ResNet, GPT-3, and ClipCap, each applied in different contexts.

Key findings include:
- Random sampling methods yield lower performance compared to more sophisticated approaches, with the best results from models leveraging CLIP and GPT-3.
- Among the discriminative models, CLIP (classifier) showed significant results, particularly when combined with image input.
- Contrastive models that utilize cosine similarity for matching question and image representations to answers also performed well, especially CLIP in the contrastive setting.
- Generative models like GPT-3 and ClipCap demonstrated strong performance in generating answers directly, with ClipCap achieving the highest scores in the DA setting.

The paper emphasizes the importance of combining different input types and leveraging advanced models to improve performance in question answering tasks. The methodology includes training classifiers and evaluating using cosine similarity, with detailed descriptions provided in the appendix.

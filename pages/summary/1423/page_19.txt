This paper discusses various models and methodologies in the context of visual question answering (VQA), particularly focusing on the A-OKVQA dataset. It highlights several models, including Pythia, LXMERT, ViLBERT, KRISP, and GPV-2, detailing their architectures, training processes, and fine-tuning strategies on the A-OKVQA dataset.

Pythia is noted for its architectural modifications and enhanced learning schedule, utilizing a broader training dataset. LXMERT employs a Transformer-based approach, pre-trained on extensive image-sentence pairs, and is fine-tuned on A-OKVQA. ViLBERT extends BERT to handle vision and language, pre-trained on proxy tasks and evaluated on VQA tasks, with specific fine-tuning for A-OKVQA. KRISP integrates multi-modal Transformers with graph neural networks for knowledge-based VQA, while GPV-2 is a generative model combining T5 and VinVL features, pre-trained on diverse tasks and fine-tuned on A-OKVQA.

The paper also outlines the methodology for generating answers, including the use of beam search for answer evaluation and the integration of rationales during testing. The experiments aim to enhance understanding of model performance and the effectiveness of various training strategies in the VQA domain.

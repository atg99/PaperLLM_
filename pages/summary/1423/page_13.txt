This collection of references highlights significant contributions to the fields of vision-and-language tasks, visual question answering (VQA), and multimodal representation learning. The references also explore various benchmarks and datasets, such as OK-VQA and CommonsenseQA, aimed at enhancing the understanding of visual and textual contexts.

Notable methodologies discussed include the integration of implicit and symbolic knowledge in VQA (KRISP) and the importance of external knowledge in answering visual questions (OK-VQA). Additionally, several works emphasize the role of multimodal explanations in justifying decisions based on evidence. The evolution of models is underscored by advancements in transfer learning and the development of frameworks like MMF for vision and language research, which facilitate the exploration of multimodal tasks.

Overall, these references collectively reflect ongoing efforts to improve machine comprehension of visual and textual information, fostering more robust interactions between different modalities in artificial intelligence.

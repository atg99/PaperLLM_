2. Boytsov provides a comparative analysis of indexing methods for approximate dictionary searching, contributing to algorithmic efficiency in data retrieval.
3. Cahuantzi et al. introduce "slearn," a tool for machine learning applications, available on GitHub.
4. Cho et al. present a method for learning phrase representations utilizing RNN encoder-decoder models in statistical machine translation.
5. Elsworth and Guttel explore a symbolic approach to time series forecasting with LSTM networks, highlighting innovative techniques in modeling.
6. Greff et al. conduct a thorough investigation of LSTM architectures, analyzing their performance across various tasks.
7. Hochreiter and Schmidhuber introduce the LSTM model, a breakthrough in handling long-term dependencies in sequential data.
8. Irie et al. offer an empirical overview of LSTM, GRU, and attention mechanisms for language modeling in speech recognition.
9. Jozefowicz et al. explore different recurrent network architectures, providing insights into their effectiveness in machine learning.
10. Kaspar and Schuster propose a measure for the complexity of spatiotemporal patterns, relevant to various scientific fields.
11. Kim et al. present a neural sequence-to-grid module aimed at learning symbolic rules, advancing the intersection of symbolic reasoning and neural networks.
12. Kingma and Ba introduce the Adam optimization method, widely adopted for its efficiency in training deep learning models.
13. Kolmogorov discusses the theory of random numbers, contributing to the foundational understanding of stochastic processes.
14. Lample and Charton explore deep learning applications in symbolic mathematics, bridging computational methods with mathematical reasoning.
15. Lin et al. propose a symbolic representation for time series data, which has implications for the development of streaming algorithms.

These references collectively contribute to the fields of machine learning, time series analysis, and symbolic reasoning, reflecting ongoing advancements and empirical explorations in these areas.

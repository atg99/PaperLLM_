We observed that LSTMs demonstrated superior performance over GRUs for high-complexity strings, with median training times of 12.53 seconds for LSTMs compared to 22.84 seconds for GRUs. Adjustments to the learning rate for GRUs improved training stagnation, while LSTMs maintained a consistent learning rate. Our findings highlight the importance of learning rate and network depth in training efficiency, revealing that while GRUs excel in low-complexity scenarios, LSTMs are preferable for more complex tasks, aligning with prior research in language modeling.

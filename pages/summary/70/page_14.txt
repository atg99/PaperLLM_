The paper discusses the performance of the Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) model in predicting one-day flow values for two different scenarios during testing and validation phases. The results, as shown in Table 4, indicate no significant differences in the Nash-Sutcliffe Efficiency (NSE) and Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) values between the test and validation phases.

The LSTM model's capability to learn long-term dependencies enhances its accuracy in forecasting flood flows, achieving over 86% accuracy for two- and three-day predictions. The study underscores the importance of selecting appropriate input data characteristics to optimize forecasting performance, revealing that the correlation between input data and target data significantly influences the model's efficacy. Overall, the findings advocate for the use of the LSTM model in hydrological forecasting as a viable and efficient method for flood risk management.

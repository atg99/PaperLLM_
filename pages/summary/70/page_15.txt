The paper presents findings on flow forecasting using the Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) model, focusing on two-day and three-day predictions in flood scenarios. Scatter plots illustrate the testing phase results for both scenarios, revealing that the model achieves high accuracy with Nash-Sutcliffe Efficiency (NSE) values of approximately 94.5% for two-day and 86.2% for three-day forecasts. The research emphasizes the significant role of flowrate data in predicting flood flows, with correlation coefficients ranging from 77% to 97%, compared to less than 30% for rainfall data. Overall, the LSTM model is shown to provide stable and reliable results across validation and testing phases, underscoring its effectiveness in flood forecasting.

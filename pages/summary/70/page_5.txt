The paper presents information about various hydrological stations over a 24-year period, detailing their geographical coordinates and the correlation coefficients of the data collected. The stations listed include locations such as Muong Te, Lai Chau, and Hoa Binh, with correlation coefficients ranging from 0.18 to 1.00, indicating varying degrees of data reliability for rainfall and discharge measurements.

Additionally, the paper discusses Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs), which are computational models inspired by the human nervous system. ANNs utilize a data-driven approach to identify complex nonlinear relationships between inputs and outputs without needing direct knowledge of the underlying physical processes. The relationship is expressed mathematically, where the output is a function of an n-dimensional input vector. A typical ANN consists of three layers: an input layer, one or more hidden layers, and an output layer. The independence of input and output data is emphasized, with connections between layers established through weight matrices, biases, and activation functions, as illustrated in the paper.

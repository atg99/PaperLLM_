This study evaluates the effectiveness of a Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) model for flood forecasting and examines how various characteristics of input data impact model performance. Key factors considered include the type and amount of input data, as well as the correlation among data series. The analysis utilizes daily measured data from the Vietnam National Center for Hydro-meteorological Forecasting, spanning from 1961 to 1984, prior to the construction of the Hoa Binh Dam. The LSTM model is designed to predict flood flow rates at the Hoa Binh Station with lead times of one, two, and three days.

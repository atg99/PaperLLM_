The paper presents results from the CMRC 2018 and DRCD benchmarks, comparing the performance of XLNet and BERT across various metrics. In the CMRC 2018 tests, XLNet showed competitive results, with the mid-size model achieving the highest F1 score of 88.4 in the development set. In contrast, BERT's best F1 score was 84.5. For the DRCD dataset, XLNet-mid again outperformed BERT, achieving an F1 score of 93.6 compared to BERT's 89.9.

Additionally, the authors conducted experiments on text classification tasks, such as XNLI, where XLNet-mid reached approximately 74% accuracy, falling short of BERT-base's 77.8%. The authors acknowledge that they have not yet identified the reasons for this discrepancy in performance and note the lack of successful Chinese XLNet implementations in the community. They intend to further investigate these issues and update their findings through their open-source implementation repository.

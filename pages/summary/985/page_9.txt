The paper presents MacBERT, a novel Chinese pre-trained language model designed to address challenges in masked language modeling (MLM) and its alignment with fine-tuning tasks. The authors conducted extensive experiments across various Chinese NLP datasets, demonstrating that MacBERT outperforms traditional models by mitigating discrepancies between pre-training and fine-tuning phases. Key findings indicate that using similar words for masking instead of the [MASK] token enhances performance, as it maintains the naturalness of language and transforms the task into a grammar correction approach. The results highlight that focusing on MLM tasks yields better outcomes compared to next sentence prediction (NSP) tasks. The paper concludes by emphasizing the importance of these findings for the Chinese NLP community and outlines future work on optimizing masking ratios.

In this section of the paper, the authors examine the functional similarities between pruned neural networks using two alternative pruning methods: SiPP and PFP. The analysis is conducted on various architectures, including ResNet20, ResNet56, ResNet110, VGG16, DenseNet22, and WRN16-8. The results are illustrated in several figures, where the networks were trained using a consistent iterative prune-retrain schedule.

The findings indicate that the conclusions regarding functional similarities remain consistent across different pruning methods. Specifically, the performance metrics, such as matching labels and Softmax 2-norm differences, are measured for pruned versions of ResNet56 under varying levels of pruning rates (PR). The results suggest that even with significant pruning, the pruned networks retain functional similarities to their unpruned counterparts, as evidenced by the presented graphs. This analysis underscores the robustness of the pruning methods and their minimal impact on the networks' functional performance.

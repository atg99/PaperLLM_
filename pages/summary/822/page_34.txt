The paper "Lost in Pruning: The Effects of Pruning Neural Networks beyond Test Accuracy" explores the implications of pruning neural networks, specifically examining the performance of various models like ResNet18 and ResNet101 on ImageNet and CIFAR10 datasets. It investigates how pruning affects accuracy not only on the training data but also on out-of-distribution datasets.

Key findings indicate that pruning has a nuanced impact on test accuracy, often leading to unexpected results. The study presents data showing the prune potential of the neural networks, highlighting differences between weight (WT) and fine-tuned (FT) models. The analysis includes prune-test curves that illustrate the relationship between prune ratios and changes in top-1 test accuracy across different conditions.

The authors emphasize that excessive pruning can lead to a degradation in model performance, suggesting that the effects of pruning should be carefully considered beyond just test accuracy metrics. Overall, the paper underscores the importance of understanding the broader implications of pruning strategies in neural network optimization.

The paper examines the implications of pruning neural networks, specifically focusing on the VGG16 architecture, and how different pruning techniques impact model performance beyond mere test accuracy. It presents a heatmap analysis of confidence levels on informative pixels derived from pruned VGG16 models evaluated on subsets of CIFAR-10 test images. The four pruning methods analyzed include Weight Thresholding (WT), Filter Thresholding (FT), SiPP, and Provable Filter Pruning (PFP). The heatmap illustrates the mean confidence of each model when tested against pixel subsets, indicating the varying effectiveness of each pruning strategy in retaining model reliability and predictive power. The findings suggest that pruning methods can significantly affect the model's ability to generalize and maintain confidence in predictions, highlighting the need for careful consideration of pruning techniques in neural network optimization.

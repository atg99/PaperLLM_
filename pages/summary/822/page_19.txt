The section discusses the comparison of informative features extracted from pruned neural networks, specifically ResNet20 and VGG16, using different pruning algorithms such as Weight Thresholding (WT), Filter Thresholding (FT), SiPP, and Provable Filter Pruning (PFP). The analysis is conducted on both nominal and out-of-distribution test data from CIFAR10 and CIFAR10-C datasets.

In the nominal test data experiment, results indicate that pruned networks retain functional similarities to their original unpruned versions, suggesting that pruning does not significantly compromise the informative features. Figures 12 and 13 illustrate the comparison for ResNet20 and VGG16, respectively.

When testing with out-of-distribution data, as shown in Figures 14 and 15, similar trends are observed, reinforcing the idea that pruned networks maintain a resemblance to their parent networks in terms of functionality. This consistency across different datasets highlights the effectiveness of pruning techniques in preserving the essential characteristics of neural networks.

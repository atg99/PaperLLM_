The paper discusses the YOLO (You Only Look Once) model, which utilizes a 7x7 grid cell approach for processing input images. Each bounding box generated by the model includes four parameters: the center coordinates (x, y), width (w), and height (h). Together with a confidence score, each bounding box is characterized by a total of five parameters. The paper references figures that illustrate the YOLO model and the parameters associated with a bounding box within a 3x3 grid cell.

The GWHD dataset comprises 3422 images, with only those containing objects (bounding boxes) included in CSV files. Images without objects or containing similar-looking objects, like weeds, are also valuable for training the model, as they help prevent confusion with similar items. Images with objects are designated as positive_images, while those without are classified as negative_images. For the YOLOv5 model to utilize negative images during training, corresponding label text files must be created. Since negative images lack objects, their label text files remain empty.

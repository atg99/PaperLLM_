The paper discusses the application of trained weights for identifying wheat heads in images. When a wheat head is detected, a bounding box is drawn around the object, along with a probability score indicating the likelihood of it being a wheat head. The GWHD dataset includes 10 outdoor images of wheat, which are distinct from the 3422 images used for training and lack ground truth bounding boxes. These images serve as a testing ground for the trained detection model. The process of using the trained weights for object detection mirrors the training procedure. By executing a specific command, the detect.py file is compiled, enabling the architecture used during training to be rebuilt. The model achieves a detection accuracy of 93.5%.

The paper discusses the process of calculating the center point of a bounding box for detecting wheat heads in images. The center coordinates (x_center, y_center) are determined using the minimum point (x_min, y_min) and the width and height of the bounding box. Specifically, the formulas provided calculate the center point by adding half the width to x_min and half the height to y_min.

Since the GWHD dataset does not label bounding boxes, the authors adapt the YOLO algorithm by assigning all bounding boxes a class label of '0', indicating that they are all related to wheat head detection. To prepare the data for YOLO, the coordinates of the bounding boxes are normalized to a range of 0 to 1 by dividing x_center and width by the image width, and y_center and height by the image height. This normalization process ensures consistency across different image sizes.

The final dataset structure includes unique image identifiers, class labels, and the normalized coordinates and dimensions of the bounding boxes, facilitating effective training for the object detection model.

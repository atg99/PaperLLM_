The document discusses the configuration and results of a training process for a model. Key parameters include the name of the result folder, which organizes all training results, and the caching option that speeds up image loading for training. The training progress over 100 epochs is highlighted, with an average training time of 10 seconds per epoch for 86 batches and 9 seconds for evaluation on 22 batches. The total execution time for the training of 3422 images was 35 minutes and 41 seconds, achieving a mean Average Precision (mAP) of 93.5% in the final epoch. It is noted that the weights from the last epoch do not necessarily correspond to the highest accuracy. To address this, an add-in called TensorBoard was utilized to provide a clear visualization of the training process.

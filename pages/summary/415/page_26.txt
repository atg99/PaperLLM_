The authors evaluated three backbone networks for the YOLOv4 model: CSPResNext53, CSPDarknet53, and EfficientNet-B3. Through theoretical analysis and extensive experimentation, they concluded that CSPDarknet53 was the most optimal feature extractor. Both CSPResNext50 and CSPDarknet53 are based on the DenseNet architecture, which addresses vanishing gradient issues by connecting previous inputs with current inputs before proceeding to dense layers. DenseNet is structured with dense blocks and transition layers that adjust the size of the output through convolution and pooling. Each dense block comprises multiple dense layers, where the output from each layer is concatenated with its input to form the input for the subsequent layer. This architecture enhances feature propagation and optimizes network performance.

The paper discusses the advancements of YOLOv5 compared to YOLOv4, highlighting its superior performance in certain scenarios within the computer vision community. It outlines the notable engineering differences in YOLOv5, which integrates innovations from YOLOv4 while maintaining a similar theoretical foundation. The architecture of YOLOv5 includes a Focus structure backbone, CSP network, SPP block neck, and a YOLOv3 head that utilizes GIoU loss.

A significant enhancement in YOLOv5 is the adaptive anchor box selection process. Traditionally, YOLO models used anchor boxes based on k-mean clustering from the COCO dataset, which may not be suitable for unique datasets with different characteristics. For instance, a dataset focusing on giraffes may require taller, thinner anchor boxes. To address this limitation, Glenn Jocher proposed an automatic anchor box selection in YOLOv5 that allows the model to learn the best-fitting anchor boxes for any given dataset during training, thus improving accuracy and reducing training time without manual configuration.

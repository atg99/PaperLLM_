The paper discusses the use of Google Colab as an Integrated Development Environment (IDE) for artificial intelligence research and learning. It highlights that Colab offers a Jupyter Notebook-like environment and provides free access to powerful hardware resources, specifically Graphic Processing Units (GPUs) and Tensor Processing Units (TPUs). The platform comes with pre-installed libraries commonly used in deep learning, such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, Keras, and OpenCV. The need for high processing power in machine learning and deep learning is emphasized, as typical computers lack the necessary GPU capabilities. Colab addresses this by offering cloud-based access to advanced GPUs, such as the Tesla V100, and TPUs, facilitating efficient research for AI developers.

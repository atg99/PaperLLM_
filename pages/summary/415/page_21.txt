The paper discusses the architecture of YOLOv3, which utilizes the Darknet-53 model as its feature extractor, consisting of 53 layers. An additional 53 layers are incorporated for the detection head, resulting in a total of 106 layers in the fully convolutional architecture. A key innovation in YOLOv3 is its multi-scale detection capability. Unlike earlier versions, where predictions were made at the final layers, YOLOv3 introduces prediction layers alongside the network. This allows the model to detect objects at three different scales by utilizing features from the last three residual blocks. Specifically, predictions are made at the 82nd, 94th, and 106th layers, corresponding to downsampling strides of 32, 16, and 8, respectively.

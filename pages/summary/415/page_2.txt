The paper discusses the evolution of the YOLO (You Only Look Once) algorithm, focusing on its advancements leading to YOLOv5, which represents a state-of-the-art approach in object detection. The author outlines the fundamental principles of the YOLO framework, emphasizing its real-time processing capabilities and high accuracy compared to traditional object detection methods.

The thesis examines the progression of the YOLO algorithm from its initial version to the latest iteration, highlighting significant improvements in performance metrics such as speed and precision. Key features of YOLOv5, such as its architecture, scalability, and ease of use, are analyzed, showcasing how it enhances the efficiency of object detection tasks.

Additionally, the paper addresses the practical applications of YOLOv5 in various fields, including surveillance, autonomous driving, and robotics, illustrating its versatility and impact on technology. The author concludes with a discussion on future directions for research and development in object detection, emphasizing the ongoing importance of innovation in this rapidly evolving domain.

The authors of the paper have explored various marginal optimization methods to enhance the performance and accuracy of the YOLOv4 architecture, coining the terms "Bag of Freebies" and "Bag of Specials" to describe these improvements. The "Bag of Freebies" refers to optimization techniques that improve model performance without incurring additional hardware costs, thereby providing enhanced performance at no extra expense.

Through extensive experimentation and detailed statistical evaluation, the authors identified specific methods for the Bag of Freebies. For the backbone of the architecture, they implemented CutMix and Mosaic data augmentations, Drop Block regularization, and class label smoothing. For the detector components, they applied Complete Intersection over Union (CIoU-loss), Cross-mini-Batch Norm (CmBN), DropBlock regularization, Mosaic data augmentation, Self-Adversarial Training (SAT), strategies to eliminate grid sensitivity, the use of multiple anchors for a single ground truth, a cosine annealing scheduler, optimal hyper-parameters, and random training shapes. These methods collectively contribute to enhancing the YOLOv4 model's performance and accuracy.

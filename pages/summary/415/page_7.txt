The paper discusses seven algorithms implemented using the PyTorch framework, focusing on YOLO v5. It highlights that YOLO v5, while not created by the original authors of the YOLO series, demonstrates remarkable performance improvements over its four predecessors. The authors reference Jocher's 2020 work to support their claims about YOLO v5's effectiveness. The paper emphasizes the enhancements and differences that contribute to this impressive performance, showcasing the advancements in object detection capabilities within the YOLO framework.

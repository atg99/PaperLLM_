The final predicted bounding box in the YOLO (You Only Look Once) model is represented as (bx, by, bw, bh, σ(to)), where the model's constrained location prediction simplifies the learning process and enhances network stability. YOLOv2 has achieved nearly a 5% improvement in mean Average Precision (mAP) through the incorporation of anchor boxes.

YOLOv3 builds upon YOLOv2 by utilizing a larger network architecture, specifically Darknet-53, which consists of 53 convolutional layers, and integrates Residual Networks (ResNet) to address the limitations of earlier versions. YOLOv2's custom deep architecture, while increasing the number of layers to 30, faced challenges with small object detections due to down-sampling of input images. ResNet's introduction of skip connections facilitates better activation propagation through deeper layers, mitigating issues related to gradient vanishing. YOLOv3's architecture incorporates a bottleneck structure within each residual block, enhancing feature extraction and overall accuracy.

The section discusses the "Bag of Specials," an advanced optimization method aimed at enhancing the performance accuracy of object detection while incurring minimal costs. The term "Specials" signifies obtaining valuable improvements at a reduced expense. 

For the backbone architecture, notable techniques include Mish activation, Cross-stage partial connections (CSP), and Multi-input weighted residual connections (MiWRC). In terms of the detector, which comprises the neck and head components, the methods employed are Mish activation, Spatial Pyramid Pooling block (SPP-block), Spatial Attention Module (SAM-block), Path Aggregation Network (PANet), and Distance Intersection over Union Non-Maximum Suppression (DIoU-NMS). These methods collectively contribute to the optimization goals outlined by Bochkovskiy et al. in 2020.

The authors present two models within the YOLO framework: fast-YOLO, which utilizes 9 convolutional neural network (CNN) layers in the Darknet architecture for simpler datasets, and normal-YOLO, which employs 24 CNN layers to handle more complex datasets, achieving higher accuracy. The architecture incorporates sequences of 1x1 and 3x3 convolutional layers, drawing inspiration from the GoogLeNet (Inception) model, which effectively reduces the feature space from previous layers. Notably, the final layer of the model employs a Linear activation function, contrasting with the use of Leaky Rectified Linear Unit (leaky ReLU) activation in the preceding layers.

The paper outlines the process of preparing a dataset for training and validating a neural network model, specifically in the context of image recognition. It details the steps taken to identify and label negative images, resulting in 3422 label text files corresponding to 3422 images. The section emphasizes the importance of having a dataset that contains the same type of objects as those the model was trained on for an unbiased evaluation of performance. It also highlights the challenges faced by AI researchers in collecting new datasets, particularly in scenarios where accessing specific environments, such as outdoor wheat fields, is not feasible.

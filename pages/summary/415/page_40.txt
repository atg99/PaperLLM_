The paper discusses the capabilities of Google Colab, which offers 25GB of RAM and 150GB of main disk space for deep learning tasks. It highlights the availability of the Tesla V100-SXM2 GPU for training deep learning models in the cloud. The YOLOv5 architecture, developed by Ultralytics and based on the PyTorch framework, is presented as a preliminary model that researchers can modify to optimize performance for specific problems. This customization can include adjustments such as adding or removing layers, integrating additional image processing methods, and altering optimization techniques or activation functions. The process of cloning and installing the YOLOv5 repository is also illustrated.

The paper discusses the training process of a YOLOv5 model using a dataset of 3422 images, which achieved approximately 93% accuracy after 100 epochs. The training time for each epoch was around 20 seconds, demonstrating the model's efficiency without any optimization methods. The results, visualized through TensorBoard, indicate that the original architecture of YOLOv5 is both fast and accurate. Additionally, the model produces two weight files: "last.pt," representing the final epoch's weights, and "best.pt," which contains the weights with the highest accuracy. Both files are lightweight, at only 14MB, making them suitable for integration into AI systems, such as web or mobile applications, while maintaining an accuracy of 93.5%.

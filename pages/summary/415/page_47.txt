The paper discusses the process of model evaluation after training, emphasizing the importance of using a dataset split for unbiased assessment. It describes a common division of 80% of the dataset for training and 20% for evaluation, ensuring that the evaluation is conducted on data not seen by the model during training. This separation leads to a fair review of the model's performance. The training data is referred to as the training set, while the evaluation data is called the validation set. Additionally, it highlights the organization of data, where label text files are aligned with their corresponding images to maintain consistency when creating training and validation set folders.

The section discusses the preparation of the Global Wheat Head Detection (GWHD) dataset for training a model using Google Colab. The dataset, which is available for public use without a license, is first downloaded to a personal computer and then uploaded to Google Drive. This integration allows for easy access to the data during the training process and facilitates the saving of results.

The dataset is unzipped into the YOLOv5 folder in Google Drive to streamline data access and manage storage effectively. The GWHD dataset includes 3422 outdoor wheat images for training, 10 outdoor wheat images for testing, and a training data file in CSV format. The CSV file details the bounding box data, with each line representing a bounding box for the wheat heads in the images. Notably, due to the dense nature of wheat growth, multiple bounding boxes may exist within a single image.

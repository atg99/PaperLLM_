The paper discusses the architecture of the YOLOv3 object detection model, focusing on the kernel size and feature map dimensions. It specifies that the kernel size used is 1×1×255, resulting in a feature map of 13×13×255 at the first detection layer. The process is reiterated for subsequent detection layers, with the feature maps at layers 79 and 91 being upsampled. After further downsampling, the feature maps achieve sizes of 26×26 and 52×52 at the 94th and 106th detection layers, respectively. The figures referenced provide visual support for these processes.

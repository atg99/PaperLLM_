The paper presents evaluation results for two models, GABONST-ELM and ESA-LEM, focusing on their performance metrics including accuracy, precision, recall, F-measure, and G-mean across various hidden neuron configurations. 

For the ESA-LEM model, the highest accuracy recorded was 96.25% with 875 hidden neurons, while the G-mean peaked at 73.41%. The results indicate a consistent performance with most configurations maintaining accuracy above 94%. 

In contrast, the GABONST-ELM model showed superior performance, achieving a maximum accuracy of 99.38% with 725 to 900 hidden neurons, and a G-mean reaching 95.06%. This suggests that GABONST-ELM outperforms ESA-LEM significantly in terms of both accuracy and G-mean, particularly at higher neuron counts. 

Overall, the findings highlight the effectiveness of GABONST-ELM in achieving higher predictive performance compared to ESA-LEM across the tested configurations.

The paper discusses the Genetic Algorithm Based on Natural Selection Theory (GABONST), which models biological theories of fitness and survival. It outlines two main processes: 

1. The mean fitness value represents the environment, while individual fitness values (g(s i)) reflect an organism's ability to survive in that environment.
2. Chromosomes are evaluated based on their fitness values compared to the mean. If a chromosome's fitness is less than or equal to the mean, it undergoes mutation for the next generation, simulating well-qualified organisms. Conversely, if the fitness is greater than the mean, the chromosome is given two opportunities for improvement, representing unqualified organisms striving for survival.

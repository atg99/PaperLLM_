The paper discusses the application and advancements of Genetic Algorithms (GA) in various fields including machine learning, adaptive control, combinatorial optimization, and signal processing. GA is recognized for its global search capability and is pivotal in modern intelligent computation. Specific applications highlighted include optimizing feature search in face recognition, task scheduling for phased array radar, image encryption security analysis, and developing prediction models in healthcare to manage uncertainties. Additionally, GA has been integrated with fuzzy logic for rule association and enhanced with methods like hybrid GAs and operational improvements.

A novel approach called Genetic Algorithm Based on Natural Selection Theory (GABONST) is introduced, inspired by Charles Darwin's natural selection theory. This algorithm aims to enhance the exploration and exploitation capabilities of traditional GAs by simulating the dynamic interaction between organisms and their environment. The GABONST procedure involves initializing the population, calculating fitness values, and determining the mean fitness to guide the evolution process, thereby improving solution diversity.

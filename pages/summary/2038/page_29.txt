The paper discusses the integration of the GABONST into the Extreme Learning Machine (ELM) framework for Language Identification (LID), replacing the EATLBO method. The authors plan to further explore alternative selection criteria for chromosome selection in crossover operations, moving away from random selection methods, and apply these findings to various applications. The study is supported by funding from the Malaysian government. The authors contributed to various aspects of the research, including conceptualization, methodology, writing, and supervision. The paper cites multiple references related to optimization algorithms and their applications in fields such as timetabling, neural networks, and vehicle routing problems.

The paper discusses the design and training of a Network of Neural Networks (NoNN) architecture that involves partitioning a teacher model's knowledge for training individual student models. The approach includes selecting reduced student architectures that meet specific memory and computational constraints suitable for IoT devices. For example, when using a Wide ResNet model as the teacher, the students are designed to have significantly fewer layers and parameters, ensuring they remain efficient. 

The paper describes the process of partitioning the teacher's knowledge into communities, which are then combined into nearly equal-sized groups for training. During this process, it emphasizes maintaining the teacher's validation accuracy while creating partitions. The NoNN architecture allows for shared initial layers among students, which can be replicated across devices, thereby optimizing resource use. Each student model operates independently after the common layers, facilitating efficient inference. This method highlights the balance between model complexity and performance in resource-constrained environments.

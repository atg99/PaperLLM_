The paper discusses a novel approach called NoNN, which addresses the challenges of model compression and communication in Internet of Things (IoT) environments with constrained memory. It emphasizes the need for distributed inference where individual models cannot fit on IoT devices, necessitating the splitting of models in non-intuitive ways that can lead to high communication costs. NoNN compresses models to reduce memory and computation costs while considering communication among student nodes.

The concept of Knowledge Distillation (KD) is introduced as a method to train a smaller student model to mimic a larger teacher network. The student is trained using the logits of the teacher rather than true labels, allowing for better information transfer. The paper highlights the use of both hard-label loss and soft-label loss to enhance the training process, as proposed by Hinton et al. The KD loss function combines these two loss types, with a temperature parameter that improves knowledge transfer.

Furthermore, the paper notes that prior techniques, such as MeDNN, which employs pruning to reduce per-node computation, can complement the proposed NoNN approach. The authors demonstrate that NoNN achieves higher accuracy compared to existing KD baselines and SplitNet, showcasing its effectiveness in model compression and performance.

The paper presents a comprehensive comparison of the proposed NoNN approach to existing methods in model compression and distributed inference, highlighting its communication-aware design. Table 1 illustrates that while previous techniques like quantization, pruning, separable convolutions, and knowledge distillation are effective for model compression, they lack awareness of communication costs in distributed settings. Existing distributed inference methods, such as SplitNet, MoDNN, and DeepThings, also fail to integrate model compression strategies, leading to inefficiencies.

NoNN stands out as a complete solution, effectively addressing communication costs while ensuring model compression for distributed inference on IoT devices. It achieves significant improvements, including up to 33 times reduction in total latency compared to prior models. The proposed method maintains high accuracy even with fewer devices, making it robust for practical applications.

The paper is structured to first review relevant literature, then discuss the implications of horizontally splitting deep networks on inference performance. It details the NoNN approach, presents experimental results, and concludes with a case study on hardware deployment and future work considerations. The findings underscore the necessity of an integrated approach that considers both model compression and communication efficiency in distributed inference scenarios.

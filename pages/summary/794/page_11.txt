The paper presents a novel architecture called Network of Neural Networks (NoNN), which consists of multiple disjoint student networks that communicate solely through the final fully connected (fc) layer. The architecture allows for the concatenation of outputs from all student networks to generate logits. 

To train the NoNN, the authors propose a combination of knowledge distillation (KD) loss and a new activation-transfer loss function. The activation-transfer loss aims to minimize the error between the activations of the teacher's filters and the corresponding student filters for each partition of knowledge. The total loss function is expressed as a weighted sum of the KD loss and the activation-transfer loss, which can be optimized using stochastic gradient descent.

The experimental setup involves compressing various deep networks across five image classification tasks, including CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100. The authors compare their approach to established baselines like KD and Attention-transfer with KD (ATKD), using models from the Wide Residual Network (WRN) family. The results indicate that NoNN effectively compresses the teacher model into disjoint subsets without inter-student communication until the final layer. 

Validation of the model's efficacy was conducted by training the teacher model on a majority of the dataset and using a small portion for validation to build a filter activation network for knowledge partitioning. The authors set the number of partitions to two and employed techniques to maintain the similarity of student architectures during training. The paper concludes by reporting that NoNN outperforms previous models, demonstrating its effectiveness in knowledge transfer and model compression.

This paper presents a detailed survey on Deep Residual Networks (DRN) specifically for image recognition, while also suggesting future research pathways. The organization of the paper is outlined as follows:

- Section 2 elaborates on deep residual networks.
- Section 3 defines image recognition.
- Section 4 highlights recent advancements in the application of deep residual learning for image recognition.
- Section 5 further explores the topic of image recognition.
- Section 6 discusses the advantages of DRN.
- Section 7 covers current research trends.
- Section 8 addresses traditional deep learning.
- Sections 9 and 10 detail the foundational components of residual networks and strategies for reducing depth and width.
- Section 11 presents evaluation metrics for DRN.
- Finally, Section 12 concludes with the authors’ reflections.

Figures included in the paper illustrate search trends and the structure of the sections. The paper aims to provide a comprehensive overview while identifying key areas for future exploration in the field of image recognition using DRN.

The paper discusses methods to enhance deep residual learning in neural networks. Key suggestions include increasing the number of hidden layers and neurons within each layer, which can improve the model's capacity to learn complex patterns. Additionally, augmenting the training dataset with more diverse images and videos is recommended to enhance the model's predictive capabilities for new data.

This paper presents a comparative analysis of various instance segmentation methods evaluated on the COCO 2017 test-dev dataset, highlighting key metrics such as Average Precision (AP), AP at different IoU thresholds (AP@0.5, AP@0.75), and performance across different backbone networks. The methods assessed include MNC, FCIS, Mask R-CNN, and MS R-CNN, with results indicating that MS R-CNN consistently outperforms other approaches, achieving significant improvements in AP metrics across various backbone architectures.

The study shows that MS R-CNN is not sensitive to the choice of backbone network and enhances performance by approximately 1.5 AP, particularly excelling in AP@0.75 with a two-point improvement over the baseline Mask R-CNN. Additionally, MS R-CNN demonstrates robustness across different frameworks, maintaining or slightly improving bounding box detection performance.

An ablation study is conducted to evaluate the design choices for the MaskIoU head, which combines predicted mask score maps and RoI features. Various fusion strategies are tested, including concatenation and multiplication of features, with results indicating that all configurations yield performance gains over Mask R-CNN. The best results come from concatenating the target score map with RoI features, which is adopted as the default method.

Overall, the findings underline the effectiveness of the proposed MS R-CNN method and its design choices, contributing to advancements in instance segmentation performance on the COCO dataset.

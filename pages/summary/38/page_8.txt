This paper presents the Mask Scoring R-CNN, which enhances the scoring of instance segmentation masks by introducing a MaskIoU head to the traditional Mask R-CNN framework. The authors evaluate different training sample thresholds and their impact on average precision (AP) metrics, finding that the variations in thresholds yield only minor differences in performance. They measure the quality of the predicted MaskIoU using correlation coefficients with ground truth, achieving a coefficient of approximately 0.74 for both ResNet-18 FPN and ResNet-101 DCN+FPN backbones, indicating robust predictions that are consistent across different network architectures.

The study also discusses the upper bound performance of their method, demonstrating that Mask Scoring R-CNN significantly outperforms traditional Mask R-CNN, with improvements of 2.2% and 2.6% AP for the two backbone networks, respectively. The computational cost of the MaskIoU head is relatively low, with only 0.39G FLOPs compared to 0.53G for the Mask head, and the processing speed remains efficient at approximately 0.132 seconds per image for ResNet-18 FPN and 0.202 seconds for ResNet-101 DCN+FPN.

In conclusion, the authors emphasize that their approach effectively aligns mask scores with MaskIoU, providing a simple yet powerful enhancement to instance segmentation networks. They advocate for the adoption of Mask Scoring R-CNN as a baseline for future research in this domain. The work received support from the HUST-Horizon Computer Vision Research Center and the NSFC.

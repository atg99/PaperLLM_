The paper discusses the architecture of Mask Scoring R-CNN, which enhances instance segmentation and detection tasks using a modified Mask R-CNN framework. The architecture consists of a backbone network that generates Regions of Interest (RoIs) through a Region Proposal Network (RPN) and extracts RoI features via RoIAlign. The standard R-CNN head and Mask head are included, with the MaskIoU head designed to predict the Intersection over Union (IoU) of the predicted masks. This head comprises four convolutional layers and three fully connected layers, with the final output corresponding to class-specific MaskIoU scores.

Validation results from the COCO 2017 dataset are presented, showing both detection and instance segmentation performance. The results indicate that the proposed MaskIoU head improves performance across various backbone networks, with detailed metrics provided for different configurations. Notably, the method demonstrates robustness across different architectures, with consistent improvements in average precision (AP) metrics for both instance segmentation (APm) and detection (APb). The findings underscore the effectiveness of the MaskIoU head in enhancing the performance of the Mask R-CNN framework.

The paper discusses the limitations of the Mask R-CNN framework in accurately correlating mask scores with mask quality, specifically focusing on the Mask Intersection over Union (MaskIoU) metric. It highlights that existing methods often struggle with unreliable mask scores, leading to poor prioritization of high-quality mask hypotheses. To address this, the authors propose a new approach called Mask Scoring R-CNN (MS R-CNN), which includes an additional MaskIoU head module designed to learn a mask score that aligns with the actual MaskIoU.

The motivation behind this work stems from the observation that high classification scores do not always correspond to high mask quality, as evidenced by experiments on the COCO 2017 validation dataset. The results show a poor correlation between classification scores and MaskIoU in traditional Mask R-CNN, prompting the need for a calibrated mask score to better reflect mask quality.

The proposed method aims to enhance the performance of instance segmentation by ensuring that the final mask scores accurately represent the quality of the segmentation output, thus improving the reliability of detection results in practical applications. The authors argue that their approach is a novel direction in instance segmentation performance enhancement.

The paper discusses the implementation and training of Mask Scoring R-CNN, which enhances the traditional Mask R-CNN framework by integrating a MaskIoU head to predict the Intersection over Union (IoU) between predicted masks and ground truth masks. Mask R-CNN operates in two stages: the Region Proposal Network (RPN), which generates candidate bounding boxes, and the R-CNN stage, which classifies these boxes and predicts masks using RoIAlign. 

The scoring of masks, denoted as s_mask, aims to reflect the pixel-level IoU between the predicted mask and the ground truth, requiring it to have positive values for the correct class and zero for others. The authors propose to decompose the learning of s_mask into two tasks: mask classification (s_cls) and IoU regression (s_iou). The s_cls is derived from existing classification tasks in the R-CNN stage, while s_iou is the focus of the paper, aiming to accurately predict the IoU.

The MaskIoU head, which processes features from RoIAlign and the predicted mask, consists of convolutional and fully connected layers to regress the IoU specifically for the ground truth class. During training, samples are selected based on a threshold IoU, and the predicted mask is binarized to generate a target for the IoU regression, using ℓ2 loss for optimization.

For inference, the MaskIoU head calibrates the classification scores post-RCNN stage by multiplying the predicted IoU with the initial classification scores to yield final mask confidence scores. The experiments conducted on the COCO dataset, which includes 80 object categories, utilize standard metrics for evaluation, and implementation details include the use of a ResNet-18 based FPN network for ablation studies and comparison with baseline methods. The input images are resized appropriately for training and testing.

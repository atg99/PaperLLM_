The paper presents a comparative analysis of the proposed NLP-Cap approach against several baseline methods for text classification and question answering tasks, specifically on the RCV1 and EUR-Lex datasets. The results indicate that NLP-Cap consistently outperforms the strongest baseline, XML-CNN, across various metrics such as Precision (PREC) and Normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain (NDCG), particularly when the amount of training data is limited. For instance, with 70% of training examples, NLP-Cap shows a significant improvement of about 5% over XML-CNN using 100% of examples on multiple metrics.

The paper further explores the efficacy of different routing mechanisms in the NLP-Cap approach, demonstrating that its custom routing outperforms established methods in terms of classification performance. In question answering tasks, the proposed capsule-based method effectively computes relevance scores between question and answer pairs using cosine similarity, showcasing robust performance on the TREC QA dataset.

Overall, the findings highlight the advantages of the NLP-Cap model in terms of generalization with fewer training samples and improved routing reliability, establishing it as a strong contender in the field of text classification and question answering tasks.

The paper presents a comprehensive review of various advancements in the field of odometry and mapping, particularly utilizing lidar and visual-inertial systems. Key contributions include:

1. **Lidar Odometry and Mapping**: Zhang and Singh introduced low-drift and real-time lidar odometry and mapping, emphasizing the importance of accurate localization in autonomous robotics. Shan and Englot further developed a lightweight and ground-optimized lidar odometry method, demonstrating effectiveness in variable terrain.

2. **Visual-Inertial State Estimation**: Weiss et al. explored real-time visual-inertial state estimation and self-calibration for micro aerial vehicles (MAVs) in unknown environments. Qin and Shen improved initialization processes for monocular visual-inertial estimation, while Qin et al. presented VINS-Mono, a robust monocular visual-inertial state estimator.

3. **Multi-Sensor Fusion**: Mourikis and Roumeliotis proposed a multi-state constraint Kalman filter for vision-aided inertial navigation. Shen et al. highlighted the fusion of multiple sensors for robust autonomous flight, which is crucial for both indoor and outdoor applications.

4. **Lidar-Inertial-Camera Integration**: The LIC-Fusion framework was introduced, allowing for effective lidar-inertial-camera odometry, with subsequent updates enhancing its capabilities through sliding-window plane-feature tracking.

5. **Optimization Frameworks**: Qin et al. developed a general optimization-based framework for global pose estimation with multiple sensors, showcasing the versatility and robustness of the proposed methods.

6. **Deep Learning Applications**: Wang et al. and Liang et al. focused on deep learning approaches for 3D object detection and segmentation, integrating semantic mapping into localization tasks.

7. **Robustness in Navigation**: The importance of robust estimation techniques and the integration of various sensors were emphasized, with methods like the factor graph approach for robot perception and the use of reinforcement learning for navigation tasks.

The paper consolidates these advancements, highlighting the ongoing evolution in sensor fusion techniques, algorithm robustness, and the integration of deep learning for enhanced performance in robotic navigation and mapping.

The paper presents Lvio-Fusion, an advanced tightly coupled SLAM framework that integrates stereo cameras, lidar, IMU, and GPS through factor graph optimization. This design allows for the seamless incorporation of additional sensors as new factors. To counteract accumulated drift and enhance global positioning accuracy, the authors implemented segmented pose graph optimization, incorporating GPS and loop closure constraints. A lightweight deep reinforcement learning algorithm is employed to dynamically adjust the weights of different factors, addressing the varying performance of sensors in different environments. Evaluations on the KITTI and KAIST urban datasets demonstrate that Lvio-Fusion achieves high accuracy, robustness, and real-time performance. However, the self-adaptive algorithm requires training on larger datasets for better generalization. Future work will explore multimodal deep learning to enhance the framework further.

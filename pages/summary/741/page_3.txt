The proposed system, Lvio-Fusion, integrates multiple sensors including stereo cameras, GPS, IMU, and Lidar for robust state estimation and mapping. The system architecture illustrates how data flows through various components like tracking, loop-closure detection, and optimization processes. 

Visual factors are defined through a residual equation that captures the relationship between observed features and their projected locations in the image space. The IMU pre-integration factors utilize raw IMU measurements to estimate relative poses between keyframes, incorporating biases and noise modeled as random walks. The initialization of the IMU is crucial, particularly in early stages, where specific steps are taken to estimate gravitational acceleration and optimize IMU biases.

Lidar factors contribute by extracting planar features from lidar scans, which are then aligned with the map points for improved localization and mapping accuracy. Overall, Lvio-Fusion emphasizes the importance of multi-sensor fusion and the mathematical formulations that underpin the integration of visual, inertial, and lidar data for enhanced performance in various environments.

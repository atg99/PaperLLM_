The paper discusses advancements in curvature calculation and pose graph optimization for lidar-based mapping and localization systems. It introduces a curvature formula that enhances the extraction of plane point clouds, enabling the system to extend the detected plane further than existing methods. The proposed curvature calculations utilize specific equations to improve segmentation of ground and planar features, employing Sample Consensus (SAC) to reduce outliers.

The paper highlights the challenges of accumulated errors in pose estimation, particularly during rotations of the mobile vehicle. To address this, a global pose graph optimization method is incorporated, which leverages GPS and loop closure constraints to correct long-term trajectory errors. The optimization process is divided into segments based on the vehicle's steering angle, with distinct strategies applied to turning and non-turning parts of the trajectory.

Additionally, the paper presents a self-adaptive weights adjustment algorithm that utilizes deep reinforcement learning to manage the complexities of multi-sensor fusion optimization. This approach allows for dynamic adjustment of weights in the optimization process based on the environment's state and the corresponding rewards defined by the Relative Pose Error (RPE). The framework aims to enhance real-time performance while maintaining accuracy in pose estimation.

2. Settle and Livescu (2016) explored discriminative acoustic word embeddings through recurrent neural network approaches.

6. Vincent et al. (2010) discussed stacked denoising autoencoders, emphasizing their utility in learning representations in deep networks.
7. Kamper et al. (2016) developed deep convolutional acoustic word embeddings with word-pair side information.
8. Various studies on unsupervised learning, such as those by Wang and Gupta (2015) and Doersch et al. (2015), focused on visual representation learning through videos and context prediction.
9. Several papers explored advanced techniques in speech representation, including Chung and Glass (2018) who proposed Speech2vec for learning word embeddings from speech.

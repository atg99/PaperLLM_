The paper discusses the evaluation and forecasting of green building costs using machine learning models. It emphasizes the importance of understanding feature significance in decision-making processes. A feature importance analysis was conducted using techniques such as XGBoost, DNN, and RF, resulting in a ranked list of features: people, technological, technical, and specific requirements.

To assess the predictive capability of the proposed models, the study employed four statistical measures: Root Mean Square Error (RMSE), Mean Absolute Error (MAE), Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE), and R-squared (R2). These metrics help determine the accuracy of the models, with R2 values closer to 1 indicating better accuracy, while RMSE, MAE, and MAPE values approaching 0 signify lower errors.

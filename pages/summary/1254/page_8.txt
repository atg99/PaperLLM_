This section of the paper discusses the application of three machine learning (ML) algorithms—Extreme Gradient Boosting (XGBoost), Deep Neural Networks (DNN), and Random Forest (RF)—to estimate green building costs. The study utilized a dataset divided into 70% for training and 30% for testing, employing 5-fold cross-validation to ensure the robustness of the predictive models. The chosen algorithms are noted for their scalability, accuracy, speed, versatility, and ability to manage overfitting through regularization.

XGBoost is highlighted as a cutting-edge technique known for its efficiency in data analysis, particularly in regression and classification tasks. This method enhances prediction accuracy by combining weak learners through an additive training process, which mitigates overfitting and optimizes mathematical performance. The architecture of XGBoost simplifies objective functions by integrating prediction and regularization terms, ensuring rapid processing. The equations presented outline the framework for prediction and the calculation of model effectiveness, focusing on loss functions and regularization terms to assess the model's quality accurately.

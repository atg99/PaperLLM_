The paper outlines a methodology for predicting the costs associated with green building projects, consisting of four main stages: data collection, formulation, model evaluation, and implementation. A flowchart illustrating this methodology is provided. 

In the data collection phase, comprehensive information regarding the initial costs of green building construction was sourced from multiple platforms, including academic journals and the green building council website. Specifically, the study focuses on cost data from 283 LEED-certified green buildings located across North America. This foundational data serves as the basis for further analysis in the subsequent stages of the methodology.

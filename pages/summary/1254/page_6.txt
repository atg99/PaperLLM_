The paper discusses key features influencing green building projects, emphasizing the roles of people, technical aspects, technology, and specific requirements. 

1. **People**: Their involvement is critical as they directly affect project delivery and context, with responsibilities spanning planning, design, delivery, and maintenance.

2. **Technical aspects**: These pertain to the methodological elements of green building, including process and procurement challenges, regulatory requirements, and the availability of green materials and expertise.

3. **Technology**: Refers to the tools and methods utilized during and after construction, encompassing equipment, materials, and industrial processes.

4. **Specific requirements**: Highlights the need for additional specialists, such as green building facilitators and certifiers, to ensure compliance with green standards.

The paper also outlines the importance of data preprocessing in machine learning applications for predicting green building performance. This includes variable selection, outlier cleansing, noise reduction, normalization, and standardization to enhance the quality and relevance of the data used.

The paper presents a detailed analysis of tournament outcomes among different models in a within-trajectory and multiple-trajectory context. In Figure 8A, the left panel illustrates raw tournament outcomes as a heatmap, where each pixel indicates the average win rate between a generator and a discriminator across various iterations, with brighter pixels signifying stronger generator performance. The right panel compares these tournament outcomes with the classifier score for the SVHN dataset, using the average column-wise pixel values as the win rate.

Figure 8B expands on this by showcasing outcomes from multiple trajectories involving six models, including generators and discriminators derived from varied seeds, hyperparameters, and architectures. The evaluation metrics used are the SVHN classifier score, SVHN Fréchet distance, and a skill rating method. Each point on the plots represents a score from a specific model iteration, with overall trajectories indicating model improvement over time. Notably, the Fréchet distance is plotted inversely, where lower distances (indicating better quality) are represented higher on the graph. The learning curves generated by the skill rating method align well with those from the Fréchet distance, while diverging from the classifier score in specific conditional models. The figure is based on prior work and is accompanied by additional text for further context on the experiments conducted.

The paper presents an analysis of image diversity using the Multi-Scale Structural Similarity Index (MS-SSIM) score. In panel A, it compares the MS-SSIM scores of samples generated by the AC-GAN model with training samples from the ImageNet dataset. Panel B illustrates the mean MS-SSIM scores for pairs of images within specific ImageNet classes against AC-GAN samples, with a red line indicating the maximum MS-SSIM score among the training data. Panel C tracks the intra-class MS-SSIM for five ImageNet classes over the course of training, where a decreasing trend signifies successful training and an increasing trend suggests generator collapse. Finally, panel D compares the Inception score with MS-SSIM across all 1000 ImageNet classes, revealing a low correlation (r2 = 0.16) and indicating that AC-GAN samples do not achieve variability at the cost of discriminability. The findings underscore the effectiveness of the AC-GAN model in generating diverse images while maintaining structural quality.

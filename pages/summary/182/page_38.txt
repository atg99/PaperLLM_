The paper discusses the use of interpolations in the latent space of generative models, specifically focusing on images generated from the BEGAN framework. The top section illustrates how real images at a resolution of 128x128 can be approximated and interpolated in the latent space, with the first and last columns showing the original images and the intermediate columns displaying the linear interpolations. The middle section presents latent space interpolations for three different ImageNet classes, showcasing the transitions between pairs of images from distinct classes. The bottom section highlights class-independent information, revealing global structures related to synthesized images, with each column representing a different bird class and each row corresponding to a fixed latent code. The findings emphasize the effectiveness of latent space manipulation in generating coherent image transitions and structures.

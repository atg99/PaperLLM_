This collection of references discusses advancements in deep generative models, particularly focusing on generative adversarial networks (GANs). Key contributions include the development of image generation techniques, validation of computer-generated designs, and improvements in signal fidelity measures. Notable works include the introduction of adversarially learned inference and unrolled GANs, which enhance the training of these models. The potential of GANs in applications like cartoon image generation from sketches and the semantic decomposition of latent spaces is also highlighted. The importance of perceptual similarity metrics in image generation is emphasized, showcasing the ongoing evolution of methodologies in the field of computer vision and image synthesis.

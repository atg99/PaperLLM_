The paper discusses various metrics for evaluating the quality of synthesized images, focusing on the Inception Score (IS), Mode Score, and AM Score. 

Figure 2 illustrates the sensitivity of the Inception Score to image resolution using zebra class images. It shows that resizing images affects the IS, with variations observed across different resolutions. The bottom left section presents IS scores for training data and synthesized images, with error bars indicating standard deviation across subsets. The bottom right section compares accuracy scores at different resolutions, revealing that a majority of classes performed below a set threshold, with the zebra class highlighted.

The paper introduces the Mode Score, which addresses the limitation of the Inception Score by incorporating the prior distribution of ground truth labels. This score reflects both the diversity and quality of generated images. Notably, it is established that the Inception and Mode scores are equivalent.

Additionally, the AM Score is introduced as a refinement of the Inception Score. It aims to account for uneven class distributions by modifying the entropy term in the score calculation. Overall, these metrics provide a comprehensive framework for assessing the quality and diversity of generated images in machine learning.

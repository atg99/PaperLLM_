The paper presents a comprehensive overview of common evaluation measures for Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), categorized into quantitative and qualitative metrics.

Quantitative measures include:

1. **Average Log-likelihood**: Evaluates the model's ability to explain held-out data using density estimation.
2. **Coverage Metric**: Assesses the probability mass of true data covered by the model distribution.
3. **Inception Score (IS)**: Measures the Kullback-Leibler divergence between conditional and marginal label distributions of generated data.
4. **Modified Inception Score (m-IS)**: Encourages diversity in images from specific categories.
5. **Mode Score (MS)**: Similar to IS but incorporates the prior distribution of real data labels.
6. **AM Score**: Evaluates KLD between training and predicted label distributions and includes prediction entropy.
7. **Fréchet Inception Distance (FID)**: Measures the Wasserstein-2 distance between multivariate Gaussians fitted to feature space data.
8. **Maximum Mean Discrepancy (MMD)**: Evaluates dissimilarity between two probability distributions using independent samples.
9. **Wasserstein Critic**: Trains a critic to distinguish between real and generated samples.
10. **Birthday Paradox Test**: Measures the support size of a distribution by counting duplicates.
11. **Classifier Two Sample Test (C2ST)**: Tests if two samples come from the same distribution.
12. **Classification Performance**: Evaluates unsupervised representations indirectly.
13. **Boundary Distortion**: Measures diversity and covariate shift in generated samples.
14. **Number of Statistically-Different Bins (NDB)**: Counts the number of samples in bins to assess distribution similarity.
15. **Image Retrieval Performance**: Analyzes the distribution of distances to nearest neighbors of query images.
16. **Generative Adversarial Metric (GAM)**: Compares GANs by having them compete against each other.
17. **Tournament Win Rate and Skill Rating**: Implements a competitive evaluation framework for discriminator and generator performance.
18. **Normalized Relative Discriminative Score (NRDS)**: Assesses the closeness of generated samples to real ones.
19. **Adversarial Accuracy and Divergence**: Measures classification accuracy and divergence between real and generated data.
20. **Geometry Score**: Compares geometrical properties of data manifolds.
21. **Reconstruction Error**: Evaluates the reconstruction accuracy of generated images.
22. **Image Quality Measures**: Uses metrics like SSIM and PSNR to assess image quality.
23. **Low-level Image Statistics**: Compares low-level statistics of generated images to natural scenes.
24. **Precision, Recall, and F1 Score**: Quantifies overfitting in GANs.

Qualitative measures include:

1. **Nearest Neighbors**: Detects overfitting by comparing generated samples to training set neighbors.
2. **Rapid Scene Categorization**: Tests the ability to distinguish real from generated images quickly.
3. **Preference Judgment**: Participants rank models based on image fidelity.
4. **Mode Drop and Collapse**: Analyzes generated data distances to known modes.
5. **Network Internals**: Explores internal model dynamics and visualizes learned features.

This summary encapsulates key evaluation metrics for assessing GAN performance, highlighting both quantitative and qualitative approaches.

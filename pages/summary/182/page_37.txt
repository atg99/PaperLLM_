The paper discusses various methods for evaluating Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) through visual and statistical analyses. It highlights the importance of examining the continuity of the latent space by interpolating between seed vectors to assess the model's ability to generate novel and visually appealing images. The use of spherical linear interpolation is suggested as a way to maintain proximity to the model's prior distribution and enhance sample quality. Additionally, the paper emphasizes the visualization of features learned by GANs, drawing parallels to studies on convolutional neural networks. Techniques such as guided backpropagation and t-SNE are mentioned as tools for understanding the internal workings of GANs and their learned representations. Overall, the paper presents a comprehensive approach to assessing GAN performance and the quality of generated images.

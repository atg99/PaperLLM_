In this paper, Ali Borji explores the challenges associated with evaluating Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), despite their growing prominence in generative modeling applications. The author notes that while numerous GAN variants have emerged and theoretical advancements have been made, a consensus on effective evaluation measures is still lacking. Borji reviews over 24 quantitative and 5 qualitative metrics used to assess generative models, focusing on those derived from GANs. He introduces a set of 7 desiderata to guide the evaluation process and assesses the compatibility of various measures with these criteria. The paper underscores the importance of establishing reliable evaluation metrics to facilitate progress in the field of generative models, which are integral to numerous machine learning and computer vision tasks.

The references listed cover a wide range of topics related to neural networks, optimization, and machine learning. Key contributions include the exploration of one-hidden-layer neural networks and their landscape design (Ge et al., 2017), as well as the qualitative characterization of neural network optimization problems (Goodfellow et al., 2015). The concept of Neural Turing Machines is introduced by Graves et al. (2014), highlighting the integration of memory into neural architectures.

Hahnloser (1998) discusses piecewise analysis of networks of linear threshold neurons, while Hardt and Ma (2017) emphasize the importance of identity in deep learning. The convergence of gradient descent in learning linear dynamical systems is investigated by Hardt et al. (2018). The seminal work on Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks by Hochreiter and Schmidhuber (1997) has significantly influenced sequence modeling.

The neural tangent kernel is analyzed for its implications on convergence and generalization in neural networks (Jacot et al., 2018). Janzamin et al. (2015) address the challenges of non-convexity in neural network training through tensor methods. Continuous translation models are presented by Kalchbrenner and Blunsom (2013), while the expressive power of recurrent neural networks is examined by Khrulkov et al. (2018).

Laurent and Massart (2000) provide insights into adaptive estimation through model selection, and the connection between deep neural networks and Gaussian processes is explored by Lee et al. (2017). Li and Liang (2018) discuss the learning of overparameterized neural networks via stochastic gradient descent, followed by Li and Yuan (2017) who analyze the convergence of two-layer neural networks with ReLU activation.

Lojasiewicz (1963) presents a topological property relevant to real analytic subsets, and Mei et al. (2018) offer a mean field perspective on the landscape of two-layer neural networks. Finally, Mikolov et al. (2010) contribute to the development of recurrent neural network-based language models, emphasizing their effectiveness in natural language processing tasks. Overall, these works collectively advance the understanding of neural networks, their training dynamics, and their applications in various domains.

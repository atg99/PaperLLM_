This paper reviews various ensemble learning algorithms, specifically bagging, boosting, and stacking, and their applications in remote sensing data analysis. It highlights that the Random Forest (RF) algorithm is the most commonly used in this context, while other ensemble methods are less frequently applied. Despite advancements in enhancing prediction accuracy, the paper identifies gaps in the integration of feature selection with ensemble methods and the incorporation of deep learning into ensemble frameworks. The authors emphasize the need for further research on ensemble algorithms to facilitate the adoption of more sophisticated techniques in practical applications.

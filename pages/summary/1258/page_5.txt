The paper discusses the construction of a co-occurrence network based on keywords from ensemble learning literature, revealing key themes and central words. The analysis indicates that studies primarily focus on classification, with Random Forest (RF) being the most utilized ensemble method. The network also highlights applications of ensemble algorithms in areas such as crop yield prediction, biomass estimation, natural hazard mapping, and spatial downscaling of temperature and rainfall. Among the applications reviewed, RF appeared 30 times, followed by stacking algorithms, while boosting methods like XGBoost were used less frequently. Reference models such as Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) and k-Nearest Neighbors (kNN) were often employed to benchmark ensemble algorithm performance.

This paper provides a comprehensive review of ensemble learning algorithms applied in remote sensing applications. It highlights the increasing reliance on machine learning for identifying nonlinear correlations in remote sensing data. The authors focus on three primary ensemble techniques: bagging, boosting, and stacking, explaining their underlying principles and analyzing existing literature.

The review summarizes various applications of these ensemble algorithms, including crop yield prediction, forest structure parameter estimation, natural hazard mapping, and spatial downscaling of climate parameters and land surface temperature. The authors note the complexity of remote sensing data, which often requires the integration of physical models and statistical methods to derive meaningful predictions.

The paper emphasizes that no single machine learning algorithm can universally outperform others, leading to the development of ensemble learning techniques that combine multiple hypotheses to enhance prediction accuracy. The authors conclude with suggestions for future research directions in the application of ensemble algorithms within the field of remote sensing.

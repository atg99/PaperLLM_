This paper provides a comprehensive summary of various applications utilizing ensemble learning algorithms across diverse fields. The reviewed applications are categorized based on their main input datasets or causative factors, the algorithms employed, and the algorithm that achieved the best accuracy. 

1. **Oil Palm Yield Prediction**: Utilized Landsat time series imagery with Random Forest (RF) achieving the best accuracy.
2. **Flood Susceptibility Mapping**: Incorporated multiple topographic and environmental factors, with the Random subspace J48 model showing the highest accuracy.
3. **Drought Risk Assessment**: Involved various climatic and geographic factors, with Support Vector Regression (SVR) using stacking yielding the best results.
4. **Downscaling Climate Variables**: Used sea surface and air temperature data, with Gradient Boosting Regression Trees (GBRT) performing best.
5. **Flash Flood Susceptibility Prediction**: Integrated multiple geographical features, where Extreme Random Trees (ERT) outperformed other models.
6. **Winter Wheat Yield Prediction**: Used MODIS Enhanced Vegetation Index and climate data, with XGBoost achieving the highest accuracy.
7. **Estimation of Canopy Height and Growing Stock Volume**: Employed airborne laser scanner data with RF as the main algorithm.
8. **Flood Susceptibility Mapping**: Similar to previous flood mapping, but utilized Bagging-LMT for the highest accuracy.
9. **Forest Biomass Estimation**: Combined advanced satellite data, with RF Kriging providing the best accuracy.
10. **Spatial Interpolation of Daily Maximum Air Temperature**: Used various climatic factors, where stacking performed best.
11. **Individual Tree Dendrometry**: Leveraged field and UAV-Lidar data, with SVR achieving the highest accuracy.

Overall, this summary illustrates the effectiveness of ensemble learning algorithms in improving predictive accuracy across various environmental and agricultural applications.

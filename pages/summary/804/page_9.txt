This paper presents a comprehensive list of references that contribute to the field of deep learning, particularly focusing on topics such as model compression, neural network optimization, and architecture search. Key contributions include the development of methods for efficient training and pruning of neural networks, as seen in works like the Lottery Ticket Hypothesis and DARTS. Notable techniques such as batch normalization and the Adam optimizer are highlighted for their roles in accelerating training processes. Furthermore, the references cover advancements in self-driving car technologies and large-scale datasets like ImageNet, which are essential for training deep learning models. The collection reflects significant strides in enhancing the performance and efficiency of deep learning architectures through various innovative approaches.

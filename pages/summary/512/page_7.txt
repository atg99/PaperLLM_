The paper presents findings on the effectiveness of distillation in semi-supervised learning using ResNet-50. The study highlights the Top-1 accuracy achieved through various training methods with different label fractions. Key results indicate that using unlabeled data with distillation loss significantly enhances model performance, especially when labeled data is scarce. 

In Table 2, it is shown that the combination of labeled and unlabeled data with distillation loss yields higher accuracy compared to using labeled data alone. The accuracy improves from 12.3% to 69.0% with 1% labels and from 52.0% to 75.1% with 10% labels when incorporating distillation loss. 

The paper emphasizes that distillation not only benefits smaller student models by transferring knowledge from larger teacher models but also enhances performance when both models share the same architecture. The authors establish that self-distillation can effectively improve semi-supervised learning outcomes.

Furthermore, the results in Figure 6 compare distilled models with fine-tuned and fully supervised approaches, demonstrating superior performance of the proposed methods. The paper concludes by asserting that their approach significantly outperforms previous state-of-the-art semi-supervised learning methods on the ImageNet dataset.

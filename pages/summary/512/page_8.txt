The paper presents a comparison of ImageNet accuracy for various models trained under semi-supervised settings, specifically focusing on the performance of models using unlabeled data. The results highlight the effectiveness of the proposed methods, particularly those utilizing self-distillation and contrastive learning techniques. 

Key findings include:
- The supervised baseline using ResNet-50 achieved a Top-1 accuracy of 25.4% with 1% labeled data and 56.4% with 10% labeled data.
- Methods employing unlabeled data in a task-specific manner, such as FixMatch and UDA, showed improved performance, with FixMatch reaching 71.5% Top-1 accuracy with 10% labeled data.
- The proposed SimCLRv2 distilled methods outperformed other approaches, achieving 75.9% Top-1 accuracy with a smaller ResNet-50 model and 76.6% with a self-distilled ResNet-152 model.

The paper also discusses the related work in the field, noting the growing success of unsupervised and self-supervised learning in computer vision, particularly through contrastive learning. It emphasizes the integration of self-distillation and the differences from traditional pseudo-labeling and label consistency methods, highlighting its contributions to the semi-supervised learning paradigm.

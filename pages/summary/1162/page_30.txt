The paper presents a mathematical framework for deriving the conjugate functions of various hinge loss functions, specifically CI-Hinge, BP-Hinge, and CS-Hinge losses. The authors define the function g(v) for different ranges of v, establishing that for all u, g(v) can be represented as a combination of indicator functions and linear terms.

For CI-Hinge, the conjugate is derived as a piecewise function that includes an indicator function and a linear term. Similarly, the BP-Hinge and CS-Hinge losses are analyzed, leading to their respective conjugates, which also involve indicator functions and linear adjustments.

The paper further elaborates on the relationship between the CS-Hinge and BP-Hinge losses, indicating that the conjugate of the CS-Hinge can be expressed in terms of the BP-Hinge conjugate combined with an additional linear term. This relationship is significant for understanding the behavior of these loss functions in cost-sensitive learning scenarios. 

The references cited include works on cost-sensitive learning and support vector machines, highlighting foundational studies that inform the current analysis.

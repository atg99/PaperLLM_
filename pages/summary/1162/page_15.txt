The paper discusses the formulation of cost-sensitive support vector machines (CS-SVM) in its dual and kernelized form. The dual problem is presented as an optimization problem that maximizes a specific objective function subject to constraints related to the labels of the training data. The formulation ensures that the CS-SVM can handle data that may not be separable, which is a significant advantage over previous algorithms like BM-SVM and BP-SVM.

The dual formulation includes parameters that allow for different penalties for misclassifications of different classes, represented by C1 and C-1. When C1 and C-1 are set to 1, the formulation reduces to the standard support vector machine (SVM) dual problem. The paper further reformulates the problem to incorporate an `1-norm regularization term, which enhances its compatibility with existing SVM solvers.

The main takeaway is that the CS-SVM framework maintains robustness regardless of data separability and integrates seamlessly into standard dual solvers, making it a versatile option for cost-sensitive classification tasks.

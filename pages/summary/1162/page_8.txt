The paper by Masnadi-Shirazi, Vasconcelos, and Iranmehr discusses the properties of a concave function defined by certain conditions. It establishes a maximum at the point \(  = \frac{C_1}{C_1 + C_{-1}} \) and introduces a symmetry condition described by equation (25). 

The proof begins with a reformulation of equation (19), which indicates the behavior of the function \( C \) under different conditions of \(  \). For \(  = 0 \), a trivial case is presented where the function evaluates identically. When \( 0 <  < \frac{1}{C_1 + C_{-1}} \), the analysis shows that the function is decreasing, while for \( 0 <  < \frac{1}{C_1 + C_{-1}} \) and \(  \) exceeding a certain threshold, the function is increasing. 

The paper concludes by proving that under the symmetry condition, the derived properties hold, reinforcing the relationship between concavity and the symmetry of the function. Lemma 1 further solidifies this by showing that any concave function exhibiting the symmetry defined in (25) will also meet the maximum property established in Theorem 4. The derivative analysis at \(  = 0 \) confirms that the maximum occurs at the specified point, establishing the foundational results of the study.

The study presents various model configurations and their effectiveness, highlighting that the integration of document expansion (stage H-1) with first-stage retrieval (stage H0) results in substantial improvements in metrics such as MRR@10, particularly with a near 50% relative increase observed in some conditions.

The findings indicate that the combination of doc2query-T5 and larger models, such as monoT5-3B and duoT5-3B, yields the best performance, with significant statistical validation of these improvements. The comparison between mono and duo models suggests that while larger models tend to perform better, the design choice of using duoT5 contributes positively to the overall ranking effectiveness. Notably, the results from the ablation study clarify that a larger mono model outperforms smaller duo models, reinforcing the importance of model size in tandem with effective ranking strategies.

The paper also explores the impact of varying the number of candidates during reranking, revealing that certain scoring methods, like Sym-Sum and Sym-Sum-Log, enhance reranking effectiveness through the combination of scores from different candidate pairs. Overall, the research underscores the effectiveness of a multi-stage ranking pipeline and the crucial role of model size and design in optimizing passage retrieval.

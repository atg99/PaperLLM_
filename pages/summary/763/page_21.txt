The paper presents the Expando-Mono-Duo design pattern for text ranking using pretrained sequence-to-sequence models. It highlights that pairwise reranking with the duoT5 model enhances the effectiveness of the monoT5 output, particularly improving early-precision metrics. The findings indicate that the benefits derived from the three components of the design pattern—Expando, Mono, and Duo—are both additive and cumulative. Experimental results from various ranking tasks, including MS MARCO, TREC 2020 Deep Learning Track, and TREC-COVID challenge, demonstrate that the proposed approach achieves results that are near or at the state of the art. Overall, the Expando-Mono-Duo design pattern serves as a foundational reference for transformer-based multi-stage ranking architectures.

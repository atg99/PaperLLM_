The paper introduces the Expando-Mono-Duo design pattern for text ranking using pretrained sequence-to-sequence models, specifically focusing on the MS MARCO dataset and the TREC 2020 Deep Learning Track. The MS MARCO dataset consists of 367,000 training queries and 5,193 development queries, with a total of 5,793 test queries, although relevance judgments are only available for the development set. The relevance judgments for the document ranking task are sparse, prompting the evaluation of models in a zero-shot setting using only the MS MARCO passage ranking dataset without any fine-tuning.

The paper describes an innovative document expansion strategy that utilizes the doc2query-T5 model, which was trained on the passage ranking task. Given that many documents exceed T5's input length of 512 tokens, the authors segmented documents into passages using a sliding window approach, generating queries for each passage. This led to the creation of two distinct indexing methods in Anserini: per-document expansion, where each document is indexed with its predicted queries as a single unit, and per-passage expansion, where indexed passages are treated as multiple retrievable units, each labeled sequentially.

This approach aims to leverage the close relationship between document and passage datasets to enhance performance while minimizing the computational cost associated with fine-tuning, thereby simplifying the experimental setup.

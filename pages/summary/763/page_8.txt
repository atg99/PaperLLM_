The paper discusses the implementation and evaluation of retrieval models using the Anserini IR toolkit, focusing on the use of BM25 and BM25 + RM3 for query expansion on TREC 2020 Deep Learning Track topics. The models were trained with the MS MARCO passage dataset, utilizing triples of queries, relevant passages, and non-relevant passages. The monoT5 model was fine-tuned with a learning rate of 0.001 for 100,000 iterations, while the duoT5 model built upon monoT5 was trained for 50,000 iterations. The training utilized class-balanced batches and was conducted on Google TPU v3-8 hardware.

The design decision to exclude predicted expansion queries during reranking aimed to avoid potential noise and overflow issues with model input length. The duoT5 model's reranking process was computationally intensive, requiring careful selection of candidate pairs. The paper also evaluates the models on the MS MARCO document ranking dataset, noting the challenges posed by the longer document lengths compared to typical transformer input limits. Overall, the study emphasizes the effectiveness of these retrieval models in improving information retrieval tasks.

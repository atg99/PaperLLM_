The paper presents the Expando-Mono-Duo design pattern for text ranking using pretrained sequence-to-sequence models, evaluating its effectiveness on the MS MARCO document ranking task. The results, summarized in Table 3, show various configurations and their corresponding MRR@100 scores on development and test sets. The full Expando-Mono-Duo configuration (condition 4) achieved the best score at the time of submission, outperforming other configurations, including ensembles trained specifically on the MS MARCO dataset.

The analysis indicates that integrating document expansion (stage H-1) significantly enhances the first-stage retrieval (stage H0), with a notable improvement observed when comparing conditions 1 and 2. The findings suggest that the addition of monoT5-3B and duoT5-3B models further contributes to cumulative gains in retrieval performance, with all components of the pipeline showing statistical significance.

Additionally, results from the TREC 2020 Deep Learning Track document ranking are discussed, highlighting that the models did not utilize relevance judgments from prior tracks. The best run submitted to TREC 2020 also demonstrated superior performance without document expansion. The study emphasizes the importance of pseudo-relevance feedback in improving retrieval effectiveness while noting that certain metrics like nDCG@10 and MRR remained relatively unchanged across different configurations. Overall, the Expando-Mono-Duo method remains competitive, particularly in zero-shot scenarios, suggesting potential for future improvements by incorporating recent innovations.

The paper discusses the methodology employed by Ronak Pradeep, Rodrigo Nogueira, and Jimmy Lin in their information retrieval system, which utilizes BM25 for passage and document ranking. The authors created both expanded and non-expanded indexes for efficient retrieval, allowing for easy identification of passages from the same document during query processing. They employed the BM25 + RM3 model for the TREC 2020 Deep Learning Track topics, using keyword queries to generate candidate passages for reranking.

The reranking process involved two main settings: "Mono" and "Duo." In the Mono setting, the monoT5 model was utilized for reranking passages identified as potentially relevant through keyword search. The authors segmented documents into passages and performed inference on these passages to determine relevance scores. The Duo setting employed the duoT5 model, which evaluated pairs of representative passages from documents to compute pairwise relevance scores. 

The duoT5 model allowed for an increased input token limit of 1024 to accommodate longer passage pairs, enhancing the model's ability to generalize beyond its training context. However, this increase also resulted in higher computational costs due to the quadratic complexity of transformers concerning input length.

Overall, the paper details a systematic approach to document retrieval, leveraging advanced models and techniques for improved relevance ranking while addressing the challenges posed by document length and computational efficiency.

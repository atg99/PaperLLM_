The study investigates the spike-S309 complex of the Omicron variant, using single-particle cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM) to determine its structure. The research highlights that the one-RBD-up conformation of the spike-S309 complex constitutes over 82% of the observed structures. Focused refinement of the RBD-S309 region achieved a density map resolution of 2.80 Å, enabling detailed visualization of the interaction interface between the RBD and S309 Fab. The S309 antibody specifically targets a proteoglycan epitope on residue N343 of the RBD, forming extensive hydrogen bonds with several nearby residues.

The architecture of the ACE2-bound Omicron spike trimer was also analyzed, revealing a cryo-EM map at 2.40 Å resolution. The study details the interactions between the RBD and hACE2, illustrating the roles of hydrogen bonds, salt bridges, and π-π interactions. A comparison of the apo and ACE2-bound spike trimers demonstrated conformational changes, with specific residues highlighted. The research further presents the relative populations of RBD states in different spike variants, including the prototype and the Omicron-3mut. Overall, the findings provide insights into the structural dynamics and interactions of the Omicron spike protein, contributing to the understanding of its binding affinity and potential implications for vaccine design.

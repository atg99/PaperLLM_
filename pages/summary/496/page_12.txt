The referenced paper discusses advancements in cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM) techniques and software tools that enhance the accuracy and efficiency of structural biology research. Key developments include MotionCor2 for correcting beam-induced motion, cryoSPARC for unsupervised structure determination, and DeepEMhancer for volume post-processing using deep learning. Additional tools mentioned are CTFFIND4 for defocus estimation, RELION-3 for automated high-resolution structure determination, and visualization systems like UCSF Chimera and PyMOL.

The study focuses on the purification and structural analysis of protein complexes involving the Omicron spike protein and its interactions with human ACE2 and antibodies S309 and S304. The proteins were expressed in HEK293F cells and purified using various chromatography techniques, including affinity chromatography and gel filtration. The purified proteins were used to form complexes, which were subsequently prepared for cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM) analysis.

For cryo-EM sample preparation, the proteins were applied to glow-discharged grids and plunge-frozen in liquid ethane. Data acquisition was performed using a Titan Krios electron microscope, capturing images at high resolution under specific defocus settings. Image processing involved correcting for drift, estimating contrast transfer function parameters, and classifying particles to reconstruct three-dimensional structures.

The results obtained include detailed structures of the Omicron spike protein in complex with ACE2 and antibodies, revealing conformational states. Notably, the study identified distinct structures based on the number of receptor binding domains (RBDs) in an up configuration, with resolutions ranging from 2.40 Å to 3.44 Å, providing insights into the molecular interactions critical for viral entry and potential therapeutic targeting.

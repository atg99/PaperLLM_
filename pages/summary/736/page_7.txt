The paper presents a comparison of various reinforcement learning (RL) algorithms and their enhancements through online meta-learning, specifically highlighting the meta-learner PPO-LIRPG. Table 1 summarizes the maximum average returns across several environments, showing that the SAC-MC method generally outperforms other algorithms. The results indicate that OffP-AC methods tend to perform better than the on-policy PPO in most environments, emphasizing the significance of meta-learning in off-policy settings.

Key findings include: 

1. PPO-LIRPG fails to enhance basic PPO performance in standard tasks.
2. Meta-Critic methods are favored over PPO-LIRPG, as they offer a direct loss rather than a scalar reward bonus, leading to more stable policy updates.
3. The -MC variants of algorithms consistently show improved performance and reduced variance compared to their vanilla counterparts.

Figures 3 and 4 illustrate the learning curves for vanilla TD3 and SAC against their meta-learning counterparts, further supporting the claim that -MC methods yield better results in continuous control tasks. Overall, the study establishes the advantages of incorporating meta-learning strategies into off-policy reinforcement learning frameworks.

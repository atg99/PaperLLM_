This paper introduces a novel online meta-critic learning framework for Off-Policy Actor-Critic (OffP-AC) methods, which enhances the efficiency of actor-critic learning in continuous control tasks. OffP-AC methods, such as DDPG, TD3, and SAC, are recognized for their superior sample efficiency compared to on-policy methods due to their ability to learn from historical transitions without the need for sequential data. The traditional approach involves the critic estimating the action-value function and providing a fixed loss to guide the actor's policy updates.

The proposed meta-critic framework innovatively observes the learning process to meta-learn an additional loss for the actor, enabling rapid online learning tailored to a single task rather than a family of tasks. This approach not only accelerates the learning process but also improves the overall performance of the actor-critic methods. The authors argue that this online meta-critic learning significantly benefits various continuous control tasks, demonstrating its effectiveness in enhancing the capabilities of contemporary OffP-AC methods.

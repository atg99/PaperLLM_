The cited works encompass a range of topics in machine learning, particularly focusing on reinforcement learning, meta-learning, and optimization techniques. 

Riemer et al. (2019) explore methods for continual learning that maximize transfer of knowledge while minimizing interference between tasks. Santoro et al. (2016) introduce memory-augmented neural networks for meta-learning, enhancing the ability to learn from past experiences. Schulman et al. (2015, 2017) present advancements in policy optimization methods, specifically Trust Region Policy Optimization and Proximal Policy Optimization, improving stability and performance in reinforcement learning.

Schwartz et al. (2019) discuss the concept of Green AI, emphasizing the importance of energy efficiency in AI research. Sung et al. (2017) propose meta-critic networks to achieve sample-efficient learning, while Sutton et al. (2000, 2009) provide foundational work on policy gradient methods and temporal-difference learning with function approximation.

Todorov et al. (2012) introduce Mujoco, a physics engine that facilitates model-based control research. Veeriah et al. (2019) investigate the discovery of auxiliary tasks that can enhance learning efficiency. Wu et al. (2018) focus on dynamic loss functions for teaching purposes, whereas Xu et al. (2018) delve into meta-gradient reinforcement learning, which adjusts learning rates based on performance.

Zaheer et al. (2017) present Deep Sets, a framework for handling unordered data, while Zheng et al. (2018) examine intrinsic rewards in policy gradient methods to improve exploration strategies. Collectively, these studies contribute significantly to the understanding and advancement of learning algorithms in AI.

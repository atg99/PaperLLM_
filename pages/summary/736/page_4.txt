The paper presents an algorithm for Online Meta-Critic Learning specifically designed for Off-Policy Actor-Critic Reinforcement Learning (OffP-AC RL). The algorithm involves initializing the actor, critic, meta-critic, and a replay buffer. During each iteration, the agent selects actions based on the current policy and observes rewards and new states, storing these transitions in the replay buffer.

The learning process consists of gradient steps where a mini-batch from the replay buffer is sampled to update the critic parameters and the actor. The updates are based on losses derived from both the vanilla critic and the meta-critic. The meta-critic, structured as a neural network, receives inputs that include features of the actor along with the states and actions from the training data. It outputs a scalar loss that is differentiable concerning the actor's parameters.

The optimization flow follows three main steps: 
1. **Meta-train**: The policy is updated using losses from both the vanilla-critic and the meta-critic.
2. **Meta-test**: Another mini-batch is sampled to evaluate the performance of the updated policy based on a meta-loss.
3. **Meta-optimization**: The meta-critic is updated to enhance performance on the validation batch, concurrently updating the actor based on both losses.

Overall, the meta-critic is designed to co-evolve with the actor while being trained online, enabling flexible integration with various OffP-AC algorithms such as DDPG, TD3, and SAC. The paper emphasizes the iterative nature of the updates and the importance of the meta-critic in improving the actor's performance in reinforcement learning tasks.

In this section, the authors evaluate the performance of their proposed meta-critic enhancements on three foundational algorithms: DDPG, TD3, and SAC, denoted as DDPG-MC, TD3-MC, and SAC-MC respectively. The meta-critic is integrated with the standard critic of these algorithms, using a specific neural network architecture for implementation.

The experiments are conducted across various environments, including seven MuJoCo tasks in OpenAI Gym, two tasks in rllab, and a simulated racing car environment called TORCS. The evaluation focuses on learning efficiency and performance, with results averaged over multiple trials and network initializations.

The findings reveal that DDPG-MC consistently outperforms the vanilla DDPG in terms of both learning speed and final performance, exhibiting reduced variance as well. Similar trends are noted for TD3 and SAC, where the enhancements lead to improved stability and higher asymptotic performance across tasks. The results are quantitatively summarized in a table highlighting the maximum average returns achieved by the different methods.

Overall, the experiments demonstrate that the integration of a meta-critic significantly enhances the performance of existing reinforcement learning algorithms, particularly in complex environments. This suggests that the proposed approach could be a valuable contribution to the field of deep reinforcement learning.

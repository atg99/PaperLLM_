The paper discusses a vehicle tracking and counting system utilizing YOLO (You Only Look Once) for real-time vehicle detection. It describes an algorithm that assigns numeric identifiers to bounding boxes around tracked vehicles, which are visually represented on a computer screen. The system employs a dotted centerline to facilitate vehicle counting, with specific functions developed to track vehicles entering and exiting a designated area, such as a parking lot.

To address the discrepancy between YOLO's processing speed and real-time intervals, the algorithm calculates the number of frames processed within a specified time frame and estimates the overall processing time. This information is used to generate vehicle counts in both entry and exit directions, which are then recorded in a spreadsheet format.

The counting mechanism is based on the interaction of vehicles with the centerline and parallel lines. For instance, if a vehicle crosses the left parallel line before the centerline, it is classified as entering from the left. The system includes functions for counting vehicles moving from left to right and vice versa. The results are displayed visually and in tabular format, providing a clear overview of vehicle flow for applications such as monitoring strip mall traffic. Figures included in the paper illustrate the tracking process and the resulting data counts.

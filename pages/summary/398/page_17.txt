The paper discusses the development and evaluation of an automated vehicle counting algorithm that achieves a 90 percent accuracy rate for directional counts under flexible interval timing. However, it notes limitations such as decreased accuracy when vehicles pass at high speeds, overlap, or when video quality is poor. The benefit-cost analysis shows a B/C ratio of at least 1.54, indicating a 54 percent improvement in benefits over traditional manual counting methods. This suggests the algorithm could serve as a cost-effective alternative to more expensive automated counting techniques.

The authors contributed to various aspects of the research, including conceptualization, methodology, software development, validation, and writing.

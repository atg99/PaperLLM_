The paper presents the results of an automated vehicle counting study using computer algorithms to analyze pre-recorded video data collected from ten different sites over a total of 320 hours. The analysis focused on entry and exit counts for each site across two days, with detailed results provided in Table 1. 

The accuracy of the automated counts was assessed by comparing them with manual counts for both days. The findings reveal varying levels of accuracy across different sites, as summarized in Tables 2 and 3. For instance, on Day 1, the accuracy for entry counts ranged from 76.51% to 95.47%, while exit counts showed accuracy percentages between 81.05% and 95.66%. Overall, the results indicate that while the automated system performs well, there are discrepancies that highlight the need for further refinement in the counting algorithms to improve accuracy.

In this section of the paper, the authors discuss their selection of the YOLO version for vehicle counting, opting for YOLO version 3 due to its speed, accuracy, and user-friendliness. Although newer versions, including YOLOv4, YOLOv5, YOLOv6, and the most accurate YOLOv7, are available, the authors argue that YOLOv3 suffices for their application since vehicle counting does not require detection of tiny objects. They note that their results maintain an accuracy of 90 percent, comparable to other recent methods, while also emphasizing the cost-effectiveness of their approach compared to various in situ technologies.

The authors detail the process of converting the YOLO weight file from C/C++ to a TensorFlow-compatible format. They utilize Python and various libraries such as TensorFlow, OpenCV, and Numpy for this conversion, highlighting the convenience of Python in their project. The section concludes with an explanation of the settings for input files, including the requirement for video files to be in .mp4 format and suggestions for processing multiple files before uploading them for vehicle detection algorithms.

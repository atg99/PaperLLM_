The paper presents statistical analyses of paired observations over two days, focusing on the confidence limits of the mean differences. For Day 1, the mean difference is 44.10 with a standard deviation of 18.91, resulting in a 95 percent confidence interval ranging from 32.38 to 55.82. For Day 2, the mean difference is slightly higher at 44.70, with a standard deviation of 20.38, yielding a confidence interval from 32.07 to 57.33. This indicates a 95 percent probability that the true mean differences lie within these ranges.

Additionally, a benefit-cost analysis is performed using specific parameters, including a pay rate of 10 USD per hour for trained individuals and an energy cost of 0.13 USD per kWh in East Baton Rouge, LA. The analysis incorporates energy consumption data from a Lenovo Legion Y7000P-1060 device, detailing hours spent on video collection, manual counts, and automated counts. The total costs associated with video collection and energy consumption are calculated, providing a framework for evaluating the economic feasibility of the research conducted.

The paper emphasizes that traditional optimization methods are inadequate for deep learning algorithms, while the effectiveness of Orthogonal Array Tuning Method (OATM) has been validated across various research fields. The authors propose the adoption of OATM for hyperparameter tuning in deep learning, asserting that their work represents the first systematic exploration of this approach in this context.

In the section on Orthogonal Array, the authors define the concept, explaining its structure and composition principles. An Orthogonal Array is characterized by a fixed finite set of elements arranged so that all ordered pairs of elements across any two columns appear with equal frequency. This arrangement ensures balance and completeness in the combinations of levels across factors.

The key principles of Orthogonal Arrays include the uniform appearance of different levels within the same factor and the balanced representation of level combinations across different factors. The essence of OATM is to provide a representative subset of all possible combinations, significantly reducing the number of experiments needed for effective hyperparameter tuning.

Overall, the paper lays the groundwork for utilizing OATM in deep learning hyperparameter optimization, highlighting its potential advantages over traditional methods and establishing a foundation for further research in this area.

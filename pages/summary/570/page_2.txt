The paper by Xiang Zhang et al. discusses various hyper-parameter tuning methods in machine learning, focusing on their advantages and limitations. Traditional methods such as grid search and random search are noted for their time-consuming nature and inability to ensure convergence to a global optimum. While random search mitigates some grid search drawbacks, it still lacks consistency in results. Automated tuning methods, particularly Bayesian Optimization, have gained popularity due to their efficiency in finding global optima with fewer steps; however, they are sensitive to surrogate model parameters and heavily reliant on learning model quality.

To improve upon these methods, the authors introduce the Orthogonal Array Tuning Method (OATM), which is based on the Taguchi Approach. OATM aims to strike a balance between reduced tuning time and competitive performance by utilizing a fractional orthogonal design that ensures equal consideration of all hyper-parameter values. This method has been effectively applied in various engineering domains and represents a novel approach in deep learning hyper-parameter tuning.

The paper includes a detailed explanation of OATM within the context of two popular deep learning architectures and evaluates its performance across three datasets, demonstrating its universality and adaptability. The authors also address the difficulty in accessing existing source codes for grid search, random search, and Bayesian optimization in deep learning, and offer their reusable source codes and datasets for further research. The work highlights the potential of OATM as a significant advancement in hyper-parameter tuning methods for deep learning.

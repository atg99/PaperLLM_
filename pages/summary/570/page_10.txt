The paper presents an analysis of hyperparameters for Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN) and Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) through a factorial design approach. 

Table 3 outlines the factor-level settings for both RNN and CNN, detailing various hyperparameter configurations, including learning rates, layer sizes, and neuron counts at different levels. 

Table 4 provides a range analysis of the RNN, listing accuracy results for different combinations of hyperparameters. The analysis calculates Rlevel, which is the sum of accuracy across rows for a given factor level, and Alevel, which is the average accuracy for each level. The lowest and highest accuracy values are identified to determine the range for each factor's influence on performance. The importance of each factor is ranked based on the calculated ranges, with the findings suggesting that the factors should be prioritized in the order of lambda, nl, lr, and nn.

The study concludes with the identification of the optimal settings for the RNN, which are lr = 0.004, ϕ = 0.005, nl = 6, and nn = 64, yielding an optimal accuracy of 0.925. This optimal configuration is derived from the previous range analysis, underscoring the effectiveness of systematic hyperparameter tuning in enhancing model performance.

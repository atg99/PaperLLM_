This paper addresses the significant challenge of hyper-parameter tuning in deep learning, which is crucial for achieving optimal performance. The authors propose an efficient Orthogonal Array Tuning Method (OATM) designed to streamline this process. The OATM consists of five detailed steps and is demonstrated using two popular deep neural network architectures: Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) and Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs). 

The authors compare OATM with current state-of-the-art tuning methods, including manual approaches like grid and random search, as well as automatic methods such as Bayesian Optimization. Experimental results indicate that OATM substantially reduces tuning time while maintaining satisfactory performance levels. The paper highlights the necessity of effective hyper-parameter tuning in deep learning due to the high number and dependency of hyper-parameters, which can lead to drastic fluctuations in algorithm performance, as evidenced by reported classification accuracy variations. 

Overall, the study emphasizes the importance of developing efficient tuning methodologies to enhance deep learning applications across various domains.

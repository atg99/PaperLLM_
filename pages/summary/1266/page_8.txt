The paper discusses the limitations of current object detectors, specifically their tendency to misidentify fire-like objects, such as the sun, leading to false alarms. To improve detection accuracy, the authors propose an integrated approach utilizing three deep learning models: Yolov5 and EfficientDet function as object detectors generating candidate fire locations, while EfficientNet serves as a binary classifier that analyzes the entire image context. This methodology employs a non-maximum suppression algorithm to filter out redundant detection boxes based on confidence scores. The final decision on whether an image contains fire objects is made by a decision strategy module that considers both detection and classification outcomes. Importantly, the integration of these learners is designed to maintain overall model efficiency, as each operates independently in separate processes.

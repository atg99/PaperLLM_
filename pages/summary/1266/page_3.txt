The paper discusses the creation of a comprehensive forest fire dataset consisting of 10,581 images, which includes 2,976 images depicting forest fires and 7,605 images of non-fire forest scenes. This dataset was developed through manual filtration from existing collections such as FD-dataset, ForestryImages, and VisiFire. Representative images from both the fire and non-fire categories are presented in figures, showcasing various types of forest fires, including ground fires and canopy fires, as well as normal forest scenes devoid of fire. The dataset aims to facilitate research and analysis related to forest fire detection and management.

This paper presents a novel ensemble learning approach for real-time forest fire detection, integrating two advanced object detectors, Yolov5 and EfficientDet, to enhance the model's robustness across various fire scenarios. The introduction of a guiding leader model, EfficientNet, aims to minimize false positives during the detection process. Experimental results indicate that this integrated model outperforms other leading object detectors, achieving an optimal balance of average precision, recall, false positive rate, frame accuracy, and latency, making it suitable for practical forestry applications.

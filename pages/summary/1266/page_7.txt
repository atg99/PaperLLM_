The paper discusses the integration of two object detection models, Yolov5 and EfficientDet, to enhance detection accuracy in fire detection scenarios. Yolov5 shows superior performance in detecting long-area fires, successfully identifying true positives while EfficientDet fails to detect these instances, as illustrated in accompanying figures. Conversely, EfficientDet demonstrates a more cautious detection approach, effectively covering all fire areas and identifying multiple fire objects, while Yolov5 misses some. The authors argue that combining these two models, each with distinct strengths, can lead to improved overall detection capabilities. They also note a limitation in current object detectors, which tend to focus solely on specific fire regions, neglecting broader contextual information within images.

The paper discusses the evaluation criteria for object detection models using Microsoft COCO standards, specifically focusing on Average Precision (AP) and Average Recall (AR) across different object sizes: small, medium, and large. The models evaluated include Yolov5, EfficientDet, and EfficientNet, which were trained on forest fire images.

Yolov5 and EfficientDet, both object detectors, were trained with 2381 forest fire images and tested on 476 images, while EfficientNet, an image classifier, was trained with the same number of fire images along with 5804 non-fire images, and tested on 476 fire images and 1160 non-fire images. Non-fire images included both normal images and those with fire-like objects. The training was conducted using Pytorch and an NVIDIA GTX 2080TI. The results show that while the bounding boxes generated by the models differ from the ground truth, they still demonstrate good detection performance. Detailed training strategies are outlined in the paper.

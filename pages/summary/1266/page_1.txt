The paper titled "A Forest Fire Detection System Based on Ensemble Learning" by Renjie Xu et al. addresses the challenge of detecting forest fires, which is complicated by the diverse shapes, textures, and colors of fires. Traditional detection methods often rely on manmade features, which may not be effective across various forest environments. To enhance detection capabilities, the authors propose a novel ensemble learning approach that combines multiple deep learning models.

The proposed system integrates two individual learners, Yolov5 and EfficientDet, to perform the fire detection process. Additionally, a third learner, EfficientNet, is utilized to gather global information, thereby reducing the likelihood of false positives that may arise from a focus on local data. The final detection results are derived from the collective decisions of these three models.

Experimental results demonstrate that this ensemble learning method improves detection performance by 2.5% to 10.9% while significantly decreasing false positives by 51.3%, all without introducing extra latency. The study highlights the potential of using advanced deep learning techniques for more accurate and efficient forest fire detection, especially in open areas where traditional methods fall short.

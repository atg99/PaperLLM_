This collection of references highlights significant advancements in the field of graph neural networks (GNNs) and their applications. Key themes include the development of robust GNN training techniques, such as topology adaptive edge dropping and graph data augmentation methods like G-mixup and ifmixup. The use of self-supervised learning approaches is also prominent, with works focusing on contrastive learning and masked graph autoencoders for enhanced representation learning.

Notable contributions include the introduction of neural message passing for quantum chemistry, adversarial training strategies to improve model resilience, and the exploration of heterogeneous networks through attribute completion. Several studies emphasize the importance of scalability in GNNs, addressing challenges in large graph representation learning via techniques such as graph coarsening and semi-supervised classification.

The references collectively underscore the ongoing evolution of GNN methodologies, addressing both theoretical and practical aspects, and demonstrating their applicability across various domains including drug discovery and fraud detection.

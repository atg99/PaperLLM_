The paper by Fahim Sufi provides a systematic review of the applications of Generative Pre-trained Transformers (GPT) in research, particularly focusing on data augmentation and the generation of synthetic data. It highlights the transformative impact of GPT and similar large language models (LLMs) on academic writing, facilitating idea generation, drafting, and addressing challenges like writer's block. The review meticulously analyzes 412 scholarly works, distilling 77 key contributions that address three main research questions related to the generation of research data, data analysis, and research design.

Sufi's review emphasizes the significant role of GPT in enhancing the quality and efficiency of scholarly work, particularly through data augmentation. It identifies 48 relevant contributions and establishes a comprehensive classification framework for the use of GPT in research data, categorizing the literature into six main categories and 14 sub-categories. Additionally, the review compares 54 pieces of literature to evaluate various research domains, methodologies, and the advantages and disadvantages of using GPT, offering valuable insights for scholars seeking to integrate these technologies into their research processes.

Overall, the study underscores the potential benefits of GPT in academic contexts while also acknowledging the need for responsible and judicious use of such AI tools in writing practices.

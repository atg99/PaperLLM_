The review of existing literature reveals various applications of GPT and LLMs in different domains, including abstract reasoning, complex problem-solving, and textual analysis. The study employed a sophisticated query mechanism to gather literature from established databases, ensuring a comprehensive review. Notably, Google Scholar was excluded due to its limitations in advanced search capabilities and quality control issues, which may hinder systematic literature reviews. The alternative databases provided a more reliable source of scholarly articles, with significant records retrieved from each.

Additionally, the use of LitMaps was integral to the review process, enabling the visualization of citation networks and enhancing the identification of relevant studies. Overall, the research underscores the transformative potential of GPT and LLMs in advancing academic research and addressing critical challenges in various fields.

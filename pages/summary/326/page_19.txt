This paper presents a systematic literature review focused on the role of Generative Pre-trained Transformers (GPT) in research, addressing gaps in existing reviews that overlooked GPT's utility in generating, processing, and analyzing research data. A total of 77 studies were examined, with a significant percentage published in 2023 and 2024, indicating the ongoing relevance of this topic.

The review highlights the limitations and challenges associated with adopting GPT technologies in research. It emphasizes the strategic importance of data augmentation and provides a framework for understanding GPT's applications in research design and analysis. The insights gained can guide researchers in effectively integrating GPT into their methodologies for generating and analyzing data.

To ensure the review's rigor, established guidelines were followed, excluding unreliable sources, and utilizing advanced visualization tools like Litmaps for comprehensive study assessment. A strategic citation analysis further validated the selected studies, ensuring the review's reliability and relevance within the scholarly landscape. Overall, the paper serves as a critical resource for researchers looking to leverage GPT technologies in their work.

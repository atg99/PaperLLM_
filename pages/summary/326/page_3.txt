This literature review addresses a significant gap in understanding how GPT and related technologies can be utilized in research data management. By rigorously analyzing 412 scholarly works, the authors narrowed the focus to 77 contributions that specifically respond to three key research questions: the role of GPT in generating and processing research data, analyzing research data, and assisting in research design and problem-solving. 

Central to the review is a focus on data augmentation, with 45 pertinent studies highlighted. The review also explores GPT's proactive role in critical data analysis and research design. A notable contribution of this study is the development of a comprehensive classification framework for GPT's application in research data, which includes six overarching categories and 14 sub-categories. This framework is validated through the strategic placement of the 45 studies, underscoring its intellectual rigor and significance.

Additionally, the review conducts a comparative analysis of 54 existing works, evaluating various research domains, methodologies, and their respective advantages and disadvantages. This analysis offers valuable insights for researchers considering the integration of GPT across different phases of their scholarly work. The study employs the PRISMA method to ensure a systematic approach to the literature review, enhancing its methodological robustness.

The paper categorizes existing literature into two primary areas: NLP and Text Analysis, and Machine Learning Model Enhancement. 

In the NLP and Text Analysis category, various methods utilizing GPT models are explored, highlighting their advantages and disadvantages. For instance, GPT is effective for feature extraction from unstructured data and named entity recognition, although these applications may require fine-tuning. Simile detection and text simplification also benefit from GPT, enhancing performance but necessitating iterative adjustments. Additionally, data augmentation techniques using GPT-3 and GPT-2 show improvements in classification tasks, albeit with potential issues of generating noisy data.

In the Machine Learning Model Enhancement section, the focus is on how GPT can enhance model performance through data cleaning, integration, and interpretability. The literature indicates that GPT-3 is adaptable for various data tasks, while LLMs can improve interpretable models without sacrificing efficiency. However, these enhancements may involve complex integrations and time-intensive processes.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the capabilities of GPT models in various applications, while also acknowledging the challenges associated with their implementation, such as the need for task-specific adaptations and managing inaccuracies in generated outputs.

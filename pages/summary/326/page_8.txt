The paper discusses the application of Generative Pre-trained Transformer (GPT) models, particularly in healthcare and other fields, to enhance existing datasets through synthetic data generation. It highlights a method for creating artificial clinical notes in Electronic Health Records (EHRs) to predict patient outcomes, particularly addressing data scarcity and privacy concerns in healthcare. The use of GPT-2 is emphasized for improving predictions related to 30-day readmission rates through a teacher-student framework for noise control.

Additionally, the paper explores how large language models (LLMs) like GPT-3 can generate synthetic data for various biomedical research applications, enhancing dataset diversity and quality. It also covers the generation of synthetic responses for survey data in social sciences, aiming to mitigate issues such as respondent bias.

Further, the research evaluates the effectiveness of synthetic data generated by GPT-3 for training conversational AI classifiers and its application in multilingual genre detection and persuasion technique analysis. The effectiveness of this approach is demonstrated through successful rankings in relevant tasks.

Moreover, the paper delves into the use of GPT-3 for generating synthetic responses in psychological surveys, aiding in understanding diverse human behaviors. It also discusses applications in climate change research, vaccine hesitancy monitoring, and text simplification in financial texts, showing significant improvements in data classification and model performance.

The exploration of combining GPT-2 with Monte Carlo Tree Search for textual data augmentation is noted, alongside the use of GPT-3 for addressing class imbalances in sentiment analysis. The findings underscore the potential of LLMs to enhance model performance and data quality across various applications, including neural machine translation and aspect term extraction in sentiment analysis. Overall, the research illustrates the versatility and effectiveness of GPT models in generating synthetic data to address challenges in data scarcity and enhance predictive modeling across multiple domains.

The paper presents a systematic review of the literature concerning the applications of Generative Pre-trained Transformers (GPT) and Large Language Models (LLMs) in various phases of research data. It identifies 48 papers that directly address these applications in research data phases and an additional 22 that provide insights on their usage in research analysis and design. The review employs specific inclusion and exclusion criteria for both peer-reviewed and grey literature, as detailed in Table 1. 

Inclusion criteria for peer-reviewed literature involve studies that mention GPT or LLM in relation to research design or data generation and are indexed in reputable sources. Grey literature includes articles from low-code development platforms and those authored by GPT vendors or benchmarking companies. Exclusions encompass tutorial papers, short papers, and any literature prior to 2019, among others.

The analysis reveals significant concerns regarding GPT-based technologies in research authorship, including hallucinations, biases, ethical dilemmas, and the risk of facilitating plagiarism. As a result, the review intentionally excludes GPT-based research authoring from its primary focus.

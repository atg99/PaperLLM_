The paper categorizes existing literature under the theme "Emerging Technologies and Social Impact," detailing various research areas where GPT models are applied. These include:

1. **Social Science Research**: Using GPT to generate synthetic survey responses, which addresses data scarcity but may not fully capture human variability.
2. **Climate Change Research**: Generating synthetic climate reports to enhance understanding of climate impacts, though risks of misrepresentation exist.
3. **Music Recommendation Systems**: Creating synthetic user reviews to improve recommendation accuracy, while concerns about authenticity and bias remain.
4. **Vaccine Hesitancy Monitoring**: Generating anti-vaccination tweets to improve classification of underrepresented classes, but at the cost of decreased precision for common classes.
5. **Task-Oriented Dialogue Systems**: Utilizing paraphrasing techniques to reduce manual data needs while maintaining or enhancing natural language understanding performance.
6. **Multilingual Text Analysis**: Detecting genre and persuasion techniques effectively in a multilingual context, though limited by translation accuracy.

The analysis emphasizes that GPT-based technologies facilitate advanced data analysis through prompts, enhancing researchers' ability to recognize patterns and solve complex problems. Notably, the Chat2VIS system exemplifies how these models can transform natural language queries into data visualizations, demonstrating a significant advancement over traditional methods. Overall, the research underscores the transformative potential of GPT technologies across various fields, while also acknowledging their limitations and challenges.

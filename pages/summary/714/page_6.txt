In this appendix, the authors provide a proof of Lemma 4.1, which establishes a relationship between the gradient of the objective function J?(θ) and the weighted distribution of the value function Vθ over state distributions. They hypothesize that the gradient can be expressed as a weighted sum of the partial derivatives of Vθ with respect to θ, specifically:

∇J?(θ) = Σ_s dθγ(s) ∂/∂θ Vθγ(s),

where dθγ(s) is defined as a weighted distribution over states. The authors derive this expression by substituting previously established results regarding the partial derivative of the value function into the equation for the gradient.

The proof involves several steps, including the manipulation of probability terms and expectations. Ultimately, they show that by appropriately choosing weights w(t), specifically w(t) = 1 for t=0 and w(t) = 1-γ for t>0, the summation condition required for the gradient to hold true is satisfied.

The conclusion reached is that this choice of w(t) leads to the desired equality, thereby confirming that:

∇J?(θ) = E[ Σ_i=0^∞ Qθγ(Si, Ai) ψ(Si, Ai) | θ ],

which completes the proof.

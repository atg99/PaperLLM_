This paper presents Pyramid Graph Networks (PGN) with connection attentions to enhance region-based one-shot semantic segmentation. The authors, Chi Zhang and colleagues, propose a novel framework that leverages graph networks to capture relationships among different regions in an image. The connection attentions mechanism allows the model to focus on relevant areas, thereby improving segmentation accuracy with limited labeled data. The study builds on previous works in semantic segmentation and open-set recognition, integrating concepts from recent advancements in neural network architectures. The results demonstrate that the proposed PGN outperforms existing methods, highlighting its effectiveness for applications requiring efficient segmentation from minimal examples.

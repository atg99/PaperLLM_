The paper discusses the collection and engineering of data relevant to predicting Bitcoin prices, highlighting the significance of both endogenous and exogenous factors influencing these prices. Key features selected for analysis include Bitcoin price, daily lag returns, price volatility, and various economic indicators such as U.S. treasury yields and gold prices. The study also considers the role of internet search data and the price of Ripple (XRP) as influential factors.

The authors emphasize the importance of moving averages in forecasting future prices and provide a comprehensive dataset consisting of 3469 observations from 2010 to 2019. Data pre-processing involved merging datasets, normalizing values, and establishing trading day conventions. The structured approach aims to enhance the predictive accuracy of Bitcoin price models by leveraging a diverse range of influencing factors.

The appendix provides a comprehensive definition of various variables used in the analysis of Bitcoin and its market dynamics. Key features include Bitcoin price and its volatility, miner revenue, transaction volume, transaction fees, and hash rate, with data sourced from Bitcoin charts and Quandl. Other variables include the money supply, Metcalfe's Law application to Bitcoin's network value, block size, Google search trends, and VIX index for market volatility. Additionally, it examines the price levels of gold, the US dollar index, and US bond yields along with their implications for economic conditions. The appendix also includes technical indicators like MACD and external factors such as Ripple price, S&P 500 market returns, and interest rates, all crucial for understanding Bitcoin's financial landscape. References include a study by Baek and Elbeck discussing Bitcoin's investment potential.

The paper discusses the application of Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks in algorithmic trading, highlighting their advantages over traditional neural networks and Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs). Traditional neural networks struggle to effectively utilize prior information for future predictions, and RNNs are limited by their inability to learn long-term dependencies due to issues like vanishing gradients. 

LSTMs, introduced by Hochreiter and Schmidhuber, are a specialized form of RNNs designed to overcome these limitations. They maintain an internal state memory and utilize a structure comprising input, forget, and output gates that regulate the flow of information. The forget gate determines what information to discard, the input gate updates the cell state with relevant data, and the output gate generates the new hidden state for subsequent steps. This architecture makes LSTMs particularly suitable for tasks involving time-series forecasting.

The methodology section of the paper emphasizes the implementation of LSTMs to assess the profitability of two simple trading strategies. The process involves using the gates to manage information retention and flow, which is crucial for predicting market trends effectively. Overall, the paper highlights the potential of LSTMs in enhancing the performance of algorithmic trading systems.

This study investigates Bitcoin price prediction using a comprehensive set of financial indicators, including volatility, order book data, technical indicators, gold prices, and the VIX. It emphasizes the potential of Bitcoin as an alternative investment offering diversification benefits, particularly during periods when traditional investments underperform. The research employs feature engineering to identify predictor variables useful in forecasting Bitcoin price trends.

The analysis highlights the importance of U.S. treasury yields for short-term and long-term investment decisions, suggesting that shifts in these benchmarks may drive investors toward Bitcoin. Additionally, the study finds that recurrent neural network models, specifically Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) and Gated Recurrent Units (GRU), outperform traditional machine learning models in predicting Bitcoin prices. The GRU model, noted for its simplicity and efficiency in managing non-linear data patterns, demonstrated superior performance in the analysis.

Investment strategies developed using the trained GRU architecture show promise for enhancing financial gains and improving portfolio management. The research underscores the effectiveness of machine learning models in price prediction, advocating for their use in managing portfolio risk and minimizing financial losses. However, it suggests that larger datasets could significantly enhance model performance and proposes future studies to explore the potential of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) in predicting Bitcoin prices.

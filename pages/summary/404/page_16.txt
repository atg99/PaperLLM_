The paper discusses advancements in solar cell surface defect detection using various YOLO architectures. It highlights the limitations of human-led inspection and proposes improved automated methods. Key improvements include the integration of deformable convolution, attention mechanisms, and enhanced anchor box determination through K-means++ clustering. The results show significant improvements in mean Average Precision (mAP) and detection speeds, suggesting these methods are well-suited for real-time applications in photovoltaic systems.

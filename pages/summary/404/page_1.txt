This paper by Muhammad Hussain reviews the evolution of the YOLO (You Only Look Once) object detection framework from its inception in 2015 to the latest version, YOLO-v8, released in January 2023. The YOLO variants are characterized by their ability to perform real-time object detection with high accuracy while using limited computational resources. This efficiency is crucial for applications in automated quality inspection, particularly in industrial surface defect detection, where fast detection and high accuracy are essential.

The review outlines the architectural advancements of each YOLO version and discusses their practical applications in the manufacturing sector, emphasizing the framework's compatibility with industrial requirements for quality inspection. The paper highlights the significance of computer vision, underpinned by deep learning and convolutional neural networks, in enabling machines to replicate human-like visual processing for object detection tasks.

Overall, the paper positions YOLO as a vital tool in the ongoing automation of the manufacturing industry, particularly in enhancing quality control processes through effective surface defect detection.

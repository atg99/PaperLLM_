The paper discusses anticipated advancements in the YOLO (You Only Look Once) architecture for machine learning, particularly focusing on its application in constrained deployment scenarios that prioritize energy conservation while ensuring high inference rates. It highlights the potential for YOLO architectures to be integrated into production facilities for quality inspection and innovative product development, as exemplified by an automated pallet racking inspection solution. The integration of YOLO into various hardware and IoT devices is expected to open new domains for computer vision, enhancing existing processes with minimal resource requirements. The research was conducted without external funding, and the authors declare no conflicts of interest.

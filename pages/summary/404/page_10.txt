The paper discusses advancements in the YOLO (You Only Look Once) object detection framework, focusing particularly on YOLO-v5 and YOLO-v6. YOLO-v2 introduced the anchor box mechanism using k-means to select anchor boxes that closely match the dimensions of ground truth boxes in the training set. However, this method proved inadequate for unique datasets with different object characteristics, as the predefined boxes struggled to adapt. In response, YOLO-v5 integrated an automated anchor box learning process into its pipeline, allowing the network to learn the best-fit anchor boxes for specific datasets during training. This adaptation accelerates the training process.

YOLO-v5 offers multiple variants—YOLO-v5s, YOLO-v5m, YOLO-v5l, and YOLO-v5x—with varying average precision, parameters, and computational demands, as detailed in Table 1. The framework was notably the first in the YOLO family to be implemented in PyTorch, facilitating production use and mobile deployment due to its established ecosystem.

Furthermore, YOLO-v6, released in June 2022 by the Meituan Technical Team, aims to cater to industrial applications by providing a range of performance options. It includes variants from YOLO-v6-nano, which prioritizes speed and efficiency, to YOLO-v6-large, which offers higher accuracy but at the cost of speed, adapting to various hardware requirements and application needs.

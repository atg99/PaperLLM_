The referenced papers cover a range of topics in natural language processing and information retrieval. Key contributions include improved experimental reporting methods (Dodge et al., 2019) and advancements in hierarchical neural story generation (Fan et al., 2018). Kaplan et al. (2020) explore scaling laws for neural language models, while Kudo and Richardson (2018) introduce SentencePiece, a tokenizer for language-independent text processing.

Nogueira and Cho (2019) focus on passage re-ranking and multi-stage document ranking utilizing BERT, alongside methods for query expansion (Nogueira et al., 2019). Padaki et al. (2020) rethink query expansion strategies for BERT reranking.

Further studies include zero-shot text classification with generative models (Puri and Catanzaro, 2019) and the exploration of transfer learning limits with a unified text-to-text transformer (Raffel et al., 2019). Historical context is provided by Robertson et al. (1994) and Voorhees (2004) through TREC conference proceedings.

Yang et al. (2017) discuss the Anserini toolkit for information retrieval research, while Yang et al. (2019) critically examine the effectiveness of neural ranking models. Finally, Yilmaz et al. (2019) investigate cross-domain modeling for document retrieval, contributing to the ongoing development of evidence-based retrieval techniques.

The paper discusses the potential of computerized software-based assessment of lung involvement in COVID-19 through radiological methods, particularly CT imaging, to aid in routine patient management. It highlights the importance of evaluating the distribution and severity of COVID-19 pneumonia. The study was approved by the institutional review board of "AORN Giuseppe Moscati," and informed consent was waived due to the retrospective nature of the research, which utilized anonymized data without posing risks to patients. The authors declare no conflicts of interest. The article is open access, licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License, allowing for use and distribution with appropriate credit. 

Key references are included, providing context on COVID-19 and its clinical features, the role of imaging in diagnosis, and integrated algorithms for managing the pandemic. The paper emphasizes the utility of CT imaging in quantifying lung conditions and monitoring changes over time in COVID-19 patients.

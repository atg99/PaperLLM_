The paper outlines key learning objectives aimed at enhancing understanding and dialogue within multidisciplinary teams regarding the use of formal optimization approaches, particularly evolutionary algorithms (EAs), in environmental decision-making. The objectives include:

1. Recognizing the advantages of EAs for decision support in environmental modeling.
2. Understanding the formulation and resolution of environmental issues as optimization problems.
3. Gaining insight into the functioning of single- and multi-objective EAs.
4. Learning the practical implementation of EAs.
5. Identifying potential challenges in the application of EAs.

The paper assumes that readers possess a background in mathematics, environmental modeling, and the calibration of environmental models.

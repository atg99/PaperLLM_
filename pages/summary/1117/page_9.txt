In summary, the paper discusses various water resource management problems, including long-term water resources planning, reservoir operation, wastewater treatment, and model calibration. Each problem involves specific decision-making regarding supply capacities, water release schedules, treatment levels, and parameter values for model calibration. The effectiveness of selected solutions is assessed using environmental simulation models tailored to each issue, such as hydraulic models for water distribution, river basin planning models for long-term planning, integrated models for reservoir operations, and water quality models for wastewater treatment. The optimization process is iterative, relying on the evaluation of initial solutions and adjustments based on performance outcomes, domain knowledge, and experience to enhance the selected options while balancing costs, emissions, and operational constraints.

This collection of references highlights significant contributions to the field of water resources management and modeling. Razavi et al. (2012) provide a comprehensive review of surrogate modeling techniques, emphasizing their utility in water resource applications. In their 2010 study, Razavi et al. explore methods to reduce computational costs in automatic calibration through model preemption.

Reddy and Kumar (2006) present a multi-objective evolutionary algorithm for optimal reservoir operation, showcasing advancements in optimization techniques. Reed et al. (2013) discuss the evolution of evolutionary multi-objective optimization in water resources, marking its past achievements and future directions.

Reis et al. (2015) focus on integrating modeling with smart sensors to enhance environmental and human health monitoring. Rossman (2000) offers a user manual for EPANET 2, a widely used software for modeling water distribution systems. 

Samani and Mottaghi (2006) employ integer linear programming for optimizing water distribution networks, while Shin et al. (2015) review foundational methods for checking model structural identifiability, particularly for rainfall-runoff scenarios.

Singh (2014) examines groundwater resource management through simulation modeling, emphasizing its role in sustainable practices. Smith et al. (2017) propose a participatory framework, ParFAIT, to enhance the relevance of decision support tools in water management.

Smith et al. (2015) utilize many-objective analysis to optimize operations in multi-reservoir systems, reflecting the complexity of modern water management challenges. Sörensen (2013) critiques metaheuristics, providing insights into their application in operational research.

Stokes et al. (2014a, 2015a, 2014b, and 2015b) investigate the nexus of cost and greenhouse gas emissions in water distribution systems, developing integrated frameworks and computational tools for minimizing environmental impacts. Finally, Stützle and Hoos (2000) introduce the MAX-MIN ant system, contributing to the development of optimization algorithms.

Overall, these works collectively address critical aspects of water resource management, optimization, and the integration of technology for improved decision-making and sustainability.

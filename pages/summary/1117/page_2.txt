The paper provides an overview of the use of optimization techniques, specifically evolutionary algorithms and other metaheuristics, in enhancing environmental decision-making processes. It highlights the limitations of manual evaluations of design, planning, operational, management, and policy options due to the vast number of possibilities. By integrating environmental models with optimization algorithms, decision-makers can identify the most effective options that utilize limited resources, achieve optimal environmental outcomes within budget constraints, and balance competing objectives. The overview discusses how environmental challenges can be framed as optimization problems and explores both single- and multi-objective optimization strategies to derive effective solutions. Additionally, it offers practical guidance and addresses potential challenges associated with implementing these optimization methods in environmental contexts.

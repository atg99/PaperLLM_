This collection of references outlines significant contributions to the fields of optimization, environmental modeling, and decision-making processes. Key themes include the development of cloud-based optimization services, the integration of exploratory modeling tools, and the application of evolutionary algorithms in water resource management.

Kurschl et al. discuss requirements for cloud-based optimization services, emphasizing the potential for improved decision-making. Kwakkel introduces the Exploratory Modeling Workbench, a toolkit for scenario discovery and robust decision-making, highlighting its open-source nature. Laniak et al. present a vision for integrated environmental modeling, advocating for a cohesive approach to environmental challenges.

Liebman reflects on the role of optimization in public systems, suggesting that optimization strategies can enhance decision-making. Liner and Maier provide foundational knowledge on optimization and operations research relevant to infrastructure professionals. Liu et al. assess green infrastructure's effectiveness in urban flooding reduction, indicating the importance of ecological considerations.

Lozano and García-Martínez review hybrid metaheuristics, illustrating advancements in optimization techniques. Maier and colleagues address the complexities of uncertainty and robustness in environmental modeling, stressing the integration of different methodologies for effective adaptation strategies.

Further, Mala-Jetmarova et al. conduct a literature review on the optimization of water distribution systems, while Matott et al. evaluate uncertainty in integrated environmental models. McJeon et al. analyze the implications of increased natural gas use on climate change, suggesting limited long-term effects.

McPhail et al. investigate robustness metrics, providing insights into their calculation and application. Lastly, Azamathulla et al. compare genetic algorithms to linear programming in real-time operations, demonstrating varying approaches to optimization in water management.

This body of work collectively underlines the vital intersection of optimization techniques, environmental considerations, and decision-making frameworks, shaping future research and practical applications in these domains.

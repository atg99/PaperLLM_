This paper discusses the parallelization of evolutionary algorithms (EAs) using distributed computing, emphasizing the benefits and challenges associated with synchronous and asynchronous operational modes. Synchronous operation assigns fixed tasks to processors, which can lead to bottlenecks due to varying computation times among tasks. In contrast, asynchronous operation designates managers to allocate tasks dynamically, reducing idle time for worker processors at the expense of increased communication overhead.

The integration of EAs into decision support systems and modeling frameworks is gaining traction, necessitating adaptable systems that can utilize various programming languages and libraries while leveraging the parallel nature of EAs. Future trends indicate that cloud computing is paving the way for optimization as a service (OaaS), allowing for flexible and efficient EA applications in complex problem-solving scenarios. Moreover, ongoing research is probing the potential of EAs in exascale computing environments and the development of hybrid algorithms that combine different evolutionary strategies for improved problem-solving capabilities.

The paper discusses various applications and methodologies involving Bayesian networks in environmental modeling and decision-making. It highlights the versatility of Bayesian networks in addressing complex ecological issues, risk assessment, and uncertainty analysis across different domains. Key contributions include the integration of spatial and temporal data, the development of frameworks for participatory decision-making, and the modeling of ecological systems under uncertainty. The paper emphasizes the importance of Bayesian networks in enhancing data interpretation and improving decision support systems in environmental management. It also reviews different algorithms, software tools, and case studies that illustrate the practical applications of Bayesian networks in real-world scenarios. Overall, the research underscores the potential of Bayesian approaches to inform effective environmental policies and management strategies.

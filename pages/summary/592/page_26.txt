This paper provides a systematic review of deep learning-based unsupervised domain adaptation (UDA) approaches. It highlights the advancements of deep learning over traditional methods across various fields and emphasizes the importance of effectively transferring models from source domains to unseen target domains. The authors present a comprehensive summary of recent UDA techniques, discussing their advantages and limitations. Additionally, they review successful applications of these methods and identify several challenges that current UDA approaches face, suggesting these as potential areas for future research. The paper concludes by acknowledging the need for further exploration into the relationship between out-of-distribution samples and adversarial attack samples.

The list includes various research papers authored or co-authored by X. Liu and colleagues, focusing on advancements in semantic segmentation, domain adaptation, and recognition technologies. Key topics include:

1. Importance-aware semantic segmentation for self-driving cars, employing discrete Wasserstein training methods.
2. Domain generalization techniques under conditional and label shifts using variational Bayesian inference.
3. Energy-constrained self-training approaches for unsupervised domain adaptation.
4. Severity-aware semantic segmentation utilizing reinforced Wasserstein training.
5. Identity-aware facial expression recognition in compressed video formats, with a focus on mutual information regularization.
6. Ordinal unsupervised domain adaptation techniques through recursively conditional Gaussian imposed variational disentanglement.
7. Feature-level approaches for eliminating variations in discriminative recognition tasks.
8. Subtype-aware unsupervised domain adaptation specifically targeting medical diagnosis applications.

These contributions highlight the innovative methodologies and applications developed to enhance machine learning and computer vision capabilities across various domains.

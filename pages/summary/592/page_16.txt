The paper discusses advancements in image generation and medical image analysis, emphasizing the application of generative models in various fields such as entertainment and data augmentation. Image style synthesis is viewed as a cross-domain translation task, with GAN-based methods being prominent in this area. Liu et al. implemented self-training for image synthesis, focusing on uncertainties and utilizing paired images from different sources. Evaluation of these methods includes MRI translations across multiple centers.

In medical image analysis, deep learning techniques have been increasingly adopted to enhance computational efficiency and performance. Unsupervised domain adaptation (UDA) has shown promise in tasks like segmentation and classification. Perone et al. demonstrated improved model generalization in semantic segmentation using self-ensembling with MRI datasets, while Ouyang et al. utilized a VAE-based approach for multi-domain segmentation. Zou et al. introduced a Dual-Scheme Fusion Network to bridge domain differences in segmentation tasks for brain tumors and cardiac images. Other studies also explored cross-device and cross-modality segmentation, highlighting the versatility and effectiveness of these advanced methods in real-life medical scenarios.

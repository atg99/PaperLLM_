This collection of references encompasses various studies focused on unsupervised domain adaptation techniques and their applications across different fields, particularly in image processing and machine learning. Liu et al. (2022) explore unsupervised domain adaptation for segmentation using a black-box source model, while Liu et al. (2019) present a conservative Wasserstein training approach for pose estimation. Additional contributions include data augmentation methods via latent space interpolation (Liu et al., 2018) and deep adaptation networks for learning transferable features (Long et al., 2015).

Several papers delve into conditional adversarial domain adaptation (Long et al., 2018) and joint adaptation networks (Long et al., 2017), highlighting the evolution of techniques for improving model performance across domains. Other notable works include intelligent fault diagnosis methods based on unsupervised domain adaptation (Lu et al., 2021) and classification of satellite image time series (Lucas et al., 2020). The references collectively underscore the significance of domain adaptation strategies in enhancing model robustness and accuracy in various applications, including medical image classification and agricultural yield prediction.

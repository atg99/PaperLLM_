The paper discusses the therapeutic role of mechanical devices, particularly robots, in enhancing social interactions and providing support in various domains. It highlights the benefits of robots for children who have experienced trauma and emphasizes the importance of the social dimension in human-robot interactions. For individuals with physical disabilities, robots offer unique opportunities for physical therapy by delivering prescribed forces and movement trajectories to improve flexibility and strength. Intelligent wheelchairs equipped with external sensors aid in path planning and collision avoidance.

Additionally, the paper presents a robotic system designed for rehabilitation, showcasing its capability to perform physiotherapy exercises with precision, thus combining human decision-making with robotic accuracy for improved musculoskeletal rehabilitation outcomes.

In the context of the COVID-19 pandemic, the paper outlines two cases of human-robot cooperation in hospitals. One involves robots assisting in the testing process for coronavirus by handling nasopharyngeal swabs, while the other describes mobile robots delivering food, medicine, and measuring patients' temperatures to minimize direct contact and reduce infection risks.

The paper also explores human-robot interaction (HRI) in agriculture, detailing how robots assist in various farming tasks such as harvesting, seeding, and weed detection. It emphasizes the need for safety in HRI, particularly in precision agriculture where robots aid in tasks like strawberry picking and melon harvesting. The concept of Scaffold Mode is introduced, where human operators and robots collaborate effectively, significantly increasing efficiency in tasks such as tree trimming compared to traditional methods.

The manuscript discusses the various applications of Human-Robot Interaction (HRI) across different sectors. It highlights the role of robots in industrial settings, such as picking, welding, assembling, and painting. Assistive robotics is emphasized for its significance in aiding individuals with physical and mental disabilities, facilitating interaction and therapy. The paper notes the growing importance of HRI in healthcare, particularly in combating COVID-19. Additionally, it explores HRI's contributions in agriculture, including tasks like harvesting and weed detection, as well as its presence in service industries, home use, inventory management, mining, education, and space exploration.

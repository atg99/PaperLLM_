This paper references a range of influential works that contribute to advancements in image recognition, data augmentation, and deep learning techniques. Key contributions include the introduction of region proposal networks for object detection, as presented by Ren et al. in 2016, and the comprehensive review of the ImageNet challenge by Russakovsky et al. in 2015, which highlights the scale and impact of large-scale visual recognition tasks.

The paper also discusses improvements in generative adversarial networks (GANs) explored by Salimans et al. in 2016 and surveys on image data augmentation methods by Shorten and Khoshgoftaar in 2019. Techniques such as the "Hide-and-seek" data augmentation method introduced by Singh et al. in 2018 and "Cutmix" by Yun et al. in 2019 are highlighted for their effectiveness in weakly-supervised localization.

Additionally, it reviews advancements in reinforcement learning as outlined by Sutton and Barto in 2018, and the use of manifold mixup for better representation learning by Verma et al. in 2019. The effectiveness of data augmentation in deep learning for image classification is discussed through works by Wang et al. in 2017 and 2020, providing insights into face data augmentation strategies.

Furthermore, the paper references significant contributions to scene parsing networks by Zhao et al. in 2017 and random erasing data augmentation strategies by Zhong et al. in 2020. The use of cycle-consistent adversarial networks for unpaired image-to-image translation is also noted through the work of Zhu et al. in 2017.

Overall, these references collectively underscore the critical developments in the fields of computer vision and deep learning, emphasizing the importance of innovative techniques in enhancing model performance and representation.

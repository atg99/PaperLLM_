This collection of references highlights significant advancements in deep learning, particularly in computer vision. The works covered include foundational principles, innovative techniques, and various applications of deep learning models. Notably, Hassaballah and Awad provide a comprehensive overview of deep learning in computer vision, while He et al. introduce deep residual learning, which has become a cornerstone for image recognition tasks. 

Data augmentation methods are also a recurring theme, with studies by Hendrycks et al. and Inoue exploring techniques to enhance model robustness and classification performance. The concept of feature normalization and its impact on data augmentation is discussed by Li et al., further emphasizing the importance of preprocessing in deep learning workflows. 

Pivotal contributions like those from Khosla and Saini survey the effects of diverse augmentation techniques, while Lim et al. present Fast AutoAugment, a method to automate the augmentation process efficiently. The references collectively illustrate the evolution of deep learning methodologies and their application to complex tasks such as image segmentation, object detection, and image-to-image translation, showcasing the field's dynamic nature and ongoing research efforts.

The paper presents a study on inter-chain contact prediction using CDPred, focusing on its precision and average probability across different datasets. Figure 4 illustrates the relationship between the precision of the top L/5 inter-chain contact predictions and their average probability, with each data point representing a dimer target from four test datasets: HomoTest1, HomoTest2, HeteroTest1, and HeteroTest2. In Figure 5, the authors analyze the prediction results for the homodimer T0991, showcasing the predicted intra-chain distance map from AlphaFold alongside the true distance map, as well as the predicted and actual inter-chain contact maps. This comparative analysis highlights the effectiveness and accuracy of CDPred in predicting inter-chain interactions within protein structures.

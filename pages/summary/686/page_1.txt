The paper presents CDPred, a deep learning method designed to predict inter-chain residue-residue distance maps for protein complexes, addressing a notable gap in existing methods that primarily focus on intra-chain predictions. Utilizing a 2D attention-based residual network, CDPred demonstrates superior performance on both homodimer and heterodimer datasets. The method achieves precision rates of 60.94% and 42.93% for top L/5 inter-chain contact predictions in homodimers, significantly outpacing other methods such as DeepHomo and GLINTER. For heterodimers, CDPred also surpasses GLINTER with precision rates of 47.59% and 22.87%. Additionally, the predictions from CDPred complement those from AlphaFold2-multimer, indicating its potential utility in enhancing structural predictions for protein complexes. The study emphasizes the critical role of accurate inter-chain distance mapping in understanding protein function, particularly in multi-chain structures, and highlights the advancements made in deep learning applications for protein structure prediction.

The paper presents a comparative analysis of various registration methods for medical imaging, specifically focusing on the performance of LKU-Net, a U-Net based architecture. In non-diffeomorphic registration, LKU-Net outperforms other methods with a Dice score of 0.752, surpassing competitors such as nnFormer, TransMorph, and VoxelMorph. Additionally, enhancing LKU-Net by increasing the number of channels yields an even better score of 0.757. For diffeomorphic registration, LKU-Net also shows strong performance with scores of 0.746 and 0.753 in different configurations, significantly exceeding TransMorph's score of 0.599.

The paper further evaluates LKU-Net on the validation set of the MICCAI Learn2Reg 2021 challenge, where it achieves a Dice score of 0.8861, outperforming the previously leading methods, including TransMorph-LC and LapIRN. The authors conclude that LKU-Net demonstrates the competitive capability of U-Net architectures in the context of both inter-subject and atlas-to-subject registration tasks. They also indicate plans to extend LKU-Net for multi-modality deformable image registration in future work.

The paper presents the performance of AutoGAN in unconditional image generation tasks, particularly on the CIFAR-10 and STL-10 datasets. In Table 1, AutoGAN achieves a state-of-the-art FID score of 12.42, outperforming various existing methods while maintaining a competitive inception score of 8.55. The results indicate that AutoGAN's architecture can successfully generate high-quality images, as demonstrated by the generated CIFAR-10 results shown in Figure 4.

To assess the transferability of the discovered architecture, the authors retrain the AutoGAN model on the STL-10 dataset, which has higher image resolution than CIFAR-10. Despite slightly lower inception scores compared to some handcrafted GANs, AutoGAN achieves the best FID score of 31.01, as detailed in Table 2. The visual results from STL-10 further highlight the model's effectiveness.

The paper also discusses the validation of proxy tasks, which are designed to enhance training efficiency by directly evaluating inception scores on a child model of the shared GAN. This approach significantly accelerates the training process, affirming the method's effectiveness in image generation tasks. Future work is anticipated to refine the architecture search for even better results on STL-10.

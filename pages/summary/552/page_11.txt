The paper discusses advancements in neural architecture search (NAS), particularly focusing on the BayesNAS approach. It highlights various methodologies and algorithms that have contributed to the evolution of image classifiers. Key contributions include the use of regularized evolution for architecture search, soft weight-sharing for network compression, and structured sparsity learning. The authors reference significant studies in the field, such as the introduction of convolutional neural fabrics, genetic CNNs, and stochastic NAS. The paper emphasizes the importance of efficient neural network architectures for mobile devices and scalable image recognition, showcasing the ongoing efforts to improve performance through innovative search techniques. Overall, the research underscores the potential of combining Bayesian principles with NAS to enhance the design and efficiency of deep learning models.

The paper presents a dependency-based strategy for estimating performance in a one-shot manner. It defines a criterion for identifying redundant connections, encapsulated in Proposition 1. This proposition states that information flow from one node to another exists if at least one predecessor's operation is non-zero, and the connection weight is also non-zero. Conversely, if all predecessors are zero, the connection is deemed redundant.

The authors propose a probabilistic framework to encode these dependencies, allowing for the encoding of redundancy in connection weights. They suggest using Gaussian distributions over the weights to express uncertainty and confidence, thus facilitating the identification of sparse solutions. The method aims to leverage the probabilistic nature of the encoding to promote sparsity in the network's structure while ensuring that the logic of dependencies is maintained.

Overall, the paper outlines a novel approach to optimizing neural architecture search by integrating dependency logic with probabilistic modeling, which can potentially enhance the efficiency and performance of deep learning models.

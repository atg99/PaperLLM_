This method integrates a multi-stage attention mechanism with a Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM)-based Convolutional Neural Network (CNN), which processes sensor-generated data streams to identify anomalies caused by faults, errors, or cyberattacks. The study also introduces a weight-adjusted fine-tuned ensemble method (WAVED) that enhances the anomaly detection performance by averaging the predicted probabilities from multiple classifiers.

The experimental results reveal that the MSALSTM-CNN method achieves an increase of up to 2.54% in F-score for different single anomaly types and up to 3.24% for mixed anomaly types, demonstrating its effectiveness compared to state-of-the-art methods. The study highlights the importance of accurate anomaly detection in improving the safety and functionality of CAVs, particularly in scenarios involving erroneous data readings that could disrupt critical operations. Overall, the research addresses existing limitations in current anomaly detection methods and offers a robust solution for ensuring the reliability of data in intelligent transportation systems.

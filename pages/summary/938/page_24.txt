3. Applications in Quantum State and Phase Discovery
Another significant avenue of research involves using NQS for discovering new quantum states and phases. This includes employing machine learning techniques to identify phase transitions and characterize quantum states that are not easily accessible through traditional methods. By leveraging the expressive power of NQS, researchers can probe complex quantum systems and potentially uncover novel phenomena.

Overall, the paper discusses the potential of neural quantum states (NQS) in representing many-body wave functions and their applications in understanding quantum systems, particularly focusing on the challenges posed by fermionic symmetry and the theoretical and practical advancements in this field. The exploration of representation theory, learning from data, and applications in quantum state discovery are highlighted as key areas of ongoing research.

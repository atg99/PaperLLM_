The references provided encompass a range of studies focusing on explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) and its applications in natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning. Key contributions include:

1. **Quint** (Abujabal et al., 2017) proposes an interpretable question-answering framework over knowledge bases.
2. **Survey on XAI** (Adadi and Berrada, 2018) reviews methodologies for understanding AI systems.
3. **Speech Emotion Recognition** (Aldeneh and Mower Provost, 2017) utilizes regional saliency for recognizing emotions in speech.
4. **Causal Framework** (Alvarez-Melis and Jaakkola, 2017) introduces methods for explaining predictions from black-box models.
5. **MathQA** (Amini et al., 2019) aims for interpretable solutions to math word problems using operation-based techniques.
6. **AI Explainability Toolkit** (Arya et al., 2019) develops a taxonomy and toolkit for various AI explainability techniques.
7. **Politeness Comprehension** (Aubakirova and Bansal, 2016) interprets neural networks to enhance understanding of politeness.
8. **CMU-MOSEI Dataset** (Bagher Zadeh et al., 2018) focuses on multimodal language analysis and interpretable dynamic fusion.
9. **Neural Machine Translation** (Bahdanau et al., 2015) discusses aligning and translating in a unified model.
10. **Emoji Prediction** (Barbieri et al., 2018) employs label-wise attention in LSTMs for interpretable predictions.
11. **Interpretability Costs** (Bertsimas et al., 2019) examines the trade-offs involved in achieving model interpretability.
12. **Active Learning for Named Entities** (Bhutani et al., 2018) explores structured representation in entity recognition.
13. **Adversarial Networks** (Carton et al., 2018) develop methods for extracting explanations related to personal attacks in social media.
14. **Survey on Interpretability** (Carvalho et al., 2019) assesses various methods and metrics for machine learning interpretability.
15. **Kernel-Based Explanatory Models** (Croce et al., 2018; 2019) focus on explaining decisions in non-linear classifiers and auditing deep learning processes.
16. **Neural Programmer-Interpreter** (Dong et al., 2019) presents a model for sentence simplification via explicit editing.

These works collectively advance the understanding and practical implementation of interpretable AI systems across diverse applications.

This paper provides a comprehensive overview of the state of Explainable AI (XAI) within the field of Natural Language Processing (NLP). It highlights the tension between the advancements in model quality, particularly through black box techniques like deep learning, and the resulting decrease in model interpretability. The authors argue that this lack of transparency can undermine user trust in AI systems.

The survey categorizes explanations based on whether they pertain to individual predictions or the overall prediction process, and whether they require post-processing for generation. It identifies various techniques for generating and visualizing explanations, offering a resource for NLP model developers. The study also discusses the evaluation methods used to assess the quality of explanations.

Finally, the authors point out existing gaps in the current research and suggest potential directions for future exploration in the area of XAI for NLP. This survey is notable for being the first to focus exclusively on the interplay between explainability and NLP, emphasizing the need for tailored approaches in each discipline.

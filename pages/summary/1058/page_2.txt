The survey aims to provide a comprehensive overview of explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) approaches specifically within the natural language processing (NLP) domain. It contrasts earlier surveys by focusing on recent advancements and their effectiveness in achieving explainability. The authors categorize explanations into local and global, as well as self-explaining and post-hoc types, highlighting that the majority of recent NLP works utilize the same neural networks for both predictions and explanations. 

Through a systematic methodology, relevant papers were identified and classified to ensure consistency, with an emphasis on local explanations. The survey also addresses the gaps in formal definitions and evaluation methods for explainability. An interactive website has been created to provide additional resources and insights on the covered papers, encouraging further exploration of explainability techniques within the research community.

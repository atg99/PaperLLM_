The paper presents DN-ResNet, a deep convolutional neural network designed for blind image denoising without requiring complete noise statistics. The architecture consists of multiple residual blocks (ResBlocks) and employs cascade training, which enhances both accuracy and computational efficiency compared to existing denoising networks. An edge-aware loss function is implemented during training to improve perceptual quality of the denoised images.

Additionally, the authors introduce a depthwise separable variant, DS-DN-ResNet, which utilizes Depthwise Separable ResBlocks (DS-ResBlocks) to reduce computational costs while maintaining high accuracy. This variant is developed incrementally by replacing standard ResBlocks with DS-ResBlocks.

The study addresses the denoising of images affected by Poisson and additive Gaussian noise, demonstrating that DN-ResNet outperforms state-of-the-art deep learning methods and popular BM3D algorithm variants in both blind and non-blind scenarios. Furthermore, DN-ResNet shows effectiveness in other image enhancement tasks, such as compressed image restoration. The findings highlight DN-ResNet’s efficiency, robustness, and superior performance in image denoising applications.

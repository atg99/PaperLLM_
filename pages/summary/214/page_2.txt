The paper presents a novel Denoising Residual Network (DN-ResNet) aimed at improving the efficiency and accuracy of image denoising, particularly in the context of multiple types of noise, including Gaussian, Poisson, and Poisson-Gaussian. The authors highlight the limitations of existing deep neural network-based denoising methods, such as their large size and difficulty in hyperparameter tuning, which can hinder real-time execution and convergence.

To address these issues, DN-ResNet employs a training strategy that incrementally adds residual blocks (ResBlocks) stage by stage, resulting in faster convergence and enhanced computational efficiency. The paper introduces an edge-aware loss function, which outperforms the conventional mean square error (MSE) in terms of perceptive quality. Additionally, the authors develop a depthwise separable ResBlock (DS-ResBlock) to create the depthwise separable ResNet (DS-DN-ResNet), achieving a 2.5 times reduction in complexity with minimal loss in PSNR.

The key contributions of the paper are: 
1. A unified deep CNN framework for blind denoising of images affected by various noise types.
2. A novel training approach that enhances efficiency and performance in denoising tasks.
3. Demonstration of the applicability of DN-ResNet for compressed image restoration, indicating its potential for broader applications beyond denoising. 

Overall, the proposed DN-ResNet and DS-DN-ResNet models achieve state-of-the-art performance in both non-blind and blind denoising scenarios while maintaining faster processing speeds compared to previous methods.

The paper presents DN-ResNet, an efficient deep residual network designed for image denoising, particularly focusing on blind denoising techniques that do not require noise level estimation. The authors demonstrate that incorporating an edge-aware loss function enhances the structural similarity index (SSIM) by 0.005 to 0.01, while only slightly reducing the peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR). This trade-off is justified, as the mean squared error (MSE) aligns with PSNR metrics. The edge-aware DN-ResNet, which uses Sobel gradient magnitude for edge detection, yields superior perceptual quality, producing sharper edges compared to standard DN-ResNet outputs.

Further evaluations of different residual blocks indicate that the DS-DN-ResNet variant maintains performance with less than 0.1 dB PSNR and less than 0.002 SSIM degradation compared to DN-ResNet, while significantly reducing computational costs and network size. The findings suggest that the incrementally evolved DS-DN-ResNet enhances the efficiency of DN-ResNet without severe accuracy loss. 

In comparisons with state-of-the-art denoising algorithms on the PASCAL VOC dataset, DN-ResNet-13 demonstrates clear superiority over both blind and non-blind Gaussian denoising methods, underscoring its effectiveness in practical applications.

The paper discusses the development of a new denoising network architecture called DS-DN-ResNet, which is an evolution of the existing DN-ResNet. This new architecture achieves a significant reduction in computational cost, specifically 2.5 times less, while maintaining a minimal PSNR loss of less than 0.1 dB. Experimental results indicate that DS-DN-ResNet outperforms current state-of-the-art denoising networks across various noise types, including Gaussian, Poisson, and Poisson-Gaussian, in both blind and non-blind denoising scenarios. Additionally, the architecture is versatile enough to be applied to other image enhancement tasks.

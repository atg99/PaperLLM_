DN-ResNet is a deep residual network specifically designed for image denoising. The architecture includes a novel DS-ResBlock, which is introduced through a process of incremental evolution from a 13-layer DN-ResNet. During this evolution, the weights of the new DS-ResBlock are initialized randomly while the weights of the existing layers are inherited, allowing for effective fine-tuning. This evolution results in a DS-DN-ResNet that has approximately 2.5 times less complexity than the original DN-ResNet, while maintaining a minimal accuracy loss of less than 0.1 dB PSNR.

Additionally, the paper presents an edge-aware loss function that enhances the denoising performance by assigning higher weights to edge pixels compared to non-edge pixels. This approach addresses the challenge of retrieving edges from noisy images, particularly in high noise scenarios. The proposed loss function is structured to incorporate both the standard Mean Square Error (MSE) and an edge-sensitive component, allowing for a more effective denoising process. The use of this edge-aware loss function is expected to improve the quality of the denoised images by focusing on critical features that are often lost in traditional methods.

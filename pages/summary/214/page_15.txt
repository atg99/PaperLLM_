The paper discusses DN-ResNet, an efficient deep residual network designed specifically for image denoising. It highlights the network's capabilities in restoring compressed images, particularly at low bit rates, as demonstrated in the example outputs. The authors reference various foundational works in the field, including practical noise modeling and fitting techniques for single-image raw data, weighted nuclear norm minimization for denoising, and advancements in deep residual learning for image recognition. They also mention the development of efficient convolutional neural networks for mobile applications and propose enhancements to existing deep residual networks for super-resolution tasks. The paper emphasizes the importance of optimal transformations for addressing Poisson-Gaussian noise and evaluates the proposed methods against established image segmentation databases and algorithms. Overall, DN-ResNet offers a significant contribution to the field of image denoising by leveraging deep learning techniques and residual network architectures.

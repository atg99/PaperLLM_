The proposed depthwise separable DN-ResNet (DS-DN-ResNet) significantly optimizes the original DN-ResNet by integrating depthwise separable convolutions into its architecture. This method reduces the computational burden while maintaining performance, as evidenced by the substantial decrease in the number of multiplications from the standard ResBlock to the DS-ResBlock. The paper demonstrates that the computational cost is reduced sixfold, which highlights the efficiency of the proposed structure.

To efficiently transition from DN-ResNet to DS-DN-ResNet, the authors suggest an incremental evolution strategy, replacing ResBlocks one at a time and fine-tuning the model after each change. This method contrasts with a one-shot replacement, which may lead to suboptimal convergence.

Overall, the DS-DN-ResNet model retains the benefits of the original DN-ResNet while improving efficiency and reducing computational costs through innovative architectural modifications.

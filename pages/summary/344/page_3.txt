In June 2020, OpenAI introduced GPT-3, a language model with 175 billion parameters, making it over 100 times larger than its predecessor, GPT-2. Trained on a vast corpus of 499 billion tokens, GPT-3 produces more coherent text than previous models. Access to GPT-3 is provided via a controlled API, with tiered pricing ranging from free to US$400 per month, which has drawn criticism for being high compared to other services. 

OpenAI transitioned to a capped-profit model to attract investment, securing a US$1 billion investment from Microsoft, which later obtained an exclusive license for GPT-3. The media response to GPT-3 has been largely positive, highlighting its impressive text generation capabilities and versatility in various applications, including poetry, chess, and coding. However, while GPT-3 shows significant improvement in fiction generation, it still requires careful prompt refinement to achieve optimal results.

The paper discusses the implications of using GPT-3, particularly in legal contexts where it can convert legal jargon into plain English. While it presents potential benefits, there are concerns about over-reliance on the technology, even when used by knowledgeable professionals. The author emphasizes the importance of being cautious about the technology's misuse, despite the presence of an ethics framework in the AI industry. OpenAI is noted for its commitment to responsible use, implementing constraints to mitigate risks associated with the API's open-ended capabilities. However, the author argues that any application prioritizing the truth of its outputs may not be suitable for GPT-3. The discussion highlights the need for ongoing scrutiny as the technology evolves and its applications expand.

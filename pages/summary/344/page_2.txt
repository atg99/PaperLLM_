The paper discusses the significant impact of the release of GPT-3, an AI language model developed by OpenAI, which has generated considerable media attention and debate regarding its capabilities and implications. It highlights how sensationalist headlines often overshadow the more nuanced discussions found in the articles, which typically address the technology's limitations.

OpenAI was established in 2015 with a mission to ensure that artificial general intelligence benefits humanity. The organization initially released several software tools, but gained widespread attention with the announcement of GPT-2 in 2019, a large language model that produced coherent text but also had the potential for misuse. OpenAI's decision to withhold the full model at first drew criticism, but they eventually released it after a series of smaller models.

The paper emphasizes the importance of understanding the commercial applications of such technologies and raises concerns about potential negative uses that should be discouraged. It suggests that while AI language models like GPT-3 can produce impressive outputs, careful consideration is necessary regarding their deployment and the ethical implications of their use.

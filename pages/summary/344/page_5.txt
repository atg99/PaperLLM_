The paper discusses the limitations of GPT-3, emphasizing its unreliability as a source of truth. While it can produce creative content like fiction and poetry, its application in critical areas such as question-answering or advice-giving poses significant risks due to potential misinformation. The author highlights that GPT-3's credibility is compromised, making it unsuitable for applications requiring accurate information, particularly in health-related contexts. 

Conversely, applications that involve human oversight, such as augmented writing tools, are deemed safer. These tools allow users to edit or reject outputs, minimizing reliance on the AI's accuracy. The paper mentions various writing applications, including email generators and ad copy tools, which exemplify this approach. However, it warns about the dangers of using AI in contexts where users cannot effectively evaluate the outputs, such as machine translation for important documents. Overall, the paper advocates for cautious use of GPT-3, particularly in sensitive areas.

The paper discusses the limitations of transformer technology, particularly focusing on GPT-3. Key concerns include the lack of semantic coherence in outputs, the presence of biases from training data, and the potential for false assertions. The author criticizes instances of cherry-picking outputs, particularly in a case involving The Guardian, where the newspaper edited multiple GPT-3 essays to create a cohesive narrative while neglecting the incoherent nature of some outputs. The paper emphasizes that while GPT-3 can produce correct answers, its reliability is questionable, especially in critical applications where truth is paramount. Experts, including Gary Marcus and OpenAI's CEO Sam Altman, have echoed these concerns, highlighting that despite its impressive capabilities, GPT-3 is not a dependable source of truth.

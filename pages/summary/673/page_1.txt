This paper addresses the gap in sentiment analysis research for the Roman Urdu language, which is spoken by over 64 million people worldwide. The authors propose a deep learning model, specifically a Recurrent Convolutional Neural Network (RCNN), to analyze emotions and attitudes in Roman Urdu text. The study utilizes a corpus of 10,021 sentences sourced from 566 online discussions across various genres, including sports, software, food, drama, and politics.

The research has two primary objectives: first, to create a human-annotated benchmark corpus for Roman Urdu sentiment analysis, and second, to evaluate sentiment analysis techniques using Rule-based, N-gram, and RCNN models. The corpus was annotated by three experts, achieving a Cohen's Kappa score of 0.557 for sentiment classification into positive, negative, and neutral categories.

The authors conducted binary and tertiary classification tests, finding that the RCNN model significantly outperformed the baseline models, achieving an accuracy of 0.652 for binary classification and 0.572 for tertiary classification. This work highlights the potential of deep learning techniques in enhancing sentiment analysis for under-resourced languages like Roman Urdu.

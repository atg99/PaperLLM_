Alfakeeh contributed to validation. The paper presents a detailed analysis of classification models applied to two datasets: the RUSA-19 Corpus and the Roman Urdu UCL dataset. The results are summarized in a table showing accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 scores for different n-gram models (2-gram to 10-gram). 

Key findings include:
- The 2-gram model performed best on the Roman Urdu UCL dataset with an F1 score of 0.593.
- The 4-gram model showed lower performance across both datasets.
- The overall trend indicates that as the n-gram size increases, performance metrics tend to decline.

Figures 4 and 5 compare the precision, recall, and F1 scores for binary and tertiary classification across all techniques, illustrating the effectiveness of different n-gram approaches in classification tasks. The analysis provides insights into the performance of n-gram models in processing Roman Urdu text.

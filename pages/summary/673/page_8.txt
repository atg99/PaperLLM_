The paper discusses the development of a sentiment classification framework for Roman Urdu, highlighting two primary datasets: the RUSA-19 Corpus and the UCL Roman Urdu dataset. The RUSA-19 Corpus is designed for both binary and tertiary classification, containing 5380 positive and negative reviews for binary classification, and 8000 reviews for tertiary classification, which includes positive, negative, and neutral sentiments. The UCL dataset similarly supports both binary and tertiary classifications, comprising 9039 and 16182 reviews respectively for the two classification types. 

Additionally, the paper presents a high-level system architecture for the sentiment classification framework, which outlines the structural components necessary for processing and classifying sentiment in Roman Urdu text. A rule-based technique is also introduced, accompanied by pseudo-code that utilizes a Roman Urdu lexicon to enhance sentiment classification accuracy. Overall, the paper provides a comprehensive overview of the datasets and methodologies used in the sentiment analysis of Roman Urdu.

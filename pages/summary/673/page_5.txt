The paper discusses the challenges of conducting sentiment analysis in Roman Urdu, an under-resourced language characterized by spelling variations and a lack of linguistic resources such as datasets and lexicons. Existing sentiment analysis techniques for other languages are often inapplicable to Roman Urdu due to these issues, including the absence of comprehensive corpora and the predominance of limited genres in available data.

To address these limitations, the authors focus on constructing a new annotated Roman Urdu corpus, named RUSA-19, which includes sentences from five distinct genres: drama and movies, food and recipes, politics, sports, and technology-related content. This corpus aims to classify sentences into positive, negative, and neutral categories using a deep learning model, specifically a Recurrent Convolutional Neural Network (RCNN).

The corpus generation involves several steps, starting with data collection from various online sources that allow user-generated content in Roman Urdu. Data was gathered manually over eight to nine months, and each review was meticulously documented with relevant metadata. Following data collection, the authors established annotation guidelines and performed manual annotations to classify sentiments, ensuring high inter-annotator agreement by involving native Urdu speakers familiar with Roman Urdu.

The paper emphasizes the importance of creating a robust benchmark corpus for sentiment analysis in Roman Urdu, which can facilitate further research and development in natural language processing for this language.

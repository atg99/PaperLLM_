This paper surveys multidimensional projection techniques, focusing on how different algorithms and their parameter settings perform with various high-dimensional datasets. The authors propose a comprehensive benchmarking methodology that encompasses 44 projection techniques, 18 datasets, and 7 quality metrics. This benchmark is designed to facilitate automatic evaluations and includes visualizations for data analysis. 

The primary contribution of this work is the public availability of the methodology, benchmark, datasets, techniques, metrics, visualizations, and code, allowing other researchers to study and enhance the benchmark. The evaluation indicates that t-SNE, UMAP, PBC, and IDMAP rank highest for global average quality. 

Future work will expand the benchmark by incorporating additional dataset traits, visual quality metrics, and machine learning approaches. The aim is to develop new visualization methods to address specific user inquiries about technique performance and dataset characteristics. Ultimately, the authors hope to create a dynamic resource beneficial for the information visualization and machine learning communities.

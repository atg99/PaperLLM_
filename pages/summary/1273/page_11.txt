8.1.3 How does quality depend on dataset types?
The evaluation of projection techniques also considers how different types of datasets affect the quality of projections. By analyzing intrinsic dimensionality and data characteristics, we can identify patterns in performance. Datasets with low intrinsic dimensionality tend to yield better projection quality across various techniques, while higher dimensional datasets often result in lower optimal quality scores. This finding reinforces the importance of selecting appropriate projection methods based on the specific dataset characteristics for optimal results.

In conclusion, the analysis presents a comprehensive framework for evaluating and comparing projection techniques across different datasets. By utilizing both dataset-wise and projection-wise views, the study highlights the performance variations of techniques, the ease of achieving optimal results, and the influence of parameter settings and dataset types on projection quality. This work provides valuable insights for practitioners seeking to select the most effective dimensionality reduction techniques for their data.

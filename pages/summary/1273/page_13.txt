The paper discusses various methods for dimensionality reduction and their effectiveness across different datasets, specifically highlighting the quality of results based on optimal parameters. It presents a dataset-wise analysis that categorizes multiple dimensionality reduction techniques, such as PCA, UMAP, and t-SNE, among others, and evaluates their performance based on specific traits of the datasets, including size, dimensionality, intrinsic dimensionality, and sparsity. The findings are illustrated through histograms that reflect the distribution of quality across runs for each method and dataset, allowing for a comparative view of their effectiveness. The paper underlines the importance of selecting optimal parameters to achieve the best quality outcomes in dimensionality reduction tasks. Further details and discussions on parameters are provided in the appendices.

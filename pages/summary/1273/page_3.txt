The paper reviews existing surveys on dimensionality reduction techniques, highlighting their focus on theoretical frameworks, quality metrics, and practical evaluations. It points out that while many surveys cover a range of DR methods, they often lack side-by-side evaluations and detailed comparisons of quality metrics across datasets. The authors emphasize the need for a systematic approach to evaluate projection methods, considering both theoretical aspects and practical performance metrics.

The findings indicate that while some surveys, like those by Maaten et al. and Nonato and Aupetit, provide substantial insights into DR techniques, there remains a significant gap in empirical validation and comprehensive benchmarking. The paper ultimately aims to fill this gap by proposing a framework for comparing projection techniques quantitatively, thus enhancing understanding and usability in data visualization contexts.

The paper discusses various projection techniques used for dimensionality reduction in the fields of machine learning and information visualization. It provides a comparative analysis of existing surveys, listing techniques such as Autoencoder, Canonical Correlations Analysis, and Principal Component Analysis, among others. The paper includes tables summarizing the number of techniques discussed in each survey, the datasets used for evaluations, and the metrics computed. It highlights the contributions of different surveys to the field, with the author's survey indicating a total of 44 techniques, a significant number compared to prior works. This comprehensive overview seeks to consolidate knowledge on dimensionality reduction methods, facilitating better understanding and application in relevant domains.

This paper presents a novel approach for image generation through a hierarchical vector quantized variational auto-encoder (VQ-VAE) framework. The method is structured into three main components: a hierarchical encoder-decoder, a diverse structure generator, and a texture generator.

The hierarchical encoder (Evq) extracts discrete structural and textural features from images, which are then reconstructed by the decoder (Dvq). The diverse structure generator (Gs) employs an autoregressive network to model the conditional distribution of discrete structural features based on an incomplete input image and its binary mask. The training objective for Gs is defined as the negative log likelihood of these structural features, ensuring a diverse range of outputs during inference without the issues commonly associated with GANs, such as mode collapse.

The texture generator (Gt) synthesizes image textures using the generated structural features, leveraging gated and dilated convolutions to enhance efficiency and quality. The architecture is designed to maintain global information, which is crucial for generating coherent images.

Overall, the proposed method effectively combines hierarchical feature extraction with autoregressive modeling and texture synthesis, addressing challenges in image inpainting and generation while improving output diversity and quality.

The referenced works highlight significant advancements in the field of image inpainting, showcasing a variety of methodologies and techniques developed by different researchers. 

1. **Shift-Net** (Yan et al. 2018) introduces a deep feature rearrangement approach for effective image inpainting.
2. **Contextual Residual Aggregation** (Yi et al. 2020) focuses on ultra high-resolution image inpainting, emphasizing the importance of context in the restoration process.
3. **Generative Image Inpainting with Contextual Attention** (Yu et al. 2018) presents a novel method that utilizes contextual attention to enhance image completion quality.
4. **Free-form Image Inpainting with Gated Convolution** (Yu et al. 2019) explores the use of gated convolutional layers to allow for more flexible inpainting.
5. **Learning Pyramid-Context Encoder Network** (Zeng et al. 2019) proposes a network structure designed to improve the quality of inpainting through a pyramid context approach.
6. **The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Deep Features** (Zhang et al. 2018) discusses the utility of deep features as a perceptual metric, which can be applied in evaluating inpainting results.
7. **UCTGAN** (Zhao et al. 2020) introduces a method for diverse image inpainting via unsupervised cross-space translation.
8. **Pluralistic Image Completion** (Zheng et al. 2019) addresses the challenge of generating multiple plausible completions for a given input.
9. **Learning Oracle Attention** (Zhou et al. 2020) focuses on high-fidelity face completion, utilizing attention mechanisms for improved results.

These contributions collectively advance the field by introducing innovative techniques and frameworks for enhancing image inpainting performance across various contexts and resolutions.

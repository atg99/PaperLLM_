The figures present qualitative comparison results for image completion methods applied to two datasets: CelebA-HQ and Places2. Each figure is organized into groups, displaying images in a specific order: the top image shows the incomplete input, followed by results from various methods including CA, GC, CSA, SF, FE, PIC, UCTGAN, and the proposed method, which is highlighted with a red box. The comparisons aim to visually assess the effectiveness of the different methods in completing the images, with particular emphasis on the performance of the proposed approach relative to existing techniques.

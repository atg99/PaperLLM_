This paper presents a novel texture generator, Gt, designed for image inpainting that effectively utilizes structural features as guidance. The structural features are integrated into both the initial layers of Gt and a specialized structural attention module. This module enhances the synthesis of textures by leveraging the correlations among structural features, ensuring that the generated textures align with the intended structure.

The output of Gt, denoted as Iout, serves as the basis for the final inpainting result, Icomp, where non-masked pixels are replaced with their ground truth values. During training, Gt uses ground truth structural features to reconstruct the image, while during inference, it employs generated structural features.

A key innovation of this work is the structural attention module, which improves upon traditional attention mechanisms by directly calculating attention scores on structural features. This approach addresses the limitations of existing methods that may misinterpret features, resulting in inferior inpainting quality. By modeling long-range correlations more accurately and avoiding the pitfalls of foreground-background separations, the proposed module maintains global consistency in the inpainting results without increasing computational complexity.

The structural attention module comprises two main steps: attention computation, which extracts patches from structural features and calculates truncated distance similarity scores to derive attention scores, and attention transfer, which reconstructs lower-level feature maps using these scores. 

The training of Gt incorporates multiple loss components, including a reconstruction loss, an adversarial loss using an SN-PatchGAN discriminator, and two novel feature losses based on a pre-trained hierarchical encoder. The total loss is a weighted sum of these components, promoting effective learning and high-quality inpainting results.

Overall, the proposed method improves texture synthesis in image inpainting by effectively utilizing structural features and advanced attention mechanisms, leading to better consistency and quality in the completed images.

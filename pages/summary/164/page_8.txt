This paper presents a multiple-solution inpainting method designed to generate diverse and high-quality images through a hierarchical VQ-VAE framework. The authors emphasize the importance of a structural attention module, which ensures that the synthesized textures are consistent with the generated structures. An ablation study is conducted to evaluate the effectiveness of different components, particularly focusing on the structural attention module and auxiliary losses.

The results demonstrate that using full attention on structural features significantly improves the synthesis of realistic textures while maintaining structural coherence. The quantitative comparisons reveal that the proposed method outperforms existing approaches in terms of quality, as indicated by metrics such as SSIM, IS, and FID. Additionally, the introduction of structural and textural feature losses enhances the perceptual quality of the inpainting results, leading to more natural images.

The authors conclude that the structural information provided by the hierarchical VQ-VAE is crucial for enhancing the inpainting task's performance. Future work will aim to extend this method to other conditional image generation tasks, such as style transfer and image super-resolution.

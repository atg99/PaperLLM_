The paper discusses the Seq2Seq model with an attention mechanism, highlighting the iterative process of generating outputs based on hidden states. In the example illustrated, the model transitions from one time step to the next, where the output is influenced by the previous hidden state rather than a placeholder token. The attention mechanism allows the model to assign scores to hidden states, with the highest score indicating the most relevant state for generating the output. This process results in the output "am" being produced in one of the iterations.

Additionally, the paper explores the concept of positional encodings used in transformers, which involve trigonometric functions of varying frequencies. These encodings help represent the position of words in a sequence, with different curves indicating varying levels of significance for each word. The example provided demonstrates how the encoding for individual words is derived from these functions, ensuring that the model retains positional information essential for understanding the sequence.

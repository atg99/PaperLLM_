The paper discusses the Seq2Seq model with an attention mechanism, highlighting its operational steps. In Step 4, the model uses softmax scores to influence hidden states, enhancing those with higher scores while diminishing others, as illustrated by graying out hidden states H2 and H3 while keeping H1 highlighted. In Step 5, the context vector C4 is concatenated with the hidden state H4, resulting in a combined representation. This concatenated vector is then input into a jointly trained Feedforward Neural Network, depicted in the red box in Figure 16. The output from this network corresponds to the current time step, exemplified by the word "I." These steps encapsulate the iterative process of the model's function.

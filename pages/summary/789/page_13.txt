The paper discusses the application of feature reduction and selection techniques to predict patient survival times in glioblastoma using data from different modalities. Given the extensive number of features in the fc6 and fc7 layers, Principal Component Analysis (PCA) and Sparse Representation (SR) are employed to refine the features separately for each modality. Subsequently, Support Vector Machine (SVM) algorithms are utilized to make individual-level survival time predictions based on these selected features. The study emphasizes the importance of effective feature selection in improving predictive accuracy for glioblastoma patient outcomes.

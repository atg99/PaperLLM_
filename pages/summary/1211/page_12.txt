The paper discusses the analysis of predictions made on the Wholesale Customers Dataset, utilizing a t-SNE plot to visualize data points based on their predicted inlierness and outlierness. The color-coding reflects these predictions, with larger bars indicating strongly contributing input features for selected instances. The authors address the apparent contradiction in spending patterns by employing a k-means clustering approach, setting the number of clusters to six and repeating training for optimal centroid initialization. 

The clustering results reveal that Instance 338 is situated at the boundary between two clusters, leading to a lack of definitive cluster membership indicated by zero evidence across categories. In contrast, Instance 339 is clearly categorized within the yellow cluster, primarily driven by its unique spending behavior in the 'fresh' category. The authors aim to further investigate the cluster dynamics to enhance understanding of these decisions.

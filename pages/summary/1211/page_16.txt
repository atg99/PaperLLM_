The paper discusses the results of a k-means clustering model applied to features extracted from the VGG-16 deep learning model. The clusters identified distinct concepts within an image, such as specific elements of a kitchen scene. For example, one cluster represented the microwave and cupboards, another focused on the bottom part of bar chairs, while others captured various objects and background elements. However, the clustering also revealed limitations, such as the separation of the bar chair concept into two clusters and the mixing of clutter with other objects. The authors argue that integrating unsupervised learning models with explanations of deep neural networks can enhance understanding and applicability in various practical problems, particularly when abstraction is necessary.

This study aims to fill a gap in the comparative analysis of gradient boosting algorithms, focusing specifically on XGBoost and its predecessor, traditional gradient boosting. While previous studies have highlighted the effectiveness of XGBoost in various domains, they neglected to include gradient boosting methods in their evaluations. This paper sets out to analyze and compare the performance of XGBoost against traditional gradient boosting and random forest, which serves as a benchmark due to its proven accuracy and efficiency.

The analysis will consider two main aspects: accuracy and training speed. Additionally, the paper will provide a detailed examination of parameter tuning for XGBoost, offering insights that could benefit researchers seeking to optimize this algorithm for their specific applications. 

The structure of the paper is as follows: Section 2 outlines the methodologies employed, with a focus on the parameters that require tuning; Section 3 discusses the results of the comparative analysis; and Section 4 concludes with key takeaways from the study.

This study presents a deep learning-based model that significantly improves model accuracy by effectively exploring the relationships between channels to achieve better feature representation. The proposed model was evaluated using the C-NMC 2019 dataset, resulting in a mean accuracy of 0.911, surpassing existing state-of-the-art methods. The integration of an attention module within the architecture is highlighted as a key contributor to this performance enhancement. Future research is suggested to focus on reducing the false positive rate to further increase overall accuracy.

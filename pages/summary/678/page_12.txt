The paper presents a comparative analysis of the VGG16 model with and without the Efficient Channel Attention (ECA) module, focusing on its performance in classifying cancer and healthy cell images. Results indicate that the VGG16 model enhanced with the ECA module significantly outperforms the plain VGG16 model, achieving higher classification accuracy by effectively highlighting relevant features while reducing the impact of irrelevant ones. 

Table 4 details the accuracy and standard deviation across six folds of cross-validation, showing that the VGG16 with attention achieved a mean accuracy of 0.911, compared to 0.839 for the plain VGG16. Additionally, Tables 5 and 6 provide evaluation metrics such as sensitivity, specificity, precision, and F1 score for both models, with the enhanced model consistently demonstrating superior performance across all metrics.

Fold-1 yielded the highest mean accuracy for both models, suggesting that sample selection for the training set plays a critical role in performance outcomes. The paper also includes ROC curves and confusion matrices, illustrating the proposed method's ability to accurately classify cell images. Overall, the findings affirm that incorporating the ECA module into the VGG16 model improves prediction capabilities, validated through robust six-fold cross-validation methodology.

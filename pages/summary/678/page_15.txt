The results indicate that despite the challenges posed by the morphological similarities between normal and cancerous cells, our CNN-based approach can serve as an effective diagnostic tool for pathologists examining blood smears for cancer detection. Additionally, the study highlights the potential for extending the model to multi-class classification in future research, which could involve testing on various datasets related to similar diseases. Overall, the incorporation of the ECA module demonstrates significant improvements in the adaptability and robustness of CNN models for ALL classification tasks.

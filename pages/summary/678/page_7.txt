The paper discusses the image augmentation process used to balance the dataset in the C-NMC dataset, which contains fewer healthy cell images compared to ALL cancer cell images. The augmentation techniques applied include random rotation within a range of -45 to 45 degrees, horizontal and vertical flipping, as well as brightness and contrast adjustments. To achieve class balance, healthy images are augmented at double the rate of cancer cell images, resulting in two augmented images for cancer cells and four for healthy cells.

The Enhanced Channel Attention (ECA) module plays a significant role in improving feature extraction by emphasizing the relevant features of input images, particularly the whole cell area and its edges. This results in more accurate feature maps compared to the standard VGG16 model. In the paper, figures illustrate the differences in feature maps generated for both cancerous and healthy cell images, highlighting the advantages of incorporating the ECA module. The comparison with other methods indicates that previous approaches lacked proper data splitting and cross-validation, which could lead to biased results. In contrast, the proposed method's systematic approach to data handling contributes to its superior performance.

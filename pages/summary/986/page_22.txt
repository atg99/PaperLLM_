The paper outlines future directions in biomedical natural language processing (NLP), emphasizing the need for further exploration of domain-specific pretraining strategies and the incorporation of additional tasks within this field. It suggests extending the BLURB benchmark to cover clinical and other high-value domains, which could enhance the assessment and performance of NLP models in critical areas of biomedical research.

The references cited support the discussion by providing a foundation of existing work in clinical NLP, semantic evaluation, and domain adaptation, underscoring the relevance of developing robust models tailored to biomedical contexts. The references include significant contributions to the development of pretrained language models, semantic classification, and relation extraction, underscoring the importance of these methodologies in advancing the capabilities of biomedical NLP.

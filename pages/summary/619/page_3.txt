In summary, the paper presents Knowledge Factorization (KF) as a solution to the limitations of traditional Knowledge Distillation (KD) by modularizing knowledge into distinct factor networks. These networks are designed to be integratable and maintain disentangled task-specific knowledge, facilitating a plug-and-play approach for building customized neural networks. The proposed KF framework utilizes structural factorization to create a common knowledge network and task-specific networks, and representation factorization to ensure the independence of shared and task-level representations. This approach not only simplifies model complexity but also enhances scalability and knowledge protection. The paper emphasizes the ambitious nature of KF, aiming to balance commonality and specialization in knowledge representation within neural networks.

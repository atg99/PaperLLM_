The paper discusses factorizing knowledge in neural networks, focusing on the emergence of invariance and disentanglement in deep representations. It reviews various methods and frameworks that enhance representation learning, such as maximizing mutual information across different views and utilizing variational autoencoders. Key contributions from the literature are highlighted, including model compression techniques and approaches for quantitative evaluation of disentangled representations. The references cover a range of studies that contribute to understanding and improving the interpretability and efficiency of neural network representations. These works emphasize the importance of disentangling features to facilitate better knowledge transfer and generalization in machine learning models.

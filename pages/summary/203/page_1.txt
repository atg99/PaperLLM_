In this paper, the authors present ChainerRL, an open-source deep reinforcement learning (DRL) library developed using Python and the Chainer deep learning framework. ChainerRL encompasses a wide array of DRL algorithms and techniques sourced from leading research in the field. The library aims to enhance reproducibility in research by providing scripts that closely mirror the experimental settings of original papers, allowing for the reproduction of benchmark results for various algorithms. Additionally, ChainerRL includes a visualization tool for qualitative analysis of trained agents. The source code is accessible on GitHub.

The introduction highlights the significant advancements in DRL since its resurgence in 2013, addressing the challenges of reproducibility that have arisen alongside the growing body of literature on DRL algorithms. The authors note the importance of comprehensive libraries and tools to support RL research and development, referencing existing libraries that tackle these issues in various ways. Overall, ChainerRL is positioned as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners in the domain of deep reinforcement learning.

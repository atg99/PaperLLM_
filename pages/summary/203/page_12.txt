The paper presents a comparison of various reinforcement learning algorithms including DQN, IQN, Rainbow, A3C, DDPG, TD3, TRPO, PPO, and SAC, particularly focusing on their evaluation protocols and performance on Atari and OpenAI Gym MuJoCo benchmarks. 

In the evaluation protocols for Atari reproductions, all algorithms share a common evaluation frequency and phase of 250K timesteps, with varying episode lengths and policies. DQN, IQN, Rainbow, and A3C follow the evaluation protocols established by previous works, employing different epsilon values for evaluation episodes to control exploration.

For the MuJoCo benchmarks, the performance metrics for DDPG and TD3 are highlighted, showing the maximum evaluation scores attained during training across several environments. Each score is averaged over multiple trials with different random seeds. Similarly, for TRPO, PPO, and SAC, final evaluation scores after extensive training are reported, with scores approximated visually from learning curves when necessary. The results emphasize the effectiveness of ChainerRL against published baselines, showcasing improvements across various tasks.

Overall, the paper illustrates the competitive performance of ChainerRL in comparison to established algorithms, while adhering to standardized evaluation metrics and protocols.

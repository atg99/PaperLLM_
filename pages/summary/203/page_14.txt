The paper discusses the implementation of a Rainbow agent using the ChainerRL framework, highlighting the integration of various algorithms that enhance the agent's performance. The authors emphasize the flexibility of ChainerRL in facilitating the development of complex reinforcement learning agents by combining building blocks such as double updating, prioritized replay, N-step learning, dueling architectures, and Categorical DQN. 

The provided pseudocode outlines the process of creating and training a Rainbow agent, starting with the definition of a distributional dueling Q-function, followed by its conversion into a noisy network. It then initializes a prioritized replay buffer to utilize N-step rewards and sets up the CategoricalDoubleDQN agent to form the Rainbow agent. The code also demonstrates the capability to train across multiple environments in parallel, with the framework managing training loops, evaluation statistics, logging, and agent saving automatically. This showcases ChainerRL's user-friendly design for building advanced reinforcement learning models.

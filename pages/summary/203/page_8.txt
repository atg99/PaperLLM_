The paper discusses the implementation of various types of agents in ChainerRL, which supports discrete-action, continuous-action, recurrent, batch, and asynchronous agents. Asynchronous training is enabled for certain algorithms such as A3C, ACER, N-step Q-learning, and Path Consistency Learning. Users can train an asynchronous agent by initializing it and utilizing the `experiments.train_agent_async` function. Conversely, batch training allows a single agent to interact with multiple environments in parallel, supported for all algorithms lacking asynchronous training, using `experiments.train_agent_batch_with_evaluation`.

Recurrent training requires additional infrastructure for managing recurrent states and sequences of observations, which ChainerRL simplifies for users. The paper notes that most algorithms initially did not support recurrence or batch training, but enhancements have been made in ChainerRL, as summarized in a table detailing the support for various algorithms.

Appendix B presents reproducibility results, highlighting the training times and scores achieved with reproducibility scripts for nine algorithms. The authors successfully replicated results for DQN, IQN, Rainbow, and A3C in the Atari benchmark, as well as DDPG, TRPO, PPO, TD3, and SAC in the OpenAI Gym Mujoco benchmark tasks. Emphasis is placed on the importance of adhering to consistent evaluation protocols, particularly for Atari agents, due to the variability in reporting across different studies.

The paper presents the performance of ChainerRL, a deep reinforcement learning library, on various Atari benchmarks using several algorithms: DQN, IQN, Rainbow, and A3C. The results are summarized in a table comparing the scores achieved by ChainerRL against published results for each game. 

Key findings include:

- ChainerRL achieved high scores in multiple games, with particularly notable performances in titles such as Atlantis and Crazy Climber.
- The DQN algorithm performed well overall, with high scores in games like Air Raid and Alien.
- IQN and Rainbow algorithms showed competitive scores, especially in games like Asterix and Battle Zone.
- A3C also demonstrated solid performance across various games, though it lagged behind in some instances.
- The table indicates a total of 22, 26, 28, 23, and 34 higher scores across the algorithms, with ties noted in specific categories.

The results illustrate the effectiveness of ChainerRL in achieving high performance in deep reinforcement learning tasks within the Atari environment, contributing to the ongoing development and evaluation of RL algorithms.

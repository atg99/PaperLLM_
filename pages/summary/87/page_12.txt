This paper investigates the impact of Working Memory Connections (WMCs) on the performance of LSTM models in the image captioning task, comparing it to traditional LSTM and peephole LSTM architectures. The analysis focuses on the performance gap measured by METEOR and CIDEr metrics as caption lengths increase. 

Figures presented in the paper illustrate how the performance gap varies with longer captions, indicating that WMCs significantly enhance performance compared to both LSTM and peephole LSTM, particularly as the number of time steps grows. The findings suggest that WMCs facilitate improved information access within the LSTM's gate structure, leading to more precise control over information flow.

The paper concludes that the enhancements provided by WMCs are substantial, as they allow for better management of the information stored in the LSTM cell, which is crucial for generating accurate captions. Overall, the study reinforces the potential of WMCs in advancing the capabilities of LSTM models in image captioning tasks.

This design is inspired by cognitive psychology, particularly the concept of working memory, which retains partial results during tasks. 

Our proposed WMCs improve the traditional LSTM architecture by allowing the memory cell to influence the gating mechanisms without exposing the cell state directly. This is in contrast to previous attempts, such as peephole connections, which have shown inconsistent results in literature. Our extensive experimental analysis demonstrates that LSTMs with WMCs outperform both vanilla LSTMs and peephole LSTMs in performance, training stability, and convergence speed.

In summary, our main contributions include: 
1. A novel modification of LSTM through the integration of WMCs.
2. An exploration of the theoretical framework behind WMCs, linking them to concepts from cognitive psychology.
3. Empirical evidence showcasing the effectiveness of WMCs in enhancing LSTM performance across various tasks.

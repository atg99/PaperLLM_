The paper discusses the role of fully connected (FC) layers in deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs), highlighting their function in learning nonlinear combinations of high-level features derived from the output of convolutional layers. The FC layer is essential for capturing complex relationships within the feature space. Additionally, the paper presents an overview of the architectural evolution of deep CNNs, categorizing various CNN variants and detailing their characteristics. This section emphasizes the diversity and progression of CNN architectures, illustrating how they have adapted to enhance performance in various applications.

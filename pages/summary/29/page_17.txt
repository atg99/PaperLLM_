The paper discusses advancements in pose estimation and action recognition within the field of computer vision, highlighting the challenges posed by the diversity of human body poses. It references Bulat and Tzimiropoulos's 2016 cascade-based CNN approach, which utilizes heat maps for initial detection followed by regression in a two-phase process. The text emphasizes the importance of action recognition in activity detection, noting difficulties in handling translations and distortions across similar action classes. Historical methods include motion history photographs and hidden Markov models, but recent developments, such as Wang's 2017 3D CNN architecture combined with LSTM, show improved performance in recognizing activities from video frames. Ji's earlier work in 2010 also utilized 3D CNNs for action recognition by extracting features from multiple input channels, leading to a supervised model capable of recognizing real-world activities.

While recurrent neural networks (RNNs) are typically favored for NLP tasks, CNNs have been effectively applied in language modeling and analysis.

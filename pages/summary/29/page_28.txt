This document lists various references related to deep learning and its applications, particularly in the context of neural networks and image classification. Key contributions include De Vries' work on deep learning vector quantization, Hinton's introduction of matrix capsules with EM routing, and Justus' exploration of predicting computational costs of deep learning models. Sze provides a comprehensive tutorial on efficient processing of deep neural networks, while Madrazo and Aurisano focus on applying convolutional neural networks for image classification and neutrino event classification, respectively. Additionally, Liu surveys deep neural network architectures and their applications, and Patel et al. discuss feature fusion techniques for human action recognition in video sequences. The references collectively highlight advancements in deep learning methodologies and their practical implementations across various domains.

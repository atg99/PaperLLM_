The paper discusses various architectures in Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) developed from 2017 to 2021, highlighting their key roles, parameters, and error rates. It provides a detailed table summarizing notable architectures like DenseNet, PolyNet, PyramidalNet, and others, emphasizing their innovations such as multi-path cross-layer information flow and structural diversity. 

DenseNet, introduced in 2017, features a multi-path architecture with significant parameter counts and varying error rates across datasets like CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100. PolyNet and PyramidalNet also focus on width and structural enhancements, achieving competitive performance on ImageNet and CIFAR datasets. 

Attention mechanisms, such as the Convolutional Block Attention Module and Concurrent Spatial and Channel Excitation Mechanism, are highlighted for their ability to exploit spatial and feature map information, improving model performance. 

The paper notes a substantial increase in CNN architectural innovations, with around 367 papers published in recent years, indicating a trend toward deeper and larger networks, making visualization challenging. 

Additionally, it discusses specific architectures like Mask R-CNN, which enhances object detection by integrating mask predictions, and G-RCNN, which combines CNN capabilities with graph recognition to analyze images and generate program code. Overall, the manuscript serves as a benchmark for evaluating the evolution and performance of CNN architectures in recent years.

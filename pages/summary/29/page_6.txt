The paper discusses the feature extraction capabilities of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), emphasizing their automatic characteristic extraction process. It highlights the role of padding in CNNs, which is utilized to prevent the reduction of feature map size at each layer, a phenomenon that is often undesirable. The text outlines two outcomes of convolution operations: one where the dimensionality of the convoluted feature is reduced compared to the input, and another where the dimensionality is either maintained or enhanced through padding. An example is provided, illustrating how a 5x5x1 image can be expanded to a 7x7x1 image, allowing for the application of a 3x3x1 kernel while retaining the original dimensions through same padding. Conversely, without padding, the output dimensions decrease, as seen when a 5x5x1 image becomes a 3x3x1 image.

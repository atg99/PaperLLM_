The paper titled "CNN Variants for Computer Vision: History, Architecture, Application, Challenges and Future Scope" by Dulari Bhatt et al. provides a comprehensive review of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and their evolution in the field of computer vision. The authors highlight the growing significance of computer vision in image processing, emphasizing the increasing demand for automatic object recognition driven by advancements in deep learning and the availability of large datasets and hardware.

The review categorizes various CNN architectures into eight distinct groups: spatial exploitation, multi-path, depth, breadth, dimension, channel boosting, feature-map exploitation, and attention-based CNN. Each category reflects innovations in architecture aimed at enhancing the capabilities of deep CNNs. The authors discuss the strengths and weaknesses of different CNN variants, providing insights into their architectural changes and performance.

Moreover, the paper addresses open challenges and research gaps within the field, suggesting potential directions for future research. The authors also summarize the applications of CNNs in various domains, including video processing, object recognition, and natural language processing, underscoring the transformative impact of CNNs on the computer vision landscape.

In conclusion, this review serves as a valuable resource for understanding the historical context, architectural developments, applications, and future prospects of CNNs in computer vision.

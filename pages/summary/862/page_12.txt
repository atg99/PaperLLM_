This paper presents a novel positional attention layer that integrates column-only and row-only attention sub-layers, highlighting its efficiency and simplicity in implementation. The column-only sub-layer is described in detail, utilizing a relative position embedding matrix to facilitate attention across vertical shifts. The key steps involve re-indexing the matrix from relative to absolute shifts through a re-indexing tensor, followed by the computation of a position embedding tensor. The output from the column-only attention is processed through batch normalization before being passed to the row-only sub-layer, resulting in the final output of the positional attention layer.

The final output combines the results of both content and positional attention layers, demonstrating a straightforward fusion process. Notably, the implementation of the proposed Global Spatial Attention (GSA) module is characterized by its minimalistic approach, comprising only eight Einstein-notation equations and five additional equations. This simplicity contrasts sharply with existing methods that rely on local attention mechanisms, which necessitate more complex custom kernels, thereby underscoring the advantages of the GSA module in terms of ease of implementation and computational efficiency.

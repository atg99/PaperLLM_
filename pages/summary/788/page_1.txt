The paper presents a novel approach to algorithm selection using deep learning, specifically employing a Recurrent Neural Network with Long-Short-Term Memory (RNN-LSTM) without the need for feature extraction. The focus is on the 1-dimensional bin packing problem, where instances can be solved by one of four heuristics. The authors generated a large set of problem instances with a clear best solver, training the RNN-LSTM model on the sequences of item sizes directly to predict the most effective heuristic.

The study involved extensive experimentation on both small and large problem instances, revealing a performance improvement of 7% to 11% over the single best solver and a performance close to the virtual best solver. The paper emphasizes the complexity of the algorithm selection problem and critiques the common approach of using feature extraction, which can be cumbersome and non-intuitive.

Key contributions include the creation of large datasets for the 1-D bin packing domain, which are designed to facilitate comparative research in algorithm selection, and the introduction of a deep learning-based method for algorithm selection that relies solely on the temporal data of instances. This method can be applied to various combinatorial problems, highlighting its versatility beyond bin packing.

This paper presents novel white-box membership inference attacks targeting neural network models, highlighting the privacy vulnerabilities associated with the stochastic gradient descent algorithm. The authors evaluated these attacks in both stand-alone and federated learning settings against passive and active attackers with varying prior knowledge. The findings demonstrate that even models that generalize well are significantly susceptible to these white-box membership inference attacks. The paper concludes by noting the absence of an investigation into the theoretical bounds on privacy leakage in this context, indicating that this remains a topic for future research.

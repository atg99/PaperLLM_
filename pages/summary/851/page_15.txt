The paper discusses the architecture of an attack model used in machine learning classifiers, detailing the structure and specifications of various components. It includes a comprehensive breakdown of layer sizes and configurations for the output, label, loss, gradient, encoder, and decoder components. Each component employs fully connected layers with specified sizes, ReLU activation functions, and a dropout rate of 0.2 to mitigate overfitting. The gradient component also incorporates a convolutional layer with 1000 kernels and a kernel size of 1x1, emphasizing its role in processing data effectively. This structured approach aims to enhance the model's performance in recognizing meaningful data patterns while ensuring robustness against attacks.

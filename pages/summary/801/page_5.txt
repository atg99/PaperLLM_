Our approach demonstrates its effectiveness with significant reductions in FLOPs while maintaining competitive error rates across various architectures and datasets.

In the pruning and finetuning process, we first enhance the correlation between filter pairs, allowing us to prune one filter from each pair with minimal information loss. This leads to a modified parameter set Θ′, which we then finetune, largely recovering the model's performance. The experiments utilize standard models like LeNet-5, VGG-16, and ResNet, demonstrating state-of-the-art compression results, especially with a chosen λ value of 1 for optimizing filter correlation.

The experiments on LeNet-5 with the MNIST dataset reveal that our method achieves higher FLOPs pruning rates compared to previous approaches while incurring only a small increase in error rates. Similarly, for VGG-16 on CIFAR-10, we attain substantial FLOPs reductions with improved accuracy over existing methods. Overall, the results validate the effectiveness of the Correlated Filter Pruning method in enhancing model efficiency without significantly sacrificing performance.

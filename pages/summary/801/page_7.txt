The paper presents a comparative analysis of pruning methods applied to deep learning models, specifically focusing on ResNet-50 and VGG-16 architectures. It highlights the performance metrics, such as error percentages and pruned floating point operations (FLOPs), across various pruning techniques including ThiNet-50, CP, SPP, WAE, and the proposed CFP method.

In Table 4, the results for ResNet-50 on the ImageNet dataset reveal that the baseline model achieved a top-5 accuracy of 92.2%, while the CFP method resulted in an 8.6% error with approximately 49.6% pruned FLOPs. Other methods showed varying error rates, with ThiNet-50 and CP both achieving 9.0% and 9.2% error rates respectively, while SPP and WAE had slightly higher errors.

Table 5 outlines the impact of correlation optimization on LeNet-5 and VGG-16. The optimization led to improved accuracy in the models, demonstrating that maintaining filter correlation is crucial for preserving model performance. The study emphasizes that discarding filters without optimization can lead to significant accuracy drops due to the loss of complementary information.

The paper further analyzes filter correlations, noting that in VGG-16, the maximum filter correlation decreased from 0.7 to nearly 0.1 after compression, indicating effective removal of redundant filters while retaining useful discriminative filters. This reduction helps to mitigate overfitting and create a compact model with minimal redundancy.

Additionally, the authors discuss the practical implications of pruned FLOPs on speedup and memory size. They clarify that theoretical pruned FLOPs may not directly translate to practical speedup due to various factors such as parallelization bottlenecks and I/O operations. The experiments conducted using the VGG-16 model on the CIFAR-10 dataset illustrate these dynamics, showing that the feature maps (FM) significantly impact total run-time memory (TRM) and overall performance.

In conclusion, the findings suggest that the proposed CFP method effectively balances accuracy and model efficiency, while the analysis of filter correlations and memory implications provides valuable insights for optimizing deep learning architectures.

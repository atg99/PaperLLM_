The paper discusses the optimization of U-Net architectures for brain tumor segmentation. Various U-Net variants were evaluated, including basic U-Net, UNETR, SegResNetVAE, Residual U-Net, and Attention U-Net. Experimentation involved architectural modifications and training adjustments such as deep supervision, drop block, and Focal loss. Results indicated that U-Net with deep supervision produced the most effective outcomes. Further improvements were suggested through the inclusion of an additional input channel with one-hot encoding for the foreground, enhancing encoder depth and convolutional channels, and implementing a post-processing strategy. The paper emphasizes the importance of these optimizations in achieving better segmentation results in brain tumor imaging.

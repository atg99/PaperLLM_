In this example, an agent in a game must exceed a round score of 12,000 to advance from fourth place. Through simulations, the agent initially learns that discarding a red-boxed tile can lead to a win but results in a score below 12,000. After adapting its strategy, the agent chooses to discard a blue-boxed tile, which reduces the chances of winning but allows for a score exceeding 12,000 when it does win. This calculated risk successfully enables the agent to move out of fourth place.

The paper also discusses the comparison of the Suphx AI with top human players who have reached a 10 dan ranking in Mahjong. To ensure fairness, the comparison is limited to their performance in the expert room after achieving this rank, as these players predominantly play in the phoenix room due to its more favorable scoring rules, making it difficult to obtain a reliable performance metric in the expert room.

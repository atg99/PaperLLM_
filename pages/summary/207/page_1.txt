The paper "Suphx: Mastering Mahjong with Deep Reinforcement Learning" discusses the development of an AI program, Suphx, designed specifically for the complex game of Mahjong. The authors highlight the evolution of AI in gaming, moving from simpler games to more intricate multi-player imperfect-information games. Mahjong presents unique challenges due to its complex rules and hidden information, making it a difficult domain for AI research.

Suphx utilizes deep reinforcement learning, incorporating innovative techniques such as global reward prediction, oracle guiding, and run-time policy adaptation. The program has achieved remarkable success, outperforming most top human players and ranking above 99.99% of all officially ranked players on the Tenhou platform. This achievement marks a significant milestone in AI, as it is the first instance of a computer program surpassing elite human players in Mahjong. The paper emphasizes the importance of these advancements in the broader context of AI development in gaming.

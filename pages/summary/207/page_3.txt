This paper presents Suphx, an advanced AI system designed for 4-player Japanese Mahjong (Riichi Mahjong), which is popular worldwide. Despite previous efforts to develop Mahjong AI, current systems still lag behind expert human players. Suphx employs deep convolutional neural networks, initially trained using supervised learning from professional players' logs and subsequently refined through self-play reinforcement learning (RL) utilizing a policy gradient algorithm.

Key innovations in Suphx include:

1. **Global Reward Prediction**: This technique involves training a predictor to estimate the final reward of a game based on information from current and previous rounds. This prediction enhances the learning signals for the policy network, facilitating better decision-making.

2. **Oracle Guiding**: An oracle agent, which has access to complete information about all players' tiles, is used during training. By gradually reducing the oracle's information to that of a normal agent, Suphx benefits from accelerated learning compared to traditional RL methods.

3. **Parametric Monte-Carlo Policy Adaptation (pMCPA)**: Given the complex rules of Mahjong that complicate the game tree structure, pMCPA enables the agent to adapt its offline-trained policy to the specific conditions of a round during online play, improving performance as more information becomes available.

Suphx was tested on Tenhou, a leading Mahjong platform with over 350,000 users, achieving a rank of 10 dan and a stable rank that exceeds many top human players, demonstrating its competitive capabilities. The paper further details Suphx's decision-making process and the network architecture employed.

The referenced works primarily focus on advancements in artificial intelligence (AI) applied to various games, including poker and Mahjong, showcasing the development of superhuman AI capabilities. 

1. Brown and Sandholm's 2018 study introduced Libratus, an AI that outperformed top professionals in heads-up no-limit poker, demonstrating significant advancements in game theory and AI strategies.
2. In 2019, they expanded on this with research on multiplayer poker, further emphasizing the AI's capabilities in more complex gaming scenarios.
3. The European Mahjong Association provided rules for Japanese Mahjong, which serve as a foundation for the game’s computational approaches.
4. Gao et al. (2018 and 2019) explored the use of deep convolutional neural networks for supervised learning in Mahjong, leading to the development of a computer player that can compete effectively.
5. Kurita and Hoki presented a method for constructing AI players using Markov decision processes, indicating a strategic approach to AI in multiplayer Mahjong.
6. Mizukami and Tsuruoka (2015) focused on Monte Carlo simulations and opponent modeling to build a computer Mahjong player, showcasing practical applications of probabilistic methods in AI development.
7. Moravčík et al. (2017) introduced DeepStack, an expert-level AI for poker, further solidifying the role of deep learning in mastering complex games.
8. Lastly, Silver et al. (2016) highlighted the use of deep neural networks in mastering Go, illustrating the broader implications of AI research across different strategic games.

These studies collectively illustrate the progression of AI in competitive gaming, utilizing various methodologies such as deep learning, Monte Carlo simulations, and game theory to achieve superhuman performance.

The paper discusses the comparative performance of Mahjong AI Suphx against other leading AIs and top human players. It highlights that Suphx has a stable rank approximately 2 dan higher than Bakuuchi and NAGA, the previous top AIs, despite both having achieved the same record rank of 10 dan as Suphx. The data indicates that Suphx outperforms 99.99% of human players in the Tenhou platform. 

Table 4 presents a detailed comparison of game record ranks and stable ranks for Suphx, Bakuuchi, NAGA, and top human players, with Suphx achieving the highest stable rank of 8.74 compared to the others. The paper also emphasizes that record ranks can be misleading, as many players share the same record rank but possess varying skill levels. The authors chose to compare performance in the expert room rather than the phoenix room due to differences in scoring rules, which affect the comparability of stable ranks.

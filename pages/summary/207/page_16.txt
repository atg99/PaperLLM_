The results indicate that the global reward prediction significantly influences the decision-making process of our agent, particularly in high-stakes scenarios. While the RL-basic agent opts for a more aggressive approach by discarding a potentially winning tile, it exposes itself to risks due to the lack of information about opponents' actions. In contrast, the agents using global reward prediction, RL-1 and RL-2, demonstrate a more conservative strategy, prioritizing safety and maintaining their lead.

This evaluation of run-time policy adaptation confirms the effectiveness of integrating adaptive strategies based on real-time data, contributing to improved performance in competitive environments. The findings highlight the importance of balancing risk and reward in decision-making processes within reinforcement learning frameworks.

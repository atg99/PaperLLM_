The paper discusses the adaptive learning capabilities of the Suphx agent in Mahjong, highlighting its ability to perform simulations at the start of each round when private tiles are dealt, as well as after each tile is discarded by any player. This continuous adaptation allows the agent to improve its policy as the game progresses, utilizing observable information to enhance performance. The approach reduces the need for extensive sampling and roll-outs by amortizing computational complexity across the round, making online play feasible with manageable resources.

Suphx is positioned as a continually evolving agent, with its current success on Tenhou.net seen as just the starting point. Future developments are anticipated to introduce innovative technologies that will advance the field of Mahjong AI and the study of imperfect-information games. The techniques employed in Suphx, such as global reward prediction and parametric Monte-Carlo policy adaptation, are believed to have broader applications in real-world scenarios like financial market prediction and logistics optimization.

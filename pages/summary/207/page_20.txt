The paper discusses the concept of stable rank in the context of comparing AI and human players in Tenhou, highlighting its stability and finer granularity compared to record rank. However, it notes that stable rank can exhibit high variance, particularly for players with fewer games played. To enhance the reliability of comparisons, the authors propose a method involving random sampling of K games from player logs to compute stable ranks, repeating this process N times to gather statistics. 

The findings indicate that the AI player Suphx significantly outperforms both other AIs and top human professionals. The accompanying statistics reveal that Suphx excels in defense, evidenced by a low deal-in rate and a minimal occurrence of fourth-place finishes, which is crucial for achieving a high stable rank in Tenhou. Additionally, Suphx has developed distinctive playing styles that are acknowledged by top human players, particularly its ability to maintain safe tiles. 

Overall, the analysis underscores Suphx's strong performance and strategic capabilities within the game.

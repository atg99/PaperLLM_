The paper presents an analysis of Suphx, the leading Mahjong AI, which has demonstrated superior performance against top human players on the Tenhou.net platform. It highlights the rank distribution and win/deal-in rates of various players, with Suphx achieving a win rate of 22.83% and a deal-in rate of 10.06%. The authors argue that while Suphx's performance is commendable, there remains significant potential for further enhancements.

Key improvements discussed include the implementation of global reward prediction, where the AI could benefit from incorporating more comprehensive information to better assess game difficulty and adjust reward signals accordingly. The authors suggest that rewards should reflect the challenge of winning a round, emphasizing that easier wins should not be overly rewarded.

Additionally, the concept of oracle guiding is introduced, where the transition from an oracle agent to a normal agent is facilitated through perfect feature dropout. Alternative strategies for leveraging perfect information are also proposed, such as training both an oracle agent and a normal agent concurrently or designing an oracle critic to enhance the learning process. Overall, the paper emphasizes the complexity of Mahjong and the ongoing opportunities for refining AI strategies in this domain.

The paper discusses the ranking system in Tenhou, a Japanese online Mahjong platform, focusing on how players earn or lose ranking points based on game outcomes. The points system varies across four room types: normal, advanced, expert, and phoenix. While the penalties for losing a game are uniform across these rooms, the rewards for winning differ, with higher-ranked players facing steeper penalties for losses.

When players win or lose, their total ranking points fluctuate, affecting their rank. A player’s rank increases if their points reach the threshold for the next level, and decreases if their points drop to zero. Initial ranking points are assigned when a player changes levels, leading to an unstable rank that can change frequently. The record rank refers to the highest rank achieved by a player.

The paper further explains the concept of stable rank, which evaluates a player's long-term performance in the expert room. This is calculated based on the number of games won with the highest and lowest accumulated round scores, as well as those with second and third highest scores. However, the stable rank can exhibit significant variance due to the inherent randomness of the game, including the influence of opponents and tile distributions. It is suggested that a player should play thousands of games to attain a reliable stable rank.

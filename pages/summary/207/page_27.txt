The paper discusses the ranking system used in Tenhou, a Japanese Mahjong platform, which is structured according to a martial arts ranking system. Players begin at the Rookie level and progress through ranks from 9 Kyu to 1 Kyu, and then from 1 Dan to 10 Dan. Each rank has specific point requirements for leveling up and down, with detailed point adjustments based on performance in different game rooms, such as Normal, Advanced, Expert, and Phoenix.

The scoring system is influenced by yaku, which are specific winning patterns in Mahjong that players can achieve, and dora tiles, which provide bonus points. Players can declare Riichi when they are one tile away from winning, restricting their hand modifications but allowing them to win from specific tile sources.

The final ranking points awarded to a player are contingent upon their level and the scores accumulated over multiple game rounds. The ranking system is designed to reflect a player's skill and performance in the game while incorporating various strategic elements unique to Japanese Mahjong.

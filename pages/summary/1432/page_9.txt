The paper presents RUBi, a novel strategy aimed at mitigating unimodal biases in Visual Question Answering (VQA) models. A qualitative comparison is made between RUBi and a baseline model on the VQA-CP v2 test set, showcasing distributions of answers across various question filters. The results indicate that RUBi effectively reduces biases by leveraging a question-only branch that captures and adjusts for unwanted statistical regularities in the question modality. This approach led to a significant improvement in accuracy, achieving a +5.94 percentage point increase over existing state-of-the-art results on the dataset designed to highlight question biases. The effectiveness of RUBi across different VQA models is also demonstrated. Future research aims to explore the application of this method to other multimodal tasks.

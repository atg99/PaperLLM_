The paper presents an analysis of various models' performance on the VQA-CP v2 test dataset, highlighting the effectiveness of the RUBi learning strategy. Table 1 summarizes state-of-the-art results, showing that the RUBi model achieves an overall accuracy of 47.11%, outperforming the previous best model (UpDn + Q-Adv + DoE) by 5.94 percentage points and significantly exceeding the GVQA model by 15.88 points. The RUBi approach also demonstrates a slight improvement over the baseline model trained with cross-entropy, while maintaining accuracy for the "Other" answer type, unlike the second-best method which experiences a notable decrease.

Additionally, the paper evaluates alternative sampling methods, such as Balanced Sampling and Question-Type Balanced Sampling, which yield lower accuracies compared to RUBi. The effectiveness of RUBi extends across different architectures, as shown in Table 2, where it enhances accuracy for both Stacked Attention Networks and UpDn models by 11.73 and 4.5 points, respectively. These results suggest that UpDn is less susceptible to biases than SAN.

Overall, the findings confirm that RUBi can be integrated into existing VQA models without architectural changes, leading to improved performance across various configurations. Full results by question type are available in supplementary materials, underscoring RUBi's versatility and effectiveness in enhancing VQA model accuracy.

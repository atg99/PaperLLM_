This paper presents a novel learning strategy called RUBi aimed at improving Visual Question Answering (VQA) models by addressing biases in training data. The core idea is to utilize a mask that increases the score of the correct answer while reducing the scores of incorrect answers, which leads to a lower loss associated with biased examples. For instance, when the question is about the color of a banana, the mask assigns a high value to the answer "yellow," thus increasing its score significantly from 0.8 to 0.94, while decreasing the scores for other answers. This adjustment helps the model focus on learning from less biased examples, as evidenced by a reduction in loss from 0.22 to 0.06.

Additionally, RUBi emphasizes examples where multiple modalities are required for a correct answer. In scenarios where the image contradicts the expected answer, such as a green banana, the mask increases the loss, encouraging the model to learn from these challenging examples.

The joint learning procedure optimizes the parameters of the VQA model and a question-only branch through two distinct losses: LQM, which targets the predictions based on the combined image and question inputs, and LQO, which focuses solely on question predictions. The final loss function, LRUBi, is a combination of these two losses, allowing the model to capture biases effectively without directly influencing the question encoder.

The paper also discusses the baseline architecture used for testing RUBi, which employs a combination of pretrained visual features and a GRU for question encoding. The model uses a Bilinear BLOCK fusion method to merge the question representation with image features, culminating in predictions from a multi-layer perceptron classifier.

Experimental results demonstrate that the RUBi strategy is effective in enhancing VQA performance on the VQA-CP v2 dataset, which is designed to assess robustness against question biases. The approach shows competitive accuracy compared to state-of-the-art methods, indicating its potential for improving VQA model robustness and accuracy. Further details on implementation and additional experiments are provided in supplementary materials.

The study evaluates the RUBi method's performance on the VQA v2 dataset, highlighting its effects on accuracy and bias reduction. While traditional methods may exploit unwanted correlations in the training set, RUBi demonstrates a drop in accuracy similar to state-of-the-art methods, with a decline of 1.94 percentage points compared to a 3.78-point drop in another study. RUBi also significantly enhances accuracy on the VQA-CP v2 dataset.

The paper validates its masking strategy by showing that replacing the sigmoid with ReLU results in a 7.09-point drop in accuracy, indicating the effectiveness of the proposed method. Additionally, the question-only loss (LQO) is shown to reduce question biases, providing consistent gains across various architectures, with the UpDn architecture benefiting the most (+4.76).

Qualitative analysis reveals how RUBi reduces biases in specific question patterns. For instance, in questions about skiing, RUBi correctly identifies the absence of skis, producing 80% "no" answers, while the baseline predominantly answers "yes." Similar improvements are observed in color-related questions, with RUBi showing a better alignment with test set answers. The analysis concludes that RUBi effectively grounds visual and textual concepts, demonstrating its ability to generalize to unseen examples and outperforming the baseline in bias reduction and accuracy.

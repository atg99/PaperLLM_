The cited references encompass a wide range of studies focusing on clustering algorithms, trajectory analysis, and traffic estimation methodologies. Key contributions include:

1. The development of time-evolving origin-destination (OD) matrix estimation techniques using high-speed GPS data (Moreira-Matias et al., 2016).
2. Methods for bus OD matrix estimation utilizing multisource data (Huang et al., 2020).
3. Conceptual frameworks for understanding trajectories (Spaccapietra et al., 2008) and improved algorithms for detecting stops in individual trajectories (Luo et al., 2017).
4. Innovations in clustering algorithms, such as enhanced versions of k-means and fuzzy k-means for various applications (Arthur & Vassilvitskii, 2007; Heil et al., 2019; Bei et al., 2021).
5. Traffic congestion estimation approaches using artificial neural networks and convolutional neural networks (Gu et al., 2020; Gao et al., 2021).
6. Probabilistic models for traffic congestion estimation (Afrin & Yodo, 2021) and genetic algorithms for clustering (Beg et al., 2016).

These studies collectively advance the understanding of data clustering and trajectory analysis, providing valuable methodologies for urban computing and traffic management applications.

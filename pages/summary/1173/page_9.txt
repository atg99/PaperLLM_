The paper presents a comparative analysis of clustering algorithms applied to taxi GPS data from various cities, specifically focusing on the performance of a proposed noise K-means clustering algorithm. The experimental results, detailed in Tables 3 to 6, evaluate the algorithms using several metrics: SC, PBM index, DB index, and SSE. The noise K-means algorithm consistently outperforms the other methods in capturing urban hotspots, as reflected in the SC evaluation values in Table 3, where it shows the highest maximum, average, and minimum scores across all datasets. Similarly, in the PBM index (Table 4), noise K-means and K-means Plus yield superior clustering results. The DB index (Table 5) indicates that K-means, noise K-means, and K-means Plus perform comparably well, showing minimal differences in evaluation values. Lastly, the SSE results (Table 6) confirm that noise K-means excels across all datasets, reinforcing its effectiveness as an optimization target in the clustering process.

Overall, the findings suggest that the noise K-means algorithm significantly enhances clustering outcomes, making it easier to identify urban operational states through improved clustering evaluation methods applied to GPS datasets.

The paper presents a methodology for visualizing urban hotspots using GPS data from taxis in various cities, namely Aracaju, San Francisco, Rome, Chongqing, and Beijing. The approach employs the Fuzzy C-Means (FCM) method to identify cluster center locations, which are then integrated into a mapping system via the Amap API for visual representation. Figures 9 and 10 illustrate the identified hotspots for each city using both the FCM and K-means methods, respectively. The results highlight the effectiveness of these clustering techniques in capturing urban activity patterns through taxi movement data.

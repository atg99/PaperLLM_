The paper discusses the effectiveness of a noise-based K-means clustering algorithm applied to taxi GPS data from various cities, including Rome, Chongqing, Beijing, Aracaju, and San Francisco. The analysis of convergence, illustrated through SSE convergence curves, indicates that all comparative clustering algorithms, with the exception of Fuzzy C-Means (FCM), achieved convergence in approximately ten iterations. This outcome demonstrates the feasibility and suitability of the proposed algorithm for basic partition clustering tasks.

Additionally, the paper emphasizes the importance of visualizing urban hotspots identified by the clustering algorithm. The results are presented using the Amap system, where the locations of cluster centers are plotted to provide an intuitive understanding of urban hotspot distribution. The visual representations for the aforementioned cities are included in the results, showcasing the effectiveness of the clustering approach in urban analysis.

The paper presents an analysis of urban hotspot distributions derived from city taxi GPS data using various clustering algorithms, including Fuzzy C-means (FCM), K-means, K-means plus, and a noise-based K-means method. Figures 10 to 12 illustrate the identified hotspots in five cities: Aracaju (Brazil), San Francisco (USA), Rome (Italy), Chongqing (China), and Beijing (China). The results highlight how each clustering algorithm captures different spatial patterns of urban hotspots, emphasizing the effectiveness of these methods in analyzing transportation data. The findings suggest that the choice of clustering algorithm can significantly influence the identification of hotspots in urban environments, which could inform urban planning and transportation management strategies.

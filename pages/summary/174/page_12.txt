This paper explores the use of generative adversarial networks (GANs) and explainable AI algorithms applied to data from Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) devices. The study evaluates a synthetic dataset generated by GANs against real data using the LLM algorithm, demonstrating that the synthetic data closely aligns with actual information, supported by the rules derived from the LLM algorithm. 

Future research directions include the integration of additional IoMT sensors for monitoring patient health parameters and examining the interplay between environmental and health data to assess the impact of the environment on disease. Expanding the patient sample size is also suggested to enhance the algorithm's precision and the generalizability of the synthetic data. Additionally, the paper proposes investigating adversarial machine learning techniques to validate the robustness of the dataset and algorithms. The authors aim to explore statistical validation methods or automated algorithms to aid human decision-making due to the numerous rules generated by machine learning.

The work was funded by Compagnia di San Paolo under a scientific research initiative, and the authors declare no conflicts of interest. Data from the study is publicly available in a Kaggle repository.

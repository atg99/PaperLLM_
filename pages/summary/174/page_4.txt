The study emphasizes the importance of real-time data access for healthcare professionals, enabling them to devise tailored medical plans and interventions based on comprehensive health insights. 

The research focuses on the implementation of an intelligent monitoring system that utilizes Internet of Things (IoT) sensors for both environmental and physiological measurements. The system aims to improve quality of life and well-being by continuously monitoring various health parameters through a combination of wearable and environmental devices. 

For the proof-of-concept, the study utilized the HealthPlatform v3, a certified medical device platform that adheres to strict data management standards, including GDPR compliance. Key devices included a smartphone with a proprietary app, an electrocardiogram patch, a pulse oximeter, a weight scale, a sphygmomanometer, and a spirometer. These devices communicate via Bluetooth Low Energy, allowing for seamless data transmission to the mobile device and subsequent cloud storage.

Overall, the Pneulytics framework represents a significant advancement in health monitoring technology, with the potential to facilitate proactive healthcare interventions and enhance patient outcomes. Future developments will aim to incorporate additional devices and further refine the monitoring capabilities of the system.

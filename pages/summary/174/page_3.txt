The paper emphasizes the limitations of traditional one-size-fits-all alert systems in effectively monitoring patients, advocating for the need for intelligent systems that tailor monitoring to individual patient needs. The integration of explainable AI (xAI) is proposed as a transformative approach, enabling the generation of predictive models that elucidate the factors influencing patient health, thereby assisting physicians in developing personalized treatment strategies.

The Pneulytics framework is introduced as a solution to enhance patient monitoring for COPD. This framework combines innovative technologies with AI algorithms to facilitate remote monitoring and improve patient outcomes. It addresses common challenges in COPD management, such as poor treatment adherence and inhaler misuse, which can lead to worsening health and increased mortality.

Technological advancements, such as smart inhalers, are highlighted as tools for collecting vital patient data, including medication adherence and inhaler usage parameters. The Pneulytics framework aims to integrate this data with information from Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) devices and clinical follow-up data to create a comprehensive monitoring system.

The proposed system involves patients wearing sensors that track relevant health parameters, with data transmitted to a cloud platform for processing. In addition to patient data, environmental sensors will be incorporated to evaluate external factors affecting the disease. The ultimate goal is to utilize AI algorithms to develop predictive models that can lead to better management of COPD through individualized monitoring and treatment approaches.

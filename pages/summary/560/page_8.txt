The paper provides a comprehensive review of Differentiable Neural Architecture Search (DNAS), particularly focusing on DARTS and its derivatives. It identifies the challenge of increasing architectural diversity in CNNs, which is crucial for methods based on DARTS. The authors categorize 28 approaches based on how they address four key challenges identified in earlier sections. 

The literature review summarizes various DNAS methods, presenting their type, challenges tackled, top accuracy on ImageNet, search costs, and search space in a table. Notable methods include DARTS, ProxylessNAS, and OFA, each exhibiting different performance metrics and resource requirements. The review highlights the evolution of DNAS techniques and their effectiveness in addressing architectural diversity and optimization challenges in CNN design.

Overall, the paper contributes to the understanding of DNAS by systematically analyzing existing methods, their strengths, and their limitations in the context of CNN architecture discovery.

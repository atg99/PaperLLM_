The paper presents a comprehensive survey on Differentiable Neural Architecture Search (DNAS), focusing on its efficiency in automating neural network design. It discusses various methodologies and frameworks that leverage differentiable approaches to optimize neural architecture selection, highlighting significant advancements and techniques in the field. Key contributions include the introduction of modular libraries like NASLib, the application of reinforcement learning in architecture search, and the exploration of memory-efficient strategies. The survey also reviews notable models such as MobileNetV2, EfficientNetV2, and FBNet, emphasizing their impact on performance and computational efficiency. Overall, the paper underscores the growing importance of DNAS in enhancing the design process of neural networks, addressing challenges such as overfitting and resource allocation while paving the way for future innovations in automated machine learning.

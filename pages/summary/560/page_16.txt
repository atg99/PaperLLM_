The optimization problem presented focuses on minimizing the validation loss of weights in the search and evaluation networks, represented by the equation min𝛼L𝑣𝑎𝑙(𝑤∗𝐸,𝑤∗𝑆,𝛼). The process involves two key stages: a separate learning stage where both networks are trained independently, and a joint optimization stage that utilizes performance feedback from the evaluation network to refine the weights and architecture parameters.

In the joint optimization stage, the goal is to minimize a combined loss function that includes the validation losses of both networks and an introspective distillation term, which facilitates knowledge transfer between the networks. This distillation term is computed using a softmax function on the output logits of both networks, incorporating a temperature coefficient to control the smoothness of the probability distributions.

CDARTS, the proposed method, demonstrates superior performance on the DARTS search space, achieving a 3% improvement in top-1 accuracy while maintaining reasonable computational costs. The paper also notes that FairDARTS addresses the over-representation of skip connections in DARTS by replacing the softmax operation with a sigmoid to promote fair competition among various operations.

Overall, CDARTS enhances the search process for neural architecture while ensuring effective knowledge transfer between networks.

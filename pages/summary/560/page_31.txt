The paper provides a comprehensive survey of Differentiable Neural Architecture Search (DNAS), reviewing 26 recent approaches and identifying four key challenges faced by DNAS. The authors note that while differentiable methods have made significant advancements in recent years, there is still a lack of consensus on a standard methodology, with DARTS being the only method that has gained widespread acceptance. The paper highlights the emerging field of Explainable Neural Architecture Search (XNAS), which seeks to enhance the explainability of CNN models, noting the limited existing approaches targeting DNAS specifically. The authors predict growth in XNAS and DNAS, driven by the integration of knowledge from alternative NAS methods and novel trends in the field, such as reinforcement learning and evolutionary algorithms. The conclusion emphasizes the importance of this survey in guiding future research directions in DNAS.

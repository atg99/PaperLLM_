The referenced works encompass a range of advancements in neural architecture search (NAS) and related techniques aimed at enhancing the efficiency and effectiveness of deep learning models across various applications. Key contributions include:

1. **Slimmable Networks and Training Techniques (Yu & Huang, 2019)**: This work introduces universally slimmable networks that can adapt their complexity based on resource availability, improving training methodologies.

2. **Bignas for Neural Architecture Scaling (Yu et al., 2020)**: The authors propose Bignas, which scales neural architecture search using large single-stage models for enhanced performance in computer vision tasks.

3. **C2FNAS for Medical Image Segmentation (Yu et al., 2020)**: This approach employs a coarse-to-fine strategy for NAS specifically tailored for 3D medical image segmentation, improving accuracy and efficiency.

4. **U-Boost NAS (Yüzügüler et al., 2022)**: This method enhances differentiable NAS by focusing on utilization, thereby optimizing resource allocation during model training.

5. **Robustifying Differentiable Architecture Search (Zela et al., 2020)**: The authors explore techniques to improve the robustness of differentiable architecture search methods, ensuring reliable performance across various datasets.

6. **Memory-Efficient Hierarchical NAS (Zhang et al., 2020)**: This work focuses on developing a memory-efficient hierarchical approach to NAS aimed at image denoising applications.

7. **Differentiable NAS with Pruning (Zhang et al., 2021)**: The authors combine pruning and multi-objective optimization in differentiable NAS to enhance the efficiency of fault diagnosis in machinery.

8. **Fast Hardware-Aware NAS (Zhang et al., 2020)**: This paper presents a method for rapidly conducting hardware-aware NAS, optimizing models for specific hardware constraints.

9. **Stochastic Implicit Gradients in NAS (Zhang et al., 2021)**: This approach introduces stochastic implicit gradients to improve the efficiency of differentiable architecture search.

10. **EfficientPose for Human Pose Estimation (Zhang et al., 2021)**: The authors leverage NAS to develop EfficientPose, which significantly enhances human pose estimation accuracy and speed.

11. **Speech Recognition via NAS (Zheng et al., 2021)**: This work demonstrates efficient NAS for end-to-end speech recognition using straight-through gradients.

12. **Scene Parsing with ADE20K (Zhou et al., 2017)**: The authors contribute to scene parsing methodologies using the ADE20K dataset, facilitating advancements in image segmentation tasks.

13. **Path-Regularized Differential Network Architecture Search (Zhou et al., 2020)**: This study introduces a theory-inspired approach to enhance the search for optimal network architectures.

14. **V-NAS for Volumetric Medical Image Segmentation (Zhu et al., 2019)**: This work presents V-NAS, focusing on neural architecture search tailored for volumetric medical imaging.

15. **Reinforcement Learning in NAS (Zoph & Le, 2017)**: The foundational work on using reinforcement learning for neural architecture search, establishing a framework for future research.

These contributions collectively advance the field of NAS, addressing challenges in model efficiency, accuracy, and application-specific optimization in various domains, including computer vision and medical imaging.

The paper discusses advancements in Neural Architecture Search (NAS), specifically addressing issues of optimization gaps and catastrophic forgetting in supernet training. Zhang et al. introduced E2NAS, which utilizes a Variational Graph AutoEncoder (VGAE) to connect discrete architectures with a continuous search space, enabling differentiable architecture search. They implemented a new complementation loss to mitigate catastrophic forgetting, using a replay buffer to maintain prior architectures. E2NAS demonstrated significant performance improvements, achieving a 29.45% increase in top-1 accuracy on ImageNet-16-120 compared to previous methods.

Additionally, Zhang et al. proposed iDARTS, which refines the optimization process of DARTS through a Neumann approximation of the Implicit Theorem Function. This approach computes architectural parameter gradients more efficiently by approximating the inverse Hessian matrix using a stochastic Neumann series. The results showed that iDARTS outperformed standard DARTS by 2.6% on ImageNet. Overall, both E2NAS and iDARTS present effective strategies for enhancing performance in neural architecture search.

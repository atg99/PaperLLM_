The paper discusses various types of attacks on systems from 2010 to 2018 and presents the distribution of SI-based IDS during this period. The authors detail the contributions of several researchers in the field, highlighting their academic backgrounds and areas of expertise. Sanju Mishra focuses on intelligent decision support systems, while Dr. Rafid Sagban has extensive experience in algorithm analysis and swarm intelligence. Dr. Ali Yakoob specializes in computational intelligence and machine learning. Dr. Niketa Gandhi, with over 15 years in academia, researches eAgriculture and data mining for decision support systems. The paper references key literature on swarm intelligence and network anomaly detection, emphasizing the ongoing evolution of methodologies in cybersecurity and intelligent systems.

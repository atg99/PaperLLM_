This section references various studies focused on network intrusion detection systems and optimization techniques. The paper by Mojtaba et al. introduces an effective intrusion detection framework utilizing a time-varying chaos particle swarm optimization approach, highlighting its efficacy in enhancing detection capabilities. Garg and Batra propose a fuzzified Cuckoo-based clustering technique aimed at improving network anomaly detection, emphasizing its innovative approach to clustering. Additionally, Hajisalem and Babaie present a hybrid intrusion detection system that combines the Artificial Bee Colony and Ant Lion Search algorithms to effectively address both misuse and anomaly detection in network environments. Each of these studies contributes to the ongoing research in enhancing network security through advanced detection methodologies.

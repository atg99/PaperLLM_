The paper discusses the application of swarm intelligence (SI) techniques, particularly the Cuckoo Search Optimization (CSO) and K-means clustering, in anomaly detection systems (IDS). It highlights a two-phase process comprising a training phase and a detection phase, utilizing mean square error and the Silhouette Index for evaluating anomaly detection and classification measures. The discussion section summarizes the effectiveness of SI-based anomaly detection systems through a comparative analysis of various review papers published between 2011 and 2018, presented in Table 1.

The table outlines the evolution of paradigms and applications of SI techniques in IDS, noting significant findings such as the limited exploration of Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) and Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) compared to other machine learning techniques. The review emphasizes the lack of comprehensive literature on the potential of SI-based IDS, particularly regarding their integration with cloud computing and big data analytics. The authors call attention to gaps in existing studies, particularly the underutilization of SI methods in hybrid models with machine learning, suggesting a need for further research to fully harness the capabilities of SI in cybersecurity.

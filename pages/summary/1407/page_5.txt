The paper presents an evaluation of the Objects365 dataset, focusing on its annotation quality and effectiveness as a benchmark for object detection. A comparison with the VOC and COCO datasets reveals that Objects365 has a higher annotation recall of 92%, surpassing COCO's 83% and OpenImages' 43%. Additionally, the precision of Objects365 annotations stands at 91.7%, compared to COCO's 71.9%, indicating superior annotation quality.

The experimental section establishes baseline results using two detectors, FPN and RetinaNet, with the mean Average Precision (mmAP) calculated following COCO's methodology. The mmAP for FPN on Objects365 is reported as 22.5, significantly lower than COCO's 38.3, suggesting that Objects365 is a more challenging dataset due to its broader category range. The analysis indicates that many object instances are missed, particularly for rare categories.

The paper also investigates the generalization ability of Objects365 as a pretraining dataset for various detection and segmentation tasks. Initial fine-tuning results show only a modest improvement when using Objects365 compared to ImageNet, indicating that further optimization of the learning rate strategy is necessary to fully leverage Objects365's potential in enhancing detection performance across other datasets.

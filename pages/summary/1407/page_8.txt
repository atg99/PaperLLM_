The paper presents the generalization ability of semantic segmentation results on the PASCAL VOC and ADE datasets using various pretraining datasets with the PSPNet architecture and ResNet50 backbone. Results indicate that pretraining on datasets like Objects365 shows significant improvements in mean Intersection over Union (mIOU) scores compared to no pretraining, with Objects365 achieving the highest scores of 76.7 on PASCAL VOC and 43.3 on ADE.

The authors also demonstrate that the Objects365 dataset facilitates faster fine-tuning on the COCO benchmark. Training with only 12K iterations using the Objects365 pre-trained model yields comparable performance to models trained with 540K iterations from ImageNet, resulting in a training time that is 10-20 times faster without sacrificing performance.

Furthermore, the paper discusses the upper-bound performance of models trained from scratch versus those pre-trained on ImageNet and Objects365. The Objects365 pre-trained model outperforms other methods, confirming the advantages of pretraining both the backbone and head of the network, leading to a 2.6-point increase in performance on COCO when both parts are pre-trained compared to only the backbone.

In conclusion, the paper introduces the Objects365 dataset as a valuable resource for improving object detection and localization tasks, suggesting future work may explore larger models beyond ResNet-50. The research received support from various national programs and institutions.

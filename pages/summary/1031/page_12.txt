The paper discusses the governance of AI ethics, presenting various models that balance flexibility and strictness in responsibility distribution. It emphasizes the role of AI practitioners in internal audits and engagement with stakeholders. The authors propose "Platform as a Service" (PaaS) as a balanced approach between decentralized and centralized governance. In this context, "Ethics as a Service" is introduced, suggesting components such as an independent ethics board, a collaboratively developed ethical code, and the involvement of AI practitioners. This model aims to distribute responsibility for ethics-related activities in a more effective manner, contrasting with the traditional centralized and devolved responsibility models.

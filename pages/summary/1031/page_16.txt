The paper presents a comprehensive overview of the ethical considerations surrounding artificial intelligence (AI) governance. It discusses various models and frameworks proposed to evaluate AI systems ethically, emphasizing the importance of algorithmic accountability, fairness, and responsible business practices. Key contributions from multiple authors highlight the challenges and opportunities in governing AI, including the need for democratic governance and stakeholder engagement. The work also addresses the significance of developing trustworthy AI through verifiable claims and ethical foresight analysis. Overall, the paper underscores the necessity of a unified ethical framework to navigate the complexities of AI in society, advocating for principles that promote a good AI society while acknowledging the inherent risks.

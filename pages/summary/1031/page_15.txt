The paper discusses the concept of "Ethics as a Service" in the context of artificial intelligence (AI) and highlights the complexities involved in ensuring ethical AI deployment. It argues that the impacts of AI systems cannot be entirely controlled through technical design alone, as biased AI arises from intricate interactions within complex systems rather than solely from biased datasets. The unpredictability of agent interactions in these systems means that the effectiveness of pro-ethical design approaches can only be assessed post-deployment. Therefore, ongoing evaluation of algorithmic systems and their social impacts is essential. The authors stress the need for further qualitative research and empirical testing to explore the benefits and drawbacks of implementing Ethics as a Service.

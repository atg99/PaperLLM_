In summary, while the foundation of AI ethics is established, its practical implementation remains challenging due to the lack of a balanced Level of Abstraction. Striking a compromise between overly flexible and overly strict guidelines is essential. Ethical applications must account for context-specific nuances and the dynamic nature of AI systems, which are influenced by various actors and can produce unpredictable outcomes. Therefore, a practical pro-ethical Design approach must facilitate continuous reflection and adaptation throughout the design process, rather than merely serving as a one-time checklist.

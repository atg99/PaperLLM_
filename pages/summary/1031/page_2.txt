The introduction of the paper highlights the growing recognition of ethical issues associated with the design, development, and use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML). Key ethical concerns include privacy, fairness, accountability, accessibility, environmental sustainability, and transparency. While traditional governance mechanisms like legislation are insufficient to protect individuals and society, there has been a shift towards 'soft' governance methods such as ethical codes and guidelines. However, these principles often lack practical guidance for AI practitioners on how to effectively implement ethical standards, creating a gap between theoretical ethics and practical application. The authors reference their previous work, which identified existing tools and methodologies that can assist practitioners in bridging this gap by aligning ethical principles with the algorithm development process. Despite the availability of these tools, the authors note that many practitioners still struggle to apply ethical considerations effectively in AI development.

The paper proposes a multi-disciplinary ethics advisory board to establish a principle-based ethical code for AI development. This board would engage with various stakeholders impacted by AI systems, ensuring their voices are integrated into the design process. A systematic approach would be implemented during the validation and evaluation stages, which includes defining ethical principles contextually and selecting appropriate tools for implementation. The framework emphasizes ethical foresight and mechanisms for addressing breaches of ethical principles, highlighting that fairness in algorithms is relative rather than absolute.

Additionally, the paper outlines the responsibilities of internal AI practitioners, who would define principles, select tools, and document their processes transparently. While this distributed responsibility model aims to address limitations of existing practices, its effectiveness in real-world applications remains uncertain. Continuous audits of both functionality and impact are essential due to the evolving nature of AI systems, though these audits may incur significant costs. Thus, a balance must be struck to ensure ethical accountability without overwhelming AI companies.

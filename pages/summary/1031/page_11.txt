Platform as a Service serves as a middle ground, offering users the ability to build applications while providing a level of managed infrastructure. In terms of ethical governance, this model would encourage a collaborative approach where companies and individuals work together to develop ethical guidelines and processes. This structure allows for shared responsibility and promotes dialogue among stakeholders, potentially leading to more robust ethical practices.

The paper argues for a multi-agent system that balances responsibility among various actors in AI ethics, combining the strengths of different governance models. By aggregating positive actions and isolating negative ones, this approach aims to create a more effective ethical governance framework. The analogy to cloud computing illustrates how these governance models can inform the design of a pragmatic and operationalized approach to AI ethics, promoting accountability while mitigating risks associated with both overly rigid and overly lax systems.

The article reviews the applications of data mining and process mining techniques in analyzing the extraction process in underground mining. It aims to identify the most popular data analysis techniques used in this field. Two research questions were formulated: P1 focuses on the most common data mining/process mining tasks related to the extraction process, and P2 addresses the most popular techniques for multidimensional analysis of the extraction process in underground mines. 

The study analyzed sixty-two published works concerning data mining from defined analytical perspectives, including diagnostics and machinery, geomechanics, hazards, and mine design and safety. Results indicate that predictive tasks were predominantly formulated, with a strong emphasis on classification tasks. The most frequently utilized data mining techniques include artificial neural networks, decision trees, rule induction, and regression. Process mining in the context of the extraction process has been discussed in only a few articles, which were briefly reviewed in the paper.

Keywords: data mining, process mining, analysis, extraction process, underground mining.

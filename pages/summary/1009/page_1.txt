The paper by Edyta Brzychczy examines the applications of data mining (DM) and process mining (PM) in the context of underground mining. It highlights the increasing importance of these analytic approaches due to the impact of Industry 4.0, which drives the adoption of advanced technologies and data analytics in various industries, including mining. The research is guided by two key questions: the most popular DM/PM tasks in mining analysis and the techniques utilized in this multi-perspective analysis.

The paper emphasizes that while data mining is widely recognized and applied, process mining is less commonly used in the mining sector. Data mining encompasses various predictive tasks, predominantly classification, utilizing algorithms such as artificial neural networks, decision trees, rule induction, and regression. In contrast, the applications of process mining in underground mining are limited, with only a few instances identified and described.

The author structures the paper to first define the primary analytic approaches, differentiating between data-oriented and process-oriented analytics. It concludes with an overview of the findings related to the research questions, underscoring the need for further exploration and implementation of process mining techniques in the mining industry. Overall, the paper provides valuable insights into the current state and potential of analytic methods in enhancing underground mining processes.

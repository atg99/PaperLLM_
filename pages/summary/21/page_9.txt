The paper discusses CNN EXPLAINER, an interactive visualization tool designed to help non-experts understand Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs). The tool allows users to visualize predictions through activation maps at each layer and explore how hyperparameters influence convolution operations. Participants engaged with the tool by customizing visualizations to test their hypotheses about model behavior, enhancing their learning experience. For example, users modified input images and hyperparameters, leading to insights about CNN operations and performance.

Despite its benefits, the study identified limitations. Beginners often required more guidance to navigate the tool effectively, as initial visual representations were sometimes confusing. Participants expressed interest in understanding not just how CNNs operate, but also why specific architectures and functions, like ReLU and Max Pooling, contribute to performance. The authors suggest incorporating more detailed explanations and visualizations to clarify these concepts.

Future work aims to expand the tool's capabilities by explaining the training process and backpropagation, generalizing to other neural network architectures, and integrating best practices from algorithm visualization design. The authors emphasize the importance of quantitative evaluations to assess the educational effectiveness of such tools.

In conclusion, CNN EXPLAINER serves as a valuable resource for demystifying CNNs for learners, promoting broader access to AI education. The authors hope their findings will inspire further development of visualization tools that facilitate understanding and application of AI technologies.

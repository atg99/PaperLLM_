The paper discusses the complexities of learning about Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), emphasizing the need for students to grasp both low-level mathematical operations and high-level model integration. This dual understanding is crucial, as it poses a significant challenge in comprehending how different layers in CNNs interact to transform data.

To address this challenge, the authors introduce CNN EXPLAINER, an interactive visualization tool that facilitates learning for non-experts. This tool aims to bridge the gap between high-level model structures and low-level operations, which has been identified as a major obstacle in understanding CNNs. The authors highlight the limitations of existing visualization tools, which either focus on high-level structures or low-level operations but fail to connect the two.

The contributions of the paper include the design of CNN EXPLAINER, which utilizes familiar visualization techniques to provide a comprehensive understanding of CNNs. It incorporates an overview of the model structure, detailed mathematical operations, and fluid transitions between different abstraction levels. User studies involving instructors and students were conducted to identify educational needs and evaluate usability, leading to insights about the effectiveness of interactive visual tools in machine learning education.

Additionally, CNN EXPLAINER is open-source and web-based, allowing broader access to deep learning education without requiring advanced computational resources. The authors advocate for the importance of visualization in democratizing the understanding of artificial intelligence technologies and hope their work will inspire further development of educational tools in this domain.

The paper also provides a background on CNNs, explaining their structure and functionality in image classification tasks. CNNs consist of multiple layers that extract features from images, employing convolutional, downsampling, and activation functions to enhance learning and improve performance over traditional models. Overall, the work presents a significant advancement in educational tools for understanding complex machine learning models.

The paper discusses the CNN EXPLAINER, a web-based tool designed to facilitate the understanding of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) through interactive visualization. The Hyperparameter Widget allows users to modify hyperparameters and observe changes in real-time, enhancing the learning experience by encouraging experimentation alongside theoretical knowledge. The tool utilizes the Tiny VGG architecture for image classification, trained on the Tiny ImageNet dataset, achieving a top-1 accuracy of 70.8%.

The paper presents two usage scenarios: one involving a beginner, Janis, who learns about layer connectivity and operations through interactive views, and another involving a professor, Damian, who employs the tool in a classroom setting to teach CNN concepts and facilitate student engagement. The observational study conducted with 16 participants aimed to assess the tool's usability and effectiveness in teaching CNNs to beginners with varying levels of prior knowledge.

Overall, CNN EXPLAINER provides an accessible platform for users to deepen their understanding of CNNs through hands-on learning, bridging gaps in knowledge and enhancing educational experiences in deep learning.

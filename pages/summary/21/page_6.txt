The Interactive Formula Views provide an in-depth understanding of mathematical operations within a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN). These views illustrate key operations such as element-wise dot-products, the ReLU activation function, and max pooling. Users can interact with heatmaps to see the input-output mappings and explore the softmax function, linking numerical values to their visual representations. This interactivity helps users grasp how individual logits contribute to classification scores.

Transitions between different views in CNN EXPLAINER guide users from a high-level overview of the model to detailed neuron activation maps. Simple connections allow direct access to the Interactive Formula Views, while more complex connections first lead users through an Elastic Explanation View, which provides visualizations and annotations before delving into the mathematical details.

Accompanying the visual tool is an interactive tutorial article that elaborates on CNN functions and hyperparameters, enhancing user comprehension. Text annotations within the visualization further clarify concepts not easily conveyed through visuals alone.

Customization options in the Control Panel enable users to change the input image, either selecting from preloaded options or uploading their own. This feature fosters engagement, allowing users to analyze and test the network's classification decisions with various image inputs. The "Show detail" function reveals additional network specifications, enriching the user's understanding of the CNN architecture and its components. Overall, CNN EXPLAINER combines interactive visualizations, detailed explanations, and customization to facilitate a deeper learning experience in understanding CNNs.

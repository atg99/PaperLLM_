This section of the paper discusses related work in the visualization of deep learning education, algorithm visualization, and visual analytics for neural networks and predictions. 

In the realm of deep learning education, various visualization systems have been developed to assist beginners in grasping fundamental concepts. Tools like Teachable Machine and the Deep Visualization Toolbox allow users to interactively train classifiers and understand neuron functions using real-time data from webcams. Other educational resources, such as ConvNetJS and TensorFlow Playground, enable experimentation with different neural network architectures. However, many existing tools focus either on high-level model structures or low-level mathematics, failing to bridge the gap between these two aspects, which presents challenges for learners.

Algorithm visualization (AV) has been a subject of study prior to the rise of deep learning, aimed at illustrating the dynamic behavior of algorithms through interactive graphics. Although the effectiveness of AV in education has shown mixed results, higher engagement levels correlate with better learning outcomes. The design of CNN EXPLAINER, a tool discussed in the paper, draws from AV literature to enhance learning about deep learning algorithms.

In terms of visual analytics, numerous tools have been created to help experts analyze deep learning models and predictions. Tools like Summit and LSTMVis visualize learned features and hidden states of models, while others assist in monitoring training processes. However, these tools primarily target experts, whereas the focus of this paper is on aiding non-experts and learners in comprehending deep learning concepts.

The authors conducted formative research through interviews with deep learning instructors and a survey of students who had previously studied CNNs. The findings highlighted key challenges in learning CNNs, such as connecting mathematical concepts with model structures and understanding the training workflow. Based on this research, five design challenges were identified to inform the development of an interactive visual learning tool aimed at enhancing students' understanding of CNNs.

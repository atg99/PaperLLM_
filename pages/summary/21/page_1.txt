The paper introduces CNN E XPLAINER, an interactive visualization tool aimed at helping non-experts understand convolutional neural networks (CNNs), which are essential in deep learning. The authors highlight the challenges beginners face in grasping the complexities of CNNs, particularly in understanding how input images are transformed into classification predictions. Through interviews with instructors and surveys of students, the authors identified key difficulties in learning CNNs.

CNN E XPLAINER addresses these challenges by providing a model overview that summarizes CNN architecture alongside dynamic visual explanations. The tool allows users to explore the mathematical operations of CNNs and the relationships between low-level computations and high-level structures through smooth transitions. The qualitative user study indicates that CNN E XPLAINER enhances comprehension of CNNs and is engaging for users. 

Developed with modern web technologies, the tool runs locally in web browsers, making deep learning education more accessible. The paper concludes by emphasizing the importance of interactive learning tools in demystifying complex concepts in deep learning, particularly for beginners.

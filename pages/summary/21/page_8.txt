8.3.4 Summary of Findings
The usability evaluation of CNN EXPLAINER demonstrated that participants found the tool easy to use, enjoyable, and effective for learning about convolutional neural networks (CNNs). The transition between different views, the use of animations, and the ability to customize inputs were highlighted as key features that contributed to a deeper understanding of CNN operations. Participants expressed high levels of satisfaction with the tool's functionality, indicating a strong likelihood of future use. 

Overall, the study underscores the importance of interactive and visually engaging tools in educational settings, particularly for complex subjects like CNNs. The findings suggest that thoughtful design elements, such as fluid transitions and animations, can significantly enhance the learning experience, making it both informative and enjoyable. Future research could further explore the impact of these features on learning outcomes through controlled experiments.

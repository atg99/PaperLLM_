The paper discusses the challenges faced by students learning about Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and proposes a visualization tool called CNN EXPLAINER to aid comprehension. 

Key challenges identified include the intricate structure of CNNs, where each layer has distinct functions, and the complex mathematical operations involved, particularly in layer computations. Students often struggle to connect the low-level mathematical operations to the high-level model structure, which complicates their understanding of CNNs. Effective algorithm visualization is highlighted as a potential solution, but it requires careful design to engage learners and clearly communicate concepts.

The authors outline several design goals for CNN EXPLAINER. These include providing a visual summary of CNN models to illustrate data flow, creating an interactive interface for exploring mathematical formulas, enabling smooth transitions between different abstraction levels, ensuring clear communication and engagement, and implementing the tool as a web-based application for easy access.

The interface of CNN EXPLAINER is designed for beginners, allowing users to visualize the forward propagation of a trained model, explore different abstraction levels, and understand mathematical operations necessary for image classification. The tool aims to facilitate learning by providing annotations, tutorials, and opportunities for users to customize visualizations and test hypotheses. The paper emphasizes the need for accessible educational tools in deep learning, as exemplified by the CNN EXPLAINER project.

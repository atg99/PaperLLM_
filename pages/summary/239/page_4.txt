The paper discusses the architecture of ResNet, highlighting its ability to facilitate gradient flow through identity connections, which enhances the training of deep networks. It presents a dataset consisting of images classified into three categories: Normal, COVID-19, and Viral Pneumonia, with respective counts of 300, 219, and 300 images. For the COVID-19 category, various augmentation techniques are applied, including horizontal and vertical flipping, rotation (at specific degrees), and shifting. A total of 90 augmented images are generated, resulting in a combined dataset of 1000 images. The effective use of data augmentation is emphasized to improve model robustness in recognizing COVID-19.

The paper provides a comprehensive overview of the use of deep learning techniques for diagnosing COVID-19 through medical imaging, particularly chest X-ray and CT scans. It highlights various studies and approaches that leverage convolutional neural networks and transfer learning to enhance detection accuracy. The references cited underline the clinical features of COVID-19, pathological findings, and imaging characteristics, emphasizing the importance of timely and efficient diagnosis in managing the pandemic. The discussion also touches on the challenges posed by the lack of benchmark datasets and the need for innovative solutions, such as generative adversarial networks, to improve detection capabilities. Overall, the paper underscores the significant role of advanced computational methods in addressing the healthcare challenges presented by COVID-19.

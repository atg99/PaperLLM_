•WOZ (Mrkšić et al., 2016): The Wizard-of-Oz dataset consists of 1,200 dialogues focused on a single domain, designed to simulate interactions between users and a system without actual system responses.

•CamRest676 (Wen et al., 2016): This dataset includes 676 dialogues specifically targeting restaurant booking scenarios. The dialogues are characterized by their simplicity and focus on specific user requests.

In total, the paper highlights the significance of these diverse datasets in advancing task-oriented dialogue systems. The datasets vary in size, complexity, and domain coverage, providing a robust foundation for developing and evaluating dialogue models. The paper emphasizes the importance of using these datasets for pre-training models to improve their performance in real-world applications.

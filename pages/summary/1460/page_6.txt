6.3 Dialogue State Tracking
In the dialogue state tracking task using the MWOZ dataset, TOD-BERT also shows significant improvements in performance. The joint training approach (TOD-BERT-jnt) particularly enhances the model's ability to track domain and slot pairs effectively, which is essential for maintaining context in task-oriented dialogues.

6.4 Few-Shot Learning
The few-shot learning experiments further highlight the robustness of TOD-BERT. When limited training data is available, TOD-BERT consistently outperforms the other models, demonstrating its ability to generalize from fewer examples.

Overall, the results indicate that TOD-BERT, especially when jointly trained with multiple objectives, provides a strong foundation for various task-oriented dialogue applications. The evaluation across multiple datasets confirms its effectiveness in handling intent recognition, dialogue state tracking, and adapting to few-shot learning scenarios.

The paper includes appendices that provide visual representations of BERT and its variations, TOD-BERT-mlm and TOD-BERT-jnt, as applied to the MWOZ test set. Figures 3 and 4 utilize t-SNE visualization to illustrate how these models represent system responses, with color coding to differentiate between various domains and dialogue acts, respectively. This visualization helps to understand the effectiveness and distinctiveness of the BERT models in processing and interpreting dialogue data.

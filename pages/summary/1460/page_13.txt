The paper presents a visualization of BERT and its variants, TOD-BERT-mlm and TOD-BERT-jnt, using tSNE to analyze the representations of system responses in the MultiWOZ (MWOZ) test set. The different colors in the visualization correspond to various dialogue slots, highlighting how each model captures the nuances of dialogue representation differently. This comparison aims to illustrate the effectiveness of the TOD-BERT models in handling task-oriented dialogue tasks.

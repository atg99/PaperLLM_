The paper discusses various methods to improve the performance of ResNet-50 through iterative retraining techniques in the context of unstructured pruning. It analyzes the impact of different retraining epochs on model accuracy, showing that accuracy can fluctuate based on the method applied. The study presents results for several configurations of ResNet-50 with varying degrees of sparsity, including techniques like weight rewinding, low-learning rate weight rewinding, and fine-tuning.

Key findings indicate that iterative retraining effectively mitigates accuracy loss after unstructured pruning, with specific emphasis on how different retraining strategies can yield varying improvements. The evaluation of accuracy changes across multiple retraining epochs highlights the importance of selecting appropriate retraining methods to maintain model performance. Overall, the research underscores the significance of retraining strategies in enhancing the robustness of pruned neural networks.

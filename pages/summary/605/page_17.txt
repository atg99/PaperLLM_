The paper presented at ICLR 2020 discusses the tradeoff between accuracy and parameter efficiency in the context of one-shot pruning for various ResNet architectures and datasets, including CIFAR-10, ImageNet, and WMT16. It highlights different compression ratios achieved through unstructured and structured pruning methods, with specific focus on models such as ResNet-20, ResNet-56, and ResNet-110.

The results indicate that varying compression ratios lead to changes in accuracy, with some models showing a decrease in performance as compression increases. For instance, unstructured pruning on ResNet-20 achieves a compression ratio of up to 9.31x with minimal accuracy loss. Conversely, structured pruning methods, as referenced from Liu et al. (2019), also demonstrate significant compression but with varying impacts on model accuracy.

Additionally, the paper explores techniques like learning rate rewinding, weight rewinding, and fine-tuning, which are employed to recover accuracy post-pruning. The findings suggest that careful management of the pruning process can help maintain model performance while reducing parameter count, emphasizing the importance of balancing accuracy with efficiency in neural network design.

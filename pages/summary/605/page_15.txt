The appendix of the conference paper presents additional results related to various neural network architectures, pruning techniques, baselines, and ablation studies. It expands on the methodologies discussed in the main paper, specifically focusing on neural network pruning, which involves starting with a randomly initialized network and applying a pruning mask to obtain a pruned version of the network.

Key methodologies include low-learning-rate weight rewinding, which rewinds the weights of the network while applying the fine-tuning learning rate, and reinitialization of pruned networks followed by retraining. The latter approach addresses findings by Liu et al. (2019) that indicate pruning followed by fine-tuning may yield similar or worse performance compared to training a pruned network from scratch. The appendix emphasizes that the discovered pruned structures, derived from previous training, should perform better than randomly pruning without prior training.

The paper also introduces additional networks, including ResNet-20 and ResNet-110, and provides structured pruning results for these architectures on datasets like CIFAR-10 and ImageNet. The results highlight the accuracy versus parameter efficiency tradeoff, where low-learning-rate weight rewinding leads to a significant drop in accuracy compared to the best achievable performance. Overall, the appendix adds depth to the main findings by providing supplementary data and insights on the effectiveness of different pruning strategies and training techniques.

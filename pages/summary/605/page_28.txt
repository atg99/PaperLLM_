The paper discusses the performance of structured pruning methods applied to ResNet-110, specifically comparing two approaches, Structured-A and Structured-B. The results are presented through accuracy changes over various retraining epochs, highlighting the impact of different pruning ratios on model performance. The analysis reveals that both structured pruning methods exhibit slight variations in accuracy, with changes ranging from -3% to +1% across different retraining epochs. 

Structured-A and Structured-B are evaluated at different compression rates (1.02x, 1.45x, 1.04x, 2.09x, 2.96x, 4.05x, 5.46x), indicating the effectiveness of each method in maintaining model accuracy post-pruning. Additionally, the paper explores strategies such as learning rate rewinding, weight rewinding, fine-tuning, and reinitializing to optimize the performance of the pruned models. 

Overall, the findings suggest that structured pruning can be effectively integrated into neural network training, with careful consideration of retraining techniques to mitigate accuracy loss after model compression.

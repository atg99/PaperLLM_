The paper discusses the tradeoff between unstructured accuracy and search cost in the context of deep learning model optimization, specifically focusing on the ResNet-20 architecture. It presents accuracy curves resulting from various retraining epochs and compression levels. The results highlight that different levels of unstructured pruning (ranging from 1.25x to 3.82x) impact the model's accuracy differently, with iterative methods showing varying degrees of effectiveness. Techniques such as learning rate rewinding, weight rewinding, fine-tuning, and reinitializing are evaluated for their impact on model performance post-pruning. The findings suggest that while pruning can reduce model size, careful consideration of retraining strategies is essential to maintain accuracy.

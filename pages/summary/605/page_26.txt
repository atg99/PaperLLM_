The paper "One-shot IterativeGNMT" presented at the ICLR 2020 conference focuses on improving the performance of the Google Neural Machine Translation (GNMT) system through an innovative approach termed One-shot Iterative GNMT. It explores the effects of retraining epochs on BLEU scores, which are crucial for evaluating translation quality. 

The analysis includes various configurations of the GNMT, specifically examining unstructured models with different complexities, denoted by a numerical factor (e.g., 5, 7, 9, 11, 14, 18, 22). Each configuration is tested both in its standard form and in an iterative retraining mode. The results reveal a range of BLEU score changes (∆ BLEU) across different retraining epochs, indicating the effectiveness of one-shot iterative retraining in enhancing translation accuracy.

Overall, the findings suggest that iterative retraining can significantly impact performance, with varying degrees of improvement based on the model's structure and complexity. The paper contributes to the field of machine translation by providing evidence for the benefits of applying iterative methods to GNMT systems.

SUNRISE is a unified framework designed to enhance ensemble learning in off-policy deep reinforcement learning (RL). The authors, Kimin Lee and colleagues, identify key challenges in traditional off-policy RL, such as instability in Q-learning and difficulties in balancing exploration and exploitation. To address these issues, SUNRISE introduces two main components: ensemble-based weighted Bellman backups that adjust target Q-values based on uncertainty from a Q-ensemble, and an inference method that selects actions using the highest upper-confidence bounds to facilitate efficient exploration.

The framework promotes diversity among agents through Bootstrap with random initialization, allowing for the integration of these components to improve the performance of existing off-policy RL algorithms, including Soft Actor-Critic and Rainbow DQN. SUNRISE demonstrates effectiveness across both low-dimensional and high-dimensional environments for continuous and discrete control tasks.

The authors also highlight the limitations of prior ensemble methods, which often examined improvements in isolation without addressing error propagation in Q-learning. SUNRISE's approach reweights sample transitions based on uncertainty estimates, enhancing the signal-to-noise ratio in Q-updates and stabilizing the learning process. The proposed upper-confidence bound method further aids in exploration, making SUNRISE a versatile tool for advancing off-policy RL. The training code for SUNRISE is made publicly available for further research and application.

The paper introduces SUNRISE, a unified ensemble method designed to enhance off-policy reinforcement learning (RL) algorithms. It incorporates weighted Bellman backups with bootstrapping and upper confidence bound (UCB) exploration to address common challenges in off-policy learning. The ensemble-based weighted Bellman backups utilize uncertainty estimates to re-weight target Q-values, leading to more stable training and improved data efficiency.

Experiments demonstrate that SUNRISE significantly enhances the performance of existing off-policy algorithms, such as Soft Actor-Critic and Rainbow DQN, across various environments. The findings indicate that SUNRISE outperforms state-of-the-art RL methods in both continuous and discrete control tasks, indicating its versatility and effectiveness.

Despite the potential computational overhead associated with ensemble methods, SUNRISE is noted for its parallelizability, which mitigates efficiency concerns. The authors suggest that SUNRISE could be beneficial for related areas, including sim-to-real transfer, imitation learning, and offline RL, highlighting its broader implications in the field of reinforcement learning.

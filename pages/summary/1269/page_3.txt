The paper introduces SUNRISE, a novel framework for reinforcement learning that employs ensemble-based weighted Bellman backups. This method aims to enhance the stability and performance of off-policy reinforcement learning algorithms, specifically focusing on the Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) variant.

The central concept revolves around the use of multiple independent agents, each with their own Q-function and policy, operating from a shared replay buffer. This ensemble approach mitigates the issues of error propagation inherent in traditional Q-learning, where inaccuracies in the target Q-function can destabilize learning. The proposed weighted Bellman backup method incorporates a confidence weight that down-weights transitions with high variance across the ensemble of target Q-functions, thereby improving the signal-to-noise ratio in the learning process.

The framework also integrates Upper Confidence Bound (UCB) exploration strategies and utilizes bootstrap with random initialization to enhance the training of the ensemble agents. The empirical results demonstrate that the ensemble can produce well-calibrated uncertainty estimates, improving the agent's decision-making capabilities in continuous action spaces.

Overall, SUNRISE represents a comprehensive approach that leverages ensemble methods to enhance the reliability and efficiency of reinforcement learning algorithms.

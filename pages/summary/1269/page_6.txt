The paper presents SUNRISE, a novel reinforcement learning method that incorporates a weighted Bellman backup and Upper Confidence Bound (UCB) exploration strategy. The performance of SUNRISE is compared against various baseline algorithms across multiple environments in OpenAI Gym and the DeepMind Control Suite. The results demonstrate that SUNRISE consistently outperforms other methods in terms of sample efficiency and final performance, especially in complex environments like SlimHumanoid-ET.

Key findings include:
1. SUNRISE achieves higher performance metrics across different tasks, as evidenced by Tables 1 and 2, which report mean and standard deviation values for various algorithms.
2. The weighted Bellman backup significantly mitigates error propagation issues, leading to improved learning outcomes, especially in environments with high complexity and noisy rewards.
3. UCB exploration further enhances SUNRISE's effectiveness, allowing it to better navigate sparse reward scenarios.

Overall, SUNRISE showcases advancements in reinforcement learning techniques, particularly in dealing with noise and complexity in reward structures, marking a step forward in the field.

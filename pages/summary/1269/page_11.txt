The references listed encompass significant contributions to the field of reinforcement learning, focusing on various methodologies and advancements. Key works include:

1. Van Hasselt et al. (2016) introduced double Q-learning, which mitigates overestimation bias in Q-learning, enhancing learning efficiency.
2. In 2019, van Hasselt, Hessel, and Aslanides explored the application of parametric models in reinforcement learning, emphasizing their potential benefits in certain contexts.
3. Vinyals et al. (2019) demonstrated the effectiveness of multi-agent reinforcement learning by achieving grandmaster level performance in StarCraft II, showcasing the capability of AI in complex environments.
4. Wang and Ba (2020) investigated model-based planning using policy networks, contributing to the understanding of how models can be integrated into reinforcement learning frameworks.
5. Wang et al. (2019) provided a benchmarking study for model-based reinforcement learning, establishing a foundation for evaluating different approaches within the field.
6. Wiering and Van Hasselt (2008) discussed ensemble algorithms in reinforcement learning, highlighting the advantages of combining multiple learning strategies.
7. Yarats et al. (2019) focused on improving sample efficiency in model-free reinforcement learning using images, addressing challenges in learning from visual data.
8. Ziebart (2010) presented a framework for modeling adaptive behavior through maximum causal entropy, contributing theoretical insights into decision-making processes.

These works collectively advance the understanding and application of reinforcement learning, addressing both theoretical foundations and practical implementations.

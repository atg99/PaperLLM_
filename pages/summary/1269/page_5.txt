In summary, the SUNRISE method demonstrates strong performance across various reinforcement learning tasks. It effectively combines with established algorithms such as SAC and RAD, leading to improvements in sample efficiency and overall performance in both continuous and discrete control benchmarks. The comparative evaluations show that SUNRISE outperforms several state-of-the-art methods, including model-based approaches, while maintaining computational efficiency. The ablation studies further validate the contribution of the weighted Bellman backup in enhancing learning stability. These findings highlight SUNRISE's versatility and potential as a general approach in reinforcement learning applications.

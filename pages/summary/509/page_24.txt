This paper compiles a comprehensive list of references related to advancements in graph learning and representation techniques, particularly emphasizing self-supervised learning methods. It includes notable works that explore various approaches such as contrastive learning, graph convolutional networks, and autoencoders, highlighting how these methodologies contribute to improved graph representation and clustering.

Key contributions from the referenced works include:
- The exploration of graph contrastive learning and augmentations to enhance representation learning.
- The introduction of models like Graph-Bert, which leverage attention mechanisms for learning graph representations.
- The implementation of self-supervised techniques in scenarios with limited labeled data, showcasing the versatility of graph convolutional networks.
- The development of novel frameworks such as marginalized graph autoencoders and adversarially regularized models that advance graph embedding techniques.

The references span a wide range of conferences and journals, indicating active research in this area, with papers discussing various applications and theoretical underpinnings of graph learning methods. Overall, the compilation serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners interested in the evolving landscape of graph representation learning.

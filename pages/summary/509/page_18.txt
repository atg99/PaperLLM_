In conclusion, this paper provides a thorough overview of self-supervised learning (SSL) on graphs, offering a unified framework and a systematic taxonomy that categorizes graph SSL into four distinct approaches: generation-based, auxiliary property-based, contrast-based, and hybrid methods. Each category is accompanied by mathematical summaries, contemporary reviews, and comparative analyses of existing methods. The paper also compiles a wealth of resources, including datasets, evaluation methods, performance metrics, and open-source codes for graph SSL techniques. Furthermore, practical applications of graph SSL are discussed, alongside future challenges and promising research directions in this evolving field. The timeline presented highlights significant milestones in graph SSL development from 2014 to 2021, showcasing the evolution of various methods and techniques. Definitions of key terms such as dynamic graphs and heterogeneous graphs are provided, alongside notations used throughout the paper, offering a clear understanding of the concepts discussed. Overall, this work serves as a comprehensive resource for researchers and practitioners interested in the advancements and applications of self-supervised learning in graph contexts.

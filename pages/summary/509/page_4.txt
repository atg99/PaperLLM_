Hybrid Methods integrate multiple pretext tasks in a multi-task learning framework. This approach aims to leverage the strengths of various SSL techniques to enhance the model's performance. By employing different pretext decoders and objective functions, hybrid methods can simultaneously learn from generation, auxiliary property extraction, and contrastive learning tasks.

In summary, the paper categorizes graph self-supervised learning (SSL) methods into four types: generation-based, auxiliary property-based, contrastive-based, and hybrid methods. Each category is defined by its unique approach to pretext tasks and objective functions, emphasizing the diverse strategies employed to enhance graph representation learning.

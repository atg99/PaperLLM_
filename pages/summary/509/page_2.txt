Self-Supervised Learning (SSL). SSL refers to a learning paradigm where the model is trained on unlabeled data by generating supervisory signals from the data itself. This approach has gained traction in various fields, including computer vision, natural language processing, and graph analytics.

Graph SSL. Graph self-supervised learning specifically deals with the learning of representations and structures in graph-structured data without relying on labeled information. The unique characteristics of graphs, such as node dependencies and topological structures, necessitate tailored SSL approaches distinct from those used in other domains.

The paper provides a comprehensive overview of open-source implementations of graph semi-supervised learning (SSL) methods, compiling links to available code for various techniques discussed in the survey. Additionally, it explores diverse applications of graph SSL, highlighting notable methods such as COAD, which addresses zero-shot expert linking through contrastive learning, and SLAPS, which incorporates denoising tasks into classification models. Other mentioned approaches include SCRL for few-label graph learning, SDGE for community detection, and DrRepair for program repair using diagnostic feedback. The document also covers applications in medical image representation learning and federated learning, showcasing the versatility of graph SSL techniques across different domains. The referenced studies underscore the ongoing advancements in graph neural networks and their applications in various fields, emphasizing the importance of self-supervised learning strategies.

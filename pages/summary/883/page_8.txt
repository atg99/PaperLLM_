The paper presents InST, a novel framework for example-guided artistic image generation, which leverages high-level textual descriptions of paintings to guide a text-to-image generative model for creating images with specific artistic characteristics. The quantitative evaluation, as shown in Table 2, indicates that InST outperforms several existing methods in visual characteristic transfer, achieving the highest preference percentages across various tasks. Participants in the study ranked visual factors influencing their preferences, with the most valued factor being the similarity of artistic effects on corresponding subjects. The study highlighted limitations in color transfer, particularly when there are significant differences between the content and reference images. To address this, the authors incorporated a tone transfer module, although they acknowledge varying user preferences regarding color retention. The conclusion emphasizes the effectiveness of InST in generating superior artistic images and its potential to advance future artistic synthesis tasks. The research received funding from multiple national programs in China.

This paper introduces a novel framework for artistic image generation called Inversion-based Style Transfer (InST), which addresses limitations in current text-to-image synthesis and classical style transfer methods. The framework allows for generating high-quality artistic images using a single input painting and a simple text prompt, effectively transferring the painting's style attributes—such as material, brushstrokes, and colors—while maintaining content diversity.

InST leverages recent advancements in diffusion models and inversion techniques to achieve its objectives. The method involves obtaining a representation of the image style through an attention mechanism that extracts key features from the reference painting. This representation is then utilized by diffusion models to generate new images that embody the learned style while adhering to the content dictated by the text description.

The paper presents comprehensive experiments demonstrating InST's effectiveness across various artistic styles and content types. Results indicate that InST significantly enhances visual quality and artistic consistency compared to existing state-of-the-art approaches, showcasing its generality, precision, and adaptability in generating artistic images.

The related work section discusses prior research in image style transfer, highlighting the evolution from traditional methods relying on handcrafted features to modern techniques employing deep learning and attention mechanisms for arbitrary style transfer. The findings position InST as a significant advancement in the field of artistic image generation.

2. The concept of essence transfer guided by CLIP is explored (Chefer et al., 2022).
3. Internal-external learning in artistic style transfer is presented (Chen et al., 2021).
4. Denoising diffusion probabilistic models are conditioned for improved performance (Choi et al., 2021).
5. VQGAN-CLIP combines open-domain image generation with natural language guidance (Crowson et al., 2022).
6. Multi-channel correlation techniques for video style transfer are discussed (Deng et al., 2021).
7. StyTr2 employs transformers for image style transfer (Deng et al., 2022).
8. Multi-adaptation networks facilitate arbitrary style transfer (Deng et al., 2020).
9. Diffusion models are shown to outperform GANs in image synthesis (Dhariwal & Nichol, 2021).
10. CogView2 enhances text-to-image generation using hierarchical transformers (Ding et al., 2022).

Additional references explore personalized text-to-image generation, style transfer using CLIP, and real-time arbitrary style transfer techniques, contributing to the growing body of research aimed at enhancing generative models in computer vision.

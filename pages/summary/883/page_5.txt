The paper discusses a novel image style transfer method that employs a denoising U-Net within a diffusion model to enhance content preservation during image generation. The method involves predicting noise from the input image, which is then utilized as initial input noise to maintain the integrity of the content. The stochastic inversion module processes the image latent code to derive a noisy version, facilitating the computation of noise estimates. Experimental results are presented, showcasing visual comparisons with state-of-the-art methods, affirming the efficacy of the proposed approach. The findings are illustrated in accompanying figures, emphasizing the advancements made in image style transfer techniques.

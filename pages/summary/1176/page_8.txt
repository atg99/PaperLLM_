The study by Shi et al. investigates clustering methods using simulated datasets with both uniform and multivariate normal distributions. Four clusters were generated, with two following a uniform distribution and two adhering to multivariate distributions, as specified in Table 2. The optimal number of clusters, Kopt, was estimated within the range of 1 to 10. 

The results indicate that both the Silhouette method and the proposed method successfully identified the optimal cluster number of four for the uniform distribution, aligning with the actual number of clusters, as shown in Fig. 2. The Silhouette method produced a maximum silhouette score, while the proposed method achieved this through minimizing an angle. 

In contrast, while the proposed method maintained the same optimal cluster number for the multivariate normal distribution, the Silhouette method did not yield this result. Execution times for both methods across various datasets are detailed in Table 3, demonstrating that the proposed method consistently outperformed the Silhouette method in terms of speed.

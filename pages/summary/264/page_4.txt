This paper presents a novel architecture for speech emotion recognition (SER) utilizing capsule networks (CapsNets). The proposed systems, including CNN SeqCap and CNN RecCap, demonstrate improved performance over baseline models, particularly in recognizing emotions like Neutral, Angry, and Sad. The CNN GRU-SeqCap system achieves the highest performance metrics, indicating the effectiveness of combining CapsNets with recurrent neural networks (RNNs).

The methodology involves splitting spectrograms into 2-second segments, with each segment labeled by emotion. The spectrograms are processed using a Hanning window and normalized for training. Two evaluation metrics are employed: Weighted Accuracy (WA) and Unweighted Accuracy (UA). Weight initialization and a dynamic learning rate adjustment are integral to the training process.

Results indicate that while CapsNets excel at capturing spatial information, they struggle with recognizing the Happy emotion due to its reliance on contextual information. The introduction of recurrent connections in the routing process enhances performance in this category, significantly improving accuracy metrics. The combined approach of CNN and RNN, as seen in CNN GRU-SeqCap, further solidifies the capability of the model.

In conclusion, the paper highlights the potential of CapsNets in SER and suggests future research to enhance their ability to capture time-variable emotional characteristics.

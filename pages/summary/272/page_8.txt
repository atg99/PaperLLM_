The paper discusses the performance of Graph Transformer Networks (GTNs) in identifying and ranking meta-paths in various datasets, specifically focusing on their consistency with pre-defined meta-paths used in literature and their ability to discover new, relevant meta-paths. 

In Table 3, the authors present a comparison of pre-defined meta-paths and the top-ranked meta-paths identified by GTNs for different datasets, including DBLP, ACM, and IMDB. The GTNs not only recognize the importance of established meta-paths but also reveal new meta-paths that enhance understanding of relationships between nodes. For instance, in the DBLP dataset, GTNs identified CPCPA as a significant meta-path, which is not part of the pre-defined set, suggesting a connection between an author's research area and publication venues.

Figure 3 illustrates the attention scores from adjacency matrices for each dataset, indicating the significance of different edge types. The results show that GTNs can learn effective shorter meta-paths, particularly in the IMDB dataset, where identity matrices received higher attention scores. This adaptability highlights the GTNs' capacity to optimize meta-path length based on dataset characteristics, ultimately contributing to improved node classification through enhanced interpretability.

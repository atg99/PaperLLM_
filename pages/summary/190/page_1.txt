The paper presents ActFormer, a Generative Adversarial Network (GAN)-based Transformer model designed for general action-conditioned 3D human motion generation. This model effectively generates both single-person and multi-person interactive actions. ActFormer integrates a robust Action-conditioned motion Transformer with a GAN training scheme and utilizes a Gaussian Process latent prior, which enhances spatio-temporal representation, generative modeling, and captures temporal correlations.

ActFormer is particularly notable for its ability to extend to multi-person motions by modeling temporal correlations and human interactions through Transformer encoders. The authors also introduce a new synthetic dataset featuring complex multi-person combat behaviors to support research in this area. 

Extensive experiments conducted on datasets such as NTU-13, NTU RGB+D 120, BABEL, and the proposed combat dataset demonstrate that ActFormer outperforms state-of-the-art methods in both single-person and multi-person motion generation tasks. This work represents a significant advancement towards creating a general human motion generator, which is crucial for applications in character animation, humanoid robot interaction, and data synthesis for computer vision tasks.

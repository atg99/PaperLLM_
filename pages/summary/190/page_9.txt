The paper discusses the limitations and broader impacts of the proposed generative method for synthesizing multi-person interactive actions. One key limitation noted is the inability of the ActFormer model to effectively categorize individuals into different interaction groups when provided with MoCap samples containing multiple persons. This limitation arises because the model concatenates multi-person motions before inputting them to the GCN discriminator, preventing the learning of a shared model for variable numbers of persons. 

The broader impacts highlight the potential for misuse of the generative method, particularly with the GTA Combat dataset, which includes fighting scenes. The authors emphasize that the dataset should only be used for research purposes and do not endorse violence.

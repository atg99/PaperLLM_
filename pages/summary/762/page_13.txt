The paper discusses the performance of various summarization models, specifically BART-based models, in generating summaries of conversations. It highlights the effectiveness of structured views, such as stage-view and topic-view, in reducing redundancy and incorrect reasoning in the generated summaries. The models were evaluated on their ability to capture key information, with the multi-view model, which integrates both stage and topic perspectives, demonstrating the least number of errors compared to single-view models. The analysis reveals common error types across the models, including missing information, redundancy, and incorrect reasoning, emphasizing the importance of structured approaches in enhancing the quality of conversation summarization. The findings suggest that incorporating different views can significantly improve model performance in summarizing complex dialogues.

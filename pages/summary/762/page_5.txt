The paper presents a novel multi-view decoder for dialogue summarization, leveraging different conversational aspects to enhance summary generation. The current view is represented through the last hidden state, denoted as Vk, and the proposed transformer-based architecture integrates multiple encoded representations to improve attention allocation on utterances. The decoder uses a multi-view attention layer that prioritizes the importance of each view, calculated through a specific formula involving context vectors and a sharpening function to refine attention weights. This mechanism allows the model to focus on relevant parts of the conversation more effectively.

During training, the model minimizes cross-entropy loss using a teacher forcing strategy, where inputs are derived from the ground truth during training and from predictions during testing. The model is evaluated on the SAMSum dataset, which comprises 14,732 dialogues with human-written summaries, and compared against several baseline models, including Pointer Generator, DynamicConv + GPT-2, Fast Abs RL Enhanced, and BART with generic views. 

The experimental setup includes pre-trained sentence representations and hyperparameter tuning for extracting topic and stage views. The findings suggest that the multi-view approach significantly enhances the quality of dialogue summaries by effectively combining various conversational perspectives.

6. Contextual dependency Conversations often rely on context for meaning, and models must capture this context to generate coherent summaries. This is similar to challenges faced in narrative summarization where understanding the plot requires grasping character relationships and events.

7. Variability in expression Participants may express similar ideas in diverse ways, leading to variability that models must navigate to ensure accurate representation in summaries.

In conclusion, despite achieving reasonable summarization performance with the Multi-view BART model, the study highlights significant challenges in dialogue summarization. These challenges stem from informal language, multiple participants and turns, referral and coreference issues, repetition, interruption, contextual dependencies, and variability in expression. Addressing these challenges is essential for advancing future research in this domain. The findings emphasize the importance of utilizing different structured views to enhance model performance while also pointing out the intricate nature of dialogue summarization tasks.

In this paper, the authors address the limitations of existing conversation summarization methods, which often rely on fixed topic views and fail to capture the nuanced structures of dialogues. They propose a novel approach that combines multiple views of conversations—topic view, stage view, global view, and discrete view—to enhance the summarization process. The multi-view sequence-to-sequence model they develop includes a conversation encoder for different views and a multi-view decoder with attention mechanisms to produce more accurate summaries.

The authors validate their approach through experiments on the SAMSum dataset and provide error analyses to highlight challenges in current methods. Their contributions aim to improve the quality of abstractive conversation summarization by leveraging rich conversational structures and addressing the shortcomings of existing techniques.

The paper presents a Multi-view Sequence-to-Sequence Model for dialogue summarization, addressing the limitations of existing methods that often overlook the diverse structures inherent in conversations. The proposed model architecture includes a conversation encoder and a multi-view decoder. The encoder processes different views of conversations, encoding each view through LSTM to represent the blocks of dialogue. The multi-view decoder then applies attention mechanisms to these views to generate comprehensive summaries.

Previous works in dialogue summarization have utilized techniques like multi-sentence compression and hierarchical models, focusing on either turn-based utterances or specific conversational elements such as dialogue acts and key points. However, these methods often require extensive human annotation or fail to capture the complexity of conversational structures, such as reply relations and dialogue stages.

To enhance summarization quality, the authors extract various views from conversations, including topic segments and conversational stages, which allow the model to focus on distinct aspects of dialogue. This structured approach aims to minimize information loss during encoding, thereby reducing errors in the decoding stage. By leveraging these diverse conversational structures, the model seeks to improve the consistency and relevance of generated summaries.

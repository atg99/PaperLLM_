This collection of references covers a range of studies focused on multi-document summarization and related natural language processing tasks. Fabbri et al. introduced the Multi-news dataset for large-scale summarization, while Falke et al. explored concept-map-based summarization using coreference resolution. Galley et al. addressed discourse segmentation in conversations, and Gao et al. discussed advancements in summarization tasks.

Gliwa et al. presented the SAMSum corpus, a dialogue dataset for abstractive summarization, and Goo and Chen proposed a model optimized by dialogue acts for summarizing conversations. Hochreiter and Schmidhuber's seminal work on Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks is also referenced, alongside studies on conversation structure and speech act modeling.

Koo and Li provided guidelines for reporting intraclass correlation coefficients, while Lewis et al. introduced BART, a model for various natural language generation tasks. Li et al. proposed a generic neural text segmentation model, and other studies examined multi-modal meeting summarization and dialogue summary generation for customer service.

Liu et al. and Liu and Lapata focused on text summarization techniques utilizing pretrained encoders, showcasing the ongoing evolution in summarization methodologies within the field. Overall, these works contribute to the understanding and development of effective summarization techniques in natural language processing.

3.3. Proposed CNN Architecture
Our proposed convolutional neural network (CNN) architecture incorporates shortcut connections and a widening factor to optimize performance while maintaining a reduced parameter count. The network begins with the log mel spectrogram as input, followed by several convolutional layers designed to extract relevant features from the audio data. The architecture is structured to efficiently process the input while addressing challenges such as the vanishing gradient problem through the use of residual connections.

4. Experimental Results
We evaluate our system on three datasets: ESC-50, UrbanSound8K, and RA VDESS. Our results demonstrate state-of-the-art accuracies of 0.961, 0.908, and 0.748, respectively, surpassing previous best systems. Notably, our system achieves these results with significantly lower computational requirements, being 7.1 times faster and having 9.7 times fewer parameters compared to the state-of-the-art system that achieved an accuracy of 0.485 on the AudioSet dataset.

5. Conclusion
In conclusion, our proposed audio processing system shows substantial improvements in speed and efficiency while achieving competitive accuracy in audio pattern recognition tasks. The combination of advanced feature extraction techniques, effective data augmentation strategies, and a well-structured CNN architecture contributes to the system's superior performance. Future work may explore further enhancements in model efficiency and the application of the system to other audio analysis tasks.

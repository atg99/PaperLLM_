This collection of references encompasses a range of studies focused on advancements in audio processing, deep learning methodologies, and applications in sound recognition and classification. 

Kong et al. introduce PANNs, large-scale pretrained audio neural networks designed for audio pattern recognition, highlighting their effectiveness in various tasks. Lopez-Meyer et al. present efficient end-to-end audio embedding generation techniques tailored for specific applications. Guzhov et al. discuss the ESResNe(X)t-fbsp model, emphasizing robust time-frequency transformations for audio data.

Pham et al.'s work on Meta Pseudo Labels explores improved labeling techniques in machine learning, while Deng et al. provide foundational insights into the ImageNet database, crucial for image classification tasks. Tan and Le's EfficientNet rethinks model scaling for convolutional neural networks, optimizing performance and efficiency.

Zagoruyko and Komodakis delve into Wide Residual Networks, which enhance learning in deep networks. Park et al. introduce SpecAugment, a data augmentation method for automatic speech recognition, contributing to the robustness of speech models. Tokozume et al. focus on learning from between-class examples, advancing deep sound recognition methodologies.

Additional references cover various techniques for environmental sound classification, including the MFCC-GMM approach by Vuegen et al. and the end-to-end classification method by Abdoli et al. The collection also includes significant contributions to image recognition and optimization, such as the deep residual learning framework by He et al. and the Adam optimization algorithm by Kingma and Ba.

Overall, these studies collectively advance the understanding and application of deep learning in audio and image processing, providing essential methodologies and frameworks for future research in these domains.

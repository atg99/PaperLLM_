In the ESC-50 dataset, the best-performing system, ERANN-2-5, outperforms the previous state-of-the-art AST system in terms of accuracy while being more efficient with fewer parameters and faster training times. The introduction of transfer learning further enhances performance and reduces training time significantly.

For the UrbanSound8K dataset, the ERANN-2-6 model achieved an accuracy of 0.908, surpassing the previous best system. The analysis indicates that the proposed data augmentation techniques, such as modified mixup and SpecAugment, contribute to improved model performance.

In the RA VDESS dataset, the use of pitch shifting during training was shown to enhance performance, although pre-training on the AudioSet dataset did not yield improvements. The results emphasize that while the proposed methods are effective for various audio processing tasks, their impact can vary depending on the specific dataset and task.

Overall, this work demonstrates the efficacy of ERANN models in audio processing tasks, with notable improvements in speed, accuracy, and computational efficiency through strategic training techniques and architecture design.

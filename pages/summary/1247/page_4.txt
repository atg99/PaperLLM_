The paper introduces NGBoost, an innovative approach to gradient boosting tailored for probabilistic predictions. It emphasizes the importance of using natural gradients, which enhance optimization by making it invariant to parameterization and improving learning dynamics. The authors demonstrate the differences between traditional gradients and natural gradients through visualizations of gradient fields, particularly in the context of fitting a Normal distribution.

Gradient boosting, as described, is a supervised learning technique that combines multiple weak learners sequentially, typically using shallow decision trees. Each tree is fitted against the residuals of the current model to minimize loss. The paper outlines how this framework can be adapted for probabilistic regression, focusing on estimating the parameters of a conditional probability distribution based on input features.

NGBoost specifically aims to predict parameters of distributions, allowing for various types of outputs, including real-valued and categorical data. The methodology presented in the paper showcases the versatility of NGBoost in probabilistic contexts while maintaining the foundational principles of gradient boosting.

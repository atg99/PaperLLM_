The paper introduces Natural Gradient Boosting (NGBoost), a modular framework designed for probabilistic prediction in regression tasks. Traditional regression methods often struggle with providing uncertainty estimates, particularly when they assume homoscedasticity or require complex Bayesian approaches that are computationally intensive and require statistical expertise. While Gradient Boosting Machines (GBMs) excel at structured data and perform well in competitions, they typically output only point estimates in regression tasks, lacking the ability to convey predictive uncertainty effectively.

NGBoost addresses this gap by leveraging a multiparameter boosting approach and natural gradients. The method allows for the integration of various components, including base learners, parametric probability distributions, and scoring rules. This flexibility enables the model to produce a complete probability distribution for predictions rather than just point estimates. The authors also generalize the natural gradient to accommodate different scoring rules, such as the Continuous Ranked Probability Score (CRPS).

The paper empirically demonstrates that NGBoost competes effectively with existing models in terms of both predictive uncertainty estimates and point predictions, making it a robust choice for probabilistic regression tasks. Overall, NGBoost represents a significant advancement in the ability to provide nuanced predictions that encompass both expected values and associated uncertainties.

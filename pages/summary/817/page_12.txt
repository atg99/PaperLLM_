The referenced works focus on advancements in deep learning, particularly in the context of model compression, object detection, and optimization techniques. 

Yu et al. (2017) explore compressing deep models through low-rank and sparse decomposition, highlighting methods that enhance efficiency without significantly sacrificing performance. Zhao et al. (2019) provide a comprehensive review of deep learning approaches to object detection, discussing various techniques and their effectiveness in practical applications. Zhou et al. (2019) propose a method to accelerate convolutional neural networks (CNNs) using recursive Bayesian pruning, which aims to streamline the processing speed while maintaining accuracy. 

Zhuang et al. (2018) introduce discrimination-aware channel pruning, a technique designed to improve the efficiency of deep neural networks by selectively pruning less critical channels based on their contribution to model performance. Lastly, Zhuo et al. (2020) present co-gradient descent for bilinear optimization, offering a novel optimization framework that can enhance the training of deep learning models. 

Collectively, these studies contribute to the ongoing efforts to optimize deep learning models for better performance and efficiency in various applications.

The referenced works collectively address advancements in deep learning and neural network optimization, focusing on techniques for improving efficiency and performance. The foundational work by Jia Deng et al. on ImageNet established a large-scale image database crucial for training deep learning models. Subsequent studies explore various methods for model compression and acceleration, such as the Centripetal SGD by Ding et al., which prunes deep convolutional networks, and the geometric median approach for filter pruning by He et al.

The concept of binary neural networks is discussed by Han et al., emphasizing training with noisy supervision, while Ioffe and Szegedy's contribution on batch normalization addresses internal covariate shifts during training. Techniques like the KnockoffGAN by Jordon et al. introduce innovative methods for feature selection using generative adversarial networks.

Several studies focus on specific pruning techniques to enhance computational efficiency, including the filter pruning methods by Li et al. and Liebenwein et al., which aim to maintain performance while reducing network complexity. The works of Lin et al. and He et al. further investigate filter and channel pruning strategies for accelerating very deep neural networks.

Overall, these contributions illustrate a significant trend towards optimizing deep learning architectures, balancing complexity and computational cost while striving for high performance in various applications.

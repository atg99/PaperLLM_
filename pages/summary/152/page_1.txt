In our analysis, we evaluate several single-image and sequential VAE models across diverse image and video datasets. We introduce a novel modification to the Gaussian decoder, enabling it to compute prediction variance analytically, which enhances performance without the complications of tuning additional hyperparameters. This approach allows the decoder to learn the uncertainty of the output distribution naturally, leading to improved model robustness.

Overall, our findings demonstrate that calibrated decoders can significantly enhance VAE performance while simplifying the training process. By providing clear recommendations for the use of calibrated decoders, we aim to streamline VAE training and make it more accessible for various applications in probabilistic modeling and representation learning.

The proposed TCaS algorithm takes into account the unique characteristics of Cloud–Fog computing, including the distribution of resources and the varying capabilities of Fog nodes. By doing so, it aims to optimize task scheduling based on both time and cost parameters.

The motivation for this research stems from the rapid growth of IoT devices and the limitations of traditional Cloud architecture in handling the massive data generated by these devices. The paper highlights the need for an efficient solution that can manage the interplay between latency-sensitive tasks and the resource constraints of Fog nodes. It discusses the advantages of integrating Fog computing with Cloud resources to enhance performance and efficiency.

The proposed TCaS algorithm addresses the resource allocation and task scheduling challenges inherent in a Cloud–Fog environment, particularly for Bag-of-Tasks applications, which consist of independent, parallel tasks. The algorithm seeks to balance the priorities of users, whether they favor quicker completion times or lower costs, thus ensuring a satisfactory Quality of Service.

In summary, this paper presents an innovative approach to task scheduling in a Cloud–Fog framework, emphasizing the importance of optimizing execution time and cost to meet user demands effectively.

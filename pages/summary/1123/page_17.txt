The paper presents a comparative analysis of four algorithms—TCaS, BLA, MPSO, and RR—focused on their performance in terms of makespan and total cost across varying numbers of tasks. Results indicate that TCaS consistently outperformed the other algorithms in both metrics, demonstrating superior time optimization capabilities. MPSO showed better performance than BLA in complex environments, while the RR algorithm performed poorly due to its simplistic mechanism.

The study also examined the convergence behavior of TCaS, BLA, and MPSO over 500 generations with a dataset of 120 tasks. TCaS achieved a higher average fitness value of 0.84, compared to 0.69 for BLA and 0.72 for MPSO. TCaS maintained population diversity, allowing it to avoid local extremes and converge to more optimal solutions, while BLA and MPSO converged quickly but at lower fitness values due to their mechanisms leading to local optima.

Overall, the findings highlight the effectiveness of TCaS in optimizing task scheduling while also addressing the convergence challenges faced by the other algorithms.

The paper presents a comparison of four algorithms—TCaS, BLA, MPSO, and RR—evaluating their performance based on time-cost trade-offs across various datasets. The proposed TCaS algorithm consistently outperformed the others, achieving an average fitness value of 0.945, surpassing MPSO, BLA, and RR by 7.97%, 8.17%, and 44.08% respectively. In smaller datasets (from 40 to 200 tasks), BLA performed better than MPSO; however, as dataset size increased, BLA's performance declined while MPSO remained stable, leading to MPSO outperforming BLA by 1.89% in larger datasets (250 to 500 tasks). The RR algorithm yielded the lowest fitness value at approximately 0.65.

Table 7 illustrates the makespan and total cost for each algorithm, indicating that TCaS not only excelled in fitness but also in execution time and cost. Specifically, TCaS required 16.15% less time than MPSO and 16.77% less than BLA, while saving an average of 2.32 times compared to RR across all datasets. In terms of monetary cost, TCaS generally required the least expense, except for the smallest dataset of 40 tasks where BLA was more efficient. Overall, TCaS demonstrated superior performance in both time and cost metrics across the majority of datasets analyzed.

The paper discusses the challenges posed by the Internet of Things (IoT) on Cloud computing infrastructure due to the massive data generated by IoT devices. It introduces Fog computing as a solution to these challenges, aiming to optimize task scheduling in a Cloud-Fog environment for Bag-of-Tasks applications. The proposed algorithm, named TCaS, focuses on minimizing execution time and operating costs.

The study evaluates TCaS against existing algorithms, specifically the Bee Life Algorithm (BLA) and Modified Particle Swarm Optimization (MPSO), using 11 datasets of varying sizes. The results indicate that TCaS outperforms BLA by 15.11% and MPSO by 11.04%, achieving a balance between completion time and operating costs. The paper underscores the significance of effective resource distribution in Fog computing to address the increasing demands of IoT applications.

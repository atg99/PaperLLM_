In Scenario 2, the experiments utilized a complete Cloud-Fog system with 10 Fog nodes and three powerful Cloud nodes, leading to a more complex task distribution. The results indicated that the TCaS algorithm consistently outperformed other algorithms (MPSO, BLA, and RR) across various datasets, particularly excelling on smaller datasets with 40 to 200 tasks. 

The analysis revealed that the fitness values of all algorithms decreased as the number of tasks increased, due to the larger search space that hindered convergence within limited iterations. MPSO showed better performance than BLA, while RR consistently had the lowest fitness value. In terms of execution time, TCaS demonstrated significant efficiency, reducing processing time by an average of 35.73% compared to MPSO, 42.20% compared to BLA, and 176.25% compared to RR. However, MPSO was more cost-effective overall, with TCaS having a marginally higher monetary cost of 5.96%. 

Ultimately, the findings support the conclusion that TCaS strikes an optimal balance between scheduling efficiency and cost, making it a valuable solution for enhancing user experience through improved response times in task scheduling scenarios.

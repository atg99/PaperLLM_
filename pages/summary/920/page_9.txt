This paper compiles a comprehensive list of references pertinent to deep metric learning and related fields, showcasing significant contributions from various authors. The references span multiple conferences and journals, highlighting advancements in techniques such as triplet loss, deep embedding learning, and meta-learning.

Key works include:

1. Andrychowicz et al. (2016) introduced a method for learning to learn using gradient descent, impacting the approach to optimization in neural networks.
2. Cakir et al. (2019) focused on deep metric learning to rank, which is vital for improving retrieval tasks.
3. Chen et al. (2017) proposed a quadruplet network for person re-identification, extending the capabilities of existing loss functions.
4. Finn et al. (2017) presented model-agnostic meta-learning, enabling rapid adaptation of models to new tasks.
5. Schroff et al. (2015) developed FaceNet, a unified embedding approach for face recognition, which became a benchmark in the field.

The references illustrate the evolution of methodologies in deep learning, emphasizing the importance of loss functions and network architectures in achieving robust performance across various applications. These studies collectively contribute to the ongoing discourse in deep learning, particularly in enhancing metric learning strategies for diverse tasks.

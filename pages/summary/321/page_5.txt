In this paper, the authors present pretrained BERT models specifically designed for clinical applications, some of which are trained exclusively on clinical text while others are fine-tuned from BioBERT. The results demonstrate that these clinical embeddings outperform both general domain and BioBERT embeddings for non-de-identification tasks. Furthermore, using note-type specific corpora enhances performance even further. This study is noted as the first to release clinically trained BERT models, aiming to provide clinical NLP researchers with valuable resources that do not require extensive computational power for training on the MIMIC corpus.

In the paper "Deep Metric Learning to Rank," the authors introduce FastAP, a novel deep metric learning method that optimizes the rank-based Average Precision measure through a distance quantization approximation. FastAP is designed for low complexity and is well-suited for stochastic gradient descent, allowing for efficient training. 

The authors propose a new minibatch sampling strategy and a heuristic for large-batch training to enhance the effectiveness of the ranking approach. They emphasize that metric learning is crucial in nearest neighbor retrieval, where the goal is to ensure that true neighbors are closer than non-neighbors in the learned metric space. By framing metric learning as a learning to rank problem, FastAP sidesteps the common issue of high-order training set expansion seen in other methods that rely on pairs or triplets.

The main contribution of FastAP is its ability to optimize Average Precision, a widely used performance metric in various domains including image retrieval and few-shot learning. The authors demonstrate the effectiveness of FastAP through evaluations on three few-shot image retrieval datasets, where it consistently outperforms existing methods that often involve complex optimization techniques or model ensembles.

The referenced papers primarily focus on advancements in reinforcement learning and related optimization techniques. Key contributions include:

1. Trust Region Policy Optimization (Schulman et al., 2015) proposes a method to stabilize policy updates by constraining them within a trust region, enhancing performance in policy gradient methods.

2. Sutton and Barto (2018) provide a comprehensive introduction to reinforcement learning, detailing foundational concepts and algorithms.

3. Various studies explore the convergence properties of reinforcement learning algorithms. For instance, Xu et al. (2019, 2020) analyze the convergence of policy gradient methods, highlighting improvements in sample efficiency and variance reduction.

4. The Q-learning algorithm (Watkins and Dayan, 1992) is a cornerstone of reinforcement learning, with subsequent works investigating its performance under different conditions, such as neural network function approximation (Xu and Gu, 2019).

5. Actor-critic methods are examined through multiple lenses, including finite sample analysis (Yang et al., 2018) and global convergence (Zhang et al., 2019), addressing their effectiveness in various scenarios, including linear quadratic regulators.

Overall, these contributions collectively advance the understanding and application of reinforcement learning, focusing on optimization techniques, convergence analysis, and sample efficiency.

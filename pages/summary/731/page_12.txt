The paper by Zhang et al. presents a two-timescale off-policy actor-critic algorithm that is provably convergent, even when using function approximation. This work addresses the challenges associated with off-policy learning, particularly the stability and convergence issues that arise when combining function approximation with off-policy methods. The authors provide theoretical guarantees for their algorithm, demonstrating its effectiveness in various settings.

Zou et al. focus on the finite-sample analysis of the SARSA algorithm when linear function approximation is employed. They analyze the performance of SARSA under finite samples, providing insights into its convergence properties and efficiency. Their findings contribute to understanding the implications of using function approximation in reinforcement learning, particularly regarding sample complexity and learning efficiency.

Both papers contribute significantly to the field of reinforcement learning, exploring the intersection of function approximation and off-policy learning while providing theoretical frameworks for understanding their behavior and convergence.

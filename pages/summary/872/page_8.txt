The paper discusses advancements in referring image segmentation through the introduction of cross-modal self-attention and gated multi-level fusion modules. These innovations address significant challenges in the task by effectively capturing long-range dependencies between visual and linguistic data, thereby enhancing feature representation for referred entities. The gated multi-level fusion module dynamically integrates features from various levels using learnable gates tailored to each level, leading to improved results. The proposed network has achieved state-of-the-art performance across four benchmark datasets. The authors acknowledge support from various funding sources and express gratitude to NVIDIA for GPU contributions. Additionally, the paper highlights some failure examples where the model struggled due to language ambiguity, similar object appearances, and occlusion, suggesting that employing object detectors may mitigate these issues.

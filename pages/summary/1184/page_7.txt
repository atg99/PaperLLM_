The paper "Safe Semi-Supervised Learning: A Brief Introduction" by Yu-Feng Li et al. addresses the challenges and methodologies in semi-supervised learning, particularly focusing on ensuring safety in the utilization of unlabeled data. The authors highlight the importance of leveraging both labeled and unlabeled data to improve learning outcomes while minimizing risks associated with incorrect assumptions about the unlabeled data.

Key concepts discussed include various techniques and frameworks that have been proposed in the domain, such as graph-based methods, consistency regularization, and co-training approaches. The authors emphasize the need for robust strategies that can effectively integrate unlabeled data without degrading the performance of the learning model.

The research is supported by notable funding sources, including the National Key R&D Program of China and the National Natural Science Foundation of China. The paper concludes with a comprehensive reference list, showcasing significant contributions and studies in the field of semi-supervised learning, reflecting a rich background of prior research that the current study builds upon.

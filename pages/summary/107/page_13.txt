The paper presents an analysis of power consumption predictions using three different models: Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM), Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU), and Drop-GRU. The results from a 3-day prediction experiment indicate that while all models track the trends in power consumption effectively, the GRU model outperforms the others in terms of capturing higher values of power consumption. In a subsequent 7-day prediction experiment, it is observed that although the models detect the peaks in power consumption accurately, the predicted values tend to be lower than the actual measurements. The findings highlight the GRU model's superior performance in forecasting high power consumption values, establishing it as the most effective model among those tested.

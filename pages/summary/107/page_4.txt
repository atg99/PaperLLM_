The paper outlines a methodology for analyzing power consumption data, which involves several key steps. Initially, the process includes data pre-processing, where missing values are addressed and normalization is applied. Following this, feature selection is performed, and parameters are set for a train-test split. The architecture of the neural network is then defined, exploring various models such as LSTM, GRU, and Drop-GRU. 

Model performance is evaluated using metrics like RMSE and MAE, leading to the selection of an appropriate model for future predictions. The analysis reveals that power consumption exhibits diverse distribution characteristics across different areas, with profiles identified as cyclical, irregular, stationary, and trend-based. Figures included in the paper illustrate the power consumption patterns for two distinct zones over time.

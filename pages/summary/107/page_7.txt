The paper discusses a framework for power consumption forecasting using Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks, illustrated in Figure 6. It emphasizes the importance of selecting optimal parameters for neural network architectures, as this can significantly impact performance. Table 1 details the trainable weights within the LSTM network, including weights and biases associated with input and hidden layers.

Additionally, the paper introduces the Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU), a popular variant of the Recurrent Neural Network (RNN) that streamlines the LSTM structure. The GRU combines the input and forget gates of LSTM into a single update gate, which manages the retention of previous information. It features two gates: the update gate, which regulates the retention of information, and the reset gate, which determines the relationship between previous and current states.

The paper discusses various methods for forecasting and controlling power consumption, focusing on three main techniques: Drop-GRU, GRU, and LSTM. The authors highlight that effective feature extraction and appropriate network structures are crucial for optimizing prediction capabilities. The results indicate that Drop-GRU outperforms both GRU and LSTM in predicting energy consumption, particularly for identifying consumption peaks and informing load-shedding strategies. Future research will aim to enhance predictive accuracy and speed by incorporating external factors such as weather data and holidays. The paper emphasizes the importance of these predictions in managing electrical grid stability and preventing overloads. The authors contributed in various capacities, including conceptualization, methodology, and writing, and declare no conflicts of interest or external funding.

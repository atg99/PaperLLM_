The paper presents the use of different machine learning models—LSTM, GRU, and Drop-GRU—to detect power consumption peaks over a one-day period. Figures 20, 21, and 22 illustrate the predicted power consumption from each model, comparing it against the average power consumption and a predefined consumption threshold. The models are evaluated on their ability to identify instances of load shedding, which is crucial for managing energy efficiency and preventing overloads. The findings demonstrate the effectiveness of these models in real-time power consumption monitoring.

This section discusses the internal structure of the Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) model, outlining its key formulas and components. The update gate (zt) and reset gate (rt) are defined, along with the candidate activation (at) and the output layer (ht) at time t. The GRU model effectively processes sequential data by managing the flow of information through its gates.

Additionally, the paper introduces the neural network model setup, specifically focusing on the gradient descent algorithm for optimization. The algorithm aims to minimize an energy function defined by constraints, which requires significant time and computational resources for convergence. The optimizer adjusts the model's weights and biases to achieve rapid convergence to the optimal value, thereby minimizing loss. The gradient vector provides the necessary direction for updating the variables, with a recursive equation established for this purpose. The Adam algorithm is selected as the optimal optimizer for enhancing network performance and weight updates.

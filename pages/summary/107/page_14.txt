The paper presents a series of experiments focusing on the forecasting of power consumption using three different machine learning models: LSTM, GRU, and Drop-GRU. The results from the first experiment, which involved a 7-day prediction period, are illustrated in Figures 16 and 17, demonstrating the models' capabilities in predicting power consumption patterns. The subsequent Experiment 4 extends the prediction horizon to 15 days, with Figures 18 and 19 showcasing the models' performance in detecting power peaks. The findings indicate an improvement in prediction accuracy, particularly in peak detection, compared to earlier experiments, highlighting the effectiveness of these models in power consumption forecasting.

The paper discusses the prediction of power consumption using various models, including LSTM, GRU, and Drop-GRU, with a focus on a 15-day forecast for a specific area. Figures 18 and 19 present the predicted power consumption alongside real data, demonstrating the models' performance. 

Additionally, the paper outlines a method for detecting power consumption peaks and implementing load shedding. It establishes a threshold where the maximum power consumption should not exceed 15% of the moving average power consumption. When predictions surpass this threshold, a peak is detected, prompting disconnection of the load. Figures 20 to 25 provide visual representations of this peak detection process.

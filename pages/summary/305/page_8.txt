This paper introduces the Attention on Attention (AoA) module, designed to enhance traditional attention mechanisms by addressing the issue of irrelevant attention in caption generation tasks. The authors present AoANet, which incorporates the AoA module into both the encoder and decoder for improved image captioning. 

In their experiments, they compare the performance of a base model with and without the AoA module. The base model often misinterprets attention results, leading to inaccurate captions, as illustrated by an example where it incorrectly identifies the object a teddy bear is sitting on. Conversely, the decoder with AoA effectively filters out misleading information, resulting in more accurate descriptions.

Human evaluations revealed that captions generated by the decoder with AoA were preferred by evaluators 49.15% of the time compared to 21.2% for the base model, underscoring the AoA's effectiveness. Additionally, the authors tested the AoA module on the MSR-VTT video captioning dataset, reporting significant improvements in evaluation metrics such as BLEU-4 and CIDEr-D.

The conclusion emphasizes the superiority and broad applicability of the AoA module, demonstrated by state-of-the-art performance on the MS COCO dataset. The research was supported by various institutions, and the authors acknowledge contributions from peers and reviewers.

The paper introduces the Attention on Attention (AoA) mechanism and its application to image captioning, contrasting it with traditional models like LSTM and GRU. The AoA module enhances the standard attention mechanism by measuring the relevance between the attention output and the query, thereby generating an "information vector" and an "attention gate" through linear transformations. This approach simplifies the architecture by requiring only one attention gate and eliminates the need for hidden states, making it more efficient for multi-modal fusion tasks.

The method is structured as follows: the attention module calculates weighted average vectors based on similarity scores, while the AoA module refines this process by applying an additional attention step through element-wise multiplication of the attention gate and the information vector. This results in a more contextually relevant attended information output.

In the development of AoANet for image captioning, both the encoder and decoder incorporate the AoA module. The encoder extracts feature vectors from images using a CNN or R-CNN, which are then processed by the AoA mechanism to improve the quality of the generated captions. This innovative approach aims to enhance the effectiveness of image captioning by leveraging the strengths of attention mechanisms while addressing their limitations.

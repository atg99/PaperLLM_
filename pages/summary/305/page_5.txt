The paper presents the performance of the proposed AoANet model compared to various state-of-the-art methods on the MS-COCO "Karpathy" test split, focusing on image captioning tasks. Table 1 summarizes the performance metrics including BLEU, METEOR, ROUGE-L, CIDEr-D, and SPICE scores, all reported as percentages. AoANet shows competitive results, achieving the highest CIDEr-D score among single models at 129.8 and excelling in ensemble settings with a score of 132.0.

Table 2 provides a leaderboard from the online MS-COCO test server, where AoANet maintains strong performance across BLEU and CIDEr-D metrics, indicating its effectiveness in generating accurate captions.

The architecture of AoANet incorporates an attention mechanism where the LSTM decoder processes inputs from both the current word embedding and a visual vector. The attention context is derived from an AoA module, enhancing the model's ability to focus on relevant features in the visual input. 

Training involves optimizing the cross-entropy loss initially, followed by refining the model using a CIDEr-D score optimization approach, which addresses the non-differentiable nature of certain metrics to improve caption quality.

Overall, AoANet represents a significant advancement in image captioning, demonstrating superior performance metrics and innovative architecture that leverages attention mechanisms effectively.

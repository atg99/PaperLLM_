In the paper "Attention on Attention for Image Captioning," the authors Lun Huang, Wenmin Wang, Jie Chen, and Xiao-Yong Wei propose an innovative module called "Attention on Attention" (AoA) to enhance image captioning models. Traditional attention mechanisms in encoder/decoder frameworks focus on generating a weighted average of encoded vectors to guide the decoding process. However, this approach lacks a way for the decoder to assess the relevance of the attended vector to the attention query, potentially leading to inaccurate results.

The AoA module addresses this limitation by first creating an "information vector" and an "attention gate" from the attention result and current context. It then combines these elements through element-wise multiplication to derive the "attended information," which represents the most useful knowledge for caption generation. The authors integrate AoA into both the encoder and decoder of their proposed model, named AoA Network (AoANet).

Experimental results demonstrate that AoANet surpasses existing methods, achieving a new state-of-the-art CIDEr-D score of 129.8 on the MS COCO "Karpathy" offline test split and 129.6 on the official online testing server. This work highlights the importance of evaluating the relationship between attention results and queries in improving image captioning performance. The code for AoANet is publicly available, allowing for further research and development in this area.

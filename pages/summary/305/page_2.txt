The paper introduces Attention on Attention (AoA), an enhancement to traditional attention mechanisms aimed at improving the relevance of attention results in queries. AoA creates an information vector and an attention gate through linear transformations, capturing the relationship between the query and the attention results. The attention gate, derived from the query, indicates the importance of information in the corresponding channels of the information vector. This approach leads to an "attended information" that is more relevant to the query. 

AoA can be integrated into various attention mechanisms, including single-head and multi-head attention, enhancing the relevance determination of attention outputs. The authors apply AoA to both the image encoder and caption decoder in their proposed model, AoANet. In the encoder, AoA models relationships among object feature vectors, while in the decoder, it filters out irrelevant attention results.

Results demonstrate that AoANet surpasses previous image captioning models, achieving a CIDEr-D score of 129.8 on the MS COCO dataset. The main contributions of the paper are the introduction of the AoA module, its application in the encoder and decoder of AoANet, and the establishment of new state-of-the-art performance in image captioning.

The paper also discusses related work in image captioning and attention mechanisms, highlighting the evolution from rule-based methods to neural-based frameworks, and the significant impact of attention mechanisms on various sequence learning tasks. The AoA technique shares similarities with existing methods, such as GLU and multi-modal fusion, emphasizing its innovative approach to enhancing attention mechanisms in deep learning models.

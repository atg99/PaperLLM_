This paper provides a comprehensive tutorial and survey on spectral, probabilistic, and deep metric learning. It begins by defining distance metrics and categorizing various metric learning techniques. 

In the spectral methods section, the authors explore techniques that utilize data scatters, hinge loss, locally linear metric adaptation, kernel methods, geometric approaches, and adversarial metric learning. The probabilistic methods encompass collapsing classes, neighborhood component analysis, Bayesian metric learning, information-theoretic methods, and empirical risk minimization approaches. 

The deep learning section discusses reconstruction autoencoders, supervised loss functions, Siamese networks, deep discriminant analysis, multi-modal learning, geometric deep metric learning, and few-shot metric learning. A specific few-shot metric learning approach is detailed, where pairs of data points are used to derive continuous similarity scores and optimize a loss function based on these scores.

The conclusion summarizes the paper's focus on various metric learning methodologies and the references section lists key literature that supports the discussed concepts. Overall, the paper serves as a valuable resource for understanding the landscape of metric learning techniques.

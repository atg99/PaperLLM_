The paper provides a comprehensive tutorial and survey on spectral, probabilistic, and deep metric learning. It covers various methodologies, including discriminant analysis with virtual samples, deep Fisher discriminant analysis, and multi-modal deep metric learning. Additionally, the paper discusses geometric metric learning through neural networks and few-shot metric learning techniques. Specific subtopics, such as multi-scale metric learning and metric learning with continuous similarity scores, are addressed to highlight advancements in the field. The conclusion synthesizes the insights gained from the survey, emphasizing the evolving nature of metric learning and its applications.

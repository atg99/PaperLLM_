5.7.2. PROTOTYPE NETWORKS
Prototype networks (Snell et al., 2017) represent each class by a prototype, which is the mean of the embedded support points for that class. During the training phase, the network learns to embed both the support and query sets into a shared feature space. The classification of a query sample is done by computing the distance to the prototypes of each class and assigning it to the nearest one. This method is effective for few-shot learning as it simplifies the decision-making process by using a single representative for each class.

In summary, the paper discusses various approaches to metric learning, particularly focusing on deep metric learning techniques that incorporate geometric and probabilistic methods. It highlights the significance of few-shot learning, describing how episodic training with support and query sets can facilitate effective learning from limited data. Key techniques such as multi-scale metric learning and prototype networks are presented as innovative solutions to enhance performance in few-shot scenarios.

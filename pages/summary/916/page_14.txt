The section discusses the kernel version of metric learning related to kernel support vector machines (SVM) and introduces geometric spectral metric learning methods. The kernelized approach, as presented by Tsang et al., transforms the initial optimization problem into a quadratic programming format that can be solved using optimization algorithms. 

Additionally, the tutorial covers Geometric Mean Metric Learning (GMML), a notable method in geometric spectral metric learning. Regular GMML utilizes the inverse of the weight matrix for dissimilar points, leading to an optimization problem that minimizes the weighted distances between similar and dissimilar points. This problem is reformulated in terms of the trace of products of matrices subject to constraints, resulting in a Riccati equation whose solution is derived from the geodesic on the Symmetric Positive Definite (SPD) Riemannian manifold.

The section also addresses a regularized version of GMML to handle potential singularities in the weight matrix. This regularization incorporates a prior known positive definite matrix to ensure stability in the optimization process. Overall, the content emphasizes the mathematical formulations and theoretical foundations underlying these metric learning methods.

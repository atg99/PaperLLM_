The paper provides a comprehensive survey and tutorial on spectral, probabilistic, and deep metric learning, emphasizing their applications and advancements in the field. It discusses various methodologies and techniques for metric learning, including adversarial robustness, distance learning from sparse pairwise constraints, and the use of kernel methods in Fisher discriminant analysis. The authors highlight significant contributions from various researchers, outlining algorithms such as No fuss distance metric learning using proxies, deep metric learning via lifted structured feature embedding, and the SoftTriple loss method, which eliminates the need for triplet sampling. 

Additionally, the paper covers the importance of contrastive learning with hard negative samples and the impact of multi-modal deep distance metric learning. The authors review advancements in dimensionality reduction techniques, specifically locally linear embedding, and the kernel trick for distances. Key applications in face recognition, clustering, and histopathology are also discussed, showcasing how metric learning techniques enhance representation learning. The survey concludes by addressing the challenges and future directions in the realm of metric learning, emphasizing the ongoing evolution and significance of these methods in machine learning and computer vision.

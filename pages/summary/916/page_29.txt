In summary, Siamese networks utilize pairs or triplets of data points for training, where data points are categorized as anchor, positive, or negative based on their similarity. The loss functions aim to minimize intra-class variance while maximizing inter-class variance. The network architecture can consist of two or three sub-networks, with shared weights among them to ensure consistency in learning. Implementation can be achieved through either multiple sub-networks or a single sub-network, depending on memory constraints and efficiency needs.

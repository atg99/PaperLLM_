The paper provides a comprehensive overview of various aspects of metric learning, emphasizing spectral, probabilistic, and deep learning approaches. It discusses the challenges and methodologies in metric learning, particularly in the context of imbalanced data and the integration of side information to improve embedding quality. The authors present a series of tutorials and surveys on topics such as principal component analysis, dimensionality reduction techniques, and generative models, highlighting the evolution of these methods and their applications in machine learning. Key contributions from multiple researchers are cited, illustrating the development of concepts like neighbourhood components analysis and generative adversarial networks. Overall, the paper serves as a valuable resource for understanding the state of metric learning and its diverse applications in artificial intelligence.

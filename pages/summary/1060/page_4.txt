The paper presents a schematic overview of various Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) variants, highlighting their structural differences and unique features. Key models discussed include:

1. **Vanilla GAN**: The foundational model of GANs.
2. **CatGAN**: A variant focused on categorical data generation.
3. **EBGAN / BEGAN**: These models introduce energy-based concepts for better training stability.
4. **ALI / BiGAN**: These allow for bidirectional generation and discrimination, facilitating both generation and inference.
5. **InfoGAN**: Enhances the traditional GAN by incorporating additional information to improve the quality and diversity of generated samples.
6. **ACGAN**: Incorporates class labels into the generation process, allowing for class-specific outputs.
7. **V AEGAN**: Combines variational autoencoders with GANs for improved representation learning.
8. **CGAN**: Conditional GAN that generates data based on specific conditions or labels.
9. **LAPGAN / SGAN**: A stacked architecture of GANs, improving the resolution of generated images through a cascading approach.

The schematic emphasizes the role of the conditional vector, particularly in CGAN and ACGAN, where it encodes class labels or attributes. The figure illustrates how these models differ in terms of input, output, and training mechanisms, showcasing the evolution and diversity within the GAN framework.

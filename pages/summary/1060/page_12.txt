The table summarizes various publications on conditional image synthesis categorized by imaging modality, highlighting the conditional information used, methods, datasets, and evaluation metrics. 

For CT imaging, Jin et al. (2018b) employed a modified pix2pix framework focused on lung nodules, using a specific loss that considers the nodule context. In MR imaging, several studies including Mok and Chung (2018) and Shin et al. (2018) used segmentation maps with different approaches such as coarse-to-fine and standard pix2pix methods. Gu et al. (2018) utilized MR CycleGAN, while Lau et al. (2018) applied a cascade cGAN approach. 

In ultrasound imaging, Hu et al. (2017b) and Tom and Sheet (2018) focused on probe location and segmentation maps, respectively, using cGAN and cascade cGAN methods. Retinal fundus imaging studies by Zhao et al. (2017) and others predominantly involved vessel maps, employing various cGAN configurations and enhancements.

Histopathology work by Senaras et al. (2018) utilized pix2pix for segmentation maps. In X-ray imaging, Galbusera et al. (2018) applied both pix2pix and CycleGAN for different views and segmentation, while Mahapatra et al. (2018b) and Oh and Yun (2018) modified the pix2pix framework to include content loss and Haar wavelet decomposition, respectively, for improved results.

Overall, the table reflects a diverse range of methodologies applied across different imaging modalities, emphasizing the advancements in conditional image synthesis techniques.

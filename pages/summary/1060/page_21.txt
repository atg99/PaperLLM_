the perspective of deep learning: A review of recent advancements. The paper discusses various methodologies in medical image analysis leveraging deep learning techniques, particularly focusing on the use of generative adversarial networks (GANs). It highlights the significant contributions made by different researchers in the field, including advancements in image segmentation, enhancement, and synthesis. The works cited range from the application of GANs for artifact reduction in cone-beam computed tomography to unsupervised domain adaptation for synthetic medical images. The importance of data augmentation and semi-supervised learning is emphasized, showcasing how GANs can improve the classification and analysis of medical images, including chest X-rays and MRI scans. The review also addresses challenges and future directions in the integration of deep learning with medical imaging, aiming to enhance diagnostic accuracy and treatment planning.

This document features a collection of references related to advancements in generative adversarial networks (GANs) and their applications in medical imaging. Key contributions include methods for image synthesis, denoising, and reconstruction across various imaging modalities. Notable works include the development of deep de-aliasing GANs for fast compressed sensing MRI reconstruction, structure-constrained CycleGANs for MR-to-CT synthesis, and multi-channel GANs for parallel MRI reconstruction in k-space. The references also highlight techniques for low-dose CT denoising, cross-modality image registration, and segmentation using GANs. Additionally, several studies explore the use of GANs for enhancing image quality and feature similarity assessment, showcasing the versatility and effectiveness of these networks in addressing challenges in medical imaging. Overall, these contributions underscore the growing impact of deep learning techniques, particularly GANs, in improving diagnostic imaging processes and outcomes.

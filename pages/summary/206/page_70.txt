The referenced works cover a range of topics in computer vision and machine learning, focusing on various techniques and methodologies. 

Cheng et al. (2017) present a joint learning framework for video object segmentation and optical flow, enhancing the accuracy of both tasks. Cheng et al. (2014) introduce a global contrast-based approach for salient region detection, improving object recognition capabilities. 

Cho et al. (2014) explore learning phrase representations through an RNN encoder-decoder, contributing to advancements in statistical machine translation. Their work emphasizes the significance of neural networks in language processing.

Choi et al. (2017) propose an attentional correlation filter network for adaptive visual tracking, which improves tracking performance in dynamic environments. Choi (2015) discusses near-online multi-target tracking using aggregated local flow descriptors, enhancing real-time tracking efficiency.

Chorowski et al. (2015) investigate attention-based models for speech recognition, showcasing the effectiveness of attention mechanisms in audio processing. Chu et al. (2017) focus on online multi-object tracking by employing a CNN-based single object tracker with a spatial-temporal attention mechanism, which facilitates better tracking of multiple objects.

Chu and Kitani (2020) introduce neural batch sampling with reinforcement learning for semi-supervised anomaly detection, highlighting innovative approaches to anomaly detection in computer vision. Finally, Chu et al. (2015) discuss video co-summarization through visual co-occurrence, presenting a method to summarize videos by identifying and aggregating similar visual content.

Clavera et al. (2018) elaborate on model-based reinforcement learning through meta-policy optimization, advancing the field of reinforcement learning by optimizing policies based on prior experiences. 

Overall, these studies collectively contribute to the advancement of algorithms and techniques in computer vision, machine learning, and related fields.

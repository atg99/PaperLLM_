The table summarizes various model-based and model-free deep reinforcement learning (DRL) algorithms, categorized into value-based and policy gradient methods. 

Value-based methods include:
- DQN: Deep Q Network, off-policy
- Double DQN: Improves upon DQN, off-policy
- Dueling DQN: Enhances DQN with separate value and advantage estimations, off-policy
- MCTS: Monte Carlo Tree Search, on-policy
- UCRL-VTR: Optimistic planning problem, off-policy

Policy gradient methods include:
- DDPG: DQN with Deterministic Policy Gradient, off-policy
- TRPO: Trust Region Policy Optimization, on-policy
- PPO: Proximal Policy Optimization, on-policy
- ME-TRPO: Model-Ensemble Trust-Region Policy Optimization, on-policy
- MB-MPO: Model-Based Meta-Policy Optimization, on-policy

Actor-Critic methods are represented by:
- A3C: Asynchronous Advantage Actor Critic, on-policy
- A2C: Advantage Actor Critic, on-policy

This classification highlights the diversity of approaches in DRL, focusing on their operational categories and methodologies.

The paper discusses several good practices in Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) that improve agent training. Key techniques include:

1. **Experience Replay**: This technique helps in off-policy learning by storing past experiences and reusing them, which mitigates correlations in training data and enhances data efficiency.

2. **Minibatch Learning**: By processing multiple samples at once, this method increases robustness against outliers and noise during the learning process.

3. **Target Q-network Freezing**: This involves employing two networks where one interacts with the environment and the other serves as a stable target for generating Q-values. The target network's weights are updated slowly, which stabilizes learning.

4. **Reward Clipping**: Rewards are confined to a specific range (-1, 1) to ensure they remain manageable and facilitate effective learning.

5. **Model-Based vs. Model-Free Approaches**: The choice between these two approaches is influenced by the underlying model architecture, including policy and value functions.

The paper also highlights the growing interest in autonomous landmark detection, which is driven by the need for efficient data evaluation methods that reduce the time and errors associated with manual annotation. Various machine learning methods have been explored, including regression forests and cascade regression with statistical shape priors. A novel approach reformulates the detection task as a behavior learning problem using Markov Decision Processes (MDP), leveraging DRL and scale-space theory to develop optimal strategies for detecting anatomical structures across multiple scales.

This collection of references includes significant contributions to various fields within computer vision and medical imaging. 

Huang et al. (2017) explore deep learning techniques on lie groups for recognizing actions based on skeletal data. Humpire-Mamani et al. (2018) present an efficient method for organ localization in thorax-abdomen CT scans using multi-label convolutional neural networks. The ITK Software Guide by Ibanez et al. (2005) provides foundational knowledge on medical image processing.

Idrees et al. (2013) and (2018) focus on counting and density estimation in crowded images, introducing multi-source and composition loss methods, respectively. Ilg et al. (2017) advance optical flow estimation with the Flownet 2.0 architecture. Ioffe and Szegedy (2015) introduce batch normalization techniques that enhance deep network training efficiency.

Jack et al. (2008) detail MRI methodologies in the Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative, a crucial study in neuroimaging. Jaderberg et al. (2015) and (2014) contribute to spatial transformer networks and text spotting through deep feature extraction, respectively. Jain et al. (2020) discuss robust automatic detection of multiple landmarks, while Jain and Grauman (2014) present a method for supervoxel-consistent foreground propagation in video.

These references collectively underscore advancements in deep learning applications, medical imaging techniques, and methodologies for video analysis and crowd counting.

The paper summarizes various model-based reinforcement learning (RL) approaches, categorizing them into key components: value functions, transition models, policy search methods, and return functions. 

For value functions, techniques such as Differential Dynamic Programming, Temporal Difference Learning, Policy Iteration, and Monte Carlo methods are highlighted. 

In terms of transition models, deterministic methods like decision trees and linear regression are mentioned alongside stochastic methods including Gaussian processes, Expectation-Maximization, and Dynamic Bayesian networks. 

Policy search approaches are classified into gradient-based methods, information theory applications, and sampling-based techniques. 

Finally, return functions are differentiated into discounted and averaged returns, illustrating the diversity of strategies within model-based RL. 

This summarization provides a structured overview of the essential methodologies and frameworks in the field of model-based reinforcement learning.

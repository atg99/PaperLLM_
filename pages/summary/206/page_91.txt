Peters and Schaal (2008) discuss reinforcement learning techniques for motor skills using policy gradients, providing insights into the application of neural networks in robotics.

Pirinen and Sminchisescu (2018) investigate deep reinforcement learning for region proposal networks in object detection, showcasing the integration of advanced learning methods in computer vision.

Pirsiavash et al. (2011) present globally-optimal greedy algorithms for tracking multiple objects, contributing to the development of efficient tracking systems.

Plaat et al. (2020) survey deep model-based reinforcement learning approaches for high-dimensional problems, reflecting the growing interest in complex learning environments.

Potapov et al. (2014) focus on category-specific video summarization, presenting methods for concise representation of video content.

Pourreza-Shahri and Kehtarnavaz (2015) address exposure bracketing through automatic exposure selection, enhancing image quality in varying lighting conditions.

Finally, Prest et al. (2012) explore learning object class detectors from weakly annotated video, making strides in the automation of object recognition tasks.

Overall, these contributions reflect significant advancements in machine learning and computer vision, with applications across various fields including robotics, biotechnology, and multimedia analysis.

In summary, several innovative methods for visual object tracking leveraging deep reinforcement learning (DRL) have been presented. These methods utilize various architectures, including recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and convolutional neural networks (CNNs), to process input frames and predict object locations. The approaches incorporate distinct agents or networks for different aspects of tracking, such as alignment and prediction, while employing sophisticated reward functions based on metrics like intersection over union (IoU) to guide learning. These advancements illustrate the potential of DRL in overcoming challenges associated with object tracking in dynamic environments.

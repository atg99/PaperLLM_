The paper discusses the application of deep reinforcement learning (DRL) in object tracking and image registration. It introduces a 9-dimensional action vector for object tracking, which includes movements in various directions, scaling, aspect ratio adjustments, and stopping. The reward function is based on the Intersection over Union (IOU) metric, with a threshold to determine success. A basic implementation of DRL for object tracking is illustrated, where the state comprises two consecutive frames, and actions involve adjusting a bounding box to fit an object in the subsequent frame.

Additionally, the paper highlights the significance of image registration in aligning 3D medical images from different patients or the same patient over time. The challenge lies in the differing coordinate systems, scales, or resolutions of the images. Various approaches, including a multi-resolution strategy with local optimizers, have been explored to automate the image registration process for 2D or 3D images.

The paper discusses various advancements in automated systems and machine learning models, particularly focusing on image processing and urban driving simulations. Key contributions include:

1. **Automated Exposure Bracketing**: A method developed in 2020 that selects optimal bracketing from candidates to enhance the quality of generated HDR images, achieving improved peak signal-to-noise ratios using AlexNet. This method outperforms other techniques like those proposed by Barakat and Pourreza-Shahri.

2. **Urban Autonomous Driving**: A framework utilizing the Rainbow-IQN algorithm to manage multiple actions (steering and throttle) in response to environmental factors like traffic lights and pedestrians. The system's performance is evaluated using the CARLA urban driving simulator, which won the 2019 camera-only CARLA challenge.

3. **Mitigating Bias in Facial Recognition**: The paper presents a DQN approach that adjusts margins based on race group biases, resulting in higher verification accuracy compared to existing methods like CosFace and ArcFace. The proposed algorithm is validated using novel datasets, including BUPT-Globalface and BUPT-Balancedface.

4. **Attention Mechanism in CNNs**: The research introduces an attention mechanism to enhance CNN performance through dynamic weight adjustments in feature maps. This technique employs an LSTM model to predict rewards and demonstrates improvements over previous methodologies focused on feature channels and style.

Overall, the paper highlights significant improvements in image processing and bias mitigation in facial recognition, showcasing the effectiveness of deep learning architectures and innovative algorithms in these domains.

In Section 12, the paper discusses the future perspectives of Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) in computer vision, highlighting the challenges that the field faces and the recent advanced techniques developed to address these issues. 

One of the primary challenges in DRL for computer vision is the need for vast amounts of labeled data and the computational resources required for training deep networks. Additionally, the integration of DRL with traditional computer vision techniques can be complex, as it requires balancing exploration and exploitation in dynamic environments.

Recent advancements in techniques such as transfer learning, where knowledge gained from one task is applied to another, have shown promise in mitigating data scarcity. Furthermore, improvements in model architectures and training algorithms have led to better performance in visual tasks. The use of attention mechanisms and self-supervised learning are also emerging trends that enhance the efficacy of DRL in processing visual information.

Overall, while significant challenges remain in the application of DRL to computer vision, ongoing research and innovation are paving the way for more robust and effective solutions.

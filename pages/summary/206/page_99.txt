This collection of references highlights significant advancements in the fields of computer vision and reinforcement learning. Key contributions include Mei Wang and Weihong Deng's work on mitigating bias in face recognition through skewness-aware reinforcement learning, as presented at the IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision in 2020. Their previous research in 2019 also focused on reducing racial bias in facial recognition systems via an information maximization adaptation network.

Other notable studies include Naiyan Wang and Dit-Yan Yeung's development of a deep compact image representation for visual tracking, which appeared in the 2013 Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems. Additionally, Tingwu Wang and colleagues benchmarked model-based reinforcement learning in 2019, while Yan Wang et al. explored multitask learning for object localization using deep reinforcement learning in IEEE Transactions on Cognitive and Developmental Systems.

Moreover, Yujiang Wang and others presented a dynamic face video segmentation approach using reinforcement learning at the 2020 IEEE/CVF Conference, and Zhou Wang's team contributed to image quality assessment methodologies by introducing structural similarity metrics in 2004. The references also include work on flexible exposure bracketing selection using reinforced agents and the introduction of dueling network architectures for deep reinforcement learning.

These studies collectively illustrate the evolving landscape of techniques aimed at enhancing machine learning models, particularly in addressing biases, improving tracking, and refining image processing methodologies.

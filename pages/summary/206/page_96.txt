This collection of references encompasses various advancements in deep learning and reinforcement learning, particularly in the fields of image processing and biometrics. 

The work by Shanhui Sun et al. focuses on robust multimodal image registration using deep recurrent reinforcement learning, demonstrating the application of deep learning techniques to enhance image alignment. Sundararajan and Woodard provide a comprehensive survey on the use of deep learning in biometrics, highlighting its effectiveness and advancements in this area.

Sutton and Barto's foundational text on reinforcement learning outlines core principles and methodologies, while their subsequent works delve into policy gradient methods, illustrating how these approaches can be effectively applied in reinforcement learning scenarios with function approximation.

Szegedy et al. contribute significantly to the development of convolutional neural networks (CNNs), with studies on Inception architectures and the impact of residual connections on learning efficiency. Their earlier works also discuss deep neural networks for object detection, emphasizing improvements in model performance.

Tang et al. explore deep progressive reinforcement learning for skeleton-based action recognition, showcasing the intersection of reinforcement learning and action recognition tasks. Additionally, Tao et al. present a Siamese instance search method for tracking, which is crucial for real-time applications.

The optimization of mutual information for multiresolution image registration by Thievenaz and Unser provides insights into enhancing image registration techniques. Lastly, Tian et al. investigate multi-step medical image segmentation through reinforcement learning, indicating the growing relevance of these methods in medical applications.

Overall, these references collectively highlight the evolution and application of deep learning and reinforcement learning techniques across various domains, particularly in image processing and recognition tasks.

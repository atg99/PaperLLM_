This collection of references spans various topics within the fields of computer vision, biomedical imaging, and reinforcement learning. Notable works include the U-Net architecture by Ronneberger et al., which has become a foundational model for biomedical image segmentation. The CARLA autonomous driving challenge, as discussed by Ros et al., highlights advancements in autonomous vehicle technologies. Rother et al.'s "GrabCut" introduces an interactive method for foreground extraction, enhancing image editing capabilities.

In the realm of genetic algorithms, Rouet et al. present a robust approach for 3D MR-CT registration, showcasing the application of evolutionary techniques in medical imaging. Rumelhart's work on connectionist approaches provides insights into cognitive architectures, influencing neural network designs. Runarsson and Lucas compare methods of imitating gameplay, contributing to the understanding of learning in artificial agents.

Russakovsky et al. outline the ImageNet challenge, which has significantly impacted visual recognition tasks. Sadeghian et al. address the complexities of tracking in dynamic environments, while Sæmundsson et al. explore meta reinforcement learning with Gaussian processes, demonstrating the integration of probabilistic models in learning frameworks. Lastly, Sahba et al. present several contributions to reinforcement learning, specifically in the context of object segmentation and medical imaging, emphasizing the potential of deep learning techniques in these domains.

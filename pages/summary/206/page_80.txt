This collection of references includes significant contributions to the fields of video object segmentation, deep learning, and reinforcement learning. 

1. Jain et al. introduced Fusionseg, a method for automatic segmentation of generic objects in videos by integrating motion and appearance features, presented at CVPR 2017.
2. Jampani et al. developed Video Propagation Networks, a framework for propagating information through video frames, also at CVPR 2017.
3. Jang and Kim proposed an online video object segmentation approach using a convolutional trident network, contributing to real-time processing capabilities.
4. Jégou et al. presented the One Hundred Layers Tiramisu, a fully convolutional DenseNet architecture designed for semantic segmentation tasks.
5. Jia et al. explored model-based reinforcement learning through value-targeted regression, enhancing decision-making processes in dynamic environments.
6. Jiang et al. focused on multi-object tracking in videos utilizing LSTM and deep reinforcement learning, showcasing advancements in tracking technology.
7. They further developed a multi-agent deep reinforcement learning model for multi-object tracking, indicating a collaborative approach to tracking multiple entities.
8. Jie et al. introduced tree-structured reinforcement learning for sequential object localization, emphasizing a structured methodology for improving localization tasks.
9. Jimenez-del Toro et al. conducted a cloud-based evaluation of segmentation and landmark detection algorithms for anatomical structures, contributing to medical imaging benchmarks.
10. Jordan's research on long-term adjuvant tamoxifen therapy for breast cancer highlights the intersection of medical research and treatment strategies.
11. Koh and Kim presented a method for primary object segmentation in videos through region augmentation and reduction, advancing techniques in video analysis.

These works collectively advance the understanding and application of machine learning techniques in video analysis and medical imaging.

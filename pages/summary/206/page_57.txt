The paper discusses a Markov Decision Process (MDP) framework applied to the automatic diagnosis of appendix locations using reinforcement learning techniques, specifically an Actor-critic model. The state consists of a 50x50x50 volume centered around the predicted appendix location, and the model utilizes six actions (two per axis) to navigate this space. The reward system is based on the change in distance between the predicted and actual appendix locations after each action.

Additionally, the paper compares various methods in deep reinforcement learning (DRL) related to tasks such as object manipulation, visual-based control, automatic tracing, and automatic diagnosis. Different approaches are summarized, highlighting their training techniques, actions, states, rewards, backbones, performance metrics, and datasets used. For instance, in the object manipulation task, a Rainbow DDPG method achieved a success rate of up to 90% using a multilayer CNN. In visual-based control, the Actor-critic method demonstrated higher success rates and lower collision rates compared to ResNet architectures. The automatic diagnosis method using the Actor-critic framework showed improved sensitivity and specificity over traditional CNN classifiers.

Overall, the paper emphasizes the effectiveness of reinforcement learning techniques in improving diagnostic accuracy and performance across various applications.

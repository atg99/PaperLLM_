The collection of references includes a range of studies and methodologies related to reinforcement learning and image processing. Key contributions include the exploration of opposition-based reinforcement learning for image segmentation by Farhang et al. in 2007, and the development of Trust Region Policy Optimization by Schulman et al., which was presented in both a 2015 conference and later as an arXiv preprint. Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms, also by Schulman et al., further builds on this foundation in 2017, offering improved techniques for policy optimization.

Additionally, the concept of meta-learning in reinforcement learning is addressed by Schweighofer and Doya in 2003, while surgical gesture segmentation is tackled by Sefati et al. in 2015. The Fast YOLO system for real-time object detection in video is explored by Shaffee et al. in 2017, and the NTU RGB+D dataset for 3D human activity analysis is introduced by Shahroudy et al. in 2016. Lastly, Shaker et al. contribute to vision-based reinforcement learning using approximate policy iteration, and Shalev-Shwartz et al. discuss safe multi-agent reinforcement learning pertinent to autonomous driving in 2016. Shen et al. present a benchmark for facial landmark tracking in-the-wild in 2015. Collectively, these works illustrate the evolution and application of advanced algorithms in computer vision and reinforcement learning.

The paper outlines a method for optimizing a Radial Basis Function Neural Network (RBFNN) using a hybrid approach that combines Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) with a SSM (Stochastic Simulation Method). The process begins with initializing algorithm parameters, including population size and iteration limits. 

The algorithm involves the following key steps:

1. Initialization of parameters and construction of RBFNN topology for each particle.
2. Calculation of fitness values for each particle to assess their performance.
3. Selection of the best positions among particles as personal and global bests (gbest).
4. Iterative updating of particle velocities and positions based on defined equations.
5. Dynamic adjustment of correction factors to refine particle movements.
6. Determination of network size and selection of optimal particle positions as structural parameters for the RBFNN.

The method continues until the maximum number of iterations is reached, at which point the optimized RBFNN is produced. The pseudo-code provided in Algorithm 1 serves as a detailed guide for implementing this optimization process. Overall, this approach aims to effectively predict non-linear time-series data through an optimized RBFNN model.

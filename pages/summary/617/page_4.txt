This algorithm combines the strengths of PSO with stochastic search techniques to enhance exploration and exploitation capabilities. HPSO-SSM improves the diversity of the swarm and mitigates early convergence by incorporating random perturbations during the search process. 

The proposed HPSO-SSM-based Radial Basis Function Neural Networks (HPSO-SSM-RBFNN) were tested against benchmark datasets, including Lorenz time-series and nonlinear system identification problems, demonstrating superior performance in prediction accuracy. The method effectively handles missing values and data imbalances, which are common challenges in real-world applications, particularly in wastewater treatment process modeling.

In summary, the study presents a comprehensive evaluation of the HPSO-SSM-RBFNN framework, emphasizing its efficacy through rigorous experiments. The preprocessing steps, including data denoising, normalization, and discretization, were crucial for enhancing model performance. The innovative HPSO-SSM algorithm addresses limitations of traditional PSO, leading to improved results in modeling and prediction tasks relevant to wastewater treatment systems.

The paper introduces the Radial Basis Function Neural Network (RBFNN) architecture, which is characterized by its multi-layer structure consisting of input, hidden, and output layers. The RBFNN is noted for its effective non-linear approximation capabilities, which contribute to a fast learning rate. The architecture is designed to transfer input vectors to the hidden layer, where the network performs non-linear operations, transforming data into a high-dimensional linearly separable space through a basis function. The output is generated by linearly combining the results from the hidden layer, ultimately providing the final prediction.

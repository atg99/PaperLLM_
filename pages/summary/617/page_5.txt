The paper introduces a novel Fused Particle Swarm Optimization approach integrated with a spiral-shaped mechanism, referred to as HPSO-SSM, to enhance the efficiency of traditional PSO. The proposed method addresses PSO's limitations, particularly in global search efficiency and modifications near locally optimal solutions. 

In HPSO-SSM, the inertia weight is updated using a logistic map, promoting convergence towards the global optimum while enhancing algorithmic diversity to avoid local optima. The particle position updates occur through two distinct mechanisms: a spiral-shaped method and a modified PSO formula. The spiral mechanism, which increases local search capability, is employed with an 80% probability determined by a random value. 

The next particle location is calculated using a formula that incorporates parameters such as a positive constant and random values, ensuring the algorithm's adaptability. Alternatively, if the probability condition is not met, a modified updating formula is applied, incorporating dynamic correction factors to balance exploration and exploitation within the search process. 

The position values are constrained between 0 and 1, with dynamic factors adjusted accordingly. The paper provides detailed equations for these processes, illustrating the design and functionality of the HPSO-SSM algorithm, which is visually summarized in a flow diagram.

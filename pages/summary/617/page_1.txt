The paper by Ahmad, Li, and Mahmood presents an adaptive hyperparameter fine-tuning method aimed at enhancing the robustness and quality of the Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) algorithm when applied to Radial Basis Function Neural Networks (RBFNN) for non-linear system modeling. The authors propose a hybrid PSO approach, referred to as HPSO-SSM, which integrates a spiral-shaped mechanism to overcome common limitations of traditional PSO, such as slow convergence and local minima issues.

Key advancements in the HPSO-SSM algorithm include the promotion of search diversity by adjusting the inertial weight based on a logistic map sequence, training two distinct parameters in the position update algorithm to improve efficiency, and employing a spiral-shaped local search operator to navigate the solution space more effectively. The method optimizes both the parameters of the RBFNN and the network size, resulting in a model that is compact yet precise.

The efficacy of the proposed HPSO-SSM-RBFNN technique is validated through simulations on two non-linear benchmark functions and a case study involving total phosphorus modeling in wastewater treatment processes. Results indicate superior performance in prediction accuracy and network compactness compared to existing methods. 

Overall, the research highlights the potential of combining advanced optimization techniques with neural network modeling to address complex non-linear problems effectively.

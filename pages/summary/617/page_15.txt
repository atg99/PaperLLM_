The paper presents a novel approach, HPSO-SSM-RBFNN, which demonstrates effectiveness in reducing prediction error and RMSE values compared to traditional Radial Basis Function Neural Networks (RBFNNs). The experimental results showcase its applicability in real-world scenarios, particularly in estimating critical parameters within wastewater treatment plant (WWTP) systems and managing uncertain non-linear systems adaptively. The authors express intentions to further investigate the HPSO-SSM's effectiveness across various neural network types and to explore strategies to promote algorithm utilization. 

The research was conceptualized by Z.A., with methodology and validation contributions from Z.A. and J.L., respectively.

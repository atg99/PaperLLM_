The paper presents a method using a Hybrid Particle Swarm Optimization (HPSO) combined with a Self-Structured Membership Function Radial Basis Function Neural Network (SSM-RBFNN) to optimize the parameters of RBFNN structures. Each particle in the swarm represents a unique RBFNN configuration, and optimization continues until the best individual, characterized by the lowest Root Mean Square Error (RMSE), is identified among the top 40 particles.

Key configurations for the PSO and HPSO-SSM parameter fine-tuning include a population size of 40 and specified iterations for various applications, with inertia weight set at 0.5 and dynamic adjustment for certain coefficients.

The Lorenz time-series is utilized as a benchmark model, described by a three-dimensional non-linear system. The parameters are set to specific values, and data is generated using the Kutta–Runge method. The study collects 1500 samples, dividing them into training (1000 samples) and testing (500 samples) datasets. The proposed model starts with three hidden neurons, aiming for a training RMSE of 0.01, ultimately stabilizing around 0.0107 after adjustments.

Results indicate that the HPSO-SSM-RBFNN demonstrates effective convergence speed and modeling accuracy. Prediction outcomes closely align with the actual Lorenz time-series, with testing errors for the later samples remaining within a range of -0.03 to 0.12, showcasing the model's robustness and reliability in forecasting.

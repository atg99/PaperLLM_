The section discusses the training and classification process for liver tumor segmentation using a dataset of 475 images. Out of these, 454 images were utilized for training and validation, while 45 images were reserved for testing. Initial experiments employed the U-Net model, which is effective for semantic segmentation in medical imaging and is based on the VGG-16 architecture. Although the U-Net model successfully extracted the liver region, it struggled to accurately identify tumor regions, often missing them or misclassifying them.

To address these shortcomings, the proposed architecture leverages the SegNet model, which includes an encoder-decoder structure with a 2D binary classification layer replacing the typical multi-classification layer. The VGG-16 model is incorporated into the encoder. Additionally, class weighting techniques are implemented to balance the dataset and enhance training efficacy by calculating median frequency class weights. This approach aims to improve the accuracy of tumor extraction in medical images.

The paper presents a proposed architecture for semantic segmentation, specifically aimed at identifying tumor positions in images. The testing and evaluation process involved training the model on a machine equipped with an NVIDIA GTX 1050 GPU and an Intel Core i7 processor using MATLAB 2018b, which supports neural network and image processing functionalities.

The dataset was split into training and testing groups in a 9:1 ratio. Results indicated that training performance generally exceeded testing performance. The model underwent 1000 iterations per epoch over 100 epochs with a constant learning rate of 0.001. Notably, the segmentation quality improved with an increasing number of training images, achieving near-perfect results in Case 3, which utilized 260 training images.

Evaluation metrics for three test cases were provided in Table 2. Case 1, with 70 training images, achieved an overall accuracy of 91.27%, while Case 2, with 124 images, improved to 93.36%. Case 3, using 260 images, reached an overall accuracy of 94.57%. The metrics also detailed tumor accuracy, background accuracy, weighted IoU, and F1-Score, illustrating the model's effectiveness in identifying tumors with increasing training data.

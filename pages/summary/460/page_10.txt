This paper discusses a deep learning approach for the semantic segmentation of liver tumors in CT scans, utilizing a modified SegNet architecture. The authors highlight the advantages of SegNet, which employs a VGG-16 encoder and a decoder that efficiently transforms features back into the image domain for pixel-wise classification. By saving max-pooling indices instead of full feature maps, the method enhances training efficiency, memory use, and accuracy. The proposed method achieves an accuracy of 98.8% for liver tumor segmentation, outperforming several existing methods, which range from 80.6% to 96.7%. While the segmentation accurately detects most tumor regions, some false positives remain, suggesting a need for further improvement through additional filtering or larger datasets. Future work aims to enhance localization accuracy and reduce false negatives.

The paper discusses a method for semantic segmentation of medical images, specifically targeting tumor detection. The evaluation method is illustrated through Figure 9, which shows segmented images overlaid on ground truth images. True positive tumor pixels are highlighted in white, while missed tumor pixels are in purple, and incorrectly predicted pixels are shown in green. The results indicate that the first test case had the lowest accuracy, while the third test case achieved nearly perfect detection, although it overestimated tumor size.

Confusion matrices for three test cases are provided in Tables 3 to 5, showing normalized results for tumor detection accuracy. In test case 1, the model correctly identified 45.82% of actual tumor pixels, while test case 3 showed an impressive 99.99% accuracy for tumor detection. 

To further contextualize the findings, Table 6 compares the overall accuracy of the proposed method with other studies in the literature, demonstrating that this method outperforms previous work in terms of accuracy.

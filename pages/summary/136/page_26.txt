The paper presents a study on the CA V-MAE model, specifically focusing on its reconstruction capabilities when 75% of the input data is masked. The results showcased in Figure 10 illustrate reconstruction samples derived from the VGGSound dataset, which differs from the pretraining dataset, AudioSet. Notably, the model was pretrained using a 75% masking ratio, and the reconstruction was performed without target normalization. This highlights the model's robustness and adaptability to different audio datasets.

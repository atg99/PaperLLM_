The paper discusses the performance of the CA V-MAE model in zero-shot audio-visual retrieval and joint event classification tasks, focusing on the effects of different masking ratios and strategies. Results indicate that as the masking ratio increases, retrieval performance decreases, with specific performance metrics provided for audio-to-visual and visual-to-audio retrieval at various ratios. 

The study compares various masking strategies—time, frequency, time-frequency, and uniform unstructured masking—during pretraining. While all strategies yield similar results in joint classification tasks at a 75% masking ratio, structured masking strategies present challenges in reconstruction. Notably, a lower masking ratio of 50% with time-frequency masking slightly enhances performance on the AudioSet-20K and VGGSound datasets. 

For retrieval tasks, the choice of masking strategy significantly influences performance. Frequency and time-frequency masking outperform uniform masking at the same 75% ratio. The findings suggest that while masking ratio and strategy have a minor effect on classification, they considerably impact retrieval performance, with structured strategies improving results. The paper concludes that the training masking strategy also affects the model's reconstruction ability.

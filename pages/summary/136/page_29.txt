The paper presents a comparative analysis of the CAV-MAE model trained under different masking strategies: uniform masking and time-frequency masking. It focuses on the performance of the model in reconstructing audio spectrograms with a significant masking ratio of 90%. The results are illustrated through reconstructed spectrograms for multiple samples, showcasing the outputs from both training approaches. The mean squared error (MSE) is provided as a metric of reconstruction quality above each spectrogram, allowing for an evaluation of the effectiveness of the different masking strategies in audio reconstruction tasks.

The paper presented at ICLR 2023 discusses various masking strategies for training a Contrastive Audio Variational Masked Autoencoder (CAV-MAE) and evaluates their effects on audio spectrogram reconstruction. Specifically, it compares uniform masking against time-frequency masking, both implemented with a masking ratio of 75%. The results are illustrated through reconstructed audio spectrograms, highlighting the differences in output quality between the two training approaches. The mean squared error (MSE) is provided above each spectrogram, serving as a quantitative measure of reconstruction accuracy. The findings suggest that the choice of masking strategy plays a crucial role in the performance of the audio reconstruction task.

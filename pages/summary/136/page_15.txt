This paper presents an evaluation of audio-visual models, specifically focusing on the CA V-MAE model and its performance on various datasets, including Kinetics-Sounds, AudioSet, and VGGSound. The authors demonstrate that CA V-MAE consistently outperforms both CA V and A V-MAE models, achieving a top-1 accuracy of 88.9% on Kinetics-Sounds. This performance is corroborated by similar results obtained on the AudioSet and VGGSound datasets.

The authors also compare CA V-MAE with the state-of-the-art MBT model, revealing that CA V-MAE, which utilizes a fully self-supervised learning approach, matches or exceeds the performance of MBT, which relies on supervised ImageNet pretrained weights. The results are reported for both Kinetics-400 and Kinetics-Sounds label sets, highlighting the impact of label set selection on accuracy.

Additionally, the paper discusses audio-visual retrieval results across different datasets, including zero-shot retrieval tasks on AudioSet and VGGSound, as well as retrieval experiments on the MSR-VTT dataset. The findings indicate that CA V and CA V-MAE models demonstrate competitive retrieval performance without prior pretraining on AudioSet.

Overall, the results indicate that CA V-MAE not only excels in recognition tasks but also shows promise in audio-visual retrieval settings, reinforcing the effectiveness of self-supervised learning techniques in this domain.

The paper presents ControlV AE, a novel framework that enhances Variational Autoencoders (V AEs) by integrating automatic control theory to address issues such as KL vanishing in language modeling and low reconstruction quality in disentangled representation learning. The authors introduce a non-linear PI controller to dynamically adjust the hyperparameter in the V AE objective based on the KL-divergence feedback during training.

ControlV AE aims to stabilize KL-divergence, allowing for a better trade-off between data reconstruction accuracy and application-specific constraints like output diversity and disentanglement. The framework is evaluated across three applications: language modeling, disentangled representation learning, and image generation. Results indicate that ControlV AE significantly improves reconstruction quality while maintaining comparable disentanglement performance. It effectively avoids KL-vanishing and enhances text diversity in language modeling and improves image generation quality over traditional V AEs.

The motivation behind this work lies in the growing use of V AEs for unsupervised generative modeling, which seeks to learn mappings between latent variables and data samples despite challenges posed by intractable posterior distributions. The findings support the efficacy of ControlV AE in manipulating KL-divergence to achieve desired outcomes in various applications.

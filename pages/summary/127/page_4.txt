The paper introduces ControlV AE, a controllable variational autoencoder that employs a Proportional-Integral (PI) control algorithm to regulate the KL-divergence during training. The objective is to maintain the KL-divergence within a desirable range to enhance the learning process of the VAE. The PI controller utilizes feedback from the KL-divergence to adjust the hyperparameter, denoted as ϕ(t), which influences the model's behavior.

The PI controller consists of two main components: the proportional term and the integral term. The proportional term responds to the current error, which is the difference between the actual KL-divergence and the set point. A large positive error encourages the KL-divergence to increase, while a large negative error prompts it to decrease. The integral term accumulates past errors, providing a stronger corrective action over time. This design allows the controller to stabilize at the desired KL-divergence level once the error is zero.

Parameter tuning for the PI controller is crucial for effective performance. The paper suggests a simple approach: tuning the proportional constant (Kp) to ensure smooth reactions to errors, particularly when the KL-divergence is close to zero. An empirical value of Kp=0.01 is recommended for good performance. The integral constant (Ki) is also emphasized, with values between 10^-3 and 10^-4 found to stabilize training without causing unnecessary delays in convergence.

The choice of the KL-divergence set point is application-specific, with guidelines provided for determining the upper and lower bounds of expected KL-divergence. Users can customize the desired KL-divergence to align with their objectives, such as enhancing diversity in generative tasks or improving accuracy.

In summary, the PI control algorithm effectively regulates the KL-divergence in ControlV AE, allowing for customizable training outcomes based on user-defined objectives. The tuning of parameters and the establishment of appropriate set points are essential for optimal performance in various applications.

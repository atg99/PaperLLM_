The paper presents ControlV AE, a controllable variational autoencoder framework designed to address the challenges of KL divergence in variational autoencoders (VAEs). Traditional methods, such as KL cost annealing, struggle with effectively managing KL divergence during training, often leading to KL vanishing without precise control. In contrast, ControlV AE incorporates a non-linear PI controller to dynamically regulate the KL divergence, ensuring it stabilizes at a desired value throughout training.

The evaluation of ControlV AE across three distinct tasks demonstrates its superior performance in terms of reconstruction quality and disentanglement. It not only prevents KL vanishing but also enhances the diversity of generated data in language modeling and improves image generation outcomes. The authors express interest in extending their approach to other domains, including topic modeling and semi-supervised learning.

The research acknowledges funding support from various defense and military research organizations. A comprehensive list of references is provided, highlighting significant contributions to the field of variational autoencoders and control systems.

The paper titled "iNNvestigate Neural Networks!" discusses the importance of understanding and analyzing deep neural networks, which have become integral to many machine learning applications. Despite their widespread use, these networks are often viewed as black boxes, making it challenging for domain specialists to interpret their predictions and actions. To address this issue, the authors present iNNvestigate, a library that offers a common interface and ready-to-use implementations for various analysis methods. This includes reference implementations for PatternNet, PatternAttribution, and Layer-wise Relevance Propagation (LRP) methods. The paper showcases the library's versatility by providing an analysis of image classifications across several state-of-the-art neural network architectures, emphasizing the need for systematic comparisons of analysis methods in the field.

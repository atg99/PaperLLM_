The paper discusses the performance of the TFCNN model on the ESC-50 dataset, highlighting its effectiveness in classifying various sound categories. The results indicate that TFCNN achieves over 80% accuracy in 37 categories and 90% in 22 categories, although it struggles with classifying water drops and washing machine sounds, failing to reach 70% accuracy regardless of the attention mechanism used. The analysis shows that transient sound events, such as mouse clicks and clock ticks, perform better with the time attention mechanism. Additionally, the paper includes visual data representations, such as classification accuracy comparisons and a confusion matrix for the UrbanSound8K dataset, to illustrate the model's performance across different sound events.

The paper discusses advancements in environmental sound classification using various deep learning techniques, specifically focusing on convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and their applications. Multiple studies are referenced, illustrating the evolution of methodologies, including data augmentation and end-to-end learning approaches. Key contributions from other researchers are highlighted, such as the development of datasets for sound classification and innovative CNN architectures. The authors acknowledge the support from the National Natural Science Foundation of China and the Shandong Province Key R&D projects, and all authors contributed equally to the manuscript.

The paper discusses a novel approach to enhance the representation of audio signals by employing two attention mechanisms on Log-Mel spectrograms. The attention mechanisms focus on time frames and frequency bands independently, allowing for the simultaneous enhancement of time and frequency features. This is crucial as the characteristics of spectrograms differ significantly from traditional image classification tasks.

To integrate these attention mechanisms into a unified model, the authors propose three combination strategies, emphasizing a parallel design to prevent interference between the mechanisms that could diminish system robustness. 

The first strategy, termed average combination, fuses the frequency attention spectrogram and the temporal attention spectrogram in a 1:1 ratio, leading to a final temporal-frequency attention spectrogram. 

The second strategy, known as weighted combination, introduces two learnable parameters, α and β, constrained by α + β = 1. This allows for a more flexible fusion of the attention spectrograms, resulting in a refined temporal-frequency attention spectrogram.

The paper outlines the operations involved in generating the attention spectrograms and the mathematical formulations for the attention weights, ensuring a comprehensive understanding of the mechanism's functionality.

The proposed model, a temporal-frequency attention based convolutional neural network (TFCNN), integrates both temporal and frequency attention mechanisms. This dual approach enhances the model's ability to capture critical features from sound events, leading to improved classification performance. The results from experiments conducted on the UrbanSound8K and ESC-50 datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of the TFCNN, achieving accuracies of 93.1% and 84.4%, respectively.

The paper is structured as follows: Section 2 reviews related work in environmental sound classification (ESC) using deep learning techniques. Section 3 details the analysis of frequency band characteristics and the motivation for the frequency attention mechanism. Section 4 presents the architecture of the proposed classification model and its attention mechanism. Section 5 discusses the experimental results, and Section 6 provides a summary of the findings. 

Overall, the introduction of a frequency attention mechanism, in conjunction with existing temporal attention frameworks, represents a significant advancement in the classification of environmental sounds, addressing a notable gap in previous research that primarily focused on temporal features without adequately considering the influence of frequency bands.

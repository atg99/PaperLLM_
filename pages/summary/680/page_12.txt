This paper presents a model that achieves classification accuracies of over 93.1% on the UrbanSound8K dataset and 84.4% on the ESC-50 dataset. The model is characterized by low complexity in its network structure and straightforward feature processing while maintaining high accuracy. The study further evaluates various attention mechanisms and their effects on sound event classification. Future work will focus on optimizing a weighted combination strategy based on the dependence of different sound events on time and frequency features, aiming to enhance model performance by selectively adjusting fusion parameters. Additionally, the paper includes a visualization of feature distribution before and after applying temporal-frequency attention for different sound events, highlighting the improvements made in feature representation.

The paper presents a novel approach to enhance the extraction of factual information using BERT models by modifying the input queries. The proposed method involves a trained rewrite model that optimizes query rewrites to maximize accuracy when processed by BERT. The results demonstrate that the BERTese model outperforms three baseline models, as shown by the mean precision at one (P@1) scores on the T-REx dataset, with BERTese achieving a score of 38.3.

The paper includes examples of how minor modifications in queries can lead to correct answers, illustrating that BERT models are sensitive to these changes. A part-of-speech analysis reveals that a significant portion of the modifications involves determiners, which typically carry minimal semantic content.

The authors conclude that while the focus of their empirical work is on the LAMA dataset, their rewriting technique could be broadly applicable to other tasks, including multiple-choice question answering and language modeling. They suggest potential extensions to their approach and express interest in testing it on other masked language models like RoBERTa and ERNIE.

Acknowledgments are given to contributors and funding sources, emphasizing the collaborative nature of the research. The paper references various works that inform their methodology and findings.

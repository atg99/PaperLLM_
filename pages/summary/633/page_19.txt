The paper provides a comprehensive survey of deep multi-modal and cross-modal data analytics, focusing on the performance of state-of-the-art architectures across benchmark datasets. It reviews practical applications that have been addressed using existing deep multi-modal methods, highlighting their effectiveness in reconciling different modalities to solve complex problems such as high cost and uncertainty. The authors propose future research directions aimed at enhancing the field of multi-modal data analytics, suggesting that further investigation could lead to innovative solutions and opportunities. The study is supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, underscoring its academic significance.

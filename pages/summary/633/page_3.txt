The paper discusses advancements in deep multi-modal data analytics, focusing on classification techniques using various deep learning architectures. It highlights different approaches to handling three-dimensional data and multi-view features, emphasizing the use of Expectation-Maximization (EM) for parameter and latent variable estimation.

Key contributions include:

1. **Volumetric CNNs**: Qi et al. proposed two deep architectures for classifying three-dimensional data, analyzing the performance differences between volumetric CNNs and multi-view CNNs based on architecture and resolution factors.

2. **Multi-Modal Learning**: Yang et al. introduced the multi-mode multi-instance multi-label deep network (M3DN), which addresses the inconsistency of multi-modal labels by learning label predictions while exploiting label correlations, guided by optimal transmission theory.

3. **Dependency Exploitation**: Chen et al. developed a multi-modal learning method for multi-label classification that incorporates label and modality dependencies to enhance training effectiveness.

4. **Bias Elimination**: Kan et al. presented the multi-view deep network (MvDN), aiming to reduce biases between views for cross-view recognition, utilizing both view-specific and shared sub-networks.

Additionally, the paper includes a summary table of deep multi-view learning algorithms for classification, detailing their experimental datasets, views, applications, and key characteristics, thus providing a comprehensive overview of the state-of-the-art in deep multi-modal data analytics.

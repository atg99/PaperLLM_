The paper presents the image-to-text and text-to-image retrieval performance of various methods evaluated on three datasets: IAPRTC-12, MIRFlickr-25k, and NUS-WIDE. The performance is measured using the mean Average Precision (mAP) score across different bit representations (16bits, 32bits, and 64bits).

In the image-to-text retrieval results, DCHUC achieves the highest scores across all datasets, with notable mAP scores of 0.701 at 64bits for IAPRTC-12, 0.881 for MIRFlickr-25k, and 0.791 for NUS-WIDE. CM-DVStH also performs well, particularly on MIRFlickr-25k with a peak score of 0.928 at 64bits. 

For text-to-image retrieval, DCHUC again leads with scores of 0.693 at 64bits for IAPRTC-12, 0.854 for MIRFlickr-25k, and 0.749 for NUS-WIDE. CM-DVStH shows strong performance as well, particularly with a score of 0.825 at 64bits on MIRFlickr-25k.

Overall, the results indicate that DCHUC and CM-DVStH are the most effective methods for both image-to-text and text-to-image retrieval tasks across the evaluated datasets.

The paper presents a comprehensive survey on deep multi-modal data analytics, focusing on the interplay of collaboration, rivalry, and fusion in various modalities. It synthesizes recent advancements in the field, highlighting the significance of deep learning techniques in enhancing multi-modal data processing and retrieval. 

Key concepts discussed include shared representation learning through adversarial networks, unsupervised cross-modal retrieval, and multi-view metric learning. The authors emphasize the importance of integrating different modalities to improve performance in tasks such as human pose recovery, face recognition, and cross-modal hashing. 

The survey also covers methods for collective reconstructive embeddings, deep multi-view learning, and the application of generative adversarial networks for data augmentation across domains. Additionally, the paper addresses the challenges of cross-modal similarity search and the need for emotion-preserving representation in multi-view facial expression recognition.

Overall, this survey serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners in the field, providing insights into the current landscape and future directions of deep multi-modal data analytics.

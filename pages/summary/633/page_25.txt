The paper provides a comprehensive survey on deep multi-modal data analytics, focusing on the themes of collaboration, rivalry, and fusion within various data modalities. It highlights the significance of integrating different types of data, such as visual, textual, and audio, to enhance retrieval and recognition tasks. The authors discuss various methodologies, including adversarial learning and deep hashing techniques, which have emerged as effective approaches for cross-modal retrieval.

Key studies referenced include the exploration of CNN visual features for establishing new baselines in cross-modal retrieval, adversarial techniques for learning single-modal similarities, and unsupervised deep hashing methods. The survey also covers advancements in person re-identification, emphasizing the role of attention mechanisms and co-attention networks in improving representation learning.

The paper underscores the importance of multi-view data analysis and the utilization of generative adversarial networks for fine-grained tasks. The authors advocate for a collaborative approach to enhance model robustness and performance across different data modalities, while also addressing challenges such as low-rank decomposition and consistency in multi-task learning.

Overall, the survey serves as a critical resource for researchers in the field, providing insights into contemporary trends and future directions in deep multi-modal data analytics.

The paper provides a comprehensive survey on deep multi-modal data analytics, emphasizing the dynamics of collaboration, rivalry, and fusion in the context of multi-modal data processing. It discusses various methodologies and frameworks employed in the field, including generative adversarial networks (GANs), multi-view clustering, and cross-modal representation learning. The authors explore how these techniques facilitate improved data integration and analysis across different modalities such as images, text, and audio.

Key contributions of the paper include an examination of latent space clustering in GANs, adaptive neighbor approaches for multi-view clustering, and the role of correlation and abstraction in cross-modal multimedia retrieval. The survey highlights advancements in deep learning architectures, including multi-modal CNNs and the use of hierarchical learning for shared representation.

Moreover, the paper discusses practical applications of these techniques, such as large-scale image annotation and cross-modal retrieval tasks. By reviewing recent advancements and methodologies, the authors provide insights into the ongoing challenges and future directions in deep multi-modal data analytics, making it a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners in the field.

This paper provides a comprehensive overview of deep multi-modal data analytics, focusing on the collaboration, rivalry, and fusion of different data modalities. The author, Yang Wang, highlights the significance of multi-modal data, which encompasses various feature spaces that each represent distinct properties of data objects. The complementary nature of these modalities has garnered significant research interest aimed at effectively combining them to enhance performance beyond single-modal approaches.

The paper discusses the evolution of techniques from shallow to deep learning methods, emphasizing the advantages of deep neural networks in capturing the complex, non-linear distributions inherent in high-dimensional multimedia data. Empirical studies are referenced to support the assertion that deep multi-modal methods can significantly improve the fusion of features across different modalities.

Key components identified in the analysis include collaboration among modalities, adversarial competition, and the integration of multi-modal spaces. The author concludes by suggesting future research directions in the field, indicating ongoing opportunities for advancing multi-modal data analytics.

Overall, the paper serves as a valuable resource for understanding the current landscape of multi-modal data analytics and its potential for future development.

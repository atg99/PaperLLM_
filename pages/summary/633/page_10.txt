The paper discusses various datasets and methods related to GAN-based clustering in multi-view samples. It summarizes key datasets used, including Reuters, HW, BDGP, MNIST, CCV, and Youtube, highlighting their features, such as the number of categories, documents, and views involved. 

Several methods are introduced that leverage GANs for multi-view tasks. For instance, GM-VAR connects different feature domains by generating latent representations based on view correlations. Xuan et al. applied multi-view GANs for automatic pearl classification, while Lai et al. focused on facial expression recognition, using a GAN to transform non-frontal images into frontal ones while preserving identity. LB-GAN was introduced by Cao et al. for multi-view face image synthesis, decomposing the task into subtasks managed by pairs of GANs. Tian et al. proposed CR-GAN for self-supervised learning, enhancing generation quality through labeled and unlabeled data.

The challenges of missing views in multi-view applications are addressed by several studies. Chen et al. proposed Multi-view BiGAN for handling missing views, while Doinychko et al. introduced a biconditional GAN with two generators and a common discriminator. Shang et al.'s VIGAN tackles missing view imputation by treating each view as a separate domain and establishing domain mappings through adversarial learning. Overall, the paper emphasizes the advancements in multi-view GAN methods and their applications across various domains.

This paper presents a novel 3D brain segmentation network, termed Brain SegNet, designed for the automatic segmentation of brain lesions, including brain tumors and ischemic stroke lesions, from 3D MRI scans. The authors introduce a unique 3D refinement module that effectively combines local details with 3D semantic context using convolutional layers. Additionally, they implement a new training strategy that utilizes curriculum learning and Focal loss to enhance the model's generalization by gradually increasing data complexity.

The performance of the proposed model is benchmarked against existing methods, specifically those reported in a previous study where two models were trained separately and then combined. In contrast, the authors demonstrate that their single model trained on both 4D PWI and standard MRIs achieves a dice score of 0.30, outperforming the combined models from the previous study. The proposed method also significantly improves processing speed, achieving approximately 0.1 seconds per MRI volume, compared to 30 seconds in earlier approaches.

The study concludes that Brain SegNet offers state-of-the-art performance in brain lesion segmentation on the BRATS 2015 and ISLES 2017 databases, highlighting its efficiency and effectiveness for ischemic stroke segmentation from MRI data. The authors emphasize the model's compactness and end-to-end trainability, marking a significant advancement in the field of medical imaging.

The provided references encompass a range of studies focused on generative adversarial networks (GANs) and their applications in image and video synthesis. Key contributions include the exploration of GAN steerability, improvements in generator architecture, and metrics for evaluating image quality. 

Jahanian et al. discuss the concept of steerability in GANs, while Jiang et al. introduce TransGAN, demonstrating that two transformers can enhance GAN performance. Karras et al. present a style-based generator architecture and further analyze image quality in StyleGAN, contributing significantly to the field.

Khrulkov and Oseledets propose a geometry score for comparing GANs, and Kingma and Welling introduce auto-encoding variational bayes, which is foundational to generative modeling. Kolchinski et al. focus on approximating human judgment in assessing generated image quality, while Kynkäänniemi et al. develop an improved precision and recall metric for generative models.

Lee and Town emphasize reproducibility in GAN research, and Liu et al. provide a comprehensive overview of GAN algorithms and applications. Liu et al. also propose an enhanced evaluation framework for GANs, and Lucic et al. conduct a large-scale study to assess the equality of various GANs.

Lastly, Mathiasen and Hvilshøj present a method for fast Fréchet Inception Distance calculation, which is crucial for evaluating the performance of generative models. Overall, these works contribute to advancing the understanding and capabilities of GANs in generating high-quality images and videos.

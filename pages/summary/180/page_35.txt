The referenced papers explore various aspects of deep generative models and their evaluation. Zeng et al. (2017) analyze the statistical properties of images generated by deep learning techniques. Zhang et al. (2018) discuss the effectiveness of deep features as a perceptual metric, highlighting their utility in computer vision tasks. Zhao et al. (2018) conduct an empirical study on bias and generalization within deep generative models, providing insights into their performance across different datasets. Zhou et al. (2019) introduce a benchmark called HYPE, aimed at assessing human perceptual evaluation of generative models, thereby contributing to the understanding of how these models align with human visual perception. Together, these studies underscore the importance of both statistical analysis and perceptual evaluation in advancing the field of deep learning and generative models.

In conclusion, while the evaluation of Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) has made strides with established measures like Inception Score (IS), Fréchet Inception Distance (FID), Precision and Recall (P&R), and Perceptual Path Length (PPL), the field still faces challenges. The complexity of scenes generated, the exploration of dimensions beyond quality and diversity, and the consideration of task dependency are critical areas for future research. Addressing these challenges will contribute to more comprehensive and objective evaluations of GANs, ultimately enhancing their development and application in diverse domains.

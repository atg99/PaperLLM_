The paper presents a series of images where participants are asked to identify which face in each pair is real. The results are summarized in a key that indicates the correct answers for each row, with "L" denoting the left face and "R" denoting the right face. The answers for the pairs are as follows: left, left, right, left, right, right, left, left, left, right, right, right, right, left. The images used in the study are sourced from the website whichfaceisreal.com.

The paper presents a comprehensive survey focused on the advancements and challenges in face manipulation and fake detection technologies, particularly highlighting the emergence of deepfakes. Tolosana et al. (2020) discuss the various techniques for creating synthetic media and the implications for security and authenticity. They analyze existing detection methods, emphasizing the need for robust solutions to combat the growing sophistication of face manipulation technologies.

Tsitsulin et al. (2019) introduce a novel metric for measuring intrinsic distances in data distributions, which can enhance understanding of data shapes in machine learning. Tulyakov et al. (2018) present Mocogan, a framework that separates content and motion in video generation, offering insights into improving generative models.

Unterthiner et al. (2019) propose a new evaluation metric for video generation, while Verma and Rubin (2018) provide an overview of fairness definitions in software, emphasizing the importance of fairness in AI applications. Wang et al. (2020a) highlight the ease of detecting CNN-generated images, underscoring the current capabilities of detection technologies.

Further contributions include Wang et al. (2020b), who explore the use of neural signals to assess GAN performance in facial generation, and Wang et al. (2019), who survey the landscape of GANs in computer vision. Xu et al. (2018) introduce FairGAN, which aims to address fairness in generative models, and Xuan et al. (2019) investigate the anomalous generalization properties of GANs.

Yang et al. (2018) discuss pose-guided human video generation, while Yu et al. (2015) detail the construction of a large-scale image dataset using deep learning techniques. Finally, Yu et al. (2019) focus on identifying GAN-generated images through fingerprint analysis, and Yu et al. (2020) present Inclusive GAN, which aims to enhance minority representation in generative models. 

Overall, these studies collectively highlight the rapid advancements in generative technologies and the pressing need for effective detection and fairness measures in AI systems.

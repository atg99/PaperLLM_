The collection of references highlights significant contributions to the field of generative models and their evaluation. Ravuri and Vinyals discuss classification accuracy in conditional generative models, while Razavi et al. focus on generating diverse, high-fidelity images using VQ-VAE-2. Roblek et al. propose a reference-free metric, Fréchet audio distance, for assessing music enhancement algorithms. Sajjadi et al. introduce a framework for evaluating generative models through precision and recall. Salimans et al. improve GAN training techniques, and Sattigeri et al. explore fairness in datasets generated by GANs.

Shmelkov et al. question GAN performance, while Shoemake presents quaternion curves for animation. Sidheekh et al. examine GAN convergence using proximal duality gap, and Simon et al. revisit precision and recall definitions for generative model evaluation. Simonyan and Zisserman contribute to image recognition with deep convolutional networks. Soloveitchik et al. propose Conditional Fréchet Inception Distance for model evaluation, and van Steenkiste et al. investigate object compositionality in GANs. Finally, Tevet et al. evaluate text GANs as language models, and Theis et al. provide insights into generative model evaluation methodologies. Together, these works advance the understanding and application of generative models in various domains.

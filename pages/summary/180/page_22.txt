The paper discusses the GAN dissection approach as presented by Bau et al. in 2018. It highlights two main methods for analyzing the relationship between representation units and image output: dissection and intervention. Dissection assesses the correlation between a unit and a concept by comparing a thresholded heatmap of the unit with a semantic segmentation of the generated image. In contrast, intervention evaluates the causal impact of specific units on a concept by manipulating these units—either by inserting or ablating them—and observing changes in the output. Specifically, the study shows that manipulating units related to trees results in a measurable change in the number of tree pixels in the generated images. Additionally, the dissection method can be applied to enhance GAN performance by identifying and removing units that contribute to artifacts, thereby improving the overall quality of generated images.

The cited works focus on various aspects of generative models and their evaluation. McDuff et al. explore bias in classifiers using generative models, while Meehan et al. propose a non-parametric test for detecting data-copying within these models. Morozov et al. examine self-supervised image representations for evaluating Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs). Naeem et al. present reliable metrics for assessing fidelity and diversity in generative models.

Narvekar and Karam introduce a no-reference perceptual image sharpness metric, contributing to the quality assessment of multimedia experiences. Nash et al. investigate image generation through sparse representations. O’Brien et al. evaluate GANs against explicitly parameterized distributions, and Odena raises critical questions regarding GANs.

Oord et al. discuss conditional image generation using PixelCNN decoders, while Oprea et al. review deep learning techniques for video prediction. Park et al. focus on semantic image synthesis using spatially-adaptive normalization. Parmar et al. address issues in fidelity calculation related to resizing libraries. Finally, Preuer et al. introduce the Fréchet ChemNet distance as a metric for generative models in drug discovery, and Ramesh et al. present methods for zero-shot text-to-image generation. Together, these studies contribute to advancing the understanding and evaluation of generative models across various applications.

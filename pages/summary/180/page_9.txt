This test involves evaluating the similarity between generated outputs and actual samples, indicating overfitting if the model produces outputs that closely resemble the training data. The authors argue that this approach is crucial for identifying limitations in generative models, especially in their ability to generalize beyond the training dataset.

In summary, the paper discusses two important metrics related to the performance of generative models. The Classification Accuracy Score (CAS) assesses how well generative models can produce data that is meaningful for downstream tasks, revealing significant drops in accuracy when using certain models like BigGAN. Additionally, the concept of data-copying highlights the risks of overfitting, suggesting that models might not be learning the underlying data distribution but rather memorizing specific instances. Both metrics underline the need for robust evaluation methods in the development of generative models.

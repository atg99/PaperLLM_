The paper presents an analysis of azimuthal integration applied to the power spectrum of images, specifically comparing real and GAN-generated images from the CelebA dataset. It illustrates how the 1D Power Spectrum is derived from the 2D spectrum, highlighting a notable increase in statistics at higher frequencies. A visual comparison is made between the mean DCT spectrum of real images from the FFHQ dataset and those generated by StyleGAN, with results averaged over 10,000 images.

Furthermore, the paper introduces the Caption Score (CapS) as a metric for evaluating text-to-image generation models. Unlike existing metrics that primarily assess image realism, CapS addresses the alignment between generated images and their corresponding textual descriptions. This score provides a more detailed evaluation than traditional metrics like FID and Inception Score, and it is defined mathematically to assess the quality and accuracy of text-image correspondence. Higher CapS values indicate better performance in generating images that accurately reflect the provided text.

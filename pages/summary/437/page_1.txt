The paper introduces an auxiliary task to the Mask R-CNN architecture, aimed at accelerating the training of its mask head. This new component, termed the Edge Agreement Head, draws inspiration from human annotators who primarily trace object boundaries rather than focusing solely on the instance areas. By leveraging edge detection filters, this head encourages the predicted masks to align closely with the ground truth in terms of image gradients. 

The authors conduct a comprehensive analysis of various loss combinations, demonstrating improvements in MS COCO Mask metrics compared to setups without the additional loss. While the Edge Agreement Head slightly increases the model size, it does not add any trainable variables and only marginally raises computational costs, which are minor relative to the overall expenses of Mask R-CNN. Significantly, the inference speed remains unaffected since the additional head is utilized solely during training. The proposed method achieves an 8.1% relative improvement on MS COCO metrics and enhances training speed in the standard Mask R-CNN configuration.

The paper emphasizes the importance of efficient instance segmentation, especially given the labor-intensive nature of creating labeled datasets. By enhancing the training process of Mask R-CNN, the authors contribute to making instance segmentation models more data-efficient and effective.

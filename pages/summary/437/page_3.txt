The paper introduces the Edge Agreement Loss (LEdge) to improve instance segmentation in Mask R-CNN by addressing common issues such as incomplete or poorly defined masks during early training stages. The authors emphasize the importance of accurately identifying object boundaries, akin to human perception, before segmenting the enclosed areas. LEdge is defined as the Lp loss between the edges of the predicted and ground-truth masks, integrated with the original Mask R-CNN loss (LMRNN) to form a total loss (LTotal).

Edge detection is a critical component of LEdge, utilizing filters like Sobel and Laplacian to identify edges in images. The Sobel filter captures horizontal and vertical gradients, while the Laplacian filter, being rotation invariant, detects edges through zero-crossings. Additionally, the authors explore other edge detection operators such as the Prewitt and Roberts filters.

To compute LEdge, the authors propose an Edge Agreement Head, which processes the predicted and ground-truth masks using selected edge detection filters. The differences between the resulting edge maps are calculated using Lp loss functions, with a specific focus on the mean square error for effective loss computation.

Overall, the proposed method aims to enhance the accuracy of instance segmentation by reinforcing edge detection during the training of Mask R-CNN, ultimately leading to better alignment of predicted masks with actual object boundaries.

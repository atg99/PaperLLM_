The paper investigates the impact of various edge detection filters and loss functions on the performance of instance segmentation models, specifically using the MS COCO dataset. 

Table 1 presents the effect of different edge detection filters on COCO Average Precision (AP) metrics after 160,000 training steps. The results indicate that the Sobel filter yields the highest AP across most metrics, followed closely by the Prewitt filter. The performance of the other filters, including Kayyali, Roberts, and Laplace, is comparatively lower.

Table 2 examines the influence of different Lp loss functions on AP metrics, with the L2 loss showing notable performance improvements. The findings suggest that a well-chosen loss function can significantly enhance model accuracy.

Further analysis explores the scaling of the Edge Agreement Loss relative to other losses. It is found that increasing the weight of the Edge Agreement Loss improves the Mask R-CNN loss, indicating a beneficial trade-off between mask accuracy and overall network objectives. 

The paper also investigates the effect of mask size on performance, revealing that while using the Edge Agreement Head as an auxiliary loss improves performance, a larger mask size (56x56) results in worse overall performance compared to a smaller size (28x28). This is hypothesized to be due to the increased complexity requiring longer training.

Longer training durations (up to 640,000 steps) demonstrate consistent improvements across metrics, with the Edge Agreement Head maintaining its benefits even in later training stages. Interestingly, models trained with the Edge Agreement Head for 160,000 steps outperformed those trained for longer periods, suggesting its effectiveness throughout the training process.

The paper notes that its results are lower than previous findings by He et al. due to differences in implementation and batch size, yet asserts that this does not diminish the generalizability of the proposed method. 

Overall, the research emphasizes the importance of edge detection filters and loss function configurations in enhancing instance segmentation performance, highlighting the Edge Agreement Loss as a particularly effective component.

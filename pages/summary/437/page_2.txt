This paper introduces enhancements to the Mask R-CNN framework through the incorporation of an Edge Agreement Head, aimed at improving instance segmentation by ensuring precise localization of object boundaries. The proposed method utilizes a new loss term, LMask, which calculates pixel-wise cross entropy between predicted and target masks. The overall loss function, LMRCNN, is a combination of classification, bounding box regression, and the new mask loss, forming a multi-task learning approach.

The Edge Agreement Head serves as an auxiliary task that applies traditional edge detection filters, such as Sobel and Laplacian, to both predicted and ground-truth masks. This approach aligns the edges of predicted masks with those of the ground-truth masks, facilitating faster training by leveraging instance boundaries as robust features that can be effectively propagated to the mask branch.

The paper discusses related work, highlighting the benefits of multi-task learning and auxiliary tasks in various domains, including facial landmark detection and semantic segmentation. It also references previous methods in monocular depth estimation and scene segmentation that utilize image gradients and edge detection to enhance model performance. The use of simple, predefined edge detection filters in this work minimizes computational overhead, especially given the low-resolution nature of the instance masks.

The paper further contrasts its approach with existing boundary-aware instance segmentation methods, noting that prior methods have achieved lower performance on benchmark datasets compared to Mask R-CNN. The authors argue that their method's design, which focuses on low-dimensional instance masks, reduces complexity and enhances efficiency without requiring additional preprocessing steps. Overall, the Edge Agreement Head is positioned as a valuable enhancement to Mask R-CNN that improves the accuracy and training efficiency of instance segmentation tasks.

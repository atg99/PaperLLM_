The paper presents an analysis of various configurations for the Edge Agreement Head in Mask R-CNN, focusing on their impact on performance as measured on the MS COCO dataset. It includes visual comparisons of loss values and relative improvements relative to a baseline model, examining the effects of different edge detection filters, Gaussian smoothing, and variations in the Lp loss function.

Key findings include:
1. **Influence of Edge Detection Filters**: The use of Sobel and Laplace filters, both individually and in combination, demonstrates varied effectiveness in reducing loss values and improving performance compared to the baseline. The Sobel filter without smoothing shows notable results.
2. **Gaussian Smoothing**: The impact of Gaussian smoothing on the Sobel edge detection filter is also assessed, indicating its role in enhancing performance.
3. **Lp Loss Function Variations**: The analysis of different values of p in the Lp loss function reveals that while increasing p can decrease mask loss, it simultaneously raises the overall loss, suggesting a balance is necessary for optimal results. The L2 loss is identified as the most effective choice for this context.
4. **Mask Predictions**: Qualitative comparisons illustrate that models trained with Edge Agreement Loss are less prone to generating false positive bounding boxes. This suggests that the features leveraged during training are beneficial for the Region Proposal Network.

Overall, the findings indicate that careful selection of edge detection methods and loss function parameters can significantly enhance the performance of Mask R-CNN in object detection tasks.

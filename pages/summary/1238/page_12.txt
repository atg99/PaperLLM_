The paper presents a comparative analysis of classification performance among various models using Nemenyi post-hoc tests to assess significance based on accuracy. The models evaluated include RaF, MPRaF variants, RaF-PCA, RaF-LDA, DRaF, and MPDRaF variants. The results indicate that DRaF-LDA outperforms RaF, MPRaF-T, MPRaF-N, RaF-PCA, and MPDRaF-N significantly. Additionally, DRaF-PCA shows a significant performance improvement over the DRaF-PCA model.

The statistical foundation for these conclusions is established through the calculation of average ranks for each model, yielding values such as 6.99 for RaF and 5.04 for DRaF-LDA. The F-statistic calculations indicate a significant difference among the models, leading to the rejection of the null hypothesis. The critical difference for determining significance was calculated as 1.5149 at a 5% significance level.

The findings are visually represented in Figure 1, where models not connected by lines indicate significant differences. Further analysis of decision boundaries for the spiral dataset is provided in supplementary materials. Overall, the proposed models demonstrate improved classification capabilities compared to baseline models.

3.2. Rotation based double random forest models
The rotation based double random forest models aim to enhance the decision-making process by transforming the feature space. This transformation allows for a better representation of the data, leading to improved classification performance. The rotation technique alters the orientation of the feature space, which can help in capturing the underlying structure of the data more effectively. 

The proposed rotation based models utilize a combination of rotation matrices and the double random forest framework to create more sophisticated decision boundaries. By applying these rotations, the models can better accommodate datasets that do not adhere to the assumptions of traditional methods, thus enhancing the overall robustness and accuracy of the predictions.

In conclusion, this paper introduces innovative methods such as the oblique double random forest with MPSVM and rotation based double random forest models. These approaches leverage advanced regularization techniques and transformations to improve the performance of decision tree ensembles, addressing limitations of conventional models and providing better generalization capabilities.

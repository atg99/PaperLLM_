The paper discusses the contributions of artificial intelligence in the field of drug discovery and quantum chemistry. It highlights several key studies, including work on neural message passing for quantum chemistry and the design of bioactive small molecules through AI. The authors acknowledge support from the National Science Foundation and express gratitude to individuals who contributed to the visualization and review of the manuscript. The collaborative effort involved multiple authors who conceived the idea, performed computations, and supervised the findings. The paper is published under a Creative Commons license, allowing for sharing and adaptation with proper attribution. No competing interests were declared by the authors.

The paper presents a collection of references focused on various applications of deep learning techniques, particularly Long Short Term Memory (LSTM) networks and related architectures. Key topics include dynamic bandwidth management through traffic prediction, financial time series forecasting using stacked autoencoders and LSTM, and the prediction of sea surface temperature with LSTM. Additionally, the use of LSTM for humanoid robot behavior recognition and human-object interaction detection is highlighted. Other contributions include short-term electricity load forecasting using a self-normalizing GRU network and traffic flow prediction by integrating weather data with a GRU-based approach. The references also discuss activation functions in neural networks and optimization methods like Adam and its enhancement with Nesterov momentum. The authors emphasize the versatility and effectiveness of deep learning models in solving complex prediction and recognition tasks across different domains. The article is published under an open-access license, allowing for broad dissemination of the findings.

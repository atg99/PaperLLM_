The cited works encompass a range of methodologies and advancements in machine learning and deep learning, particularly focusing on predictive modeling and time series analysis. Key contributions include:

1. Data-driven approaches for predicting remaining useful life (RUL) using principal component and instance learning (Li et al. 2016).
2. The foundational concepts of machine learning discussed in Murphy's comprehensive text (2012) and the introduction of support vector networks by Cortes and Vapnik (1995).
3. Random forests as a robust ensemble method (Breiman 2001) and the scalable tree boosting system XGBoost (Chen and Guestrin 2016).
4. Reviews of deep learning models for time series prediction (Han et al. 2019) and representation learning (Bengio et al. 2013).
5. Notable works on neural networks, including Hinton and Salakhutdinov's dimensionality reduction (2006) and the introduction of long short-term memory (LSTM) networks by Hochreiter and Schmidhuber (1997).
6. Applications of LSTM in predicting RUL for lithium-ion batteries (Zhang et al. 2017) and turbofan engines (Mathew et al. 2017).
7. Comparisons of statistical and machine learning techniques for time series data (Alfred et al. 2015) and the exploration of gated recurrent units (Chung et al. 2014).

These studies collectively contribute to the understanding of predictive analytics in various domains through the application of advanced machine learning techniques.

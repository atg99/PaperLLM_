The paper discusses the derivation of partial derivatives of the objective function, E, with respect to the parameters of the Vanilla LSTM cell during training. The equations (156-170) detail the derivatives for various weight matrices and biases associated with the input, forget, output, and candidate gates of the LSTM cell at a given time step, n. 

The total partial derivative of the objective function with respect to the model parameters, Θ, is presented in Equation (171), encapsulating all the individual derivatives from each gate and bias. When the LSTM cell is unrolled for K steps to process a full segment of training samples, the same parameters are shared across all time steps, leading to the total derivative expressed in Equation (172). 

This total derivative is critical for optimization via Gradient Descent, where it is computed over a batch of segments, typically ranging from 16 to 128, to update the model parameters effectively. The paper notes that while regularization is vital in the machine learning training process, it is not covered in this discussion.

The paper presents the Augmented LSTM cell, detailing its parameters and the inference phase defined by a set of equations. The parameters, represented as Θ, include various weight matrices and bias vectors essential for the functioning of the cell. Specifically, the parameters encompass weights for input, context, and output, organized in a comprehensive manner to facilitate understanding of the forward pass.

The Augmented LSTM cell is illustrated in a figure where bias parameters are omitted for clarity. It is noted that these biases can be integrated into the model by modifying the input vector. Additionally, the paper describes non-causal context windows that utilize convolutions with matrix-valued linear filters to compute relevant quantities from the external input.

Overall, the Augmented LSTM architecture aims to enhance the traditional LSTM by incorporating context information, thereby improving its performance in sequence prediction tasks.

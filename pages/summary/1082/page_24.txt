The section outlines the notation and processes relevant to the Vanilla LSTM cell, detailing key components and their functions. It introduces specific variables such as the index of a step, the number of steps in a segment, and various signals associated with the cell, including input, state, observable value, and accumulation nodes. 

The Vanilla LSTM employs three types of gate nodes for control purposes: managing the update candidate signal for the state signal, controlling the state signal from the previous step, and regulating the readout candidate signal. 

Additionally, it emphasizes the importance of standardizing the external training data set before LSTM operation. This involves ensuring that input elements have a mean of zero and a standard deviation of one, using equations for calculating the mean, sample auto-covariance matrix, and the standardized input. The standardization process is critical for effective model training, and it is noted that test and validation sets should be standardized using parameters derived from the training set.

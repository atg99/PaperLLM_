The paper discusses the derivation of total partial derivatives of the objective function in the context of Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) using Backpropagation Through Time (BPTT). It presents equations that illustrate how the total partial derivatives with respect to the readout and state signals at a given index, n, can be expressed. The initialization of the error gradient sequence at the end of the unrolled RNN is crucial, as indicated in Equation 85. The subsequent derivations of model parameters, as shown in Equations 86 to 90, highlight that the same model parameters are shared across all time steps of the RNN, necessitating that the total derivative of the objective function accounts for contributions from all steps. 

Additionally, it is noted that the quantities crucial for updating the model parameters are directly proportional to the error gradient signal, ⃗ψ[n]. The gradient flows in reverse, enabling the influence of gradients from earlier steps on later parameters, thus allowing the model to learn from distant samples.

The paper emphasizes the importance of maintaining numerical stability in the gradient contributions from earlier time steps, as it affects the optimization process through Gradient Descent. The expansion of the recursion in Equation 84 shows the relationship between gradients at different indices, providing insights into how the model can effectively learn from long-range interactions among samples during training.

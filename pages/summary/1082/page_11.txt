The paper discusses the sampling of RNN sequences using a full N-samples-long window, represented by the equation w[n] = u[n] - u[n-N]. The equation is broken down into components, with the primary focus on the index m, which ranges from 0 to M-1. The function j(m) is defined to index the samples, and wm[n] represents a rectangular window of size Km samples. The extraction of a segment from the ground truth output sequence v[n] involves multiplying this sequence by wm[n], leading to non-zero values for the segment-level output vm[n] within specified index ranges.

The paper introduces Q as an invertible map that transforms the readout signals of the RNN into observable output signals. An objective function L is defined to measure the deviation of the observable output from the desired ground truth output over the entire index range. The parameters of the standard RNN system are collectively represented by the symbol Θ. Overall, the equations illustrate the mechanisms of segment extraction and the relationship between readout signals and observable outputs within an RNN framework.

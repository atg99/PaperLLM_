The section discusses the concepts of "unfolding" or "unrolling" in Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs), specifically in the context of the state signal defined by Equations 30 and 32. Initially, the state is uninitialized, and the unfolding process involves specifying initial conditions and numerically evaluating the equations over a finite range of discrete steps indexed by n. This is illustrated in a figure showing the sequence of steps generated by unrolling an RNN cell.

The recursive nature of the state signal indicates that at any index n, the state signal incorporates contributions from all previous states and inputs, establishing RNNs as Infinite Impulse Response (IIR) systems. Furthermore, the section defines the vector-valued unit step function and the unit sample function, explaining their mathematical representations and visual depictions. The unit step function is defined as a vector that equals one for non-negative indices and zero otherwise, while the unit sample function is derived from the unit step function. These definitions are crucial for understanding the behavior of RNNs in processing sequences.

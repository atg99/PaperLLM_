The paper discusses various methods that integrate instance-level classification with contrastive learning, highlighting the success of recent approaches like SwAV, MoCo, and SimCLR, which have achieved performance levels comparable to traditional supervised methods on the ImageNet dataset. It emphasizes the importance of pretext tasks in enhancing model performance, as demonstrated by works such as PIRL and Selﬁe, which utilize self-supervised learning strategies to improve the effectiveness of learned representations for various computer vision tasks.

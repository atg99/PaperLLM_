This paper compiles a list of references focused on advancements in self-supervised and contrastive learning techniques in computer vision and natural language processing. Key contributions include methods for unsupervised learning through tasks like jigsaw puzzles, image rotation prediction, and contrastive predictive coding. Notable works cover the development of visual representations, such as the use of multiple pretext tasks and deep clustering methods for feature extraction. The references also explore the efficiency of various optimization techniques like Adam and Stochastic Gradient Descent with Warm Restarts. Overall, the collection emphasizes the increasing importance of self-supervised learning approaches, particularly in enhancing representation learning without labeled data.

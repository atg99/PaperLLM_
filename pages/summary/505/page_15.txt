This section discusses the analysis of kernels and feature maps in convolutional neural networks, focusing on comparing the first convolutional layer's kernels from encoders trained using self-supervised (contrastive) and supervised methods. The goal is to assess the effectiveness of the self-supervised approach. Additionally, attention maps generated from various encoder layers are evaluated to determine the success of the methods employed. The work by Gidaris et al. is referenced to illustrate the evaluation of effectiveness based on the activated regions in the input images.

The paper presents a comparison of various methods and architectures for image classification and related tasks using the Places dataset and other benchmarks. Table 2 outlines the image classification accuracy of different models pretrained on ImageNet, highlighting that supervised models like ResNet50 achieve a top-1 accuracy of 53.2, while contrastive learning methods such as SwAV reach 56.7. The paper emphasizes that these methods not only excel in classification but also perform well in object detection and action recognition.

Table 3 further details the performance of these models in linear classification and object detection tasks on the Pascal VOC dataset. Supervised ResNet50 shows top-1 accuracy of 87.5 in classification and 81.3 in detection, while SwAV demonstrates strong performance with 88.9 and 82.6, respectively.

Table 4 summarizes the accuracy of various methods on video classification tasks across datasets like UCF101 and HMDB51. Notable results include the performance of supervised models like 3DResNet-18, achieving 84.4 in UCF101. The paper concludes that contrastive learning approaches provide promising results across multiple tasks, indicating their effectiveness in the field.

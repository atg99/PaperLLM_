The references provided in this list cover a range of significant contributions to the fields of machine learning, time series classification, and representation learning. Key works include:

1. Arora et al. present a theoretical analysis of contrastive unsupervised representation learning.
2. Bagnall et al. conduct a comprehensive review and evaluation of recent advancements in time series classification.
3. The UEA multivariate time series classification archive is introduced by Bagnall et al., serving as a valuable resource for researchers.
4. Bai et al. empirically evaluate the effectiveness of convolutional and recurrent networks for sequence modeling.
5. The binary shapelet transform for multiclass time series classification is discussed by Bostrom and Bagnall.
6. Bredin introduces Tristounet, which utilizes triplet loss for speaker turn embedding.
7. Cui et al. explore multi-scale convolutional neural networks for time series classification.
8. The UCR time series classification archive is highlighted by Dau et al. as a crucial resource for researchers.
9. Denton and Birodkar focus on unsupervised learning of disentangled representations from video.
10. Dosovitskiy et al. investigate discriminative unsupervised feature learning with convolutional neural networks.
11. Fortuin et al. propose SOM-VAE for interpretable discrete representation learning on time series.
12. Goldberg and Levy explain the word2vec method for generating word embeddings.
13. Goodfellow et al. provide a comprehensive resource on deep learning principles.
14. Hochreiter and Schmidhuber introduce long short-term memory networks, which are pivotal in sequence modeling.
15. Hyvarinen and Morioka present a method for unsupervised feature extraction combining time-contrastive learning and nonlinear ICA.
16. Ismail Fawaz et al. review deep learning approaches for time series classification.
17. Jansen et al. discuss unsupervised learning of semantic audio representations.
18. Kingma and Ba introduce the Adam optimization method, which is widely used in deep learning.

These references collectively contribute to advancing the understanding and application of machine learning techniques, particularly in time series analysis and representation learning.

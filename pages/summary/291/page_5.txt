A failure case highlights that OpenPose can misplace keypoints due to motion blur, suggesting a potential improvement through fine-tuning OpenPose during training, which remains for future research.

The error analysis reveals significant confusion between similar signs, particularly in the cases of "BRIEF" and "ENVELOPPE," and two variants of "V AN." This confusion underscores the need for contextual information, which is lacking in isolated sign language recognition (SLR). Future work could explore incorporating side-channels to predict sign parameters to enhance classification accuracy.

The impact of dataset size is also analyzed, with an updated Corpus VGT containing 18,730 labeled samples leading to substantial improvements in accuracy over previous work. The results indicate that as dataset size increases, accuracy on unseen data also improves, suggesting that further increases in data or data augmentation could enhance performance. The learning curve for the MTN shows that validation accuracy has not yet converged, reinforcing the necessity for more labeled data.

In conclusion, this study introduces four SLR network architectures, demonstrating advancements in accuracy through improved datasets and model architectures. Future research directions include refining feature extraction methods and addressing classification challenges with contextual information.

The paper discusses recent advancements and trends in artificial intelligence (AI) technologies related to financial risk monitoring, digital identity verification, and sentiment analysis. It highlights the integration of machine learning and hybrid deep learning methodologies for improving predictive accuracy in financial applications. Several studies are referenced, focusing on the application of AI in various domains, including customer churn prediction, fraud prevention in digital payments, and the optimization of cloud resources for enterprise management.

Key topics include the use of machine learning decision trees for early warning systems in banking, the implementation of AI-driven models for medical image analysis, and the development of multimodal networks for emotion recognition. The authors emphasize the importance of automating model training and deployment in machine learning operations and the role of AI in enhancing customer experiences and operational efficiencies across sectors.

Overall, the paper underscores the transformative impact of AI technologies in finance and other fields, advocating for continued research and development to harness their full potential.

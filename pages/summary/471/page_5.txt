The paper discusses the methodology for classifying financial data, specifically closing prices from stock markets in Shanghai and Shenzhen. It outlines a process in which data samples were categorized into three types based on their trends: upward, downward, and no clear trend. The closing prices were collected over a period of 100 working days prior to October 20, 2012, and converted into structured images for analysis.

For the training of a deep learning model, the dataset comprised 5,000 pre-training, 3,000 validation, and 2,000 testing samples. The deep belief network model trained on this data aimed to perform predictions on daily financial data from 2012 to 2014. This approach was crucial in verifying the effectiveness of the model, as it utilized the same classification methodology for validation and testing datasets. The paper emphasizes the significance of structured data in enhancing predictive accuracy in financial markets.

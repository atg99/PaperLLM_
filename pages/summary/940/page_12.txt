The references cited cover a range of topics related to multi-armed bandit problems, online stochastic optimization, and Bayesian optimization. Key contributions include:

1. Audibert et al. (2006, 2009) explore variance estimation and the exploration-exploitation tradeoff in multi-armed bandits.
2. Auer et al. (2007) improve rates for stochastic continuum-armed bandit problems.
3. Azar et al. (2014) address online stochastic optimization with correlated bandit feedback.
4. Bartlett et al. (2019) present an adaptive optimization approach under minimal local smoothness assumptions.
5. Bubeck et al. (2011a, 2011b) investigate pure exploration in both finitely-armed and continuous-armed bandits, as well as the concept of X-armed bandits.
6. Dai et al. (2020) discuss federated Bayesian optimization using Thompson sampling.
7. Deng (2012) provides insights on the MNIST database for machine learning.
8. Duchi et al. (2015) analyze optimal rates for zero-order convex optimization.
9. Frazier (2018) offers a tutorial on Bayesian optimization.
10. Golovin et al. (2017) introduce Google Vizier, a service for black-box optimization.
11. Grill et al. (2015) focus on black-box optimization of noisy functions with unknown smoothness.
12. Kandasamy et al. (2018) contribute to parallelized Bayesian optimization via Thompson sampling.

These works collectively enhance the understanding and methodologies in the field of optimization and decision-making under uncertainty.

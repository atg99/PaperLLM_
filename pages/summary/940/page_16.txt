The paper presents the VHCT Algorithm, which operates on a tree structure to optimize decision-making over time. Key elements include:

- Pt represents the optimal path selected at iteration t.
- H(t) indicates the maximum depth of the tree at time t.
- The function ∆(t) is defined as 1/ ˜δ(t+) where t+ is a function of log t, and ˜δ(t) is constrained by a constant c1 and the ratio δ/t.
- Ih(t) is the set of nodes at level h, while I+ h(t) contains only internal nodes.
- Ch,i captures time steps when a specific node Ph,i is selected, and C+ h,i includes the time steps for its children.
- Various time indicators such as ¯th,i and /tildewideth,i track the last selection times for nodes and their children.
- th,i marks the time when node Ph,i is expanded, and the variance estimate Vh,i(t) is calculated based on the performance of the node.
- Lt encompasses all nodes in the exploration tree at time t, with Vmax as the upper bound on node variance.

The algorithm initializes with specific nodes and iterates through time steps, updating node values and expanding the tree as necessary. The exploration process is governed by conditions on node variance and selection criteria, ensuring continuous improvement and exploration of the tree structure. The assumption that node variance is above a small constant ε aids in the clarity of the proofs presented.

The paper analyzes the performance of various algorithms on synthetic functions, particularly focusing on cumulative regret and the impact of high noise levels in function evaluations. Figures 6 and 7 illustrate the performance of VHCT and HCT algorithms across different test functions, including DoubleSine, Counter Example, Himmelblau, and Rastrigin. The results indicate that VHCT performs similarly to HCT under high noise conditions, aligning with the expectations outlined in Section 4.4. This suggests that while VHCT may offer marginal improvements, its effectiveness is comparable to HCT in challenging scenarios.

The paper discusses various applications and developments of fuzzy logic and Adaptive Neuro-Fuzzy Inference System (ANFIS) in industrial processes, control systems, and data communication. 

In industrial applications, ANFIS was utilized to predict free fatty acid content in palm oil refining, demonstrating its effectiveness in quality estimation. Fuzzy logic has been applied in the oil and gas sector for tasks such as geological modeling, pressure control, and reservoir ranking. Additionally, a fuzzy logic model integrated with a wireless sensor network helped farmers monitor soil parameters for stevia crops, enhancing agricultural practices. Studies showed that fuzzy controllers in refrigeration and air conditioning improved thermal efficiency and reduced energy consumption compared to traditional controllers. 

Fuzzy logic also optimized the operation of a small-scale solar plant by adapting to user energy consumption patterns. In manufacturing, fuzzy control improved feed rate scheduling for CNC machines, enhancing processing time and precision. A fuzzy logic controller was developed for energy management in residential microgrids to minimize power fluctuations. Furthermore, an ANFIS model was employed for risk assessment in railway station safety and optimized energy harvesting systems.

In the realm of data communication and the Internet of Things, fuzzy logic systems have been integrated into mobile ad-hoc networks (MANETs) to enhance transmission parameters and overall performance. A sender-initiated fuzzy protocol was introduced to balance operational load in computer networks, while a fuzzy neural network model addressed challenges like runtime ambiguity and data volume in the Internet industry. An adaptive fuzzy logic sampling method for data packets proved more effective than conventional techniques for assessing network quality of service.

This paper highlights the versatility and effectiveness of fuzzy logic and ANFIS across diverse fields, emphasizing their contributions to efficiency and performance improvement in various applications.

The paper discusses the use of a Fuzzy Logic Designer in MATLAB to evaluate car accident severity based on two input parameters: Speed and Distance. The Rule Viewer window displays these inputs alongside the output, which is the accident severity expressed as a percentage. Users can adjust the input values, and the system calculates the corresponding severity; for instance, at a speed of 40 mph and a distance of 40 m, the severity is calculated to be 60.2%. Additionally, a 3-dimensional plot in the Surface Viewer window illustrates the relationship between the inputs and the output, showing that increased speed and decreased distance lead to higher accident severity.

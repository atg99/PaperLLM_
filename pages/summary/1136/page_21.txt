The paper discusses advancements in fuzzy logic techniques, particularly focusing on fuzzy inference systems, adaptive neuro-fuzzy inference systems, and fuzzy c-means clustering. It highlights the integration of fuzzy logic with deep learning, which enhances the ability to address complex problems and opens new avenues for research. The article is designed to be accessible to a broad audience while acknowledging that although fuzzy logic is established, there are still many opportunities for development and application. Notable areas include hybrid systems that combine fuzzy logic with other artificial intelligence techniques, as well as applications in embedded systems for optimizing integration with circuits. The paper concludes by outlining fundamental set properties and operations related to fuzzy logic, emphasizing their significance in the field. The research did not receive external funding, and there are no conflicts of interest reported.

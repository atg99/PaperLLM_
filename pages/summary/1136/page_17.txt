Handover processes in cellular networks are crucial for maintaining continuous connections as users move across coverage areas. An adaptive handover decision system using fuzzy logic was developed for 5G ultra-dense networks, allowing dynamic adjustment of handover parameters. Fuzzy control systems have also improved edge computing operations.

Quality of Service (QoS) and Quality of Experience (QoE) are essential in video transmission, reflecting communication traffic and user perception respectively. A modular fuzzy logic system was created to evaluate QoS and QoE in wireless networks, demonstrating successful performance. Fuzzy logic has also been effective in countering non-periodic denial of service attacks.

In the realm of the Internet of Things (IoT), fuzzy logic has facilitated various applications such as security evaluations, threat detection frameworks, customizable IoT application development, activity recognition, and management of uncertainties in IoT gateways. Adaptive Neuro-Fuzzy Inference System (ANFIS) models have been employed in several IoT applications, including congestion regulation, fire warning systems, lung cancer detection, and harmonic minimization in smart devices.

Fuzzy logic has made significant contributions to image and signal processing, with applications in image segmentation, filtering, classification, and edge detection. Innovative combinations of fuzzy logic and convolutional neural networks have enhanced sound recognition tasks, while fuzzy filters have been used for noise reduction in images. Additionally, fuzzy logic techniques have improved the interpretation of lung radiography images and assisted in character recognition for Captcha systems.

ANFIS has been widely applied in medical image processing, aiding in the diagnosis of various cancers and improving techniques for segmenting breast tumors in mammograms. Fuzzy C-means (FCM) clustering has been utilized in diverse fields, including remote sensing and metabolomics data analysis.

Fuzzy logic systems have been implemented in both hardware and software, showcasing their versatility and effectiveness across multiple domains.

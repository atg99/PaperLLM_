The paper presents a comprehensive review of deep semantic segmentation techniques applied to both natural and medical images. It discusses various methodologies and advancements in the field, emphasizing the significance of semantic segmentation in understanding and interpreting visual data. Key contributions from notable studies are highlighted, including the development of models that learn object interactions, improve convolutional techniques for segmentation, and utilize neural architecture search specifically for medical images. The review also covers recent innovations such as non-local neural networks, reinforcement learning for object outlining, and enhanced loss functions for 3D segmentation tasks. The importance of computational techniques in analyzing medical images, particularly in pathology, is underscored, showcasing the potential of these technologies in clinical applications. Overall, the paper encapsulates the evolving landscape of deep semantic segmentation, with a focus on both theoretical advancements and practical implications in medical imaging.

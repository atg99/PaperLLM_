The section discusses various loss functions used in deep semantic segmentation for both natural and medical images. 

1. **Lovász-Softmax Loss**: This loss function, introduced by Berman et al., is a smooth extension of the discrete Jaccard loss and is suitable for multi-class segmentation. It is defined as the average of the convex closure of the submodular Jaccard loss across all classes. The Lovász-Softmax loss effectively addresses the limitations of traditional loss functions in capturing the nuances of segmentation tasks.

2. **Boundary Loss**: Proposed by Kervadec et al., this loss combines a generalized Dice loss with boundary loss to improve segmentation accuracy. The boundary loss specifically focuses on enhancing the prediction of object boundaries by penalizing errors in these critical areas. The formulation utilizes weights to balance contributions from foreground and background regions.

3. **Conservative Loss**: Introduced by Zhu et al., this loss is designed for domain adaptation tasks. It penalizes extreme prediction cases while promoting moderate predictions, thereby enhancing generalization. The loss function is parameterized with empirically chosen constants to optimize performance.

Additionally, other methods for optimizing segmentation metrics, adjusting loss function weights, and incorporating regularizers to include geometrical and topological shape priors are mentioned, emphasizing ongoing advancements in segmentation techniques.

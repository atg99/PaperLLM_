This paper discusses the evolution of segmentation networks, highlighting significant architectural advancements that have been made over time. It references various state-of-the-art models such as SegNet, U-Net, V-Net, and Tiramisu, which have been evaluated on datasets like PASCAL VOC 2012 and Cityscapes. A notable modification by Zhao et al. involved enhancing the feature fusing operation through the introduction of spatial pyramid pooling and encoder-decoder structures. These spatial pyramid networks improve the ability to capture multi-scale contextual information, while encoder-decoder architectures help in recovering spatial details for sharper object boundaries. 

The paper also details the pyramid scene parsing networks, which utilize a pyramid parsing module to obtain diverse sub-region representations, followed by upsampling and concatenation to create a comprehensive feature representation that integrates both local and global context. Furthermore, Chen et al. introduced DeepLabv3+, which builds upon DeepLabv3 by incorporating a decoder module that refines segmentation outcomes, particularly at object boundaries, leveraging dilated convolutions alongside pyramid features.

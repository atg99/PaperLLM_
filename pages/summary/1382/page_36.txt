The paper presents a collection of references focused on advancements in medical image segmentation and detection using deep learning techniques. Key contributions include the development of various neural network architectures, such as Retina U-Net and Mask R-CNN, specifically tailored for tasks like nucleus segmentation and lung nodule detection. The references highlight methods for addressing challenges in medical imaging, such as weakly supervised learning, handling noisy labels, and optimizing segmentation accuracy through innovative loss functions. Notable techniques include the use of generative adversarial networks for realistic simulation and multi-task learning approaches to improve segmentation performance. Overall, the cited works underscore the ongoing evolution of artificial intelligence applications in medical imaging, aiming to enhance diagnostic capabilities and clinical outcomes.

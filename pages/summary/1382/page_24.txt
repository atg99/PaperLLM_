The paper presents a summary of various studies focused on semantic segmentation of natural images, specifically utilizing the PASCAL VOC 2012 dataset. It lists several approaches along with their mean Intersection over Union (IoU) scores, highlighting advancements in architecture and loss functions. Notably, the highest performing methods include DeepLabV3+ achieving an IoU of 89.0%, followed by MSCI (88.0%) and DeepLabV3 (86.9%). The paper also provides contextual information about the PASCAL VOC dataset, which features 2,913 images annotated across 20 object classes, and discusses other datasets like PASCAL Context, Cityscapes, and ADE20K, which offer a broader range of annotations and categories for segmentation tasks. These datasets enhance the understanding of urban and natural scenes, contributing to the development of more accurate segmentation models.

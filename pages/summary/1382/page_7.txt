The paper provides a comprehensive review of deep semantic segmentation techniques applied to both natural and medical images. It discusses various architectures, including V-Net and the Tiramisu network, highlighting their structures and functionalities. The V-Net architecture features a series of convolutions and downsampling followed by upsampling paths, designed for efficient image segmentation. The Tiramisu network is characterized by its dense blocks and unique connectivity patterns, facilitating feature map concatenation and enhancing performance through skip connections. The review also notes the advancements in segmentation accuracy, particularly with the introduction of DeepLabV3+, which has set new performance benchmarks in 2018. Overall, the paper emphasizes the evolution and effectiveness of deep learning models in improving segmentation tasks across diverse domains.

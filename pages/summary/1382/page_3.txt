The paper presents a comprehensive review of deep semantic segmentation techniques applied to both natural and medical images. It outlines various categories within the segmentation pipeline, as illustrated in Figure 2, and discusses the behavior of popular loss functions when training segmentation models, particularly in scenarios with varying levels of false positives and negatives. 

Key areas of focus include model architectures such as fully convolutional networks, encoder-decoder models, and attention models, along with advanced training methodologies like adversarial training and multi-task models. The review also addresses computational complexity reduction strategies and optimization functions, emphasizing cross-entropy and energy minimization approaches.

Furthermore, the paper identifies significant research directions for each category, suggesting areas for future exploration. It includes a timeline of notable contributions to the field, highlighting the evolution of deep learning-based semantic segmentation techniques as referenced from various studies. Overall, the review aims to provide insights into current methodologies and future opportunities in deep semantic segmentation for diverse applications.

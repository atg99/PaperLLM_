The paper discusses the application of additive attention in Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs), specifically highlighting how it normalizes the output conditional probability using the softmax function, as illustrated in Equation 29. It notes that the Ptr-Net simplifies the attention mechanism by not blending encoder states into outputs, allowing the output to respond solely to positions rather than input content.

In the conclusion, the authors provide a comprehensive overview of RNN fundamentals, including backpropagation through time, issues with traditional RNNs, Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks, deep and bidirectional RNNs, as well as modern advancements such as the Encoder-Decoder architecture, sequence-to-sequence models, attention mechanisms, Transformers, and Pointer Networks. While the coverage is conceptual rather than implementation-focused, the authors recommend consulting original papers for deeper insights. They also suggest examining contemporary works that utilize these concepts, such as the paper by Vinyals et al. on training agents for the game StarCraft II, to see practical applications of LSTMs, Transformers, and Pointer Networks.

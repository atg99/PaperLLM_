The paper discusses various advancements in sequence-to-sequence models for speech recognition, highlighting the evolution and performance of different architectures. Key references include foundational works on bidirectional recurrent neural networks and training methodologies for recurrent neural networks, as well as significant contributions to sequence-to-sequence learning and attention mechanisms. The paper emphasizes the importance of attention in improving model performance, as illustrated by the "Attention is All You Need" framework. Additionally, it touches upon the application of these models in contexts such as video-to-text translation and complex game scenarios, demonstrating their versatility. The appendices provide a visual representation of Long Short-Term Memory Units (LSTMs), detailing the architecture and function of input, forget, and output gates. Overall, the paper synthesizes key developments in neural network architectures that have shaped modern sequence-to-sequence learning approaches.

The paper discusses the Seq2Seq model with an attention mechanism, focusing on how hidden states are utilized during decoding. In the example presented, during a specific iteration, the output from the previous hidden state H4 replaces the <END> token, while H2 emerges as the most significant hidden state based on attention scores. This leads to the generation of the word "am" as the output from the Feedforward Neural Network. 

The paper also mentions that there are additional attention decoder time steps that follow the same functional process as previously illustrated. 

Furthermore, it introduces the concept of positional encodings in transformers, explaining how trigonometric functions are applied to encode the position of words. Each word's encoding is influenced by multiple trigonometric functions at varying frequencies, with specific emphasis on how high, mid, and low values correspond to different curves for distinct words. This approach helps in maintaining the order of words within the model.

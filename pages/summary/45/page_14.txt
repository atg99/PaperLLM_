The paper describes the functioning of a Seq2Seq model with an attention mechanism, focusing on the processing of hidden states and context vectors. Initially, hidden states H2 and H3 are softmaxed to generate scores that highlight relevant information, while less relevant states are grayed out, emphasizing H1. This visual representation aids understanding of how attention selectively focuses on certain hidden states.

In a subsequent step, the context vector C4 is concatenated with the hidden state H4. This concatenation combines the relevant information from both vectors, which is illustrated in the accompanying figures. The combined vector is then fed into a jointly trained Feedforward Neural Network, depicted in the red box with rounded edges. The output from this network corresponds to the current time step's word, in this case, "I." This sequence outlines the iterative process of generating outputs in the Seq2Seq model with attention, highlighting the importance of both hidden states and context vectors in producing accurate predictions.

HAWQ-V3 introduces a novel approach to quantization by allowing the entire computational graph to operate with integer-only arithmetic, thus eliminating the typical reliance on floating point computations. This framework employs a hardware-aware mixed-precision quantization strategy, which optimizes bit-precision through an integer linear programming problem. This balances model perturbation with constraints such as memory footprint and latency, ultimately facilitating real-time inference without sacrificing accuracy.

The implementation of HAWQ-V3 in TVM achieves notable performance improvements, specifically a 1.45 times speedup for uniform 4-bit quantization compared to uniform 8-bit quantization when applied to ResNet50 on T4 GPUs. Evaluation results demonstrate that the INT8 quantization method maintains an accuracy of 77.58%, outperforming prior integer-only quantization efforts by 2.68%. Furthermore, the mixed-precision INT4/8 quantization reduces latency by 23% while still achieving a respectable accuracy of 76.73%.

In summary, HAWQ-V3 represents a significant advancement in quantization techniques for neural networks, addressing the limitations of existing methods by enabling efficient integer-only computations and optimizing hardware utilization for real-time applications in various domains.

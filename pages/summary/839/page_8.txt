The paper presents HAWQ-V3, a method for dyadic neural network quantization that achieves accuracy comparable to existing quantization techniques, including uniform and mixed-precision methods such as PACT, RVQuant, and HAQ. The results for models like InceptionV3 indicate similar performance levels. 

In the mixed-precision quantization results for ResNet18 and ResNet50, various constraints such as model size, BOPS, and latency are explored. The paper reports that the ILP (Integer Linear Programming) formulation allows practitioners to set constraints and optimize for accuracy. For instance, pure INT4 quantization shows an accuracy of 68.45% with a model size of 5.6MB, while INT8 offers 71.56% with a size of 11.2MB. By setting a model size constraint slightly above INT4, practitioners can achieve an accuracy of 71.09% with a model size of 7.9MB, which is 6% faster than INT8.

The paper highlights interesting observations, such as the weak correlation between model size and BOPS, and that larger model sizes do not necessarily yield higher accuracy. For example, in the results for ResNet50, a larger model size can coexist with lower accuracy compared to smaller models. 

In conclusion, HAWQ-V3 provides a flexible framework for quantization that allows for better accuracy and speed than INT8 alone, offering practitioners a broader range of quantization options while ensuring that results for ResNet18, ResNet50, and InceptionV3 have been verified through direct measurements.

The paper discusses various approaches and methodologies for quantizing neural networks, emphasizing the importance of efficient representation for deep learning models. Key contributions include HAWQ-V3, which implements dyadic neural network quantization, and gradient-based quantization techniques that utilize sinusoidal adaptive regularization. The work references several notable studies that explore hardware-aware neural network designs, such as SqueezeNext and MobileNetV3, which aim to optimize performance with reduced model sizes and computational demands.

The literature review encompasses foundational concepts in quantization, including the principles outlined by Gray and Neuhoff, as well as significant advancements in deep compression techniques that involve pruning, trained quantization, and Huffman coding. The paper also highlights recent innovations like binarized neural networks and layer-wise calibration methods that enhance post-training quantization.

Overall, the references illustrate a growing interest in improving the efficiency of neural networks through quantization, which balances the trade-off between model accuracy and resource utilization, thereby enabling broader deployment in resource-constrained environments.

The paper "HAWQ-V3: Dyadic Neural Network Quantization" discusses advancements in quantizing neural networks to reduce model size and improve efficiency while maintaining performance. It builds on prior works that focus on different aspects of network quantization. 

Key references include:

1. Zhou et al. (2017) introduced incremental network quantization aimed at achieving lossless performance in convolutional neural networks (CNNs) with low-precision weights. This work emphasizes the importance of preserving accuracy while reducing the bit-width of weights.

2. Zhou et al. (2016) presented DoReFa-Net, which explores training low bit-width CNNs using low bit-width gradients, thereby demonstrating a method to effectively train quantized networks without significant loss in performance.

3. Zhou et al. (2017) proposed adaptive quantization for deep neural networks, highlighting the need for flexibility in quantization strategies to optimize performance based on specific model requirements.

Overall, the paper contributes to the ongoing research in neural network quantization, aiming for efficient deployment of deep learning models in resource-constrained environments.

The paper introduces HAWQ-V3, a novel low-precision integer-only quantization framework designed for efficient neural network inference using only integer operations, specifically multiplication, addition, and bit shifts, without any use of FP32 arithmetic or integer division. The framework supports uniform and mixed-precision INT4/8 quantization, employing a hardware-aware integer linear programming (ILP) method to optimize the balance between model perturbation and specific application constraints, such as model size and inference speed. The ILP can be solved efficiently within a second for all models tested.

HAWQ-V3 demonstrates up to a 5% increase in accuracy compared to previous integer-only quantization methods. The authors implemented the quantized models in hardware by extending the TVM compiler to accommodate INT4 and INT4/8 inference, verifying the results by aligning layer activations with those from a PyTorch framework. The framework and the resulting models are open-sourced.

The paper also references a variety of works related to machine learning frameworks and quantization techniques, establishing a foundation for the proposed advancements in quantization methods.

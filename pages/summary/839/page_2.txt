The paper introduces HAWQ-V3, a novel approach to neural network quantization that emphasizes hardware-aware mixed-precision quantization. The key contributions include:

1. **Residual Connections**: HAWQ-V3 utilizes a unique method for implementing residual connections through integer-only arithmetic, effectively avoiding mismatches that can occur in traditional methods.

2. **Integer Linear Programming (ILP)**: The framework proposes a hardware-aware mixed-precision quantization strategy that formulates the optimization as an ILP problem. This approach aims to minimize model perturbation while adhering to constraints related to model size, latency, and total bit operations. The method leverages direct hardware measurements to optimize the balance between latency and accuracy.

3. **Deployment and Performance**: The quantized models, supporting INT8, INT4, and mixed-precision settings, are deployed using Apache TVM. The framework is noted as the first to incorporate INT4 support in TVM, demonstrating an average speedup of 1.47 times with INT4 compared to INT8 on a T4 GPU for ResNet50.

4. **Extensive Testing**: HAWQ-V3 is tested across various workloads, including ResNet18, ResNet50, and InceptionV3, achieving significant performance improvements. The paper reports an accuracy of 78.50% with INT8 quantization, surpassing previous integer-only methods for InceptionV3 by over 4%. Furthermore, mixed-precision quantization allows for enhanced speed with minimal accuracy loss, exemplified by a 23% latency speedup for ResNet50 compared to INT8 while maintaining 76.73% accuracy.

The paper also reviews related works in neural network optimization, highlighting advances in quantization strategies and the challenges associated with achieving efficient mixed-precision settings. The proposed ILP solution is positioned as a more flexible and efficient alternative to existing methods, addressing hardware-specific requirements and computational resource constraints.

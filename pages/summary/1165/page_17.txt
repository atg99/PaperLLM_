The authors evaluated the performance of BeeSwarm using various metrics, including energy consumption, network lifetime, and packet delivery rate. The results indicate that BeeSwarm outperforms traditional routing protocols by achieving a significant reduction in energy expenditure while enhancing network longevity and efficiency.

In summary, the paper introduces three novel energy-efficient mechanisms for clustering and routing in sensor networks: ABC-C, Bee-Sensor-C, and BeeSwarm. ABC-C leverages the ABC algorithm for optimal cluster head selection and data aggregation, demonstrating improved network lifetime and energy consumption compared to LEACH and PSO-C. Bee-Sensor-C adopts a dynamic clustering approach based on event detection, enhancing scalability and routing efficiency while achieving a higher packet delivery rate than existing protocols. Lastly, BeeSwarm employs a hierarchical clustering strategy to minimize energy usage while maintaining effective data transmission routes, thereby improving overall network performance.

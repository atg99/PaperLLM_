The paper discusses optimized hierarchical based routing protocols in wireless sensor networks (WSNs), focusing on intelligent clustering strategies that enhance network lifetime and energy efficiency. These strategies utilize machine learning and computational intelligence (ML/CI) techniques to address optimization issues in traditional hierarchical routing. Key paradigms in this context include Fuzzy Logic, Genetic Algorithms, Neural Networks, Reinforcement Learning, and Swarm Intelligence. 

Fuzzy Logic (FL) is highlighted as a mathematical approach that captures approximate human reasoning, allowing for imprecision through fuzzy sets. An example is provided where distances to the base station (BS) are categorized as close, medium, or far. The paper also introduces the Cluster-head Election using Fuzzy Logic (FCH), which employs FL to select cluster heads based on factors like energy levels, node concentration, and centrality. This integration of FL in clustering is supported by various literature references, demonstrating its effectiveness in optimizing WSN operations.

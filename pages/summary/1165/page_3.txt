2.4. Hierarchical Based Routing
Hierarchical routing organizes nodes into clusters, where each cluster has a leader or cluster head that manages data communication within the group and relays information to the sink node. This structure reduces the number of transmissions, effectively conserving energy and extending network lifetime. Protocols such as LEACH (Low-Energy Adaptive Clustering Hierarchy) exemplify this approach, allowing for dynamic cluster formation and efficient data aggregation.

In summary, routing in Wireless Sensor Networks involves various strategies—data-centric, location-based, group-based, and hierarchical routing—each with its unique advantages and applications. The primary goals are to enhance data communication efficiency, reduce energy consumption, and extend the network's operational lifespan.

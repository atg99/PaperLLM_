The paper provides a comprehensive review of recent hierarchical approaches in wireless sensor networks (WSNs) that utilize computational intelligence (CI) and machine learning (ML) techniques. It categorizes these algorithms based on the type of CI employed, including fuzzy logic, genetic algorithms, neural networks, reinforcement learning, and swarm intelligence. The authors evaluate and compare the algorithms using ten parameters, such as data delivery rate, energy consumption, scalability, and network homogeneity.

The findings indicate that algorithms based on swarm intelligence are the most recent and demonstrate superior performance in scalability, energy efficiency, and data delivery compared to other approaches. For future work, the authors aim to classify optimized clustering methods based on different WSN types, particularly those operating in non-terrestrial environments. They also plan to explore spectrum-aware clustering for large-scale WSNs.

The contributions of the authors are collaborative, with specific roles in reviewing, designing, and organizing the paper. The research received no external funding, and the authors declared no conflicts of interest.

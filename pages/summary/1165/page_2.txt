The paper discusses the significance of energy consumption in Wireless Sensor Networks (WSN) and the challenges posed by the deployment of numerous sensor nodes. To enhance network performance, hierarchical or cluster-based routing techniques are emphasized, particularly for large WSNs. In these systems, the network is divided into clusters, each managed by a Cluster Head (CH) that facilitates data collection from its cluster nodes. This multihop communication strategy helps conserve energy by enabling nodes to connect with nearby neighbors instead of distant ones.

The authors highlight that traditional clustering methods often struggle with the Non-deterministic Polynomial (NP) hard optimization problem, making it difficult to solve efficiently. They propose that recent advancements in Computational Intelligence (CI) and Machine Learning (ML) offer promising solutions to optimize clustering algorithms. These optimized approaches are reported to surpass traditional methods in various performance metrics, including scalability, reliability, and energy efficiency.

The paper aims to provide a comprehensive overview of ML and CI-based clustering approaches for WSN, assisting both novice and experienced researchers in selecting suitable techniques for their applications. The authors present a thorough review of recent intelligent clustering solutions, classifying them according to the ML or CI used, and evaluating their performance based on ten parameters.

The organization of the paper includes an introduction to routing techniques in WSN, a detailed presentation of optimized clustering methods based on ML and CI, a comparison of various clustering approaches, and concludes with future research directions.

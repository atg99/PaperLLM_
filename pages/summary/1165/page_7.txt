The protocol evaluates nodes based on their residual energy, proximity to the sink, and distance from the cluster center to determine suitable Cluster Heads (CHs). SIF employs a Fuzzy C-Means clustering algorithm for creating balanced clusters and utilizes a Mamdani fuzzy inference system for CH selection. Additionally, SIF incorporates a hybrid swarm intelligence approach that combines the Firefly Algorithm and Simulated Annealing to optimize the fuzzy rule base. The results indicate that SIF effectively improves energy consumption and network longevity compared to traditional methods.

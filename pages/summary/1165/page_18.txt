The study evaluates and compares various optimized clustering algorithms based on key performance metrics, including data delivery rate, energy consumption, scalability, approach type, data aggregation integration, and node homogeneity. The data delivery rate reflects the reliability of communication, while energy consumption is critical for assessing the longevity of sensor nodes and the overall network. Scalability is particularly relevant for large-scale Wireless Sensor Networks (WSNs), with algorithms capable of managing over 500 nodes being more favorable.

The BeeSwarm approach has been shown to slightly outperform existing routing protocols like MRP and ERP in packet delivery and energy consumption, thereby enhancing network lifetime. Similarly, the ABC-SD algorithm demonstrates superior energy efficiency, lower average energy consumption, and improved data delivery compared to other algorithms such as LEACH and PSO. These findings underscore the effectiveness of the proposed clustering algorithms in optimizing performance parameters essential for WSN applications.

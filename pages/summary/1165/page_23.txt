The table presents a comparison of various algorithms related to data delivery rates, data aggregation, energy consumption, and scalability in sensor networks. The algorithms discussed include EBAB, ACO-C, ACA-LEACH, MRP, ABC-C, Bee-Sensor-C, BeeSwarm, and ABC-SD, all categorized under Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) or Bee Colony Optimization (BCO) methodologies. Key features include the nature of the network (distributed or centralized), the radio model used, and the capability for multihop and multipath communication. Most algorithms show high data delivery rates, with varying levels of energy consumption and scalability. Centralized approaches tend to have lower scalability compared to distributed ones.

The paper discusses segmentation from a sequence-to-sequence perspective, utilizing Transformers to enhance the segmentation process. The authors highlight the advantages of applying Transformer architectures, which excel in capturing long-range dependencies within data sequences. The methodology aims to improve the accuracy and efficiency of semantic segmentation tasks.

Additionally, the paper references the ADE20K dataset, which plays a crucial role in advancing semantic understanding of scenes, as outlined by Zhou et al. The dataset serves as a benchmark for evaluating segmentation methods and provides comprehensive scene annotations.

The work of Zhu et al. is also mentioned, focusing on asymmetric non-local neural networks that further contribute to the field of semantic segmentation. These networks emphasize the importance of context in enhancing segmentation accuracy.

Overall, the paper integrates insights from various studies to propose a robust framework for semantic segmentation using advanced neural network architectures, particularly Transformers.

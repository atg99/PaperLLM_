The paper presents a novel transformer-based approach for semantic segmentation, specifically through the use of an end-to-end encoder-decoder model. The encoding leverages the Vision Transformer (ViT) architecture, capturing global context effectively by encoding all image patches. The method shows significant improvements in performance over traditional models, with the Seg-L-Mask/16 variant achieving a mean Intersection over Union (mIoU) of 59.0% on the ADE20K dataset and 81.3% on the Cityscapes dataset, outperforming many state-of-the-art models. 

Qualitative comparisons reveal that while DeepLabv3+ produces sharper object boundaries, the Segmenter model excels in providing consistent labels for large instances and managing partial occlusions. The findings suggest that the proposed transformer framework could serve as a foundational step towards a unified methodology for semantic, instance, and panoptic segmentation tasks.

The paper presents Segmenter, a transformer-based architecture designed for semantic image segmentation. Segmenter utilizes a Vision Transformer (ViT) backbone and features a novel mask decoder inspired by the DEtection TRansformer (DETR). Unlike traditional convolution-based methods, Segmenter captures global image context through its fully transformer-based encoder-decoder structure.

The encoder processes an input image by dividing it into patches, which are then flattened and projected into a sequence of embeddings. Positional information is incorporated through learnable position embeddings. This sequence undergoes transformation using a multi-layer transformer encoder, which employs multi-headed self-attention and point-wise feedforward networks to generate contextualized encodings.

During decoding, the model can utilize a point-wise linear mapping or a mask transformer to predict segmentation masks. The training is conducted end-to-end using a per-pixel cross-entropy loss, and inference involves applying argmax after upsampling to assign a single class to each pixel.

Overall, Segmenter demonstrates competitive performance on standard segmentation benchmarks, showcasing the effectiveness of transformer architectures in this domain.

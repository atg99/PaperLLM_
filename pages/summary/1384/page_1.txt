Segmenter builds upon the Vision Transformer (ViT) framework and adapts it for semantic segmentation tasks. It utilizes output embeddings derived from image patches to generate class labels through either a point-wise linear decoder or a mask transformer decoder. The capability to fine-tune pre-trained image classification models on moderate-sized segmentation datasets significantly enhances performance. While the linear decoder yields strong results, the addition of a mask transformer further improves performance by generating class masks.

Our extensive ablation study reveals that larger models and smaller patch sizes contribute positively to performance. Segmenter achieves superior results on the ADE20K and Pascal Context datasets, surpassing state-of-the-art methods, and demonstrates competitive performance on the Cityscapes dataset.

In summary, Segmenter presents a novel approach to semantic segmentation by utilizing transformer architecture to effectively capture global context, thereby improving segmentation accuracy and performance across various datasets.

The paper presents a novel metric learning loss that combines the strengths of proxy-based and pair-based losses, leading to faster convergence and improved accuracy in image retrieval tasks. The experiments conducted on various datasets, including CUB-200-2011, Cars-196, SOP, and In-shop, demonstrate that the proposed model achieves state-of-the-art performance. 

Key findings include the impact of batch size on Recall@1, where optimal performance is observed at batch sizes of 300 or larger. Additionally, the embedding dimension significantly influences retrieval accuracy, with stable performance noted at dimensions of 128 and above. The proposed loss consistently outperforms the existing MS loss across all tested dimensions.

The analysis of the hyperparameters α and δ indicates that a value of α greater than 16 yields high and stable accuracy, while increasing δ also enhances performance, albeit to a lesser extent when α is large. 

The authors conclude that their model not only surpasses current methods but also converges more quickly without requiring complex data sampling techniques. Future work will focus on extending the proposed loss for deep hashing networks to enhance computational efficiency in both training and testing phases.

The paper discusses the selection process for AI ethics guidelines, emphasizing the importance of comprehensive mapping and categorization of normative claims in the realm of AI ethics. It excludes documents focused solely on big data, algorithms, or robotics, as well as isolated corporate policies, except for notable exceptions like the "Information Technology Industry AI Policy Principles" and various well-known principles from major tech companies. The selection criteria were based not on the detail of the documents, but on their explicit discussion of ethical issues. A table summarizes various AI ethics guidelines, highlighting key issues they cover, including privacy protection, fairness, accountability, transparency, and more. The paper also notes the varying lengths and authorship affiliations of these documents, indicating a diverse landscape of ethical considerations in AI development.

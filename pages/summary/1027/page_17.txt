The paper discusses the evolving landscape of AI ethics, emphasizing the need for a balanced approach between adhering to ethical guidelines and recognizing the complexities of individual responsibility in decision-making. It identifies the challenge faced by future AI ethics in reconciling these two perspectives. The research acknowledges the support received from the Cluster of Excellence "Machine Learning – New Perspectives for Science" and highlights the importance of open-access dissemination of knowledge. The references cited include various works on AI safety, ethical frameworks, and the societal implications of AI technologies, reflecting a comprehensive exploration of the subject.

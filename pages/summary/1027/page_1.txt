The paper by Thilo Hagendorff evaluates the current landscape of AI ethics by analyzing 22 ethics guidelines developed in response to the rapid advancements in artificial intelligence technologies. It highlights the normative principles and recommendations within these guidelines, aiming to address the potential disruptions posed by AI. Despite the proliferation of such guidelines, Hagendorff argues that they often lack real impact on decision-making in AI research and application. The paper identifies commonalities and gaps among the guidelines and discusses the need for stronger mechanisms to enforce ethical principles in practice. Ultimately, it calls for improvements to enhance the effectiveness of AI ethics in guiding technology developers and ensuring responsible AI deployment.

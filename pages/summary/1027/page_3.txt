This paper evaluates various ethical guidelines related to artificial intelligence (AI) ethics, focusing on a selection of 22 major guidelines. It differentiates itself from other studies by not aiming for a comprehensive analysis of all existing documents but rather providing a semi-systematic overview that connects specific ethical issues to the broader context of AI ethics. The selection process for the guidelines involved a thorough literature analysis across multiple databases, with a specific emphasis on recent documents (no older than five years) and a bias towards Western/Northern sources due to the exclusion of non-English documents.

The paper acknowledges the limitations of its selection criteria, such as the exclusion of national context documents and the focus on guidelines from major global players in AI, including the European Commission, the Obama administration, and the Chinese Ministry of Science and Technology. The inclusion of the OECD Principles on AI is noted for its supranational relevance. Overall, this work aims to highlight the normative stances present in AI ethics guidelines, contributing to the understanding of how these guidelines interact within the wider ethical landscape of AI.

The cited works in this paper highlight significant advancements in natural language processing (NLP) through deep learning techniques. Key contributions include the development of neural models for semantic role labeling, which demonstrate improvements in accuracy and efficiency (Marcheggiani et al., 2017; He et al., 2017). The integration of syntax-aware methods further enhances multilingual semantic role labeling (He et al., 2019).

Recent trends in deep learning for NLP are explored, showcasing the effectiveness of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for tasks such as sentiment analysis and text categorization (dos Santos and Gatti, 2014; Johnson and Zhang, 2014). Additionally, recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and long short-term memory networks (LSTMs) are emphasized for their role in sequence learning and language modeling (Mikolov et al., 2010; Hochreiter and Schmidhuber, 1997).

Generative adversarial networks (GANs) are also reviewed, illustrating their application in unsupervised representation learning and sequence generation (Goodfellow et al., 2014; Yu et al., 2017). Evaluation metrics for summarization and machine translation, such as ROUGE and BLEU, are discussed, highlighting their importance in assessing model performance (Lin, 2004; Papineni et al., 2002).

Overall, the paper encapsulates a comprehensive overview of methodologies and advancements in deep learning-based NLP, showcasing their impact on various applications and research directions within the field.

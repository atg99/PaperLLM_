The paper by Torfi et al. discusses advancements in Natural Language Processing (NLP) facilitated by deep learning techniques. It highlights notable works in the field, covering various applications such as machine translation, question answering, and summarization. Key references include foundational models like the neural machine translation models introduced by Bahdanau et al. and the encoder-decoder approaches that have significantly influenced the development of NLP systems. The authors also address the evolution of models from recurrent networks to transformer architectures, emphasizing the importance of attention mechanisms in improving translation quality and efficiency.

In addition to machine translation, the paper explores the progress in question answering systems, referencing various approaches that integrate neural networks for enhanced comprehension and performance. The use of deep learning for summarization is also examined, detailing methods for both extractive and abstractive summarization, including the incorporation of reinforcement learning to optimize sentence selection.

The paper concludes by underscoring the impact of deep learning on NLP, noting that the continued integration of advanced architectures and training techniques is driving innovation and improving the effectiveness of language understanding tasks across diverse applications.

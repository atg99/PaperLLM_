The paper "Natural Language Processing Advancements By Deep Learning: A Survey" by Amirsina Torfi et al. explores the transformative role of deep learning in the field of Natural Language Processing (NLP). It highlights how advancements in computational power and the availability of extensive linguistic data have increased the demand for automating semantic analysis through data-driven approaches. The authors emphasize that deep learning has significantly improved various NLP tasks, paralleling its successes in Computer Vision and Automatic Speech Recognition.

The survey categorizes the applications of deep learning in NLP, analyzing core tasks such as part-of-speech tagging, named entity recognition, and semantic role labeling. It distinguishes between supervised and unsupervised learning methods utilized in NLP applications. The introduction sets the stage for the survey by discussing the theoretical dimensions of NLP and AI, addressing the motivations for employing deep learning, and examining its effectiveness in solving real-world problems.

The paper is structured into six sections, beginning with an introduction and followed by a background on NLP and AI, the fundamental concepts necessary for understanding NLP, a summary of benchmark datasets, an overview of NLP applications benefiting from deep learning, and concluding remarks that also highlight open problems and future research directions.

Overall, this survey provides a comprehensive overview of how deep learning is reshaping NLP, presenting both the challenges and opportunities it presents within the field.

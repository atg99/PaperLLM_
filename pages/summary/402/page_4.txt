The paper discusses the activity diagrams related to the workflow of a system, specifically focusing on the processes of object scanning and detection. 

The activity diagram for the object scan process illustrates how the object detection system is initiated. The user positions the object in front of the camera, prompting the scanning process to commence.

In the object detection process, the diagram outlines the steps involved in determining whether the camera successfully captures the object. It highlights two potential outcomes: if the object is recognized, the process continues; if not, the system reverts to the initial detection step to attempt capturing the object again. 

These diagrams effectively convey the sequential activities and decision points within the system's functionality.

The paper discusses the process and results of object detection within a system, highlighting the steps involved after the system identifies objects. It specifies that the system not only displays the name of the detected object but also provides a percentage indicating the accuracy of the detection. 

Additionally, the paper covers the management of object information, detailing the process through an activity diagram. It introduces class diagrams as a means to represent the static structure of the system, including attributes, operations, and relationships among classes. Class diagrams are emphasized as a crucial tool for visualizing the overall class structure of the system, making them the most commonly utilized type of diagram in this context.

The paper discusses the derivation of the loss function for the Variational Autoencoder (VAE), emphasizing its maximization objective, which can be viewed as a ‘gain’ function rather than a traditional loss function. The loss function is derived by taking the negative of the gain function, resulting in a formulation that the authors aim to minimize with respect to the network parameters. Specifically, the goal is to find the optimal parameters that minimize the loss function, expressed in Equation (44). The paper provides a detailed step-by-step derivation of this loss function, highlighting the principles of variational inference and presenting a closed-form solution for the case of Gaussian latent variables.

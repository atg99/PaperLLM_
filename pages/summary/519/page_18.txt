The paper discusses various self-supervised signal construction strategies and their impact on representation learning, specifically comparing the proposed method to the SimCLR approach. The authors highlight that their strategy incorporates label information by sampling self-supervised signal images from the same character as the input images, while SimCLR relies solely on image augmentations. This creates a challenge in making a fair comparison between the two methods, as the proposed approach implicitly leverages label information. The authors suggest that future research could explore different self-supervised signal construction strategies and the potential integration of label information into self-supervised learning.

Additionally, the paper defines key metrics for evaluating visual-textual representation learning. Subset Accuracy (also known as Exact Match Ratio) measures the proportion of completely correct multi-label outputs, while Micro AUC ROC score assesses the area under the curve of the receiver operating characteristic curve, providing insights into the performance of classification models.

The paper introduces Nouveau Variational Autoencoder (NVAE), a deep hierarchical variational autoencoder designed to enhance image generation capabilities using advanced neural network architectures. While existing variational autoencoders (VAEs) face challenges from competing models like normalizing flows and autoregressive models, NVAE aims to leverage architectural innovations rather than solely focusing on statistical improvements.

NVAE employs depth-wise separable convolutions and batch normalization to efficiently process images. It features a residual parameterization of normal distributions and utilizes spectral regularization to stabilize training. The results demonstrate that NVAE achieves state-of-the-art performance among non-autoregressive likelihood-based models on various datasets, including MNIST, CIFAR-10, CelebA 64, and CelebA HQ, with notable improvements in bits per dimension, such as reducing the CIFAR-10 state-of-the-art from 2.98 to 2.91. Furthermore, NVAE is recognized as the first effective VAE capable of generating high-quality images at resolutions as large as 256x256 pixels.

The paper emphasizes the importance of tailored neural architectures for VAEs, arguing that they have unique requirements that differ from those used in classification tasks. The source code for NVAE is made publicly available, promoting further exploration in the field of deep generative learning.

This compilation of references highlights significant contributions to the field of generative models and image synthesis. Key works include the development of high-fidelity image generation techniques, such as subscale pixel networks by Menick and Kalchbrenner, and the introduction of the Image Transformer by Parmar et al., which leverages attention mechanisms for image processing. 

PixelCNN and its enhancements, including PixelCNN++ by Salimans et al., demonstrate advancements in pixel-based generative modeling. Conditional generation techniques using PixelCNN decoders by van den Oord et al. further illustrate the versatility of these models in generating images based on specific conditions.

Datasets such as MNIST by LeCun and ImageNet by Deng et al. serve as foundational benchmarks for training and evaluating image recognition and generation systems. The references also cover deep learning methodologies applied to face attribute detection, as demonstrated by Liu et al., and innovative approaches to autoencoding by Larsen et al.

The emergence of style-based generator architectures in GANs by Karras et al. and large-scale GAN training by Brock et al. signify important strides in producing realistic images. Radford et al. discuss unsupervised learning with GANs, while Grover et al. address bias correction in generative models. The optimization strategies introduced by Kingma and Ba with the Adam algorithm are crucial for efficient training of these models.

Overall, these works collectively contribute to the ongoing evolution of generative models, enhancing their performance and applicability in various domains.

This paper introduces a novel deep hierarchical Variational Autoencoder (VAE) termed NV AE, which is designed to generate high-quality large images. The authors address two primary challenges: creating expressive neural networks tailored for VAEs and scaling training for numerous hierarchical groups and larger image sizes while ensuring stability.

The framework uses a prior represented as p(zzz) and an approximate posterior q(zzz|xxx), where both are modeled using factorial Normal distributions. The variational lower bound on the log likelihood of the data is defined, incorporating expectations and Kullback-Leibler divergences.

To implement the conditional distributions in the generative model p(xxx; zzz) and the inference model q(zzz|xxx), the authors employ a top-down network for generating parameters and a bottom-up network for extracting representations from input data. This bidirectional inference allows for efficient computation by reusing representations across models.

The NV AE architecture employs residual cells to better capture long-range correlations in data, such as those found in images of faces. This is achieved through a multi-scale hierarchical model that samples latent variables group-by-group, progressively increasing spatial dimensions to capture both global and local dependencies.

In addition to hierarchical modeling, the authors enhance correlation modeling by enlarging the receptive fields of the networks through deep residual networks, which allows for improved handling of long-range correlations in the data. The paper emphasizes the innovative architecture modules and parameterization of approximate posteriors that contribute to the stability and effectiveness of the NV AE model.

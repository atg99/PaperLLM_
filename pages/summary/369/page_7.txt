The appendix of the paper provides detailed training information, dataset specifics, and qualitative analysis results concerning the experiments conducted. 

In the training section, it is noted that experiments were conducted using Google Cloud Platform's Compute Engine, specifically on an NVIDIA Tesla V100 GPU with 16 GB of memory. The authors utilized mixed precision to accommodate batch sizes ranging from 4 to 16 samples. The pretrained roberta-large model, which comprises approximately 355 million parameters, was trained for five epochs without freezing any weights. The L2 regularization rate was set to 0.01, and a learning rate scheduler was implemented to linearly increase for the first 20% of training and decrease for the remaining 80%. The optimal peak learning rate was determined using Optuna, which ran five trials to minimize cross-entropy loss on validation data. Training lasted about 45 minutes for five epochs.

The section on the IEMOCAP dataset outlines the selection of speaker names, which included the top five male and female American names from the past century. The names chosen were Mary, Patricia, Jennifer, Linda, Elizabeth for females, and James, John, Robert, Michael, William for males.

The qualitative analysis reveals insights from the IEMOCAP dataset, highlighting a discrepancy in the model's performance compared to MELD. Only 20% of correctly classified samples showed attention from the last layer's <s> token to the current target speaker, while this dropped to 10% for incorrectly classified samples. The analysis includes examples of dialogues, illustrating both correctly and incorrectly classified cases, with attention visualizations indicating the model's focus on speaker tokens. In the correctly classified example, both prediction and truth indicated excitement, whereas, in the incorrectly classified example, the model predicted a neutral emotion despite the truth being frustration. 

Overall, the appendix emphasizes the technical details of the training process, the careful selection of dataset components, and a thorough examination of model performance through qualitative analysis.

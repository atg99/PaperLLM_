In the paper titled "EmoBERTa: Speaker-Aware Emotion Recognition in Conversation with RoBERTa," authors Taewoon Kim and Piek Vossen from Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam introduce EmoBERTa, a novel approach for emotion recognition in conversation (ERC). The method enhances emotion prediction by incorporating speaker identities into the dialogue sequences, which allows the model to learn both intra- and inter-speaker dynamics. By prepending speaker names to utterances and utilizing separation tokens, EmoBERTa effectively captures the necessary context for predicting a current speaker's emotions.

The authors highlight that their straightforward approach achieves state-of-the-art results on two prominent ERC datasets, demonstrating the efficacy of using a simple yet expressive scheme. They also acknowledge the broader context of emotion recognition, which spans various modalities, but focus primarily on text-based interactions in this study.

The paper critiques existing ERC methodologies that often rely on complex combinations of neural network architectures, arguing that such models can complicate feature extraction and lack interpretability. Instead, EmoBERTa leverages the strengths of pretrained transformer models, like RoBERTa, to streamline the process, thereby improving performance without the intricacies of multi-component systems. The authors have made their code and models publicly available to encourage further research in the field.

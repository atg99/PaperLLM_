The paper reviews various advancements in self-supervised learning, highlighting its applications across different domains, including image classification, object detection, and medical image analysis. It discusses key methodologies such as GAN-based image colorization and rotation feature decoupling, which enhance visual feature learning without the need for labeled data. The significance of techniques like jigsaw puzzles and contrastive learning is emphasized, showcasing their effectiveness in improving model performance in various tasks. Additionally, the paper covers the integration of self-supervised learning in medical contexts, underscoring its potential to utilize vast amounts of unlabeled medical images for improved diagnostic capabilities. Overall, the paper presents a comprehensive overview of the state-of-the-art self-supervised learning methods and their implications for future research and applications.

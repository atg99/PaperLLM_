The paper titled "Self-supervised Learning: A Succinct Review" provides an overview of significant contributions to the field of self-supervised learning (SSL) across various domains, particularly in computer vision and medical imaging. It highlights key studies and their findings, organized by author, publication source, and dataset used.

1. Girshick et al. focused on object detection and semantic segmentation using the PASCAL VOC 2012 dataset.
2. Jamaludin et al. applied SSL in spinal MRI analysis involving 1016 subjects.
3. Chen et al. utilized a context restoration strategy in medical imaging.
4. Lee et al. explored object detection through the recycling of bounding box annotations with PASCAL VOC and COCO datasets.
5. Feng et al. estimated rotation angles in computer vision, employing the PASCAL VOC 2007 dataset.
6. Yun et al. proposed a self-supervised method for human pose estimation using the COCO key-point detection dataset.
7. Amrani et al. worked on object detection and retrieval from videos using the HOW2 dataset.
8. Liu et al. introduced the Self-EMD method for object detection with the COCO dataset.
9. Sun et al. developed the ERNIE 2.0 framework for natural language processing, evaluated on the General Language Understanding Evaluation (GLUE).
10. Chen et al. proposed a contrastive learning framework for visual representation using the ImageNet dataset.
11. Nguyen et al. applied SSL for medical image analysis with two datasets, including images from lung cancer patients.
12. Kwasigroch et al. enhanced performance in the skincare domain using SSL on a dataset of 2000 training, 150 validation, and 600 testing images.
13. Huang et al. investigated few-shot object detection.
14. Chowdhury et al. reviewed SSL applications in healthcare.
15. Zhou et al. focused on text classification in NLP across 17 datasets.
16. Pototzky et al. addressed object detection in autonomous driving with a dataset of 1,200,000 images and 20,000 unlabeled images.
17. Jain et al. explored SSL for human action recognition with inertial sensors.
18. Breiki et al. worked on image classification in computer vision using a cassava plant disease dataset.
19. Ziegler & Asano tackled semantic segmentation in object detection with the COCO dataset.
20. Treneska et al. dealt with image colorization for visual feature learning using COCO and PASCAL VOC 2012 datasets.
21. Ding et al. proposed the Deeply Unsupervised Patch Re-ID method for unsupervised visual representation learning with the PASCAL VOC dataset.
22. Ghesu et al. solved image assessment issues in the medical domain using a vast dataset of 100 million images from various imaging modalities.

This review encapsulates the breadth of research in SSL, highlighting key methodologies and datasets that have advanced the field across diverse applications.

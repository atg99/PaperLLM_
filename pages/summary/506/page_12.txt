The paper presents a detailed overview of various datasets used in computer vision and related fields, including their sizes, class categories, and source links. Key datasets mentioned include CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100, which contain 60,000 and 50,000 images respectively, and PASCAL VOC 2007 and 2012 with 9,963 and 11,530 images, both featuring 20 classes. The paper also lists specialized datasets such as the Covid-19 images dataset, MNIST for handwritten digits, and Food 101, which has 120,216 images across 251 categories. Other notable datasets include Stanford Dogs, Kinetics for human action recognition, and MS COCO, which contains 328,000 images. Each dataset is linked to its source for easy access. The compilation of these datasets underlines their significance in advancing research in computer vision and machine learning.

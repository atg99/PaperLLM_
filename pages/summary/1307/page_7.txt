In the BERT fine-tuning stage, cross-entropy loss is used as the objective function. In the few-shot learning stage, both cross-entropy and contrastive learning loss are integrated into the objective function. The paper compares the proposed method with various data augmentation techniques implemented through open-source libraries, including nlpaug and textattack.

Several augmentation methods are described, including:

- **InsertCharAugmentation**: Injects random characters to enhance model generalization.
- **SubstituteCharAugmentation**: Randomly replaces characters.
- **SwapCharAugmentation**: Exchanges two characters.
- **DeleteCharAugmentation**: Deletes characters.
- **OCRAugmentation**: Simulates OCR errors.
- **SpellingAugmentation**: Deliberately misspells words based on a provided list.
- **KeyboardAugmentation**: Simulates typing errors using QWERTY layout.
- **SwapWordAug** and **DeleteWordAug**: Exchange or delete words, respectively, as part of Easy Data Augmentation (EDA).
- **SynonymAugmentation**: Replaces words with synonyms from PPDB or WordNet.
- **Embedding-based Substitutions**: Utilizes word embeddings from Google News or counter-fitting embeddings to replace or insert words based on their semantic relationships.
- **Contextual Word Augmentation**: Employs BERT, DistilBERT, or RoBERTA for context-aware word insertion or substitution.
- **BackTranslationAug**: Translates text into another language and back to create semantically similar but different text.

Prompts are designed for both single-turn and multi-turn dialogues, depending on the dataset used for augmentation. Evaluation metrics include cosine similarity and TransRate to assess the faithfulness and compactness of the augmented data, with cosine similarity specifically measuring the semantic similarity between generated and actual samples.

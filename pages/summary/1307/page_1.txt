The paper presents AugGPT, a novel text data augmentation approach leveraging ChatGPT to address the challenges of limited sample sizes in natural language processing (NLP), particularly in few-shot learning scenarios. The authors highlight that insufficient training data can hinder the performance of NLP models, especially when generalizing from few examples. Traditional data augmentation methods often lack faithfulness in labeling or diversity in generated samples. In contrast, AugGPT enhances the training dataset by rephrasing sentences into multiple conceptually similar yet semantically distinct variations, thereby improving the representation and sample size without compromising data integrity.

The experiments conducted demonstrate that AugGPT outperforms existing state-of-the-art text data augmentation techniques in terms of testing accuracy and the distribution of generated samples in few-shot learning text classification tasks. By integrating AugGPT with other few-shot learning methods, the authors argue that it can effectively enhance model performance without altering the underlying architecture, making it a practical solution for a variety of NLP applications. The paper emphasizes the potential of large language models to transform data augmentation strategies in NLP.

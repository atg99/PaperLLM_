The article presents a comprehensive overview of self-supervised learning (SSL) within the remote sensing domain. It categorizes SSL methods into three main groups, highlighting key studies and illustrating the transition from natural image applications to remote sensing data. The authors also introduce a preliminary benchmark and conduct an extensive analysis of four contemporary contrastive SSL techniques across three well-known remote sensing datasets. Additionally, the paper discusses fundamental challenges faced in the field and outlines potential future research directions. The work is supported by various European and German funding bodies, alongside contributions from the Helmholtz Association.

The paper discusses recent advancements in hyperspectral image classification and dimensionality reduction through various semi-supervised and unsupervised learning techniques. Key contributions include the development of frameworks like iterative multitask regression, Joint and Progressive Subspace Analysis, and CoSpace for effective feature learning and classification. The use of deep learning models, such as convolutional networks and self-supervised learning, has been highlighted to improve classification accuracy, particularly in few-shot scenarios. Additionally, methods like graph convolutional networks and manifold alignment have been proposed for change detection and data fusion across modalities. The references cited provide a comprehensive overview of existing methodologies and their applications in remote sensing, emphasizing the importance of feature extraction and clustering techniques in enhancing classification performance. Overall, the paper underscores the significance of leveraging advanced machine learning approaches for better analysis and interpretation of hyperspectral data.

Lichao Mou earned his Bachelor's degree in automation from Xi'an University of Posts and Telecommunications in 2012, followed by a Master's in signal and information processing from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2015, and a Dr.-Ing. from the Technical University of Munich in 2020. He currently serves as a Guest Professor at the Munich AI Future Lab AI4EO and leads the Visual Learning and Reasoning team at the Remote Sensing Technology Institute of the German Aerospace Center. Since 2019, he has been a Research Scientist at DLR-IMF and an AI Consultant for the Helmholtz Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Unit. His notable achievements include winning first place in the 2016 IEEE GRSS Data Fusion Contest and being a finalist for the Best Student Paper Award at two Joint Urban Remote Sensing Events.

Xiao Xiang Zhu holds a Master's degree, a Dr.-Ing. degree, and a Habilitation in signal processing from the Technical University of Munich, where she is now the Chair Professor for Data Science in Earth Observation and heads the EO Data Science Department at DLR. She co-coordinates the Munich Data Science Research School and leads the Helmholtz AI Research Field in Aeronautics, Space, and Transport. Since May 2020, she has directed the AI4EO lab and co-directed the Munich Data Science Institute. Zhu’s research focuses on remote sensing, signal processing, machine learning, and data science, addressing global challenges such as urbanization and climate change. She is a member of prestigious academies and serves on scientific advisory boards, as well as holding editorial roles in IEEE journals. Zhu is recognized as a Fellow of IEEE.

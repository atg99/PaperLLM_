Traditional genetic algorithms often utilize fixed-length chromosomes, which are not ideal for deep learning applications where the number of hyperparameters can vary significantly based on the model's architecture. Our proposed variable length GA adapts to this variability, allowing for a more efficient exploration of the hyperparameter space.

Through experimental evaluations, we demonstrate that our GA effectively identifies optimal hyperparameter configurations for CNNs, resulting in improved model performance. The results indicate that extending the optimization process can yield even better performance outcomes. Theoretically, with unlimited resources, one could achieve the absolute best hyperparameter settings.

In summary, our work addresses the challenges of hyperparameter optimization in deep learning by proposing a flexible genetic algorithm that significantly enhances the efficiency and effectiveness of the search process, ultimately facilitating better model performance in computer vision tasks.

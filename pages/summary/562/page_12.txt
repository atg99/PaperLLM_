The paper presents a method for hyperparameter optimization in deep learning using a Variable Length Genetic Algorithm (VLGA). The study highlights the efficiency of the proposed approach compared to traditional methods. In the algorithm, 20 individuals were evolved over 5 generations, with the best individual selected for further training upon meeting the stopping criterion at Phase 14. The total evolution time was under 25 hours.

A comparison of model accuracies indicates that the VLGA achieved an accuracy of 88.92% within a training time of 24.55 hours, which is significantly higher than results from random search (58.66% accuracy in 30 hours), classical genetic algorithms (80.75% accuracy in 30 hours), and large-scale evolution techniques (51.90% accuracy in 30 hours). This demonstrates the effectiveness of the VLGA in optimizing hyperparameters efficiently while achieving superior model performance.

The paper discusses an approach to hyperparameter optimization in deep learning using a variable length genetic algorithm. The authors conducted large-scale evolutionary experiments over a public repository, varying the population size across different trials. Results from the experiments, which ran for 30 hours on an Nvidia Tesla V100 GPU, are presented in three plots corresponding to population sizes of 30, 50, and 80 individuals. Each plot illustrates the fitness of the best individuals discovered, with fitness values slightly exceeding 0.5. This indicates the potential effectiveness of the proposed method in optimizing hyperparameters efficiently within a constrained timeframe.

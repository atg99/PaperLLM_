The paper presents a comparison of drug and metabolite similarities using different encoding methods, specifically focusing on RDKit patterned encoding and a novel VAE-Sim metric. Key findings include the rank ordering of similarities and heatmaps illustrating Tanimoto similarities for RDKit and Euclidean similarities for VAE-Sim in both a 100-dimensional latent vector and a 2-dimensional UMAP space. Figures demonstrate that the VAE-Sim approach yields distinct results compared to traditional fingerprint encodings. The analysis highlights the effectiveness of the new encoding method in providing a different perspective on molecular similarities, as evidenced by the comparisons between the most similar metabolites identified through Tanimoto and VAE-Sim metrics.

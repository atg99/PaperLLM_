The paper presents a table comparing the Tanimoto similarity of various drugs to clozapine using nine different RDKit encodings. It also evaluates their ability to inhibit clozapine transport, with data sourced from a prior study. The drugs listed include Olanzapine, Chlorpromazine, Quetiapine, Prazosin, Lamotrigine, Indatraline, Verapamil, and Rhein. The percentages of inhibition of clozapine uptake are provided alongside Tanimoto similarity scores for each encoding. Notably, a shaded cell indicates that the molecule did not rank in the "top 50" for that encoding. The results highlight significant variations in similarity and inhibition capabilities across the different drugs and encoding methods.

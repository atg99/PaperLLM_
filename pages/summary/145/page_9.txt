In this section of the paper, the authors utilized a new metric, VAE-Sim, to assess the similarity of various drugs to clozapine. The results, illustrated in Figure 5, reveal that several structurally and functionally related antipsychotic drugs, including loxapine, mirtazapine, and quetiapine, ranked among the most similar to clozapine. Interestingly, the analysis also identified other drugs not previously associated with clozapine, such as the antihistamines ketotifen and alcaftadine, and the anti-inflammatory COX inhibitor ketorolac, as having similarity to clozapine. This finding underscores the potential of the VAE-Sim metric in providing novel insights into drug similarity.

The authors acknowledge the complex binding nature of clozapine and other drugs, indicating that further exploration of these similarities is beyond the scope of the current discussion. Additionally, they briefly describe their methodological approach, which favored one-hot encoding over other grammar-based and junction-tree methods previously discussed in the literature. Overall, the findings support the utility of the VAE-Sim metric in drug similarity assessment.

The paper discusses the use of various encoding methods for visualizing molecular similarities, highlighting that these methods do not exhibit uniform behavior. It emphasizes the influence of the chosen similarity measure on the outcomes. Specifically, the VAE-Sim encoding demonstrates a more rapid decline in similarities, suggesting that it assesses similarities in a more localized manner compared to other encodings. The findings are illustrated through "all-vs.-all" heatmaps presented in Figure 3B and 3C, which further elucidate the differences in similarity assessments across the encoding techniques.

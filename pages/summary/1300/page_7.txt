The paper discusses the preprocessing steps and dataset statistics for two datasets used in evaluating IoT security: the IoTID20 dataset and the N-BaIoT dataset. 

For the IoTID20 dataset, the count statistics reveal the distribution of samples across different categories, including benign and various attack types such as Mirai and DoS. The preprocessing involved removing samples with NaN and INF values, excluding certain features like Timestamp and IP addresses to avoid overfitting, and scaling numerical features with a min-max scaler. Categorical features such as source and destination ports and protocols were transformed into one-hot vectors, resulting in 77 numerical and 9 categorical features.

The N-BaIoT dataset comprises traffic data from nine commercial IoT devices before and after being attacked by Mirai and BASHLITE botnets. Each sample includes 23 traffic statistics features, and the data was divided into training, validation, and test sets in an 8:1:1 ratio. The paper provides detailed count statistics for specific devices, highlighting the number of benign and attack samples per device type. The preprocessing for the N-BaIoT dataset also included scaling numerical features using a min-max scaler, leading to a total of 116 features per device after processing.

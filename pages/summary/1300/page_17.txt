The paper presents evaluation results of various device types using N-BaIoT data, focusing on the performance metrics of different model configurations based on the Matthews Correlation Coefficient (MCC) score. Each device type's best configuration is highlighted in bold, derived from averaging results over five runs.

For the Danmini Doorbell, the configurations (5,32), (5,64), and (7,64) achieved high accuracy, TPR, and AUC, with MCC scores indicating minimal false positives. The Philips B120N/10 Baby Monitor demonstrated similarly strong performance across its configurations, with the highest MCC score recorded for (7,64).

The SimpleHome XCS7-1002-WHT Security Camera showed a slight dip in MCC scores compared to the previous devices but maintained solid TPR and AUC values. The Provision PT-737E achieved high performance metrics across its configurations, particularly with (5,64) yielding the best MCC score.

Lastly, the Ecobee Thermostat configurations also displayed strong results, with high TPR and AUC scores. Figure 12 illustrates the MCC scores for each device when employing the (5,32) model, further emphasizing the performance of the evaluated devices within the dataset.

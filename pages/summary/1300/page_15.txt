The paper evaluates the performance of different models using the IoTID20 dataset, specifically focusing on the impact of latent size on metrics such as Matthews Correlation Coefficient (MCC) and True Positive Rate (TPR) for various attack types. Figures 8 through 10 illustrate the results for models with latent sizes of (5,32), (5,64), and (7,64) respectively, highlighting how MCC and TPR vary as the latent size increases. Figure 11 further explores the reconstruction distance distributions for normal samples and attack types using the (5,64) model at latent sizes of 28 and 9, revealing that both sizes effectively capture data characteristics. The analysis indicates that smaller latent sizes can still provide efficient and accurate data representation, as evidenced by the similar distribution patterns observed in the graphs.

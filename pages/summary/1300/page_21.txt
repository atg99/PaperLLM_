The paper discusses the availability of datasets for academic research in network intrusion detection, specifically mentioning NSL-KDD, IoTID20, and N-BaIoT, all accessible as of June 2021. It highlights that the authors have no conflicts of interest and provides a comprehensive list of references that cover various aspects of network security and intrusion detection methodologies. Key concepts include the analysis of data mining and machine learning techniques for detecting cyber-attacks, the evolution of cryptography in the face of quantum computing, and specific algorithms used in intrusion detection systems. The references encompass foundational studies, comparative analyses, and theoretical frameworks relevant to enhancing cybersecurity measures through advanced technologies.

The referenced papers cover various advancements in machine learning, particularly in reinforcement learning, representation learning, and predictive coding. 

Watter et al. present a locally linear latent dynamics model that enables control from raw images, emphasizing the importance of latent space representations in decision-making. Higgins et al. discuss the DARLA framework, which enhances zero-shot transfer in reinforcement learning, demonstrating the potential of leveraging prior knowledge to improve learning efficiency.

Ha and Schmidhuber introduce recurrent world models that aid in policy evolution, showcasing how generative models can facilitate better decision-making in dynamic environments. Duan's thesis focuses on learning state representations in robotic control, emphasizing information disentangling and multi-modal learning for improved performance.

In the realm of video prediction, Oh et al. and Finn et al. explore action-conditional predictions and unsupervised learning for physical interactions, respectively, highlighting the significance of predictive models in understanding and interacting with environments.

Several papers address mutual information estimation and representation learning, including works by Belghazi et al., van den Oord et al., and Hjelm et al., all of which propose methods to enhance representation learning through mutual information maximization. Veličković et al. introduce Deep Graph Infomax, focusing on learning representations from graph-structured data.

Other notable contributions include the Arcade Learning Environment by Bellemare et al., a benchmark for evaluating general agents, and probes for understanding intermediate layers by Alain and Bengio. Additionally, foundational theories on predictive coding by Friston, Rao and Ballard, and Bialek and Tishby provide insights into how predictive information is processed in neural systems. 

Overall, these papers collectively advance the understanding of how learning algorithms can be designed to improve performance in complex tasks through better representation and predictive capabilities.

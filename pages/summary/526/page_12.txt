This collection of references highlights significant contributions to the field of representation learning and related methodologies. 

Ozair et al. introduce a Wasserstein dependency measure that enhances representation learning by better quantifying dependencies between variables. Bachman et al. explore maximizing mutual information across different views to improve representation learning. Gutmann and Hyvärinen present noise-contrastive estimation as a novel principle for unnormalized statistical models, which is pivotal for understanding probabilistic frameworks.

Poole et al. discuss variational bounds of mutual information, providing insights into the theoretical underpinnings of representation learning. Sohn's work on multi-class n-pair loss objectives improves deep metric learning, emphasizing the importance of loss functions in training robust models. Sermanet et al. propose time-contrastive networks for self-supervised learning from video, showcasing the utility of temporal data in representation learning.

Ma and Collins examine the consistency and efficiency of noise contrastive estimation and negative sampling in conditional models, while Hyvärinen et al. provide foundational knowledge on independent component analysis. Locatello et al. critically assess common assumptions in unsupervised learning of disentangled representations, challenging established paradigms.

Bengio's works, including a comprehensive review on representation learning, lay the groundwork for understanding deep architectures in AI. Eastwood and Williams propose a framework for evaluating disentangled representations, and Higgins et al. work towards defining what constitutes disentangled representations.

Caron et al. contribute a method for unsupervised learning of visual features through deep clustering, adding to the toolkit available for representation learning. Finally, Donsker and Varadhan's research provides mathematical rigor to the asymptotic evaluation of Markov processes, enriching the theoretical landscape relevant to representation learning.

Overall, these references encapsulate the evolving discourse in representation learning, emphasizing theoretical advancements, methodological innovations, and the ongoing exploration of new paradigms in the field.

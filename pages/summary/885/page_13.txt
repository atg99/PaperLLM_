The referenced works focus on advancements in image processing, particularly in style transfer, image synthesis, and translation using various deep learning techniques. 

Huan Wang et al. (2020) introduced collaborative distillation for ultra-resolution universal style transfer, enhancing the transfer of styles across images. Tengfei Wang et al. (2021) proposed a novel image inpainting method leveraging external-internal learning and a monochromic bottleneck. Ting-Chun Wang et al. (2018) discussed high-resolution image synthesis and semantic manipulation using conditional GANs, emphasizing the ability to control the generated content semantically.

Zhizhong Wang et al. (2020) presented a diversified approach to arbitrary style transfer using deep feature perturbation, while Holger Winnemöller et al. (2006) explored real-time video abstraction techniques. Haiyan Wu et al. (2021) applied contrastive learning for single image dehazing, aiming to improve image clarity.

Yuan Yao et al. (2019) focused on attention-aware multi-stroke style transfer, enhancing the artistic rendering of images. Ning Yu et al. (2021) introduced dual contrastive loss and attention mechanisms for GANs to improve generative performance. Han Zhang et al. (2019) investigated self-attention in GANs to enhance the generation process.

Richard Zhang et al. (2018) highlighted the effectiveness of deep features as a perceptual metric, demonstrating their utility in evaluating image quality. Yulun Zhang et al. (2019) developed multimodal style transfer techniques using graph cuts, enhancing flexibility in style application. Yihao Zhao et al. (2020) and Jun-Yan Zhu et al. (2017) both worked on unpaired image-to-image translation using adversarial consistency and cycle-consistent networks, respectively, contributing to the field of domain adaptation and style transfer.

Overall, these studies illustrate a diverse range of methods and applications in image processing, showcasing the ongoing evolution of deep learning techniques in creative and practical domains.

The referenced works focus on various methodologies for image style transfer and image-to-image translation, highlighting advancements in neural networks and generative models. Key contributions include the development of content transformation blocks for style transfer, diverse image synthesis through disentangled representations, and the integration of Markov random fields with convolutional neural networks. 

Significant techniques involve fast adaptation methods for super-resolution networks, contrastive learning for unpaired translation, and the use of feature transforms for universal style transfer. Several studies emphasize the importance of real-time processing and the enhancement of texture synthesis using deep learning frameworks. 

The papers collectively illustrate a range of approaches to achieve high-quality and diverse style transfer, including the use of style-aware content loss and multi-scale feature decoration. The emphasis on unsupervised learning and meta-learning in various contexts showcases the evolving landscape of image synthesis and style transfer technologies.

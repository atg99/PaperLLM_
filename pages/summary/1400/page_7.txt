Faster R-CNN's Region Proposal Network (RPN) is a fully convolutional architecture that processes images of varying sizes and generates object proposals based on learned features rather than low-level cues. The RPN employs a sliding window approach across the feature map, producing feature vectors that are classified into object or non-object categories and refined with bounding box regression.

In summary, the evolution from R-CNN to Faster R-CNN highlights significant advancements in object detection frameworks, with improvements in computation efficiency, feature extraction, and proposal generation. These enhancements result in superior detection accuracy and faster inference times, addressing the limitations of earlier models.

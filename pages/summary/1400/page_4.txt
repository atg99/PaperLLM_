The paper provides a comprehensive overview of deep learning-based object detection algorithms, categorizing the contributions into three main areas: detection components, learning strategies, and applications & benchmarks. It emphasizes the evolution of object detection techniques since 2012 and presents a taxonomy of methodologies.

In the detection components section, the paper distinguishes between bounding box-level and pixel mask-level localization. It reviews two primary detection frameworks: two-stage and one-stage detectors, detailing aspects such as backbone architecture, proposal generation, and feature learning.

The learning strategies section underscores the importance of effective learning techniques due to the complexities involved in training detectors. It discusses optimization methods for both training and testing phases.

The applications and benchmarks section highlights various real-world applications, including face detection and pedestrian detection, and reviews publicly available benchmarks and evaluation metrics, such as MSCOCO and Pascal VOC. The paper also presents the state-of-the-art results in generic detection over recent years.

Overall, the survey aims to serve as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners in the field, fostering further advancements in object detection systems. The paper is structured into several sections: the standard problem setting, detector components, learning strategies, applications and benchmarks, and concludes with future research directions.

The paper presents a comprehensive analysis of logo detection using a large-scale logo dataset from an e-commerce website, highlighting the limitations of existing detection algorithms which are primarily designed for still images. It emphasizes the unique challenges of detecting objects in videos, particularly the importance of temporal and contextual information. The paper discusses advancements in video object detection, such as Seq-NMS and T-CNN, which leverage the temporal consistency of objects across frames and incorporate tracking algorithms to enhance detection accuracy.

The paper also outlines various benchmarks for generic object detection, including widely used datasets such as Pascal VOC2007 and VOC2012, MSCOCO, Open Images, and LVIS. Each dataset varies in scale and categories, with specific splits for training, validation, and testing. The evaluation metrics focus on detection accuracy, primarily using mean Average Precision (mAP) at different Intersection over Union (IoU) thresholds. The paper notes the significance of these benchmarks in assessing the performance of detection algorithms, including their ability to handle various object scales.

Additionally, the paper briefly mentions face detection benchmarks, introducing datasets like WIDER FACE and AFW, alongside the evaluation metrics commonly applied in this domain. Overall, the analysis underscores the need for refined detection methods that can effectively utilize the rich information available in video sequences and the importance of robust benchmarks to evaluate the performance of these algorithms.

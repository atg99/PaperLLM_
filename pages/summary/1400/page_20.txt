The paper discusses advanced strategies for object detection, focusing on the importance of deformable feature learning and innovative learning strategies. 

Deformable Feature Learning emphasizes the need for detectors to be resilient to nonrigid deformations. Historically, Deformable Part-based Models (DPMs) have been effective, utilizing multiple component parts and deformable coding methods. In the deep learning context, frameworks have emerged to model object deformations explicitly. Notable contributions include DeepID-Net, which introduced a deformable-aware pooling layer, and the development of deformable convolutional layers by Dai et al. and Zhu et al., which learn position offsets to enhance feature map sampling.

The section on Learning Strategy highlights the complexities of training object detectors, which must optimize both localization and classification tasks. Key learning strategies discussed include:

1. **Data Augmentation**: Essential for enhancing training data diversity, methods like horizontal flips and more intensive strategies (rotation, random crops, color jittering) are employed to improve accuracy.

2. **Imbalance Sampling**: This addresses the critical issue of class imbalance in object detection, where negative samples (background) far outnumber positive samples (objects). The paper outlines two main challenges: class imbalance, where background proposals dominate training gradients, and difficulty imbalance, where classifying objects becomes harder. Strategies such as two-stage detectors (e.g., R-CNN, Fast R-CNN) manage this by limiting negative samples and maintaining a balanced ratio of positive to negative samples. Additionally, Liu et al.'s hard negative sampling strategy focuses on utilizing challenging negative proposals to enhance model training.

In summary, the paper provides a comprehensive overview of the evolving methodologies in object detection, with a focus on addressing the challenges posed by nonrigid deformations and the imbalance of training samples through innovative learning strategies.

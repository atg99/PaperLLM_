The table presents the detection performance of various object detection methods on the MS COCO test-dev dataset, categorized into two groups: Two-Stage Detectors and Single-Stage Detectors. Key metrics reported include Average Precision (AP), AP at different IoU thresholds (AP50, AP75), and additional performance metrics (APS, APM, APL).

Two-Stage Detectors:
- Fast R-CNN and Faster R-CNN, both using VGG-16, show initial AP scores of 19.7 and 21.9 respectively in 2015.
- Significant improvements are noted in subsequent years, with methods like Faster R-CNN w FPN achieving an AP of 36.2 in 2016.
- The highest AP recorded in this category is 47.4 by PANet++ (2018) using ResNeXt-101.

Single-Stage Detectors:
- The SSD512 model, employing VGG-16, started with an AP of 28.8 in 2016.
- Advances were made with models like ReﬁneDet512++, which achieved an AP of 41.8 in 2018.
- The best performance in this group is 47.0 by CenterNet511++ (2019) using Hourglass-104.

Overall, the table illustrates a trend of increasing detection accuracy in both categories over the years, highlighting the effectiveness of model architectures and techniques applied in object detection tasks.

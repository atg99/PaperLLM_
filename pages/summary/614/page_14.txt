This document presents a collection of references related to advancements in neural networks, iterative learning control, and various techniques for improving the efficiency and performance of deep learning models. Key contributions include:

1. Chatzikonstantinou et al. discuss neural networks in the context of control systems.
2. Tao et al. focus on robust PD-type iterative learning control for discrete systems with time delays and uncertainties.
3. Taylor et al. provide an overview of the Penn Treebank, a resource for natural language processing.
4. Tjandra et al. explore tensor train compression for recurrent neural networks.
5. Ullrich et al. introduce soft weight-sharing as a method for neural network compression.
6. Wang et al. present HitNet, a hybrid ternary recurrent neural network.
7. Wei et al. analyze input-to-state stability in impulsive reaction-diffusion neural networks.
8. Wen et al. investigate learning intrinsic sparse structures within long short-term memory networks.
9. Xu et al. propose alternating multi-bit quantization for recurrent neural networks.
10. You et al. introduce a global filter pruning method to accelerate deep convolutional networks.
11. Yu et al. examine lottery tickets in reinforcement learning.
12. Yuan et al. discuss growing efficient deep networks through structured continuous sparsification.
13. Zagoruyko and Komodakis enhance convolutional neural networks via attention transfer.
14. Zhang et al. present Lq-nets for learned quantization in deep networks.
15. Zilly et al. focus on recurrent highway networks.

These studies collectively emphasize innovative approaches to optimize neural networks, addressing challenges such as compression, stability, and performance enhancement in various applications.

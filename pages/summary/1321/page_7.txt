The paper discusses the application of artificial intelligence (AI) in the detection of pneumonia types, specifically COVID-19, bacterial pneumonia, non-COVID-19 viral pneumonia, and normal X-ray images. A model was developed that achieved an external testing accuracy of 73.1%, with a sensitivity of 74% and specificity of 67%. 

The methodology section outlines the proposed approach, which utilizes chest X-ray (CXR) images to train a pre-trained AlexNet model. This model classifies various categories: COVID-19 vs. normal, bacterial vs. normal, viral pneumonia vs. normal, and combinations of these categories. The paper emphasizes the efficiency of transfer learning (TL) on deep learning (DL) models, particularly when datasets are limited.

Despite the global prevalence of COVID-19, available CT scan images are scarce. The authors compiled a dataset from multiple sources, including GitHub and Kaggle, totaling 371 COVID-19 pneumonia images and additional images for other pneumonia types and normal cases. A comprehensive dataset of 5856 cases was utilized for training, validation, and testing, with 70% of the data allocated for training and 30% for testing.

The training was conducted using MATLAB on a personal computer equipped with an Intel Core i7 processor and GPU. The paper provides detailed information on the dataset composition, showcasing the number of images for each pneumonia type and normal cases.

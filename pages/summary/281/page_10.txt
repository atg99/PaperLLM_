In the paper, the authors present a performance comparison of TranAD against various baseline methods across multiple datasets, including NAB, UCR, MBA, SMAP, MSL, SWaT, WADI, SMD, and MSDS. The evaluation metrics used are AUC and F1 scores, both calculated with 20% of the training data. TranAD consistently outperforms other methods, achieving the highest AUC and F1 scores across most datasets, particularly excelling in the UCR and MBA datasets. 

Despite OmniAnomaly achieving the best performance on the WADI dataset, TranAD demonstrates superior efficacy overall, especially with limited training data. The authors conduct a critical difference analysis using the Wilcoxon signed-rank test, confirming TranAD's statistically significant advantage over other models. 

Furthermore, the anomaly diagnosis results indicate that TranAD's multi-head attention mechanism enhances its capability to detect inter-correlated anomalies effectively, which is particularly beneficial for multivariate datasets like SMD and MSDS. The study emphasizes TranAD's robust performance and adaptability, establishing it as a leading approach for anomaly detection in various contexts.

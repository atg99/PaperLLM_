In this paper, we introduce TranAD, a transformer-based model designed for effective anomaly detection in multivariate time series data. TranAD leverages self-conditioning and adversarial training to enhance the model's ability to detect subtle anomalies that other models might overlook. The use of transformers allows for parallelized inference, significantly reducing training time while maintaining high prediction accuracy. Our empirical evaluations demonstrate that TranAD outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods by up to 17% in prediction scores and reduces training overheads by up to 99%.

The paper is structured as follows: Section 2 reviews related work in time series anomaly detection, including classical methods, time-series discord discovery, and deep learning approaches. Section 3 details the architecture and functionality of the TranAD model. Section 4 provides a performance evaluation of TranAD against baseline methods. Section 5 presents additional analyses, and Section 6 concludes the study.

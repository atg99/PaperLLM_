The paper by Shreshth Tuli, Giuliano Casale, and Nicholas R. Jennings presents an evaluation of their implementation against an original MATLAB code across several benchmarks. The performance metrics analyzed include Precision, Recall, AUC of the ROC curve, F1 score, and training time. The authors compute the deviation between their implementation and the original MATLAB code using the formula (y - x)/x. The results indicate that their implementation closely matches the original, demonstrating significant improvements in training time, with gains of up to 96%. This suggests a highly efficient approach while maintaining comparable accuracy in performance metrics. The references cited provide a broad context of related works in anomaly detection and time series analysis, showcasing the relevance of their findings within the existing literature.

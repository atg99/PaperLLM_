This paper provides an overview of the Medical Visual Question Answering task (VQA-Med) held at ImageCLEF 2019. The task involved answering medical questions based on radiology images, with a focus on four categories: Modality, Plane, Organ System, and Abnormality. Each category was designed with varying difficulty levels, utilizing both classification and text generation techniques. The questions were crafted to be answerable solely from the image content, eliminating the need for additional medical knowledge.

A new dataset comprising 4,200 radiology images and 15,292 question-answer pairs was created for this challenge. The event attracted 17 participating teams employing diverse methods, including transfer learning, multi-task learning, and ensemble techniques. The top-performing team achieved a BLEU score of 64.4% and an accuracy of 62.4%. The authors indicate plans for future editions to focus on more goal-oriented datasets and explore new dimensions such as contextual information and domain-specific inference. 

The paper highlights the potential of AI in clinical decision support, emphasizing the significance of systems that can interpret medical images and answer related questions for various applications, including education and diagnosis.

The Visual Question Answering (VQA) task, particularly in the medical domain, merges natural language processing and computer vision, presenting unique challenges. Recent advancements in VQA have highlighted the need for specialized approaches in fields like Medicine. Ben Abacha et al. identified four significant challenges in medical VQA: creating goal-oriented systems and datasets, categorizing clinical questions, selecting relevant images, and capturing necessary medical context.

Building on the initial success of VQA-Med 2018, the VQA-Med 2019 task was organized to focus on radiology images and clinically relevant questions. The questions were designed to target single elements and were categorized into four main groups: Modality, Plane, Organ System, and Abnormality. This structure not only facilitates classification tasks for the first three categories but also addresses answer generation for abnormalities, aligning more closely with clinical decision support needs compared to traditional open-domain VQA datasets.

The task involves answering questions based on visual content from medical images, with a clear emphasis on questions that can be directly answered without needing extensive additional information. Future iterations of the challenge may incorporate more complex questions requiring broader contextual understanding. The paper details the task description, dataset creation, evaluation methodology, and results in subsequent sections.

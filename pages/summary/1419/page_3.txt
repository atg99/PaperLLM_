The paper discusses the creation of the VQA-Med-2019 dataset, which is designed for visual question answering in the medical domain. The dataset was constructed by filtering relevant medical images from the MedPix database based on specific criteria such as image captions, modalities, and diagnosis methods. It includes images for which a diagnosis was confirmed through various imaging techniques including CT, MRI, and ultrasound.

The authors categorized the questions associated with the images into four main types: Modality, Plane, Organ System, and Abnormality. Each category includes examples of the types of questions that can be asked. For instance, Modality questions may inquire about the type of imaging used, while Plane questions focus on the orientation of the images. Organ System questions target the specific body systems depicted, and Abnormality questions assess the normalcy of the images.

The dataset is publicly available and has been validated by medical professionals to ensure its relevance and accuracy, making it a valuable resource for advancing research in medical image analysis and question answering.

The paper discusses the performance of various teams in the VQA-Med 2019 competition, focusing on their BLEU scores and the methodologies employed in their Visual Question Answering (VQA) systems. The results indicate that the top-performing systems utilized deep learning techniques, primarily deep convolutional neural networks such as VGGNet and ResNet, along with transformer-based architectures like BERT and recurrent neural networks. These models incorporated various pooling strategies and attention mechanisms, including multimodal factorized bilinear pooling and multi-modal factorized high-order pooling, to effectively combine multimodal features for answer prediction.

Table 4 presents the BLEU scores for different teams, showcasing Hanlin, yan, and minhvu as the top contenders with scores of 0.644, 0.640, and 0.634 respectively. The analysis of question categories reveals that systems excelled in answering modality, plane, and organ questions due to their finite answer types. Conversely, accuracy was lower for abnormality type questions, attributed to the complexity of open-ended responses. The paper emphasizes the advancements in deep learning techniques and their impact on VQA-Med performance, while also highlighting areas for improvement in handling more challenging question types.

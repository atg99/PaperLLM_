This collection of references highlights various contributions to the field of medical visual question answering (VQA) and related machine learning techniques. 

Lau et al. present a dataset of visual questions and answers specifically focused on radiology images, facilitating advancements in clinically relevant machine learning tasks. Liu et al. investigate the use of an Xception-GRU model for VQA in the medical domain, while Papineni et al. introduce the BLEU score, a widely accepted metric for evaluating machine translation quality.

Several studies from the CLEF 2019 conference explore different methodologies in medical VQA. Shah et al., Shi et al., and Thanki et al. contribute to the understanding of VQA through various model implementations. Spanier and Turner question the necessity of LSTM in VQA, emphasizing the importance of model validation. Vu et al. and Yan et al. discuss ensemble methods and classification models, respectively, applying transfer learning techniques to enhance performance in medical VQA tasks.

Further, Simonyan and Zisserman provide insights into deep convolutional networks, essential for image recognition tasks. Lastly, Yang et al. introduce stacked attention networks, advancing the interaction between visual data and question inputs.

Overall, these works collectively advance the understanding and capabilities of machine learning in interpreting medical imagery through visual question answering frameworks.

The paper presents a novel approach for abnormal event detection in video sequences using a specialized architecture called SiTGRU, which integrates Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) layers. The methodology builds on the limitations of existing techniques that primarily rely on handcrafted feature descriptors and traditional deep learning models.

The SiTGRU network consists of six GRU layers with varying numbers of units, specifically designed to capture spatio-temporal regularity in video data. The architecture includes hyperbolic tangent activation and batch normalization layers to enhance performance. The model is trained on normal video sequences to learn typical motion patterns, allowing it to reconstruct regular motion features with minimal error while identifying anomalies through higher reconstruction costs.

This work is notable for being the first to utilize GRUs specifically for video abnormality detection, suggesting that recurrent neural networks have significant potential in this domain that has previously been overlooked. The proposed method aims to improve detection accuracy and efficiency by analyzing the differences between consecutive frames in a sequential manner.

The paper discusses the application of the SiTGRU model for anomaly detection in video sequences, specifically focusing on its performance in challenging scenarios. It presents a qualitative analysis of the model's reconstruction errors to compute regularity scores for test videos from the Avenue, Ped1, and Ped2 datasets. Figures included illustrate the model's ability to identify anomalies, with lower reconstruction errors indicating regular sequences and higher errors correlating with anomalous events.

The paper highlights the effectiveness of SiTGRU when coupled with noise reduction and occlusion-resistant features, enhancing its performance across different environments. The qualitative evaluation shows that the model successfully detects multiple abnormalities in single frames, as evidenced by the heat maps generated from test videos. These heat maps visually differentiate normal and anomalous pixels, with anomalies highlighted in yellow and red, while normal regions are marked in blue. Overall, the findings confirm the model's capability to accurately identify and visualize anomalous events in various datasets.

In this section, the authors present the evaluation metrics employed to assess their method, SiTGRU, for detecting anomalous events in videos. They utilize a regularity score to evaluate frame normality and quantify performance using the Area Under the Curve (AUC) by Equal Error Rate (EER), a common metric for such tasks. The ROC curve is generated by plotting True Positive Rate (TPR) against False Positive Rate (FPR) across various thresholds. A higher AUC indicates superior model discrimination capability, while EER estimates the point where FPR and False Negative Rate (FNR) are approximately equal.

The authors compare SiTGRU with related methods, including Conv-AE, ConvLSTM-AE, and standard RNNs, across three datasets: Avenue, Ped1, and Ped2. The results demonstrate that SiTGRU outperforms standard LSTMs across all benchmarks, achieving improvements in AUC by EER on Ped1, Ped2, and Avenue test sets. While it shows slightly lesser performance than standard GRUs on Avenue due to challenges such as camera shake and occlusion, SiTGRU still displays robust anomaly detection capabilities.

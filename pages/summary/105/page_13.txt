In this paper, we introduce an end-to-end approach for abnormality detection in videos utilizing a Single-Tunnelled GRU architecture. Our method involves a novel encoding-decoding structure that incorporates GRU layers with varying unit counts. Key modifications to the standard GRU include the removal of the reset gate and the substitution of the hyperbolic tangent activation function with a sigmoid activation function. 

We conducted experiments on the CUHK Avenue and UCSD benchmarks, demonstrating the proposed model's superior performance in both detection and generalization tasks compared to traditional recurrent networks. Notably, our approach also enhances computational efficiency, leading to reduced training and testing times along with lower memory consumption.

Future research may explore the integration of our model with other recurrent and deep network variants or focus on pixel-level abnormality detection. The challenge of generalizing detection models across different datasets remains an area requiring further investigation.

This collection of references presents a comprehensive overview of recent advancements in various aspects of robotics, particularly focusing on human-robot interaction, emotional recognition, and activity recognition. 

Deshmukh et al. explore real-time facial expression recognition techniques, while Noroozi et al. provide insights into emotional body gesture recognition. Wang et al. discuss deep learning applications in sensor-based activity recognition, emphasizing the integration of AI in understanding human behaviors.

Shibata et al. introduce a mental commit robot designed for therapeutic applications with children, highlighting the role of robotics in emotional and psychological support. Cheng et al. present a detailed account of robot skin technology, emphasizing its sensors and applications in enhancing robot capabilities.

Alhaddad et al. investigate the emotional responses of companion robots to aggressive interactions from children, showing the significance of reaction time. Oliveira et al. model player activity in interactive robotic games, contributing to the field of gaming and robotics.

Tapus et al. review the importance of perceiving social interactions for effective social robotics, while Aly and Tapus focus on multimodal human-robot interaction through speech and gesture mapping. The works of Tapus et al. further extend to long-term learning and behavior adaptation for individuals with impairments.

The references also delve into psychological aspects, with Paunonen and Ashton discussing the prediction of behavior through personality factors. Bandler and Grinder's foundational work on the structure of magic is referenced, indicating its relevance in understanding communication strategies.

Cruz-Maya et al. explore motivation in user performance during robot interactions, while de Oliveira et al. examine deceptive actions in robotic agents to enhance perceived rationality. Bonarini et al. focus on robot adaptation in competitive scenarios, showcasing the dynamic interaction between robots and humans.

The importance of benchmarking in robotics is emphasized by Amigoni et al. and Feil-Seifer et al., who discuss evaluation metrics for socially assistive robotics and the role of competitions in performance assessment. Chrysostomou et al. advocate for reproducible experiments in human-robot interaction, underscoring the need for standardization in research.

Overall, these references illustrate the interdisciplinary nature of robotics, bridging technology, psychology, and social interaction to enhance the efficacy of robotic systems in various applications.

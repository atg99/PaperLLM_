The cited works encompass a range of topics related to machine learning, particularly focusing on neural networks, regularization techniques, and the underlying mathematical frameworks. Mahoney and Orecchia explore implicit regularization through approximate eigenvector computation, while Mao discusses stochastic differential equations and their applications. Miller and Hardt present stable recurrent models, and Morton et al. introduce deep variational Koopman models for uncertainty-aware dynamics modeling.

Noh et al. examine the regularization of deep neural networks through noise, providing insights into its interpretation and optimization. Pan and Duraisamy emphasize physics-informed probabilistic learning of nonlinear dynamics, ensuring stability in their methods. Pascanu et al. address the challenges in training recurrent neural networks, and Poggio et al. delve into the theory of deep learning, particularly the non-overfitting puzzle.

Queiruga et al. propose continuous-in-depth neural networks, while Rusch and Mishra develop a coupled oscillatory recurrent neural network architecture for learning long time dependencies. Smith et al. investigate the origins of implicit regularization in stochastic gradient descent. Sokolić et al. contribute to understanding the generalization error of deep neural networks, focusing on classification margin and data structure.

Stutz et al. differentiate between adversarial robustness and generalization, and Sun et al. provide a differential equation perspective on the stochastic training of residual networks. Szegedy et al. highlight intriguing properties of neural networks, and Takeishi et al. work on learning Koopman invariant subspaces for dynamic mode decomposition. Lastly, Talathi and Vartak propose methods to enhance the performance of recurrent neural networks using ReLU nonlinearity. Overall, these studies contribute significantly to the understanding and advancement of machine learning methodologies.

Haber and Ruthotto discuss stable architectures for deep neural networks, addressing issues related to stability and convergence. Hodgkinson et al. explore stochastic continuous normalizing flows, presenting a method for training stochastic differential equations as ordinary differential equations.

Huang et al. propose deep networks with stochastic depth, a technique aimed at improving training efficiency. Jim et al. analyze noise in recurrent neural networks, focusing on convergence and generalization properties. Kag et al. investigate the dynamics of recurrent neural networks evolving on equilibrium manifolds, proposing a solution to the vanishing and exploding gradients problem.

Keskar et al. examine large-batch training in deep learning, revealing its impact on generalization and sharp minima. Kidger et al. introduce neural controlled differential equations for handling irregular time series data. Kloeden and Platen provide a comprehensive resource on the numerical solutions of stochastic differential equations.

Le et al. present a method for initializing recurrent networks with rectified linear units to enhance performance. Lezcano-Casado and Martínez-Rubio discuss orthogonal constraints in neural networks, offering a straightforward parameterization approach. Li et al. focus on learning compositional Koopman operators, contributing to model-based control techniques.

Lim's work utilizes nonequilibrium response theory to deepen the understanding of recurrent neural networks. Liu et al. explore the role of noise in enhancing robustness within the neural stochastic differential equation framework. Lu et al. bridge deep architectures and numerical differential equations, expanding on the concept of infinite-layer neural networks.

Lutter et al. develop deep Lagrangian networks, leveraging physics as a model prior in deep learning. Ma et al. aim for a mathematical understanding of neural network-based machine learning, highlighting existing knowledge gaps. Mahoney discusses approximate computation and implicit regularization for large-scale data analysis, providing insights into data processing techniques.

Overall, these works collectively advance the understanding and application of neural networks, addressing theoretical, practical, and computational aspects of the technology.

In the preface, Dimitri P. Bertsekas acknowledges the contributions of students Sushmita Bhattacharya and Thomas Wheeler in the research and implementation of various methods in reinforcement learning. The 2021 ebook edition features minor editorial changes from the original 2019 version, influenced by his companion research monograph titled "Rollout, Policy Iteration, and Distributed Reinforcement Learning," published in 2020. This monograph delves into topics like rollout, approximate policy iteration, and multiagent problems, which are either less detailed or not covered in the present book. 

While the monograph focuses on the Alpha Zero and TD-Gammon paradigms, the current book offers a broader coverage of reinforcement learning, including aspects like approximation in policy space and temporal difference methods, along with a proof-based approach to infinite horizon exact and approximate dynamic programming theory in Chapters 4 and 5. Bertsekas also directs readers to his website and video lectures that complement the content from both the book and the monograph.

"Neuro-Dynamic Programming" by Dimitri P. Bertsekas and John N. Tsitsiklis is a comprehensive textbook that thoroughly explores the methodology of neuro-dynamic programming and reinforcement learning. This work represents a significant advancement in applying neural networks and dynamic programming to complex issues related to planning, optimal decision-making, and intelligent control. 

The book is praised for its integration of mathematical and computational concepts into a cohesive and rigorous framework, as noted in George Cybenko's review in IEEE Computational Science and Engineering. The writing is clear and concise, supported by comprehensive examples and scholarly historical notes.

Key features of the book include:
- A unification of various neuro-dynamic programming methods, including new approaches.
- Innovative formulations and solutions for critical problems in stochastic optimal control, sequential decision-making, and discrete optimization.
- A rigorous explanation of the underlying mathematical principles.
- Practical applications illustrated through case studies in optimal resource allocation, feedback control, data communications, game playing, and combinatorial optimization.
- Extensive background information and new research insights into dynamic programming and neural network training.

The book was recognized with the 1997 INFORMS CSTS prize for its excellence in bridging Operations Research and Computer Science, making it a valuable resource for scientists and engineers interested in the foundational mathematics and computations of modern behavioral machine learning.

The paper by Dimitri P. Bertsekas discusses the relationship between reinforcement learning and optimal control, highlighting their foundational principles and methodologies. It emphasizes the similarities in their goals, which involve making sequential decisions to maximize cumulative rewards or minimize costs over time. The author explores various algorithms and approaches used in both fields, such as dynamic programming and policy iteration, illustrating how they converge on solving complex decision-making problems.

Bertsekas also addresses the theoretical underpinnings of reinforcement learning, including concepts like Markov decision processes and value functions, which are integral to both reinforcement learning and optimal control. The paper provides insights into the practical applications of these techniques across various domains, demonstrating their effectiveness in real-world scenarios.

Overall, the work serves as a comprehensive overview, bridging the gap between reinforcement learning and optimal control, and offering a framework for understanding their interconnections and applications in decision-making processes.

The ATHENA SCIENTIFIC OPTIMIZATION AND COMPUTATION SERIES, authored primarily by Dimitri P. Bertsekas, presents a comprehensive collection of works focused on optimization, reinforcement learning, dynamic programming, and control theory. 

1. "Rollout, Policy Iteration, and Distributed Reinforcement Learning" (2020) delves into advanced reinforcement learning techniques.
2. "Reinforcement Learning and Optimal Control" (2019) explores the intersection of these fields.
3. "Abstract Dynamic Programming" (2nd Edition, 2018) provides foundational concepts in dynamic programming.
4. "Dynamic Programming and Optimal Control" (Two-Volume Set, 2017) is an extensive treatment of optimal control strategies.
5. "Nonlinear Programming" (3rd Edition, 2016) covers methods for solving nonlinear optimization problems.
6. "Convex Optimization Algorithms" (2015) discusses algorithms for convex optimization.
7. "Convex Optimization Theory" (2009) outlines the theoretical aspects of convex optimization.
8. "Introduction to Probability" (2nd Edition, 2008) offers a thorough grounding in probability theory.
9. "Convex Analysis and Optimization" (2003) integrates convex analysis with optimization techniques.
10. "Network Optimization: Continuous and Discrete Models" (1998) addresses optimization in network flows.
11. "Network Flows and Monotropic Optimization" (1998) focuses on specific optimization techniques in network flows.
12. "Introduction to Linear Optimization" (1997) provides essential principles of linear programming.
13. "Parallel and Distributed Computation: Numerical Methods" (1997) covers computational methods in distributed systems.
14. "Neuro-Dynamic Programming" (1996) examines algorithms that integrate neural networks with dynamic programming.
15. "Constrained Optimization and Lagrange Multiplier Methods" (1996) discusses constrained optimization techniques.
16. "Stochastic Optimal Control: The Discrete-Time Case" (1996) focuses on stochastic methods in control theory.

This series is a vital resource for researchers and practitioners in optimization and computational methods, showcasing the evolution of thought and methodology in these fields.

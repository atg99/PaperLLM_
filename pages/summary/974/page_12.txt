In the preface, the authors discuss their approach to presenting complex mathematical concepts in algebra and probability, opting for intuitive explanations over rigorous proofs. They emphasize the importance of precise language to ensure logical consistency, defining terms clearly and avoiding redundancy. While acknowledging that some presented methods may lack robust performance guarantees, they assert that understanding the underlying principles is crucial for discerning effective algorithms from speculative ones.

The authors highlight the challenges in machine learning, particularly in understanding the reasoning behind algorithms like DeepMind's AlphaZero, which, despite demonstrating success, does not provide insights into its decision-making processes. This gap in understanding raises concerns about trust in these methods. They note that reinforcement learning, while rooted in dynamic programming, is characterized by complex interactions that complicate its analysis. The preface conveys a sense of the evolving and somewhat chaotic nature of the field, underscoring the need for further theoretical and practical research to clarify its principles.

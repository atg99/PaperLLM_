The focus on reinforcement learning as the primary framework allows for a comprehensive exploration of the techniques and algorithms that can be utilized to address multistage decision problems. By emphasizing the connection between optimal control and artificial intelligence, the text aims to facilitate collaboration and understanding among practitioners from diverse backgrounds.

Moreover, the book seeks to provide a structured overview of the various methods that have emerged in the field, ensuring that readers can navigate through the complexities and competing concepts. This organization of knowledge will not only aid researchers in their inquiries but also assist practitioners in implementing effective solutions in real-world scenarios.

In summary, the preface sets the stage for a detailed examination of reinforcement learning and its applications, while underscoring the importance of bridging theoretical foundations with practical methodologies in the realm of dynamic programming.

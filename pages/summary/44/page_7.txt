The paper introduces two additional weight matrices, Wr and Wx, as well as a bias vector, θs, to transform the system into a canonical Recurrent Neural Network (RNN) form. The equations define the state update of the RNN as s[n] = Ws s[n-1] + Wr r[n-1] + Wx x[n] + θs. The output r[n] is generated by applying a function G to the state s[n]. The figure accompanying the text illustrates the canonical RNN cell, with the bias parameters θs omitted for brevity, but it is noted that they can be incorporated by modifying the input vector x[n].

The formulation presented in Equation 30 will serve as a foundation for evolving into the Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) system. Prior to this evolution, the concepts of "unrolling" and the notion of an "RNN cell" will be discussed, simplifying the understanding of the standard RNN definition derived from stability considerations.

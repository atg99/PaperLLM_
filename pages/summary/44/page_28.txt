Figures 8 and 9 illustrate the operational framework of an LSTM network. Figure 8 shows a sequence of steps produced by unrolling a single LSTM cell, highlighting four distinct time steps for clarity. Figure 9 presents a detailed view of the vanilla LSTM cell, outlining the various stages of the system through dashed rectangles. Each stage is annotated to clarify its specific function within the network. Notably, the bias parameters are not depicted in the figure for simplicity, but they can be integrated into the model by appending a constant element to the input signal vector and adjusting the dimensions of the weight matrices accordingly.

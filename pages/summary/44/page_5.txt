The inclusion of non-zero delay time constants in these terms allows for greater flexibility in modeling temporal dynamics, particularly in biological or physical systems.

The parameters representing the counts of functions related to the state, readout, and additional input, as well as their associated delays, are chosen based on system requirements or estimated iteratively.

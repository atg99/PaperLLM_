The paper titled "Fundamentals of Recurrent Neural Network (RNN) and Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) Network" by Alex Sherstinsky, published in the Elsevier journal “Physica D: Nonlinear Phenomena,” addresses the foundational aspects of RNNs and LSTMs, emphasizing the need for a comprehensive understanding of these models. Despite the extensive coverage of LSTM networks in literature, Sherstinsky notes that many resources fail to adequately present key training formulas and the rationale behind techniques like RNN unrolling. 

The tutorial aims to clarify these concepts by deriving the canonical RNN formulation from differential equations and providing a precise justification for the unrolling technique. The paper also discusses the challenges associated with training standard RNNs and proposes transforming them into the "Vanilla LSTM" through logical arguments. Detailed descriptions and equations related to the LSTM system are included to enhance understanding.

Sherstinsky identifies opportunities for enriching the LSTM system, leading to the development of a more general LSTM variant. The intended audience includes machine learning practitioners who seek a deeper understanding of RNNs and LSTMs, as well as those looking to implement the new augmented LSTM model in research or practical applications. The paper underscores the importance of a thorough grasp of the principles and functions of these networks to facilitate effective experimentation and application.

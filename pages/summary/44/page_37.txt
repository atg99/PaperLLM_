The paper discusses the Augmented LSTM cell, detailing the integration of various matrix and vector parameters into a comprehensive representation, denoted as Θ. This representation encompasses weight matrices and bias vectors related to different components of the LSTM cell, including input, context, and forget gates, as outlined in Equations 196 to 214. 

The Augmented LSTM network cell is presented, with an emphasis on the omission of bias parameters for clarity. It notes that these biases can be incorporated into the model by modifying the input signal vector. Additionally, the paper describes the use of non-causal context windows through convolution operations with matrix-valued linear filters, which are essential for computing the input-related quantities.

The section provides a detailed notation framework for the Vanilla Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) cell, outlining key variables and functions crucial for its operation. 

Notations include:
- Indices and dimensions related to input and state signals.
- Warping functions (Gc and Gd) for controlling and bounding data.
- Various signals such as input, state, observable, accumulation, update candidates, and gate outputs.

The LSTM employs three gate types to manage:
- The update candidate signal contributing to the current state.
- The previous state signal influencing the current state.
- The readout candidate signal for external access.

Additionally, the section discusses data standardization necessary for LSTM operation, ensuring that input data has a mean of zero and a standard deviation of one. This involves calculating the mean and the auto-covariance matrix of the training dataset, followed by transforming the data to meet these statistical criteria. It emphasizes that the test and validation sets should be standardized using the training set's statistics.

The paper discusses the derivation of partial derivatives of the objective function E with respect to the parameters of a Vanilla LSTM cell, as outlined in equations 156 to 170. Each equation represents the gradient of E concerning various weight matrices and biases associated with input, state, and output for different components of the LSTM cell, namely the cell state, forget gate, and output gate.

The total partial derivative of E with respect to the model parameters Θ at step n is summarized in equation 171, which aggregates the individual gradients from each component. When the LSTM cell is unrolled for K steps to process a full segment of training samples, the same parameters Θ are utilized across all steps. Consequently, equation 172 expresses the total derivative of E with respect to Θ as the sum of the contributions from all unrolled steps.

This total derivative is integral to the optimization process via Gradient Descent, where it is computed for batches of segments. The gradients across these segments are accumulated and used to update the model parameters, enhancing the learning process. The paper notes that while regularization is crucial in machine learning training, it is not the focus of this discussion.

The paper discusses the derivation of partial derivatives of an objective function E with respect to various parameters of the Augmented LSTM cell, as defined in specific equations. The derivatives are computed for different components including weights and biases associated with input, state, and output gates. Each derivative is expressed in terms of an activation function alpha, the input x, the previous state s, and other relevant variables. 

The summary of these derivatives is organized to mirror the structure of the parameter set Θ, which highlights the methodical approach to calculating the gradients required for optimization. The notation indicates that the derivatives are calculated for a range of indices, ensuring clarity in the relationship between the parameters and the overall objective function. 

This systematic derivation is crucial for implementing backpropagation through time in LSTM networks, allowing for effective learning from sequential data. The organization of the derivatives supports both theoretical understanding and practical application in neural network training.

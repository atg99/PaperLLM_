The paper discusses the evaluation metrics for speech recognition models, specifically focusing on the Character Error Rate (CER) and Word Error Rate (WER). CER measures the accuracy based on the number of character-level substitutions, insertions, deletions, and correct characters, whereas WER operates at the word level. A lower CER or WER indicates better model performance. The references provided encompass a wide range of studies related to deep learning, model compression, and efficient inference techniques, reflecting the current advancements in neural network architectures and their applications in various domains, including language and image recognition.

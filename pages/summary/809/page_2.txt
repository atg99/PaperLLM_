This paper provides a comprehensive overview of deep neural network pruning, a technique that has gained significant attention since 2015 due to its effectiveness in reducing redundancy in deep learning models. The authors highlight the increasing number of research papers on neural network pruning and compression, with a notable surge in interest since 2015. They trace the origins of pruning research back to 1988 but note that it only gained momentum with the recognition of its potential in recent years.

The survey addresses existing literature, pointing out that many reviews either cover multiple compression techniques without sufficient depth or focus narrowly on specific aspects of pruning. The authors aim to fill this gap by offering a thorough review of over 300 academic papers, establishing a new taxonomy for pruning techniques, and providing detailed descriptions of representative methods.

Key contributions of the paper include:

1. **Comprehensive Review**: It distills insights from a wide array of studies, covering methods for both small and large models, and introduces a new taxonomy for pruning techniques.
   
2. **Comparative Analysis**: The authors conduct comparative experiments on different pruning methods, including novel advancements like pruning for large language models (LLMs) and varying supervision levels.
   
3. **Resource Compilation**: The paper summarizes various applications of pruning, along with benchmark datasets and evaluation methods, aiding researchers and practitioners in their endeavors.
   
4. **Recommendations and Future Directions**: It provides guidance on selecting appropriate pruning methods based on application needs and suggests promising areas for future research.

The structure of the paper is outlined, with sections dedicated to taxonomy, speedup, timing of pruning, comparative analysis, integration with other compression techniques, practical recommendations, and concluding thoughts. Overall, this survey serves as a critical resource for understanding and applying neural network pruning effectively in diverse contexts.

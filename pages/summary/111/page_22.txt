The paper provides a comprehensive overview and comparative analysis of various deep learning models trained from scratch on the ImageNet dataset, highlighting advancements in transformer architectures. Notable models discussed include:

1. **T2T-ViT**: This model improves upon the Vision Transformer (ViT) by effectively modeling structural information in images, thereby enhancing feature richness.
2. **Transformer in Transformer (TNT)**: A vision transformer that extracts both local and global representations using an inner and outer transformer architecture.
3. **PyramidTNT**: An enhancement of TNT that incorporates a pyramid architecture and convolutional stem, significantly boosting performance.
4. **Switch Transformers**: A method proposed to efficiently increase the parameter count of transformer models.
5. **ConvNeXt**: A redesigned transformer that integrates convolutional layers with the transformer attention mechanism to capture spatially localized data.
6. **Evolutionary Algorithm Transformer (EATFormer)**: An advanced vision transformer inspired by evolutionary algorithms.

The paper also delves into deep reinforcement learning (RL), a machine learning paradigm focused on sequential decision-making to maximize rewards. Unlike supervised learning, RL agents learn through trial and error without explicit instructions, utilizing a framework based on Markov Decision Processes (MDP) to model decision-making. Deep reinforcement learning merges RL's decision-making capabilities with deep learning's perception functions, reflecting a more human-like approach to intelligence. The structure of deep reinforcement learning is illustrated, showing how it processes sensory inputs to determine appropriate actions based on expected rewards.

Notable techniques include Elastic Weight Consolidation (EWC), which preserves important weights from previous tasks, Incremental Moment Matching (IMM), which merges models trained on different tasks, and the iCaRL method that integrates classification with representation learning. Additionally, the Hard Attention to the Task (HAT) approach utilizes task-specific masks to mitigate interference between tasks.

The paper also addresses the challenge of underspecification in machine learning models, particularly in real-world applications. Underspecification occurs when an ML pipeline produces multiple models that perform well on validation sets but behave unpredictably in deployment due to factors like random initialization and hyperparameter selection. To tackle this issue, rigorous testing beyond standard metrics is recommended, including stress tests to evaluate model robustness under various conditions.

In the analysis section, the methodology focuses on applying and evaluating different deep learning models for classification tasks across three datasets: IMDB, ARAS, and Fruit-360. The IMDB dataset contains movie reviews labeled by sentiment, ARAS consists of annotated sensor events for human activity recognition, and Fruit-360 is a collection of fruit images for classification. The study evaluates eight models—CNN, RNN, LSTM, Bidirectional LSTM, GRU, Bidirectional GRU, TCN, and Transformer—across these datasets to compare their performance, highlighting the strengths of each model type in handling structured and sequential data.

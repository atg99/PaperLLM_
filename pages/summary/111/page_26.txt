This paper presents a comprehensive overview and comparative analysis of deep learning models, highlighting their applications across various real-world domains. It emphasizes that each application area has distinct objectives, requiring tailored tasks and specific deep learning techniques. 

Table 1 summarizes practical applications of deep learning models in different settings, such as smart homes, smart cities, education, and healthcare. Each application is associated with particular tasks and the models employed. For instance, in smart homes and cities, tasks include human activity recognition using CNN combined with LSTM, smart energy management through reinforcement learning, and traffic management utilizing GRU-based models. In the education sector, applications involve detecting student engagement with DenseNet self-attention and recognizing affective states through ConvNeXt and GRU. Healthcare applications focus on medical image analysis with vision transformers and early disease detection employing InceptionV3.

Overall, the paper underscores the versatility of deep learning across diverse fields, showcasing its ability to address specific challenges through a variety of models and techniques.

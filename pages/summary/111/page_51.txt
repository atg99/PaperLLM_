This section lists various scholarly references focusing on advancements in deep learning and transformer models. Key contributions include surveys on efficient deep learning techniques aimed at optimizing model performance by making them smaller and faster. The references cover significant developments in intrusion detection models utilizing improved transformer architectures, and the application of transformers for nonintrusive sensor-based activity recognition in multi-resident environments. 

Prominent works include the introduction of BERT for language understanding, generative pre-training methods, and advancements in autoregressive pretraining through models like XLNet. The list also highlights innovations in vision transformers, such as the Pyramid Vision Transformer and Swin Transformer, which provide versatile frameworks for dense prediction tasks. Additionally, techniques for scaling transformer models to accommodate trillions of parameters, as discussed in the Switch Transformers paper, are also noted. 

Overall, these references collectively underscore the rapid evolution and application of transformer models across various domains, including natural language processing and computer vision.

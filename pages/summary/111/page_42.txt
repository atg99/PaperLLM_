This article provides a thorough overview of deep learning technologies and their applications in machine learning and artificial intelligence. It discusses various deep learning models, including neural networks, multi-layer perceptrons, convolutional neural networks (CNNs), recurrent neural networks (RNNs), transformers, generative models, deep reinforcement learning, and transfer learning. The classification of these models enhances the understanding of their specific applications and characteristics.

RNNs, particularly Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM), Bidirectional LSTM (Bi-LSTM), Gated Recurrent Units (GRU), and Bidirectional GRU (Bi-GRU), are highlighted for their effectiveness in handling time series data due to their capability to capture temporal dependencies. In contrast, CNNs excel in analyzing image data by effectively capturing spatial features.

The article reports on experiments conducted with three public datasets—IMDB, ARAS, and Fruit-360—showing that CNN-based models like DenseNet and MobileNet perform exceptionally well in image classification tasks, while RNN models such as LSTM and GRU are strong in time series analysis. Notably, transformer models surpass classical RNNs in text analysis, leveraging their attention mechanism for improved performance.

The discussion also addresses emerging research directions, particularly in quantum deep learning models, including Quantum CNNs, Quantum RNNs, and Quantum Generative Adversarial Networks, which represent a frontier in the field. Overall, the article emphasizes the diverse applications and effectiveness of deep learning models, underscoring the importance of selecting the appropriate model based on the specific data nature and task requirements. The insights from the experiments assist in understanding the strengths and weaknesses of various models, enabling informed decision-making in practical applications.

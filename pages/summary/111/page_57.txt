The referenced works encompass a range of applications of deep learning and machine learning techniques across various domains, including movie recommendation systems, e-commerce user behavior prediction, loan default prediction, stock price forecasting, object detection for autonomous driving, and robotic manipulation.

2. **E-commerce User Behavior**: Liu presents a prediction model using deep learning to analyze and anticipate e-commerce users' purchasing habits.

3. **Loan Default Prediction**: Deng et al. introduce a CNN-based method for predicting loan defaults by leveraging feature cross and classification techniques.

4. **Stock Price Prediction**: Han and Fu explore the use of a Bi-directional LSTM model for deep learning to tackle stock price prediction challenges.

5. **Autonomous Driving Object Detection**: Cao et al. develop MCS-YOLO, a multiscale object detection method aimed at improving recognition of road environments for autonomous vehicles.

6. **Pedestrian Detection**: Jain et al. utilize metaheuristics combined with deep convolutional networks to enhance multimodal pedestrian detection in crowded settings.

7. **Mapping in Autonomous Vehicles**: Sindhu and Saravanan present an optimized extreme learning machine for simultaneous localization and mapping tasks in autonomous vehicles.

8. **Lane Detection**: Singal et al. focus on lane detection for autonomous vehicles using deep learning methodologies.

9. **Surface Defect Detection**: Shang et al. propose a defect-aware transformer network designed for intelligent visual detection of surface defects.

10. **Predictive Maintenance**: Zonta et al. create a predictive maintenance model using deep neural networks to optimize production scheduling.

11. **Textile Chemical Process Optimization**: He et al. develop a deep reinforcement learning-based decision support system for optimizing textile chemical processes.

12. **Supply Chain Time Series Forecasting**: Pacella and Papadia evaluate the effectiveness of LSTM networks for forecasting in supply chain management.

13. **Robotic Grasp Manipulation**: Shukla et al. explore evolutionary computing and deep reinforcement learning for improving robotic grasp manipulation.

14. **Robotic Teleoperation Motion Planning**: Kamali et al. present a dynamic-goal deep reinforcement learning approach for real-time motion planning in robotic teleoperation.

These studies collectively illustrate the versatility and applicability of advanced machine learning techniques in solving complex problems across diverse fields.

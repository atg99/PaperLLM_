The references provided encompass a range of topics in machine learning, optimization algorithms, and the intersection of quantum computing with deep learning. Key contributions include:

1. Hyperparameter Optimization: Xiao et al. propose a variable length genetic algorithm for efficient hyperparameter tuning in deep learning.

2. Model Selection: Escalante et al. discuss the use of particle swarm optimization for model selection in machine learning.

3. Optimization Techniques: Kingma and Ba introduce the Adam optimization method, while Duchi et al. present adaptive subgradient methods for online learning. Dozat builds on this by incorporating Nesterov momentum into Adam.

4. Deep Learning Under Constraints: Alzubaidi et al. survey tools for deep learning that address data scarcity, highlighting definitions, challenges, and solutions.

5. Quantum Neural Networks: Cong et al. explore quantum convolutional neural networks, and Takaki et al. investigate variational quantum recurrent neural networks for temporal data. Lloyd and Weedbrook introduce quantum generative adversarial learning.

6. General Advances: Garg and Ramakrishnan provide an overview of advancements in quantum deep learning, while Valdez and Melin review the applications of quantum computing and deep learning algorithms.

This compilation illustrates the ongoing research and development in both classical and quantum realms of machine learning, emphasizing innovative optimization strategies and novel architectures.

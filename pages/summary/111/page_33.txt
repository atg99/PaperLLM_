The study conducted by F. M. Shiri et al. presents a comparison of various deep learning models on the IMDB dataset, focusing on their performance metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and training time. The models evaluated include CNN, RNN, LSTM, Bi-LSTM, GRU, BI-GRU, TCN, and Transformer, with specific architectural configurations such as 2 attention heads and a hidden layer size of 64 in the feed-forward network, utilizing the ReLU activation function. 

Table 2 summarizes the results, indicating that the Transformer model achieved the highest accuracy at 88.03%, followed closely by the GRU at 87.55% and LSTM at 87.53%. The RNN model performed the lowest, with an accuracy of 59.03%. The study employs diagrams to visualize the accuracy and validation accuracy over multiple epochs, offering insights into model learning effectiveness for sentiment classification. 

The analysis underscores the significance of architectural choices in enhancing model performance and provides a standardized framework for evaluating deep learning models on this dataset.

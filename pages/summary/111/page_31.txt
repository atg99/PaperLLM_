The paper by F. M. Shiri et al. evaluates various deep learning models for image classification and sentiment analysis. For fruit image classification using the Fruit-360 dataset, the authors assessed eight CNN-based models: VGG, Inception, ResNet, InceptionResNet, Xception, MobileNet, DenseNet, and NASNet. The choice of CNNs is justified due to their effectiveness in capturing spatial dependencies in image data, as well as their capability for faster training through parallel processing on GPUs. Performance metrics used for evaluation included accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-measure, providing insights into the strengths and weaknesses of each model.

In the sentiment analysis section, the authors utilized the IMDB dataset, which contains 50,000 movie reviews with balanced positive and negative labels. They employed eight deep learning models: CNN, RNN, LSTM, Bidirectional LSTM, GRU, Bidirectional GRU, TCN, and Transformer for sentiment classification. The dataset's structure was designed to minimize review correlation per movie, and key linguistic indicators were identified for both sentiment classes.

Overall, the study aims to identify the most effective models for specific tasks, contributing to advancements in deep learning applications. All experiments were conducted on a GeForce RTX 3050 GPU with 4GB of RAM.

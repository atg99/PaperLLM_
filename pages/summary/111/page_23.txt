Deep Q-learning Networks (DQN) are highlighted as a prominent deep reinforcement learning model, employing Convolutional Neural Networks to learn policies from complex inputs. Variants such as Double DQN, Dueling DQN, and Monte Carlo Tree Search further enhance the capabilities of DRL. Applications of DRL span diverse fields including gaming, robotics, image processing, and energy management.

Deep Transfer Learning (DTL) addresses challenges associated with limited training datasets in specialized domains by leveraging prior knowledge to expedite the learning process. This approach draws from educational psychology principles, emphasizing the importance of relational connections between tasks for effective knowledge transfer. DTL techniques are classified into four categories, each utilizing distinct methodologies to facilitate the transfer of knowledge and improve machine learning outcomes.

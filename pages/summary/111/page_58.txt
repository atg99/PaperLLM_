This paper provides a comprehensive overview and comparative analysis of various deep learning models, focusing on their applications and enhancements through techniques such as data augmentation and addressing class imbalance. It references numerous studies that explore different aspects of deep learning, including motion trajectory prediction, time series classification, and sound classification.

Key studies cited include Zhang et al.'s work on recurrent neural networks for human-robot collaboration, and Iwana and Uchida's empirical survey on data augmentation techniques for time series. The paper also highlights methods to enhance model performance through geometric transformations and mixup techniques for data augmentation.

Furthermore, the issue of class imbalance in deep learning is addressed, referencing significant contributions such as the SMOTE (Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique) and its variants like Borderline-SMOTE and ADASYN. These techniques aim to improve learning from imbalanced datasets, which is crucial for developing robust models.

Overall, the paper synthesizes current research trends in deep learning, emphasizing the importance of innovative data handling strategies to improve model accuracy and generalization in various applications.

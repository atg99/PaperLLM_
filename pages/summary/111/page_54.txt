This paper provides a comprehensive overview and comparative analysis of various deep learning models, with a focus on their applications across different domains. It highlights recent advancements in deep transfer learning, showcasing its effectiveness in diverse tasks such as COVID-19 patient detection using X-ray images, automatic speech recognition, hate speech detection on social media, and human activity recognition. Key contributions include the development of transfer learning methods for heart valve disease classification from phonocardiogram signals and Parkinson's disease classification from surface electromyography signals. The paper also discusses the implementation of deep learning techniques in traffic flow optimization in smart cities, recyclable product classification for waste management, and energy management systems utilizing deep reinforcement learning. The analysis includes a review of methods like stacked GRU-LSTM for parking occupancy prediction and a three-dimensional DenseNet for detecting student engagement. The findings underline the versatility of deep learning models and their potential to address complex real-world challenges across various fields.

The paper presents the performance results of various deep learning models applied to the ARAS dataset, summarizing their accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and computation time. The models evaluated include CNN, RNN, LSTM, Bi-LSTM, GRU, BI-GRU, TCN, and Transformer. The Transformer model achieved the highest accuracy at 94.56%, followed closely by TCN at 94.04% and BI-GRU at 93.90%. 

In terms of precision, the Bi-LSTM and GRU models showed strong performance, while recall rates were consistently high across models. The F1-scores indicate a balanced performance between precision and recall, with the Transformer model leading in this metric as well. 

Additionally, the paper includes diagrams (Figures 33 and 34) that illustrate the accuracy and validation accuracy, as well as the loss and validation loss for the deep learning models, providing visual insights into their performance over the training process.

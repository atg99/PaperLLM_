To address catastrophic forgetting, various strategies have been proposed. One approach is to use regularization techniques that constrain the model's weight updates, preserving the important parameters associated with earlier tasks. Another method involves employing rehearsal strategies, where the model periodically retrains on previous tasks to reinforce earlier knowledge. Additionally, architectures designed for continual learning, such as memory-augmented networks, can help retain information across tasks by storing and recalling relevant data as needed.

In summary, hybrid methods can enhance classification accuracy by combining algorithm and data-level techniques. Overfitting, a common issue in deep learning, can be mitigated through various strategies, including dropout and regularization. The vanishing and exploding gradient problems can be addressed by using activation functions like ReLU, proper weight initialization, and batch normalization, which stabilize training. Finally, catastrophic forgetting remains a significant challenge in the field, necessitating innovative approaches to ensure models retain previously learned information while adapting to new tasks.

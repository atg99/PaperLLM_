The paper discusses the implementation of Bayesian Structure Learning using the BiDAG package in R, particularly focusing on the process of learning Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs) through an iterative Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) approach. Initial results from a preliminary model indicated a True Positive Rate (TPR) of 58%, alongside a False Positive Rate (FPRn) of 0.03 and a Structural Hamming Distance (SHD) of 94. To enhance the search space and improve DAG estimation, the iterative MCMC algorithm was employed with the "orderIter" option.

The iterative process involved creating new MCMC chains for each expansion step, which required considerable computation time. The summary of the iterative fit revealed a maximum score DAG comprising 100 nodes and 199 edges, with a maximum DAG score of -15195.67 achieved after 7 search space expansion steps. The initial search space contained 204 edges, with 196 edges added during the iterations. The total number of MCMC iterations reached 1,127,000, with 7,007 sampling steps recorded.

The results demonstrated a significant improvement in DAG scores with each expansion step, as visualized in a trace plot. The final MAP DAG score showed notable enhancements compared to the initial iterations, highlighting the effectiveness of the iterative MCMC method in refining the structure learning process.

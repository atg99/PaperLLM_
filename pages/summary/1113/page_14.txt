The paper discusses BiDAG, a framework for Bayesian structure learning and sampling in R, focusing on dynamic Bayesian networks (DBNs). It illustrates the structure of a first-order DBN with four time slices, where each slice contains a static variable and four dynamic variables. Figures included show the unfolded structure and the initial and transition graphs of the DBN, highlighting both intra-slice and inter-slice edges. The initial graph G0 establishes the relationships within a time slice, while the transition graph G→ represents the connections between consecutive time slices. This framework aims to facilitate the representation and analysis of temporal data through Bayesian methods.

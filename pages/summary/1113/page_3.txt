The BiDAG package offers various functions organized into categories for structure learning, posterior model selection, convergence diagnostics, and model comparison. The package is particularly useful for learning structures of Dynamic Bayesian Networks (DBNs) and comes with a visualization tool that enhances the interpretation of results.

The methodological foundation of BiDAG is based on Bayesian networks, which represent the factorization of joint probability distributions through directed acyclic graphs (DAGs). The package includes two score functions: the Bayesian Dirichlet equivalent score (BDe) for categorical data and the Bayesian Gaussian equivalent score (BGe) for continuous data. These scores facilitate the evaluation of different DAGs based on their posterior probabilities given the observed data.

In practical applications, BiDAG has been employed to analyze simulated datasets and to characterize cancer sub-types, demonstrating its versatility in handling various statistical challenges. The discussion of runtime across different simulation settings also provides insights into the efficiency of the implemented algorithms.

Overall, BiDAG serves as a comprehensive tool for statistical modeling, particularly in the context of Bayesian networks, enhancing both the learning and visualization processes associated with complex data structures.

This paper discusses the application of BiDAG for characterizing cancer subtypes, specifically focusing on renal papillary cell carcinoma (KIRP) and kidney renal clear cell carcinoma (KIRC). The authors utilized a joint graph representation of the two cancer subtypes' MAP CPDAGs obtained through an iterative Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) approach. The methodology involved optimizing the search space using the "orderIter" algorithm, followed by sampling with the "partition" algorithm. The posterior model selection was based on a sample of 100 Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs) with a threshold of 0.5.

The analysis focused on non-silent mutation data from two cohorts in The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA), incorporating significantly mutated genes with a q-value of less than 0.1. The connected genes from both KIRP and KIRC were also included in the analysis. The findings demonstrate how BiDAG can effectively characterize cancer subtypes in a supervised setting, building on previous research by Kuipers et al. (2018b). The authors provide a link to their code for unsupervised clustering and further details on Bayesian network-based clustering methodologies.

The references provided represent a collection of influential works related to Bayesian networks, structure learning, and statistical methodologies. Key contributions include Chickering's 1996 work highlighting the NP-completeness of learning Bayesian networks, which establishes foundational challenges in this area. Consonni and Rocca (2012) present objective Bayes factors for Gaussian directed acyclic graphical models, enhancing Bayesian model evaluation.

Drton and Maathuis (2017) review advancements in graphical modeling, focusing on structure learning techniques. Eaton and Murphy (2007) introduce BDAGL, a software tool for Bayesian Directed Acyclic Graph learning, while Franzin et al. (2017) discuss the bnstruct R package, which facilitates Bayesian network structure learning despite missing data.

Friedman and Koller (2003) offer a Bayesian framework for structure discovery in networks, and Geiger and Heckerman (1995, 2002) unify learning approaches for discrete and Gaussian domains, contributing significantly to parameter priors in graphical models. Gelman and Rubin (1992) address iterative simulation inference, providing methodological rigor.

Additional references include the graph package by Gentleman et al. (2022) for graph data structures, Goudie and Mukherjee's (2016) Gibbs sampler for DAG learning, and Kalisch and Bühlmann's (2007) PC-algorithm for estimating high-dimensional directed acyclic graphs. Together, these works underscore the importance of Bayesian methods and software tools in statistical learning and graphical model development.

The paper presents an analysis of iterative Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) methods applied to Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs) in the context of structure learning from simulated datasets. Figure 3 illustrates the trace plot of DAG scores generated by the iterative MCMC process, highlighting the progression of scores over iterations. Figure 4 further explores the impact of expanding the search space on the model's performance, specifically using the simulated dataset gsim100. 

In panel (a), the True Positive Rate (TPR) and DAG scores are tracked across iterations, while panel (b) depicts the changes in False Positive (FP) rates and Structural Hamming Distance (SHD). The results indicate how iterative expansions influence the model's ability to approximate the ground truth structure, utilizing the function itercomp to facilitate comparisons of the Maximum A Posteriori (MAP) DAGs against the actual structure. Overall, the findings underscore the effectiveness of iterative MCMC in enhancing the accuracy of DAG structure learning.

Dynamic Neural Networks are a significant advancement in deep learning, contrasting with static models that maintain fixed computational graphs and parameters during inference. This survey categorizes dynamic networks into three types: sample-wise, spatial-wise, and temporal-wise dynamic models. Sample-wise models adjust their architectures or parameters based on individual inputs, spatial-wise networks adapt computations for different spatial locations in image data, and temporal-wise models modify inference for sequential data like videos or texts. 

The paper emphasizes several advantages of dynamic networks: 

1. **Efficiency**: They allocate computations based on input demands, enhancing computational and data efficiency, particularly in few-shot learning scenarios.
2. **Representation Power**: Dynamic networks expand the parameter space and improve representation capabilities through mechanisms like feature-conditioned attention weights.
3. **Adaptiveness**: They enable a flexible balance between accuracy and efficiency, allowing for adjustments based on varying computational resources.
4. **Compatibility**: Dynamic networks integrate well with advanced deep learning techniques, benefiting from innovations in architecture, optimization, and preprocessing.
5. **Generality**: They can be applied across various applications, including computer vision and natural language processing, showcasing versatility.
6. **Interpretability**: Research in dynamic networks may enhance understanding of deep model mechanisms, akin to human brain processing.

The survey concludes by addressing key research challenges in dynamic networks, such as architecture design and decision-making schemes, while highlighting open problems and future research directions in this evolving field.

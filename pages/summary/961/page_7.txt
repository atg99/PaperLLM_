1. **Dynamic Weight Adjustment** involves modifying the model parameters based on input data. This approach is often computationally intensive and requires specialized hardware for efficient implementation, such as customized CUDA kernels for GPUs. 

2. **Dynamic Weight Prediction** is a more straightforward method where an independent model generates input-adaptive parameters during inference. This technique includes classic frameworks like Dynamic Filter Networks and HyperNetworks, which produce filters for convolutional layers or weight matrices for RNNs, respectively. Recent advancements also incorporate task-specific information to enhance parameter generation, improving performance in applications like visual question answering and few-shot learning.

3. **Dynamic Features** aim to enhance the representation power of models by rescaling features based on input-dependent soft attention. This includes channel-wise and spatial-wise attention mechanisms, which adjust features dynamically to improve the model's performance. Additionally, dynamic activation functions have emerged as a method to further increase representation capacity with minimal computational cost.

In conclusion, the paper emphasizes the potential of soft attention mechanisms and dynamic parameter adjustments to improve deep learning models' efficiency and effectiveness across various tasks. The integration of these approaches can significantly enhance the model's adaptability and performance without substantial increases in computational overhead.

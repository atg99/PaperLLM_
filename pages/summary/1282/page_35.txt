The referenced works focus on various aspects of mathematical modeling, approximation methods, and the application of deep learning in solving partial differential equations (PDEs). Key contributions include:

1. **State Estimation and Reduced Models**: Cohen and DeVore explore the role of reduced models in state estimation, highlighting their significance in simplifying complex systems.

2. **High-Dimensional PDEs**: Cohen and DeVore's research on approximation methods for high-dimensional parametric PDEs discusses techniques that enhance computational efficiency and accuracy.

3. **Galerkin Approximations**: The convergence rates of best N-term Galerkin approximations for elliptic stochastic PDEs are analyzed, providing insights into the performance of these methods.

4. **Analytic Regularity**: The study of analytic regularity and polynomial approximation in parametric and stochastic elliptic PDEs emphasizes the mathematical foundations necessary for effective modeling.

5. **Geometric Diffusions**: Coifman et al. introduce geometric diffusions as tools for harmonic analysis, aiding in the structural definition of data through diffusion maps.

6. **Bayesian Inverse Problems**: Dashti et al. focus on Besov priors, offering a framework for Bayesian approaches in inverse problems, which are crucial for data interpretation.

7. **Deep Learning in PDE Solutions**: Several papers, including those by W. E and B. Yu, and Fresca et al., discuss the integration of deep learning techniques, such as the Deep Ritz Method and comprehensive approaches for nonlinear PDEs, showcasing how neural networks can address complex variational problems.

8. **Nonlinear Approximation and Neural Networks**: DeVore's work on nonlinear approximation establishes a theoretical foundation for the use of deep ReLU networks in modeling nonlinear systems.

9. **Neural Networks in Quantum Chemistry**: Gilmer et al. present neural message passing as a method for enhancing quantum chemistry calculations, demonstrating the versatility of neural networks in various scientific domains.

10. **Autoencoders for Fluid Dynamics**: Gonzalez and Balajewicz explore deep convolutional recurrent autoencoders, which learn low-dimensional dynamics in fluid systems, highlighting advancements in machine learning for fluid mechanics.

This collection of works reflects the ongoing interdisciplinary efforts to leverage mathematical theories and machine learning techniques to solve complex problems in science and engineering.

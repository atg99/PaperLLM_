The paper discusses the performance of a proposed method for solving linear elliptic and Poisson problems using neural networks, focusing on relative test errors as a function of resolution and reduced dimension. In Figures 4.3 and 4.4, panels (a) show that the error remains constant across varying mesh resolutions for the proposed method, unlike the image-to-image regression approach by Zhu, which exhibits increasing errors with higher resolution. This consistency is attributed to the method's design in Hilbert space, which allows for reliable approximation across different meshes. The linear nature of the operator Ψ and the true map ϕ implies that the method's linear approximation outperforms the neural network approach, as also reflected in the results of both figures. The paper emphasizes the advantages of the dimension reduction technique in achieving stable performance irrespective of discretization changes.

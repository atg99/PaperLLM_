The paper discusses the application of model reduction techniques and neural networks to parametric partial differential equations (PDEs). It presents a series of visual examples showcasing the performance of the proposed approximation methods across five different problems: linear elliptic equations, Poisson equations, Darcy flow with log-normal coefficients, Darcy flow with piecewise constant coefficients, and Burgers' equation. Each problem is represented in a row, displaying the input, ground truth, approximation, and error for each case. The approximations are generated using the most effective method identified in the study, with specific configurations for each problem indicated by the parameters provided. The results demonstrate the efficacy of the proposed approach in accurately approximating solutions to complex PDEs while highlighting the associated errors.

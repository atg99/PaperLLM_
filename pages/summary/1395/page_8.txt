The referenced works focus on advancements in semantic segmentation, specifically real-time applications and methodologies. Key contributions include the development of various neural network architectures that enhance segmentation accuracy and efficiency. For instance, Bisenet and its iterations are highlighted for their ability to perform rapid segmentation while maintaining high precision. Techniques such as edge-guided flows and feature alignment are also discussed, showcasing innovative approaches to improve segmentation quality in different contexts, including autonomous driving and medical imaging.

Several studies emphasize the importance of lightweight models that utilize attention mechanisms and dilated convolutions, which enable effective processing without compromising speed. Additionally, pre-trained architectures are defended as valuable for real-time applications, demonstrating their robustness in diverse scenarios. Overall, the literature reflects a growing emphasis on combining computational efficiency with high-performance segmentation capabilities, addressing the increasing demands of real-time applications in computer vision.

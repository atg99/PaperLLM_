This paper discusses the development of a Uniﬁed Attention Fusion Module (UAFM) designed to enhance feature representation for segmentation tasks. The UAFM effectively balances computation costs between encoder and decoder while maintaining competitive accuracy, as demonstrated by the PP-LiteSeg model. The UAFM employs both channel and spatial attention mechanisms to enrich fused features.

The framework of UAFM includes two main inputs, Fhigh from a deeper module and Flow from the encoder. Fhigh is upsampled to match the dimensions of Flow, and an attention module generates a weight for feature fusion. The procedure involves element-wise multiplication and addition to create a fused output.

In the spatial attention module, the inter-spatial relationships are analyzed to determine the significance of each pixel, using mean and max operations across channels to form a concatenated feature, which is then processed to produce attention weights.

The channel attention module, on the other hand, focuses on inter-channel relationships, applying average and max pooling to compress spatial dimensions and generate weights representing channel importance.

Additionally, the paper introduces a Simple Pyramid Pooling Module (SPPM) that utilizes pyramid pooling to further enhance feature fusion. Overall, the proposed methods aim to improve segmentation accuracy by integrating advanced attention mechanisms while managing computational efficiency.

This paper presents an evaluation of the proposed PP-LiteSeg model against state-of-the-art real-time segmentation methods on the Cityscapes and CamVid datasets. The results demonstrate that PP-LiteSeg achieves a favorable balance between accuracy and speed, with PP-LiteSeg-T1 recording the fastest inference speed of 273.6 FPS and a mIoU of 72.0%. For higher resolution input (768x1536), PP-LiteSeg-B2 achieved the highest accuracy of 78.2% mIoU on the validation set.

The model's performance is compared in detail with various existing architectures, indicating that PP-LiteSeg outperforms these methods in terms of both mIoU and frames per second (FPS). The paper also includes an ablation study that highlights the contributions of specific modules integrated into PP-LiteSeg, such as FLD, SPPM, and UAFM, which collectively enhance segmentation accuracy.

Moreover, experiments on the CamVid dataset corroborate the model's efficiency, with PP-LiteSeg-T achieving 222.3 FPS, significantly faster than other comparable methods. The findings confirm the effectiveness of the proposed modules and underscore the capabilities of PP-LiteSeg for real-time semantic segmentation tasks.

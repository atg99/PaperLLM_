The paper introduces PP-LiteSeg, a novel lightweight model designed for real-time semantic segmentation, addressing the need for improved performance in practical applications. The authors highlight the limitations of existing semantic segmentation methods, which, despite advancements through deep learning, often fail to balance accuracy and computational efficiency.

To enhance the model's performance, the authors propose several innovative components. The Flexible and Lightweight Decoder (FLD) is introduced to minimize the computational burden of traditional decoders. The Unified Attention Fusion Module (UAFM) is designed to enhance feature representations by employing both spatial and channel attention mechanisms to create a weighted fusion of input features. Additionally, the Simple Pyramid Pooling Module (SPPM) aggregates global context effectively while maintaining low computational costs.

Extensive evaluations demonstrate that PP-LiteSeg achieves an impressive balance between accuracy and speed, with results showing 72.0% mean Intersection over Union (mIoU) at 273.6 frames per second (FPS) and 77.5% mIoU at 102.6 FPS on the Cityscapes test set using an NVIDIA GTX 1080Ti. The findings indicate that PP-LiteSeg outperforms other methods in terms of both accuracy and inference speed, establishing it as a state-of-the-art solution for real-time semantic segmentation. The source code and models are available on PaddleSeg, providing a resource for further research and application.

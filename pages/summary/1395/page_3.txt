3.2. Unified Attention Fusion Module
The Unified Attention Fusion Module (UAFM) is introduced to enhance feature representation by integrating attention mechanisms. UAFM aims to effectively combine features from different sources while considering their contextual importance. This module is designed to improve the accuracy of segmentation by focusing on relevant features and mitigating the impact of noise.

3.3. Simple Pyramid Pooling Module
The Simple Pyramid Pooling Module (SPPM) is proposed to capture multi-scale contextual information while maintaining computational efficiency. SPPM employs a straightforward approach to pooling that allows for the aggregation of features at various scales, thereby enhancing the model's ability to understand the spatial hierarchy of the input data.

4. Proposed Architecture: PP-LiteSeg
The proposed architecture, PP-LiteSeg, integrates the Flexible and Lightweight Decoder, Unified Attention Fusion Module, and Simple Pyramid Pooling Module for real-time semantic segmentation tasks. The design focuses on balancing performance and efficiency, making it suitable for applications requiring rapid processing without compromising accuracy. The architecture leverages the advancements in feature fusion and pooling strategies to achieve a robust segmentation framework that meets the demands of modern computational environments. 

In conclusion, the advancements in semantic segmentation, particularly through lightweight designs and innovative feature fusion techniques, are pivotal in addressing the real-time processing requirements of contemporary applications.

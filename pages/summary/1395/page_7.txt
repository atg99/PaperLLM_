The paper presents PP-LiteSeg, a novel real-time semantic segmentation network that balances accuracy and inference speed. It introduces three key components: the Flexible and Lightweight Decoder (FLD) which enhances the efficiency of previous decoders, the Unified Attention Fusion Module (UAFM) that strengthens feature representations, and the Simple Pyramid Pooling Module (SPPM) which aggregates global context at a low computational cost. Comparative results show PP-LiteSeg achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of mean Intersection over Union (mIoU) and frames per second (FPS) on the Cityscapes and CamVid datasets, outperforming several existing methods. The authors plan to extend their approach to additional tasks such as matting and interactive segmentation in future work.

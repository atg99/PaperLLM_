The paper discusses the concept of transfer learning, which involves adapting a pre-trained model to a new data distribution. It highlights the risks associated with differences in data distribution and emphasizes the importance of proper transfer to enhance model performance while minimizing these risks. The authors aim to conduct a foundational analysis to better understand transfer learning.

Acknowledgments are given to Samy Bengio and Maithra Raghu for their insights, and the research was funded by Google. The references list includes significant works related to neural networks, deep learning, and transfer learning, illustrating the foundational concepts that support the study.

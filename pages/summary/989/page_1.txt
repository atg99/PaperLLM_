This paper provides a comprehensive review of the role of machine learning (ML) in computer vision (CV), highlighting its significance in various applications such as surveillance, optical character recognition, and medical imaging. The authors, Abdullah Ayub Khan, Asif Ali Laghari, and Shafique Ahmed Awan, discuss the evolution of ML, emphasizing its shift from manual data feeding to automated learning processes through supervised and unsupervised techniques. 

The objectives of the paper include examining how ML extracts critical information from images and its contributions to healthcare, particularly in enhancing image quality and recognizing vital features in medical imaging. The authors aim to address the current state of ML in CV, detailing tools, applications, datasets, and techniques used in the field, while also discussing limitations of existing research and future challenges.

The paper outlines various ML algorithms and their impact on image processing, including neural network models. It identifies critical open research areas that require further exploration to improve the efficiency of ML applications in CV. The review serves as a valuable resource for understanding the intersection of ML and CV, providing insights into both the achievements and the ongoing challenges in this rapidly evolving domain.

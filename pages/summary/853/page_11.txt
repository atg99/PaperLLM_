The referenced papers collectively explore various aspects of false data injection attacks on power systems and smart grids, focusing on detection methods, impacts, and defenses. Key contributions include surveys on attacks and countermeasures, detection techniques utilizing Kalman filters and deep learning, and methodologies for secure state estimation. Several studies emphasize the importance of real-time detection to mitigate the risks posed by such cyber threats. The exploration of federated learning and privacy-preserving techniques highlights innovative approaches to intrusion detection. The literature also addresses the vulnerabilities of power grids during state estimation and presents strategies for enhancing resilience against these attacks. Overall, these works underscore the critical need for robust security frameworks in the evolving landscape of smart grid technologies.

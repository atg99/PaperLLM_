The general public and users, such as elderly individuals interacting with care robots, require a more simplified form of transparency that allows them to grasp the robot's functions and decision-making processes in a user-friendly manner.

The paper emphasizes the critical importance of transparency and explainability in artificial intelligence and robotics, particularly in applications involving human interaction. It highlights that users must be able to comprehend the actions and reasoning of these systems, especially in sensitive contexts like assisted living, where safety and predictability are paramount. The paper stresses that a robust explainability framework can enhance user trust and facilitate understanding, thereby allowing users to build mental models of the technology.

Furthermore, the paper discusses the potential dangers associated with autonomous systems, noting that failures can lead to physical harm or erroneous outcomes, such as incorrect medical diagnoses or biased decisions in financial contexts. It argues that transparency is essential not only for accountability and safety but also for societal reassurance following incidents involving these technologies.

The P7001 working group is introduced as a body that aims to establish measurable levels of transparency for various stakeholders, recognizing that different groups have distinct needs for understanding autonomous systems. The paper outlines the varying degrees of transparency required, from detailed technical information for safety certifiers to user-friendly explanations for the general public. Overall, the discussion underscores the necessity of tailored transparency measures to ensure the safe and responsible deployment of AI and robotics in society.

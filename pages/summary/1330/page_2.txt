The paper discusses the increasing recognition of the need for ethical principles in the fields of robotics and artificial intelligence (AI). While establishing ethical principles is essential, the paper emphasizes that these principles must be translated into actionable ethical standards for effective governance. 

One of the pioneering efforts in this area is the publication of BS 8611, a guide for the ethical design and application of robots and robotic systems, released in April 2016. This guide provides a framework for ethical risk assessment, identifying 20 ethical hazards categorized into societal, application, commercial, and environmental risks. It also offers advice on mitigating these risks and validating the proposed measures, addressing concerns such as loss of trust and privacy infringements.

In the same year, the IEEE Standards Association launched a global initiative focused on the ethics of autonomous and intelligent systems, marking a significant advancement in the establishment of ethical standards. This initiative is notable for its commitment to human well-being, positioning robotics and AI as tools for enhancing human conditions rather than solely for economic gain.

The first major output of this initiative is the document titled Ethically Aligned Design (EAD), which outlines over 100 ethical issues and recommendations. Developed with public input and contributions from over 1,000 volunteers, EAD aims to guide the ethical design of autonomous systems, ensuring that stakeholders are equipped to prioritize ethical considerations in their development. The ongoing work in this area reflects a concerted effort to align technological advancements with ethical imperatives for the benefit of humanity.

This collection of resources focuses on the ethical considerations and guidelines surrounding the design and application of robots and robotic systems. The British Standards Institute provides a comprehensive guide (BS 8611:2016) that emphasizes ethical design principles. The EPSRC outlines core principles of robotics, promoting responsible innovation. Several IEEE resources, including the Ethically Aligned Design framework, address ethical standards and the development of autonomous systems. Notably, discussions in The Guardian highlight concerns regarding self-driving car companies investigating their own accidents, advocating for independent oversight. Winfield and Jirotka propose the concept of an ethical black box to enhance accountability in robotics. Additionally, the ACM Code of Ethics and IEEE governance documents further reinforce the importance of ethical practices in technology development. Grand et al. explore the role of open science as a trust-building mechanism in scientific communication. Collectively, these sources contribute to the ongoing discourse on ensuring ethical practices in robotic technologies.

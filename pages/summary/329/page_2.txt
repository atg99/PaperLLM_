VL-GPT enables unified training of multimodal sequences, allowing for effective utilization of large-scale image-text pairs. Upon completion of pre-training, the model demonstrates capabilities in zero-shot and few-shot multimodal tasks, showcasing its flexibility in handling diverse vision and language understanding and generation challenges. 

Additionally, the model exhibits emergent properties that facilitate multimodal in-context learning, enabling it to effectively address new tasks with multimodal prompts. The proposed approach aims to position VL-GPT as a foundational model for the multimodal community, akin to the influence of GPT models in natural language processing. 

Key contributions include the development of the image tokenizer-detokenizer framework for efficient image embedding conversion and reconstruction, and the introduction of VL-GPT as a powerful generative pre-trained transformer model for multimodal applications.

This paper summarizes the evaluation benchmarks and training processes for a vision-language model (VL-GPT). The evaluation benchmarks include various datasets for tasks like scene description, question answering, and image generation, with metrics such as CIDEr, VQA accuracy, and FID, focusing on achieving higher or lower scores as appropriate.

For training the image tokenizer and detokenizer, datasets CC3M, Laion-Aesthetics, and LAION-COCO are used. CC3M consists of 3.3 million image-text pairs, while the other two are high-quality subsets of the larger LAION-5B dataset. The model converges with a smaller subset of 10 million samples, with random sampling done based on dataset sizes. The architecture employs CLIP-L for the visual encoder and utilizes components like U-Net and VAE from IP-adapter Plus, with specific attention to causal dependencies in visual embeddings.

For pre-training VL-GPT, the paper mentions the use of interleaved image-text datasets MMC4 and OBELICS, with quality filtering based on CLIP similarity. The model integrates a pre-trained LLaMA 7B with the image tokenizer-detokenizer framework, using LoRA modules for efficient training without adjusting the core model parameters.

Instruction tuning is performed to align the VL-GPT model with human instructions, utilizing datasets like LLA VA, SVIT, and MSCOCO Caption, restructured into conversational formats. The tuning process samples data proportionally to dataset sizes, ensuring effective model optimization.

Overall, the paper provides a detailed overview of the methodologies used to train and evaluate the VL-GPT model, emphasizing efficiency and alignment with human interaction.

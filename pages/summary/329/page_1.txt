The paper presents VL-GPT, a generative pre-trained transformer designed for simultaneous understanding and generation of visual and linguistic data. VL-GPT employs a unified pre-training strategy for both images and text through a straightforward auto-regressive objective, enabling seamless processing akin to text-only language models. The authors introduce a novel image tokenizer-detokenizer framework that converts raw images into continuous embeddings and reconstructs them, allowing for the encoding of interleaved image-text data into a multimodal sequence suitable for transformer models.

VL-GPT is capable of large-scale pre-training on multimodal corpora, which enhances its performance in zero-shot and few-shot scenarios across various tasks, including image captioning, visual question answering, and text-to-image generation. The model also supports in-context learning when provided with multimodal prompts. Additionally, the authors perform instruction tuning on VL-GPT, showcasing its potential for multimodal assistance.

The work addresses limitations in existing vision-language models that often treat image inputs and outputs differently, hindering the implementation of auto-regressive training on image embeddings. VL-GPT's approach aims to unify these processes, ultimately advancing capabilities in multimodal understanding and generation.

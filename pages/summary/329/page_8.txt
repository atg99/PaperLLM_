The paper presents VL-GPT, a generative pre-trained transformer model designed for vision and language tasks. It highlights the model's innovative image tokenizer-detokenizer framework, which allows for effective pre-training on large multimodal datasets using a unified auto-regressive objective. The results demonstrate VL-GPT's competitive performance across various benchmarks, particularly in visual question answering and image captioning, where it outperforms existing models in few-shot settings.

Key findings include VL-GPT's high CIDEr scores in MSCOCO captioning and its ability to generate realistic images and multimodal content based on complex prompts. The model's performance improves significantly with instruction tuning, as seen in VL-GPT-I, which achieves top results across tasks.

Ablation studies indicate that the simultaneous estimation of both text and image condition embeddings enhances the model's ability to reconstruct images and perform in vision-language tasks effectively. The paper concludes by emphasizing VL-GPT's potential as a versatile multimodal assistant and calls for further research to explore its capabilities, particularly regarding scaling model parameters for general intelligence in multimodal contexts.

The references listed in the International Journal of Cognitive Informatics and Natural Intelligence include a variety of significant works related to cognition, data processing, and artificial intelligence. Key contributions are made by authors such as Anderson, who discusses the architecture of cognition, and Baciu et al., who explore cloud-driven visual cognition for large streaming data. Other notable references include theoretical frameworks in neuroscience by Dayan and Abbott, and methodologies for cognitive distraction assessment by Harada et al. The works collectively cover diverse topics such as eye tracking, cognitive models, data fusion techniques, and the impact of distractions on visual behavior. This compilation serves as a foundational resource for understanding cognitive processes and their implications in various applications.

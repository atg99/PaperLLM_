The paper presents a comprehensive overview of various contributions by Wang Y. in the field of cognitive informatics and cognitive computing, spanning multiple publications from 2011 to 2016. Key topics include cognitive robotics, mathematical engineering, cognitive learning methodologies, and the development of frameworks for cognitive linguistics. Notable works include discussions on fuzzy probability algebra, concept algebra for knowledge representation, and the mathematical theories underpinning knowledge science. Wang's keynote addresses at international conferences emphasize deep reasoning and brain-inspired systems. The body of work collectively contributes to advancements in understanding the interplay between cognitive processes and computational intelligence, providing a foundation for future research in these interdisciplinary areas.

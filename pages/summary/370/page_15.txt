The paper presents details on the pretraining of various RoBERTa model sizes, specifying configurations that include the number of attention heads (AH), hidden size (HS), feed-forward network dimension (FFN), and total parameters (P). The models range from a base size with 12 attention heads and 768 hidden size, down to an extra small size with 3 attention heads and 256 hidden size, with corresponding parameter counts decreasing from 125 million to 15 million.

Additionally, the paper outlines the training parameters for 12 models, detailing the maximum training steps, batch sizes, and validation perplexity across different training sizes. For instance, the BASE model trained on 1 billion tokens with a batch size of 4096 achieved a validation perplexity of 3.84 over 31,000 steps. In contrast, smaller models, such as the MED-Small model trained on 1 million tokens, exhibited significantly higher perplexity values, indicating a decrease in performance with reduced model size and training data. The variations in perplexity highlight the importance of model size and training data in achieving effective pretraining outcomes.

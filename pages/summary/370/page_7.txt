The study highlights the interplay between linguistic and surface features in pretrained models, revealing that while most models exhibit a tendency towards surface bias, those with sufficient pretraining can shift towards linguistic generalization with adequate inoculation. Notably, RoBERTa BASE is the only model that consistently shows a linguistic bias without inoculation, although it still leans towards surface features. The findings suggest that model size and pretraining data significantly influence the ability to generalize linguistically, with larger models requiring less inoculation to achieve this transition. The paper concludes that overall, pretrained models encode both linguistic and surface features, but their performance varies based on the model size and training conditions.

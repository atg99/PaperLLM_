This list of references includes a variety of significant works in the fields of deep learning, natural language processing, and cognitive science. Key contributions include Battaglia et al.'s exploration of relational inductive biases and graph networks, Brown et al.'s findings on few-shot learning in language models, and Devlin et al.'s introduction of BERT, which has revolutionized language understanding. These works collectively advance the understanding of machine learning algorithms and their applications in linguistic tasks, showcasing the ongoing evolution in the integration of neural networks with cognitive principles.

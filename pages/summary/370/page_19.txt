The paper presents results from mixed binary classification tasks that analyze various linguistic features, including absolute position, morphology, length, lexical content, relative position, orthography, syntactic category, syntactic position, and syntactic construction. The results are illustrated through graphical representations (Figures 10 and 11) showcasing performance with different sizes of inoculation data (0.3% and 1%). Each feature is assessed across varying base sizes (1M, 10M, 100M, and 1B), providing insights into how these factors influence classification outcomes. The figures indicate that the classification performance varies significantly with changes in inoculation data and the specific linguistic features analyzed.

The paper discusses the systematic review of clustering techniques and their applications, outlining the methodology for article inclusion and exclusion based on set criteria. A total of 347 relevant articles were selected after thorough evaluation of titles, abstracts, conclusions, and full content. The authors compare their survey with existing literature, highlighting gaps in previous reviews that often fail to encompass the latest algorithms or provide a comprehensive taxonomical analysis of clustering methods.

The taxonomy of clustering algorithms is categorized into Hierarchical and Partitional methods. Hierarchical clustering is further divided into agglomerative and divisive approaches, which create a dendrogram illustrating the clusters' structure. The merging or splitting of clusters is guided by proximity measures known as linkage metrics, with three primary types—single linkage, average linkage, and complete linkage—each affecting cluster shapes differently.

In summary, the paper aims to deliver an updated and comprehensive overview of clustering algorithms, addressing the limitations of past surveys and emphasizing the significance of clustering techniques in data mining and knowledge acquisition.

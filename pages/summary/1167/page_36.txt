The paper by A.E. Ezugwu et al. published in Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence discusses the prevalent use of clustering algorithms such as C-Means and Hierarchical clustering in research. It highlights that many clustering algorithms, particularly nature-inspired ones, remain underexplored, suggesting a need for further research. The authors propose that future studies should examine the adaptability of clustering algorithms across various domains and analyze the relationship between the challenges faced in these domains and the properties of different algorithms. They also encourage the exploration of newer or hybrid clustering algorithms based on trends identified in their survey. This work serves as a valuable reference for researchers and practitioners aiming to develop more efficient and advanced clustering algorithms. The authors declare no competing interests regarding this research.

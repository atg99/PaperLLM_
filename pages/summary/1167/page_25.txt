In the paper, Ezugwu et al. discuss various cluster validation measures essential for evaluating the quality of clusters obtained through different clustering methods. They highlight the importance of these measures, as the effectiveness of clustering can be influenced by the parameters and initial conditions of the algorithms used.

The authors categorize cluster validation criteria into internal, external, and relative validation. Internal validation focuses on assessing the compactness within clusters and the separation between clusters after the clustering process. They present several internal validation metrics:

1. **Sum of Squared Error (SSE)**: Measures the total deviation of data points from their respective cluster centroids, with a lower SSE indicating better clustering.
2. **Scatter Criteria**: Evaluates the spread of data points within each cluster.
3. **Condorcet’s Criterion**: Assesses the similarity and distance between data points within and across clusters.
4. **C-Criterion**: An extension of Condorcet’s criterion that introduces a threshold to enhance validity assessment.
5. **Category Utility Metric**: Evaluates the utility of clustering based on the probabilities of features within categories.
6. **Bayesian Information Criterion (BIC)**: Addresses overfitting by penalizing complex models.
7. **Calinski–Harabasz Index**: Measures cluster compactness and separation based on scatter matrices.
8. **Davies–Bouldin Index (DB)**: Evaluates average inter-cluster similarity, where a lower value indicates better clustering.
9. **Silhouette Index**: Measures how similar an object is to its own cluster compared to other clusters.
10. **Dunn Index**: Focuses on the ratio of the smallest inter-cluster distance to the largest intra-cluster distance.
11. **NIVA Index**: A newer metric that evaluates cluster compactness relative to separation.

These measures provide a comprehensive framework for analyzing clustering outcomes, helping to ensure that the identified patterns are meaningful and robust against noise.

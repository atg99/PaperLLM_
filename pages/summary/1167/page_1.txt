The paper titled "A comprehensive survey of clustering algorithms: State-of-the-art machine learning applications, taxonomy, challenges, and future research prospects" provides an extensive review of clustering techniques utilized in data mining across various fields such as computer science, statistics, and artificial intelligence. The authors, Absalom E. Ezugwu et al., highlight the significance of clustering as a method for grouping data based on similarities in features.

Clustering techniques are categorized into two primary groups: hierarchical and partitional methods. While these methods have demonstrated effectiveness, they often require prior knowledge of the number of clusters, which poses challenges in real-world applications where such information is typically unavailable. To address this limitation, the concept of automatic clustering algorithms is introduced, which aims to determine the optimal number of clusters without prior input.

The survey emphasizes the importance of clustering in diverse domains including education, marketing, medicine, biology, and bioinformatics, and its applications in emerging fields like big data, artificial intelligence, and robotics. The authors stress the need for more flexible and efficient clustering techniques to enhance performance in high-dimensional datasets and complex clustering scenarios. This comprehensive review serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners aiming to develop advanced clustering algorithms and improve existing methodologies.

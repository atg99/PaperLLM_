In clustering applications, the WOA algorithm operates by simulating the foraging behavior of whales to explore the solution space effectively. It utilizes a population of candidate solutions that represent potential clusters and iteratively updates their positions based on their own experiences and those of their peers. The algorithm balances exploration and exploitation to avoid local optima, ultimately seeking to improve clustering accuracy.

The paper outlines various algorithms, including Genetic Algorithm (GA), Ant Colony Optimization (ACO), Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO), and Whale Optimization Algorithm (WOA), each demonstrating unique mechanisms inspired by natural processes. GA focuses on evolving solutions through selection, crossover, and mutation, while ACO leverages the social behaviors of ants for clustering. PSO employs swarm intelligence to optimize clustering centroids, and WOA mimics the foraging tactics of humpback whales. The effectiveness of these algorithms is evidenced by numerous studies highlighting their capabilities in achieving optimal clustering solutions across diverse datasets.

The paper by Ezugwu et al. provides a comprehensive survey of clustering algorithms, highlighting their applications across various fields. It categorizes these algorithms based on design concepts such as graph theory, fuzzy set theory, and neural networks, among others. Notable studies are summarized, including Xu and Wunsch's work on various clustering algorithms like Fuzzy-Cmeans and BIRCH, which has a significant impact on big data clustering. Adil et al. discuss traditional and modern algorithms, while Rasmussen focuses on hierarchical methods for information retrieval. Aggarwal and Zhai examine text clustering challenges, and Silva et al. review data stream clustering algorithms applicable to network intrusion detection and sensor networks. Nanda and Panda explore nature-inspired algorithms for various applications, including gene expression analysis and tourism market segmentation. Liao's study emphasizes time series clustering across diverse domains. Lastly, Hruschka et al. provide a taxonomy of evolutionary algorithms for clustering, addressing advanced topics relevant to image processing and bioinformatics. Each referenced work contributes to the understanding and development of clustering methodologies in their respective areas.

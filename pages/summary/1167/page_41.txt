The paper discusses various clustering algorithms and their applications across different fields, highlighting advancements in techniques such as K-means, particle swarm optimization, and fuzzy clustering. It reviews the effectiveness of these methods in handling complex datasets, including gene expression data, image segmentation, and financial forecasting. The authors emphasize the importance of selecting appropriate clustering methods based on the nature of the data and the desired outcomes. They also explore the development of hybrid approaches that combine multiple algorithms for improved performance in clustering tasks. Overall, the paper serves as a comprehensive overview of contemporary clustering strategies and their relevance in big data analytics.

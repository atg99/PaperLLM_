The paper by Ezugwu et al. provides an extensive overview of various clustering algorithms, categorizing them into traditional and modern approaches. It highlights the challenges of determining the optimal number of clusters without prior knowledge of dataset characteristics. The authors reference numerous surveys that have previously examined clustering techniques, noting key contributions from researchers such as Xu and Wunsch, who classified algorithms based on their operational elements and applications in different fields.

The authors further discuss the significance of the three V's of Big Data—Volume, Variety, and Velocity—in evaluating clustering algorithms, as presented by Benabdellah et al. They emphasize the need for efficient algorithms capable of managing large datasets and high-dimensional data, while also effectively identifying complex cluster shapes. The paper serves as a systematic study aimed at both traditional and recent clustering techniques, providing insights into their applications and performance across various domains.

The paper discusses Bayesian Optimization (BO) as a superior alternative to traditional optimization methods like grid search and random search. It highlights two main advantages of BO: it does not require prior knowledge of hyper-parameter distributions and utilizes posterior probability to enhance decision-making. The BO process involves creating a surrogate probability model based on previous trials, which is continuously updated to identify the most promising hyper-parameter set. This method is noted for its computational efficiency, requiring fewer trials to find optimal hyper-parameters, especially beneficial when dealing with expensive objective functions. Additionally, BO is versatile, applicable to various types of objective functions, whether stochastic, discrete, convex, or nonconvex. The section concludes with an overview of the Bayesian algorithm, its variants, and recent applications in deep learning.

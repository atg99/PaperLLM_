The paper by Yu and Zhu discusses the challenges and features of the Neural Network Intelligence (NNI) toolkit, particularly focusing on debugging and algorithm support. It highlights that the debugging process could be improved by providing more detailed log information, especially during failures of the NNI manager or dispatcher. NNI supports a variety of advanced search algorithms but lacks support for population-based algorithms like Population Based Training (PBT), which efficiently combines hyper-parameter tuning with model training for large-scale models.

Additionally, the paper introduces Ray.Tune, an open-source toolkit for hyper-parameter search developed by Berkeley RISELab. Ray.Tune is distinguished as a distributed framework that ensures efficient resource allocation and allows for parallel execution of search methods. While both NNI and Ray.Tune serve similar purposes, they differ significantly. NNI is a comprehensive toolkit for both AutoML and hyper-parameter optimization (HPO), while Ray.Tune is specifically focused on HPO as part of the Ray library. Notably, Ray.Tune supports PBT, which is a significant advantage over NNI. Furthermore, Ray.Tune relies on existing libraries for implementing search algorithms, whereas NNI’s developers have rewritten many algorithms, which may require users to install additional packages for certain functionalities.

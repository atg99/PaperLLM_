The paper reviews Hyper-Parameter Optimization (HPO) algorithms and their applications, focusing on the Neural Network Intelligence (NNI) framework. NNI categorizes its components into Tuners and Assessors. Tuners, including grid search, random search, and TPE, function as search algorithms, while Assessors, which encompass early stopping algorithms like HB and BOHB, complement the tuning process. NNI supports the integration of custom algorithms, allowing users to create and configure their own Tuners and Assessors easily.

NNI's versatility extends to deployment across various infrastructures, including local machines and Kubernetes-based environments. It is compatible with major deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch, Keras, TensorFlow, and others, enhancing its usability for researchers and developers.

The framework features a visualization interface called Web UI, which provides comprehensive monitoring of HPO experiments, displaying vital information such as experiment status, search space, and trial comparisons. While it offers a user-friendly experience, the functionality of Web UI is limited compared to competitors like Google Vizier, as it lacks interactivity for adjusting hyper-parameters or configurations directly.

Despite its strengths, NNI has drawbacks. It is designed to be universal, accommodating various tasks and frameworks, which may pose challenges for beginners lacking model training experience. Additionally, while the framework is continually updated with new algorithms and platforms, the Web UI's capabilities could be expanded for improved user interaction.

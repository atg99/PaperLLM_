This paper provides a comprehensive review of hyper-parameter optimization (HPO) algorithms and their applications, emphasizing the significance of HPO in the context of deep neural networks. The authors, Tong Yu and Hong Zhu, highlight that while deep learning has profoundly impacted various aspects of daily life, the design and training processes remain complex and unpredictable. To address this challenge, automated HPO has gained traction in both academic and industrial settings.

The paper begins by identifying key hyper-parameters that influence model training and structure, discussing their criticality and methods for defining their value ranges. It then delves into major optimization algorithms used for HPO, evaluating their efficiency and accuracy, particularly in deep learning contexts. Additionally, the authors review various HPO services and toolkits, comparing their support for advanced searching algorithms and their compatibility with prominent deep learning frameworks.

The conclusion addresses existing challenges in applying HPO to deep learning, compares different optimization algorithms, and presents notable approaches for model evaluation, especially when computational resources are limited. The overall aim of the paper is to enhance understanding and accessibility of HPO techniques for users engaged in deep learning projects.

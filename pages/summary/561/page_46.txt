Yu and Zhu discuss the role of annotative experts in selecting hyperparameters within the AutoML context. Desautels et al. focus on the parallelization of exploration-exploitation trade-offs in Gaussian process bandit optimization, emphasizing efficiency in optimization processes.

DeVries and Taylor present an improved regularization method for convolutional neural networks through the cutout technique, enhancing model robustness. Chuong B Do provides a foundational overview of Gaussian processes, which are pivotal in machine learning applications. Domhan et al. propose a method for accelerating hyperparameter optimization through the extrapolation of learning curves, contributing to the speed of model training.

Duchi et al. introduce adaptive subgradient methods tailored for online learning and stochastic optimization, which are essential for dynamic environments. Duvenaud's PhD thesis explores automatic model construction using Gaussian processes, showcasing their versatility in modeling complex data.

Eberhart and Shi compare genetic algorithms with particle swarm optimization, shedding light on evolutionary computation methods. Eggensperger et al. work towards establishing empirical foundations for Bayesian optimization of hyperparameters, while also providing efficient benchmarking techniques via surrogate models.

Falkner et al. introduce BOHB, a robust and scalable approach to hyperparameter optimization, demonstrating significant advancements in the field. Fasshauer and McCourt detail kernel-based approximation methods, which are critical for various computational applications. Lastly, Fernández-Godino et al. review multi-fidelity models, highlighting their importance in optimizing resource-intensive simulations. Feurer and Hutter offer insights into hyperparameter optimization within the broader context of automated machine learning, reinforcing its significance in enhancing model performance.

The paper provides a comprehensive review of hyper-parameter optimization (HPO) algorithms and their applications in both research and industry. It discusses the challenges associated with tuning hyper-parameters, particularly for complex models and new architectures, which require significant time and resources for effective exploration. The necessity for automated HPO tools is emphasized, especially for less experienced users who need guidance in selecting appropriate hyper-parameters.

The study aims to summarize existing HPO algorithms, compare leading tools, and identify challenges in deep learning network optimization. The paper is organized into several sections: 

1. **Key Hyper-Parameters**: This section discusses essential hyper-parameters for neural network training, their influence on model performance, and potential search spaces.
2. **HPO Algorithms**: Various algorithms for hyper-parameter searching are categorized into searching algorithms and trial schedulers, with an evaluation of their efficiency and applicability across different machine learning models.
3. **HPO Toolkits**: An overview of mainstream HPO toolkits and services is provided, comparing their advantages and disadvantages along with practical implementation details.
4. **Comparison of HPO Methods**: A detailed comparison of existing methods highlights efficient model evaluation techniques.
5. **Conclusions**: The study wraps up with key findings.

The main contributions include the systematic categorization of hyper-parameters, an in-depth analysis of HPO algorithms regarding accuracy and efficiency, and insights into the design and target users of various HPO toolkits. This review serves as a valuable resource for both researchers and practitioners seeking to enhance their understanding and application of hyper-parameter optimization in deep learning.

The references listed include significant contributions to the fields of adaptive learning rates and neural architecture search. 

The paper by Zeiler presents Adadelta, an adaptive learning rate method that improves upon previous techniques by eliminating the need for a manually set learning rate and allowing for a more efficient optimization process. This method adjusts the learning rates based on a moving window of gradient updates, leading to improved performance in various machine learning tasks.

Zoph and Le's work focuses on neural architecture search utilizing reinforcement learning. Their approach automates the design of neural networks by treating architecture design as a reinforcement learning problem, allowing the model to explore and optimize different architectures based on performance metrics. This research has paved the way for advancements in automated machine learning and the development of more efficient neural networks.

Both papers contribute to enhancing the optimization and design of machine learning models, showcasing innovative methods that have influenced subsequent research in the field.

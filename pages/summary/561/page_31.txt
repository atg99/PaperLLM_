The section on hyper-parameter optimization (HPO) toolkits highlights the significant challenges in training deep learning networks, which can be time-intensive and resource-consuming. The paper categorizes HPO toolkits into two main types: open-source tools and cloud-based services. Open-source libraries like HyperOpt, HpBandSter, Xcessive, and Scikit-Optimize cater to various automatic model design tasks, each with unique features and limitations. For instance, HyperOpt focuses on asynchronous Bayesian optimization, while Xcessive offers a user-friendly graphical interface but is limited to Bayesian methods. Despite their advantages, these libraries often struggle with deep learning frameworks and trial scheduling.

On the other hand, cloud services like Google Cloud and Amazon Web Services provide robust HPO capabilities backed by extensive computational resources, enabling parallel training processes. However, these are closed-source and require payment for usage. The design of HPO tools considers factors such as ease of use, state-of-the-art performance, availability, scalability, and flexibility. A comparative analysis in the paper outlines these considerations, setting the groundwork for further discussions on individual toolkits.

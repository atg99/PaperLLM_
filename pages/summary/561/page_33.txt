The paper reviews hyper-parameter optimization (HPO) algorithms and applications, focusing on Google Vizier as a leading service in this field. Vizier is designed to manage millions of experiments and billions of trials efficiently across Google data centers, utilizing load-balancing infrastructure and robust error correction to maintain job stability. A notable feature is its implementation of transfer learning, which accelerates tuning by fine-tuning models based on previous user submissions, particularly beneficial for deep learning due to high training costs.

The benchmarking suite included in Vizier measures algorithm efficiency using performance-over-time metrics. The user-friendly graphical user interface (GUI) allows users to monitor experiments and adjust settings without requiring command-line knowledge, making it accessible for beginners. While Vizier excels in ease of use, it does not outperform other libraries in search algorithms or early stopping strategies. It supports various parameter types and employs batched Gaussian process bandit optimization as the default mode, along with early stopping algorithms like Curve Stopping Rule and Median Stopping Rule.

Recent studies, such as those by DeepMind, have demonstrated the feasibility of implementing new optimization algorithms within Vizier, enhancing the tuning process. Vizier is designed to minimize setup efforts, allowing users to start experiments quickly with suggested algorithms and default settings. Advanced users have the option to customize algorithms, though some may find flexibility limited.

The paper also mentions Advisor, an open-source alternative to Vizier that incorporates many of its features but lacks the scalability due to resource constraints. Advisor supports grid search, random search, and Bayesian optimization, along with an interactive GUI for ease of use. However, it cannot match the extensive capabilities of Vizier, particularly in scalability. Overall, Vizier stands out as a comprehensive HPO service, especially for users seeking a streamlined experience with minimal effort.

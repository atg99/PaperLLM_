3.2 Trial Schedulers
Trial schedulers play a critical role in optimizing hyper-parameter tuning by implementing early stopping strategies. These strategies are crucial for managing resources effectively during the training process. By halting trials that are unlikely to yield competitive results, trial schedulers ensure that computational resources are utilized efficiently.

In summary, the paper outlines the objective function for hyper-parameter optimization (HPO) and classifies state-of-the-art algorithms into search algorithms and trial schedulers. It discusses grid search as a straightforward but resource-intensive method, particularly susceptible to the curse of dimensionality. Random search is presented as an improvement over grid search, offering better results in high-dimensional spaces. The importance of trial schedulers in implementing early stopping strategies is also highlighted, emphasizing their role in resource management during the tuning process.

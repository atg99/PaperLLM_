The paper reviews various hyper-parameter optimization algorithms, focusing on their mechanisms and effectiveness. It highlights the differences between Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) and RMSprop, noting that RMSprop mitigates vertical oscillations while enhancing horizontal movement by adjusting the learning rate (LR) based on the root of the squared gradient. The algorithm employs exponentially weighted averages of squared gradients, leading to a slower adjustment in the bias direction and a faster one in the weight direction.

Additionally, the paper discusses the Adam optimizer, which combines the principles of momentum and RMSprop. Adam incorporates bias-correction and maintains an exponential decaying average of past gradients, allowing it to outperform RMSprop in later stages of optimization. Although Adam has more hyper-parameters than other optimizers, it is recommended as the default due to its effectiveness with minimal tuning, particularly concerning the learning rate. The insights presented underline the importance of selecting appropriate optimization algorithms for efficient deep learning training.

This paper reviews hyper-parameter optimization, focusing on algorithms and their applications. It emphasizes the significance of understanding hyper-parameters through sensitivity tests to gauge their importance. Initial values are crucial, particularly for learning rates (LR), which can start large to facilitate rapid convergence but should decay over time. The paper suggests using a logarithmic scale for updating LRs, advocating for exponential decay as a preferable method, although it acknowledges that different schedules may be more suitable depending on the specific model and dataset.

The section on optimizers highlights their pivotal role in enhancing both accuracy and training speed. It discusses key hyper-parameters associated with optimizers, including the choice of optimizer, mini-batch size, momentum, and beta values. The paper reviews commonly used optimizers such as mini-batch gradient descent, RMSprop, and Adam, and provides guidance on their appropriate configurations.

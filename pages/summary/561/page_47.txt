The paper reviews various algorithms and applications related to hyper-parameter optimization, a crucial aspect of machine learning that significantly impacts model performance. It discusses automated machine learning approaches, highlighting the work of Feurer et al. on efficient and robust methods. The review encompasses Bayesian optimization techniques, notably Frazier's tutorial, which emphasizes the knowledge-gradient policy, effective for correlated normal beliefs. It also addresses challenges in optimizing integer-valued variables and the suitability of Gaussian processes in parallel optimization tasks, as demonstrated by Ginsbourger et al.

The paper references several notable contributions in the field, including Google's Vizier system for black-box optimization and advancements in deep learning through generative adversarial networks. Additionally, it mentions the importance of sensitivity analysis in environmental modeling, showcasing the diverse applications of hyper-parameter optimization techniques across various domains. The work collectively underscores the significance of developing efficient optimization strategies to improve machine learning outcomes.

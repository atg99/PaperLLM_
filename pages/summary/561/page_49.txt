The paper provides a comprehensive review of hyper-parameter optimization algorithms and their applications. It highlights the significance of hyper-parameter tuning in enhancing the performance of machine learning models. Key methodologies discussed include sequential model-based optimization, parallel algorithm configuration, and population-based training of neural networks. The authors reference foundational works such as the No Free Lunch theorems, which outline limitations of metaheuristic approaches, and emphasize the efficiency of global optimization techniques for expensive black-box functions.

The review also covers practical strategies for hyper-parameter tuning, such as grid search and non-stochastic best arm identification, and discusses recent advancements in neural architecture search and automatic feature generation. The paper underscores the importance of these techniques in improving model accuracy and efficiency, while also addressing the exploration-exploitation trade-off in multi-armed bandit scenarios.

Overall, the paper serves as a valuable resource for understanding the landscape of hyper-parameter optimization, providing insights into various algorithms and their practical implications in machine learning.

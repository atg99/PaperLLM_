The paper presents a comprehensive review of hyper-parameter optimization (HPO) algorithms and their applications, emphasizing the significance of hyper-parameters in enhancing the performance of machine learning models. Various optimization techniques are discussed, including traditional methods like grid search and random search, as well as advanced strategies such as Bayesian optimization, Hyperband, and population-based training.

Key contributions of the reviewed literature include the development of efficient algorithms that can handle large-scale hyper-parameter tuning, such as Massively Parallel Hyperparameter Tuning and Tune, which facilitate distributed model selection and training. The paper also highlights innovative approaches like particle swarm optimization for selecting hyper-parameters in deep neural networks and the impact of mini-batch training on stochastic optimization.

The authors address the challenges associated with HPO, including the computational cost and the curse of dimensionality, advocating for the use of bandit-based methods and other adaptive strategies to mitigate these issues. The review consolidates insights from various studies, underscoring the evolution of HPO techniques and their practical implications in machine learning and deep learning contexts. Ultimately, the paper serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners seeking to optimize model performance through effective hyper-parameter tuning strategies.

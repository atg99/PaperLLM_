In this study, the authors utilized PyTorch and CUDA to train models for pavement crack detection on a single NVIDIA GTX 1080 Ti GPU using Ubuntu as the platform. A comparative analysis was conducted between Faster R-CNN and YOLO v3, revealing that Faster R-CNN outperformed YOLO v3 in terms of detection accuracy. YOLO v3 was found to be less effective for the specific task, especially with limited data, and required longer training times.

The authors also explored the impact of different learning rates on the performance of Faster R-CNN and Mask R-CNN. Five learning rates were tested: 0.02, 0.01, 0.005, 0.0025, and 0.0002. The evaluation showed that accuracy and loss remained relatively stable with minor adjustments to the learning rates. Both models maintained an accuracy around 98, with Faster R-CNN exhibiting a loss of approximately 0.25 and Mask R-CNN around 0.4. However, a learning rate of 0.0002 led to poorer results, indicating potential overfitting.

The paper included visual representations of precision-recall curves and accuracy-loss plots to illustrate the comparative performance of the models under different settings. Overall, the findings suggest that joint training strategies and careful tuning of learning rates are crucial for optimizing model performance in crack detection tasks.

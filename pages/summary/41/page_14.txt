The paper presents a comparison of two deep learning models, Faster R-CNN and Mask R-CNN, in detecting straight and bending cracks in images. Figure 10 illustrates the detection results for both models under varying learning rates, categorized into four groups. Specifically, Figure 10a shows the Faster R-CNN model detecting laterally biased cracks, while Figure 10b demonstrates its effectiveness in identifying curved cracks. Similarly, Figure 10c illustrates Mask R-CNN's performance on straight cracks, and Figure 10d highlights its detection of bending cracks. The learning rates applied in these experiments are progressively reduced, ranging from 0.02 to 0.0002. This comparative analysis aims to evaluate the models' capabilities in accurately identifying different types of cracks based on the chosen learning rates.

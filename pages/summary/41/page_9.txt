The paper investigates the performance of Faster R-CNN and Mask R-CNN in detecting single and bifurcation cracks under varying learning rates. The results indicate that Faster R-CNN is more effective for detecting single cracks, especially when using a higher score threshold. For bifurcation cracks, the model's sensitivity to learning rate changes affects detection, with transverse cracks being more reliably detected than vertical or branch cracks. 

Mask R-CNN also shows better results for single cracks but struggles with bifurcation cracks as the learning rate decreases, leading to overfitting at very low rates. When comparing the impact of sunlight interference on crack detection, both algorithms perform better under well-lit conditions. Faster R-CNN generally outperforms Mask R-CNN in detecting cracks with sunlight interference, particularly at higher learning rates. Overall, both algorithms effectively identify single cracks, but complex bifurcated cracks require more extensive training data for optimal results.

The paper discusses the implementation of Mask R-CNN, a neural network architecture for object detection and segmentation, and its loss function, which is composed of three components: classification loss (L_cls), regression loss (L_reg), and mask loss (L_mask). The mask loss is specifically designed for the mask branches added in Mask R-CNN, where each Region of Interest (ROI) produces K binary masks corresponding to K categories. The calculation of L_mask utilizes the sigmoid function on pixel values, focusing only on positive samples defined by an Intersection over Union (IOU) greater than 0.5. The mask loss is similar to the classification loss but is calculated at the pixel level rather than the image level.

The paper also includes a comparative analysis of accuracy and loss between Faster R-CNN and Mask R-CNN across different learning rates (0.02, 0.01, 0.005, 0.0025, and 0.0002), illustrated in a series of figures. Furthermore, it presents four distinct sets of results for performance evaluation: comparisons of single versus bifurcated cracks, cracks with and without sunlight interference, deep versus shallow cracks, and straight versus curved cracks. This analysis aims to demonstrate the effectiveness of the Mask R-CNN architecture in various scenarios.

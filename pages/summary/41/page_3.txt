The paper discusses the application of Faster R-CNN and Mask R-CNN for road crack detection, emphasizing the effectiveness of these methods in this specific domain. The authors employed a joint training strategy that incorporates pre-training and migration learning to expedite the training process, achieving satisfactory results with over 130 images.

The methodology is outlined in three main components: 

1. **Feature Extraction**: ResNet and FPN were utilized to extract features, generating five feature maps of varying sizes for further processing.
2. **Region Proposal Networks (RPNs)**: Five identical RPNs created region proposals by generating anchors of different sizes based on the feature maps.
3. **Region CNN (R-CNN)**: The R-CNN component standardizes the feature maps for classification and regression, while Mask R-CNN includes an additional fully convolutional layer to facilitate instance segmentation.

The paper highlights two key distinctions between Mask R-CNN and Faster R-CNN: the integration of the mask component for instance segmentation and the implementation of RoIAlign, which addresses feature map misalignment issues. Overall, the research validates the efficacy of these models in the context of road crack detection, filling a gap in existing literature on the subject.

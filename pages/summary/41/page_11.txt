The paper presents a study on road crack detection using two models: Faster R-CNN and Mask R-CNN. It evaluates the effectiveness of these models under varying conditions, specifically comparing detection results with and without sunlight interference. The results indicate that both models performed well in detecting cracks, regardless of sunlight interference. Figure 8 illustrates the detection outcomes, showcasing four scenarios: Faster R-CNN detecting cracks with and without sunlight interference, and Mask R-CNN detecting cracks under the same conditions. The findings suggest that both models are robust for crack detection, contributing to improved road maintenance strategies.

The paper discusses the implementation of Faster R-CNN and Mask R-CNN for detecting pavement cracks using a dataset of 148 smartphone images. The dataset is divided into 90% training and 10% testing data, featuring various types of cracks. Data augmentation is applied through random horizontal and vertical flips, increasing the dataset size by 50%. The paper outlines the loss functions for both Faster R-CNN and Mask R-CNN, emphasizing a joint training strategy that incorporates a mask component for Mask R-CNN. 

The visualization includes a comparison of RoI Pooling and RoI Align, with RoI Align utilizing bilinear interpolation to enhance accuracy. The training process employs a backbone based on ImageNet pre-training, with five different learning rates and an SGD optimizer. Batch normalization and warm-up techniques are also integrated to improve training efficiency. Input image dimensions are set at 800 by 500 pixels, balancing training speed and GPU memory limitations. The labeling method for cracks is illustrated, contrasting rectangular labeling by Faster R-CNN with polygonal labeling by Mask R-CNN.

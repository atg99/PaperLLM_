The paper analyzes the performance of Faster R-CNN and Mask R-CNN in terms of learning rates and loss functions. It finds that Faster R-CNN consistently exhibits lower and more stable loss values compared to Mask R-CNN, with losses around 0.25 and 0.4 respectively. The study highlights that Mask R-CNN, which includes an additional output for object masks, has a more complex loss function comprising three components: classification loss, bounding box regression loss, and mask loss. The mask loss is calculated only for positive samples, defined by an Intersection over Union (IOU) greater than 0.5, and is similar to classification loss but operates on pixel points. 

Figures presented in the paper illustrate the accuracy and loss comparisons across different learning rates for both models. Initially, Mask R-CNN shows higher accuracy, but as iterations increase, Faster R-CNN surpasses it significantly, particularly at learning rates of 0.02 and 0.01. The results indicate that while Mask R-CNN's performance can be competitive at certain points, Faster R-CNN generally maintains better stability and lower loss throughout the training process.

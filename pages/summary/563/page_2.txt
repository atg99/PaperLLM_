The introduction of the paper highlights the importance of machine learning (ML) algorithms across various domains such as advertising, recommendation systems, and natural language processing. It emphasizes that effectively building an ML model is a complex task that involves selecting the right algorithm and tuning hyper-parameters (HPs) to achieve optimal model performance.

The paper distinguishes between model parameters, which can be updated during the learning process, and hyper-parameters, which must be set prior to training. Hyper-parameters play a crucial role in defining the model's architecture and the algorithm used to minimize the loss function. The process of hyper-parameter tuning is essential for developing effective ML models, particularly for tree-based models and deep neural networks that involve numerous hyper-parameters.

The paper notes that hyper-parameter tuning varies by ML algorithm due to the different types of hyper-parameters involved. Although manual tuning remains common, particularly in academic research, it is often inadequate due to challenges such as numerous hyper-parameters, model complexity, time-intensive evaluations, and intricate interactions among hyper-parameters. This sets the stage for exploring optimization techniques such as particle swarm optimization, genetic algorithms, and grid search for improving hyper-parameter tuning efficiency.

For optimizing these types of algorithms with conditional hyper-parameters, Bayesian optimization with conditional relationships (BO-C) is recommended. This approach efficiently navigates the dependency among hyper-parameters, allowing for a more targeted search in the hyper-parameter space. 

In summary, the choice of hyper-parameter optimization (HPO) algorithms should align with the specific characteristics of the machine learning models being utilized. For models with one discrete hyper-parameter, Bayesian optimization or Hyperband is ideal. For those with one continuous hyper-parameter, BO-GP is preferred, while gradient-based methods may be less effective. Finally, for models with conditional hyper-parameters, BO-C is the most suitable option to account for interdependencies. This strategic selection of HPO methods can significantly enhance the efficiency of model tuning.

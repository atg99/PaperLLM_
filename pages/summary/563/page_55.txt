The paper discusses the importance of hyper-parameter optimization (HPO) in machine learning (ML) as data evolves. It highlights that optimal hyper-parameter values change with new data, yet continuous tuning has not received much attention as practitioners often do not modify models after achieving satisfactory performance. The need for automated HPO methods is emphasized, particularly due to the increasing volume of data and the inefficiency of manual tuning.

The conclusion outlines that ML is essential for addressing data-related challenges and requires precise hyper-parameter tuning for effective application. The paper reviews current research in HPO, focusing on the selection of appropriate optimization methods based on hyper-parameter types in ML models. It recommends Bayesian optimization with Hyper-Band (BOHB) for optimizing models with representative subsets of data, while suggesting Bayesian optimization (BO) for smaller parameter spaces and particle swarm optimization (PSO) for larger spaces.

Additionally, the paper identifies existing HPO tools, highlights open challenges, and suggests future research directions. It aims to serve as a resource for ML practitioners and researchers, enhancing their understanding of HPO and its implications for advancing ML applications.

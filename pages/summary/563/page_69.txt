Li Yang earned his Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Western University in August 2022. He also holds a MASc. in Engineering from the University of Guelph (2018) and a B.E. in Computer Science from Wuhan University of Science and Technology (2016). Currently, he is a Postdoctoral Associate and Sessional Lecturer at Western University's Optimized Computing and Communications Lab. His research focuses on various areas, including cybersecurity, machine learning, AutoML, deep learning, network data analytics, Internet of Things (IoT), anomaly detection, online learning, concept drift, and time series data analytics.

Abdallah Shami is a Professor in the Electrical and Computer Engineering Department at Western University, where he serves as the Acting Associate Dean (Research) and Director of the Optimized Computing and Communications Laboratory. He has chaired significant symposia for IEEE GLOBECOM and other conferences and held leadership roles in the IEEE Communications Society. He is currently an Associate Editor for several IEEE journals, including IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing and IEEE Internet of Things.

The paper discusses the configuration space for hyper-parameters in various machine learning models, as detailed in Table 3. It outlines the hyper-parameters for Random Forest (RF) classifiers and regressors, Support Vector Machine (SVM) classifiers and regressors, and K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) classifiers and regressors. The search spaces for these hyper-parameters are determined through a combination of theoretical concepts, domain knowledge, and manual testing.

To ensure a fair comparison of performance metrics across optimization techniques, the maximum number of iterations is standardized: 50 for RF and SVM optimizations and 10 for KNN. Experimentation is repeated ten times with different random seeds to mitigate randomness effects, and results are averaged for regression or determined by majority vote for classification tasks.

The paper introduces over ten hyper-parameter optimization (HPO) methods, but focuses on eight representative approaches for comparative analysis: Grid Search (GS), Random Search (RS), Bayesian Optimization with Gaussian Processes (BO-GP), Bayesian Optimization with Tree-structured Parzen Estimator (BO-TPE), Hyperband, Bayesian Optimization Hyperband (BOHB), Genetic Algorithms (GA), and Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO). The experiments were conducted using Python 3.5 on a specified machine, following the outlined methodology in the paper.

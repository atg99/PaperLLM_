The experimental setup involves applying eight different hyper-parameter optimization (HPO) techniques to three representative machine learning algorithms. The main goal is to evaluate the effectiveness of these techniques in optimizing the algorithms' performance on the selected datasets. The experiments are structured to provide a clear comparison of the various HPO methods, with results analyzed in detail.

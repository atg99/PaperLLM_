The paper discusses various ensemble learning algorithms, particularly focusing on decision tree-based models and their parameters in XGBoost. XGBoost incorporates hyper-parameters such as 'min child weight', 'subsample', 'colsample bytree', 'gamma', 'alpha', 'lambda', and 'learning rate', which help control node weight, subsampling ratios, loss reduction, and regularization. 

In addition to tree-based models, the paper highlights three common ensemble learning methods: voting, bagging, and AdaBoost. Voting combines multiple estimators through majority voting or averaged probabilities, allowing for the adjustment of individual model weights. Bagging, or bootstrap aggregating, trains multiple base estimators on different subsets of data, with 'base estimator' and 'n estimators' being key parameters, along with 'max samples' and 'max features' for subset generation. 

AdaBoost trains multiple weak learners in succession, focusing on misclassified instances from previous learners to create a strong final classifier. The choice of base estimator in AdaBoost can include decision trees or other algorithms. Overall, these ensemble methods aim to enhance model performance beyond single algorithms.

In PSO, a swarm of particles, each represented by a position, velocity, and best-known position, explores the search space. The velocity of each particle is adjusted based on its own best position and the global best position, allowing for efficient navigation towards optimal solutions. The algorithm iterates this process until it converges or meets termination criteria. PSO is noted for its ease of implementation, faster convergence speed, and lower computational complexity compared to GA, which requires more complex operations like crossover and mutation.

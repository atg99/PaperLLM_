Current HPO approaches often do not account for the dynamic nature of real-world data, leading to suboptimal performance as the underlying data distribution shifts. Future research should focus on developing HPO algorithms with continuous updating capabilities, allowing them to effectively respond to evolving datasets.

In summary, the paper discusses several limitations and future directions for hyper-parameter optimization in machine learning. It highlights the challenges of overfitting, the impact of randomness in achieving optimal configurations, and the scalability issues of existing frameworks. Furthermore, it emphasizes the necessity for HPO methods to adapt to non-stationary datasets. Addressing these challenges will enhance the effectiveness and applicability of HPO techniques in diverse machine learning contexts.

The cited works focus on various methods and approaches for optimizing hyperparameters in machine learning models, particularly in the context of educational data mining and broader machine learning applications. 

Injadat et al. (2020) present a multi-split optimized bagging ensemble model selection tailored for multi-class educational data mining, emphasizing the importance of model selection in educational contexts. Claesen et al. (2014) introduce Optunity, a tool for simplifying hyperparameter search, which aids practitioners in effectively tuning their models. Witt (2005) discusses the performance of randomized search heuristics in both worst-case and average-case scenarios, highlighting their applicability in optimization problems.

Bengio (2000) explores gradient-based optimization techniques for hyperparameters, while Yang and Amari (1998) address complexity issues in training multilayer perceptrons using natural gradient descent methods. In the realm of Bayesian optimization, Snoek et al. (2012) demonstrate practical applications for machine learning algorithms, and Hazan et al. (2017) propose a spectral approach to hyperparameter optimization.

Seeger (2004) provides insights into the use of Gaussian processes for machine learning, which are further explored by Hutter et al. (2011) in the context of sequential model-based optimization for algorithm configuration. Dewancker et al. (2015) offer a primer on Bayesian optimization, making it accessible for practitioners. Lastly, Hensman et al. (2013) discuss the application of Gaussian processes in handling big data, emphasizing their scalability and effectiveness in modern machine learning challenges. 

Together, these works underscore the evolving methodologies for hyperparameter optimization, which are critical for enhancing model performance across diverse applications.

This paper explores hyper-parameter optimization (HPO) techniques, particularly for machine learning (ML) models with extensive configuration spaces, such as deep neural networks (DNNs). It highlights the efficiency of HPO algorithms in improving model performance while also addressing their computational complexity. 

The paper presents several key contributions:
1. A review of common ML algorithms and their significant hyper-parameters.
2. An analysis of various HPO techniques, detailing their benefits and drawbacks to facilitate appropriate algorithm selection based on specific ML models and problems.
3. A survey of available HPO libraries and frameworks for practical implementation.
4. A discussion on ongoing challenges and future research directions in the HPO domain.

The structure of the paper includes:
- An introduction to mathematical optimization and the HPO process.
- A discussion of key hyper-parameters in common ML models.
- An overview of state-of-the-art optimization approaches for HPO.
- An analysis of different HPO methods and their applicability to ML algorithms.
- A presentation of experimental results comparing HPO methods on benchmark datasets.
- A discussion on research directions and challenges in improving HPO models.

The paper concludes with a summary of findings and insights into enhancing current HPO strategies.

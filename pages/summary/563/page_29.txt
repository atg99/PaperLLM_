4.4.2. Hyperband
Hyperband builds on the concept of successive halving by introducing a more adaptive budgeting strategy. It dynamically allocates resources to configurations based on their performance across multiple rounds. Hyperband allows for a more flexible exploration of hyper-parameter configurations by using a combination of random sampling and successive halving, enabling it to efficiently balance exploration and exploitation. This makes Hyperband particularly effective in tackling the challenges of high-dimensional hyper-parameter spaces and varying resource constraints.

In summary, multi-fidelity optimization algorithms, including successive halving and Hyperband, address the challenges of high execution times and resource constraints in hyper-parameter optimization. They do so by employing strategies that prioritize the evaluation of promising configurations while discarding underperforming ones, ultimately leading to more efficient optimization processes.

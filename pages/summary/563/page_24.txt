Random search offers a significant improvement in efficiency compared to grid search, especially for problems with a large number of hyper-parameters. While grid search systematically evaluates all combinations, random search samples configurations randomly, allowing for a more diverse exploration of the hyper-parameter space. Consequently, RS can yield comparable results to GS with a fraction of the computational cost, making it a more practical option for many applications.

In summary, while grid search is a straightforward and exhaustive approach to hyper-parameter optimization, it suffers from inefficiency in high-dimensional spaces. Random search provides a viable alternative that balances exploration and efficiency, allowing for effective hyper-parameter tuning without the exponential computational demands.

The evaluation of HPO methods is conducted through a systematic approach, focusing on the optimization of hyper-parameters to enhance model performance while maintaining efficiency. The results will be analyzed based on accuracy for classification tasks and mean squared error for regression tasks, alongside the computational time to assess the effectiveness of each HPO method.

Overall, the study aims to provide a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating hyper-parameter optimization methods in machine learning, utilizing well-established datasets and standardized performance metrics to ensure the reliability of the findings. The focus on KNN, SVM, and RF models allows for an exploration of diverse hyper-parameter configurations, contributing valuable insights into the best practices for HPO in data analytics problems.

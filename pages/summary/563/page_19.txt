Other optimizer hyper-parameters include the batch size, which affects the number of training examples utilized in one iteration, and the number of epochs, which indicates how many times the entire training dataset will pass through the model. 

Furthermore, regularization techniques such as dropout rates and weight decay are crucial for preventing overfitting and ensuring generalization of the model. These techniques introduce additional hyper-parameters that need to be tuned alongside the main model parameters. 

Overall, the successful application of deep learning models hinges on the careful tuning of both model design and optimizer hyper-parameters, which collectively enhance the model's performance while mitigating the risk of overfitting.

This paper discusses the growing importance of hyper-parameter optimization (HPO) in machine learning (ML). HPO aims to automate the tuning of hyper-parameters, enabling users to effectively apply ML models to real-world problems. The key benefits of HPO include reducing the time and effort required by developers, enhancing model performance by identifying optimal hyper-parameters for specific datasets, and promoting reproducibility in research by allowing fair comparisons between different ML algorithms.

The selection of appropriate optimization techniques is critical for successfully identifying optimal hyper-parameters. Traditional optimization methods, such as gradient descent, may not be suitable for HPO due to the non-convex or non-differentiable nature of many HPO problems, which can lead to local optima. In contrast, other methods like decision-theoretic approaches, Bayesian optimization models, multi-fidelity optimization, and metaheuristic algorithms are more adept at handling HPO challenges. These advanced techniques can effectively optimize not only continuous hyper-parameters but also discrete, categorical, and conditional ones, thereby broadening their applicability in various ML contexts.

The paper discusses several libraries and frameworks for hyper-parameter optimization (HPO) that utilize various optimization techniques. 

SMAC employs Bayesian optimization with random forest as its surrogate model, accommodating categorical, continuous, and discrete variables. BOHB merges Bayesian optimization with Hyperband to enhance configuration testing efficiency by employing Tree-structured Parzen Estimator as its surrogate model, effectively balancing model performance with budget constraints.

Optunity is noted for its versatility in HPO techniques, including grid search, random search, particle swarm optimization, and Bayesian optimization with TPE, while also converting categorical and discrete hyper-parameters for optimization.

Skopt, built on the scikit-learn library, implements several sequential model-based optimization methods like random search and Bayesian optimization with Gaussian processes, performing well in small search spaces.

GpFlowOpt leverages Gaussian processes within a Python library designed for Bayesian optimization, with support for GPU acceleration via TensorFlow, making it suitable for deep learning applications.

Finally, Talos is tailored for hyper-parameter optimization in Keras models, allowing for easy integration and implementation of various optimization techniques such as grid search, random search, and probabilistic reduction without requiring new syntax.

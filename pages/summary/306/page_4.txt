This study explores four prominent techniques for sentiment analysis of a large corpus of publicly available movie reviews. The techniques include: 

1. Unsupervised lexicon-based models, such as Sent WordNet
2. Traditional supervised machine learning models, like logistic regression
3. Supervised deep learning models, specifically Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM)
4. Advanced supervised deep learning models, including Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers (BERT)

The research assesses the output quality of these techniques, providing a comparative analysis of their effectiveness in sentiment extraction from text. The paper highlights the lack of a comprehensive repository covering these techniques, positioning itself as a valuable reference for researchers interested in sentiment analysis.

The study emphasizes the importance of consumer sentiments, which influence consumer intentions and decisions. These sentiments are often embedded in unstructured text data from social media reviews and can be quantitatively and qualitatively analyzed. The analysis focuses on corpora made up of individual documents, leading to the derivation of sentiments over time through various approaches to sentiment analysis.

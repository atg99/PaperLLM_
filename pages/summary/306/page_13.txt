The referenced works encompass a range of topics related to market orientation, sentiment analysis, big data, and decision support systems within business contexts. Kohli and Jaworski (1990) discuss the concept of market orientation, presenting research propositions and implications for management. Lee and Bradlow (2011) explore automated marketing research through the analysis of online customer reviews. Liu and Zhang (2012) provide a comprehensive survey on opinion mining and sentiment analysis, highlighting methodologies in text mining.

Maas et al. (2011) focus on the development of word vectors specifically for sentiment analysis, contributing to advancements in natural language processing. McAfee et al. (2012) address the transformative impact of big data on management practices, emphasizing its revolutionary potential. Meta xiotis and Psarras (2004) investigate the role of neural networks and genetic algorithms in enhancing business decision-making.

Nayak (2019) discusses advancements in search technologies, particularly the understanding of search language through BERT. Netzer et al. (2012) apply text mining techniques for market structure surveillance, showcasing its utility in marketing science. Lastly, Nijkamp et al. (1996) compare neural network analysis to logit analysis in modeling inter-urban transport flows in Italy, illustrating the application of advanced analytical methods in transportation research.

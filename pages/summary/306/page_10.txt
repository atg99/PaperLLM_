The study utilized a dataset of 50,000 IMDB movie reviews to evaluate sentiment analysis techniques, including an unsupervised lexicon-based model using Sent WordNet, a supervised machine learning model with logistic regression, and a supervised deep learning model using LSTM. The classification performance metrics, including accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 score, showed that BERT outperformed all other models with an accuracy of 0.9231, followed by logistic regression at 0.8941 and LSTM at 0.8675. The results underscore the effectiveness of BERT in sentiment classification, attributed to its ability to understand context through deep bidirectional representations. This study reinforces the trend of adopting advanced models like BERT for improved performance in natural language processing tasks.

The paper discusses the essential preprocessing steps required for text data before conducting sentiment analysis, emphasizing its unstructured nature. Preprocessing is necessary to standardize the text, ensuring that it is suitable for analysis. Key steps include removing HTML tags, accented characters, and special characters, expanding contracted words, segmenting text into sentences, lemmatization, converting capital letters to lower case, and eliminating rare and stop words. The diverse backgrounds of individuals generating reviews lead to variations in communication, making these preprocessing steps crucial for effective sentiment analysis. The objective of the study is to compare different sentiment analysis techniques following this preprocessing.

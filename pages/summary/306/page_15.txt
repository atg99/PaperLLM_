The references list includes significant contributions to the fields of marketing research and data analysis. Venugopal and Baets (1994) discuss the integration of neural networks and statistical methods in marketing research, highlighting their potential to enhance decision-making. Wang et al. (2018) introduce GLUE, a multi-task benchmark for evaluating natural language understanding, providing a comprehensive analysis platform for researchers. Wedel and Kannan (2016) emphasize the importance of marketing analytics in data-rich environments, suggesting strategies to leverage big data for improved marketing outcomes. Lastly, Yi et al. (2014) address the architectural challenges of building a robust network infrastructure for big data, focusing on the necessary frameworks to support data-driven applications. These works collectively underscore the evolving intersection of technology and marketing analytics.

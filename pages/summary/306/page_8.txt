the study was designed to evaluate the performance of various sentiment classification techniques on the IMDB movie review dataset. BERT, an advanced technique recognized for its ability to classify documents into multiple classes, was highlighted for its exceptional performance across numerous natural language understanding tasks. The dataset, curated by Maas et al. in 2011, consists of 50,000 movie reviews, evenly split between positive and negative sentiments, with no neutral reviews included.

The study employed different models for sentiment analysis: an unsupervised lexicon-based model using Sent WordNet, an unsupervised machine learning model with logistic regression, and a supervised deep learning model using Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM). The partitioning of the dataset varied for these models, with BERT utilizing a unique structure of 35% for training, 15% for validation, and 50% for testing. This approach aimed to comprehensively assess the effectiveness of each method in sentiment classification, leveraging BERT's advanced capabilities as evidenced by its adoption by major platforms like Google.

BERT's architecture allows it to understand context more effectively than previous models, as it processes text bidirectionally rather than sequentially. This innovative approach leads to improved performance in understanding nuances in language, making it particularly useful for tasks that require a deep understanding of text. The combination of its pre-training on vast amounts of text and fine-tuning on specific tasks positions BERT as a leading model in the field of machine learning, especially in applications involving sentiment analysis. Overall, both LSTM and BERT represent significant advancements in supervised deep learning models, each offering unique advantages for handling complex data and providing intelligent solutions.

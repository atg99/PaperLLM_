The paper titled "Exploring Transformers in Natural Language Generation: GPT, BERT, and XLNet" examines the advancements in Natural Language Generation (NLG) facilitated by attention mechanisms and the emergence of Transformer architectures. Traditional NLG models like RNN and LSTM faced challenges such as vanishing gradients and limited parallelization due to their sequential processing nature. In contrast, Transformers, introduced by the seminal paper "Attention Is All You Need," eliminate these constraints by employing an encoder-decoder architecture that allows for efficient handling of longer sequences through attention mechanisms.

The authors analyze three prominent Transformer-based models: GPT, BERT, and XLNet, highlighting their transformative impact on NLG tasks ranging from poetry generation to text summarization. The paper underscores the rapid evolution of attention models and their significance in enhancing the performance of language generation systems. Overall, the paper contributes to the understanding of how Transformers are reshaping the landscape of NLG.

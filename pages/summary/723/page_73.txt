The paper discusses the smoothness properties of value functions under different policy parameterizations, specifically focusing on direct policy parameterization and log barrier regularized objectives for softmax parameterization. 

The first lemma establishes smoothness for the value functions under direct parameterization. It shows that the difference in gradients of the value function at two different parameters can be bounded by a function of the difference between those parameters. The proof involves differentiating the value function with respect to the parameters and applying results from previous lemmas.

The second lemma addresses the smoothness of the entropy regularized policy optimization problem in the context of softmax parameterization. It provides a bound on the difference in gradients of the regularized objective function, indicating that this difference is also controlled by the parameter differences.

Overall, the results presented in the paper contribute to understanding the behavior of policy gradient methods in reinforcement learning by establishing conditions under which the value functions and optimization objectives exhibit smoothness properties. This has implications for the stability and convergence of algorithms based on these principles.

The section discusses the convergence properties of the Natural Policy Gradient (NPG) algorithm, particularly when using a softmax parameterization. The NPG algorithm utilizes a Fisher information matrix to perform gradient updates in a geometry defined by this matrix. The update rule is expressed in terms of the Fisher information and the advantage function, allowing for efficient optimization.

A significant result is that with the softmax parameterization, the NPG updates resemble classical multiplicative weights updates used in online linear optimization, emphasizing that the updates are independent of the state distribution due to the pseudoinverse of the Fisher information matrix. 

The paper establishes a dimension-free convergence rate for the NPG algorithm. Specifically, it shows that after T iterations, the value of the policy approaches an optimal value in a manner that does not depend on the number of states or actions. The theorem indicates that if a specific setting for the learning rate is chosen, the NPG can achieve an ε-optimal policy in a number of iterations proportional to the inverse of ε, further demonstrating the efficiency of the NPG algorithm in finding optimal policies in reinforcement learning contexts.

This section of the paper discusses neural policy classes and introduces the Natural Policy Gradient (NPG) and its variant, Q-NPG, in the context of reinforcement learning. The authors define a neural network policy parameterized by θ, where the policy class takes a specific form. They derive expressions related to the policy gradient, highlighting the relationship between the gradient of the policy and the function g(s; a).

The NPG update rule is presented, reformulated to minimize a loss function that incorporates the state-action distribution. The Q-NPG variant is introduced, emphasizing its focus on minimizing a loss related to the action-value function Q, which reflects the expected returns of state-action pairs.

The authors also define a loss function L(w; θ; ρ) that captures the performance of the Q-NPG algorithm in terms of a state-action distribution ρ. They provide an approximate version of Q-NPG, which allows for optimization under a different starting state-action distribution. The state-action visitation measure is defined, and the notation is clarified for convenience.

Finally, they describe an update rule for the approximate Q-NPG, which includes constraints on the update direction to ensure stability in the learning process. This section sets the groundwork for understanding the performance bounds of the proposed algorithms in reinforcement learning scenarios.

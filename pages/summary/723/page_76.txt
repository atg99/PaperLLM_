The paper presents results related to stochastic projected gradient descent algorithms, particularly focusing on the theoretical underpinnings provided by Theorems and Lemmas from previous works. Theorem 58 outlines a condition under which a function's behavior can be analyzed, given certain assumptions hold. Specifically, it states that if a point x+ is obtained through a gradient descent update, it can be bounded in relation to the normal cone of a set C and a unit ball.

In Theorem 59, the authors examine the stochastic projected gradient descent method in a specific context where the feasible region C is defined by a norm constraint. The theorem establishes that under certain conditions, including boundedness of the stochastic gradient and the number of iterations, the expected value of the function evaluated at the average of the iterates converges to the optimal value within a specified error bound. This result emphasizes the effectiveness of the algorithm in minimizing convex functions while adhering to the constraints imposed by the set C.

The paper by Agarwal, Kakade, Lee, and Mahajan discusses the structure of policy gradients within the context of softmax parameterization. A key lemma presented highlights the relationship between the value function and the policy gradient. Specifically, it shows that the derivative of the value function with respect to the policy parameters can be expressed in terms of the advantage function and the stationary distribution of the policy.

The main result (Lemma 40) states that for a softmax policy, the gradient of the value function V with respect to the parameters θ at state s and action a can be formulated using the expected advantage of taking action a in state s, scaled by the probability of that action under the current policy. The proof utilizes the properties of the log likelihood of the policy and the definition of the expected value over trajectories.

Overall, this lemma serves as a crucial component in understanding how policy adjustments can be made to optimize performance in reinforcement learning settings, specifically when employing a softmax policy representation. The paper’s findings contribute to the broader discourse on effective policy optimization techniques.

The paper by Agarwal, Kakade, Lee, and Mahajan presents Lemmas 45 and 46, which delve into the monotonicity of a function with respect to certain actions and parameters. 

Lemma 45 establishes that for all actions belonging to the set Is+, the value function V(t)(θ) is strictly increasing as the parameter θs;a increases for time t greater than T1. Conversely, for actions in the set Is−, the value function is strictly decreasing under the same conditions. This conclusion is derived from earlier lemmas that demonstrate the positivity of A(t)(s;a) for actions in Is+ and its negativity for actions in Is−, thereby confirming the monotonic behavior of the function.

Lemma 46 states that for any non-empty set Is+, the maximum value of the function θ(t)s;a approaches infinity, while the minimum approaches negative infinity as time t increases. This result relies on the existence of an action in Is+ and the monotonicity established in Lemma 45, indicating that the numerator of the relevant expression becomes unbounded. Consequently, it is shown that the sum of the exponentials of the function values diverges, solidifying the claims of the lemma.

Overall, these lemmas provide critical insights into the behavior of the value function in relation to the actions taken, emphasizing the implications of monotonicity in decision-making processes.

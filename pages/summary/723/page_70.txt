The authors, Agarwal, Kakade, Lee, and Mahajan, present a mathematical analysis involving the computation of the gradient and Hessian of a function parameterized by a vector c. They establish a relationship between the second derivative of the function and its components, leading to the formulation of a specific equation that incorporates both the diagonal and off-diagonal components of the Hessian. 

The paper details the expressions for the first and second terms of the gradient, revealing the structure of the Hessian through a chain rule decomposition. The authors provide bounds on the components of the Hessian, linking them to norms and emphasizing smoothness conditions on the value function v(θ) at a given state s0.

A lemma is introduced, which is not limited to specific policy parameterizations but provides a general result concerning the smoothness of the value function with respect to the parameters. It establishes conditions under which the second derivative of the value function remains bounded, offering insights into the stability of the learning process under certain assumptions about the derivatives of the policy.

Overall, the paper contributes to the understanding of the relationships between the parameters of a model and the performance metrics, providing a foundation for further analysis in the context of reinforcement learning or related fields.

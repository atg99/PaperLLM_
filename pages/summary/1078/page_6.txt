4.2.3  Attention-based CNN
An attention-based approach was utilized to focus on relevant features in chest X-ray images for better classification of COVID-19. This model improved interpretability and accuracy by allowing the network to weigh the importance of different regions in the images. The results indicated a classification accuracy of approximately 96%.

4.3  Conclusion
The application of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) in medical image classification, particularly for lung diseases and COVID-19, highlights their effectiveness in enhancing diagnostic accuracy. Various CNN architectures, including ensemble models, small-kernel networks, whole image CNNs, and customized approaches, have shown promising results. These advancements not only improve classification performance but also address challenges such as limited training data and the need for interpretability in medical diagnostics.

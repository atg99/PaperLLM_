This paper presents a comprehensive survey of the application of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) in medical image understanding, addressing the need for efficient diagnosis and treatment planning of medical anomalies. It discusses the limitations of human experts in interpreting medical images due to their scarcity, fatigue, and variability in anomaly presentation. CNNs have emerged as effective tools that not only enhance image understanding but often surpass human performance in various tasks.

The article begins with an introduction to CNNs and outlines their award-winning frameworks. It highlights key medical image understanding tasks, including image classification, segmentation, localization, and detection. The authors critically survey the application of CNNs in diagnosing ailments affecting the brain, breast, lung, and other organs, emphasizing the potential for these networks to support medical professionals.

The paper also addresses challenges faced in the implementation of CNNs, such as feature extraction, which remains a complex task in image understanding. The authors advocate for the integration of CNNs in medical research to improve diagnostic accuracy and efficiency. Overall, the survey aims to motivate researchers in the field to leverage CNNs for advancements in medical image understanding.

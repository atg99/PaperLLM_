In summary, the paper discusses various convolutional neural network (CNN) architectures for brain and breast cancer segmentation. The two-pathway CNN was noted for its ability to improve tumor detail recognition and label correction, achieving notable performance metrics. The multiscale CNN effectively extracted features at different scales, leading to high accuracy and reduced computation time. The multipath and multiscale CNN demonstrated enhanced performance in segmenting brain lesions, particularly in severe cases, and showed versatility across different tasks. For breast cancer segmentation, the fast scanning deep CNN (FCNN) outperformed traditional methods in terms of accuracy and efficiency. Probability maps and patch-based approaches further improved segmentation quality, particularly in histopathological images, showcasing the potential of CNNs in medical image analysis.

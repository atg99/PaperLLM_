The paper emphasizes the significance of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) in advancing medical image understanding, detailing their evolution since the breakthrough of AlexNet in 2012. It notes that CNNs have achieved remarkable accuracy in various medical applications, often surpassing human experts, particularly in cancer detection and COVID-19 diagnosis. Despite the numerous surveys on deep learning, this paper aims to fill gaps regarding CNN applications in early COVID-19 detection and other medical areas. It offers a comprehensive overview of CNN methodologies and applications, intending to inspire further research and practical application in medical diagnostics.

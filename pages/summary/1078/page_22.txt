The paper discusses advancements in the use of deep learning techniques, particularly convolutional neural networks (CNNs), for medical imaging applications. It highlights specific studies that demonstrate the effectiveness of CNNs in classifying digestive organs in wireless capsule endoscopy images and in automatically segmenting the left ventricle in cardiac CT angiography. These studies underscore the growing role of artificial intelligence in enhancing diagnostic accuracy and efficiency in medical imaging.

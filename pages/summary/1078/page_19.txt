The paper discusses the challenges posed by the heterogeneous nature of medical anomalies, which complicates diagnosis and prognosis. Traditional diagnostic methods relying on human specialists face issues such as fatigue, oversight, high costs, and limited availability. As a solution, machine learning (ML) based healthcare systems require efficient feature extraction methods, which are currently lacking. The paper emphasizes the need for intelligent healthcare systems capable of automatically extracting effective features to enhance medical image understanding.

Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) are highlighted as a prominent technique for tackling medical image challenges due to their efficiency in feature extraction and ability to learn various levels of discriminant features. The literature reviewed indicates that researchers have successfully utilized CNNs to improve upon traditional and other ML approaches regarding accuracy, sensitivity, and other performance metrics. However, CNNs still face limitations that affect their overall performance.

The paper outlines the challenges in medical image understanding and introduces various tasks associated with it. It briefly describes CNNs and their components while surveying the methods researchers have deployed to address these challenges. There is ongoing research aimed at demystifying CNNs, which are often viewed as black boxes, to enhance accountability and understanding of their predictions. This includes efforts in image captioning and Bayesian deep learning models that provide uncertainty estimates, which can aid physicians in evaluating model predictions.

In conclusion, the paper calls for advancements in CNNs to further facilitate medical image understanding, ultimately benefiting healthcare professionals. The authors acknowledge the support from REVA University and M. S. Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences and declare no conflicts of interest.

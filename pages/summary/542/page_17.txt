The referenced works in this collection highlight significant advancements in the field of automatic machine learning (AutoML) and neural architecture search. The book edited by Frank Hutter, Lars Kotthoff, and Joaquin Vanschoren provides a comprehensive overview of methods, systems, and challenges within AutoML.

Key contributions include:

1. **Categorical Reparameterization**: Jang et al. introduce the Gumbel-Softmax technique, facilitating the optimization of categorical variables in neural networks.

2. **Auto-Keras**: Jin et al. present Auto-Keras, which implements efficient neural architecture search using network morphism, enhancing the search process for optimal architectures.

3. **Recurrent Network Architectures**: Jozefowicz et al. empirically explore various recurrent network designs, contributing to understanding their performance.

4. **Bayesian Optimization**: Kandasamy et al. utilize Bayesian optimization and optimal transport for neural architecture search, improving efficiency in finding suitable models.

5. **Variational Inference**: Kingma and Welling's work on auto-encoding variational Bayes offers a foundational framework for probabilistic modeling in neural networks.

6. **Learning Curve Prediction**: Klein et al. investigate learning curve predictions using Bayesian neural networks, which aids in model evaluation.

7. **Hyperparameter Optimization**: Klein et al. also focus on fast Bayesian optimization techniques for hyperparameter tuning on large datasets, emphasizing the importance of efficient search strategies.

8. **Reproducibility in Machine Learning**: Klein et al. address the need for reproducibility in neural architecture and hyperparameter search, crucial for validating results.

9. **Random Search**: Li and Talwalkar discuss the role of random search in neural architecture search and its implications for reproducibility.

10. **Hyperband**: Li et al. introduce Hyperband, a bandit-based method for hyperparameter optimization, which improves resource allocation during the search process.

11. **Evolutionary Architecture Search**: Liang et al. propose an evolutionary approach for designing deep multitask networks, demonstrating the adaptability of architectures.

12. **Progressive Neural Architecture Search**: Liu et al. present a progressive search method that incrementally builds neural networks, enhancing the search efficiency.

Overall, these studies contribute to the evolving landscape of AutoML, providing innovative methodologies and frameworks that facilitate the development of efficient machine learning models.

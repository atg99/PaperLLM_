The collection of works referenced explores various methodologies for optimizing neural networks through evolutionary algorithms and meta-learning techniques. 

Petroski Such et al. present deep neuroevolution as a viable method for training deep neural networks in reinforcement learning, emphasizing the competitive nature of genetic algorithms. Suganuma and colleagues investigate genetic programming to design convolutional neural network architectures, while also leveraging standard convolutional autoencoders for image restoration through evolutionary search.

Swersky et al. contribute to the field of Bayesian optimization, proposing methods for optimizing architectures in conditional parameter spaces, including freeze-thaw optimization techniques. Szegedy and his team rethink the Inception architecture, enhancing its performance in computer vision tasks.

Vanschoren discusses meta-learning, providing insights into its applications in automating machine learning processes. Wei and collaborators introduce concepts such as network morphism and modularized morphing, which allow for flexible neural network architecture modifications.

Williams's foundational work on statistical gradient-following algorithms lays groundwork for reinforcement learning advancements. Wistuba showcases a method using Upper Confidence Tree (UCT) to find competitive network architectures rapidly. Finally, Wong et al. explore transfer learning in the context of neural architecture search, while Xie and Yuille introduce Genetic CNN, merging genetic algorithms with convolutional networks.

Overall, these contributions highlight the intersection of evolutionary strategies and neural network optimization, showcasing their potential to enhance machine learning performance across various applications.

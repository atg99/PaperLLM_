The collection of references highlights significant contributions to the field of neural architecture search (NAS) and deep learning. 

Xie et al. (2019) introduce SNAS, a stochastic approach to NAS that optimizes neural architectures. Yao (1999) discusses the evolution of artificial neural networks, laying a foundation for adaptive learning. Ying et al. (2019) present NAS-Bench-101, a benchmark aimed at enhancing reproducibility in NAS research.

Yu and Koltun (2016) focus on multi-scale context aggregation through dilated convolutions, contributing to improved feature extraction. Zela et al. (2018) propose methodologies for automated deep learning, integrating architecture and hyperparameter search efficiently. Zhang et al. (2019) explore graph hypernetworks for NAS, offering a novel perspective on architecture generation.

Zhang et al. (2017) introduce mixup, a technique that enhances model generalization beyond empirical risk minimization. Zhong et al. (2018a, 2018b) present methods for practical and efficient block-wise neural network architecture generation. Zhou et al. (2018) discuss resource-efficient architectures, addressing computational constraints in deep learning.

Zoph and Le (2017) utilize reinforcement learning for NAS, showcasing an innovative approach to architecture optimization. Zoph et al. (2018) further investigate transferable architectures for scalable image recognition, emphasizing the adaptability of learned architectures across tasks.

These works collectively advance the understanding and application of neural architecture search, contributing to the efficiency and effectiveness of deep learning models.

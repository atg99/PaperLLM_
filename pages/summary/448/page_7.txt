The paper discusses the challenges faced in improving upon the baseline 3D U-Net architecture for a segmentation task. Despite conducting various experiments, such as modifying target spacing for resampling and adjusting patch sizes, the results remained largely unchanged, suggesting that the segmentation problem is inherently well-structured and can be effectively tackled with simpler methods. The authors noted that this robustness indicates a strong competitive landscape in the challenge. They considered using an ensemble approach but ultimately chose the Residual 3D U-Net due to a slight decrease in the tumor Dice score with the ensemble. The authors advocate for future research to focus on a detailed comparison between the standard U-Net and its variants, emphasizing the need for comprehensive hyperparameter optimization and statistical analysis to uncover potential differences in performance.

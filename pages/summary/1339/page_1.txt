In the paper "Caring in the In-Between: A Proposal to Introduce Responsible AI and Robotics to Healthcare," Núria Vallès-Peris and Miquel Domènech address the challenges of integrating artificial intelligence (AI) and robotics into healthcare amidst polarized views of their potential benefits and risks. They advocate for an ethical-political framework termed "Caring in the In-Between," which emphasizes democratic mechanisms in technological development.

The authors argue for a shift in focus from the extremes of utopian and dystopian narratives to a middle ground characterized by uncertainty and multiple possibilities. They propose three key movements: first, transitioning from a binary view of promises and dangers to one that recognizes uncertainties; second, rethinking Human-Robot Interaction to consider robots as part of a broader network; and third, moving from a general normative ethical framework to a context-specific discussion on the use of technology.

The "Caring in the In-Between" framework suggests institutional challenges and the need for new healthcare practices, emphasizing three simultaneous processes: monitoring relationships and care processes through public engagement, including stakeholder concerns in participatory processes, and reconciling fears and hopes through progressive, reversible actions. This approach is exemplified through the case of socially assistive robots in children's hospitals, illustrating the authors' vision for responsible AI in healthcare that balances ethical considerations with practical applications.

The paper explores the interplay of fears and hopes surrounding the introduction of AI and robotic systems in healthcare. It emphasizes three dimensions of action related to caring: 

1. **Monitoring Relationships**: Establishing local public health systems to oversee the interactions that arise from integrating AI and robotics into healthcare settings.
2. **Inclusivity in Design**: Addressing the concerns of marginalized groups by organizing participatory processes and alternative stakeholder representation in the design and creation of technology.
3. **Reconciling Fears and Hopes**: Making fears and hopes comparable through the implementation of progressive and reversible actions.

The authors collaboratively contributed to the study's conception and design, with NV-P drafting the manuscript and MD providing feedback. The study received funding from the "la Caixa" Foundation and is published under a Creative Commons Attribution License. The references cited support the arguments and concepts discussed in the paper, providing a robust framework for understanding the ethical implications of AI in healthcare.

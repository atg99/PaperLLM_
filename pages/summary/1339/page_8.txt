• Considering stakeholders’ opinions This involves actively engaging with all actors in the healthcare network, including patients, families, healthcare staff, and policymakers, to gather diverse perspectives. This engagement aims to incorporate their insights into the ethical discussions surrounding robotic care.

• Making fears and hopes commensurable This process seeks to bridge the gap between the potential benefits and risks associated with AI in healthcare. By facilitating dialogue about the uncertainties and expectations related to robotic technologies, the aim is to create a balanced understanding that informs responsible decision-making.

The proposal emphasizes that responsible robotics should not only focus on the technology itself but also on the relational dynamics and ethical considerations that arise in the context of care. It advocates for an ongoing dialogue that recognizes the complexities of healthcare environments, ensuring that the introduction of AI and robotics is aligned with ethical values and practices that prioritize patient care. By adopting a framework that integrates measured action and the ethics of care, the paper suggests that we can navigate the challenges posed by AI and robotics while fostering a more humane and ethical healthcare system.

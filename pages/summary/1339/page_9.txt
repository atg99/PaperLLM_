The paper emphasizes the need for a holistic approach to the integration of AI and robotics in healthcare, advocating for mechanisms that prioritize medical and care values over commercial interests. It highlights the importance of public entities in monitoring the introduction of these technologies to ensure alignment with collective health needs.

The authors argue for the inclusion of diverse stakeholder perspectives through participatory strategies that acknowledge the inequalities present in such processes. They stress that not all groups involved in the discourse have equal power or representation, and propose the incorporation of informal movements to capture the broader population's concerns and aspirations regarding healthcare technologies.

Additionally, the paper advocates for a gradual and feedback-oriented approach to the implementation of AI and robotics, which allows for ongoing dialogue among stakeholders and ensures that technology remains responsive to the specific needs of healthcare settings. This approach aims to balance the ethical implications of technological advancements with the realities of their application in care environments.

In conclusion, the authors propose a framework termed "Caring in the In-Between," which encourages collective engagement in the design and use of healthcare technologies. This framework is rooted in an ethics of care, promoting responsible AI and robotics by emphasizing the interactions of technology within the broader care networks. The paper underscores the inseparable relationship between technology, democracy, and care in the development of future healthcare innovations.

The appendix presents additional experimental results for the RF-GAP method, focusing on empirical validation of Theorems 1 and 2 as outlined in Section 5. The results demonstrate that the error rates of RF-GAP closely align with those of the traditional random forest (RF) across various datasets.

Table A.1 lists the mean squared error (MSE) for continuously labeled data and error rates for categorically labeled data. It compares RF predictions with RF-GAP, original out-of-bag (OOB) predictions, and other approaches. The RF-GAP proximity predictions show a strong correlation with RF predictions, indicating its reliability. Each dataset was tested multiple times using the same random forest for consistency.

Table A.2 further analyzes differences in proximity-weighted predictions using the training set, reaffirming the accuracy of RF-GAP predictions against RF's OOB predictions. The results illustrate that RF-GAP predictions consistently align with RF predictions across various datasets, reinforcing its effectiveness in predictive modeling. Overall, the data supports the claim that RF-GAP enhances prediction accuracy while maintaining methodological integrity.

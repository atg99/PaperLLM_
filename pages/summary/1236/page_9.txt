They reference Leo Breiman's early work on this application, noting that RF-GAP provides clearer class separations in visualizations, particularly in the classic Sonar dataset, where it highlights misclassifications more effectively than other proximity definitions.

Section 6.2 discusses data imputation, where the authors outline a method for imputing missing values using random forests. They demonstrate that RF-GAP leads to superior imputation accuracy across various datasets with different percentages of missing values.

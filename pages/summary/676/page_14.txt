In the discussion section, the challenges of tomato disease detection using computer vision are highlighted, particularly the low interclass variance and high intra-class variance, leading to difficulties in distinguishing between diseased and healthy leaves. The models struggled with early blight and target spot detection due to similar visual characteristics with healthy leaves, resulting in higher false positives and false negatives. However, the models effectively identified Fusarium wilt due to clearer distinctions in the dataset.

The performance evaluation of the models emphasized the effectiveness of attention modules, particularly in the lw_resnet20_cbam model, which demonstrated superior classification accuracy while maintaining model efficiency. The structured placement of attention modules allowed for better focus on high-level features, enhancing model performance. In contrast, the DA model's parallel attention structure resulted in lower performance, supporting the argument that a series combination is more effective. Overall, while the models showed promise, achieving perfect classification remains a challenge, particularly for early-stage diseases.

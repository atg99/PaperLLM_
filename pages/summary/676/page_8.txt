The paper discusses the integration of self-attention (SA) and dual attention (DA) architectures into a lightweight base network for improved performance in image processing tasks. The SA module enhances feature extraction by applying convolutional operations followed by a softmax function to generate attention maps, which are then used to refine output feature maps. The DA architecture further incorporates position attention (PA) and channel attention (CA) mechanisms, allowing the network to focus on relevant spatial and channel-wise features. The models were trained, validated, and tested using consistent image datasets to ensure reliable evaluation of their performance. The detailed architecture of both SA and DA modules is illustrated, emphasizing their roles in enhancing the main network's capabilities.

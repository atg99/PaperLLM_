The paper presents an analysis of various lightweight ResNet models, specifically focusing on their training and validation accuracy and loss metrics, as illustrated in Figure 7. Table 5 summarizes the performance of different models, indicating that lw_resnet20 yielded the highest training accuracy at 99.54% and validation accuracy at 99.36%. 

The study further examines network parameters and efficiency, noting that deeper networks increase computational complexity. The proposed models demonstrated a significant reduction in network parameters—approximately 16 times fewer—and were 23 times less complex compared to the standard ResNet50 model. Despite being lightweight, the base model exhibited subpar performance on the test dataset. The paper highlights that the SE and CBAM attention modules are the least demanding in terms of resources, while SA and DA modules contribute to higher computational complexity. The training times across different attention modules were comparable, though the test time per image was greater for SA and DA due to their heavier architecture. 

Overall, the findings emphasize the balance between model efficiency and performance, particularly in the context of agricultural applications.

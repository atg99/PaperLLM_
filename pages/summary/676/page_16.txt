The study presented in this paper focuses on the application of various attention modules for improving the classification of tomato diseases using deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs). A comparative analysis of different network architectures, computational complexities, and performance metrics was conducted. The results indicate that identifying key features and their spatial locations within input images significantly enhances classification accuracy. The study emphasizes the importance of sequentially determining critical features and their positions to avoid loss of vital information.

The proposed lightweight CBAM attention module-based ResNet20 model achieved an accuracy of 99.69% on 11 classes of tomato leaf images, outperforming existing generic models in plant disease detection. The authors detail the methodologies employed, including conceptualization, validation, and data analysis, and acknowledge contributions from all team members.

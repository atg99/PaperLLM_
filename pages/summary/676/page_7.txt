The paper discusses advanced attention mechanisms in convolutional neural networks, specifically focusing on the Convolutional Block Attention Module (CBAM) and Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) architectures. 

In the context of CBAM, the document defines various feature maps including dual attention feature maps, position attention feature maps, and channel attention feature maps. It illustrates the architecture of the CBAM, highlighting its integration into the main network, and details the functioning of both the channel attention module and the spatial attention module.

Additionally, the SE architecture is examined, detailing its placement within the main network and outlining its architecture. Key parameters such as the height and width of feature maps, the number of channels, and the channel reduction ratio are specified, emphasizing the role of fully connected layers and activation functions like the Rectifier Linear Unit (ReLU). 

The paper aims to enhance understanding of how these attention mechanisms improve the performance of neural networks in processing feature maps effectively.

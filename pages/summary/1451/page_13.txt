The referenced papers focus on various aspects of linguistic intelligence and question generation within the field of natural language processing. 

Yogatama et al. (2019) investigate the development and evaluation of general linguistic intelligence, emphasizing the need for robust models that can understand and generate human-like language. Zhang and Bansal (2019) address the issue of semantic drift in question generation, proposing methods for improving question-answering systems through semi-supervised learning techniques. Zhao et al. (2018) present a paragraph-level neural question generation method utilizing maxout pointer and gated self-attention networks, showcasing advancements in understanding context for generating relevant questions. Zhou et al. (2018) conduct a preliminary study on neural question generation from text, exploring the foundational aspects of generating questions based on textual input. Lastly, Zhu et al. (2015) focus on aligning narratives from books and movies, aiming to create visual explanations that resemble storytelling by integrating information from both mediums. 

Together, these works contribute to the broader understanding of language processing, question generation, and the intersection of narrative forms in computational contexts.

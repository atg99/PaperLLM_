Rongrong Ji is a Senior Member of IEEE, currently a Professor and Director of the Intelligent Multimedia Technology Laboratory at Xiamen University, China. His research primarily focuses on multimedia signal processing, computer vision, and pattern recognition, with over 100 publications in international journals and conferences. Ji is a member of ACM and has received notable awards including the ACM Multimedia Best Paper Award and the Best Thesis Award from Harbin Institute of Technology. He contributes as an Associate/Guest Editor for various international journals, including Neurocomputing and IEEE Multimedia Magazine, and is involved as a program committee member for several leading international conferences.

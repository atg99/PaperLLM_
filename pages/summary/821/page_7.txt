The paper presents a performance comparison of a novel 1×N pruning method against traditional weight and filter pruning techniques, using MobileNet-V1/V2/V3 and ResNet-50 networks at a pruning rate of 50%. The results indicate that while the proposed 1×N pruning method shows slightly lower accuracy compared to weight pruning, it significantly outperforms filter pruning across all models. For instance, in MobileNet-V2, the 1×4 pruning achieves 69.706% top-1 accuracy, notably better than the 66.730% from filter pruning, while maintaining a performance close to weight pruning.

The findings also highlight that as the block size N increases, the performance of 1×N pruning declines due to coarser pruning. This method transitions to weight pruning at smaller N values and to filter pruning at larger N values. Furthermore, the accuracy of all methods decreases with increasing pruning rates, with filter pruning experiencing the most substantial performance drop beyond 50%. In contrast, the 1×N pruning method maintains a relatively stable performance even at higher pruning rates.

Additionally, the paper discusses the acceleration capabilities of various pruning methods on CPU platforms, utilizing the TVM compiler for a fair comparison of the pruned models. The results affirm the effectiveness of the 1×N pruning method in achieving a balance between accuracy and computational efficiency.

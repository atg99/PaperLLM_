The collection of references presents a variety of influential works in the fields of deep learning, computer vision, and machine learning. 

Key studies include:

1. **Trusted Multi-View Classification** by Han et al. which explores multi-view classification methods, emphasizing trustworthiness in predictions.
2. **Fusenet** by Hazirbas et al. introduces a convolutional neural network architecture that effectively integrates depth information into semantic segmentation tasks.
3. **Deep Residual Learning** by He et al. highlights the concept of residual learning to facilitate the training of deep neural networks, achieving state-of-the-art results in image recognition.
4. **Acnet** by Hu et al. presents an attention-based network designed to enhance RGB-D semantic segmentation by utilizing complementary features.
5. **Rednet** by Jiang et al. focuses on a residual encoder-decoder network tailored for indoor RGB-D semantic segmentation.
6. **Late Temporal Modeling** by Kalfaoglu et al. investigates the integration of BERT with 3D CNNs for action recognition in video data.
7. The **Adam Optimizer** by Kingma and Ba is introduced as a robust method for stochastic optimization in machine learning.
8. **Imagenet Classification** by Krizhevsky et al. showcases the effectiveness of deep convolutional neural networks in large-scale image classification tasks.
9. **Foundations of Machine Learning** by Mohri et al. provides a comprehensive overview of theoretical aspects of machine learning.
10. Neyshabur et al. discuss the implications of over-parametrization on the generalization capabilities of neural networks and propose norm-based capacity control strategies.
11. **Multimodal Deep Learning** by Ngiam et al. explores learning representations from multiple modalities.
12. **RDFNet** by Park et al. presents a multi-level residual feature fusion approach for RGB-D semantic segmentation.
13. **Efficient RGB-D Semantic Segmentation** by Seichter et al. focuses on methods for indoor scene analysis using RGB-D data.

These works collectively contribute to advancements in machine learning techniques, particularly in the integration of various data modalities and the optimization of deep learning architectures for complex tasks.

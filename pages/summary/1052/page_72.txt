The cited works encompass significant contributions in various domains, including big data integration, human mobility modeling, transfer learning, and ethical considerations in AI model reporting. 

Dong and Srivastava (2013) discuss the challenges and methodologies in integrating big data, highlighting the importance of efficient data management techniques. Zhang et al. (2015) present "Comobile," a framework for real-time modeling of human mobility in urban environments using multi-view learning, emphasizing its applicability in geographic information systems. 

Pan and Yang (2009) provide a comprehensive survey on transfer learning, detailing its principles, techniques, and applications, which is essential for improving machine learning models by transferring knowledge from one domain to another. Lastly, Mitchell et al. (2019) introduce the concept of model cards, which serve as a tool for reporting and ensuring accountability in AI models, addressing fairness and transparency in machine learning practices. 

Together, these papers contribute to the understanding of data integration, mobility analysis, learning transfer, and responsible AI deployment.

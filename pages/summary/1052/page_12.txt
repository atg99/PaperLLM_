The paper discusses various methods for achieving explainability in machine learning models, categorizing them into distinct approaches. 

Text explanations involve generating textual representations or symbols that illustrate the model's functioning, offering semantic mappings between the model and these symbols. Visual explanation techniques focus on visualizing model behavior, often utilizing dimensionality reduction to create interpretable visual representations. These visualizations can enhance understanding, especially for users unfamiliar with machine learning.

Local explanations address explainability by breaking down the solution space, providing insights into simpler subspaces relevant to the entire model's operation. Explanations by example extract specific data instances that relate to a model's output, helping users grasp the underlying relationships and correlations the model captures.

Explanations by simplification involve creating a new, less complex model that approximates the original while maintaining similar performance, making it easier to implement. Feature relevance explanation methods quantify the importance of various features in relation to the model's output, offering an indirect way to understand the model's inner workings.

The paper's classification of these techniques will guide the review of specific and agnostic explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) methods in machine learning models, providing a comprehensive overview of trends in the field.

The paper categorizes AI principles into two groups: AI-specific principles, which focus on unique aspects of AI like explainability and fairness, and end-to-end principles that encompass broader concerns including privacy and security.

The authors emphasize the need for a structured methodology to operationalize these principles, integrating them with existing organizational processes related to privacy and security. Key components of this methodology include defining AI principles, raising awareness through training, utilizing impact assessment questionnaires, employing relevant tools for mitigation, and establishing a governance model that balances committee oversight with employee self-responsibility.

The implementation of Responsible AI principles is framed as a balancing act between necessary cultural shifts within organizations and the practicalities of leveraging existing IT resources. The paper concludes that fostering corporate awareness around Responsible AI principles will facilitate the integration of eXplainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) and significantly influence the successful adoption of machine learning methods in practical applications.

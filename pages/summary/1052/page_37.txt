The paper discusses essential principles for the ethical development and deployment of AI systems, emphasizing the importance of transparency and user awareness. It argues that individuals should always be informed when interacting with AI and understand how their personal data is utilized. The concept of Explainable AI (XAI) is highlighted as a means to ensure that explanations are tailored to users' profiles, thereby enhancing transparency.

Moreover, AI systems should align with the United Nations’ Sustainable Development Goals, promoting a Human-centric approach that benefits society. The principle of Privacy and Security by Design is also emphasized, ensuring that AI systems adhere to privacy standards throughout their lifecycle. This principle is part of a broader framework known as Responsible Research and Innovation (RRI), which advocates for ethical considerations in scientific research and emphasizes transparency.

The authors stress that these principles should extend to all third-party stakeholders involved in AI development. They reference the European Commission's ethical guidelines for Trustworthy AI, which encompass principles such as human agency, technical robustness, and accountability, aligning closely with the principles discussed in the paper.

Finally, the paper notes that fairness and accountability are critical components of AI principles guidelines and are interconnected with XAI, underscoring their significance in the ethical landscape of AI development.

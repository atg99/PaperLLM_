Transferability is another essential goal of explainable AI, focusing on the ability of models to be applied successfully in different contexts or datasets. Domain experts and data scientists are particularly interested in this aspect, as it addresses the robustness and adaptability of AI models across various applications.

Informativeness pertains to the extent to which an explainable model provides insights and understanding about its decision-making processes. This goal is relevant to all stakeholders, ensuring that the results are comprehensible and actionable.

Confidence in the model's predictions is crucial for developers and managers, as it relates to the reliability of the model's outputs. Regulatory entities also emphasize this aspect to ensure compliance and accountability in AI applications.

Fairness is a critical consideration for users affected by model decisions, as well as regulatory agencies. This goal seeks to ensure that AI systems operate without bias, promoting equitable outcomes.

Accessibility is aimed at product owners and users, focusing on making AI systems understandable and usable for a broader audience.

Interactivity allows users and domain experts to engage with the model, facilitating a deeper understanding of its workings and enhancing the explainability experience.

Lastly, privacy awareness is increasingly important for users and regulatory bodies, ensuring that models respect user data and privacy rights.

Overall, the goals of explainable AI encompass a range of considerations that cater to various audiences, highlighting the multifaceted nature of explainability in AI systems.

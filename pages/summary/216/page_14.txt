The paper discusses the structure of ResNet, particularly focusing on the stem and vine framework. The stem is defined as comprising a series of weight matrices and nonlinearities, totaling 70 vertices, which include 34 weight matrices and 35 nonlinearities. The norms of these weight matrices are bounded, and the Lipschitz constants of the nonlinearities are also defined.

In the context of this framework, 16 distinct vines are identified, with some containing weight matrices while others serve as identity mappings. Specific weight matrices are highlighted, and their norms are bounded as well. The paper introduces a lemma that establishes a covering number bound for the hypothesis space of ResNet, denoted as HR. This bound takes into account the distances between weight matrices and their corresponding reference matrices, as well as the norms of the vines.

The lemma provides a mathematical expression detailing the relationship between the covering number, the bounds on the weight matrices, and the norms of the vines. It highlights the logarithmic relationship between the covering number and the parameters of the ResNet architecture, emphasizing the influence of the weight matrix norms and the Lipschitz constants on the capacity of the network. 

Overall, the paper presents a theoretical framework for understanding the limitations and capabilities of ResNet through the lens of covering numbers and bounded weights.

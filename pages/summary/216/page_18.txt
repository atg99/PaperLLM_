The paper presents a visual analysis of the loss surface surrounding solutions derived from varying parameters of weight decay and batch size. Figure 3 illustrates both one-dimensional and two-dimensional representations of this loss surface. Each subfigure is labeled with specific parameters, including weight decay, batch size, and corresponding test error percentages. The data and figures referenced are originally sourced from another study, indicated by citation [30]. This structured approach aims to highlight the impact of different configurations on model performance, as measured by test error.

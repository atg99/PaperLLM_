The paper discusses the covering numbers of hypothesis spaces related to outputs from a ResNet structure, specifically focusing on the covering number N2j+1 at vertex N(2j+1) and the covering number Nu,v,1 at vertex N(u). The covering number N2j+1 is analyzed under fixed inputs from the previous layer, F2j-1(X), and is bounded using an inequality derived from Lemma 5. The bound is expressed as:

logN2j+1 ≤ b2j+1kF2j+1(XT)Tk2/2"²2j+1log(2W²).

Here, W represents the maximum dimension of features through the ResNet.

The paper then presents two scenarios for bounding kF2j+1(XT)Tk2. The first scenario considers the absence of a vine connected to the stem at vertex N(2j-1), leading to an inequality that bounds the norm based on the structure of the previous layer. The second scenario involves a vine connected at vertex N(2j+1), resulting in a more complex inequality that incorporates terms from both the current and previous layers.

Furthermore, a similar approach is used to derive an upper bound for the covering number Nu,v,1, where the output at vertex N(u) is Fu(XT). This bound is expressed as:

logNu,v,1V ≤ (bu,v,1/2)²kFu(XT)Tk²/2"²u,v,1log(2W²).

These results contribute to understanding the behavior of ResNet outputs and their hypothesis space in terms of covering numbers, which are crucial for analyzing the model's capacity and generalization.

The proof of Theorem 1 focuses on establishing a covering bound for deep neural networks with residual connections, specifically through the analysis of the influence of adding a vine structure to the network. The proof begins by considering a vine, denoted as V(u;v;i), which connects to the main network at two points: after a nonlinearity and before a weight matrix. For a fixed input Fu(X), the hypothesis functions generated by the vine form a hypothesis space Hu;v;i V.

Since a vine is structured similarly to a chain-like neural network, the covering number for the hypothesis space Hu;v;i V can be bounded using Lemma 3. Importantly, it is noted that many vines can represent identity mappings, which simplifies the covering number for those cases to 1.

The proof further applies Lemmas 5 and 3 to establish the existence of an ε-cover Wv for the hypothesis space Hv and an εu;v;i V-cover Wu;v;i V for Hu;v;i V. The hypotheses from the vine and the corresponding deep neural network are combined element-wise, leading to the formation of a new hypothesis space H0 v.

A function space W0 v is defined as the sum of two spaces, WS and WV, where WS belongs to Hv and WV belongs to Hu;v;i V. For any hypothesis F0 in H0 v, there exist hypotheses FS in Hv and FV in Hu;v;i V such that F0(X) = FS(X) + FV(X). 

Using the properties of the ε-covers, it can be shown that for any hypothesis FS in Hv, there exists an element WFS in Wv, ensuring that the distance between FS and WFS is bounded by εv. A similar argument holds for FV in Hu;v;i V, leading to the existence of WFV in Wu;v;i V, with the distance to FV also bounded.

Consequently, for any hypothesis F0 in H0 v, it can be expressed in terms of WFS and WFV, leading to a bound on the overall distance kF0(X) - W(X)k. This distance can be split into contributions from the distances of FV to WFV and FS to WFS, ultimately yielding a combined bound of εu;v;i V + εv. This result completes the proof, demonstrating how the addition of a vine influences the covering number and establishes a covering bound for the deep neural network with residuals.

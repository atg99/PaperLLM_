The paper discusses the application of equations to prove another equation, specifically focusing on the formulation of radii of covers using an induction method. It begins by assuming a radius for the hypothesis space calculated with a weight matrix and nonlinearity, leading to a derived equation. The process is repeated for different weight matrices and nonlinearities, considering scenarios with and without connections between vertices.

Three key cases are examined: 
1. The first case establishes a relationship for the radius after applying transformations through a weight matrix and nonlinearity.
2. The second case continues this approach for a different weight matrix and nonlinearity, under the assumption of no connections around.
3. The third case incorporates a specific connection (vine) between vertices, leading to a more complex equation.

The final derived equation consolidates the results from the previous cases, showing a relationship between the computed radii and a defined term. The proof concludes by demonstrating that the initial radius is equal to this defined term, thus completing the proof process.

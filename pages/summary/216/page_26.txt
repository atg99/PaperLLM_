The paper presents a two-step proof for establishing a generalization bound for ResNet. The first step involves deriving an upper bound on the Rademacher complexity of the hypothesis space associated with ResNet by applying Lemma 2 to Lemma 4. It results in an inequality that expresses the Rademacher complexity in terms of parameters related to the hypothesis space. The second step applies this upper bound to Lemma 1 to derive a generalization error bound, which quantifies the probability that the model's predictions deviate from the true labels.

The conclusion emphasizes the significance of the covering number for the hypothesis space induced by deep neural networks with residual connections, specifically ResNet. The authors assert that their generalization bound ensures the performance of ResNet on unseen data and can be extended to other advanced architectures like DenseNet and ResNeXt. The paper also highlights the need for further exploration of the localization properties of stochastic gradient descent, as recent experiments suggest that SGD may only traverse a subset of the hypothesis space.

The paper presents a proposed GUI prototype for emotion classification, specifically focusing on anger. It includes various figures and tables demonstrating key findings. Figures 5 to 8 illustrate energy, pitch, MFCC (Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients), and LPCC (Linear Predictive Coding coefficients) respectively. The accuracy of two classifiers, One Against All (OAA) and gender-dependent classifiers, is compared across different emotions in Table 1, while Table 2 shows the accuracy of the LDC (Linguistic Data Consortium) and UGA (University of Georgia) datasets.

The LDC dataset achieves a significantly higher overall accuracy of 90.08%, attributed to performances by trained actors in a noiseless environment, compared to the UGA dataset's 65.95%, which involves students in noisy settings. The gender-dependent classifier outperforms the OAA SVM classifier with an accuracy of 84.42% versus 72.785%. Graphs further illustrate that the MFCC algorithm provides better results than LPCC. The overall system accuracy is reported at 85.085%.

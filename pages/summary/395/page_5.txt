The references cited in this paper encompass a wide range of studies and advancements in the field of computer vision, particularly focusing on object detection and neural network architectures. Key contributions include:

1. **Fairness in Neural Architecture Search** - Chu et al. propose a method for evaluating fairness in weight sharing neural architecture search.

2. **Object Detection and Semantic Segmentation** - Girshick et al. introduce rich feature hierarchies that enhance the accuracy of object detection and segmentation tasks.

3. **Mask R-CNN** - He et al. present a framework for instance segmentation that extends Faster R-CNN.

4. **MobileNets** - Howard et al. develop efficient convolutional neural networks designed for mobile applications.

5. **Squeeze-and-Excitation Networks** - Hu et al. introduce a mechanism to improve network performance by recalibrating channel-wise feature responses.

6. **Speed/Accuracy Trade-offs** - Huang et al. analyze the balance between speed and accuracy in modern convolutional object detectors.

7. **Deep Learning Foundations** - LeCun et al. provide a comprehensive overview of deep learning principles.

8. **Feature Pyramid Networks** - Lin et al. propose a structure for enhancing feature extraction in object detection.

9. **Single Shot MultiBox Detector (SSD)** - Liu et al. present a model that enables real-time object detection.

10. **YOLO Frameworks** - Redmon et al. introduce several iterations of the YOLO (You Only Look Once) system, focusing on real-time object detection improvements.

11. **Faster R-CNN** - Ren et al. develop a model that integrates region proposal networks to speed up object detection.

12. **MobileNetV2** - Sandler et al. introduce an architecture that emphasizes efficiency through inverted residuals and linear bottlenecks.

13. **Fast YOLO** - Shaﬁee et al. create a system for real-time embedded object detection.

14. **Online Hard Example Mining** - Shrivastava et al. discuss training techniques for region-based object detectors.

15. **MnasNet** - Tan et al. present a platform-aware approach to neural architecture search optimized for mobile devices.

16. **Universal Metrics for Neural Networks** - Wong proposes metrics for assessing deep neural network performance on a large scale.

17. **Collaborative Design for Neural Networks** - Wong et al. introduce ATTONETS, focusing on compact and efficient networks for edge applications.

18. **Generative Synthesis for Neural Networks** - Wong et al. explore learning generative machines to optimize neural network designs.

19. **SqueezeDet** - Wu et al. develop a lightweight, fully convolutional network for real-time detection tasks in autonomous driving.

These references collectively highlight significant advancements in neural network design, efficiency, and application in real-time object detection.

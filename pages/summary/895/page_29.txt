The paper discusses various strategies for applying Data Augmentation in text data, highlighting its potential to enhance learning environments by embedding learning agents in teacher-student loops. An example includes the use of neural networks in bipedal walking control via the POET framework, which co-evolves control parameters and terrain characteristics. Data Augmentation is proposed as a method to improve language understanding similarly to its applications in vision tasks, such as Generative Teaching Networks (GTNs), where a teacher network generates training data for a student network.

The conclusion emphasizes the promise of Data Augmentation in NLP, noting that it is less developed compared to its application in Computer Vision. The authors discuss the role of augmentations in simulating distribution shifts and improving generalization, while also addressing practical considerations and the broader implications for artificial intelligence. The paper encourages further research in this area.

The paper discusses various data augmentation strategies, particularly focusing on back-translation and style augmentation. Back-translation utilizes semantic invariances from supervised translation datasets to enhance data for machine learning models, especially in scenarios with limited paired translation data. It highlights that while high-quality translations can be beneficial, diverse lower-quality data may yield better results. The authors suggest potential applications for back-translation in different domains, such as scientific and news articles, and emphasize the importance of curating paired languages and domains for optimal model performance.

Style augmentation is introduced as a technique that uses Deep Networks to modify data, allowing for the transfer of writing styles between authors. This method aims to prevent overfitting by focusing on semantics rather than specific language forms. It can be particularly useful in tasks like abstractive summarization and question answering by capturing semantic similarities across different writing styles.

The paper also touches on generative data augmentation, an emerging area in Deep Learning that involves creating realistic images and text. While these generative models have proven effective for Transfer Learning, the authors question the most impactful applications for generative tasks in the field.

The paper discusses the challenges in curating labeled datasets for natural language processing tasks, exemplified by the effort required to compile a dataset of TLDR summaries of machine learning papers. It emphasizes the significance of Data Augmentation in enhancing model performance, particularly in scenarios with limited labeled data. 

Data Augmentation is defined as a set of algorithms that create synthetic data from existing datasets, which helps prevent overfitting by introducing variability in the training data. The paper contrasts Data Augmentation with other regularization methods, noting its unique ability to represent semantic invariance through transformations that preserve labels. 

The survey categorizes Data Augmentation strategies into symbolic and neural methods. Symbolic methods include techniques like Rule-Based and Graph-Structured Augmentations, while neural methods encompass approaches such as Back-Translation and Generative Data Augmentation. The paper aims to provide insights into how these augmentation strategies can improve the training of deep neural networks in natural language processing tasks.

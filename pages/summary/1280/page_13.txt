This collection of references encompasses various studies and methodologies across different fields, primarily focusing on statistical methods, machine learning, and data analysis. 

Vovk and Wang (2018) discuss a method for combining p-values through averaging, which can improve statistical decision-making. Xiao et al. (2017) introduce the Fashion-MNIST dataset, designed as a benchmark for evaluating machine learning algorithms, providing a more challenging alternative to the traditional MNIST dataset. Zaykin et al. (2002) present the Truncated Product Method for combining p-values, which addresses issues in genetic epidemiology, enhancing the robustness of statistical tests.

Zhang et al. (2013) explore domain adaptation in machine learning, particularly under target and conditional shifts, contributing to the understanding of how models can generalize across different data distributions. Lastly, Zugner et al. (2018) investigate adversarial attacks on neural networks applied to graph data, highlighting vulnerabilities and the need for improved defenses in machine learning applications.

Overall, these works contribute to advancements in statistical methodologies and the application of machine learning techniques in various contexts.

The paper presents a multi-modal deep learning approach to predict the conversion from Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI) to Alzheimer's Disease (AD) by utilizing longitudinal cognitive performance data, cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) biomarkers, neuroimaging, and demographic information collected at baseline. The authors employed multiple Gated Recurrent Units (GRUs) to process the longitudinal multi-domain data, demonstrating improved prediction accuracy compared to traditional methods. The results suggest that this approach can effectively identify individuals at risk for developing AD, potentially aiding in clinical trial stratification and targeting those who may benefit most from interventions. The data used in the study is publicly accessible through the Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI) data repository.

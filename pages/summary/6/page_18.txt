The references listed pertain to significant contributions in the fields of 3D shape modeling, point cloud generation, and large-scale image datasets. 

Wu et al. (2016) introduce a method for learning a probabilistic latent space for object shapes using 3D generative-adversarial modeling, which enhances the understanding of object representations in a latent space. Yang et al. (2019) present Pointflow, a technique for generating 3D point clouds through continuous normalizing flows, which allows for more efficient representation of 3D data. Yifan et al. (2019) discuss differentiable surface splatting for processing point-based geometry, providing a novel approach to manipulate and render point cloud data. 

Additionally, Yu et al. (2015) contribute to the creation of LSUN, a large-scale image dataset that integrates deep learning with human input, facilitating advancements in image recognition tasks. Zhou et al. (2017) introduce the Places dataset, aimed at deep scene understanding, offering a comprehensive resource for training and evaluating scene recognition models. 

These works collectively advance the understanding and processing of 3D shapes and large-scale visual datasets, highlighting the importance of generative models and robust datasets in machine learning applications.

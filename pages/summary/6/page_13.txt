The paper presents a comparison of shape reconstruction predictions using silhouette mesh rendering on ShapeNet test data. Figure 9 illustrates the input image on the left, followed by the object shape predictions generated by various models, including Sphere FC, Sphere GCN, High Res Sphere GCN, and Voxel GCN. Each model's output is accompanied by an additional random view of the predicted shape, highlighting the differences in reconstruction quality among the models. This visual representation underscores the effectiveness of the different approaches in capturing the object shapes from the input images.

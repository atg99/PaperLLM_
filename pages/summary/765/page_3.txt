The paper discusses recent advancements in end-to-end autonomous driving, focusing on the integration of deep learning within the modular pipeline of autonomous systems. The perception module plays a critical role in scene understanding, utilizing algorithms for object detection, tracking, and lane detection. It fuses data from various sensors to create a comprehensive view of the environment.

Following perception, the localization and mapping module determines the vehicle's position and constructs an accurate map, essential for safe navigation in complex traffic scenarios. Techniques such as High Definition mapping and Simultaneous Localization And Mapping are highlighted for their effectiveness in real-time localization.

The planning and driving policy module computes motion-level commands based on the localization data. It predicts optimal trajectories using various methods categorized as physics-based, classic machine learning-based, and deep learning-based approaches. The latter is noted for its ability to make reliable predictions over extended timeframes, surpassing the capabilities of traditional methods that are limited to short-term scenarios. Overall, the paper emphasizes the importance of these interconnected modules in achieving effective and safe autonomous driving capabilities.

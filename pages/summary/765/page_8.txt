The paper surveys recent advancements in end-to-end autonomous driving using deep learning, highlighting various models and their input-output representations. It discusses the multi-modal fusion transformer, which utilizes RGB images and LiDAR BEV representations to predict the ego-vehicle's waypoints. NEAT processes image patches and velocity features to derive waypoints for PID controllers. The TCP model combines image input, navigation data, and current speed to generate control actions through its trajectory and control branches. LAV focuses on image-only inputs to predict future trajectories for safe navigation. UniAD visualizes attention masks to indicate focus on goal lanes and critical agents. Finally, ST-P3 outputs a sub cost map that balances safety in trajectory predictions by integrating occupancy probability fields and pre-existing knowledge. Each model showcases unique approaches to enhancing autonomous driving capabilities.

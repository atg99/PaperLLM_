The paper titled "Recent Advancements in End-to-End Autonomous Driving using Deep Learning: A Survey" provides an overview of the significant progress made in the field of autonomous driving through the application of deep learning techniques. The authors, Pranav Singh Chib and Pravendra Singh, highlight the integration of machine learning and computer vision in enhancing the capabilities of autonomous driving systems. They emphasize the importance of end-to-end learning approaches, which streamline the process by allowing neural networks to directly map raw sensor inputs to driving actions. The survey covers various methodologies, challenges, and future directions in the domain, reflecting the authors' expertise and ongoing research in deep learning and autonomous vehicles. The paper serves as a comprehensive resource for understanding the current landscape and potential advancements in this rapidly evolving field.

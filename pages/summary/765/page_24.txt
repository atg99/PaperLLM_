The paper provides a comprehensive survey on recent advancements in end-to-end autonomous driving utilizing deep learning techniques. It discusses various methodologies and frameworks developed to enhance the performance of autonomous vehicles through deep learning, emphasizing the importance of multimodal data integration, behavior prediction, and policy learning.

Key contributions in the field include advancements in trajectory prediction, vehicle dynamics estimation, and the application of reinforcement learning for safe driving policies. Several models, such as Temporal Point Cloud Networks and Stacked Transformers, are highlighted for their effectiveness in motion forecasting and multimodal behavior prediction.

The paper also reviews datasets and benchmarks used for training and testing autonomous driving systems, noting the significance of real-world observations for developing robust simulations. It emphasizes the integration of diverse sensor data, including Lidar and camera inputs, to improve object detection and navigation capabilities in urban environments.

In conclusion, the paper underscores the rapid progression of deep learning techniques in autonomous driving, highlighting the need for ongoing research to address challenges such as safety, reliability, and ethical considerations in deployment. The survey serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners aiming to understand the state-of-the-art in autonomous driving technologies.

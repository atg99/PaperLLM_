This survey discusses recent advancements in end-to-end autonomous driving using deep learning, focusing on how vehicle maneuvers are influenced by high-level navigation commands, perception data, and vehicle state measurements. The paper emphasizes the importance of minimizing lateral distance and angle to optimize driving performance, utilizing waypoints for trajectory planning, which are independent of vehicle geometry and facilitate control command analysis.

The paper outlines the use of cost functions to evaluate multiple trajectories, assigning weights based on user-defined parameters such as safety and comfort. Various methods are highlighted, including the predictive consistency map and neural motion planners that leverage cost volumes to predict future trajectories. Safety considerations are addressed through the application of safety maps to analyze actions within a safe set.

Moreover, the paper discusses how end-to-end models produce steering angles and speed as outputs while requiring calibration according to vehicle dynamics. Auxiliary outputs, such as segmentation maps and occupancy predictions, enhance model functionality and improve the interpretability of driving actions.

Finally, the survey touches on learning approaches, particularly imitation learning, which focuses on learning from expert demonstrations to refine the autonomy of driving systems. Overall, the paper presents a comprehensive overview of the techniques and methodologies driving advancements in autonomous vehicle navigation and control.

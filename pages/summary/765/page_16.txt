The paper discusses recent advancements in end-to-end autonomous driving, focusing on safety testing methodologies and evaluation metrics. Key methods for ensuring safety include generating neuron coverage to identify false actions, designing diverse unsafe test cases, and employing optimization-based and GAN-based attacks to create adversarial scenarios. 

The paper highlights various testing oracles, such as metamorphic testing, which ensures control signals remain unchanged under varying conditions, and differential testing, which mandates consistent safe control for identical scenarios. Model-based oracles predict critical scenarios that could lead to system failure. Real-time monitoring is emphasized as crucial for detecting deviations from safe operation, with planners that identify collision-free routes and compensate for potential inaccuracies.

Safety evaluation metrics are classified into temporal and non-temporal categories. Temporal metrics include Time to Collision (TTC), Worst Time to Collision (WTTC), and Time to Maneuver (TTM), which assess the timing related to potential collisions. Non-temporal metrics like Crash Potential Index (CPI) and Conflict Index (CI) evaluate the probability and severity of collisions. Overall, the paper presents a comprehensive overview of methodologies and metrics that enhance the safety of autonomous driving systems through deep learning.

The paper "Recent Advancements in End-to-End Autonomous Driving using Deep Learning: A Survey" by Pranav Singh Chiba and Pravendra Singh provides an extensive overview of the End-to-End (E2E) autonomous driving paradigm, which presents a solution to the complexities and error propagation issues found in traditional modular systems. Unlike modular architectures that divide tasks into discrete sub-tasks, E2E driving maps sensory inputs directly to control outputs, enhancing efficiency and robustness.

The authors categorize recent advancements in E2E autonomous driving into various research areas, including the types of sensor inputs, outputs, learning approaches (such as imitation and reinforcement learning), and model evaluation techniques. They also discuss the explainability and safety features of these systems, highlighting advancements that have improved the interpretability of E2E models through auxiliary outputs and attention maps.

The survey identifies the current state-of-the-art in E2E autonomous driving, outlines existing challenges, and considers future research directions. The increasing interest in E2E approaches, as evidenced by a growing number of related publications, underscores the potential for this paradigm to enhance autonomous driving technologies.

This survey paper discusses recent advancements in end-to-end autonomous driving using deep learning techniques. It highlights the integration of various deep learning models in the development of autonomous driving systems and emphasizes the importance of large-scale datasets for training these models effectively.

Key concepts include the utilization of various driving simulators such as Mechanical Simulation, PreScan, and Gazebo, which facilitate the testing and validation of autonomous driving algorithms in controlled environments. The paper also references numerous public datasets that have been instrumental in advancing the field, including the BDD100K and Comma2K19 datasets, which provide diverse scenarios for training and evaluating driving models.

Additionally, the survey covers the evolution of algorithms for tasks such as vehicle detection, traffic sign recognition, and understanding driver behavior through causal reasoning. It discusses the significance of multimodal approaches and cooperative perception in enhancing the robustness and reliability of autonomous systems.

The paper concludes by underscoring the ongoing challenges and the need for further research to improve the capabilities of autonomous driving technologies, particularly in real-world applications.

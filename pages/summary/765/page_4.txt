The paper discusses recent advancements in end-to-end autonomous driving using deep learning, highlighting various driving policy approaches and their performance metrics as summarized in Table 1. The approaches include Physics-Based (PB), Classical Machine Learning (CML), Deep Learning (DL), and Reinforcement Learning (RL). DL and RL are noted for their high accuracy and wide prediction distances, albeit with high computational costs.

Table 2 provides an overview of deep learning-based motion prediction methods that utilize different backbone networks. Each method is characterized by its architecture, including CNN, RNN, and GNN, and their specific functionalities such as trajectory generation, motion behavior modeling, and trajectory prediction. The paper emphasizes the importance of these networks in extracting features, calculating interaction strengths, and integrating map information.

Reinforcement learning methods are discussed as they mimic human decision-making and optimize driving strategies through expert demonstrations, though they are often computationally intensive. The control mechanisms for motion planning are also addressed, detailing how trajectories are generated and refined to enhance safety and efficiency in real-world driving scenarios. Controllers like Proportional Integral Derivative (PID) and Model Predictive Control (MPC) are mentioned as tools for executing the computed commands for vehicle operation.

The paper discusses recent advancements in end-to-end autonomous driving using deep learning, emphasizing the importance of safety in these systems. Key methodologies such as InterFuser are highlighted, which processes multisensory data to detect adversarial events and constrain driving actions within safe parameters. The KING framework enhances collision avoidance through scenario generation, illustrating safe maneuvers during lane merges and potential collisions.

Training systems with diverse and high-quality data is crucial for safety, as evidenced by various studies that show the effectiveness of learning from critical scenarios like unprotected turns and aggressive lane changes. Techniques such as inducing adversarial scenarios and collecting expert observation datasets help improve robustness.

Incorporating safety constraints into the learning process is vital. This includes defining safety cost functions, avoiding unsafe maneuvers, and establishing collision avoidance strategies. Specific approaches include penalizing unsafe actions and ensuring that the system operates within predefined safety boundaries.

The paper underscores that implementing additional safety modules and testing mechanisms significantly enhances the overall safety of autonomous driving systems, ensuring they can navigate complex environments while minimizing risks.

The paper presents the Point 4D Transformer Network (P4Transformer), a novel approach for modeling spatio-temporal structures in raw point cloud videos without the need for point tracking. Traditional methods often rely on voxelization or point tracking, which can be computationally inefficient and challenging, especially with long videos or colorless point clouds. The proposed P4Transformer employs a point 4D convolution that encodes local spatio-temporal structures directly from raw points, significantly reducing computation time and the number of points processed by subsequent transformer layers. 

By utilizing self-attention mechanisms, the model captures global appearance and motion information across the entire video, adaptively merging related local areas based on attention weights. The effectiveness of the P4Transformer is demonstrated through experiments on video-level classification (3D action recognition) and point-level prediction (4D semantic segmentation) tasks using multiple datasets, including MSR-Action3D and NTU RGB+D.

Key contributions of the paper include:
1. The introduction of P4Transformer for spatio-temporal modeling in point cloud videos, marking the first application of transformers in this domain.
2. Development of a point 4D convolution that embeds spatio-temporal structures while minimizing the number of points for processing.
3. Extensive validation showing improved accuracy in 3D action recognition and 4D semantic segmentation across four datasets.

The paper also reviews related work in spatio-temporal modeling for grid-based videos, deep learning applications on static point clouds, and existing methods for point cloud video processing, positioning the P4Transformer as a significant advancement in the field.

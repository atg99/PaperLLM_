The referenced works focus on advancements in action recognition, particularly using deep learning techniques. Key contributions include the development of various neural network architectures, such as Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks with global context-aware attention for 3D action recognition, and the PointNet and PointNet++ frameworks for processing point clouds. Several studies emphasize the importance of temporal dynamics and spatial features, employing techniques like 3D convolutional networks and graph convolutional networks.

Datasets such as NTU RGB+D and SYNTHIA have been pivotal, providing large-scale data for training and testing models. Attention mechanisms and novel pooling strategies have also been explored to enhance recognition accuracy. The integration of depth information and skeleton-based approaches is highlighted, showcasing a trend towards using multi-modal data for improved performance. Overall, these works represent significant strides in the field, addressing challenges in recognizing human actions from both video and depth data.

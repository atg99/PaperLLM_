The paper presents the Point 4D Transformer (P4Transformer), a novel network designed to effectively model spatio-temporal dynamics in point cloud videos. Unlike traditional methods that rely on point tracking, which can be unreliable due to the irregular emergence of points across frames and dependency on point colors, P4Transformer utilizes a more robust approach. 

The architecture consists of two main components: a point 4D convolution that captures local spatio-temporal structures within the point cloud video, and a transformer that employs self-attention to analyze appearance and motion information across the entire video. This allows for the merging of related local areas based on attention weights, rather than explicit tracking of points.

Point cloud videos provide valuable visual information about the dynamics of 3D objects and environments, offering advantages in action recognition, especially under poor visibility conditions. The P4Transformer effectively addresses the challenges posed by the irregular and unordered nature of point clouds, which are often sparse and inconsistent across frames. 

Extensive experiments demonstrate the effectiveness of the P4Transformer in tasks such as 3D action recognition and 4D semantic segmentation across four benchmark datasets, highlighting its potential for improving interactions in intelligent systems with the 3D world.

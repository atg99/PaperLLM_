The paper presents results on 4D semantic segmentation using the Synthia 4D dataset, highlighting the performance of the proposed P4Transformer model compared to existing methods. The evaluation metric employed is mean Intersection over Union (mIoU), with results indicating that P4Transformer outperforms state-of-the-art methods when using three input frames. Notably, it achieves the highest accuracies across several categories, demonstrating its effectiveness in capturing temporal correlations that enhance segmentation accuracy and robustness.

The dataset used comprises videos of driving scenarios, reconstructed into 3D point cloud videos from stereo RGB-D images. The experiments reveal that while tracking can aid segmentation, it does not always lead to improved accuracy, potentially due to unreliable point tracking affecting temporal modeling.

Additionally, the paper discusses an ablation study focused on the components of the P4Transformer, specifically examining the impact of point 4D convolution's temporal and spatial radii on performance. The results suggest that these design choices are critical for effective spatio-temporal modeling in point cloud videos.

Overall, the findings underscore the potential of P4Transformer in point-level prediction tasks, particularly in the context of 4D semantic segmentation.

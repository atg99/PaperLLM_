The paper presents a network architecture for Cross DQN, focusing on user behavior modeling and decision-making in recommendation systems. The architecture includes several key components:

1. **Embedding Layer**: This layer processes user behaviors and generates embeddings for ads and organic items, which are crucial for understanding user preferences.

2. **State and Action Crossing Unit (SACU)**: This unit creates a cross matrix by combining embeddings from ads and organic items based on the user's actions. It enables the extraction of arrangement signals, which are essential for the decision-making process.

3. **Multi-Channel Attention Unit (MCAU)**: MCAU models the user's attention to various aspects of the mixed sequence, such as discounts or delivery times. It allows simultaneous focus on multiple channels, generating a latent representation that captures this attention.

4. **Sequential Decision Module (SDM)**: This module utilizes the embeddings produced by the Item Representation Module (IRM) and the SACU outputs to compute Q-values for different candidate actions. It integrates information from the previous components to facilitate sequential decision-making.

The architecture emphasizes parameter sharing and the use of auxiliary loss for batch-level constraints. The combination of SACU and MCAU enables efficient modeling of user preferences and decision-making in a structured manner, ultimately enhancing the effectiveness of recommendations.

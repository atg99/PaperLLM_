The provided references highlight significant contributions in the fields of recommendation systems and online advertising, particularly focusing on the application of reinforcement learning and optimization techniques.

Xie et al. propose a hierarchical reinforcement learning approach for integrated recommendation systems, emphasizing the need for structured decision-making in complex environments. Yan et al. explore constrained optimization for ad allocation in feeds, addressing the balance between revenue and user experience. Zhang et al. introduce dynamic ad allocation methods for whole-page optimization, demonstrating the importance of real-time strategies.

Zhao et al. tackle e-commerce challenges by applying deep reinforcement learning with action norm penalties to combat fraud through impression allocation. They also present DEAR, a framework for optimizing online advertising impressions within recommender systems, illustrating the synergy between recommendations and advertising. Their joint learning approach further emphasizes the interconnectedness of recommending and advertising tasks.

Lastly, Zhou et al. introduce the Deep Interest Network for click-through rate prediction, showcasing advancements in predicting user interactions based on deep learning methodologies. Collectively, these works contribute to enhancing the effectiveness and efficiency of recommendation and advertising systems in various contexts.

In summary, the Cross DQN model exhibits superior performance compared to existing baselines in revenue and experience metrics, achieving improvements of 3.09% in 𝑅ad, 2.05% in 𝑅fee, 0.83% in 𝑅cxr, and 1.58% in 𝑅ex.

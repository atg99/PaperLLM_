This paper introduces a novel framework called Cross Deep Q Network (Cross DQN) aimed at improving ad allocation strategies in feeds while balancing user experience and platform revenue. The existing methods often apply a uniform constraint on the percentage of ads exposed (PAE), leading to a lack of personalization in ad placement. Cross DQN addresses this limitation by utilizing deep reinforcement learning techniques, specifically through the design of two innovative units: the State and Action Crossing Unit (SACU) and the Multi-Channel Attention Unit (MCAU). These units enhance the extraction of arrangement signals, allowing for a more tailored ad allocation process.

The paper outlines its contributions, including a superior ads allocation strategy that dynamically adjusts the number and slots of ads in a feed while maintaining a balance between personalization and PAE constraints. Additionally, the framework has been successfully deployed in the Meituan feed, yielding significant improvements in both revenue and user experience.

The related works section distinguishes between non-RL-based and RL-based dynamic ad allocation strategies, highlighting how Cross DQN incorporates arrangement signals into the RL framework to enhance performance.

The problem formulation describes the ads allocation as a Constrained Markov Decision Process (CMDP), detailing the state space, action space, reward structure, transition probabilities, discount factor, and constraints involved in the ad allocation process. The state includes candidate items, user information, and context, while actions determine whether to display ads in specific slots. The reward system integrates ad revenue, fees, and user experience, ensuring that the approach considers both immediate and long-term outcomes.

Overall, Cross DQN represents a significant advancement in personalized ad allocation strategies, addressing previous limitations and demonstrating practical effectiveness in real-world applications.

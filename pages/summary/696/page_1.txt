The paper presents Cross Deep Q Network (Cross DQN), a novel approach to ads allocation in e-commerce feeds, which aims to optimize revenue while enhancing user experience. The challenge in ads allocation arises from the need to balance the percentage of ads exposed (PAE) and user engagement, as excessive ads can detract from user experience, while too few ads can limit revenue.

Cross DQN addresses these challenges by leveraging the arrangement signal, which captures how the positioning of ads and organic items influences user behavior. Instead of focusing solely on individual item impacts, the model crosses item embeddings and utilizes multi-channel attention to better understand the sequence of displayed items. This innovative approach allows for more effective allocation strategies.

Additionally, the authors introduce an auxiliary loss function to manage PAE at the batch level, ensuring that the proportion of ads remains within an optimal range. The results from offline experiments indicate that Cross DQN outperforms existing state-of-the-art methods in terms of revenue generation and user satisfaction. Furthermore, the model has been validated through online A/B testing and is currently deployed on Meituan's platform, serving over 300 million customers.

The study highlights the importance of considering arrangement signals in ads allocation and presents a robust solution that effectively balances user experience with revenue maximization.

The paper presents the Cross DQN model, which is designed for real-time ad ranking and organic item ranking in an online service environment. It is structured into two main components: the Item Representation Model (IRM) and the Service Decision Model (SDM), both of which are trained end-to-end but deployed separately to optimize for latency. The IRM operates in parallel with ad and organic ranking systems, allowing for rapid inference without latency. A key feature of Cross DQN is parameter sharing between IRM and SDM, which enhances efficiency by enabling simultaneous computation for all items and reduces memory usage.

The experimental evaluation consists of both offline and online tests. The offline experiments involve comparing the Cross DQN model against several baselines, while the online tests utilize an A/B testing framework on the Meituan platform. The dataset for these experiments was collected in March 2021, encompassing over 12 million requests and various user and item features. The results are assessed using revenue indicators (ads revenue and service fees) and experience indicators (average conversion rate and experience score).

The paper also details the hyperparameter settings used for the model and outlines the baseline methods for comparison, which include fixed slot strategies, non-reinforcement learning strategies, and various reinforcement learning approaches like GEA, CTLRL, HRL-Rec, and DEAR. The goal is to evaluate how well Cross DQN performs relative to these methods and to investigate the impact of different design choices and hyperparameter settings on its performance.

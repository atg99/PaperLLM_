The SACU then intersects the state embeddings with these action offset matrices to form a unified matrix representation of the mixed list. 

4.4 Multi-Channel Attention Unit
The Multi-Channel Attention Unit (MCAU) further processes the unified matrix representation generated by SACU. It splits the crossing matrix into several channels, allowing for the calculation of multi-channel attention weights. This mechanism enables the model to capture different aspects of the interactions between ads and organic items, enhancing the overall ability to evaluate the arrangement signals within the mixed list.

4.5 Sequential Decision Module
The Sequential Decision Module (SDM) utilizes the outputs from SACU and MCAU to generate Q-values for various actions. By incorporating an auxiliary loss that enforces batch-level constraints, the SDM ensures that the learning process adheres to the platform-level performance constraints. Finally, the action with the highest Q-value is selected as the optimal choice for ads allocation.

In summary, this paper introduces a novel reinforcement learning architecture, Cross DQN, designed to improve ads allocation in a mixed list context. The proposed model effectively integrates state and action representations through specialized units, enhancing the extraction of meaningful signals from complex interactions between ads and organic items. The methodology aims to maximize total rewards while adhering to platform constraints, ultimately contributing to stable advertising revenue.

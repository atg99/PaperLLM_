This paper examines the impact of Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) modules on the performance of EfficientNet-B0, a foundational model in the EfficientNets series. The authors conducted experiments by repositioning and removing SE modules within the Mobile Inverted Residual Bottleneck Convolution (MBConv) blocks. The results indicate that while SE modules can enhance accuracy—evidenced by the model with SE blocks achieving a Top-1 accuracy of 76.2% compared to 75.8% without them—their use is not necessary for all MBConv blocks. 

The findings suggest that repositioning SE modules to the end of blocks may not yield beneficial outcomes. The study highlights the need for a careful balance in utilizing SE modules to optimize network efficiency. Future work should focus on modifying the architectures of EfficientNet and MBConv blocks to further enhance their efficiency.

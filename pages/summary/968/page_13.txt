This collection of references highlights significant advancements in the field of deep reinforcement learning and robotics. Key contributions include the development of algorithms that enable human-level control and effective learning strategies in complex environments. Notable works include Mnih et al.'s asynchronous methods for deep reinforcement learning, which enhance training efficiency, and Oh et al.'s value prediction network that improves decision-making in Atari games.

Schulman et al.'s proximal policy optimization algorithms provide a robust framework for policy optimization, while Sutton and Barto's foundational texts on reinforcement learning outline essential concepts and methodologies. The exploration of model-based reinforcement learning is furthered by research from Wang et al. and Watter et al., which focus on improving dynamics modeling for better control from raw sensory inputs.

The references also address the importance of intrinsic motivation and creativity in learning systems, as articulated by Schmidhuber. Overall, this body of work emphasizes the interplay between theoretical insights and practical implementations, driving progress in machine learning and robotic autonomy.

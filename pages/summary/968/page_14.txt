The paper presents a scalable trust-region method for deep reinforcement learning that utilizes Kronecker-factored approximation. The authors, Yuhuai Wu, Elman Mansimov, Roger B. Grosse, Shun Liao, and Jimmy Ba, focus on addressing the computational challenges faced in reinforcement learning by proposing an efficient approach that maintains the benefits of trust-region optimization. The method leverages Kronecker-factored curvature information to achieve scalability, enabling the application of trust-region methods to larger and more complex problems in deep reinforcement learning. The paper's findings demonstrate improved performance and stability in learning, showcasing the effectiveness of the proposed approach in various experimental settings.

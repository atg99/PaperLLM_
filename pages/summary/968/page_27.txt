The paper presents a series of figures illustrating the performance of Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) scores at various sample sizes, specifically at 100K, 200K, 500K, and 1M samples. The fractions of the PPO scores are calculated using the formula (SimPLe_score - random_score) / (ppo_score - random_score). In cases where the denominator is less than zero, both the numerator and denominator are incremented by one to ensure valid calculations. This methodology aims to provide insights into the effectiveness of PPO in comparison to random scoring at different sample sizes.

The paper presents a figure illustrating the fractions of rainbow scores at various sample sizes, specifically at 100K, 200K, 500K, and 1M samples. The fractions are calculated using the formula (SimPLe_score - random_score) / (rainbow_score - random_score). Notably, if the denominator is found to be less than 0, both the numerator and the denominator are incremented by 1 to ensure valid calculations. The figure aims to provide insights into the performance of the SimPLe method in relation to random and rainbow scores at different sampling levels.

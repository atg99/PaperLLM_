The paper discusses the performance of a model in various gaming environments, specifically highlighting its challenges in adapting to significant global changes. Notably, in the Private Eye game, the agent experiences abrupt transitions between scenes, which the model finds difficult to manage. The figures included illustrate frames from the Pong and Kung Fu Master environments, providing visual context for the model's application. Overall, the findings indicate that while the model performs well in certain scenarios, it encounters limitations when faced with drastic environmental shifts.

The paper presents a comparison of the SimPLe algorithm against Rainbow and PPO using scores at two different interaction levels, 100K and 200K. The results are illustrated in Figure 14, which shows the performance of SimPLe relative to the baselines. The scores are calculated using the formula (SimPLe_score @100K - baseline_score) / human_score, with normalization based on the average human score to facilitate comparison in a single graph. This approach highlights the effectiveness of SimPLe in various scenarios and provides insights into its performance relative to established algorithms.

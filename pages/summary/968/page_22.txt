Each model's performance is quantified by mean scores and standard deviations over five training runs, alongside benchmarks for random agents and human players.

Key findings indicate significant variability in scores across games and models. Notably, the results reveal that certain models excel in specific environments, suggesting that model architecture and training strategies profoundly influence performance. The paper emphasizes the importance of tailored approaches in reinforcement learning to optimize outcomes in diverse gaming contexts.

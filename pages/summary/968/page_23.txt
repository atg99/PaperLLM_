The paper presents a comparison of the SimPLe method against model-free benchmarks such as PPO and Rainbow across various games, evaluated over different training steps (100k, 500k, and 1 million). The results indicate that SimPLe consistently outperforms the model-free methods across most games, demonstrating superior performance metrics.

Key findings include:

1. **Alien**: SimPLe achieved a score of 616.9, significantly higher than PPO and Rainbow scores.
2. **Assault**: SimPLe scored 527.2, while Rainbow at 1 million steps reached up to 1581.8.
3. **Atlantis**: A standout performance by SimPLe with a score of 20992.5 compared to Rainbow's highest score of 848800.
4. **CrazyClimber**: SimPLe outperformed PPO and Rainbow with a score of 62583.6.
5. **PrivateEye**: SimPLe's score of 35.0 contrasts with Rainbow's notable score of 2336.7.

Overall, the findings suggest that SimPLe offers a significant advantage in training efficiency and performance in a variety of gaming environments, highlighting its potential as a robust method for reinforcement learning tasks.

In this paper presented at ICLR 2020, the authors evaluate the performance of their proposed algorithm, SimPle, by comparing it with model-free algorithms, specifically Rainbow and PPO. To ensure a fair comparison, they conducted a hyperparameter tuning for the Rainbow algorithm in a low data regime. The tuned hyperparameters included update_horizon, min_replay_history, update_period, target_update_period, and replay_scheme, with the optimal values determined through experimentation.

Five Rainbow agents were trained on the game of Pong using the selected hyperparameters, reaching a total of 1 million interactions with the environment. The average performance of these agents was then used to identify the best hyperparameter set. For the PPO algorithm, the authors utilized the standard hyperparameters outlined in the OpenAI Baselines repository. The results of these experiments, particularly focusing on performance across different interaction counts, are discussed in detail in the paper.

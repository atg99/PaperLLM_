This section presents the numerical results from experiments conducted using the SimPLe framework across seven configurations. Each configuration was tested with five experiments where the policy was determined using a softmax function with a temperature parameter T. An optimal temperature of T=0.5 was identified for most cases, as it produced less stochastic and more stable policies. However, setting T to 0 had adverse effects, likely due to increased susceptibility to model imperfections.

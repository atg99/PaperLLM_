The paper discusses the architecture of the OURDCGAN model, which is designed for efficient performance in ablation studies on the CIFAR-10 dataset. It employs a smaller number of feature maps, specifically D = 64, to enhance efficiency. The architecture includes an encoder that mirrors the generator, with the slopes of the Leaky ReLU functions set to 0.2. The model incorporates a uniform distribution U(0,1) and utilizes M = 32 for the discriminator, which features three different heads for various GAN tasks and auxiliary tasks, with K set to 4.

The architecture is detailed for both CIFAR-10 and STL-10 datasets, utilizing ResNet structures. For CIFAR-10, the encoder processes RGB images through a series of convolutional layers followed by dense layers, while the generator employs deconvolutional layers to produce images. The discriminator also follows a similar layered structure. 

For the STL-10 dataset, the architecture is adapted to handle larger input sizes, with the encoder and generator featuring multiple ResBlocks and a final sigmoid layer for output. The discriminator is similarly structured with different heads for GAN tasks and auxiliary tasks. 

Overall, the paper presents a systematic approach to configuring GAN architectures for specific datasets, highlighting the use of uniform distributions and the mirroring of encoder and generator structures.

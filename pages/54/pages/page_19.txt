positives and false negatives have similar costs, accuracy will work best.

Recall (R): The ratio (in percentile) of the total number of correctly classiﬁed anomalous records
to the total number of positive records. High recall value speciﬁes the class is correctly recognized
with a small number of FNs.

F-Score: This is the harmonic mean (in percentile) of precision and recall, and always has a value

using both precision and recall. If the costs of false positives and false negatives are very di erent,

3.1.2. Basic knowledge  of inception  
The original  Inception,  which jointly uses ﬁlters of different  
sizes ( 1 ×1 , 3 ×3 , and 5 ×5 ), is proposed  in GoogleNet  [39] . Due 
to the utilization  of these ﬁlters, Inception  can adapt to multi- 
scale objects.  However,  there are also some disadvantages,  such 
as, the increasing  of parameters,  overﬁtting,  and vanishing  gradi- 
ent. To address  these problems,  Inception-V2  combines  a couple of 
3 ×3 convolution  ﬁlters to replace  a 5 ×5 convolution  ﬁlter [7] . 
As shown in Fig. 3 (a) and 3 (b), a 5 ×5 ﬁlter and a sequence  of 
two 3 ×3 ﬁlters have the same receptive  ﬁelds, where the for- 
mer has more parameters  than the latter [7] . For further  optimiza-  
tion, Inception-V3  proposes  a better approach,  which combines  a 
1 ×Nconvolution  ﬁlter and a N ×1 convolution  ﬁlter instead  of a 
N ×Nconvolution  ﬁlter [7] . As shown in Fig. 3 (c) and 3 (d), a N ×N
ﬁlter and a sequence  of 1 ×Nand N ×1 ﬁlters have the same 
receptive  ﬁeld, where the former  has more parameters  than the 
latter. 3.1.3. The structure  of LCU-Net  
There are multi-scale  objects  in EM images,  as shown in Fig. 4 . 
Considering  that U-Net is diﬃcult  to adapt to this situation  of 
multi-scale  objects  in EM images  as shown in Fig. 4 , we propose  
LCU-Net  to address  this problem.  As the U-Net structure  shown 
in Fig. 2 , there is a sequence  of two 3 ×3 convolution  operations  
before each pooling  operation,  each up-convolution  operation,  and 
the ﬁnal convolution  operation  with Sigmoid,  so the receptive  ﬁeld 
is limited.  In contrast,  in Inception  series, convolution  ﬁlters of 
different  sizes are used to obtain various  receptive  ﬁelds. Hence, 
we propose  BLOCK-I  as shown in Fig. 5 , which incorporates  1 ×1 , 
3 ×3 , 5 ×5 , and 7 ×7 convolution  ﬁlters in parallel  [40] .

work, it increases  the memory  cost. As mentioned  in Section  3.1.2 , 
Inception-V2  uses a sequence  of two 3 ×3 convolution  ﬁlters to re- 
place a 5 ×5 convolution  ﬁlter, and Inception-V3  uses a sequence  
of 1 ×Nand N ×1 convolution  ﬁlters to replace  a N ×Nconvo-

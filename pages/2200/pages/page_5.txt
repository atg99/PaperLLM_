orders, and track shipments.  
o Inventory management: AI can track inve ntory levels, forecast demand, and

o Warehouse operations: AI -powered robots can pick, pack, and ship orders.

o Identify potential risks, such as supply chain disruptions or product recalls.  
o Optimize transportation routes to reduce costs and emissions.

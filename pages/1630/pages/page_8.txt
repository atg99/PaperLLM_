Identifying and Overcoming Limitations Four domains,
namely Grid, Logistics, Reward, and Spanner, were not
solved nearly perfectly in any mode. Interestingly, failures
can be more instructive than successes, and this is no ex-
ception. Below, we analyze the causes of these failures and
the ways to address them. Methodologically, this is impor-
tant, as the first instinct when an RL algorithm fails is to
try a different one. This may improve the numbers, but in
this case, as we will see, these changes will not be sufficientor necessary. We will demonstrate how to achieve perfect
coverage in these domains by sticking to our basic RL algo-
rithms. The limitations do not stem from RL algorithms, but
from logical and complexity considerations.

2022b). GNNs can capture the features that can be expressed

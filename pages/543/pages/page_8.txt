each cell can be made different. AutoDispNet [ 38] proposes to apply automatic architecture search
technology in order to optimize large-scale U-Net-like encoder-decoder architectures. Therefore, it
searches for three types of cells: normal, reduction, and upsampling. In the coding stage, the neural
architecture comprises alternate connections of normal cells and reduction cells. In the decoding

4 Geomet ric interpretation of 
transfer learning : positive, 
negati ve, and tabula rasa   
From Figure 3 we can see that the solution region of the 
teaching process is a convex cone defined by two 
parameters: 1) the position of the c oordinate origin 
relative to the ver tex of the cone, and 2) the angle of the 
convex cone. The  orientation of the cone is always such 
that most of it lies within the first quadrant, although the 
vertex may be in any quadrant. We call this a positive 
convex cone.  
The angle of th e convex cone i s determined solely by 
inner products between patterns . The angle represents the 
similarity between patterns in a sense of overlapping 
features.

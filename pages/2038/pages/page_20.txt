3.2.3. LID Dataset
This study has used the exact same dataset of the benchmark [ 29]. Eight spoken languages namely,
(1) English, (2) Malay, (3) Arabic, (4) Urdu, (5) German, (6) Spanish, (7) French and (8) Persian were
chosen and veriﬁed for the purpose of recognition. Audio ﬁles were recorded for each language from
its respective country’s broadcasting media channel as Table 5 provides.

We show additional calibration results for `2-robust models in Fig. 11. The overall trend of the ECE
is the same as for `1-robust models: most of the `2models are underconﬁdent (since the optimal
temperature is less than one) and lead to worse calibration before and after temperature rescaling.
The main difference compared to the `1threat model is that among the `2models there are two
models that are better-calibrated : one before (Engstrom et al. [37] with 1.41% ECE vs 3.71% ECE
of the standard model) and one after (Gowal et al. [50] with 1.00% ECE vs 1.11% ECE of the
standard model) temperature rescaling. Moreover, we can see that similarly to the `1case, the only
overconﬁdent models are either the standard one or models that maximize the margin instead of using
norm-bounded perturbations, i.e. Ding et al. [34] and Rony et al. [114].

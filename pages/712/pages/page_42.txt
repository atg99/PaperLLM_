Robust Actor-Critic.
In Figure 10, we compare robust actor-critic and vanilla one on Garnet problem G(40,15)using neural policy and neural
network to approximate robust value functions. We plot the discounted accumulative reward under the worst case v.s.
number of iterations. Our results suggest that robust actor-critic is more robust than vanilla actor-critic under the model
mismatch.

In Figure 11, we consider Garnet problem G(30,10)using direct policy parameterization, and we use a two-layer neural

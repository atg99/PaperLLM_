VideoGPT
6. Conclusion
We have presented VideoGPT, a new video generation archi-
tecture adapting VQ-V AE and Transformer models typically
used for image generation to the domain of videos with mini-
mal modiﬁcations. We have shown that VideoGPT is able to
synthesize videos that are competitive with state-of-the-art
GAN based video generation models. We have also pre-
sented ablations on key design choices used in VideoGPT
which we hope is useful for future design of architectures in
video generation.

Our method opens several avenues of research. In this paper, we only focused on direct
TD methods. In future work, we will consider the Bellman-constrained optimization problemand extend the regularization to residual methods, as they have stronger convergence guar-antees even when nonlinear function approximation is used to learn the critic (Baird 1995 ).
We will also study equivalent formulations of the constrained problem with stronger guaran-tees. For instance, the approximation of the integral introduced by the expectation over theBellman equation constraint could be addressed by using the representation theorem. Fur-thermore, we will also investigate different techniques to solve the constrained optimizationproblem. For instance, we could introduce slack variables or use different penalty functions.

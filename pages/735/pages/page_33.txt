TRPO Hyperparameters
– Hidden units per layer 128.
– Policy initial standard deviation 1.– GAE hyperparameters: λ=0.97 and γ=0.995
– ADAM hyperparameters: 20 epochs, learning rate 0 .0003, mini-batch size 128.
– Policy KL divergence bound /epsilon1=0.01.
– We also add a damping factor 0 .1 to the diagonal entries of FIM for numerical stability.
– The maximum conjugate gradient step is set to 10.
PPO Hyperparameters
– Hidden units per layer 64.
– Policy initial standard deviation 2.– GAE hyperparameters: λ=0.95 and γ=0.99
– ADAM hyperparameters (for both the actor and the critic): 20 epochs, learning rate
0.0001, mini-batch size 64.
– Policy clipping value ε=0.05.

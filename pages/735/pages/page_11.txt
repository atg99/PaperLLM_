4.5 GAE-regularization
In Sects. 4.3and4.4, we have discussed how to apply the TD-regularization when a V-function
critic is learned. The algorithms discussed, TRPO and PPO, maximize the advantage function
/hatwideA(s,a;ω)estimated using a V-function critic /hatwideV(s,ω). Advantage learning has a long history
in RL literature (Baird 1993 ) and one of the most used and successful advantage estimator is
the generalized advantage estimator (GAE) (Schulman et al. 2016 ). In this section, we build
a connection between GAE and the well-known TD( λ) method (Sutton and Barto 1998 )t o
propose a different regularization, which we call the GAE-regularization . We show that this
regularization is very convenient for algorithms already using GAE, as it does not introduceany computational cost, and has interesting connections with other RL methods.

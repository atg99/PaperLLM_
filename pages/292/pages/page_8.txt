Only using global average pooling in the squeeze

more powerful representation ability, Qin et al. [ 57]

viewpoint of compression and analysed global average
pooling in the frequency domain. They proved that
global average pooling is a special case of the discretecosine transform (DCT) and used this observation to
propose a novel multi-spectral channel attention.

pre-processing results to reduce computation. After
processing each part, all results are concatenated

activation, and a sigmoid are used to get the attention

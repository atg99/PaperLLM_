3.7.4 STGCN
To model the spatial relations within a frame and
temporal relations across frames, Yang et al. [ 178]
proposed a novel spatiotemporal graph convolutional
network (STGCN) to learn a discriminative descriptorfor a video. It constructs a patch graph using
pairwise similarity, and then uses graph convolution
to aggregate information.

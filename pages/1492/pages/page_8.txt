coming from the text mining is very detailed, the label-ing rules can be fairly simple. For example, to choose
theSmall vessel disease labeltherulesneedonly

disease entity in either the report or conclusions
part of the report. To choose the label Ischaemic

(mod:time )w i t hatime:recent entity. There are
a few added complexities to these rules, for exam-ple, a deep ischaemic stroke which is not in an

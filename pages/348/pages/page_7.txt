3.1.1 Language- and Vision-Centric Challenges
The performance of the GPT-4 model (without vision) on the USMLE Self Assessment and Sample Exam
is especially surprising as both exams make frequent use of media elements (e.g. graphs, photographs,
charts) in their questions, which do not get passed to the model. In a manual labeling exercise, we
found that the Self Assessment had 314 questions with references to media out of 2173 total (14.4% of
the dataset), while the Sample Exam had 49 questions referencing media out of 376 total (13.0% of the
dataset).

4 Medical Challenge Benchmarks
We present benchmarks for four multiple-choice datasets from MultiMedQA [SAT+22]. The benchmarks
include MedQA, PubMedQA, MedMCQA, and medical components of MMLU. MultiMedQA contains
three more datasets which are not tested here. The untested datasets are LiveQA, MedicationQA,
and HealthSearchQA; all have long answer formats that require extensive expert analysis to determine
answer quality. Additionally, HealthSearchQA does not appear to be publicly available.

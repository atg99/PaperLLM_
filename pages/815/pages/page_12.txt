Our proposed pruning evaluation based on adaptive BN turn the prediction
of sub-net accuracy into a very fast and reliable process, so EagleEye is much
less time-consuming to complete the entire pruning pipeline than other heavy
evaluation based algorithms. In this part, we compare the execution cost for
various state-of-the-art algorithms to demonstrate the eciency of our method.
Table 2 compares the computational costs of picking the best pruning strat-
egy among 1000 potential pruning candidates. As ThiNet [22] and Filter Prun-
ing [13] require manually assigning layer-wise pruning ratio, The nal GPU hours
are the estimation of completing the pruning pipeline for 1000 random strategies.
In practice, the real computation cost highly depends on the expert's heuristic
practice of trial-and-error. The computation time for AMC [7] and Meta-pruning
can be long because training either an RL network or an auxiliary network it-
self is time-consuming and tricky. Among all compared methods, EagleEye is
the most ecient method as each evaluation takes no more than 100 iterations,
which takes 10 to 20 seconds in a single Nvidia 2080 Ti GPU. So the total can-
didate selection is simply an evaluation comparison process, which also can be
done in negligible time.

•One train an autoencoder on the entire dataset (or if possible, on a

•For each observation (or input) of the portion of the dataset known to

•One sorts the observations by the RE.
•One classiﬁes the observations with the highest RE as outliers. Note
that how many observations are outliers will depend on the problem at
hand and require an analysis of the results and usually lot of knowledge
of the data and the problem.

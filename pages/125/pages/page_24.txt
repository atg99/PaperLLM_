Another important aspect is that the feature generating layer could be a
convolutional layer but could also be a dense one. There is not ﬁx rule and
testing is required to ﬁnd the best architecture for your problem and how you
want to model your latent features: as a tensor (multi-dimensional array) or
as a one-dimensional array of real numbers.

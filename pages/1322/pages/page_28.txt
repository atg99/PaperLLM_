4.2 Solution Creation
This category is composed of algorithms that explore the domain search by generating new solutions, not by moving
existing ones. This group is a significant ratio (almost 31%) of all proposals, and includes many classical algorithms like
GA [98]. A very widely exploited advantage of these methods is the possibility to adapt the generation method to the
particular problem, hence allowing for different possible representations and, therefore, easing its application to a wider
range of problems. In the following, we describe the different subcategories that result from the diverse mechanisms by which
solutions can be created.

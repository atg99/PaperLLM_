Explainable AI In contrast to the traditional approach, XAI provides us with an 
advanced method for making appropriate decisions regarding secu-
rity background processes. The user receives additional information 
with the output explaining why a prediction has been completed. 
This method can generally detect attacks and threats such as Fraud 
Detection, Intrusion Detection, Spam Detection, Malware Detection, 
and Traffic Analysis and Identification (Sharma et al. 2022)Black box models are these models. It is sometimes difficult to justify 
the predictions or decisions made due to the opacity of these complex 
models. Interpretability is one of the most critical factors in cyber 
security because it promotes trust in the model. Failure to do so may 
compromise vital information and the organisation’s vulnerability

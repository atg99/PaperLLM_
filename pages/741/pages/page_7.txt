VI. CONCLUSION
We proposed Lvio-Fusion, a tightly coupled SLAM frame-
work with stereo camera, lidar, IMU, and GPS, which fuses
sensors based on factor graph optimization. Beneﬁting from
this, other sensors can be easily fused into the framework
as new factors. To eliminate accumulated drifts and provide
global positioning, we design the segmented pose graph
optimization with GPS constraint and loop closure constraint.
To minimize the error caused by the fact that sensors have
different performance in different environments, we used a
lightweight deep reinforcement learning algorithm to adjust
each factor’s weight. The proposed method is thoroughly
evaluated on the KITTI dataset and the KAIST urban dataset.
The results show that Lvio-Fusion can achieve high accuracy,
high robustness and real-time performance. But the self-
adaptive algorithm need to be trained and evaluated with
larger datasets for generalization. Also, we plan to conduct
in-depth research on multimodal deep learning in the future

database is not very large. There is a common trend in training deep learning algorithms to make
the comparatively smaller dataset a large one using data augmentation techniques. It is reported
that data augmentation can improve the classiﬁcation accuracy of the deep learning algorithms.
The performance of the deep learning models can be improved by augmenting the existing data rather
than collecting new data. Some of the deep learning frameworks have data augmentation facility built
into the algorithms; however, in this study, authors have utilized three augmentation strategies to

3.3. Visualization of the Activation Layer  245 
We investigated the features of the image by observing which areas in the convolutional layers 246 
activated on an image by comparing with the matchi ng regions in the original images. Each layer of 247 
a CNN consists of many 2D arrays called channels. The input image was applied to different 248 
networks, and the output activations of the first convolution layer was examined.  249 
The activations for different network models is sh own in Figure 10. The activation map can take 250 
different range values and was therefore normal ized between 0 and 1. The strongest activation 251 
channels were observed and compared with the orig inal image. It was noticed that this channel 252 
activates on edges. It activates positively on li ght left/dark right edges and negatively on dark 253 
left/light right edges.  254 
Most convolutional neural networks learn to det ect features like color and edges in their first 255 
convolutional layer. In deeper convolutional layers , the network learns to detect features that are 256 
more complicated. Later layers build up their features  by combining features of earlier layers. Figure 257 
10 shows the activation map in early convolutional layers, the deep convolutional layer, and the 258

3.4. Different Experiments  260 
Three different forms of performance evaluations and comparisons were carried out in this 261 
study: two classes (normal and pneumonia), three classes (normal, bacterial pneumonia, and viral 262 
pneumonia), and two classes (bacterial pneumonia and viral pneumonia) classification using four 263 
different deep learning algorith ms through transfer learning. 264 
The experiment carried out in this study consists  of three steps. In the first step, the dataset 265 
divided into normal and pneumonia. In the second step, the dataset divided into normal, bacterial, 266 
and viral pneumonia. In the last step, the dataset divided into bacterial and viral pneumonia. An end- 267 
to-end training approach was adopted to classify normal, bacterial, and viral pneumonia images. 268

Progress in clinical laboratory medicine
The development of laboratory technology has created conditions for the establishment 
of Clinlabomics (Fig.  3). In the past decade, clinical laboratory medicine has progressed 
in four distinct areas.
Development of medical equipment
Before the 1980s, clinical laboratory equipment was relatively straightforward. And this 
situation led to the type of clinical laboratory test indexes being limited [11]. Whereas, 
with the continuous development and the progress of society and science, clinical labo -
ratory medicine has reached an unprecedented prosperous stage from the era of manual 
medical tests to semi-automatic and full-automatic analysis. Now, clinical blood, bio -
chemistry, and microbial testing in the clinical laboratory have been automated [12–14]. 
Automated equipment rapidly and efficiently increased the throughput of a laboratory

interesting  to observe whether AI and Big Data will lead to a transformation of entrepreneurship

will indeed drive a complete paradigm shift and an “explosion of knowledge ” that change the rule 
of the game (Kuh n 1970).  
Notwithstanding these essential questions, in the following we would like to “risk”  some 
concrete suggestions  and reflections for both entrepreneurship research  and practice , thereby 
considering the academic challenge of reflecting on entrepreneurship in terms of a research domain 
and a real -world phenomenon which are interlinked and co-evolving (Davidsson 2016 ).

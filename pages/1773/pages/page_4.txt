2.1 StatisticalChallengesofFederatedLearning
The naive way to solve the federated learning problem is through Federated Aver-
aging ( FedAvg )[69]. It is demonstrated can work with certain non independent
identical distribution (non-IID) data by requiring all the clients to share the same
model. However, FedAvg does not address the statistical challenge of strongly skewed
data distributions. The performance of convolutional neural networks trained with
FedAvg algorithm can reduce significantly due to the weight divergence [ 111]. Exist-
ing research on dealing with the statistical challenge of federated learning can be
grouped into two fields, i.e., consensus solution and pluralistic solution.

 The Internet of Things
The Internet of Things refers to the rapidly increasing variety of smart, 
interconnected devices and sensors and the volumes of data they generate 
(Figure  2-3). This data is transferred between devices, services, and 
ultimately, people. You may also hear similar terms such as the Industrial 
Internet of Things and the Healthcare Internet of Things, which refer to 
their respective vertical, or industry, within the Internet of Things.

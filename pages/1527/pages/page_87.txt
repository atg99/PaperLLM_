Security challenges include authentication for every team and team

Security of medical devices poses a unique threat because of their 
technological diversity. Medical devices, from health applications on a 
smartphone to insulin pumps, are becoming increasingly networked, 
leaving unique openings for hackers.[41] This kind of activity is regarded 
as dangerous, although for those more technical, it is understood that this 
can be utilized for positive benefit. Insulin pumps and continuous glucose 
monitors have been “hacked” to function as an artificial pancreas by 
enthusiasts way before pharmaceutical company or digital companies had. 
However, the key concern is that, if exploited, similar device vulnerabilities 
could lead not only to data breaches but fatalities in people relying on 
medical devices. Today, the balance between disruption and progress is 
one of real-world experience.

external vendor services and APIs are only as good as the developers that 
have developed them.

The process of machine learning begins with input data provided as 
examples, direct experience, or instructions—to identify patterns within 
the data and make better decisions in the future based on the data that was 
provided. The aim is to enable the program to learn automatically without 
human intervention or assistance and adjust rational actions accordingly.

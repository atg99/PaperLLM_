 Defining Your Machine Learning Project
Tom Mitchell provides a concise definition of machine learning:  
“ A computer program is said to learn from experience E with respect to 
some class of tasks T and performance measure P , if its performance at 
tasks in T, as measured by P , improves with experience E. ”[50]
This definition can be used to aid machine learning projects in 
assisting us to think clearly about what data is collected and utilized (E), 
what the task at hand is (T), and how we will evaluate the results (P).
 Task (T)

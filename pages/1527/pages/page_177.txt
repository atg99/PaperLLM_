154 Feedforward Neural Network
In a feedforward neural network, the sum of the weighted inputs is fed to 
the output (see Figure  4-13 ). The output is activated (typically 1) when 
the sum exceeds the unit function threshold. If it doesn’t fire, it typically 
outputs –1.

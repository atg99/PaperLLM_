 Establish a Performance Baseline
It is crucial to have a baseline to measure the performance of your 
algorithm against other iterations and model types. As there is no one 
perfect algorithm for every problem, attempt many algorithms to identify 
the relative performance of your models.
 Spend Time Cleaning Your Data
The time required to train a machine learning model varies significantly 
between algorithms. The accuracy of data and time spent on training the 
model positively influence model accuracy. Take time to clean your data to

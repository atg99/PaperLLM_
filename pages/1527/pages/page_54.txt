 Variety
The second V of data is variety. This refers not only to variations in the 
types of data but also sources and use cases. Twenty years ago, we used to 
store data in the form of spreadsheets and databases. Today, data may be 
in the form of photographs, sensor data, tweets, encrypted files, and so on. 
This variety of unstructured data creates problems for storage, mining, and 
analyzing data. This is also an area that machine learning can significantly 
assist humans in.Chapter 2  Data

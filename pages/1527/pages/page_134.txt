111Bias and variance can best be illustrated using a dartboard analogy 
(see Figure  3-9). Take a dartboard where the bull’s-eye of the dartboard 
represents a model that perfectly predicts correct values. As you move 
further away from the bullseye, the predictions become worse. If each 
new model was represented by a new dart, each throw represents a 
realization of the model given the variability in the training data. With a 
good distribution of data for training, models can predict well. Data with 
anomalies and redundancies will result in poor predictions. This can be 
visualized on a dartboard.

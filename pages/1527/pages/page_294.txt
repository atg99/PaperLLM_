• Smart medication
There are many directions to which medication 
is being made smart. Smart medication can tell a 
patient and their respective healthcare team as to 
the amount of medicine consumed, the time it was 
taken, and provide timely reminders if it looks like it 
may be missed. Today, Bluetooth-connected bottle 
caps and pill packets provide reminders to patients 
to encourage medication adherence. Bluetooth 
insulin pens and pen caps that log the time, amount, 
and kind of insulin injected typically push data from 
the device to cloud, with users engaging in a digital 
app interface to query their data. Smart asthma 
inhalers, for instance, can identify an oncoming 
attack before the wearer recognizes the symptoms.

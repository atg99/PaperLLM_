162The Apriori algorithm uses a bottom-up approach; frequent subsets are 
identified and increased one item at a time.
The Apriori method is relatively simple (see Figure  4-17 ):
• Apply minimum support to find all frequent sets of k 
items within dataset T where a frequent itemset has 
support ≥ min(sup)
• Expand selection to find frequent sets of k + 1 items 
using frequent k-itemset
The maximum size of an itemset is k, the number of items.

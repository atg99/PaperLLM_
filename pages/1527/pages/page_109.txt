86 Supervised Learning
In supervised learning, algorithms are presented with example inputs and 
the subsequently desired outputs in the form of training data, with the goal 
to learn general rules that map inputs to outputs (see Figure  3-4). Input 
data is called training data and has a known output (result) associated with 
it. The training data guides the development of the algorithm. A model is 
created through a training process in which the model makes predictions 
and is corrected when predictions are incorrect.

• Stress bands
Stress tracking and calming of the mind are two 
key markets for wearables. Wearables currently 
monitor breathing and heart rate, detecting signs 
of tension to improve breathing and the ability to 
reach a calmer state of mind. Fitness trackers offer 
mindfulness functions, and there are headbands 
that deliver waveforms to the brain.
• UV sensor
Northwestern University has developed UV sensors 
that precisely monitor a person’s UV light exposure. 
The wearable sensors, which are small enough to fit 
on a human fingernail, are wafer-thin. As a known 
and potent carcinogen, this has the potential to 
reduce overexposure to UV , complications such as 
heatstroke, and melanoma.[128]

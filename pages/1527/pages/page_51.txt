Patients and physicians are driving the adoption of big data. Patients 
drive data through clinical data, wearable devices, mobile health apps, 
and telehealth remote healthcare services. Physicians meanwhile leave 
an exhaust of clinical data (such as written notes, imaging, and insurance 
data), patient records, and machine-generated data.

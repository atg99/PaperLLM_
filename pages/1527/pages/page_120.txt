97 Specifying the Problem
The first thing when it comes to machine learning is specifying the 
problem. This involves understanding what the problem is, how we can 
solve the problem, and how it can be evaluated. It is useful to understand 
why you want to solve the problem, especially if you are creating a budget 
case for a machine learning project. This includes evaluating the following:
• Why is it important?
• What do you hope to achieve? What are your best 
hopes?
• What are the inputs and outputs for the task at hand?
• Is this data available?
• How will the results be beneficial?
• Is this exploratory?
• What are the KPIs (key performance indicators); how is 
performance measured?
• What does success look like?
• Do we have the talent on hand to attempt the task?
• What are the limitations to the task at hand? This could 
include time, finance, skillsets, experience, domain 
knowledge, data availability, and so forth.

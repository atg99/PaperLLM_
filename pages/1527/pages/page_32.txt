 What Is Machine Learning?
Machine learning is a term credited to Arthur Samuel of IBM, who in 1959 
proposed that it may be possible to teach computers to learn everything 
they need to know about the world and how to carry out tasks for 
themselves. Machine learning can be understood as an application of AI.

Summers et al. developed a predictive model utilizing a dataset 
consisting of over 120 features to identify comorbid depression and 
diabetes-related distress. Summers et al. explored several techniques 
including random forest decision trees, SVMs, naïve Bayes, and neural 
networks. Patient age and employment status were defined as the variables 
most likely to affect predictive accuracy.[64]

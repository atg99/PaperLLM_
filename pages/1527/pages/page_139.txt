 Implementation Problems
The user interface or UI is the key component in a machine learning 
application that has human interaction. This is not only where results will 
be displayed but where the most intelligence can be gathered. Rethinking 
the user interface and user experience is worth considering when engaging 
with people.
 Reporting the Results
In many settings, this is the most crucial aspect to ensure internal 
stakeholders support machine learning projects. Typically, a PowerPoint 
presentation and white paper will be good enough to present to your 
company or clients.
Frame the problem as a scientific experiment, assessing the following:
• Why—define the context of the problem and the 
motivation
• Problem—describe the issue as a question
• Solution—explain the answer to the questions 
proposed
• Findings—describe the discoveries made in the data 
and inferences made by the models
• Conclusions—evaluate what was learned from the 
project, addressing the limitations, ethical concerns, 
privacy, and data sensitives (particularly with GDPR).
Once you’ve reported your findings, it’s up to your stakeholders to

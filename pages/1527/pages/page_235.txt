 Informed Consent
Informed consent refers to the user (or patient) being aware of what their 
data will be used for. Informed consent refers to an individual being legally 
able to give consent. Typically this requires an individual to be over 18, 
of sound mind, and able to exercise choice. Consent should ideally be 
voluntary.
Scenario A demonstrates how useful data can be given a particular 
context (or use case) and demonstrates the many intricacies of informed 
consent.

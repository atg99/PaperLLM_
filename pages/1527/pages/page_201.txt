The similarity between a query q and document d is calculated as 
the product of the TF-IDF weights for the term in both the document 
and query summed over all the terms in both the query and document; 
this is divided by the product of the length of the document and length 
of the query. Practically, however, calculating Sim(q,d) would prove 
computationally expensive as the number of documents used grows.

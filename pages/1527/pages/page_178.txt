 Convolutional Neural Network
Convolutional neural networks are deep, feedforward neural networks. 
Layers are known as convolutional layers and are often used in speech 
recognition, spatial data, NLP , and computer vision problems. Separate 
convolutional layers typically apply to different aspects of a particular 
problem. For instance, in understanding whether there is a face present 
within a photograph, separate convolutional layers within a DNN may be 
used to identify different aspects of the face—the eyes, nose, ears, mouth, 
and so forth.

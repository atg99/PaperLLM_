dendrites receive an input. If the signal is high enough within a set 
duration of time, the neuron sends an electrical pulse to the terminals then 
received by dendrites of other neurons. Each perceptron has a bias, similar 
to b of the linear function y = ax + b. It moves the line up and down to fit 
the prediction with the data better.

7 CONCLUSION
In this paper, we introduce a novel self-attentional architecture
named DySAT for dynamic graph representation learning. Specif-
ically, DySAT computes dynamic node representations through
joint self-attention over the structural neighborhood and histori-
cal representations , thus effectively capturing temporal evolution
of graph structure. Our experimental results on four real-world
datasets indicate significant performance gains for DySAT over sev-
eral state-of-the-art static and dynamic graph embedding baselines.
An interesting future direction is exploring continuous-time gener-
alizations to incorporate more fine-grained temporal variations.

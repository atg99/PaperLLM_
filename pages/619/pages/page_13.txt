Results. Figure 5 visualizes the CKA confusion matrix between all model pairs
on 4 tasks. We made the following observations. First, models mastering the
same subtask has high feature similarity. Second, our factorized TSN captures
more \pure" knowledge compared with MTL2STL students. On each heatmap,
the bottom left region has high similarity (in darker red), suggesting that the
conventional distilled models still maintains high similarity with its peers even
though they are trained on dedicated tasks. In comparison, factorized TSNs
achieve smaller similarities (in upper right region), again supporting our argu-
ment that factor networks capture the disentangled factors across tasks.

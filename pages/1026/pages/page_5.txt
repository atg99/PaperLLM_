In terms of geographic distribution, data show a significant representation of more economically developed countries (MEDC), with the USA (n=20; 23.8%) and the UK (n=14; 16.7%) together accounting for more than a third of all ethical AI principles, followed by Japan (n=4; 4.8%), Germany, France, and Finland (each n=3; 3.6% each). The cumulative number of sources from the European Union, comprising both documents issued by EU institutions (n=6) and documents issued within each member state (13 in total), accounts for 19 documents overall. African and South-American countries are not represented independently from international or supra-national organisations (cf. Figure 1).

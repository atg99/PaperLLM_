3.3.5 Head – YOLOv3  
In the case of a one -stage detector, the function of the head  is to perform dense predictions. The 
dense prediction is the final prediction composed of a vector containing the predicted bounding box 
coordinates (center, height, width), the prediction confidence score, and the probability classes.  
YOLOv4 deploys the identical head as YOLOv3 for detection with the anchor -based detection 
steps, and three levels of detection granularit y (Solawetz, 2020) .

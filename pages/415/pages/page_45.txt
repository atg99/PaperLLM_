The GWHD dataset contain 3422 images, but only images containing objects (i.e., bounding 
boxes) are written in csv files.  Meanwhile, images that do not contain or contain near wheat -like 
objects (such as weeds) can be helpful in training the model, preventin g the model from being 
fooled by similar objects.   
 
Images containing objects are called positive_images , and images that do not contain 
objects are called negative_images . In order for the YOLOv5 model to access negative 
images and use them during trainin g, there also should be label text files corresponding to those 
negative images.  Since these images do not contain any objects, they do not contain any bounding 
boxes. Therefore, their label text files will be empty.

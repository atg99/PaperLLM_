2.2.2 Copy and Pointing Mechanisms .CopyNet and Pointer-generator (PG) are used to choose
subsequences in the input sequence and put them at proper places in the output sequence.
CopyNet and PG have a differentiable network architecture [ 43]. They can be easily trained
in an end-to-end manner. In CopyNet and PG, the probability of generating a target token is a
combination of the probabilities of two modes, generate-mode and copy-mode. First, they represent
unique tokens in the global vocabulary Vand the vocabulary of source sequence V𝑋. They build
an extended vocabulary Vext=V∪V𝑋∪{unk}. The difference between CopyNet and PG is the
way to calculate distribution over the extended vocabulary. CopyNet calculates the distribution by
𝑝(𝑦𝑡)=𝑝𝑔(𝑦𝑡)+𝑝𝑐(𝑦𝑡), (9)
where𝑝𝑔(·|·)and𝑝𝑐(·|·)stand for the probability of generate-mode and copy-mode. Differently, PG
explicitly calculates a switch probability 𝑝𝑚between generate-mode and copy-mode. It recycles the
attention distribution to serve as the copy distribution. The distribution over Vextis calculated by

used as the base module for a lot of knowledge-enhanced NLG work.

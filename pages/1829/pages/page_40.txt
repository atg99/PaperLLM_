A.1 Evaluation Metrics
BLEU-𝑚(short as B- 𝑚):BLEU is a weighted geometric mean of 𝑛-gram precision scores.
ROUGE-𝑚(short as R- 𝑚):ROUGE measures the overlap of n-grams between the reference and

5.3 Ablations
Varying the amount of unlabeled audio. In this study, we train on several different randomly-selec ted subsets of
pseudo-labels from the original collection generated as de scribed in Section 3. Results are given in Table 3. Increasin g
the amount of pseudo-labels strictly improves performance . The listed 53.8k hour result is using the fully-prepared
dataset as outlined in Section 3.1. WERs given are without de coding after 800k iterations of training.
Generating pseudo-labels with an LM containing overlappin g text. As discussed in Section 3.1, using an LM to
generate pseudo-labels that was trained with a corpus that i ncludes ground truth text from unlabeled audio introduces
an overlap that may unrealistically improve the quality of p seudo-labels. We show that the effect of using an LM
trained with a small amount of overlapping text to generate p seudo-labels has only a small effect on the performance
of models trained on those pseudo-labels.
Table 4 contains results for Transformer AMs with both CTC an d Seq2Seq loss as described in 2.1 trained on pseudo-
labels generated with a decoding step that uses an LM trained on an overlapping versus non-overlapping corpus. The
models used are of the same architecture as described in Sect ion 2.1. There is a small improvement in dev-other per-
formance for pseudo-labels generated from an overlapping L M, but both models generalize very similarly.

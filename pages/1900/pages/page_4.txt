Meanwhile, we summarize the infrastructure support for
PPML techniques in Section III from both software-level and
hardware-level. As corroborated by the quantitative results
in Section IV, we emphasize that software/hardware co-
design principle plays an important role in developing a fully
optimized PPML system.

7. Conclusion
This was a tutorial paper on BM, RBM, and DBN. Af-
ter some background, we covered the structure of BM and
RBM, Gibbs sampling in RBM for generating visible and
hidden variables, training RBM using contrastive diver-
gence, and training BM. Then, we introduced various cases
for states of visible and hidden units. Thereafter, CRBM
and DBN were explained in detail.

Lang et al. explore the capacity measures for deep neural networks from a geometrical

Novak et al. conduct comparative experiments to study the generalization ability

Jacobian norm and linear region counting play vital roles in the generalization ability of
networks. Additionally, the generalization bound is also highly dependent on how close
the output hypothesis is to the data manifold.

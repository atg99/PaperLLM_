One initial technique for verb phrases  
(Li et al. 2015)  
“The mouse doubleclicks  on a single click.”  
An initial technique  
Step 1: extract verb phrases using a chunker .  
Step 2: use Markov Networks to infer the 
sentiment orientation or polarity class of each 
candidate verb phrase.  
The network represents grammatical dependency 
between words in a phrase.  
Initial data with labels are extracted from titles of 
negative (1 star) and positive (5 star) reviews.

Indirect opinion sentences  
See these two sentences in a medical domain:  
“I come to see my doctor because of severe 
stomach pain”  
“After taking the drug, I got severe stomach pain”  
If we want opinions about a drug or doctor, the 
first sentence has no opinion, but the second 
implies an negative opinion about the drug.  
Some understanding is needed

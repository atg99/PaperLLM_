(1) Frequent nouns and noun phrases  
 
Nouns (NN) that are frequently mentioned are 
likely to be true aspects (Hu and Liu 2004). 
Improved (Hu and Liu, 2004) by removing some 
frequent noun phrases that may not be 
aspects (Popescu  and Etzioni , 2005)  
It identifies part -of relationship  
Each noun phrase  is given a pointwise  mutual information score 
between the phrase and part discriminators  associated with 
the product class, e.g., a scanner class.

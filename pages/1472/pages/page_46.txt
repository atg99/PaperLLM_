Implicit aspect extraction  
Many types of implicit aspect expressions: 
Adjectives/adverbs are most common.  
Lexical semantics problem  (Hartung  and Frank , 2010 ) 
Most adjectives modify specific attributes.  
“expensive”  “price,” “beautiful”  “appearance”, 
“heavy”  “weight” (Fei et al., 2012)  
Different contexts, different meanings  
“The traffic is very heavy.”  
More complex implicit aspects not studied

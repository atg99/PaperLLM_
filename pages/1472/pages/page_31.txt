Topic & word sharing: an example  
We have reviews from 3 domains and each 
domain gives a topic related to price . 
Domain 1: {price , color , cost, life} 
Domain 2: {cost, picture , price, expensive}  
Domain 3: {price , money , customer , expensive}  
If we require words appear in at least two 
domains, we get two sets (i.e., knowledge):  
{price , cost} and {price , expensive }. 
These can be used by a KBTM for better results.

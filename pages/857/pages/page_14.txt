3.  Evolution of FL   
The primitive framework of FL is FedAvg. Though it could deal with some 
lightweight Non -IID data. It is still faced with challenges of high communication 
overhead and structural heterogeneity. Recent works focus on algorithm optimization 
to improve efficie ncy and accuracy and participants’ privacy to enhance data 
protection. In this section, this study discusses about the evolution and optimization in 
the following. We mainly explore development path in algorithm optimization level 
as well as security level .

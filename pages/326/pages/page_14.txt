4.5. Security, Privacy, and Ethics
• Data Privacy and Anonymization: Addresses the use of GPT to anonymize sensitive
data, striking a balance between maintaining data utility and protecting privacy [ 32,34].
• Content Moderation and Ethical Applications: This involves using GPT for ethical
applications, such as detecting hate speech and extracting technical requirements,
ensuring content moderation, and adhering to ethical standards. Research works
in [42,45,49,50] (Technical Requirements Extraction) directly fall into this sub-category.

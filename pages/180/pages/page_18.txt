3.3. Seeing What a GAN Can Not Generate
Bau et al. (2019) visualize mode collapse at both distribution level and
instance level. They employ a semantic segmentation network to compare the
distribution of segmented objects in the generated images versus the real images.
Diﬀerences in statistics reveal object classes that are omitted by a GAN. Their
approach allows to visualize the GAN’s omissions for an omitted class and to
compare diﬀerences between individual photos and their approximate inversions
by a GAN. Fig. 16 illustrates this approach.

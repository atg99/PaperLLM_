In this appendix, we present how the internal state vector sequence h1:T
can be “marginalized” in a DVAE model formulation; that is, how we
can express htas a deterministic function of the other random variables
(and thus move from the developed form of the model to the compact
form). This is presented for the STORN model, but a similar derivation
can be obtained for the other models as well.

10.2 Inference model
As for the inference model, Leglaive et al.(2020) ﬁrst remarked that,
using the chain rule and D-separation, the posterior distribution of the
latent vectors can be expressed as follows:
pθ(z1:T|x1:T) =T/productdisplay
t=1pθ(zt|z1:t−1,xT/prime), (10.16)
where in the causal case, T/prime={t:T}, and in the noncausal case,
T/prime={1 :T}. For the causal generative model, the latent vector at a
given time step depends (a posteriori) on past latent vectors and on
present and future observations, whereas for the noncausal generative
model, it also depends on the past observations. Therefore, the authors
chose to deﬁne the variational distribution qφwith the same form:
qφ(z1:T|x1:T) =T/productdisplay
t=1qφ(zt|z1:t−1,xT/prime). (10.17)

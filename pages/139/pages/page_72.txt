STOchastic Recurrent Networks
To the best of our knowledge, STORN (Bayer and Osendorfer, 2014) is
the ﬁrst DVAE model to combine an internal deterministic state htand
an internal stochastic state zt. Bayer and Osendorfer (2014) presented
STORN in the undriven and predictive modes (i.e., with ut=xt−1).
Hereinafter, we retain this mode for STORN, VRNN, and SRNN, for
an easier comparison, but these models can also be easily set up in the
driven mode with an external input ut.

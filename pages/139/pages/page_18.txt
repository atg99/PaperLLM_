In summary, we believe that comparison of models across papers is a
diﬃcult task in essence, regardless of the eﬀorts spent by the authors of
theoriginalpapers,becauseoftheuseofdiﬀerentnotations,presentation
lines, missing information, etc. We hope that the present review paper
and accompanying code will enable the readers to access the technical
substance of the diﬀerent DVAE models, their connections with classical
models, their cross-connections, and their uniﬁcation in the DVAE class
more rapidly and “comfortably” than by analyzing and comparing the
original papers by themselves.

Similarly, we can point out the superiority of DVAE over classical
(nondeep) DBNs and SSMs, owing to the deep nonlinear layers of
information processing. Again, let us quote Chung et al.(2015):
“Drawing inspiration from simpler dynamic Bayesian net-
works (DBNs) such as HMMs and Kalman ﬁlters, the pro-

simpler DBN models, the VRNN retains the ﬂexibility to

Of course, such a statement applies to the entire DVAE family of models.

Notation remark : To ensure homogeneous notations across models,
we slightly change the notation used by Bayer and Osendorfer (2014)
by “synchronizing” xtandht; that is, in our presentation of STORN,
xtis generated from ht(which is generated from xt−1,ht−1, and zt).
In contrast, in Eq. (4) in Bayer and Osendorfer’s (2014) paper, xt+1
is generated from ht(which is generated from xt,ht−1, and zt). In
other words, we replace xtwithxt−1. This change of notation does
not change the model in essence but makes the comparison with other
models easier.

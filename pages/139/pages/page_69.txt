6.2 Inference model
For the KVAE model, the posterior distribution of all latent variables
given the observed variables, pθ(a1:T,z1:T|x1:T,u1:T), can be factorized
as follows:
pθ(a1:T,z1:T|x1:T,u1:T) =pθ(z1:T|a1:T,x1:T,u1:T)pθ(a1:T|x1:T,u1:T),
=pθ(z1:T|a1:T,u1:T)pθ(a1:T|x1:T,u1:T),

of the SSM/DKF posterior distribution pθ(z1:T|x1:T,u1:T). Let us ﬁrst
recall that applying the chain rule enables us to rewrite this distribution
as follows:
pθ(z1:T|x1:T,u1:T) =T/productdisplay
t=1pθ(zt|z1:t−1,x1:T,u1:T).(5.1)
Then, D-separation can be used to simplify each term of the product.
The structure presented in Figure 3.1 (right) shows that the zt−1node
“blocks” all information coming from the past and ﬂowing to zt(i.e.,
z1:t−2,x1:t−1, and u1:t−1). In other words, zt−1has accumulated this

pθ(zt|z1:t−1,x1:T,u1:T) =pθ(zt|zt−1,xt:T,ut:T), and therefore (with z0
being arbitrarily set)

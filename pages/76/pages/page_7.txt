We use 12-fold cross-validation for 36 monitors so that we can
get detailed knowledge from a total of 432 experiments. Fig. 7
shows the prediction error for 1 e6 h predictions on each monitor
in each folder, where the lighter the colour, the smaller the pre-diction error. From the geographical perspective, the forecast re-
sults for the downtown and the city's western monitors are better
than the average, including monitor 1 e7, 27 and 29 e33. This is
because the density of monitoring stations in the centre of Beijing is
higher than the surrounding, and the NN-based spatial combinator
can capture the spatial dependent better. On the other hand, there
are more green areas on the west of Beijing than other places
leading to stable and good air quality. However, LSTM-FC performs
poorly on the monitors located on the edge of Beijing without close
surrounding monitors, such monitor 34 e36. From the aspect of
seasonal variation, the forecast results from March to August are
more accurate than other months, and the forecast results from
November to January in winter are the worst. This is because the
winter heating in Beijing makes the smog pollution severe and the
north wind is severe sometimes so that the air quality changes
signiﬁcantly.

4. Conclusion
In this paper, we propose an LSTM-FC neural network model to
predict PM 2.5concentrations over 48 h, based on historical airquality data, meteorological data, weather forecast data. The LSTM-
FC model could memorize long-range temporal dependence and
integrate spatial information. To evaluate the performance of the
LSTM-FC model, we predict PM 2.5concentration of 36 air quality
stations in Beijing. Compared with ANN and LSTM, our proposed
model gives better predictive results based on RMSE and MAE
metrics. In the future, we will try to construct a data-driven model
to predict sudden changes in air pollution.

While the dynamic credit risk evaluation method proposed in this paper is applied to Taobao
sellers, we can also apply a hybrid AI model to carryout empirical studies of other e-commerce
platforms. Whether the conclusion in this paper is applicable to other platforms, need to be investigated
for the future study. Furthermore, it would be interesting to apply the suggested approach to other
interesting problems; e.g., dynamic evaluation of the performance of government or enterprises, and
dynamic evaluation of regional economic development based on a hybrid AI model. The authors will
follow up these issues in a future study.

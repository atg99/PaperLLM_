3.4. Machine Learning Algorithm  
Three ML techniques were employed in this study to estimate the green building 
costs. Training and testing processes were also conducted to check the suggested ML al-
gorithm’s efficiency. The training applied  70% of the database to train the proposed 
model, and the testing part applied 30% of the database to carry out the test process. We 
utilized 5-fold cross -validation to ensure the robustness and effectiveness of the suggested 
prediction models.

are the most contemporary and efficient machine learning -based prediction algorithms 
available. A review was conducted of the many ML prediction algorithms published in 
the li terature . These two methods were chosen for various reasons, including the fact that 
they are scalable, accurate, relatively quick, versatile, and give regularized model formal-
ization to control overfitting [56–58].

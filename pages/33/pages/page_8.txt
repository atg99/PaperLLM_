6. Conclusion
In this paper, we have proposed a generic, effective and
fast two-stage framework for 3D object detection. Our
method makes use of both voxel representation and raw
point cloud to beneﬁt from both of them. The ﬁrst stage
takes voxel representation as input and applies convolu-
tional operations to acquire a set of initial predictions. Then
the second stage further reﬁnes them based on raw point
clouds and extracted convolution features.
With this conceptually simple but practically powerful
design, our method is on par with existing solutions while
maintaining higher detection speed. We believe our re-
search shows a new way to properly utilize different dimen-
sions of information for this challenging and yet practically
fundamental task.

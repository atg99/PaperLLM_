5.4 Ablation study
To further explain our performance improvement, Table 2 compares our full
method with its weaker variants. Specically, to investigate the eectiveness of
commonsense knowledge, we remove the commonsense graph and instead classify
each node in our graph using a 2-layer fully connected classier after message
passing. This negatively impacts performance in all metrics, proving our method
is able to exploit commonsense knowledge through the proposed bridging tech-
nique. Moreover, to highlight the importance of our proposed message passing
and bridge renement process, we repeated the experiments with fewer steps.
We observe the performance drops signicantly with fewer steps, proving the
eectiveness of our model, but it saturates as we go beyond 3 steps.
6 Conclusion
We proposed a new method for Scene Graph Generation that incorporates exter-
nal commonsense knowledge in a novel, graphical neural framework. We unied
the formulation of scene graph and commonsense graph as two types of knowl-
edge graph, which are fused into a single graph through a dynamic message
passing and bridging algorithm. Our method iteratively propagates messages to
update nodes, then compares nodes to update bridge edges, and repeats until the
two graphs are carefully connected. Through extensive experiments, we showed
our method outperforms the state of the art in various metrics.

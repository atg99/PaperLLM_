According to Occam’s razor,  or the law of parsimony , for any two models that 
explain the occurrence of a certain event , the simple r one is preferable . To test this law 
of parsimony, a new XGBoost  model was fitted considering  only the four most important 
variables:  P11, P9, C13, and P1. Figure 15 shows that the parsimonious model , fitted to 
the test data,  had a high predictive global accuracy  (94.74%) and a  high  AUC value 
(0.928). Therefore , a parsimonious approach that considers only the most important 
variables yields an excellent  final model. With only four variables or ratios , it is possible 
to predict bank failure highly  accurate ly using the XGBoost  algorithm, after  tuning and 
selecting the best model parameter configuration.

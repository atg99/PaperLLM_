understanding how the log  odds contribution of each feature is calculated. It involves 
adding up the contributions of each feature for every tree in the ensemble in exactly the 
same way as for a single decision tree. Thus, each prediction is expressed as the sum of 
feature impacts. These impacts are not static coef ficients as in a logistic regression. The 
impact of a feature depends  on the specific path that the observation took through the 
ensemble of trees (Foster, 2017b).

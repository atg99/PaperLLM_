In summary, we built  a classification model to predict  a qualitative response 
variable consisting of banks that failed between 2001  and 2015. The model was based 
on the XGBoost  algorithm explained above . The model computes the probability that a 
bank failure occurs. In successive rounds , the algorithm seeks to fit a model that 
maximizes  its performance  for the best combination of model parameters , learning from 
the relationship between the response and its predictor s. As one of the most widely 
used  method s, cross -validation is applied  for model selection and for choosing tuning 
parameter values.  
All models were fitted in R  (R Core Team, 201 6) version 3. 3.0 using  the XGBoost  
package2 version 0.4-3 (Chen and Benesty , 201 6) and the caret  package version 6.0 -68 
(Kuhn, 201 6).

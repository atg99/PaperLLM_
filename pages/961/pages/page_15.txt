7.6 Interpretability
Dynamic networks inherit the black-box nature of deep
learning models, and thus also invite research on inter-
preting their working mechanism. What is special here is
that the adaptive inference paradigm, e.g., spatial/temporal
adaptiveness, conforms well with that of the human visual
system, and may provide new possibilities for making the
model more transparent to humans. In a dynamic network,
it is usually convenient to analyze which part of the model
is activated for a given input or to locate which part of the
input the model mostly relies on in making its prediction.
It is expected that the research on dynamic network will
inspire new work on the interpretability of deep learning.

The above training process was repeated for all 24 experimental groups (Figure 1). All deep CNN
models were trained and evaluated on an NVIDIA DGX StationTM (NVIDIA Corp., Santa Clara,
CA, USA) with an Ubuntu 18 operating system, 256 GB system memory, and four NVIDIA Telsa
V100 GPU. The building, training, validation, and prediction of DL models were performed using
the Keras [ 36] library and TensorFlow [ 37] backend engine. The initial training rate of each model
was 0.00001. A ReduceLROn-Plateau method was employed because it reduces the learning rate
when it stops improving the training performance. The RMSprop algorithm was used as the solver.

6. CONCLUSION
In this work, we showcased the potential of goal-oriented data
generation and communication policies in a remote real-time
tracking and actuation scenario. Accounting for the semantics
of information semantics could lead to significant reduction in
task-dependent error metrics and in ineffective traffic volume.

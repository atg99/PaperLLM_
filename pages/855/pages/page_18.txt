Jiangshan Yu received the Ph.D. degree from
the University of Birmingham (UK) in 2016.
He is currently Associate Director (Research)
at Monash Blockchain Technology Centre at
Monash University, Australia. Previously, he was
a research associate at SnT, University of
Luxembourg (LU). The focus of his research
has been on design and analysis of crypto-
graphic protocols, cryptographic key manage-
ment, blockchain consensus, and ledger-based
applications. In particular, Jiangshan’s recent re-
search challenges the soundness of the foundational security mod-
els and design principles of existing blockchain systems, where the
blockchain ecosystem of hundreds of billions of dollars is based upon.
He won numerous prestigious awards, including Dean’s Research Im-
pact Award (2019) and the Chinese Government Award for Outstanding
Scholar Abroad (1% worldwide, 2016).

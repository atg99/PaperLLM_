9. Conclusion
In this work, we have extended the recently introduced probability elicitation view of loss
function design to the cost sensitive classication problem. This extension was applied to
the SVM problem, so as to produce a cost-sensitive hinge loss function. A cost-sensitive
SVM learning algorithm was then derived, as the minimizer of the associated risk. Unlike
previous SVM algorithms, the one now proposed enforces cost sensitivity for both separable
and non-separable training data, enforcing a larger margin for the preferred class, indepen-

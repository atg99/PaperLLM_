6. Limitations and Future Work
The performance analysis of the state-of-the-art CNN architectures is presented in
this study to classify lung cancer into benign and malignant. The study comprised ﬁve
CNN architectures: LeNet, AlexNet, VGG16, ResNet-50, and Inception-V1, with the Adam,
RMSprop, and SGD optimizers. Future work should include a performance analysis that
can be increased to improve the classiﬁcation system using other state-of-the-art CNN
architectures, such as Darknet, EfﬁcientNet, VGG19, Xception, Inception-V3, Inception-V4,
Inception-ResNet-V2, and ResNeXt50. Various optimizers and cross-validation techniques
can be adapted to remove the randomness effect to achieve better accuracy.

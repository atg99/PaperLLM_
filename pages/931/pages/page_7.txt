B.   AI by the Technology 
A different approach to understa nding AI is to examine, not the  
problems it can or cannot solve, but instead the research and 
technology from the discipline. At a high level, AI is generall y 
considered a subfield of computer science.22 However, AI is truly an 
interdisciplinary enterprise tha t incorporates ideas, technique s, and 
researchers from multiple fields, including statistics, linguis tics, 
robotics, electrical engineering, mathematics, neuroscience, economics, logic, and philosophy, to name just a few.

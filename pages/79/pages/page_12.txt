5. Experimental Results
In this section, we present the classiﬁcation performance of the LSTM-RNN classiﬁer according to
accuracy, precision, recall, f-score, TPR and FPR on 5, 10, 20, 40, 60, 80, and 100 neurons in the hidden
layers, respectively. The classiﬁcation performance is analyzed for both binary and 5-class classiﬁcation.
For 5-class classiﬁcation, the classes include normal, DoS, probe, U2R, and R2L attacks, while for
binary classiﬁcation, the classes include normal and anomaly. We also compared the performance
of LSTM-RNN with traditional machine learning approaches such as SVM and RF using the same
mixed feature sets. We compared the performance using the 122-feature set and the 99-feature set,
which were obtained with the genetic algorithm.

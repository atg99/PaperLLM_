21 Defensive 
distillation  Distillation  A procedure to train deep neural 
network (DNN) -based classifier 
models that are more robust to perturbations. Distillation extracts additional knowledge about training points as probability vectors produced by a DNN, which is fed back into the training regimen. Distillation generates smoother classifier models by reducing their sensitivity to input perturbations. These smoother DNN classifiers are found to be more resilient to adversarial samples and have improved class generalizability properties. A type of gradient masking. [19], [4]

ALIBI. Alibi [ 34] is an open source Python library which aims at ML-based model inspection and
interpretation. The library consists of a wide range of algorithms, most of which focus on black box
models and local interpretation i.e. interpretation for the prediction to be explained at a particular
instance. This library comprises different types of explainers depending on data we are dealing with.

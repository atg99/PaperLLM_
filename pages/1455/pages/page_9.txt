5 C ONCLUSION
In this work, we have developed an adversarial training appr oach, FreeLB, to improve natural lan-
guage understanding. The proposed approach adds perturbat ions to continuous word embeddings
using a gradient method, and minimizes the resultant advers arial risk in an efﬁcient way. FreeLB
is able to boost Transformer-based model (BERT, RoBERTa and ALBERT) on several datasets and
achieve new state of the art on GLUE and ARC benchmarks. Empir ical study demonstrates that
our method results in both higher robustness in the embeddin g space than natural training and better
generalization ability. Such observation is also consiste nt with recent ﬁndings in Computer Vision.
However, adversarial training still takes signiﬁcant over head compared with vanilla SGD. How to
accelerate this process while improving generalization is an interesting future direction.

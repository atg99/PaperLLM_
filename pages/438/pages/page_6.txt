As shown in Tab. 2, with the proposed MLP mask decoder, a pure in-
crease of 3.5% is achieved, illustrating the benefits of the unshared
weight setting and using more context information. The use of
the MLP mask decoder alleviates the learning confusion issue in
the mask head and promotes robustness in dense text detection
significantly. A decrease is also observed when using IAML. The
reason behind this phenomenon is quite simple. Since the IAML
is decoupled with the proposal box in learning, it relies more on
the accuracy of the detected bounding box. As a contrast, in pixel-
aligned learning, the variation of proposal boxes serves as a data
augmentation in training, which makes it more robust on unseen
images from the testing set.

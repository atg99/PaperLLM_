4.1.2 NTU RGB+D 60 and NTU RGB+D 120
The NTU RGB+D 60 [ 45] is the second largest dataset for
3D action recognition. It consists of 56K videos, with 60
action categories and 4M frames in total. The videos are
captured using Kinect v2, with 3 cameras and 40 subjects
(performers). The dataset deﬁnes two types of evaluation,
i.e., cross-subject and cross-view. The cross-subject evalu-
ation splits the 40 performers into training and test groups.
Each group consists of 20 performers. The cross-view eval-
uation uses all the samples from camera 1 for testing and
samples from cameras 2 and 3 for training.
The NTU RGB+D 120 [ 30] dataset, the largest dataset
for 3D action recognition, is an extension of NTU RGB+D
60. It consists of 114K videos, with 120 action categoriesTable 3. Running time (ms) per video on NTU RGB+D 60 [ 45].

2.1 Question Length
The average length of questions in VQA-v2 [ 3] is6.1 words . Our datasets have a
average length of 12.25 words forVQA-Compose and15.17 forVQA-Supplement .
This is longer than VQA-v2 since each of our logically composed questions is
made up of multiple component questions.

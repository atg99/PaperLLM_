4.4 Inﬂuence of Parameters on Variable Importance
If the only goal is to achieve good predictive performance with RF, we recommend
adjusting the RF parameters based on the OOB error. However, when we also wish
to have information on the variables, or even to select variables, we must try to adjust
the RF parameters by looking at their impact on VI.

model. We start from the framework where n=100 and p=200 and represent in

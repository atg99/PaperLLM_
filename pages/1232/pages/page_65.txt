Conclusion
The main conclusion is twofold:
•The effects of pollutants are always increasing, and are therefore markers of local
pollution useful for dust modeling.
•For weather variables, we distinguish (by eliminating HR which is of low impor-
tance)
– Those that have a rather unfavorable (decreasing) effect on pollution: PL and
VV (for all stations except AIL).
– Those that have a favorable (increasing) effect: GT, PA, and VV (for AIL).
– Those that have an unfavorable and then favorable effect: T (unfavorable if cold
then favorable when it is not cold), DV .
This study on marginal effects will be supplemented by the relative importance
of the variables (see Sect. 4.5.4 ).

2.2 Sentiment analysis
The terms of sentiment analysis and opinion mining are usually used interchangeably in
research papers of data mining, machine learning, etc., although these concepts are notequivalent. The meaning of sentiment itself is still ambiguous. We will distinguish themwhen needed. To simplify the presentation, we will use the term of sentiment to denotecorresponding opinion, sentiment, appraisal, evaluation, attitude, and emotion.

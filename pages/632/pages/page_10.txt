In this paper, we introduced a fully automated deep learning framework for 3D multi-modal medical image 
registration. To this end, we considered four aspects of image registration (data augmentation, ground truth 
generation, image registration, and identification of image modality) and introduced methods to address each. 
We validated the performance of the proposed methods on CT and MRI images of the head obtained from a 
publicly available database and showed that they outperformed similar existing methods. Although this frame -
work was only tested on CT and MRI images of the head, it should be applicable to images of other organs and 
images of different modalities with minimal modifications (for example, more complex symmetry detection for 
data augmentation) and retraining.

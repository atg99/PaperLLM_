Each type of biological data has prediction tasks where it has been used effectively, machine 
learning models that are appropriate and specific challenges when using machine learning. Some 
challenges, such as high dimensionality, affect most biological data types.  
*  Normalisation means rescaling or otherwise transfor ming variables from different data sets 
with the intention that their contributions should carry roughly equal weight and their ranges are  
so that they become  comparable on a joint common scale of values. The most common way of 
achieving this is by subtracting the means of each variable and dividing by their standard 
deviations, which can also be called “standardisation”.  This is required because different 
instruments, experimental protocols etc. can produce systematic differences in measurements of  
the same quantities, rendering it difficult or impossible to compare trends between experiments.

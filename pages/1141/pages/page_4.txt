V. P ERFORMANCE ANALYSIS
In this section, we analyze the learning performance of the
proposed Z-GT2-FLS compared to MJ-GT2-FLS and two IT2-
FLSs on the high dimensional benchmark datasets, which are
White Wine, Parkinson Motor UPDRS, and AIDS.
A. Design of Experiments
All datasets are preprocessed by Z-score normalization, and
we split 70% of the data into the training set and 30% of the
data into the test set. For each dataset, we trained all FLSs us-
ing identical hyperparameters, including mbs and learning rate,
for 100 epochs, except for the Parkinson dataset, where we
extended the training to 1000 epochs. The desired PI coverage
was set as 99%( φ= [0.005,0.995]) . The experiments were
conducted within MATLAB®and repeated with 20 different
initial seeds for statistical analysis.

5.7. Image Recognition Algorithms
There are many different image recognition algorithms available, each with its own
strengths and weaknesses. The most popular algorithm is the convolutional neural network
(CNN), which is able to learn complex patterns in data. However, CNNs are not perfect, and
deep residual learning (DRL) has emerged as a promising alternative. DRL is able to learn
features that are much deeper than those learned by CNNs, and thus can achieve better
performance [ 81] on image recognition tasks. In this survey, we will review the current

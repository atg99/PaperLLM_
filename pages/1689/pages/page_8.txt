5. Conclusion
In this work, we have presented an approach to few-
shot detection for novel classes that simultaneously tackles
few-shot classiﬁcation and localization in a uniﬁed, coher-
ent way via meta-learning. We propose specialized meta-
strategies to disentangle the learning of category-agnostic
and category-speciﬁc components in a CNN based detec-
tion model. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art detec-
tion performance in a variety of realistic scenarios, includ-
ing within-domain, cross-domain, and long-tailed settings,
and under different notions of novel classes.

4.4. Performance of Wall-Following with Different Lateral Distances
In the third phase of the validation, the wall-following ability of the proposed method was
examined by conﬁguring different lateral distances (i.e., dT). Only the proposed IT2-FLS was
considered in this regard since superior performance was observed. Environments similar to the
ﬁrst four cases of the ﬁrst phase of the experiment were considered. Figure 12 shows the resulting
trails of the robot with different conﬁgurations of dT. Three settings of dT,dT= 0.5 m, 0.75 m, and 1.0 m
were considered for the analysis.

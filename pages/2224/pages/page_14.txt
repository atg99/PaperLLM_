quantum kernel, but one derived directly from the kernel and with no further notion of a quantum model. This time
the feature space is a Hilbert space Foffunctions , and due to its special construction it is called the reproducing
kernel Hilbert space (RKHS). The relevance of this feature space is that the functions it contains turn out to be
exactly the quantum model functions f(which is a bit surprising at rst: this feature space contains linear models

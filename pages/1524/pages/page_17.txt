the methodology, insights, advantages, and drawbacks. In
addition, we provide a comprehensive analysis of differ-
ent explanation methods. Furthermore, we introduce and
analyze the commonly employed datasets and metrics for
evaluating GNN explanation methods. Last but not the
least, we build three graph datasets from text data, which
are human-understandable and can be directly used for
GNN explanation tasks.

3.2. CompCos: A Closed World Model
In this section, we focus on the closed world setting,
whereCs⊂ Ct⊂ C. Since in this scenario |Ct| ≪ |C|
and the number of unseen compositions is usually lower

avoid being overly biased toward seen class predictions. In-
spired by incremental learning [ 11] and generalized few-
shot learning [ 9], we reduce this problem by replacing the
logits of the classiﬁcation layer with cosine similarities be-

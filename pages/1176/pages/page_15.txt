The proposed method depends on the estimated range of the cluster number. For each 
estimated number of clusters, the entire dataset needs to be trained, which increases 
the computational cost. The clustering algorithm used in this study is K-means++, 
which is a centroid-based clustering algorithm. What seems certain is that our method 
can also be applied to other centroid-based clustering algorithms, such as K-means and 
K-medoids. However, for non-centroid-based clustering algorithms, this may not be the 
case. The focus of our future work will be to improve the performance and suitability of 
the proposed method to estimate the potential optimal cluster number.

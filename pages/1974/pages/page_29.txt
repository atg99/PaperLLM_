This work proposes a prescriptive analytics framework that demonstrates how both
transparent predictive analytics can be achieved and combined with prescriptive analyt-
ics techniques. The study develops predictive models for identifying at-risk learners
of programme non-completion. This work demonstrates through case studies howtransparent and responsible predictive modelling can be augmented with prescriptive
analytics to produce human-readable prescriptive feedback to those at risk via recent
advances in AI using large language models.

fusion helps to improve the accuracy of emotion recognition 
and enhances the model's ability to distinguish different 
emotion categories.

capabilities, with an AUC value of 0.756 and an F1 value of

time series data. The combination of the two enables the 
model to capture emotional features more comprehensively.

the Transformer architecture to adaptively integrate 
multimodal information can effectively improve the accuracy 
and robustness of the model.

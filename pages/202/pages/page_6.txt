2.2  Multi‑agent reinforcement learning
When the sequential decision-making is extended to multiple agents, Markov Games1 are 
commonly applied as framework. The Markov Game was originally introduced by Littman 
(1994) to generalize MDPs to multiple agents that simultaneously interact within a shared 
environment and possibly with each other. The definition is formalized in a discrete-time 
setting and is denoted as follows (Littman 1994).

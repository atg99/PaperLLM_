As can be seen from Table 7, the average iteration time of the noise K-means clustering
algorithm is longer, because the noise algorithm needs to capture the clustering number of
a given dataset and initialize the clustering center. The noise K-means clustering algorithm
can obtain the better clustering number of a given GPS data, and much easier to ﬁnd
urban hotspots. It can also obtain the better clustering evaluation results, capture the
urban hotspots.

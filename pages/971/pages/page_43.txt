6. Benchmarks of Safe Reinforcement Learning

baselines have been compared on the safe RL benchmarks [ 259]. More
importantly, the Safe RL benchmarks, including single-agent and multi-agent
benchmarks, have made massive contributions to the RL community and
helped safe RL move toward real-world applications. In this section, we
investigate the popular safe RL benchmarks and try to answer the “Safety
Benchmarks” problem.

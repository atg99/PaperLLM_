5 Conclusion
With two public 3D brain datasets, we have shown that the proposed method
built on a U-Net architecture can outperform modern transformer-based meth-
ods for both inter-subject and atlas-to-subject registration tasks. Our LKU-Net
is conceptually simple, easy to implement, and has achieved state-of-the-art re-
sults, which proves U-Net is still competitive if devised properly. LKU-Net, how-
ever,istestedforuni-modalitydeformableimageregistration,andwewillextend

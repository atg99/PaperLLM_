4.3. Combination with Model-free RL 
As a natural extension, we show that the learned context 
latent vector can be utilized for improving the generalization 
abilities of model-free RL methods. Here, we briefy men-
tion that various model-free RL methods are also known 
to suffer from poor generalization (Yu et al., 2018; Packer 
et al., 2018; Zhou et al., 2019). One of the major research di-
rections for dynamics generalization in model-free RL is de-
veloping a context-conditional policy. Specifcally, Yu et al. 
(2018); Rakelly et al. (2019); Zhou et al. (2019) showed 
that a policy can be more robust to dynamics changes when 
it takes context information as an additional input. Mo-

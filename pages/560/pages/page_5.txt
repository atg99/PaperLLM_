3 DARTS AND THE CHALLENGES OF DIFFERENTIABLE NAS
In this section, we present the Differentiable ARchiTecture Search (DARTS) family, which is currently the most popular
DNAS family of approaches, and we analyze its limitations along with the development it has inspired in the literature.

Finally, once the search phase is over, the internal structure of each type of cell is discretized into the final model by
edge selection through a softmax . It is possible to derive a final architecture of any size by simply stacking repetitive
sequences of the two types of cells.
This way DARTS’ search process does not need to fully train each candidate architecture and instead use the supernet
as an approximator to get performance feedback by considering the current weights 𝑤as equivalent to the optimal
weights𝑤∗. Hence, DARTS is significantly faster than RL-based or EA-based approaches.

When to apply deep learning
Machine intelligence is useful in many situations which is equal or better than human 
experts in some cases [49–52], meaning that DL can be a solution to the following 
problems:
• Cases where human experts are not available.
• Cases where humans are unable to explain decisions made using their expertise (lan -
guage understanding, medical decisions, and speech recognition).
• Cases where the problem solution updates over time (price prediction, stock prefer -
ence, weather prediction, and tracking).
• Cases where solutions require adaptation based on specific cases (personalization, 
biometrics).
• Cases where size of the problem is extremely large and exceeds our inadequate rea -
soning abilities (sentiment analysis, matching ads to Facebook, calculation webpage 
ranks).

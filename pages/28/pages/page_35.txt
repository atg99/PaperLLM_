ResNext
ResNext is an enhanced version of the Inception Network [115]. It is also known as the 
Aggregated Residual Transform Network. Cardinality, which is a new term presented by 
[115], utilized the split, transform, and merge topology in an easy and effective way. It 
denotes the size of the transformation set as an extra dimension [116–118]. However, the 
Inception network manages network resources more efficiently, as well as enhancing the 
learning ability of the conventional CNN. In the transformation branch, different spatial 
embeddings (employing e.g. 5×5 , 3×3 , and 1×1 ) are used. Thus, customizing each 
layer is required separately. By contrast, ResNext derives its characteristic features from 
ResNet, VGG, and Inception. It employed the VGG deep homogenous topology with 
the basic architecture of GoogleNet by setting 3×3 filters as spatial resolution inside 
the blocks of split, transform, and merge. Figure  23 shows the ResNext building blocks.

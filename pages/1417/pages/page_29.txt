Traffic Elements Identify
Q: Is there any traffic element in the front view?
A: Yes, there are some traffic elements in the front view.
Q: Identify all the traffic elements in the front view, categorize them,
determine their status, and predict the bounding box around each one.
The output should be a list formatted as (c, s, x1, y1, x2, y2), where c
represents the category, s denotes the status, and x1, y1, x2, y2 are the
offsets of the top-left and bottom-right corners of the box relative to the
center point.
A: There are three traffic elements in the front view. The information of
these traffic elements are [(road sign, go straight, 907.58, 590.67, 992.54,
630.95)...].

4.2. ImageNet experiments
ImageNet is the benchmark dataset for classiﬁcation
tasks to evaluate and compare different approaches. Our
implementation details follow ResNet [17]. Speciﬁcally, we
adopt the commonly used random 224×224cropping with
scale and aspect ratio augmentation for training and adopt
SGD as the optimizer. For typical training on ImageNet,
8 GPUs are used and the batch size is set to 256 [17]. We
used 4 GPUs to train the proposed DSNet and the batch size
is set to 128 (32 per GPU). Accordingly, taking linear scal-
ing rule into account, we set the initial learning rate to 0.05
instead of the commonly used 0.1. We train the network
for 100 epochs and the learning rate is divided by 10 after
every 30 epochs. We report the single-crop classiﬁcation

Data quality is essential for developing effective modeling and planning. Data with
discrepant values, as those shown in the charts, can pose difﬁculties to machine learning
models. Therefore, data need to be processed and structured prior to analysis.
There are several treatment methods designed for this purpose, but a careful selection
is needed so that information is not impaired. In the present work, the approach followed
was the quantile method [ 59]. The quantile method removes extreme values, which are
often due to sensor reading errors, stops, or other abnormal situations. After those samples
are removed, it is possible to see more normal data distributions, such as those shown in
Figures 5 and 6.

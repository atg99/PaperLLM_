5. Conclusion
In this paper, we propose a new object detection
method called DAMO-YOLO, the performance of
which is superior to other methods in YOLO series.
Its advantages come from new techs, including
MAE-NAS backbone, efﬁcient RepGFPN neck,
ZeroHead, AlignedOTA label assignment and distillation
enhancement.

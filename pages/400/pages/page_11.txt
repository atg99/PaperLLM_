4.6. Online Video Traffic Test Experiment
We set NT at 0.08, adjust ed the CT value to find the optimal parameters, and test ed traffic videos 
that were taken using cell phones in different scenes and weathers to verify the effect of the YOLO-
UA model video traffic flow monitoring. Each video was 35 s long. Video A was shot from the 
roadside on a sunny evening, video B was shot from an overpass at noon on a sunny day, and video 
C was shot from a sidewalk on a cloudy morning. Better test results were obtained at CT = 0.78, as 
the results in Tables 5 and 6 show.

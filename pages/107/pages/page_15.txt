4.3. Detection of Power Consumption Peaks and Load Shedding
To deﬁne the instant at which the load should be disconnected, we determine the
moving mean power consumption and we consider that the maximum power consump-
tion should not exceed 15% of the average power. So, if the predicted power consump-
tion exceeds the predeﬁned value, a peak is detected and then the load is disconnected.

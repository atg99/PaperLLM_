5.2 Results from the NSL-KDD Data Set
Before analyzing the eectiveness of the perturbation methods for adversarial example gener-
ation, the 11 machine learning models are rst evaluated for performance (accuracy) using the
full NSL-KDD data set. The MLP performed the best with an accuracy of 83 :27% with the
BAG (80:20%) and LDA (79 :68%) models serving second and third best, respectively.
Recall that the PSO, GA, GAN and MC perturbation methods are used to generate ad-
versarial malicious vectors to emulate an evasion attack against the NIDS. To compare the
eectiveness of these four adversarial generation techniques, Table 1 shows the evasion rate
of malicious vectors in terms of percentage of attack trac classied as normal using the 11
classication models and compared against the unmodied, original vectors.
Compared to the baseline Monte Carlo perturbation technique, the PSO, GA and GAN
performed strictly better for the SVM, DT, MLP, and BAG machine learning models. For
the other classication models, there were varying results across the four perturbation meth-
ods. When comparing these adversarial generation techniques against the original vectors,
the NIDS evasion rate was strictly better for all classiers except for RF, GB, and LR. This
also holds true for the average evasion rate across all classiers for each adversarial example
generation algorithm. The ensemble method classiers were more resilient on average to ad-
versarial attacks though the GAN had more success against them than the other methods.
The evolutionary computation methods (PSO and GA) performed strictly better than the
deep learning method (GAN) for the SVM, NB, KNN, and LDA classiers, whereas the GAN
performed better than both PSO and GA for the DT, RF, MLP, GB, LR and BAG classiers.

3.3.1 Network Architecture
The network has three components: a base encoder, a pro-
jection head, and an output layer. The base encoder is used
to extract representation vectors from inputs. Like [ 3], an
additional projection head is introduced to map the repre-
sentation to a space with a ﬁxed dimension. Last, as we
study on the supervised setting, the output layer is used to
produce predicted values for each class. For ease of pre-
sentation, with model weight w, we useFw(·)to denote the
whole network and Rw(·)to denote the network before the
output layer (i.e., Rw(x)is the mapped representation vec-
tor of input x).

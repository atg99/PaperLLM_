4.1.5  Correlation coefficient
Correlation coefficients are used in statistics to measure how strong a relationship is 
between two variables. It is the statistical measure of the linear relationship between 
a dependent variable and an independent variable. It is represented by a lowercase 
letter ‘r’.
Here, the correlation between all the columns of the datasets is calculated to 
measure their relationship (Table  1). The results give the correlation value of each 
column in a dataset against another column in that dataset. If two columns in a data-
set have same correlated values, then one among them is removed to avoid repetition 
of values.

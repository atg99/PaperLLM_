Emre Akbas is an assistant professor at the De-
partment of Computer Engineering, Middle East
Technical University (METU), Ankara, Turkey. He
received his Ph.D. degree from the Department
of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Univer-
sity of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign in 2011. His
M.Sc. and B.Sc. degrees in computer science
are both from METU. Prior to joining METU, he
was a postdoctoral research associate at the De-
partment of Pyschological and Brain Sciences,
University of California Santa Barbara. His re-
search interests are in computer vision and deep learning with a focus
on object detection and human pose estimation.

4. Discussion
4.1. RFSI Performance
In the synthetic case study, OK performed the best because the realities were created using
OK simulation. Note that the accuracy of OK was probably overestimated because we ignored
semivariogram estimation error. The effect of that error may be substantial in case of small sample
sizes ([ 7], Chapter 6). IDW, RFsp and RFSI had similar performance and were slightly worse
than OK. Worse performance of IDW compared to OK in synthetic case studies was also found
in Zimmerman et al. [50], MacCormak et al. [51], and Nevtipilova et al. [52].

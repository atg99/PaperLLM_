•KoLA [11]: The KoLA benchmark, designed to evaluate
the knowledge-oriented capabilities of LLMs, incorpo-

understanding and application of knowledge. However,

human beliefs and values. The study acknowledged the
challenges in ensuring fairness and reducing biases in

5. Conclusions
Brain tumor segmentation is a difﬁcult task due to the complexity of MRI brain images, and it aims
to predict tumors by segmenting them through artiﬁcial intelligence models. We propose BU-Net to
segment and classify the brain tumor regions. For the deﬁnite segmentation of brain tumors, we have
proposed a novel model with modiﬁcations in encoder–decoder architecture. We have introduced
two new blocks, namely, residual extended skip (RES) and wide context (WC), into the existing U-Net
architecture. Special attention is given to the contextual features of the MRI scans which have proved to
be beneﬁcial for the segmentation of tumor regions. An increase in the valid receptive ﬁeld is achieved

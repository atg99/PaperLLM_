Unsupervised concept annotation . While COMET achieves remarkable results with human-
validated concepts given as external knowledge, we next investigate COMET’s performance on
automatically inferred concepts. In addition to CUB dataset, we consider Flowers dataset for ﬁne-
grained image classiﬁcation. To automatically extract visual concepts, we train the autoencoding
framework for landmarks discovery proposed in (Zhang et al., 2018). The encoding module outputs
landmark coordinates that we use as part coordinates. We generate a concept mask by creating a
bounding box with a ﬁxed length around landmark coordinates. Although extracted coordinates are
often noisy and capture background (Appendix F), we ﬁnd that COMET outperforms all baselines
on both CUB and Flowers ﬁne-grained classiﬁcation datasets (Table 3). This analysis shows that
the beneﬁts of our method are expected even with noisy concepts extracted in a fully automated and
unsupervised way.

4.4. Experimental Results in a Dynamic Environment
Experiments based on the proposed algorithm in a dynamic environment is performed to compare
the learning results of the D* algorithm. D* [ 43] and its variants have been widely used for mobile robot
navigation because of its adaptation on dynamic environment. During the navigation of the robot, new
information is used for a robot to replan a new shortest path from its current coordinates repeatedly.
Under the 5000 di erent dynamic environments consisting of the smallized static environment as done
in Section 4.3, and dynamic obstacles with random initial positions, the simulation was performed
around 100 times per environment.

the path of movement. In the same situation, di erent paths may occur depending on the circumstances

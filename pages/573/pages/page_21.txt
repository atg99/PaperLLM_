The training time of RFR is the time used to evaluate the splitting nodes  of all trees. In this way, t he time of 
RFR training is closely related  to the  number of trees (Trees)  and NF . Min TS and Max TN can also affect the 
number of non-leaf nodes  since they  are related to  the single tree scale and then  the RFR model size . However, the 
degree of their influences on the final training time is  less compared with the  Trees and NF . A large r number of  trees 
and more NF  can improve  prediction accuracy while  they increase the model scale and then the training time .

C. Computation Complexity
The complexity of SDR approach is O(N6.5)[20], while
the complexity of RISBFNN is only O((64M+ 804)N2).
The average running time consumed by both algorithms under
various system setups are compared in Table II. For a fair
comparison, both algorithms are executed on the same Intel
i7-8700 CPU. P4 is solved by the popular convex optimization
solver CVX [21]. As we can see, the RISBFNN runs thousands
of times faster than the SDR approach.
In practice, the computation time of passive beamforming
algorithm should not exceed the channel coherence time. In
the considered indoor scenario, assuming the maximal movin g
speed being vmax= 1.5m/s and the frequency being fc=
2.6GHz, the channel coherence time can be computed as
Tc= 13.77ms. As such, the SDR approach is inapplicable,
which makes RISBFNN a promising candidate for practical
implementation.

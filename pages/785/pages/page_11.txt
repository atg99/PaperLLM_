4.3. Results based on transfer learning with deep learning models
In this section, we performed fine-tuning based on transfer learning using deep learning models from pretrained
CNN networks. F or transfer learning parameters, the batch size was chosen as 10, the maximum epoch number
was set to 7, and the initial learning rate was assigned as 0.0004. In addition, training of the deep networks
was achieved with the stochastic gradient descent with momentum (SGDM) learning method. The training
procedure ended after 1250 iterations. The accuracy scores of these experimental studies are given in T able 8
and these scores were evaluated according to the calculated average accuracy score across the folds and their
standard deviations.

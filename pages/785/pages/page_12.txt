4.4. Results based on traditional methods
In this section, traditional methods of LBP , HOG, GLCM, and color features, which are widely used for plant
disease detection systems, have been applied. These methods extracted features directly from the images
without the requirement for a segmented operation. Properties obtained from these methods were calculated
for performance using the SVM, ELM, and KNN methods. The accuracy scores related to these studies are
given in T able 10.
As seen in T able 10, the best performances obtained using the SVM classifier were 70.9% for the LBP
method, 56.8% for the HOG method, 45.3% for color features, and 45.2% for the GLCM method.

4.2. Results based on deep feature extraction with other deep learning models
In this section, we used the fully connected layer for deep feature extraction, based on pretrained CNN models of
GoogleNet, ResNet50, ResNet101, Inception V3, InceptionResNetV2, and SqueezeNet. F or each of these models,
deep features were extracted from the layers specified in Section 3. Then these features were calculated for their
performance using the SVM, ELM, and KNN methods. The accuracy scores of these experimental studies are
given in T able 5. In addition, these accuracy scores were evaluated according to the calculated average accuracy
score across the folds and their standard deviations.

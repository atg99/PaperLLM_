Figure 12 shows an example of how Mask CTC works. The target sequence
was first initialized with the CTC outputs. Then tokens with low confidence
scores are masked and are iteratively optimized conditioning on the unmasked
tokens and input speech sequence. The length of the final token sequence
generated by Mask CTC is the same as the length of the CTC initial output,
therefore Mask CTC can only deal with the substitution error. In Higuchi
et al. [87], the length of a partial target sequence is predicted to enable Mask
CTC’s ability of handling deletion and insertion errors.

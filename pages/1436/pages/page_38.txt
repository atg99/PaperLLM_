Biography
Dr. Jinyu Li receivedhisPh.D.degreefromTheGeor-
gia Institute of Technology, Atlanta, in 2008. From 2000
to 2003, he was a Researcher in the Intel China Re-
search Center and Research Manager in iFlytek, China.
Currently, he is a Partner Applied Scientist and Techni-
cal Lead at the Microsoft Corporation, USA. He leads
a team to innovate speech modeling algorithms and
technologies that ensure industry state-of-the-art speech
recognition accuracy for Microsoft products. His major
research interests cover several topics in speech recog-
nition and processing, including end-to-end modeling, deep learning, acoutic
modeling, speech separation, and noise robustness, etc. He has published
150+ peer-reviewed scientific papers and filed around 50 patents. He is the
leading author of the book “Robust Automatic Speech Recognition – A Bridge
to Practical Applications”, Academic Press, 2015. He has been a member of
the IEEE Speech and Language Processing Technical Committee since 2017,
serving as the area chair of the International Conference on Acoustics, Speech,
and Signal Processing since 2018. He also served as the associate editor of
IEEE/ACM Transactions on Audio, Speech and Language Processing from
2015 to 2020.

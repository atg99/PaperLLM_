strong performance with only limited pretrain-
ing data; our largest dataset contains 6M transi-

compares favourably to recent works on unsu-
pervised exploration such as APT or CPT, which
require far larger amounts of data and environ-
ment interaction (250M steps or 193 days for
APT, 4B steps or 8.45 years for CPT). We expect
SGI would perform even better if used in these
large-data settingss, as we ﬁnd that it scales ro-
bustly with data (see Section 5.2).

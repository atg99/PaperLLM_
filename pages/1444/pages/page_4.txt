4. Conclusion
In this work, we proposed and evaluated a CNN based archi-
tecture for end-to-end speech recognition. A couple of model-
ing choices are discussed and compared. This model achieves
a better accuracy on the LibriSpeech benchmark with much
fewer parameters compared to previously published CNN mod-
els. The proposed architecture can easily be used to search for
small ASR models by limiting the width of the network. Ini-
tial study on a much larger and more challenging dataset also
conﬁrms our ﬁndings.

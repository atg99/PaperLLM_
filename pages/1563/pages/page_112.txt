The primary objective of the RegTech Sprint was to provide a  
Proof of Concept (PoC) that demonstrated the feasibility of the straight-through processing of regulations and semi-automated regu-latory reporting—this process is termed Digital Regulatory Reporting.  
Figure 6.2 presents the key activities that realised this objective.
The first step in Digital Regulatory Reporting is to digitise the regu-
latory provisions. As indicated in Fig. 6.2, the FCA currently publishes its Handbook of Regulations in HTML and PDF formats. Key con-cepts are linked using Hypertext. In the case of the FCA, specific FCA concepts are defined in the Handbook Glossary. In its current form, the Handbook provisions and rules are not readily machine-readable or machine-executable. The first part of the PoC was to investigate how AI could be employed to process regulatory provisions and provide Regulatory Alerts.

CHAPTER 10
Blockchain Beyond Cryptocurrencies
Pierangelo Rosati and Tilen Čuk
Abstract  It is claimed that blockchain technology has the potential to 
revolutionise how financial services firms conduct their business. This 
chapter presents the main characteristics of blockchain technology and summarises the extant research around the potential implications of blockchain adoption for four main financial activities: payments and remittance, credit and lending, trading and settlement, and compliance. Current gaps in the literature are discussed in order to identify avenues for future research.

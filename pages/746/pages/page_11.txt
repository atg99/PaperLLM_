Properties of Fenchel-Young losses.
1.Non-negativity. LΩ(z;y)≥0for anyz∈

2.Zero loss. LΩ(z;y) = 0 if and only if y=

3.Convexity. LΩis convex in z.
4.Differentiability. LΩis differentiable with

5.Smoothness. IfΩis strongly convex, then

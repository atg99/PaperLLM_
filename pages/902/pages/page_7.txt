It is noteworthy that many GCL methods usually leverage
combinations of augmentation strategies. For instance, MV-
GRL [38] rst augments the input graph via graph diu-
sion . Afterward, two graph views are generated by sub-
graph sampling and the model learns to contrast node rep-
resentations to global representations across the two views.
GRACE [148] adopts two augmentation strategies, i.e., edge
perturbation (dropping) andfeature masking , to gener-
ate augmented views of graph data. It jointly considers both
intra-view and inter-view negative pairs for the contrastive
learning objectives. gCooL [69] uses the same augmenta-
tions and considers the community information in GCL.
GraphCL [128] considers four GraphDA operations: node
dropping ,edge perturbation ,feature masking , and sub-
graph sampling , while MERIT [52] leverages graph dif-
fusion ,edge perturbation (dropping) ,subgraph sam-
pling , and feature masking to generate augmented graphs.
CSSL [133] augments graphs with the edge perturbation
(dropping) andnode dropping . Recent work SUGRL [78]
leverages feature shuing andgraph sampling , S3-CL
uses feature propagation andpseudo labeling to e-
ciently perform GCL.
Dierent from the aforementioned pre-dened GraphDA strate-gies, another line of research proposes to perform data aug-
mentation on graphs in a learnable manner. Specically,
Zhu et al. [149] propose a joint, adaptive data augmentation
scheme based on edge perturbation (dropping) andfea-
ture masking to provide diverse contexts for nodes in dier-
ent views, so as to boost optimization of the contrastive ob-
jective. GASSL [123] is an adversarial self-supervised learn-
ing framework for learning unsupervised representations of
graph data without any handcrafted views. It learns to
perform feature corruption on either the input or latent
space. AD-GCL [93] enables GNNs to avoid capturing re-
dundant information during the training by optimizing edge
perturbation (dropping) strategy used in GCL in an ad-
versarial fashion. To automatically select optimal augmen-
tation combinations for the given graph dataset, JOAO [127]
and LG2AR [39] are proposed to automatically select aug-
mentations from a given pool of augmentation types: fnode
dropping ,subgraph sampling ,edge perturbation ,fea-
ture maskingg. It is worth mentioning that there are in-
creasingly more works in GCL, we will not cover all of them
due to the space limit.

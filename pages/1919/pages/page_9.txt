Distance variance It is a special case of distance covariance where the two variables are 
the same
Properties of distance variance are: 
 I. DVar(X) = 0 if and only if X = E[X], where E is the Euclidean Distance.
 II. DVar(X) = 0 if and only every observation is the same.
 III. DVar(A+bCX) = |b| DVar(x) for all constant orthonormal matrices C, constant 
vectors A, and scalars B
 IV. DVar(X+Y) ≤ DVar(X) + DVar(Y) if and only if X and Y are independent.

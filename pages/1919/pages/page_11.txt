Step 4—Noise Addition—The noise gets calculated using the correlation sensitiv -
ity (as calculated in Step 3) and using the Laplace Mechanism. This step solves the 
problems discussed in observations 3 and 4. It is a crucial step as the working and 
effectiveness of the differential privacy algorithm primarily depends on the sensitiv -
ity parameter. By reducing the magnitude of noise to be added, one can increase the 
data utility of the protected dataset. The calculated noise is added to the record value 
to generate a noisy value.

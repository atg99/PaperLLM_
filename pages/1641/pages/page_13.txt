The following lemma estimates the diﬀerence between the
costC(θ)and the optimal value of Cby means of gradients.
It has been referred to as the “gradient domination” lemma
in [10], which serves as one of the cornerstones in proving
the convergence of the algorithms. This result is in line with
the Polyak- Lojasiewicz condition for gradient descent in
convex optimization ([17]).

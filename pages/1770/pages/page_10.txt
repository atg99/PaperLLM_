ABSTRACT
Federated learning (FL) is a machine learning setting where
many clients (e.g., mobile devices or whole organizations)
collaboratively train a model under the orchestration of
a central server (e.g., service provider), while keeping the
training data decentralized. FL embodies the principles of
focused data collection and minimization, and can mitigate
many of the systemic privacy risks and costs resulting from
traditional,centralizedmachinelearninganddatascienceap-
proaches. Motivated by the explosive growth in FL research,
this monograph discusses recent advances and presents an
extensive collection of open problems and challenges.
Peter Kairouz and H. Brendan McMahan conceived, coordinated, and edited
this work.

6. Bringing it all Together
We have explored the creation of a true many-to-many data set for the multilingual translation
of 100 languages, as well as how to eﬀectively scale Many-to-Many models through a mix of
dense and sparse scaling. In this section, we summarize our ﬁnal results, compare to existing
published work—both multilingual benchmarks and competitive directions in WMT—and
end with a human evaluation of the overall quality of our translation quality.

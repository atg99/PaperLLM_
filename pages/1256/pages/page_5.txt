Bias-Variance Decomposition
An important tool for analyzing machine learning approaches. Given a learning target and the size of training data set, it
breaks the expected error of a learning approach into the sum of three non-negative quantities, i.e., the intrinsic noise , the
biasand thevariance. The intrinsic noise is a lower bound on the expected error of any learning approach on the target;
the bias measures how closely the average estimate of the learning approach is able to approximate the target; the variance
measureshowmuch the estimate of the learning approach ﬂuctuates for the differenttraining sets of the same size.

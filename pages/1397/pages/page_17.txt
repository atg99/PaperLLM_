6 Conclusions
In this work, we present an object-contextual representation approach for se-
mantic segmentation. The main reason for the success is that the label of a
pixel is the label of the object that the pixel lies in and the pixel representa-
tion is strengthened by characterizing each pixel with the corresponding object
region representation. We empirically show that our approach brings consistent
improvements on various benchmarks.

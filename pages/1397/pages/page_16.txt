the more challenging panoptic segmentation task [31], which unies both the
instance segmentation task and the semantic segmentation task.
Dataset. We choose the COCO dataset [43] to study the eectiveness of our
method on panoptic segmentation. We follow the previous work [30] and uses all
2017 COCO images with 80 thing and 53 stu classes annotated.

Segmentation Baselines with Panoptic FPN (3 learning schedule)"8in Detec-
tron2 [66]. The reproduced Panoptic FPN reaches higher performance than the
original numbers in the paper [30] (Panoptic FPN w/ ResNet-50, PQ: 39 :2% /
Panoptic FPN w/ ResNet-101, PQ: 40 :3%) and we choose the higher reproduced

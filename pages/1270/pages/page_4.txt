Two pioneering papers on ensemble learning from the year 1990 provided initial evidence that this type
of model conguration may result in improved performance. Schapire [11] proved theoretically that a strong
classier can be generated by combining several weak classiers. Hansen and Salamon [12], on the other
hand, showed empirically that the prediction error made by an ensemble of several classiers can be smaller 75
than that of the best single classier.

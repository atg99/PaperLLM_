3.2. AlexNet  
Alex Krizhevsky introduced AlexNet in 2012 on a research project called ImageNet LargeScale 
Visua l Recognition Challenge [31]. This architecture is one of CNN architectures with a basic, simple, 
yet effective layer design. AlexNet has five convolutional layers, followed by a pooling layer on its 
fourth layer and three layers of a fully connected layer on its fifth. In AlexNet architecture, the 
convolutional kernels are extracted during the back -propagation optimization proce dure by optimizing 
with the stochastic gradient function [31]. The convolutional layer acts with the sliding convolutional 
kernel, cr eating convolved feature maps to gain information within a given neighborhood window.

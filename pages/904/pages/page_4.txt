2.2  Rotation
Rotation (Sifre and Mallat 2013) is another type of classical geometric image data augmenta-
tion; the rotation process is done by rotating the image around an axis whether in the right 
direction or the left direction by angels between 1 and 359. Rotation may be applied to images 
by a certain angle degree in an additive way. For example, rotate the image at about 30-degree 
angles. It will produce 11 images by rotation with angles 30,60,90,120,150,180,210,240,270,
300,330 angles. The rotation equation is presented in Eq. (4 ). The fx and fy is the new position 
of each pixel after the rotation process and the x and y pair of coordinates is the raw image.

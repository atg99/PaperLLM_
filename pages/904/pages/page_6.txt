2.4  Cropping
Cropping (Sifre and Mallat 2013) may also be noted as “zooming” or “scaling” in other 
scientific researches. Cropping is a process of magnifying the original image. This type 
of classical geometric image data augmentation consists of two different methods. The 
first operation is cutting an image from a start X, Y location to another X, Y location. For 
example, if the image size is 200 * 200 pixel, the image may be cut from (0, 0) to (150, 
150) location or from (50, 50) to (200, 200) location. The second operation is the image 
scaling to its original size. In the above example, the image should be rescaled to 200* 200 
pixels after the cutting operation. Equation (7) presents the scaling equation. The fx and fy 
is the new coordinates of each pixel after scaling operation and the x and y represent the 
coordinates of the original location on the image. Figure  6 illustrates examples of cropping.

2.9  Random erasing
The random erasing technique is one of the image data augmentation techniques introduced 
in Zhong et al. (2017). This is not part of geometry transformation. The basic principle of 
random erasing is to randomly erase one square in the square region of the picture. which 
proved to be effective as illustrated in Zhong et al. (2017). Figure  12 presents a sample of 
removing random rectangles from the original image.

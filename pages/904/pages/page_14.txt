There are many artistic styles for neural style transfer such as the starry night by 
Vincent Van Gogh in 1889, the muse by Pablo Picasso in 1935, composition vii by Was-
sily Kandinsky in 1913, the great wave off by Kanagawa Hokusai from 1829–1832, the 
sketch style, and the Simpsons style (Johnson et al. 2016). Figure  16 presents a different 
NST sample image.
Selecting which trends may be incredibly difficult, particularly for specialists. For 
instances like self-driving vehicles, it’s normal to think about data from day to night, 
summer to winter, or sunny to rainy days. Nevertheless, in other types of applications, 
the conventional styles to transfer into are not so clear (Shorten and Khoshgoftaar 
2019). The choice of the neural style transfer again depends on the characteristics of the 
original dataset. The method of image data augmentation includes the careful collection 
of type images to be transmitted to various image datasets. If the sample range is so 
tiny, depending on the outcome, the results will be in danger of being skewed. (Shorten 
and Khoshgoftaar 2019).

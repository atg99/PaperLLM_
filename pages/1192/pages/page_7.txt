4.5. Convergence Speed
In Fig. 3, we study the convergence speed using CC-
SSL on MixMatch and FixMatch. We observe that CCSSL
methods achieve the best performance with much fewer
training iterations. This phenomenon is the same when
training on clean pseudo-labels [19] because of the noise al-
leviation effect of CCSSL. More specifically, better pseudo
labels in the early phase helps better guide the learning pro-
cess and translate to faster convergence.

4.2. In-distribution Data Evaluation
For CIFAR10, we set λc= 0.2withTpush = 0 due
to low noise level of the dataset and no out-of-distribution
data. For high noise level STL10 and CIFAR100, we set
λc= 1.0withTpush = 0. We train 512 epochs for all
experiments because of fast convergence speed. For other
hyperparameters, optimizer and learning rate schedule, our
setting is the same as FixMatch [25]. We compare CCSSL
with MixMatch [3], ReMixMatch [2], FixMatch [25], SS-
WPL [28], LaplaceNet [23] and Comatch [19]. Since the
last three SSL methods can be viewed as an extension of
FixMatch, we directly apply CCSSL on FixMatch for a fair
comparison.

V. C ONCLUSIONS
This paper points out two problems lying in current
RNN/CNN-based scene text recognizers and proposes a no-
recurrence model aiming at increasing computation par-
allelization and performance. Experiments demonstrate its
superiority on accuracy and training speed. We intend to
extend the idea to end-to-end text spotting system.

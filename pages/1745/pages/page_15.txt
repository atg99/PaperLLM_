in the cyber security field, they are affected by several open issues. In this paper, we consider adversarial attacks where the machine-learning model is compromised to induce an output favourable to the attacker. Literature on this subject is still immature, and most documented examples of adversarial attacks against security systems consider only few algorithms and few application areas.

4.2 Google Vizier
Google Vizier (Golovin et al., 2017) is a scalable service instead of a library for black-box
optimization based on Googles Cloud Machine Learning subsystem. The most prominent
advantage is its ease of use. With the service, users only need to choose an involved search
algorithm and submit a conguration le, and then they will be provided with a suggested
hyper-parameter set. All other setup tasks have been conducted by Google Vizier, such
as the deployment of the system, management of computational resources and memory,
and scheduling of trials. The simple client work
ow and minimal conguration make it
especially friendly for starters (Figure 17), while for experienced users it also allows them
to customize their algorithm with the algorithm playground. Although Google Vizier is
closed-source in infrastructure, it is easy to change or design new algorithms for HPO.

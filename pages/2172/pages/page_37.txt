5.1.4 Modied Condition/Decision Coverage (MC/DC)
Modied Condition/Decision Coverage (MC/DC) [Hayhurst et al., 2001] is a
method of ensuring adequate testing for safety-critical software. At its core is
the idea that if a choice can be made, all the possible factors (conditions) that
contribute to that choice (decision) must be tested. For traditional software, both
conditions and the decision are usually Boolean variables or Boolean expressions.

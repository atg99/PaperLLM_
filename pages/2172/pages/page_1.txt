In the past few years, signicant progress has been made on deep neural
networks (DNNs) in achieving human-level performance on several long-standing
tasks. With the broader deployment of DNNs on various applications, the con-
cerns over their safety and trustworthiness have been raised in public, especially
after the widely reported fatal incidents involving self-driving cars. Research to
address these concerns is particularly active, with a signicant number of papers
released in the past few years. This survey paper conducts a review of the current
research eort into making DNNs safe and trustworthy, by focusing on four
aspects: verication, testing, adversarial attack and defence, and interpretability.
In total, we survey 202 papers, most of which were published after 2017.

Broader Impact
The results of this paper improves the performance of policy-gradient methods for reinforcement
learning, as well as our understanding to the existing methods. Through reinforcement learning, our
study will also beneﬁt several research communities such as machine learning and robotics. We do
not believe that the results in this work will cause any ethical issue, or put anyone at a disadvantage
in our society.

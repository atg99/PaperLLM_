Contraction mapping
LetB(E) be the set of all bounded real-valued functions defined on an arbitrary
setE. With every function R:E→Rthat belongs to B(E), we associate the
scalar:
∥R∥∞= sup
e∈E|R(e)|. (23)
A mapping G:B(E)→B(E) is said to be a contraction mapping if there exists a
scalar ρ <1 such that:
∥GR−GR′∥∞≤ρ∥R−R′∥∞∀R, R′∈B(E). (24)

Human role in future human -computer symbiosis . Licklider (1960) proposed the well-
known concept of human -computer symbiosis, pointing out that the human brain could be tightly 
coupled with computers to form a collaborative relationship. In the long run, people argue that the 
human-machine hybrid intelligence will form an effective productive partnership to achieve a true 
human -machine symbiosis at both systemic and biological levels, eventually enabling a merger 
between humans and machines ( e.g., Gerber  et al., 2020 ). HCI profes sionals need to ensure that 
such a human -machine symbiosis will deliver the most value to humans  and fully ensure that 
human functions and roles are seamlessly integrated into AI systems as the ultimate decision 
makers without harming humans , eventually moving towards a so called “human -machine 
symbiosis world” with humans at the center ( Gerber  et al., 2020), instead of a “machine world” .

From the HCAI perspective, we adv ocate human -controlled AI  through human -controlled 
autonomy  (see Figure 1) . At present, w e are in the transition from human -centered automation to 
human -controlled autonomy. The effort to address the classic “ironies of automation” issue has 
existed over 30 years, but is still not completely solved ( Bainbridge, 1983; Strauch, 2017 ). Today, 
we encounter new ironies : autonomous systems that exhibit  unique characteristics compared with 
traditional automation . The potentially amplified impacts of autonomy plus its unique autonomous 
characteristics compels us to take the lessons learned from automation and explore new approaches 
beyond what we have done in autom ation to address the new challenges , driving towards human -
controlled autonomy and avoiding excessive autonomy (Shneiderman, 2020a) , based on the HCAI 
design goal of human -controlled AI (see Figure 1) .

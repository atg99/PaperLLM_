4.1 Enhancing current HCI methods  
Research shows that t here are a lack of effective methods for designing AI systems  and HCI 
professionals  have had difficulty perform ing the typical HCI activities of conceptualization, rapid 
prototyping, and testing ( Yang  et al., 2020; Holmquist , 2017; Dove et al., 2017; van Allen. , 2018 ). 
Driven  by the unique characteristics of AI technology  as discussed earlier, there are new needs that 
pose challenges to the existing HCI methods. For instance, the pervasive computing environment 
and eco systems of artifacts, services, and data has challenged the existing HCI methods that fo cus 
on single user -artifact interaction  (Brown et al., 2017) .    
The HCI community has realized the need to enhance exi sting methods ( Stephanidis, 
Salvendy et al., 2019; Palvend o, 2019 ; Xu, 201 8; Xu & Ge, 2018) . To effectively address the 
identified unique issues of AI systems  as discussed earlier , we need to enhance existing methods 
and leverage the methods from other disciplines . To this end , we assessed  the over 20 existing 
methods of HCI, human factors, and other related disciplines  from the HCAI perspective . As a 
result , Table 4 summarizes a comparison between the current  HCI methods ( i.e., typical HCI
